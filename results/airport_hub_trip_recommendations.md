## Seasonal Considerations

### Spring (March-May)
**Best for:**
- Desert Southwest (PHX) - before extreme heat
- Texas (DFW) - wildflowers in bloom
- California Coast (LAX/SFO) - mild weather

**Avoid:**
- Mountain West (BOI, DEN) - snow/road closures
- Upper Midwest (MSP) - unpredictable weather
- Alaska - still winter conditions

### Summer (June-August)
**Best for:**
- Mountain West (BOI, DEN) - all roads open
- Upper Midwest (MSP) - ferries running, Isle Royale accessible
- Alaska (ANC) - only viable season
- New England (BOS) - peak season

**Avoid:**
- Desert Southwest (PHX) - extreme heat (110°F+)
- Southern sites (ATL, DFW) - high heat and humidity

### Fall (September-November)
**Best for:**
- New England (BOS) - fall foliage
- Southeast (ATL) - cooler temps
- Mid-Atlantic (DCA) - pleasant weather
- California (LAX/SFO) - fewer crowds

**Moderate:**
- Southwest (PHX) - still warm but bearable
- Mountain West (BOI, DEN) - early snow possible

### Winter (December-February)
**Best for:**
- Southern sites (ATL, DFW) - mild temperatures
- Desert Southwest (PHX) - ideal weather
- Florida sites (from ATL)

**Avoid:**
- Mountain West (BOI, DEN) - road closures
- Upper Midwest (MSP) - extreme cold
- New England (BOS) - snow and ice
- Alaska - not accessible

## Optimization Tips

### Multi-Hub Combinations

Some trips can be efficiently combined:

**Northeast Mega-Trip (28 days):**
- Combine DCA Trip 2 + DCA Trip 3 + BOS Trip
- Covers 35+ sites in one extended trip
- Fly into DC, work north to Boston, fly home from Boston

**Western Road Trip (28 days):**
- Combine DEN + PHX + LAX/SFO
- Fly into Denver, drive to Phoenix, continue to California
- Covers 25+ sites across Mountain West and Southwest

**Southern Circuit (21 days):**
- Combine ATL + DFW trips
- Covers 35 sites across the South

### Time-Saving Strategies

1. **Fly One-Way:** Book flights into one city, out of another
   - Example: Fly into LAX, drive to San Francisco, fly home from SFO
   - Saves backtracking time

2. **Use Regional Airports:** Sometimes smaller airports are closer
   - Example: Fly into Albuquerque for El Morro NM instead of Phoenix

3. **Prioritize Clusters:** Group nearby sites together
   - Example: All DC monuments in 2-3 days vs. multiple trips

4. **Skip Return Days:** Leave early morning, return late night
   - Maximizes on-ground time

### Cost-Saving Strategies

1. **Travel Off-Peak:**
   - Shoulder seasons (April-May, September-October)
   - Tuesday-Thursday flights are cheaper

2. **Use Airline Miles/Credit Card Points:**
   - Can eliminate flight costs entirely
   - Best value for expensive Alaska trips

3. **Mix Camping and Hotels:**
   - Camp near national parks (cheaper, better access)
   - Hotels in cities (convenience, safety)

4. **Cook Some Meals:**
   - Breakfast and lunch from groceries
   - Dinner at local restaurants (experience local culture)

5. **Annual Pass:**
   - America the Beautiful Pass pays for itself after 3-4 parks
   - Essential for multi-trip planning
