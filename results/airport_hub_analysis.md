## Methodology

**Distance Calculation:** Haversine formula (great-circle distance)
**Coverage Radius:** 500 miles from airport
**Assignment:** Each site assigned to closest airport within radius
**Source Data:** Skipped sites from `/workspace/results/trips/14day_20trips.yaml`

## Notes

- Distances are straight-line (as the crow flies), not driving distances
- Actual trip planning should account for:
  - Road networks and driving routes
  - Operating hours and seasonal closures
  - Site visit durations (typically 4 hours per site)
  - Recommended 2 sites per day maximum for sustainable travel
- Some sites appear reachable from multiple hubs - choose based on:
  - Flight costs and availability
  - Other sites in the region you want to combine
  - Seasonal weather considerations
  - Rental car vs. other transportation options
