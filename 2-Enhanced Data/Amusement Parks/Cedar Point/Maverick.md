# Maverick

**Park:** [Cedar Point](Cedar Point.md)
**Rank:** #5
**Manufacturer:** Intamin
**Rating:** 99,1% (1,817 duels)

## Height Requirement

52-78 inches (raised from 48" in May 2007 per Intamin recommendation)

*[Source](https://www.sixflags.com/cedarpoint/attractions/maverick)*
## Subjective Roughness

Smooth with quick but comfortable changes in direction. The train hugs the terrain through hairpin turns with aggressive but controlled transitions. Intamin engineering delivers a great, smooth, intense ride despite the extreme elements. Some riders note the forces can be intense but the track itself remains smooth throughout.

*[Source](https://www.themeparkjames.co.uk/theme-parks/northamerica/usa/cedar-point/rides/maverick/), [Web Search 2024](https://flowergardennews.com/what-height-is-required-to-ride-every-attraction.html)*
## Memorable Moments

- 100 ft drop at 95° beyond-vertical angle - steepest at Cedar Point
- First Twisted Horseshoe Roll ever built - two 360° corkscrews in opposite directions separated by 180° banked curve
- Surprise tunnel launch in pitch black - 400 ft at 70 mph in 3 seconds
- Two LSM launches total - lift hill launch and mid-ride tunnel launch
- Western-themed rockwork creates numerous headchopper moments
- High-speed hairpin turns that "throw you out of your seat"
- Best banked turns many riders have experienced
- 2 inversions seamlessly integrated into 4,450 ft layout

*[Source](https://coastercritic.com/2010/07/02/maverick-cedar-point-roller-coaster-reviews/), [RCDB](https://rcdb.com/3570.htm)*
## Reviews

### Golden Ticket Awards (2007)
**Rating:** Best New Ride 2007
Won Best New Ride in inaugural year. Consistently ranks in top 10 steel coasters worldwide (#10 in 2024).

### Coaster Critic Review
**Rating:** 5/5 - Favorite Coaster of All Time
"Not only the best coaster at Cedar Point but one of the best anywhere. Insane first drop, high-speed turns, airtime, great loops, intense launch, and crazy throw-you-out-of-your-seat twists."

### Incredible Coasters Review
**Rating:** 5/5
"Brilliant first drop and second launch. The best banked turns I've experienced."

### Historical Note
Original Heartline Roll element after second launch was removed during testing due to engineering concerns - replaced with straight track, becoming a unique part of Maverick's history.
