# Steel Vengeance

**Park:** [Cedar Point](Cedar Point.md)
**Rank:** #1
**Manufacturer:** Rocky Mountain Construction
**Rating:** 100,0% (1,809 duels)

## Height Requirement

52 inches minimum

*[Source](https://www.sixflags.com/cedarpoint/attractions/steel-vengeance)*
## Subjective Roughness

Superbly smooth with butter-smooth transitions. Riders consistently report it as "intense but not rough at all" despite the aggressive pacing. The RMC steel I-Box track provides smoothness throughout the 5,740 ft layout.

*[Source](https://www.coaster101.com/2018/04/30/steel-vengeance-review/), [Community Reviews](https://coasterforce.com/forums/threads/my-first-ride-on-steel-vengeance.43785/)*
## Memorable Moments

- 200 ft drop at 90° vertical angle from 205 ft lift hill
- Longest sustained airtime on massive overbanked hill
- Aggressive overbanked turn in second half with "launched" sensation
- 4 inversions including Zero-G stall fully enveloped in structure
- Continuous ejector airtime throughout - "airtime, airtime, airtime"
- No lull zones - relentless pacing for full 2:30 duration

*[Source](https://www.coaster101.com/2018/04/30/steel-vengeance-review/), [RCDB](https://rcdb.com/15411.htm)*
