# Millennium Force

**Park:** [Cedar Point](Cedar Point.md)
**Rank:** #28
**Manufacturer:** Intamin
**Rating:** 95,9% (1,817 duels)

## Height Requirement

48 inches minimum

*[Source](https://www.sixflags.com/cedarpoint/attractions/millennium-force)*
## Subjective Roughness

Very smooth and comfortable. Riders consistently describe it as "smooth and very comfortable" with a "well-tuned ride experience." The Intamin giga coaster delivers relentless speed while maintaining exceptional smoothness throughout the 6,595 ft layout. Many riders find it "extremely relaxing and purely enjoyable" despite the high speeds and forces. Smooth operation maintained for over 24 years since opening in 2000.

*[Source](https://www.themeparkinsider.com/reviews/cedar-point/), [Community Reviews](https://coasterforce.com/forums/threads/millenium-force-good-or-plain-overrated.32598/)*
## Memorable Moments

- 300 ft drop at 80° angle with "loads of airtime" - riders out of their seats the whole way down
- Cable lift hill climbs to 310 ft with stunning Lake Erie views
- Reaches 93 mph at bottom of first drop
- Massive 169 ft overbanked turn at 122° from horizontal immediately after first drop
- Three overbanked turns total - described as "wonderful and a lot of fun"
- Two tunnels providing contrast to open-air sections
- Three additional hills with smooth speed transitions
- Relentless pacing for full 2:20 duration
- Jaw-dropping lake views throughout the ride

*[Source](https://www.coaster101.com/2020/08/31/20-in-20-millennium-force-at-cedar-point/), [RCDB](https://rcdb.com/594.htm), [Coaster Critic](https://coastercritic.com/2006/08/17/millennium-force-cedar-point-coaster/)*
## Reviews

### Amusement Today Golden Ticket Awards
**Rating:** #1 Steel Coaster (10 times winner)
Voted number one steel roller coaster ten times in annual Golden Ticket Awards. Consistently ranked among world's best steel coasters since opening.

### Coaster Critic Review
**Rating:** 5/5
"Easily one of the best drops on any coaster. The overbanked turns are wonderful and a lot of fun. Smooth, well-tuned ride experience."

### Medium (Enthusiast Review)
**Rating:** 5/5 - "Hands Down, Millennium Force"
"Jaw-dropping first drop, relentless speed, and stunning lake view. A beloved classic that remains purely enjoyable."

### Historical Significance
World's first giga coaster (over 300 ft), setting new standards when it opened in 2000. Cost $25 million to construct and remains a flagship attraction 24 years later.
