# Fury 325

**Park:** [Carowinds](Carowinds.md)
**Rank:** #7
**Manufacturer:** Bolliger & Mabillard
**Rating:** 99,0% (1,772 duels)

## Height Requirement

54 inches minimum

*[Source](https://www.sixflags.com/carowinds/attractions/fury-325)*
## Subjective Roughness

Exceptionally smooth with "buttery smoothness" throughout the 6,602 ft layout. Riders consistently praise it as "surprisingly smooth for such an intense ride" and "smooth and comfortable enough for the masses" while maintaining extreme speed and forces. No reports of roughness even after 9+ years of operation.

*[Source](https://coastercritic.com/2015/03/26/fury-325-full-review-rating/), [Reviews 2024](https://www.themeparkjames.co.uk/theme-parks/northamerica/usa/carowinds/rides/fury-325/)*
## Memorable Moments

- 320 ft drop at 81° angle from 325 ft height - "feeling like you're about to fall to your death"
- Sustained 95 mph speed throughout with multiple high-speed transitions
- Airtime camel humps at the end - notable weightlessness, especially in middle seats
- Bridge tunnel passage - dramatic transition from open air to enclosed space beneath North Gate Bridge
- Turn-around spiral hill combining banking angles with airtime before bridge dive
- Lateral forces in left-side seats during high-speed curves
- Zero inversions but extreme airtime and speed throughout

*[Source](https://coastercritic.com/2015/03/26/fury-325-full-review-rating/), [RCDB](https://rcdb.com/12273.htm)*
