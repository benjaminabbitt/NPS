# Iron Gwazi

**Park:** [Busch Gardens Tampa](Busch Gardens Tampa.md)
**Rank:** #2
**Manufacturer:** Rocky Mountain Construction
**Rating:** 99,8% (1,794 duels)

## Height Requirement

48 inches minimum

*[Source](https://buschgardens.com/tampa/roller-coasters/iron-gwazi/)*
## Subjective Roughness

Surprisingly smooth for an RMC hybrid. Riders report being "pleasantly surprised at how smooth and enjoyable the ride was all the way through." Front rows offer comfortable airtime while back rows deliver intense but smooth ejection. Smoother than the original wooden Gwazi and smoother than some compare it favorably to Steel Vengeance. Some riders note shin discomfort from RMC restraints during intense moments, but track itself is smooth.

*[Source](https://www.coaster101.com/2022/02/11/iron-gwazi-our-review-and-reactions/), [Community Reviews](https://www.disboards.com/threads/intensity-of-iron-gwazi.3903000/)*
## Memorable Moments

- 206 ft drop at 91° with sustained airtime - riders "grey out at the bottom on every ride"
- Wave turn with "violent sideways airtime" even in front rows over park entrance
- Signature 540° death roll creating tumbling sensation with continuous yanking forces
- Massive outer-banked hill with sustained ejector air and lateral forces
- 12 airtime moments total - "airtime from every direction"
- Double-down and closing hill with strong twisting ejector into brake run
- 2 inversions integrated seamlessly into relentless pacing
- No downtime from lift hill through brake run

*[Source](https://www.coaster101.com/2022/02/11/iron-gwazi-our-review-and-reactions/), [RCDB](https://rcdb.com/16985.htm)*
## Reviews

### Amusement Today Golden Ticket Awards (2022)
**Rating:** Best New Roller Coaster 2022
Won Best New Roller Coaster category. North America's tallest (206 ft), fastest (76 mph), and steepest (91°) hybrid coaster.

### Coaster101 Review
**Rating:** 5/5
"Not about chucking you as hard as possible but more like an elegant and exciting hyper experience with trademark RMC flair and dynamism. Relentless pacing with no downtime."

### Theme Park Insider (2022)
**Rating:** Highly Recommended
"Busch Gardens Tampa Bay Has a Winner With Iron Gwazi" - praised for smoothness and intensity balance.

### Community Consensus (2024-2025)
**Rating:** "RMC masterpiece"
Consistently praised as smoother and more comfortable than original Gwazi. Average riders amazed at smoothness despite extreme intensity.

*[Source](https://www.coaster101.com/2022/02/11/iron-gwazi-our-review-and-reactions/), [Theme Park Insider](https://www.themeparkinsider.com/flume/202202/8787/)*
