# WORLD'S LARGEST BALL OF TWINE (Rolled by One Man) ✓ [CONTESTED]

**Location:** Darwin, Minnesota

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 12 feet diameter, 40 feet circumference, 17,400 pounds - **created by ONE MAN only** (this is the distinction from Cawker City's community ball)

**Address:** 1st St, Darwin, MN 55324 [45.0986, -94.4103]

**Hours:** Visible 24/7 (enclosed in glass gazebo); Museum varies (typically summer daily)

**Fees:** FREE; donations accepted at museum

**History:** Created by Francis A. Johnson starting March 1950. Worked 4 hours daily for 29 years. Stopped 1979 due to emphysema. Used railroad jack to rotate. Featured in "Weird Al" Yankovic's 1989 song. Town renamed street "Weird Alley" in Yankovic's honor (2019).

**Seasonal Information:** Museum limited winter hours; ball visible year-round.

**Status:** Preserved as Francis left it 1979 - no additions allowed. Protected in gazebo. Town celebrates "Twine Ball Day" second Saturday in August.

**Note:** Distinction from Cawker City: Darwin's rolled by one person vs. community effort.

**Source:** RoadsideAmerica.com
