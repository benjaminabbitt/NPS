# ENCHANTED HIGHWAY ✓

**Location:** Regent, North Dakota

**What Makes It World's Largest:** Largest Collection of World's Largest Scrap Metal Sculptures. Seven massive sculptures along 32-mile highway including Geese in Flight (110 feet tall - world's largest scrap metal sculpture).

**Address:** Exit 72 I-94, Gladstone, ND 58630 [46.8549, -102.5436]

**Hours:** 24/7

**Fees:** FREE

**History:** Created 1989-2012 by Gary Greff to attract tourism. Each sculpture over 40 feet tall. Guinness World Record.

**Seasonal Information:** Best viewed May-September; accessible in winter but snow may impact driving

**Status:** Active. [LIMITED ACCESS] Remote location, 32-mile drive along rural highway.

**Source:** Allianz Travel
