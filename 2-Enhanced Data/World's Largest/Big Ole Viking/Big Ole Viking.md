# BIG OLE VIKING [SELF-DECLARED]

**Location:** Alexandria, Minnesota

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 28 feet tall helmeted Viking warrior

**Address:** Alexandria, MN (downtown area)

**Hours:** 24/7 visible

**Fees:** FREE

**History:** Built 1965 as exhibit for New York World's Fair. Shield reads "Alexandria, Birthplace of America." Symbolizes early Norse exploration claim (based on controversial Kensington Runestone discovered 1898, later proven fake). Legend persisted.

**Status:** Active.

**Source:** Explore Minnesota
