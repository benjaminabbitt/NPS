# WORLD'S LARGEST SIX-PACK [SELF-DECLARED]

**Location:** La Crosse, Wisconsin

**What Makes It World's Largest:** World's Largest Six-Pack. Each "can" 22 feet diameter, 69 feet tall. Holds 7 million gallons total capacity.

**Address:** 1111 South 3rd Street, La Crosse, WI 54601

**Hours:** 24/7 (exterior)

**Fees:** FREE

**History:** Built 1969 as storage tanks for Heileman Brewery (now City Brewery). Painted to resemble Old Style beer cans.

**Seasonal Information:** Permanent structure year-round

**Status:** Active brewery storage tanks.

**Source:** Various
