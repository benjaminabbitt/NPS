# WORLD'S LARGEST BALL OF SISAL TWINE ✓

**Location:** Cawker City, Kansas

**What Makes It World's Largest:** Community-built ball; 46 feet circumference, 27,017+ pounds, 8,507,430+ feet of twine (~1,600+ miles), 14.6 feet diameter

**Address:** 804 Locust St, Cawker City, KS 67430 [39.5092, -98.4337]

**Hours:** Visible 24/7; call Linda Clover to add twine

**Fees:** FREE

**History:** Started December 24, 1953 by Kansas farmer Frank Stoeber. Moved to town for 1961 Centennial. Guinness certified 1973; Frank died 1974. Lost record to Minnesota 1978. Cawker City began annual "Twine-a-thon" 1982. Public can add twine year-round.

**Seasonal Information:** Accessible year-round. Annual Twine-a-thon in August.

**Status:** Active, well-maintained under protective shelter. Only sisal twine can be added. CCTV monitors prevent climbing.

**Source:** RoadsideAmerica.com
