# WORLD'S LARGEST BUFFALO (DAKOTA THUNDER) [SELF-DECLARED]

**Location:** Jamestown, North Dakota

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 60-ton concrete tribute, 26 feet tall

**Address:** Frontier Village, 500 17th Street SE, Jamestown, ND 58401

**Hours:** Check Frontier Village hours

**Fees:** Check locally

**History:** Built 1959, first giant animal roadside attraction in North Dakota. Overlooks Frontier Village. Accompanied by buffalo herd roaming nearby.

**Seasonal Information:** Best viewed May-September; viewable year-round

**Status:** Active at Frontier Village attraction

**Source:** Allianz Travel
