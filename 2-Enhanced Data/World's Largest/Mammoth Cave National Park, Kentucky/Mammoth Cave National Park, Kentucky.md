# MAMMOTH CAVE NATIONAL PARK, Kentucky ✓

**World's Longest Cave System**

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 426+ miles of surveyed passageways - 1.5 times longer than second-longest cave system globally

**Address:** 1 Mammoth Cave Parkway, Mammoth Cave, KY 42259
**GPS:** 37.1862° N, 86.1000° W

**Hours:**
- Park grounds: Open 24/7 year-round
- Visitor Center: 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM (seasonal variations)
- Cave tours: Require advance reservations via recreation.gov or 877-444-6777

**Fees:**
- Park entrance: Free
- Cave tours: $6-$60 depending on tour type and length
- Reservations strongly recommended

**History:** Native Americans explored approximately 5,000 years ago. European discovery around 1797. Formed in 325-350 million-year-old Mississippian limestone. Became National Park July 1, 1941. UNESCO World Heritage Site (1981) and International Biosphere Reserve (1990).

**Seasonal Information:** Best visited spring through fall. Cave maintains constant 54°F year-round. Peak visitation summer months and holiday weekends. Some surface trails challenging in winter though cave tours continue.

**Source:** National Park Service
