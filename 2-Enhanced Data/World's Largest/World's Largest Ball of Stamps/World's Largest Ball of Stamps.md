# WORLD'S LARGEST BALL OF STAMPS ✓

**Location:** Boys Town, Nebraska

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 32 inches diameter, 600 pounds, 4,655,000 stamps from around world

**Address:** Leon Myers Stamp Center, Boys Town Visitors Center, Omaha, NE

**Hours:** Call for current hours; (402) 498-1140

**Fees:** FREE (part of Visitors Center)

**History:** Started 1953 by Boys Town Stamp Collecting Club using golf ball as core. Created over 3 years. Featured in Ripley's Believe It or Not July 1955.

**Status:** Well-preserved; displayed in front of framed sunburst mural made of stamps. Touching allowed but adding stamps prohibited.

**Source:** Boys Town
