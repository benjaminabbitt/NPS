# WORLD'S LARGEST FRYING PAN [CONTESTED]

**Location:** Rose Hill, North Carolina

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 15 feet diameter, 45 feet circumference, 6-foot handle, 2 tons, holds 200 gallons oil, 40 gas burners, capacity 365 chickens

**Address:** Rose Hill Town Square, 510 East Main Street, Rose Hill, NC 28458

**Hours:** Visible 24/7 under gazebo (usually covered with tarp except during events)

**Fees:** FREE to view

**History:** Created 1963 by Ramsey Feed Company as tribute to poultry industry. Rose Hill is major poultry production center.

**Seasonal Information:** Best viewed in action during NC Poultry Jubilee (November annually)

**Status:** Active, used for Poultry Jubilee and fundraisers. **NOTE:** Covered most of year for protection; visible handle only unless event occurring.

**Note:** Multiple towns claim "world's largest frying pan" - this is operational version.

**Source:** Apple Maps, local sources
