## What Makes It World's Largest

Largest freestanding arch in Arches National Park. Height 52 feet, opening 46 feet high × 32 feet wide. Featured on Utah license plates.

## Location

Arches National Park, PO Box 907, Moab, UT 84532
Trailhead: Wolfe Ranch Road, 11.7 miles from entrance

GPS: 38.7444° N, 109.4992° W

## Hours

Park open 24 hours. Visitor Center 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM. Trail: 3-mile round trip, strenuous, 480 ft gain, 2-3 hours.

## Fees

- Park entrance: $30 per vehicle (7-day pass)
- Timed entry required April-October ($2 reservation)
- Bring 1 liter water per person minimum
