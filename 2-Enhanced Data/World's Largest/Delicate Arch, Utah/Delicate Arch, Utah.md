# DELICATE ARCH, Utah 

**Most Famous Natural Arch, Largest Freestanding in Park**

**What Makes It World's Largest:** Largest freestanding arch in Arches National Park. Height 52 feet, opening 46 feet high × 32 feet wide. Featured on Utah license plates.

**Address:** Arches National Park, PO Box 907, Moab, UT 84532
Trailhead: Wolfe Ranch Road, 11.7 miles from entrance
**GPS:** 38.7444° N, 109.4992° W

**Hours:** Park open 24 hours. Visitor Center 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM. Trail: 3-mile round trip, strenuous, 480 ft gain, 2-3 hours.

**Fees:**
- Park entrance: $30 per vehicle (7-day pass)
- Timed entry required April-October ($2 reservation)
- Bring 1 liter water per person minimum

**History:** Formed from Entrada Sandstone (Jurassic, ~140 million years). Featured on Utah quarter 2014. Climbing banned 2006.

**Seasonal Information:** Best spring (April-May) and fall (September-October). Summer extreme heat - hike early/late. Sunset most popular.

**Source:** National Park Service
