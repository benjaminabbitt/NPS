# WORLD'S LARGEST BASKET BUILDING [CLOSED]

**Location:** Newark, Ohio

**What Makes It World's Largest:** World's Largest Basket-Shaped Building. 9,000-ton structure, 180,000 square feet, seven stories.

**Address:** 1500 East Main Street, Newark, OH 43055

**Hours:** Exterior viewable 24/7; **building currently vacant**

**Fees:** FREE to view from exterior (not open to public)

**History:** Built 1997 as Longaberger Basket Company headquarters. Designed to replicate company's Medium Market Basket at 160x scale. Company declared bankruptcy 2018; building abandoned. Features 150-ton heated handles.

**Seasonal Information:** Exterior viewing year-round

**Status:** [CLOSED] Building vacant since 2018. For sale intermittently. Previous plans for conversion to corporate headquarters, boutique hotel, communal offices, or housing - none materialized. Structure maintained but empty.

**Source:** Roadtrippers, Apple Maps
