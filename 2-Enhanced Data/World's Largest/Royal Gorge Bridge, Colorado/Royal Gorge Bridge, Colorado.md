# ROYAL GORGE BRIDGE, Colorado ✓

**Highest Suspension Bridge in United States**

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 955 feet (291 meters) above Arkansas River. Length 1,260 feet.

**Address:** 4218 County Rd 3A, Cañon City, CO 81212

**Hours:** Daily 10am-5pm (seasonal variations)

**Fees:** ~$30-40 adults; children vary. Gondola $30 additional

**History:** Built June-November 1929. 46-meter-high towers. 18 feet wide, 1,292 wooden planks. Part of Royal Gorge Bridge & Park.

**Seasonal Information:** Best spring through fall.

**Source:** Royal Gorge Bridge & Park
