# EL CAPITAN, California ✓

**World's Largest Granite Monolith**

**What Makes It World's Largest:** Largest exposed granite monolith on Earth. Height 3,000 feet from base, summit elevation 7,569 feet, width approximately 1.5 miles at base.

**Address:** Yosemite National Park, Yosemite Valley, CA
**GPS:** 37.7341° N, 119.6374° W

**Hours:** Park open 24 hours year-round. Viewing always accessible. Trail to summit: 17-mile roundtrip, 2,900 ft elevation gain (10-15 hours).

**Fees:**
- Park entrance: $35 per vehicle (7-day pass)
- Reservation system required April-October
- Climbing: No permit required

**History:** Native name "Tutokanula" (Rock Chief). Spanish "El Capitán" given 1851. Approximately 100 million years old. First ascent 1958. First free solo climb 2017 by Alex Honnold (film "Free Solo").

**Seasonal Information:** Best April-October. Spring features Horsetail Fall "firefall" phenomenon (mid-February). Best views from El Capitan Meadow and Tunnel View.

**Source:** Wikipedia, National Park Service
