## What Makes It World's Largest

Largest exposed granite monolith on Earth. Height 3,000 feet from base, summit elevation 7,569 feet, width approximately 1.5 miles at base.

## Location

Yosemite National Park, Yosemite Valley, CA

GPS: 37.7341° N, 119.6374° W

## Hours

Park open 24 hours year-round. Viewing always accessible. Trail to summit: 17-mile roundtrip, 2,900 ft elevation gain (10-15 hours).

## Fees

- Park entrance: $35 per vehicle (7-day pass)
- Reservation system required April-October
- Climbing: No permit required
