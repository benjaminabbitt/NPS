## What Makes It World's Largest

Largest hot spring in US, third-largest globally. Diameter 370 feet, depth over 160 feet, temperature 147-189°F, discharges approximately 560 gallons per minute.

## Location

Yellowstone National Park, Midway Geyser Basin

GPS: 44.5247° N, 110.8378° W

## Hours

Park open year-round. Boardwalk accessible during park hours. Best viewing late morning to early afternoon for clearest colors.

## Fees

- Park entrance: $35 per vehicle (7-day pass)
- $30 motorcycle; $20 per person (foot/bicycle)
- Annual pass: $70 for Yellowstone only
