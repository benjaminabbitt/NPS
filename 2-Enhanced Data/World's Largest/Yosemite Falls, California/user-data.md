## What Makes It World's Largest

2,425 feet total drop (Upper Fall 1,430 ft, Middle Cascades 675 ft, Lower Fall 320 ft)

## Location

Yosemite National Park, Yosemite Valley, CA

## Hours

Park open 24 hours year-round. Lower Falls Trail: 1-mile loop, paved, wheelchair accessible. Upper Falls Trail: 7.2-mile round trip, strenuous (2,700 ft elevation gain).

## Fees

- Park entrance: $35 per vehicle (7-day pass)
- Reservation system required April-October for day use
- Timed entry reservation ($2) plus entrance fee peak season
