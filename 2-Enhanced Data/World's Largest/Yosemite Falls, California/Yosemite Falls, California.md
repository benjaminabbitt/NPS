# YOSEMITE FALLS, California ✓

**Tallest Waterfall in North America**

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 2,425 feet total drop (Upper Fall 1,430 ft, Middle Cascades 675 ft, Lower Fall 320 ft)

**Address:** Yosemite National Park, Yosemite Valley, CA
**GPS (Lower Falls):** 37.7440° N, 119.5968° W

**Hours:** Park open 24 hours year-round. Lower Falls Trail: 1-mile loop, paved, wheelchair accessible. Upper Falls Trail: 7.2-mile round trip, strenuous (2,700 ft elevation gain).

**Fees:**
- Park entrance: $35 per vehicle (7-day pass)
- Reservation system required April-October for day use
- Timed entry reservation ($2) plus entrance fee peak season

**History:** Indigenous name "Choo-look" meaning "the fall." First survey 1913 by USGS. Valley carved during Pleistocene glaciation 2.6 million years ago.

**Seasonal Information:** Peak flow May (snowmelt season). Water flows November-July. Often completely dry July-October. "Moonbow" phenomenon visible during full moon in April-May.

**Source:** National Park Service
