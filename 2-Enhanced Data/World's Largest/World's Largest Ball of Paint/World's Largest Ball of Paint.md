# WORLD'S LARGEST BALL OF PAINT ✓

**Location:** Alexandria, Indiana

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 25,000+ layers, 11,600+ pounds (over 5 tons), 30,000+ coats. Visitors from 100+ countries contributed.

**Address:** 10696 N 200 W, Alexandria, IN

**Hours:** By appointment only; call (765) 724-4088

**Fees:** FREE; donations accepted

**History:** Started January 1, 1977 by Michael Carmichael and son who dipped baseball in baby blue house paint. Continued adding layers; invited neighbors, friends, then strangers. Built special barn with girder to support weight.

**Status:** Active and growing! Visitors can choose color and add layer. Receive certificate. Featured on Jimmy Kimmel Live, Ripley's, Guinness.

**Source:** Visit Anderson Madison County
