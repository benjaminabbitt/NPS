# VULCAN STATUE ✓

**Location:** Birmingham, Alabama

**What Makes It World's Largest:** World's largest cast-iron statue. 56 feet tall; 51 tons.

**Address:** 1701 Valley View Dr, Birmingham, AL 35209

**Hours:** Park: 7am-10pm; Museum: 10am-6pm

**Fees:** Park free; Museum/Tower ~$6-7

**History:** Created 1904 for St. Louis World's Fair. Represents Vulcan (Roman god of fire and forge), symbolizing Birmingham's iron and steel industry. Restored 1999-2004 ($14 million). Holds torch that glows red when traffic fatality occurs in city, green otherwise.

**Seasonal Information:** Park open year-round

**Status:** Active. Vulcan Park includes museum, observation tower (spectacular views), gift shop. Elevator to top.

**Source:** Vulcan Park
