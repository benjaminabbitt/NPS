# CAESARS SUPERDOME, Louisiana ✓

**World's Largest Fixed-Dome Structure**

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 272 feet height, 679 feet diameter. Covers 13 acres.

**Address:** 1500 Sugar Bowl Dr, New Orleans, LA 70112

**Hours:** Tours available; varies by season and events

**Fees:** Tours ~$15-25

**History:** Inaugurated 1975. Home to New Orleans Saints. Survived Hurricane Katrina (2005), renovated. Hosts Super Bowls, Sugar Bowl.

**Seasonal Information:** Tours on non-event days year-round.

**Source:** Superdome Management
