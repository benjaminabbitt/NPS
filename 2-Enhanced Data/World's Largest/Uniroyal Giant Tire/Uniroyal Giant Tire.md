# UNIROYAL GIANT TIRE ✓

**Location:** Allen Park, Michigan

**What Makes It World's Largest:** World's Largest Tire. 80 feet tall, 12 tons. Made of fiberglass and steel. Contains 8 windows (former gondola openings).

**Address:** Detroit Industrial Expy, Allen Park, MI 48101 [42.2706, -83.2091]

**Hours:** Viewable 24/7 from I-94

**Fees:** Free (viewable from highway)

**History:** Built 1964-65 as Ferris wheel for New York World's Fair (held 96 people in 24 gondolas). Moved to Detroit 1966 as Uniroyal advertising.

**Seasonal Information:** Permanent installation year-round

**Status:** One of Michigan's most recognizable landmarks. No public access to base.

**Source:** RoadsideAmerica.com
