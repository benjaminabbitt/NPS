# DOG BARK PARK INN - WORLD'S LARGEST BEAGLE [SELF-DECLARED]

**Location:** Cottonwood, Idaho

**What Makes It World's Largest:** "Sweet Willy" - 30 feet tall beagle-shaped structure

**Address:** 2421 Business Loop 95, Cottonwood, ID (US Highway 95)

**Hours:** Gift shop/museum open sporadically or by appointment. Beagle visible 24/7 from highway.

**Fees:** FREE to view exterior and visit museum/gift shop. **B&B CLOSED 2024** (formerly $88/night)

**History:** Built by chainsaw artists Dennis J. Sullivan and Frances Conklin. Opened August 1, 2003. Took 4+ years. Interior featured bedroom in belly, bathroom in rear, loft in head. Smaller beagle "Toby" (12 feet) built first as roadside ambassador.

**Seasonal Information:** B&B operated April-October (now closed as accommodation)

**Status:** **B&B CLOSED 2024 (owners retired).** Still operates as roadside attraction and museum. 1 hour from Lewiston Airport, 4 hours from Boise/Missoula/Spokane.

**Contact:** 208-962-3647, frances@dogbarkpark.com
