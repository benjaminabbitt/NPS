# WORLD'S LARGEST STRAWBERRY [SELF-DECLARED] [CONTESTED]

**Location:** Poteet, Texas

**What Makes It World's Largest:** Water tower painted as strawberry, approximately 130 feet tall

**Address:** Poteet, TX (water tower)

**Hours:** 24/7 visible

**Fees:** FREE

**History:** Original strawberry statue at fire station was outdone by larger strawberry in Strawberry Point, Iowa. Solution: paint water tower to become world's tallest strawberry.

**Status:** Active water tower.

**Note:** Contested with Strawberry Point, Iowa.

**Source:** Texas Highways
