# LUCY THE ELEPHANT ✓

**Location:** Margate City, New Jersey

**What Makes It World's Largest:** World's largest elephant-shaped structure and oldest roadside attraction. 65 feet tall, 60 feet long, 18 feet wide; 90 tons.

**Address:** 9200 Atlantic Ave, Margate City, NJ 08402 [39.3209, -74.5116]

**Hours:** Tours year-round; seasonal schedules (typically closed January-March). Check website.

**Fees:** Tours: Adults $8.50, children (3-12) $4, seniors $7

**History:** Built 1881 by James V. Lafferty as real estate publicity stunt (patented design). First "world's largest" roadside attraction in America. Originally cost $38,000. Made of wood and tin sheeting. Served as hotel, bar, real estate office, vacation home. National Historic Landmark. Saved from demolition 1970s.

**Seasonal Information:** Reduced hours winter; closed major holidays

**Status:** Guided tours climb inside elephant to howdah (viewing platform on top). Six-story structure. Popular photo destination.

**Source:** RoadsideAmerica.com
