## What Makes It World's Largest

Longest natural arch span in US, 5th longest globally. Span: 290.1 feet ± 0.8 feet. Height 77.5 feet, width 18 feet, thickness only 6 feet at thinnest.

## Location

Arches National Park, Devils Garden area
Trailhead: Devils Garden parking (18 miles from visitor center)

GPS: 38.7838° N, 109.6061° W

## Hours

Park open 24 hours. Trail: 1.6 miles round trip, relatively flat, 30-45 minutes each way.

## Fees

- Park entrance: $30 per vehicle
- Timed entry April-October
- Viewing only - cannot pass beneath (trail closed for safety)
