# WORLD'S LARGEST PEACH (THE PEACHOID) [SELF-DECLARED]

**Location:** Gaffney, South Carolina

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 135 feet tall water tower, holds 1 million gallons water, 60-foot peach with 7-ton leaf on side

**Address:** Visible from I-85 West between Charlotte, NC and Atlanta, GA, Gaffney, SC

**Hours:** 24/7 visible from I-85

**Fees:** FREE

**History:** Commissioned 1981. Built to honor local peach industry - Cherokee County produced more peaches than Georgia at that time. South Carolina produces 200+ million pounds of peaches annually, second to California.

**Status:** Active water tower, highly visible landmark referenced in "The Twilight" film.
