# STEAMBOAT GEYSER, Wyoming ✓

**World's Tallest Active Geyser**

**What Makes It World's Largest:** World's tallest currently active geyser. Major eruptions 300-400+ feet (record 403 feet in 2019). Water phase 3-40 minutes; steam phase can last hours to 2+ days.

**Address:** Yellowstone National Park, Norris Geyser Basin, WY
**GPS:** 44.7256° N, 110.7032° W

**Hours:** Park and basin accessible 24/7 weather permitting. Norris Visitor Center seasonal 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM. Boardwalk approximately 0.5 miles from parking.

**Fees:**
- Park entrance: $35 per vehicle (7-day pass)
- Stay on boardwalks - thermal waters cause severe burns

**History:** Likely formed around 1878. Named for steam phase. Dormant 1911-1961 (50 years). Active phases 1960s, 1980s, 2018-present. Recent activity: 32 eruptions (2018), 48 (2019), 48 (2020).

**Seasonal Information:** Accessible year-round weather dependent. Eruptions unpredictable (intervals 3 days to 50 years). Monitor GeyserTimes.org for reports.

**Source:** Wikipedia, National Park Service
