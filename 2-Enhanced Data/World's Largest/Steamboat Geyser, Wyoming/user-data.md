## What Makes It World's Largest

World's tallest currently active geyser. Major eruptions 300-400+ feet (record 403 feet in 2019). Water phase 3-40 minutes; steam phase can last hours to 2+ days.

## Location

Yellowstone National Park, Norris Geyser Basin, WY

GPS: 44.7256° N, 110.7032° W

## Hours

Park and basin accessible 24/7 weather permitting. Norris Visitor Center seasonal 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM. Boardwalk approximately 0.5 miles from parking.

## Fees

- Park entrance: $35 per vehicle (7-day pass)
- Stay on boardwalks - thermal waters cause severe burns
