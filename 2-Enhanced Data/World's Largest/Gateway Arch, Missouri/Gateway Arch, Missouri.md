# GATEWAY ARCH, Missouri ✓

**World's Tallest Arch and Tallest Monument in Western Hemisphere**

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 630 feet tall and 630 feet wide at base

**Address:** Gateway Arch National Park, St. Louis, MO 63102 [38.6247, -90.1848]
**GPS:** 38.6247° N, 90.1848° W

**Hours:** Varies seasonally; typically 9am-6pm

**Fees:** Adults (16+) start at $15; Children (3-15) start at $11; Infants free

**History:** Designed by Eero Saarinen 1947, completed 1965. Made of stainless steel. Commemorates westward expansion. Visitors take tram to top for 360-degree views.

**Seasonal Information:** Open year-round except Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's. Summer extended hours.

**Source:** National Park Service
