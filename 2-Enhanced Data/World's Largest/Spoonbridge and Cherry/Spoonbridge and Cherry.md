# SPOONBRIDGE AND CHERRY ✓

**Location:** Minneapolis, Minnesota

**What Makes It World's Largest:** World's Largest Spoonbridge and Cherry Sculpture. 52 feet long, 30 feet tall, 13 feet wide. Cherry weighs 1,199 pounds, spoon weighs 5,800 pounds.

**Address:** Minneapolis Sculpture Garden, Walker Art Center, Minneapolis, MN

**Hours:** Sculpture Garden: 6 AM - midnight daily, FREE

**Fees:** FREE

**History:** Designed by Claes Oldenburg and Coosje van Bruggen. Funded by $500,000 donation from Frederick R. Weisman. Completed and installed 1988 for Sculpture Garden opening. Stainless steel and aluminum coated with polyurethane enamel. Water emits from tip and base of cherry's stem. Became "unofficial symbol of Minneapolis." Walker Art Center receives more image requests for this than any other work.

**Status:** Active, iconic Twin Cities symbol. Became one of most celebrated Pop Art outdoor works.

**Source:** World Record Academy, Walker Art Center
