## What Makes It World's Largest

World's Largest Spoonbridge and Cherry Sculpture. 52 feet long, 30 feet tall, 13 feet wide. Cherry weighs 1,199 pounds, spoon weighs 5,800 pounds.

## Location

Minneapolis Sculpture Garden, Walker Art Center, Minneapolis, MN
