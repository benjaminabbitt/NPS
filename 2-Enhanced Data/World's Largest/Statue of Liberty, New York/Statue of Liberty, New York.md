# STATUE OF LIBERTY, New York ✓

**Tallest Statue in United States (until 2024)**

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 151 feet (statue alone); 305 feet from ground to torch

**Address:** Liberty Island, New York, NY 10004 [40.6892, -74.0445]

**Hours:** Daily 9:30am-5pm (ferry dependent)

**Fees:** General admission ~$24 adults; Crown access requires advance reservation

**History:** Gift from France designed by Frédéric Auguste Bartholdi with framework by Gustave Eiffel, dedicated 1886. Copper construction. Symbol of freedom and immigration.

**Seasonal Information:** Accessible year-round weather permitting. Crown access limited - book well ahead.

**Source:** National Park Service
