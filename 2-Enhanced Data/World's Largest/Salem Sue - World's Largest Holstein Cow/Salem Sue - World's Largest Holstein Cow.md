# SALEM SUE - WORLD'S LARGEST HOLSTEIN COW ✓

**Location:** New Salem, North Dakota

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 38 feet high, 50 feet long, 12,000 pounds (6 tons), hollow fiberglass, visible 5+ miles

**Address:** 8th Ave N, New Salem, ND 58563 (School Hill) [46.8598, -101.4176]

**Hours:** Daytime 6am-11pm daily (not visible at night)

**Fees:** FREE; donations accepted (drop in milk can)

**History:** Built 1974 for $40,000 by New Salem Lions Club to honor dairy farmers. Constructed by Sculpture Mfg. Co. in La Crosse, Wisconsin. New Salem High School teams named "Holsteins" after Sue.

**Seasonal Information:** Difficult to access in severe winter; gravel road can be challenging

**Status:** Active, celebrated 50th anniversary 2024. Anatomically correct with painted udders and veins. Spectacular prairie views.

**Directions:** I-94 Exit 127 (34 miles west of Bismarck), south 1/2 mile on N. 8th St., turn right at Salem Sue sign, follow dirt/gravel road up hill

**Phone:** 701-843-7828

**Source:** RoadsideAmerica.com
