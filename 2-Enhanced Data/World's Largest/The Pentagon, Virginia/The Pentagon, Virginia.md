# THE PENTAGON, Virginia ✓

**World's Largest Low-Rise Office Building**

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 6.5 million square feet total; 3.7 million sq ft office. Five-sided, five floors above ground, two basement levels. 17.5 miles of corridors.

**Address:** Washington Blvd, Arlington, VA 22202

**Hours:** Tours Monday-Friday by advance reservation only (14-90 days ahead)

**Fees:** Free (security clearance required)

**History:** Designed by George Bergstrom, built 1941-1943. Headquarters of U.S. Department of Defense. Construction cost $63 million. ~23,000 military and civilian employees plus 3,000 support.

**Seasonal Information:** Tours year-round weekdays only.

**Source:** Department of Defense
