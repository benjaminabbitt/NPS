# AT&T STADIUM (COWBOYS STADIUM), Texas ✓

**World's Largest Domed Stadium**

**What Makes It World's Largest:** World's largest column-free interior. Capacity 80,000 (expandable to 105,000). Features world's 4th-largest HD video screen. Roof retractable; 660,800 sq ft can open/close in under 12 minutes.

**Address:** One AT&T Way, Arlington, TX 76011

**Hours:** Tours available daily; hours vary by event schedule

**Fees:** Tours ~$30-40

**History:** Completed May 27, 2009. Designed by HKS, Inc. Home of Dallas Cowboys.

**Seasonal Information:** Tours year-round when no events.

**Source:** AT&T Stadium
