# WORLD'S LARGEST WORKING FIRE HYDRANT [SELF-DECLARED]

**Location:** Beaumont, Texas

**What Makes It World's Largest:** World's Largest Working Fire Hydrant. Black-and-white Dalmatian spots, 4,500 pounds fiberglass-and-steel, capable of spraying 1,500 gallons/minute.

**Address:** Fire Museum of Texas, Beaumont, TX

**Hours:** Fire Museum hours (check locally)

**Fees:** Museum admission

**History:** Donated to Beaumont by Walt Disney Company 1999 to celebrate re-release of "101 Dalmatians." Originally built at Disneyland in Anaheim, California.

**Seasonal Information:** Year-round

**Status:** Active, functional working hydrant at museum.

**Source:** Roadtrippers
