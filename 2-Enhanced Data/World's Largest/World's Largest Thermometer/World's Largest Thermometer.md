# WORLD'S LARGEST THERMOMETER [SELF-DECLARED]

**Location:** Baker, California

**What Makes It World's Largest:** World's Tallest Thermometer. 134 feet tall (one foot per degree recorded in nearby Death Valley in 1934). Digital display shows current temperature.

**Address:** 72157 Baker Blvd, Baker, CA 92364 [35.2665, -116.0728]

**Hours:** 24/7; visitor center varies

**Fees:** FREE

**History:** Built 1991 by Young Electric Sign Company. Fully functional; visible from miles away.

**Seasonal Information:** Especially popular in summer to see extreme temperatures

**Status:** Active.

**Source:** Roadtrippers
