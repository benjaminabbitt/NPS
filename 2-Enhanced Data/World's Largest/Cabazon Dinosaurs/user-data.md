## What Makes It World's Largest

World's Biggest Dinosaur Sculptures. "Dinny" (Apatosaurus): 150 feet long, 45 feet high, 50 tons. "Mr. Rex" (T. Rex): 65 feet tall.

## Location

GPS: 33.9187° N, 116.7704° W

## Hours

Open daily, hours vary (check cabazondinosaurs.com)
