# CABAZON DINOSAURS ✓

**Location:** Cabazon, California

**What Makes It World's Largest:** World's Biggest Dinosaur Sculptures. "Dinny" (Apatosaurus): 150 feet long, 45 feet high, 50 tons. "Mr. Rex" (T. Rex): 65 feet tall.

**Address:** 50770 Seminole Dr, Cabazon, CA 92230 [33.9187, -116.7704]

**Hours:** Open daily, hours vary (check cabazondinosaurs.com)

**Fees:** $13 adults to enter park and climb Mr. Rex (2019 pricing). Free to view from outside.

**History:** Built by Claude Bell, Knott's Berry Farm sculptor. Dinny built 1964-1975 (11 years) to attract customers to Wheel Inn Restaurant. Mr. Rex completed 1986. Bell died 1988 at age 91. Featured in "Pee-wee's Big Adventure" (1985).

**Seasonal Information:** Desert location accessible year-round

**Status:** Active attraction with 50+ additional dinosaur displays, dino dig, fossil panning, gift shop in Dinny's belly. 12+ million people drive by annually on I-10. Mr. Rex temporarily repainted in Pee-wee Herman's tuxedo after Paul Reubens' death (2023).

**Directions:** I-10 Main Street exit, visible from freeway

**Source:** Roadtrippers, various
