## What Makes It World's Largest

World's Largest Musical Instrument (Guinness). Spans 3.5 acres of caverns, sound audible throughout 64-acre caverns. Uses stalactites struck by rubber mallets controlled by organ keyboard.

## Location

Luray Caverns, Luray, VA (Shenandoah Valley)
