# GREAT STALACPIPE ORGAN ✓

**Location:** Luray Caverns, Virginia

**What Makes It World's Largest:** World's Largest Musical Instrument (Guinness). Spans 3.5 acres of caverns, sound audible throughout 64-acre caverns. Uses stalactites struck by rubber mallets controlled by organ keyboard.

**Address:** Luray Caverns, Luray, VA (Shenandoah Valley)

**Hours:** Cavern tour hours (check website)

**Fees:** Luray Caverns admission (includes organ)

**History:** Designed and implemented 1954-1956 by Leland W. Sprinkle (Pentagon mathematician/electronics scientist). Tested 2,000+ stalactites to find correct tones. Takes 150+ years for 1 inch of stalactite growth, so organ doesn't need tuning.

**Seasonal Information:** Underground, accessible year-round

**Status:** Active, mostly automated. Otto Pebworth is long-time cave organist for special occasions. Maintenance required for hammers, metal pickups, wires. 2011: First original composition recorded by Pepe Deluxé.

**Source:** Luray Caverns
