# WORLD'S LARGEST PEANUT [SELF-DECLARED]

**Location:** Pearsall, Texas

**What Makes It World's Largest:** Monument to peanut industry

**Address:** Intersection of FM 140 and S Trevino St, Pearsall, TX

**Hours:** 24/7

**Fees:** FREE

**History:** Built to honor Pearsall's peanut industry. Lettering states "55,000,000 Lbs. Marketed Annually."

**Status:** Active. Remote location - 50 miles between World's Largest Strawberry and this attraction described as "deserted and scary."

**Source:** RoadsideAmerica.com
