# WORLD'S LARGEST COWBOY BOOTS ✓

**Location:** San Antonio, Texas

**What Makes It World's Largest:** Guinness World Record. 35 feet tall, 33 feet long, 9 feet wide. Each boot can hold 300,000 gallons of beer. Person would need to be 200 feet tall to wear them.

**Address:** North Star Mall, 7400 San Pedro Avenue (Loop 410), San Antonio, TX 78216 [29.5185, -98.4963]

**Hours:** 24/7 visible from Loop 410, mall parking lot access during mall hours

**Fees:** FREE

**History:** Created by artist Bob "Daddy-O" Wade 1979. Originally built in Washington, D.C. for art exhibit. Purchased by Rouse Corporation for $20,000, transported to San Antonio January 16, 1980. Made of concrete and fiberglass over foam. 2012: mall spent $80,000 refurbishing boot tops. 2019 repainting by muralist Style Read.

**Seasonal Information:** Decorated with 8,000 LED lights Thanksgiving-early January

**Status:** Active. Recognized by Guinness 2016. Named to Guinness 2025 "record-breaking road trip" list. Featured in numerous commercials. Regular maintenance performed.

**Directions:** I-410 Exits 20B (westbound) or 20 (eastbound), south side frontage road near Saks Fifth Avenue

**Source:** Various
