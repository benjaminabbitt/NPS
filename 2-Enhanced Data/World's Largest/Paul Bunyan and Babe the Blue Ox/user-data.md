## What Makes It World's Largest

Historic Paul Bunyan statues. Paul: 18 feet tall, 7.5 tons. Babe: 10 feet tall, 23 feet nose to tail. Second-most-photographed sculptures in America in 1940s (per Kodak), topped only by Mt. Rushmore.
