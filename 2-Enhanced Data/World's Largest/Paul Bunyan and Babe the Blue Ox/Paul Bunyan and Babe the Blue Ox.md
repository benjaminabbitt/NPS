# PAUL BUNYAN AND BABE THE BLUE OX ✓

**Location:** Bemidji, Minnesota

**What Makes It World's Largest:** Historic Paul Bunyan statues. Paul: 18 feet tall, 7.5 tons. Babe: 10 feet tall, 23 feet nose to tail. Second-most-photographed sculptures in America in 1940s (per Kodak), topped only by Mt. Rushmore.

**Address:** 300 Bemidji Ave N, Bemidji, MN 56601 [47.4735, -94.8803]
On shore of Lake Bemidji near Tourist Information Center
**GPS:** Approximately 47.4725° N, 94.8839° W

**Hours:** 24/7 viewable year-round. Tourist Information Center open year-round with varying hours.

**Fees:** FREE

**History:** Built January 1937 for winter carnival. Designed by Cyril M. Dickinson and Jim Payton. Paul modeled after then-Mayor Earl Bucklen. Unveiled January 14, 1937. Babe originally on wheels, traveled to carnivals 1937-1938, joined Paul permanently 1939. Featured full-page in Life Magazine February 1, 1937. Added to National Register of Historic Places 1988. Originally had PA system (Paul could "talk") and Babe had exhaust pipes through nostrils - both disabled. Eyes were lights that illuminated. Most recently repainted 2016. Featured in "National Lampoon's Vacation" (1983), "Fargo" (2014), "Kumiko, the Treasure Hunter" (2014).

**Status:** Active, well-maintained. Protected by city ordinance - Paul can only wear his painted-on clothes (no dressing him up, rule since 2014). Adjacent Tourist Information Center features Paul's giant artifacts: boxer shorts, flyswatter, toothbrush, dice, playing cards, belt, chocolate bar, lighter, plus Fireplace of States (constructed using 900 rocks from every state/Canadian province).

**Seasonal Information:** Accessible year-round. Winter can be extremely cold. Best photos during warmer months.

**Additional Paul Bunyans in Minnesota:**
- Brainerd: Talking Paul at Paul Bunyan Land (26 feet, says visitors' names)
- Akeley: World's Largest Paul Bunyan (60 feet tall, kneeling with hand outstretched)
- Hackensack: Features Paul's sweetheart "Lucette"
