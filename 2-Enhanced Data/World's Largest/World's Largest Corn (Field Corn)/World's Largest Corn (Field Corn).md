# WORLD'S LARGEST CORN (FIELD CORN) [SELF-DECLARED]

**Location:** Dublin, Ohio

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 109 people-sized corn stalks (6+ feet tall each) standing in former corn field.

**Address:** Dublin, OH (Field of Corn - Corn Osama)

**Hours:** 24/7 visible

**Fees:** FREE

**History:** Created as art installation "Field of Corn (with Osage Orange Trees)" by Malcolm Cochran, 1994. Tribute to Ohio's agrarian roots and Sam Frantz, pioneer in hybrid corn development.

**Status:** Active art installation.

**Source:** Only In Your State (Ohio)
