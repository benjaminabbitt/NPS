# DEVILS TOWER, Wyoming ✓

**America's First National Monument**

**What Makes It World's Largest:** World's largest and most spectacular example of columnar jointing. Height 867 feet above base, 1,267 feet above river. Summit 180 ft × 300 ft (1.5 acres). Columns up to 20 feet wide, some 600 feet tall.

**Address:** Devils Tower National Monument, PO Box 10, Devils Tower, WY 82714
**GPS:** 44.5902° N, 104.7145° W

**Hours:**
- Monument: Open 24 hours year-round
- Visitor Center: Typically 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (closed winter)
- Tower Trail: 1.3-mile paved loop (45 minutes-1 hour)

**Fees:**
- Entrance: $25 per vehicle (7-day pass)
- Camping: Belle Fourche Campground $20/night
- Climbing: No permit required but register at ranger station

**History:** Sacred site to 20+ Northern Plains tribes for thousands of years. Indigenous names include Matȟó Thípila (Bear Lodge). First US National Monument (September 24, 1906 by Theodore Roosevelt). Igneous intrusion ~40.5 million years ago. First recorded climb July 4, 1893.

**Seasonal Information:** Best April-October. Voluntary climbing closure month of June during sacred tribal ceremonies. Featured in "Close Encounters of the Third Kind" (1977).

**Source:** National Park Service
