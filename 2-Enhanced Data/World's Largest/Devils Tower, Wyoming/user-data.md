## What Makes It World's Largest

World's largest and most spectacular example of columnar jointing. Height 867 feet above base, 1,267 feet above river. Summit 180 ft × 300 ft (1.5 acres). Columns up to 20 feet wide, some 600 feet tall.

## Location

Devils Tower National Monument, PO Box 10, Devils Tower, WY 82714

GPS: 44.5902° N, 104.7145° W

## Hours

- Monument: Open 24 hours year-round
- Visitor Center: Typically 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (closed winter)
- Tower Trail: 1.3-mile paved loop (45 minutes-1 hour)

## Fees

- Entrance: $25 per vehicle (7-day pass)
- Camping: Belle Fourche Campground $20/night
- Climbing: No permit required but register at ranger station
