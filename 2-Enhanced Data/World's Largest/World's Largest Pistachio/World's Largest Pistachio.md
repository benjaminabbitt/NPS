# WORLD'S LARGEST PISTACHIO [SELF-DECLARED]

**Location:** Alamogordo, New Mexico

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 30 feet tall, made from 5+ yards concrete, covered in 35 gallons paint

**Address:** McGinn's PistachioLand, 7320 Highway 54-70, Alamogordo, NM 88310

**Hours:** Gift shop 9 AM - 5 PM daily (closed Thanksgiving/Christmas); pistachio visible 24/7

**Fees:** FREE to view; farm tours $10-15 per person

**History:** Created 2008 to honor Thomas McGinn, founder of PistachioLand (died 2007). McGinn built 111-acre pistachio farm and vineyard.

**Seasonal Information:** Tours may be limited in extreme weather; monument viewable year-round

**Status:** Active roadside attraction and working pistachio farm. Gift shop sells pistachio products, wine tasting available.

**Source:** Roadtrippers
