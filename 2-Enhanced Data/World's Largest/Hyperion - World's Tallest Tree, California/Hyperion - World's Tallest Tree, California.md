# HYPERION - WORLD'S TALLEST TREE, California ✓ [LIMITED ACCESS]

**World's Tallest Known Living Tree**

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 380.3 feet tall (115.92 meters) coast redwood

**Location:** Redwood National Park, California (exact location undisclosed)

**Access:** **CLOSED TO PUBLIC** - Exact location kept secret to prevent damage. $5,000 fine and up to 6 months jail time for attempting to find it. General park access available.

**History:** Discovered August 25, 2006 by naturalists Chris Atkins and Michael Taylor. Located in remote area of park. Named after Greek Titan. Coast redwood (Sequoia sempervirens), estimated 600-800 years old.

**Why Secret:** Heavy foot traffic causes soil compaction and root damage. Previous publicized tall trees suffered significant damage from visitors.

**Seasonal Information:** Park open year-round. Visitors can see other tall trees on designated trails.

**Fees:** Park entrance $30 per vehicle (7-day pass)

**Source:** National Park Service, Redwood Parks
