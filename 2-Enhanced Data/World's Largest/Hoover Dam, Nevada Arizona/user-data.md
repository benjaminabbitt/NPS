## What Makes It World's Largest

726 feet tall, 1,244 feet long, 660 feet thick at base. 3 million cubic yards concrete. Creates Lake Mead (largest U.S. reservoir by volume).

## Location

U.S. Highway 93, Boulder City, NV 89005 (30 miles SE of Las Vegas)

## Hours

- Visitor Center: Daily 9am-5pm (closed Thanksgiving/Christmas)
- Guided Dam Tour: $40 (1 hour)
- Power Plant Tour: $25 adults, $15 ages 4-16
- Visitor Center only: $15 adults
- Parking: $10 per vehicle
