# HOOVER DAM, Nevada/Arizona ✓

**Among Largest Concrete Arch-Gravity Dams in U.S.**

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 726 feet tall, 1,244 feet long, 660 feet thick at base. 3 million cubic yards concrete. Creates Lake Mead (largest U.S. reservoir by volume).

**Address:** U.S. Highway 93, Boulder City, NV 89005 (30 miles SE of Las Vegas)

**Hours:**
- Visitor Center: Daily 9am-5pm (closed Thanksgiving/Christmas)
- Guided Dam Tour: $40 (1 hour)
- Power Plant Tour: $25 adults, $15 ages 4-16
- Visitor Center only: $15 adults
- Parking: $10 per vehicle

**History:** Construction 1931-1936, dedicated by FDR September 30, 1935. Built during Great Depression. Provides water to 20 million people. 7+ million visitors annually.

**Seasonal Information:** Open year-round. Summer extreme temperatures. December 1-February 27: Night closures Monday-Friday (7pm-5am) for maintenance.

**Source:** Bureau of Reclamation
