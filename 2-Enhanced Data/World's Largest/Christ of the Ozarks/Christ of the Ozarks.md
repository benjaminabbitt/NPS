# CHRIST OF THE OZARKS [SELF-DECLARED]

**Location:** Eureka Springs, Arkansas

**What Makes It World's Largest:** Tallest Jesus statue in North America; third-tallest in world. 67 feet tall on 1,500-foot mountain.

**Address:** 937 Passion Play Rd, Eureka Springs, AR 72632

**Hours:** Grounds dawn to dusk; Great Passion Play seasonal performances

**Fees:** Free to view statue; Passion Play tickets separate

**History:** Built 1966 by evangelist Gerald L.K. Smith. Made of mortar and steel. Stark white, visible for miles. Arms spread wide.

**Seasonal Information:** Statue viewable year-round. Passion Play May-October.

**Status:** Active. Part of Great Passion Play complex (outdoor religious drama).

**Source:** Various
