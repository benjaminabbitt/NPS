# WORLD'S LARGEST CATSUP BOTTLE ✓

**Location:** Collinsville, Illinois

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 170 feet tall total (70-foot bottle on 100-foot stand), holds 100,000 gallons water, could hold 640,000 bottles of catsup

**Address:** 800 S. Morrison Avenue, Collinsville, IL 62234 [38.6629, -89.9822]

**Hours:** 24/7 public viewing

**Fees:** FREE

**History:** Built October 1949 by W.E. Caldwell for G.S. Suppiger plant that bottled Brooks Old Original Catsup. Near demolition 1990s, saved by Catsup Bottle Preservation Group who raised $70,000+ for restoration completed 1995. Added to National Register of Historic Places 2002.

**Seasonal Information:** Annual Catsup Bottle Festival in July. Illuminated at night.

**Status:** Active, well-maintained. 20 minutes from downtown St. Louis Gateway Arch. Exit 159 off I-55/70.

**Source:** Rivers and Routes
