## What Makes It World's Largest

120 feet tall, 68,000 pounds. Exact-scale replica of Babe Ruth's 34-inch Louisville Slugger.

## Location

Louisville Slugger Museum & Factory, 800 West Main Street, Louisville, KY 40202 [38.2555, -85.7619]
