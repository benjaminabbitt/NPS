# WORLD'S LARGEST BASEBALL BAT (REPLICA) ✓

**Location:** Louisville, Kentucky

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 120 feet tall, 68,000 pounds. Exact-scale replica of Babe Ruth's 34-inch Louisville Slugger.

**Address:** Louisville Slugger Museum & Factory, 800 West Main Street, Louisville, KY 40202 [38.2555, -85.7619]

**Hours:** 24/7 outside; Museum Mon-Sat 9am-5pm, Sun 11am-5pm

**Fees:** FREE to view bat; Museum tour ~$19-22

**History:** Built by Caldwell Tanks, Inc. when museum opened 1996. Made of steel. Functions as plumbing vent for museum basement bathrooms.

**Seasonal Information:** Viewable year-round

**Status:** Excellent; leans against museum building. Guinness World Record.

**Source:** Louisville Slugger Museum
