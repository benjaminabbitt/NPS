# GEORGIA AQUARIUM, Georgia ✓

**Largest Aquarium in United States**

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 11 million gallons water; 100,000+ animals. World's largest collection of beluga whales and whale sharks.

**Address:** 225 Baker St NW, Atlanta, GA 30313

**Hours:** Typically 9am-5pm daily (extended seasonally)

**Fees:** $52.99 adults at door; $44.99 online advance

**History:** Founded 2005 by Bernard Marcus (Home Depot co-founder) with $250 million donation. Opened November 23, 2005. 2.5+ million visitors annually.

**Seasonal Information:** Open year-round. Peak summer/holidays.

**Source:** Georgia Aquarium
