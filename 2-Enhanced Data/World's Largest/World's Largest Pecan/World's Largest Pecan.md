# WORLD'S LARGEST PECAN ✓ [CONTESTED]

**Location:** Seguin, Texas

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 16 feet long (newer version, 2011). Original 5 feet long, 1,000 pounds (1962).

**Address:** Texas Agricultural and Heritage Center, Seguin, TX

**Hours:** Viewable during center hours

**Fees:** FREE

**History:** Original created 1962 by dentist, held record for 20 years. Brunswick, Missouri built larger (12 feet, 12,000 pounds) in 1982. Seguin created new 16-foot version in 2011. Additional 10-foot mobile pecan on trailer for parades.

**Status:** Active. Two pecans: stationary 16-foot and mobile 10-foot.

**Note:** Contested with Brunswick, Missouri.

**Source:** Atlas Obscura, various
