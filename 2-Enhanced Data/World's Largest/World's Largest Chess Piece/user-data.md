## What Makes It World's Largest

20 feet tall, 9 feet 2 inches diameter at base, 10,860 pounds. 53 times larger than standard "Championship Staunton" king it replicates.

## Location

World Chess Hall of Fame, 4652 Maryland Avenue, St. Louis, MO 63108

## Hours

Building hours vary; chess piece visible outside 24/7
