# WORLD'S LARGEST CHESS PIECE ✓

**Location:** St. Louis, Missouri

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 20 feet tall, 9 feet 2 inches diameter at base, 10,860 pounds. 53 times larger than standard "Championship Staunton" king it replicates.

**Address:** World Chess Hall of Fame, 4652 Maryland Avenue, St. Louis, MO 63108

**Hours:** Building hours vary; chess piece visible outside 24/7

**Fees:** FREE to view outside; Hall of Fame admission separate

**History:** Created for World Chess Hall of Fame. King chess piece. Guinness World Record recognized August 2023.

**Status:** Active. Popular photo backdrop for visitors.

**Source:** World Record Academy, World Chess Hall of Fame
