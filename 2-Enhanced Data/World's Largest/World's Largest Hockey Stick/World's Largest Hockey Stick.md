# WORLD'S LARGEST HOCKEY STICK ✓

**Location:** Eveleth, Minnesota

**What Makes It World's Largest:** Largest free-standing hockey stick. 110 feet long, 10,000 pounds. Giant rubber puck (700 lbs) nearby.

**Address:** 801 Hat Trick Ave, Eveleth, MN 55734 [47.4641, -92.5405]

**Hours:** 24/7; U.S. Hockey Hall of Fame: Mon-Sat 9 AM - 5 PM, Sun 10 AM - 3 PM (summer)

**Fees:** Free to view stick; Hall of Fame admission $10 adults, $6 children

**History:** First stick 1995 (107 ft), second stick 2002 "Big Stick" made by Sentinel Structures. Hockey Plaza surrounds it with illustrated history.

**Seasonal Information:** Viewable year-round; Hall of Fame hours vary seasonally

**Status:** Excellent; "Hockeytown USA" pride. Located at U.S. Hockey Hall of Fame entrance.

**Source:** U.S. Hockey Hall of Fame
