## What Makes It World's Largest

23 feet tall, 24 feet wide. Every half hour: cuckoo bird pops out, followed by 5-piece robot oomph-pa band and dancing couple performing to Swiss polka music.

## Location

Corner of Main Street and N. Broadway, Sugarcreek, OH 44681

## Hours

Performs every half hour daily. Does not operate in winter months. Always visible.
