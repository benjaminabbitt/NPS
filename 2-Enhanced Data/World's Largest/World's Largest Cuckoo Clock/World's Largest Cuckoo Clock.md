# WORLD'S LARGEST CUCKOO CLOCK ✓ [CONTESTED]

**Location:** Sugarcreek, Ohio

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 23 feet tall, 24 feet wide. Every half hour: cuckoo bird pops out, followed by 5-piece robot oomph-pa band and dancing couple performing to Swiss polka music.

**Address:** Corner of Main Street and N. Broadway, Sugarcreek, OH 44681

**Hours:** Performs every half hour daily. Does not operate in winter months. Always visible.

**Fees:** FREE

**History:** Commissioned by Alpine-Alpa restaurant (Wilmot, OH) in 1963. Took 12 years and $50,000 to build by clockmaker Karl Schleutermann. Featured on cover of Guinness Book of World Records 1977. Stood at restaurant for 30+ years. Restaurant closed 2009. Walnut Creek Cheese purchased clock and donated to Village of Sugarcreek 2010. Moved to current location May 2012. Fully restored 2007 (Hampton Hotel "Save-A-Landmark" program). Restored again by volunteers 2012 to working condition. Historical marker erected 2015 by Village of Sugarcreek and Ohio History Connection.

**Seasonal Information:** Performs daily spring through fall. Winter: no performances (mechanisms protected from cold), but clock always visible.

**Status:** Active. Well-maintained centerpiece of "Little Switzerland of Ohio" town. Sugarcreek offers Swiss/German heritage, cheese shops, Amish country.

**Note:** Contested - other cuckoo clocks have since claimed to be larger, but this held Guinness record in 1977.

**Source:** Visit Sugarcreek, World Record Academy, Historical Marker Database
