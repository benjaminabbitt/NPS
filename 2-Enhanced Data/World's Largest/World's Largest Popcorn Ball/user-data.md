## What Makes It World's Largest

9,370 pounds, 8-12 feet diameter, 24 feet circumference. Must be edible and handmade for Guinness.

## Location

1300 West Main Street, Sac City, IA (Sac City Museum Complex)

## Hours

Visible 24/7 in glass shelter; Museum varies
