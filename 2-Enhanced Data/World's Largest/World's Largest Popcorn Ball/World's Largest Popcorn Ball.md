# WORLD'S LARGEST POPCORN BALL ✓

**Location:** Sac City, Iowa

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 9,370 pounds, 8-12 feet diameter, 24 feet circumference. Must be edible and handmade for Guinness.

**Address:** 1300 West Main Street, Sac City, IA (Sac City Museum Complex)

**Hours:** Visible 24/7 in glass shelter; Museum varies

**Fees:** FREE

**History:** Sac City's 4th attempt at record. Current ball created June 18, 2016 by 250+ volunteers. Required 12 hours 40 minutes. Used 900 lbs popcorn, 2,500 lbs sugar, 1,400 lbs syrup.

**Status:** Displayed in custom glass pavilion. Some deterioration but still impressive.

**Source:** Roadtrippers
