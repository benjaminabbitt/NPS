# WASHINGTON MONUMENT, District of Columbia ✓

**World's Tallest Stone Structure and Tallest Obelisk**

**What Makes It World's Largest:** 555 feet tall (554.5 feet precisely), 898 steps to 50 landings

**Address:** 2 15th St NW, Washington, DC 20024 [38.8895, -77.0353]

**Hours:** 9am-5pm daily (extended summer hours to 10pm)

**Fees:** Free (timed tickets required via recreation.gov)

**History:** Construction began 1848, interrupted by Civil War, completed 1884. Designed by Robert Mills. Built to honor George Washington.

**Seasonal Information:** Open year-round. Peak summer.

**Source:** National Park Service
