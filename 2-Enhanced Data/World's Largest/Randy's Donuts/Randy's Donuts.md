# RANDY'S DONUTS [SELF-DECLARED]

**Location:** Inglewood, California

**What Makes It World's Largest:** Iconic giant donut structure. One of most recognizable donut buildings remaining from California's "Mimetic Architecture" era.

**Address:** 805 W Manchester Blvd, Inglewood, CA 90301 [33.9618, -118.3704]

**Hours:** Donut shop hours (typically early morning through evening)

**Fees:** FREE to view; purchases optional

**History:** Built 1953, designed by Robert Graham. One of five remaining from original 10 in "Big DoNut" chain. Part of California's 1950s programmatic/novelty architecture movement. Featured in numerous films, TV shows, music videos, commercials. Became LA cultural icon.

**Status:** Active donut shop. Highly photographed landmark.

**Source:** World's Largest Things, various
