# CARLSBAD CAVERNS BIG ROOM, New Mexico ✓

**Largest Cave Chamber in North America**

**What Makes It World's Largest:** Largest single cave chamber in North America by volume. Dimensions: 4,000 feet long × 625 feet wide × 255 feet high. Floor area 8.2 acres.

**Address:** 727 Carlsbad Caverns Highway, Carlsbad, NM 88220
**GPS:** 32.1477° N, 104.4435° W

**Hours:**
- Park: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily (ticket sales close 3:30 PM)
- Closed Christmas Day only
- Big Room Trail: 1.25-mile loop, 1.5 hours

**Access:** Natural Entrance Trail (1.25 miles, steep 750 ft descent) or Elevator. Temperature constant 56°F - bring jacket.

**Fees:**
- Entrance: $15 per person (16+); children under 15 free
- Reservations required via recreation.gov
- Audio guide $5
- Closed-toe shoes required

**History:** Known to Native Americans 1,000+ years. Modern exploration 1898. Capitan Reef limestone 250 million years old. Cave formation 4-6 million years ago. National Monument 1923, National Park 1930, UNESCO World Heritage 1995.

**Seasonal Information:** Best spring and fall. Bat Flight Program May-October at sunset.

**Source:** USGS, National Park Service
