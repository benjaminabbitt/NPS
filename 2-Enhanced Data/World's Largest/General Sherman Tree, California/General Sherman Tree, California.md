# GENERAL SHERMAN TREE, California ✓

**World's Largest Tree by Volume**

**What Makes It World's Largest:** Largest living single-stem tree on Earth at 52,500 cubic feet. Height 275 feet, base diameter 36 feet, circumference over 102 feet.

**Address:** Sequoia National Park, 47050 Generals Highway, Three Rivers, CA 93271
**GPS:** 36.5816° N, 118.7516° W

**Hours:** Park open year-round, 24 hours. Lodgepole Visitor Center typically 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM.

**Access:** Half-mile paved trail from parking lot (downhill to tree, uphill return). Wheelchair-accessible alternative trail available from disability parking.

**Fees:**
- Park entrance: $35 per vehicle (7-day pass)
- America the Beautiful Annual Pass: $80 (all National Parks)

**History:** Estimated 2,200-2,700 years old. Named 1879 by naturalist James Wolverton after General William Tecumseh Sherman. Officially identified as world's largest 1931. Protected by Sequoia National Park since 1890.

**Seasonal Information:** Best May-October for easiest access. Snow possible October-June; roads may require chains. Peak season summer - arrive early for parking.

**Source:** National Park Service, Wikipedia
