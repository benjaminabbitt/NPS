## What Makes It World's Largest

Largest living single-stem tree on Earth at 52,500 cubic feet. Height 275 feet, base diameter 36 feet, circumference over 102 feet.

## Location

Sequoia National Park, 47050 Generals Highway, Three Rivers, CA 93271

GPS: 36.5816° N, 118.7516° W

## Hours

Park open year-round, 24 hours. Lodgepole Visitor Center typically 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM.

- America the Beautiful Annual Pass: $80 (all National Parks)
