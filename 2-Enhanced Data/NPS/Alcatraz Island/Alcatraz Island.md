# Alcatraz Island

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-sally-port-museum"></a>**Sally Port Museum** (Alcatraz Island, San Francisco Bay, CA 94133; accessible during ferry operating hours, open all year except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 415-561-4900) [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/alca/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/alca/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Waymarking](https://www.waymarking.com/waymarks/wm11H1_Alcatraz_Island)]

- <a id="stamp-building-64"></a>**Building 64** (Alcatraz Island, San Francisco Bay, CA 94133; accessible during ferry operating hours, open all year except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 415-561-4900) [Source: [NPS Building 64](https://www.nps.gov/places/000/alcatraz-building-64.htm), [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/alca/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** Call 415-561-4900 or contact Alcatraz City Cruises at (415) 981-7625 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. Ferries depart from Pier 33, The Embarcadero & Bay Street, San Francisco, CA 94133.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-cellhouse-audio-tour"></a>**Cellhouse Audio Tour** (30-45 minutes) - Experience the award-winning "Doing Time: The Alcatraz Cellhouse Tour," a self-guided audio tour included with ferry admission that features personal stories from former inmates and correctional officers, narrating the prison's history as you explore cell blocks, the dining hall, and solitary confinement; Alcatraz Island, San Francisco Bay, CA 94133; accessible during ferry operating hours (ferries depart about every half hour starting at 8:45 AM), open all year except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 415-561-4900 or (415) 981-7625 for ferry tickets [Source: [NPS Tour the Cellhouse](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/tour-the-cellhouse.htm), [Alcatraz Cellhouse Audio Tour](https://alcatraz-island.com/alcatraz-cellhouse-tour/), [Audio Trails Review](http://audiotrails.co.uk/review-alcatraz-island-cellhouse-audio-tour/), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/alca/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- <a id="activity-explore-island-exhibits-and-historic-buildings"></a>**Explore Island Exhibits and Historic Buildings** (45-60 minutes) - Discover exhibits including "The Big Lockup" examining mass incarceration, "Red Power on Alcatraz" commemorating the 1969-1971 American Indian occupation, and the "Welcome to Indian Land" exhibit in Building 64; explore the historic Guardhouse (oldest building from 1857), sally port entrance, and New Industries Building; Alcatraz Island, San Francisco Bay, CA 94133; accessible during ferry operating hours, open all year except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; free with ferry admission; 415-561-4900 [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/alca/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/alca/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Alcatraz Buildings](https://alcatrazguidedtours.com/blog/buildings-on-alcatraz-island-history-and-what-to-see-now/)]

- <a id="activity-scenic-ferry-ride-with-bay-views"></a>**Scenic Ferry Ride with Bay Views** (12-15 minutes each way) - Enjoy spectacular views of San Francisco, the Golden Gate Bridge, Angel Island, and Coit Tower during the roundtrip ferry journey across San Francisco Bay; departs from Pier 33, The Embarcadero & Bay Street, San Francisco, CA 94133; ferries depart about every half hour starting at 8:45 AM, open all year except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; tickets start at $41 per person (book weeks to months in advance); (415) 981-7625 [Source: [Alcatraz City Cruises](https://www.cityexperiences.com/san-francisco/city-cruises/alcatraz/tour-options/), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/alca/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [The Tour Guy](https://thetourguy.com/travel-blog/usa/san-francisco/alcatraz/how-to-visit-alcatraz-island/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-alcatraz-historic-gardens"></a>**Alcatraz Historic Gardens** (30-45 minutes, or 1.5 hours with docent tour) - Explore beautifully restored gardens originally planted by military families and later maintained by inmates, featuring plants from around the world that have survived decades of neglect; docent-led tours available Fridays and Sundays at 9:30 AM (take first or second ferry), and gardeners available for questions Wednesdays 11:00 AM - 2:00 PM at Officer's Row Overlook; Alcatraz Island, San Francisco Bay, CA 94133; accessible during ferry operating hours; free with ferry admission; 415-561-4900 [Source: [Gardens of Alcatraz](https://www.parksconservancy.org/programs/npn/alcatraz-historic-gardens), [Alcatraz Gardens Tours](https://www.oursausalito.com/alcatraz/gardens-of-alcatraz.html), [City Experiences Gardens](https://www.cityexperiences.com/blog/alcatraz-islands-hidden-gem-the-gardens-of-alcatraz/)]

- <a id="gem-bird-sanctuary-and-wildlife-viewing"></a>**Bird Sanctuary and Wildlife Viewing** (15-30 minutes) - Observe hundreds of seabirds including western gulls, Brandt's cormorants, black-crowned night herons, and pigeon guillemots at this protected sanctuary; spring and summer visitors may find nesting areas roped off; best viewing late spring through summer; Alcatraz Island, San Francisco Bay, CA 94133; accessible during ferry operating hours year-round; free with ferry admission; 415-561-4900 [Source: [Travel Like a Pro](https://lucky-tuk-tuk.com/travel-like-a-pro-17-tips-for-visiting-alcatraz-island/), [Alcatraz Hidden Gems](https://www.alcatrazticketing.com/alcatraz-tours-2/)]

- <a id="gem-civil-war-era-military-fortifications"></a>**Civil War-Era Military Fortifications** (20-30 minutes) - Discover remains from Alcatraz's role as a military fortress before it became a federal prison, including Civil War-era cannons, guard posts, defensive walls, and the 1857 Guardhouse (oldest building on the island); scattered throughout the island beyond the main cellhouse area; Alcatraz Island, San Francisco Bay, CA 94133; accessible during ferry operating hours, open all year except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; free with ferry admission; 415-561-4900 [Source: [Alcatraz Hidden Gems](https://www.alcatrazticketing.com/alcatraz-tours-2/), [Lucky Tuk Tuk](https://lucky-tuk-tuk.com/travel-like-a-pro-17-tips-for-visiting-alcatraz-island/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-pier-39-and-sea-lions"></a>**Pier 39 and Sea Lions** (1-2 hours for quick visit, 3-4 hours to explore fully) - Watch hundreds of California sea lions hauled out on K-Dock (best viewing late July to mid-May with 150-600+ sea lions; fewer in June-July during breeding season), visit the Sea Lion Center, enjoy shops, restaurants, Aquarium of the Bay, street performances, and a two-story carousel; The Embarcadero and Beach Street, San Francisco, CA 94133 (2 minutes from Alcatraz ferry departure at Pier 33); open daily with stores 10:00 AM - 8:00 PM, restaurants 11:00 AM - 9:00 PM, Sea Lion Center Sunday-Thursday 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Friday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM; free to visit (individual attractions have fees); sea lion viewing is free [Source: [Pier 39](https://pier39.com/), [California Sea Lions](https://pier39.com/sealions/), [SF Tourism Tips](https://www.sftourismtips.com/san-francisco-sea-lions.html), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60713-d6352219-Reviews-Sea_Lion_Center-San_Francisco_California.html)]

- <a id="nearby-fort-point-national-historic-site"></a>**Fort Point National Historic Site** (1-1.5 hours) - Explore this Civil War-era brick fortress built in 1861, located directly beneath the Golden Gate Bridge's southern anchorage; watch the 30-minute award-winning video "Guardian of the Golden Gate," view exhibits including "Ready & Forward" and "Bridging the Golden Gate," and attend ranger programs including cannon loading demonstrations and crab fishing demonstrations (spring-fall); Fort Point, end of Marina Drive, Presidio, San Francisco, CA 94129 (4.5 miles from Pier 33); open Thursday-Monday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM (roof access closes 4:30 PM), exterior accessible 7 days a week, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; free admission; 415-561-4959 [Source: [NPS Fort Point Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/fopo/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Fort Point Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/fopo/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [SF Tourism Tips](https://www.sftourismtips.com/fort-point-national-historic-site.html)]

- <a id="nearby-crissy-field"></a>**Crissy Field** (1-3 hours) - Enjoy this restored tidal marsh and beach with spectacular views of Alcatraz, the Golden Gate Bridge, and San Francisco Bay; activities include walking, biking, bird watching, picnicking, beach activities, kite flying, kite surfing, kayaking, windsurfing, and fishing/crabbing at Torpedo Wharf (no license required); 603 Mason Street, San Francisco, CA 94129 (3.5 miles from Pier 33); open 24 hours, seven days a week; free admission with free parking at Crissy Field Beach Parking Lot off Mason Street; served by Muni buses 28 and 30 and Presidio GO Shuttle [Source: [Crissy Field Parks Conservancy](https://www.parksconservancy.org/parks/crissy-field), [NPS Crissy Field](https://www.nps.gov/prsf/planyourvisit/crissy-field-marsh-and-beach.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60713-d144990-Reviews-Crissy_Field-San_Francisco_California.html)]
