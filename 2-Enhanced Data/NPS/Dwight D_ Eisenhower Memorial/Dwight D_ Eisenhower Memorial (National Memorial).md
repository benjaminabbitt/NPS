# Dwight D. Eisenhower Memorial (National Memorial)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Survey Lodge Ranger Station** (1100 Ohio Drive SW, Washington, DC 20242 [38.887, -77.018]; Open 9:30 AM - 5:00 PM daily; 202-426-6841)

**Note:** Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. The Survey Lodge serves as a centralized location for National Mall and Memorial Parks passport stamps. [Source: [NPS Dwight D. Eisenhower Memorial](https://www.nps.gov/ddem/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [TripAdvisor NPS Cancellation Stamps Forum](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g28970-i40-k11696057-NPS_Cancellation_Stamps-Washington_DC_District_of_Columbia.html)]

## Key Activities

- **Explore the Memorial Sculptures and Tapestry** (30-45 minutes) - Walk through the four-acre urban park featuring three bronze statues by sculptor Sergey Eylanbekov depicting Eisenhower as a young boy ("Little Ike"), as Supreme Allied Commander with paratroopers before D-Day, and as President surrounded by advisors; view the striking 447-foot-long, 60-foot-tall stainless steel tapestry depicting the Normandy coastline; examine bas-relief panels showing the D-Day beach landings and a world map; 540 Independence Avenue SW (between 4th and 6th Streets SW), Washington, DC 20024 [38.887, -77.018]; Memorial grounds open 24 hours daily, Rangers available 9:30 AM - 5:00 PM. [Source: [Washington.org Visiting Guide](https://washington.org/dc-guide-to/dwight-d-eisenhower-memorial), [Wikipedia Dwight D. Eisenhower Memorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwight_D._Eisenhower_Memorial), [NPS Memorial Introduction](https://www.nps.gov/ddem/ddemintro.htm), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g28970-d21339252-Reviews-Dwight_D_Eisenhower_Memorial-Washington_DC_District_of_Columbia.html)]

- **Read Memorial Inscriptions** (15 minutes) - Review inscriptions from Eisenhower's own speeches, including his 1945 Abilene Homecoming Speech where he declared "the proudest thing I can claim is that I am from Abilene"; 540 Independence Avenue SW, Washington, DC; Memorial open 24 hours daily. [Source: [NPS Memorial Inscriptions](https://www.nps.gov/ddem/learn/historyculture/ddeminscriptions.htm), [National Park Foundation](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/dwight-d-eisenhower-memorial)]

- **Take Self-Guided Audio Tour** (30-45 minutes) - Access the free smartphone audio tour to learn about the memorial's design by architect Frank Gehry and the symbolism behind each element; 540 Independence Avenue SW, Washington, DC; Available during all hours. [Source: [Washington.org Visiting Guide](https://washington.org/dc-guide-to/dwight-d-eisenhower-memorial)]

- **Attend Ranger Programs** (varies) - Meet with rangers and park volunteers who are present on the memorial grounds to answer questions about Eisenhower's life and legacy; 540 Independence Avenue SW, Washington, DC; Rangers available 9:30 AM - 5:00 PM daily. [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/ddem/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- **Evening Visit for Tapestry Viewing** (20-30 minutes) - View the stainless steel tapestry illuminated at night when it is particularly stunning; 540 Independence Avenue SW, Washington, DC; Memorial open 24 hours daily. [Source: [L'Observatoire International](https://lobsintl.com/project/dwight-d-eisenhower-memorial/), [Washington.org](https://washington.org/dc-guide-to/dwight-d-eisenhower-memorial)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Under the Stars at the Eisenhower Memorial** (seasonal event, 90 minutes) - Attend special stargazing programs celebrating Eisenhower's contributions to space exploration and science, including telescope viewing and educational presentations; 540 Independence Avenue SW, Washington, DC; Seasonal evening programs, check Trust for the National Mall website for dates. [Source: [Trust for the National Mall](https://nationalmall.org/calendar/eisenhower-under-the-stars-2024)]

- **Photography from Capitol View** (15 minutes) - The memorial location offers a direct view of the US Capitol Building, creating unique photo opportunities that combine both memorials in one frame; 540 Independence Avenue SW, Washington, DC; Best views during daylight hours. [Source: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g28970-d21339252-Reviews-Dwight_D_Eisenhower_Memorial-Washington_DC_District_of_Columbia.html)]

## Also Nearby

- **National Air and Space Museum** (2-3 hours, directly across Independence Avenue) - Explore the Smithsonian's aviation and space collection including the Wright Brothers' 1903 Flyer, Apollo 11 command module, and Spirit of St. Louis; view IMAX films and planetarium shows; 600 Independence Ave SW, Washington, DC 20560; Open daily 10:00 AM - 5:30 PM. [Source: [National Air and Space Museum](https://airandspace.si.edu/visit), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g28970-d107967-t339607-How_much_time_should_we_allocate_for_this_museum.html)]

- **United States Capitol Building Tour** (1 hour tour, 5-minute walk) - Take a guided tour of the Capitol including the Rotunda, National Statuary Hall, and the Crypt, beginning with a 13-minute orientation film; US Capitol Visitor Center, First Street SE, Washington, DC 20004; Visitor Center open Monday-Saturday 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM, last tour begins 3:20 PM. [Source: [US Capitol Visitor Center](https://www.visitthecapitol.gov/visit/book-a-tour), [Capitol Hours & Info](https://www.visitthecapitol.gov/visit/know-before-you-go/hours-info)]

- **Library of Congress** (90 minutes - 2 hours, 10-minute walk) - Visit the Thomas Jefferson Building to view the historic Main Reading Room, Great Hall, and rotating exhibitions; take a free one-hour guided tour or explore self-guided; 101 Independence Ave SE, Washington, DC 20540; Open Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM (open late until 8:00 PM Thursdays). [Source: [Library of Congress Visit](https://www.loc.gov/visit/), [TripAdvisor Library of Congress](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g28970-d104859-t3627410-How_much_time_should_I_allot_for_visiting_this.html)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 1-2 hours for a comprehensive visit to the memorial itself, including viewing all sculptures, the tapestry, inscriptions, and taking the audio tour.
