# Fort Frederica NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Fort Frederica Visitor Center** - Passport stamps are located beside the front ranger information desk in the visitor center; 6515 Frederica Rd, St. Simons Island, GA 31522 (31.223111, -81.389279); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 912-638-3639 [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/fofr/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/fofr/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [NPPlan Visitor Center](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/georgia/fort-frederica-national-monument-park-at-a-glance/fort-frederica-national-monument-visitor-center/)]

**Note:** Call 912-638-3639 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. Admission to the park is free.

## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center Film & Exhibits (1 hour)** - Watch the 23-minute historical film "History Uncovered" shown every 30 minutes from 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM, and explore museum exhibits displaying archaeological artifacts and a model of the 18th-century fort and town; 6515 Frederica Rd, St. Simons Island, GA 31522 (31.223111, -81.389279); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily [Source: [NPPlan Visitor Center](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/georgia/fort-frederica-national-monument-park-at-a-glance/fort-frederica-national-monument-visitor-center/), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fofr/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [GoSouthSavannah](https://gosouthsavannah.com/things-to-do/historic-forts/fort-frederica.html)]

- **Self-Guided Walking Tour of Fort and Town Site (1 hour)** - Explore the ruins of the 18th-century British fort and colonial town on a roughly one-mile trail system with interpretive signs throughout the grounds; 6515 Frederica Rd, St. Simons Island, GA 31522 (31.223111, -81.389279); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily [Source: [NPPlan Touring Fort Frederica](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/georgia/fort-frederica-national-monument-park-at-a-glance/fort-frederica-national-monument-touring-fort-frederica/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g35241-d143131-Reviews-Fort_Frederica_National_Monument-Saint_Simons_Island_Golden_Isles_of_Georgia_Georgi.html), [Lake.com](https://www.lake.com/parks/fort-frederica-national-monument/)]

- **Ranger-Guided Tours (30-45 minutes)** - Join Park Rangers for guided tours covering approximately a quarter mile of the site, typically offered at 11:00 AM when the visitor center is open; 6515 Frederica Rd, St. Simons Island, GA 31522 (31.223111, -81.389279); typically 11:00 AM when available [Source: [NPPlan Touring Fort Frederica](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/georgia/fort-frederica-national-monument-park-at-a-glance/fort-frederica-national-monument-touring-fort-frederica/), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fofr/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Full Trail System Hike (1.5 hours)** - Walk the complete trail system that loops around the park property, covering 2.3 miles with time to explore all historical features and the nature trail; 6515 Frederica Rd, St. Simons Island, GA 31522 (31.223111, -81.389279); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/georgia/fort-frederica-national-monument), [NPPlan Hiking Trails](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/georgia/fort-frederica-national-monument-park-at-a-glance/fort-frederica-national-monument-hiking-trails/)]

- **Bloody Marsh Unit Visit (30 minutes)** - Visit the site of the 1742 Battle of Bloody Marsh where British forces defeated Spanish troops, featuring interpretive signs and marsh views; 11806 Old Demere Rd, St. Simons Island, GA 31522 (31.156628, -81.380510); 8:30 AM - 4:00 PM daily, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day [Source: [NPS Bloody Marsh Unit](https://www.nps.gov/fofr/learn/historyculture/bloodymarshunit.htm), [Explore Georgia Bloody Marsh](https://exploregeorgia.org/st-simons-island/general/historic-sites-trails-tours/bloody-marsh-battle-site), [NPPlan Bloody Marsh](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/georgia/fort-frederica-national-monument-park-at-a-glance/fort-frederica-national-monument-bloody-marsh-memorial-unit/)]

- **Picnic Area (flexible timing)** - Enjoy a picnic at one of seven tables located behind the Visitor Center in the designated picnic area; 6515 Frederica Rd, St. Simons Island, GA 31522 (31.223111, -81.389279); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fofr/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Junior Archaeologist & Junior Ranger Programs (1-2 hours)** - Participate in the Junior Archaeologist Program or Junior Ranger Underwater Explorer Program designed for young visitors; 6515 Frederica Rd, St. Simons Island, GA 31522 (31.223111, -81.389279); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fofr/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Oak-Lined Avenue Walk (15-20 minutes)** - Stroll beneath towering live oaks dripping with Spanish moss on the scenic path that once led to the heart of the bustling 18th-century town of Frederica; 6515 Frederica Rd, St. Simons Island, GA 31522 (31.223111, -81.389279); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily [Source: [Travel The Parks](https://traveltheparks.com/no-one-talks-about-treasured-fort-frederica-national-monument/), [The Outbound](https://www.theoutbound.com/adventures/discover-fort-frederica-national-monument)]

- **Wesley Memorial & Gardens (20-30 minutes)** - Visit the peaceful memorial dedicated to John and Charles Wesley, founders of the Methodist Church, featuring an 18-foot Celtic cross crafted from Georgia stone and over 4,000 azaleas representing 60+ varieties in serene wooded grounds; adjacent to Wesley United Methodist Church near Fort Frederica, St. Simons Island, GA 31522 (approximately 31.220, -81.387); open during daylight hours, free admission [Source: [Explore Southern History Wesley Memorial](https://www.exploresouthernhistory.com/wesleymemorial.html), [HMDB Wesley Memorial](https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=58588), [TripAdvisor Wesley Gardens](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g35241-d4760248-Reviews-Wesley_Memorial_Gardens-Saint_Simons_Island_Golden_Isles_of_Georgia_Georgia.html)]

- **Colonial Kitchen Garden (15 minutes)** - Explore the recreated colonial kitchen garden displaying historical plant specimens and medicinal herbs used during the 18th century; 6515 Frederica Rd, St. Simons Island, GA 31522 (31.223111, -81.389279); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily [Source: [Travel The Parks](https://traveltheparks.com/no-one-talks-about-treasured-fort-frederica-national-monument/)]

- **Frederica River Views (15 minutes)** - Take in sunset views west across the salt Marshes of Glynn along the beautiful bend in the Frederica River from the fort site; 6515 Frederica Rd, St. Simons Island, GA 31522 (31.223111, -81.389279); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily [Source: [Travel The Parks](https://traveltheparks.com/no-one-talks-about-treasured-fort-frederica-national-monument/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Christ Church, Frederica (30-45 minutes, 0.5 miles)** - Visit this historic 1884 Gothic-style Episcopal church with roots dating to 1736 when John and Charles Wesley preached on these grounds, Georgia's second-oldest parish founded in 1808; 6329 Frederica Rd, St. Simons Island, GA 31522 (approximately 31.225, -81.393); Tuesday-Sunday 2:00 PM - 5:00 PM, cemetery sunrise-sunset, closed Mondays; 912-638-8683 [Source: [Christ Church Official](https://www.ccfssi.org/), [Explore Southern History Christ Church](https://www.exploresouthernhistory.com/christchurch.html), [Golden Isles Christ Church](https://www.goldenisles.com/listing/christ-church-frederica/129/)]

- **St. Simons Lighthouse Museum (1-1.5 hours, 5 miles)** - Climb 129 steps to the top of the 1872 lighthouse for panoramic views of the Golden Isles and explore the museum in the historic Keeper's Dwelling with exhibits on coastal Georgia history; 101 12th St, St. Simons Island, GA 31522 (31.134090, -81.393600); Monday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM - 5:00 PM, tickets sold until 4:30 PM; $12 adults, $6 children ages 6-12, free under 6 [Source: [Coastal Georgia Historical Society](https://www.coastalgeorgiahistory.org/visit/st-simons-lighthouse-museum/), [Explore Georgia Lighthouse](https://exploregeorgia.org/st-simons-island/general/historic-sites-trails-tours/st-simons-lighthouse-museum), [Golden Isles Lighthouse](https://www.goldenisles.com/listing/st-simons-lighthouse-museum/183/)]

- **Georgia Sea Turtle Center (1-2 hours, 15 miles)** - Visit Georgia's only sea turtle rehabilitation hospital and education center on Jekyll Island to see sea turtle patients in recovery and learn about conservation through interactive exhibits; 214 Stable Rd, Jekyll Island, GA 31527 (31.059948, -81.419765); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas, and New Year's Day; $11 ages 13+, $9 ages 4-12, free ages 3 and under [Source: [Jekyll Island Sea Turtle Center](https://www.jekyllisland.com/activities/georgia-sea-turtle-center/), [Explore Georgia Sea Turtle](https://exploregeorgia.org/jekyll-island/entertainment/animal-parks-zoos/georgia-sea-turtle-center), [Explore Jekyll Island](https://www.explorejekyllisland.com/Sea_Turtle_Center.shtml)]

- **Jekyll Island Historic District (2-3 hours, 15 miles)** - Explore more than 30 restored late 19th and early 20th century structures that now house cottages, bookstores, art galleries, and museums, including the Gothic-influenced Historic Faith Chapel with ornate stained-glass windows; Jekyll Island, GA 31527 (approximately 31.065, -81.417); hours vary by venue [Source: [Explore Georgia Jekyll Island](https://exploregeorgia.org/things-to-do/blog/8-cant-miss-sites-on-jekyll-and-st-simons-islands), [The Fun Times Guide](https://travel.thefuntimesguide.com/things-to-do-in-jekyll-island-georgia/)]
