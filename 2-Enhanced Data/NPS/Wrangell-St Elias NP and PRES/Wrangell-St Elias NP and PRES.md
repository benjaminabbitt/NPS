# Wrangell-St Elias NP and PRES (National Park and Preserve)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-copper-center"></a>**Wrangell-St. Elias Visitor Center, Copper Center** (Mile 106.8 Richardson Highway, Copper Center, AK 99573 (61.836564, -145.2257613); May 12–September 14, daily 9:00 AM–5:00 PM; 907-822-7250) - Main visitor center with exhibits, interpretive programs, ranger-led activities, and Junior Ranger Program. [Source: [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/wrst/planyourvisit/wrangell-st-elias-visitor-center.htm), [2025 Hours](https://www.nps.gov/wrst/learn/news/wrangell-st-elias-national-park-and-preserve-visitor-center-hours-for-summer-2025.htm)]

- <a id="stamp-kennecott"></a>**Kennecott Visitor Center** (Kennecott Mill Town, McCarthy, AK (61.48123, -142.88529); May 24–September 5, daily 9:00 AM–4:30 PM, closed for lunch; 907-554-1105) - Located in historic Blackburn School building in the heart of the Kennecott mining town. [Source: [NPS Kennecott VC](https://www.nps.gov/wrst/planyourvisit/kennecott-visitor-center.htm), [2025 Hours](https://www.nps.gov/wrst/learn/news/wrangell-st-elias-national-park-and-preserve-visitor-center-hours-for-summer-2025.htm)]

- <a id="stamp-chitina"></a>**Chitina Ranger Station** (Downtown Chitina at Mile 33 Edgerton Highway, Chitina, AK (61.5149, -144.4400); May 24–August 30, limited hours, staffing dependent; 907-823-2205) - Located in downtown Chitina; upon entering town southbound, turn left at Chitina Hotel, drive 0.2 mile down gravel road, station on left. Call ahead for current hours. [Source: [NPS Chitina RS](https://www.nps.gov/wrst/planyourvisit/chitina-ranger-station.htm), [2025 Hours](https://www.nps.gov/wrst/learn/news/wrangell-st-elias-national-park-and-preserve-visitor-center-hours-for-summer-2025.htm)]

- <a id="stamp-slana"></a>**Slana Ranger Station** (Mile 0.5 Nabesna Road at Mile 59 Tok-Cutoff Highway, Slana, AK (62.7129, -143.9899); May 24–September 21, daily 9:00 AM–5:00 PM, closed for lunch; 907-822-7401) - Gateway to the northern portion of the park and start of the Nabesna Road scenic drive. [Source: [NPS Slana RS](https://www.nps.gov/wrst/planyourvisit/slana-ranger-station.htm), [2025 Hours](https://www.nps.gov/wrst/learn/news/wrangell-st-elias-national-park-and-preserve-visitor-center-hours-for-summer-2025.htm)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-kennecott-mill-tour"></a>**Kennecott Mill Town Tour** (2 hours) - Historic guided tour of the 14-story Concentration Mill and other historic buildings in the Kennecott Mines National Historic Landmark, the largest free-standing wooden structure in North America. Exclusive concession tour operated by St. Elias Alpine Guides provides access inside the mill. Tours offered at 3:30 PM daily, May–September; Kennecott Mill Town, McCarthy, AK (61.48123, -142.88529); $65 per person; 907-554-4445. [Source: [St. Elias Alpine Guides](https://www.steliasguides.com/trips/kennecott-mill-town-tour/), [ALASKA.ORG](https://www.alaska.org/detail/kennecott-mill-town-tour-with-st-elias-alpine-guides), [NPS Kennecott Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/wrst/planyourvisit/kennecott-visitor-center.htm)]

- <a id="activity-root-glacier"></a>**Root Glacier Trail Hike** (3–5 hours) - Most accessible glacier hike in the park; 4.8 miles round trip (2.4 miles one-way) from Kennecott to glacier overlook, with 839 feet elevation gain. Trail is relatively level for first 1.5 miles, then becomes moderately difficult with steep, loose rock in final section. Allow additional time (5–6 hours total) to explore on the glacier itself with crampons strongly recommended for ice walking. Trailhead in Kennecott, McCarthy, AK (61.48123, -142.88529); accessible May–September; free. [Source: [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/root-glacier-trail-wrangell-st-elias/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/alaska/root-glacier-trail), [Lita of the Pack](https://www.litaofthepack.com/hiking-the-root-glacier-trail/), [NPS Root Glacier](https://www.nps.gov/places/root-glacier.htm)]

- <a id="activity-glacier-hike-guided"></a>**Half-Day Guided Glacier Hike** (5–6 hours) - Guided glacier hiking experience covering approximately 5 miles over 5–6 hours, including 1 hour of instruction and glacier trekking on Root Glacier. Includes crampons, trekking poles, and expert guide. Tours at 9:00 AM daily, May–September; departs from Kennecott, McCarthy, AK (61.48123, -142.88529); $135 per person; 907-554-4445. [Source: [St. Elias Alpine Guides](https://www.steliasguides.com/trips/half-day-glacier-hike/), [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/root-glacier-trail-wrangell-st-elias/)]

- <a id="activity-flightseeing"></a>**Flightseeing Tours** (50–120 minutes) - Aerial tours of glaciers, mountains, and icefields. Multiple tour options: 50-minute tour ($330) covers Mile High Cliffs, Nizina Lake, Rohn and Regal Glaciers; 70-minute tour ($405) includes Chitistone Canyon and 13 glaciers (most recommended); 90-minute tour ($445) features Bagley Icefield and Mt. St. Elias or University Range; custom 2-hour tours available. Tours operate May 15–September 15 with 2-person minimum; departs from McCarthy Airport (61.437, -142.903); operated by Wrangell Mountain Air, 800-478-1160. [Source: [Wrangell Mountain Air](https://www.wrangellmountainair.com/alaska-flightseeing-tours), [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/wrangell-st-elias-national-park-flightseeing-tour/), [WMA Rates](https://www.wrangellmountainair.com/rates-overview)]

- <a id="activity-mccarthy-kennecott"></a>**McCarthy & Kennecott Exploration** (3–4 hours) - Explore the historic mining towns of McCarthy and Kennecott, including walking Main Street, visiting the Historical Museum, general store, restaurants, and viewing historic buildings. Towns are 4 miles apart (1.5-hour walk) or use authorized shuttle service. McCarthy offers restaurants, lodging, gift shops, and museum; Kennecott features the massive Concentration Mill and historic mining infrastructure. McCarthy town center and Kennecott Mill Town, AK; accessible year-round, best May–September; free to explore. [Source: [Travel Alaska McCarthy/Kennecott](https://www.travelalaska.com/destinations/cities-towns/kennicott-mccarthy), [ALASKA.ORG McCarthy](https://www.alaska.org/destination/mccarthy), [Travel Alaska 6 Things](https://www.travelalaska.com/explore-alaska/articles/6-things-do-kennicott-mccarthy)]

- <a id="activity-visitor-center-programs"></a>**Visitor Center Programs & Ranger-Led Activities** (30–90 minutes) - Free interpretive programs including ranger-led walks, talks, and evening programs offered daily late May through mid-September at Copper Center, Kennecott, and Slana visitor centers. Junior Ranger Program available for children. Activities include guided walks down Main Street in Kennecott (30–60 minutes) and interpretive talks about park history, geology, and wildlife. Wrangell-St. Elias Visitor Center, Mile 106.8 Richardson Hwy, Copper Center, AK 99573 (61.836564, -145.2257613); May–September, check with visitor center for daily schedule; 907-822-7250; free. [Source: [NPS Ranger Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/wrst/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-things-to-do-in-wrangell-st-elias-national-park/), [Copper Center VC](https://www.alaska.org/detail/wrangell-st-elias-natl-park-vis-ctr)]

- <a id="activity-bonanza-mine"></a>**Bonanza Mine Trail** (7–8 hours) - Strenuous hike to historic Bonanza Mine with spectacular views over Kennicott and Root Glaciers; 8.25–8.4 miles round trip with 3,930 feet elevation gain from 2,030 to 5,865 feet elevation. Trail climbs steadily up Bonanza Ridge to mine buildings at 5,865 feet. Features panoramic mountain and glacier views. Allow 4–6 hours for faster hikers, 7–7.5 hours for average pace. Trailhead in Kennecott, McCarthy, AK (61.48123, -142.88529); accessible mid-June through September; free. [Source: [Earth Trekkers Bonanza](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/bonanza-mine-trail-wrangell-st-elias/), [AllTrails Bonanza](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/alaska/bonanza-mine-trail), [Adventures of a PLUSk](https://adventuresofaplusk.com/bonanza-mine-trail-in-wrangell-st-elias-national-park/)]

- <a id="activity-jumbo-mine"></a>**Jumbo Mine Trail** (8–10 hours) - Strenuous hike to Jumbo Mine claims on Bonanza Ridge; 10 miles round trip (5 miles one-way) with 3,400 feet elevation gain. Branches off from Bonanza Mine Trail approximately 2 miles up with slightly less elevation gain but longer distance. Features spectacular mountain scenery and historical mining artifacts. Guided tours available with St. Elias Alpine Guides. Trailhead in Kennecott, McCarthy, AK (61.48123, -142.88529); accessible mid-June through September; free (self-guided) or fee for guided tours; 907-554-4445. [Source: [NPS Jumbo Mine Trail PDF](https://www.nps.gov/wrst/planyourvisit/upload/Jumbo-Mine-Trail-508-compliant.pdf), [ALASKA.ORG Jumbo](https://www.alaska.org/detail/jumbo-mine-trail), [St. Elias Alpine Guides](https://www.steliasguides.com/trips/jumbo-mine-day-hike/)]

- <a id="activity-nabesna-road"></a>**Nabesna Road Scenic Drive** (3 hours) - 42-mile scenic gravel road through northern portion of park with mountain views, wildlife viewing (moose, caribou, nesting ducks, trumpeter swans), hiking trails, picnic areas, and creek crossings. Takes 1.5 hours one-way at careful pace. Best visited May–September. Features views of Mt. Sanford (16,237 feet, tallest peak visible from road) and Mt. Wrangell (14,163 feet, park's only active volcano). Download free Nabesna Road Audio Guide. 4WD recommended for stream crossings. Starts at Slana Ranger Station, Mile 0.5 Nabesna Road, Slana, AK (62.7129, -143.9899); accessible year-round, best May–September; free. [Source: [NPS Nabesna Road](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/nabesna-road-drive-hike-bike-view-wildlife-picnic-scenic-vistas.htm), [ALASKA.ORG Nabesna](https://www.alaska.org/guide/nabesna-road), [NPS Nabesna Guide](https://www.nps.gov/wrst/planyourvisit/nabesna-road-guide.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="hidden-nikolai-pass"></a>**Nikolai Pass Backcountry Route** (2–3 days) - Remote backcountry hiking route starting with charter flight to Nikolai Pass, following sheep trails along ridge south of Nikolai Creek down to McCarthy Creek, then following overgrown old roadbed to McCarthy; approximately 15 miles. Requires flight charter for drop-off. Features expansive alpine tundra, wildlife viewing, and extreme solitude. Charter from McCarthy Airport; accessible July–August; flight charter $800–1,200. [Source: [Indigo Alpine Guides](https://www.indigoalpineguides.com/news/backpacking-wrangell-st-elias-national-park-a-planning-guide), [NPS Backpacking](https://www.nps.gov/wrst/planyourvisit/backpacking.htm)]

- <a id="hidden-skolai-pass"></a>**Skolai Pass Backpacking** (7–10 days) - One of Alaska's most spectacular backcountry routes with views of massive glaciers, alpine tundra, and four mountain ranges. Requires fly-in/fly-out charter service. Route offers unparalleled landscape and wildlife photography opportunities in extreme wilderness setting. Most backpackers rarely encounter other hikers. Charter from McCarthy Airport or Chitina; accessible late June–August; flight charter $1,500–2,500. [Source: [Expeditions Alaska](https://www.expeditionsalaska.com/trip/photo-tour/alaska-landscapes-photo-tour/), [NPS Backpacking in Wrangell-St Elias](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/backpacking-in-wrangell-st-elias.htm)]

- <a id="hidden-trail-lost-creek"></a>**Trail Creek to Lost Creek Loop** (2–3 days) - Backcountry loop hike following Trail Creek ORV trail to its end, navigating up creek and over pass, then descending Lost Creek to connect with Lost Creek ORV trail back to road; 24 miles total, can be hiked in either direction. Features remote valleys, mountain passes, and creek navigation. Trailhead on McCarthy Road, approximately Mile 40–45; accessible July–September; free. [Source: [Indigo Alpine Guides](https://www.indigoalpineguides.com/news/backpacking-wrangell-st-elias-national-park-a-planning-guide), [NPS Backcountry](https://www.nps.gov/wrst/planyourvisit/backcountry.htm)]

- <a id="hidden-nabesna-rambler"></a>**Rambler Mine Hike (Nabesna Road)** (4–6 hours) - Beautiful hike at end of Nabesna Road to historic Rambler Mine. Less-visited trail in northern park section with excellent mountain views and historical mining remnants. Trailhead at end of Nabesna Road (Mile 42), Slana, AK; accessible July–September; free. [Source: [Indigo Alpine Guides](https://www.indigoalpineguides.com/news/backpacking-wrangell-st-elias-national-park-a-planning-guide), [NPS Nabesna Trails](https://home.nps.gov/wrst/planyourvisit/nabesna-road-trails.htm)]

- <a id="hidden-multiday-rafting"></a>**Multi-Day River Rafting Expeditions** (3–15 days) - Remote river rafting trips on Kennicott, Nizina, Chitina, and Copper Rivers. "Rivers to Riches" 3–4 day trip combines four rivers through park wilderness; 5-day trip from Chitina to Cordova; 8-day Copper River expedition; 15-day trip from Nizina River headwaters to ocean near Cordova. Daily river time 2–6 hours. Features pristine wilderness, wildlife viewing, glaciers, and extreme remoteness. Operated by Raft the Wrangells and St. Elias Alpine Guides; trips run June–August; $1,800–6,500 depending on length. [Source: [Raft the Wrangells](https://raftthewrangells.com/alaska-rafting-trips/overnight-multiday-rafting-trips/), [St. Elias Copper River](https://www.steliasguides.com/trips/copper-river-rafting/), [ALASKA.ORG Rafting](https://www.alaska.org/detail/copper-river-guides-rafting-trips)]

- <a id="hidden-photography"></a>**Photography Locations & Tours** (4–8 hours) - Exceptional photography opportunities throughout park including Mt. Blackburn (16,390 feet) towering over Kennicott Glacier valley, Root Glacier, the Concentration Mill (largest free-standing wooden structure in North America), Skolai Pass landscapes, and four converging mountain ranges. Best timing: early morning and late evening for wildlife and optimal light, summer (late May–early September) for access. Guided photography tours available focusing on landscape, glacier, and aerial photography. Various locations throughout park; accessible May–September; free (self-guided) or guided tours $400–800/day. [Source: [Expeditions Alaska Photos](https://www.expeditionsalaska.com/photo/wrangell-st-elias-national-park-photos/), [Photographers Wrangells](https://www.steliasguides.com/blog/photographers-wrangells-big-8/), [National Park Magazine](https://nationalparkmagazine.com/2025/05/18/wrangell-st-elias-national-preserve/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-george-ashby"></a>**George Ashby Memorial Museum** (1 hour, 0 miles from Copper Center Visitor Center) - Historic museum in rustic log bunkhouses featuring exhibits on early gold and copper mining, Russian artifacts, and Trail of '98 history. Located next to original Copper Center Lodge site. Mile 101–104.6 Old Richardson Highway/Copper Center Loop Road, Copper Center, AK 99573; May 15–September 15, Monday–Sunday 10:00 AM–5:00 PM; 907-822-3922; free, donations accepted. [Source: [ALASKA.ORG George Ashby](https://www.alaska.org/detail/george-ashby-memorial-museum), [Old Town Copper Center](https://www.oldtowncoppercenter.com/history/), [Museums USA](https://www.museumsusa.org/museums/info/8807)]

- <a id="nearby-copper-river-rafting"></a>**Copper River Rafting (Day Trips)** (4 hours, departs from Copper Center area, 45 minutes from Copper Center) - Day rafting trips on the Copper River with options ranging from gentle floats to challenging Class III–IV rapids. Full-day trips include scenic float through Copper River Valley with mountain and glacier views. Operated by Copper River Guides and other local outfitters; trips run June–August; Copper Center area; $150–250 per person. [Source: [Copper River Guides](https://www.alaska.org/detail/copper-river-guides-rafting-trips), [Princess Lodges Copper Center](https://www.princesslodges.com/princess-alaska-lodges/copper-river-lodge/copper-activities/), [ALASKA.ORG Copper Rafting](https://www.alaska.org/destination/copper-center/rafting-tours)]

- <a id="nearby-glennallen"></a>**Glennallen Services & Gateway** (3 hours for exploration, 65 miles / 1.5 hours north from Copper Center) - Primary service hub for Wrangell-St. Elias National Park visitors with lodging, restaurants, gas stations, grocery stores, and visitor services. Jumping-off point for backcountry adventures. Features Ahtna Cultural Center. Glennallen, AK, at junction of Glenn Highway and Richardson Highway; accessible year-round; services vary. [Source: [Travel Alaska Copper River Valley](https://www.travelalaska.com/destinations/regions/southcentral/copper-river-valley), [Princess Lodges Copper Valley](https://www.princesslodges.com/trip-planner/parks-destinations/copper-valley/)]

- <a id="nearby-valdez"></a>**Valdez** (full day, 115 miles / 2.5 hours south from Copper Center) - Coastal town on Prince William Sound offering glacier cruises, kayaking, fishing, and access to Columbia Glacier. Popular destination to combine with Wrangell-St. Elias visit. Features Alaska Marine Highway ferry terminal and Richardson Highway scenic drive through Keystone Canyon. Valdez, AK; accessible year-round; activities vary by season. [Source: [Travel Alaska Copper River Valley](https://www.travelalaska.com/destinations/regions/southcentral/copper-river-valley), [Valdez Alaska](https://www.valdezalaska.org/)]

**Total Recommended Time:** 3–4 days minimum for accessible highlights (Kennecott Mill Tour, Root Glacier hike, McCarthy/Kennecott exploration, flightseeing, visitor center programs), with 5–7 days recommended for additional activities like Bonanza/Jumbo Mine trails, Nabesna Road drive, and day rafting. Extended backcountry trips (Skolai Pass, Nikolai Pass, multi-day rafting) require 7–15 additional days. [Source: [Sunshine Whispers 3 Days](https://sunshinewhispers.com/3-days-in-wrangell-st-elias-national-park-itinerary/), [Earth Trekkers Guide](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/wrangell-st-elias-national-park/), [ALASKA.ORG 5 Day Itinerary](https://www.alaska.org/trip-ideas/5-day/anchorage-wrangell-st-elias-anchorage), [Through My Lens 2 Day](https://www.throughmylens.com/alaska/how-to-visit-wrangell-st-elias-national-park/)]

**Access Note:** McCarthy/Kennecott area requires driving 314 miles (7–8 hours) from Anchorage, including 60-mile gravel McCarthy Road (2–3 hours) from Chitina. Road ends at Kennicott River footbridge; no vehicle access beyond this point. Shuttle service available between McCarthy and Kennecott (4 miles). Many rental car agencies prohibit vehicles on McCarthy Road. [Source: [Handpicked Alaska McCarthy Road](https://handpickedalaska.com/blog/ultimate-guide-to-driving-the-mccarthy-road-2021), [ALASKA.ORG Anchorage to McCarthy](https://www.alaska.org/guide/anchorage-to-mccarthy-kennicott), [Alaska Adventurers How to Get to McCarthy](https://alaskaadventurers.com/how-to-get-to-mccarthy-alaska/)]
