# Canaveral NS (National Seashore)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Apollo Beach Visitor Center** (7611 S. Atlantic Ave, New Smyrna Beach, FL 32169 (29.0339, -80.9098); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, closed Christmas Day; 386-428-3384) [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/cana/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/cana/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Contact](https://www.nps.gov/cana/contacts.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Explore 24 Miles of Pristine Beach** (30 minutes to all day) - Walk or swim along Florida's longest undeveloped Atlantic coastline; five beach parking lots along north entrance, with Lot #1 most popular and Lots 2-4 offering more solitude; best for swimming, surfing, shelling, and surf fishing [Source: [Visit Florida](https://www.visitflorida.com/travel-ideas/articles/beach-guide-canaveral-national-seashore/), [Florida Rambler](https://www.floridarambler.com/beaches/canaveral-national-seashore/)]

- **Kayak Mosquito Lagoon** (2-4 hours) - Launch kayaks from picnic area behind Turtle Mound and paddle to pristine islands; access to 14 primitive tent sites on select islands with picnic tables and fire rings [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/cana/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Florida State Parks Guide](https://www.floridastateparks.org/sites/default/files/media/file/Canaveral%20National%20Seashore%20Real%20Florida%20Guide.pdf)]

- **Hike Turtle Mound Trail** (15-30 minutes) - Climb 0.3-mile trail with 22 feet elevation gain to prehistoric shell midden rising 50 feet above sea level offering panoramic views and potential manatee sightings [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/florida/canaveral-national-seashore), [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/cana/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)]

- **Wildlife Viewing and Birdwatching** (1-2 hours) - Observe diverse wildlife including sea turtles, manatees, and thousands of migratory birds; best viewing at Black Point Wildlife Drive in adjacent Merritt Island NWR [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/cana/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Hike Castle Windy Trail** (30-45 minutes) - Walk 0.8-mile trail at Parking Area #3 through shade between Mosquito Lagoon and ocean to large Timucuan Indian midden dating to 1000 AD [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/florida/canaveral-national-seashore), [Florida State Parks Guide](https://www.floridastateparks.org/sites/default/files/media/file/Canaveral%20National%20Seashore%20Real%20Florida%20Guide.pdf)]

- **Camp on Remote Islands** (overnight) - Experience unique primitive camping on 14 small islands in Mosquito Lagoon accessible only by boat, canoe, or kayak; reservations required [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/cana/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [The Sunshine Republic](https://thesunshinerepublic.com/2025/06/28/canaveral-national-seashore-rocket-trails-turtle-tracks-and-floridas-last-wild-beach/)]

- **Explore Eldora State House** (30 minutes) - Visit restored historic home along 0.5-mile Eldora Hammock Trail on Eldora Road [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/florida/canaveral-national-seashore)]

## Also Nearby

- **Merritt Island National Wildlife Refuge** (2-3 hours, adjacent) - Drive Black Point Wildlife Drive to see thousands of migratory birds and diverse wildlife in NASA buffer zone; free admission [Source: [The Sunshine Republic](https://thesunshinerepublic.com/2025/06/28/canaveral-national-seashore-rocket-trails-turtle-tracks-and-floridas-last-wild-beach/)]

- **Historic Natchitoches** (1-2 hours, 12 miles) - Explore Louisiana's oldest permanent settlement with 33-block historic district, antique shops, art galleries, and beautifully restored homes [Source: [Cane River NHA](https://www.canerivernha.org/cane-river-creole-national-historical-park)]

- **Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex** (3-4 hours, 20 miles south) - Experience NASA's rocket launches, space shuttle exhibits, and astronaut encounters; 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM daily; admission required [Source: [Visit Florida](https://www.visitflorida.com/travel-ideas/articles/beach-guide-canaveral-national-seashore/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** Half day to full day; quick visit 2-3 hours for beach and Turtle Mound, full day 6-8 hours to explore multiple beaches and trails. [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g28930-d130212-Reviews-Canaveral_National_Seashore-Florida.html)]
