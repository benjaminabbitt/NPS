# Fort Scott NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Post Hospital Visitor Center** (199 Old Fort Blvd, Fort Scott, KS 66701; 37.8436759, -94.7065998; Daily 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM; Phone: 620-223-0310) - The visitor center is located in the restored 1846 Post Hospital building. Passport stamps available during operating hours. Closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day. [Source: [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/fosc/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** Site is free and open daily for self-guided tours. Call 620-223-0310 to confirm hours or arrange guided tours.

## Key Activities

- **Self-Guided Historic Buildings Tour** (1.5-2 hours minimum) - Explore 20 historic structures on the parade ground, eleven of which are original 1840s buildings. Buildings include the infantry barracks, officers' quarters, post hospital, dragoon stables, and guardhouse. Interpretive exhibits in each building tell the story of frontier military life, Bleeding Kansas, and the Civil War. Cell phone audio tour available. 199 Old Fort Blvd, Fort Scott, KS 66701; Daily 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fosc/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/fosc/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Orientation Film** (23 minutes) - Watch the introductory film providing an overview of Fort Scott's complex history spanning from 1842-1873, including its role as a peacekeeping post, its involvement in the Bleeding Kansas period, and service during the Civil War as a Union supply depot and headquarters. Shown in the visitor center. 199 Old Fort Blvd, Fort Scott, KS 66701; Daily during operating hours. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fosc/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Guided Tours** (1-1.5 hours) - Ranger-led tours provide in-depth interpretation of the fort's history and the people who lived and worked here, including soldiers, laundresses, sutlers, surgeons, and their families. Tours available year-round depending on staffing; call ahead at 620-223-0310 to arrange. 199 Old Fort Blvd, Fort Scott, KS 66701; By arrangement. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/fosc/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Tallgrass Prairie Trail** (30-45 minutes) - Walk through five acres of restored tallgrass prairie on this self-guided trail within the park. Interpretive signs explain the prairie ecosystem that once covered much of Kansas. The trail showcases native grasses and wildflowers. Within park grounds; accessible during park hours. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/fosc/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Living History Programs** (1-2 hours, special events) - Experience soldiers drilling on horseback, artillery demonstrations, and period activities during special event weekends. Costumed interpreters bring the fort's history to life. Check park calendar for scheduled events. 199 Old Fort Blvd, Fort Scott, KS 66701; Special event dates. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/fosc/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (30-60 minutes) - Children can earn a Junior Ranger badge by completing activities in the Junior Ranger booklet. Activities teach about the fort's history, military life, and the Kansas-Missouri border conflicts. Available at the visitor center. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/fosc/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Dragoon Stables and Cavalry History** (20-30 minutes) - Explore the dragoon stables where mounted soldiers and their horses were housed. Learn about the elite dragoon units that patrolled the frontier, enforced peace between Native American nations, and protected travelers. Original 1840s structure. Within fort complex; Daily 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM. [Source: [NPS History](https://www.nps.gov/fosc/learn/historyculture/index.htm)]

- **Bleeding Kansas Exhibits** (30-45 minutes) - Discover the fort's role during the violent "Bleeding Kansas" period (1854-1861) when pro-slavery and free-state forces clashed. Fort Scott was caught in the middle of this pre-Civil War conflict that foreshadowed the nation's division. Exhibits in multiple buildings. Within fort complex; Daily 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM. [Source: [NPS History](https://www.nps.gov/fosc/learn/historyculture/index.htm)]

- **Fort Scott National Cemetery** (20-30 minutes, 0.5 miles) - Established in 1862 as one of the original twelve national cemeteries, this active cemetery holds veterans from the Civil War through present conflicts. The cemetery provides important context for the fort's Civil War role. 900 E National Ave, Fort Scott, KS 66701; Daily dawn to dusk. [Source: [VA National Cemeteries](https://www.cem.va.gov/cems/nchp/fortscott.asp)]

- **Cell Phone Audio Tour** (included with self-guided tour) - Access detailed narration of each historic building through your cell phone. The audio tour provides stories and historical context beyond what's shown on exhibit panels, bringing voices from the past to life. Free; dial posted numbers at each stop. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/fosc/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Fort Scott Downtown Historic District** (1-2 hours, adjacent) - Explore the charming Victorian-era downtown with over 130 historic buildings, antique shops, restaurants, and the beautiful Lowell Milken Center for Unsung Heroes. The town grew up around the fort and retains much of its 19th-century character. Downtown Fort Scott, KS; Business hours vary. [Source: [Visit Fort Scott](https://www.visitfortscott.com/)]

- **Gordon Parks Museum at Fort Scott Community College** (1 hour, 2 miles) - Celebrates the life and work of Gordon Parks, the renowned photographer, filmmaker, and writer who grew up in Fort Scott. Features exhibitions of his photography and memorabilia. 2108 S Horton St, Fort Scott, KS 66701; Monday-Friday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; Free admission. [Source: [Gordon Parks Museum](https://gordonparkscenter.org/)]

- **Mine Creek Battlefield State Historic Site** (1-1.5 hours, 25 miles south) - Site of the second-largest Civil War cavalry battle, fought on October 25, 1864. The museum tells the story of the Union victory that helped secure Kansas for the Union. 20485 KS-52, Pleasanton, KS 66075; Wednesday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; Admission charged. [Source: [Kansas Historical Society](https://www.kshs.org/p/mine-creek-battlefield-state-historic-site/11671)]

- **Little House on the Prairie Museum** (1-1.5 hours, 30 miles south) - A replica of the cabin where Laura Ingalls Wilder and her family lived in 1869-1870, inspiring the "Little House on the Prairie" book. The site includes a one-room schoolhouse and post office. 2507 CR 3000, Independence, KS 67301; April-October, hours vary; Small admission. [Source: [Little House on the Prairie](https://littlehouseontheprairie.com/)]

## Total Recommended Time

**Total recommended visit time for Fort Scott NHS: 2-4 hours**

A minimum of 1.5 hours is needed to view the 23-minute orientation film and walk through the buildings. For a comprehensive visit including reading all exhibits and participating in summer interpretive activities, plan 3-4 hours. The site is free to enter and open daily except major holidays. Guided tours are available by arrangement.

[Sources: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/fosc/planyourvisit/index.htm), [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/fosc/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fosc/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]
