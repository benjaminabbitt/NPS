# Fort Davis NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Fort Davis National Historic Site Visitor Center** (101 Lt. Flipper Dr., Fort Davis, TX 79734; Daily 8:00 AM-5:00 PM, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 432-426-3224; $10 per person or $20 per vehicle) [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/foda/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/foda/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** Passport cancellation stamps available at WNPA Partner Park Store in visitor center. Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [[Source](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/fort-davis-national-historic-site)]

## Key Activities

- **Self-Guided Historic Fort Tour** (1.5-2 hours) - Walk among 24 restored historic buildings and over 100 ruins and foundations at one of the best surviving examples of an Indian Wars frontier military post in the Southwest; explore officers' quarters, enlisted men's barracks, and the hospital from the fort's operational period (1854-1891) protecting emigrants and mail coaches; Fort Davis NHS grounds; Daily 8:00 AM-5:00 PM; $10 per person or $20 per vehicle [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/foda/index.htm), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60807-d143128-Reviews-Fort_Davis_National_Historic_Site-Fort_Davis_Texas.html)]

- **Visitor Center Museum and Film** (30-45 minutes) - Watch introductory video about the fort's history and explore museum exhibits featuring artifacts, photographs, and interpretive displays about frontier military life and the Buffalo Soldiers who served here; Visitor center building; Daily 8:00 AM-5:00 PM; Included with admission [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/foda/), [Source](https://home.nps.gov/foda/daily-activities.htm)]

- **Back Country and Front Country Hiking** (1-2 hours) - Explore 4 miles of hiking trails through the historic fort grounds and surrounding Davis Mountains landscape with opportunities for wildlife viewing and photography; Trails throughout fort grounds; Dawn to dusk; Included with admission [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/foda/planyourvisit/hiking-fort-davis-national-historic-site.htm), [Source](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/fort-davis-national-historic-site)]

- **Living History Demonstrations** (30-60 minutes, seasonal) - Experience frontier military life through costumed interpreters demonstrating drill formations, military tactics, and daily routines of soldiers and officers; common during summer months; Fort grounds; Summer schedule varies, check at visitor center; Included with admission [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60807-d143128-Reviews-Fort_Davis_National_Historic_Site-Fort_Davis_Texas.html)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (1-2 hours) - Complete activity booklet exploring fort history, military life, and the Buffalo Soldiers to earn Junior Ranger badge; pick up booklet at visitor center; During operating hours; Free with admission [[Source](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/fort-davis-national-historic-site)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Officers' Quarters Tours** (30-45 minutes) - Step inside restored officers' quarters furnished with period furniture and artifacts showing how military families lived on the frontier; interpretive exhibits explain social hierarchy and domestic life at the fort; Officers' Row area; Daily during operating hours; Included with admission [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/foda/)]

- **Hospital Building** (15-30 minutes) - Visit the restored post hospital featuring medical instruments and exhibits about frontier medicine and the challenges of providing healthcare in remote military posts; Hospital building; Daily during operating hours; Included with admission [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60807-d143128-Reviews-Fort_Davis_National_Historic_Site-Fort_Davis_Texas.html)]

- **Sunrise/Sunset Photography** (1 hour) - Capture stunning photos of historic buildings bathed in golden light with the Davis Mountains as backdrop; the fort's high desert location (5,000 feet elevation) provides exceptional lighting conditions; Fort grounds; Dawn to dusk [[Source](https://visitalpinetx.com/places/fort-davis-national-historic-site/)]

## Also Nearby

- **McDonald Observatory** (2-3 hours, 16 miles northwest) - Tour one of the world's premier astronomical research facilities and attend star parties with telescope viewing under some of the darkest skies in North America; 3640 Dark Sky Drive, Fort Davis, TX 79734; Visitor center daily 10:00 AM-5:30 PM, star parties Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday evenings; Tours $8-12, star parties $18 [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g60807-Activities-Fort_Davis_Texas.html)]

- **Davis Mountains State Park** (1-3 hours, 4 miles northwest) - Hike trails through Chihuahuan Desert landscape including the 1.75-mile CCC Trail connecting to Fort Davis NHS, camp, or stay at historic Indian Lodge; park also offers wildlife viewing and scenic drives; Park Road 3, Fort Davis, TX 79734; Daily 6:00 AM-10:00 PM; $5 day use [[Source](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/davis-mountains), [Source](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/texas/davis-mountains-state-park)]

- **Chihuahuan Desert Nature Center** (1-2 hours, 5 miles south) - Walk botanical gardens and trails showcasing native desert plants, hummingbird garden, and exhibits about the Chihuahuan Desert ecosystem; 4 miles south on TX 118, Fort Davis, TX 79734; Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; $5 adults [[Source](https://yonderlustramblings.com/the-best-outdoor-things-to-do-in-fort-davis-texas/)]

- **Balmorhea State Park** (1-2 hours, 35 miles east) - Swim in the world's largest spring-fed swimming pool (1.75 acres) fed by San Solomon Springs at a constant 72-76°F; excellent for swimming, snorkeling, and scuba diving; 9207 TX-17, Toyahvale, TX 79786; Daily 8:00 AM-sunset; $7 day use [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g60807-Activities-Fort_Davis_Texas.html)]
