# Arkansas Post N MEM (National Memorial)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Arkansas Post National Memorial Visitor Center** (1741 Old Post Road, Gillett, AR 72055 (34.0166096, -91.3451369); 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 870-548-2207) [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/arpo/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/arpo/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** Call 870-548-2207 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. Stamps are available at the visitor center. Arkansas Post N MEM is part of the 1993 Passport Stamp Set.

## Key Activities

- **Visit the Visitor Center and Watch the Film** (30-45 minutes) - Explore museum exhibits reflecting over 300 years of human history at Arkansas Post, including artifacts from the French colonial period, Spanish rule, Revolutionary War, territorial capital era, and Civil War battle, then watch the 20-minute feature film "Arkansas Post: Echoes of the Past" providing insights into the storied history of the Post; 1741 Old Post Road, Gillett, AR 72055 (34.0166096, -91.3451369); museum and theater open 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily (film available on request 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM), closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 870-548-2207 [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/arpo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60725-d324793-Reviews-Arkansas_Post_National_Memorial-Gillett_Arkansas.html), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/arkansas-post-national-memorial/)]

- **Hike the Arkansas Post Trail System** (40-60 minutes) - Walk the 2.3-mile loop trail system through hardwood forest and historic townsite featuring paved trails (majority) and grass trails, with interpretive signs along the path telling the story of Arkansas Post as the first European settlement and original capital of Arkansas, including its French and Spanish colonial history, Revolutionary War connection, and Civil War significance; trailhead at Visitor Center, 1741 Old Post Road, Gillett, AR 72055 (34.0166096, -91.3451369); open year-round during daylight hours; free admission; 870-548-2207 [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/arkansas/arkansas-post-national-memorial-trail-system), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/arkansas-post-national-memorial/), [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/arpo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Explore the Civil War Earthworks and Confederate Fort Site** (30-45 minutes) - View the remnants of Confederate trenches extending approximately 720 yards westward from Fort Hindman toward a bayou, with depressions indicating two probable artillery positions, and learn about the January 9-11, 1863 Battle of Arkansas Post where nearly 25,000 Union soldiers overwhelmed almost 6,000 Confederate troops defending the river approach to Little Rock; visible earthworks located in northwestern section of site, accessed via walking trails from Visitor Center, 1741 Old Post Road, Gillett, AR 72055 (34.0166096, -91.3451369); open during daylight hours; free admission; 870-548-2207 [Source: [American Battlefield Trust](https://www.battlefields.org/visit/heritage-sites/arkansas-post-national-memorial), [World Atlas](https://www.worldatlas.com/historic-sites/4-must-see-historic-forts-in-arkansas.html), [Battlefields.org](https://www.battlefields.org/learn/civil-war/battles/arkansas-post-1863)]

- **Walk to the Underwater Fort Hindman Site** (15-20 minutes) - Visit the first site along the driving route where an oxbow lake has formed over the submerged remains of Fort Hindman, the Confederate defensive position built in 1862 on a bluff 25 feet above the Arkansas River, now underwater due to the McClellan-Kerr Arkansas River Navigation System's pool following damming in the 1960s; site marked with interpretive signage near parking area, accessed from entrance road off Highway 169, 1741 Old Post Road, Gillett, AR 72055 (34.0166096, -91.3451369); open during daylight hours; free admission; 870-548-2207 [Source: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60725-d324793-Reviews-Arkansas_Post_National_Memorial-Gillett_Arkansas.html), [American Battlefield Trust](https://www.battlefields.org/visit/heritage-sites/arkansas-post-national-memorial)]

- **Fishing on the Arkansas River and Backwaters** (1-4 hours) - Fish the lakes, bayous, and backwaters at this peninsula site bordered by the Arkansas River and two backwaters, with fishing areas located along the oxbow lake where Fort Hindman once stood; multiple fishing access points throughout the memorial, 1741 Old Post Road, Gillett, AR 72055 (34.0166096, -91.3451369); open during daylight hours; free admission, Arkansas fishing license required; 870-548-2207 [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/arpo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60725-d324793-Reviews-Arkansas_Post_National_Memorial-Gillett_Arkansas.html)]

- **Picnicking at the Memorial** (30-90 minutes) - Enjoy picnic areas with great views of the river and lake, featuring tables in scenic natural settings near hiking trails; picnic area accessed via separate road from main entrance, 1741 Old Post Road, Gillett, AR 72055 (34.0166096, -91.3451369); open during daylight hours; free admission; 870-548-2207 [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/arkansas-post-national-memorial/), [State Parks](https://stateparks.com/arkansas_post_national_memorial_in_arkansas.html)]

- **Birdwatching and Wildlife Viewing** (30 minutes - 2 hours) - Observe diverse bird species including the Black-bellied Whistling-Duck and other waterfowl, waders, and migratory birds along the Mississippi Flyway, plus wildlife such as deer, in the varied habitats of this peninsula bordered by river and backwaters; best viewing during spring migration (mid-March to early May peak in first week of May) and fall migration (late September to early October), with winter bringing waterfowl concentrations; wildlife viewing opportunities throughout memorial grounds, 1741 Old Post Road, Gillett, AR 72055 (34.0166096, -91.3451369); open during daylight hours; free admission; 870-548-2207 [Source: [Audubon Delta](https://delta.audubon.org/news/arkansas-birding-destinations), [Arkansas.com Birding Tips](https://www.arkansas.com/articles/birding-watching-tips), [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/arpo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Historic Weapons Demonstration** (30-45 minutes) - Attend special programs featuring demonstrations of historic weapons used during Arkansas Post's 300-year history, offered on select dates throughout the year; check with Visitor Center for current schedule, 1741 Old Post Road, Gillett, AR 72055 (34.0166096, -91.3451369); times vary seasonally, call ahead for schedule; free admission; 870-548-2207 [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/arpo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Complete the Junior Ranger Program** (1.5-2 hours) - Pick up a free Junior Ranger Workbook at the Visitor Center for children ages 7-12, then tour the visitor center, watch the park film, hike the nature trail, take a self-guided tour of the historic townsite, and complete activities in the workbook to earn an official Junior Ranger badge from a park ranger; available at Visitor Center, 1741 Old Post Road, Gillett, AR 72055 (34.0166096, -91.3451369); 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily; free; 870-548-2207 [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/arkansas-post-national-memorial/), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60725-d324793-Reviews-Arkansas_Post_National_Memorial-Gillett_Arkansas.html)]

- **Explore the French Colonial Trading Post History** (included in visitor center time) - Learn about Henri de Tonti's 1686 establishment of "Poste de Arkansea" on behalf of Louis XIV of France, the first semi-permanent French settlement in the lower Mississippi River Valley built as a trading post with the Quapaw Nation at their village of Osotouy, including the 1783 "Colbert Raid" - the only Revolutionary War action in Arkansas where Spanish soldiers and British partisans clashed; exhibits at Visitor Center, 1741 Old Post Road, Gillett, AR 72055 (34.0166096, -91.3451369); 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily; free admission; 870-548-2207 [Source: [Wikipedia Arkansas Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Post), [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/Arkansas-Post), [Encyclopedia of Arkansas](https://encyclopediaofarkansas.net/entries/arkansas-post-3/)]

- **Trail to Confederate Rifle Positions** (20-30 minutes) - Hike a short trail from the picnic area to barely visible Confederate rifle positions from the 1863 Civil War battle, marked with good interpretive signage explaining the defensive positions used during the Union assault on Fort Hindman; accessed via separate road to picnic area, 1741 Old Post Road, Gillett, AR 72055 (34.0166096, -91.3451369); open during daylight hours; free admission; 870-548-2207 [Source: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60725-d324793-Reviews-Arkansas_Post_National_Memorial-Gillett_Arkansas.html)]

- **Ranger-Led Personal History Lessons** (varies, 15-45 minutes) - Talk with rangers on duty during quiet times for personal history lessons and deeper insights into Arkansas Post's complex 300-year history spanning multiple cultures including the Quapaw, French, Spanish, American territorial period, and Civil War; available when staff is available at Visitor Center, 1741 Old Post Road, Gillett, AR 72055 (34.0166096, -91.3451369); 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily; free; 870-548-2207 [Source: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60725-d324793-Reviews-Arkansas_Post_National_Memorial-Gillett_Arkansas.html)]

## Also Nearby

- **Arkansas Post Museum State Park** (45-60 minutes) - Explore life on Arkansas' Grand Prairie and in the Arkansas Delta from 1877 to today through a complex of five exhibit buildings including the 1877 Refeld-Hinman Loghouse (dogtrot cabin furnished in 1800s style) and the 1933 Carnes-Bonner Playhouse (miniature built-to-scale family home), complementing the National Memorial's focus on earlier historical periods; 5530 Highway 165 South, Gillett, AR 72055 (2 miles from Arkansas Post National Memorial at intersection of US 165 and Highway 169); Wednesday-Saturday 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM, closed Monday-Tuesday; free admission [Source: [Arkansas State Parks](https://www.arkansasstateparks.com/parks/arkansas-post-museum), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60725-d299050-Reviews-Arkansas_Post_Museum-Gillett_Arkansas.html), [Arkansas State Parks Article](https://www.arkansasstateparks.com/articles/arkansas-post-museum-arkansas-51st-state-park)]

- **Museum of the Arkansas Grand Prairie** (1-1.5 hours) - Discover the history of agriculture and rice production on the Arkansas Grand Prairie from the 1800s to 1921 through over 10,000 artifacts in 20,000 square feet of exhibits plus five furnished exterior exhibits depicting pioneer life and farming heritage; 921 E. 4th Street, Stuttgart, AR 72160 (34.4993351, -91.541187) (approximately 30 miles/35 minutes north of Gillett); Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM, closed Sunday-Monday and major holidays; admission: adults $5, seniors $4, children $3 [Source: [Museum Website](https://www.grandprairiemuseum.org/), [Arkansas.com](https://www.arkansas.com/stuttgart/landmarks/museum-arkansas-grand-prairie), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g31942-d299857-Reviews-Museum_of_the_Arkansas_Grand_Prairie-Stuttgart_Arkansas.html)]

- **Delta Rivers Nature Center** (1-2 hours) - Experience Arkansas's first nature center featuring a 22,000-gallon aquarium displaying native fish, live Birds of Prey including eagles, owls and hawks, American Alligators and native turtles, plus 130 acres of bottomland forest with over two miles of trails and a 3D archery range with 20 targets; 1400 Black Dog Road, Pine Bluff, AR 71611 (approximately 40 miles/45 minutes northwest of Gillett); Tuesday-Saturday 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM, closed Mondays and major holidays; free admission [Source: [Arkansas Game & Fish](https://www.agfc.com/things-to-do/nature-centers/pine-bluff/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g31858-d299275-Reviews-Delta_Rivers_Nature_Center-Pine_Bluff_Arkansas.html), [Encyclopedia of Arkansas](https://encyclopediaofarkansas.net/entries/governor-mike-huckabee-delta-rivers-nature-center-3369/)]

- **Arkansas Railroad Museum** (1-1.5 hours) - Explore the history of railroading in Arkansas with special emphasis on the St. Louis Southwestern Railway (Cotton Belt Railroad (31.5597455, -97.1241903)), featuring the only remaining 1920s steam locomotive Engine 819 on display plus extensive railroad exhibits and rolling stock; 1700 Port Road, Pine Bluff, AR 71601 (34.227641, -91.9850581) (approximately 40 miles/45 minutes northwest of Gillett); Saturday 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM, or by appointment; admission: adults $5, children 6-12 $3, under 6 free [Source: [City of Pine Bluff](https://www.cityofpinebluff-ar.gov/attractions), [TripAdvisor Pine Bluff](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g31858-Activities-Pine_Bluff_Arkansas.html)]

- **Rohwer Japanese American Relocation Center Site** (30-45 minutes) - Visit this National Historic Landmark commemorating one of ten World War II camps where Japanese Americans were forcibly relocated, featuring the Rohwer Memorial Cemetery with monument and preserved structures, offering sobering insights into this chapter of American history; County Road 4 & Highway 1, Rohwer, AR 71666 (approximately 45 miles/50 minutes southeast of Gillett via Dumas); open during daylight hours; free admission [Source: [Wango Kart](https://wangokart.com/blogs/usa-travel/things-to-do-in-pine-bluff-arkansas-ar), [Arkansas.com Delta](https://www.arkansas.com/attractions-arkansas-delta-1)]

**Total Recommended Time at Arkansas Post N MEM (National Memorial):** 3-4 hours minimum for a complete visit including Visitor Center, film, museum exhibits, trail system, and Civil War earthworks; can easily extend to a full day with fishing, extended wildlife viewing, and picnicking.
