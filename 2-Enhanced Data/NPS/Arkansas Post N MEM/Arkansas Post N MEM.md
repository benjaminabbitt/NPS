# Arkansas Post N MEM (National Memorial)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Arkansas Post National Memorial Visitor Center** - 1741 Old Post Rd, Gillett, AR 72055 (34.0166096, -91.3451369); Daily 8:00 AM–5:00 PM (closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day); 870-548-2207. [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/arpo/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Contact](https://www.nps.gov/arpo/contacts.htm)]

**Note:** Call 870-548-2207 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [NPS Arkansas Post](https://www.nps.gov/arpo)]

## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center Museum and Film** (45 minutes to 1 hour) - Explore exhibits reflecting more than 300 years of human history and watch the 20-minute feature film "Arkansas Post: Echoes of the Past" that provides insights into the storied history of the first European settlement in the lower Mississippi Valley; 1741 Old Post Rd, Gillett, AR 72055 (34.0166096, -91.3451369); Daily 8:00 AM–5:00 PM; 870-548-2207. [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/arkansas-post-national-memorial/), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/arpo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/arpo/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- **Arkansas Post Trail System** (40 minutes to 1 hour) - Walk the 2.3-mile loop trail system featuring 2.5 miles of mostly paved trails through the historic townsite and hardwood forest, wheelchair and stroller accessible, with interpretive signage throughout; 1741 Old Post Rd, Gillett, AR 72055 (34.0166096, -91.3451369); Park grounds open during daylight hours; 870-548-2207. [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/arkansas/arkansas-post-national-memorial-trail-system), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/arkansas-post-national-memorial/), [Encyclopedia of Arkansas](https://encyclopediaofarkansas.net/entries/arkansas-post-national-memorial-3804/)]

- **Confederate Trenches and Artillery Positions** (30 minutes) - Explore remnants of the Civil War Battle of Arkansas Post with still-visible Confederate road, trenches, and artillery positions, plus a short hike to rifle positions with interpretive signage; located along trail system at Arkansas Post National Memorial; Park grounds open during daylight hours. [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/arkansas-post-national-memorial/), [Explore Southern History](https://exploresouthernhistory.com/arkansaspost.html)]

- **Historic Weapons Demonstrations** (30 minutes to 1 hour) - Attend regularly scheduled musket and cannon demonstrations showcasing historic weapons from the site's military history; Arkansas Post National Memorial; Check park calendar for current schedule, 870-548-2207. [Source: [NPS Articles](https://www.nps.gov/articles/arkansaspost.htm), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/arkansas-post-national-memorial/), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/arpo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Fishing** (1 to 4 hours) - Fish in the Arkansas River, Post Bayou, and backwaters for catfish, bass, and other species, with excellent fishing opportunities year-round on the peninsula bordered by the Arkansas River and two backwaters; Arkansas Post National Memorial; Park grounds open during daylight hours; Arkansas fishing license required for ages 16 and older. [Source: [National Park Foundation](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/arkansas-post-national-memorial), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/arpo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Arkansas Game & Fish](https://www.agfc.com/things-to-do/water-trails/arkansas-post/)]

- **Wildlife Watching and Birding** (1 to 2 hours) - Observe diverse wildlife including white pelicans, great egrets, bald eagles, alligators (some reaching fourteen feet), beavers, nutria, muskrats, deer, and raccoons in the unique habitats featuring terrace landscapes, hardwood forests, bayous, and swamps; Arkansas Post National Memorial; Park grounds open during daylight hours. [Source: [National Park Foundation](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/arkansas-post-national-memorial), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/arkansas-post-national-memorial/), [Explore Southern History](https://exploresouthernhistory.com/arkansaspost.html)]

- **Picnicking** (1 to 2 hours) - Enjoy the picnic area with barbecues, picnic tables, and covered shelter, located near the historic rifle positions with access to hiking trails; Arkansas Post National Memorial; Park grounds open during daylight hours. [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/arkansas-post-national-memorial/), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/arpo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (1 to 2 hours) - Complete the Junior Ranger program by watching the park movie, touring the visitor center, taking a self-guided tour of the historic townsite or walking the nature trail, and completing 5 activities in the pamphlet; Arkansas Post National Memorial Visitor Center, 1741 Old Post Rd, Gillett, AR 72055 (34.0166096, -91.3451369); Daily 8:00 AM–5:00 PM. [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/arkansas-post-national-memorial/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Ranger-Led Canoe Tours** (2 to 3 hours) - Explore 4.5 miles of canoe trail with rangers on weekends during summer months, paddling through backwaters and experiencing the memorial from a unique aquatic perspective; Arkansas Post National Memorial; Weekend summer schedule, contact 870-548-2207 for current dates and reservations. [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/arkansas-post-national-memorial/), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/arpo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Replica Frontier Fort** (15 to 30 minutes) - Visit the replica section of the frontier fort near the museum, providing a tangible connection to the early 1800s military presence at Arkansas Post; located near Arkansas Post Visitor Center; Park grounds open during daylight hours. [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/arkansas-post-national-memorial/), [Explore Southern History](https://exploresouthernhistory.com/arkansaspost.html)]

- **Historic Well and Cistern** (10 to 15 minutes) - View the intact well and cistern built at the post in the early 1800s, providing a glimpse into daily life at the historic settlement; located along trails at Arkansas Post National Memorial; Park grounds open during daylight hours. [Source: [Explore Southern History](https://exploresouthernhistory.com/arkansaspost.html)]

- **American Lotus and Wetland Habitats** (30 minutes to 1 hour) - Discover unique aquatic plants like the American lotus thriving in the oxbow lake formed where an underwater fort once stood, with diverse wetland ecosystems including bayous and swamps throughout the memorial; Arkansas Post National Memorial; Best viewing during growing season (late spring through summer). [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/arkansas-post-national-memorial/)]

- **Underwater and Submerged Historic Resources** (Viewing from shore, 15 to 30 minutes) - Learn about the half of Arkansas Post that has disappeared since 1912 due to erosion and changes in the Arkansas River's course, leaving historic resources underground and underwater; interpretive information available along trails; Park grounds open during daylight hours. [Source: [Explore Southern History](https://exploresouthernhistory.com/arkansaspost.html)]

## Also Nearby

- **Arkansas Post Museum State Park** (1 to 2 hours, 2 miles away) - Explore five exhibit buildings showcasing life on Arkansas' Grand Prairie and in the Arkansas Delta from 1877 to today, including the 1877 Refeld-Hinman Loghouse (dogtrot cabin furnished in 1800s style) and the 1933 Carnes-Bonner Playhouse; 5530 Hwy 165 South, Gillett, AR 72055 (34.031704, -91.373288); Wednesday–Saturday 8:00 AM–5:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM–5:00 PM, closed Monday-Tuesday (open Memorial Day, Labor Day, Fourth of July); 870-548-2634; Free admission. [Source: [Arkansas State Parks](https://www.arkansasstateparks.com/parks/arkansas-post-museum), [NPS Nearby Attractions](https://www.nps.gov/arpo/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm)]

- **Museum of the Arkansas Grand Prairie** (1 to 2 hours, 25 miles to Stuttgart) - Discover extensive exhibits on agriculture, prairie life, duck hunting culture, and the Arkansas Waterfowler Hall of Fame in eastern Arkansas; 921 East 4th Street, Stuttgart, AR 72160 (34.5004, -91.5526); Wednesday–Friday 8:00 AM–4:00 PM, Saturday 10:00 AM–4:00 PM, closed Sunday-Tuesday; 870-673-7001. [Source: [Grand Prairie Museum](https://www.grandprairiemuseum.org/), [Arkansas.com Stuttgart](https://www.arkansas.com/stuttgart/landmarks/museum-arkansas-grand-prairie)]

- **Lake Chicot State Park** (3 to 6 hours, approximately 60 miles to Lake Village) - Visit the largest natural lake in Arkansas and the largest oxbow lake in North America, featuring fishing for crappie, bass, bream, and catfish, plus boating, kayak rentals, hiking trails, swimming pool, cabins, and campsites; located 8 miles northeast of Lake Village on Hwy 144; Visitor Center daily 8:00 AM–5:00 PM (closed New Year's Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day); 870-265-5480. [Source: [Arkansas State Parks](https://www.arkansasstateparks.com/parks/lake-chicot-state-park), [Arkansas.com Lake Chicot](https://www.arkansas.com/lake-village/accommodations/lake-chicot-state-park)]

- **Dale Bumpers White River National Wildlife Refuge** (2 to 4 hours, approximately 50 miles to St. Charles) - Explore 160,756 acres comprising the nation's largest contiguous block of bottomland hardwood forest under single ownership, with wildlife viewing, fishing, and seasonal road/trail access; Visitor Center at 57 South CC Camp Road, St. Charles, AR 72140; Call 870-282-8200 ext 1 for current hours and seasonal access; Free admission. [Source: [U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service](https://www.fws.gov/refuge/dale-bumpers-white-river), [Arkansas.com White River](https://www.arkansas.com/st-charles/outdoor-recreation/dale-bumpers-white-river-national-wildlife-refuge), [Encyclopedia of Arkansas](https://encyclopediaofarkansas.net/entries/dale-bumpers-white-river-national-wildlife-refuge-2469/)]

**Total Recommended Time:** 3 to 4 hours for a comprehensive visit including the visitor center, film, museum exhibits, trail system, and exploration of Civil War sites. Visitors can easily spend a full day if combining multiple activities such as fishing, extended wildlife watching, picnicking, or participating in ranger-led programs.
