# US Navy MEM (Memorial)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a name="stamp-fords"></a>**Ford's Theatre Box Office** (511 10th St NW, Washington, DC 20004 (38.896678, -77.025818); Daily 8:30 AM-5:00 PM; 202-347-4833) - The NPS passport cancellation stamp for US Navy Memorial is available here, along with stamps for Ford's Theatre NHS and Petersen House. [Source: [NPS National Mall Passport Stamps](https://www.nps.gov/nama/learn/https-www-nps-gov-nama-learn-where-can-you-get-passport-stamps-htm.htm)]

**Note:** Call ahead to confirm stamp availability before visiting.

## Key Activities

- <a name="activity-memorial-plaza"></a>**Memorial Plaza and Granite Sea** (15-30 minutes) - Explore the outdoor memorial plaza featuring the world's largest granite map ("Granite Sea"), the iconic Lone Sailor statue, 26 bronze bas-relief sculptures depicting naval history, fountain pools, and commemorative inscriptions from naval leaders including John Paul Jones and Admiral Farragut; 701 Pennsylvania Ave NW, Washington, DC 20004 (38.893682, -77.021915); Open 24 hours. [Sources: [US Navy Memorial](https://www.navymemorial.org/memorial-plaza), [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/000/us-navy-memorial.htm), [Thrillophilia](https://www.thrillophilia.com/attractions/us-navy-memorial-plaza)]

- <a name="activity-heritage-center"></a>**Naval Heritage Center Exhibits** (45-60 minutes) - Visit the underground museum featuring interactive exhibits including "The American Sailor" (evolution of Navy from colonial times to present), "Maritime Industrial Base and Submarine Force" (with USS District of Columbia submarine model), "America's Navy" (current missions and deployments via interactive touchscreens), "Navy After 9/11" (GWOT operations with multimedia presentations), and "Service and Sacrifice" statue honoring military dogs; 701 Pennsylvania Ave NW, Washington, DC 20004 (38.893682, -77.021915); Daily 9:30 AM-5:00 PM; 202-737-2300. [Sources: [Navy Memorial Visitor Center](https://www.navymemorial.org/visitors-center), [Navy Memorial Exhibits](https://www.navymemorial.org/exhibits), [Washington.org](https://washington.org/find-dc-listings/us-navy-memorial)]

- <a name="activity-burke-theater"></a>**Arleigh & Burke Theatre Film** (20-30 minutes) - Watch "A Day In the Life of the Blue Angels" Discovery Channel documentary in the 222-seat theater with 30-foot LED screen; 701 Pennsylvania Ave NW, Washington, DC 20004 (38.893682, -77.021915); Daily screenings during visitor center hours 9:30 AM-5:00 PM; Free admission. [Sources: [Navy Memorial Visitor Center](https://www.navymemorial.org/visitors-center), [LocationsHub](https://rs.locationshub.com/Home/LocationDetail?rsLocationId=063-10088063)]

- <a name="activity-navy-log"></a>**Navy Log Room** (15-30 minutes) - Search the nation's largest publicly available database of Sea Service personnel (751,832+ entries from US Navy, Marine Corps, Coast Guard, and Merchant Marines) using touch-screen kiosks, or enroll yourself or loved ones in the registry; 701 Pennsylvania Ave NW, Washington, DC 20004 (38.893682, -77.021915); Daily 9:30 AM-5:00 PM; Free admission. [Sources: [Navy Log Search](https://navylog.navymemorial.org/search-navy-log), [Navy Memorial Enroll](https://www.navymemorial.org/navy-log), [Washington.org](https://washington.org/find-dc-listings/us-navy-memorial)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a name="gem-chiefs-mess"></a>**Delbert D. Black National Chief's Mess Exhibit** (10-15 minutes) - Interactive exhibit dedicated to Navy Chief Petty Officers, featuring the story and role of CPOs in the service, including "Sailors of the Year" displays honoring newly promoted Chief Petty Officers; Located within Naval Heritage Center at 701 Pennsylvania Ave NW, Washington, DC 20004 (38.893682, -77.021915); Daily 9:30 AM-5:00 PM. [Sources: [Navy Memorial Exhibits](https://www.navymemorial.org/exhibits), [Navy Memorial Visitor Center](https://www.navymemorial.org/visitors-center)]

- <a name="gem-ships-store"></a>**Ship's Store** (10-20 minutes) - Browse military-themed merchandise for all branches including branded apparel, mugs, challenge coins, and naval memorabilia on the Gallery Deck; Located within Naval Heritage Center at 701 Pennsylvania Ave NW, Washington, DC 20004 (38.893682, -77.021915); Daily 9:30 AM-5:00 PM. [Source: [Navy Memorial Visitor Center](https://www.navymemorial.org/visitors-center)]

## Also Nearby

- <a name="nearby-archives"></a>**National Archives Museum** (1.5-2 hours, 2 minute walk) - View the original Declaration of Independence, Constitution, and Bill of Rights in the Rotunda for the Charters of Freedom, plus rotating historical exhibits; 701 Constitution Ave NW, Washington, DC 20408 (38.892494, -77.022309); Daily 10:00 AM-5:30 PM (closed Thanksgiving and Christmas); Free admission (no reservations required). Located directly across Pennsylvania Avenue from the Navy Memorial, sharing the Archives-Navy Memorial Metro station. [Sources: [National Archives Visit](https://visit.archives.gov/visit), [Washington.org Archives Guide](https://washington.org/visit-dc/guide-to-national-archives-museum-washington-dc)]

- <a name="nearby-fords"></a>**Ford's Theatre National Historic Site** (1-1.5 hours, 5 minute walk) - Tour the historic theater where President Lincoln was assassinated, view the Presidential Box, visit the Lincoln Museum in the basement, and see Petersen House across the street where Lincoln died; 511 10th St NW, Washington, DC 20004 (38.896678, -77.025818); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; Free timed-entry tickets (available same-day or reserved online). [Sources: [NPS Ford's Theatre](https://www.nps.gov/foth/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Ford's Theatre Hours](https://www.nps.gov/foth/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- <a name="nearby-portrait-gallery"></a>**Smithsonian National Portrait Gallery and American Art Museum** (2-3 hours, 6 minute walk) - Explore American art and portraiture in adjoining museums sharing the historic Patent Office Building, featuring presidential portraits, contemporary art, and the Luce Foundation Center's visible storage collection; 8th and G Streets NW, Washington, DC 20001 (38.897664, -77.022963); Daily 11:30 AM-7:00 PM; Free admission. [Sources: [Smithsonian Museums](https://www.si.edu/visit/museums), [Moovit Transit Guide](https://moovitapp.com/index/en/public_transit-Smithsonian_National_Museum_of_Natural_History-Washington_DCBaltimore-site_18947500-142)]

- <a name="nearby-natural-history"></a>**Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History** (2-3 hours, 6 minute walk) - Visit one of the world's most-visited natural history museums featuring the Hope Diamond, dinosaur fossils, ocean life exhibits, and the Sant Ocean Hall; 10th Street and Constitution Ave NW, Washington, DC 20560 (38.891266, -77.026065); Daily 10:00 AM-5:30 PM; Free admission. [Sources: [Smithsonian Museums](https://www.si.edu/visit/museums), [Moovit Transit Guide](https://moovitapp.com/index/en/public_transit-Smithsonian_National_Museum_of_Natural_History-Washington_DCBaltimore-site_18947500-142)]
