# The Lockkeeper's House

## <a name="cancellation-stamps"></a>Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a name="stamp-washington-monument-lodge"></a>**Washington Monument Lodge** (2 15th Street NW, Washington, DC 20024 (38.889475, -77.035243); Sun-Fri: 8:45 AM-6:00 PM; Sat: 10:00 AM-6:00 PM; 202-426-6841) - The Washington Monument Lodge opens at 8:45 a.m. for distribution of free, same-day, timed tickets on a first-come, first-served basis. Visitors can obtain NPS passport stamps here, use public restrooms, and visit America's National Parks store. The ticket window is located at the back of the building facing the Washington Monument. The lodge offers stamps for multiple National Mall sites including The Lockkeeper's House. [Source: [NPS - Passport Stamps](https://www.nps.gov/nama/learn/https-www-nps-gov-nama-learn-where-can-you-get-passport-stamps-htm.htm), [NPS - Washington Monument](https://www.nps.gov/wamo/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Free Tours by Foot](https://freetoursbyfoot.com/washington-monument-tickets/)]

**Note:** Call ahead to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. The Lockkeeper's House itself is a small historic structure that may not have stamps on-site; stamps for this memorial are typically available at the Washington Monument Lodge.

## <a name="key-activities"></a>Key Activities

- <a name="activity-lockkeepers-house-tour"></a>**Lockkeeper's House Tour** (15-20 minutes) - Experience the oldest structure on the National Mall through an innovative 6-minute immersive video program, interactive kiosks, and a guided tour of this compact 540-square-foot fieldstone house built in 1837. The exhibits explore civics, commerce, development, and the National Mall's ecological history, showcasing the role of the C&O Canal and Washington City Canal in the development of Washington, D.C. The house originally served at the junction where the lockkeeper managed traffic and collected tolls when the two canals intersected. Located in a landscaped stone plaza with seating areas and a 25-foot willow oak tree; Constitution Avenue NW & 17th Street NW, Washington, DC 20006 (38.8894754, -77.0352426); Thu-Sun: 11:00 AM-3:00 PM. [Source: [Trust for the National Mall](https://nationalmall.org/content/lockkeepers-house), [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/000/lockkeepers-house.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g28970-d10375543-Reviews-Lockkeeper_s_House-Washington_DC_District_of_Columbia.html), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2018/07/05/625669691/181-year-old-lockkeepers-tiny-house-ready-for-its-next-chapter)]

- <a name="activity-washington-city-canal-history"></a>**Washington City Canal History** (5-10 minutes) - Learn about the Washington City Canal that operated from 1815 until the mid-1850s, connecting the Anacostia River to Tiber Creek, the Potomac River, and later the Chesapeake and Ohio Canal. View exhibits showing how the canal enabled commerce and shaped Washington's development, and see granite stones in nearby sidewalks that mark the building's two previous locations (1837 original, 1915 move, and 2017 restoration). Constitution Avenue NW & 17th Street NW, Washington, DC 20006 (38.8894754, -77.0352426); Thu-Sun: 11:00 AM-3:00 PM. [Source: [NPS - Washington Canal](https://www.nps.gov/articles/history-archeology-of-dc-washington-canal.htm), [Boundary Stones](https://boundarystones.weta.org/2015/08/21/rise-and-fall-washington-city-canal), [DC Historic Sites](https://historicsites.dcpreservation.org/items/show/340)]

## <a name="hidden-gems"></a>Hidden Gems

- <a name="gem-lockkeeper-architectural-details"></a>**Lockkeeper's Architectural Details** (5 minutes) - Examine the authentic fieldstone construction and restored architectural features from the 1837 original building, including the reconstructed chimneys using historic brick, replaced cedar shingle roofing, and restored stone foundation. The $6 million restoration project completed in 2018 preserved these original elements while moving the structure 36 feet south and 35 feet west using hydraulic systems. The house was originally a 2½-story structure, later reduced to 1½ stories, and served various roles including tool shed, watchman's lodge, and police holding area before its restoration. Constitution Avenue NW & 17th Street NW, Washington, DC 20006 (38.8894754, -77.0352426); Thu-Sun: 11:00 AM-3:00 PM; free admission. [Source: [Trust for the National Mall](https://nationalmall.org/content/lockkeepers-house), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockkeeper's_House,_C_&_O_Canal_Extension), [Atlas Obscura](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/canal-lockkeepers-house)]

- <a name="gem-braddocks-rock"></a>**Braddock's Rock** (5 minutes) - Visit this lesser-known historical marker near the ramp leading from Constitution Avenue to Roosevelt Bridge, where a circular stone well protects a rock where General George Braddock supposedly arrived with troops to fight the French and Indian War in 1755. This hidden gem is easily missed by tourists but provides a fascinating glimpse into pre-Revolutionary War history. Near Constitution Avenue NW & 17th Street NW (approximately 38.890, -77.036); accessible 24/7; free. [Source: [Tiber Creek Tours DC](https://www.tibercreektoursdc.com/10-hidden-gems-of-the-national-mall), [Washingtonian](https://www.washingtonian.com/2020/01/26/92-hidden-gems-attractions-in-washington/)]

## <a name="also-nearby"></a>Also Nearby

- <a name="nearby-wwii-memorial"></a>**World War II Memorial** (30-45 minutes, 0.3 miles west/5-minute walk) - Visit this impressive memorial honoring the 16 million who served in the U.S. armed forces during World War II. Located on 17th Street between Constitution and Independence Avenues, the memorial features two 43-foot arches, a plaza with 56 pillars representing U.S. states and territories, and beautiful fountains. The memorial is positioned between the Washington Monument to the east and the Lincoln Memorial to the west. 1750 Independence Ave SW, Washington, DC 20006 (approximately 38.889, -77.040); open 24 hours; free admission. [Source: [Free Tours by Foot](https://freetoursbyfoot.com/national-mall-dc/), [Moon Travel Guides](https://www.moon.com/travel/outdoors/walking-dc-national-mall-memorials/), [Washington.org](https://washington.org/DC-guide-to/national-world-war-ii-memorial)]

- <a name="nearby-constitution-gardens"></a>**Constitution Gardens and Signers Memorial** (15-30 minutes, 0.2 miles northwest/3-minute walk) - Enjoy a tranquil escape with 50 acres of beautiful scenery and a calm pond, providing a quiet retreat from the busy National Mall. Walk across the footbridge to Signers Island to view the Memorial to the 56 Signers of the Declaration of Independence, featuring 56 granite blocks organized into thirteen groups representing the original states, each carved with the signature, name, hometown, and occupation of a signer. Constitution Avenue NW between 17th and 19th Streets NW, Washington, DC 20245 (38.8917886, -77.0426023); open 24 hours; free admission. [Source: [NPS - Constitution Gardens](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/constitution-gardens.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g28970-d564521-Reviews-Constitution_Gardens-Washington_DC_District_of_Columbia.html), [We Go Trip](https://wegotrip.com/blog/constitution-gardens-washington-dc/)]

- <a name="nearby-albert-einstein-memorial"></a>**Albert Einstein Memorial** (10-15 minutes, 0.4 miles north/8-minute walk) - Discover this often-overlooked bronze statue by sculptor Robert Berks at the National Academy of Sciences, featuring Einstein seated with manuscript papers in hand. The base features a star map engraved with astronomical objects, and if you stand in the center and speak, your voice will echo back. Visitors are encouraged to interact with the statue and climb on it for photos. The memorial is located about 700 feet from the Vietnam Veterans Memorial. 2101 Constitution Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20418 (38.89294, -77.0477); open 24 hours; free admission. [Source: [Tiber Creek Tours DC](https://www.tibercreektoursdc.com/10-hidden-gems-of-the-national-mall), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/the-albert-einstein-memorial-washington), [Cherry Blossom Watch](https://cherryblossomwatch.com/exploredc/albert-einstein-memorial/)]

- <a name="nearby-dc-war-memorial"></a>**DC War Memorial** (10-15 minutes, 0.2 miles south/4-minute walk) - Visit this intimate and lesser-known memorial dedicated to the 499 residents of Washington, D.C., who served in World War I. The small but elegant domed structure, dedicated on November 11, 1931, offers a more personal memorial experience compared to the larger monuments. Located between the World War II Memorial and the Korean War Veterans Memorial near the intersection of Independence Avenue SW and 17th Street SW, Washington, DC 20006 (38.8894, -77.0406); open 24 hours; free admission. [Source: [NPS - DC War Memorial](https://www.nps.gov/nama/planyourvisit/dc-war-memorial.htm), [Tiber Creek Tours DC](https://www.tibercreektoursdc.com/10-hidden-gems-of-the-national-mall), [CityBit Travel](https://www.citybit.in/usa/dc-war-memorial/)]
