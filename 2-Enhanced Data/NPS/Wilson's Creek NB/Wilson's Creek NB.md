# Wilson's Creek NB (National Battlefield)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Wilson's Creek National Battlefield Visitor Center** (6424 W. Farm Road 182, Republic, MO 65738 (37.1000, -93.4333); Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; 417-732-2662) - Passport cancellation stamp available at the visitor center or park store. [Source: [Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/wicr/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Contact Us](https://www.nps.gov/wicr/contacts.htm), [Visitor Center Hours](https://wilsonscreek.com/visitor-center-hours)]

**Note:** Call 417-732-2662 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. Mailing address: 5242 S. State Hwy ZZ, Republic, MO 65738. Park located 10 miles southwest of Springfield, Missouri. As of January 1, 2023, no entrance fee.

## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center Museum and Film** (1-2 hours) - Watch 30-minute orientation film about the August 10, 1861 Battle of Wilson's Creek (second major battle of the Civil War), explore museum exhibits featuring artifacts and information about the Trans-Mississippi Theater of the American Civil War including Civil War weapons, uniforms, and personal items; 6424 W. Farm Road 182, Republic, MO 65738 (37.1000, -93.4333); Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; FREE admission [Source: [Visit Wilson's Creek](https://wilsonscreek.com/battlefield), [Things to Do](https://www.visitmo.com/things-to-do/wilsons-creek-national-battlefield), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60994-d258062-Reviews-Wilson_s_Creek_National_Battlefield-Republic_Missouri.html)]

- **Battlefield Driving Tour** (1.5-2 hours with stops) - Drive the paved 4.9-mile loop road with seven interpretive stops including historic Ray Farm, Edwards Cabin/Price's Headquarters, and Bloody Hill battlefield; tour takes 15 minutes to drive without stops; park at each stop to read interpretive signs and explore short walking trails; 6424 W. Farm Road 182, Republic, MO 65738; grounds open 30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset daily; FREE [Source: [Travel the Tour Road](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/travel-the-tour-road.htm), [Visit Wilson's Creek](https://wilsonscreek.com/battlefield), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilson's_Creek_National_Battlefield)]

- **Bloody Hill Walking Trail** (45-60 minutes) - Hike the 0.75-mile trail across Bloody Hill, site of the heaviest fighting and General Nathaniel Lyon's death, with interpretive stops including the 1928 "Lyon Marker" (only monument on battlefield) and natural sinkhole that served as temporary mass grave for Union soldiers; accessed from Stop 2 on driving tour; 6424 W. Farm Road 182, Republic, MO 65738; grounds open 30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset daily; FREE [Source: [Bloody Hill](https://www.nps.gov/places/wilson-s-creek-bloody-hill.htm), [Travel the Tour Road](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/travel-the-tour-road.htm), [Bloody Hill Foundation](https://wilsonscreek.com/tag/bloody-hill)]

- **Ray House Tour** (30-45 minutes) - Tour the restored 1852 farmhouse used as field hospital following the battle, one of the few original Civil War structures remaining on any battlefield; includes springhouse and cornfield battle site; family living history experiences depict the Ray family's experiences during the battle; located at Stop 3 on driving tour; open for tours during limited hours in summer, check at visitor center; FREE [Source: [Visit Wilson's Creek](https://wilsonscreek.com/battlefield), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilson's_Creek_National_Battlefield), [Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/wicr/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Gibson's Mill, Ray Cornfield and Wire Road Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - Hike the most popular 2.7-mile trail (4.5-star rating) with 190 feet elevation gain through historic cornfield where intense fighting occurred and along Wire Road used by both armies; trail connects multiple battlefield sites; moderate difficulty; accessed from tour road; grounds open 30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset daily; FREE [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/missouri/wilsons-creek-national-battlefield), [Outdoor Activities](https://www.nps.gov/wicr/planyourvisit/outdooractivities.htm)]

- **Wilson's Creek Battlefield Loop Trail** (2.5-3 hours) - Complete the longest trail at 5.5 miles featuring 538 feet of elevation gain, circling the entire battlefield and connecting all major sites; moderate to strenuous difficulty; accessed from visitor center or tour road stops; grounds open 30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset daily; FREE [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/missouri/wilsons-creek-national-battlefield), [Outdoor Activities](https://www.nps.gov/wicr/planyourvisit/outdooractivities.htm)]

- **Living History Programs** (30-60 minutes) - Experience Civil War soldier life through musket and artillery firing demonstrations, Civil War medicine presentations, and other period programs; presented Memorial Day to Labor Day on selected weekends; check visitor center for current schedule; 6424 W. Farm Road 182, Republic, MO 65738; times vary; FREE [Source: [Outdoor Activities](https://www.nps.gov/wicr/planyourvisit/outdooractivities.htm), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilson's_Creek_National_Battlefield)]

## Hidden Gems

- **John K. and Ruth Hulston Civil War Research Library** (1-2 hours) - Research in collection of over 12,000 books about the Civil War with emphasis on Trans-Mississippi Theater; one of the most comprehensive Civil War research libraries in the region; located in visitor center; 6424 W. Farm Road 182, Republic, MO 65738; Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM; FREE [Source: [Outdoor Activities](https://www.nps.gov/wicr/planyourvisit/outdooractivities.htm), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/wilsons-creek-national-battlefield/)]

- **Manley Uplands Trail** (2-3 hours) - Hike this less-traveled trail featuring 528 feet elevation gain and native Missouri prairie plant species unique to southern sections of the park; experience quieter, more contemplative battlefield exploration away from main driving tour; accessed from tour road; grounds open 30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset daily; FREE [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/missouri/wilsons-creek-national-battlefield), [Outdoor Activities](https://www.nps.gov/wicr/planyourvisit/outdooractivities.htm)]

- **Horseback Riding Trail** (2-3 hours) - Ride seven miles of equestrian trail through the battlefield landscape (bring your own horse); unique perspective on the battlefield terrain and troop movements; accessed from designated trailheads; grounds open 30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset daily; FREE [Source: [Outdoor Activities](https://www.nps.gov/wicr/planyourvisit/outdooractivities.htm), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilson's_Creek_National_Battlefield)]

- **Explorer Quest Summer Camp** (full day, June) - Kids become Junior Rangers through free summer camp program offering hands-on Civil War history education; registration required; 6424 W. Farm Road 182, Republic, MO 65738; specific dates in June, check website; FREE [Source: [Outdoor Activities](https://www.nps.gov/wicr/planyourvisit/outdooractivities.htm)]

- **August 10 Battle Anniversary Commemoration** (half day, annually) - Attend special commemorative events on the anniversary of the Battle of Wilson's Creek including ranger programs, living history demonstrations, and memorial ceremonies; August 10 annually; 6424 W. Farm Road 182, Republic, MO 65738; times vary; FREE [Source: [Outdoor Activities](https://www.nps.gov/wicr/planyourvisit/outdooractivities.htm)]

- **Ranger-Led Battlefield Tours** (1-1.5 hours) - Join park rangers for in-depth guided tours providing deeper insights into battle tactics, soldier experiences, and aftermath; offered seasonally, check visitor center for schedule; 6424 W. Farm Road 182, Republic, MO 65738; times vary; FREE [Source: [Outdoor Activities](https://www.nps.gov/wicr/planyourvisit/outdooractivities.htm)]
