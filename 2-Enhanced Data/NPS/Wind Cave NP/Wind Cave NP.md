# Wind Cave NP (National Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="wind-cave-visitor-center"></a>**Wind Cave Visitor Center** (26611 US Highway 385, Hot Springs, SD 57747 (43.5566, -103.4783); Daily 8:00 AM - 4:30 PM with expanded hours during summer, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 605-745-4600) - The visitor center is located 11 miles north of Hot Springs off U.S. Highway 385, about 1/2 mile west of the highway. All cave tours depart from here. Passport cancellation stamps are available for free inside the visitor center during operating hours. [Source: [NPS Wind Cave Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/places/wind-cave-visitor-center.htm), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/wica/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Wind+Cave+National+Park+Visitor+Center/@43.5565516,-103.48049,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x877cd5b235bdc539:0xb76c4b445d41b3ff!8m2!3d43.5565516!4d-103.4783013)]

**Note:** Call 605-745-4600 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- <a id="garden-of-eden-tour"></a>**Garden of Eden Tour** (1 hour) - The least strenuous cave tour option featuring boxwork, frostwork, flowstone, and cave popcorn formations. This 1/4-mile tour includes 150 stairs and uses the elevator for both entry and exit, making it accessible for families and those preferring a moderate experience. Tours depart from the visitor center. Tickets available at Recreation.gov (3-120 days in advance) or same-day at visitor center. Adults $14, Children (6-15) $7, Seniors (62+) $7; 26611 US Highway 385, Hot Springs, SD 57747 (43.5566, -103.4783); 605-745-4600. [Source: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/wica/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)]

- <a id="natural-entrance-tour"></a>**Natural Entrance Tour** (1 hour 15 minutes) - A moderate tour showcasing boxwork and cave popcorn while viewing the natural entrance to the cave. This 2/3-mile tour includes 300 stairs and exits by elevator, offering a good balance for families wanting to see more of the cave. Tours depart from the visitor center. Tickets available at Recreation.gov or same-day at visitor center. Adults $16, Children (6-15) $8, Seniors (62+) $8; 26611 US Highway 385, Hot Springs, SD 57747 (43.5566, -103.4783); 605-745-4600. [Source: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/wica/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)]

- <a id="fairgrounds-tour"></a>**Fairgrounds Tour** (1 hour 30 minutes) - The most strenuous walking tour exploring the upper and middle levels of the cave with boxwork, frostwork, and cave popcorn formations. This 2/3-mile tour includes 450 stairs (including 89 consecutive steps) and offers the most comprehensive ranger-led walking experience. Tours depart from the visitor center. Tickets available at Recreation.gov or same-day at visitor center. Adults $16, Children (6-15) $8, Seniors (62+) $8; 26611 US Highway 385, Hot Springs, SD 57747 (43.5566, -103.4783); 605-745-4600. [Source: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/wica/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)]

- <a id="wildlife-viewing"></a>**Wildlife Viewing** (1-2 hours) - Wind Cave National Park is home to several hundred bison, elk herds, pronghorn, prairie dogs, coyotes, and various birds that roam the rolling prairie grasslands and forested hillsides. East Bison Flats is ideal for viewing bison drawn to large prairie dog towns and open spaces. Boland Ridge (near NPS 6) is home to one of the park's elk herds, best viewed at sunrise and sunset. NPS 5 and NPS 6 gravel roads offer excellent wildlife viewing opportunities. Early morning and evening are the best times. During September-October, bull elk may be heard bugling in twilight hours. Stay at least 25 yards from all wildlife (farther from bison recommended). Park grounds open 24 hours; 26611 US Highway 385, Hot Springs, SD 57747 (43.5566, -103.4783); 605-745-4600. [Source: [NPS Watch Wildlife](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/view-wildlife-wica.htm), [Travel South Dakota](https://www.travelsouthdakota.com/trip-ideas/photo-essay/wildlife-wind-cave)]

- <a id="prairie-vista-trail"></a>**Prairie Vista Trail** (30-45 minutes) - A 1-mile loop around the visitor center with 104 feet of elevation gain. This easy, family-friendly trail features interpretive signs and views, offering an accessible introduction to the prairie ecosystem surrounding Wind Cave. Trailhead at visitor center, 26611 US Highway 385, Hot Springs, SD 57747 (43.5566, -103.4783); Park open 24 hours; 605-745-4600. [Source: [Dirt in My Shoes - Easy Hikes](https://www.dirtinmyshoes.com/the-best-easy-hikes-in-wind-cave-national-park/)]

- <a id="rankin-ridge-trail"></a>**Rankin Ridge Trail** (30-45 minutes) - A 1-mile roundtrip trail with 239 feet of elevation gain leading to the highest point in Wind Cave National Park. On clear days, visitors can see panoramic views into Badlands National Park 40 miles away. Easy difficulty, suitable for most visitors. Trailhead location available at visitor center, 26611 US Highway 385, Hot Springs, SD 57747 (43.5566, -103.4783); Park open 24 hours; 605-745-4600. [Source: [Simply Awesome Trips](https://www.simplyawesometrips.com/itinerary-wind-cave-national-park/)]

- <a id="cold-brook-canyon-trail"></a>**Cold Brook Canyon Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - A popular 1.4-mile one-way trail (2.8 miles roundtrip) winding through Cold Brook Canyon and crossing a prairie dog town. Bison are often seen along this trail, offering excellent wildlife viewing opportunities. Easy difficulty. Trailhead location available at visitor center, 26611 US Highway 385, Hot Springs, SD 57747 (43.5566, -103.4783); Park open 24 hours; 605-745-4600. [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hiking-wica.htm), [Dirt in My Shoes - Easy Hikes](https://www.dirtinmyshoes.com/the-best-easy-hikes-in-wind-cave-national-park/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="candlelight-tour"></a>**Candlelight Tour** (2 hours) - A unique summer-only specialty tour where each participant carries a candle bucket to experience the cave as early explorers did. This strenuous 2/3-mile tour includes 424 stairs and explores the cave by candlelight only. Limited to 10 people, minimum age 8 years. Long pants and sturdy hiking boots required. No photography allowed. Cannot be combined with Wild Cave Tour on the same day. Reservations required by calling visitor center up to 1 month in advance. Ages 8+ $16; 26611 US Highway 385, Hot Springs, SD 57747 (43.5566, -103.4783); 605-745-4600. [Source: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/wica/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)]

- <a id="wild-cave-tour"></a>**Wild Cave Tour** (4 hours) - A very strenuous summer-only adventure tour covering approximately 2/3 mile with extensive crawling, climbing, and tight spaces. Most of the tour is spent crawling on hands and knees. Limited to 6 people, minimum age 16 years (parental release required for under 18). Jeans or hiking pants without holes and sturdy hiking boots required. Park provides helmets, lights, gloves, and pads. No photography allowed. Reservations required by calling visitor center up to 1 month in advance. Ages 16+ $45; 26611 US Highway 385, Hot Springs, SD 57747 (43.5566, -103.4783); 605-745-4600. [Source: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/wica/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)]

- <a id="lookout-point-trail"></a>**Lookout Point Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - A 2.2-mile trail following rolling prairie hills to Beaver Creek with an amazing lookout at the end. The trail offers solitude with prairie dogs and bison herds visible in the distance, providing a peaceful escape from busier areas of the park. Moderate difficulty. Trailhead location available at visitor center, 26611 US Highway 385, Hot Springs, SD 57747 (43.5566, -103.4783); Park open 24 hours; 605-745-4600. [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hiking-wica.htm)]

- <a id="centennial-trail-lookout-point-loop"></a>**Centennial Trail and Lookout Point Loop** (3-3.5 hours) - A 4.8-mile loop combining two trails that passes through prairie grass fields, ponderosa pine forests, and prairie dog towns. Offers likely wildlife sightings including bison, elk, or pronghorn in a less-visited area of the park. Moderate difficulty. Trailhead location available at visitor center, 26611 US Highway 385, Hot Springs, SD 57747 (43.5566, -103.4783); Park open 24 hours; 605-745-4600. [Source: [Earth Trekkers - Lookout Point](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/lookout-point-trail-wind-cave/)]

- <a id="highland-creek-trail"></a>**Highland Creek Trail** (5-6 hours) - The longest and most diverse trail in Wind Cave at 8.6 miles, taking hikers through Wind Cave Canyon with varied terrain including grasslands, ponderosa forests, and canyon views. A challenging hike with significant elevation gain, offering solitude and varying landscapes. Strenuous difficulty. Trailhead location available at visitor center, 26611 US Highway 385, Hot Springs, SD 57747 (43.5566, -103.4783); Park open 24 hours; 605-745-4600. [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hiking-wica.htm), [National Park Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/park/subpage/hiking-wind-cave-national-park)]

- <a id="east-bison-flats-trail"></a>**East Bison Flats Trail** (2.5-3 hours) - A 3.7-mile trail going down Wind Cave Canyon and back up to open prairie land with panoramic views. This trail allows hikers to walk through open prairie along a marked route with excellent bison sighting opportunities in the large prairie dog towns. Moderate difficulty. Trailhead location available at visitor center, 26611 US Highway 385, Hot Springs, SD 57747 (43.5566, -103.4783); Park open 24 hours; 605-745-4600. [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hiking-wica.htm)]

- <a id="gravel-roads-nps-5-6"></a>**Gravel Roads NPS 5 and NPS 6** (1-2 hours) - Lesser-visited gravel roads that travel through areas full of wildlife, offering very good chances of seeing bison or other animals. These roads provide a peaceful driving alternative with excellent wildlife viewing away from main park areas. Drive slowly and watch for animals. Roads accessible from main park road; Park grounds open 24 hours; 605-745-4600. [Source: [NPS Watch Wildlife](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/view-wildlife-wica.htm)]

- <a id="backcountry-hiking"></a>**Backcountry Hiking** (Variable) - Wind Cave has an open hike policy allowing visitors to create their own backcountry hiking routes. Check park guidelines online or at the visitor center before embarking on off-trail adventures. Offers ultimate solitude and exploration opportunities for experienced hikers. Visitor center at 26611 US Highway 385, Hot Springs, SD 57747 (43.5566, -103.4783); Park open 24 hours; 605-745-4600. [Source: [Tripadvisor Wind Cave](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g143055-d10162780-Reviews-Wind_Cave_National_Park-Wind_Cave_National_Park_South_Dakota.html)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="custer-state-park-wildlife-loop"></a>**Custer State Park - Wildlife Loop Road** (1.5-2 hours, 10 miles / 25 minutes from Wind Cave) - An 18-mile scenic drive through one of South Dakota's premier wildlife viewing areas. The loop takes 1.5-2 hours to complete depending on wildlife jams. Visitors commonly see bison, pronghorn, deer, and burros. The road connects directly to Wind Cave via State Road 87. State park pass required. Located approximately 10 miles north of Wind Cave; Contact Custer State Park at 605-255-4515. [Source: [Black Hills Parks - Wildlife Loop](https://blackhillsparks.org/wind-cave-road-trips-connecting-the-black-hills-and-beyond/), [Custer Resorts](https://www.custerresorts.com/activities/scenic-drives/wildlife-loop)]

- <a id="custer-state-park-needles-highway"></a>**Custer State Park - Needles Highway** (1 hour drive, 10 miles / 25 minutes from Wind Cave) - A spectacular 14-mile scenic drive through granite spires and pine forests, taking approximately one hour to complete. The highway features narrow tunnels and hairpin turns with stunning rock formations. One of the most scenic drives in the Black Hills. State park pass required. Part of Custer State Park, 10 miles from Wind Cave; Contact Custer State Park at 605-255-4515. [Source: [SD Game Fish & Parks](https://gfp.sd.gov/csp-scenic-drives/), [Earth Trekkers - Custer](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/custer-state-park/)]

- <a id="jewel-cave-national-monument"></a>**Jewel Cave National Monument** (1 hour Scenic Tour / 3-4 hours Spelunking Tour, 20 miles west of Wind Cave) - The world's third longest cave with over 120 miles of explored passageways featuring calcite crystals and unique formations. The Scenic Tour is a 1-hour, half-mile loop with 723 stair steps (limited to 30 people). The Spelunking Tour lasts 3-4 hours and includes belly crawling. Tickets available 30 days in advance at 10 a.m. ET on Recreation.gov. Visitor center open Monday-Saturday 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM; 11149 US Hwy 16, Bldg B12, Custer, SD 57730; 605-673-8300. [Source: [NPS Jewel Cave Tours](https://www.nps.gov/jeca/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [Recreation.gov Jewel Cave](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/facility/10088103)]

- <a id="mount-rushmore"></a>**Mount Rushmore National Memorial** (1-2 hours, 40 miles / 1 hour from Wind Cave) - Iconic sculpture of four U.S. presidents carved into granite. The site includes a half-mile walking trail, Lincoln Borglum Visitor Center with museum exhibits, gift shop, and dining. Grounds open 5:00 AM - 9:00 PM (October-March 11) or 5:00 AM - 11:00 PM (March 12-September). Visitor center hours vary seasonally (8:00 AM opening, closing 5:00 PM - 10:00 PM depending on season). Parking fee $10 (annual pass accepted). 13000 Highway 244, Building 31, Suite 1, Keystone, SD 57751; 605-574-2523. [Source: [NPS Mount Rushmore Hours](https://www.nps.gov/moru/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Mount Rushmore Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/moru/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

- <a id="crazy-horse-memorial"></a>**Crazy Horse Memorial** (2.5-4 hours, approximately 15-20 miles from Wind Cave) - A massive mountain carving in progress honoring Lakota leader Crazy Horse, featuring museums, Native American cultural exhibits, and educational programs. The welcome center and museums are open daily 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM year-round. Plan 2.5-4 hours to fully experience the memorial, museums, and grounds without feeling rushed. Located 4 miles north of Custer, SD on US Highway 16/385; 12151 Avenue of the Chiefs, Crazy Horse, SD 57730; Contact for current admission prices. [Source: [Crazy Horse Hours](https://crazyhorsememorial.org/visit/memorial-hours-and-schedules), [We're in the Rockies - Crazy Horse](https://wereintherockies.com/crazy-horse/), [Tripadvisor Crazy Horse](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g54568-d144991-t3440058-How_much_time_should_a_plan_for_a_visit.html)]

## Review / Personal Notes

---

**Total Recommended Time:** A full day (6-8 hours) is recommended to experience the main attractions at Wind Cave National Park. This includes one cave tour (1-1.5 hours), wildlife viewing opportunities (1-2 hours), and 2-3 shorter hiking trails (2-3 hours total), plus time at the visitor center and travel between locations. For visitors wanting to experience specialty tours like the Candlelight or Wild Cave Tour, or tackle longer trails like Highland Creek, plan for 1-2 full days. [Source: [Dirt in My Shoes - Visit Duration](https://www.dirtinmyshoes.com/how-many-days-should-i-spend-in-wind-cave-national-park/), [Simply Awesome Trips](https://www.simplyawesometrips.com/itinerary-wind-cave-national-park/)]
