# Vicksburg NMP (National Military Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

<a name="visitor-center-stamp"></a>
- **Visitor Center** (3201 Clay Street, Vicksburg, MS 39183 (32.3548532, -90.8452588); Wednesday-Sunday 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM; 601-636-0583) - The primary stamp location is at the Ranger desk inside the visitor center. The visitor center features exhibits, artifacts, a bookstore, and a 20-minute orientation film shown every half hour. [Source: [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/vick/planyourvisit/visitctr.htm), [America's National Parks](https://americasnationalparks.org/passport-to-your-national-parks/passport-cancellation-locations/)]

<a name="uss-cairo-stamp"></a>
- **USS Cairo Museum** (Located within the park on the tour route; 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM daily; 601-636-0583) - Stamp available at the museum alongside the restored Civil War ironclad gunboat. [Source: [America's National Parks](https://americasnationalparks.org/passport-to-your-national-parks/passport-cancellation-locations/), [Murphy's Odyssey](https://murphysodyssey.com/vicksburg_national_military_park/)]

<a name="national-cemetery-stamp"></a>
- **Vicksburg National Cemetery** (Located within the park on the tour route; grounds open sunrise to sunset; 601-636-0583) - Stamp available at the cemetery which holds over 17,000 Union soldiers' graves. [Source: [America's National Parks](https://americasnationalparks.org/passport-to-your-national-parks/passport-cancellation-locations/), [NPS National Cemetery](https://www.nps.gov/vick/learn/historyculture/natcem.htm)]

**Note:** Call 601-636-0583 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

<a name="orientation-film"></a>
- **Orientation Film "Here Brothers Fought"** (20 minutes) - Watch this essential 2009 film at the visitor center theater covering Grant's campaign, the five major battles leading to Vicksburg, and the eventual siege. The film provides crucial historical context before exploring the battlefield. Shows every half hour from 8:30 AM to 4:00 PM; 3201 Clay Street, Vicksburg, MS 39183 (32.3548532, -90.8452588); Wednesday-Sunday 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM. [Source: [NPS Orientation Film](https://www.nps.gov/vick/planyourvisit/movie.htm), [NPS Watch Film](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/watch-vicksburg-national-military-park-s-orientation-film.htm)]

<a name="auto-tour"></a>
- **16-Mile Auto Tour** (2-4 hours) - Drive through the battlefield following 22 designated stops with 1,325+ historic monuments and markers along the route. The tour includes key battle sites, state memorials, and interpretive exhibits. Most visitors spend 2-3 hours making stops; history enthusiasts often take longer. Tour road open 8:30 AM with last vehicle entry at 4:40 PM, closing at 5:00 PM; 3201 Clay Street entrance, Vicksburg, MS 39183 (32.3548532, -90.8452588); 7 days a week; $20 per vehicle entrance fee. [Source: [Visit Vicksburg](https://www.visitvicksburg.com/blog/how-to-spend-a-day-at-the-vicksburg-national-military-park/), [NPS Tour Options](https://www.nps.gov/vick/planyourvisit/park-tour-options.htm), [Action Tour Guide](https://actiontourguide.com/how-long-should-i-spend-at-vicksburg-national-military-park/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60985-d104724-Reviews-Vicksburg_National_Military_Park-Vicksburg_Mississippi.html)]

<a name="uss-cairo"></a>
- **USS Cairo Gunboat and Museum** (30 minutes - 1 hour) - Explore the restored Civil War ironclad that was sunk in 1862 and recovered in 1964, with hundreds of recovered artifacts on display. You can step onto the ironclad and examine naval warfare technology. Located on the tour route; 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM daily; within Vicksburg National Military Park. [Source: [NPS USS Cairo](https://www.nps.gov/vick/u-s-s-cairo-gunboat.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60985-d146271-Reviews-U_S_S_Cairo_Museum-Vicksburg_Mississippi.html), [Visit Vicksburg USS Cairo](https://www.visitvicksburg.com/directory/uss-cairo-museum/)]

<a name="national-cemetery"></a>
- **Vicksburg National Cemetery** (30-45 minutes) - Visit this solemn burial ground containing graves of over 17,000 Union soldiers, including the unique grave of Doug the camel (the military was experimenting with camels in battle) and one Commonwealth war grave of a Royal Australian Air Force airman from WWII. Located on the tour route; grounds open sunrise to sunset; within Vicksburg National Military Park. [Source: [NPS National Cemetery](https://www.nps.gov/vick/learn/historyculture/natcem.htm), [Roadside America](https://www.roadsideamerica.com/story/43439)]

<a name="ranger-programs"></a>
- **Ranger Programs and Tours** (varies, 30 minutes - 2 hours) - Join ranger-led walking tours (0.5-1 mile), historical talks, and artillery demonstrations at key siege locations. Programs offer deeper historical context about the Vicksburg Campaign. Walking tours may include uneven terrain; wear appropriate footwear and bring water. Check schedule at visitor center or call 601-636-0583; programs offered throughout the park; varies by season. [Source: [NPS Ranger Programs](https://www.nps.gov/vick/planyourvisit/ranger-programs.htm), [Vicksburg Daily News](https://vicksburgnews.com/vicksburg-military-park-to-host-weeklong-civil-war-commemoration-and-memorial-day-events/)]

## Hidden Gems

<a name="african-american-monument"></a>
- **African-American Monument** (15 minutes) - One of the park's more recent additions (2004), this thoughtful memorial honors the formerly enslaved people who fought at Vicksburg. The monument takes a quieter, more contemplative approach compared to other park memorials. Located on the tour route; grounds open 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM; within Vicksburg National Military Park. [Source: [Vicksburg Post Art Park](https://www.vicksburgpost.com/2023/02/25/vicksburgs-art-park-sculptures-monuments-are-vnmps-understated-treasures/)]

<a name="kentucky-monument"></a>
- **Kentucky Monument** (15 minutes) - A unique monument staging a fictitious meeting between bronze statues of Abraham Lincoln and Jefferson Davis, both Kentucky-born presidents of opposing sides. This unusual memorial highlights Kentucky's divided loyalties during the Civil War. Located on the tour route; grounds open 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM; within Vicksburg National Military Park. [Source: [Stephen Travels](https://stephentravels.com/the-great-outdoors/vicksburg-national-military-park/), [Vicksburg Post Art Park](https://www.vicksburgpost.com/2023/02/25/vicksburgs-art-park-sculptures-monuments-are-vnmps-understated-treasures/)]

<a name="lloyd-tilghman-statue"></a>
- **Lloyd Tilghman Statue** (10 minutes) - A dramatic monument depicting Confederate General Lloyd Tilghman at the exact moment he was killed by Union shrapnel. The melodramatic sculpture captures the tragedy of war. Located on the tour route; grounds open 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM; within Vicksburg National Military Park. [Source: [Roadside America](https://www.roadsideamerica.com/story/43439)]

<a name="illinois-memorial"></a>
- **Illinois State Memorial** (20 minutes) - A grand domed temple modeled after the Roman Pantheon honoring the 36,000+ Illinois soldiers who fought in the campaign. Climb the 47 steps (one for each day of the siege) to the temple floor. Note: As of September 2025, this monument is undergoing a year-long $5.2 million restoration and may be closed. Located on the tour route; grounds open 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM; within Vicksburg National Military Park. [Source: [NPS Illinois Memorial](https://www.nps.gov/places/illinois-memorial.htm), [Vicksburg Post Restoration](https://www.vicksburgpost.com/2025/08/21/restoring-the-illinois-memorial-vicksburg-national-military-park-to-begin-a-major-restoration-of-the-illinois-memorial/), [National Parks Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/2025/08/illinois-memorial-vicksburg-national-military-park-be-restored)]

<a name="pennsylvania-memorial"></a>
- **Pennsylvania Memorial** (15 minutes) - Dedicated in 1906, this memorial is known among park visitors for stories of strange occurrences, particularly the "bleeding faces" phenomenon. Located on the tour route; grounds open 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM; within Vicksburg National Military Park. [Source: [NPS State Memorials](https://www.nps.gov/vick/learn/historyculture/vnmp-state-memorials.htm), [Visit Vicksburg Myths](https://www.visitvicksburg.com/blog/myths-of-vicksburg-national-military-park/)]

## Also Nearby

<a name="riverfront-murals"></a>
- **Vicksburg Riverfront Murals** (30-60 minutes, 3 miles from park) - View Robert Dafford's 32 spectacular murals (12' x 20' panels) depicting Vicksburg's history painted on the Mississippi River floodwall. Each mural has bronze plaques with historical information. Walk along Levee Street and take time to read about each historical period depicted. Free outdoor public art accessible anytime (daylight viewing recommended); 1201-1299 Levee Street, Vicksburg, MS 39180 (32.3512, -90.8838); free admission. [Source: [Visit Vicksburg Murals](https://www.visitvicksburg.com/directory/vicksburg-riverfront-murals/), [TripAdvisor Murals](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60985-d3877887-Reviews-Vicksburg_Riverfront_Murals-Vicksburg_Mississippi.html), [Visit Mississippi Murals](https://visitmississippi.org/things-to-do/arts-culture/vicksburg-riverfront-murals/)]

<a name="biedenharn-museum"></a>
- **Biedenharn Coca-Cola Museum** (30-45 minutes, 2 miles from park) - Visit the historic building where Coca-Cola was first bottled anywhere in the world in 1894. The museum features vintage memorabilia, soda fountains, and the original candy company. 1107 Washington Street, Vicksburg, MS 39183 (32.35171, -90.88168); Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday 1:30 PM - 4:30 PM; closed major holidays; 601-638-6514. [Source: [Biedenharn Museum](https://www.biedenharncoca-colamuseum.com/), [Visit Vicksburg Biedenharn](https://www.visitvicksburg.com/directory/biedenharn-coca-cola-museum-1890/), [TripAdvisor Biedenharn](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60985-d144604-Reviews-Biedenharn_Coca_Cola_Museum-Vicksburg_Mississippi.html)]

<a name="old-courthouse"></a>
- **Old Court House Museum** (1-1.5 hours, 2.5 miles from park) - Explore this 1858-1860 Greek Revival courthouse, now a comprehensive museum featuring Civil War artifacts, Confederate history, and Vicksburg's broader historical collections. 1008 Cherry Street, Vicksburg, MS 39183 (32.35251, -90.878193); Monday-Saturday 8:30 AM - 4:00 PM (until 5:00 PM during Daylight Saving Time), Sunday temporarily closed; 601-636-0741. [Source: [Old Court House Museum](https://oldcourthouse.org/), [Visit Vicksburg Court House](https://www.visitvicksburg.com/directory/old-court-house-museum-circa-1858-1860/), [Visit Mississippi Court House](https://visitmississippi.org/things-to-do/attractions/old-court-house-museum/)]

<a name="jesse-brent-museum"></a>
- **Jesse Brent Lower Mississippi River Museum** (30 minutes, 4 miles from park) - A free museum where you can learn about Mississippi River history and how the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers manages the river, plus tour a retired Corps vessel. Great quick overview of river engineering and history. Located in downtown Vicksburg near the riverfront; typical hours 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; free admission. [Source: [Oak Hall BNB](https://oakhallbnb.com/things-to-do-vicksburg-ms/), [TripAdvisor Vicksburg](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g60985-Activities-Vicksburg_Mississippi.html)]
