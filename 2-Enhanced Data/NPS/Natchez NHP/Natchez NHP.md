# Natchez NHP (National Historical Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="melrose-visitor-center"></a>**Melrose Visitor Center** - Located at the Melrose estate, this is the primary location for obtaining NPS passport cancellation stamps; 122 Melrose Ave, Natchez, MS 39120 (31.5470831, -91.3855572); Daily 8:30 AM-5:00 PM; Phone: 601-446-5790 [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/natc/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Visit Natchez](https://visitnatchez.org/listing/natchez-national-historical-park/)]

- <a id="william-johnson-house-stamps"></a>**William Johnson House** - First floor visitor center; 210 State St, Natchez, MS 39120 (31.56011, -91.405808); Daily 8:30 AM-5:00 PM; Phone: 601-445-5345 [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/natc/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/item/ms0298/)]

**Note:** The main Natchez Visitor Center at 640 S. Canal Street is currently closed for interior rehabilitation. Call ahead to confirm current stamp availability: 601-446-5790 (Melrose) or 601-445-5345 (William Johnson House). [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/natc/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="melrose-estate-tour"></a>**Melrose Estate Guided Tour** (30-45 minutes) - Tour this Greek Revival mansion distinguished by original furnishings, decorative arts, and landscape, showcasing Southern planter class history and the enslaved people who lived and worked on the estate; mansion tours include first and second floors with self-guided access to outbuildings; 122 Melrose Ave, Natchez, MS 39120 (31.5470831, -91.3855572); Grounds open daily 8:30 AM-5:00 PM, mansion tours offered at 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 2:00 PM, 3:00 PM, 4:00 PM; $11 per person (under 17 free), reservations required via Recreation.gov [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/natc/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Recreation.gov Tours](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/10089703/ticket/10089704), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60910-d107299-Reviews-Melrose-Natchez_Mississippi.html)]

- <a id="william-johnson-house-visit"></a>**William Johnson House Museum** (30-45 minutes) - Explore the home of William Johnson, a free African American barber and businessman whose published diary provides remarkable insight into 19th-century Natchez life; first floor visitor center and exhibits currently open with interpretive displays; 210 State St, Natchez, MS 39120 (31.56011, -91.405808); Daily 8:30 AM-5:00 PM (second floor family quarters currently closed) [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/natc/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/natchez-national-historical-park/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natchez_National_Historical_Park)]

- <a id="fort-rosalie"></a>**Fort Rosalie Historic Site** (30 minutes) - Visit the site of the 1716 French fort with interpretive panels explaining the fort's role in early Mississippi history and the Natchez Revolt; scenic overlook of the Mississippi River; 528 S Canal St, Natchez, MS 39120 (31.55980, -91.40858); Daily sunrise to sunset [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/natc/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [HMDB Fort Rosalie](https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=126870), [Darkhorse Press](https://darkhorsepressnow.com/news/2025-03-20/echoes-of-the-mississippi-visit-fort-rosalie/)]

- <a id="forks-of-the-road"></a>**Forks of the Road Slave Market Site** (30-45 minutes) - Explore exhibits and wayside panels documenting the second-largest domestic slave market in the Deep South, which operated from 1833-1863; artistic depictions include slave manacles and chains; provides essential context about Natchez as a center of slavery; intersection of Liberty Rd and D'Evereux Dr, Natchez, MS 39120 (31.55561, -91.38482); Daily sunrise to sunset [Source: [NPS Visit Forks of the Road](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/about-forks-of-the-road.htm), [MS History Now](https://www.mshistorynow.mdah.ms.gov/issue/the-forks-of-the-road-slave-market-at-natchez), [Visit Natchez Forks](https://visitnatchez.org/listing/forks-of-the-road-slave-market/)]

- <a id="downtown-walking-tours"></a>**Downtown Walking Tours** (60-90 minutes) - Join ranger-led walking tours alternating weekly between "The Great Tornado of 1840" and "William Johnson's Natchez," offered during special programming periods including National Park Week; downtown Natchez starting location varies by tour; seasonal availability, check with park for current schedule; Phone: 601-446-5790 [Source: [Natchez Democrat](https://www.natchezdemocrat.com/2025/04/13/natchez-national-historical-park-kicks-off-national-park-week-april-19-through-27/), [Visit Natchez Events](https://visitnatchez.org/event/natchez-national-historical-park-kicks-off-national-park-week/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="melrose-outbuildings"></a>**Melrose Estate Outbuildings Self-Guided Tour** (30 minutes) - After the mansion tour, explore the estate's historic outbuildings independently, including dependencies that housed enslaved workers and support structures that illustrate the plantation's operations; accessible during mansion tour hours; 122 Melrose Ave, Natchez, MS 39120 (31.5470831, -91.3855572); Daily 8:30 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [Recreation.gov Tours](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/10089703/ticket/10089704), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/natchez-national-historical-park/)]

- <a id="melrose-gardens"></a>**Melrose Landscape and Gardens** (20-30 minutes) - Stroll the grounds of Melrose estate featuring period-appropriate landscaping that reflects 19th-century Southern design principles; free access even without mansion tour; 122 Melrose Ave, Natchez, MS 39120 (31.5470831, -91.3855572); Daily 8:30 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/natc/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60910-d107299-Reviews-Melrose-Natchez_Mississippi.html)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="grand-village"></a>**Grand Village of the Natchez Indians** (1-2 hours, 3 miles south of downtown) - Explore this 128-acre Mississippi state historic site featuring three prehistoric ceremonial mounds, a reconstructed Natchez Indian house, and museum with artifacts interpreting the culture of the Natchez people who inhabited the area before European contact; 400 Jefferson Davis Blvd, Natchez, MS 39120 (31.5365, -91.3802); Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday 1:30 PM-5:00 PM; Free admission [Source: [MDAH Grand Village](https://www.mdah.ms.gov/explore-mississippi/grand-village-natchez-indians), [Visit Natchez Grand Village](https://visitnatchez.org/listing/grand-village-of-the-natchez-indians/), [TripAdvisor Grand Village](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60910-d107300-Reviews-Grand_Village_of_the_Natchez_Indians-Natchez_Mississippi.html)]

- <a id="stanton-hall"></a>**Stanton Hall** (45-60 minutes, downtown Natchez) - Tour one of the largest and most lavish antebellum mansions in the United States, built in 1857 by cotton grower Frederick Stanton; features opulent Greek Revival architecture and period furnishings; 401 High St, Natchez, MS 39120 (31.5609, -91.3966); Daily tours 9:00 AM-4:00 PM (last tour), tours at top of each hour; prices vary, two-house packages available with Longwood [Source: [Visit Natchez Stanton Hall](https://visitnatchez.org/listing/stanton-hall/), [TripAdvisor Stanton Hall](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60910-d634844-Reviews-Stanton_Hall-Natchez_Mississippi.html), [Natchez Pilgrimage Tours](https://natchezpilgrimage.com/year-round-two-house-package-longwood-and-stanton-hall/)]

- <a id="longwood"></a>**Longwood** (45-60 minutes, 0.5 miles south of downtown) - Visit the largest octagonal house in America, an unfinished antebellum mansion with an extraordinary story; construction halted when the Civil War began, leaving the upper floors incomplete; 140 Lower Woodville Rd, Natchez, MS 39120 (31.5504, -91.3984); Daily tours 9:00 AM-4:00 PM (last tour), tours at top of each hour; ~$25 per person, two-house packages available [Source: [TripAdvisor Longwood](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60910-d265976-Reviews-Longwood-Natchez_Mississippi.html), [NatchezTrace Travel](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-mississippi/lorman-natchez-ms/natchez-mississippi/longwood.html), [Natchez Pilgrimage Tours](https://natchezpilgrimage.com/year-round-two-house-package-longwood-and-stanton-hall/)]

- <a id="rosalie-mansion"></a>**Rosalie Mansion** (45-60 minutes, downtown on bluff) - Tour this 1823 mansion that became the architectural prototype for later antebellum homes across the South; located near the Fort Rosalie massacre site with Mississippi River views; 100 Orleans St, Natchez, MS 39120 (31.5598, -91.4027); Daily tours 9:00 AM-4:00 PM (last tour); part of three-mansion ticket options [Source: [Visit Natchez Rosalie](https://visitnatchez.org/listing/rosalie/), [TripAdvisor Rosalie](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60910-d506133-Reviews-Rosalie_Mansion-Natchez_Mississippi.html), [Wanderlog Antebellum Homes](https://wanderlog.com/list/geoCategory/1583441/best-antebellum-homes-in-natchez)]

- <a id="natchez-under-the-hill"></a>**Natchez Under-the-Hill Historic District** (1-2 hours, downtown riverfront below the bluff) - Explore this 75-acre historic riverfront district with restaurants, shops, and bars including the Under the Hill Saloon; enjoy Mississippi River views and sunset watching; restaurants and shops along Silver St near foot of bluff (31.5560, -91.4085); District open 24/7, individual business hours vary; free to walk around [Source: [TripAdvisor Under-the-Hill](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60910-d18981087-Reviews-Natchez_Bluffs_and_Under_the_Hill_Historic_District-Natchez_Mississippi.html), [Visit Mississippi Under-the-Hill](https://visitmississippi.org/things-to-do/architecture/natchez-under-the-hill-historic-district/), [Wikipedia Under-the-Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natchez_Bluffs_and_Under-the-Hill_Historic_District)]

- <a id="emerald-mound"></a>**Emerald Mound** (30-45 minutes, 10 miles northeast on Natchez Trace Parkway) - Visit the second-largest Mississippian Period ceremonial mound in the United States; this 35-foot-high mound covers eight acres at its base; accessible from milepost 10.3 via MS Highway 553; exit Natchez Trace Parkway at MS-553, go west 1 mile (31.4167, -91.2833); Daily sunrise to sunset; Free [Source: [NPS Emerald Mound](https://www.nps.gov/natr/learn/historyculture/emeraldmound.htm), [NatchezTrace Travel Emerald Mound](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-mississippi/lorman-natchez-ms/194-emerald-mound.html), [Explore Southern History](https://exploresouthernhistory.com/natchezemerald.html)]

- <a id="natchez-trace-parkway-southern-terminus"></a>**Natchez Trace Parkway Southern Terminus** (accessible anytime, downtown Natchez) - Begin or end your journey on the scenic 444-mile Natchez Trace Parkway at milepost 0 in Natchez; enjoy parkway highlights near Natchez including loess bluffs and historic sites; southern terminus at intersection of US-84 and Canal St, Natchez (31.5606, -91.4031); Open 24/7; Free [Source: [NPS Natchez Trace](https://www.nps.gov/natr/), [Scenic Trace](https://www.scenictrace.com/), [Visit Mississippi Parkway](https://visitmississippi.org/things-to-do/attractions/natchez-trace-parkway/)]
