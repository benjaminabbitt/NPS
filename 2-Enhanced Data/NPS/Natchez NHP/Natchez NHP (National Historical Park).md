## Key Activities

- **Melrose Mansion Ranger-Guided Tour** (45 minutes) - Explore this distinguished Greek Revival mansion built circa 1845, featuring original furnishings, decorative arts, and an 80-acre estate with outbuildings including slave quarters, stable, and carriage house; ranger-guided tours only, held daily at 10 AM, 11 AM, 2 PM, and 3 PM; 1 Melrose-Montebello Parkway, Natchez, MS 39120 [31.55, -91.40]; Daily 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/natc/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60910-d107299-Reviews-Melrose-Natchez_Mississippi.html), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/natc/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- **Melrose Grounds and Gardens Walk** (30-45 minutes) - Walk the 80-acre estate grounds featuring wooded and landscaped park with ponds, formal gardens, orchard, and manicured lawns surrounding the mansion and historic outbuildings; 1 Melrose-Montebello Parkway, Natchez, MS 39120 [31.55, -91.40]; Grounds open Daily 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/natc/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Visit Natchez](https://visitnatchez.org/listing/melrose/)]

- **William Johnson House Self-Guided Tour** (30-45 minutes) - Tour the 1840 home of William Johnson, a free African American businessman and barber who kept detailed diaries documenting antebellum Natchez life; self-guided tour with park ranger on duty to answer questions; 210 State Street, Natchez, MS 39120 [31.55998, -91.40577]; Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (closed 12:00 PM - 1:00 PM for lunch), Sunday 12:00 PM - 5:00 PM, closed Monday-Tuesday in December-January, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day; 601-445-5345 [Source: [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/visit-william-johnson-house.htm), [NatchezTraceTravel](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-mississippi/lorman-natchez-ms/natchez-mississippi/williamjohnsonhouse.html), [Visit Natchez](https://visitnatchez.org/listing/william-johnson-house/)]

- **Fort Rosalie Site Visit** (15-30 minutes) - Visit the green space maintained by NPS with five information panels telling the history of Fort Rosalie, founded August 3, 1716 as a French colonial fort and the oldest permanent settlement on the lower Mississippi River; nothing of the original fort remains but interpretive signage and picnic tables available; 100 Orleans Street, Natchez, MS 39120 [31.5598, -91.40858]; Daily dawn to dusk (sunrise to sunset) [Source: [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/visit-fort-rosalie.htm), [Visit Natchez](https://visitnatchez.org/listing/fort-rosalie/), [HMDB](https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=126870)]

- **Longwood Mansion Tour** (45-60 minutes) - Tour the largest octagonal house in the United States, an unfinished antebellum mansion where construction was halted in 1861 at the start of the Civil War with only nine basement rooms completed out of 32 planned rooms; hourly guided tours start at 9:00 AM with last tour at 3:00 PM; 140 Lower Woodville Road, Natchez, MS 39120 [31.54, -91.41]; Daily 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM (9:00 AM - 3:00 PM during summer months); Note: NOT part of NPS, operated by Natchez Pilgrimage Tours [Source: [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/longwood-natchez), [Natchez Pilgrimage Tours](https://natchezpilgrimage.com/year-round/longwood-circa-1860-1861/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60910-d265976-Reviews-Longwood-Natchez_Mississippi.html), [Visit Natchez](https://visitnatchez.org/listing/longwood/)]

- **Stanton Hall Tour** (45-60 minutes) - Tour one of the most magnificent Greek Revival homes in America, occupying an entire city block in downtown Natchez, built by wealthy cotton merchant Frederick Stanton in 1857; guided tours held hourly from 9:00 AM with last tour at 3:00 PM; 401 High Street, Natchez, MS 39120 [31.56195, -91.40007]; Daily 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM; Note: NOT part of NPS, operated by Natchez Pilgrimage Tours [Source: [Visit Natchez](https://visitnatchez.org/listing/stanton-hall/), [Natchez Pilgrimage Tours](https://natchezpilgrimage.com/year-round/stanton-hall-circa-1857/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60910-d634844-Reviews-Stanton_Hall-Natchez_Mississippi.html)]

- **Grand Village of the Natchez Indians** (1-1.5 hours) - Tour the museum, reconstructed traditional Natchez Indian house, and three preserved earthen mounds on this 128-acre site that was the main ceremonial mound center of the Natchez people from 1682-1730, a National Historic Landmark; free admission with group tours available by appointment; 400 Jefferson Davis Boulevard, Natchez, MS 39120 [31.523, -91.38]; Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday 1:30 PM - 5:00 PM; 601-446-6502 [Source: [MDAH](https://www.mdah.ms.gov/explore-mississippi/grand-village-natchez-indians), [Visit Natchez](https://visitnatchez.org/listing/grand-village-of-the-natchez-indians/), [Visit Mississippi](https://visitmississippi.org/things-to-do/arts-culture/grand-village-of-the-natchez/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Natchez Bluff Trail Walk** (15-20 minutes) - Walk this easy 0.6-mile out-and-back trail atop Natchez Bluff with scenic views of the Mississippi River, benches at strategic locations, and interpretive panels telling the history of Natchez founded in 1716; includes option to descend a 200-foot wooden staircase to the base of the bluff; trail access near downtown Natchez along the bluff; Daily dawn to dusk [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/mississippi/natchez-bluff-trail), [Visit Natchez](https://visitnatchez.org/listing/natchez-trails/), [NatchezTraceTravel](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-mississippi/lorman-natchez-ms/natchez-mississippi/natchezblufftrail.html)]

- **Natchez City Cemetery Walking Tour** (2-3 hours for thorough exploration) - Explore this historic cemetery dating back to 1822, filled with intricate mausoleums, towering tombstones, and captivating stories; free self-guided tours with brochures and maps available at cemetery office, or audio CD tours available for purchase ($20 for online streaming tour); cemetery located in downtown Natchez; Daily 7:00 AM to dusk [Source: [City of Natchez](https://natchez.ms.us/263/Cemetery-Tours), [Visit Natchez](https://visitnatchez.org/listing/natchez-city-cemetery/), [NatchezTraceTravel](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-mississippi/lorman-natchez-ms/natchez-mississippi/natchez-city-cemetery.html)]

- **Natchez Spring and Fall Pilgrimage Home Tours** (varies, plan for multiple hours or full day) - Tour over 20 private antebellum homes opened to the public during annual pilgrimages; Spring Pilgrimage runs March 8 - April 19 with special weekends March 27-30 and April 3-6; Fall Pilgrimage runs September 27 - October 18; tickets available online, by phone, or at each home's door; various locations throughout Natchez historic district [Source: [Visit Natchez Spring](https://visitnatchez.org/event/natchez-garden-clubs-spring-pilgrimage-2025/), [Natchez Pilgrimage Tours](https://natchezpilgrimage.com/), [Visit Natchez Fall](https://visitnatchez.org/event/natchez-garden-clubs-fall-pilgrimage-of-historic-homes/)]

- **Propinquity** (45-60 minutes) - Visit one of the earliest Federal-style homes in Mississippi located in the historic territorial capital of Washington, MS (about 6 miles from Natchez), surrounded by ancient live oaks offering a secluded retreat; hours vary, check availability; Washington, MS [Source: [Little Easy Tours](https://littleeasytours.com/2024/01/07/uncovering-natchezs-best-kept-secrets-hidden-gems-and-off-the-beaten-path-attractions/)]

- **Concord Quarters** (30-45 minutes) - Visit the only freestanding former slave dwelling in Mississippi listed on the National Register of Historic Places, providing insight into the African American experience in the antebellum South; hours vary, check availability; Natchez area [Source: [Little Easy Tours](https://littleeasytours.com/2024/01/07/uncovering-natchezs-best-kept-secrets-hidden-gems-and-off-the-beaten-path-attractions/)]

- **Under-the-Hill Saloon** (1-2 hours) - Visit this historic saloon in what was once the port area of Natchez, offering live music, views of the Mississippi River, and a history involving the rough-and-tumble river trade era; 25 Silver Street, Natchez, MS; hours vary [Source: [Little Easy Tours](https://littleeasytours.com/2024/01/07/uncovering-natchezs-best-kept-secrets-hidden-gems-and-off-the-beaten-path-attractions/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Emerald Mound** (30-45 minutes, 10 miles / 15-20 minutes drive north) - Visit one of the largest and best-preserved Mississippian period ceremonial mounds in the United States, built by Indigenous people between 1200-1730 CE, the second-largest Pre-Columbian earthwork in the U.S.; located at milepost 10.3 on Natchez Trace Parkway, exit and go west one mile on Mississippi Highway 553; Daily dawn to dusk, free admission [Source: [NPS Natchez Trace](https://www.nps.gov/natr/learn/historyculture/emeraldmound.htm), [ExploreHistory](https://exploresouthernhistory.com/natchezemerald.html), [Visit Natchez](https://visitnatchez.org/listing/emerald-mound/)]

- **Windsor Ruins** (30-45 minutes, 52-54 miles / 60-70 minutes drive northeast) - Explore the 23 remaining columns that are all that's left of the largest antebellum mansion constructed in Mississippi in the 1860s; hauntingly beautiful ruins located near Port Gibson, MS; exit Natchez Trace Parkway at milepost 30 onto Mississippi Highway 552 going west and follow signs; Open 24/7, free admission [Source: [NatchezTraceTravel Windsor Loop](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-mississippi/loop-routes/windsor-ruins.html), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g43963-d676885-Reviews-Windsor_Ruins-Port_Gibson_Mississippi.html), [Distance Calculator](https://www.trippy.com/distance/Port-Gibson-to-Natchez)]

- **Natchez Trace Parkway Scenic Drive** (varies, full day recommended for complete drive) - Drive the historic 444-mile parkway from Natchez to Nashville following the historic travel corridor used by American Indians, European settlers, and traders; numerous historical sites, hiking trails, and scenic overlooks along the route; northern terminus begins in Natchez; Open 24/7, free admission [Source: [NPS Natchez Trace](https://www.nps.gov/natr/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 4-6 hours for the three main NPS sites (Melrose mansion tour and grounds, William Johnson House, Fort Rosalie). Plan for a full day (8-10 hours) to include key non-NPS Natchez historic homes like Longwood and Stanton Hall, plus the Grand Village. Add 2-3 additional hours for hidden gems like the Natchez Bluff Trail and cemetery. Plan 2-3 days total to thoroughly experience Natchez and the surrounding area during pilgrimage season.
