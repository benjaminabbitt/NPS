# Canyonlands NP (National Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-hike-mesa-arch-trail"></a>**Hike Mesa Arch Trail** (30 minutes) - Walk easy 0.5-mile round-trip trail to the park's most famous arch, especially stunning at sunrise with panoramic views; minimal elevation gain; located in Island in the Sky [Source: [Visit Utah](https://www.visitutah.com/places-to-go/parks-outdoors/canyonlands/travel-tips), [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-things-to-do-in-canyonlands-island-in-the-sky/)]

- <a id="activity-drive-island-in-the-sky-scenic-road"></a>**Drive Island in the Sky Scenic Road** (2-3 hours with stops) - Travel through mesa resting on steep sandstone cliffs over 1,000 feet high with stops at Grand View Point Overlook, Green River Overlook, Upheaval Dome, and Shafer Trail Overlook; closest district to Moab [Source: [NPS Island in the Sky](https://www.nps.gov/cany/planyourvisit/islandinthesky.htm), [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-things-to-do-in-canyonlands-island-in-the-sky/)]

- <a id="activity-hike-white-rim-overlook-trail"></a>**Hike White Rim Overlook Trail** (1-2 hours) - Walk 1.8-mile round-trip out-and-back trail with interesting terrain and stunning views in Island in the Sky [Source: [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-things-to-do-in-canyonlands-island-in-the-sky/)]

- <a id="activity-explore-the-needles-district"></a>**Explore The Needles District** (4-6 hours) - Drive 90 minutes from Moab to experience unique striped sandstone spires, low crowds, and thrilling hikes including Chesler Park Loop (11 miles), Druid Arch (11 miles), and Slickrock Trail [Source: [NPS Trip Itineraries](https://www.nps.gov/cany/planyourvisit/itineraries.htm), [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-things-to-do-in-canyonlands-national-park/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-drive-shafer-trail-switchbacks"></a>**Drive Shafer Trail Switchbacks** (1-2 hours) - Experience frightening but incredible unpaved drive down steep switchbacks into the canyon floor; high-clearance vehicle recommended [Source: [We're in the Rockies](https://wereintherockies.com/guide-to-visiting-canyonlands/)]

- <a id="gem-hike-to-grandstaff-canyon"></a>**Hike to Grandstaff Canyon** (2-3 hours) - Trek moderately challenging trail off River Road 128 leading to Morning Glory Natural Bridge; popular Moab-area hike [Source: [Carey On Travels](https://careyontravels.com/moab-things-to-do-off-the-beaten-path/)]

- <a id="gem-visit-horseshoe-canyon"></a>**Visit Horseshoe Canyon** (4-5 hours) - Explore remote western section of park known for striking rock formations and ancient rock art including the Great Gallery pictograph panel [Source: [Full Suitcase](https://fullsuitcase.com/best-things-to-do-in-canyonlands-national-park/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-dead-horse-point-state-park"></a>**Dead Horse Point State Park** (1-2 hours, 14 miles from Island in the Sky) - Experience the most beautiful sunset views in the Moab area from dramatic overlook 2,000 feet above the Colorado River; $20 per vehicle [Source: [Carey On Travels](https://careyontravels.com/moab-things-to-do-off-the-beaten-path/), [More Than Just Parks](https://morethanjustparks.com/things-to-do-canyonlands-national-park/)]

- <a id="nearby-corona-arch"></a>**Corona Arch** (1-2 hours, near Moab) - Hike 3-mile round-trip trail to massive arch with 140 by 105 foot opening, also known as Little Rainbow; trailhead off Highway 279 [Source: [Carey On Travels](https://careyontravels.com/moab-things-to-do-off-the-beaten-path/)]

- <a id="nearby-fisher-towers"></a>**Fisher Towers** (2-3 hours, near Moab) - Walk 5.2-mile round-trip trail through photogenic dark red sandstone formations carved into spires; trailhead off Highway 128 [Source: [Carey On Travels](https://careyontravels.com/moab-things-to-do-off-the-beaten-path/)]
