# Canyonlands NP (National Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Island in the Sky Visitor Center** (Grand View Point Road, Moab, UT 84532 (38.473405, -109.809587); 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM daily, extended hours spring through fall, closed for a few winter holidays; 435-259-4712) [Source: [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/cany/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Utah.com](https://www.utah.com/destinations/national-parks/canyonlands-national-park/visitor-information/visitor-center/)]

- **Needles Visitor Center** (Route 211, Needles District (38.168509, -109.753276); 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM daily, extended hours March-October, closes for winter February-mid February; 435-259-4711) [Source: [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/cany/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Go Utah](https://www.go-utah.com/Canyonlands-National-Park-Needles-District-Visitor-Center-Address/)]

- **Hans Flat Ranger Station** (The Maze District; 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM year-round; no phone listed) [Source: [Canyonlands Passport Stamps](http://nostalgia.esmartkid.com/canyonlandspasprt.html)]

**Note:** Eight official stamps available across locations. Park headquarters in Moab at 2282 Resource Blvd also has stamps; 435-719-2313. [Source: [NPS Contact](https://www.nps.gov/cany/contacts.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Hike Mesa Arch Trail** (30 minutes) - Walk easy 0.5-mile round-trip trail to the park's most famous arch, especially stunning at sunrise with panoramic views; minimal elevation gain; located in Island in the Sky [Source: [Visit Utah](https://www.visitutah.com/places-to-go/parks-outdoors/canyonlands/travel-tips), [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-things-to-do-in-canyonlands-island-in-the-sky/)]

- **Drive Island in the Sky Scenic Road** (2-3 hours with stops) - Travel through mesa resting on steep sandstone cliffs over 1,000 feet high with stops at Grand View Point Overlook, Green River Overlook, Upheaval Dome, and Shafer Trail Overlook; closest district to Moab [Source: [NPS Island in the Sky](https://www.nps.gov/cany/planyourvisit/islandinthesky.htm), [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-things-to-do-in-canyonlands-island-in-the-sky/)]

- **Hike White Rim Overlook Trail** (1-2 hours) - Walk 1.8-mile round-trip out-and-back trail with interesting terrain and stunning views in Island in the Sky [Source: [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-things-to-do-in-canyonlands-island-in-the-sky/)]

- **Explore The Needles District** (4-6 hours) - Drive 90 minutes from Moab to experience unique striped sandstone spires, low crowds, and thrilling hikes including Chesler Park Loop (11 miles), Druid Arch (11 miles), and Slickrock Trail [Source: [NPS Trip Itineraries](https://www.nps.gov/cany/planyourvisit/itineraries.htm), [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-things-to-do-in-canyonlands-national-park/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Drive Shafer Trail Switchbacks** (1-2 hours) - Experience frightening but incredible unpaved drive down steep switchbacks into the canyon floor; high-clearance vehicle recommended [Source: [We're in the Rockies](https://wereintherockies.com/guide-to-visiting-canyonlands/)]

- **Hike to Grandstaff Canyon** (2-3 hours) - Trek moderately challenging trail off River Road 128 leading to Morning Glory Natural Bridge; popular Moab-area hike [Source: [Carey On Travels](https://careyontravels.com/moab-things-to-do-off-the-beaten-path/)]

- **Visit Horseshoe Canyon** (4-5 hours) - Explore remote western section of park known for striking rock formations and ancient rock art including the Great Gallery pictograph panel [Source: [Full Suitcase](https://fullsuitcase.com/best-things-to-do-in-canyonlands-national-park/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Dead Horse Point State Park** (1-2 hours, 14 miles from Island in the Sky) - Experience the most beautiful sunset views in the Moab area from dramatic overlook 2,000 feet above the Colorado River; $20 per vehicle [Source: [Carey On Travels](https://careyontravels.com/moab-things-to-do-off-the-beaten-path/), [More Than Just Parks](https://morethanjustparks.com/things-to-do-canyonlands-national-park/)]

- **Corona Arch** (1-2 hours, near Moab) - Hike 3-mile round-trip trail to massive arch with 140 by 105 foot opening, also known as Little Rainbow; trailhead off Highway 279 [Source: [Carey On Travels](https://careyontravels.com/moab-things-to-do-off-the-beaten-path/)]

- **Fisher Towers** (2-3 hours, near Moab) - Walk 5.2-mile round-trip trail through photogenic dark red sandstone formations carved into spires; trailhead off Highway 128 [Source: [Carey On Travels](https://careyontravels.com/moab-things-to-do-off-the-beaten-path/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 1-2 days ideal; one day sufficient for Island in the Sky district (6-8 hours), two days to visit both Island in the Sky and Needles districts.
