# Pu'uhonua o Hōnaunau NHP (National Historical Park)

## <a id="cancellation-stamps"></a>Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-visitor-center"></a>**Visitor Center** (State Hwy 160, Hōnaunau, HI 96726 (19.4892248, -155.9206453); Daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM; (808) 328-2326 ext. 1702) - The NPS passport cancellation stamp is located in the park store area where you enter the park. [Source: [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/puho/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/puho/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

**Note:** The park grounds are open daily from 8:15 AM until sunset. Call ahead to confirm stamp availability and current hours.

## <a id="key-activities"></a>Key Activities

- <a id="activity-self-guided-tour"></a>**Self-Guided Walking Tour (1-2 hours)** - The main half-mile circular trail takes visitors through the royal grounds and pu'uhonua (place of refuge), featuring the Great Wall (built around 1550, over 1,000 feet long and 10 feet high), reconstructed houses, fishponds, and the sacred Hale o Keawe temple where the bones of 23 ali'i (chiefs) were once housed. The temple area is guarded by ki'i (wooden carved figures representing Hawaiian gods). Park grounds at State Hwy 160, Hōnaunau, HI 96726 (19.4892248, -155.9206453); Daily 8:15 AM-sunset. [Source: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60941-d108277-Reviews-Pu_uhonua_O_Honaunau_National_Historical_Park-Honaunau_Island_of_Hawaii_Hawaii.html), [NPS Royal Grounds](https://home.nps.gov/puho/planyourvisit/royalgrounds.htm), [Lonely Planet Great Wall](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/usa/hawaii/pu-uhonua-o-honaunau-national-historical-park/attractions/great-wall/a/poi-sig/1554413/1333357)]

- <a id="activity-audio-tour"></a>**1871 Trail with NPS Audio Tour (1.5-2 hours)** - Extended walking trail with comprehensive audio tour available through the NPS app, providing detailed historical and cultural context about the royal grounds, the kapu system, and the place of refuge tradition. Same park location and hours as above. [Source: [TripAdvisor FAQ](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g60941-d108277-t3617224-How_much_time_do_you_need_to_see_the_sites_here.html)]

- <a id="activity-ranger-talks"></a>**Daily Ranger Talks (30-45 minutes)** - Park rangers provide presentations about Hawaiian history, culture, and the significance of the pu'uhonua system at the park's amphitheater. Cultural demonstrators also practice traditional techniques including weaving baskets from lauhala, making lei, and playing Hawaiian games. Park amphitheater, check at visitor center for daily schedule; Daily during park hours. [Source: [NPS Puho Article](https://www.nps.gov/articles/puohonau.htm), [Oh Ranger Things to Do](http://www.ohranger.com/puuhonua-o-honaunau/things-do)]

- <a id="activity-papamu-game"></a>**Play Konane (Papamu) (15-30 minutes)** - Try papamu, a traditional Hawaiian strategy game played by royalty on lava rock game boards with black and white pieces. Game boards are located at Marker 3 along the trail. Available during park hours. [Source: [Hikespeak Royal Grounds Loop](https://www.hikespeak.com/trails/puuhonau-o-honaunau-royal-grounds-loop-hawaii/), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60941-d108277-Reviews-Pu_uhonua_O_Honaunau_National_Historical_Park-Honaunau_Island_of_Hawaii_Hawaii.html)]

- <a id="activity-tidepools"></a>**Tidepool Exploration (30-60 minutes)** - Walk out onto the rugged lava fields along the coastline to observe marine life in the tidepools. The dark lava rock and pale trail provide access to pools teeming with sea life. Best visited during low tide. Accessed from the main trail along the coastline. [Source: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60941-d108277-Reviews-Pu_uhonua_O_Honaunau_National_Historical_Park-Honaunau_Island_of_Hawaii_Hawaii.html), [Gone with the Gastons](https://paulagaston.com/puuhonua-o-honaunau-national-historic-park-hawaii/)]

## <a id="hidden-gems"></a>Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-cultural-festival"></a>**Annual Cultural Festival (3-5 hours)** - Held annually on the last weekend of June or early July (typically 9:00 AM-2:00 PM or 3:00 PM), this festival celebrates Hawaiian culture with traditional foods, hula, music, Hawaiian crafts demonstrations, games, outdoor workshops under palm trees, and hukilau net fishing demonstrations. State Hwy 160, Hōnaunau, HI 96726 (19.4892248, -155.9206453); Late June/Early July annually. [Source: [Aloha Visitor Guides Festival](https://alohavisitorguides.com/annual-cultural-festival-puuhonua-o-honaunau-national-historical-park/), [NPS Cultural Festival](https://www.nps.gov/puho/planyourvisit/culturalfestival.htm), [Big Island Now](https://bigislandnow.com/2022/06/23/festival-at-puuhonua-o-honaunau-national-historical-park-celebrates-hawaiian-culture/)]

- <a id="gem-early-morning"></a>**Early Morning Visit for Wildlife (1-2 hours)** - Arrive between 8:15-8:30 AM when the park opens to experience the site with fewer crowds and better lighting for photography. Early morning also provides optimal conditions for spotting marine life in tidepools and coastal waters before the afternoon overcast conditions set in. Park grounds accessible from 8:15 AM. [Source: [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/puho/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

- <a id="gem-kiilae-village"></a>**Kiilae Village Archaeological Site (30-45 minutes)** - Explore the lesser-known Kiilae village area near the park, which provides additional insight into ancient Hawaiian daily life beyond the royal grounds and pu'uhonua. Located within the park boundaries, accessible from main trail. [Source: [Hikespeak Royal Grounds Loop](https://www.hikespeak.com/trails/puuhonau-o-honaunau-royal-grounds-loop-hawaii/), [Hawaii Pacific Parks](https://www.hawaiipacificparks.org/national-park-partners/puuhonua-o-honaunau)]

## <a id="also-nearby"></a>Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-two-step"></a>**Honaunau Bay Two Step Snorkeling (1-2 hours, adjacent to park)** - World-class snorkeling spot located just outside the park boundary at Honaunau Bay, famous for its crystal clear water, green sea turtles, colorful tropical fish, and natural "two step" lava rock entry point. Best visited in the morning before afternoon overcast reduces visibility. Limited free parking (13 spaces) or $5 paid lot; arrive early. Honaunau Bay, Captain Cook, HI (19.42592, -155.91171); Dawn to dusk, county-managed. [Source: [Big Island Guide Two Step](https://bigislandguide.com/honaunau-bay-two-step), [Tropical Snorkeling](https://www.tropicalsnorkeling.com/snorkeling-two-steps/), [NPS Snorkeling Info](https://www.nps.gov/puho/planyourvisit/snorkeling.htm), [Live That Adventure](https://livethatadventure.com/snorkeling-at-honaunau-bay-two-step/)]

- <a id="nearby-captain-cook-monument"></a>**Captain Cook Monument at Kealakekua Bay (2-4 hours round trip, 3.7 miles/9 minutes by car)** - White obelisk marking the spot where Captain Cook was killed in 1779, accessible only by water (kayak/snorkel tour) or a moderately steep 3.6-mile hiking trail (1,256 feet elevation change). The bay offers some of Hawaii's best snorkeling and diving. Kayak tours require permits from only two authorized companies. Napo'opo'o Road trailhead, Captain Cook, HI (19.481306, -155.933444); Dawn to dusk. [Source: [Explore Big Island Kealakekua Bay](https://www.explore-the-big-island.com/kealakekua-bay.html), [TripAdvisor Captain Cook Monument](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60580-d3519179-Reviews-The_Captain_Cook_Monument-Captain_Cook_Island_of_Hawaii_Hawaii.html), [Wikipedia Kealakekua Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kealakekua_Bay), [Big Island Hikes](https://bigislandhikes.com/kealakekua-bay)]

- <a id="nearby-kona-coffee-farms"></a>**Kona Coffee Farm Tours (1-2.5 hours, 10-15 miles north in Coffee Belt)** - Tour working coffee farms in the Kona Coffee Belt region stretching from Kailua-Kona to Captain Cook and Honaunau. Greenwell Farms (81-6581 Mamalahoa Highway, Kealakekua, HI 96750; approximately 19.52, -155.92) offers complimentary 45-60 minute tours with coffee sampling (Daily 9:00 AM-3:00 PM, retail store 8:30 AM-5:00 PM). Other farms offer 30-minute to 2.5-hour experiences showcasing coffee cultivation, processing, and tasting. [Source: [Greenwell Farms](https://www.greenwellfarms.com/pages/big-island-kona-coffee-tours), [Love Big Island Coffee Tours](https://www.lovebigisland.com/kona-coffee-tasting/), [Go Hawaii Coffee Plantations](https://www.gohawaii.com/islands/hawaii-big-island/things-to-do/land-activities/coffee-plantations), [Big Island Guide Kona Coffee](https://bigislandguide.com/kona-coffee-guide-and-tasting-tours)]

- <a id="nearby-hikiau-heiau"></a>**Hikiau Heiau (30-45 minutes, at Kealakekua Bay)** - Ancient luakini temple located at the south end of Kealakekua Bay, part of the state historical park. This sacred site offers historical context for understanding ancient Hawaiian religious practices. Located at Napo'opo'o Road end, Kealakekua Bay State Historical Park; Dawn to dusk. [Source: [Explore Big Island Kealakekua Bay](https://www.explore-the-big-island.com/kealakekua-bay.html)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 3-4 hours for comprehensive park exploration including the self-guided walking tour (1-2 hours), ranger talk and cultural demonstrations (30-45 minutes), playing konane (15-30 minutes), and tidepool exploration (30-60 minutes). Add 1-2 additional hours for the extended 1871 Trail audio tour. [Source: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60941-d108277-Reviews-Pu_uhonua_O_Honaunau_National_Historical_Park-Honaunau_Island_of_Hawaii_Hawaii.html), [Thoroughly Travel Guide](https://www.thoroughlytravel.com/puuhonua-o-honaunau-national-historical-park/)]
