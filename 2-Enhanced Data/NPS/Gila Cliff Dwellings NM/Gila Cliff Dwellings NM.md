# Gila Cliff Dwellings NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Gila Visitor Center** (26 Jim Bradford Trail, Mimbres, NM 88049 (33.227161, -108.270647); 8:00 AM-4:30 PM daily except December 25 and January 1; 575-536-9461) [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/gicl/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/gicl/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://maps.apple.com/place?address=26+Jim+Bradford+Trail,+Mimbres,+NM+88049)]

**Note:** Call 575-536-9461 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Gila Cliff Dwellings Trail** (1-2 hours) - Self-guided one-mile loop trail with 181 feet elevation gain visiting five natural caves containing the cliff dwellings built by the Mogollon people between 1275-1300 AD; visitors climb ladders to explore approximately 40 rooms including living quarters, communal and ceremonial spaces; 26 Jim Bradford Trail, Mimbres, NM 88049; Trail open 9:00 AM-5:00 PM daily, last entry 4:30 PM [[Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/gila-cliff-dwellings-trail), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/gicl/planyourvisit/hike-to-the-cliff-dwellings.htm), [Source](https://www.gjhikes.com/2017/12/gila-cliff-dwellings.html), [Source](https://thetouristchecklist.com/gila-cliff-dwellings/)]

- **Visitor Center and Film** (30-45 minutes) - Watch a 12-minute interpretive film about the history and culture of the Mogollon people who built the dwellings, view exhibits about the archaeological site and local natural history; 26 Jim Bradford Trail, Mimbres, NM 88049; 8:00 AM-4:30 PM daily [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g47046-d267067-Reviews-Gila_Cliff_Dwellings_National_Monument-Gila_New_Mexico.html), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/gicl/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

- **Lightfeather Hot Springs** (30-45 minutes) - Short 0.5-mile hike (20 minutes each way) from the visitor center along Middle Fork Trail #157 to natural hot springs along the Gila River; includes two river crossings; hot water mixes with cold river water in maintained pools; accessible during visitor center hours [[Source](https://backcountrypost.com/threads/three-short-hikes-at-gila-cliff-dwellings.2516/), [Source](https://www.bearmountainlodge.com/gila-cliff-dwellings-and-more), [Source](https://www.americansouthwest.net/new_mexico/gila-cliff-dwellings/hot-springs_l.html)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Little Bear Canyon Trail via TJ Corral Trailhead** (4-5 hours for 9-mile round trip) - Moderate 4.4-mile trail from TJ Corral trailhead (1 mile from visitor center) climbing over the watershed divide between the West and Middle Forks of the Gila River; trail passes through exposed terrain before entering narrow canyon with towering hoodoo cliffs; 630 feet elevation gain; TJ Corral Trailhead near 26 Jim Bradford Trail, Mimbres, NM 88049; daylight hours [[Source](https://www.outdoorproject.com/nm/little-bear-canyon-hike), [Source](https://stavislost.com/hikes/trail/middle-fork-of-gila-river-and-little-bear-canyon-loop/), [Source](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/gila/recreation/trails/little-bear-trail-729)]

- **Jordan Hot Springs** (10-12 hours for 13.7-mile round trip, or backpacking trip) - Challenging day hike or overnight backpacking destination featuring two crystal-clear turquoise pools fed by natural hot springs; accessed via Little Bear Canyon Trail #729 (7 miles one-way) from TJ Corral trailhead with approximately 15 river crossings; 1,443 feet elevation gain; requires wilderness permit; TJ Corral Trailhead near 26 Jim Bradford Trail, Mimbres, NM 88049; daylight hours recommended [[Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/jordan-hot-springs), [Source](https://www.livesmallridefree.com/blog/backpack-to-jordan-hot-springs-gila-wilderness), [Source](https://www.findinghotsprings.com/mag/jordan-hot-springs-new-mexico), [Source](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/new-mexico/hidden-cliff-dwellings-of-new-mexicos-gila-river/)]

- **Additional Mogollon Ruins Sites** (variable time, requires wilderness hiking) - Lesser-visited archaeological sites including Javalina House (0.3 miles above main cliff dwellings), West Fork Ruin, Three Mile Ruin along the West Fork of the Gila River, and the 11-room Cosgrove Ruin at the Middle Fork; accessed via various wilderness trails; daylight hours [[Source](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/new-mexico/hidden-cliff-dwellings-of-new-mexicos-gila-river/), [Source](https://wnpa.org/about-wnpa/newsroom/irresistible-distractions-at-gila-cliff-dwellings-national-monument-3)]

## Also Nearby

- **Catwalk National Recreation Trail** (1.5-2 hours, 90 minutes from Gila Cliff Dwellings) - Scenic 1-mile (one-way) trail featuring a metal walkway suspended above Whitewater Creek in a narrow canyon; follows historic pipeline route; 242 feet elevation gain; rated as one of the most scenic trails in the West; Catwalk Road (NM Highway 174), 5 miles east of Glenwood, NM; $5 day-use fee; daylight hours [[Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/the-catwalk-national-recreation-trail), [Source](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/gila/recreation/trails/catwalk-national-recreation-trail-207), [Source](https://newmexiconomad.com/catwalk-recreation-area/), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g60831-i1922-k12231286-Gila_cliff_dwellings_and_Catwalk_on_same_day-Silver_City_New_Mexico.html)]

- **City of Rocks State Park** (2-3 hours, 33 miles south of Silver City) - Unique geological formation of volcanic rock pillars creating a maze-like landscape; opportunities for hiking, rock climbing, and stargazing; 327 Highway 61, Faywood, NM 88034; 7:00 AM-9:00 PM gate hours, visitor center 8:00 AM-5:00 PM; $5 per vehicle; 575-536-2800 [[Source](https://www.newmexico.org/places-to-visit/regions/southwest/city-of-rocks/), [Source](https://www.emnrd.nm.gov/spd/find-a-park/city-of-rocks-state-park/), [Source](https://www.desertusa.com/ctsrocks/cityrock.html)]

- **Western New Mexico University Museum** (1-2 hours, in Silver City, 44 miles south) - Features the world's largest and most comprehensive collection of Mimbres pottery from a single prehistoric site (NAN Ranch Collection); black-on-white pottery dating from 9th-12th centuries with distinctive geometric and figurative designs; free admission; 1000 West College Ave, Fleming Hall, Silver City, NM 88061; Monday-Friday 9:00 AM-4:30 PM, Saturday-Sunday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM; call 575-538-6386 to confirm hours [[Source](https://www.yelp.com/biz/western-new-mexico-university-museum-silver-city), [Source](https://museum.wnmu.edu/), [Source](https://www.go-newmexico.com/The-WNMU-Museum-and-Mimbres-Pottery-Collection/), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60831-d318400-Reviews-Western_New_Mexico_University_Museum-Silver_City_New_Mexico.html)]
