# Little Rock Central High School NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-visitor-center"></a>**Visitor Center** (2120 W. Daisy L. Gatson Bates Drive, Little Rock, AR 72202-5212 (34.7365, -92.2984); Tuesday-Saturday, 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM CST (Summer: 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM CDT); Phone: (501) 516-7230) - [Source: [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/chsc/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** Cancellation stamps are located inside the visitor center near the information desk. Ranger or volunteer staff can assist with stamping. Free admission. [Source: [NPS Stamp Your Passport](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/stamp-your-park-passport.htm)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-visitor-center-exhibits"></a>**Visitor Center Exhibits** (1-2 hours) - Explore detailed exhibits featuring interviews, news footage, and audio/video recordings from those directly involved in the 1957 desegregation crisis and the Little Rock Nine story. Includes a park interpretive film, accessible restrooms, and a Jefferson National Parks Association bookstore; 2120 W. Daisy L. Gatson Bates Drive, Little Rock, AR 72202-5212 (34.7365, -92.2984); Tuesday-Saturday, 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM CST (Summer: 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM CDT); Phone: (501) 516-7230 [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/chsc/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/chsc/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-ranger-led-guided-tour"></a>**Ranger-Led Guided Tour** (approximately 2-2.5 hours) - Expert-guided outdoor walking tour exploring the history of desegregation, the Little Rock Nine, and the civil rights movement. Accommodates up to 50 people. Tours must be reserved at least 48 hours in advance (recommended: 2 months ahead); offered Tuesday-Saturday at 1:00 PM CST/CDT; available by phone reservation only; 2120 W. Daisy L. Gatson Bates Drive, Little Rock, AR 72202-5212; Phone: (501) 516-7230 [Source: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/chsc/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [NPS Park Ranger Tips](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/plan-like-a-park-ranger-top-10-tips-for-visiting-little-rock-central-high-school-nhs.htm)]

- <a id="activity-self-guided-audio-walking-tour-via-nps-app"></a>**Self-Guided Audio Walking Tour via NPS App** (approximately 1-1.5 hours) - Download the NPS app for a self-guided walking tour featuring maps, audio narration, and written transcripts created by students from the Little Rock Central High School Memory Project. Tours the grounds and follows Elizabeth Eckford's path on September 4, 1957. Available anytime the grounds are accessible; Commemorative Garden open daily 7:00 AM - sunset [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/chsc/planyourvisit/index.htm), [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/chsc/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)]

- <a id="activity-commemorative-garden-exploration"></a>**Commemorative Garden Exploration** (30-45 minutes) - Walk through the beautifully landscaped Commemorative Garden featuring nine trees and nine benches honoring the Little Rock Nine. Discover the circular plaza with triumphant arches designed by Michael Warrick and Aaron Hussey, featuring a poem etched in concrete ("The Spirit of Central High") and a mirror-like photographic collage documenting Central High's history from 1927 through desegregation to present. Accessible ramp on south and east entrances; open daily 7:00 AM - sunset; adjacent to visitor center on South Park Street [Source: [NPS Commemorative Garden](https://www.nps.gov/places/commemorative-garden.htm), [NPS Cultural Landscape](https://www.nps.gov/articles/little-rock-central-high-school-501428.htm)]

- <a id="activity-elizabeth-eckford-commemorative-bench"></a>**Elizabeth Eckford Commemorative Bench** (15-20 minutes) - Visit the replica of the original 1957 public bus bench where Elizabeth Eckford sought shelter from the mob on September 4, 1957. Located at the corner of Park and 16th Streets in Little Rock, this historically significant spot serves as a place for reflection and remembrance of her courageous nonviolent response to violent opposition to desegregation. Open daily. [Source: [NPS Elizabeth Eckford Bus Bench](https://www.nps.gov/places/elizabeth-eckford-bus-bench.htm)]

- <a id="activity-magnolia-mobil-gas-station-historic-building"></a>**Magnolia Mobil Gas Station Historic Building** (15-30 minutes) - View the preserved 1920s Magnolia Mobil gas station building restored to its 1957 appearance. During the desegregation crisis, this location served as an impromptu media hub where reporters from The New York Times, Life magazine, Der Spiegel, Chicago Defender, and other national and international news outlets covered the events at Central High. Now serves as an education center about social justice and civil rights; located at the corner of Daisy L. Gatson Bates Drive and Park Street, across from the visitor center. [Source: [NPS Magnolia Mobil](https://www.nps.gov/places/magnolia-mobil-gas-station.htm), [NPS Cultural Landscape](https://www.nps.gov/articles/little-rock-central-high-school-501428.htm)]

- <a id="activity-junior-ranger-program"></a>**Junior Ranger Program** (30-45 minutes) - Pick up a free Junior Ranger Activity Booklet at the Visitor Center and complete age-appropriate activities to earn your badge and special stamp; available at the visitor center information desk; 2120 W. Daisy L. Gatson Bates Drive, Little Rock, AR 72202-5212; Tuesday-Saturday, 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM CST (Summer: 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM CDT) [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/chsc/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- <a id="activity-central-high-school-grounds-observation"></a>**Central High School Grounds Observation** (20-30 minutes) - View the exterior of Little Rock Central High School, an active school with over 2,500 students. Note: The school building itself is not open to the public. Best times to visit: avoid 8:30-9:00 AM and 3:30-4:00 PM on weekdays to prevent traffic congestion during school pickup/dropoff times; located adjacent to the visitor center [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/chsc/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-testament-the-little-rock-nine-monument-at-arkansas-state-capitol"></a>**Testament: The Little Rock Nine Monument at Arkansas State Capitol** (30-45 minutes) - Visit the first civil rights monument erected on any state capitol grounds in the South. This striking bronze sculpture memorial features life-sized statues of the nine students walking together with books, along with individual plaques bearing quotations from each student. Dedicated August 30, 2005, and designed by artists John and Kathy Deering. Located on the grounds of the Arkansas State Capitol, directly north of the capitol building, facing the Arkansas Governor's office. Open daily; 500 Woodlane Street, Little Rock, AR (approximately 1.5 miles from the NHS visitor center) [Source: [NPS Testament Statues](https://www.nps.gov/places/testament-statues.htm), [Civil Rights Trail](https://civilrightstrail.com/attraction/little-rock-nine-memorial-at-state-capitol/)]

- <a id="gem-daisy-l-bates-house-museum"></a>**Daisy L. Bates House Museum** (1-1.5 hours) - Tour the home that served as the command post during the 1957 desegregation crisis and haven for the Little Rock Nine. This nationally significant site where the students and their mentor Daisy L. Bates planned their integration efforts remains a powerful connection to this pivotal moment in civil rights history. Free tours available, but must be scheduled in advance; open Friday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM; 1207 West 28th Street, Little Rock, AR; Phone: (501) 375-1957 [Source: [NPS Home of Daisy Bates](https://www.nps.gov/places/home-of-daisy-and-l-c-bates.htm), [Civil Rights Trail](https://civilrightstrail.com/attraction/daisy-bates-house/)]

- <a id="gem-paul-laurence-dunbar-high-school-historic-site"></a>**Paul Laurence Dunbar High School Historic Site** (30-40 minutes) - Explore the historic 1929 school building considered "The Finest High School for Negro Boys and Girls in Arkansas" when constructed. Several of the Little Rock Nine attended Dunbar before being recommended for Central High by Principal Edward Luther Hawkins Sr. The building, added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1980, continues to serve the community as Dunbar Magnet Middle School. Visiting arrangements should be coordinated with the Little Rock School District or NPS; located at Wright Avenue and Ringo Street, Little Rock, AR (approximately 1-1.5 miles from visitor center) [Source: [NPS Paul Laurence Dunbar](https://www.nps.gov/places/paul-laurence-dunbar-high-school.htm), [Encyclopedia of Arkansas](https://encyclopediaofarkansas.net/entries/paul-laurence-dunbar-high-school-2859/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-arkansas-state-capitol-and-grounds"></a>**Arkansas State Capitol and Grounds** (1-1.5 hours) - Tour the historic capitol building with its magnificent grounds featuring multiple memorials and monuments, including Testament: The Little Rock Nine Monument, Vietnam Veterans Memorial, Medal of Honor Memorial, and Law Enforcement Officers' Memorial. Beautiful grounds ideal for walking and reflection; 500 Woodlane Street, Little Rock, AR; open for tours; approximately 1.5 miles from NHS visitor center [Source: [Arkansas.com](https://www.arkansas.com/little-rock/landmarks/arkansas-state-capitol-grounds-monuments)]

- <a id="nearby-museum-of-discovery"></a>**Museum of Discovery** (2 hours) - Little Rock's leading science center featuring permanent exhibits including "Earth Journeys" exploring Arkansas weather and native fauna, "Amazing You" explaining human physiology, and "Tinkering Studio" for hands-on creation. Notable attractions include a Guinness World Record Tesla coil producing 200,000 volts, and the Tornado Alley Theater (5 minutes) recreating the 1999 tornadoes with full sensory experience; admission $10 adults, $8 children/seniors/military; 500 President Clinton Ave., Suite 150, Little Rock, AR (River Market district); approximately 1.5 miles from NHS visitor center [Source: [Museum of Discovery](https://museumofdiscovery.org/), [Arkansas.com](https://www.arkansas.com/little-rock/museums-libraries/museum-discovery)]

- <a id="nearby-william-j-clinton-presidential-library"></a>**William J. Clinton Presidential Library** (2-3 hours) - Explore the Clinton Presidential Center and Park on the banks of the Arkansas River, featuring interactive museum exhibits including a replica Oval Office, dynamic educational programs, award-winning restaurant (42 bar and table), museum store, and 50-acre presidential park providing a natural riverfront oasis; admission and grounds open daily; approximately 1.5-2 miles from NHS visitor center [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60766-d509012-Reviews-William_J_Clinton_Presidential_Library-Little_Rock_Arkansas.html)]

- <a id="nearby-river-market-district-and-arkansas-river-trail"></a>**River Market District and Arkansas River Trail** (1-2 hours) - Explore Little Rock's historic and vibrant River Market district featuring shops, restaurants, galleries, and public art. Walk or bike the scenic Arkansas River Trail, a multi-use trail system along the water. Close proximity to museums and cultural attractions; approximately 1-1.5 miles from NHS visitor center [Source: [LR Culture Vulture](https://lrculturevulture.com/), [Arkansas.com](https://www.arkansas.com/)]

- <a id="nearby-old-state-house-museum"></a>**Old State House Museum** (1-1.5 hours) - Tour the 1836 Arkansas State House, the oldest standing state capitol building west of the Mississippi River. Features historical exhibits related to Arkansas government and history; located in downtown Little Rock; approximately 1.5 miles from NHS visitor center [Source: [Arkansas.com](https://www.arkansas.com/little-rock/museums-libraries)]

**Total Recommended Visit Time for Little Rock Central High School NHS: 3-4 hours** (includes visitor center exhibits (1-2 hours), Commemorative Garden walk (30-45 minutes), Elizabeth Eckford Bench (15-20 minutes), and Magnolia Mobil building viewing (15-30 minutes)). Ranger-led tours can extend the visit to 4-5 hours. Self-guided audio tour through NPS app requires approximately 1-1.5 hours. Site staff recommend planning for approximately half a day to fully experience this important civil rights history. [Source: [NPS Half Day Trip](https://www.nps.gov/tripideas/central-high-half-day.htm), [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/chsc/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/chsc/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

**Important Visitor Notes:**
- Visitor Center Hours: Tuesday-Saturday, 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM CST (Summer June 1-August 31: 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM CDT); Closed Sundays and Mondays
- Commemorative Garden and outdoor spaces: Open daily 7:00 AM - sunset
- Free admission to all NPS areas
- Free parking available with accessible spaces
- No food services at visitor center; dining available in nearby areas
- Advance reservation required for ranger-led programs (minimum 48 hours, recommend 2 months)
- School building interior not open to public; school active weekdays with over 2,500 students
- Accessibility accommodations available including mobility devices, audio description, assistive listening, and ASL tours (2 weeks notice)
- Phone: (501) 516-7230 [Source: [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/chsc/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]
