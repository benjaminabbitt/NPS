# Padre Island NS (National Seashore)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Malaquite Visitor Center** (20420 Park Road 22, Corpus Christi, TX 78418 (27.4243667, -97.2990078); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; (361) 949-8068) [Source: [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/visitor-center.htm), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

**Note:** Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Sea Turtle Hatchling Releases** (1 hour) - Watch critically endangered Kemp's Ridley sea turtle hatchlings make their way to the ocean at sunrise during summer months (mid-June through August); releases occur at 6:45 AM on Malaquite Beach in front of the visitor center, with ranger program starting at 6:20 AM; call Hatchling Hotline at (361) 949-7163 for schedule; no white clothing or flash photography permitted as it disorients hatchlings; 20420 Park Road 22, Corpus Christi, TX 78418; releases typically draw 1,300 people [Source: [NPS Sea Turtle Releases](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/hatchlingreleases.htm), [Visit Corpus Christi](https://www.visitcorpuschristi.com/beaches/sea-turtle-releases/), [Express News](https://www.expressnews.com/lifestyle/travel-outdoors/article/sea-turtle-hatchling-releases-north-padre-island-19492241.php)]

- **Beach Driving** (2-8 hours) - Drive down 60 miles of pristine beach with majority of seashore open to motorized vehicles; 2WD vehicles permitted up to Mile Marker 5, 4WD required beyond; check tide conditions before heading out; park is open 24 hours but visitor center hours 9 AM-5 PM daily; entrance fee $10 per vehicle (valid 7 consecutive days) or $20 annual pass; 20420 Park Road 22, Corpus Christi, TX 78418 [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Western National Parks](https://wnpa.org/plan-your-trip/trip-ideas/park-stories/how-to-plan-your-perfect-trip-to-padre-island-national-seashore-1), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- **Grasslands Nature Trail** (30 minutes) - Easy 0.75-0.8 mile paved loop through coastal prairie and dunes; wheelchair accessible with benches along the way; numbered posts correspond to trail brochure; no shade so full sun exposure; located just south of park entrance station; open during park hours; 20420 Park Road 22, Corpus Christi, TX 78418 [Source: [NPS Grasslands Trail](https://www.nps.gov/places/grasslands-nature-trail.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/texas/padre-island-national-seashore), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60927-d146791-Reviews-Grasslands_Nature_Trail-Corpus_Christi_Texas.html)]

- **Bird Island Basin Kayaking** (2-4 hours) - Paddle through calm, shallow waters of Laguna Madre with easy access for fishing, birding, and kayaking; ideal for beginners; kayak rentals available at Worldwinds Windsurfing concession ((361) 949-7472); launch at Bird Island Basin Day Use Area (turn left when road forks); located 1.5 miles inside park entrance, turn right onto Bird Island Basin Road, go 0.25 mile to beach; open during park hours [Source: [Islands Magazine](https://www.islands.com/1959393/texas-padre-island-national-seashore-beach-campground-bird-island-basin-kayak/), [NPS Boating](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/boating.htm), [WorldWinds](http://www.southpoint.com/states/tx/worldwinds.html)]

- **Windsurfing at Bird Island Basin** (2-6 hours) - Experience one of the best windsurfing locations in the United States with year-round wind and warm water nine months per year; equipment rentals and lessons available through Worldwinds Windsurfing ((361) 949-7472); wetsuit rentals available; located at Bird Island Basin, 1.5 miles inside park entrance; open during park hours [Source: [NPS Boating](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/boating.htm), [Outdoorsy](https://www.outdoorsy.com/guide/padre-island-national-seashore), [WorldWinds](http://www.southpoint.com/states/tx/worldwinds.html)]

- **Beachcombing and Shelling** (1-3 hours) - Search for unique seashells, sea beans, and historic artifacts along 70 miles of white sand beach; best time is right after storms when strong tides bring incredible finds; visitors allowed to keep up to 1 gallon of seashells and sea beans; accessible year-round along Gulf beaches; 20420 Park Road 22, Corpus Christi, TX 78418 [Source: [NPS Beachcombing](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/beachcombing.htm), [TouristSecrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/texas/secrets-of-texass-padre-island-national-seashore-barrier-islands/)]

- **Swimming and Beach Activities** (2-4 hours) - Enjoy 70-mile stretch of white sand beach with warm Gulf of Mexico waters; popular swimming area at Malaquite Beach near visitor center; beach open 24 hours, lifeguards not always present so swim at own risk; 20420 Park Road 22, Corpus Christi, TX 78418 [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Visit Corpus Christi](https://www.visitcorpuschristi.com/beaches/locations/padre-island-national-seashore/)]

- **Fishing** (2-8 hours) - Fish anywhere along Gulf of Mexico shore and Laguna Madre, or from boats; exception: no fishing on beach in front of Malaquite Visitor Center; valid Texas fishing license required (children under 17 exempt); excellent year-round fishing opportunities; accessible 24 hours; 20420 Park Road 22, Corpus Christi, TX 78418 [Source: [NPS Fishing](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/fishing.htm), [Texas Saltwater Fishing](https://www.texassaltwaterfishingmagazine.com/fishing/locations/lower-coast/padre-island-national-seashore)]

- **Ranger-Led Programs** (30-45 minutes) - Free programs appropriate for all ages including deck talks, beach walks, star parties, and Cowboy Connections; topics include migrating/resident birds, seashells, island plant life, animals, and beach findings; check calendar or NPS App for daily schedule; stop at visitor center upon arrival for program schedule; 20420 Park Road 22, Corpus Christi, TX 78418; Daily 9 AM-5 PM [Source: [NPS Ranger Programs](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/ranger-led-programs.htm), [Western National Parks](https://wnpa.org/how-to-plan-your-perfect-trip-to-padre-island-national-seashore/)]

- **Bird Watching** (2-4 hours) - Observe over 300 bird species at key stop on Central Flyway migration route, making it a top birding spot in Texas; Grasslands Nature Trail and Bird Island Basin are prime locations; accessible year-round with peak migration seasons; 20420 Park Road 22, Corpus Christi, TX 78418 [Source: [TouristSecrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/texas/secrets-of-texass-padre-island-national-seashore-barrier-islands/), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Camping at Malaquite Campground** (overnight) - 48 sites (6 tent-only) including 2 accessible sites (#12 and #13) on gulf side just north of visitor center; short walk to beach; first-come, first-served basis; $14 per night (50% discount for Senior/Access passholders); amenities include restrooms, cold water showers, picnic tables, some grills and shade structures; no hookups but dump station and potable water available; campers can pitch tent at site or on beach in front of campground; open year-round; 20420 Park Road 22, Corpus Christi, TX 78418 [Source: [NPS Malaquite Campground](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/malaquite_campground.htm), [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/259111), [National Parks Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/park/subpage/lodging-and-camping-padre-island-national-seashore)]

- **Primitive Beach Camping** (overnight) - Free beach camping on North Beach (1.5 miles) and South Beach (60 miles total: Mile 0-5 accessible by 2WD, Mile 5-60 requires 4WD); self-service permit required at registration station where paved roads end; may close temporarily due to coastal flooding; no facilities; 20420 Park Road 22, Corpus Christi, TX 78418 [Source: [NPS Camping](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/camping.htm), [Nomads RV](https://nomadsrv.com/the-ultimate-guide-to-camping-on-padre-island-national-seashore/)]

- **Horseback Riding** (2-4 hours) - Permitted on Gulf beaches except in front of Malaquite Beach Visitor Center and campground; bring your own horses as no guided services available within national seashore; open during park hours; 20420 Park Road 22, Corpus Christi, TX 78418 [Source: [StatePark.com](https://stateparks.com/padre_island_national_seashore_in_texas.html), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Yarborough Pass Fishing** (3-6 hours) - Remote spot accessible only by 4WD vehicle, favorite among anglers for excellent redfish, trout, and flounder fishing; located on south end of island beyond Mile 5; requires beach driving permit; accessible 24 hours but 4WD essential; 20420 Park Road 22, Corpus Christi, TX 78418 [Source: [TouristSecrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/texas/secrets-of-texass-padre-island-national-seashore-barrier-islands/)]

- **Big Shell Beach** (2-4 hours) - Popular spot for beach camping and fishing known for abundance of shells and more remote feel; accessible by 4WD beyond Mile 5 on South Beach; ideal for solitude seekers; requires beach driving permit; accessible 24 hours; 20420 Park Road 22, Corpus Christi, TX 78418 [Source: [TouristSecrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/texas/secrets-of-texass-padre-island-national-seashore-barrier-islands/)]

- **Remote Beach Camping** (overnight) - Free backcountry camping 5-60 miles down South Beach; requires 4WD and free permit; increasingly remote and pristine the further south you travel; experience solitude and dark skies far from development; self-service registration at beach entrance; may close during coastal flooding; accessible 24 hours [Source: [Travel.com](https://travel.com/regions/north-america/united-states/texas/padre-island-national-seashore-texas-best-things-to-do-top-picks/), [NPS Camping](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/camping.htm)]

- **Stargazing** (1-3 hours) - Experience amazing night skies thanks to remote location and minimal light pollution; ranger-led star parties offered periodically (check schedule); best viewing from remote beach camping areas; accessible 24 hours, star parties scheduled through visitor center; 20420 Park Road 22, Corpus Christi, TX 78418; (361) 949-8068 [Source: [TouristSecrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/texas/secrets-of-texass-padre-island-national-seashore-barrier-islands/), [NPS Ranger Programs](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/ranger-led-programs.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Texas State Aquarium** (2-3 hours, 15 minutes from park) - Explore over 300 species of marine life including dolphins, sharks, and rays from the Gulf of Mexico; daily presentation schedule with talks at different exhibits; cashless payment system; 2710 N Shoreline Blvd, Corpus Christi, TX 78402 (27.8136, -97.3905); Daily 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [Texas State Aquarium](https://www.texasstateaquarium.org/visit/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60927-d106061-Reviews-Texas_State_Aquarium-Corpus_Christi_Texas.html)]

- **USS Lexington Museum** (2-4 hours, 15 minutes from park) - Tour this 900-foot WWII aircraft carrier known as the "Blue Ghost" with 16 decks, military artifacts, and over 20 aircraft on display; includes self-guided tours, flight simulator, escape rooms, and 3D theater; parking $5 (free for members); 2914 N Shoreline Blvd, Corpus Christi, TX 78402 (27.8158, -97.3892); Memorial Day-Labor Day: Daily 9 AM-6 PM; Labor Day-Memorial Day: Daily 9 AM-5 PM; closed Thanksgiving and Christmas [Source: [USS Lexington](https://usslexington.com/visit/hours-admission/), [Visit Corpus Christi](https://www.visitcorpuschristi.com/listing/uss-lexington-museum/416/)]

- **Mustang Island State Park** (2-4 hours, 30 miles north) - 3,954-acre park with 5-mile beachfront offering hiking/biking trails, camping, exceptional birding (over 400 species in area), and extensive fishing; includes Mustang Island Paddle Park Trail with three paddling trail segments totaling over 20 miles; 17047 State Highway 361, Port Aransas, TX 78373 (27.6485, -97.1647); Daily 8:00 AM-5:00 PM; $5 entry fee for adults (children 12 and under free) [Source: [Texas Parks & Wildlife](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/mustang-island), [Port Aransas](https://www.portaransas.org/things-to-do/nature-and-parks/mustang-island-state-park/)]

- **Port Aransas** (45 minutes north) - Charming beach community known as "Fishing Capital of Texas" offering deep-sea fishing charters, dolphin watching tours, beach activities, and coastal dining; ferry access from mainland; approximately 30 miles north of national seashore on Mustang Island; Port Aransas, TX 78373; varies by business [Source: [Port Aransas](https://www.portaransas.org/), [NPS Nearby Attractions](https://www.nps.gov/pais/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm)]

- **Selena Memorial (Mirador De La Flor)** (30 minutes, downtown Corpus Christi) - Life-sized bronze statue of late Tejano singer Selena Quintanilla-Perez (1971-1995) draws estimated 30,000 visitors annually; waterfront location with bay views; 600 N Shoreline Blvd, Corpus Christi, TX 78401 (27.8078, -97.3931); Open 24 hours [Source: [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/usa/texas/padre-island-national-seashore/attractions/padre-island-national-seashore/a/poi-sig/1123919/1318229)]
