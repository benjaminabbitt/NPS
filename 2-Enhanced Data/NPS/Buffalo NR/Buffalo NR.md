# Buffalo NR (National River)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-tyler-bend-visitor-center"></a>**Tyler Bend Visitor Center** (170 Ranger Road, St. Joe, AR 72675 (36.0097, -92.7447); 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM daily, closed federal holidays except Memorial Day, Independence Day, and Labor Day; 870-439-2502) [Source: [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/258701), [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/buff/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]
- <a id="stamp-buffalo-point-ranger-station"></a>**Buffalo Point Ranger Station** (Buffalo Point, AR (36.0667, -91.9333); 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM daily as staffing permits; 870-449-4311) [Source: [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/buff/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

**Note:** Call ahead to confirm stations are open.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-float-the-buffalo-river"></a>**Float the Buffalo River** (2 hours to multi-day) - Paddle America's first National River through 135 miles; March-June best for upper river, year-round for middle/lower sections [Source: [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/buff/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Arkansas.com](https://www.arkansas.com/articles/your-spring-break-guide-buffalo-national-river)]

- <a id="activity-hike-to-whitaker-point-hawksbill-crag"></a>**Hike to Whitaker Point/Hawksbill Crag** (3 hours) - Trek to Arkansas' most-photographed spot; 2.5-mile round-trip [Source: [Arkansas.com](https://www.arkansas.com/articles/discover-crown-jewel-buffalo-national-river)]

- <a id="activity-explore-lost-valley-trail"></a>**Explore Lost Valley Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - Walk 2.2 miles to Eden Falls Cave; best September-April [Source: [Arkansas.com](https://www.arkansas.com/articles/discover-crown-jewel-buffalo-national-river)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-hike-big-bluff-goat-trail"></a>**Hike Big Bluff/Goat Trail** (4-5 hours) - Strenuous 6-mile roundtrip with 350-foot views [Source: [Arkansas.com](https://www.arkansas.com/articles/discover-crown-jewel-buffalo-national-river)]
- <a id="gem-boxley-valley-elk-viewing"></a>**Boxley Valley Elk Viewing** (1-2 hours) - View restored elk herd at dawn/dusk [Source: [Buffalo River Outfitters](https://www.buffaloriveroutfitters.com/blog/page-10/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-jasper-ar"></a>**Jasper, AR** (30 minutes) - Gateway town with shops and services
- <a id="nearby-marshall-leslie"></a>**Marshall & Leslie** (10-20 minutes) - Small towns with local amenities [Source: [Buffalo River](https://buffaloriver.org/river-lodging/surrounding-cities/)]
