# Boston African American NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-faneuil-hall-visitor-center"></a>**Faneuil Hall Visitor Center** (1 Faneuil Hall Square, Boston, MA 02109; 42.3600, -71.0562; 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, check website for specific days; Phone: 617-429-6760) - Boston NHP visitor center with passport stamps for Boston African American NHS. Located on first floor of historic Faneuil Hall. [Source: [NPS Boston African American](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="stamp-museum-of-african-american-history"></a>**Museum of African American History** (46 Joy Street, Boston, MA 02114; 42.3591, -71.0673; Check website for current hours; Phone: 617-725-0022) - Museum in the historic Abiel Smith School building with additional stamps and exhibits. Admission charged for museum. [Source: [NPS Boston African American](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

**Note:** Call 617-429-6760 (Dial A Ranger, 10am-5pm daily) for current hours and tour availability.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-black-heritage-trail"></a>**Black Heritage Trail®** (1.5-2.5 hours) - 1.6-mile walking trail through Beacon Hill linking 15 sites that tell the story of Boston's free Black community from the late 1700s through the 1800s. Self-guided or ranger-led tours available. Free admission. Downtown Boston. [Source: [NPS Boston African American](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-ranger-led-walking-tours"></a>**Ranger-Led Walking Tours** (1.5-2 hours) - Guided tours of the Black Heritage Trail offered Wednesday-Sunday at 10am and 1pm (2025 schedule). Advance registration available on Eventbrite. Free. Meet at Robert Gould Shaw and the 54th Regiment Memorial. [Source: [NPS Boston African American](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-african-meeting-house"></a>**African Meeting House** (45-60 minutes) - Oldest surviving Black church building in the United States (1806). Site of abolitionist speeches by Frederick Douglass and William Lloyd Garrison. 8 Smith Court, Beacon Hill. Part of Museum of African American History; admission fee. [Source: [NPS Boston African American](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-abiel-smith-school"></a>**Abiel Smith School** (45-60 minutes) - First public school for Black children in the nation (1835). Now houses the Museum of African American History with exhibits on Boston's Black community. 46 Joy Street. Adults $15, Youth $8, Seniors $8. [Source: [NPS Boston African American](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-robert-gould-shaw-and-54th-regiment-memorial"></a>**Robert Gould Shaw and 54th Regiment Memorial** (15-30 minutes) - Bronze relief sculpture by Augustus Saint-Gaudens honoring the first Black regiment recruited in the North during the Civil War. Across from State House on Boston Common. Free. [Source: [NPS Boston African American](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-self-guided-tour-via-nps-app"></a>**Self-Guided Tour via NPS App** (1.5-2 hours) - Download the NPS App for self-guided audio tour of the Black Heritage Trail. Explore at your own pace with detailed site information. Available anytime. [Source: [NPS Boston African American](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-lewis-and-harriet-hayden-house-site"></a>**Lewis and Harriet Hayden House Site** (10-15 minutes) - Stop on the Black Heritage Trail marking the home of fugitive slaves who became prominent abolitionists and housed other freedom seekers on the Underground Railroad. 66 Phillips Street (exterior only). [Source: [NPS Boston African American](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/index.htm)]

- <a id="gem-george-middleton-house"></a>**George Middleton House** (10-15 minutes) - Oldest surviving residence on Beacon Hill built by African Americans (c. 1787). Middleton was a Revolutionary War veteran who commanded the Bucks of America. 5 Pinckney Street (exterior only). [Source: [NPS Boston African American](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/index.htm)]

- <a id="gem-coburn-s-gaming-house-site"></a>**Coburn's Gaming House Site** (10-15 minutes) - Site of John P. Coburn's home and gaming establishment, a gathering place for Boston's Black community. Coburn was a major Underground Railroad conductor. 2 Phillips Street. [Source: [NPS Boston African American](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/index.htm)]

- <a id="gem-smith-court-residences"></a>**Smith Court Residences** (15-20 minutes) - Row of historic residences on Smith Court that formed the heart of Boston's 19th-century Black community. Context for understanding the African Meeting House. [Source: [NPS Boston African American](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/index.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-boston-nhp-freedom-trail"></a>**Boston NHP - Freedom Trail** (2-4 hours, overlapping) - 2.5-mile trail through Revolutionary War sites including Paul Revere House, Old North Church, and Bunker Hill. Freedom Trail and Black Heritage Trail can be combined. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/index.htm)]

- <a id="nearby-massachusetts-state-house"></a>**Massachusetts State House** (30-60 minutes, adjacent) - Golden-domed seat of state government with tours. Beacon Hill. Free tours. [Source: Visit Boston]

- <a id="nearby-boston-common-and-public-garden"></a>**Boston Common and Public Garden** (30-60 minutes, adjacent) - America's oldest public park (1634) with swan boats and gardens. Adjacent to Beacon Hill. [Source: Visit Boston]

- <a id="nearby-museum-of-fine-arts"></a>**Museum of Fine Arts** (2-4 hours, 2 miles) - World-class art museum with American, European, Asian, and African art collections. 465 Huntington Avenue. Admission charged. [Source: MFA Boston]

## Total Recommended Time

**Total recommended visit time for Boston African American NHS: 2-4 hours**

Allow 2 hours for a ranger-led walking tour of the Black Heritage Trail. Add an hour for museum exhibits at the Abiel Smith School and African Meeting House. The trail winds through Beacon Hill's historic streets and pairs well with the Freedom Trail for a full day exploring Boston's history.

[Sources: [NPS Boston African American](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/index.htm), [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/planyourvisit/index.htm)]
