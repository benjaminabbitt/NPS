# Boston African American NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Faneuil Hall Visitor Center** (1 Faneuil Hall Square, Boston, MA 02109 [42.3600335, -71.0562341]; Winter Hours Oct 28 - May 26: Sun 10am-5pm, Mon-Tue Closed, Wed 10am-5pm, Thu 12pm-5pm, Fri-Sat 10am-5pm; 617-242-5642) - Stamps are located near the visitor center desk on the first floor; this is the main visitor center for both Boston National Historical Park and Boston African American National Historic Site [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/planyourvisit/passport-stamps.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/visit-historic-faneuil-hall.htm)]

**Note:** Call ahead at 617-242-5642 to confirm current stamp availability before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Black Heritage Trail Ranger-Guided Tour** (90 minutes) - Free guided walking tour led by National Park Service rangers exploring the rich history of Boston's 19th-century Black community on Beacon Hill; tour covers approximately 1.4 miles and visits 14 historic sites including the Robert Gould Shaw Memorial, George Middleton House, Lewis and Harriet Hayden House, Smith Court Residences, African Meeting House, and Abiel Smith School; starts at Massachusetts 54th Regiment Memorial across from Massachusetts State House, ends at Museum of African American History; tours run May 24 - October 13 on Wed-Sun at 10am and 1pm; advance registration required via Eventbrite; best for ages 8+; 617-429-6760 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/black-heritage-trail-tour.htm), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60745-d106839-Reviews-Black_Heritage_Trail-Boston_Massachusetts.html), [Source](https://www.meetboston.com/things-to-do/sightseeing-and-tours/walking-tours/black-heritage-trail/)]

- **Black Heritage Trail Self-Guided Tour** (60 minutes) - Walk the trail at your own pace any day of the week; maps and brochures available at Abiel Smith School (46 Joy Street, Boston, MA 02114 [42.358, -71.068]) and Faneuil Hall Visitor Center during operating hours; virtual tour and NPS mobile app also available; features sharp inclines and uneven sidewalks [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60745-d106839-Reviews-Black_Heritage_Trail-Boston_Massachusetts.html)]

- **Museum of African American History** (60-90 minutes) - Visit the oldest Black church building in the United States (African Meeting House, built 1806) and the first public school for Black children in the nation (Abiel Smith School, 1835); exhibits include Frederick Douglass's dress uniform from Robert Gould Shaw, original pamphlets from the 1808 celebration of slave trade abolition, and guided tours of the African Meeting House; 46 Joy Street, Boston, MA 02114 [42.358, -71.068]; Tue-Sun 10am-4pm, closed Mon and major holidays; timed ticketing with entry at 10am, 12pm, or 2pm; admission fees apply (National Park Passes not accepted as museum is privately operated); 617-725-0022 [[Source](https://www.maah.org/boston-location), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://whichmuseum.com/museum/museum-of-african-american-history-boston-9093/tickets-prices)]

- **Robert Gould Shaw and 54th Massachusetts Regiment Memorial Viewing** (15-30 minutes) - View the bronze relief sculpture honoring Colonel Robert Gould Shaw and the Massachusetts 54th Regiment, the first African American regiment recruited in the North during the Civil War; created by Augustus Saint-Gaudens and dedicated in 1897; located at 24 Beacon Street, Boston, MA 02133 [42.3587, -71.0636], across from Massachusetts State House at edge of Boston Common; accessible 24/7 as outdoor monument [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/places/robert-gould-shaw-and-massachusetts-54th-regiment-memorial.htm), [Source](https://stepboston.com/robert-gould-shaw-memorial/), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60745-d15554676-Reviews-Robert_Gould_Shaw_and_the_54th_Regiment_Memorial-Boston_Massachusetts.html)]

- **Women of Beacon Hill Audio Tour** (60 minutes) - Self-guided audio tour exploring the contributions of women in Boston's 19th-century African American community; available through NPS website and mobile app [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/index.htm)]

- **Underground Railroad in Boston Audio Tour** (60 minutes) - Self-guided audio tour tracing Underground Railroad routes and safe houses in Boston's Beacon Hill neighborhood; available through NPS website and mobile app [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/index.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Lewis and Harriet Hayden House Exterior Viewing** (10-15 minutes) - View the exterior of Boston's preeminent Underground Railroad safe house from the 1850s where escaped slaves including Ellen and William Craft were protected; Lewis Hayden was an escaped slave who became a leading abolitionist and conductor on the Underground Railroad; 66 Phillips Street, Boston, MA 02114 [42.3584, -71.0697]; private residence, not open to the public but viewable from street as part of Black Heritage Trail [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/places/lewis-and-harriet-hayden-house.htm), [Source](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/lewis-and-harriet-hayden-house-boston-underground-railroad)]

- **George Middleton House Exterior Viewing** (10-15 minutes) - View the oldest extant home on Beacon Hill, built 1787 by George Middleton who led the Bucks of America, a Black militia during the American Revolution, and helped found the Free African Society; 5 Pinckney Street, Boston, MA 02114 [42.3582, -71.0689]; private residence, not open to the public but viewable from street as part of Black Heritage Trail [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/places/george-middleton-house.htm), [Source](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/george-middleton-house)]

- **Smith Court Residences Viewing** (15-20 minutes) - View the cluster of homes at 2-7 Smith Court that typified housing for Black Bostonians in the 1800s; these six homes housed multiple families and boarders including notable residents William Cooper Nell (first Black person to hold a federal position) and James Scott; the wooden houses (3, 5, 7, and 7A) date from the early 1800s and are among the few wooden structures remaining on Beacon Hill; Smith Court, Boston, MA 02114 [42.358, -71.068]; private residences viewable from street, located adjacent to African Meeting House at 46 Joy Street [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/places/smith-court-residences.htm), [Source](https://www.moontravelguides.com/travel/trip-ideas/boston-black-heritage-trail/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Boston Freedom Trail** (2-3 hours, 2.5 miles) - Walk Boston's iconic red-brick trail connecting 16 nationally significant historic sites from the American Revolution including Boston Common, Massachusetts State House, Park Street Church, Granary Burying Ground, King's Chapel, Benjamin Franklin Statue, Old Corner Bookstore, Old South Meeting House, Old State House, Boston Massacre Site, Faneuil Hall, Paul Revere House, Old North Church, Copp's Hill Burying Ground, USS Constitution, and Bunker Hill Monument; trail winds from downtown Boston through North End to Charlestown; self-guided or ranger-led tours available; starts at Boston Common Visitor Center, 139 Tremont Street, Boston, MA 02111 [42.3554, -71.0640]; accessible 24/7 for self-guided walks; free ranger-led tours available seasonally [[Source](https://www.thefreedomtrail.org/), [Source](https://www.trolleytours.com/boston/freedom-trail)]

- **Bunker Hill Monument** (30 minutes, 2 miles from Beacon Hill) - Climb 294 steps to the top of the 221-foot granite obelisk commemorating the Battle of Bunker Hill, one of the first major battles of the American Revolution; includes small museum at base; 43 Monument Square, Charlestown, MA 02129 [42.3768, -71.0607]; daily 10am-5pm (summer Jul-Aug 9am-6pm), last climb 4:30pm (summer 5:30pm), closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day; free admission; 617-242-5641 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bost/learn/historyculture/bhm.htm), [Source](https://www.bostonteapartyship.com/boston-attractions/bunker-hill-monument), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60745-d133763-Reviews-Bunker_Hill_Monument-Boston_Massachusetts.html)]

- **Boston Tea Party Ships & Museum** (60-75 minutes, 1.5 miles from Beacon Hill) - Interactive museum featuring costumed interpreters, full-scale replica 18th-century ships, and hands-on tea-dumping reenactment; one-hour guided tour with multimedia exhibits about the famous 1773 protest; 306 Congress Street, Boston, MA 02210 [42.3520, -71.0515]; Apr-Nov 10am-5pm, Nov-Mar 10am-4pm, closed Thanksgiving and Christmas; advance timed tickets required, tours every 15 minutes; adults $29.95, children $21.95 (save $1.50 buying online); check in 15-5 minutes before tour time [[Source](https://www.bostonteapartyship.com/), [Source](https://www.bostonteapartyship.com/faq), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60745-d103645-Reviews-Boston_Tea_Party_Ships_Museum-Boston_Massachusetts.html)]

- **Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum** (2-3 hours, 2.5 miles from Beacon Hill) - Venetian-style palace museum featuring Isabella Gardner's eclectic art collection in intimate galleries surrounding a stunning courtyard garden; original home was in Beacon Hill before she built this larger museum; 25 Evans Way, Boston, MA 02115 [42.3381, -71.0992]; Mon 11am-5pm, Tue closed, Wed 11am-5pm, Thu 11am-9pm, Fri 11am-5pm, Sat-Sun 10am-5pm; adults $20, seniors 65+ $18, students $13, under 18 free [[Source](https://www.gardnermuseum.org/), [Source](https://www.gardnermuseum.org/visit/hours), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60745-d108823-Reviews-Isabella_Stewart_Gardner_Museum-Boston_Massachusetts.html)]

- **Museum of Fine Arts Boston** (3-4 hours, 2.5 miles from Beacon Hill) - Comprehensive art museum with world-class collections spanning ancient Egyptian artifacts to contemporary art; one of the most comprehensive art museums in the United States; 465 Huntington Avenue, Boston, MA 02115 [42.3394, -71.0948]; Thu/Sat/Sun/Mon 10am-5pm, Fri 10am-10pm, closed Tue-Wed; adults $27, ages 7-17 $10, 6 and under free [[Source](https://www.mfa.org/visit), [Source](https://www.visit-massachusetts.com/boston/museums-and-galleries/)]
