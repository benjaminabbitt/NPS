## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center Exhibits and Film** (20-30 minutes) - The visitor center features exhibits and an audiovisual presentation titled "Measure of a Man" that orients visitors to the life of Booker T. Washington, detailing his journey from enslavement to becoming an influential educator and leader; 12130 Booker T Washington Highway, Hardy, VA 24101 [37.119434, -79.731651]; Wednesday-Sunday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/bowa/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Visit Roanoke VA](https://www.visitroanokeva.com/listings/booker-t-washington-national-monument/5609/), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/bowa/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- **Plantation Trail Walk** (30-60 minutes) - A quarter-mile paved loop trail through the historic area passes by reconstructions of nineteenth-century farm buildings similar to those that stood on the Burroughs Plantation when Booker T. Washington lived here as an enslaved child, including a tobacco barn (on National Register of Historic Places, built c. 1896), slave cabin reconstruction, and outline of the former plantation house; 12130 Booker T Washington Highway, Hardy, VA 24101 [37.119434, -79.731651]; Grounds open 9:00 AM-5:00 PM year-round [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/bowa/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [TripAdvisor Review](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g60744-d124639-r269824102-Booker_T_Washington_National_Monument-Hardy_Virginia.html), [America's Best History](https://americasbesthistory.com/spotlight2021-3.html)]

- **Farm Area Visit** (15-20 minutes) - Interactive farm area features heritage breed animals including sheep, pigs, horses, and chickens that represent the types of livestock present on 1850s plantations, providing hands-on glimpses into nineteenth-century farm life; 12130 Booker T Washington Highway, Hardy, VA 24101 [37.119434, -79.731651]; Grounds open 9:00 AM-5:00 PM year-round [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/bowa/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Charlottesville Family](https://charlottesvillefamily.com/booker-t-washington-national-monument/)]

- **Historical Garden Area** (10-15 minutes) - Garden area demonstrates subsistence gardening techniques used in the 1850s, with period-appropriate plantings including dark tobacco visible during summer and fall months; 12130 Booker T Washington Highway, Hardy, VA 24101 [37.119434, -79.731651]; Grounds open 9:00 AM-5:00 PM year-round [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/bowa/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [America's Best History](https://americasbesthistory.com/spotlight2021-3.html)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (varies, typically 1-2 hours) - Youth program where participants explore the birthplace of Booker T. Washington, hike the Jack-O-Lantern Branch Heritage Trail, visit the farm area, learn about tobacco cultivation, and discover the history of the Tuskegee Institute and Civil War era; 12130 Booker T Washington Highway, Hardy, VA 24101 [37.119434, -79.731651]; Wednesday-Sunday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM [Source: [Charlottesville Family](https://charlottesvillefamily.com/booker-t-washington-national-monument/), [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/bowa/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Jack-O-Lantern Branch Heritage Trail** (45 minutes) - A 1.5-mile meandering loop trail of medium difficulty through fields and forests along the streambed of Jack-O-Lantern Branch, a natural boundary with neighboring property where young Booker T. Washington spent time; the trail features a few short, steep sections and trail guides are available at the visitor center; 12130 Booker T Washington Highway, Hardy, VA 24101 [37.119434, -79.731651]; Grounds open 9:00 AM-5:00 PM year-round [Source: [NPS Jack-O-Lantern Trail](https://www.nps.gov/places/jack-o-lantern-branch-trail.htm), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/bowa/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [HMDB Historical Marker](https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=137982)]

- **Living History Demonstrations** (30-60 minutes when available) - On special days throughout the year, living history interpreters demonstrate activities indigenous to the 1850s era and location, providing immersive experiences into plantation life; availability varies and is not a daily occurrence; 12130 Booker T Washington Highway, Hardy, VA 24101 [37.119434, -79.731651]; Check with park at 540-682-0173 for scheduled dates [Source: [America's Best History](https://americasbesthistory.com/spotlight2021-3.html), [NPS Getaway](https://www.nps.gov/articles/getaway-bowa.htm)]

- **Birdwatching Opportunities** (30-90 minutes) - The monument's diverse habitats support various bird species including less common species such as prairie warbler, blue grosbeak, and orchard oriole; during migration seasons, visitors may observe warblers, vireos, and tanagers along the Jack-O-Lantern Branch Heritage Trail and throughout the grounds; 12130 Booker T Washington Highway, Hardy, VA 24101 [37.119434, -79.731651]; Grounds open 9:00 AM-5:00 PM year-round [Source: [Virginia DWR](https://dwr.virginia.gov/vbwt/sites/booker-t-washington-national-monument/)]

- **Picnic Area** (variable) - Wooded picnic area with tables, trash receptacles, water fountains, and nearby restrooms provides a peaceful setting for lunch or rest; 12130 Booker T Washington Highway, Hardy, VA 24101 [37.119434, -79.731651]; Grounds open 9:00 AM-5:00 PM year-round [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/bowa/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Smith Mountain Lake State Park** (half-day to full-day, 20 miles/25 minutes southwest) - Virginia's second-largest lake offers 500-foot guarded beach (Memorial Day-Labor Day), boat rentals (tritoon boats, ski boats, jetskis, kayaks, paddleboards), 13 hiking trails (0.5-3 miles), fishing for striped bass and largemouth bass, camping, cabins, visitor center, and amphitheater programs; 1235 State Park Road, Huddleston, VA 24104 [37.079625, -79.610993]; Beach and boat rentals Memorial Day-Labor Day, trails open year-round during daylight hours [Source: [Virginia State Parks](https://www.dcr.virginia.gov/state-parks/smith-mountain-lake), [Visit Roanoke VA](https://www.visitroanokeva.com/listings/smith-mountain-lake-state-park/7955/), [Smith Mountain Lake Guide](https://smith-mountain-lake.com/things-to-do/)]

- **Mill Mountain Star and Park** (1-2 hours, 20 miles/25 minutes northwest) - Home to the world's largest free-standing illuminated star (88.5 feet tall, lit 7 AM-midnight daily), featuring two scenic overlooks with 20 and 60-mile vistas across Roanoke, 10 miles of multi-use hiking and mountain biking trails, Mill Mountain Zoo, Discovery Center, 2.5-acre wildflower garden, and picnic areas; 2198 Mill Mountain Spur, Roanoke, VA 24014 [37.2508, -79.9324]; Park open 7 AM-11 PM daily [Source: [Visit Roanoke VA](https://www.visitroanokeva.com/things-to-do/attractions/roanoke-star/), [Play Roanoke](https://www.playroanoke.com/parks-facilities/mill-mountain-park/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g58134-d279566-Reviews-Mill_Mountain_Star_Park-Roanoke_Virginia.html)]

- **Explore Park at Blue Ridge Parkway** (2-4 hours, 25 miles/30 minutes northwest) - 1,200-acre outdoor recreation park at Blue Ridge Parkway Milepost 115 featuring 14+ miles of hiking and mountain biking trails, Tree Top Quest aerial adventure course with zip-lines (ages 4+), Roanoke River access for fishing, canoeing and kayaking, historic buildings from 1800s-1900s Southwest Virginia, cabins and camping, Twin Creeks Brewpub, and Blue Ridge Parkway Visitor Center; 3900 Rutrough Road/56 Roanoke River Parkway Road, Roanoke, VA 24014 [37.2383, -79.8520]; Visitor Center: Friday-Sunday 11 AM-5 PM (winter), seasonal hours April-October; 540-427-1800 [Source: [Roanoke County Parks](https://www.roanokecountyparks.com/231/Explore-Park), [Blue Ridge Parkway](https://www.blueridgeparkway.org/poi/explore-park/), [Visit Roanoke VA](https://www.visitroanokeva.com/listings/explore-park/6614/)]

- **Blue Ridge Parkway** (variable, 25 miles/30 minutes west) - America's most scenic drive connects Great Smoky Mountains and Shenandoah National Parks with 469 miles of stunning mountain vistas, hiking trails, historic sites, and overlooks; nearest access at Milepost 115 (Explore Park) and Milepost 120 (Mill Mountain); open year-round with seasonal closures possible due to weather [Source: [Visit Roanoke VA](https://www.visitroanokeva.com/things-to-do/blue-ridge-parkway/), [Blue Ridge Parkway Association](https://www.blueridgeparkway.org/poi/explore-park/)]
