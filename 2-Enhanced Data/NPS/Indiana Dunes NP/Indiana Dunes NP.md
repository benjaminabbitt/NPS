# Indiana Dunes NP (National Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Indiana Dunes Visitor Center** (1215 N. State Road 49, Porter, IN 46304 (41.6122, -87.0472); Daily 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM CT Memorial Day weekend-Labor Day weekend, 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM CT remainder of year, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 219-395-1882) - National Park Service passport cancellation stamps available at visitor center bookstore. [Source: [Indiana Dunes Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/indu/planyourvisit/idnlvc.htm), [Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/indu/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [South Shore CVA](https://www.southshorecva.com/listing/indiana-dunes-national-park-visitor-center/1582/)]

**Note:** Call 219-395-1882 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. Indiana's newest National Park (designated 2019), 15 miles of Lake Michigan shoreline featuring towering sand dunes.

## Key Activities

- **West Beach Swimming and Sunbathing** (2-4 hours) - Swim, sunbathe, and picnic at most visited beach in national park with soft sand, seasonal lifeguards (Memorial Day-Labor Day), and beautiful Lake Michigan views; parking lot has seasonal restrooms; 376 N County Line Road, Gary, IN 46403 (41.6132309, -87.2226319); daily dawn to dusk; parking fee applies [Source: [Earth Trekkers Best Things](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-things-to-do-in-indiana-dunes/), [Dunes Beaches](https://www.visitindiana.com/blog/post/dunes-101-where-are-the-indiana-dunes-beaches/), [Hiking Guide](https://www.nps.gov/indu/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)]

- **Dunes Succession Trail at West Beach** (1-1.5 hours) - Climb 0.9-mile moderate trail featuring challenging ascent of approximately 250-270 wooden stairs to summit showcasing ecological succession from beach to forest; one of most popular hikes in park; accessed from West Beach parking; daily dawn to dusk; parking fee applies [Source: [West Beach Trails](https://www.nps.gov/indu/planyourvisit/wb16.htm), [Best Hikes Lost with Lydia](https://www.lostwithlydia.com/destinations/usa/in/Best-Hikes-in-Indiana-Dunes/), [One Day at Indiana Dunes](https://www.dangerous-business.com/one-day-at-indiana-dunes-national-park/)]

- **3 Dune Challenge** (1.5-2 hours) - Hike 1.5-mile trail climbing the three tallest dunes on Lake Michigan lakeshore (at Indiana Dunes State Park adjacent to National Park); rugged Trail 8 goes over tops of highest dunes; strenuous difficulty; accessed from State Park; State Park daily hours; State Park entrance fee [Source: [One Day Indiana Dunes](https://www.dangerous-business.com/one-day-at-indiana-dunes-national-park/), [Lost with Lydia Hikes](https://www.lostwithlydia.com/destinations/usa/in/Best-Hikes-in-Indiana-Dunes/)]

- **Cowles Bog Trail** (2.5-3 hours) - Hike AllTrails' #1 trail in Indiana covering 4.7 miles (moderate difficulty, 202 feet elevation gain) through National Natural Landmark exploring ponds, marshes, swamps, black oak savannas; features lovely raised boardwalk over wetlands; ends at secluded Lake Michigan beach; steep sand dunes make finale strenuous; multiple trailheads; daily dawn to dusk; FREE [Source: [Cowles Bog NPS](https://www.nps.gov/indu/planyourvisit/cb16.htm), [Indiana Dunes Cowles Bog](https://www.indianadunes.com/explore-the-dunes/experiences/discovery-trail/discover-cowles-bog/), [5 Incredible Trails](https://anappleaplane.com/indiana-dunes-trails/)]

- **Porter Beach Sunset Viewing** (1-2 hours) - Enjoy soft sand beach popular for spectacular Lake Michigan sunsets; visitors climb small back dune as sun sets; no lifeguards but seasonal restrooms at parking lot; within walking distance to State Park bathhouse; accessed from Waverly Road; daily dawn to dusk; FREE [Source: [Dunes Beaches](https://www.visitindiana.com/blog/post/dunes-101-where-are-the-indiana-dunes-beaches/), [One Day Indiana Dunes](https://www.dangerous-business.com/one-day-at-indiana-dunes-national-park/)]

- **Paul H. Douglas Trail** (2-2.5 hours) - Hike 3.4-mile moderate out-and-back trail through wetlands and savanna-covered dunes; less crowded alternative to more popular trails; accessed from parking areas; daily dawn to dusk; FREE [Source: [Lost with Lydia Hikes](https://www.lostwithlydia.com/destinations/usa/in/Best-Hikes-in-Indiana-Dunes/), [Hiking Guide](https://www.nps.gov/indu/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)]

- **West Beach Dune Climbing** (30-60 minutes) - Climb designated dune climbing area at West Beach; experience unique challenge of scaling loose sand dunes; West Beach parking area; daily dawn to dusk; parking fee applies [Source: [Lost with Lydia Hikes](https://www.lostwithlydia.com/destinations/usa/in/Best-Hikes-in-Indiana-Dunes/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Hike-To Beaches** (2-3 hours) - Discover secluded "undiscovered" Lake Michigan beaches accessible only by hiking trails where you can enjoy waves and breeze with beach almost to yourself; includes Cowles Bog beach terminus and other trail-accessed beaches; various trailheads; daily dawn to dusk; FREE [Source: [South Shore Hike-To Beaches](https://www.southshorecva.com/things-to-do/beaches/hike-to-beaches/)]

- **Tolleston Dunes Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - Hike 2.9-mile moderate loop through quieter section of park featuring diverse dune ecosystems; less crowded than main trails; accessed from parking areas; daily dawn to dusk; FREE [Source: [Lost with Lydia Hikes](https://www.lostwithlydia.com/destinations/usa/in/Best-Hikes-in-Indiana-Dunes/), [Hiking Guide](https://www.nps.gov/indu/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)]

- **Portage Lakefront & Riverwalk** (1-2 hours) - Walk beach and pavilion area offering different perspective on Lake Michigan with views of Chicago skyline; includes riverwalk along trail; Portage, IN; daily dawn to dusk; FREE [Source: [Dunes Beaches](https://www.visitindiana.com/blog/post/dunes-101-where-are-the-indiana-dunes-beaches/)]

- **Mount Baldy Viewing** (30-45 minutes) - View (but don't climb) the largest living dune in national park with peak 126 feet above Lake Michigan; climbing off-trail prohibited but viewing area accessible; Mount Baldy parking area; seasonal access (closed during migration season); FREE [Source: [Lost with Lydia Hikes](https://www.lostwithlydia.com/destinations/usa/in/Best-Hikes-in-Indiana-Dunes/), [National Park Info](https://www.national-park.com/indiana-dunes-national-park/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Chicago, Illinois** (1-2 hours drive, 50 miles/1 hour west) - Visit nearby major city featuring world-class museums, architecture, dining, and entertainment; Indiana Dunes is one of best places to hike near Chicago making it Chicago's "best kept secret"; Chicago, IL; hours vary by attraction [Source: [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-things-to-do-in-indiana-dunes/), [Indiana Dunes Official](https://www.indianadunes.com/explore-the-dunes/plan-your-visit/faq/)]

- **Indiana Dunes State Park** (adjacent to National Park) - Visit adjacent state park featuring 3 Dune Challenge, additional beaches with lifeguards, nature center, and camping; 1600 N 25 E, Chesterton, IN 46304; daily hours vary seasonally; state park entrance fee separate from National Park [Source: [DNR State Parks](https://www.in.gov/dnr/state-parks/parks-lakes/indiana-dunes-state-park/), [Indiana's Cool North](https://www.indianascoolnorth.com/things-to-do/attractions/indiana-dunes-national-park-and-state-park/)]

- **South Shore Arts and Dining** (varies, throughout South Shore region) - Explore South Shore region's local restaurants, breweries, shops, and cultural attractions in nearby towns of Chesterton, Valparaiso, and Michigan City; various locations; hours vary [Source: [South Shore CVA](https://www.southshorecva.com/listing/indiana-dunes-national-park-visitor-center/1582/)]

**Total Recommended Time at Indiana Dunes NP (National Park):** Minimum 3-4 hours for single beach visit and short hike; full day (6-8 hours) recommended for comprehensive experience including West Beach swimming, Dunes Succession Trail, Cowles Bog Trail, and sunset at Porter Beach; multi-day visit ideal for exploring all 50+ miles of trails and 15 miles of shoreline. Peak season: summer for swimming, fall for hiking.
