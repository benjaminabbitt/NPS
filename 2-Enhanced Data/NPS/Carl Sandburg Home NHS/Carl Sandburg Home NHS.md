# Carl Sandburg Home NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-visitor-center-bookstore"></a>**Visitor Center/Bookstore** (81 Carl Sandburg Lane, Flat Rock, NC 28731 (35.2681, -82.4592); grounds and trails open dawn to dusk daily, barn open 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM daily, visitor center/bookstore open Wednesday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM; GPS use: 1800 Little River Road) [Source: [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/carl/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/carl-sandburg-home-national-historic-site-flat-rock), [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/carl/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** Passport stamps typically available at visitor center/bookstore (open Wed-Sun 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM). Free entry to park, bookstore, trails, and barn; fee charged for house tours. Pedestrian detour in place from main parking lot due to post-Hurricane Helene conditions. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/carl/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-tour-connemara-house"></a>**Tour Connemara House** (30 minutes, plus 30 minutes walking/shuttle) - Experience guided tour through 11 furnished rooms over top two floors of three-story home where Carl Sandburg lived 1945-1967; view Sandburg's study with thousands of original books lining walls; tours by reservation only, arrive 30 minutes early for 15-minute walk or shuttle from parking [Source: [Recreation.gov Tours](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/facility/300005), [Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/carl/planyourvisit/index.htm), [Romantic Asheville](https://www.romanticasheville.com/sandburg.htm)]

- <a id="activity-visit-working-goat-farm-and-barn"></a>**Visit Working Goat Farm and Barn** (30-60 minutes) - Meet descendants of Lilian Sandburg's champion dairy goats at working farm she managed; explore historic barn open daily 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM; free admission; great for children [Source: [Visit NC](https://www.visitnc.com/carl-sandburg-home-national-historic-site), [Romantic Asheville](https://www.romanticasheville.com/sandburg.htm)]

- <a id="activity-hike-5-miles-of-trails"></a>**Hike 5 Miles of Trails** (1-3 hours) - Explore 264-acre estate on moderate to steep terrain trails through woods and around property; grounds open dawn to dusk; free access [Source: [HikeWNC](https://www.hikewnc.info/trailheads/carl-sandburg-home-national-historic-site/), [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/carl/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-attend-special-events"></a>**Attend Special Events** (varies by season) - Participate in Folk Music Festival in May, cheese-making demonstrations, musical events, poetry readings, and performances during summer months; check schedule for current offerings [Source: [Visit Hendersonville](https://visithendersonvillenc.org/businesses/carl-sandburg-home-national-historic-site), [Romantic Asheville](https://www.romanticasheville.com/sandburg.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-explore-post-helene-recovery"></a>**Explore Post-Helene Recovery** (ongoing) - Witness park's resilience and recovery efforts following Hurricane Helene damage; observe restoration work while park remains open with modified access; educational opportunity about natural disaster impact on historic sites [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/carl/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="gem-poetry-and-literature-programs"></a>**Poetry and Literature Programs** (varies) - Attend intimate poetry readings and literary discussions celebrating Sandburg's work as Pulitzer Prize-winning poet, biographer of Abraham Lincoln, and folk music collector; check calendar for scheduled programs [Source: [Visit NC](https://www.visitnc.com/carl-sandburg-home-national-historic-site)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-dupont-state-recreational-forest"></a>**DuPont State Recreational Forest** (2-3 hours, 25 minutes southeast near Brevard) - Hike to spectacular waterfalls including 150-foot High Falls, Triple Falls, and Hooker Falls on 2.2-mile three-waterfall loop; 10,400 acres with nearly 100 miles of trails; open 5:00 AM - 10:00 PM daily; free admission [Source: [Romantic Asheville](https://www.romanticasheville.com/dupont.htm), [Explore Asheville](https://www.exploreasheville.com/things-to-do/outdoors/dupont-state-forest)]

- <a id="nearby-historic-hendersonville"></a>**Historic Hendersonville** (10 minutes, 3 miles north) - Explore charming downtown with antique shops, local restaurants, and mountain town atmosphere; visit during apple harvest season (September-October) for festivals [Source: [Visit Hendersonville](https://visithendersonvillenc.org/businesses/carl-sandburg-home-national-historic-site)]
