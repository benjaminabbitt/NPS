# Blue Ridge Parkway (PKWY)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Blue Ridge Parkway Visitor Center** (199 Hemphill Knob Road, Asheville, NC 28803; Milepost 384; 35.5746, -82.5524; Open year-round except Thanksgiving, Dec 25, Jan 1; Phone: 828-348-3400) - Main visitor center with comprehensive exhibits, films, maps, and bookstore. Only year-round facility on the parkway. [Source: [NPS Blue Ridge Parkway](https://www.nps.gov/blri/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

- **Humpback Rocks Visitor Center** (Milepost 5.8, Virginia; 37.9712, -78.8985; May-October; Phone: 828-348-3400) - Mountain homestead exhibits and cultural demonstrations. Living history farm nearby. [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/blri/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

- **Peaks of Otter Visitor Center** (Milepost 85.6, Virginia; 37.4445, -79.6064; May-October; Phone: 828-348-3400) - Lake and mountain setting with exhibits on local natural history. [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/blri/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

- **Mabry Mill Visitor Center** (Milepost 176, Virginia; 36.7526, -80.4030; May-October; Phone: 828-348-3400) - Most photographed spot on the parkway with working grist mill and blacksmith shop. [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/blri/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

- **Blue Ridge Music Center** (Milepost 213, Virginia; Seasonal; Phone: 828-348-3400) - Exhibits on Appalachian music heritage with live performances. [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/blri/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

- **Moses Cone Manor and Visitor Center** (Milepost 294, North Carolina; 36.1468, -81.6988; May-October; Phone: 828-348-3400) - Historic manor house with Southern Highland Craft Guild gift shop. [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/blri/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

- **Linville Falls Visitor Center** (Milepost 316.4, North Carolina; 35.9517, -81.9240; May-October; Phone: 828-348-3400) - Gateway to Linville Falls and Linville Gorge hiking. [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/blri/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

- **Craggy Gardens Visitor Center** (Milepost 364.4, North Carolina; 35.6977, -82.3795; May-October; Phone: 828-348-3400) - High-elevation visitor center with spectacular views. [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/blri/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

- **Waterrock Knob Visitor Center** (Milepost 451.2, North Carolina; 35.4597, -83.1412; May-October; Phone: 828-348-3400) - Near the southern end at nearly 6,000 feet elevation. Exceptional sunrise/sunset views. [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/blri/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

**Note:** Most visitor centers are seasonal (May-October). The Asheville visitor center (MP 384) is open year-round. Call 828-348-3400 for current hours. Road sections may be closed due to weather or Hurricane Helene damage.

## Key Activities

- **Scenic Driving** (variable, 469 miles total) - "America's Favorite Drive" connecting Shenandoah National Park (VA) to Great Smoky Mountains National Park (NC). Designed for 45 mph maximum with 200+ overlooks. Allow 2-3 hours per 50-mile section. Year-round (weather/closures permitting). [Source: [NPS Blue Ridge Parkway](https://www.nps.gov/blri/index.htm)]

- **Linville Falls** (1.5-3 hours, Milepost 316.4) - Multiple hiking trails (0.6-2 miles) lead to views of the dramatic waterfall cascading into Linville Gorge, the "Grand Canyon of the East." Easy to moderate difficulty. Linville Falls Visitor Center. [Source: [Visit NC Mileposts](https://www.visitnc.com/itinerary/must-see-mileposts-blue-ridge-parkway)]

- **Rough Ridge** (1-1.5 hours, Milepost 302.8) - Short but steep 0.7-mile round-trip hike to a boardwalk with breathtaking panoramic views. One of the most photographed vistas on the parkway, especially stunning in autumn. [Source: [Blue Ridge Mountain Life](https://blueridgemountainlife.com/blue-ridge-parkway-travel-guide/)]

- **Linn Cove Viaduct** (30-60 minutes, Milepost 304.4) - Engineering marvel: 1,234-foot S-curve bridge built into the side of Grandfather Mountain at 4,100 feet elevation. Visitor center with construction exhibits (seasonal). [Source: [Romantic Asheville](https://www.romanticasheville.com/BlueRidgeParkway.htm)]

- **Craggy Pinnacle Trail** (1.5-2 hours, Milepost 364.1) - Popular 1.5-mile round-trip hike through rhododendron tunnels to 360-degree summit views. Spectacular in June when Catawba rhododendrons bloom. Craggy Gardens area. [Source: [Miles with McConkey](https://www.mileswithmcconkey.com/27-superb-blue-ridge-parkway-overlooks-and-stops/)]

- **Mabry Mill** (30-60 minutes, Milepost 176) - Most photographed location on the parkway. Working waterwheel-powered grist mill with demonstrations, blacksmith shop, and restaurant serving buckwheat pancakes. May-October. [Source: [Road Trips and Coffee](https://www.roadtripsandcoffee.com/visitor-centers-blue-ridge-parkway/)]

- **Folk Art Center** (1-2 hours, Milepost 382) - Southern Highland Craft Guild showcase with daily demonstrations of traditional and contemporary Appalachian crafts. Gallery, gift shop, and craft demonstrations. Year-round. [Source: [Romantic Asheville](https://www.romanticasheville.com/BlueRidgeParkway.htm)]

- **Mount Mitchell** (2-3 hours, exit at Milepost 355) - Highest peak east of the Mississippi (6,684 feet). Short drive off parkway to summit with observation tower, museum, and hiking trails. Mount Mitchell State Park. [Source: [Visit NC Mileposts](https://www.visitnc.com/itinerary/must-see-mileposts-blue-ridge-parkway)]

- **Price Lake** (1-3 hours, Milepost 297) - Rent kayaks, canoes, or paddleboards. Easy 2.7-mile loop trail around the lake. Camping available. Julian Price Park. [Source: [Miles with McConkey](https://www.mileswithmcconkey.com/27-superb-blue-ridge-parkway-overlooks-and-stops/)]

- **Sharp Top Trail at Peaks of Otter** (2-3 hours, Milepost 85.6) - Strenuous 1.5-mile hike (or shuttle bus) to Sharp Top Mountain summit with 360-degree panoramic views. Peaks of Otter Lodge offers lodging and dining. [Source: [Blue Ridge Mountain Life](https://blueridgemountainlife.com/blue-ridge-parkway-travel-guide/)]

- **Graveyard Fields** (1-3 hours, Milepost 418) - High-altitude bog with two waterfalls accessible by hiking trails. Easy 0.6-mile round-trip to Second Falls or longer 6-mile trek to Upper Falls. Popular blueberry picking in late summer. [Source: [Romantic Asheville](https://www.romanticasheville.com/BlueRidgeParkway.htm)]

- **Doughton Park** (2-6 hours, Milepost 241) - Largest recreation area on parkway with nearly 7,000 acres. Meadow trails, wildflowers, historic cabins, camping, and Bluffs Restaurant. North Carolina. [Source: [Visit NC Mileposts](https://www.visitnc.com/itinerary/must-see-mileposts-blue-ridge-parkway)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Richland Balsam Overlook** (30-60 minutes, Milepost 431.4) - Highest point on the Blue Ridge Parkway at 6,053 feet. 1.5-mile self-guiding loop trail through spruce-fir forest. Less crowded than other high-elevation stops. [Source: [Romantic Asheville](https://www.romanticasheville.com/BlueRidgeParkway.htm)]

- **Thunder Hill Overlook** (15-30 minutes, Milepost 290.4) - North of Blowing Rock with expansive view plummeting east to the Piedmont from 3,795 feet. Excellent for photography. [Source: [Blue Ridge Mountain Life](https://blueridgemountainlife.com/blue-ridge-parkway-travel-guide/)]

- **Sliding Rock** (1-2 hours, near Milepost 413) - Natural 60-foot rock water slide into a refreshing 8-foot-deep pool. Pisgah National Forest, short detour off parkway. Popular summer activity. [Source: [Miles with McConkey](https://www.mileswithmcconkey.com/27-superb-blue-ridge-parkway-overlooks-and-stops/)]

- **James River** (30-60 minutes, Milepost 63.6) - Lowest point on parkway at 650 feet elevation. Historic canal locks, Otter Creek Trail, and James River Visitor Center. Virginia. [Source: [Road Travel America](https://www.roadtravelamerica.com/blue-ridge-parkway.htm)]

- **Waterrock Knob Sunrise** (1-1.5 hours, Milepost 451.2) - One of the best sunrise locations on the parkway at nearly 6,000 feet. Short trail to summit. Near Great Smoky Mountains connection. [Source: [Miles with McConkey](https://www.mileswithmcconkey.com/27-superb-blue-ridge-parkway-overlooks-and-stops/)]

- **Mount Pisgah** (2-4 hours, Milepost 408.6) - Pisgah Inn lodge (one of only two lodges on parkway) with sit-down dining and lodging. Hiking trails and stunning sunrise views. North Carolina. [Source: [Romantic Asheville](https://www.romanticasheville.com/BlueRidgeParkway.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Asheville, North Carolina** (variable, adjacent at MP 384) - Mountain city known for arts, craft breweries, Biltmore Estate, and vibrant downtown. Major services hub for parkway. [Source: [Romantic Asheville](https://www.romanticasheville.com/BlueRidgeParkway.htm)]

- **Grandfather Mountain** (2-4 hours, Milepost 305 exit) - Nature preserve with Mile High Swinging Bridge, wildlife habitats, museum, and hiking trails. Admission fee. Linville, NC. [Source: [Visit NC Mileposts](https://www.visitnc.com/itinerary/must-see-mileposts-blue-ridge-parkway)]

- **Shenandoah National Park** (half-day+, northern terminus) - Connects to Skyline Drive at northern end of Blue Ridge Parkway. Additional scenic driving and hiking. Virginia. [Source: [NPS Shenandoah](https://www.nps.gov/shen/index.htm)]

- **Great Smoky Mountains National Park** (half-day+, southern terminus) - Connects at Milepost 469 near Cherokee, NC. Most visited national park. North Carolina/Tennessee. [Source: [NPS Great Smoky Mountains](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/index.htm)]

- **Roanoke, Virginia** (variable, Milepost 120 area) - Largest city along parkway with Mill Mountain Star, museums, and dining. Full services. [Source: [Road Travel America](https://www.roadtravelamerica.com/blue-ridge-parkway.htm)]

## Total Recommended Time

**Total recommended visit time for Blue Ridge Parkway: 1-5 days (or multiple trips)**

The full 469-mile drive takes about 15 hours without stops (at 45 mph), but the parkway is designed for leisurely exploration. A day trip allows visiting one concentrated section with a few overlooks and one major attraction. Three days enables a full Virginia-to-North Carolina drive with key stops. Five or more days allows thorough exploration including hiking, lodging at Peaks of Otter or Pisgah Inn, and visiting adjacent national parks.

**Note:** Check road conditions before visiting as sections may be closed due to weather or Hurricane Helene damage.
