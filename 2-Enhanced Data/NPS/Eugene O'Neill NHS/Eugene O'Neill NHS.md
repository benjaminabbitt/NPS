# Eugene O'Neill NHS (National Historic Site)

## <a id="stamps"></a>Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-tao-house"></a>**Eugene O'Neill National Historic Site** (1000 Kuss Rd, Danville, California 94526 (37.8254254, -122.0221967); Sun: 10:00 AM-4:30 PM, Mon-Tue: Closed, Wed-Sat: 10:00 AM-4:30 PM; (925) 228-8860)

**Important:** Visitors must take the free NPS shuttle from the Museum of the San Ramon Valley at 205 Railroad Avenue, Danville, CA 94526 (37.8207871, -122.0004874). Private vehicles are not allowed. Advance reservations are required by calling (925) 228-8860 x6422. [Source: [NPS Eugene O'Neill](https://www.nps.gov/euon/planyourvisit/directions.htm), [How to Visit Eugene O'Neill NHS](https://wnpa.org/plan-your-trip/trip-ideas/park-stories/how-to-visit-eugene-oneill-national-historic-site-in-danville-california-1)]

**Note:** Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## <a id="key-activities"></a>Key Activities

- <a id="activity-guided-tour"></a>**Guided Tour of Tao House** (2 hours) - Tour the thoughtfully preserved rooms where America's only Nobel Prize-winning playwright Eugene O'Neill wrote his final masterpieces including The Iceman Cometh, Long Day's Journey Into Night, and A Moon for the Misbegotten; 1000 Kuss Rd, Danville, California 94526 (37.8254254, -122.0221967); Tours offered Wed-Fri and Sun at 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM; (925) 228-8860 x6422 [Source: [NPS Guided Tour](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/guided-tour-of-tao-house.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60802-d110260-Reviews-Eugene_O_Neill_National_Historic_Site_Tao_House-Danville_Tri_Valley_California.html)]

- <a id="activity-self-guided-tour"></a>**Self-Guided Saturday Tours** (2 hours) - Explore Tao House at your own pace on Saturdays with shuttle service starting at 10:00 AM from downtown Danville; 1000 Kuss Rd, Danville, California 94526 (37.8254254, -122.0221967); Saturdays starting at 10:00 AM; (925) 228-8860 x6422 [Source: [Visit California](https://www.visitcalifornia.com/experience/eugene-oneill-national-historic-site/), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/euon/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- <a id="activity-grounds-exploration"></a>**Grounds Exploration** (30-60 minutes) - Wander through the courtyard's gardens, walk the path-lined gardens with black-walnut and almond orchards, explore hillside hiking trails overlooking San Ramon Valley, and visit O'Neill's favorite workday diversion—the cliffside swimming pool and the 19th century barn that predates O'Neill's residence; 1000 Kuss Rd, Danville, California 94526 (37.8254254, -122.0221967); Sun: 10:00 AM-4:30 PM, Mon-Tue: Closed, Wed-Sat: 10:00 AM-4:30 PM [Source: [Visit California](https://www.visitcalifornia.com/experience/eugene-oneill-national-historic-site/), [Discover San Francisco Bay Area](https://visittrivalley.com/2018/03/discover-san-francisco-bay-area-eugene-oneill-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="activity-las-trampas-trails"></a>**Adjacent Wilderness Hiking** (1-3 hours) - Access hiking trails from the adjacent Las Trampas Regional Wilderness that lead to the site and offer extended hiking opportunities beyond the property; trails connect to 1000 Kuss Rd, Danville, California 94526 (37.8254254, -122.0221967); Dawn to dusk [Source: [Carrie Green Zinn](https://carriegreenzinn.com/eugene-oneill-national-historic-site/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60802-d110260-Reviews-Eugene_O_Neill_National_Historic_Site_Tao_House-Danville_Tri_Valley_California.html)]

## <a id="hidden-gems"></a>Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-junior-ranger"></a>**Junior Ranger Program** (30-45 minutes) - Complete a booklet filled with fun and educational activities designed to help visitors learn more about Eugene O'Neill and the park's unique history, then earn a badge; available at 1000 Kuss Rd, Danville, California 94526 (37.8254254, -122.0221967); Sun: 10:00 AM-4:30 PM, Mon-Tue: Closed, Wed-Sat: 10:00 AM-4:30 PM [Source: [NPS Junior Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/earn-your-junior-ranger-badge-at-eugene-o-neill-nhs.htm)]

- <a id="gem-oneill-festival"></a>**Eugene O'Neill Festival** (varies by performance) - Attend the annual Eugene O'Neill Festival featuring live theatrical productions of O'Neill plays performed at Tao House, along with concerts and lectures exploring dramatic theatre, held in partnership with the Eugene O'Neill Foundation; 1000 Kuss Rd, Danville, California 94526 (37.8254254, -122.0221967); August 23 - September 21 annually [Source: [NPS Eugene O'Neill Festival](https://www.nps.gov/euon/planyourvisit/eugene-o-neill-festival.htm), [Eugene O'Neill Foundation Festival 2025](https://eugeneoneill.org/productions/festival/)]

- <a id="gem-old-barn-performances"></a>**Old Barn Theatre Performances** (2-3 hours) - Experience staged performances of O'Neill's plays in the historic Old Barn on the property during the annual festival; 1000 Kuss Rd, Danville, California 94526 (37.8254254, -122.0221967); During festival season (late August - September) [Source: [Discover San Francisco Bay Area](https://visittrivalley.com/2018/03/discover-san-francisco-bay-area-eugene-oneill-national-historic-site/)]

## <a id="also-nearby"></a>Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-blackhawk-museum"></a>**Blackhawk Museum** (2 hours, 5 miles/10 minutes from Eugene O'Neill NHS) - Explore five distinct galleries featuring more than 55 vintage, classic, and sports automobiles showcasing the evolution of design and engineering, plus Spirit of the Old West gallery and World of Nature gallery with over 600 animal species; 3700 Blackhawk Plaza Circle, Danville, CA 94506 (37.7993685, -121.9170719); Fri-Sun: 10:00 AM-5:00 PM, Mon-Thu: Closed; (925) 736-2280 [Source: [Blackhawk Museum](https://blackhawkmuseum.org/), [California Through My Lens](https://californiathroughmylens.com/blackhawk-museum/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60802-d279494-Reviews-Blackhawk_Museum-Danville_Tri_Valley_California.html)]

- <a id="nearby-lindsay-wildlife"></a>**Lindsay Wildlife Experience** (1-2 hours, 8 miles/15 minutes from Eugene O'Neill NHS) - Visit one of the oldest and largest animal rehabilitation centers in the country, a natural history museum where you can see more than 50 species of non-releasable native California wildlife, with interactive exhibits and animal presentations; 1931 First Avenue, Walnut Creek, CA 94597 (37.9233951, -122.07572); Wed-Sun: 10:00 AM-5:00 PM (Fri closes at 3:00 PM), Mon-Tue: Closed; Contact via website [Source: [Lindsay Wildlife Experience](https://lindsaywildlife.org/hours-prices-directions/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g33235-d1175688-Reviews-Lindsay_Wildlife_Museum-Walnut_Creek_California.html), [510 Families](https://www.510families.com/field-trip-lindsay-wildlife-museum-in-walnut-creek/)]

- <a id="nearby-mount-diablo"></a>**Mount Diablo State Park** (4-8 hours for summit hike, 6 miles/15 minutes from Eugene O'Neill NHS) - Hike to the summit of Mount Diablo with panoramic views of the Bay Area, explore over 150 miles of trails, visit Rock City's unique sandstone formations, and stop by the Summit Visitor Center; Summit: approximately 37.8817, -121.9142; South Gate entrance via Diablo Road, Danville; Park gates: 8:00 AM-sunset, Summit Visitor Center: 10:00 AM-4:00 PM daily; $10 vehicle entrance fee [Source: [Mount Diablo State Park](https://www.parks.ca.gov/mountdiablo), [Visit Tri-Valley](https://visittrivalley.com/2018/04/mtdiablodaytrip/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/mount-diablo-via-summit-trail--2), [California Through My Lens](https://californiathroughmylens.com/mt-diablo-state-park/)]
