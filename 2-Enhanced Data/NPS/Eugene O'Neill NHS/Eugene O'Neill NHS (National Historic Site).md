# Eugene O'Neill NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Tao House Visitor Contact Station** - Cancellation stamp available at Tao House (accessible only by NPS shuttle); 1000 Kuss Rd, Danville, CA 94526 (37.8254254,-122.0221967); Tours available Wednesday-Sunday, guided tours at 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM (Wed/Thu/Fri/Sun), self-guided tours Saturdays starting at 10:00 AM; Phone: (925) 228-8860 x6422 [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/euon/planyourvisit/index.htm), [Guided Tour Info](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/guided-tour-of-tao-house.htm), [Western National Parks](https://wnpa.org/explore-parks/eugene-oneill)]

- **Museum of the San Ramon Valley (Shuttle Pickup Location)** - NPS shuttle departs from this downtown Danville location to Tao House; 205 Railroad Ave, Danville, CA 94526 (37.8207871,-122.0004874); Office hours: Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/euon/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

**Note:** Advance reservations are required for guided tours. Call at least two weeks ahead to secure your spot. The historic site is only accessible by National Park Service shuttle from the Museum of the San Ramon Valley in downtown Danville. Private vehicles are not permitted at Tao House.

## Key Activities

- **Guided Tour of Tao House** (2 hours) - Explore the thoughtfully preserved rooms where Eugene O'Neill wrote acclaimed plays including "The Iceman Cometh," "Long Day's Journey Into Night," and "A Moon for the Misbegotten," with park rangers providing insightful narratives bringing the spaces to life; Tao House (accessible only by NPS shuttle from 205 Railroad Ave, Danville, CA 94526); Guided tours offered Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, and Sunday at 10:00 AM or 2:00 PM, Saturday self-guided tours starting at 10:00 AM [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/euon/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Visit California](https://www.visitcalifornia.com/experience/eugene-oneill-national-historic-site/), [Western National Parks](https://wnpa.org/plan-your-trip/trip-ideas/park-stories/how-to-visit-eugene-oneill-national-historic-site-in-danville-california-1)]

- **Self-Guided Saturday Tours** (2 hours) - On Saturdays, visitors can pick up the shuttle in downtown Danville starting at 10:00 AM for self-guided exploration of Tao House, allowing approximately 2 hours to explore the grounds at your own pace; Tao House (accessible only by NPS shuttle from 205 Railroad Ave, Danville, CA 94526); Saturdays starting at 10:00 AM [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/euon/planyourvisit/index.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60802-d110260-Reviews-Eugene_O_Neill_National_Historic_Site_Tao_House-Danville_Tri_Valley_California.html)]

- **Explore the Courtyard Gardens** (30 minutes) - Wander through the courtyard's path-lined gardens featuring black-walnut and almond orchards that O'Neill tended during his time at Tao House; Tao House grounds; Accessible during tour hours [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/euon/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Visit Tri-Valley](https://visittrivalley.com/what-to-do/eugene-oneill-national-historic-site/)]

- **Walk O'Neill's Trails** (30-45 minutes) - Explore the hillside hiking trails overlooking San Ramon Valley that Eugene O'Neill enjoyed walking during his residency, providing scenic views and connecting to Las Trampas Wilderness Regional Preserve; Tao House grounds and adjacent trails; Accessible during tour hours [Source: [Visit California](https://www.visitcalifornia.com/experience/eugene-oneill-national-historic-site/), [Irish America](https://www.irishamerica.com/2025/05/eugene-oneills-tao-house/)]

- **Visit the 19th Century Barn** (15 minutes) - Explore the historic barn that predates O'Neill's residence and now serves as the venue for the annual Eugene O'Neill Festival performances each September; Tao House grounds; Accessible during tour hours [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/euon/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Eugene O'Neill Foundation](https://eugeneoneill.org/productions/festival/)]

- **See Blemie's Grave** (5 minutes) - Visit the touching grave marker for Silverdene "Blemie" Emblem O'Neill, the family's beloved Dalmatian, at the far end of the property, for whom the playwright crafted a magnificent "last will and testament"; Tao House grounds; Accessible during tour hours [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60802-d110260-Reviews-Eugene_O_Neill_National_Historic_Site_Tao_House-Danville_Tri_Valley_California.html), [American Heritage](https://www.americanheritage.com/content/tao-house-eugene-oneill-national-historic-site)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Hike to Tao House via Iron Horse Trail** (1.5-2 hours round trip) - The Park Service occasionally offers guided hikes to Tao House from parking along the Iron Horse Trail, though visitors can also obtain the map and walk without a group, providing a real flavor of the locale and why Eugene O'Neill loved this location, featuring an easy and shaded hike through neighborhood access with only a short uphill stretch; Iron Horse Trail access point near Tao House; Check with park rangers for scheduled hikes or self-guided map availability [Source: [TripAdvisor Review](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g60802-d110260-r616046998-Eugene_O_Neill_National_Historic_Site_Tao_House-Danville_Tri_Valley_California.html)]

- **Rosie's Room and the Player Piano** (Included in tour) - Seek out the special room built for O'Neill's pea-green player piano that reportedly came from a New Orleans bordello, a unique architectural and historical detail rarely highlighted in standard tour narratives; Tao House interior; Accessible during guided tours [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60802-d110260-Reviews-Eugene_O_Neill_National_Historic_Site_Tao_House-Danville_Tri_Valley_California.html)]

- **The Cliffside Swimming Pool** (10 minutes) - Discover O'Neill's favorite workday diversion, the cliffside swimming pool offering views of the valley below, a lesser-known feature of the property that provided the playwright with respite from his intense writing sessions; Tao House grounds; Viewable during tour hours [Source: [Irish America](https://www.irishamerica.com/2025/05/eugene-oneills-tao-house/)]

- **Annual Eugene O'Neill Festival** (3-4 hours per performance) - Attend professionally acted and produced performances of O'Neill's plays in the Old Barn Theatre, where you can bring a picnic dinner and eat on the grounds before the show, held over three weekends each September since 1999; Tao House Old Barn Theatre; September, check Eugene O'Neill Foundation website for exact dates and productions [Source: [Eugene O'Neill Foundation Festival](https://eugeneoneill.org/productions/festival/), [Pleasanton Weekly](https://www.pleasantonweekly.com/ae/2023/12/11/tao-house-tours-in-danville-offer-insight-into-life-of-playwright-eugene-oneill-wife-carlotta/), [DanvilleSanRamon.com](https://danvillesanramon.com/news/2014/07/14/eugene-oneill-festival-to-celebrate-theater)]

- **Elliott's Bar Connection** (30 minutes) - Visit Elliott's bar in downtown Danville, established in 1907 and one of the oldest San Francisco Bay Area dive bars, where Eugene O'Neill used to hang out during his time living at Tao House; Elliott's, downtown Danville (exact address available from tour guides); Check current bar hours [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60802-d110260-Reviews-Eugene_O_Neill_National_Historic_Site_Tao_House-Danville_Tri_Valley_California.html), [Visit Tri-Valley](https://visittrivalley.com/blog/what-to-do-in-danville-ca/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Mount Diablo State Park** (3-4 hours, 15 miles) - Accessible from Danville via its South Gate, this spectacular state park offers hiking, biking, and horseback riding trails with summit views at 3,849 feet extending almost 200 miles on clear days, encompassing a 40,000-square-mile region including the Sacramento Valley and parts of the Sierra Nevada; 96 Mitchell Canyon Rd, Clayton, CA; Park gates open 8:00 AM to sunset daily, Summit Visitor Center open 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM [Source: [Visit Concord](https://www.visitconcordca.com/outdoors/mount-diablo/), [California State Parks](https://www.parks.ca.gov/mountdiablo), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g32217-d117270-Reviews-Mount_Diablo_State_Park_Mitchell_Canyon_Visitor_Center-Clayton_California.html)]

- **Blackhawk Museum** (1.5-2 hours, 5 miles) - World-renowned museum featuring five galleries including distinctive automobile makes built between 1894 and 1971, Spirit of the Old West gallery, and World of Nature gallery with over 600 animal species, located in an outdoor shopping plaza; 3700 Blackhawk Plaza Circle, Danville, CA 94506; Friday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed Monday-Thursday; Admission $10-15 [Source: [Blackhawk Museum](https://blackhawkmuseum.org/admission/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60802-d279494-Reviews-Blackhawk_Museum-Danville_Tri_Valley_California.html), [California Through My Lens](https://californiathroughmylens.com/blackhawk-museum/)]

- **Las Trampas Regional Wilderness** (2-3 hours, 3 miles) - This 5,778-acre wilderness preserve adjacent to Tao House features rugged trails including the popular Rocky Ridge/Elderberry loop (2-3 hours), offering amazing views of Mount Diablo and sweeping valley vistas, with trails ranging from 5-7 miles; Las Trampas Regional Wilderness entrance, San Ramon/Danville area; Open dawn to dusk daily [Source: [East Bay Parks](https://www.ebparks.org/parks/las-trampas), [Visit Tri-Valley](https://visittrivalley.com/what-to-do/las-trampas-regional-wilderness-park/), [Hiking Project](https://www.hikingproject.com/trail/7015165/las-trampas-north-loop)]

- **Lindsay Wildlife Experience** (1.5-2 hours, 8 miles) - Family museum and the first wildlife rehabilitation center established in the United States, showcasing over 50 species of native California wildlife with hourly animal experiences featuring snakes, birds, reptiles, bunnies, and insects; 1931 First Avenue, Walnut Creek, CA 94597; Wednesday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [Lindsay Wildlife](https://lindsaywildlife.org/hours-prices-directions/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g33235-d1175688-Reviews-Lindsay_Wildlife_Museum-Walnut_Creek_California.html)]

- **Museum of the San Ramon Valley** (30-45 minutes, 0 miles - shuttle departure point) - Charming museum located in a former train depot offering fascinating exhibits about local pioneers and the development of the San Ramon Valley, where visitors also meet for the Tao House shuttle; 205 Railroad Ave, Danville, CA 94526; Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [Visit Tri-Valley](https://visittrivalley.com/blog/what-to-do-in-danville-ca/), [Danville CA](https://www.danville.ca.gov/467/Museum-of-the-San-Ramon-Valley)]
