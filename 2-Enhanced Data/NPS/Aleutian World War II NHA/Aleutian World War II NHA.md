# Aleutian World War II NHA (National Heritage Area)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Aleutian World War II Visitor Center** (2716 Airport Beach Road, Unalaska, AK 99692 (53.8845151, -166.5545882); Summer: Tuesday-Sunday 11am-6pm, Winter: Tuesday-Saturday 11am-5pm; 907-581-9944) [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/aleu/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Source](https://www.alaska.org/detail/aleutian-world-war-ii-national-historic-area-visitor-center)]

**Note:** Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. The visitor center is owned and operated by the Ounalashka Corporation and located in a historic 1940s aerology building adjacent to the Unalaska Airport.

## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center and Museum Tour (1-1.5 hours)** - Explore exhibits on the Aleutian Campaign and the evacuation and internment of native Unangan people, watch WWII documentaries in the 1940s-style theater including "Report from the Aleutians" and "Alaska at War," and visit the reconstructed radio room upstairs featuring original WWII teletype machines and equipment; 2716 Airport Beach Road, Unalaska, AK 99692; Summer: Tuesday-Sunday 11am-6pm, Winter: Tuesday-Saturday 11am-5pm; 907-581-9944 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/aleu/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Source](https://www.ci.unalaska.ak.us/community/page/aleutian-world-war-ii-national-historic-area-visitor-center), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g30980-d7053240-Reviews-or40-Aleutian_World_War_II_National_Historic_Area_Visitor_Center-Dutch_Harbor_Unal.html)]

- **Fort Schwatka Self-Guided Tour (2-3 hours)** - Explore the highest coastal battery ever constructed in the United States at 897 feet above sea level on Mount Ballyhoo, viewing remarkably intact gun mounts, tunnels, bunkers, and the remains of over 100 structures that once supported 250 soldiers during WWII; accessible via hiking trails from Airport Beach Road or Henry Swanson Drive; open year-round during daylight hours; land use permit required (daily $10, weekly $20 from Ounalashka Corporation) [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/places/fort-schwatka.htm), [Source](https://www.alaska.org/detail/fort-schwatka-and-battery-402), [Source](https://www.alaskacenters.gov/trip-planning/itineraries/outings/explore-world-war-ii-ruins-unalaska), [Source](https://ounalashka.com/our-lands/land-use-permit/)]

- **Mount Ballyhoo Hike (1-3 hours)** - Hike the 3-mile round-trip trail to the peak of Mount Ballyhoo (1,634 feet) offering 360-degree views of Captains Bay, Amaknak Island, Unalaska Bay, and Iliuliuk Harbor, with excellent opportunities to observe bald eagle nesting areas and spot whales in Captains Bay during July-August; trailhead at Airport Beach Road; open year-round during daylight; land use permit required (daily $10, weekly $20) [[Source](https://www.alaska.org/detail/mount-ballyhoo), [Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/alaska/mount-ballyhoo), [Source](https://ounalashka.com/our-lands/land-use-permit/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Bunker Hill Trail (1-1.5 hours)** - Hike this 2-mile round-trip easy trail to the summit of Hill 400 featuring the remains of a WWII battery command station and four circular Panama mounts for 155mm guns placed after the 1942 Japanese bombing, with spectacular 360-degree views and excellent whale watching in July-August; accessible from Airport Beach Road (shorter but steeper) or Henry Swanson Drive (more gradual); open year-round during daylight; land use permit required (daily $10, weekly $20) [[Source](https://www.alaska.org/detail/bunker-hill), [Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/alaska/bunker-hill-trail), [Source](http://www.unalaska.org/bunker-hill-hike.html), [Source](https://ounalashka.com/our-lands/land-use-permit/)]

- **USS Northwestern Memorial (15-30 minutes)** - Visit the hillside memorial featuring the salvaged 9-foot brass propeller from the SS Northwestern, a ship destroyed during the June 3, 1942 Japanese bombing raid, with approximately 50 feet of the ship's hull normally visible at the head of Captains Bay (site listed on National Register of Historic Places); Unalaska Memorial Park and Cemetery, accessible via Bayview Avenue and Memorial Drive; open year-round [[Source](https://www.alaska.org/detail/ss-northwestern), [Source](https://www.unalaska.gov/parksrec/page/memorial-park), [Source](https://cockpitusa.com/blogs/posts-without-blog/see-america-uss-northwestern-memorial)]

- **Pyramid Valley Trail (1.5-2 hours)** - Hike through pristine tundra areas in Pyramid Valley surrounding 2,300-foot Pyramid Peak, offering different birding experiences than shoreside locations with opportunities to spot various tundra species in a scenic valley setting; trailhead access via Captains Bay area; open year-round during daylight; land use permit required (daily $10, weekly $20) [[Source](https://www.alaska.org/detail/pyramid-valley-trail), [Source](https://ounalashka.com/our-lands/land-use-permit/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Museum of the Aleutians (1-2 hours)** - Explore one of Alaska's best native cultural centers showcasing Unangan way of life from prehistory through the Russian-America period to WWII, featuring gut parkas, baskets, masks, ancient tools, repatriated religious artifacts, and original drawings from Captain Cook's third voyage, located about 1 mile from the WWII Visitor Center; 314 Salmon Way, Unalaska, AK 99685; Summer: Tuesday-Sunday 11am-4pm, Winter: Thursday-Sunday 12pm-4pm; 907-581-5150; Adults $10, Seniors $7, Veterans free [[Source](https://www.aleutians.org/), [Source](https://www.unalaska.gov/community/page/museum-aleutians), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g31155-d487057-Reviews-Museum_of_the_Aleutians-Unalaska_Aleutian_Islands_Alaska.html)]

- **Cathedral of the Holy Ascension (30-45 minutes)** - Visit the oldest Russian-built church still standing in Alaska, built in 1825 and enlarged in 1894, containing almost 700 pieces of art including Russian Orthodox icons, rare books, and the largest collection of 19th-century paintings in Alaska, a National Historic Landmark located about 1.5 miles from the WWII Visitor Center; W Broadway Avenue, Unalaska, AK 99685; best viewing after services (Saturday 6pm, Sunday 11am) or by calling for tour arrangements; 907-581-5883 [[Source](https://www.oca.org/parishes/oca-ak-unahak), [Source](https://www.alaska.org/detail/holy-ascension-russian-orthodox-church), [Source](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/usa/alaska/unalaska-and-dutch-harbor/attractions/cathedral-of-the-holy-ascension/a/poi-sig/1465681/361723)]
