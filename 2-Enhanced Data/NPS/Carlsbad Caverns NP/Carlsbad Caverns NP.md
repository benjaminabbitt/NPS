# Carlsbad Caverns NP (National Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Carlsbad Caverns Visitor Center** (3225 National Parks Highway, Carlsbad, NM 88220 (32.1478, -104.5567); cavern entrance 9:30 AM - 2:30 PM, last ticket 2:15 PM, visitors have until 4:45 PM to complete tour; 575-785-2232) [Source: [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/cave/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/cave/planyourvisit/visitor-center.htm), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/carlsbad-caverns-national-park-carlsbad-4)]

**Note:** Timed entry reservations required ($1 per person) via Recreation.gov; entrance pass purchased upon arrival at visitor center. Passport stamps available at visitor center. Must enter between reserved time and 2:30 PM. [Source: [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/facility/234637), [NPS Self-Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/cave/planyourvisit/selfguided_tours.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Tour Big Room Self-Guided** (1.5 hours) - Walk spectacular 1.25-mile paved trail around massive underground chamber after taking elevator from visitor center; entry 9:30 AM - 2:30 PM; reservations required [Source: [NPS Self-Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/cave/planyourvisit/selfguided_tours.htm), [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/facility/234637)]

- **Descend Natural Entrance Route** (2-2.5 hours total) - Hike steep 1.25-mile paved trail through cave's natural entrance connecting to Big Room trail; more strenuous option than elevator; reservations required [Source: [NPS Self-Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/cave/planyourvisit/selfguided_tours.htm)]

- **Join King's Palace Ranger Tour** (90 minutes) - Experience ranger-led tour through historic formations covering 1 mile; pick up tickets 30-45 minutes before scheduled time; additional fee [Source: [NPS Ranger-Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/cave/planyourvisit/tour_schedule.htm), [National Parks Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/park/subpage/carlsbad-caverns-cave-tours)]

- **Watch Bat Flight Program** (1-2 hours evening, May-October) - View 400,000+ Brazilian free-tailed bats spiral from cave at sunset; free program, no reservations, arrive 1 hour early for amphitheater seating; best flights August-September [Source: [NPS Bat Flight Program](https://www.nps.gov/cave/planyourvisit/bat_flight_program.htm), [TripAdvisor Bat Flight](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g143018-d2264088-Reviews-Bat_Flight_Program_in_Carlsbad_Caverns_National_Park-Carlsbad_Caverns_National_Pa.html)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Tour Slaughter Canyon Cave** (3-4 hours) - Explore undeveloped cave 23 miles southwest of main visitor center featuring 89-foot Monarch mineral column; ranger-led adventure caving tour only; more secluded experience [Source: [WorldAtlas](https://www.worldatlas.com/national-parks/carlsbad-caverns-national-park-is-where-stone-monarchs-and-bats-take-flight.html), [Travel the Parks](https://traveltheparks.com/complete-guide-to-carlsbad-caverns-national-park/)]

- **Hike Lower Cave Tour** (3 hours) - Navigate 1-mile ranger-led tour through undeveloped sections including 39 steps; advanced reservation required; limited availability [Source: [NPS Ranger-Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/cave/planyourvisit/tour_schedule.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Living Desert Zoo & Gardens State Park** (1.5-2 hours, 12 miles north at 1504 Skyline Drive) - Explore 40+ species of native Chihuahuan Desert animals and hundreds of plant species on 1.3-mile self-guided trail atop Ocotillo Hills; open 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily (last entry 3:30 PM), closed Christmas; visit early morning to avoid summer heat [Source: [Living Desert Zoo](https://livingdesertnm.org/plan-your-trip), [Desert USA](https://www.desertusa.com/desert-new-mexico/living-desert-zoo-nm.html)]

- **Sitting Bull Falls Recreation Area** (2-3 hours, 42 miles southwest in Lincoln National Forest) - Hike to stunning 150-foot waterfall and natural pools in desert canyon setting; open until 5:00 PM October-March, 6:00 PM April-September; potable water and restrooms available [Source: [USFS Lincoln](https://www.fs.usda.gov/recarea/lincoln/recarea/?recid=34238), [Visit Carlsbad](https://carlsbadnewmexico.com/place/sitting-bull-falls/)]
