# Natchez Trace NST (National Scenic Trail)

## <a id="cancellation-stamps"></a>Cancellation Stamp Locations

The Natchez Trace National Scenic Trail shares stamp locations with the Natchez Trace Parkway at visitor centers along the 444-mile corridor.

- <a id="stamp-tupelo"></a>**Natchez Trace Parkway Visitor Center** (2680 Natchez Trace Parkway, Tupelo, MS 38804 (34.2576, -88.7034); Open 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM daily, closed Thanksgiving, December 25, and January 1; 662-680-4027) - The main visitor center for the Natchez Trace Parkway, located at milepost 266 near Tupelo, Mississippi. This is the primary location to obtain NPS passport cancellation stamps for the trail. [Source: [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-mississippi/tupelo-ms/139-tupelo-visitors-center.html), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/natr/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/natchez-trace-parkway-headquarters-and-visitors-center-tupelo)]

**Note:** Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting, as stamps may be available at other visitor centers along the parkway.

## <a id="key-activities"></a>Key Activities

The Natchez Trace National Scenic Trail consists of five separate sections totaling over 60 miles, running roughly parallel to the Natchez Trace Parkway. These sections are not a continuous footpath but rather limited trail segments developed for hiking and horseback riding.

- <a id="activity-highland-rim"></a>**Highland Rim Section** (2-7.5 hours for full 20 miles) - The northernmost section of the trail, a 20-mile hiking and equestrian corridor south of Nashville, Tennessee from milepost 427.4 to 407.9. One hiker completed 25.6 miles in approximately 7 hours and 30 minutes, while shorter segments can be completed in 2-3 hours. Features mostly flat terrain with some uneven surfaces, following parts of the original Old Trace. Northern trailhead is at Garrison Creek (milepost 427.6, approximately 35.87443, -87.0323; dawn to dusk); includes restrooms, picnic tables, drinking water, and horse trailer parking. Trail can be overgrown in summer but is excellent in late fall to early spring. [Source: [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-national-scenic-trail/highlandrim-trail.html), [The Thousand Miler](https://thethousandmiler.com/day-23-highway-7-to-north-of-highland-rim/), [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/highland-rim-section.htm), [NPS Garrison Creek](https://home.nps.gov/places/garrison-creek.htm)]

- <a id="activity-blackland-prairie"></a>**Blackland Prairie Section** (2-2.5 hours for 6 miles) - A 6-mile section near Tupelo, Mississippi from milepost 266 to 259.9. The trail runs through forested areas, bottomlands, and the Chickasaw Village site at milepost 261.8. Northern trailhead is located across from the Natchez Trace Parkway Visitor Center at milepost 266; southern trailhead is on Jackson Street in Tupelo (note: currently closed due to severe storm damage). Trail surface varies from grass, gravel, wooden boardwalks, and pavement. Considered an easy route with 269 feet of elevation gain. [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/mississippi/natchez-trace-national-scenic-trail-blackland-prairie-section), [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hiking-the-blackland-prairie.htm), [NPS Northern Terminus](https://www.nps.gov/places/blackland-prairie-section-northern-terminus-trailhead.htm)]

- <a id="activity-yockanookany"></a>**Yockanookany Section** (2-3.5 hours for shorter segments, full day for 26 miles) - The longest of the five developed trail sections at 26 miles, located north of Jackson, Mississippi. Northern terminus at milepost 130.9 near Madison, MS; southern terminus at West Florida Boundary. For the southern 7.8-mile segment, it takes an average of 2 hours 47 minutes to 3.5 hours. The trail features 8 miles alongside Ross R. Barnett Reservoir and 16 miles through dense forests, seasonal streams, and open pastureland. Multiple access points including Highway 43 Trailhead (milepost 114.9). Considered moderately challenging with 531 feet of elevation gain. Note: Some sections may have maintenance issues and storm damage. [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/mississippi/natchez-trace-national-scenic-trail-yockanookany-segment), [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/yockanookanysection.htm), [The Thousand Miler](https://thethousandmiler.com/day-6-yockanookany/)]

- <a id="activity-rocky-springs"></a>**Rocky Springs Section** (3-4 hours for full 6+ miles, 30-60 minutes for 2.4-mile loop) - A little over 6 miles located near Port Gibson, Mississippi. The shorter Old Natchez Trace loop at Rocky Springs is 2.4 miles and takes about 30-60 minutes to complete. The full section is moderately strenuous in places with terrain through deep ravines. Three main trailheads: Northern Terminus at Highway 27/Fisher Ferry Road (milepost 59), Rocky Springs Picnic Area (milepost 54.8), and Southern Terminus at Owens Creek (milepost 52.4). The 2.4-mile loop has 177 feet of elevation gain. [Source: [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/rockyspringssection.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/mississippi/rocky-springs-old-natchez-trace), [NPS Rocky Springs Northern Terminus](https://www.nps.gov/places/rocky-springs-section-northern-terminus.htm)]

- <a id="activity-potkopinu"></a>**Potkopinu Section** (1-1.5 hours for 3.4 miles) - The southernmost section at 3 miles long, featuring the longest stretch of "sunken" historic Trace remaining with embankments over 20 feet high. Named for the Natchez word meaning "little valley." The point-to-point trail is 3.4 miles with 242 feet of elevation gain. Considered a moderate hike. Hikers should prepare to walk through water at stream crossings, with conditions varying from muddy areas to knee-deep water depending on rainfall. Northern terminus trailhead is the larger of the two and the only place to park oversized vehicles. Trail may be overgrown with challenging conditions including heat and bugs. [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/mississippi/natchez-trace-national-scenic-trail-potkopinu-section), [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/potkopinu-section.htm)]

## <a id="hidden-gems"></a>Hidden Gems

- <a id="hidden-cypress-swamp"></a>**Cypress Swamp Trail** (15-30 minutes) - A short half-mile boardwalk trail at milepost 122 (Canton, MS 39046, approximately 32.5795, -89.8700; open dawn to dusk) that winds through a cypress swamp with its position right over the water. Named among the "Top 20 Natural Wonders" along the Natchez Trace, offering unparalleled views of the cypress swamp ecosystem. The 0.4-mile loop trail provides an atmospheric walk through one of the most unique environments on the parkway. [Source: [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/cypress-swamp.htm), [Visit Mississippi](https://visitmississippi.org/things-to-do/hiking/cypress-swamp/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/mississippi/natchez-trace-parkway-cypress-swamp)]

- <a id="hidden-fall-hollow"></a>**Fall Hollow Waterfall Trail** (10-60 minutes) - A short 0.4-mile trail at milepost 391.9 near Hohenwald, Tennessee. A five-minute walk takes you to a viewing platform to see a 20-foot waterfall, and those continuing on will be rewarded with numerous small cascades via steeper, unimproved trails. Takes an average of 10 minutes for the main viewing platform or 30 minutes to 1 hour to explore all waterfalls. Elevation gain of 55 feet. Considered an easy route. One of only two waterfalls along the Natchez Trace Parkway. [Source: [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/fall-hollow-waterfall.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/tennessee/fall-hollow-falls-trail), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-tennessee/columbia-centerville-tn/51-fall-hollow-waterfall.html)]

- <a id="hidden-old-trace-drive"></a>**Old Trace Drive** (15-30 minutes) - A two-mile, one-way drive near the historic tobacco barn that follows the actual Old Trace trail with spectacular views of the forest. This atmospheric section features the "sunken trace" where the path was trodden down by travelers over many years so that it now makes a hollow through the forest. A hidden gem offering a more intimate connection with the parkway's history. [Source: [TouristSecrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/hidden-river-ports-along-mississippis-natchez-trace/), [Lazy Trips](https://lazytrips.com/trip/the-scenic-natchez-trace-parkway-our-ultimate-road-trip-guide)]

- <a id="hidden-gordon-house"></a>**Gordon House Ferry Site** (20-30 minutes) - A 10-minute walk from the site leads to a section of the original Natchez Trace and the Duck River ferry site, which was in operation until a bridge was finally built at the end of the 1800s. Offers a glimpse into the early transportation history of the Natchez Trace. A quiet, off-the-beaten-path stop rarely crowded with tourists. [Source: [Lazy Trips](https://lazytrips.com/trip/the-scenic-natchez-trace-parkway-our-ultimate-road-trip-guide)]

- <a id="hidden-french-camp"></a>**French Camp Historic Village** (45-90 minutes) - A preserved village offering a glimpse into the days of the early Natchez Trace, comprised of several buildings from the 1800s. This living history site showcases early settlement life along the trace. A lesser-known attraction that provides educational insight into frontier life. [Source: [TouristSecrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/hidden-river-ports-along-mississippis-natchez-trace/)]

## <a id="also-nearby"></a>Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-elvis"></a>**Elvis Presley Birthplace & Museum** (2 hours, near Tupelo) - A comprehensive historic site at 306 Elvis Presley Drive, Tupelo, MS 38804. Includes a tour of Elvis's humble birth home, a museum chronicling his life, the "Elvis at 13" statue, and the original Assembly of God Church where he was first exposed to gospel music. Open Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM. Admission: $8 adults, $5 children 7-12, free for children under 7. The grounds are open for exploration anytime, but the Museum, house, and church have set hours. Tickets sold only at the front desk at the Welcome Center. [Source: [Visit Tupelo](https://www.tupelo.net/directory/elvis-presley-birthplace-and-museum/), [Official Site](https://elvispresleybirthplace.com/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/elvis-presley-birthplace-and-museum-tupelo)]

- <a id="nearby-pharr"></a>**Pharr Mounds** (20-30 minutes, milepost 286.7) - A 90-acre archaeological site at milepost 286.7 on the Natchez Trace Parkway, near Tupelo, featuring eight dome-shaped burial mounds constructed around 2,000 to 1,800 years ago during the Middle Woodland period. One of the largest Middle Woodland era mound sites in the region. Mounds are visible from the parking area with wayside exhibits describing village life 2,000 years ago. Modern Chickasaw consider these mounds sacred; visitors are requested to respect them as if at a cemetery. Amenities include parking, accessible restrooms, picnic shelters and tables, drinking water, and wheelchair accessibility. [Source: [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/pharr-mounds.htm), [Visit Mississippi](https://visitmississippi.org/things-to-do/history/pharr-mounds/), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-mississippi/tishomingo-belmont-ms/134-pharr-mounds.html)]

- <a id="nearby-tishomingo"></a>**Tishomingo State Park** (2-4 hours, approximately 45 miles from Tupelo) - Located at the foothills of the Appalachian Mountains, this state park intersects with the Natchez Trace and offers hiking trails, rock formations, and scenic beauty. About 45 miles from Tupelo, it provides excellent opportunities for outdoor recreation including hiking, camping, and exploring unique geological features. A worthwhile day trip from the parkway. [Source: [Visit Tupelo](https://www.tupelo.net/blog/seven-things-you-must-see-on-the-natchez-trace-parkway/)]

- <a id="nearby-leipers-fork"></a>**Leiper's Fork** (1-3 hours, near Nashville) - A quaint small Tennessee town full of great restaurants and boutique shops with a buzzing live music scene. Located just a few minutes from the Parkway's Highway 96 exit near Nashville. Features antique stores, gift shops, and art galleries. A charming stop that offers a taste of local culture and hospitality. [Source: [TouristSecrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/hidden-river-ports-along-mississippis-natchez-trace/), [Visit Franklin](https://visitfranklin.com/blog/your-guide-to-the-natchez-trace-parkway-in-franklin-tn/)]

- <a id="nearby-jackson-falls"></a>**Jackson Falls** (30-45 minutes, near Nashville) - A popular stop on the Natchez Trace Parkway with an Instagram-worthy scenic overlook and a short 0.2-mile paved hike into a small gorge to see the falls. One of the most photographed locations on the parkway. Easily accessible for visitors of all abilities. [Source: [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/drive/between/58953/59187/tupelo-to-jackson-drive)]

## Review / Personal Notes

---

**Total Recommended Time:** For a comprehensive experience of the Natchez Trace National Scenic Trail's five main hiking sections, plan for 2-3 full days of hiking (approximately 15-20 hours total trail time for all sections). Individual sections can be visited in 1-7.5 hours depending on length and difficulty. Day hikers can experience highlight segments in 2-4 hours per visit. The trail is best experienced over multiple trips given the 400+ mile span between sections along the parkway. [Source: [NPS Hiking Sections](https://www.nps.gov/natt/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [AllTrails reviews](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/tennessee/natchez-trace-national-scenic-trail--2), [NPS Overview](https://www.nps.gov/natt/index.htm)]
