# Kaloko-Honokōhau NHP (National Historical Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Hale Ho'okipa Visitor Center** (73-4786 Kanalani St. #14, Kailua-Kona, HI 96740 [19.677, -156.024]; 8:30 AM - 4:00 PM daily; 808-329-6881 x1329)

**Note:** Call ahead to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [NPS Contact Page](https://www.nps.gov/kaho/contacts.htm), [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/kaho/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Kaloko Fishpond Trail** (2-3 hours) - Walk to the restored eleven-acre Kaloko Fishpond, an impressive feat of ancient Hawaiian engineering with a nearly 800-foot seawall that has been restored to its ancient state; accessed via 0.8-mile trail from visitor center or Kaloko Road gate entrance; 73-4786 Kanalani St. #14, Kailua-Kona, HI 96740 (trail access); 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily (gate hours). [Source: [Big Island Hikes](https://bigislandhikes.com/kaloko-honokohau-park), [Explore Big Island](https://www.explore-the-big-island.com/honokohau-beach.html)]

- **'Ai'ōpio Fishtrap** (30-45 minutes) - Visit this 1.7-acre traditional Hawaiian fish trap with stone walls forming an artificial enclosure along the naturally curved shoreline; best viewed at low tide when more features are revealed; 5-minute walk from Kaloko Fishpond; accessible 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily via Kaloko Road gate. [Source: [Big Island Hikes](https://bigislandhikes.com/kaloko-honokohau-park), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaloko-Honok%C5%8Dhau_National_Historical_Park)]

- **Aimakapa Fishpond Wildlife Viewing** (30-60 minutes) - Observe endangered Hawaiian Stilts and migratory birds that winter in the park at this excellent bird-watching location; part of the 4.5-mile loop trail system; accessible during park hours 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily. [Source: [Go Hawaii](https://www.gohawaii.com/islands/hawaii-big-island/regions/kona/kaloko-honokohau-national-historical-park), [Hawaiian Airlines](https://www.hawaiianairlines.com/island-guide/hawaii-island/places/adventures/kaloko-honokohau)]

- **Petroglyphs Boardwalk (Ki'i Pōhaku)** (45-60 minutes) - Walk the boardwalk over incredible ancient Hawaiian rock art including post-western contact images such as a long rifle, western sailing vessels, konane game boards, and human figures with spears; 0.8-mile trail from visitor center to coastal petroglyph field; accessible during park hours 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily. [Source: [Big Island Hikes](https://bigislandhikes.com/kaloko-honokohau-park), [Tourist Secrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/hawaii/hidden-stories-of-hawaiis-ancient-petroglyphs/)]

- **Hawaiian Green Sea Turtle Viewing** (1-2 hours) - Observe federally protected young Hawaiian green sea turtles (honu) feeding nearshore or basking onshore along Honokōhau Beach; maintain 20-foot distance per federal law; beach accessible via 0.25-mile walk from Honokōhau Harbor parking or 1-mile trail from visitor center; daylight hours. [Source: [Go Hawaii](https://www.gohawaii.com/islands/hawaii-big-island/regions/kona/kaloko-honokohau-national-historical-park), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60872-d560882-Reviews-Kaloko_Honokohau_National_Historical_Park-Kailua_Kona_Island_of_Hawaii_Hawaii.html)]

- **Honokōhau Beach Swimming & Snorkeling** (1-2 hours) - Swim and snorkel in calm, clear waters protected by an offshore reef at this expansive gray and white sand beach; rocky entry requires reef shoes; access via Honokōhau Harbor (0.25-mile walk) or visitor center (1-mile trail); daylight hours, best conditions in morning. [Source: [Boss Frog's](https://bossfrog.com/kona/honokokau-beach/), [To-Hawaii.com](https://www.to-hawaii.com/big-island/beaches/honokohaubeach.php), [TripAdvisor FAQ](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g60872-d560882-t4126004-Is_there_snorkeling_at_this_beach_There_s_a_lot.html)]

- **Ala Kahakai Trail Hike** (2-3 hours) - Hike the 2.9-mile coastal trail with 118 feet elevation gain, rated 4.5 stars by 429 reviewers; experience ancient Hawaiian cultural sites, lava landscapes, and coastal views; sturdy shoes and water required; trailhead at Honokōhau Harbor or visitor center; 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily (gate hours). [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/hawaii/hawaii/kaloko-honokohau-national-historical-park), [Big Island Hikes](https://bigislandhikes.com/kaloko-honokohau-park)]

- **Cultural Programs** (1-2 hours) - Participate in Hawaiian cultural activities including Hawaiian games, hula lessons, bamboo stamping, 'ukulele lessons in the visitor center breezeway, and geology walks highlighting petroglyphs; check visitor center for daily schedule; Hale Ho'okipa Visitor Center, 73-4786 Kanalani St. #14, Kailua-Kona, HI 96740; 8:30 AM - 4:00 PM daily; 808-329-6881 x1329. [Source: [Go Hawaii](https://www.gohawaii.com/islands/hawaii-big-island/regions/kona/kaloko-honokohau-national-historical-park), [Hawaiian Airlines](https://www.hawaiianairlines.com/island-guide/hawaii-island/places/adventures/kaloko-honokohau)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Post-Western Contact Petroglyphs** (30-45 minutes) - Discover rare petroglyphs depicting western sailing vessels, long rifles, canons, and flintlock muskets near Honokōhau Harbor, representing unique historical documentation of first contact era; located mauka (mountain-side) from harbor; accessible during daylight hours; 40% of park petroglyphs depict women, double the typical 20% found elsewhere in Hawaii. [Source: [Big Island Hikes](https://bigislandhikes.com/kaloko-honokohau-park), [Tourist Secrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/hawaii/hidden-stories-of-hawaiis-ancient-petroglyphs/)]

- **Mysterious Lava Mounds** (20-30 minutes) - Explore large mounds of stacked lava rocks past the barren lava field looking mauka (toward mountains); purpose unknown but may have served as lookout points or held spiritual significance; accessible via main trail system; daylight hours. [Source: [Big Island Hikes](https://bigislandhikes.com/kaloko-honokohau-park)]

- **Secluded Northern Beach Stretch** (1-2 hours) - Hike past the main Honokōhau Beach heading north to find a quieter strip of beach for peaceful relaxation and turtle watching; less crowded than main beach area; accessed by continuing north on coastal trail from Honokōhau Beach; daylight hours. [Source: [Big Island Hikes](https://bigislandhikes.com/kaloko-honokohau-park)]

- **Konane Game Boards** (15-30 minutes) - Examine ancient Hawaiian game boards (similar to checkers) carved into lava rock near the harbor petroglyphs; cultural artifact showing recreational activities of ancient Hawaiians; located in petroglyph field mauka from Honokōhau Harbor; accessible during daylight hours. [Source: [Big Island Hikes](https://bigislandhikes.com/kaloko-honokohau-park)]

## Also Nearby

- **Kealakekua Bay & Captain Cook Monument Snorkeling** (3-6 hours, 12 miles south) - Snorkel at one of the Big Island's premier underwater destinations with 100+ feet visibility and abundant marine life at protected Kealakekua Bay Marine Life Conservation District; access via boat tour (45 minutes from Honokōhau Harbor), kayak (1.5 miles across bay), or 4-mile round-trip Ka'awaloa Trail hike; boat tours from Honokōhau Harbor, Keauhou Bay, or Kailua-Kona Pier; best conditions in morning; private tour operators. [Source: [Love Big Island](https://www.lovebigisland.com/kealakekua-bay/), [Hawaii Tour Boat](https://hawaiitourboat.com/kealakekua-bay-snorkel/), [Big Island Guide](https://bigislandguide.com/kealakekua-bay)]

- **Pu'uhonua o Hōnaunau National Historical Park** (2-3 hours, 25 miles south/30-40 minute drive) - Visit this well-restored Hawaiian "place of refuge" where ancient Hawaiians who broke kapu (sacred law) could seek absolution; features temple platforms, royal grounds, wooden ki'i (carved images), and excellent snorkeling at adjacent Two Step; 1871 Trail, Honaunau, HI 96726 (19.421, -155.907); 7:00 AM - 8:00 PM daily; $15 per vehicle (valid 7 days); 808-328-2288. [Source: [Love Big Island](https://www.lovebigisland.com/state-national-parks/puuhonua-o-honaunau/), [VELTRA](https://www.hawaiiactivities.com/en/hawaii/big_island/a/135873), [Thoroughly Travel](https://www.thoroughlytravel.com/puuhonua-o-honaunau-national-historical-park/)]

- **Kona Coffee Farm Tours** (1.5-3 hours, 10-20 miles south) - Tour working coffee farms along scenic country roads in the Kona Coffee Belt, taste world-famous 100% Kona coffee, and learn about cultivation and processing; options include Greenwell Farms (est. 1850, free tours, no reservations), Hula Daddy Kona Coffee (Holualoa, 3.2 miles), and Kona Coffee Living History Farm (Captain Cook, 12 miles); various hours, most free or low-cost. [Source: [Love Big Island](https://www.lovebigisland.com/kona-coffee-tasting/), [Love Big Island Activities](https://www.lovebigisland.com/kailua-kona/activities/), [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/usa/hawaii/captain-cook/attractions/kona-coffee-living-history-farm/a/poi-sig/454303/1333354)]

- **Two Step (Hōnaunau Bay) Snorkeling** (1-2 hours, 25 miles south/30-40 minute drive) - Snorkel at one of the Big Island's best shore-accessible snorkeling sites with easy entry via natural lava "steps" and abundant marine life; located 300 feet from Pu'uhonua o Hōnaunau entrance; Hōnaunau Bay, near 1871 Trail, Honaunau, HI 96726; daylight hours, best in morning; free public access. [Source: [Love Big Island](https://www.lovebigisland.com/state-national-parks/puuhonua-o-honaunau/), [VELTRA](https://www.hawaiiactivities.com/en/hawaii/big_island/a/135873)]
