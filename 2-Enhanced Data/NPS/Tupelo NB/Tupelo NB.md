# Tupelo NB (National Battlefield)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-natchez-trace"></a>**Natchez Trace Parkway Visitor Center** (2680 Natchez Trace Parkway, Tupelo, MS 38804 (34.294444, -88.746111); 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM daily except Thanksgiving, December 25, and January 1; 662-680-4027) - The official visitor center for Tupelo and Brices Cross Roads National Battlefields, located at Milepost 266 near the intersection of Highway 145 (Gloster St.) and the Parkway. [Source: [NPS Tupelo](https://www.nps.gov/tupe/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/tupe/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Natchez Trace Travel](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-mississippi/tupelo-ms/139-tupelo-visitors-center.html), [NPS Contacts](https://www.nps.gov/natr/contacts.htm)]

**Note:** Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. The Mississippi Hills National Heritage Area exhibit center across the street from the visitor center is also a National Parks Passport cancellation stamp station. [Source: [Mississippi Hills Heritage](https://mississippihills.org/passport/)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-battlefield-tour"></a>**Self-Guided Battlefield Walking Tour** (1 hour) - Walk across the one-acre commemorative site on Main Street to view the large stone monument dedicated to the 2,000 Confederate and Federal soldiers who perished during the Battle of Tupelo in July 1864, examine two authentic Civil War cannons and gravestones of 1st Lieutenant John Stone and 2nd Lieutenant William Cox, and read informational panels about the battle; 2005 W Main Street, Tupelo, MS 38801 (34.255655, -88.737221); grounds open dawn to dusk daily. [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/tupe/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/tupelo-national-battlefield/), [AAA](https://www.aaa.com/travelinfo/ms/tupelo/attractions/tupelo-national-battlefield-511991.html), [AllTopSights GPS](https://www.alltopsights.com/attraction-tupelo-national-battlefield-mississippi-ms/)]

- <a id="activity-visitor-center"></a>**Natchez Trace Parkway Visitor Center Tour** (30 minutes - 1 hour) - Visit the Parkway Visitor Center before touring the battlefield to view exhibits, watch a short film, learn about the 20,000 soldiers who fought in the battle, collect maps and brochures, and consult with rangers on duty; 2680 Natchez Trace Parkway, Tupelo, MS 38804 (34.294444, -88.746111); 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM daily except major holidays; free admission. [Source: [Backroad Planet](https://backroadplanet.com/brices-crossroads-tupelo-national-battlefield/), [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/tupe/planyourvisit/index.htm), [NatchezTrace Travel](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-mississippi/tupelo-ms/139-tupelo-visitors-center.html)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-confederate-graves"></a>**Confederate Soldier Graves** (15 minutes) - View the graves of two Confederate soldiers buried on the battlefield grounds, though the reason for their burial at this location remains unclear, as the area may have been part of the Old Harrisburg Cemetery; 2005 W Main Street, Tupelo, MS 38801 (34.255655, -88.737221); grounds open dawn to dusk daily. [Source: [Backroad Planet](https://backroadplanet.com/brices-crossroads-tupelo-national-battlefield/), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/tupelo-national-battlefield/)]

- <a id="gem-final-stands"></a>**Mississippi's Final Stands Interpretive Center** (1-1.5 hours) - Explore Smithsonian-quality exhibits, dioramas, and period artifacts at this locally-operated interpretive center that provides deeper context about the Civil War battles in the region; operated by a local Civil War board with visitor information desk, gift shop, and museum; contact local tourism for current hours and location. [Source: [Backroad Planet](https://backroadplanet.com/brices-crossroads-tupelo-national-battlefield/), [Final Stands](https://finalstands.com/battlegrounds/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-elvis"></a>**Elvis Presley Birthplace & Museum** (2-3 hours, 4 miles east) - Explore the 15-acre Elvis Presley Park including the two-room shotgun house where Elvis was born on January 8, 1935, a comprehensive museum chronicling his life, the "Elvis at 13" statue, Elvis' childhood church, a meditation chapel, landscaped walkways, and memorial fountain; 306 Elvis Presley Drive, Tupelo, MS 38804 (34.259971, -88.679963); Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM (grounds open 24 hours); $25 adults, $20 seniors. [Source: [Elvis Birthplace Visit](https://elvispresleybirthplace.com/visit/), [Midwest Wanderer](https://midwestwanderer.com/?p=18089), [Visit Tupelo](https://www.tupelo.net/directory/elvis-presley-birthplace-and-museum/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elvis_Presley_Birthplace)]

- <a id="nearby-chickasaw"></a>**Chickasaw Village Site** (30 minutes - 1 hour, 5 miles north) - Walk the 0.5-mile interpretive trail and hike portions of the 6-mile Blackland Prairie Trail at this Natchez Trace Parkway site believed to be the location of a Chickasaw fort and village from the 1700s, with informative signage about Chickasaw Nation history and plant uses by American Indians; Natchez Trace Parkway Milepost 261.8, Tupelo, MS (34.322000, -88.748000); open daily dawn to dusk; free admission. [Source: [Natchez Trace Travel](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-mississippi/tupelo-ms/141-chickasaw-village-site.html), [Visit Tupelo](https://www.tupelo.net/blog/seven-things-you-must-see-on-the-natchez-trace-parkway/), [Explore Southern History](https://www.exploresouthernhistory.com/chickasaw.html)]

- <a id="nearby-veterans"></a>**Tupelo Veterans Museum** (1-1.5 hours, 3 miles northeast) - View an extensive military collection spanning from the Civil War to modern conflicts including guns, swords, uniforms, maps, pictures, artifacts, and a document signed by Abraham Lincoln, shared by U.S. Army Veteran Tony Lute; 689 Rutherford Road, Tupelo, MS 38804 (34.2477, -88.7648); Tuesday-Friday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Saturday 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM; contact for admission fees. [Source: [Visit Tupelo](https://www.tupelo.net/directory/tupelo-veterans-museum/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/tupelo-veterans-museum-tupelo), [2Markers](https://vg.2markers.com/1305418)]

- <a id="nearby-buffalo"></a>**Tupelo Buffalo Park & Zoo** (2-3 hours, 7 miles northwest) - Experience a 210-acre park with over 260 animals including buffalo, with opportunities for feeding animals, bison tours, pony rides, and zip line activities; 2272 North Coley Road, Tupelo, MS 38803 (34.320000, -88.800000); Monday-Thursday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Friday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday 11:00 AM - 4:00 PM; $11 adults, $9 seniors. [Source: [Visit Tupelo](https://www.tupelo.net/directory/tupelo-buffalo-park-zoo/), [Tupelo Buffalo Park](https://www.tupelobuffalopark.com/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/tupelo-buffalo-park-and-zoo-tupelo)]

**Total Recommended Time:** 2-3 hours for the battlefield site and visitor center. The one-acre battlefield memorial requires approximately 1 hour for a thorough self-guided tour, while the Natchez Trace Parkway Visitor Center adds another 30 minutes to 1 hour for exhibits and orientation. Plan an additional 1-1.5 hours if visiting Mississippi's Final Stands Interpretive Center for deeper historical context.
