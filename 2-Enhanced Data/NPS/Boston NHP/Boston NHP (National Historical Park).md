## Key Activities

- **Freedom Trail Walking Tour** (2-6 hours) - Self-guided or ranger-led tour along the 2.5-mile red-lined route connecting 16 historically significant Revolutionary War sites from Boston Common to USS Constitution; starts at Boston Common Visitor Center, 139 Tremont Street, Boston, MA 02111; sites generally open 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; walking the trail itself takes 52 minutes but allow 4-6 hours to visit sites, or 2-3 full days for thorough exploration of all museums and tours [Source: [Sample Itineraries](https://www.thefreedomtrail.org/visit/sample-itineraries-resources), [Walk the Freedom Trail](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/walk-the-freedom-trail.htm), [Freedom Trail Tips](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/walking-freedom-trail-boston/)]

- **NPS Ranger-Guided Freedom Trail Tours** (60-90 minutes) - Free guided tours led by National Park Service rangers covering key downtown sites; depart from Faneuil Hall Visitor Center, 1 Faneuil Hall Square, Boston, MA 02109 [42.360031, -71.054749]; seasonally available, check with visitor center for schedule; 617-429-6760 [Source: [Faneuil Hall Visitor Center](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/massachusetts-national-parks/boston-national-historical-park-park-at-a-glance/boston-national-historical-park-visitor-centers/boston-national-historical-park-faneuil-hall-visitor-center/), [Freedom Trail FAQs](https://www.thefreedomtrail.org/faqs)]

- **USS Constitution Tour** (30-45 minutes ship tour, 1.5-3 hours total with museum) - Free guided tours aboard "Old Ironsides," the world's oldest commissioned warship still afloat, plus adjacent museum exhibits; Charlestown Navy Yard, Boston, MA 02129 [42.379360, -71.051057]; ship tours Friday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM, museum daily 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM; photo ID required for visitors 18+; closed Martin Luther King Jr.'s Birthday, Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day [Source: [USS Constitution Hours](https://www.navy.mil/USS-CONSTITUTION/Hours-Visitor-Info/), [USS Constitution Museum Plan Your Visit](https://ussconstitutionmuseum.org/plan-your-visit/), [Travel US News](https://travel.usnews.com/Boston_MA/Things_To_Do/USS_Constitution_Museum_64068/)]

- **Bunker Hill Monument Climb** (30 minutes round-trip for climb, 1-2 hours total) - Climb 294 steps to the top of the 221-foot granite obelisk commemorating the Battle of Bunker Hill; 43 Monument Square, Charlestown, MA 02129 [42.376354, -71.060776]; Wednesday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM summer (July 1-October 9), 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM standard season, closed Monday-Tuesday; free tickets required, available at Bunker Hill Museum across the street, limited to 20 people at a time [Source: [Bunker Hill Monument](https://www.yelp.com/biz/bunker-hill-monument-charlestown), [Travel US News](https://travel.usnews.com/Boston_MA/Things_To_Do/Bunker_Hill_Monument_64069/), [Bunker Hill Monument Guide](https://www.trolleytours.com/boston/bunker-hill-monument)]

- **Faneuil Hall & Quincy Market Visit** (1-2 hours) - Explore the historic "Cradle of Liberty" meeting hall and adjacent marketplace with shops, restaurants, and street performers; Faneuil Hall, 1 Faneuil Hall Square, Boston, MA 02109 [42.360031, -71.054749]; Great Hall typically open daily 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM, marketplace shops 10:00 AM - 9:00 PM; 617-429-6760 [Source: [Visit Faneuil Hall](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/visit-historic-faneuil-hall.htm), [Faneuil Hall Visitor Info](https://www.bostonteapartyship.com/boston-attractions/faneuil-hall)]

- **Old State House Museum Tour** (30-60 minutes) - Visit Boston's oldest public building (1713) and site of the Boston Massacre, featuring museum exhibits on Revolutionary history; 206 Washington St, Boston, MA 02109 [42.3587352, -71.057454]; Monday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM; adult admission $15 (combo ticket with Old South Meeting House available); accessible via State Street MBTA station [Source: [Old State House](https://www.yelp.com/biz/the-old-state-house-boston), [Old State House Hours](https://www.bostonhistory.org/hours-and-admission), [Old State House Museum Tips](https://freetoursbyfoot.com/old-state-house-museum/)]

- **Old South Meeting House Tour** (45-60 minutes) - Visit the site where the Boston Tea Party began in 1773, with docent-led tours and exhibits on colonial resistance; 310 Washington St, Boston, MA 02108 [42.3571796, -71.0584927]; daily 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM (in-season, last tour 5:00 PM), 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM November-March (last tour 4:00 PM); adult admission $15, combo ticket with Old State House available, tours limited to 20 people [Source: [Old South Meeting House Visitor Info](https://www.bostonteapartyship.com/boston-attractions/old-south-meeting-house), [Old South Meeting House Guide](https://www.trolleytours.com/boston/old-south-meeting-house)]

- **Paul Revere House Tour** (30-45 minutes) - Self-guided tour of Paul Revere's 1680 colonial home in the North End, Boston's oldest building; 19 North Square, Boston, MA 02113 [42.363724, -71.053689]; April 15-October 31 daily 10:00 AM - 5:15 PM, November 1-April 14 daily 10:00 AM - 4:15 PM, closed Mondays January-March and on Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve/Day, New Year's Day; adult admission $6, children 5-17 $1; 617-523-2338 [Source: [Paul Revere House Hours](https://www.paulreverehouse.org/hours-prices/), [Travel US News](https://travel.usnews.com/Boston_MA/Things_To_Do/Paul_Revere_House_52618/)]

- **Old North Church Visit** (20-45 minutes) - View the church where "one if by land, two if by sea" lanterns were hung in 1775 to warn of British troop movements; 193 Salem St, Boston, MA 02113 [42.3663259, -71.0543944]; Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday 12:30 PM - 5:00 PM, closed Monday; general admission $5, self-guided audio tour $7-8, Crypt Combo tour $9-10; closed Thanksgiving (November 26-27), modified hours December 24 and 31 (10:00 AM - 2:00 PM), closed December 25 and January 1 [Source: [Old North Church](https://www.oldnorth.com/), [Old North Church Admission](https://www.oldnorth.com/admission-pricing/), [Travel US News](https://travel.usnews.com/Boston_MA/Things_To_Do/Old_North_Church_52540/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **USS Cassin Young Destroyer Tour** (45 minutes below-deck guided tour, May 24-October 13 only) - Explore the WWII Fletcher-class destroyer with guided tours of engine room, crew quarters, mess, and officers' quarters; Charlestown Navy Yard, Boston, MA 02129 [42.379360, -71.051057]; guided tours Wednesday-Sunday at 11:00 AM, 2:00 PM, 3:00 PM (May 24-October 13), self-guided top deck access 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM Memorial Day through October; free admission, first-come first-served, limited to 12 people per tour, visitors must be over 42 inches tall to climb ladders [Source: [Tour USS Cassin Young](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/tour-uss-cassin-young.htm), [USS Cassin Young Museum](https://ussconstitutionmuseum.org/locations/uss-cassin-young/)]

- **Dorchester Heights Monument** (30-60 minutes, currently closed for restoration) - Visit the site where George Washington fortified positions to force British evacuation of Boston in 1776; Telegraph Hill, Thomas Park, South Boston, MA; currently closed for restoration through March 17, 2026, grounds of Thomas Park open for walking; free admission when reopened; tower observatory will be accessible for special events and as staffing allows [Source: [Dorchester Heights](https://www.nps.gov/bost/learn/historyculture/dohe.htm), [Dorchester Heights Restoration](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/gaoa-dorchester-heights-monument.htm)]

- **Granary Burying Ground** (20-30 minutes) - Historic cemetery (1660) containing graves of Paul Revere, Samuel Adams, John Hancock, and victims of the Boston Massacre; Tremont Street between Park Street and Beacon Street, Boston, MA 02108; daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; free admission; Freedom Trail site often overlooked by visitors focusing on indoor attractions [Source: [Freedom Trail](https://www.thefreedomtrail.org/), [Boston Discovery Guide](https://www.boston-discovery-guide.com/old-state-house.html)]

- **Boston Massacre Site Marker** (10-15 minutes) - Circle of cobblestones marking the location of the 1770 confrontation between colonists and British soldiers, directly in front of Old State House; intersection of State and Congress Streets, Boston, MA 02109 [42.3587352, -71.057454]; viewable 24/7; free; small ground marker easily missed by tourists rushing between larger sites [Source: [Old State House Freedom Trail](https://www.boston-discovery-guide.com/old-state-house.html)]

## Also Nearby

- **New England Aquarium** (2 hours, 3-4 hours with whale watch) - Major aquarium featuring Giant Ocean Tank, penguin colony, touch tanks, and seasonal whale watching tours; 1 Central Wharf, Boston, MA 02110; Monday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM; approximately 0.5 miles from Faneuil Hall, 10-minute walk along waterfront [Source: [New England Aquarium Visit](https://www.neaq.org/visit/), [Travel US News](https://travel.usnews.com/Boston_MA/Things_To_Do/New_England_Aquarium_38488/)]

- **Museum of Science** (3-5 hours recommended for thorough visit) - Interactive science museum with 700+ exhibits, live animals, IMAX theater, and planetarium; 1 Science Park, Boston, MA 02114; Exhibit Halls daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; approximately 2.5 miles from Faneuil Hall, accessible via MBTA Green Line; least crowded before 11:00 AM or after 4:00 PM [Source: [Museum of Science Hours](https://www.mos.org/visit/hours), [Museum of Science Visit Guide](https://www.trolleytours.com/boston/museum-of-science), [Travel US News](https://travel.usnews.com/Boston_MA/Things_To_Do/Museum_of_Science_31762/)]

- **Harvard University & Museums** (2-4 hours) - Tour historic Harvard Yard and visit Harvard Art Museums, Harvard Museum of Natural History, or Peabody Museum; Cambridge, MA (Harvard Square area); Harvard Art Museums Tuesday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM with free admission daily, Natural History Museum 8 minutes from Harvard Square Red Line; approximately 3 miles from downtown Boston, 15-20 minutes via MBTA Red Line [Source: [Harvard Museums Guide](https://oge.mit.edu/a-guide-to-museums-in-cambridge-and-boston/), [Cambridge Travel Guide](https://www.trolleytours.com/boston/cambridge)]

- **Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum** (2 hours) - Venetian-style palazzo housing eclectic art collection and stunning courtyard garden; 25 Evans Way, Boston, MA 02115; Monday, Wednesday, Friday 11:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Thursday 11:00 AM - 9:00 PM, Saturday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed Tuesday; approximately 2.5 miles from Faneuil Hall, accessible via MBTA Green Line to Museum of Fine Arts station; free first Thursday of month 3:00 PM - 9:00 PM [Source: [Gardner Museum Hours](https://www.gardnermuseum.org/visit/hours), [How Long at Gardner Museum](https://www.thetravel.com/how-long-does-it-take-to-walk-through-the-isabella-stewart-gardner-museum/)]

- **Salem Witch Museum & Historic Salem** (5 hours for day trip including transportation) - Day trip to Salem featuring witch trial history, museums, and historic sites; Salem, MA; accessible via ferry from Boston Harbor (passes harbor islands, docks in Salem) or train from Boston North Station (under 1 hour); includes Salem Witch Museum, Salem Witch Trial Memorial, Old Burying Point cemetery, Bewitched Statue, and Ropes Mansion from Hocus Pocus; approximately 16 miles from Boston [Source: [Salem Day Trip by Ferry](https://www.takewalks.com/boston-tours/salem-witches-day-tour/), [Salem Witch Trials Tour](https://www.viator.com/tours/Salem/Salem-Witch-Trials-from-Past-to-Present-Day-Tour-from-Boston/d22414-7167P80), [Salem Witch City Day Trip](https://www.viator.com/tours/Boston/Salem-Witch-City-Day-Trip-From-Boston/d678-3978TOUR5)]
