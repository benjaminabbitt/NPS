# Boston NHP (National Historical Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-faneuil-hall-visitor-center"></a>**Faneuil Hall Visitor Center** (1 Faneuil Hall Square, First Floor, Boston, MA 02109; 42.3600, -71.0562; 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, check website for days; Phone: 617-242-5601) - Main NPS visitor center in historic Faneuil Hall. Rangers provide information, maps, and brochures. Summer ranger-guided Freedom Trail tours depart here. Free admission. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="stamp-charlestown-navy-yard-visitor-center"></a>**Charlestown Navy Yard Visitor Center** (Building 5, Charlestown Navy Yard, Boston, MA 02129; 42.3730, -71.0554; Check website for hours; Phone: 617-242-5601) - Visitor center with exhibits, restrooms, and access to USS Constitution tours. Passport stamps available. Free admission. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

**Note:** Call Dial A Ranger (617-429-6760, 10am-5pm daily) or main line (617-242-5601) for current hours. Individual Freedom Trail sites have varying hours as many are operated by partners.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-freedom-trail-walking-tour"></a>**Freedom Trail Walking Tour** (2-4 hours) - Self-guided 2.5-mile walking trail marked by red line/bricks connecting 16 Revolutionary War-era historic sites from Boston Common to Bunker Hill. Audio tour available via NPS App. Start at Boston Common or Charlestown Navy Yard. Free. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-ranger-led-freedom-trail-tours"></a>**Ranger-Led Freedom Trail Tours** (1-1.5 hours) - Free guided tours covering sections of the Freedom Trail. Summer programs depart from Faneuil Hall. Check schedule for availability. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-uss-constitution"></a>**USS Constitution ("Old Ironsides")** (1-2 hours) - World's oldest commissioned warship afloat (launched 1797). Free tours led by active-duty Navy sailors. Charlestown Navy Yard. Photo ID required for adults. Check website for current tour times. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-uss-constitution-museum"></a>**USS Constitution Museum** (1-1.5 hours) - Free interactive museum adjacent to the ship with hands-on exhibits and artifacts. Donations appreciated. Charlestown Navy Yard. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-bunker-hill-monument"></a>**Bunker Hill Monument** (45 minutes - 1 hour) - 294-step climb to panoramic views from the 221-foot granite obelisk commemorating the 1775 battle. Free admission. Bunker Hill Museum across the street has exhibits. Monument Square, Charlestown. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-uss-cassin-young"></a>**USS Cassin Young** (30-45 minutes) - WWII Fletcher-class destroyer museum ship at Charlestown Navy Yard. Ranger-led tours available. Free admission. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-old-north-church"></a>**Old North Church** (30-45 minutes) - Where Robert Newman hung the lanterns signaling Paul Revere on April 18, 1775 ("One if by land, two if by sea"). 193 Salem Street, North End. Operated by partner; admission fee for Behind the Scenes tour. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-paul-revere-house"></a>**Paul Revere House** (30-45 minutes) - Oldest remaining residential building in downtown Boston (c. 1680), home to Paul Revere during his midnight ride. 19 North Square, North End. Operated by partner; admission fee. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-old-south-meeting-house"></a>**Old South Meeting House** (30-60 minutes) - Site where colonists gathered before the Boston Tea Party (December 16, 1773). 310 Washington Street. Operated by partner; admission fee. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-old-state-house"></a>**Old State House** (30-60 minutes) - Boston's oldest public building where the Declaration of Independence was first read aloud to Bostonians (July 18, 1776). Site of the Boston Massacre (1770). 206 Washington Street. Operated by partner; admission fee. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-faneuil-hall"></a>**Faneuil Hall** (15-30 minutes) - "The Cradle of Liberty" where Samuel Adams and others debated independence. NPS visitor center on first floor; historic Great Hall upstairs. 1 Faneuil Hall Square. Free. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-dorchester-heights"></a>**Dorchester Heights** (30-45 minutes) - Revolutionary War site where Washington's forces placed cannons overlooking Boston Harbor, forcing British evacuation. Thomas Park, South Boston. Monument and views. Less visited than downtown sites. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="gem-granary-burying-ground"></a>**Granary Burying Ground** (20-30 minutes) - Final resting place of Samuel Adams, John Hancock, Paul Revere, and victims of the Boston Massacre. Tremont Street near Park Street Church. Free. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/index.htm)]

- <a id="gem-king-s-chapel-and-burying-ground"></a>**King's Chapel and Burying Ground** (30-45 minutes) - Boston's first Anglican church and oldest burying ground (1630). Tremont Street. Free to enter; donation requested. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/index.htm)]

- <a id="gem-copp-s-hill-burying-ground"></a>**Copp's Hill Burying Ground** (20-30 minutes) - North End cemetery with views of Charlestown. British cannon positions during Battle of Bunker Hill. Free. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/index.htm)]

- <a id="gem-virtual-freedom-trail-tour"></a>**Virtual Freedom Trail Tour** - Online virtual tour option available through NPS website for those unable to visit in person or for pre-visit planning. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-boston-african-american-nhs"></a>**Boston African American NHS** (2-3 hours, Beacon Hill) - Black Heritage Trail exploring Boston's free Black community. Adjacent to/overlapping with Freedom Trail. [Source: [NPS Boston African American](https://www.nps.gov/boaf/index.htm)]

- <a id="nearby-boston-harbor-islands-nra"></a>**Boston Harbor Islands NRA** (half-day+, ferry from Long Wharf) - 34 islands in Boston Harbor with forts, beaches, and hiking. Seasonal ferry service. [Source: [NPS Boston Harbor Islands](https://www.nps.gov/boha/index.htm)]

- <a id="nearby-new-england-aquarium"></a>**New England Aquarium** (2-3 hours, adjacent) - Major aquarium on Central Wharf near Freedom Trail's waterfront section. [Source: New England Aquarium]

- <a id="nearby-boston-tea-party-ships-museum"></a>**Boston Tea Party Ships & Museum** (1-2 hours, Congress Street Bridge) - Interactive museum with replica ships and tea party reenactment. Seaport. Admission fee. [Source: Boston Tea Party Ships & Museum]

- <a id="nearby-massachusetts-state-house"></a>**Massachusetts State House** (30-60 minutes, Beacon Hill) - Gold-domed seat of state government with free tours. Near Freedom Trail start. [Source: Visit Boston]

## Total Recommended Time

**Total recommended visit time for Boston NHP: Half-day to full day**

A half-day allows walking the Freedom Trail with exterior views and one or two site interiors. A full day enables thorough exploration including USS Constitution, Bunker Hill climb, and multiple historic site interiors. Combine with Boston African American NHS for a comprehensive day exploring American history. Note that many partner sites charge admission; NPS sites (Faneuil Hall, Navy Yard, Bunker Hill) are free.

[Sources: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/index.htm), [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/bost/planyourvisit/index.htm)]
