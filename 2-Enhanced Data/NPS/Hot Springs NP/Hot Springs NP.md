# Hot Springs NP (National Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-fordyce-bathhouse-visitor-center"></a>**Fordyce Bathhouse Visitor Center** (369 Central Avenue, Hot Springs, AR 71901; 34.5215, -93.0422; 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily; 501-624-2701) - The NPS passport stamp is located at the visitor center inside the Fordyce Bathhouse, the largest bathhouse on Bathhouse Row. [Source: [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/hosp/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** Call 501-620-6715 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting, as bathhouse operations may change seasonally.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-soak-in-historic-bathhouses-buckstaff-bathhouse"></a>**Soak in Historic Bathhouses - Buckstaff Bathhouse** (1.5 hours average) - Experience traditional hydrotherapy treatments at the only bathhouse offering the complete historic bathing ritual since 1912, featuring whirlpool, sitz bath, sauna/steam cabinet, hot packs, and optional massage ($38-$71 depending on package); walk-ins only, no reservations; 509 Central Avenue, Hot Springs, AR 71901 (34.5195, -93.0448); Open daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; 501-623-2308 [Source: [Buckstaff Baths](https://buckstaffbaths.com/services), [NPS Buckstaff](https://www.nps.gov/hosp/learn/historyculture/buckstaff-bathhouse.htm)]

- <a id="activity-soak-in-modern-thermal-pools-quapaw-baths-spa"></a>**Soak in Modern Thermal Pools - Quapaw Baths & Spa** (2 hours) - Relax in four large communal thermal pools with different temperature settings, using mineral-rich water heated naturally by the park's 47 thermal springs; spa services including massages and facials also available ($25 per person for unlimited soaking time); 413 Central Avenue, Hot Springs, AR 71901 (34.5182, -93.0468); Open Wednesday-Monday 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM (closed Tuesdays); 501-624-9564 [Source: [Quapaw Baths](https://quapawbaths.com/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60856-d672213-Reviews-Quapaw_Baths_Spa-Hot_Springs_Arkansas.html)]

- <a id="activity-tour-the-fordyce-bathhouse-museum"></a>**Tour the Fordyce Bathhouse Museum** (1-2 hours) - Explore the beautifully restored 28,000-square-foot Gilded Age bathhouse featuring three floors, ornate stained-glass ceilings, ceramic fountains, intricately tiled floors, and historical exhibits; self-guided tours available (30 minutes minimum, 1-2 hours recommended) with free access to visitor center; ranger talks available throughout the day; 369 Central Avenue, Hot Springs, AR 71901 (34.5215, -93.0422); Open daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; 501-624-2701 [Source: [NPS Fordyce](https://www.nps.gov/hosp/planyourvisit/fordyce-bathhouse.htm), [US News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/Hot_Springs_AR/Things_To_Do/Fordyce_Bathhouse_Visitor_Center_64674/)]

- <a id="activity-hike-hot-springs-mountain-trail"></a>**Hike Hot Springs Mountain Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - A popular 1.7-mile loop ascending Hot Springs Mountain with scenic views of the Ouachita Mountains and Bathhouse Row; start directly from downtown near Bathhouse Row; trail accessible year-round; rated moderate difficulty with approximately 380 feet elevation gain [Source: [NPS Trails](https://www.nps.gov/hosp/planyourvisit/trails.htm), [AllTrails](https://alltrails.com/parks/us/arkansas/hot-springs-national-park)]

- <a id="activity-visit-hot-springs-mountain-tower-observation-decks"></a>**Visit Hot Springs Mountain Tower Observation Decks** (45 minutes to 1.5 hours) - Ride elevator 216 feet to panoramic observation decks offering 140-square-mile views of the Ouachita Mountains and surrounding park; two observation levels include an open-air upper deck (1,256 feet elevation) and enclosed, air-conditioned lower deck with historical exhibits; free parking at tower base or optional 1.5-mile hiking trail from downtown; $13 adults, $9 children (5-11), $11 seniors (55+); open daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (November-February) and 9:00 AM - 8:00 PM (March-October) [Source: [Hot Springs Tower](https://hotspringstower.com/), [US News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/Hot_Springs_AR/Things_To_Do/Hot_Springs_Mountain_Tower_64679/)]

- <a id="activity-hike-west-mountain-trail"></a>**Hike West Mountain Trail** (1.5-3.3 hours) - Two options: a 1.3-mile loop with steady uphill climb offering views of the Ouachita Mountains, or an extended 3.3-mile moderate-to-strenuous loop ascending to West Mountain Summit; accessible from Bathhouse Row area [Source: [Hot Springs Blog](https://www.hotsprings.org/blog/take-a-hike-top-10-hikes-to-explore-in-hot-springs-arkansas/)]

- <a id="activity-enjoy-the-grand-promenade"></a>**Enjoy the Grand Promenade** (45 minutes to 1.5 hours) - Walk the scenic, self-guided historic pathway starting behind Bathhouse Row featuring natural beauty, historical significance, views of thermal springs and historic architecture; multiple short historic trails depart from this location [Source: [Hot Springs Blog](https://www.hotsprings.org/blog/top-10-adventures-off-the-beaten-path-hot-springs-national-park-arkansas/)]

- <a id="activity-touch-thermal-water-at-natural-springs"></a>**Touch Thermal Water at Natural Springs** (30 minutes) - Visit the Display Spring directly behind the Maurice Bathhouse or the Hot Water Cascade at Arlington Lawn (the park's largest visible thermal spring) to touch naturally heated water; free thermal water fountains available throughout downtown for filling bottles [Source: [NPS Soak](https://www.nps.gov/hosp/planyourvisit/soak-in-the-springs.htm)]

- <a id="activity-hike-north-mountain-trail"></a>**Hike North Mountain Trail** (45 minutes to 1 hour) - A moderate 1.1-mile trail featuring interesting geological formations and views of the Ouachita Mountains; rated as one of the most geologically interesting hikes in the park [Source: [The Wander Club](https://thewanderclub.com/blogs/blog/things-to-do-in-hot-springs-national-park)]

- <a id="activity-hike-sunset-trail"></a>**Hike Sunset Trail** (4-6 hours for full trail) - The park's longest trail at 10 miles one-way, snaking around the majority of Hot Springs National Park with varied elevation gain; beginning at Gulpha Gorge Campground and ending at West Mountain Summit; excellent for experienced hikers seeking solitude; can be hiked in sections [Source: [Hot Springs Blog](https://www.hotsprings.org/blog/take-a-hike-top-10-hikes-to-explore-in-hot-springs-arkansas/), [NPS Trails](https://www.nps.gov/hosp/planyourvisit/trails.htm)]

- <a id="activity-hike-peak-trail-to-hot-springs-mountain-loop"></a>**Hike Peak Trail to Hot Springs Mountain Loop** (1.5-2 hours) - A 2.9-mile combined hike: Peak Trail (0.6 miles) ascends directly to Hot Springs Mountain, then connects to the 1.7-mile Hot Springs Mountain Loop; moderate difficulty with rewarding panoramic views [Source: [Komoot](https://www.komoot.com/guide/1843942/hiking-in-hot-springs-national-park)]

- <a id="activity-hike-oertel-gulpha-gorge-loop"></a>**Hike Oertel/Gulpha Gorge Loop** (2.5-3 hours) - A dynamic 5.4-mile loop offering steep sections and varied terrain; starts on Oertel Trail climbing one of the steepest sections in the park, then circles Hot Springs Mountain [Source: [Hot Springs Blog](https://www.hotsprings.org/blog/take-a-hike-top-10-hikes-to-explore-in-hot-springs-arkansas/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-superior-bathhouse-brewery"></a>**Superior Bathhouse Brewery** (1.5-2.5 hours) - The only brewery located within a U.S. National Park, housed in a beautifully restored 1916 bathhouse and using natural 144-degree thermal spring water to brew 18 craft beers on tap; sample all beers via a "Beer Bath" tasting, enjoy farm-to-table cuisine, and experience the historic interior transformed into a brewery; dog and family-friendly with indoor and outdoor seating; 329 Central Avenue, Hot Springs, AR 71901 (34.5149, -93.0536); Monday-Saturday 11:00 AM - 9:00 PM (Friday-Saturday until 10:00 PM), Sunday 11:00 AM - 9:00 PM, closed Tuesdays [Source: [Superior Bathhouse](https://www.superiorbathhouse.com), [Arkansas.com](https://www.arkansas.com/articles/craft-beer-superior-bathhouse-brewery-hot-springs-national-park)]

- <a id="gem-northwoods-trail-system-mountain-biking-hiking"></a>**Northwoods Trail System Mountain Biking & Hiking** (2-4 hours depending on route) - Over 31 miles of world-class mountain biking and hiking trails with varying difficulty; Waterworks Trailhead at 300 Pineland Drive, Hot Springs, AR 71913 (34.4825, -93.0625) features parking on serene Bethel Lake shore and represents the main trailhead system for Northwoods [Source: [Hot Springs Blog](https://www.hotsprings.org/blog/top-10-adventures-off-the-beaten-path-hot-springs-national-park-arkansas/)]

- <a id="gem-tiny-town-model-railroad"></a>**Tiny Town Model Railroad** (1-1.5 hours) - A sprawling, interactive miniature railroad meticulously handcrafted over 75 years by the Moshinskie family using homemade materials like sawdust, paper clips, toothpicks, and matches; depicts animated scenes from across the United States in incredible detail [Source: [Atlas Obscura](https://www.atlasobscura.com/things-to-do/hot-springs-arkansas)]

- <a id="gem-entergy-park-nature-loop"></a>**Entergy Park Nature Loop** (1-1.5 hours) - A hidden 30-acre nature oasis on Lake Hamilton featuring a 2.3-mile scenic loop trail, picnic areas, two playgrounds, and fishing access; connects to Sonnybrook Landing boat launch and fishing pier [Source: [Hot Springs Blog](https://www.hotsprings.org/blog/top-10-adventures-off-the-beaten-path-hot-springs-national-park-arkansas/)]

- <a id="gem-rooftop-dining-experience-the-waters-hotel"></a>**Rooftop Dining Experience - The Waters Hotel** (1.5-2 hours) - The Rooftop Bar at The Waters Hotel offers spectacular panoramic views of Hot Springs and the surrounding mountains from atop one of downtown's tallest historic buildings; 340 Central Avenue, Hot Springs, AR 71901 (34.5153, -93.0521); best experienced at sunset or night with string lights and moonlight illuminating the cityscape [Source: [Atlas Obscura](https://www.atlasobscura.com/things-to-do/hot-springs-arkansas)]

- <a id="gem-mountain-valley-spring-water-visitor-center-historic-building"></a>**Mountain Valley Spring Water Visitor Center & Historic Building** (45 minutes) - Tour the 1910 Classic Revival headquarters building on the National Register of Historic Places, featuring towering period-authentic columns and historical exhibits about the famous spring water company; located within Hot Springs National Park near Bathhouse Row; 150 Central Avenue, Hot Springs, AR 71901 (34.5232, -93.0403); Open Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM [Source: [Mountain Valley Spring](https://www.mountainvalleyspring.com/pages/visitor-center)]

- <a id="gem-babe-ruth-baseball-history-majestic-park"></a>**Babe Ruth Baseball History - Majestic Park** (30 minutes) - Visit the statue commemorating the exact location where Babe Ruth played during his 1910s spring training visits to Hot Springs [Source: [Atlas Obscura](https://www.atlasobscura.com/things-to-do/hot-springs-arkansas)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-lake-ouachita-state-park"></a>**Lake Ouachita State Park** (20 minutes driving; 15 miles northwest from downtown Hot Springs) - Arkansas's largest lake with crystal-clear waters, featuring boating, fishing, scuba diving, swimming, and water sports in 40,000 acres with 975 miles of pristine shoreline; hiking trails include the 4-mile Caddo Bend Trail with observation deck views over the lake (approximately 3 hours for full trail); winter eagle watch tours (December-February) to view bald eagle nesting; full-service marina with boat and kayak rentals; visitor center and Three Sisters Springs healing springs; 5451 Mountain Pine Road, Mountain Pine, AR 71956 (34.5461, -93.1889) [Source: [Lake Ouachita](https://lakeouachita.org/), [US News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/Hot_Springs_AR/Things_To_Do/Lake_Ouachita_State_Park_64676/)]

- <a id="nearby-garvan-woodland-gardens"></a>**Garvan Woodland Gardens** (8 miles south; 15-20 minutes driving) - University of Arkansas botanical garden featuring 210 acres on Lake Hamilton shoreline with thousands of plant species, seasonal displays (tulips in spring, 5-million-light holiday display in winter), walking paths, observation points, and garden structures; plan for 2-3 hours minimum; golf cart rentals available; $25 adults, $15 children (4-12), free for age 3 and under; 550 Arkridge Road, Hot Springs, AR 71913 (34.4347, -93.0456); Open daily 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM (closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and January) [Source: [Garvan Gardens](https://www.garvangardens.org/), [US News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/Hot_Springs_AR/Things_To_Do/Garvan_Woodland_Gardens_64680/)]

- <a id="nearby-magic-springs-theme-water-park"></a>**Magic Springs Theme & Water Park** (within city limits; 10-15 minutes from park) - Arkansas's premier amusement park featuring rides for all ages, water park (Crystal Falls) with multiple slide towers and tube slides, concerts at Timberwood Amphitheater; seasonal operation; visit website for current hours and admission prices; 1701 East Grand Avenue, Hot Springs, AR 71901 (34.5324, -93.1148); Phone: 501-624-0100 [Source: [Hot Springs Blog](https://www.hotsprings.org/blog/your-guide-to-magic-springs-theme-water-park/)]

- <a id="nearby-ouachita-national-forest"></a>**Ouachita National Forest** (adjacent to park) - 1.7-million-acre mountainous forest spanning western Arkansas and eastern Oklahoma offering camping, hiking, biking, scenic driving, trail riding, water recreation, fishing, and hunting; numerous trailheads accessible within 30-60 minutes of downtown Hot Springs [Source: [NPS Nearby](https://www.nps.gov/hosp/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm)]

- <a id="nearby-oaklawn-racing-casino-resort"></a>**Oaklawn Racing Casino Resort** (within city; 10-15 minutes) - Historic racing venue operating since 1905, featuring premier thoroughbred racing, casino gaming, restaurants, and entertainment events [Source: [NPS Nearby](https://www.nps.gov/hosp/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm)]

## Review / Personal Notes

---

**Total Recommended Time for Hot Springs NP:** Plan for a minimum of 4-6 hours for a focused visit including one bathhouse experience (1.5-2 hours), Fordyce Bathhouse Museum (1-2 hours), and one hiking trail (1.5-3 hours). A full day (8-10 hours) allows for bathing, museum exploration, multiple hiking trails, tower visit, and appreciation of historic Bathhouse Row architecture. Multi-day visits enable comprehensive exploration of all 26 miles of trails and additional activities. Note: Hot Springs NP is unique as a completely free park with no entrance fees; only campground facilities ($34/night) and paid activities (bathhouses, tower) require payment. The park is open year-round 5:00 AM - 10:00 PM daily; however, Fordyce Visitor Center operates 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM. [Source: [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/hosp/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [How Life USA](https://travel.howlifeusa.com/hot-springs/)]
