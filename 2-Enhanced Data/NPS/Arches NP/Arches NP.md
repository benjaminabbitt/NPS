# Arches NP (National Park)

## Key Activities

- **Hike to Delicate Arch** (2-3 hours) - Complete the park's most iconic 3.2-mile round-trip hike across sun-exposed slickrock with steep sections to reach Utah's famous freestanding 65-foot arch featured on license plates, offering unbeatable views after 1.5 miles of moderately difficult hiking; trailhead at Wolfe Ranch, 13.4 miles from park entrance on Arches Scenic Drive, Moab, UT 84532; trail open 24/7, but best during spring (April-May) and fall (September-October) when temperatures range from 60°F to 80°F; 435-719-2299 [Source: [NPS Delicate Arch](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/delicate-arch.htm), [Discover Moab](https://www.discovermoab.com/arches-national-park/delicate-arch/), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g143011-d117172-Reviews-Delicate_Arch-Arches_National_Park_Utah.html), [National Park Magazine](https://nationalparkmagazine.com/2025/04/29/delicate-arch-hike-arches-national-park-guide/)] [324cf8]

- **Explore The Windows Section** (1-2 hours) - Walk easy trails totaling 1.1 miles to see some of the park's largest arches including North Window, South Window, Turret Arch, and Double Arch, with the Windows Loop taking 30-60 minutes and Double Arch trail adding another 15-30 minutes; Windows Section located 12 miles from park entrance (9 miles on Main Park Road, then 2.5 miles east on Windows Road), Moab, UT 84532; open 24/7; 435-719-2299 [Source: [NPS Windows Section](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/the-windows.htm), [AllTrails Windows Loop](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/windows-loop-and-turret-arch-trail), [NPS Double Arch](https://www.nps.gov/places/double-arch.htm), [Dueling Journeys](https://www.duelingjourneys.com/travel/hike-the-windows-section-arches-national-park)] [799202]

- **Hike Devils Garden to Landscape Arch** (1-5 hours depending on distance) - Walk the easy 1.6-mile round-trip trail to see the longest arch span in North America at Landscape Arch (1 hour), or continue on the strenuous 7.8-mile primitive loop to see eight arches including Double O Arch (4-5 hours total); Devils Garden Trailhead at end of Arches Scenic Drive, 18 miles from park entrance, Moab, UT 84532; open 24/7; 435-719-2299 [Source: [NPS Devils Garden](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/devils-garden.htm), [Earth Trekkers Devils Garden](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/devils-garden-trail-best-hike-in-arches-national-park/), [AllTrails Devils Garden](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/devils-garden-loop-trail-with-7-arches), [Ulum Devils Garden Guide](https://www.ulumresorts.com/stories/guide-to-devils-garden-in-arches-national-park/)] [ccdbac]

- **Walk Park Avenue Trail** (30-60 minutes) - Hike an easy 1.8-mile round-trip trail (or 1 mile one-way with shuttle) through a canyon flanked by towering sandstone monoliths resembling a city skyline, with formations named The Organ, Tower of Babel, and The Three Gossips visible from the trail; Park Avenue Trailhead located 2.2 miles from park entrance on Arches Scenic Drive, Moab, UT 84532; open 24/7; 435-719-2299 [Source: [NPS Park Avenue](https://www.nps.gov/places/park-avenue-viewpoint-and-trailhead.htm), [AllTrails Park Avenue](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/park-avenue), [Utah.com Park Avenue](https://www.utah.com/destinations/national-parks/arches-national-park/things-to-do/hiking/park-avenue-trail/), [TripAdvisor Park Avenue](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g143011-d144935-Reviews-Park_Avenue_Trail-Arches_National_Park_Utah.html)] [741501]

- **Drive Arches Scenic Drive with Viewpoint Stops** (1.5-2 hours without hikes, full day with stops) - Complete the 36-mile round-trip scenic drive along the main park road featuring numerous pull-offs and viewpoints including Courthouse Towers (5-10 minutes), Balanced Rock viewpoint (5-15 minutes), Garden of Eden viewpoint (10-15 minutes), and Panorama Point (10-15 minutes); starts at park entrance, Moab, UT 84532; road open 24/7; timed entry tickets required 7:00 AM - 4:00 PM April 1-July 6 and August 28-October 31, 2025 ($2 fee); 435-719-2299 [Source: [Full Suitcase Scenic Drive](https://fullsuitcase.com/arches-scenic-drive/), [Organized Adventurer Scenic Drive](https://organizedadventurer.com/arches-scenic-drive/), [NPS Viewpoints](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/viewpoints.htm), [Moab Adventure Center](https://www.moabadventurecenter.com/arches-national-park/driving)] [59b16f]

- **Walk Balanced Rock Loop Trail** (15-30 minutes) - Hike an easy 0.3-mile paved loop around the base of the iconic 128-foot-tall Balanced Rock featuring a 3,600-ton boulder perched atop a pedestal, with views of The Windows section and La Sal Mountains; trailhead at Balanced Rock parking area, 9.2 miles from park entrance on Arches Scenic Drive, Moab, UT 84532; open 24/7; 435-719-2299 [Source: [NPS Balanced Rock](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/balancedrock.htm), [AllTrails Balanced Rock](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/balanced-rock-loop-trail), [Utah's Adventure Family Balanced Rock](https://www.utahsadventurefamily.com/balanced-rock-at-arches-national-park/), [Dueling Journeys Balanced Rock](https://www.duelingjourneys.com/travel/hike-balanced-rock-arches-national-park)] [2a71eb]

- **Visit Wolfe Ranch and Petroglyph Panel** (30-60 minutes) - Walk an easy 0.5-mile loop trail to explore the historic 1898 homestead cabin where John Wesley Wolfe and his family lived, then continue 0.2 miles further to see Ute petroglyphs depicting people on horseback created between 1650-1850 CE; trailhead at Wolfe Ranch parking area, 11.7 miles from park entrance on Arches Scenic Drive, Moab, UT 84532; open 24/7; 435-719-2299 [Source: [Hike Utah Wolfe Ranch](https://hike-utah.com/wolferanchandpetroglyphs/), [AllTrails Wolfe Ranch](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/wolfe-ranch-and-petroglyph-trail), [10 Adventures Wolfe Ranch](https://www.10adventures.com/hikes/moab-and-arches-national-park/wolfe-ranch-and-petroglyph-trail/), [TripAdvisor Wolfe Ranch](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g143011-d7807986-Reviews-Wolfe_Ranch_Rock_Art-Arches_National_Park_Utah.html)] [4ab5fa]

- **Hike to Pine Tree Arch and Tunnel Arch** (1-1.5 hours) - Walk a moderate 2.8-mile round-trip trail from Devils Garden parking area to reach two beautiful arches, with Tunnel Arch visible after 0.7 miles (21 minutes) and Pine Tree Arch appearing shortly after along the same path; Devils Garden Trailhead at end of Arches Scenic Drive, 18 miles from park entrance, Moab, UT 84532; open 24/7; 435-719-2299 [Source: [AllTrails Pine Tree Arch](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/wall-arch-and-pine-tree-arch), [AllTrails Tunnel Arch](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/tunnel-arch-trail--4)] [722511]

- **Watch Sunset at Delicate Arch Viewpoint** (30-45 minutes) - View Delicate Arch from a distance without the strenuous hike, with two viewpoints accessible via short walks: Lower Viewpoint (100 yards from parking, wheelchair accessible) and Upper Viewpoint (0.5-mile walk with stairs), both offering spectacular sunset views of the arch framed against the La Sal Mountains; Delicate Arch Viewpoint parking area, 13 miles from park entrance on Arches Scenic Drive, Moab, UT 84532; open 24/7; 435-719-2299 [Source: [NPS Delicate Arch](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/delicate-arch.htm), [Full Suitcase Delicate Arch Viewpoint](https://fullsuitcase.com/delicate-arch-viewpoint/)] [7f0f37]

- **Stargaze at Panorama Point or The Windows** (1-3 hours) - Experience world-class stargazing at this International Dark Sky Park, with ranger-led night sky programs offered May through October (1-3 hours) typically starting at Panorama Point or the Visitor Center; best stargazing spots include Panorama Point, The Windows Section, Balanced Rock picnic area, and Garden of Eden viewpoint; Panorama Point located 9.5 miles from park entrance, The Windows 12 miles from entrance, Moab, UT 84532; park open 24/7, ranger programs seasonal; 435-719-2299 [Source: [NPS Stargazing](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/stargazing.htm), [Park Chasers Stargazing](https://www.parkchasers.com/2021/07/the-5-best-spots-for-stargazing-in-arches-national-park/), [Get Nomad Stargazing](https://www.getnomad.app/destination-guides/stargazing-in-arches-national-park), [NPS Ranger Programs](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/programs.htm)] [52cb5a]

## Hidden Gems

- **Ranger-Guided Fiery Furnace Tour** (2.5-3 hours) - Join a ranger-led exploration through a maze of narrow sandstone canyons and fins in one of the park's most spectacular and restricted areas, requiring scrambling and route-finding through passages that aren't marked trails; Fiery Furnace viewpoint located 14.5 miles from park entrance on Arches Scenic Drive, Moab, UT 84532; tours offered seasonally spring through fall, reservations required through Recreation.gov starting in February, book well in advance; 435-719-2299 [Source: [NPS Fiery Furnace](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/fiery-furnace-hike.htm), [Recreation.gov Tours](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/234668/ticket/116), [Fiery Furnace Tour Guide](https://fieryfurnacetour.com/arches-national-park-fiery-furnace/), [My Utah Parks](https://www.myutahparks.com/things-to-do/hiking-trails/arches-fiery-furnace/)] [b952ca]

- **Hike to Tower Arch** (2-3 hours) - Trek a less-traveled 3.4-mile round-trip moderate trail through a remote section of the park to reach the impressive 92-foot Tower Arch, where you'll likely have this prehistoric natural wonder to yourself; Tower Arch trailhead in Klondike Bluffs area accessed via rough, unpaved Salt Valley Road (high-clearance vehicle recommended), approximately 23 miles from park entrance, Moab, UT 84532; trail open 24/7; 435-719-2299 [Source: [Organized Adventurer Tower Arch](https://organizedadventurer.com/tower-arch-hike/), [PMags Hidden Arches](https://pmags.com/the-obscure-arches-national-park), [TripAdvisor Tower Arch](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g143011-d7762109-Reviews-Tower_Arch-Arches_National_Park_Utah.html), [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)] [4d84a1]

- **Sand Dune Arch and Broken Arch Loop** (1-2 hours) - Follow a short 0.4-mile trail through deep sand to discover Sand Dune Arch hidden between towering sandstone walls, then continue on a primitive trail connecting to Broken Arch for a 2.8-mile loop passing through Devils Garden campground; Sand Dune Arch trailhead located off Arches Scenic Drive near Devils Garden, 16 miles from park entrance, Moab, UT 84532; open 24/7; 435-719-2299 [Source: [NPS Sand Dune Arch](https://www.nps.gov/places/sand-dune-arch-trail.htm), [Utah Outdoor Activities](https://www.utahoutdooractivities.com/sandbrokenarch.html), [Camping Kiddos Trail Guide](https://www.campingkiddos.com/blog/sand-dune-and-broken-arch-trail-guide), [AllTrails Sand Dune](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/sand-dune-arch)] [1f474b]

## Also Nearby

- **Dead Horse Point State Park** (1-2 hours for viewpoint and short trails) - Experience one of the most photographed overlooks in the world with dramatic views 2,000 feet down to the Colorado River's gooseneck bend and Canyonlands below; 313 State Highway 313, Moab, UT 84532 (approximately 45 minutes/33 miles from Moab, 9 miles off Highway 191 via Highway 313); park open 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM daily, Visitor Center open 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM (summer), 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (winter), closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; $20 per vehicle day-use fee; 435-259-2614 [Source: [Utah State Parks Dead Horse](https://stateparks.utah.gov/parks/dead-horse/), [Discover Moab Dead Horse](https://www.discovermoab.com/dead-horse-point-state-park/), [Utah.com Dead Horse](https://www.utah.com/destinations/state-parks/dead-horse-point-state-park/)] [d24df3]

- **Canyonlands National Park - Island in the Sky District with Mesa Arch** (3-4 hours for district highlights) - Drive 40 minutes from Moab to hike the easy 0.5-mile Mesa Arch trail offering an iconic sunrise photo opportunity, then explore additional overlooks like Grand View Point providing sweeping vistas of the canyon country below; Island in the Sky Visitor Center located at end of Highway 313, approximately 32 miles from Moab (40-50 minutes drive), Moab, UT 84532; park open 24/7, entrance fee $30 per vehicle valid for 7 days and covers both Canyonlands and Arches; 435-719-2313 [Source: [NPS Island in the Sky](https://www.nps.gov/cany/planyourvisit/islandinthesky.htm), [AllTrails Mesa Arch](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/mesa-arch), [Full Suitcase Mesa Arch](https://fullsuitcase.com/mesa-arch/), [We're in the Rockies](https://wereintherockies.com/mesa-arch-canyonlands/)] [4c1572]

- **Mill Canyon Dinosaur Trail** (30-60 minutes) - Walk an easy, well-marked 0.4-mile interpretive trail to see actual dinosaur bones still embedded in the ground and cliff faces from 150 million years ago, offering a fascinating paleontology experience just minutes from Arches; trailhead located 15 miles north of Moab on US 191, turn left at intersection north of mile marker 141, cross railroad tracks and continue 2 miles; open dawn to dusk daily; free; no phone [Source: [Visit Utah Mill Canyon](https://www.visitutah.com/things-to-do/hiking/moab-hiking/mill-canyon-dinosaur-trail), [BLM Mill Canyon](https://www.blm.gov/visit/mill-canyon-dinosaur-bone-and-track-interpretive-sites), [AllTrails Mill Canyon](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/mill-canyon-dinosaur-trail), [Discover Moab Dinosaurs](https://www.discovermoab.com/dinosaur-museums-and-hikes/)] [f68e34]

**Total Recommended Time at Arches NP (National Park):** 2-3 days for a comprehensive visit including major hikes to Delicate Arch, Devils Garden primitive loop, Fiery Furnace tour, and all scenic viewpoints; minimum 4-5 hours for basic sightseeing of main viewpoints and short walks; 1 full day allows coverage of iconic stops with moderate hikes.
