# Arches NP (National Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Arches Visitor Center** (5 miles north of Moab on US Highway 191, Moab, UT 84532; April-September: 7:30 AM - 6:00 PM, October-early November: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, November: 8:00 AM - 4:30 PM, December-February: 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM Mountain Time, closed December 25; 435-719-2299) [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Utah.com Visitor Center](https://www.utah.com/destinations/national-parks/arches-national-park/visitor-information/visitor-center/)]

**Note:** Call 435-719-2299 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Hike to Delicate Arch** (2-3 hours) - Complete the park's most iconic 3.2-mile round-trip hike across sun-exposed slickrock with steep sections to reach Utah's famous freestanding 65-foot arch featured on license plates, offering unbeatable views after 1.5 miles of moderately difficult hiking; trailhead at Wolfe Ranch, 13.4 miles from park entrance on Arches Scenic Drive, Moab, UT 84532; trail open 24/7, but best during spring (April-May) and fall (September-October) when temperatures range from 60°F to 80°F; 435-719-2299 [Source: [NPS Delicate Arch](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/delicate-arch.htm), [Discover Moab](https://www.discovermoab.com/arches-national-park/delicate-arch/), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g143011-d117172-Reviews-Delicate_Arch-Arches_National_Park_Utah.html), [National Park Magazine](https://nationalparkmagazine.com/2025/04/29/delicate-arch-hike-arches-national-park-guide/)]

- **Explore The Windows Section** (1-2 hours) - Walk easy trails totaling 1.1 miles to see some of the park's largest arches including North Window, South Window, Turret Arch, and Double Arch, with the Windows Loop taking 30-60 minutes and Double Arch trail adding another 15-30 minutes; Windows Section located 12 miles from park entrance (9 miles on Main Park Road, then 2.5 miles east on Windows Road), Moab, UT 84532; open 24/7; 435-719-2299 [Source: [NPS Windows Section](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/the-windows.htm), [AllTrails Windows Loop](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/windows-loop-and-turret-arch-trail), [NPS Double Arch](https://www.nps.gov/places/double-arch.htm), [Dueling Journeys](https://www.duelingjourneys.com/travel/hike-the-windows-section-arches-national-park)]

- **Hike Devils Garden to Landscape Arch** (1-5 hours depending on distance) - Walk the easy 1.6-mile round-trip trail to see the longest arch span in North America at Landscape Arch (1 hour), or continue on the strenuous 7.8-mile primitive loop to see eight arches including Double O Arch (4-5 hours total); Devils Garden Trailhead at end of Arches Scenic Drive, 18 miles from park entrance, Moab, UT 84532; open 24/7; 435-719-2299 [Source: [NPS Devils Garden](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/devils-garden.htm), [Earth Trekkers Devils Garden](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/devils-garden-trail-best-hike-in-arches-national-park/), [AllTrails Devils Garden](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/devils-garden-loop-trail-with-7-arches), [Ulum Devils Garden Guide](https://www.ulumresorts.com/stories/guide-to-devils-garden-in-arches-national-park/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Ranger-Guided Fiery Furnace Tour** (2.5-3 hours) - Join a ranger-led exploration through a maze of narrow sandstone canyons and fins in one of the park's most spectacular and restricted areas, requiring scrambling and route-finding through passages that aren't marked trails; Fiery Furnace viewpoint located 14.5 miles from park entrance on Arches Scenic Drive, Moab, UT 84532; tours offered seasonally spring through fall, reservations required through Recreation.gov starting in February, book well in advance; 435-719-2299 [Source: [NPS Fiery Furnace](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/fiery-furnace-hike.htm), [Recreation.gov Tours](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/234668/ticket/116), [Fiery Furnace Tour Guide](https://fieryfurnacetour.com/arches-national-park-fiery-furnace/), [My Utah Parks](https://www.myutahparks.com/things-to-do/hiking-trails/arches-fiery-furnace/)]

- **Hike to Tower Arch** (2-3 hours) - Trek a less-traveled 3.4-mile round-trip moderate trail through a remote section of the park to reach the impressive 92-foot Tower Arch, where you'll likely have this prehistoric natural wonder to yourself; Tower Arch trailhead in Klondike Bluffs area accessed via rough, unpaved Salt Valley Road (high-clearance vehicle recommended), approximately 23 miles from park entrance, Moab, UT 84532; trail open 24/7; 435-719-2299 [Source: [Organized Adventurer Tower Arch](https://organizedadventurer.com/tower-arch-hike/), [PMags Hidden Arches](https://pmags.com/the-obscure-arches-national-park), [TripAdvisor Tower Arch](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g143011-d7762109-Reviews-Tower_Arch-Arches_National_Park_Utah.html), [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)]

- **Sand Dune Arch and Broken Arch Loop** (1-2 hours) - Follow a short 0.4-mile trail through deep sand to discover Sand Dune Arch hidden between towering sandstone walls, then continue on a primitive trail connecting to Broken Arch for a 2.8-mile loop passing through Devils Garden campground; Sand Dune Arch trailhead located off Arches Scenic Drive near Devils Garden, 16 miles from park entrance, Moab, UT 84532; open 24/7; 435-719-2299 [Source: [NPS Sand Dune Arch](https://www.nps.gov/places/sand-dune-arch-trail.htm), [Utah Outdoor Activities](https://www.utahoutdooractivities.com/sandbrokenarch.html), [Camping Kiddos Trail Guide](https://www.campingkiddos.com/blog/sand-dune-and-broken-arch-trail-guide), [AllTrails Sand Dune](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/sand-dune-arch)]

## Also Nearby

- **Dead Horse Point State Park** (1-2 hours for viewpoint and short trails) - Experience one of the most photographed overlooks in the world with dramatic views 2,000 feet down to the Colorado River's gooseneck bend and Canyonlands below; 313 State Highway 313, Moab, UT 84532 (approximately 45 minutes/33 miles from Moab, 9 miles off Highway 191 via Highway 313); park open 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM daily, Visitor Center open 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM (summer), 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (winter), closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; $20 per vehicle day-use fee; 435-259-2614 [Source: [Utah State Parks Dead Horse](https://stateparks.utah.gov/parks/dead-horse/), [Discover Moab Dead Horse](https://www.discovermoab.com/dead-horse-point-state-park/), [Utah.com Dead Horse](https://www.utah.com/destinations/state-parks/dead-horse-point-state-park/)]

- **Canyonlands National Park - Island in the Sky District with Mesa Arch** (3-4 hours for district highlights) - Drive 40 minutes from Moab to hike the easy 0.5-mile Mesa Arch trail offering an iconic sunrise photo opportunity, then explore additional overlooks like Grand View Point providing sweeping vistas of the canyon country below; Island in the Sky Visitor Center located at end of Highway 313, approximately 32 miles from Moab (40-50 minutes drive), Moab, UT 84532; park open 24/7, entrance fee $30 per vehicle valid for 7 days and covers both Canyonlands and Arches; 435-719-2313 [Source: [NPS Island in the Sky](https://www.nps.gov/cany/planyourvisit/islandinthesky.htm), [AllTrails Mesa Arch](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/mesa-arch), [Full Suitcase Mesa Arch](https://fullsuitcase.com/mesa-arch/), [We're in the Rockies](https://wereintherockies.com/mesa-arch-canyonlands/)]

- **Mill Canyon Dinosaur Trail** (30-60 minutes) - Walk an easy, well-marked 0.4-mile interpretive trail to see actual dinosaur bones still embedded in the ground and cliff faces from 150 million years ago, offering a fascinating paleontology experience just minutes from Arches; trailhead located 15 miles north of Moab on US 191, turn left at intersection north of mile marker 141, cross railroad tracks and continue 2 miles; open dawn to dusk daily; free; no phone [Source: [Visit Utah Mill Canyon](https://www.visitutah.com/things-to-do/hiking/moab-hiking/mill-canyon-dinosaur-trail), [BLM Mill Canyon](https://www.blm.gov/visit/mill-canyon-dinosaur-bone-and-track-interpretive-sites), [AllTrails Mill Canyon](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/mill-canyon-dinosaur-trail), [Discover Moab Dinosaurs](https://www.discovermoab.com/dinosaur-museums-and-hikes/)]

**Total Recommended Time at Arches NP (National Park):** 2-3 days for a comprehensive visit including major hikes to Delicate Arch, Devils Garden primitive loop, Fiery Furnace tour, and all scenic viewpoints; minimum 4-5 hours for basic sightseeing of main viewpoints and short walks; 1 full day allows coverage of iconic stops with moderate hikes.
