# Fort McHenry NM & HS (National Monument and Historic Shrine)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Paul S. Sarbanes Visitor and Education Center** - Passport cancellation stamps are typically located in the visitor center, likely near the front desk or in the gift shop area; 2400 East Fort Avenue, Baltimore, MD 21230 (39.264, -76.580); Open daily 9:00 AM - 4:45 PM, closed Thanksgiving, December 25, and January 1; 410-962-4290 x250 [Source: [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/fomc/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/fomc/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Visit Baltimore](https://baltimore.org/listings/fort-mchenry-national-monument-and-historic-shrine-and-hampton-national-historic-site/)]

**Note:** Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. Fort McHenry is also a stamp location on the Star-Spangled Banner Trail passport program.

## Key Activities

- **Explore the Historic Star Fort** (1-1.5 hours) - Walk through the star-shaped fort with 80% original brick, exploring restored buildings including barracks, powder magazine, and guardhouse with Civil War prison cells; self-guided tour with audio available via NPS app; fort represents War of 1812 through World War I military history; 2400 East Fort Avenue, Baltimore, MD 21230; Open 9:00 AM - 4:45 PM daily; $15 for ages 16+, free for 15 and under, 7-day pass included [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fomc/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Visit Baltimore](https://baltimore.org/what-to-do/behind-the-ramparts-of-fort-mchenry/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g60811-d109069-t1049074-How_long_does_it_take_to_see_the_displays_and.html)]

- **Watch Orientation Film** (10-15 minutes) - View film about the Battle of Baltimore and the writing of "The Star-Spangled Banner"; shown every 30 minutes from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM in the visitor center; free with park admission [Source: [Visit Baltimore](https://baltimore.org/what-to-do/behind-the-ramparts-of-fort-mchenry/), [NPS Movie](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/fort-mchenry-movie.htm)]

- **Attend Flag Change Ceremony** (30 minutes) - Participate in the raising or lowering of the 42-foot replica Star-Spangled Banner flag; daily at 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM; special "Fort, Flag and Fire!" ceremony every Saturday at 2:00 PM in July and August; free with fort admission [Source: [NPS Ranger Programs](https://www.nps.gov/fomc/planyourvisit/ranger-programs.htm), [Visit Baltimore](https://baltimore.org/what-to-do/behind-the-ramparts-of-fort-mchenry/)]

- **Ranger Programs** (30-45 minutes) - Join daily ranger-led programs including "Defenders Through the Ages" at 11:15 AM and "Flag Talk" at 1:15 PM; programs run daily and are free with park admission; meet outside visitor center on the concrete map of the Chesapeake Bay [Source: [NPS Ranger Programs](https://www.nps.gov/fomc/planyourvisit/ranger-programs.htm)]

- **Living History Demonstrations** (30-60 minutes) - Watch Fort McHenry Guard perform cannon and musket firing demonstrations, and fife and drum performances; weekends during spring and summer months, daily in summer; free with fort admission [Source: [Visit Baltimore](https://baltimore.org/what-to-do/behind-the-ramparts-of-fort-mchenry/), [US News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/Baltimore_MD/Things_To_Do/Fort_McHenry_National_Monument_52191/)]

- **Walk the Seawall Trail** (20-30 minutes) - Follow the 1-mile paved loop trail around the peninsula with views of Baltimore Harbor, cherry blossoms, monuments, and the Patapsco River; accessible year-round during park hours; free, wheelchair accessible, pet-friendly (leashed), bikes permitted (10 mph limit) [Source: [NPS Seawall Trail](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/walk-the-seawall-trail.htm), [NPS Sea Wall Trail](https://www.nps.gov/places/sea-wall-trail.htm)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (1-2 hours) - Children ages 5-13 complete activity booklets and earn a badge; booklets available at visitor center; free [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fomc/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Visit Baltimore](https://baltimore.org/what-to-do/behind-the-ramparts-of-fort-mchenry/)]

- **Explore Visitor Center Exhibits** (30-60 minutes) - View interactive exhibits about the War of 1812, Fort McHenry's history, and the Star-Spangled Banner; includes gift shop with books, souvenirs, and apparel; free admission to exhibits [Source: [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/fomc/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Civil War Prison Cells in the Guardhouse** (15-20 minutes) - Enter the actual prison cells used during the Civil War to hold Southern sympathizers, including 23 members of the Maryland state legislature; interactive exhibit reflecting its use as a holding facility; included in fort admission [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60811-d109069-Reviews-Fort_McHenry_National_Monument_And_Historic_Shrine-Baltimore_Maryland.html)]

- **Interactive George Armistead Exhibit** (10 minutes) - View statue of Fort McHenry's commander leaning over his desk with a video screen that plays a presentation about Armistead's preparations for the British attack when visitors approach; located in visitor center exhibits; free with park admission [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60811-d109069-Reviews-Fort_McHenry_National_Monument_And_Historic_Shrine-Baltimore_Maryland.html)]

- **Free Grounds and Waterfront Access** (30-60 minutes) - Enjoy the grounds surrounding the fort with walking, running, or biking paths along the water's edge; picnic areas and open lawns for family activities and kite flying; accessible without paying fort entrance fee; free [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fomc/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60811-d109069-Reviews-Fort_McHenry_National_Monument_And_Historic_Shrine-Baltimore_Maryland.html)]

- **Wildlife and Nature Watching** (flexible) - Observe diverse species of wildlife and plants in this unexpected urban nature destination; wide variety of birds and river wildlife visible from the Seawall Trail; memorial cherry blossoms; free [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60811-d109069-Reviews-Fort_McHenry_National_Monument_And_Historic_Shrine-Baltimore_Maryland.html), [NPS Seawall Trail](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/walk-the-seawall-trail.htm)]

- **Fleet Week Air Show Viewing** (3-4 hours, biennial event) - Prime viewing spot for Fleet Week air shows; bring blanket or chair to watch from the lawn; free grounds access; occurs every two years in Baltimore [Source: [Visit Baltimore](https://baltimore.org/what-to-do/behind-the-ramparts-of-fort-mchenry/)]

## Also Nearby

- **National Aquarium** (2-3 hours, 3 miles) - Major public aquarium at Baltimore's Inner Harbor featuring diverse marine life exhibits across multiple levels; 501 E. Pratt St., Baltimore, MD 21202; Hours vary by day: Mon-Thu 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Fri-Sat 9:00 AM - 8:00 PM, Sun 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM; advance tickets recommended [Source: [Visit Baltimore National Aquarium](https://baltimore.org/listings/national-aquarium-baltimore/), [WhichMuseum](https://whichmuseum.com/museum/national-aquarium-baltimore-8797/opening-hours)]

- **Maryland Science Center** (2-3 hours, 3 miles) - Interactive science museum with three levels of exhibits on astronomy, paleontology, human body, and physics; includes Davis Planetarium, IMAX theater, and rooftop observatory; 601 Light St, Baltimore, MD 21230; Wed-Fri 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Sat-Sun 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed Mon-Tue [Source: [Visit Baltimore Maryland Science Center](https://baltimore.org/listings/maryland-science-center/), [US News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/Baltimore_MD/Things_To_Do/Maryland_Science_Center_42366/)]

- **American Visionary Art Museum** (2-3 hours, 1.5 miles) - Unique museum specializing in outsider art, intuitive art, and art brut; located in Federal Hill neighborhood; 800 Key Highway, Baltimore, MD; Wed-Sun 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed Mon-Tue [Source: [Visit Baltimore AVAM](https://baltimore.org/listings/american-visionary-art-museum/), [WhichMuseum AVAM](https://whichmuseum.com/museum/american-visionary-art-museum-baltimore-10843/opening-hours)]

- **Baltimore Museum of Industry** (2-4 hours, 1 mile) - Museum in an 1860s oyster cannery offering indoor and outdoor exhibitions, live demonstrations, and hands-on activities about Baltimore's industrial history; 1415 Key Hwy, Baltimore, MD 21230; Tue-Sun 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM with one extended day per month 9:00 AM - 8:00 PM; free parking; $15 adults, $10 seniors 62+, $8 students/youth 6-17 [Source: [Baltimore Museum of Industry](https://www.thebmi.org/), [Visit Baltimore BMI](https://baltimore.org/listings/baltimore-museum-of-industry/), [TripAdvisor BMI](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60811-d116832-Reviews-Baltimore_Museum_of_Industry-Baltimore_Maryland.html)]

- **Fells Point Historic District** (2-4 hours, 2 miles) - Historic waterfront neighborhood established 1763 with over 300 buildings on the National Register; features cobblestone streets, maritime museums, Broadway Market, shops, restaurants, and pubs including the oldest continually operated saloon in North America; accessible 24/7 for walking, visitor center with guided and self-guided tours available; 1.5 miles east of downtown Baltimore, accessible via water taxi from Inner Harbor [Source: [Visit Baltimore Fells Point](https://baltimore.org/neighborhoods/fells-point/), [NPS Fells Point](https://www.nps.gov/places/fells-point.htm), [TripAdvisor Fells Point](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60811-d109074-Reviews-Fell_s_Point-Baltimore_Maryland.html)]

- **Federal Hill Park** (1 hour, 1.5 miles) - Historic park perched on a hill offering panoramic views of Baltimore's Inner Harbor and downtown waterfront; free access [Source: [Visit Baltimore Federal Hill](https://baltimore.org/neighborhoods/federal-hill/), [TripAdvisor Federal Hill](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60811-d278609-Reviews-Federal_Hill_Park-Baltimore_Maryland.html)]
