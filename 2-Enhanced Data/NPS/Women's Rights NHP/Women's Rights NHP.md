# Women's Rights NHP (National Historical Park)

## <a name="cancellation-stamps"></a>Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Women's Rights NHP Visitor Center** (136 Fall Street, Seneca Falls, NY 13148 (42.9108267, -76.8001575); May 24-Nov 1: Mon-Thu 10:00 AM-4:00 PM, Fri-Sun 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Off-season: Tue-Sat or Fri-Sat 10:00 AM-4:00 PM; 315-568-0024) [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/wori/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

**Note:** Call 315-568-0024 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## <a name="key-activities"></a>Key Activities

- **<a name="visitor-center-exhibits"></a>Visitor Center Museum Exhibits** (1-2 hours) - Explore comprehensive exhibits on the women's rights movement, view interactive displays, and watch the 22-minute orientation film "Dreams of Equality" shown on demand in the theater; 136 Fall Street, Seneca Falls, NY 13148 (42.9108267, -76.8001575); May 24-Nov 1: Mon-Thu 10:00 AM-4:00 PM, Fri-Sun 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/wori/learn/historyculture/visitor-center.htm), [Travel US News](https://travel.usnews.com/The_Finger_Lakes_NY/Things_To_Do/Womens_Rights_National_Historical_Park_65871/)]

- **<a name="wesleyan-chapel"></a>Wesleyan Chapel Tour** (30 minutes) - Visit the historic setting where the first Women's Rights Convention was held in 1848, with short ranger presentations available to learn about this pivotal moment in American history; 136 Fall Street, Seneca Falls, NY 13148 (42.9108267, -76.8001575); May 24-Nov 1: Mon-Thu 10:00 AM-4:00 PM, Fri-Sun 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Wesleyan Chapel](https://www.nps.gov/wori/learn/historyculture/wesleyan-chapel.htm), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/wori/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- **<a name="declaration-park"></a>Declaration Park and Waterwall** (20-30 minutes) - Walk through the outdoor park featuring the 100-foot bluestone waterwall inscribed with the complete text and signers of the Declaration of Sentiments, and view sculptor Lloyd Lillie's "The First Wave" life-size bronze sculptures of convention participants including Elizabeth Cady Stanton and Frederick Douglass; 136 Fall Street, Seneca Falls, NY 13148 (42.9108267, -76.8001575); daily dawn to dusk [Source: [NPS Declaration Park](https://www.nps.gov/wori/learn/historyculture/declaration-park-and-waterwall.htm), [I Love NY](https://www.iloveny.com/blog/post/48-hours-in-seneca-falls/)]

- **<a name="elizabeth-cady-stanton-house"></a>Elizabeth Cady Stanton House Tour** (1 hour including tour) - Take a ranger-led tour of the home where Elizabeth Cady Stanton lived and worked, seeing firsthand the connection between her domestic life and her women's rights advocacy where she helped initiate the movement; 32 Washington Street, Seneca Falls, NY 13148 (42.9126878, -76.788356); May 24-Nov 1: Fri-Sat 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM tours, extended to include Sun during peak season May 24-Aug 30 [Source: [NPS Elizabeth Cady Stanton House](https://www.nps.gov/wori/learn/historyculture/elizabeth-cady-stanton-house.htm), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/wori/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- **<a name="ranger-programs"></a>Ranger-Led Programs** (30 minutes) - Attend daily outdoor ranger talks covering various topics related to the women's rights movement, with schedules varying by topic and time of day; offered year-round at various park locations; check calendar for current schedule [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/wori/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **<a name="historic-district-walk"></a>Historic District Walking Tour** (1-2 hours, 2 miles) - Walk through the historic district visiting multiple sites spread over 5 miles, including the visitor center, Wesleyan Chapel, and other significant locations in Seneca Falls' role in the women's rights movement; self-guided or ranger-led options available; begins at 136 Fall Street, Seneca Falls, NY 13148 (42.9108267, -76.8001575) [Source: [Travel US News](https://travel.usnews.com/The_Finger_Lakes_NY/Things_To_Do/Womens_Rights_National_Historical_Park_65871/), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/wori/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## <a name="hidden-gems"></a>Hidden Gems

- **<a name="mclintock-house"></a>M'Clintock House** (30-45 minutes) - Visit the red brick Quaker home in nearby Waterloo where Mary Ann M'Clintock hosted the planning session for the 1848 convention and where the Declaration of Sentiments was drafted, featuring exhibits about the family's Quaker faith and their work in anti-slavery and women's rights movements; 14 East Williams Street, Waterloo, NY 13165 (42.90479, -76.86274); open during Independence Day, Convention Days, and special Waterloo events [Source: [NPS M'Clintock House](https://www.nps.gov/wori/learn/historyculture/mclintock-house.htm), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/wori/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- **<a name="junior-ranger"></a>Junior Ranger Program** (1-2 hours) - Complete interactive workbooks available at the visitor center or downloadable for home exploration, earning a Junior Ranger badge while learning about women's rights history; suitable for all ages; 136 Fall Street, Seneca Falls, NY 13148 (42.9108267, -76.8001575); available during visitor center hours [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/wori/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **<a name="special-events"></a>Convention Days** (varies) - Attend the annual July commemoration celebrating the anniversary of the 1848 Women's Rights Convention with special programs, ranger talks, and commemorative activities; held each July; check calendar for specific dates and times; 136 Fall Street, Seneca Falls, NY 13148 (42.9108267, -76.8001575) [Source: [NPS Calendar](https://www.nps.gov/wori/planyourvisit/calendar.htm)]

## <a name="also-nearby"></a>Also Nearby

- **<a name="national-womens-hall-of-fame"></a>National Women's Hall of Fame** (1-1.5 hours) - Explore a nonprofit museum dedicated to honoring and celebrating the achievements of distinguished American women from Anne Hutchinson to Oprah Winfrey, located in the historic Seneca Knitting Mill on the Erie Canal; 1 Canal Street, Seneca Falls, NY 13148 (42.9092493, -76.7994057); Tue-Sat 11:00 AM-4:00 PM, closed Sun-Mon (seasonal hours vary Jan-Mar); 0.3 miles from Women's Rights NHP [Source: [Finger Lakes Tourism](https://www.fingerlakes.org/partners/national-womens-hall-of-fame), [WhichMuseum](https://whichmuseum.com/museum/national-women-s-hall-of-fame-seneca-falls-16064/opening-hours)]

- **<a name="its-a-wonderful-life-museum"></a>It's A Wonderful Life Museum** (30-45 minutes) - Explore how Seneca Falls inspired the classic 1946 film starring James Stewart, with exhibits featuring items from and related to the movie; admission is free with donations encouraged; 76 Fall Street, Seneca Falls, NY 13148 (42.9106201, -76.7974243); Tue-Sat 11:00 AM-4:00 PM, Sun 10:00 AM-3:00 PM (Jul-Aug only); 315-568-5838; 0.2 miles from Women's Rights NHP [Source: [It's A Wonderful Life Museum](https://www.wonderfullifemuseum.com/visit-1-2/), [I Love NY](https://www.iloveny.com/listing/its-a-wonderful-life-museum/123681/)]

- **<a name="montezuma-wildlife-refuge"></a>Montezuma National Wildlife Refuge** (2-3 hours) - Drive the scenic wildlife drive (Apr-Nov) or walk year-round trails to observe 242 bird species, 43 mammals, and other wildlife across 9,000+ acres of wetlands at the north end of Cayuga Lake; 3395 US Route 20 East, Seneca Falls, NY 13148 (42.9968644, -76.7655671); Wildlife Drive: Apr 1-Nov 30 sunrise-sunset, Visitor Center: Apr 1-Nov 30 10:00 AM-3:00 PM, walking trails year-round sunrise-sunset; in Seneca Falls city limits [Source: [Friends of Montezuma](https://friendsofmontezuma.org/visit-the-mwc/montezuma-national-wildlife-refuge/), [US Fish & Wildlife](https://www.fws.gov/refuge/montezuma/visit-us)]

- **<a name="harriet-tubman-nhp"></a>Harriet Tubman National Historical Park** (2-3 hours) - Visit the home and sites associated with Harriet Tubman's later life in Auburn, including the Thompson Memorial AME Zion Church, the Harriet Tubman Visitor Center, the Tubman Home for the Aged, and her residence; operated in conjunction with Women's Rights NHP sharing connections to women's rights and abolition movements; 180 South Street, Auburn, NY 13021 (42.911525, -76.5637234); check NPS website for current hours; 315-568-0024; 15 miles east of Seneca Falls, approximately 20-25 minutes drive [Source: [NPS Nearby Attractions](https://www.nps.gov/wori/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm), [NPS Harriet Tubman](https://www.nps.gov/hart/)]

- **<a name="finger-lakes-wine-trails"></a>Finger Lakes Wine Trails** (half day to full day) - Sample award-winning wines along the Seneca Lake Wine Trail and Cayuga Lake Wine Trail (America's First Wine Trail), and explore the region's agricultural bounty on the Finger Lakes Cheese Trail; various locations throughout Finger Lakes region; hours vary by winery/venue; 10-30 miles from Seneca Falls [Source: [Discover Seneca](https://www.discoverseneca.com/things-to-do/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g60997-Activities-Seneca_Falls_Finger_Lakes_New_York.html)]
