## Key Activities

- **Oakland Plantation Guided Tours** (1 hour) - Park ranger-led tours of Oakland Plantation grounds offered at 10:30 AM and 1:30 PM Wednesday-Sunday, exploring 17 original outbuildings including two pigeonniers, overseer's house, massive roofed log corn crib, carriage house, mule barn, carpenter's shop, and worker cabins; meet at visitor pavilion near parking lot, no reservation needed, tour size limited to 15 people; 4386 Highway 494, Natchez, LA 71456 (31.5172609, -91.3884127); Wednesday-Sunday 10:30 AM and 1:30 PM; free admission [Source: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/cari/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/cari/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Oakland Plantation Main House Self-Guided Tour** (1 hour) - Self-guided tour of the main house built in 1821, showcasing French Creole architecture constructed with bousillage (mud and moss mixture) and featuring rooms without closets, a distinctive architectural feature of the period; learn how people prepared meals in this historic home and why workers constructed it without any closets; 4386 Highway 494, Natchez, LA 71456; Saturday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM; free admission [Source: [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/cari/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Oakland History](https://www.nps.gov/places/oakland-plantation.htm)]

- **Oakland Plantation Cell Phone Audio Tour** (1-2 hours) - Self-guided audio tour accessed via cell phone by dialing the number on placards at each historic building and entering the stop number to hear narrations about the building's history, allowing visitors to explore at their own pace through all 17 outbuildings; 4386 Highway 494, Natchez, LA 71456; Daily 9:00 AM - 3:30 PM; free admission [Source: [Oakland Cell Phone Tour](https://www.nps.gov/cari/learn/news/history-comes-alive-at-oakland-plantation.htm)]

- **Cabin Talk at Oakland Plantation** (30 minutes) - Ranger presentation at 1:00 PM focusing on enslaved peoples' living conditions, sharecropper history, and Creole culture, with discussion of how families of original enslaved workers remained as tenant farmers and sharecroppers until 1999; Oakland Plantation; Wednesday-Sunday 1:00 PM; free admission [Source: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/cari/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [Oakland History](https://www.nps.gov/places/oakland-plantation.htm)]

- **Magnolia Plantation Self-Guided Tours** (1-2 hours) - Explore 18 acres featuring outbuildings including blacksmith shop, plantation store, historic gin barn with the last remaining wooden screw-type cotton press in its original site in the United States, eight worker cabins, and former slave hospital; the main house is privately owned and not open to the public; 5549 Highway 119, Derry, LA 71416 (40.398071, -79.4238871) (40.398071, -79.4238871); Daily 9:00 AM - 3:30 PM; free admission [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/cari/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Magnolia Features](https://www.nps.gov/cari/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)]

- **Magnolia Plantation Grounds Tours** (1 hour) - Park staff-conducted tours of plantation grounds exploring the most intact 19th-century plantation complex in the nation with 21 buildings including eight brick slave quarters cabins; tours offered daily; meet at designated tour area; 5549 Highway 119, Derry, LA 71416; Wednesday-Sunday (specific times vary); free admission [Source: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/cari/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [Magnolia Plantation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnolia_Plantation_(Derry,_Louisiana))]

- **Texas & Pacific Railway Depot Tour** (30-45 minutes) - Explore one of the last remaining buildings in Louisiana where the architecture of segregation can still be clearly seen, featuring separate entrances for "White" and "Colored" passengers leading to two waiting rooms segregated by race and marked by different building materials; completed in 1927 and never integrated before passenger rail service ended in 1965, now serving as park visitor center; 1927 Remembrance Way, Natchitoches, LA 71457; Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 3:30 PM; free admission [Source: [Texas and Pacific Railway Depot](https://www.nps.gov/cari/learn/historyculture/texas-and-pacific-railway-depot.htm), [Visitor Center](https://www.canerivernha.org/cane-river-creole-national-historical-park)]

- **Enslaved Workers and Sharecroppers History Exploration** (1-2 hours) - Self-guided exploration of worker cabins and quarters at both plantations, learning about the lives of enslaved laborers and later sharecroppers and tenant farmers who worked the land for nearly 200 years, with special museum exhibits of tools used by enslaved carpenters, masons, and blacksmiths including works of enslaved blacksmith Solomon Williams; both Oakland and Magnolia plantations; Daily 9:00 AM - 3:30 PM; free admission [Source: [Oakland History](https://www.nps.gov/places/oakland-plantation.htm), [Oakland Plantation History](https://www.nps.gov/cari/learn/historyculture/oakland-plantation-history.htm)]

- **Oak Allee and Bottle Garden Walk** (30 minutes) - Walk the historic oak-lined pathway and explore the bottle garden at Oakland Plantation, distinctive features of the Creole plantation landscape; 4386 Highway 494, Natchez, LA 71456; Daily 9:00 AM - 3:30 PM; free admission [Source: [Oakland Features](https://www.canerivernha.org/cane-river-creole-national-historical-park)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Blacksmith Solomon Williams Exhibition** (20-30 minutes) - View the works of enslaved blacksmith Solomon Williams, whose craftsmanship is preserved in many of the iron works throughout Oakland Plantation, offering insight into the skilled trades practiced by enslaved artisans; Oakland Plantation; Daily 9:00 AM - 3:30 PM; free admission [Source: [Solomon Williams](https://www.nps.gov/places/oakland-plantation.htm)]

- **Cotton-to-Cloth Demonstration** (30 minutes) - Learn traditional Creole techniques for spinning cotton into cloth, a hands-on experience demonstrating the labor-intensive process that drove the plantation economy; Oakland Plantation (check with park rangers for demonstration schedule); varies; free admission [Source: [Oakland Activities](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/louisiana-national-parks/cane-river-creole-national-historical-park-park-at-a-glance/cane-river-creole-national-historical-park-oakland-plantation/)]

- **Bousillage Building Material Demonstration** (30 minutes) - Discover how to make bousillage, a traditional French Creole building material made from mud, moss, and other materials, used in the construction of many plantation buildings including the Oakland Main House; Oakland Plantation (check with park rangers for demonstration schedule); varies; free admission [Source: [Oakland Activities](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/louisiana-national-parks/cane-river-creole-national-historical-park-park-at-a-glance/cane-river-creole-national-historical-park-oakland-plantation/)]

- **Talks with Former Plantation Residents** (varies) - On select days, visitors have the unique opportunity to meet and talk with former plantation residents and their descendants during self-guided tours, as generations of the same families of workers and owners lived at Oakland Plantation for over 200 years; Oakland Plantation; Saturday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM (check park schedule for specific dates); free admission [Source: [History Comes Alive](https://www.nps.gov/cari/learn/news/history-comes-alive-at-oakland-plantation.htm)]

- **Astronomy Nightscapes at Magnolia Plantation** (2-3 hours) - Special evening events offering stargazing opportunities in the dark skies of the plantation grounds, away from city lights; Magnolia Plantation (check park schedule for specific dates); evening hours; free admission [Source: [Astronomy Nightscapes](https://www.nps.gov/cari/learn/news/cane-river-creole-astronomy-nightscapes-at-magnolia-plantation.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Melrose Plantation** (1-2 hours, 16 miles south) - Tour historic plantation built by family of Marie Therese Coincoin, who was born to slaves and gained freedom; view Clementine Hunter artwork collection in African House; guided tours available [Source: [Cane River NHA](https://www.canerivernha.org/cane-river-creole-national-historical-park), [Explore Louisiana](https://www.explorelouisiana.com/articles/cane-river-national-heritage-area)]

- **Historic Natchitoches Downtown** (30 minutes, 3 miles) - Explore Louisiana's oldest permanent settlement in Louisiana Purchase with 33-block historic district featuring antique shops, art galleries, cafés, and restored homes along Cane River Lake [Source: [National Park Foundation](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/cane-river-creole-national-historical-park-and-heritage-area)]

- **Fort St. Jean Baptiste State Historic Site** (1-2 hours, 3 miles) - Visit replica of 18th-century French fort near original location; 155 Jefferson St, Natchitoches, LA; hours vary seasonally [Source: [Cane River NHA](https://www.canerivernha.org/cane-river-creole-national-historical-park)]
