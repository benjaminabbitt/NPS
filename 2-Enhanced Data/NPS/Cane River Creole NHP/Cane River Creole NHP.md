# Cane River Creole NHP (National Historical Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-texas-pacific-railway-depot-visitor-center"></a>**Texas & Pacific Railway Depot Visitor Center** (1927 Remembrance Way, Natchitoches, LA 71457 (31.7608, -93.0865); Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 3:30 PM, closed Monday-Tuesday and all federal holidays; 318-352-0383) [Source: [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/cari/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Contact](https://www.nps.gov/cari/contacts.htm)]

**Note:** This newly opened visitor center (November 2024) is the park's first dedicated facility with passport stamps. Call 318-352-0383 ext. 316 for updates or check the park's Facebook page for latest information. [Source: [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/cari/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-tour-oakland-plantation-main-house"></a>**Tour Oakland Plantation Main House** (1-1.5 hours) - Join guided tours at 10:30 AM and 1:30 PM or self-guided tours Saturday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM to explore one of the most intact Creole cotton plantations; includes 17 original outbuildings like pigeonniers, corn crib, and slave cabins; 4386 LA-494, Natchez, LA; grounds open 9:00 AM - 3:30 PM daily; free admission [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/cari/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/cari/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [Cane River NHA](https://www.canerivernha.org/cane-river-creole-national-historical-park)]

- <a id="activity-explore-magnolia-plantation-grounds"></a>**Explore Magnolia Plantation Grounds** (1-1.5 hours) - Walk grounds and outbuildings with ranger-led tours Wednesday-Sunday at 2:30 PM; nearly 80 original plantation buildings remain from 200 years of plantation life; grounds open 9:00 AM - 3:30 PM daily; main house privately owned and not open; free admission [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/cari/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/cari/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)]

- <a id="activity-attend-cabin-talk"></a>**Attend Cabin Talk** (30 minutes) - Join ranger presentation at Oakland Plantation at 1:00 PM to learn about enslaved peoples' living conditions and Creole culture; Wednesday-Sunday [Source: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/cari/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)]

- <a id="activity-visit-texas-pacific-railway-depot"></a>**Visit Texas & Pacific Railway Depot** (30-45 minutes) - Explore visitor center in restored 1927 depot with separate "White" and "Colored" entrances showing architecture of segregation; exhibits on railroad history and civil rights; 1927 Remembrance Way, Natchitoches, LA; Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 3:30 PM [Source: [NPS Texas & Pacific Railway Depot](https://www.nps.gov/cari/learn/historyculture/texas-and-pacific-railway-depot.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-self-guided-audio-tours"></a>**Self-Guided Audio Tours** (1-2 hours) - Download NPS App for audio tours of historically furnished plantation buildings at your own pace; available daily during grounds hours [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/cari/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [NPS App](https://home.nps.gov/cari/learn/the-national-park-service-app-nps-app.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-melrose-plantation"></a>**Melrose Plantation** (1-2 hours, 16 miles south) - Tour historic plantation built by family of Marie Therese Coincoin, who was born to slaves and gained freedom; view Clementine Hunter artwork collection in African House; guided tours available [Source: [Cane River NHA](https://www.canerivernha.org/cane-river-creole-national-historical-park), [Explore Louisiana](https://www.explorelouisiana.com/articles/cane-river-national-heritage-area)]

- <a id="nearby-historic-natchitoches-downtown"></a>**Historic Natchitoches Downtown** (30 minutes, 3 miles) - Explore Louisiana's oldest permanent settlement in Louisiana Purchase with 33-block historic district featuring antique shops, art galleries, cafés, and restored homes along Cane River Lake [Source: [National Park Foundation](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/cane-river-creole-national-historical-park-and-heritage-area)]

- <a id="nearby-fort-st-jean-baptiste-state-historic-site"></a>**Fort St. Jean Baptiste State Historic Site** (1-2 hours, 3 miles) - Visit replica of 18th-century French fort near original location; 155 Jefferson St, Natchitoches, LA; hours vary seasonally [Source: [Cane River NHA](https://www.canerivernha.org/cane-river-creole-national-historical-park)]
