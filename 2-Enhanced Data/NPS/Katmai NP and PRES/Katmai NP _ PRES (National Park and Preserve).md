# Katmai NP & PRES (National Park and Preserve)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **King Salmon Visitor Center** (1 King Salmon Airport Rd, King Salmon, AK 99613 [58.69, -156.66]; Open 7 days a week 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, June 1 - mid-September; 907-246-4250)

**Note:** The King Salmon Visitor Center is located next door to the King Salmon airport and serves as an interagency visitor center for Katmai, Lake Clark, Aniakchak, and other NPS units in Southwest Alaska. Cancellations are occasionally moved to seasonal locations or pop-up events. Contact the park directly to confirm current stamp locations. If visiting after the season or if the visitor center is closed, you can send a request and self-addressed stamped envelope to: Katmai National Park & Preserve, PO Box 7, 1000 Silver Street, Building 603, King Salmon, AK 99613. [Source: [Visitor Centers - Katmai NPS](https://home.nps.gov/katm/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Contact Us - Katmai NPS](https://www.nps.gov/katm/contacts.htm), [Tips for Passport Stampers in Alaska](https://www.alaska.org/advice/tips-for-national-park-passport-stampers-visiting-alaska)]

## Key Activities

- **Bear Viewing at Brooks Falls** (4-7 hours on-ground time) - Watch brown bears fishing for salmon at the iconic Brooks Falls, with up to 50 bears visible during peak season; access via float plane from King Salmon (35 minutes) or water taxi (45-60 minutes) to Brooks Camp (58.56, -155.78); viewing platforms open during daylight hours, June 1 - September 29; platform time may be limited to 30 minutes during peak season with 40-person capacity, unlimited time at other viewing areas; bear orientation required upon arrival; best viewing mid-July and September. [Source: [Brooks Falls Bear Viewing - ALASKA.ORG](https://www.alaska.org/destination/katmai-national-park-and-preserve/bear-viewing), [Katmai Bear Viewing Tour - Fly Katmai](https://www.flykatmai.com/katmai-tour), [Brooks Falls - TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g143038-d145317-Reviews-Brooks_Falls-Katmai_National_Park_and_Preserve_Alaska.html), [Best Time to See Bears](https://katmaibnb.com/blog/best-time-to-see-bears-at-brooks-falls)]

- **Valley of Ten Thousand Smokes Bus Tour** (7-8 hours) - Explore the dramatic volcanic landscape created by the 1912 Novarupta eruption, one of the 20th century's largest volcanic events; daily bus tour from Brooks Camp covers 23 miles one-way with 1.5-2 hour drive each way; includes stops at scenic viewpoints, time at Robert F. Griggs Visitor Center, and optional ranger-guided hike to valley floor (3.4 miles round trip, 1,000 feet elevation change); tour runs 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM; bring own lunch or purchase from Brooks Lodge. [Source: [Valley of Ten Thousand Smokes Tour - Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/valley-of-ten-thousand-smokes/), [Valley Tour - Brooks Lodge](https://katmailand.com/katmai-national-park/valley-of-ten-thousand-smokes/), [Exploring the Valley - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/katm/planyourvisit/exploring-the-valley-of-ten-thousand-smokes.htm)]

- **Brooks River Fishing** (2-4 hours) - Fish one of the world's premier rainbow trout streams with abundant arctic char, grayling, and lake trout; Brooks River Corridor permit required June 15 - October 31 ($6 for 7-day access); Brooks Camp area; fishing available throughout summer but coordinate timing to avoid bear conflicts; waders can be rented from Katmai Trading Post. [Source: [Things to Do - Katmai NPS](https://www.nps.gov/katm/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Brooks Camp - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/katm/planyourvisit/brooks-camp.htm), [Brooks River Permits - Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/permits/249991)]

- **Dumpling Mountain Overlook Hike** (2-3 hours round trip) - Moderate to strenuous hike climbing 800 feet over 1.5 miles to scenic overlook with views of Brooks Camp, Naknek Lake, and surrounding wilderness through boreal forest, subalpine meadows, and alpine tundra; trailhead at Brooks Camp; can continue to summit (additional 2.5 miles one-way, unmaintained trail, 5.5-6 hours total). [Source: [Dumpling Mountain Trail - AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/alaska/dumpling-mountain--2), [Hike Dumpling Overlook - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hike-to-dumpling-overlook.htm), [Hiking in Brooks Camp - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/katm/planyourvisit/hiking-in-brooks-camp.htm)]

- **Brooks Camp Cultural Site Trail** (30-45 minutes) - Walk the short 0.1-mile trail from Brooks Camp Visitor Center to a reconstructed traditional semi-subterranean home (barabara) dating to AD 1200-1300, built in the footprint of an actual prehistoric house excavated in the 1960s; part of Brooks River Archeological District National Historic Landmark with 22 sites dating from 2,500 BCE to recent times; self-guided with interpretive displays. [Source: [Brooks Camp Cultural Site Exhibit - NPS](https://home.nps.gov/katm/learn/historyculture/cultural-site-exhibit.htm), [Brooks River Archeological District - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/katm-brooks-river-archeological-district-nhl-100128.htm)]

- **Kayaking Brooks Camp Area Lakes** (2-4 hours) - Paddle Naknek Lake and Brooks Lake with kayak rentals available at Brooks Camp; opportunities for wildlife viewing and fishing; calm waters suitable for beginners. [Source: [Brooks Camp - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/katm/planyourvisit/brooks-camp.htm), [Katmai Activities - Travel Alaska](https://www.travelalaska.com/destinations/parks-public-lands/katmai-national-park-preserve)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Brooks River Off-Trail Walking Permit** (1-2 hours) - Obtain permit ($6 for two people) to walk off-trail along Brooks River for incredibly unique and thrilling wildlife viewing; waders can be rented from Katmai Trading Post; provides close encounters with Katmai's bears and other wildlife in their natural habitat. [Source: [11 Things to Do in Katmai - 90summers](https://90summers.com/things-to-do-in-katmai-national-park/)]

- **Savonoski Loop Kayaking** (5-10 days) - Multi-day paddle route through chain of lakes and rivers around the park; remote wilderness experience with opportunities for fishing, wildlife viewing, and backcountry camping; suitable for experienced kayakers. [Source: [Things to Do in Katmai - Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-things-to-do-in-katmai-national-park/), [Outdoor Activities - Katmai NPS](https://www.nps.gov/katm/planyourvisit/outdooractivities.htm)]

- **Remote Coastline Exploration** (variable) - Explore the park's remote coves and bays along the coastline; accessible via chartered boat or kayak; opportunities for wildlife viewing, tide pooling, and experiencing Katmai's marine environment away from the crowds. [Source: [Best Things to Do in Katmai - Parks Expert](https://parksexpert.com/best-things-to-do-in-katmai-national-park/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Alagnak Wild River Rafting** (6 days, ~90 minutes flight from King Salmon) - Float the 70-mile Alagnak River from Kukaklek Lake or Nonvianuk Lake through moderate Class III whitewater and pristine wilderness; 6-day trips typical; fly from King Salmon to headwater lakes via charter floatplane; suitable for rafts and inflatable kayaks with paddling experience; excellent fishing and wildlife viewing. [Source: [Alagnak River - ALASKA.ORG](https://www.alaska.org/detail/alagnak-river), [Alagnak Wild River - Kenai Backcountry Adventures](https://kenaibackcountryadventures.com/adventures/alagnak-wild-river)]

- **Lake Clark National Park & Preserve** (short flight, ~50 miles from Katmai) - Explore another remote Alaska wilderness featuring turquoise lakes, smoking volcanoes, and abundant wildlife including bears; 1-hour flight from Anchorage or short flight from Katmai area; day trips or multi-day experiences available; shares King Salmon Visitor Center with Katmai. [Source: [Katmai vs Lake Clark Comparison](https://seatosummitalaska.com/katmai-national-park-vs-lake-clark-national-park/), [Lake Clark and Katmai Tours](https://lakeclarklodgealaska.com/experience/lake-clark-and-katmai-national-park-tours)]

- **Aniakchak National Monument & Preserve** (90 minutes flight from King Salmon) - Visit one of Alaska's most remote and least-visited national monuments featuring a massive volcanic caldera with Surprise Lake; hike the ash and cinder fields of the caldera floor (2-8 miles typical); experienced paddlers can raft the 32-mile Aniakchak River through Class IV rapids; 150 miles from King Salmon, accessible via 90-minute flight; shares King Salmon Visitor Center infrastructure. [Source: [Aniakchak - Alaska Alpine Adventures](https://alaskaalpineadventures.com/destination/aniakchak-national-monument/), [Aniakchak - Travel Alaska](https://www.travelalaska.com/destinations/parks-public-lands/aniakchak-national-monument-preserve)]

- **Naknek River Wildlife & Fishing Tours** (4-8 hours, King Salmon area) - Jet boat tours and guided fishing on the Naknek River near King Salmon; bear and wildlife viewing along the river; eagles and other wildlife regularly seen; excellent fishing for salmon and rainbow trout; alternative or complement to Brooks Camp experience. [Source: [King Salmon Activities - Travel Alaska](https://www.travelalaska.com/destinations/cities-towns/king-salmon), [Nearby Attractions - Katmai NPS](https://www.nps.gov/katm/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 3 days (2-3 nights) provides the most complete experience of Katmai National Park, allowing time for bear viewing at Brooks Falls, the full-day Valley of Ten Thousand Smokes tour, cultural site exploration, and flexibility for weather delays. Day trips from Anchorage are possible but provide limited time (4-6 hours on-ground) primarily for Brooks Falls viewing only.
