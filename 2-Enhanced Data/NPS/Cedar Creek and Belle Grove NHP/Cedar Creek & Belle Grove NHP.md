## Key Activities

- **Take the Self-Guided Driving Tour** (2 hours) - Follow the 17.5-mile, 9-stop tour that traces the Battle of Cedar Creek chronologically from where Confederate soldiers surprised sleeping Federal camps in foggy dawn on October 19, 1864, to the final counterattack late that afternoon; pick up tour map at NPS Visitor Contact Station; 7712 Main Street, Middletown, VA 22645; 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM daily; 540-490-9569; free [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/cedar-creek-belle-grove-national-historical-park/), [NPS Explore Cedar Creek](https://www.nps.gov/tripideas/explore-cedar-creek-battlefield.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g57964-d4292148-Reviews-Cedar_Creek_Belle_Grove_National_Historical_Park-Middletown_Virginia.html)]

- **Tour Belle Grove Plantation House** (45-60 minutes) - Take a guided tour of the 1797 Federal-style manor house, the last tour departing at 3:15 PM on weekdays and 4:15 PM on Sundays; tours depart 15 minutes past the hour; you must take a guided tour to go inside, but you can tour the grounds on your own; 336 Belle Grove Road, Middletown, VA 22645; November 1-December 4: Saturday 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM; December 5-30: Monday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM; closed December 31-March 20; 540-869-2028 [Source: [Belle Grove Tours](https://bellegrove.org/visit/tours), [NPS Visit Belle Grove](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/tour-belle-grove.htm), [Virginia.org](https://www.virginia.org/listing/cedar-creek-and-belle-grove-national-historical-park/4985/)]

- **View the 18-Minute Lighted Battle Map** (20-30 minutes) - Watch the illuminated map presentation illustrating the Battle of Cedar Creek at the NPS Visitor Contact Station; 7712 Main Street, Middletown, VA 22645; 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM daily; 540-490-9569; free [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/cebe/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g57964-d4292148-Reviews-Cedar_Creek_Belle_Grove_National_Historical_Park-Middletown_Virginia.html)]

- **Hike the 19th Corps Trails** (1-1.5 hours) - Follow woodland trails covering 2.7 miles through gentle terrain along trenches built by the US 19th Corps in fall 1864, connecting the 128th New York Monument with Belle Grove Plantation; trails are mainly dirt paths through forest with interpretive markers; trailhead near Belle Grove; open sunrise to sunset; free [Source: [NPS 19th Corps Trails](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/walk-the-19th-corps-trails.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/virginia/19th-corps-trails), [NPS 19th Corps Trenches](https://www.nps.gov/places/19th-corps-trenches.htm)]

- **Join a Ranger-Guided Battlefield Tour** (1-2 hours) - Participate in ranger-led driving tours of the battlefield or special programs offered throughout the year; check with Visitor Contact Station for current schedule; 7712 Main Street, Middletown, VA 22645; 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM daily; 540-490-9569; free [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/cebe/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Virginia.org](https://www.virginia.org/listing/cedar-creek-and-belle-grove-national-historical-park/4985/)]

- **Explore Cedar Creek Battlefield Foundation Museum** (30-45 minutes) - View Civil War artifacts, use the Digital Media Center, and browse the research library and gift shop featuring exhibits about the Battle of Cedar Creek; 8437 Valley Pike, Middletown, VA 22645; Thursday-Monday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed Tuesday and Wednesday; 540-869-2064; free admission [Source: [NPS CCBF Museum](https://www.nps.gov/places/ccbf-museum-visitor-center.htm), [CCBF Visit](https://ccbf.us/visit/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Walk the Hayes-Ramseur Loop Trail** (30-45 minutes) - Explore this 0.7-mile interpretive trail through the Confederate attack area with color-blazed markers showing historic Civil War earthworks; managed by Cedar Creek Battlefield Foundation; trailhead at CCBF property; open sunrise to sunset; free [Source: [National Park Directory](https://www.nationalparkdirectory.com/blog/2025-05-29-ultimate-guide-to-cedar-creek-belle-grove-national-historical-park-winchester-virginia-complete-visitor-experience), [CCBF Visit](https://ccbf.us/visit/)]

- **Download the Free Podcast Tour** (varies) - Listen to the self-guided audio tour covering the Battle of Cedar Creek while driving or walking the battlefield; available for download from the park website; free [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/cebe/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Virginia.org](https://www.virginia.org/listing/cedar-creek-and-belle-grove-national-historical-park/4985/)]

- **Visit During the Annual Battle Re-enactment** (full day, October) - Experience the largest annual event at the park with living history demonstrations, battle re-enactments, and period camps; held annually in October; check park website for exact dates; could easily be a two-day event [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g57964-d4292148-Reviews-Cedar_Creek_Belle_Grove_National_Historical_Park-Middletown_Virginia.html)]

## Also Nearby

- **Museum of the Shenandoah Valley** (3+ hours, 10 miles north) - Explore galleries featuring Shenandoah Valley art and history, tour the historic 1794 Glen Burnie House, and stroll through seven acres of formal gardens; the Trails at MSV is a free-admission public park; 901 Amherst Street, Winchester, VA 22601; Tuesday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed Mondays; adults $15, seniors (60+) $10, youth (13-18) $10, members and ages 12 & under free, free Wednesday admission to galleries; 540-662-1473 [Source: [Visit Winchester VA](https://visitwinchesterva.com/locations/museum-of-the-shenandoah-valley/), [MSV](https://www.themsv.org/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g58318-d561581-Reviews-Museum_of_the_Shenandoah_Valley-Winchester_Virginia.html)]

- **Shenandoah Caverns** (2-3 hours, 25 miles south) - Take a fully-guided one-hour tour through spectacular caverns with elevator service (only Virginia cavern with elevator), plus explore the Family of Attractions; 261 Caverns Road, Quicksburg, VA 22847; Monday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM, closed December 25; 540-477-3115 [Source: [Shenandoah Caverns](https://shenandoahcaverns.com/), [Shenandoah Caverns FAQ](https://shenandoahcaverns.com/plan-your-visit-faqs/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g58097-d1791066-Reviews-Shenandoah_Caverns-Quicksburg_Shenandoah_County_Virginia.html)]

- **Skyline Drive, Shenandoah National Park** (3+ hours, 13 miles east to Front Royal entrance) - Drive the scenic 105-mile National Parkway through Blue Ridge Mountains with 75 overlooks providing views of Shenandoah Valley and Piedmont; takes about 3 hours to drive entire length at 35 mph speed limit; northern entrance at intersection with U.S. Route 340 near Front Royal; open year-round weather permitting [Source: [Distance Calculator](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Front-Royal/Middletown-Frederick-County-VA-USA), [NPS Skyline Drive](https://www.nps.gov/shen/planyourvisit/driving-skyline-drive.htm), [Virginia.org](https://www.virginia.org/listing/skyline-drive-in-shenandoah-national-park/7765/)]

- **Hupp's Hill Civil War Park** (1-1.5 hours, 7 miles south) - Tour the museum displaying artifacts from the 1864 Shenandoah Valley campaign, walk interpretive trails along original trenches, and explore an 18-acre park; visitor center and gift shop are free, museum admission charged; 33229 Old Valley Pike, Strasburg, VA 22657; 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily; parking free [Source: [NPS Strasburg Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/places/hupps-hill-park-museum.htm), [American Battlefield Trust](https://www.battlefields.org/visit/heritage-sites/hupps-hill-civil-war-park), [Shenandoah at War](https://www.shenandoahatwar.org/hupps-hill)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 4-6 hours for a comprehensive visit including the Visitor Contact Station (30 minutes), lighted battle map (20 minutes), self-guided driving tour (2 hours), Belle Grove Plantation tour (1 hour), and either the 19th Corps Trails hike (1.5 hours) or CCBF Museum visit (45 minutes). Plan at least half a day to experience both the battlefield and Belle Grove Plantation. A full day allows for a more leisurely experience with all activities.
