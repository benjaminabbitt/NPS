# Pullman NHP (National Historical Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Pullman NHP Visitor Center** (610 E 111th St, Chicago, Illinois 60628; 41.6928561, -87.6096053; Wednesday-Sunday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM, closed Monday-Tuesday and federal holidays; Phone: 773-928-7257) - Located in the historic Administration Clock Tower Building, this is the primary location for obtaining NPS passport cancellation stamps. [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/pull/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

**Note:** Call ahead at (773) 928-7257 to confirm current stamp availability before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center Exhibits and Orientation** (1-1.5 hours) - Explore comprehensive exhibits about Pullman's history as America's first planned industrial community, learn about the 1894 Pullman Strike, and discover the stories of the diverse workers who lived here, including African American porters and the Brotherhood of Sleeping Car Porters. The visitor center features interactive displays, artifacts, and a short film about the Pullman Company's history and labor movement significance. 610 E 111th St, Chicago, Illinois 60628; Wednesday-Sunday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM [Source: [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/pull/index.htm), [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/pull/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

- **Ranger-Guided Walking Tour** (1-1.5 hours) - Join a National Park Service ranger for a guided exploration of the historic district, learning detailed stories about the architectural significance, urban design innovations, and complex narratives of the people who lived and worked in Pullman. Tours are offered daily and typically last 45-90 minutes, with weekend tours featuring interior visits to restored buildings. Free with park admission. 610 E 111th St, Chicago, Illinois 60628; Tours offered throughout the day [Source: [NPS Grand Opening](https://www.nps.gov/pull/learn/news/celebrate-the-grand-opening.htm), [NPS Self-Guided Tour](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/take-a-self-guided-tour-of-pullman.htm)]

- **Self-Guided Walking Tour of Historic District** (1.5-2 hours) - Explore the meticulously preserved 1880s planned community at your own pace, walking past the distinctively designed residential row houses, historic commercial buildings, and architectural landmarks that reflect George Pullman's vision of a model industrial town. Self-guided tour maps are available free at the visitor center or Exhibit Hall. Pullman Historic District, various locations throughout neighborhood [Source: [NPS Self-Guided Tour](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/take-a-self-guided-tour-of-pullman.htm), [Visit Chicago Southland](https://www.visitchicagosouthland.com/1549/historic-pullman-district)]

- **Greenstone Church Exterior Viewing** (0.5 hours) - Admire the distinctive serpentine stone architectural masterpiece built in 1882, featuring a 92-foot bell tower and unique green limestone specially imported from Pennsylvania. The church exemplifies Pullman's commitment to planned community design. Note: Interior access may be limited; call ahead for current status. 11211 S. St. Lawrence Ave., Chicago, Illinois 60628 [Source: [Chicago Tribune](https://www.chicagotribune.com/2025/04/14/pullman-greenstone-church-funding/), [NPS Grand Opening](https://www.nps.gov/pull/learn/news/celebrate-the-grand-opening.htm)]

- **Market Square Complex Exploration** (0.75 hours) - Wander through the beautifully reconstructed colonnade apartments surrounding the 1893 Market Hall, a central gathering place designed to serve Pullman residents. The 2008 streetscape restoration recreated the historic utilities, lighting, and street design. Market Square buildings, 11100 block S. Cottage Grove Ave., Chicago, Illinois 60628 [Source: [Visit Chicago Southland](https://www.visitchicagosouthland.com/1549/historic-pullman-district), [Pullman IL](https://pullmanil.org/plan-a-visit/)]

- **Labor Movement and Pullman Strike Exhibition** (1-1.5 hours) - Delve into the compelling and contentious history of the 1894 Pullman Strike, one of the largest labor actions in American history, and learn how it shaped national labor policy and led to the establishment of Labor Day. Discover the experiences of workers across different social classes and backgrounds. Pullman NHP Visitor Center, 610 E 111th St, Chicago, Illinois 60628; Wednesday-Sunday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM [Source: [NPS 1894 Strike](https://www.nps.gov/pull/learn/historyculture/the-strike-of-1894.htm)]

- **Hotel Florence Exterior Tour** (0.5 hours) - View the impressive Queen Anne-style brick and limestone structure built in 1881 as the only lodging with a bar in the otherwise carefully controlled company town. This 50-room hotel, costing $100,000 at the time, housed visiting businessmen and dignitaries. Note: Interiors currently closed but exterior can be viewed. Illinois General Assembly has allocated $21 million for future restoration. 1141 S. Cottage Grove Ave., Chicago, Illinois 60628 [Source: [DNR Historic Illinois](https://dnrhistoric.illinois.gov/experience/sites/site.hotel-florence.html)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (1-2 hours) - Families with children can pick up a Junior Ranger activity booklet at the visitor center and complete it while exploring the museum and historic district to earn an official NPS badge. Pullman NHP Visitor Center, 610 E 111th St, Chicago, Illinois 60628; Wednesday-Sunday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM [Source: [NPS Self-Guided Tour](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/take-a-self-guided-tour-of-pullman.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **A. Philip Randolph Pullman Porter Museum** (1 hour) - Explore the nation's first Black labor history museum, founded in 1995, dedicated to telling the story of African American Pullman porters and the Brotherhood of Sleeping Car Porters union. Learn how porters, who comprised 44% of Pullman's workforce, achieved the first successful labor contract with a major corporation in 1937. The museum features artifacts and exhibitions celebrating this pivotal moment in American labor and civil rights history. 10406 S. Maryland Ave., Chicago, Illinois 60628 (entrance at 817 E. 104th Street; 41.705386, -87.6031392); Hours: April-November Thursday-Saturday 11:00 AM-4:00 PM (winter visits by appointment; reopening Spring 2026 Monday-Friday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM); Phone: 773-850-8580 [Source: [APR Pullman Porter Museum](https://aprpullmanportermuseum.org/), [The Pullman Porters Museum](https://thepullmanportersmuseum.org/visit)]

- **Historic Pullman Foundation Exhibit Hall and Guided Tours** (1.5-2 hours) - Visit the separate Heritage Foundation facility featuring rotating exhibits on the Pullman family, the town's evolution, and local history. The foundation offers more specialized and in-depth guided tours (90 minutes minimum, $20 per person; $15 seniors/students) on the first Sunday of each month May through October at 1:00 PM. 11141 S. Cottage Grove Ave., Chicago, Illinois 60628; Hours: Thursday-Sunday 11:00 AM-3:00 PM, free entry (donations appreciated); Phone: (773) 785-8901 [Source: [Pullman IL](https://pullmanil.org/plan-a-visit/), [Visit Chicago Southland](https://www.visitchicagosouthland.com/272/pullman-national-historical-park-visitor-information-center)]

- **Pullman Stables Historic Site** (0.5 hours) - View the 1885 livery stable building that housed the community's horses and carriages, a remnant of how George Pullman's vision extended to managing every aspect of daily life. The building reflects the urban planning and class structure embedded in Pullman's design. 11201 S. Cottage Grove Ave., Chicago, Illinois 60628 [Source: [Visit Chicago Southland](https://www.visitchicagosouthland.com/1549/historic-pullman-district)]

- **Engine Company 80 Firehouse** (0.5 hours) - Discover this historic firehouse, one of the community's essential service buildings, reflecting Pullman's comprehensive approach to creating a self-contained town with all necessary infrastructure and services integrated into the planned design. Located within the historic district [Source: [Pullman IL](https://pullmanil.org/plan-a-visit/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Museum of Science and Industry Chicago** (3-4 hours; 5 miles north, 10-15 minutes by car or 15-20 minutes via Metra Electric Line) - One of Chicago's premier museums features interactive science exhibits, the German U-505 submarine, historic aircraft, and immersive experiences exploring innovation and technology. Both Pullman and this museum are serviced by the Metra Electric commuter line, making it convenient for multi-site touring. 5700 S. DuSable Lake Shore Drive, Chicago, Illinois 60637; Hours: Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM (hours vary seasonally); Phone: (773) 684-1414 [Source: [MSI Chicago](https://www.msichicago.org/visit/getting-here), [Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Chicago/Museum-of-Science-and-Industry)]

- **Field Museum of Natural History and Shedd Aquarium on Museum Campus** (4-5 hours combined; 10-12 miles north, 20-30 minutes by car) - Two major attractions located side-by-side on Chicago's lakefront Museum Campus. The Field Museum offers natural history exhibits and Egyptian mummies, while Shedd Aquarium features thousands of marine animals and interactive exhibits. These attractions showcase Chicago's world-class cultural institutions. 1200 S. DuSable Lake Shore Drive, Chicago, Illinois 60605; Both accessible via Roosevelt Station or Museum Campus/11th Street Metra station [Source: [Shedd Aquarium](https://www.sheddaquarium.org/plan-a-visit/directions-and-parking), [Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Field-Museum-of-Natural-History/Shedd-Aquarium)]

- **Beaubien Woods - Forest Preserves of Cook County** (1-2 hours; 5-10 miles south, 15-25 minutes by car) - Natural area featuring Flatfoot Lake (a 19-acre former strip mine converted to recreational use), prairie and woodland habitats, boat launch, picnic areas, and excellent birding opportunities with over 200 species of birds documented. A hidden ecological restoration success story near the industrial Calumet region. Offers kayaking, fishing, and nature observation. Located south of Pullman via Cottage Grove Ave [Source: [FPDCC](https://fpdcc.com/places/locations/beaubien-woods/)]

- **Hegewisch Marsh Park - Chicago Park District** (1-2 hours; 8-10 miles southeast, 20-25 minutes by car) - A 133-acre wetland restoration project showcasing impressive biodiversity with over 200 bird species, 20 species of fish, and rare amphibians and reptiles. Bordered by the Calumet River with views of the Thomas J. O'Brien Lock and Dam. Offers peaceful opportunities for passive eco-recreation, birdwatching, and nature photography. Located at the western side of the Hegewisch neighborhood [Source: [Chicago Park District](https://www.chicagoparkdistrict.com/parks-facilities/hegewisch-marsh-park)]

- **Calumet Park - Chicago Park District** (1-2 hours; 3-5 miles southeast, 10-15 minutes by car) - Sprawling 181.70-acre lakeside park offering beach access, boat launch, multiple sports fields, Lake Shore Model Train exhibit, and extensive recreational facilities. Originally envisioned in 1903 as part of the South Park Commission's revolutionary neighborhood park system, it provides lakefront recreation opportunities with both active sports and passive recreation options. [Source: [Chicago Park District](https://www.chicagoparkdistrict.com/parks-facilities/calumet-park)]

This allows time to explore the visitor center (1-1.5 hours), join a ranger tour (1-1.5 hours), and walk through key historic sites. Additional time can be added for the A. Philip Randolph Pullman Porter Museum (1 hour) if visiting as part of the same trip.
