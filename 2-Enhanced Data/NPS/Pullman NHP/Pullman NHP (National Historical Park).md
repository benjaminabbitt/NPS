# Pullman NHP (National Historical Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Administration Clock Tower Building Visitor Center** (610 E 111th St, Chicago, IL 60628 (41.6936, -87.6089); Wednesday-Sunday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM, closed Monday-Tuesday, Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; (773) 928-7257) [Source: [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/pull/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/pullman-national-historical-park-chicago-2)]

- **Historic Pullman Foundation Exhibit Hall** (11141 S Cottage Grove Ave, Chicago, IL 60628; Thursday-Sunday; (773) 785-8901) [Source: [Visit Chicago Southland](https://www.visitchicagosouthland.com/272/pullman-national-historical-park-visitor-information-center)]

**Note:** Call ahead at (773) 928-7257 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center Exhibits and Museum** (1-1.5 hours) - Explore audio and visual exhibits about Pullman's industrial history, labor movement, and civil rights legacy, including the special "100 Years of Brotherhood" exhibit commemorating the Brotherhood of Sleeping Car Porters; 610 E 111th St, Chicago, IL 60628 (41.6936, -87.6089); Wednesday-Sunday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/pull/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [National Park Foundation](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/pullman-national-monument), [NPS Randolph Museum](https://www.nps.gov/places/national-a-philip-randolph-pullman-porter-museum.htm)]

- **Free Ranger-Guided Tours** (1-1.5 hours) - Join daily ranger-led tours of the historic factory grounds and clock tower building (weather permitting, indoor tours available during inclement weather); tours depart from Visitor Center at 11:00 AM and 2:00 PM daily (winter hours: 2:00 PM only); 610 E 111th St, Chicago, IL 60628; Wednesday-Sunday [Source: [NPS Ranger Tours](https://home.nps.gov/thingstodo/take-a-pullman-ranger-guided-tour.htm), [Beyond My Door](https://www.beyondmydoor.com/pullman-national-historical-park/)]

- **Self-Guided Walking Tour of Historic Row Houses** (1.5-2 hours) - Walk through America's first planned industrial community viewing the original 1880s Queen Anne and brick row houses; pick up a free Walking Tour Brochure from the Visitor Center or Pullman Exhibit Hall; accessible year-round during daylight hours [Source: [Historic Pullman Foundation Tours](https://pullmanil.org/take-a-tour/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g35805-d534488-Reviews-Pullman_Historic_District-Chicago_Illinois.html)]

- **Hotel Florence Exterior Viewing and Grounds** (30-45 minutes) - Walk around the grounds of this historic 1881 Queen Anne style hotel, view the elaborate Victorian veranda and ornate architectural details (interior currently closed for renovation); 11111 S Forrestville Ave, Chicago, IL (41.6926, -87.6094); exterior accessible year-round [Source: [Hotel Florence Illinois Historic Preservation](https://dnrhistoric.illinois.gov/experience/sites/site.hotel-florence.html), [Beyond My Door](https://www.beyondmydoor.com/pullman-national-historical-park/)]

- **Greenstone Church Viewing** (15-30 minutes) - Admire the 1882 Richardsonian Romanesque church featuring a distinctive 92-foot tower and large stained glass rose window made from green serpentine stone quarried in Pennsylvania; 11211 S Saint Lawrence Ave, Chicago, IL; exterior viewing available year-round [Source: [Historic Pullman Foundation Greenstone](https://pullmanil.org/greenstone-church/), [SAH Archipedia](https://sah-archipedia.org/buildings/IL-01-031-0073-03)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (30-60 minutes) - Complete activity booklet while exploring the Visitor Center museum, share answers with a park ranger, and receive an official Pullman Junior Ranger badge; available for ages 4-99 year-round at no cost; 610 E 111th St, Chicago, IL 60628; Wednesday-Sunday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM [Source: [Ranger Trek Junior Ranger](https://rangertrek.us/ranger-treks-guide-to-the-pullman-national-historical-park-junior-ranger-program/), [NPS Junior Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/become-a-pullman-junior-ranger.htm)]

- **Pullman House Project Historic Home Tours** (1 hour 15 minutes) - Tour the interiors of the restored Jordan and Dunbar executive homes styled to the 1880s-1900 period with interpreters escorting visitors; Main Tour departs daily at 10:00 AM from Welcome Center; $20 per person (purchase online before 9:00 AM day of tour); approximately 1/4 mile walking [Source: [Pullman at Home Tours](https://www.pullmanathome.org/tours), [Choose Chicago House Tour](https://www.choosechicago.com/blog/architecture-history/step-inside-historic-homes-during-the-50th-annual-pullman-house-tour/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **National A. Philip Randolph Pullman Porter Museum** (1-1.5 hours) - Visit the nation's only African-American labor history museum dedicated to preserving the heritage of the Brotherhood of Sleeping Car Porters, the first Black labor union recognized by the American Federation of Labor; 10406 S Maryland Ave, Chicago, IL 60628 (entrance at 817 E 104th St); Thursday-Saturday 11:00 AM-4:00 PM, April-November (winter by appointment only); call (773) 850-8580 to confirm hours [Source: [Pullman Porter Museum](https://thepullmanportersmuseum.org/visit), [NPS Randolph Museum](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/explore-the-national-a-philip-randolph-pullman-porter-museum.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g35805-d7729879-Reviews-A_Philip_Randolph_Pullman_Porter_Museum-Chicago_Illinois.html)]

- **Historic Pullman Foundation Monthly Walking Tours** (90 minutes) - Join volunteer resident guides for in-depth walks through Pullman's history, architecture, and labor legacy on the first Sunday of each month, May-October; tours begin at Pullman Exhibit Hall at 1:00 PM; $20 ($15 for seniors/students) includes Exhibit Hall access and 17-minute documentary film; 11141 S Cottage Grove Ave, Chicago, IL 60628 [Source: [Historic Pullman Foundation Tours](https://pullmanil.org/take-a-tour/), [Visit Chicago Southland](https://www.visitchicagosouthland.com/1026/HISTORICPULLMANFOUNDATIONWALKINGTOUR)]

- **Annual Historic Pullman House Tour** (self-guided, 3-4 hours) - Step inside seven privately owned historic row homes representing different residential styles from Pullman's 1880s heyday (held annually in mid-October); October 11-12, 2025, 11:00 AM-5:00 PM both days; tickets required [Source: [Choose Chicago House Tour](https://www.choosechicago.com/blog/architecture-history/step-inside-historic-homes-during-the-50th-annual-pullman-house-tour/), [Historic Pullman Foundation](https://pullmanil.org/event/50th-annual-pullman-house-tour-weekend-save-the-date/2023-10-07/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Griffin Museum of Science and Industry** (3-4 hours, 5.7 miles north) - Explore the largest science museum in the Western Hemisphere housed in the only remaining building from the 1893 World's Fair, featuring a WWII German U-boat, coal mine shaft, Science Storms exhibit, renovated Henry Crown Space Center with Apollo 8 command module, and The Great Train Story celebrating Chicago's rail legacy; 5700 S DuSable Lake Shore Dr, Chicago, IL 60637; daily hours vary by season; (773) 684-1414 [Source: [MSI Chicago](https://www.msichicago.org/), [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/usa/chicago/hyde-park-and-south-side/attractions/museum-of-science-industry/a/poi-sig/378729/1319217), [Choose Chicago](https://www.choosechicago.com/articles/museums-art/museum-science-and-industry-chicago/)]

- **Garden of the Phoenix (Japanese Garden in Jackson Park)** (45-60 minutes, 6 miles north) - Wander through this serene 130-year-old traditional Japanese garden on Wooded Island featuring koi pond, historic Kasuga Lantern from 1893, zigzag stone paths, nearly 250 cherry blossom trees, and Yoko Ono's "Sky Landing" sculpture; located in Jackson Park on Wooded Island, Chicago, IL 60637; free admission year-round (suggested $10 donation); no dogs, bikes, strollers, or picnics allowed [Source: [Timeout Chicago](https://www.timeout.com/chicago/attractions/garden-of-the-phoenix), [Garden of the Phoenix Foundation](https://www.gardenofthephoenix.org/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g35805-d3250604-Reviews-Jackson_Park_s_Japanese_Garden-Chicago_Illinois.html)]

- **DuSable Black History Museum and Education Center** (1.5-2 hours, 5.5 miles north) - Visit the oldest and largest repository of African American culture in the United States with 13,000 artifacts, photographs, and art objects; 740 E 56th Pl, Chicago, IL 60637 (Washington Park); Wednesday-Sunday 11:00 AM-4:00 PM, closed Monday-Tuesday; (773) 947-0600 [Source: [DuSable Museum](https://dusablemuseum.org/), [Choose Chicago](https://www.choosechicago.com/listing/dusable-museum-of-african-american-history-2/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/the-dusable-black-history-museum-and-education-center-chicago-2)]

- **Frank Lloyd Wright's Robie House** (1 hour guided tour, 6 miles north) - Tour one of the ten most significant structures of the 20th century and a perfect expression of Wright's Prairie School style, completed in 1910 as a U.S. National Historic Landmark; 5757 S Woodlawn Ave, Chicago, IL 60637; Thursday-Monday 10:00 AM-3:00 PM, closed Tuesday-Wednesday; purchase tickets in advance; check in 15 minutes prior to tour [Source: [UChicago Arts Robie House](https://arts.uchicago.edu/explore/museums-galleries/robie-house), [Chicago Traveler](https://www.chicagotraveler.com/architecture/frank-lloyd-wrights-robie-house/)]

- **Smart Museum of Art** (1-1.5 hours, 6 miles north) - Discover over 17,000 objects in this free University of Chicago museum including works by Mark Rothko, Arthur Dove, and Frank Lloyd Wright's dining room furniture from Robie House, plus East Asian ritual objects, Renaissance/Baroque art, and contemporary works; 5550 S Greenwood Ave, Chicago, IL 60637; Tuesday-Wednesday and Friday-Sunday 10:00 AM-4:30 PM, Thursday 10:00 AM-8:00 PM, closed Monday; free admission [Source: [Smart Museum](https://smartmuseum.uchicago.edu/visit/), [Chicago Traveler](https://www.chicagotraveler.com/museums/smart-museum-of-art/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g35805-d526117-Reviews-Smart_Museum_of_Art-Chicago_Illinois.html)]
