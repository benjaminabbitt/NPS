# Bent's Old Fort NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Bent's Old Fort Visitor Center** (35110 State Highway 194 E, La Junta, CO 81050 (38.0397748, -103.42665); Summer (March-October): 8:00 AM - 5:30 PM, Winter (October-March): Friday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM; 719-383-5010) [Sources: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/beol/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Contact](https://www.nps.gov/beol/contacts.htm), [Beyond My Door](https://www.beyondmydoor.com/bents-old-fort-national-historic-site/), [2024-2025 Winter Operations](https://www.nps.gov/beol/learn/news/offseasonhours_24-25.htm)]

**Note:** Call 719-383-5010 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. Park rangers are generally available from 9:00 AM - 3:30 PM Mountain Time. The first building on your left as you enter into the fort serves as the visitor center where passport stamps are available. [Sources: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/bents-old-fort-national-historic-site/), [NPS Contact](https://www.nps.gov/beol/contacts.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Ranger-Guided Fort Tour** (75 minutes) - Join a park ranger on a guided tour through the reconstructed 1840s adobe trading post exploring furnished rooms including the billiard room, council room, stores, blacksmith shop, and living quarters while learning about the fort's role in the Santa Fe Trail trade between Taos and St. Louis and its significance to Plains Indians, traders, and travelers; tours limited to 25 participants; 35110 State Highway 194 E, La Junta, CO 81050 (38.0397748, -103.42665); Summer 2025: tours at 9:00 AM and 11:00 AM on Saturdays, 11:00 AM only on Sundays, Mondays, Thursdays, and Fridays; 719-383-5010 [Sources: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/guided_tours_bentsoldfort.htm), [CPR News May 2025](https://www.cpr.org/2025/05/29/bents-old-fort-guided-park-ranger-tour/), [NPS Summer 2025 Announcement](https://www.nps.gov/beol/learn/news/guidedtours-summer2025.htm)]

- **Walk the Arkansas River Trail** (30-60 minutes) - Hike the 1.5-1.7 mile easy looping trail system along the Arkansas River meandering through blue grama and buffalo grasses hemmed by cottonwood groves, passing interpretive signs about the fort's history and natural environment; trailhead begins after the quarter-mile paved path from parking lot to fort; 35110 State Highway 194 E, La Junta, CO 81050 (38.0397748, -103.42665); open daily 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM; 719-383-5010 [Sources: [NPS Hiking Along Arkansas River](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hiking_at_bents_old_fort.htm), [AllTrails Bent's Old Fort Trail](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/colorado/bents-old-fort-national-historic-site-trail), [Beyond My Door](https://www.beyondmydoor.com/bents-old-fort-national-historic-site/)]

- **Watch "Traders, Tribes, and Travelers" Documentary** (20 minutes) - View the orientation film inside the visitor center that brings the fort to life and tells the story of Bent's Old Fort, providing historical context before exploring the reconstructed trading post; 35110 State Highway 194 E, La Junta, CO 81050 (38.0397748, -103.42665); shown during visitor center hours; 719-383-5010 [Sources: [Uncover Colorado](https://www.uncovercolorado.com/national-lands/bents-old-fort-national-historic-site/), [Oh, Ranger! Things To Do](http://www.ohranger.com/bents-old-fort/things-do), [CMC Library](https://cmc.marmot.org/Record/.b3411466x?searchId=770022583&recordIndex=2&page=1)]

- **Living History Demonstrations** (varies, typically 15-30 minutes each) - Interact with interpretive rangers dressed in period costumes portraying fort workers, traders, and inhabitants from the 1830s and 1840s who share stories about daily life, trade practices, and historical events at this important Santa Fe Trail crossroads; 35110 State Highway 194 E, La Junta, CO 81050 (38.0397748, -103.42665); offered regularly during summer months (Memorial Day to Labor Day); 719-383-5010 [Sources: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60731-d536487-Reviews-Bent_s_Old_Fort_National_Historic_Site-La_Junta_Colorado.html), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/bents-old-fort-national-historic-site/), [WNPA](https://wnpa.org/discover_parks/bents-old-fort/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Junior Ranger Program** (90 minutes) - Pick up a free Junior Ranger booklet at the visitor center and complete age-appropriate activities while exploring the fort to learn about its history and significance, then return to be sworn in as a Junior Ranger and receive a badge; recommended allowing at least 90 minutes to complete the program; 35110 State Highway 194 E, La Junta, CO 81050 (38.0397748, -103.42665); available during visitor center hours; free; 719-383-5010 [Sources: [NPS Junior Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/beol/learn/kidsyouth/junior-ranger.htm), [Bent County Democrat](https://www.bcdemocratonline.com/bents-old-fort-announces-special-junior-ranger-day-program-this-saturday/), [A Humble Place](https://ahumbleplace.com/bents-old-fort-junior-ranger-adventures/)]

- **Bead Trading Activity** (15-30 minutes) - Participate in a hands-on bead trading activity where visitors can make a lovely necklace, learning about the trading practices that were central to the fort's operations in the 1830s-1840s; available during special programs and events; 35110 State Highway 194 E, La Junta, CO 81050 (38.0397748, -103.42665); check calendar for availability; 719-383-5010 [Sources: [Uncover Colorado](https://www.uncovercolorado.com/national-lands/bents-old-fort-national-historic-site/), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60731-d536487-Reviews-Bent_s_Old_Fort_National_Historic_Site-La_Junta_Colorado.html)]

- **Evening Candlelight Tours** (duration varies, event starts 5:30 PM or 6:30 PM) - Experience the fort by candlelight during special evening tours where living history staff and volunteers in period dress provide an atmospheric view into the lives of fort workers, Plains Indians, travelers, soldiers, and Mexican families from the 1830s-1840s; offered during holiday celebrations (typically first weekend in December); 35110 State Highway 194 E, La Junta, CO 81050 (38.0397748, -103.42665); reservations required via Recreation.gov, arrive 15 minutes before reservation time; 719-383-5010 [Sources: [Recreation.gov Evening Candlelight Tour](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/10089883/ticket/10089884), [National Parks Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/2010/11/candlelight-tours-bents-old-fort-national-historic-site-part-holiday-celebration7268), [FOX21 News](https://www.fox21news.com/news/evening-candlelight-tours-at-bents-old-fort/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Koshare Indian Museum** (1-2 hours, 7 miles) - Explore one of the largest collections of Native American artifacts and art in the world, featuring the world's largest self-supported log ceiling, traditional Native American dance performances during summer months, and a trading post; 115 W 18th St, La Junta, CO 81050 (37.9719229, -103.5446126) (approximately 37.9859, -103.5482); Wednesday-Sunday 11:00 AM - 3:00 PM, closed Mondays and Tuesdays; 719-384-4411 [Sources: [Koshares Official Site](https://koshares.com/), [Choose La Junta](https://chooselj.com/koshare-museum/), [Colorado.com](https://www.colorado.com/la-junta/koshare-indian-museum)]

- **Vogel Canyon Recreation Area** (1-2 hours hiking, 13 miles) - Hike through scenic canyonlands with pinon pine and sandstone formations, discover Native American rock art and remnants of the old Westbrook settlement ruins, and explore natural springs on trails ranging from the easy 1-mile Overlook Trail (handicap accessible) to the moderately challenging 1.75-mile Canyon Trail loop; Comanche National Grassland, 13 miles south of La Junta on Highway 109, then west on CR 802 for 1.5 miles, then south on FS Road 505A for 1.5 miles (37.7700129, -103.5126424); open year-round during daylight hours; free; 719-384-2181 [Sources: [Uncover Colorado Vogel Canyon](https://www.uncovercolorado.com/activities/vogel-canyon-picnic-area/), [US Forest Service Vogel Canyon](https://www.fs.usda.gov/recarea/psicc/recarea/?recid=81660), [AllTrails Vogel Canyon](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/colorado/vogel-canyon-trail)]

- **Picket Wire Canyonlands** (4-4.5 hours hiking or all-day guided auto tour, 30 miles) - Hike the 11.2-mile round-trip trail or take a guided auto tour to see North America's largest dinosaur tracksite with over 1,900 individual footprints across 130+ trackways, plus Native American rock art and historic ruins along the Purgatoire River; Withers Canyon Trailhead via CO-109 south 13 miles, west on FS Road 2200 for 8 miles, south on CR 25 for 6 miles, then 3.2 miles following signs (37.659770, -103.570910); trails open year-round, guided auto tours offered Saturdays 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM during Spring and Fall; reservations at Recreation.gov or 877-444-6777 [Sources: [Uncover Colorado Picketwire](https://www.uncovercolorado.com/activities/picketwire-canyonlands/), [US Forest Service Picket Wire](https://www.fs.usda.gov/recarea/psicc/recarea/?recid=77620), [AllTrails Picketwire](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/colorado/picketwire-canyon-trail)]

- **Otero Museum** (45-60 minutes, 7 miles) - Discover exhibits covering Otero County heritage from 1875-1945 including displays on transportation, pioneer life, and local history; 706 W 3rd St, La Junta, CO 81050 (37.985809, -103.5521372) (approximately 37.9546, -103.5152); June 1-September 30: Monday-Friday 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM (closed Tuesdays), off-season tours by appointment; 719-384-7500 [Sources: [Otero Museum Official Site](https://www.oteromuseum.org/), [Colorado.com Otero Museum](https://www.colorado.com/la-junta/history-heritage/history-museums/otero-museum), [TripAdvisor Otero Museum](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60731-d534492-Reviews-Otero_Museum-La_Junta_Colorado.html)]

- **John Martin Reservoir State Park** (half-day to full day, 40 miles) - Enjoy fishing, boating, swimming, hiking, camping, and birdwatching at Colorado's second largest lake with 373 recorded bird species making it one of the state's most prolific birding areas, featuring 13,176 acres with a massive dam; from La Junta, take US-50 west to Hasty, turn south on CR 24 for approximately 2 miles to visitor center; park open 24 hours for camping/boating/fishing, other activities 5:00 AM - 10:00 PM, visitor center Monday-Friday 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM; 719-829-1801 [Sources: [Colorado Parks and Wildlife John Martin](https://cpw.state.co.us/state-parks/john-martin-reservoir-state-park), [Colorado.com John Martin](https://www.colorado.com/hasty/john-martin-reservoir-state-park), [Uncover Colorado John Martin](https://www.uncovercolorado.com/state-parks/john-martin-reservoir/)]
