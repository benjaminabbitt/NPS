# Johnstown Flood N MEM (National Memorial)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Lake View Visitor Center** - 733 Lake Road, South Fork, PA 15956 (40.34028, -78.77083); Open Fridays through Tuesdays from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, closed Wednesdays, Thursdays, Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 814-886-6171 [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/jofl/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Basic Information](https://home.nps.gov/jofl/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** Call 814-886-6171 to confirm current stamp locations and hours before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center Museum Exhibits (1-2 hours)** - Explore two floors of exhibits including maps, views of the former dam, tactile displays, historic photographs of the South Fork Fishing and Hunting Club, a reproduction morgue book, oral history of flood survivor Victor Heiser, animated light map showing the flood path, and a large diorama of young Victor Heiser clinging to his house during the flood; 733 Lake Road, South Fork, PA 15956; Fridays-Tuesdays 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPPlan Visitor Center](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/pennsylvania-national-parks/johnstown-flood-national-memorial-park-at-a-glance/johnstown-flood-national-memorial-visitor-center-and-museum/), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/jofl/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Black Friday Film (35 minutes)** - Watch the park's signature 35-minute film "Black Friday" in the large auditorium, shown every hour at 15 minutes past the hour (9:15, 10:15, 11:15, 12:15, 1:15, 2:15, 3:15, 4:15); 733 Lake Road, South Fork, PA 15956; Fridays-Tuesdays 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPPlan Visitor Center](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/pennsylvania-national-parks/johnstown-flood-national-memorial-park-at-a-glance/johnstown-flood-national-memorial-visitor-center-and-museum/), [NPS Black Friday Film](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/view-the-park-film-black-friday.htm)]

- **South Fork Dam Trail/South Abutment Trail (30 minutes)** - Loop trail descending from the south abutment overlook down to the dry lake bed of former Lake Conemaugh, passing through the gap in the dam caused by the 1889 flood waters, featuring numerous interpretive panels along the 0.4-mile route with stairs leading back up to the south abutment; trailhead at visitor center area; dawn to dusk [Source: [NPPlan South Fork Dam Trail](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/pennsylvania-national-parks/johnstown-flood-national-memorial-park-at-a-glance/johnstown-flood-national-memorial-hiking-trails/johnstown-flood-national-memorial-south-fork-dam-trail/), [NPS Trails](https://www.nps.gov/jofl/planyourvisit/trails.htm)]

- **Dam Remnants Exploration (30-45 minutes)** - Walk on both sides of the South Fork Dam remnants from the south abutment overlook, viewing where the dam broke and examining the remaining earthworks and stonework from the 1889 structure; accessible from visitor center area; dawn to dusk [Source: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60869-d294405-Reviews-Johnstown_Flood_National_Memorial-South_Fork_Pennsylvania.html), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/jofl/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **St. Michael Historic District Self-Guided Auto Tour (30-45 minutes)** - Drive past 9 of the original 16 cottages from the South Fork Fishing and Hunting Club days in the nearby town of St. Michael, viewing the exterior of the historic clubhouse (acquired by NPS in 2006 and renovated 2014-2019); St. Michael, PA (adjacent to memorial); accessible during daylight hours [Source: [NPPlan St. Michael Historic District](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/pennsylvania-national-parks/johnstown-flood-national-memorial-park-at-a-glance/johnstown-flood-national-memorial-self-guided-auto-tour/johnstown-flood-national-memorial-st-michael-historic-district/), [NPS Cottages](https://www.nps.gov/jofl/learn/historyculture/the-cottages-of-the-south-fork-fishing-and-hunting-club.htm)]

- **Ranger-Guided Van Tours (2-3 hours, seasonal)** - Free ranger-led van tour around Lake Conemaugh stopping at north and south abutments overlooks and the South Fork Fishing and Hunting Club clubhouse, offered Fridays-Sundays from June through September; registration required by calling 814-886-6170 or at visitor center day-of; 733 Lake Road, South Fork, PA 15956 [Source: [NPPlan Ranger-Guided Tours](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/pennsylvania-national-parks/johnstown-flood-national-memorial-park-at-a-glance/johnstown-flood-national-memorial-ranger-guided-van-tour/), [NPS Club House Tours](https://www.nps.gov/jofl/learn/news/club-house-and-cottage-row-walking-tours.htm)]

- **Picnicking (flexible)** - Accessible picnic areas throughout the memorial with restroom facilities; accessible areas at visitor center; dawn to dusk [Source: [NPS Accessibility](https://www.nps.gov/jofl/planyourvisit/accessibility.htm), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60869-d294405-Reviews-Johnstown_Flood_National_Memorial-South_Fork_Pennsylvania.html)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Carriage Road Nature Trail (30-45 minutes)** - Follow the historic carriage road once used by South Fork Fishing and Hunting Club members, offering a quieter experience and forest scenery; trailhead near visitor center area; dawn to dusk [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/jofl/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [NPS Trails](https://www.nps.gov/jofl/planyourvisit/trails.htm)]

- **Path of the Flood Guided Hikes (4-6 hours, seasonal)** - Special ranger-led hikes following portions of the flood path from the dam site toward Johnstown, offered as Part I and Part II experiences during special events from Memorial Day through October; registration required, call 814-886-6170; various trailheads [Source: [NPS Path of Flood Hikes](https://home.nps.gov/jofl/planyourvisit/path-of-the-flood-hike.htm), [Path of Flood Trail](https://www.cambriaconservationrecreation.com/path-of-the-flood-trail/)]

- **Club House and Cottage Row Walking Tours (1-2 hours, seasonal)** - Special ranger-guided walking tours of the South Fork Fishing and Hunting Club facilities including interior access to the clubhouse when available, offered during special events Memorial Day weekend through October; free but registration required; St. Michael, PA [Source: [NPS Club House Tours](https://www.nps.gov/jofl/learn/news/club-house-and-cottage-row-walking-tours.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Johnstown Flood Museum (1.5-2 hours)** - Comprehensive museum dedicated to the 1889 Johnstown Flood with exhibits, artifacts, and film presentations housed in the former Cambria Public Library in downtown Johnstown, about 10 miles from the memorial; admission includes Wagner Ritter House and Johnstown Heritage Center; 304 Washington Street, Johnstown, PA 15901; Tuesday-Sunday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM; $10 adults, $8 seniors/children 3-18; 814-539-1889 [Source: [Heritage Johnstown Flood Museum](https://www.heritagejohnstown.org/attractions/johnstown-flood-museum/), [TripAdvisor Flood Museum](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g52908-d585661-Reviews-Johnstown_Flood_Museum-Johnstown_Pennsylvania.html)]

- **Johnstown Inclined Plane (30-45 minutes)** - World's steepest vehicular inclined plane built in 1890 following the 1889 flood, offering panoramic views of Johnstown and surrounding valleys (note: currently closed for major renovation, call to verify status before visiting); 711 Edgehill Drive, Johnstown, PA 15905; 814-536-1816; 10 miles from memorial [Source: [Heritage Johnstown Other Attractions](https://www.heritagejohnstown.org/attractions/other-locations/), [Uncovering PA Flood Museum](https://uncoveringpa.com/johnstown-flood-museum)]

- **Grandview Cemetery - Plot of the Unknown (45 minutes)** - Final resting place of 777 unidentified victims of the 1889 flood under identical blank marble headstones, featuring the 21-foot tall Monument to the Unknown Dead with three allegorical figures (Faith, Hope, and Charity); free and open to public; 801 Millcreek Road, Johnstown, PA (40.315606, -78.927417); accessible daily dawn to dusk; 10 miles from memorial [Source: [Roadside America Plot Unknown](https://www.roadsideamerica.com/story/20053), [Visit Johnstown Grandview](https://www.visitjohnstownpa.com/partner/grandview-cemetery-plot-of-the-unknown)]

- **Heritage Discovery Center (2-3 hours)** - Museum exploring immigrant experiences and Johnstown's iron and steel heritage in the Cambria City National Historic District, including Johnstown Children's Museum, "America: Through Immigrant Eyes" exhibit, and Iron & Steel Gallery; 201 6th Avenue, Johnstown, PA; May-November: Monday-Saturday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM-5:00 PM; December-April: Monday, Friday, Saturday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM-5:00 PM; $10 adults, $8 seniors/children; 12 miles from memorial [Source: [Heritage Discovery Center](https://www.heritagejohnstown.org/attractions/heritage-discovery-center/), [Heritage Discovery Hours](https://www.heritagejohnstown.org/attractions/heritage-discovery-center/visitor-information/admissions-hours/)]

- **Horseshoe Curve National Historic Landmark (1-2 hours)** - Engineering marvel of 19th-century railroad construction featuring observation areas, exhibit hall, and museum store; visitors can climb 194 stone stairs or use funicular (call ahead for funicular availability); 3499 Beale Avenue, Altoona, PA 16601; open daily 9:30 AM with varying closing times, admission stops 45 minutes prior to close; reduced admission fees while funicular unavailable; 814-946-0834 x2101; 25 miles from memorial [Source: [Railroaders Memorial Museum Horseshoe Curve](https://www.railroadcity.org/horseshoecurve), [TripAdvisor Horseshoe Curve](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g30034-d117097-Reviews-Horseshoe_Curve_National_Historic_Landmark-Altoona_Pennsylvania.html)]

- **Allegheny Portage Railroad National Historic Site (2-3 hours)** - National historic site preserving the first railroad to cross the Allegheny Mountains, featuring Summit Level Visitor Center, historic Lemon House tavern, Engine House #6, Skew Arch Bridge, and hiking trails; 110 Federal Park Road, Gallitzin, PA 16641; December-April: Fridays-Tuesdays 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; May-November: Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; free admission; 814-886-6150; 20 miles from memorial [Source: [NPS Allegheny Portage Hours](https://www.nps.gov/alpo/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Allegheny Portage](https://www.nps.gov/alpo)]
