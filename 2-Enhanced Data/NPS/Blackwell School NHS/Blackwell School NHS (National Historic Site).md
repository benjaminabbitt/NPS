# Blackwell School NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Blackwell School National Historic Site** (501 S Abbot St, Marfa, TX 79843 [30.3058, -104.0223]; Saturday & Sunday 12:00 PM - 4:00 PM year-round, weekday tours available by advance request 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM with at least one month notice; 432-426-3224 ext 223) [Source: [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/blsc/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Texas Time Travel](https://texastimetravel.com/directory/blackwell-school/)]

**Note:** Call ahead to confirm current stamp locations and hours before visiting. As of July 2024, the National Park Service has assumed operations and the site is in transition with limited hours and services.

## Key Activities

- **Self-Guided Tour of Historic Buildings** (1-1.5 hours) - Explore the 1909 adobe schoolhouse and 1927 Band Hall annex, viewing photographs, memorabilia, and interpretive panels featuring stories from students and teachers who experienced segregated education for Mexican and Mexican American children; 501 S Abbot St, Marfa, TX 79843 [30.3058, -104.0223]; Saturday & Sunday 12:00 PM - 4:00 PM, weekday tours by advance request [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/blsc/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Austin Chronicle](https://www.austinchronicle.com/columns/2024-08-23/day-trips-blackwell-school-national-historic-site-marfa/)]

- **Architectural Study** (30 minutes) - Observe the original adobe construction and clay tile work representing early 20th-century local building methods on the exterior of the two-building complex; 501 S Abbot St, Marfa, TX 79843 [30.3058, -104.0223]; viewable 24/7 from exterior [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/blsc/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Playground Reflection Area** (15-30 minutes) - Use the playground area for quiet reflection on the significance of this segregated school's history and its impact on the Mexican American community of Marfa; 501 S Abbot St, Marfa, TX 79843 [30.3058, -104.0223]; accessible during site hours [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/blsc/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]
