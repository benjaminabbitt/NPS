## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-self-guided-museum-tour"></a>**Self-Guided Museum Tour** (30-60 minutes) - Explore photographs, memorabilia, and interpretive panels featuring quotes and stories from students and teachers who experienced de facto segregation at this Mexican American school from 1909-1965; includes viewing the original 1909 adobe schoolhouse with preserved desks and the 1927 Band Hall building; 501 South Abbott Street, Marfa, Texas 79843; Saturday-Sunday 12:00 PM-4:00 PM CST, weekdays by advance request 10:00 AM-4:00 PM CST [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/blsc/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [The Blackwell School Alliance](https://www.theblackwellschool.org/), [Austin Chronicle](https://www.austinchronicle.com/columns/2024-08-23/day-trips-blackwell-school-national-historic-site-marfa/)]

- <a id="activity-building-exterior-viewing"></a>**Building Exterior Viewing** (15-30 minutes) - View the exteriors of the 1909 adobe schoolhouse and 1927 Band Hall building that tell the story of Mexican American educational segregation, accessible when site is staffed or unstaffed; 501 South Abbott Street, Marfa, Texas 79843; viewable anytime from exterior [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/blsc/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- <a id="activity-historic-walking-tour"></a>**Historic Walking Tour** (30-45 minutes) - Self-guided walking tour around Marfa using historic school photos and interviews from the Blackwell School Alliance archive; pick up free newspaper guide at Marfa Library, City Hall, or Visitor Center; starts at various locations in Marfa, Texas [Source: [Blackwell Historic Walking Tour](https://www.theblackwellschool.org/historic-walking-tour)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-virtual-collection-exploration"></a>**Virtual Collection Exploration** (flexible timing) - Access the Blackwell School Alliance's online collection of photographs, artifacts, and oral histories from alumni and teachers; provides deeper context about life at the segregated school and Mexican American educational experiences in Texas; accessible online at any time [Source: [The Blackwell School Alliance](https://www.theblackwellschool.org/), [Texas Monthly](https://www.texasmonthly.com/being-texan/marfa-blackwell-school-texas-national-park-site/)]

- <a id="gem-playground-area"></a>**Playground Area** (15-30 minutes) - Small playground area on site where visitors can relax and reflect on the school's history in an outdoor setting; 501 South Abbott Street, Marfa, Texas 79843; accessible during site hours [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/blsc/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- <a id="gem-educational-history-context"></a>**Educational History Context** - Learn about the broader history of over 120 school districts across Texas that operated segregated schools for Mexican American students through de facto segregation, including the practice of punishing students for speaking Spanish through ceremonies like mock funerals for the Spanish language; context provided through exhibits [Source: [Smithsonian Magazine](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/the-newest-national-park-site-tells-the-story-of-school-segregation-in-americas-hispanic-communities-180984796/), [SDCELAR British Museum](https://www.sdcelarbritishmuseum.org/projects/blackwell-school-a-history-of-latinx-segretation-in-texas/), [Marfa Public Radio](https://www.marfapublicradio.org/2022-05-26/with-marfas-blackwell-school-poised-to-become-a-national-historic-site-a-look-at-what-segregated-education-was-like-in-west-texas)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-chinati-foundation"></a>**Chinati Foundation** (3-6 hours) - Contemporary art museum based on Donald Judd's ideas, featuring permanent large-scale installations across 340 acres of former Fort D.A. Russell; includes Judd's 100 untitled works in aluminum and 15 untitled works in concrete; full collection tour takes 6 hours, selections tour takes 3 hours, focus tours available; 1 Cavalry Row, Marfa, Texas 79843 (30.3075, -104.0182); Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM (last entry 4:00 PM); located in Marfa [Source: [Chinati Foundation Tours](https://chinati.org/visit-chinati/tours/), [Chinati Hours](https://chinati.org/hours-locations/), [Perceptive Travel](https://perceptivetravel.com/blog/2024/02/14/marfa-west-texas-prada-lights-chinati/)]

- <a id="nearby-prada-marfa"></a>**Prada Marfa** (15-30 minutes) - Permanent art installation by Scandinavian artists Elmgreen and Dragset featuring a freestanding Prada store stocked with 14 right-footed shoes and 6 purses, intended to be abandoned to the elements; not a retail outlet but a sculpture; Highway 90, Valentine, Texas (30.5958, -104.5208); accessible 24/7; 30 minutes west of Marfa, just past Valentine [Source: [Ballroom Marfa Guide](https://www.ballroommarfa.org/wp-content/uploads/Visitors-Guide.pdf), [Perceptive Travel](https://perceptivetravel.com/blog/2024/02/14/marfa-west-texas-prada-lights-chinati/), [Local Adventurer](https://localadventurer.com/things-to-do-in-marfa-tx/)]

- <a id="nearby-marfa-mystery-lights-viewing-area"></a>**Marfa Mystery Lights Viewing Area** (1-2 hours) - Watch for mysterious unexplained lights that appear in the distance over the desert; viewing area with benches, binoculars, restrooms, and interpretive plaques about the lights, vegetation, and land history; best viewing after dark (after 10:30 PM in summer, after 6:00 PM in winter); Highway 90, Marfa, Texas (30.2736, -103.9853); accessible 24 hours, restrooms open 24 hours; 9 miles east of Marfa [Source: [Salty Canary Guide](https://www.saltycanary.com/guide-to-the-marfa-lights/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g56230-d105949-Reviews-The_Marfa_Mystery_Lights-Marfa_Texas.html), [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/usa/texas/marfa/attractions/marfa-mystery-lights-viewing-area/a/poi-sig/1353314/1315496)]

- <a id="nearby-ballroom-marfa"></a>**Ballroom Marfa** (30-60 minutes) - Non-collecting contemporary art space in a historic 1920s ballroom supporting artists, musicians, and visionary thinkers; features exhibitions, public artworks, performances, screenings, and concerts; free admission (suggested $5 donation); 108 East San Antonio Street, Marfa, Texas 79843 (30.3065, -104.0174); Wednesday-Saturday 10:00 AM-6:00 PM, Sunday 10:00 AM-3:00 PM; in downtown Marfa [Source: [Ballroom Marfa Hours](https://www.ballroommarfa.org/stone-circle/hours/), [Ballroom Marfa](https://www.ballroommarfa.org/)]

- <a id="nearby-fort-davis-national-historic-site"></a>**Fort Davis National Historic Site** (2-3 hours) - Well-preserved 1850s frontier military post with 24 restored historic buildings and over 100 ruins showing frontier military life; includes self-guided building tours, visitor center with 15-minute video, and hiking trails; $10 per person or $20 per vehicle (7-day pass); 101 Lieutenant Henry Flipper Drive, Fort Davis, Texas 79734 (30.5881, -103.8942); daily 8:00 AM-5:00 PM (visitor center closes 4:30 PM), closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's, MLK Day; 21 miles north of Marfa (20-minute drive) [Source: [NPS Fort Davis Hours](https://www.nps.gov/foda/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Fort Davis](https://www.fortdavis.com/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60807-d143128-Reviews-Fort_Davis_National_Historic_Site-Fort_Davis_Texas.html)]

- <a id="nearby-mcdonald-observatory"></a>**McDonald Observatory** (1.5-6 hours) - World-class astronomical observatory with daytime tours and evening star parties; 82-inch telescope tour takes 75 minutes, multi-telescope tour takes 90-120 minutes, star parties available Tuesday/Friday/Saturday evenings; general admission includes self-guided summit tours; $8-25 depending on program; 3640 Dark Sky Drive, Fort Davis, Texas 79734 (30.6716, -104.0150); Tuesday-Saturday 12:00 PM-5:00 PM; reservations required; 25 miles from Marfa (37-minute drive via Fort Davis) [Source: [McDonald Observatory Programs](https://mcdonaldobservatory.org/visitors/programs/daytime), [McDonald Visitor Info](https://mcdonaldobservatory.org/visitors), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60807-d145621-Reviews-McDonald_Observatory-Fort_Davis_Texas.html)]

- <a id="nearby-davis-mountains-state-park"></a>**Davis Mountains State Park** (2-4 hours) - Scenic desert mountain state park offering hiking trails, wildlife viewing, and access to McDonald Observatory; park entrance and trails; standard Texas state park fees apply; Park Road 3, Fort Davis, Texas 79734 (30.5928, -103.9456); daily during daylight hours; 25 miles from Marfa (37-minute drive) [Source: [Davis Mountains State Park](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/davis-mountains), [Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Marfa/Davis-Mountains-State-Park-Headquarters-TX-118-Fort-Davis-TX-79734-USA), [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/usa/texas/fort-davis-davis-mountains/attractions/davis-mountains-state-park/a/poi-sig/1353316/1315497)]

- <a id="nearby-big-bend-national-park"></a>**Big Bend National Park** (full day or multi-day) - Massive 801,163-acre national park featuring the Chisos Mountains, Rio Grande canyons, desert landscapes, and extensive hiking trails; park entrance and numerous activity options; $30 per vehicle (7-day pass); various locations within park, nearest entrance via Highway 118 south; park open 24 hours, visitor centers vary; 103-142 miles south of Marfa (2-3 hour drive depending on destination) [Source: [Wanderlog Marfa to Big Bend](https://wanderlog.com/drive/between/60127/58815/marfa-to-big-bend-national-park-drive), [Trippy Distance](https://www.trippy.com/distance/Marfa-to-Big-Bend-National-Park), [Lost With Lydia](https://www.lostwithlydia.com/destinations/usa/tx/Marfa-Big-Bend-Road-trip/)]
