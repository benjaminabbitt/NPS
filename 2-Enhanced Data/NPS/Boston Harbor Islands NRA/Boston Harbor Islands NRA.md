# Boston Harbor Islands NRA (National Recreation Area)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Boston Harbor Islands Welcome Center** (191 W Atlantic Avenue, Boston, MA 02110; 42.3558, -71.0509; Seasonal hours, check website; Phone: 617-819-3587) - Main visitor center on the Rose Kennedy Greenway near Long Wharf. Information, exhibits, and ferry tickets. Stamps available during season. [Source: [NPS Boston Harbor Islands](https://www.nps.gov/boha/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Georges Island Visitor Contact** (Georges Island; 42.3185, -70.9306; Seasonal, ferry required) - Contact station at Fort Warren with ranger information and passport stamps. Open when island is accessible by ferry. [Source: [NPS Boston Harbor Islands](https://www.nps.gov/boha/index.htm)]

**Note:** Call 617-819-3587 during season for current information. Off-season contact via email. Check BostonHarborIslands.org for ferry schedules.

## Key Activities

- **Georges Island and Fort Warren** (3-5 hours) - Civil War-era fort and National Historic Landmark on the most visited island. Explore the stone fortifications, learn the legend of the "Lady in Black," and enjoy harbor views. Ranger programs and self-guided tours. Ferry from Long Wharf (seasonal). [Source: [NPS Boston Harbor Islands](https://www.nps.gov/boha/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Spectacle Island** (3-5 hours) - Hiking trails leading to the harbor's highest point with panoramic Boston skyline views. Visitor center, supervised swimming beach, cafe, and marina. Former landfill transformed into recreation destination. Ferry from Long Wharf. [Source: [NPS Boston Harbor Islands](https://www.nps.gov/boha/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Peddocks Island** (half-day to overnight) - One of the largest islands with rich biodiversity, historic military structures, and camping. Multiple trails through diverse habitats. Yurt camping available. Weekend ferry access during 2025 season. [Source: [NPS Boston Harbor Islands](https://www.nps.gov/boha/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Thompson Island (Cathleen Stone Island)** (4-6 hours) - Weekend access during 2025 season. Environmental education programs, hiking trails, and salt marsh exploration. Limited ferry service. [Source: [NPS Boston Harbor Islands](https://www.nps.gov/boha/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Lighthouse Cruises** (3-4 hours) - Special ferry cruises viewing three historic lighthouses: Boston Light (America's first lighthouse, 1716), Graves Light, and Long Island Light. Seasonal departures. [Source: [NPS Boston Harbor Islands](https://www.nps.gov/boha/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Camping** (overnight) - Camping on Peddocks Island in yurts or tent sites. Reservations required. Seasonal availability. Bring all supplies as no services on island. [Source: [NPS Boston Harbor Islands](https://www.nps.gov/boha/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Kayaking and Boating** (2-6 hours) - Paddle between islands in protected harbor waters. Public mooring reservations available for private boats. Rental kayaks available through outfitters. [Source: [NPS Boston Harbor Islands](https://www.nps.gov/boha/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Ranger Programs** (1-2 hours) - Guided tours, history programs, and nature walks on various islands. Program schedules available at visitor center and online. Seasonal. [Source: [NPS Boston Harbor Islands](https://www.nps.gov/boha/index.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Boston Light Tour** (half-day, limited dates) - Tour America's first lighthouse (1716) on Little Brewster Island. Limited access requires advance reservations. See the keeper's quarters and climb the tower. [Source: [NPS Boston Harbor Islands](https://www.nps.gov/boha/index.htm)]

- **All Access Boston Harbor** - Free and discounted ferry ticket programs for families, individuals, and groups to increase access to the islands. Apply through BostonHarborIslands.org. [Source: [NPS Boston Harbor Islands](https://www.nps.gov/boha/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Fort Andrews on Peddocks Island** (1-2 hours) - Explore the ruins of this early 20th-century coastal defense fort. Less visited than Fort Warren with atmospheric abandoned buildings. [Source: [NPS Boston Harbor Islands](https://www.nps.gov/boha/index.htm)]

- **Island-Hopping** (full day) - Inter-island ferry service allows visiting multiple islands in one day. Georges and Spectacle are the easiest combination. Check schedules carefully. [Source: [NPS Boston Harbor Islands](https://www.nps.gov/boha/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Night Sky Programs** (seasonal) - Special evening programs and stargazing on select islands. Limited dates. Check park calendar. [Source: [NPS Boston Harbor Islands](https://www.nps.gov/boha/index.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Boston NHP** (2-4 hours, downtown) - Freedom Trail historic sites including Faneuil Hall, Paul Revere House, and USS Constitution. Downtown Boston. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/index.htm)]

- **New England Aquarium** (2-3 hours, adjacent to ferry) - Major aquarium with Giant Ocean Tank, penguin colony, and marine mammal center. Central Wharf, next to ferry departure. [Source: New England Aquarium]

- **Boston Seaport District** (variable, adjacent) - Waterfront neighborhood with restaurants, Institute of Contemporary Art, and Boston Tea Party Ships & Museum. Walking distance from ferry. [Source: Visit Boston]

- **USS Constitution Museum** (1-2 hours, Charlestown Navy Yard) - Free museum adjacent to "Old Ironsides" with interactive exhibits on the ship's history. Part of Boston NHP. [Source: [NPS Boston NHP](https://www.nps.gov/bost/index.htm)]

## Total Recommended Time

**Total recommended visit time for Boston Harbor Islands NRA: Half-day to full day (per island)**

A half-day allows visiting one island (Georges or Spectacle). A full day enables island-hopping or thorough exploration with hiking and programs. Overnight camping on Peddocks extends the experience. Note: Ferry schedules dictate visit timing; check BostonHarborIslands.org for current service. Season runs approximately May-October.
