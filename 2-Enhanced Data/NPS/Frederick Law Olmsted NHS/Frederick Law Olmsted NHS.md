## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-guided-tour-of-fairsted-office"></a>**Guided Tour of Fairsted Office** (45-60 minutes) - Ranger-led tour through the historic office where Olmsted and his associates designed approximately 6,000 landscape projects, exploring the working areas of the buildings and learning about the firm's design process; 99 Warren Street, Brookline, MA 02445 (42.3253679, -71.1323087); Tours offered hourly during visitor center hours (late May-October, Friday-Sunday); 617-566-1689 [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60823-d532166-Reviews-Frederick_Law_Olmsted_National_Historic_Site-Brookline_Massachusetts.html), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/frla/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/massachusetts-national-parks/frederick-law-olmsted-national-historic-site-park-at-a-glance/frederick-law-olmsted-national-historic-site-house-tour/)]

- <a id="activity-landscape-tour"></a>**Landscape Tour** (30 minutes) - Guided walk through the 1.75-acre grounds designed by Olmsted himself, exploring his design principles including curvilinear paths, balance of shadow and sun, layered plant material, and borrowed views; 99 Warren Street, Brookline, MA 02445 (42.3253679, -71.1323087); Tours offered during visitor center hours; 617-566-1689 [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60823-d532166-Reviews-Frederick_Law_Olmsted_National_Historic_Site-Brookline_Massachusetts.html), [Source](https://olmsted.org/spotlight-on-frederick-law-olmsted-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="activity-self-guided-grounds-exploration"></a>**Self-Guided Grounds Exploration** (15-45 minutes) - Walk the restored cultural landscape at your own pace year-round, exploring the grounds that served as a living textbook for landscape architecture principles; 99 Warren Street, Brookline, MA 02445 (42.3253679, -71.1323087); Grounds open dawn to dusk daily year-round; 617-566-1689 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/frla/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60823-d532166-Reviews-Frederick_Law_Olmsted_National_Historic_Site-Brookline_Massachusetts.html)]

- <a id="activity-visitor-center-exhibits"></a>**Visitor Center Exhibits** (15-30 minutes) - View exhibits featuring video about Olmsted and Fairsted, displays from the extensive archives, and learn about the firm's impact on American landscape architecture; 99 Warren Street, Brookline, MA 02445 (42.3253679, -71.1323087); Friday-Sunday 10:00 AM-4:30 PM (late May-October); 617-566-1689 [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60823-d532166-Reviews-Frederick_Law_Olmsted_National_Historic_Site-Brookline_Massachusetts.html), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/frla/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-the-archives-vault-tour"></a>**The Archives Vault Tour** (varies) - View the climate-controlled vault containing 140,000 original landscape architectural plans and drawings plus 66,000 photographs from over 6,000 landscape projects designed by the Olmsted firm (1850s-1979), making it the world's most comprehensive landscape architecture archive; 99 Warren Street, Brookline, MA 02445 (42.3253679, -71.1323087); By advance reservation only; 617-566-1689 [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60823-d532166-Reviews-Frederick_Law_Olmsted_National_Historic_Site-Brookline_Massachusetts.html), [Source](https://olmsted.org/spotlight-on-frederick-law-olmsted-national-historic-site/), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/frla/olmsted-archives-collections.htm)]

- <a id="gem-winter-season-tours"></a>**Winter Season Tours** (45-60 minutes) - Tours of the design office available by advance reservation during the winter season (November-May) when the visitor center is closed to the public, offering a more intimate experience; 99 Warren Street, Brookline, MA 02445 (42.3253679, -71.1323087); By reservation Wednesday-Friday during winter season; Email park at least two weeks in advance; 617-566-1689 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/frla/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/massachusetts-national-parks/frederick-law-olmsted-national-historic-site-park-at-a-glance/frederick-law-olmsted-national-historic-site-house-tour/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-john-f-kennedy-nhs"></a>**John F. Kennedy NHS** (30-45 minutes tour, 10 minutes from Olmsted NHS) - Birthplace and childhood home of the 35th president with ranger-guided tours through the restored 1917 house where JFK was born; 83 Beals Street, Brookline, MA 02446 (42.3467, -71.1253); Closed seasonally November-May, open Thursday-Sunday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM with tours on the hour and half hour; Free admission; 617-566-7937 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/jofi/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60823-d532166-Reviews-Frederick_Law_Olmsted_National_Historic_Site-Brookline_Massachusetts.html), [Source](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/massachusetts-national-parks/john-fitzgerald-kennedy-national-historic-site-park-at-a-glance/)]

- <a id="nearby-arnold-arboretum"></a>**Arnold Arboretum** (1-3 hours, 10 minutes from Olmsted NHS) - North America's oldest public arboretum featuring 281 acres with over 15,000 plants, part of Boston's Emerald Necklace designed by Frederick Law Olmsted, with free guided tours mid-April through November; 125 Arborway, Boston (Jamaica Plain), MA 02130 (42.3011, -71.1258); Open sunrise to sunset daily, visitor center 10:00 AM-4:00 PM; Free admission [[Source](https://arboretum.harvard.edu/visit/), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60745-d104571-Reviews-Arnold_Arboretum-Boston_Massachusetts.html), [Source](https://www.bu.edu/articles/2024/to-do-today-arnold-arboretum-of-harvard-university/)]

- <a id="nearby-isabella-stewart-gardner-museum"></a>**Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum** (2+ hours, 15 minutes from Olmsted NHS) - Unique Venetian palazzo-style museum featuring European, Asian, and American art in an intimate setting with a stunning courtyard garden; 25 Evans Way, Boston, MA 02115 (42.3381, -71.0990); Wednesday-Monday 11:00 AM-5:00 PM (Thursday until 9:00 PM), closed Tuesday; 617-566-1401 [[Source](https://www.gardnermuseum.org/visit/hours), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60745-d108823-Reviews-Isabella_Stewart_Gardner_Museum-Boston_Massachusetts.html)]

- <a id="nearby-longfellow-house-washington-s-headquarters-nhs"></a>**Longfellow House - Washington's Headquarters NHS** (50 minutes tour, 20 minutes from Olmsted NHS) - Georgian mansion that served as George Washington's Revolutionary War headquarters and later home to poet Henry Wadsworth Longfellow for nearly 50 years, with ranger-led tours offered hourly; 105 Brattle Street, Cambridge, MA 02138 (42.3764, -71.1267); Closed seasonally November-May, open late May-October Wednesday-Sunday, visitor center 9:30 AM-5:00 PM with tours 10:00 AM-4:00 PM; Free admission; 617-876-4491 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/long/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/long/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [Source](https://travel.usnews.com/Cambridge_MA/Things_To_Do/Longfellow_House_Washingtons_Headquarters_63894/)]

- <a id="nearby-freedom-trail"></a>**Freedom Trail** (2-4 hours walk, 20 minutes from Olmsted NHS) - 2.5-mile walking path through downtown Boston connecting 16 historically significant Revolutionary War and colonial sites including Boston Common, Paul Revere's House, and the Old North Church; Trail begins at Boston Common, 139 Tremont Street, Boston, MA 02111 (42.3551, -71.0656); Open daily, most sites 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; Some sites charge admission, trail itself is free; Guided tours available [[Source](https://www.thefreedomtrail.org/faqs), [Source](https://actiontourguide.com/how-long-does-it-take-to-walk-the-freedom-trail/), [Source](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/walking-freedom-trail-boston/)]
