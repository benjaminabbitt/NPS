## Key Activities

- **Guided Tour of Fairsted Office** (45-60 minutes) - Ranger-led tour through the historic office where Olmsted and his associates designed approximately 6,000 landscape projects, exploring the working areas of the buildings and learning about the firm's design process; 99 Warren Street, Brookline, MA 02445 (42.3253679, -71.1323087); Tours offered hourly during visitor center hours (late May-October, Friday-Sunday); 617-566-1689 [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60823-d532166-Reviews-Frederick_Law_Olmsted_National_Historic_Site-Brookline_Massachusetts.html), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/frla/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/massachusetts-national-parks/frederick-law-olmsted-national-historic-site-park-at-a-glance/frederick-law-olmsted-national-historic-site-house-tour/)]

- **Landscape Tour** (30 minutes) - Guided walk through the 1.75-acre grounds designed by Olmsted himself, exploring his design principles including curvilinear paths, balance of shadow and sun, layered plant material, and borrowed views; 99 Warren Street, Brookline, MA 02445 (42.3253679, -71.1323087); Tours offered during visitor center hours; 617-566-1689 [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60823-d532166-Reviews-Frederick_Law_Olmsted_National_Historic_Site-Brookline_Massachusetts.html), [Source](https://olmsted.org/spotlight-on-frederick-law-olmsted-national-historic-site/)]

- **Self-Guided Grounds Exploration** (15-45 minutes) - Walk the restored cultural landscape at your own pace year-round, exploring the grounds that served as a living textbook for landscape architecture principles; 99 Warren Street, Brookline, MA 02445 (42.3253679, -71.1323087); Grounds open dawn to dusk daily year-round; 617-566-1689 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/frla/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60823-d532166-Reviews-Frederick_Law_Olmsted_National_Historic_Site-Brookline_Massachusetts.html)]

- **Visitor Center Exhibits** (15-30 minutes) - View exhibits featuring video about Olmsted and Fairsted, displays from the extensive archives, and learn about the firm's impact on American landscape architecture; 99 Warren Street, Brookline, MA 02445 (42.3253679, -71.1323087); Friday-Sunday 10:00 AM-4:30 PM (late May-October); 617-566-1689 [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60823-d532166-Reviews-Frederick_Law_Olmsted_National_Historic_Site-Brookline_Massachusetts.html), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/frla/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **The Archives Vault Tour** (varies) - View the climate-controlled vault containing 140,000 original landscape architectural plans and drawings plus 66,000 photographs from over 6,000 landscape projects designed by the Olmsted firm (1850s-1979), making it the world's most comprehensive landscape architecture archive; 99 Warren Street, Brookline, MA 02445 (42.3253679, -71.1323087); By advance reservation only; 617-566-1689 [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60823-d532166-Reviews-Frederick_Law_Olmsted_National_Historic_Site-Brookline_Massachusetts.html), [Source](https://olmsted.org/spotlight-on-frederick-law-olmsted-national-historic-site/), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/frla/olmsted-archives-collections.htm)]

- **Winter Season Tours** (45-60 minutes) - Tours of the design office available by advance reservation during the winter season (November-May) when the visitor center is closed to the public, offering a more intimate experience; 99 Warren Street, Brookline, MA 02445 (42.3253679, -71.1323087); By reservation Wednesday-Friday during winter season; Email park at least two weeks in advance; 617-566-1689 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/frla/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/massachusetts-national-parks/frederick-law-olmsted-national-historic-site-park-at-a-glance/frederick-law-olmsted-national-historic-site-house-tour/)]
