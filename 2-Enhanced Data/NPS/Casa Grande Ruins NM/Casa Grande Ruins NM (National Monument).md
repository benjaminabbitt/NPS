# Casa Grande Ruins NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Visitor Center** - 1100 W. Ruins Drive, Coolidge, AZ 85128 [32.9993, -111.5344]; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, Juneteenth, Independence Day); (520) 723-3172 [Source: [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/cagr/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/cagr/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

**Note:** Call ahead to confirm stamp availability and current hours.

## Key Activities

- **Self-Guided Walking Tour of Casa Grande (Great House)** (30-45 minutes) - Explore the nearly 700-year-old four-story Hohokam adobe structure (Siwan Wa'a ki in O'odham); walk through remnants of extensive compound and see canal irrigation systems; Compound A open daily 9 AM - 4 PM; 1100 W. Ruins Drive, Coolidge, AZ 85128 [32.9993, -111.5344]; free admission [Source: [Visit Arizona](https://www.visitarizona.com/places/parks-monuments/casa-grande-ruins-national-monument), [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/cagr/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Visitor Center Exhibits and Film** (20-30 minutes) - Interactive exhibits and park film introducing cultures connected to the structure; same address and hours as monument [Source: [Visit Arizona](https://www.visitarizona.com/places/parks-monuments/casa-grande-ruins-national-monument)]

- **Ballcourt Viewing** (10-15 minutes) - See ballcourt shaped from packed desert earth, part of Hohokam cultural complex; included in self-guided walk; same address and hours [Source: [Visit Arizona](https://www.visitarizona.com/places/parks-monuments/casa-grande-ruins-national-monument)]

- **Pollinator Garden** (10-15 minutes) - Explore native plant garden designed to attract pollinators; same address and hours [Source: [Visit Arizona](https://www.visitarizona.com/places/parks-monuments/casa-grande-ruins-national-monument)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (30-60 minutes) - Earn Junior Ranger badge during visit; pick up booklet at visitor center; same address and hours [Source: [Visit Arizona](https://www.visitarizona.com/places/parks-monuments/casa-grande-ruins-national-monument)]

- **Guided Tours** (45-60 minutes) - Ranger-led tours presenting agricultural community backstory; offered late fall and winter as staff available; check with visitor center for schedule; same address; (520) 723-3172 [Source: [Visit Arizona](https://www.visitarizona.com/places/parks-monuments/casa-grande-ruins-national-monument)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Birdwatching - Great Horned Owl Nest** (15-30 minutes) - Bring binoculars to spot great horned owls that have nested in the protective roof since 2006; best viewing early morning or late afternoon; same address and hours as monument [Source: [Visit Arizona](https://www.visitarizona.com/places/parks-monuments/casa-grande-ruins-national-monument)]

- **Native American Craft Demonstrations** (1-2 hours) - Late fall/winter weekend demonstrations by Native American locals showing traditional craft and farming techniques; check schedule with visitor center; same address; (520) 723-3172 [Source: [Visit Arizona](https://www.visitarizona.com/places/parks-monuments/casa-grande-ruins-national-monument)]

- **American Indian Arts and Music Festival** (3-4 hours) - Annual festival in late November featuring traditional arts and performances; same address; check current year dates with visitor center [Source: [Visit Arizona](https://www.visitarizona.com/places/parks-monuments/casa-grande-ruins-national-monument)]

## Also Nearby

- **McFarland State Historic Park** (1-2 hours, 15 minutes / 10 miles northeast) - Arizona's first county courthouse (1878) now museum with variety of historic buildings preserving area history; 24 Ruggles Street, Florence, AZ 85132 [33.0316, -111.3872]; Hours vary seasonally [Source: [Visit Arizona Nearby](https://www.visitarizona.com/places/parks-monuments/casa-grande-ruins-national-monument)]

- **Picacho Peak State Park** (2-4 hours, 30 minutes / 30 miles northwest) - Five hiking trails (easy to difficult) through Sonoran desert; site of Arizona's only Civil War battle; Interstate 10, Exit 219, Picacho, AZ 85141 [32.6431, -111.5168]; Daily sunrise to 10 PM [Source: [Visit Arizona Nearby](https://www.visitarizona.com/places/parks-monuments/casa-grande-ruins-national-monument)]

- **Rooster Cogburn Ostrich Ranch** (1-2 hours, 20 minutes / 20 miles north) - Interactive ranch featured on TV shows; feed ostriches, lorikeets, and other animals; 17599 Peak Lane, Picacho, AZ 85141 [32.7089, -111.5543]; Daily 9 AM - 5 PM [Source: [Visit Arizona Nearby](https://www.visitarizona.com/places/parks-monuments/casa-grande-ruins-national-monument)]

- **Coolidge Historical Society & Museum** (30-45 minutes, 5 minutes / 2 miles) - Housed in former jail and courthouse with photography exhibits honoring early 1900s pioneers; 161 W. Central Avenue, Coolidge, AZ 85128 [32.9776, -111.5173]; Hours vary [Source: [Trip101](https://trip101.com/article/things-to-do-coolidge-arizona)]

- **San Tan Mountain Regional Park** (2-4 hours, 30 minutes / 25 miles northwest) - Hiking trails with scenic desert views; 6533 W. Phillips Road, Queen Creek, AZ 85142 [33.2389, -111.6881]; Daily 6 AM - 8 PM [Source: [Visit Arizona Nearby](https://www.visitarizona.com/places/parks-monuments/casa-grande-ruins-national-monument)]
