# Casa Grande Ruins NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-casa-grande-ruins-visitor-center"></a>**Casa Grande Ruins Visitor Center** (1100 W. Ruins Drive, Coolidge, AZ 85128 (32.995459, -111.535528); Open 9 AM - 4 PM daily, 7 days a week; Closed on Juneteenth, Independence Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas; 520-723-3172) - Cancellation stamp available at the visitor center during operating hours. Call ahead to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/cagr/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Source: America's National Parks](https://americasnationalparks.org/passport-to-your-national-parks/passport-cancellation-locations/)

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-self-guided-walking-tour-of-the-great-house-and-ruins"></a>**Self-Guided Walking Tour of the Great House and Ruins** (1-2 hours) - Walk the 0.7-mile round-trip trail through the ancient Hohokam farming community to view the four-story Great House structure built around 1350 CE, preserved under a protective shelter, along with compound walls, canal irrigation systems, and other archaeological features; 1100 W. Ruins Drive, Coolidge, AZ 85128 (32.995459, -111.535528); Open 9 AM - 4 PM daily, 7 days a week, closed on Juneteenth, Independence Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas; 520-723-3172 [Source: National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/cagr/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Source: gjhikes](https://www.gjhikes.com/2014/06/casa-grande-ruins-national-monument.html), [Source: AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/arizona/casa-grande)

- <a id="activity-visitor-center-museum-and-video"></a>**Visitor Center Museum and Video** (30-45 minutes) - Explore museum exhibits featuring Hohokam artifacts from the site and watch the park video presenting perspectives from the O'odham, Zuni, and Hopi peoples on the cultural significance of Casa Grande; 1100 W. Ruins Drive, Coolidge, AZ 85128 (32.995459, -111.535528); Open 9 AM - 4 PM daily, 7 days a week, closed on Juneteenth, Independence Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas; 520-723-3172 [Source: National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/cagr/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Source: Western National Parks](https://wnpa.org/explore-parks/casa-grande-ruins)

- <a id="activity-ranger-led-guided-tours"></a>**Ranger-Led Guided Tours** (30-45 minutes) - Join one-hour guided tours led by park rangers or volunteers through the ruins site, available late November through early April as staffing permits; visitors can hear stories interweaving personal experiences and insights of the ancient civilizations; 1100 W. Ruins Drive, Coolidge, AZ 85128 (32.995459, -111.535528); Tours offered weekends in late fall and winter, call ahead for schedule; 520-723-3172 [Source: National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/cagr/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [Source: Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/casa-grande-ruins-national-monument/), [Source: Wikivoyage](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Casa_Grande_Ruins_National_Monument)

- <a id="activity-junior-ranger-program"></a>**Junior Ranger Program** (30-45 minutes) - Complete an activity sheet and take the ranger pledge to earn a Junior Ranger badge during your visit; 1100 W. Ruins Drive, Coolidge, AZ 85128 (32.995459, -111.535528); Available during visitor center hours 9 AM - 4 PM daily; 520-723-3172 [Source: National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/cagr/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-great-horned-owl-nesting-site"></a>**Great Horned Owl Nesting Site** (15-30 minutes) - Bring binoculars to spot the resident great horned owls that have nested in the protective roof sheltering the Great House since 2006, with notable triplets born in 2012; best viewing opportunities vary by season and time of day; 1100 W. Ruins Drive, Coolidge, AZ 85128 (32.995459, -111.535528); Open 9 AM - 4 PM daily; 520-723-3172 [Source: National Park Service Birds](https://www.nps.gov/cagr/learn/nature/birds.htm), [Source: 2TravelDads](https://2traveldads.com/casa-grande-national-monument-arizona/)

- <a id="gem-native-american-demonstrations"></a>**Native American Demonstrations** (varies) - Visit on weekends during late fall and winter for demonstrations by Native American locals showcasing traditional craft and farming techniques, including basket making and pottery; 1100 W. Ruins Drive, Coolidge, AZ 85128 (32.995459, -111.535528); Weekends late fall through winter, call for specific dates; 520-723-3172 [Source: National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/cagr/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Source: National Park Service Demonstrations](https://www.nps.gov/cagr/learn/news/basket-and-pottery-demo.htm)

- <a id="gem-speaker-series-and-cultural-events"></a>**Speaker Series and Cultural Events** (varies) - Attend special evening events during summer featuring speakers discussing their archaeological and cultural work in the area, plus storytelling sessions and Native American Heritage Month celebrations; 1100 W. Ruins Drive, Coolidge, AZ 85128 (32.995459, -111.535528); Seasonal programming, check website or call for schedule; 520-723-3172 [Source: TouristSecrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/arizona/12-unforgettable-experiences-in-casa-grande-arizona/), [Source: Western National Parks](https://wnpa.org/american-west-monthly-casa-grande-ruins-national-monument/)

- <a id="gem-birdwatching-and-desert-wildlife-viewing"></a>**Birdwatching and Desert Wildlife Viewing** (30-60 minutes) - Explore vegetation around the visitor center and picnic areas to spot Gambel's Quail, Gilded Flicker, Verdin, American Kestrel, round-tailed ground squirrels, and western burrowing owls among the park's cacti and desert flora; 1100 W. Ruins Drive, Coolidge, AZ 85128 (32.995459, -111.535528); Open 9 AM - 4 PM daily; 520-723-3172 [Source: National Park Service Animals](https://www.nps.gov/cagr/learn/nature/animals.htm), [Source: National Park Service Birds](https://www.nps.gov/cagr/learn/nature/birds.htm)

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-picacho-peak-state-park"></a>**Picacho Peak State Park** (3-4 hours for hiking) - Hike the challenging Hunter Trail with cable-assisted sections to reach the 3,374-foot summit, a 2-mile climb taking approximately 3 hours round-trip for most hikers, or enjoy easier trails like the Nature Trail and Children's Cave Trail; park also features wildflower displays mid-February to early April and visitor center with exhibits; Exit 219 off Interstate 10, Picacho, AZ (P.O. Box 907, Eloy, AZ 85131) (32.645952, -111.401153); Park open 5 AM - 8 PM daily year-round, visitor center typically 8 AM - 5 PM; 520-466-3183 [Source: Arizona State Parks](https://azstateparks.com/picacho), [Source: AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/arizona/picacho-peak-via-hunter-trail), [Source: Visit Arizona](https://www.visitarizona.com/places/parks-monuments/picacho-peak-state-park), [Source: TucsonTopia](https://tucsontopia.com/picacho-peak-state-park/)

- <a id="nearby-rooster-cogburn-ostrich-ranch"></a>**Rooster Cogburn Ostrich Ranch** (1-2 hours) - Hand-feed ostriches, fallow deer, miniature donkeys, parakeets, lorikeets, stingrays, goats, bunnies, ducks, and sheep at this family-owned interactive ranch; optional 45-minute Monster Truck Tour on weekends covers ostrich raising, Sonoran Desert ecology, and desert 4-wheeling; 17599 E Peak Lane, Picacho, AZ 85141 (Exit 219 off I-10) (32.6347304, -111.3773963); Open Monday-Sunday 9 AM - 4:30 PM (October-April), Friday-Monday 8 AM - 2 PM (May-September); check website for current hours [Source: Rooster Cogburn](https://www.roostercogburn.com/), [Source: TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g31311-d597047-Reviews-Rooster_Cogburn_Ostrich_Ranch-Picacho_Arizona.html), [Source: TucsonTopia](https://tucsontopia.com/rooster-cogburn-ostrich-ranch/)

- <a id="nearby-mcfarland-state-historic-park"></a>**McFarland State Historic Park** (30-60 minutes) - Tour the museum in one of Arizona's first territorial courthouses with exhibits on Florence's local history, POW camps, and Ernest McFarland; includes guided walking tours of Florence's historic district by appointment, Junior Ranger program, and farmers market third Saturday monthly; 24 W Ruggles Street, Florence, AZ 85132 (33.0315, -111.3873); Open Monday-Saturday 9 AM - 5 PM (October-May), 9 AM - 2 PM (June-September), closed Sundays; free admission [Source: Arizona State Parks](https://azstateparks.com/mcfarland), [Source: Visit Arizona](https://www.visitarizona.com/places/parks-monuments/mcfarland-state-historic-park), [Source: Florence Town](https://www.florenceaz.gov/area-attractions/)

- <a id="nearby-pinal-geology-and-mineral-museum"></a>**Pinal Geology and Mineral Museum** (30-60 minutes) - Explore exhibits on Arizona geology, paleontology, and minerals with hands-on kids' activities including fossil dig and mineral dig, plus outdoor rock pit where guests can search for stones to take home; 351 N. Arizona Boulevard, Coolidge, AZ 85128 (32.977839, -111.517624); Open Wednesday-Saturday 10 AM - 4 PM (until May 31), Fridays only in June-August; free admission; 520-723-3009 [Source: Pinal Geology Museum](https://pinalgeologymuseum.org/), [Source: TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60762-d21180107-Reviews-Pinal_Geology_and_Mineral_Museum-Coolidge_Arizona.html), [Source: Visit Arizona](https://www.visitarizona.com/directory/pinal-geology-and-mineral-museum)

- <a id="nearby-casa-grande-neon-sign-park"></a>**Casa Grande Neon Sign Park** (15-20 minutes) - View 14 beautifully restored vintage neon signs from the 1940s-1950s era at this outdoor museum in downtown Casa Grande; signs illuminated nightly from 7-11 PM for best viewing and photography; 408 N Sacaton Street, Casa Grande, AZ 85122 (approximately 32.8795, -111.7574); Open 24 hours daily, signs lit 7-11 PM; free admission [Source: Neon Sign Park](https://www.neonsignpark.com/), [Source: TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g31182-d17848995-Reviews-Casa_Grande_Neon_Sign_Park-Casa_Grande_Arizona.html), [Source: Atlas Obscura](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/casa-grande-neon-sign-park)
