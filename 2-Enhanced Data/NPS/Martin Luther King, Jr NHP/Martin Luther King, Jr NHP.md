# Martin Luther King, Jr NHP (National Historical Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-visitor-center"></a>**Martin Luther King, Jr. National Historical Park Visitor Center** (450 Auburn Ave NE, Atlanta, GA 30312 (33.756382, -84.373466); Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, closed Sundays and major holidays; (404) 331-1401) [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/malu/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/malu/planyourvisit/visitor_center.htm)]

- <a id="stamp-freedom-hall"></a>**Freedom Hall at The King Center** (449 Auburn Ave NE, Atlanta, GA 30312 (33.7551, -84.3730); Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day; The King Center general line) [Source: [The King Center Visit](https://thekingcenter.org/visit/), [Discover Atlanta](https://discoveratlanta.com/things-to-do/history/mlk-jr-national-historic-site/)]

**Note:** Call (404) 331-1401 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. The Park Visitor Center is currently undergoing renovations. [Source: [NPS Current Conditions](https://www.nps.gov/malu/planyourvisit/conditions.htm)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-birth-home"></a>**Birth Home Tour** (30 minutes) - Ranger-led tours of Dr. Martin Luther King Jr.'s childhood home at 501 Auburn Ave NE, Atlanta, GA 30312 (33.7560, -84.3710); currently closed for renovations through November 2025 with NPS providing presentations about the home in the interim; when open, tours are first-come, first-served (limited to 10 people per tour), with the first tour at 10:00 AM and last tour at 5:00 PM daily (summer hours Memorial Day-Labor Day: 9:30 AM-5:30 PM with tours every half hour; regular season tours hourly); register at the Information Desk in the Visitor Center; tours fill quickly on weekends and holidays. [Source: [NPS Birth Home Tours](https://www.nps.gov/malu/planyourvisit/birth_home_tours.htm), [NPS Birth Home](https://www.nps.gov/malu/planyourvisit/birth-home.htm), [Discover Atlanta MLK Site](https://discoveratlanta.com/things-to-do/history/mlk-jr-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="activity-ebenezer-church"></a>**Historic Ebenezer Baptist Church** (30-60 minutes) - Self-guided tours of the historic sanctuary where Dr. King, his father, and grandfather preached at 407 Auburn Ave NE, Atlanta, GA 30312 (33.7552, -84.3741); Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM-5:00 PM; closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; Park Ranger on duty to answer questions; visitors can watch a 20-minute video interview in Fellowship Hall (basement auditorium) and explore the sanctuary; note that the Horizon Sanctuary (newer church building) is not open for tours. [Source: [NPS Ebenezer Baptist Church](https://www.nps.gov/malu/planyourvisit/ebenezer_baptist_church.htm), [Discover Atlanta Ebenezer](https://discoveratlanta.com/things-to-do/history/ebenezer-baptist-church/), [US Civil Rights Trail](https://civilrightstrail.com/attraction/ebenezer-baptist-church/)]

- <a id="activity-freedom-hall"></a>**Freedom Hall Exhibits** (60 minutes) - Exhibits about Dr. and Mrs. King, Mahatma Gandhi, and Rosa Parks at The King Center, 449 Auburn Ave NE, Atlanta, GA 30312 (33.7551, -84.3730); Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day; free admission; explore exhibits about Dr. King's life, legacy, and the Civil Rights Movement. [Source: [The King Center Visit](https://thekingcenter.org/visit/), [Discover Atlanta MLK Site](https://discoveratlanta.com/things-to-do/history/mlk-jr-national-historic-site/), [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/malu/planyourvisit/visitor_center.htm)]

- <a id="activity-king-crypt"></a>**Dr. and Mrs. King's Crypt and Eternal Flame** (15-30 minutes) - Outdoor memorial featuring the crypts of Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. and Coretta Scott King, Eternal Flame, Freedom Walkway, and Reflecting Pool at The King Center, 449 Auburn Ave NE, Atlanta, GA 30312 (33.7551, -84.3730); outdoor campus accessible during The King Center hours: daily 10:00 AM-5:00 PM; closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day; free admission; self-guided viewing; the Eternal Flame symbolizes the continuing effort to realize Dr. King's dream of the "Beloved Community". [Source: [The King Center Visit](https://thekingcenter.org/visit/), [NPS The King Center](https://home.nps.gov/malu/planyourvisit/the_king_center.htm), [NP Plan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/georgia/martin-luther-king-jr-national-historical-park-park-at-a-glance/martin-luther-king-jr-national-historical-park-tomb-of-martin-luther-and-coretta-scott-king/)]

- <a id="activity-fire-station"></a>**Historic Fire Station No. 6** (20-30 minutes) - Self-guided visits to the 1894 firehouse museum featuring a 1927 American LaFrance Metropolitan Pumper Hose Car, watch desk, telegraph machine, original fire poles, and exhibits on desegregation of the Atlanta Fire Department at 39 Boulevard NE, Atlanta, GA 30312 (33.7554, -84.3720); open 9:00 AM-5:00 PM when volunteers are available, closed Sundays and major holidays; free admission; staffed by park volunteers so availability may vary. [Source: [NPS Fire Station No. 6](https://www.nps.gov/malu/planyourvisit/firestation_no_6.htm), [NPS Fire Station Brief History](https://www.nps.gov/malu/learn/historyculture/fsn6-brief-history.htm), [Discover Atlanta MLK Site](https://discoveratlanta.com/things-to-do/history/mlk-jr-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="activity-rose-garden"></a>**International World Peace Rose Garden** (15-20 minutes) - One of five major World Peace Rose Gardens established worldwide, featuring 185 roses in various colors and fragrances arranged in a starburst design symbolizing Dr. King's life and work, surrounded by plaques with poetry written by children from around the world; located at 450 Auburn Ave NE, Atlanta, GA 30312 (33.756382, -84.373466) bordering the Peace Plaza in front of the Visitor Center; open daily sunrise to sunset; free admission; (404) 331-1401. [Source: [NPS World Peace Rose Garden](https://www.nps.gov/malu/planyourvisit/mlk_jr_world_peace_rose_garden.htm), [World Peace Rose Gardens](https://worldpeacerosegardens.org/the-martin-luther-king-jr-national-historic-site/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60898-d25185883-Reviews-Martin_Luther_King_Jr_i_Have_A_Dream_World_Peace_Rose_Garden-Atlanta_Georgia.html)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="hidden-civil-rights-walk"></a>**International Civil Rights Walk of Fame** (15-20 minutes) - Commemorative walkway honoring courageous pioneers who worked for social justice, where visitors can step into the footprints of activists like Congressman John Lewis; located along Auburn Avenue at the Martin Luther King Jr. National Historical Park; accessible during park hours; free admission; self-guided viewing. [Source: [Explore Georgia Top Spots](https://exploregeorgia.org/things-to-do/article/6-cant-miss-spots-at-martin-luther-king-jr-historical-park), [Discover Atlanta Auburn Avenue](https://discoveratlanta.com/explore/itineraries/auburn-avenue-black-history/)]

- <a id="hidden-junior-ranger"></a>**Junior Ranger Program** (30-45 minutes) - Free Junior Ranger Activity booklet available at the Ranger Station that guides children through the museum with fun activities to help them understand the struggle for civil rights; participants who complete the booklet earn a Junior Ranger badge; available during visitor center hours at 450 Auburn Ave NE; free program. [Source: [Explore Georgia MLK Park](https://exploregeorgia.org/things-to-do/article/martin-luther-king-jr-national-historical-park)]

- <a id="hidden-ranger-talks"></a>**Ranger-Led Church History Talks** (20-30 minutes) - Park Rangers give talks on the history of Ebenezer Baptist Church at 2:00 PM and 4:00 PM at the original historic church building at 407 Auburn Ave NE; free program included with park admission; check with visitor center for current schedule. [Source: [Explore Georgia MLK Park](https://exploregeorgia.org/things-to-do/article/martin-luther-king-jr-national-historical-park)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-center-civil-rights"></a>**National Center for Civil and Human Rights** (2 hours, 1.3 miles/5 minutes drive) - Engaging museum connecting the American Civil Rights Movement to today's global human rights movements with vivid and informative displays at 100 Ivan Allen Jr Blvd NW, Atlanta, GA 30313 (33.7640, -84.3931); Tuesday-Friday and Sunday 12:00 PM-5:00 PM, Saturday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM, closed Mondays; admission $19.99 adults, $17.99 seniors/military, $15.99 ages 7+, free for children under 7; last admission 4:00 PM; purchase tickets online to save time. [Source: [Discover Atlanta Center](https://discoveratlanta.com/things-to-do/history/center-for-civil-and-human-rights/), [US News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/Atlanta_GA/Things_To_Do/Center_for_Civil_and_Human_Rights_64000/), [CityPASS](https://www.citypass.com/atlanta/center-for-civil-and-human-rights)]

- <a id="nearby-carter-library"></a>**Jimmy Carter Presidential Library and Museum** (2 hours, 1.2 miles/30-minute walk via Freedom Park Trail) - Presidential library featuring exhibits on President Carter's life and presidency, including replica Oval Office, Nobel Peace Prize, and interactive displays at 441 John Lewis Freedom Pkwy NE, Atlanta, GA 30307; Monday-Saturday 9:30 AM-4:30 PM, closed Sundays and major holidays; admission $12 adults, $10 seniors/military/students with ID, free for ages under 16; last admission 4:15 PM; tickets available online or on-site; connected to MLK site via scenic 1.5-mile Freedom Park Trail; (404) 865-7100. [Source: [Jimmy Carter Library Visit](https://www.jimmycarterlibrary.gov/visit-the-museum), [Discover Atlanta Carter Library](https://discoveratlanta.com/things-to-do/history/jimmy-carter-presidential-library-museum/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60898-d126907-Reviews-Jimmy_Carter_Presidential_Library_Museum-Atlanta_Georgia.html)]

- <a id="nearby-sweet-auburn-market"></a>**Sweet Auburn Curb Market** (30-45 minutes, 9 minutes walk) - Historic 1924 municipal market featuring 30 local businesses including fresh produce, butchers, seafood, full-service bakery, cooking school, and 10 popular eateries at 209 Edgewood Ave SE, Atlanta, GA 30303 (33.7541, -84.3800); Monday-Saturday 8:00 AM-8:00 PM, Sunday closed; free to explore (food/goods prices vary); on the Streetcar route with stop directly across from entrance; many eateries close before dinner so plan for lunch. [Source: [Municipal Market Atlanta](https://municipalmarketatl.com/), [Discover Atlanta Sweet Auburn District](https://discoveratlanta.com/things-to-do/history/visiting-sweet-auburn/), [Explore Georgia Sweet Auburn](https://exploregeorgia.org/things-to-do/article/how-to-spend-a-day-in-atlantas-sweet-auburn-historic-district)]

- <a id="nearby-apex-museum"></a>**APEX Museum** (45-60 minutes, 10 minutes walk) - African American Panoramic Experience Museum devoted to African American history and culture in Georgia with rotating quarterly exhibits at 135 Auburn Ave NE, Atlanta, GA 30303; Tuesday-Saturday 11:00 AM-3:00 PM, closed Sunday-Monday; admission $12; (404) 523-2739; can be easily combined with MLK National Historical Park visit. [Source: [APEX Museum Visit](https://www.apexmuseum.org), [US Civil Rights Trail APEX](https://civilrightstrail.com/attraction/apex-museum/), [Discover Atlanta APEX](https://discoveratlanta.com/things-to-do/history/atlanta-apex-museum/)]

- <a id="nearby-auburn-library"></a>**Auburn Avenue Research Library on African American Culture and History** (30-60 minutes, 7 minutes walk) - First public library in the Southeast offering specialized reference and archival collections dedicated to African American culture and history at 101 Auburn Ave NE, Atlanta, GA 30303; Monday-Tuesday 10:00 AM-8:00 PM, Wednesday-Saturday 10:00 AM-6:00 PM, closed Sundays; free admission (parking is fee-based); Archives Division accessible by appointment (email aarl.archives@fultoncountyga.gov 24 hours in advance); (404) 613-4001. [Source: [Fulton County Library AARL](https://www.fulcolibrary.org/auburn-avenue-research-library/), [Discover Atlanta Auburn Library](https://discoveratlanta.com/things-to-do/history/auburn-avenue-research-library/), [Georgia Encyclopedia AARL](https://www.georgiaencyclopedia.org/articles/education/auburn-avenue-research-library-on-african-american-culture-and-history/)]

- <a id="nearby-georgia-aquarium"></a>**Georgia Aquarium** (3-4 hours, 1.2 miles/10 minutes drive) - World's largest aquarium featuring all available galleries, dolphin presentations, and sea lion presentations at 225 Baker St NW, Atlanta, GA 30313 (33.7634, -84.3951); open daily 365 days/year starting at 9:00 AM with closing times varying by season; general admission tickets available (purchase online for savings); Plan Ahead & Save tickets require selecting specific day/time; combo packages available with World of Coca-Cola (save 20%+); weekdays less crowded, especially January-April. [Source: [Georgia Aquarium Visitor Info](https://www.georgiaaquarium.org/visitor-information/), [Georgia Aquarium Tickets](https://www.georgiaaquarium.org/tickets/), [Access Atlanta Aquarium Guide](https://www.accessatlanta.com/atlanta-things-to-do/your-insiders-guide-to-the-georgia-aquarium/PHMQHU7CJ5DGZHGTSCCIZ7GL5I/)]

- <a id="nearby-world-coca-cola"></a>**World of Coca-Cola** (2-3 hours, 1.2 miles/10 minutes drive) - Interactive museum with 11 sections exploring Coca-Cola history, including tasting room with 100+ international beverages at 121 Baker St NW, Atlanta, GA 30313 (33.7626, -84.3931); Monday-Thursday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM, Friday-Sunday 10:00 AM-6:00 PM; closed Thanksgiving and Christmas; admission varies (purchase online in advance recommended); mornings least crowded; combo packages available with Georgia Aquarium; located in Centennial Park District next to Georgia Aquarium and Center for Civil and Human Rights. [Source: [World of Coca-Cola Plan Visit](https://www.worldofcoca-cola.com/plan-your-visit), [Discover Atlanta Coca-Cola](https://discoveratlanta.com/things-to-do/attractions/world-of-coca-cola/), [Wonderful Museums Coke Hours](https://www.wonderfulmuseums.com/museum/coke-museum-atlanta-hours/)]

**Total Recommended Time:** 2.5-3 hours for a thorough visit to the Martin Luther King Jr. National Historical Park, including time to explore the visitor center, birth home tour (when available), Ebenezer Baptist Church, Freedom Hall, the King Center with crypt and eternal flame, and Fire Station No. 6. Multiple visitor reviews on TripAdvisor recommend 2-3 hours to visit the site comprehensively, with some noting that everything is within a 1-2 block radius. The NPS suggests visitors can experience the park in 1-2 hours for a basic visit. [Source: [TripAdvisor MLK Site FAQ](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g60898-d103496-t3817475-How_long_should_I_allow_for_a_thorough_visit.html), [US News Travel MLK](https://travel.usnews.com/Atlanta_GA/Things_To_Do/Martin_Luther_King_Jr_National_Historic_Site_16131/), [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/malu/planyourvisit/index.htm)]
