# Cape Cod NS (National Seashore)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Salt Pond Visitor Center** (50 Nauset Road, Eastham, MA 02642 (41.8356, -69.9686); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily May-October, 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM rest of year, closed Thanksgiving and Christmas; 508-255-3421) [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/caco/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/caco/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- **Province Lands Visitor Center** (171 Race Point Road, Provincetown, MA (42.0627, -70.2039); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily May-October, observation deck open year-round; 508-487-1256) [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/caco/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

**Note:** Passport stamps available at both visitor centers. Email: caco_information@nps.gov. [Source: [Yelp Salt Pond](https://www.yelp.com/biz/salt-pond-visitor-center-eastham)]

## Key Activities

- **Swim at Six Beaches** (2-4 hours) - Enjoy forty miles of pristine sandy beaches including Race Point Beach, Herring Cove Beach (bay side, calmer waves, handicapped accessible with beach wheelchair rentals), Coast Guard Beach, Nauset Light Beach, Marconi Beach, and Head of the Meadow Beach; all have showers, restrooms, changing rooms, and summer lifeguards; parking lots open 6:00 AM - midnight; $25 per vehicle May-September [Source: [New England](https://newengland.com/travel/massachusetts/cape-cod-national-seashore-beaches/), [US News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/Cape_Cod_MA/Things_To_Do/Cape_Cod_National_Seashore_62044/)]

- **Bike Province Lands Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - Ride paved 5.5-mile scenic loop through open dunes, forest, and beach with connections to Herring Cove and Race Point beaches, plus Bennett Pond; multiple access points at Race Point Beach, Herring Cove Beach, Province Lands Visitor Center, and Beech Forest Parking Lot [Source: [Coast2Coast With Kids](https://coast2coastwithkids.com/10-ways-to-explore-the-cape-cod-national-seashore/), [New England](https://newengland.com/travel/massachusetts/cape-cod-national-seashore-beaches/)]

- **Hike Great Island Trail** (3-4 hours) - Trek one of eleven hiking trails through marshes, ponds, and uplands; Great Island Trail is most popular along with Buttonbush Trail (short, multi-sensory, kid-friendly) [Source: [New England](https://newengland.com/travel/massachusetts/cape-cod-national-seashore-beaches/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/massachusetts/cape-cod-national-seashore)]

- **Join Ranger Programs** (2-4 hours) - Participate in saltwater fishing basics, tidal flat exploration at Coast Guard Beach, canoeing lessons, beach yoga, or cardiovascular-workout hikes with rangers [Source: [US News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/Cape_Cod_MA/Things_To_Do/Cape_Cod_National_Seashore_62044/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Explore Salt Pond** (30-45 minutes) - Walk around peaceful salt pond near visitor center offering serene water views and easy access trails [Source: [Cape Cod Museum Trail](https://www.capecodmuseumtrail.com/museumdirectory/salt-pond-visitor-center-museum)]

- **Visit Off-Season** (varies) - Experience quieter beaches and trails during winter months; open year-round with most parking lots accessible 6:00 AM - midnight [Source: [New England](https://newengland.com/travel/massachusetts/cape-cod-national-seashore-beaches/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Highland Light (Cape Cod Light)** (30 minutes, Truro) - Visit historic lighthouse with NPS passport stamp available; open seasonally [Source: [Highland Lighthouse](https://www.highlandlighthouse.org/passport-to-your-national-parks)]

- **Provincetown** (30 minutes) - Explore vibrant arts community, shops, galleries, restaurants, and whale watching tours; adjacent to Province Lands [Source: [Cape Cod Chamber](https://www.capecodchamber.org/articles/stories/post/your-guide-to-the-cape-cod-national-seashore/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** Half day to full day; quick visit 3-4 hours for beach and one trail, full day 6-8 hours for multiple beaches and bike trail. [Source: [Your Travel Cap](https://yourtravelcap.com/things-to-do-at-cape-cod-national-seashore/)]
