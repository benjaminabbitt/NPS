# Aniakchak NM & PRES (National Monument & Preserve)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **King Salmon Visitor Center** (1 King Salmon Airport Rd, King Salmon, AK 99613; Monday, Wednesday, Friday 10:00 AM–4:00 PM; 907-246-3305)

**Note:** The passport cancellation stamp for Aniakchak is located in King Salmon, as the park itself is only accessible by aircraft or boat. Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [America's National Parks](https://americasnationalparks.org/passport-to-your-national-parks/passport-cancellation-locations/), [Alaska.org](https://www.alaska.org/advice/tips-for-national-park-passport-stampers-visiting-alaska), [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/ania/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Aniakchak River Rafting** (3-4 days) - Float 35 miles from Surprise Lake through the caldera breach to Aniakchak Bay on the Pacific Ocean, including challenging Class IV rapids through "The Gates" with a gradient of 75 feet per mile; accessed via 90-minute flight from King Salmon or 30-minute flight from Port Heiden; July is the most popular month. [Source: [NPS Rafting](https://www.nps.gov/ania/planyourvisit/rafting.htm), [Alaska.org Aniakchak River](https://www.alaska.org/detail/aniakchak-river), [NPS Raft the Aniakchak](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/raft-the-aniakchak-river.htm)]

- **Caldera Floor Hiking & Backpacking** (3-12 days) - Explore the 6-mile-wide, 2,000-foot-deep caldera with excellent hiking conditions on ash and cinder fields, no maintained trails or facilities; guided trips typically 12 days total or 3 full days exploring the caldera; daily distances of 7-12 miles with elevation gains of 1,000-3,000 feet; accessed via floatplane from King Salmon. [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/ania/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [Alaska Alpine Adventures](https://alaskaalpineadventures.com/tour/backpacking-the-ring-of-fire-aniakchak-national-monument/), [Travel Alaska](https://www.travelalaska.com/destinations/parks-public-lands/aniakchak-national-monument-preserve)]

- **King Salmon Visitor Center** (1-2 hours) - Learn about Aniakchak and other federal public lands of Southwest Alaska through exhibits, films, and information; Alaska Geographic bookstore on-site; 1 King Salmon Airport Rd, King Salmon, AK 99613; Monday, Wednesday, Friday 10:00 AM–4:00 PM; 907-246-3305. [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/ania/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/king-salmon-visitor-center-king-salmon)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Surprise Lake Thermal Springs** (1-2 hours exploration) - Visit 15 warm springs ranging from 64-72°F and pumpkin-colored hot springs emerging from the volcano floor, with warm ground reaching 185°F at shallow depths near Half Cone; turquoise-colored Surprise Lake heated by thermal activity; located inside the caldera, accessible via floatplane landing. [Source: [Wikipedia Aniakchak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aniakchak_National_Monument_and_Preserve), [NPS Geodiversity Atlas](https://www.nps.gov/articles/nps-geodiversity-atlas-aniakchak-national-monument-preserve-alaska.htm), [Public Lands Blog](https://www.publiclands.com/blog/a/exploring-the-aniakchak-national-monument-and-preserve)]

- **Vent Mountain Ascent** (3-5 hours) - Climb the 2,200-foot cinder cone rising from the caldera floor for panoramic views of the volcanic landscape and crater; part of multi-day backpacking expeditions exploring the 1930 eruption site and rugged rim. [Source: [Alaska Alpine Adventures](https://alaskaalpineadventures.com/tour/backpacking-the-ring-of-fire-aniakchak-national-monument/), [Travel Alaska](https://www.travelalaska.com/destinations/parks-public-lands/aniakchak-national-monument-preserve)]

## Also Nearby

- **Katmai National Park - Brooks Camp Bear Viewing** (4-7 hours on-ground, full day trip; 30 air miles from King Salmon) - Watch dozens of brown bears fishing for sockeye salmon at Brooks Falls and Brooks River from four wildlife viewing platforms; peak seasons July (salmon migration) and September (spawned salmon); accessible only by floatplane from King Salmon. [Source: [NPS Brooks Camp](https://www.nps.gov/katm/planyourvisit/brooks-camp.htm), [Fly Katmai](https://www.flykatmai.com/katmai-tour), [Alaska Tours](https://alaskatours.com/day-tours/katmai-brooks-falls-bear-day-tour/)]

- **Katmai National Park - Valley of 10,000 Smokes Tour** (7 hours; 30 air miles from King Salmon) - Take a 23-mile guided bus tour departing 9:00 AM from Brooks Camp to explore the moon-like volcanic landscape created by the 1912 Novarupta eruption; single daily departure; booking required well in advance. [Source: [Alaska.org Valley](https://www.alaska.org/detail/the-valley-of-ten-thousand-smokes), [Katmai B&B](https://katmaibnb.com/valley-of-10000-smokes), [NPS Katmai](https://www.nps.gov/katm/planyourvisit/brooks-camp.htm)]

- **Naknek River King Salmon Fishing** (half to full day; King Salmon, AK) - Fish for king salmon averaging 15-30 pounds with some reaching 50 pounds on the Naknek River; prime season mid-June through late July, with second half of July being optimal; world-class rainbow trout fishing also available year-round. [Source: [Crystal Creek Lodge](https://crystalcreeklodge.com/fishing/king-salmon-fishing/), [Alaska Department of Fish & Game](https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/static-sf/Region2/pdfpubs/NaknekRiver.pdf), [Norrik Salmon Run Dates](https://norrik.com/alaska-salmon-run-dates-and-charts-by-species/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 3-12 days for Aniakchak activities (3-4 days minimum for river rafting, 3-12 days for backpacking/hiking expeditions), plus additional weather buffer days; note that fewer than 200 visitors per year reach this extremely remote location due to unpredictable weather and access challenges. [Source: [NPS Rafting](https://www.nps.gov/ania/planyourvisit/rafting.htm), [Alaska Alpine Adventures](https://alaskaalpineadventures.com/tour/backpacking-the-ring-of-fire-aniakchak-national-monument/), [Travel Alaska](https://www.travelalaska.com/destinations/parks-public-lands/aniakchak-national-monument-preserve)]
