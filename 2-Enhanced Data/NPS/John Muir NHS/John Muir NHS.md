# John Muir NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-john-muir-national-historic-site-visitor-center"></a>**John Muir National Historic Site Visitor Center** - Passport cancellation stamps available at the visitor center front entrance
  **Address:** 4202 Alhambra Ave, Martinez, CA 94553
  **Geocoordinates:** (37.9912921, -122.1313223)
  **Hours:** Daily 10:00 AM-5:00 PM except Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day
  **Phone:** 925-228-8860
  [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/jomu/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](http://nostalgia.esmartkid.com/capassport.html)]

**Note:** Call 925-228-8860 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-john-muir-house-tour"></a>**John Muir House Tour** (30-45 minutes) - Self-guided or ranger-led tours of John Muir's 14-room Italianate Victorian mansion where he lived from 1890 to 1914; explore the ground floor rooms, climb to the large attic, and ascend narrow stairs to the bell tower for panoramic views (visitors are allowed to ring the bell); first-floor tours offered daily at 2:00 PM with additional tours on weekends at 11:00 AM; free audio tours available; 4202 Alhambra Ave, Martinez, CA 94553; Daily 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/jomu/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60868-d107828-Reviews-John_Muir_National_Historic_Site-Martinez_California.html), [Source](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/john-muir-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="activity-a-glorious-journey-introductory-film"></a>**"A Glorious Journey" Introductory Film** (20 minutes) - Watch an orientation film providing background about John Muir's conservation work, family life, and legacy; shown in the visitor center; 4202 Alhambra Ave, Martinez, CA 94553; Daily 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/jomu/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Source](https://www.yelp.com/biz/john-muir-national-historic-site-martinez)]

- <a id="activity-historic-grounds-tour"></a>**Historic Grounds Tour** (30 minutes) - Ranger-led or self-guided tour of the estate grounds featuring historic fruit orchards from Muir's farming days, native vegetation, and the Martinez Adobe home (note: Martinez Adobe currently closed for structural repairs); rangers provide tour booklets with numbered points of interest along the path; 4202 Alhambra Ave, Martinez, CA 94553; Daily 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/jomu/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60868-d107828-Reviews-John_Muir_National_Historic_Site-Martinez_California.html), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/jomu/planyourvisit/martinez-adobe.htm)]

- <a id="activity-mount-wanda-nature-trail-hike"></a>**Mount Wanda Nature Trail Hike** (1-2 hours) - Moderate 2.7-2.9 mile loop trail with 610-660 feet elevation gain through the 325-acre nature preserve named after Muir's daughter; summit offers inspiring panoramas of Mount Diablo and rolling Contra Costa County hills; dogs allowed on-leash; trailhead at CalTrans Park & Ride lot, corner of Franklin Canyon Road and Alhambra Avenue, Martinez, CA 94553 [[Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/muir-farm-and-mount-wanda-john-muir-nature-trail), [Source](https://jmlt.org/2023/04/11/mount-wanda-hike/), [Source](https://ridechronicles.com/2015/05/15/john-muir-nhs-mt-wanda-nature-trail-hike/)]

- <a id="activity-muir-strentzel-family-gravesite-tour"></a>**Muir/Strentzel Family Gravesite Tour** (30-45 minutes) - Ranger-led tour of the family cemetery where John Muir and his family are buried; tours depart from visitor center every Wednesday, Saturday, and Sunday at 3:00 PM; reservations recommended by calling 925-228-8860; 4202 Alhambra Ave, Martinez, CA 94553 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/strentzel-muir-gravesite-tour.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/jomu/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-historic-fruit-orchards-walk"></a>**Historic Fruit Orchards Walk** (15-30 minutes) - Self-guided exploration of the working fruit orchards from Muir's farming days; provides insight into Muir's lesser-known role as a successful fruit rancher who managed the family's Alhambra Valley vineyard and orchards; trees still produce fruit seasonally; 4202 Alhambra Ave, Martinez, CA 94553; Daily 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [[Source](https://vault.sierraclub.org/john_muir_exhibit/bio/nhs.aspx), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/jomu/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- <a id="gem-bell-tower-experience"></a>**Bell Tower Experience** (included in house tour) - Climb the narrow stairs to the bell tower atop the Muir house for excellent photo opportunities and panoramic views; visitors are allowed to ring the historic bell; often overlooked by those who don't complete the full house tour; 4202 Alhambra Ave, Martinez, CA 94553; Daily 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60868-d107828-Reviews-John_Muir_National_Historic_Site-Martinez_California.html)]

- <a id="gem-mural-tunnel"></a>**Mural Tunnel** (varies, ranger-dependent) - Some park rangers occasionally take visitors through a tunnel featuring murals depicting John Muir's life; not widely advertised and availability varies by ranger and day; ask rangers during your visit about special access opportunities; 4202 Alhambra Ave, Martinez, CA 94553 [[Source](https://nationalparkswitht.com/2018/02/06/john-muir-nhs/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-port-chicago-naval-magazine-national-memorial"></a>**Port Chicago Naval Magazine National Memorial** (1.5 hours, 8 miles) - National memorial commemorating the 1944 Port Chicago disaster, the largest domestic loss of life during WWII (320 killed); tours offered Thursday-Saturday at 12:30 PM by advance reservation only (minimum 2 weeks); government photo ID required for military base access; visitors meet at John Muir NHS and are shuttled by NPS vehicle to Military Ocean Terminal Concord; 4202 Alhambra Ave, Martinez, CA 94553 (meeting point); 925-228-8860 x6520 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/poch/planyourvisit/index.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/poch/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/poch/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

- <a id="nearby-eugene-o-neill-national-historic-site"></a>**Eugene O'Neill National Historic Site** (2 hours, 12 miles) - Tao House, the secluded home where playwright Eugene O'Neill wrote his final and most important plays including "Long Day's Journey Into Night" and "The Iceman Cometh"; accessible only by NPS shuttle from Museum of the San Ramon Valley, 205 Railroad Ave, Danville, CA 94526; guided tours Wednesday-Friday and Sunday at 10:00 AM or 2:00 PM, self-guided Saturdays starting 10:00 AM; advance reservations required; free; 925-228-8860 x6422 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/euon/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://www.danville.ca.gov/464/Eugene-ONeill-National-Historic-Site), [Source](https://wnpa.org/plan-your-trip/trip-ideas/park-stories/how-to-visit-eugene-oneill-national-historic-site-in-danville-california-1)]

- <a id="nearby-rosie-the-riveter-wwii-home-front-national-historical-park"></a>**Rosie the Riveter WWII Home Front National Historical Park** (2-3 hours, 9 miles) - Richmond's flagship WWII home front site preserving the legacy of Richmond's Kaiser Shipyards (which produced 747 ships, more than any other shipyard complex in the US); visitor center in historic Ford Building Oil House features exhibits about wartime production and Rosie the Riveter; SS Red Oak Victory ship, Whirley Crane, and Rosie Memorial at Marina Bay Park; 1414 Harbour Way South, Richmond, CA 94804; Daily 10:00 AM-5:00 PM except Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day; free admission; 510-232-5050 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/rori/), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/rori/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://rosietheriveter.org/pages/the-national-park)]

- <a id="nearby-lindsay-wildlife-experience"></a>**Lindsay Wildlife Experience** (2 hours, 10 miles) - Northern California's premier wildlife rehabilitation hospital and natural history museum featuring live native California animals, hands-on exhibits, and daily wildlife encounters; observe rehabilitation work with injured and orphaned animals; programs every hour included with admission; 1931 First Avenue, Walnut Creek, CA 94597; Wednesday-Sunday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM (Friday 10:00 AM-3:00 PM), closed Monday-Tuesday and major holidays; admission $17 adults, $15 children 2-17 [[Source](https://lindsaywildlife.org/hours-prices-directions/), [Source](https://www.yelp.com/biz/lindsay-wildlife-experience-walnut-creek), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g33235-d1175688-Reviews-Lindsay_Wildlife_Museum-Walnut_Creek_California.html)]
