# Home of Franklin D. Roosevelt NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Henry A. Wallace Visitor Center** - The primary stamp location at Springwood, the FDR family home estate; 4097 Albany Post Rd, Hyde Park, NY 12538 (41.7698549, -73.9326457); Visitor Center open daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 845-229-5320 [[NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/hofr/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g60801-d106611-t8229824-How_long_should_you_allow_for_the_tour.html)]

- **FDR Presidential Library and Museum** - Additional stamp location; 4079 Albany Post Rd, Hyde Park, NY 12538 (adjacent to the home); Open daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; separate admission $10; 845-486-7770 [[FDR Library](https://www.fdrlibrary.org/hours), [Hudson River Valley](https://www.hudsonrivervalley.com/explore-more/passport-stamp)]

**Note:** Call ahead at 845-229-5320 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Springwood House Tour (45 minutes - 1 hour)** - Guided tour presenting fifteen rooms across two floors of Franklin D. Roosevelt's lifelong family home where he was born in 1882 and returned throughout his presidency; 4097 Albany Post Rd, Hyde Park, NY 12538 (41.7698549, -73.9326457); Tours offered daily 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM (no 12:00 PM tour), closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; access by guided tour only, $15 per person (children 15 and under free), tickets available at visitor center or online; 5-10 minute walk from visitor center to house [[NPS Tour Info](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/tour-fdr-home.htm), [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/facility/300000), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g60801-d106611-t8229824-How_long_should_you_allow_for_the_tour.html)]

- **FDR Presidential Library and Museum (1-5 hours)** - The nation's first presidential library, established by FDR in 1941, featuring exhibits on FDR's presidency, the Great Depression, World War II, and Eleanor Roosevelt's work; 4079 Albany Post Rd, Hyde Park, NY 12538; Open daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; $10 admission; allow 1-5 hours depending on interest level [[FDR Library Hours](https://www.fdrlibrary.org/hours), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/home-of-franklin-d-roosevelt-national-historic-site/)]

- **Wallace Visitor Center Orientation (30 minutes)** - View the orientation film "Rendezvous with History," explore exhibits about the Roosevelt family, and visit the café; Henry A. Wallace Visitor Center, 4097 Albany Post Rd, Hyde Park, NY 12538; Open daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; free admission [[NP Plan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/new-york-national-parks/home-of-franklin-d-roosevelt-national-historic-site-park-at-a-glance/)]

- **Rose Garden and Gravesites (15-30 minutes)** - Visit the final resting place of Franklin and Eleanor Roosevelt in the estate's peaceful rose garden; grounds accessible from 4097 Albany Post Rd, Hyde Park, NY 12538; Open daily sunrise to sunset; free admission [[Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/home-of-franklin-d-roosevelt-national-historic-site/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60801-d106611-Reviews-Home_of_Franklin_D_Roosevelt_National_Historic_Site-Hyde_Park_New_York.html)]

- **Top Cottage Tour (2 hours including transportation)** - Tour FDR's private retreat built in 1938 at the eastern edge of his estate, where he entertained close friends including Winston Churchill and King George VI; accessible only by shuttle bus from Wallace Visitor Center; Tours depart at 11:10 AM, 1:10 PM, and 3:10 PM (arrive 10 minutes early); $10 per person; reservations recommended through Recreation.gov [[Recreation.gov Top Cottage](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/300000/ticket/321), [Dutchess County](https://www.dutchess.org/historic/fdr_top_cottage)]

- **Estate Grounds and Gardens (1-2 hours)** - Explore over 1,000 acres of gardens, flower beds, outbuildings, and scenic overlooks of the Hudson Valley; trails accessible from 4097 Albany Post Rd, Hyde Park, NY 12538; Open daily sunrise to sunset; free admission [[Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/home-of-franklin-d-roosevelt-national-historic-site/), [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/hofr/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Roosevelt Forest Trail (1-1.5 hours)** - Walk through Roosevelt's working forest on trails ranging from 1.1 to 2.9 miles featuring gravel and paved paths, filtered Hudson River views, and connections to Vanderbilt Mansion and Val-Kill trails; trailhead at FDR NHS main parking area, 4097 Albany Post Rd, Hyde Park, NY 12538; Open dawn to dusk daily; free admission; trails marked with tulip-tree markers and green blazes [[AllTrails FDR Cove](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-york/fdr-cove-and-forest-loop), [Hike the Hudson Valley](https://hikethehudsonvalley.com/hikes/fdr-natl-historic-site/), [Hyde Park Trails](https://www.hydeparkny.us/DocumentCenter/View/1136/Home-of-FDR-Trail-Map-PDF?bidId=)]

- **FDR's Hand-Operated Elevator (included in house tour)** - View the unique elevator FDR designed and operated himself using a large rope to reach the second floor in his wheelchair, showcasing his determination and innovative problem-solving; visible during Springwood house tour; access included with $15 house tour admission [[TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60801-d106611-Reviews-Home_of_Franklin_D_Roosevelt_National_Historic_Site-Hyde_Park_New_York.html)]

- **FDR's Personal Effects and Collections (30-45 minutes)** - Explore lesser-known exhibits in the Presidential Library including FDR's naval collection, ship models he built himself, and personal correspondence; FDR Presidential Library, 4079 Albany Post Rd, Hyde Park, NY 12538; Open daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; $10 admission [[FDR Library](https://www.fdrlibrary.org/hours)]

## Also Nearby

- **Eleanor Roosevelt National Historic Site - Val-Kill (1.5-2 hours, 2 miles east)** - The only National Historic Site dedicated to a First Lady, featuring Eleanor Roosevelt's personal retreat and cottage where she lived after FDR's death, showcasing her independent work and legacy; 54 Valkill Park Rd, Hyde Park, NY 12538 (41.7642, -73.9023); Guided tours Thursday-Monday at 1:00 PM and 3:00 PM, visitor center opens 12:30 PM, closed Tuesday-Wednesday; grounds open sunrise to sunset daily; $10 tour fee; tours often sell out early, reserve online recommended [[NPS Val-Kill Hours](https://www.nps.gov/elro/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/facility/300001), [Val-Kill](https://www.valkill.org/visit)]

- **Vanderbilt Mansion National Historic Site (1-1.5 hours, 3 miles north)** - Gilded Age mansion showcasing opulent 54-room, 45,000-square-foot Beaux-Arts estate of Frederick and Louise Vanderbilt from 1896-1938; 119 Vanderbilt Park Rd, Hyde Park, NY 12538 (41.79784, -73.9418); Visitor Center open daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, mansion tours usually at 10:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 2:00 PM, and 3:30 PM (January-May 2025, times subject to change); $15 per person (children 15 and under free); grounds open sunrise to sunset; 845-229-7770 [[NPS Vanderbilt Hours](https://www.nps.gov/vama/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/facility/300002), [TripExpress](https://www.tripexpress.org/attraction-vanderbilt-mansion-national-historic-site-211672/)]

- **Culinary Institute of America (1-3 hours, 3 miles south)** - World-renowned culinary college featuring student-run fine dining restaurants and campus tours showcasing culinary education; 1946 Campus Dr, Hyde Park, NY 12538 (41.7531, -73.9293); Campus office hours Monday-Friday 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM; Restaurant hours Tuesday-Saturday lunch 11:30 AM - 1:00 PM, dinner 6:00 PM - 8:30 PM; CIA Experience tours Tuesday/Thursday 10:15 AM, Wednesday/Friday/Saturday 2:30 PM and 4:30 PM (approximately 1 hour); restaurant reservations 845-471-6608 [[CIA Foodies](https://www.ciafoodies.com/cia-new-york/), [Bocuse Restaurant](https://www.bocuserestaurant.com/location/bocuse-restaurant/), [CIA Directions](https://www.ciachef.edu/new-york-campus-directions/)]

- **Walkway Over the Hudson State Historic Park (1-2 hours, 6 miles south)** - The world's longest elevated pedestrian bridge at 1.28 miles long and 212 feet above the Hudson River, offering spectacular views and connecting Poughkeepsie and Highland; 61 Parker Ave, Poughkeepsie, NY 12601 (east entrance); Open daily 7:00 AM until sunset (7:30 PM until April 23, 2025); free admission; elevator open 9:00 AM until 90 minutes before close; parking available at Pulaski Park, 170 Washington St, or Brookside Avenue (main lot closed until January 2026); 845-454-9649 [[NYS Parks](https://parks.ny.gov/parks/178/details.aspx), [Walkway](https://walkway.org/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/walkway-over-the-hudson-state-historic-park-poughkeepsie)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 4-5 hours for a complete visit including the Springwood house tour (45 minutes - 1 hour), FDR Presidential Library and Museum (1-2 hours minimum), Wallace Visitor Center orientation film and exhibits (30 minutes), Rose Garden and gravesites (15-30 minutes), and estate grounds exploration (1 hour). Add 2 hours if including Top Cottage tour. Full-day visitors interested in all aspects should plan 6-8 hours [[TripAdvisor FAQ](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g60801-d106611-t8229824-How_long_should_you_allow_for_the_tour.html), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/home-of-franklin-d-roosevelt-national-historic-site/)].
