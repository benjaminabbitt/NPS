## Key Activities

- **La Ventana Natural Arch Walk** (10-30 minutes) - Easy 0.25-mile walk up a graveled path to the base of one of New Mexico's largest natural arches, offering spectacular photo opportunities; La Ventana Arch Trailhead, Highway 117, approximately 17 miles south of I-40; Dawn to dusk [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/elma-la-ventana-arch.htm), [Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/la-ventana-arch), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60799-d2355442-Reviews-La_Ventana_Natural_Arch-Grants_New_Mexico.html)]

- **El Calderon Trail Loop** (1.5-2 hours) - Moderate 3.7-4.8 mile loop showcasing lava tubes, cinder cones, and volcanic features including the publicly accessible Junction Bridge Cave in the first quarter mile; El Calderon Trailhead, Highway 53; Dawn to dusk; **Note: Trail closed through May 2026 for accessibility upgrades** [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/elma-hike-el-calderon.htm), [Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/el-calderon-and-continental-divide-trail-loop), [Source](https://www.cibolacitizen.com/news/el-calderon-trail-closes-major-upgrade-lava-tube-access-limited-through-may-2026)]

- **Sandstone Bluffs Overlook** (5-30 minutes) - Very easy 0.2-mile walk to dramatic viewpoint overlooking the lava flow with amazing views of the valley and mountains; Sandstone Bluffs parking area, Highway 117; Dawn to dusk [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/places/elma-sandstone-bluffs-overlook.htm), [Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/el-malpais-national-monument-sandstone-bluffs-overlook), [Source](https://www.scenicusa.net/080906.html)]

- **Lava Falls Trail** (30-45 minutes) - Easy 1-mile loop exploring some of the youngest lava flows in the region with interpretive signs explaining volcanic formation; Lava Falls Trailhead, Highway 117; Dawn to dusk [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/elma/planyourvisit/lava-falls.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/elma/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)]

- **Narrows Rim Trail** (4-6 hours) - Moderate to strenuous 8-mile round trip climbing to the top of sandstone cliffs that overlook the lava flow, with spectacular views including La Ventana Arch from above; Narrows Trailhead, Highway 117; Dawn to dusk [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/elma-narrows-rim-trail.htm), [Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/la-ventana-arch)]

- **Scenic Drive Along Highway 117** (1-2 hours) - Leisurely drive along the east side of the monument with multiple pullouts, overlooks, and trailhead access to key features; Highway 117 from I-40 Exit 89 south approximately 30 miles; Dawn to dusk [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/elma/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Source](https://www.desertusa.com/elmalpais/)]

- **Zuni-Acoma Trail** (5-6 hours) - Strenuous 7.5-15.8 mile historic trail (depending on route) crossing jagged lava flows along an ancient footpath that connected the Zuni and Acoma pueblos; Trailheads at Highway 53 or Highway 117; Dawn to dusk; Requires sturdy hiking boots [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/elma/planyourvisit/zuni-acoma-trail.htm), [Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/zuni-acoma-trail), [Source](https://www.billandcori.com/newmexico/mp_zuniacoma.htm)]

- **Bat Cave Viewing** (1-2 hours at dusk) - Experience thousands of bats emerging from the cave on warm summer evenings; Bat Cave area, accessible from Big Tubes area road; Best during summer at dusk [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/elma/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Big Tubes Lava Cave Exploration** (2-4 hours) - Remote lava tube system including Giant Ice Cave with permanent ice formations dating back 2,000 years and Lava Bomb Cave; Big Tubes area, 7 miles on unpaved forest road from Highway 53; Dawn to dusk; **Requires free permit from visitor center and ranger safety briefing; caves closed November 1-April 30 annually** [[Source](https://www.americansouthwest.net/new_mexico/el_malpais_lavabeds/big-tubes.html), [Source](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/new-mexico/secrets-of-new-mexicos-el-malpais-ruins/), [Source](https://www.blm.gov/visit/el-malpais-nca)]

- **Continental Divide Trail Section** (variable, 2-8 hours) - Hike a portion of the 3,100-mile Continental Divide Trail that passes through El Malpais, offering solitude and wilderness experience; Multiple access points including El Calderon Trailhead; Dawn to dusk [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/elma/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [Source](https://cdtcoalition.org/hike_suggestion/zuni-acoma-and-chain-of-craters-el-malpais-national-monument/)]

- **Four Windows and Big Skylight Caves** (3-4 hours) - Less-visited lava tubes containing permanent ice formations and natural skylights; Big Tubes area; **Requires permit and proper caving equipment; high clearance vehicle recommended** [[Source](https://www.americansouthwest.net/new_mexico/el_malpais_lavabeds/big-tubes.html)]

- **Backcountry Camping** (overnight) - Primitive camping experiences in the rugged lava landscape and wilderness areas; Various backcountry locations; **Free permit required from visitor center** [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/elma/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Bandera Volcano & Ice Cave** (1.5-2 hours, adjacent to monument) - Private attraction featuring accessible ice cave with year-round ice and trail around Bandera Volcano, one of the best examples of volcanic eruption geology in North America; 12000 Ice Cave Road, Grants, NM 87020 (off Highway 53); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM (seasonal hours vary); $15 adults; 888-423-2283 [[Source](https://www.visitgallup.com/things-to-do/nearby-day-trips/el-malpais-national-monument/), [Source](https://www.newmexico.org/places-to-visit/regions/northwest/grants-attractions/)]

- **Acoma Sky City** (2-3 hours, 30 miles east) - The oldest continuously inhabited community in the United States, perched atop a 367-foot sandstone mesa with guided tours of the historic pueblo; Sky City Cultural Center, I-40 Exit 102, Acoma Pueblo, NM 87034; Tours daily, hours vary seasonally; Tour fees apply; 800-747-0181 [[Source](https://www.newmexico.org/places-to-visit/regions/northwest/grants-attractions/), [Source](https://wnpa.org/discover_parks/el-malpais/)]

- **El Morro National Monument** (2-3 hours, 42 miles southwest) - Historic site featuring ancient petroglyphs and Spanish explorer inscriptions dating to 1609 carved into a massive sandstone bluff, with trails to Inscription Rock and pueblo ruins; El Morro NM, Highway 53, Ramah, NM 87321; Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; Free admission; 505-783-4226 [[Source](https://www.newmexico.org/places-to-visit/regions/northwest/grants-attractions/)]

- **New Mexico Mining Museum** (1-1.5 hours, 15 miles north in Grants) - Only underground uranium mining museum in the world with elevator descent into simulated underground mine; 100 Iron Ave, Grants, NM 87020; Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM-4:00 PM; $4 adults; 505-287-4802 [[Source](https://www.newmexico.org/places-to-visit/regions/northwest/grants-attractions/), [Source](https://koa.com/campgrounds/grants/local-area/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 4-6 hours for a comprehensive visit including the visitor center, La Ventana Arch, Sandstone Bluffs Overlook, Lava Falls Trail, and scenic drive. Full-day visit (8+ hours) recommended if hiking longer trails like Narrows Rim or Zuni-Acoma Trail. Multi-day visit ideal for backcountry exploration and lava tube caving.
