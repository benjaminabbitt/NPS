# Fort Union NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-fort-union-nm-visitor-center"></a>**Fort Union NM Visitor Center** (3115 NM Highway 161, Watrous, New Mexico 87753 (35.903755, -105.010803); Labor Day - Memorial Day: 8:00 AM-4:00 PM, Memorial Day - Labor Day: 8:00 AM-5:00 PM; closed Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day; (505) 425-8025) [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/foun/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/258767)]

**Note:** Admission is free. Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [NPS Fort Union](https://www.nps.gov/foun/index.htm)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-self-guided-trail-walk"></a>**Self-Guided Trail Walk** (45-90 minutes) - Explore the ruins of the second and third forts via a 1.4-1.6 mile self-guided interpretive trail through the remains of the largest 19th-century military fort in the Southwest; visitors can choose between a long route (1.9 miles round trip) that includes walking around the first Fort Union (1851-1861), the star-shaped Civil War fort (1861-1862), and a short excursion to the Santa Fe Trail, or shorter routes closer to a half mile round trip; Fort Union National Monument, 3115 NM Highway 161, Watrous, NM 87753 (35.903755, -105.010803); same hours as visitor center [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/fort-union-national-monument), [gjhikes.com](https://www.gjhikes.com/2019/09/fort-union-national-monument.html), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60818-d604837-Reviews-Fort_Union_National_Monument-Watrous_New_Mexico.html), [Fat Man Little Trail](https://fatmanlittletrail.com/2023/04/19/fort-union-national-monument-overview/)]

- <a id="activity-visitor-center-museum-film"></a>**Visitor Center Museum & Film** (30-45 minutes) - Watch a short orientation film about Fort Union and the Santa Fe Trail, explore exhibits about life at the fort from multiple perspectives, and view historical artifacts from the area in the newly renovated museum; 3115 NM Highway 161, Watrous, NM 87753 (35.903755, -105.010803); same hours as visitor center [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/fort-union-national-monument/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60818-d604837-Reviews-Fort_Union_National_Monument-Watrous_New_Mexico.html), [Fat Man Little Trail](https://fatmanlittletrail.com/2023/04/19/fort-union-national-monument-overview/)]

- <a id="activity-ranger-guided-tours"></a>**Ranger-Guided Tours** (45-60 minutes) - Join daily ranger-led walking tours of the monument at 10:00 AM and 1:00 PM, with Fort Orientation programs offered at 4:00 PM; tours discuss the fort's history, preservation efforts, and life along the Santa Fe Trail; Fort Union National Monument; same hours as visitor center [Source: [Fort Union Drive-In](https://www.visitlasvegasnm.com/things-to-do/fort-union-drive-in/), [Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/foun/planyourvisit/guided-tours-and-talks.htm)]

- <a id="activity-cannon-demonstrations"></a>**Cannon Demonstrations** (20 minutes) - Watch artillery firing demonstrations representing Civil War-era drill and dress, held every Saturday between Memorial Day weekend and Labor Day weekend at 11:00 AM; Fort Union National Monument; Memorial Day - Labor Day season [Source: [ASCHG](https://aschg.org/hike/fort-union-national-monument/), [Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/foun/planyourvisit/guided-tours-and-talks.htm)]

- <a id="activity-santa-fe-trail-ruts-viewing"></a>**Santa Fe Trail Ruts Viewing** (15-30 minutes) - Walk to see the largest visible network of Santa Fe Trail ruts, with wagon ruts from both the Mountain and Cimarron Branches of the old Santa Fe Trail visible from the monument grounds and access road; ruts can be followed for miles; Fort Union National Monument; same hours as visitor center [Source: [Santa Fe Trail](https://www.nps.gov/places/fort-union-national-monument-the-santa-fe-trail.htm), [Legends of America](https://www.legendsofamerica.com/nm-fortunion/)]

- <a id="activity-wildlife-prairie-ecology-observation"></a>**Wildlife & Prairie Ecology Observation** (flexible) - Observe diverse shortgrass prairie wildlife including pronghorns and elk roaming freely, black bears and mountain lions in the shadows, western meadowlarks, cliff swallows, kangaroo rats, Botta's pocket gophers, hawks, eagles, and songbirds while walking the trails; Fort Union National Monument; same hours as visitor center [Source: [WNPA](https://wnpa.org/explore-parks/fort-union), [Lake.com](https://www.lake.com/parks/fort-union-national-monument/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-dark-sky-stargazing-moonlight-tours"></a>**Dark Sky Stargazing & Moonlight Tours** (1-2 hours) - Attend special nighttime programs throughout the year to learn about the night skies over Fort Union and tour the fort by moonlight, including the 45-minute Lantern Lit Legacies tour in September; as an International Dark Sky Park, Fort Union offers exceptional celestial viewing with minimal light pollution, best during new moon and astronomical twilight; telescopes sometimes provided or bring your own with a star chart; Fort Union National Monument; check event calendar for specific dates [Source: [Outdoorsy](https://www.outdoorsy.com/guide/fort-union-national-monument), [Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/foun/planyourvisit/guided-tours-and-talks.htm)]

- <a id="gem-photography-at-the-ruins"></a>**Photography at the Ruins** (flexible) - Capture territorial-style adobe remnants set within a sweeping valley of short grass prairie with snow-capped Sangre de Cristo mountains to the west and wide-open plains; the open skies and vast landscapes create stunning photographic opportunities; Fort Union National Monument; same hours as visitor center [Source: [TouristSecrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/fort-union-national-monument-new-mexicos-santa-fe-trail-fort/), [WNPA](https://wnpa.org/discover_parks/fort-union/)]

- <a id="gem-living-history-demonstrations"></a>**Living History Demonstrations** (varies) - Experience summer weekend living history talks and demonstrations during the annual two-day Fort Union special event that includes speakers, demonstrations, and food; Fort Union National Monument; summer weekends, check calendar for specific events [Source: [Lake.com](https://www.lake.com/parks/fort-union-national-monument/), [Fort Union Days Weekend](https://www.nps.gov/foun/learn/news/fort-union-days-weekend.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-pecos-national-historical-park"></a>**Pecos National Historical Park** (2-3 hours, 1 hour drive west) - Explore ancient pueblo ruins, Spanish colonial mission churches, Civil War battlefield sites, and remnants of the Santa Fe Trail; this multi-layered historic site preserves 12,000 years of history including the massive Pecos Pueblo and Spanish mission ruins; 1 Pecos National Historical Park Dr, Pecos, NM 87552; check NPS website for hours [Source: [Visiting Pecos NHP and Fort Union](https://wanderfilledlife.com/pecos-national-historical-park-fort-union-national-monument/), [gjhikes.com](https://www.gjhikes.com/2019/09/pecos-national-historical-park.html)]

- <a id="nearby-city-of-las-vegas-museum-rough-rider-memorial-collection"></a>**City of Las Vegas Museum & Rough Rider Memorial Collection** (1-2 hours, 28 miles south) - Discover the cultural heritage of Las Vegas, NM through over 7,000 artifacts including exhibits about Theodore Roosevelt's Rough Riders, the Santa Fe Trail, railroad history, and objects dating from as early as the 1300s; housed in the Historic Municipal Building, a 1940 Works Progress Administration project; 727 Grand Ave, Las Vegas, NM 87701 (35.597679, -105.213687); Monday-Friday 9:00 AM-4:00 PM; free admission, $2 suggested donation; (505) 426-3205 [Source: [Visit Las Vegas NM](https://www.visitlasvegasnm.com/things-to-do/rough-rider-museum/), [Things to Do Around Las Vegas](https://santafe.com/las-vegas-nm/), [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/place/35.5976794,-105.2136865)]

- <a id="nearby-montezuma-hot-springs"></a>**Montezuma Hot Springs** (1-2 hours, 35 miles southwest) - Soak in free natural hot springs with three different soaking areas, each offering three man-made, rock, and cement-lined pools ranging in temperature from 95°F to 120°F; set in a scenic location with views of the Montezuma Castle; 400 NM Hwy 65, Montezuma, NM 87731; dawn to dusk (closes 10:00 PM); free, donations accepted; 6 miles/12 minutes from Las Vegas, NM [Source: [Visit Las Vegas NM](https://www.visitlasvegasnm.com/things-to-do/outdoor-adventures/montezuma-hot-springs/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g47123-d8286092-Reviews-Montezuma_Hot_Springs-Montezuma_New_Mexico.html), [Top Hot Springs](https://www.tophotsprings.com/montezuma-hot-springs-new-mexico/)]

- <a id="nearby-cleveland-roller-mill-museum"></a>**Cleveland Roller Mill Museum** (1 hour, ~35 miles northwest) - Tour a historic flour mill dedicated to wheat farming and processing in the 1800s and early 1900s, featuring a mill powered by a water wheel and exhibits on the agricultural heritage of the Mora Valley; NM-518, Cleveland, NM 87715; Memorial Day weekend through October 26, weekends only 10:00 AM-3:00 PM or by appointment; (505) 387-2645 [Source: [Cleveland Roller Mill Museum](https://clevelandrollermillmuseum.org/), [New Mexico Tourism](https://www.newmexico.org/listing/cleveland-roller-mill-museum/5491/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g46986-d534406-Reviews-Cleveland_Roller_Mill_Museum-Cleveland_New_Mexico.html)]

- <a id="nearby-fort-union-drive-in-theatre"></a>**Fort Union Drive-In Theatre** (2-3 hours, 25-30 miles south) - Experience one of only two remaining drive-in theaters still operating in New Mexico at this single screen theater with capacity for over 300 cars; features double features with modern digital projection; 3300 7th St, Las Vegas, NM 87701; Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays, May through September; check schedule for showtimes [Source: [Visit Las Vegas NM](https://www.visitlasvegasnm.com/things-to-do/fort-union-drive-in/), [DriveInMovie.com](https://www.driveinmovie.com/nm/las-vegas/fort-union-drive-in/), [Santa Fe Reporter](https://sfreporter.com/news/drive-in/)]
