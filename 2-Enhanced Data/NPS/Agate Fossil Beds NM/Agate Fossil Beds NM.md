# Agate Fossil Beds NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Visitor Center** (301 River Road, Harrison, NE 69346; Summer hours: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM Mountain Time daily, May 15 - September 30; Winter hours: 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM Mountain Time daily, October 1 - May 14; closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 308-665-4113) [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/agfo/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Contact Information](https://www.nps.gov/agfo/contacts.htm), [Recreation.gov Visitor Center](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/258590)]

**Note:** Call 308-665-4113 to confirm current stamp location and seasonal hours before visiting. The nearest gas station is 34 miles south of the monument in Crawford, Nebraska.

## Key Activities

- **Explore the Visitor Center Museum and Watch the Orientation Film** (30-60 minutes) - View life-size dioramas of 23-million-year-old Miocene Epoch mammal fossils including Dinohyus, Moropus, and beardog species, watch a 12-minute orientation movie in the theater, and explore the James H. Cook Collection of Northern Plains Indian artifacts featuring gifts from Chief Red Cloud and other Lakota and Cheyenne leaders; 301 River Road, Harrison, NE 69346; Summer hours: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM Mountain Time daily, May 15 - September 30; Winter hours: 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM Mountain Time daily, October 1 - May 14; closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; free admission; 308-665-4113 [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/agfo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/258590), [Just Go Travel Studios](https://www.justgotravelstudios.com/blogs/just-go-travel-blog/agate-fossil-beds-national-monument), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60711-d566462-Reviews-Agate_Fossil_Beds_National_Monument-Harrison_Nebraska.html)]

- **Hike the Fossil Hills Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - Walk a 2.7-2.8 mile round-trip trail with approximately 170 feet of elevation gain leading to historic fossil quarries on University Hill and Carnegie Hill where paleontologists excavated in the early 1900s, featuring interpretive signs about paleontology history and a boardwalk section over wetlands and the Niobrara River; trailhead at Visitor Center, 301 River Road, Harrison, NE 69346; open sunrise to sunset daily year-round; free admission; 308-665-4113 [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/agfo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Just Go Travel Studios](https://www.justgotravelstudios.com/blogs/just-go-travel-blog/agate-fossil-beds-national-monument), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60711-d566462-Reviews-Agate_Fossil_Beds_National_Monument-Harrison_Nebraska.html), [NPS Outdoor Activities](https://www.nps.gov/agfo/planyourvisit/outdooractivities.htm)]

- **Walk the Daemonelix Trail to See Devil's Corkscrew Fossils** (30-60 minutes) - Explore a 1.0 mile easy trail with 137 feet of elevation gain featuring preserved spiral burrow fossils (Daemonelix) created by prehistoric beaver-like mammals called Paleocastor, displayed in encased exhibits in their original locations alongside ancient sand dunes and fossil grassland soils; trailhead at west end of park, approximately 1 mile west of Visitor Center on River Road; open sunrise to sunset daily year-round; free admission; 308-665-4113 [Source: [NPS Daemonelix Trail](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/daemonelix-trail.htm), [NPS Outdoor Activities](https://www.nps.gov/agfo/planyourvisit/outdooractivities.htm), [Live and Let Hike](https://liveandlethike.com/2022/12/03/daemonelix-trail-agate-fossil-beds-national-monument-ne/), [Trailsnet](https://trailsnet.com/trails/daemonelix-trail/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **View the James H. Cook Collection - The Monument's True Hidden Gem** (20-30 minutes) - Examine one of the most impressive collections of Northern Plains Indian artifacts, comprised of gifts given to rancher James H. Cook by Chief Red Cloud of the Lakota Sioux and other Northern Plains tribal leaders in the late 1800s, representing a lasting friendship and cultural exchange; located in Visitor Center museum, 301 River Road, Harrison, NE 69346; Summer hours: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM Mountain Time daily, May 15 - September 30; Winter hours: 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM Mountain Time daily, October 1 - May 14; free admission; 308-665-4113 [Source: [NPS Collections](https://www.nps.gov/agfo/learn/historyculture/collections.htm), [Atlas Obscura](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/agate-fossil-beds-national-monument), [Black Hills Parks Association](https://blackhillsparks.org/agate-fossil-beds-national-monument/), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g60711-d566462-r214359307-Agate_Fossil_Beds_National_Monument-Harrison_Nebraska.html)]

- **Complete the Junior Ranger or Pee Wee Ranger Program** (30-60 minutes for Junior Ranger, 15-30 minutes for Pee Wee Ranger) - Pick up a free age-appropriate activity booklet at the Visitor Center (Junior Ranger for ages 6+, Pee Wee Ranger coloring program for ages 2-5), complete activities exploring both fossils and Native American history while touring the park, then share answers with a ranger to receive a special wooden ranger badge; available at Visitor Center, 301 River Road, Harrison, NE 69346; Summer hours: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM Mountain Time daily, May 15 - September 30; Winter hours: 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM Mountain Time daily, October 1 - May 14; free; 308-665-4113 [Source: [NPS Junior Ranger Program](https://www.nps.gov/agfo/learn/kidsyouth/beajuniorranger.htm), [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/agfo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [The Adventuring Heidi](https://theadventuringheidi.com/2022/07/05/agate-fossil-beds-national-monument-fort-laramie-national-historic-site/)]

- **Experience Lakota Tipis in Summer Months** (10-15 minutes) - View authentic tipis raised by Lakota tribe members during summer months, offering a tangible connection to the Plains Indian heritage celebrated in the Cook Collection and honoring the friendship between James Cook and Chief Red Cloud; located on monument grounds near Visitor Center; visible during summer season only; free admission; 308-665-4113 [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/agfo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Outdoorsy Guide](https://www.outdoorsy.com/guide/agate-fossil-beds-national-monument)]

## Also Nearby

- **Fort Robinson State Park** (2-4 hours) - Explore Nebraska's largest state park featuring the Fort Robinson History Center with exhibits about military history and Lakota heritage, the Trailside Museum with geology and natural history displays including the "Clash of the Mammoths" fossil exhibit, plus 60 miles of hiking trails, 20 miles of mountain biking trails, wrangler-led horseback rides, and an Olympic-sized indoor pool; 3200 West Highway 20, Crawford, NE 69339 (24 miles from Harrison, 2 miles west of Crawford); park grounds open year-round, Activity Center open daily 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM Mountain Time, visitor activities run Memorial Day through Labor Day plus limited fall schedule; 308-665-2900 [Source: [Nebraska Game & Parks](https://outdoornebraska.gov/location/fort-robinson/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g45508-d268048-Reviews-Fort_Robinson_State_Park-Crawford_Nebraska.html), [Visit Nebraska](https://visitnebraska.com/crawford/fort-robinson-state-park)]

- **Scotts Bluff National Monument** (2-3 hours) - Walk or drive to the summit of this 800-foot bluff that served as a landmark on the Oregon Trail, featuring nearly 4 miles of trails including a 1.6-mile paved trail to the top, plus a visitor center housing the world's largest collection of William Henry Jackson artwork documenting westward expansion; 190276 Old Oregon Trail, Gering, NE 69341 (approximately 43-49 miles south of Agate Fossil Beds); park grounds and trails open sunrise to sunset daily, Visitor Center hours vary by season, Summit Road open 8:00 AM - 6:30 PM in summer and 8:00 AM - 4:30 PM in off-season; 308-436-9700 [Source: [NPS Scotts Bluff Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/scbl/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Visit Nebraska](https://visitnebraska.com/gering/scotts-bluff-national-monument), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60962-d2016156-Reviews-Scotts_Bluff_National_Monument-Gering_Nebraska.html)]

- **Toadstool Geologic Park** (1-2 hours for hiking) - Hike through otherworldly badlands formations featuring mushroom-shaped rock formations, fossil beds from 30 million years ago, and dramatic eroded landscapes in the Oglala National Grassland; located north of Crawford off Nebraska Highway 2/71 on Toadstool Road (approximately 30-40 miles north of Agate Fossil Beds); open 24 hours daily year-round; day use $3 per vehicle, camping $15 per night; administrative office: 125 N Main St, Chadron, NE 69337, 308-432-0300 (Monday-Friday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM Mountain Time) [Source: [USFS Recreation Area](https://www.fs.usda.gov/recarea/nebraska/recarea/?recid=10616), [Visit Nebraska](https://visitnebraska.com/crawford/toadstool-geologic-park), [Discover Northwest Nebraska](https://discovernwnebraska.com/toadstool-geological-park/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g28948-d269782-Reviews-Toadstool_Geologic_Park-Nebraska.html)]

**Total Recommended Time at Agate Fossil Beds NM (National Monument):** 2-3 hours for visitor center, film, and one trail; plan 3-4 hours to experience both trails and all exhibits thoroughly. [Source: [TripAdvisor Visitor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60711-d566462-Reviews-Agate_Fossil_Beds_National_Monument-Harrison_Nebraska.html), [Just Go Travel Studios](https://www.justgotravelstudios.com/blogs/just-go-travel-blog/agate-fossil-beds-national-monument), [NPS Top 10 Tips](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/top-10-tips-for-visiting-agate-fossil-beds.htm)]

**Important Visitor Notes:** The monument is located in a remote area at over 4,000 feet elevation. The nearest gas station is 34 miles away, so fuel up in Harrison or Crawford. Pack lunch, snacks, water, sunscreen, and bug spray as both trails are fully exposed. Summer heat and elevation can cause rapid dehydration. Check for prairie rattlesnakes and ticks in grassy areas. Limited cell reception, but free WiFi available at visitor center. [Source: [NPS Top 10 Tips](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/top-10-tips-for-visiting-agate-fossil-beds.htm)]
