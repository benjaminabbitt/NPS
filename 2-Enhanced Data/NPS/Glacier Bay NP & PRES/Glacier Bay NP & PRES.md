# Glacier Bay NP & PRES (National Park & Preserve)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-visitor-center"></a>**Glacier Bay National Park Visitor Center** (179 Bartlett Cove Road, Gustavus, Alaska, 99826 (58.451265, -135.829998); Open 8:00 AM–5:00 PM daily in summer (late May to mid-September), staffed daily with park rangers; 907-697-2230) - Located on the second floor of Glacier Bay Lodge in Bartlett Cove, 10 miles from Gustavus. Features exhibits, park information, a bookstore, an underwater hydrophone listening station, an auditorium for park films and programs, and views of Bartlett Cove. National Park Rangers will also bring passport stamps aboard cruise ships that visit Glacier Bay, with a desk set up with park souvenirs. [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/glacier-bay-national-park-visitor-center-gustavus), [My Perfect Alaska Cruise](https://myperfectalaskacruise.com/alaska-travel/alaska-national-park-passport-stamps/), [Alaska.org](https://www.alaska.org/advice/tips-for-national-park-passport-stampers-visiting-alaska)]

**Note:** Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. Cancellations are occasionally moved to seasonal locations, and the exact location is determined by park staff.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-day-boat-tour"></a>**Glacier Bay Day Boat Tour** (8 hours) - The signature experience and only scheduled day tour permitted inside Glacier Bay National Park. This full-day cruise aboard a 149-passenger high-speed catamaran covers the west arm of Glacier Bay and highlights two towering tidewater glaciers: Margerie Glacier and Grand Pacific Glacier (rising 245 feet above the waterline and stretching another 100 feet below). Boarding begins at 7:00 AM with departure at 7:30 AM sharp. Park Naturalist provides narration about natural history, wildlife, geography, and glaciers. Tour includes lunch and opportunities to see humpback whales, sea lions, bears, seals, and bald eagles along the pristine coastline. Cost: $265 per adult, $138.76 for children ages 3-12. Glacier Bay Lodge, Bartlett Cove (58.451265, -135.829998); Daily departures late May through mid-September; 907-697-2230. [Source: [Glacier Bay Day Tour](https://www.visitglacierbay.com/tours/glacier-bay-day-tour/), [NPS Tour Boat](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/glacier-bay-tour-boat.htm), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/glacier-bay-boat-tour-glacier-bay-national-park/), [Upon Arriving Review](https://www.uponarriving.com/glacier-bay-day-boat-tour-review/)]

- <a id="activity-kayaking-bartlett"></a>**Kayaking in Bartlett Cove** (Half-day: 3.5 hours; Full-day: 6 hours) - Guided kayak tours or independent rentals in the protected rain forest environment of Bartlett Cove. Rentals available daily at 9:00 AM with half-day return at 12:30 PM or full-day return at 3:00 PM. Single kayaks rent for $50/day, double kayaks $65/day. Guided tours include instruction and equipment. Meet at the NPS Visitor Information Station (VIS) in Bartlett Cove, near Glacier Bay Lodge. Bartlett Cove, Gustavus, AK 99826 (58.451265, -135.829998); Summer season; 907-697-2230. [Source: [Glacier Bay Sea Kayaks](https://glacierbayseakayaks.com/day-trips/one-day-kayak-adventure/), [NPS Kayaking](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/kayaking.htm), [Visit Glacier Bay](https://www.visitglacierbay.com/things-to-do/guided-kayaking-rentals/)]

- <a id="activity-forest-loop"></a>**Forest Loop Trail** (1 mile, 30-90 minutes) - An accessible nature walk through pond-studded spruce/hemlock temperate rainforest. The trail surface varies from dirt and crushed rock to sections of boardwalk, and it's almost entirely flat. The first half-mile is handicap accessible. Can be combined with Beach Trail for a complete 1-mile loop. Ranger-led guided walks offered daily at 1:30 PM, taking about 90 minutes. Self-guided hikers can complete it in 20-30 minutes. Departs from Glacier Bay Lodge, Bartlett Cove (58.451265, -135.829998); Open year-round. [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/glba-hiking.htm), [Visit Glacier Bay Hiking](https://www.visitglacierbay.com/things-to-do/hiking/), [National Parks Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/2010/07/easy-park-hikes-%E2%80%93-forest-loop-trail-glacier-bay-national-park6254), [AllTrails](https://fatmap.com/discover/route/united-states-of-america/alaska/hiking/bartlett-cove-forest-trail-loop/2747796)]

- <a id="activity-beach-trail"></a>**Beach Trail** (1 mile roundtrip, 30-60 minutes) - A pleasant shoreline stroll starting at the south end of the parking lot at the public boat dock. Runs parallel to the rocky beach with excellent opportunities to explore intertidal life at low tide. Can be combined with Forest Loop Trail for a complete 1-mile circuit taking 20-30 minutes or longer when exploring tidepools. Bartlett Cove, Gustavus, AK 99826 (58.451265, -135.829998); Open year-round. [Source: [NPS Exploring Bartlett Cove](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/exploring-bartlett-cove.htm), [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [Best Hikes](https://www.travel-experience-live.com/best-hikes-in-glacier-bay-national-park/)]

- <a id="activity-whale-watching"></a>**Whale Watching Tours in Icy Strait** (3.5 hours) - Half-day whale watching cruises into Icy Strait to see humpback whales feeding in nutrient-rich waters, along with potential sightings of Minke whales, orcas, Stellar sea lions, sea otters, Dall's porpoise, harbor porpoise, bald eagles, puffins, and various shore birds. Tours typically depart at 10:00 AM and return at 1:30 PM. Glacier Bay is one of the best places in Alaska to see humpback whales. Gustavus operators; Summer season. [Source: [Visit Glacier Bay Whale Watching](https://www.visitglacierbay.com/things-to-do/whale-watching/), [TAZ Wildlife Tours](https://www.tazwhalewatching.com/), [Glacier Bay Alaska Whale Watching](https://glacierbayalaska.com/experience-alaska/whale-watching/), [Alaska.org Whale Watching](https://www.alaska.org/destination/glacier-bay-national-park/whale-watching)]

- <a id="activity-bartlett-river"></a>**Bartlett River Trail** (4.5 miles out-and-back, 1.5-2 hours) - An easy scenic journey through lush spruce and hemlock forests, winding along an intertidal lagoon before reaching the stunning Bartlett River estuary. Elevation gain: 482 feet. Trail can be challenging with lots of roots, rocks, and mud; wet surfaces can be very slippery. Trailhead at Bartlett Cove (58.451265, -135.829998); Open year-round, best May-September. [Source: [AllTrails Bartlett River](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/alaska/bartlett-river-trail), [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [Alaska.org](https://www.alaska.org/detail/bartlett-river-trail)]

- <a id="activity-ranger-programs"></a>**Ranger Programs** (Various times, 30-90 minutes) - National Park Service Rangers offer a variety of programs including daily guided Forest Loop Trail walks at 1:30 PM and evening presentations at Glacier Bay Lodge. Programs may include cultural presentations with local Huna Tlingit guides. Rangers also board cruise vessels for shipwide commentary and presentations. Schedule posted at Glacier Bay Lodge and Visitor Center or check www.nps.gov/glba. Glacier Bay Lodge, Bartlett Cove (58.451265, -135.829998); Summer season; 907-697-2230. [Source: [Visit Glacier Bay Ranger Programs](https://www.visitglacierbay.com/things-to-do/national-park-ranger-programs/), [Royal Caribbean Guide](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/guides/glacier-bay-national-park)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-east-arm-kayaking"></a>**East Arm Multi-Day Kayaking Expedition** (5-10 days typical, 6-day guided trips available) - Explore the remote East Arm, a seldom-visited paddler's paradise closed to cruise ships and most motorized boat traffic, offering true wilderness solitude. Tours typically move camp nightly, paddling 10-15 miles (3-5 hours on water) per day. Access via tour boat drop-off service from Bartlett Cove to Muir Inlet, McBride Glacier, or the Beardslee Islands. Wildlife includes whales, bears, wolves, seals, sea lions, otters, and diverse sea birds with minimal competition for campsites and no boat noise. Self-guided rentals: single kayaks $50/day, double kayaks $65/day. Departs from Bartlett Cove (58.451265, -135.829998); Summer season; 907-697-2230 or contact outfitters. [Source: [Spirit Walker Expeditions](https://seakayakalaska.com/trips/glacier-bay-east-arm-kayaking-trip/), [Alaska Mountain Guides](https://alaskamountainguides.com/tour-page/glacier-bay-east-arm/), [NPS Kayaking](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/kayaking.htm), [Glacier Bay Sea Kayaks](https://glacierbayseakayaks.com/guided-backcountry-trips/guided-rental-trips/)]

- <a id="gem-bartlett-lake"></a>**Bartlett Lake Trail** (8.5 miles roundtrip, 3.5-4 hours) - A moderate, primitive, rugged hike branching off from Bartlett River Trail that leads to remote Bartlett Lake. Elevation gain: 935 feet. Challenging trail conditions with roots, rocks, mud, and often very slippery surfaces. Far less traveled than other Bartlett Cove trails, offering solitude and wilderness experience. Trailhead at Bartlett Cove (58.451265, -135.829998); Open year-round, best May-September. [Source: [AllTrails Bartlett Lake](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/alaska/bartlett-lake-trail--2), [Virtual Sherpa Guide](https://www.thevirtualsherpa.com/bartlett-lake-glacier-bay-national-park-hike/), [NPS Bartlett Cove Trails](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/bartlett-cove-trails.htm)]

- <a id="gem-fishing-charter"></a>**Charter Fishing Excursions** (Full-day: 8-9 hours; Half-day: 3.5-4 hours) - Experience world-class fishing in Icy Straits and Outer Coast targeting halibut (including 70+ pound fish), all five species of Pacific salmon, ling cod, and rockfish. Gustavus is a top destination for charter fishing away from the crowds. Travel time to fishing grounds takes less than 20 minutes. Local guides with decades of experience provide deep knowledge of the area. Various operators in Gustavus; Summer season. [Source: [Glacier Bay Alaska Fishing](https://glacierbayalaska.com/alaska-fishing/salt-water-fishing/), [Fairweather Adventures](https://www.fairweatheradventures.net/fishing/), [Gustavus Fishing](https://www.gustavus.com/activities/fishing.html), [Off the Beaten Path](https://www.offthebeatenpath.com/an-experience-to-remember-in-glacier-bay-national-park/)]

- <a id="gem-flightseeing"></a>**Flightseeing Tours** (1-2 hours) - Scenic flights offering aerial views of the snow-capped Fairweather Mountain range, alpine and tidewater glaciers, fjords, bays, alpine lakes, the Juneau Icefield, and Mendenhall Glacier. Spot bears roaming beaches and mountain goats on rock outcroppings from above. Tours range from 1 hour ($1,250 charter for up to 5 people) to 2 hours ($1,750), with customizable options. Gustavus Airport (58.4214, -135.7364); Summer season. [Source: [Mountain Flying Service](https://mountainflyingservice.com/haines-skagway-glacier-bay-flightseeing-tours), [NPS Flightseeing](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/flightseeing.htm), [Visit Glacier Bay Flightseeing](https://www.visitglacierbay.com/things-to-do/flightseeing/)]

- <a id="gem-xunaa-shuka-hit"></a>**Xunaa Shuká Hít (Huna Tribal House)** (30-60 minutes) - Traditional Tlingit tribal house memorializing the clan houses that once lined the shores of present-day Bartlett Cove. Located on the Łingít Trail, about 1/4 mile northeast past Glacier Bay Lodge. Cultural programs and special events offered in summer depending on tribal use and availability. Learn about the Huna Tlingit culture and see demonstrations at this significant cultural site on traditional Huna Tlingit land. Łingít Trail, Bartlett Cove (58.451265, -135.829998); Summer season, check for program availability; 907-697-2230. [Source: [NPS Huna Tribal House](https://www.nps.gov/glba/learn/historyculture/huna-tribal-house-project.htm), [NPS Places](https://www.nps.gov/places/huna-tribal-house.htm), [NPS Experience](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/huna-tribal-house.htm), [Colossal Article](https://www.thisiscolossal.com/2025/03/xunaa-shuka-hit-glacier-bay/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-mt-fairweather-golf"></a>**Mount Fairweather Golf Course** (2-3 hours, 10 minutes from Bartlett Cove) - Alaska's most unlikely attraction: a 9-hole, par-36 golf course in a town where moose outnumber people. Operates on an honor system with $15/person per round. Walk-ons welcome. Located in the charming village of Gustavus with refurbished 1930s gas pumps and a community porch swing. 1 Mile Dock Road (also 1 State Dock Road), Gustavus, AK 99826 (58.4214, -135.7364); Self-service hours; 907-697-2214. [Source: [PGA Mount Fairweather](https://www.pga.com/play/ak/gustavus/mount-fairweather-golf-course/27632466), [Gustavus Golf](https://www.gustavus.com/activities/golf.html), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60833-d622635-Reviews-Mt_Fairweather_Golf_Course-Gustavus_Alaska.html)]

- <a id="nearby-gustavus-exploration"></a>**Explore Gustavus Village** (1-2 hours, 15 minutes from Bartlett Cove) - Visit the tiny, charming village where moose outnumber people. Community features include a porch swing, refurbished 1930s gas pumps, local dining options, and small lodges. Gustavus serves as the gateway to Glacier Bay National Park and offers a peaceful small-town Alaska experience. Gustavus, AK (58.4214, -135.7364); Various businesses have different hours. [Source: [Travel Alaska Gustavus](https://www.travelalaska.com/destinations/cities-towns/glacier-bay-gustavus), [NPS Gustavus](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/gustavus.htm), [Gustavus.com Guide](http://www.gustavus.com/)]

## Review / Personal Notes

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 2-3 days minimum for a comprehensive experience. Essential activities include the 8-hour Glacier Bay Day Boat Tour (the signature park experience), hiking the Forest Loop and Beach Trails (1.5-2 hours combined), and a half-day kayaking excursion in Bartlett Cove (3.5 hours). A 3-4 day itinerary allows time for the boat tour, hiking, kayaking, whale watching, and ranger programs while accounting for travel to/from Gustavus from Juneau. For those seeking an immersive wilderness experience, multi-day kayaking expeditions in the remote East Arm require 5-10 days. [Source: [We Seek Travel Itinerary](https://www.weseektravel.com/3-day-glacier-bay-national-park-itinerary/), [Visit Glacier Bay Itinerary](https://www.visitglacierbay.com/things-to-do/sample-itineraries/our-favorite-3-4-day-summer-itinerary/), [Through My Lens Guide](https://www.throughmylens.com/alaska/how-to-visit-glacier-bay-national-park/)]
