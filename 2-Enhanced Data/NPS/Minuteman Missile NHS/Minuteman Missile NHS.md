# Minuteman Missile NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="visitor-center-stamp"></a>**Visitor Center at Exit 131** (21280 SD Highway 240, Philip, SD 57567 (43.833353, -101.900492); Tuesday-Saturday 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM; 605-433-5552) [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/mimi/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Visit Rapid City](https://www.visitrapidcity.com/blog/post/why-you-need-to-spend-a-day-at-minuteman-missile-national-historic-site/)]

**Note:** Call 605-433-5552 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- <a id="delta-01-tour"></a>**Delta-01 Launch Control Facility Tour** (45 minutes) - Descend 31 feet underground in the original elevator to explore the launch control center where missileers controlled ten Minuteman II missiles during the Cold War; limited to six participants per tour with advance reservations required (24 hours to 90 days prior); reservations harder to obtain than Broadway tickets according to rangers; Exit 127, I-90, approximately 1.7 miles north (43.8776407, -101.9609102); tours generally 9:00 AM - 3:00 PM Tuesday-Saturday; reservations at npsreservations.com/minuteman-missile or by phone [Source: [NPS Delta-01 Tours](https://www.nps.gov/mimi/planyourvisit/launch-control-facility-delta-01-tours-and-tickets.htm), [NPS Reservations](https://www.npsreservations.com/minuteman-missile/), [Visit Rapid City](https://www.visitrapidcity.com/blog/post/why-you-need-to-spend-a-day-at-minuteman-missile-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="delta-09-silo"></a>**Delta-09 Missile Silo Self-Guided Tour** (30 minutes) - View an unarmed Minuteman II missile replica through a glass viewing platform above the actual silo; self-guided tour with cell phone audio guide available; no reservation required; Exit 116, I-90, Wall, SD 57775 (43.931563, -102.159966); daily 9:00 AM - 3:00 PM; free admission [Source: [NPS Delta-09](https://www.nps.gov/mimi/planyourvisit/visiting-launch-facility-missile-silo-delta-09.htm), [Atlas Obscura](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/delta-09-minuteman-missle-site), [Simply Awesome Trips](https://www.simplyawesometrips.com/minuteman-missile-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="visitor-center-exhibits"></a>**Visitor Center Exhibits and Film** (1-1.5 hours) - Explore exhibits on the Minuteman Intercontinental Ballistic Missile System and Cold War history; watch "Beneath the Plains: The Minuteman Missile On Alert" (30-minute film shown every 40 minutes in 50-seat theater); browse bookstore; 21280 SD Highway 240, Philip, SD 57567 (43.833353, -101.900492); Tuesday-Saturday 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM; free wi-fi and restrooms available [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/mimi/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [NPS Park Film](https://www.nps.gov/articles/mimi-feedollars-parkfilm.htm), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/minuteman-missile/)]

- <a id="virtual-tour"></a>**Virtual Tour at Visitor Center** (30-45 minutes) - Experience the underground Delta-01 control center through virtual reality for those unable to safely navigate the underground tour; available at Visitor Center at Exit 131; same hours as visitor center [Source: [National Parks Data](https://nationalparksdata.com/a-guide-to-visiting-minuteman-missile-national-historic-site/), [Atomic Archive](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/atomic-tourist/minuteman.html)]

- <a id="junior-ranger"></a>**Junior Ranger Program** (varies) - Complete activities to learn about the park and earn a Junior Ranger badge; available for all ages; free online program also available; pick up materials at Visitor Center [Source: [NPS Junior Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/mimi/learn/kidsyouth/junior-ranger.htm), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/minuteman-missile/)]

- <a id="ranger-programs"></a>**Ranger-Led Programs and Talks** (varies) - Attend ranger presentations to gain deeper understanding of the site's significance in American Cold War history; check with Visitor Center for current schedule; various times throughout the day [Source: [National Parks Data](https://nationalparksdata.com/a-guide-to-visiting-minuteman-missile-national-historic-site/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="prairie-landscape-tour"></a>**Prairie Cultural Landscape Self-Guided Tour** (2-3 hours) - Discover former missile silo sites hidden in plain sight across the South Dakota landscape between Minuteman Missile NHS and Badlands National Park; many Cold War remnants easily found once you know where to look; the missile field once consisted of 150 Minuteman II missiles, 15 launch-control centers covering over 13,500 square miles; access via self-guided driving tour; sites scattered along I-90 and surrounding prairie [Source: [NPS Trip Ideas](https://www.nps.gov/tripideas/mimi-missilefieldlandscape.htm), [National Parks Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/2025/09/day-park-minuteman-missile-national-historic-site)]

- <a id="hidden-in-plain-sight"></a>**Delta-01 "Hidden Ranch House" Experience** (included in tour) - Appreciate how the Delta-01 Launch Control Facility looks like an ordinary ranch house from Interstate 90, disguising the military capabilities hidden within; most travelers passed by for decades without recognizing its significance; this camouflage was intentional Cold War design; viewable from highway, best experienced during the underground tour [Source: [National Parks Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/2025/09/day-park-minuteman-missile-national-historic-site), [Black Hills and Badlands](https://www.blackhillsbadlands.com/parks-monuments/minuteman-missile-national-historic-site)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="badlands-np"></a>**Badlands National Park** (half to full day, 4 miles / 6-7 minutes) - Explore dramatic eroded buttes, pinnacles, and spires with hiking trails, scenic drives, and wildlife viewing; Ben Reifel Visitor Center at 25216 Ben Reifel Road, Interior, SD 57750 (43.749382, -101.941881); northeast entrance is 4 miles south of Minuteman Missile Visitor Center; park open 24/7, visitor center hours vary by season; entrance fee required [Source: [Wall-Badlands Chamber](https://www.wall-badlands.com/things-to-do/attractions/), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/minuteman-missile/), [Badlands Info](https://www.badlandsinfo.com/things-to-do-in-the-park/visitor-centers/)]

- <a id="wall-drug"></a>**Wall Drug** (1-2 hours, 6 miles / 10 minutes) - Famous roadside attraction featuring free family entertainment including old-time five-and-dime, art gallery, animated dinosaur exhibits, gift shops, and restaurants; 510 Main St, Wall, SD 57790 (43.9928454, -102.2414196); just north of I-90 Exit 110; daily hours vary by season, generally 6:00 AM - 6:00 PM in summer; free admission; 605-279-2175 [Source: [Wall Drug](https://www.walldrug.com/plan-your-visit/minuteman-missile-national-historic-site/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/wall-drug-store-wall), [Apple Maps](https://maps.apple.com/place?address=510+Main+St,+Wall,+SD++57790)]

- <a id="prairie-homestead"></a>**Prairie Homestead Historic Sod House** (1 hour, 20 miles / 25 minutes) - Tour an authentic 1909 sod house built by Ed Brown and his wife, added to National Register of Historic Places in 1974; experience pioneer life on the South Dakota plains; 21070 South Dakota Highway 240, north of Interior, SD (43.801266, -101.90572); located one-half mile north of Badlands National Park east entrance on Highway 240; hours vary by season; admission fee; 605-433-5400 [Source: [Prairie Homestead](https://www.prairiehomestead.com/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prairie_Homestead), [SAH Archipedia](https://sah-archipedia.org/buildings/SD-01-071-0048)]

## Review / Personal Notes

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 4-5 hours for comprehensive site visit (45 min Delta-01 tour + 30 min Delta-09 silo + 1.5 hours visitor center exhibits/film + 30 min travel between three sites + 1.5 hours buffer for parking, photos, and exploration). Visitors keeping to a tight schedule can complete the essentials in 2 hours (visitor center + one facility tour). For those wishing to fully explore without rushing, allow a half day (4-5 hours).
