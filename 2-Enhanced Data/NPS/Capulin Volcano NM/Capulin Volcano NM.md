# Capulin Volcano NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Capulin Volcano Visitor Center** (46 Volcano Highway, Capulin, NM 88414 (36.7828, -103.9703); 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM daily winter, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM summer, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; email: cavo_interpretation@nps.gov) [Source: [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/cavo/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/capulin-volcano-national-monument-capulin), [Maker Mama](https://www.makermama.com/2020/10/visiting-capulin-volcano-national-monument.html)]

**Note:** Passport station located inside visitor center. Volcano Road (two-mile paved drive to summit) closes to vehicles going up 30 minutes before visitor center closes. [Source: [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/258541), [Maker Mama](https://www.makermama.com/2020/10/visiting-capulin-volcano-national-monument.html)]

## Key Activities

- **Drive to Volcano Summit** (10-20 minutes) - Ascend two-mile paved Volcano Road winding around Capulin Volcano from 7,300 to 8,182 feet elevation; accessible to most vehicles; stunning views of Raton-Clayton Volcanic Field [Source: [NPS Drive to Top](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/drive-to-the-top-of-capulin-volcano.htm), [Maker Mama](https://www.makermama.com/2020/10/visiting-capulin-volcano-national-monument.html)]

- **Hike Crater Rim Trail** (1 hour) - Walk one-mile loop around crater rim at 8,182 feet elevation; paved trail provides panoramic views of volcanic field, four states (New Mexico, Colorado, Oklahoma, Texas), and surrounding landscape; moderate difficulty due to elevation [Source: [NPS Choosing Your Hike](https://home.nps.gov/cavo/planyourvisit/choosing-your-hike.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60759-d668100-Reviews-Capulin_Volcano_National_Monument-Capulin_New_Mexico.html)]

- **Descend Crater Vent Trail** (30 minutes round-trip) - Hike quarter-mile trail down into bottom of volcanic crater; experience interior of dormant cinder cone volcano [Source: [NPS Choosing Your Hike](https://home.nps.gov/cavo/planyourvisit/choosing-your-hike.htm), [Average Wild Experience](https://averagewildexperience.com/capulin-volcano-national-monument/)]

- **Explore Visitor Center Exhibits** (30-60 minutes) - Watch informative videos about volcanic formation and geology; examine exhibits about Raton-Clayton Volcanic Field; learn about cinder cone volcano formation [Source: [Maker Mama](https://www.makermama.com/2020/10/visiting-capulin-volcano-national-monument.html)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Night Sky Viewing** (evening, varies) - Experience exceptional stargazing from high elevation (8,182 feet at summit) in remote location with minimal light pollution; clear views of Milky Way and constellations [Source: [Western NM Parks](https://wnpa.org/explore-parks/capulin-volcano)]

- **Boca and Folsom Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - Hike less-traveled 1.5-mile trail around base of volcano; observe different ecological zones and volcanic features from ground level; fewer crowds than rim trails [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/new-mexico/capulin-volcano-national-monument)]

## Also Nearby

- **Sugarite Canyon State Park** (30-40 minutes, 25 miles north near Raton) - Explore hiking trails, fishing lakes, and camping in scenic canyon; view wildlife including black bears and wild turkeys; day-use or overnight camping available [Source: [Beyond My Door](https://www.beyondmydoor.com/capulin-volcano-national-monument/)]

- **Folsom Museum** (30 minutes, 25 miles east) - Discover archaeological artifacts from Folsom culture (10,000+ years ago) including famous Folsom points; learn about early human habitation of region [Source: [Visit Raton](https://ratonnm.gov/business_detail_T7_R8.php)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** Half day ideal; minimum 1-2 hours for visitor center and drive to summit, 2-3 hours adds Crater Rim Trail, half day (3-4 hours) for comprehensive visit including all trails and exhibits. Plan for high elevation effects (7,300-8,182 feet).
