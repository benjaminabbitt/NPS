# Saint Paul's Church NHS (National Historic Site)

## Key Activities

<a name="activity-church-tour"></a>
- **Historic Church Tour** (30-45 minutes) - Tour the 18th century stone church built in 1763-1787 that served as a hospital during the American Revolution for both British and American forces; visitors can explore the church interior and climb the 225-year-old church tower on ranger-guided or self-guided tours with interpretive materials; 897 South Columbus Avenue, Mount Vernon, NY 10550 (40.8935185, -73.8253367); Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/sapa/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [Source](https://www.visitwestchesterny.com/listing/saint-pauls-church-national-historic-site/22/), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60952-d105847-Reviews-St_Paul_s_Church_National_Historic_Site-Mount_Vernon_New_York.html)]

<a name="activity-museum"></a>
- **Museum and Visitor Center** (30-45 minutes) - Explore exhibits on local and national history in the carriage house museum, featuring displays about the Great Election of 1733, the John Peter Zenger trial and freedom of the press, and the Revolutionary War period; facilities include restrooms and parking; 897 South Columbus Avenue, Mount Vernon, NY 10550 (40.8935185, -73.8253367); Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60952-d105847-Reviews-St_Paul_s_Church_National_Historic_Site-Mount_Vernon_New_York.html), [Source](https://www.visitwestchesterny.com/listing/saint-pauls-church-national-historic-site/22/)]

<a name="activity-cemetery"></a>
- **Historic Cemetery Walk** (30-45 minutes) - Walk through one of America's oldest continuously used burial grounds with headstones dating back to 1704; the five-acre cemetery remains an active burial ground for families who own plots; self-guided exploration with printed materials available; 897 South Columbus Avenue, Mount Vernon, NY 10550 (40.8935185, -73.8253367); accessible during site hours [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/sapa/learn/historyculture/stpaulschurchcemetery.htm), [Source](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/facility/10088445), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60952-d105847-Reviews-St_Paul_s_Church_National_Historic_Site-Mount_Vernon_New_York.html)]

<a name="activity-ranger-programs"></a>
- **Educational Programs and Special Events** (varies, typically 1-2 hours) - Participate in ranger-led programs, costumed reenactments, educational talks, and special Saturday programs (July-December at 1:00 PM); programs cover colonial history, the Great Election of 1733, freedom of the press, and Revolutionary War history; check program schedule for dates; 897 South Columbus Avenue, Mount Vernon, NY 10550 (40.8935185, -73.8253367); varies by program [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/sapa/sapaprogsched.htm), [Source](https://www.iloveny.com/listing/saint-pauls-church-national-historic-site/3486/), [Source](https://www.visitwestchesterny.com/listing/saint-pauls-church-national-historic-site/22/)]

## Hidden Gems

<a name="hidden-great-election"></a>
- **Great Election of 1733 Site** (included in tour time) - Stand on the Village Green in the colonial town of Eastchester where one of the most significant political events in colonial America took place on October 29, 1733; this disputed election raised unprecedented issues of freedom of religion and freedom of the press, leading to printer John Peter Zenger's trial and acquittal in 1735 that established foundations for press freedom in America; the New York Weekly Journal carried one of the only complete accounts of a colonial election; interpretive materials available [[Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Paul's_Church_National_Historic_Site), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/sapa/learn/historyculture/index.htm)]

<a name="hidden-bell-tower"></a>
- **Church Bell Tower Access** (included in church tour) - Climb the historic 225-year-old church tower for unique views; access available during ranger-guided tours; this architectural feature survived centuries of history including use during the Revolutionary War; 897 South Columbus Avenue, Mount Vernon, NY 10550 (40.8935185, -73.8253367); during tour hours [[Source](https://www.visitwestchesterny.com/listing/saint-pauls-church-national-historic-site/22/)]
