# James A. Garfield NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **James A. Garfield NHS Visitor Center** (8095 Mentor Avenue, Mentor, OH 44060 [41.6639835, -81.3509326]; Fridays-Sundays 10:00 AM-5:00 PM; 440-255-8722)

**Note:** Call 440-255-8722 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [NPS Contact Info](https://www.nps.gov/jaga/contacts.htm), [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/jaga/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Guided Home Tour** (45-60 minutes) - Rangers and volunteers lead visitors through the beautifully restored Victorian-era home of President and Mrs. James A. Garfield, featuring over 80% original Garfield family furniture from the 1880s; 8095 Mentor Avenue, Mentor, OH 44060; Fridays-Sundays 10:00 AM-5:00 PM; reservations required for groups of 6+, first-come first-served for smaller groups [Source: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/jaga/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [NPS Plan Like a Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/plan-like-a-james-a-garfield-nhs-park-ranger.htm), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g50641-d103628-Reviews-James_A_Garfield_National_Historic_Site-Mentor_Lake_County_Ohio.html)]

- **Visitor Center Exhibits and Film** (20-30 minutes) - Explore exhibits spanning the life, presidency, and assassination of President Garfield, plus watch an 18-minute biographical film available upon request in the theater; 8095 Mentor Avenue, Mentor, OH 44060; Fridays-Sundays 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/james-a-garfield-national-historic-site/), [NPS Plan Like a Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/plan-like-a-james-a-garfield-nhs-park-ranger.htm)]

- **Self-Guided Grounds Walk** (30 minutes) - Walk the quarter-mile paved path connecting all the buildings with wayside exhibits explaining the history of the Lawnfield estate; 8095 Mentor Avenue, Mentor, OH 44060; Fridays-Sundays 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Grounds Tour](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/ohio-national-parks/james-a-garfield-national-historic-site-park-at-a-glance/james-a-garfield-national-historic-site-grounds-tour/)]

- **Cell Phone Audio Tour** (50-60 minutes) - Self-guided tour with 10 stops along the loop path focusing on the 1880 presidential campaign; dial 440-230-3952 and enter stop numbers 11-20; each stop is at least 5 minutes long; 8095 Mentor Avenue, Mentor, OH 44060; Fridays-Sundays 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Cell Phone Tours](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/cell-phone-tours-at-james-a-garfield-national-historic-site.htm), [NPS 1880 Campaign Tour](https://www.nps.gov/jaga/planyourvisit/1880-campaign-cell-phone-tour.htm)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (30-45 minutes) - Activity booklet program available for visitors of all ages to learn about President Garfield and the site; 8095 Mentor Avenue, Mentor, OH 44060; Fridays-Sundays 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/james-a-garfield-national-historic-site/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Memorial Library** (included in home tour) - View Lucretia Garfield's addition to the home following her husband's assassination, containing around 1,400 books including Garfield's personal collection ranging from the Congressional Record to Edgar Allan Poe volumes and biographies; this is recognized as the first presidential library established in the United States; 8095 Mentor Avenue, Mentor, OH 44060; Fridays-Sundays 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g50641-d103628-Reviews-James_A_Garfield_National_Historic_Site-Mentor_Lake_County_Ohio.html)]

- **Photography Inside the Home** (included in home tour) - Unlike many historic presidential sites, visitors are permitted to take photographs inside the Garfield home during the guided tour, capturing the exceptional Victorian-era restoration and original furnishings; 8095 Mentor Avenue, Mentor, OH 44060; Fridays-Sundays 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g50641-d103628-Reviews-James_A_Garfield_National_Historic_Site-Mentor_Lake_County_Ohio.html)]

- **Rare Tree Collection** (self-guided, 15-20 minutes) - Explore the well-maintained grounds featuring excellent examples of rare and little-seen tree species from the Victorian era; 8095 Mentor Avenue, Mentor, OH 44060; Fridays-Sundays 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g50641-d103628-Reviews-James_A_Garfield_National_Historic_Site-Mentor_Lake_County_Ohio.html)]

## Also Nearby

- **Headlands Beach State Park** (45 minutes-2 hours, 4 miles) - Visit Ohio's longest natural sand beach at over a mile long, featuring swimming, fishing for bass and walleye at the breakwall, beach volleyball, and opportunities for beach glass hunting; the park attracts two million visitors annually; 9601 Headlands Rd, Mentor, OH 44060; 30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset; free admission [Source: [Ohio DNR](https://ohiodnr.gov/go-and-do/plan-a-visit/find-a-property/headlands-beach-state-park), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g50641-d2228283-Reviews-Headlands_Beach_State_Park-Mentor_Lake_County_Ohio.html)]

- **Lake Metroparks Farmpark** (2-3 hours, 9 miles) - Experience a hands-on agricultural education center on 235 acres with opportunities to milk dairy cows, watch cheese-making demonstrations, make ice cream the old-fashioned way on weekends, meet dozens of livestock breeds including llamas, alpacas, and draft horses, enjoy horse- or tractor-drawn wagon rides, and watch sheep herding demonstrations with border collies; 8800 Euclid Chardon Rd, Kirtland, OH 44094; Tuesday-Sunday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM (closed Monday); 440-256-2122; admission fee [Source: [Lake Metroparks](https://www.lakemetroparks.com/parks-trails/farmpark/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g50517-d1869581-Reviews-Lake_Metroparks_Farmpark-Kirtland_Lake_County_Ohio.html)]

- **Holden Arboretum** (2-4 hours, 12 miles) - Explore 3,500 acres of gardens, trails, lakes and meadows in Northeast Ohio including the iconic Murch Canopy Walk and Kalberer Emergent Tower, Holden Wildflower Garden, Holden Butterfly Garden, 20-acre Layer Rhododendron Garden, and collections of lilacs, viburnums, crabapples and conifers; guided walking tours available April-May, tram tours May-October; 9550 Sperry Road, Kirtland, OH; Tuesday-Sunday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM (closed Monday); adults $20, children 3-12 $14 [Source: [Holden Forests & Gardens](https://holdenfg.org/holden-arboretum/plan-your-visit/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g50517-d1869577-Reviews-The_Holden_Arboretum-Kirtland_Lake_County_Ohio.html)]

- **Lake County Captains Baseball (Classic Auto Group Park)** (2.5-3 hours, 11 miles) - Watch the Cleveland Guardians' High-A Minor League affiliate play at Classic Park, one of the shortest distances (18 miles) between a Major League club and Minor League affiliate in baseball; 35300 Vine Street, Eastlake, OH 44095; game times vary by schedule, front office Monday-Friday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; 440-975-8085; ticket prices vary [Source: [Lake County Captains](https://www.milb.com/lake-county), [MLB.com](https://www.mlb.com/news/featured/explore-classic-park-home-of-the-lake-county-captains)]
