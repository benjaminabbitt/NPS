# Vietnam Women's MEM (Vietnam Women's Memorial)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-lincoln-bookstore"></a>**Lincoln Memorial Bookstore** (2 Lincoln Memorial Circle NW, Washington, DC 20037 (38.889272, -77.048963); Daily: 10:00 AM-6:00 PM; 202-426-6841) - Primary location for collecting passport stamps for multiple National Mall memorials including Vietnam Women's Memorial. The bookstore offers 7 different stamps and stays open later than most locations. ## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-memorial-viewing"></a>**Vietnam Women's Memorial Viewing** (15-30 minutes) - View the powerful bronze sculpture depicting three women attending to a wounded soldier, designed by sculptor Glenna Goodacre and dedicated in 1993. The 2,000-pound structure stands 15 feet tall and honors the approximately 265,000 women who served during the Vietnam War, with 90% serving as nurses. The sculpture deliberately includes no identifying insignia to symbolically represent all women who served - military, medical, and civilian volunteers. Eight yellowwood trees surrounding the memorial represent the eight American servicewomen who died during the Vietnam War; 5 Henry Bacon Drive SW, Washington, DC 20007 (38.891254, -77.047715); Open 24 hours, Rangers on duty 9:30 AM-10:00 PM daily [[Source: NPS Vietnam Women's Memorial](https://www.nps.gov/places/000/vietnam-womens-memorial.htm), [Source: Washington.org](https://washington.org/find-dc-listings/vietnam-womens-memorial), [Source: Vietnam Women's Memorial Foundation](https://vietnamwomensmemorial.org/about-us/the-memorial/), [Source: Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_Women's_Memorial), [Source: Library of Congress](https://blogs.loc.gov/manuscripts/2022/11/the-vietnam-womens-memorial-representations-of-women-in-the-nations-public-memory/)]

- <a id="activity-vietnam-wall"></a>**Vietnam Veterans Memorial Wall** (30-60 minutes) - Walk along the iconic black granite wall containing the names of more than 58,000 American service members who died or remain missing from the Vietnam War. The wall consists of two identical walls that each stretch 246 feet and 9 inches. Multiple directories are located at the entrances to help visitors locate specific names alphabetically. Visitors often leave mementos and do rubbings of names; 5 Henry Bacon Drive NW, Washington, DC 20002 (38.891112, -77.047626); Open 24 hours, Rangers on duty 9:30 AM-10:00 PM daily [[Source: NPS Vietnam Veterans Memorial](https://www.nps.gov/vive/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Source: Washington.org](https://washington.org/DC-guide-to/vietnam-veterans-memorial), [Source: TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g28970-d104863-t5518823-Hi_How_long_do_you_recommend_you_allow_for_a.html)]

- <a id="activity-three-servicemen"></a>**The Three Servicemen Statue** (10-15 minutes) - View the bronze sculpture added in 1984 depicting three American servicemen (purposely identifiable as Latino American, European American, and African American) standing seven feet tall. The statue was created to complement the Wall and is located at the northwest corner of the memorial complex; Constitution Avenue NW between 21st and 23rd Streets, Washington, DC (38.891254, -77.047715); Open 24 hours, Rangers on duty 9:30 AM-10:00 PM daily [[Source: NPS Three Servicemen](https://www.nps.gov/places/000/vietnam-veterans-memorial-three-servicemen-statue.htm), [Source: Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Soldiers_(statue)), [Source: Washington.org](https://washington.org/DC-guide-to/vietnam-veterans-memorial)]

- <a id="activity-ranger-programs"></a>**Ranger-Led Interpretive Programs** (30-45 minutes) - Participate in informal educational programs and storytelling events presented by National Park Service rangers throughout the day. Programs provide historical context about the Vietnam War, the memorial's design and construction, and the service of American women during the conflict; Various times throughout the day, available upon request 9:30 AM-10:00 PM daily [[Source: NPS Vietnam Veterans Memorial](https://www.nps.gov/vive/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Source: Washington.org](https://washington.org/find-dc-listings/vietnam-womens-memorial)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-sculptor-details"></a>**Glenna Goodacre's Design Intent** (5-10 minutes) - Look closely at the sculpture to appreciate sculptor Glenna Goodacre's artistic intent emphasizing the women's emotions: "their compassion, their anxiety, their fatigue, and above all, their dedication." Goodacre, internationally renowned for her bronze work, also designed the Sacagawea dollar coin issued in 2000. Notice the deliberate absence of identifying insignia, symbolizing all women who served - military, medical, and civilian volunteers; Vietnam Women's Memorial site (38.891254, -77.047715) [[Source: Vietnam Women's Memorial Foundation](https://vietnamwomensmemorial.org/about-us/the-memorial/), [Source: Library of Congress](https://blogs.loc.gov/manuscripts/2022/11/the-vietnam-womens-memorial-representations-of-women-in-the-nations-public-memory/)]

- <a id="gem-yellowwood-trees"></a>**Eight Yellowwood Trees Memorial** (5 minutes) - Observe the eight yellowwood trees planted in the surrounding landscape by landscape architect George Dickie. Each tree represents one of the eight American servicewomen who died during the Vietnam War, creating a living memorial within the memorial; Grove surrounding Vietnam Women's Memorial (38.891254, -77.047715) [[Source: Trust for the National Mall](https://nationalmall.org/monuments-memorials/vietnam-womens-memorial), [Source: Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_Women's_Memorial)]

- <a id="gem-dedication-history"></a>**1993 Dedication Historical Context** (10-15 minutes research) - Learn about the memorial's challenging path to approval, which took years of lobbying by co-founder Diane Carlson Evans (former U.S. Army nurse) and dozens of meetings before Congress passed legislation in 1988. The memorial was dedicated on Veterans Day 1993 with approximately 25,000 people in attendance, including Vice President Al Gore and former Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff William J. Crowe; Available through on-site interpretive materials and ranger discussions [[Source: Vietnam Women's Memorial Foundation](https://vietnamwomensmemorial.org/about-us/dedication-of-the-memorial/), [Source: Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_Women's_Memorial)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-lincoln-memorial"></a>**Lincoln Memorial** (30-45 minutes, 0.2 miles / 4-minute walk) - Visit the iconic memorial to President Abraham Lincoln featuring the 19-foot marble statue and inscriptions of the Gettysburg Address and Second Inaugural Address. The memorial offers stunning views down the Reflecting Pool to the Washington Monument; 2 Lincoln Memorial Circle NW, Washington, DC 20037 (38.889272, -77.048963); Open 24 hours, Rangers on duty 9:30 AM-10:00 PM daily [[Source: NPS Lincoln Memorial](https://www.nps.gov/linc/learn/historyculture/memorial-features.htm), [Source: Washington.org](https://washington.org/DC-guide-to/vietnam-veterans-memorial)]

- <a id="nearby-korean-war"></a>**Korean War Veterans Memorial** (20-30 minutes, 0.3 miles / 6-minute walk) - Explore this memorial dedicated in 1995 consisting of 19 stainless steel statues representing a squad on patrol, with soldiers from each branch of the Armed Forces. Located south of the Reflecting Pool near the Lincoln Memorial; Daniel French Drive SW, Washington, DC 20024; Open 24 hours, Rangers on duty 9:30 AM-10:00 PM daily [[Source: NPS Korean War Memorial](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/korean-war-memorial.htm), [Source: Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/usa/washington-dc/national-mall/attractions/vietnam-veterans-memorial/a/poi-sig/385391/1329647)]

- <a id="nearby-constitution-gardens"></a>**Constitution Gardens** (20-30 minutes, immediate area) - Stroll through this 50-acre pastoral oasis on the National Mall featuring a lake with a small island containing the Memorial to the 56 Signers of the Declaration of Independence. The Vietnam Women's Memorial is located within Constitution Gardens; Constitution Avenue NW, Washington, DC; Open 24 hours [[Source: Dark Tourism](https://www.dark-tourism.com/index.php/729-vietnam-veterans-memorial-on-the-mall-washington-d-c), [Source: Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_Veterans_Memorial)]

- <a id="nearby-world-war-ii"></a>**World War II Memorial** (30-45 minutes, 0.5 miles / 10-minute walk) - Visit the memorial honoring the 16 million Americans who served in World War II, featuring 56 granite pillars representing U.S. states and territories, arranged around the Rainbow Pool. Located at the eastern end of the Reflecting Pool between the Lincoln Memorial and Washington Monument; 1750 Independence Avenue SW, Washington, DC 20024; Open 24 hours, Rangers on duty 9:30 AM-10:00 PM daily [[Source: Washington.org](https://washington.org/visit-dc/must-see-memorials-monuments-national-mall), [Source: NPS National Mall](https://www.nps.gov/places/000/lincoln-memorial-reflecting-pool.htm)]

- <a id="nearby-washington-monument"></a>**Washington Monument** (45-60 minutes with timed entry, 0.9 miles / 19-minute walk) - Ascend the 555-foot marble obelisk honoring George Washington for panoramic views of Washington, D.C. Free timed-entry tickets required (reserve in advance or obtain same-day tickets at the monument). The walk from the Lincoln Memorial along the Reflecting Pool is approximately half a mile; 2 15th Street NW, Washington, DC 20024; Daily: 9:00 AM-5:00 PM (hours vary seasonally) [[Source: MyPacer](https://www.mypacer.com/routes/216968/lincoln-memorial-reflecting-pool-walking-and-running-trail-washington-district-of-columbia-usa), [Source: Free Tours by Foot](https://freetoursbyfoot.com/lincoln-memorial-parking-and-metro/), [Source: Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Memorial_Reflecting_Pool)]

- <a id="nearby-mlk-memorial"></a>**Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial** (30-45 minutes, 0.7 miles / 15-minute walk) - Experience the 30-foot Stone of Hope sculpture featuring Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. emerging from the Mountain of Despair, surrounded by the Inscription Wall with quotes from his speeches and sermons. Located on the northwest corner of the Tidal Basin; 1964 Independence Avenue SW, Washington, DC 20024; Open 24 hours, Rangers on duty 9:30 AM-10:00 PM daily [[Source: NPS MLK Memorial](https://www.nps.gov/mlkm/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Source: Washington.org](https://washington.org/visit-dc/martin-luther-king-jr-memorial), [Source: Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Luther_King_Jr._Memorial)]

- <a id="nearby-fdr-memorial"></a>**Franklin Delano Roosevelt Memorial** (45-60 minutes, 1.0 miles / 20-minute walk) - Walk through four outdoor "rooms" representing FDR's four terms in office, featuring sculptures, waterfalls, and quotations from his speeches. Located along the Tidal Basin's southeastern shore; 1850 West Basin Drive SW, Washington, DC 20024; Open 24 hours, Rangers on duty 9:30 AM-10:00 PM daily [[Source: Monuments of the Tidal Basin](https://cherryblossomwatch.com/monuments-tidal-basin/), [Source: NPS Walk Around Tidal Basin](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/tidal-basin.htm)]

- <a id="nearby-jefferson-memorial"></a>**Thomas Jefferson Memorial** (30-45 minutes, 1.2 miles / 25-minute walk) - Visit the neoclassical domed memorial housing the 19-foot bronze statue of Thomas Jefferson, surrounded by excerpts from the Declaration of Independence and other writings. Located on the south shore of the Tidal Basin with beautiful views, especially during cherry blossom season; 16 East Basin Drive SW, Washington, DC 20024; Open 24 hours, Rangers on duty 9:30 AM-10:00 PM daily [[Source: NPS Tidal Basin](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/tidal-basin.htm), [Source: How Long Is The Walk Around The Tidal Basin](https://www.exoticplacestotravel.com/archives/8834)]

- <a id="nearby-smithsonian"></a>**Smithsonian Museums on National Mall** (2-4 hours each, 1.0-1.5 miles / 20-30 minute walk) - Explore eleven world-class Smithsonian museums located along the National Mall between 15th and 3rd Streets, including the National Museum of American History, National Air and Space Museum, National Museum of Natural History, and National Gallery of Art. All offer free admission; Various locations on Constitution Avenue NW and Independence Avenue SW; Most museums open daily 10:00 AM-5:30 PM (closed December 25) [[Source: Smithsonian Museums](https://www.si.edu/visit/museums), [Source: Washington DC Wikivoyage](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C./National_Mall)]
