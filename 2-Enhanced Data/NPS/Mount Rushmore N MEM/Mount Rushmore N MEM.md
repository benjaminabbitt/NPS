# Mount Rushmore N MEM (National Memorial)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-visitor-center"></a>**Lincoln Borglum Visitor Center** (13000 Highway 244 Building 31, Suite 1, Keystone, SD 57751 (43.8791, -103.4591); Open 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM late May through mid-August, 8:00 AM - 9:00 PM mid-August through September 30, 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM October 1-31, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM November through late May, closed December 25; 605-574-2523) - The primary location for NPS passport cancellation stamps. Contact the visitor center to confirm current stamp availability before visiting. [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/moru/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/moru/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** There is a $10 parking fee per vehicle ($5 for visitors age 62+, free for active duty military). The parking pass is valid for one year. [Source: [NPS Fees](https://www.nps.gov/moru/planyourvisit/fees.htm)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-grand-view"></a>**Grand View Terrace Viewing** (30 minutes) - The main viewing area of Mount Rushmore, offering the first closer and unobstructed view of the carved faces of Washington, Jefferson, Roosevelt, and Lincoln; this is the most popular spot for photos and is located above the Museum at 13000 SD-244, Keystone, SD 57751; open during visitor facility hours (8:00 AM - 10:00 PM during peak season, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM November through late May). Many visitors don't realize they are standing on the roof of the Lincoln Borglum Visitors Center when viewing from this terrace. [Source: [NPS Grand View Terrace](https://www.nps.gov/places/grand-view-terrace.htm), [Visit Rapid City](https://www.visitrapidcity.com/blog/post/enhancements-and-experiences-many-visitors-overlook-at-mount-rushmore/)]

- <a id="activity-presidential-trail"></a>**Presidential Trail Loop** (0.5-2 hours) - A 0.6-mile walking trail and boardwalk that starts at Grand View Terrace and winds through Ponderosa pine forests to provide close-up views of the memorial from different angles; the final 0.4 miles includes 422 stairs with 131 feet of elevation gain; a stair-free accessible route is available for the first 0.2 miles; trail is at 13000 SD-244, Keystone, SD 57751; open year-round weather permitting, though frequently closed during winter when covered with snow and ice. Most visitors take 1-1.5 hours depending on pace and photo stops. [Source: [AllTrails Presidential Trail](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/south-dakota/mount-rushmore-and-presidential-trail-loop), [NPS Presidential Trail](https://www.nps.gov/places/presidential-trail.htm), [Black Hills Hiking](https://www.blackhillshikingbikingandmore.com/the-presidential-trail-at-mount-rushmore)]

- <a id="activity-lincoln-borglum"></a>**Lincoln Borglum Visitor Center and Museum** (30-45 minutes) - Features two 125-seat theaters showing a 13-minute movie about Mount Rushmore, museum exhibits about the memorial's creation and history, bookstore, and park information; located at 13000 Highway 244 Building 31, Suite 1, Keystone, SD 57751; same hours as visitor facilities. Only about 35% of visitors find the Visitor Center. [Source: [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/places/lincoln-borglum-visitor-center.htm), [Miles with McConkey](https://www.mileswithmcconkey.com/how-to-visit-mount-rushmore-what-you-need-to-know/), [Visit Rapid City](https://www.visitrapidcity.com/blog/post/enhancements-and-experiences-many-visitors-overlook-at-mount-rushmore/)]

- <a id="activity-sculptors-studio"></a>**Sculptor's Studio** (15-30 minutes) - Built by Gutzon Borglum, features discussion about the construction of the monument and the tools used; located at the base of the Presidential Trail at Mount Rushmore; open seasonally late May through September 30, 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM during peak season (May 23 - August 9), weather and staffing permitting; wheelchair accessible; rangers present 15-minute talks daily at 9 AM, 10 AM, 11 AM, 12 PM, 1 PM, 2 PM, 3 PM, 4 PM, and 5 PM focusing on the workers who helped create Mount Rushmore. [Source: [NPS Sculptor's Studio](https://www.nps.gov/places/sculptor-s-studio.htm), [Mt. Rushmore Memorial](https://www.mtrushmorenationalmemorial.com/experiences/sculptors-studio/)]

- <a id="activity-evening-ceremony"></a>**Evening Lighting Ceremony** (45 minutes) - A ranger-led program including a ranger talk, short film, sculpture illumination, and veteran's ceremony held in the outdoor amphitheater at 13000 SD-244, Keystone, SD 57751; runs May 23 - August 9, 2025 at 9:00 PM, August 10 - September 30, 2025 at 8:00 PM; sculpture remains illuminated until 11:00 PM; free to attend. Arrive 15 minutes early for seating, though arriving 1-2 hours early during peak season is recommended for good seats. [Source: [NPS Evening Lighting](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/event-details.htm?id=EE65CD8A-B9FB-620A-7AA4BAA9D098928F), [Travel South Dakota](https://www.travelsouthdakota.com/keystone/events/state-park-events/mount-rushmore-evening-lighting-ceremony), [Under Canvas](https://www.undercanvas.com/blog/mount-rushmore-lighting-ceremony-all-you-need-to-know/)]

- <a id="activity-ranger-talks"></a>**Ranger Talks** (30 minutes) - Interpretive programs on historical or cultural topics related to the park; all talks start at the Grand View Terrace at 13000 SD-244, Keystone, SD 57751; schedule dependent on available staff; offered daily during summer season (May 23 - September 30); check at Visitor Center for current times. [Source: [NPS Ranger Programs](https://www.nps.gov/moru/planyourvisit/ranger-programs.htm)]

- <a id="activity-audio-tour"></a>**Audio Tour** (1-2 hours) - Self-guided audio tour with 29 tour stops located around the park that visitors can experience at their own pace; available in English, Spanish, German, French, and Lakota; audio-described tour available in English for sight-impaired visitors; costs $6-$8 depending on tour type; available at the visitor center at 13000 Highway 244 Building 31, Suite 1, Keystone, SD 57751 during operating hours. [Source: [NPS Self-Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/moru/planyourvisit/self-guided-tours.htm), [Mount Rushmore Society](https://mountrushmoresociety.com/visit/self-guided-tours/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="hidden-heritage-village"></a>**Lakota, Nakota, and Dakota Heritage Village** (30-45 minutes) - Features Lakota interpreters, storytellers, and musicians sharing the history and beauty of Native American culture; the memorial is located on Lakota, Nakota, and Dakota land on a mountain originally known to the Lakota as the Six Grandfathers; found along the Nature Trail at Mount Rushmore; open 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday during summer season. [Source: [NPS Heritage Village](https://www.nps.gov/places/youth-exploration-area-and-lakota-nakota-and-dakota-heritage-village-summer-only.htm), [Visit Rapid City](https://www.visitrapidcity.com/blog/post/enhancements-and-experiences-many-visitors-overlook-at-mount-rushmore/)]

- <a id="hidden-blackberry-trail"></a>**Blackberry Trail** (1-2 hours for 2-mile round trip) - The only traditional dirt trail at Mount Rushmore and the only trail that allows pets; offers a way to escape crowds and provides another spectacular view of Mount Rushmore while enjoying the beauty of the Black Hills; 1 mile one-way (not a loop) trail connects to the 111-mile Centennial Trail; mainly used by equestrians; trailhead located directly across from the parking lot at Mount Rushmore; open year-round. This trail is fairly unknown despite its proximity to one of the biggest tourist spots in the Black Hills. [Source: [Visit Rapid City](https://www.visitrapidcity.com/blog/post/enhancements-and-experiences-many-visitors-overlook-at-mount-rushmore/), [Tranquil Trekker](https://tranquiltrekker.com/blackberry-trail-hiking-the-black-elk-wilderness/), [Dirt in My Shoes](https://www.dirtinmyshoes.com/the-best-easy-hikes-at-mount-rushmore/)]

- <a id="hidden-nature-trail"></a>**Nature Trail** (15-20 minutes) - A short, not-well-publicized trail that allows visitors to walk through Ponderosa Pine trees in solitude even during peak season while the rest of the memorial is crowded; connects the Sculptor's Studio to the parking garage at Mount Rushmore; open during visitor facility hours. [Source: [Visit Rapid City](https://www.visitrapidcity.com/blog/post/enhancements-and-experiences-many-visitors-overlook-at-mount-rushmore/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-crazy-horse"></a>**Crazy Horse Memorial** (2-3 hours, 17 miles/30 minutes southwest) - An ongoing mountain carving of Lakota warrior Crazy Horse, the world's largest mountain carving in progress; includes an orientation film, 3 museums, cultural events, and views of the mountain carving; bus rides to the base of the carving available weather permitting; located at 12151 Avenue of the Chiefs, Crazy Horse, SD 57730 (43.8366, -103.6234); admission $15 for 1 person, $30 for 2 people, $35 for 3+ people per car (May 25 - September 30, 2025), $12/$24/$30 (October 1, 2025 - May 24, 2026); Welcome Center open 9 AM - 8 PM during peak season. [Source: [Crazy Horse Memorial](https://crazyhorsememorial.org/plan-your-visit/hours-and-schedule), [We're in the Rockies](https://wereintherockies.com/crazy-horse-mount-rushmore-in-a-day/), [GPS Coordinates](https://3planeta.com/coordinates/places-to-visit/crazy-horse-memorial.html/)]

- <a id="nearby-custer-wildlife"></a>**Custer State Park Wildlife Loop Road** (1.5-2 hours, within 18 miles via Iron Mountain Road) - An 18-mile U-shaped scenic byway known for exceptional wildlife viewing including bison, pronghorn, deer, elk, burros, and prairie dogs; best viewing times are early morning or late evening; starts at the east entrance near Custer State Park Visitor Center on Highway 16A and goes to the Blue Bell entrance on the park's west side at 13329 US Highway 16A, Custer, SD 57730 (43.7617, -103.3681); requires South Dakota State Park Entrance License; open 24 hours per day year-round. [Source: [SD Game, Fish, and Parks](https://gfp.sd.gov/csp-scenic-drives/), [Custer Resorts](https://www.custerresorts.com/activities/scenic-drives/wildlife-loop), [Under Canvas](https://www.undercanvas.com/blog/guide-to-driving-the-custer-state-park-wildlife-loop/)]

- <a id="nearby-needles-highway"></a>**Needles Highway** (1 hour drive, 18 miles/30+ minutes from Mount Rushmore) - A 14-mile scenic drive featuring narrow tunnels, granite spires rising like needles above the forest canopy, and spectacular overlooks; located within Custer State Park; take Highway 87; approximately 30 minutes or longer to reach from Mount Rushmore, with expected travel time of one hour to drive due to winding roads; requires South Dakota State Park Entrance License. [Source: [Six Suitcase Travel](https://sixsuitcasetravel.com/blog/custer-state-park-iron-mountain-road-and-needles-highway/), [We're in the Rockies](https://wereintherockies.com/needles-highway-south-dakota/), [SD Game, Fish, and Parks](https://gfp.sd.gov/csp-scenic-drives/)]

- <a id="nearby-iron-mountain"></a>**Iron Mountain Road** (45 minutes drive, 18 miles connecting Custer State Park to Mount Rushmore) - An 18-mile scenic drive along US Highway 16A North featuring winding roads, narrow tunnels with framed views of Mount Rushmore, and pigtail bridges; takes around 45 minutes to drive due to very winding nature; directly connects Mount Rushmore to Custer State Park. [Source: [Custer Resorts](https://www.custerresorts.com/activities/scenic-drives/iron-mountain-road), [Six Suitcase Travel](https://sixsuitcasetravel.com/blog/custer-state-park-iron-mountain-road-and-needles-highway/), [AllBlackHills](https://www.allblackhills.com/scenic_drives/iron_mountain_road.php)]
