# George Washington Carver NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **George Washington Carver Visitor Center** (5646 Carver Road, Diamond, MO 64840 (36.9817, -94.3553); Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 417-325-4151) - National Park Service passport cancellation stamp available at museum gift shop. [Source: [Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/george-washington-carver-national-monument-diamond), [Midwest Nomads](https://midwestnomads.com/2023/04/01/exploring-george-washington-carver-national-monument/)]

**Note:** Call 417-325-4151 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. First national monument dedicated to an African American and first to a non-president. Located 2 miles west of Diamond, approximately 10 miles southeast of Joplin. FREE admission and parking.

## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center Museum** (45-60 minutes) - Explore three floors of exhibits about George Washington Carver's life, contributions to science, and legacy; includes interactive Discovery area for kids, model classroom and laboratory, observation deck, and gift shop; 5646 Carver Road, Diamond, MO 64840; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; FREE [Source: [Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Visit Missouri](https://www.visitmo.com/things-to-do/george-washington-carver-national-monument), [Park Chasers 9 Things](https://www.parkchasers.com/2020/04/9-things-to-see-at-george-washington-carver-national-monument/)]

- **Park Film: "Struggle and Triumph"** (26-28 minutes) - Watch "The Legacy of George Washington Carver" film providing excellent introduction to Carver's story, showing before starting trail walk; screened in visitor center theater; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; FREE [Source: [Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60830-d281981-Reviews-George_Washington_Carver_National_Monument-Diamond_Missouri.html)]

- **Carver Trail** (45-60 minutes) - Walk 0.75-1 mile easy loop trail winding around Carver property visiting birthplace site, Moses Carver home (1881 replica), Carver family cemetery, and Williams Pond; paved portions available for accessibility; accessed from visitor center; trail open daily dawn to dusk; FREE [Source: [Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Park Chasers](https://www.parkchasers.com/2020/04/9-things-to-see-at-george-washington-carver-national-monument/), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/george-washington-carver-national-monument/)]

- **Ranger-Guided Trail Tours** (1 hour 15 minutes) - Join National Park Service rangers for guided interpretive tours of Carver Trail with in-depth stories about Carver's childhood, family, and early influences that shaped the great scientist; tours offered daily at 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM; meet at visitor center; FREE [Source: [Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Midwest Nomads](https://midwestnomads.com/2023/04/01/exploring-george-washington-carver-national-monument/)]

- **Carver Birthplace Site** (15-20 minutes) - View reconstructed wooden frame showing likely location where George Washington Carver was born into slavery around 1864; includes interpretive signage about his early life; accessed via Carver Trail; trail open dawn to dusk; FREE [Source: [Park Chasers](https://www.parkchasers.com/2020/04/9-things-to-see-at-george-washington-carver-national-monument/), [Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Moses Carver House** (15-20 minutes) - Visit replica of 1881 home where Moses and Susan Carver raised George Washington Carver after his mother was kidnapped; representative of type of home the family would have lived in; accessed via Carver Trail; trail open dawn to dusk; FREE [Source: [Park Chasers](https://www.parkchasers.com/2020/04/9-things-to-see-at-george-washington-carver-national-monument/), [Midwest Nomads](https://midwestnomads.com/2023/04/01/exploring-george-washington-carver-national-monument/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Contemplative Loop Trail Around Williams Pond** (20-30 minutes) - Walk short peaceful meditation trail circling Williams Pond designed for reflection on Carver's legacy and connection to nature; quiet alternative to main Carver Trail; accessed from Carver Trail; dawn to dusk; FREE [Source: [Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Park Chasers](https://www.parkchasers.com/2020/04/9-things-to-see-at-george-washington-carver-national-monument/)]

- **Carver Family Cemetery** (10-15 minutes) - Pay respects at small cemetery where members of the Carver family are buried; quiet contemplative spot with historical significance; accessed via Carver Trail; dawn to dusk; FREE [Source: [Park Chasers](https://www.parkchasers.com/2020/04/9-things-to-see-at-george-washington-carver-national-monument/), [Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (45-60 minutes) - Children complete activity booklet learning about George Washington Carver's life and contributions to earn official Junior Ranger badge; booklets available at visitor center; complete independently while exploring park; FREE [Source: [Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Special Events and Programs** (varies) - Attend seasonal special events throughout year celebrating Carver's legacy including Black History Month programs, National Park Week activities, and Carver Day celebrations; check park calendar for current schedule; 5646 Carver Road; times vary; FREE [Source: [Calendar](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/calendar.htm)]
