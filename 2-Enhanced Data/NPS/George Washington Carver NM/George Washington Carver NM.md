# George Washington Carver NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **George Washington Carver Visitor Center** (5646 Carver Road, Diamond, MO 64840; 36.9872, -94.3552; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; Phone: 417-325-4151) - Passport stamps available at the visitor center information desk. The monument is closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day. Established in 1943, this was the first unit of the National Park Service dedicated to an African American. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

**Note:** Free admission. Call 417-325-4151 to confirm current hours and guided tour availability.

## Key Activities

- **Carver Trail Self-Guided Walk** (45 minutes - 1.5 hours) - Walk the one-mile nature loop trail through woodlands and restored prairie to visit significant sites from Carver's childhood, including his birthplace site, the 1881 Moses Carver house (where young George lived), the family cemetery with historic graves, and the famous Boy Carver Statue depicting young George with a plant and frog. Interpretive signs along the trail explain Carver's remarkable journey from enslaved infant to world-renowned scientist. 5646 Carver Road, Diamond, MO 64840; Trail accessible during park hours. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/index.htm), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Ranger-Guided Trail Tours** (1 hour 15 minutes) - Join rangers for in-depth guided tours of the Carver Trail offered daily at 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM. Rangers share stories of Carver's life, his scientific achievements, and the Moses and Susan Carver family who raised him. Tours cover the birthplace site, historic buildings, and natural areas that influenced the "Plant Doctor." 5646 Carver Road, Diamond, MO 64840; Daily 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Museum Exhibits** (45 minutes - 1 hour) - Explore three floors of interactive exhibits about George Washington Carver's life, scientific work, and legacy. Displays cover his groundbreaking agricultural research with peanuts, sweet potatoes, and soil conservation, his art and music, and his philosophy of service to others. The museum tells the story from his birth into slavery through his death in 1943. 5646 Carver Road, Diamond, MO 64840; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/index.htm), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **"Struggle and Triumph" Film** (28 minutes) - Watch the documentary film chronicling Carver's remarkable life from his kidnapping as an infant and return to the Moses Carver farm, through his education and teaching career at Tuskegee Institute, to his lasting scientific legacy. The film provides essential context for understanding the exhibits and trail. Visitor Center theater; Daily during operating hours. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Discovery Area Interactive Exhibits** (30-45 minutes) - The Discovery Area offers hands-on interactive exhibits designed especially for children but engaging for all ages. Activities explore Carver's scientific methods, plant biology, and creativity. An excellent way to introduce young visitors to science and history. Visitor Center; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (30-60 minutes) - Complete activities in the Junior Ranger booklet to learn about George Washington Carver's life, his scientific discoveries, and his connection to nature. Upon completion, participants earn a Junior Ranger badge. Available year-round at the visitor center. 5646 Carver Road, Diamond, MO 64840; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Observation Deck** (15-20 minutes) - Take in panoramic views of the monument grounds and surrounding Ozark landscape from the observation deck in the visitor center. The elevated vantage point provides perspective on the farm where Carver spent his early childhood. Visitor Center; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Moses Carver House** (15-20 minutes) - Visit the 1881 walnut log house where George Washington Carver lived as a child after returning from the kidnapping that killed his mother. The modest home was built by Moses Carver, the German immigrant farmer who raised George and his brother Jim. Along Carver Trail; accessible during park hours. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Carver Family Cemetery** (10-15 minutes) - Pay respects at the small family cemetery on the monument grounds containing the graves of Moses and Susan Carver, who raised George as their own son after emancipation. The quiet site provides a contemplative connection to the family that shaped Carver's early life. Along Carver Trail; accessible during park hours. [Source: [NPS History](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/learn/historyculture/index.htm)]

- **Prairie Restoration Area** (20-30 minutes) - Explore the restored native prairie on the monument grounds. Carver's lifelong love of plants began on this Missouri farm, and the prairie features native species he would have encountered as a child. Seasonal wildflowers bloom from spring through fall. Along Carver Trail; accessible during park hours. [Source: [NPS Nature](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/learn/nature/index.htm)]

- **Special Event Days** (varies) - The monument hosts special programming throughout the year:
  - Junior Ranger Day (April 19, 10 AM - 3 PM)
  - Carver Day Celebration (July 12, 10 AM - 3 PM)
  - Storytelling Day (August 23, 11 AM - 1 PM)
  - Prairie Day (September 13, 10 AM - 3 PM)
  - Holiday Open House (December 13, 1 PM - 3 PM)
  All events are free. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Joplin Museum Complex** (1.5-2 hours, 20 miles west) - Features mineral collections showcasing the Tri-State Mining District's lead and zinc heritage, plus regional history and art exhibits. The area's mining history provides context for the economy when Carver lived here. 504 S Schifferdecker Ave, Joplin, MO 64801; Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM. [Source: [Joplin Museum Complex](https://www.joplinmuseum.org/)]

- **Bonnie and Clyde Hideout** (45 minutes - 1 hour, 20 miles west) - The restored apartment where the infamous Depression-era outlaws hid out in 1933 after a shootout, now a museum with period furnishings and exhibits about their criminal exploits. 3347½ S Oak Ridge Dr, Joplin, MO 64804; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; Admission charged. [Source: [Bonnie and Clyde Hideout](https://bonnieandclydehideout.com/)]

- **Route 66 Mural Park** (30-45 minutes, 20 miles west in Joplin) - Outdoor murals celebrating Joplin's position along historic Route 66. The Mother Road passed through the Ozarks region during its heyday. Main Street, Joplin, MO; Accessible 24/7; Free. [Source: [Visit Joplin](https://www.visitjoplinmo.com/)]

- **Precious Moments Chapel** (1-1.5 hours, 30 miles north in Carthage) - Features artwork by Samuel J. Butcher, creator of Precious Moments figurines, including a Sistine Chapel-style painted ceiling. The chapel and surrounding gardens draw visitors from around the world. 4321 S Chapel Rd, Carthage, MO 64836; Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM - 5:00 PM; Free admission. [Source: [Precious Moments Chapel](https://preciousmoments.com/chapel)]

## Total Recommended Time

**Total recommended visit time for George Washington Carver NM: 2-3 hours**

This includes 28 minutes for the orientation film, 45 minutes to 1 hour for the museum exhibits, and 45 minutes to 1.5 hours for the Carver Trail (self-guided or ranger-led). The ranger-guided tours at 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM add approximately 1 hour 15 minutes if joining one. The site is free to enter and open daily except major holidays.

[Sources: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/index.htm), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/gwca/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]
