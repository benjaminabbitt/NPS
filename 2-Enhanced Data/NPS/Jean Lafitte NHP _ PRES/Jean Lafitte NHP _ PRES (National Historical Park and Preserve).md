# Jean Lafitte NHP & PRES (National Historical Park and Preserve)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

Jean Lafitte NHP & PRES consists of six separate sites across southern Louisiana, with passport cancellation stamps available at each location:

- **French Quarter Visitor Center** - 419 Decatur St, New Orleans, LA 70130 (29.9574, -90.0632); Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM, closed Monday, Sunday, federal holidays, and Mardi Gras; 504-589-2636 [Source: [NPS Jean Lafitte Contacts](https://www.nps.gov/jela/contacts.htm), [NPS French Quarter Site](https://www.nps.gov/jela/planyourvisit/french-quarter-site.htm)]

- **Barataria Preserve Visitor Center** - 6588 Barataria Blvd, Marrero, LA 70072 (29.7839, -90.1155); Visitor Center hours: Wednesday-Sunday 9:30 AM - 4:30 PM, closed federal holidays and Mardi Gras; Trails access: Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; 504-689-3690 [Source: [NPS Barataria Preserve](https://www.nps.gov/jela/planyourvisit/barataria-preserve.htm), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/jela/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- **Chalmette Battlefield Visitor Center** - 8606 West St. Bernard Highway, Chalmette, LA 70043 (29.9451, -89.9861); 7 days/week 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM, closed Mardi Gras; 504-281-0510 [Source: [NPS Chalmette Battlefield](https://www.nps.gov/jela/planyourvisit/chalmette-battlefield.htm)]

- **Acadian Cultural Center (Lafayette)** - 501 Fisher Rd, Lafayette, LA 70508 (30.2167, -92.0333); Temporarily relocated to Eunice due to air conditioning issues; services available at Prairie Acadian Cultural Center [Source: [NPS Acadian Cultural Center Lafayette](https://www.nps.gov/jela/planyourvisit/new-acadian-cultural-center.htm)]

- **Prairie Acadian Cultural Center (Eunice)** - Currently hosting Lafayette services; Tuesday-Saturday varying hours (Tuesday-Wednesday, Friday-Saturday: 9:30 AM - 4:30 PM; Thursday: 12:00 PM - 7:00 PM) [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/jela/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- **Wetlands Acadian Cultural Center (Thibodaux)** - 314 St. Mary Street, Thibodaux, LA 70301 (29.7942, -90.8164); Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; Free admission [Source: [NPS Wetlands Acadian Cultural Center](https://www.nps.gov/jela/planyourvisit/wetlands-acadian-cultural-center.htm), [TripAdvisor Wetlands](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g40459-d109472-Reviews-Wetlands_Acadian_Cultural_Center-Thibodaux_Louisiana.html)]

**Note:** Call visitor centers to confirm current stamp locations and hours before visiting, as hours may vary by season and some facilities have been affected by recent maintenance issues.

## Key Activities

- **Barataria Preserve Boardwalk Trails** (1.5-2 hours) - Walk through cypress swamps on well-maintained boardwalk trails including Visitor Center Trail (0.25 miles), Palmetto Trail (0.9 miles, currently closed indefinitely due to structural failures), Bayou Coquille Trail (0.5 miles), and Marsh Overlook Trail (0.4 miles); spot alligators, turtles, egrets, herons, eagles, and over 200 species of birds; trails are either boardwalk or gravel-covered, making most wheelchair and stroller accessible; 6588 Barataria Blvd, Marrero, LA 70072; Parking lot gates open daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (closed Mardi Gras) [Source: [NPS Barataria Trails](https://www.nps.gov/jela/planyourvisit/barataria-preserve-trails-and-waterways.htm), [AllTrails Palmetto Bayou Coquille](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/louisiana/palmetto-bayou-coquille-and-marsh-overlook), [TripAdvisor Jean Lafitte](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60864-d210266-Reviews-Jean_Lafitte_National_Historical_Park_and_Preserve-New_Orleans_Louisiana.html)]

- **Chalmette Battlefield Walking Tour** (1.5-2 hours) - Self-guided tour of the January 8, 1815 Battle of New Orleans site via a 1.8-mile walking loop; view reconstructed American rampart, 1830s Malus-Beauregard House, and outdoor exhibits; interpretive panels throughout; 8606 West St. Bernard Highway, Chalmette, LA 70043; 7 days/week 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM (closed Mardi Gras) [Source: [TripAdvisor Chalmette Battlefield](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g40073-d15198054-Reviews-Chalmette_Battlefield-Chalmette_Louisiana.html), [UponArriving Chalmette](https://www.uponarriving.com/chalmette-battlefield/), [American Battlefield Trust](https://www.battlefields.org/visit/battlefields/chalmette)]

- **Climb Chalmette Monument** (15-30 minutes) - Ascend 122 steps to the top of the 100-foot-tall monument for panoramic views of the battlefield and surrounding area; 8606 West St. Bernard Highway, Chalmette, LA 70043; 7 days/week 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM [Source: [TripAdvisor Chalmette Battlefield](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g40073-d15198054-Reviews-Chalmette_Battlefield-Chalmette_Louisiana.html), [UponArriving Chalmette](https://www.uponarriving.com/chalmette-battlefield/)]

- **French Quarter Ranger-Led Walking Tour** (60-90 minutes) - Free guided tour of the French Quarter led by National Park Service rangers; 25 free tickets per tour given out beginning at 9:00 AM first-come, first-served (each person must pick up their own ticket); 419 Decatur St, New Orleans, LA 70130; Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM [Source: [TripAdvisor French Quarter Tour](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g60864-d210266-r62686023-Jean_Lafitte_National_Historical_Park_and_Preserve-New_Orleans_Louisiana.html), [NPS French Quarter Site](https://www.nps.gov/jela/planyourvisit/french-quarter-site.htm)]

- **Kayak or Canoe Rental at Barataria Preserve** (4 hours) - Self-guided paddling through the wetlands to spot alligators, egrets, herons, eagles, and other wildlife; rentals available approximately 30 minutes south of New Orleans right outside the entrance to Barataria Preserve; rental operators include Swamp Explorers; 6588 Barataria Blvd area, Marrero, LA 70072 [Source: [Swamp Explorers](https://swampexplorers.com/), [TripAdvisor Swamp Explorers](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g40303-d10162612-Reviews-Swamp_Explorers-Marrero_Louisiana.html), [Paddling.com Jean Lafitte](https://paddling.com/paddle/trips/jean-lafitte-preserve-louisiana-1)]

- **Wetlands Acadian Cultural Center Film and Exhibits** (30-60 minutes) - Watch a thirty-minute film on the history of the Cajuns; view permanent and special exhibits; interactive playroom for children; art gallery featuring Louisiana artists; craft demonstrations and changing art exhibits; 314 St. Mary Street, Thibodaux, LA 70301; Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Wetlands Acadian Cultural Center](https://www.nps.gov/jela/planyourvisit/wetlands-acadian-cultural-center.htm), [LA Cajun Bayou](https://www.lacajunbayou.com/things-to-see-and-do/tours-and-attractions/jean-lafitte-national-historical-park-and-preserve-wetlands-acadian-cultural-center)]

- **Acadian Cultural Center Programs** (30-90 minutes) - Ranger programs, films, exhibits, and events sharing local Cajun traditions including music, storytelling, dance, and food; portable media players with guided museum tours in French, Spanish, and English available; Junior Ranger program for kids; 501 Fisher Rd, Lafayette, LA 70508 (currently relocated to Eunice) [Source: [Lafayette Travel Acadian Center](https://www.lafayettetravel.com/listing/jean-lafitte-national-historical-park-&-preserve-acadian-cultural-center/1357/), [NPS Acadian Cultural Center Lafayette](https://www.nps.gov/jela/planyourvisit/new-acadian-cultural-center.htm)]

- **Ranger-Led Boat Tours** (varies) - Seasonal boat tours on Bayou Lafourche narrated by park rangers; tours to Madewood Plantation House or E.D. White Historic Site available from Wetlands Acadian Cultural Center; spring and fall boat tours from Acadian Cultural Center in Lafayette; check with visitor centers for current schedules; Free for most programs [Source: [NPS Wetlands Acadian Cultural Center](https://www.nps.gov/jela/planyourvisit/wetlands-acadian-cultural-center.htm), [Lafayette Travel](https://www.lafayettetravel.com/listing/jean-lafitte-national-historical-park-&-preserve-acadian-cultural-center/1357/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **French Quarter Visitor Center Hidden Courtyard** (15-30 minutes) - Quiet oasis featuring a bricked alley with historical information leading to a courtyard with fountain, plants, and benches; live piano performances occasionally scheduled; pick up weekly events schedule including concerts and speakers; 419 Decatur St, New Orleans, LA 70130; Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM [Source: [National Parks Data Guide](https://nationalparksdata.com/a-visitors-guide-to-jean-lafitte-national-historical-park-and-preserve/), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/jean-lafitte-national-historical-park-preserve/)]

- **Chalmette National Cemetery** (30-45 minutes) - Historic cemetery adjacent to Chalmette Battlefield; established for Union soldiers who died in the Civil War; provides peaceful grounds for reflection and photography among mature live oak trees; 8606 West St. Bernard Highway, Chalmette, LA 70043; 7 days/week 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM [Source: [Coastal Wanderer Chalmette](https://coastalwanderer.net/chalmette-battlefield-and-national-cemetery), [NPS Chalmette Battlefield](https://www.nps.gov/jela/planyourvisit/chalmette-battlefield.htm)]

- **Prairie Acadian Cultural Center Saturday Night Events** (2-3 hours) - Saturday night "Cajun Grand Ole Opry" featuring music, dancing, and craft demonstrations; exhibits and cultural presentations; currently hosting services from Lafayette center; check for current schedule; Eunice location; varying hours [Source: [The Armchair Explorer](https://www.thearmchairexplorer.com/louisiana/jean-lafitte-national-historical-park.php)]

- **Barataria Preserve Short Boardwalk Behind Visitor Center** (15-20 minutes) - Quick 0.25-mile boardwalk trail accessible directly from the visitor center; excellent for visitors with limited time or mobility; can extend to Palmetto Trail for longer hike to overlook (when reopened); 6588 Barataria Blvd, Marrero, LA 70072; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [Say Sarah Barataria](https://saysarah.org/usa-travel/barataria-preserve-stroll-through-the-louisiana-swamps/), [NPS Barataria Trails](https://www.nps.gov/jela/planyourvisit/barataria-preserve-trails-and-waterways.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **The National World War II Museum** (3-4 hours, 2 miles from French Quarter) - Designated by Congress as the official WWII museum for the United States; immersive exhibits across multiple pavilions covering all aspects of World War II including aircraft displays, interactive experiences, and historical artifacts; 945 Magazine Street, New Orleans, LA 70130; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (closed Mardi Gras, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day); Admission charged [Source: [US News WWII Museum](https://travel.usnews.com/New_Orleans_LA/Things_To_Do/National_World_War_II_Museum_National_D_Day_Museum_34078/), [Lonely Planet WWII Museum](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/usa/new-orleans/cbd-and-warehouse-district/attractions/national-wwii-museum/a/poi-sig/381219/1329659)]

- **Audubon Zoo** (3-5 hours, 4 miles from French Quarter) - 58-acre zoo housing over 2,000 animals; Louisiana Swamp exhibit with native species; various themed areas; 6500 Magazine Street, New Orleans, LA 70118; Thursday-Monday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM (closed Tuesday-Wednesday, Mardi Gras, Thanksgiving, Christmas); Last entry 4:30 PM; Admission charged [Source: [Audubon Zoo](https://audubonnatureinstitute.org/zoo), [TripAdvisor Audubon Zoo](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60864-d104457-Reviews-Audubon_Zoo-New_Orleans_Louisiana.html)]

- **New Orleans City Park and Botanical Garden** (1-2 hours, 4 miles from French Quarter) - Historic city park featuring the New Orleans Botanical Garden with the Helis Foundation Enrique Alférez Sculpture Garden; Sydney and Walda Besthoff Sculpture Garden at New Orleans Museum of Art with over 60 sculptures among mature live oak trees and lagoons; Botanical Garden at 5 Victory Avenue, New Orleans, LA 70124; Botanical Garden: Daily 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM (last entry 4:00 PM), free for Louisiana residents on Wednesdays; Besthoff Sculpture Garden: Monday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [New Orleans City Park Botanical Garden](https://neworleanscitypark.org/visit-city-park/botanical-garden/), [TripAdvisor New Orleans Botanical Gardens](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60864-d561882-Reviews-New_Orleans_Botanical_Gardens-New_Orleans_Louisiana.html), [Yelp Besthoff Sculpture Garden](https://www.yelp.com/biz/the-sydney-and-walda-besthoff-sculpture-garden-new-orleans)]

- **Oak Alley Plantation** (1-2 hours, 60 minutes from New Orleans) - Historic plantation with iconic alley of 28 massive live oak trees; guided tours of the 1839 Greek Revival mansion; exhibits on slavery and plantation history; located on Great River Road; 3645 Highway 18, Vacherie, LA 70090; Check website for current hours and tours; Admission charged [Source: [Marquita's Travels Plantations](https://www.marquitastravels.com/plantations-near-new-orleans/), [Oak Alley Plantation](https://oakalleyplantation.com/new-orleans-plantation-tours/)]

- **Laura Plantation** (1-2 hours, 60 minutes from New Orleans) - Creole plantation offering historically accurate tours focused on the lives of enslaved people and Creole culture; birthplace of the Br'er Rabbit folktales; 2247 Highway 18, Vacherie, LA 70090; Daily 9:30 AM - 3:20 PM; Tours in English every 40 minutes starting at 10:00 AM; Located within 3 miles of Oak Alley Plantation on Great River Road; Admission charged [Source: [Marquita's Travels Plantations](https://www.marquitastravels.com/plantations-near-new-orleans/), [A Journey We Love Plantations](https://ajourneywelove.com/plantations-near-new-orleans/)]

- **Destrehan Plantation** (1-2 hours, 35 minutes from New Orleans) - Oldest documented plantation home in the lower Mississippi Valley; closest plantation to New Orleans Airport; tours start at 9:30 AM; 13034 River Road, Destrehan, LA 70047; Check website for current hours; Admission charged [Source: [Marquita's Travels Plantations](https://www.marquitastravels.com/plantations-near-new-orleans/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 6-8 hours to visit the primary sites most accessible from New Orleans (French Quarter Visitor Center, Barataria Preserve, and Chalmette Battlefield). Allow 1-2 days for comprehensive coverage of all six park sites including the Acadian Cultural Centers.
