# Arlington House, Robert E. Lee MEM (Memorial)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Arlington House** (Located within Arlington National Cemetery, accessed through cemetery entrance, Arlington, VA 22211 (38.8821953, -77.0734759); September-May: 9:30 AM-4:30 PM, June-August: 9:00 AM-4:30 PM, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 703-235-1530)
- **George Washington Memorial Parkway Headquarters** (700 George Washington Memorial Parkway, McLean, VA 22101 (38.961154, -77.1463994); All 31 parkway-related stamps available weekdays 8:00 AM-4:15 PM)

**Note:** Arlington House is located within Arlington National Cemetery. Access is through the Arlington National Cemetery Visitor Center Security Access Point. Visitors cannot drive directly to Arlington House. Call 703-235-1530 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. ## Key Activities

- **Arlington House Mansion Tour** (30-45 minutes guided, 20-60 minutes self-guided) - Tour the historic Custis-Lee mansion built 1803-1818 by George Washington Parke Custis (adopted grandson of George Washington) as a memorial to George Washington, featuring period furnishings, George and Martha Washington's china, Revolutionary War artifacts including Washington's field tents, family portraits, and Greek Revival architecture; accessed within Arlington National Cemetery; September-May: 9:30 AM-4:30 PM, June-August: 9:00 AM-4:30 PM; Free admission, no reservations required; Guided tours start on the hour (available primarily on weekends in winter, daily in summer), self-guided tours available anytime. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/arho/planyourvisit/index.htm), [Arlington House Foundation](https://www.arlingtonhouse.org/visit), [NPPlan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/virginia-national-parks/arlington-house-the-robert-e-lee-memorial-park-at-a-glance/arlington-house-the-robert-e-lee-memorial-arlington-house-tour/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g30242-d218817-Reviews-Arlington_House_The_Robert_E_Lee_Memorial-Arlington_Virginia.html)]

- **North and South Slave Quarters Museum Exhibits** (30 minutes) - Explore two slave quarters buildings transformed into museums; North Slave Quarters features a memorial wall with names of indentured, enslaved, and free laborers who built and contributed to Arlington House, plus "The Longest Wait" exhibit about enslaved people promised freedom after Custis's death; South Slave Quarters focuses on the continuing influence of formerly enslaved people and their descendants who built new lives during and after the Civil War; located on Arlington House grounds; Same hours as Arlington House; Free admission. [Source: [NPS North Slave Quarters](https://www.nps.gov/arho/learn/historyculture/north-slave-quarters-museum-exhibit.htm), [NPS South Slave Quarters](https://www.nps.gov/arho/learn/historyculture/south-slave-quarters-museum-exhibit.htm), [Arlington House Foundation](https://www.arlingtonhouse.org/Visit/Virtual-Tour/north-slave-quarters-museum-exhibit), [NPPlan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/virginia-national-parks/arlington-house-the-robert-e-lee-memorial-park-at-a-glance/arlington-house-the-robert-e-lee-memorial-museums/arlington-house-the-robert-e-lee-memorial-slave-quarters-museum/)]

- **Arlington House Gardens and Grounds** (15-30 minutes) - Stroll the 16-acre grounds featuring a flower garden and kitchen garden (vegetable garden and orchard) growing period-authentic plants from 1861 including roses, peas, beans, potatoes, corn, asparagus, strawberries, cherries, and pears; view the Custis grave sites and enjoy sweeping views of Washington, D.C. including the Lincoln Memorial, Washington Monument, and U.S. Capitol; located on Arlington House grounds; Same hours as Arlington House; Free admission. [Source: [NPPlan Grounds](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/virginia-national-parks/arlington-house-the-robert-e-lee-memorial-park-at-a-glance/arlington-house-the-robert-e-lee-memorial-arlington-house-grounds/), [Travel and Dish](https://travelanddish.com/2023/01/26/arlington-house-at-arlington-national-cemetery-worth-a-visit/), [NPS Explore Arlington House](https://home.nps.gov/thingstodo/explore-arlington-house.htm)]

- **Robert E. Lee Museum** (15-20 minutes) - Visit the 1920s brick building housing a selection of artifacts and exhibits about Lee's life; located on Arlington House grounds; Same hours as Arlington House; Free admission. [Source: [NPPlan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/virginia-national-parks/arlington-house-the-robert-e-lee-memorial-park-at-a-glance/arlington-house-the-robert-e-lee-memorial-arlington-house-tour/), [The District](https://www.thedistrict.com/sightseeing/other-washington-d-c-attractions/arlington-house-custis-lee-mansion/)]

- **John F. Kennedy Grave Site and Eternal Flame** (15-20 minutes) - Visit one of Arlington National Cemetery's most visited sites just down the hill from Arlington House, featuring the eternal flame lit by Jackie Kennedy at the funeral, a 5-foot circular flat granite stone, and graves of President Kennedy, Jackie Kennedy, and two children who died as infants (daughter Arabella and son Patrick); half-mile walk from Arlington Cemetery Visitor Center, 10 minutes, portions hilly; Located within Arlington National Cemetery; Open during cemetery hours. [Source: [JFK Library](https://www.jfklibrary.org/learn/about-jfk/life-of-john-f-kennedy/fast-facts-john-f-kennedy/president-kennedys-grave-in-arlington-national-cemetery), [Roadside America](https://www.roadsideamerica.com/story/32268), [Arlington Cemetery](https://www.arlingtoncemetery.mil/Explore/Monuments-and-Memorials/President-John-F-Kennedy-Gravesite), [Everyday Wanderer](https://everydaywanderer.com/kennedy-family-graves-at-arlington)]

- **Tomb of the Unknown Soldier Changing of the Guard Ceremony** (20-30 minutes) - Witness the formal changing of the guard ceremony at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier, guarded 24/7/365 by sentinels from the elite 3rd U.S. Infantry Regiment; guard changes every 30 minutes April 1-September 30, every hour October 1-March 31 during public hours (every 2 hours overnight when cemetery closed); ceremony includes detailed inspection of rifle and formal exchange between outgoing and incoming sentinels, with sentry crossing 63-foot walkway in exactly 21 steps; Located within Arlington National Cemetery; Cemetery hours: October-March 8:00 AM-5:00 PM, April-September 8:00 AM-7:00 PM. [Source: [Arlington Cemetery Hours](https://arlingtoncemetery.mil/Media/News/Post/4512/Hours-of-operation-change-April-1-Changing-of-the-Guard-takes-place-every-30-minutes), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomb_of_the_Unknown_Soldier_(Arlington_National_Cemetery)), [Changing Guard](https://changing-guard.com/changing-guard-worldwide/arlington-usa.html), [Free Tours by Foot](https://freetoursbyfoot.com/changing-of-the-guard-in-arlington-cemetery/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **George Washington Artifacts Collection** (included in mansion tour) - View Custis's extensive collection of George Washington memorabilia displayed throughout Arlington House, including Washington's Revolutionary War field tents, personal china, family portraits, and engravings - often overlooked aspects of the house's original purpose as a living memorial to George Washington; Located within Arlington House mansion; Same hours as Arlington House; Free admission. [Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlington_House,_The_Robert_E._Lee_Memorial), [Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/Arlington_House,_The_Robert_E._Lee_Memorial), [NPS Explore Arlington House](https://home.nps.gov/thingstodo/explore-arlington-house.htm)]

- **Sweeping Panoramic Views** (10-15 minutes) - Enjoy the commanding hilltop views from Arlington House overlooking the Potomac River and Washington, D.C., with direct sightlines to the Lincoln Memorial, Washington Monument, and U.S. Capitol - the same strategic location George Washington Parke Custis chose for maximum visibility of the growing national capital; accessible from Arlington House grounds; Same hours as Arlington House; Free admission. [Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlington_House,_The_Robert_E._Lee_Memorial), [Everyday Wanderer](https://everydaywanderer.com/kennedy-family-graves-at-arlington)]

## Also Nearby

- **U.S. Marine Corps War Memorial (Iwo Jima Memorial)** (15-30 minutes, 0.6 miles walking from Arlington House) - View the iconic 78-foot bronze statue depicting six servicemen raising the American flag on Iwo Jima, designed by Felix de Weldon based on Joe Rosenthal's famous 1945 photograph; located in Arlington Ridge Park with parking available; Marshall Drive, Arlington, VA (38.890436, -77.069721); Open daily 6:00 AM-midnight year-round; Free admission; Rosslyn or Arlington Cemetery Metro stations 10-15 minute walk; Summer Tuesday evening Sunset Parades feature U.S. Marine Corps Band performances, ranger programs available by calling 703-235-1530. [Source: [NPS GWMP](https://www.nps.gov/gwmp/planyourvisit/usmc_memorial.htm), [Washington.org](https://washington.org/DC-guide-to/marine-corps-war-memorial), [Stay Arlington](https://www.stayarlington.com/directory/marine-corps-war-memorial-iwo-jima/), [Latitude.to](https://latitude.to/articles-by-country/us/united-states/4452/marine-corps-war-memorial)]

- **Netherlands Carillon** (20-30 minutes, adjacent to Marine Corps War Memorial) - Experience the 127-foot tower with 50 bells gifted by the Netherlands as gratitude for American aid during and after World War II, featuring patriotic chimes every 15 minutes from 10:00 AM-6:00 PM and stunning gardens with 10,000 tulips planted annually; free carillon concerts Saturdays and national holidays 6:00 PM from late May through early September with pop, jazz, and classical music; Marshall Drive, Arlington, VA; Grounds open daily 6:00 AM-midnight year-round; Free admission; Rosslyn or Arlington Cemetery Metro stations 10-15 minute walk. [Source: [NPS GWMP](https://www.nps.gov/gwmp/planyourvisit/netherlandscarillon.htm), [Rosslyn VA](https://www.rosslynva.org/go/netherlands-carillon), [Stay Arlington](https://www.stayarlington.com/directory/netherlands-carillon/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g30242-d2701503-Reviews-Netherlands_Carillon-Arlington_Virginia.html)]

- **Women in Military Service for America Memorial** (30-45 minutes, at Arlington Cemetery entrance) - Explore the nation's only major memorial dedicated to women who have served in the U.S. Armed Forces, featuring an education center, interactive exhibits, and a world-class collection of military women's stories from the American Revolution through present day; located in the Hemicycle at the ceremonial entrance to Arlington National Cemetery; Memorial Avenue & Schley Drive, Arlington, VA 22202; Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM-5:00 PM, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day; Free admission, subject to ANC security screening; Paid parking at Arlington Cemetery Visitor Center. [Source: [Military Women's Memorial](https://womensmemorial.org/), [Visit Page](http://beta.womensmemorial.org/visit), [Stay Arlington](https://www.stayarlington.com/directory/women-in-the-military-service-for-america-memorial/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/military-womens-memorial-arlington)]

- **National 9/11 Pentagon Memorial** (30-45 minutes, 2 miles south) - Pay respects at the memorial honoring the 184 victims who died in the Pentagon and on American Airlines Flight 77 during the September 11 attacks, featuring 184 memorial benches over reflecting pools with a moment of silence at 9:37 AM daily when water flow stops; includes free 24-minute audio tour; 1 Rotary Road, Arlington, VA (38.8692, -77.0552); Open 24 hours daily, 365 days a year, restrooms open 7:00 AM-10:00 PM; Free admission, no passes required; Pentagon Metro stop (Blue/Yellow lines), public parking at Pentagon City Mall. [Source: [Washington.org](https://washington.org/DC-guide-to/pentagon-memorial), [Pentagon Memorial FAQ](https://pentagonmemorial.org/visitor-faq/), [Stay Arlington](https://www.stayarlington.com/directory/pentagon-memorial/), [Free Tours by Foot](https://freetoursbyfoot.com/pentagon-memorial/), [Latitude.to](https://latitude.to/articles-by-country/us/united-states/13875/pentagon-memorial)]

- **Lincoln Memorial** (30-45 minutes, 1 mile / 20-25 minute walk across Memorial Bridge) - Walk across the scenic Memorial Bridge from Arlington Cemetery to visit the iconic Lincoln Memorial featuring the 19-foot marble statue of Abraham Lincoln, inscribed Gettysburg Address and Second Inaugural Address, and reflecting pool views; beautiful, easy, shadeless walk with broad sidewalks; 2 Lincoln Memorial Circle NW, Washington, DC 20002; Open 24 hours daily, rangers on duty 9:30 AM-10:00 PM; Free admission. [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g28970-i40-k7704681-Walking_From_the_Lincoln_Memorial_to_Arlington_National_Cem-Washington_DC_District_of_Columbia.html), [Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Arlington-National-Cemetery/Lincoln-Memorial), [Free Tours by Foot](https://freetoursbyfoot.com/arlington-national-cemetery/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 2-3 hours for a comprehensive visit to Arlington House and immediate grounds (30-45 minutes guided mansion tour or 20-60 minutes self-guided, 30 minutes slave quarters museums, 15-30 minutes gardens and grounds, 15-20 minutes Robert E. Lee Museum). Add 1-2 hours if visiting JFK grave site (15-20 minutes) and Tomb of the Unknown Soldier (20-30 minutes) within Arlington National Cemetery. Note that walking from Arlington Cemetery Visitor Center to Arlington House takes 15-20 minutes uphill.
