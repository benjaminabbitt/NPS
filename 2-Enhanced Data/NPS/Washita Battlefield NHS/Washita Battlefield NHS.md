# Washita Battlefield NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Washita Battlefield National Historic Site Visitor Center** (18555 Hwy 47A, Ste. A, Cheyenne, OK 73628; Daily 8:00 AM-4:30 PM, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 580-497-2742; Free admission) [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/waba/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/waba/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** National Park Passport stamps available in visitor center. The overlook and trail are open daily from dawn until dusk. Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [[Source](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/259276), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/waba/planyourvisit/the-washita-battlefield-national-historic-site-visitor-center.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center Museum and Film** (45-60 minutes) - Watch "Destiny at Dawn," a 27-minute film providing historical context for the November 27, 1868 dawn attack by Lt. Colonel George Armstrong Custer and the 7th U.S. Cavalry on Peace Chief Black Kettle's Cheyenne village; explore exhibits with artifacts, touch tables featuring bison objects the Cheyenne used, and items a 7th Cavalry soldier would have carried; Visitor center building, 18555 Hwy 47A; Daily 8:00 AM-4:30 PM; Free [[Source](https://wnpa.org/explore-parks/washita-battlefield), [Source](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/washita-battlefield-national-historic-site/)]

- **Battlefield Trail** (45-60 minutes) - Walk the 1.5-mile self-guided trail through the battlefield with interpretive signs and audio QR codes providing more information about the attack that killed Black Kettle and his wife Medicine Woman Later as they tried to cross the Washita River; trail offers peaceful setting for reflection on these tragic events; Trailhead at visitor center; Dawn to dusk; Free [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/waba/index.htm), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60988-d325167-Reviews-Washita_Battlefield_National_Historic_Site-Cheyenne_Oklahoma.html)]

- **Ranger-Led Programs** (30-60 minutes) - Join ranger-led talks offering deeper insights into the battle, Native American perspectives, and the broader conflicts of the Indian Wars era; programs explore the complex history and multiple viewpoints of this controversial attack; Check at visitor center for schedule; Seasonal, varies; Free [[Source](https://wnpa.org/plan-your-trip/trip-ideas/park-stories/how-to-plan-your-trip-to-washita-battlefield-national-historic-site-2)]

- **Overlook Viewing** (15-30 minutes) - Visit the battlefield overlook for panoramic views of the Washita River valley where the Cheyenne village stood and the attack unfolded; interpretive panels explain the landscape and battle positions; Overlook area; Dawn to dusk; Free [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/waba/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Audio Tour with QR Codes** (included in trail time) - Enhance your battlefield trail walk with audio content accessed via QR codes throughout the 1.5-mile loop, providing first-person accounts and historical context from multiple perspectives; Trail stations; Dawn to dusk; Free [[Source](https://wnpa.org/explore-parks/washita-battlefield)]

- **Touch Table Exhibits** (15-20 minutes) - Handle replica artifacts at visitor center touch tables including bison stomach and bladder that Cheyenne would have used for water storage, and examine items a 7th Cavalry soldier carried into battle; Visitor center; During operating hours; Free [[Source](https://wnpa.org/explore-parks/washita-battlefield)]

- **Quiet Contemplation Areas** (flexible timing) - Find peaceful spots along the river and trail for personal reflection on the tragic events and their lasting impact on Cheyenne people and American history; Throughout site; Dawn to dusk; Free [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60988-d325167-Reviews-Washita_Battlefield_National_Historic_Site-Cheyenne_Oklahoma.html)]

## Also Nearby

- **Black Kettle Museum** (1-2 hours, 1 mile north in Cheyenne) - Explore additional exhibits and artifacts related to the Battle of the Washita and Southern Plains Indian culture in this small local museum; 101 S. L.L. Males Avenue, Cheyenne, OK 73628; Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; Free (donations welcome) [[Source](https://www.travelok.com/listings/view.profile/id.8400)]

- **Washita National Wildlife Refuge** (2-3 hours, 40 miles northeast) - Observe migratory waterfowl, wildlife, and prairie landscapes along the Washita River; excellent for birding, hiking, and photography; Refuge headquarters near Butler, OK; Dawn to dusk; Free [[Source](https://www.fws.gov/refuge/washita)]

- **Antelope Hills** (1-2 hours, 20 miles south) - Explore scenic red rock formations and canyons where Custer's forces camped before the attack; hiking and photography opportunities in unique Oklahoma landscape; Near Cheyenne; Accessible during daylight; Free [[Source](https://www.travelok.com/listings/view.profile/id.8400)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 1.5-2 hours for visitor center, film, and battlefield trail with audio tour. Visitors report spending 45 minutes at the museum and another 45 minutes on the trail, though some spend up to 4 hours exploring thoroughly and reflecting on the site's significance.
