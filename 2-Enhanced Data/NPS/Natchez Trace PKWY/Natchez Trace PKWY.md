## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-emerald-mound"></a>**Emerald Mound** (10-30 minutes) - Second-largest ceremonial mound in the United States, covering eight acres and standing 35 feet high, built 1250-1600 A.D. by ancestors of the Natchez Indians during the Mississippian period; designated a National Historic Landmark; Emerald Mound Road, near Stanton, MS (31.635878, -91.248034); Milepost 10.3 (exit parkway at MS Hwy 553, go west 1 mile); Sunrise to sunset daily [Source: [Scenic Trace](https://www.scenictrace.com/top-stops-natchez-trace-parkway/), [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/emerald-mound.htm), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

- <a id="activity-mount-locust"></a>**Mount Locust Historic House** (15-60 minutes) - One of the original stands (inns) from the frontier days in the early 1800s, the only remaining inn on the Parkway with exhibits on early travel; Natchez Trace Parkway, near Stanton, MS (31.6855666, -91.1876); Milepost 15.5; Seasonal hours [Source: [Scenic Trace](https://www.scenictrace.com/top-stops-natchez-trace-parkway/), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

- <a id="activity-sunken-trace"></a>**The Sunken Trace** (10-30 minutes) - One of the most photographed sites along the Parkway where the Trace appears sunken due to thousands of travelers walking on easily eroded loess soil; short walking path through this historic section; Milepost 41.5 [Source: [Scenic Trace](https://www.scenictrace.com/top-stops-natchez-trace-parkway/), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

- <a id="activity-rocky-springs"></a>**Rocky Springs Town Site and Old Trace Trail** (30-90 minutes) - Abandoned 19th-century town site with historic church and cemetery; Old Trace trail is a moderate 2.4-mile hike (52 minutes average) through historic sections of the original Natchez Trace; Milepost 54.8; Always open [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/mississippi/rocky-springs-old-natchez-trace), [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/rockyspringssection.htm), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

- <a id="activity-cypress-swamp"></a>**Cypress Swamp** (10-30 minutes) - Self-guided elevated boardwalk trail through water tupelo/bald-cypress swamp where visitors may spot alligators; serene wetland environment; Milepost 122.0 [Source: [Scenic Trace](https://www.scenictrace.com/top-stops-natchez-trace-parkway/), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

- <a id="activity-jeff-busby"></a>**Jeff Busby Site** (15-45 minutes) - Highest point on the Mississippi section of the Parkway at 603 feet elevation; features scenic overlook, hiking trail to summit, picnic area, and campground; Milepost 193.1 [Source: [Scenic Trace](https://www.scenictrace.com/top-stops-natchez-trace-parkway/), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

- <a id="activity-bynum-mounds"></a>**Bynum Mounds** (10-20 minutes) - Archaeological site containing six burial mounds, five excavated in the 1940s, dating to the Middle Woodland period; Milepost 232.4 [Source: [Scenic Trace](https://www.scenictrace.com/top-stops-natchez-trace-parkway/), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

- <a id="activity-pharr-mounds"></a>**Pharr Mounds** (10-20 minutes) - 90-acre complex containing eight burial mounds between 2 and 18 feet high, built 1,800-2,000 years ago (1-200 A.D.) during the Middle Woodland period; includes walking paths and interpretive exhibits; Milepost 286.7 [Source: [Scenic Trace](https://www.scenictrace.com/top-stops-natchez-trace-parkway/), [Some Where Down South](https://somewheredownsouth.com/stops-on-the-natchez-trace-parkway/), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

- <a id="activity-colbert-ferry"></a>**Colbert Ferry** (15-45 minutes) - Historic ferry crossing site on the Tennessee River with 20-minute walk along the Old Trace leading to scenic bluff overlook; includes picnic area and boat launch; Milepost 327.3 [Source: [Scenic Trace](https://www.scenictrace.com/top-stops-natchez-trace-parkway/), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

- <a id="activity-meriwether-lewis-site"></a>**Meriwether Lewis Death and Burial Site** (15-45 minutes) - 300-acre park commemorating the location where explorer Meriwether Lewis died on October 11, 1809; features gravesite monument, visitor center with exhibits, nature trails, campground, and section of Old Trace; 190 Meriwether Lewis Park, Hohenwald, TN 38462 (35.522516, -87.455926); Milepost 385.9; Seasonal hours [Source: [Scenic Trace](https://www.scenictrace.com/top-stops-natchez-trace-parkway/), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

- <a id="activity-jackson-falls"></a>**Jackson Falls** (40-60 minutes) - 0.2-mile paved trail into small gorge with scenic overlook of waterfall named after Andrew Jackson; flow varies with rainfall; Milepost 404.7 [Source: [Scenic Trace](https://www.scenictrace.com/top-stops-natchez-trace-parkway/), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

- <a id="activity-gordon-house"></a>**Gordon House and Duck River Ferry Site** (10-30 minutes) - Built in 1818, one of only two surviving historic buildings on the Parkway; 10-minute walk from Gordon House leads to section of original Natchez Trace and historic ferry site that operated until late 1800s; Milepost 407.7 [Source: [Scenic Trace](https://www.scenictrace.com/top-stops-natchez-trace-parkway/), [Visit Franklin](https://visitfranklin.com/blog/your-guide-to-the-natchez-trace-parkway-in-franklin-tn/), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

- <a id="activity-birdsong-hollow"></a>**Birdsong Hollow and Double Arch Bridge** (15-60 minutes) - Nation's first double arch bridge, spanning 1,648 feet and standing 155 feet tall; one of the most popular stops on the northern end of the parkway with scenic overlook and modern engineering marvel; Milepost 438.0 [Source: [Scenic Trace](https://www.scenictrace.com/top-stops-natchez-trace-parkway/), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

- <a id="activity-water-valley-overlook"></a>**Water Valley Overlook** (10-20 minutes) - Scenic overlook with 180-degree view of Water Valley; short drive (0.3 miles) up to hilltop ridge with picnic table; Milepost 411.8 [Source: [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-tennessee/columbia-centerville-tn/91-water-valley-overlook.html), [Scenic Overlooks](https://www.scenictrace.com/scenic-overlooks-on-the-natchez-trace-parkway/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="hidden-fall-hollow"></a>**Fall Hollow Trail and Waterfall** (10-60 minutes) - Paved walk to observation deck overlooking a waterfall, with dirt trail continuing to additional waterfalls including one with crystal-clear pool perfect for swimming; Milepost 391.9 [Source: [Suburban Turmoil](https://suburbanturmoil.com/best-stops-natchez-trace-parkway/2016/09/19/), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

- <a id="hidden-rock-spring"></a>**Rock Spring Nature Trail** (10-60 minutes) - Half-mile loop trail popular with bird watchers where you cross a creek on stepping stones and pass abandoned beaver dams to see a natural spring; possible sightings of rare hellbender salamander; Milepost 330.2 [Source: [Visit Franklin](https://visitfranklin.com/blog/your-guide-to-the-natchez-trace-parkway-in-franklin-tn/), [Scenic Trace](https://www.scenictrace.com/top-stops-natchez-trace-parkway/)]

- <a id="hidden-tobacco-farm"></a>**Tobacco Farm and Old Trace Drive** (10-20 minutes) - Historic tobacco barn marking a two-mile, one-way drive along the actual Old Trace trail with beautiful valley views on bumpy dirt road; Milepost 401.4 [Source: [Visit Franklin](https://visitfranklin.com/blog/your-guide-to-the-natchez-trace-parkway-in-franklin-tn/), [Scenic Trace](https://www.scenictrace.com/top-stops-natchez-trace-parkway/)]

- <a id="hidden-metal-ford"></a>**Metal Ford and Buffalo River** (10-30 minutes) - Scenic hiking trail along Buffalo River with access to historic Old Trace and picnic area; peaceful riverside setting; Milepost 382.8 [Source: [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

- <a id="hidden-witch-dance"></a>**Witch Dance** (10-20 minutes) - Folklore location where legend says grass never grows due to witches dancing; includes bicycle-only campground and historical Cherokee legend interpretation; Milepost 233.2 [Source: [Scenic Trace](https://www.scenictrace.com/top-stops-natchez-trace-parkway/), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-mississippi/houston-mantee-ms/151-witch-dance)]

- <a id="hidden-wichahpi-stone-wall"></a>**Wichahpi Commemorative Stone Wall** (15-60 minutes) - Modern memorial stone wall built by Tom Hendrix to honor his great-great-grandmother's journey; considered a modern marvel with over 8 million pounds of stone; Milepost 337.9 [Source: [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

- <a id="hidden-chickasaw-village"></a>**Chickasaw Village Site** (10-30 minutes) - Site once occupied by the Chickasaw Nation with access to Natchez Trace National Scenic Trail for hiking; interpretive exhibits on Chickasaw history; Milepost 261.8 [Source: [Visit Tupelo](https://www.tupelo.net/blog/seven-things-you-must-see-on-the-natchez-trace-parkway/)]

- <a id="hidden-baker-bluff"></a>**Baker Bluff Overlook** (10-20 minutes) - Scenic overlook with hiking trail offering forest views and peaceful natural setting; Milepost 405.1 [Source: [Scenic Trace](https://www.scenictrace.com/top-stops-natchez-trace-parkway/), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-elvis-birthplace"></a>**Elvis Presley Birthplace & Museum** (1-2 hours, ~2 miles from Milepost 259.7) - Museum and grounds telling the story of Elvis's Tupelo years (until age 13), featuring the house where he was born, museum exhibits, church, and park with reflecting pond; 306 Elvis Presley Drive, Tupelo, MS 38804; Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM; 662-841-1245; Exit parkway at Milepost 259.7 onto MS Hwy 6 (Main Street), travel east couple miles through town, turn left onto Elvis Presley Drive after crossing US 45 [Source: [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-mississippi/tupelo-ms/tupelo-mississippi/elvis-presley.html), [Visit Tupelo](https://www.tupelo.net/), [Midwest Wanderer](https://midwestwanderer.com/?p=18089)]

- <a id="nearby-vicksburg"></a>**Vicksburg National Military Park** (2-4 hours, ~20-30 miles west of parkway) - Civil War battlefield commemorating the campaign and siege of Vicksburg, featuring over 1,330 monuments and 16-mile tour road; 3201 Clay Street, Vicksburg, MS 39183; Park gates open October-March 7:30 AM - 5:00 PM, April-September 7:30 AM - 7:00 PM, Visitor Center 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily except Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day; $20 per vehicle (7-day pass); Access via I-20, MS Hwy 27, Fisher Ferry Road, or US 61 from parkway [Source: [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-mississippi/vicksburg-port-gibson-ms/264-vicksburg-national-military-park.html), [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/vick/planyourvisit/directions.htm)]

- <a id="nearby-tishomingo"></a>**Tishomingo State Park** (20-90 minutes, near Milepost 304.5) - Inexpensive state park featuring hiking trails, very old cable foot bridge over creek, picnic areas, and nature exhibits; scenic rock formations and forest setting [Source: [Visit Tupelo](https://www.tupelo.net/blog/seven-things-you-must-see-on-the-natchez-trace-parkway/), [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

- <a id="nearby-natchez-downtown"></a>**Historic Downtown Natchez and Under-the-Hill** (1-3 hours, southern terminus) - Historic downtown featuring antebellum mansions including Longwood (largest octagonal house in U.S.) and Stanton Hall; Natchez Under-the-Hill along the Mississippi River with shops, restaurants, and Under-the-Hill Saloon (oldest bar in town); beautiful river views from Natchez Bluff Walking Trail; Natchez, MS (southern terminus at Milepost 0) [Source: [Visit Natchez](https://visitnatchez.org/plan/natchez-itineraries/natchez-city-tour-itinerary/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60910-d18981087-Reviews-Natchez_Bluffs_and_Under_the_Hill_Historic_District-Natchez_Mississippi.html)]

- <a id="nearby-loveless-cafe"></a>**The Loveless Cafe** (30-60 minutes dining, ~150 yards from northern terminus) - Famous for world-renowned biscuits and made-from-scratch southern cooking including fried chicken, country ham, BBQ pulled pork, omelets, and desserts; Tennessee Highway 100, Nashville, TN (near northern terminus at Milepost 442); 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM seven days a week [Source: [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-tennessee/nashville-franklin-tn/nashville-tennessee/loveless.html)]

- <a id="nearby-belle-meade"></a>**Belle Meade Plantation** (1-2 hours, ~15-20 minutes from northern terminus) - Historic 1820s mansion on former Thoroughbred horse farm offering tours of mansion and grounds plus winery; Nashville, TN; Access via TN Hwy 100 east from parkway [Source: [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-tennessee/nashville-franklin-tn/nashville-tennessee/375-nashville-tennessee-location-map.html)]

- <a id="nearby-french-camp"></a>**French Camp Historic Village** (10-60 minutes, Milepost 180.7) - Historic village with Old Trace section, history exhibits, and restaurant; living history interpretation of 1800s frontier life [Source: [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

- <a id="nearby-windsor-ruins"></a>**Windsor Ruins** (15-30 minutes, ~10 miles northwest of Milepost 30) - Haunting remains of antebellum mansion with 23 standing columns; off-parkway side trip near southern section; Accessible via side roads from parkway [Source: [NatchezTraceTravel.com](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/types-of-trace-stops/natchez-trace-favorites.html)]

## Review / Personal Notes

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 2-4 days for complete parkway experience. The full 444-mile drive takes approximately 8-10 hours of straight driving at the 50 mph speed limit, but NPS recommends breaking it into 3-4 days to allow for stops at historic sites, trails, and scenic overlooks. For each nature trail, add approximately 30 minutes; for each larger historic site, add approximately 1 hour. Most visitors spend 15-60 minutes at major stops. A relaxed 4-day trip allows time to visit key attractions, enjoy campgrounds, and explore sites off the parkway. [Source: [The Travel](https://www.thetravel.com/how-long-to-drive-the-natchez-trace-and-more-tips/), [Detour Effect](https://thedetoureffect.com/blog/natchez-trace-parkway-itinerary/), [NPS Insider Tips](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/insider-tips.htm)]
