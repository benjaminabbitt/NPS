# Great Sand Dunes NP & PRES (National Park and Preserve)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Great Sand Dunes Visitor Center** (11999 State Highway 150, Mosca, CO 81146 [37.7935, -105.5931]; Open daily 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM in winter, 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM during summer months, except federal holidays; (719) 378-6395)

**Note:** Call (719) 378-6395 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/grsa/planyourvisit/visitor-center.htm), [Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/grsa/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Hiking to High Dune** (2-4 hours round trip) - Nearest dune to the parking lot at approximately 692 feet from base to summit; this moderate 2.5-3 mile round trip hike gains 692 feet elevation and takes 2 hours for acclimated hikers, up to 4 hours if not adjusted to high altitude; no designated trails on the dunefield, allowing exploration anywhere; Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve, accessible from Dunes Parking Area; Park open 24/7 year-round. [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/grsa/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [High Dune NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/high-dune-on-first-ridge.htm), [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-things-to-do-great-sand-dunes/)]

- **Hiking to Star Dune** (6-9 hours round trip) - Tallest dune in North America (tied with Hidden Dune) at 741 feet from base to summit; this strenuous 6-mile round trip hike takes an average of 6 hours for acclimated hikers, up to 9 hours if not adjusted to altitude; majestic pyramid-shaped dune offers 360-degree views; Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve, accessible from Dunes Parking Area; Park open 24/7 year-round. [Source: [NPS Star Dune](https://www.nps.gov/places/star-dune.htm), [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/grsa/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [Steph Abegg](https://stephabegg.com/trip-reports/colorado/high-dune-star-dune/)]

- **High Dune and Star Dune Loop** (5-8+ hours) - Combined loop hike covering 8+ miles with 1,300 feet elevation gain; takes 5 hours minimum for experienced hikers, potentially 8+ hours for those not acclimated; experience both major dunes in one trek; Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve, accessible from Dunes Parking Area; Park open 24/7 year-round. [Source: [Strava Route](https://www.strava.com/routes/hiking/usa/colorado/salida/3247884055237884930), [Fulfilling Travel](https://www.fulfillingtravel.com/great-sand-dunes-national-park-guide/)]

- **Sandboarding and Sand Sledding** (2+ hours) - Permitted anywhere on the dunefield away from vegetated areas; plan minimum 2 hours to hike to slopes and sled down several times; children typically sled on lower slopes while teens and adults hike to taller slopes; park does not rent equipment but sandboards and sleds available at Oasis Store (4 miles from visitor center, open March-October) and Kristi Mountain Sports; Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve; Park open 24/7 year-round; avoid mid-day in summer when sand reaches 150°F. [Source: [NPS Sandboarding](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/sandboarding-and-sand-sledding.htm), [Bucket List Mermaid](https://thebucketlistmermaid.com/sandboarding-the-great-sand-dunes-review-and-guide/), [Visit Alamosa](https://www.alamosa.org/blog/great-sand-dunes-national-park-and-preserve-activities/)]

- **Wading and Playing in Medano Creek** (1-3 hours) - Seasonal creek flows from late April through early July creating a "beach" at the base of the dunes with unique surge flow phenomenon; best during peak flow in late May to early June when water is several inches deep; ideal for children to splash and build sandcastles, adults to cool off; flows dry up typically by July 4th; Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve, at Dunes Parking Area; Park open 24/7 year-round; note that late May/early June weekends are extremely crowded. [Source: [NPS Medano Creek](https://www.nps.gov/grsa/planyourvisit/medano-creek.htm), [Medano Creek NPS Place](https://www.nps.gov/places/medano-creek.htm), [Denver Post](https://www.denverpost.com/2021/05/05/medano-creek-great-sand-dunes-national-park/)]

- **Mosca Pass Trail** (3.5-5 hours round trip) - Moderate 7-mile round trip (3.5 miles one-way) forest hike following a fairly narrow valley to a low mountain pass; 1,407-1,520 feet elevation gain that is never particularly steep; historic route offering views of the San Luis Valley; Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve, Mosca Pass Trailhead; Park open 24/7 year-round. [Source: [AllTrails Mosca Pass](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/colorado/mosca-pass-trail--3), [American Southwest](https://www.americansouthwest.net/colorado/great_sand_dunes/mosca-pass-trail.html), [NPS Mosca Pass](https://www.nps.gov/places/mosca-pass.htm)]

- **Night Sky Viewing and Stargazing** (2-4 hours) - Certified International Dark Sky Park in 2019 with ability to see up to 15,000 stars; Milky Way most visible on clear, moonless evenings in late summer (August-September); combination of dry air, minimal light pollution, and high elevation creates exceptional viewing; bring tripod for astrophotography; Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve, best viewing from dunefield or campground; Park open 24/7 year-round; plan for moonless nights or late moonrise. [Source: [NPS Experience the Night](https://www.nps.gov/grsa/planyourvisit/experiencethenight.htm), [Travels and Curiosities](https://www.travelsandcuriosities.com/stargazing-at-great-sand-dunes-national-park), [Visit Alamosa Astrophotography](https://www.alamosa.org/blog/how-to-capturing-astrophotography-in-colorado-s-great-sand-dunes-national-park/)]

- **Wildlife Viewing** (1-3 hours) - Park hosts 78 mammal species and 250+ bird species including elk (best viewed dawn/dusk in meadows, fall/winter; rutting season late August-October with bugling calls), mule deer, pronghorn, black bears, mountain lions, and various birds of prey; spring and fall feature 20,000+ migrating sandhill cranes through San Luis Valley; Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve, throughout park but especially meadows and grasslands; Park open 24/7 year-round. [Source: [A-Z Animals Wildlife](https://a-z-animals.com/articles/wildlife-watching-in-great-sand-dunes-national-park-a-complete-list/), [Colorado Birding Trail](https://coloradobirdingtrail.com/site/great-sand-dunes-national-park-and-preserve/), [National Parks Traveler Wildlife](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/park/subpage/wildlife-great-sand-dunes)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (2+ hours) - Activity booklets for ages 3-6, 7-9, 10-12, and 13+; complete activities while exploring the park to earn certificate, badge, or patch; special Junior Ranger Day events feature interactive stations about dunes, wildlife, fossils, and cultural artifacts; Great Sand Dunes Visitor Center, 11999 State Highway 150, Mosca, CO 81146; Visitor Center hours: 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM daily in winter, 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM during summer. [Source: [NPS Junior Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/grsa/learn/kidsyouth/junior-ranger.htm), [WNPA Junior Ranger](https://wnpa.org/things-to-do/junior-ranger-programs), [Junior Ranger Day News](https://www.nps.gov/grsa/learn/news/07-17-2025-great-sand-dunes-offers-living-history-and-junior-ranger-day-programming.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Music Pass Trail to Upper Sand Creek Lake** (3-4 hours round trip from trailhead) - Steep 2.4-mile round trip (1 mile one-way to pass) gaining 741 feet; views of alpine lake reflecting 13,357' Music Mountain; requires 4WD vehicle and 2.5-hour drive from main park area; 4WD vehicles can drive additional 2.5 rough miles reducing hike to steep final mile; rewards include above-treeline alpine lake scenery; Music Pass Trailhead (requires 4WD, 2.5 hours from main park); accessible during summer months (typically June-September). [Source: [AllTrails Music Pass](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/colorado/music-pass-trail), [NPS Sand Creek Lakes](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hike-sand-creek-lakes.htm), [Unknown Colorado](https://unknowncolorado.com/hike/hikes-great-sand-dunes-national-park/)]

- **Backcountry Camping on Sand Ramp Trail** (overnight, permits required) - Seven designated sites along Sand Ramp Trail (Buck Creek, Escape Dunes, Indian Grove, Little Medano, Aspen, Cold Creek, Sand Creek) plus 20 non-designated dunes backcountry sites; experience variety of landscapes from dunes views to mixed conifer forests at base of Sangre de Cristo Mountains; permits $6 via Recreation.gov, reservations available 3 months in advance; must camp within 50 feet of metal arrowhead post at designated sites; no campfires except metal fire ring at Sand Creek site; Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve, Sand Ramp Trail; accessible year-round but best summer-fall. [Source: [Recreation.gov Permits](https://www.recreation.gov/permits/4675316), [NPS Backpacking](https://www.nps.gov/grsa/planyourvisit/backpacking.htm), [Recreation.gov Article](https://www.recreation.gov/articles/location-spotlight/backcountry-camping-in-great-sand-dunes-national-park/2094)]

- **Medano Lake** (full day, 4WD required) - Remote alpine lake above the dunes reached via challenging 4WD road plus hiking; outstanding high-altitude scenery; significantly less visited than main dunefield; requires high-clearance 4WD vehicle; Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve, accessed via Medano Pass Primitive Road; best visited July-September when road typically passable. [Source: [Unknown Colorado](https://unknowncolorado.com/hike/hikes-great-sand-dunes-national-park/), [Enjoy National Parks](https://enjoynationalparks.com/great-sand-dunes-national-park/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Zapata Falls** (1-2 hours, 5 miles south) - Secluded 25-30 foot waterfall sheltered in rocky crevasse in Sangre de Cristo Mountains; 0.5-mile moderately steep hike from parking lot at 9,088 feet elevation; ford chilly stream and maneuver over slippery rocks to view falls (or balance on log bridge); breathtaking views of Great Sand Dunes layered against mountains; Zapata Falls Recreation Area, off CO-150 between mile markers 10-11, approximately 5 miles south of park entrance (37.6217, -105.5590); accessible year-round but best May-October; free day use. [Source: [Colorado.com Zapata Falls](https://www.colorado.com/articles/hidden-colorado-gem-zapata-falls), [BLM Zapata Falls SRMA](https://www.blm.gov/visit/zapata-falls-srma), [AllTrails Zapata Falls](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/colorado/zapata-falls-trail)]

- **Colorado Gators Reptile Park** (1-2 hours, 17 miles north of Alamosa) - Unique Colorado attraction featuring hundreds of rescued alligators, crocodiles, and exotic reptiles; educational tours, optional gator-wrestling classes to help keepers care for injured reptiles; one of the area's most unusual roadside attractions; 9162 County Rd 9 N, Mosca, CO 81146; Open daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (March 1 - November 2), 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM (November 3 - February 26), closed Thanksgiving and Christmas; located between Alamosa and Great Sand Dunes making it a convenient stop. [Source: [Colorado Gators](https://coloradogators.com/), [TripAdvisor Colorado Gators](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60841-d2243649-Reviews-Colorado_Gators_Reptile_Park-Mosca_Colorado.html), [Visit Alamosa Colorado Gators](https://www.alamosa.org/listing/colorado-gators-alamosa/)]

- **UFO Watchtower** (1-2 hours, 32 miles from visitor center) - Quirky observation platform offering 360-degree views of San Luis Valley, a hotspot for reported UFO sightings; outdoor "garden" with unusual items and visitor-left offerings; open year-round; located north of Hooper on Highway 17, approximately 32 miles from Great Sand Dunes Visitor Center; unique roadside attraction perfect for breaking up drives. [Source: [Visit Alamosa UFO Watchtower](https://www.alamosa.org/directory/835-ufo-watchtower), [NPS Nearby Attractions](https://www.nps.gov/grsa/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm)]

- **San Luis Valley Brewing Company** (1-2 hours, 30 miles/30 minutes from park) - Craft brewery creating specialty beers on premises with savory food menu; popular stop for Great Sand Dunes visitors; inviting atmosphere with American cuisine, daily specials, and homemade sodas; 631 Main Street, Alamosa, CO 81101; approximately 30-minute drive from park. [Source: [San Luis Valley Brewing](https://www.slvbrewco.com/), [TripAdvisor SLV Brewing](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g29129-d1417290-Reviews-San_Luis_Valley_Brewing_Company-Alamosa_Colorado.html), [Visit Alamosa SLV Brewing](https://www.alamosa.org/listing/san-luis-valley-brewing-company/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** A minimum of 4-6 hours is needed to visit the visitor center, take photos, and hike onto the dunes, but a full day (8-12 hours) is recommended for those wanting to hike to High Dune, try sandboarding, and wade in Medano Creek (when flowing). Visitors interested in hiking Star Dune, stargazing, or exploring backcountry areas should plan 2-3 days.
