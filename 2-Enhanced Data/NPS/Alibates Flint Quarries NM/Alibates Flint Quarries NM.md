# Alibates Flint Quarries NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Alibates Visitor Center** (37084 Alibates Rd., Potter County, TX 79036 (35.561499, -101.6960061); 9:00 AM-4:00 PM daily; 806-857-6680)

**Note:** The contact station has NPS passport cancellation stamps for both Alibates Flint Quarries NM and Lake Meredith National Recreation Area. Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/alfl/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/258598)]

## Key Activities

- **Ranger-Guided Quarries Tour** (2 hours) - Join a park ranger for a moderate 2-mile roundtrip hike with 170 feet of elevation gain to the flint quarries, learning about the geology, anthropology, ethnobotany, and history of the region and how Native Americans used Alibates flint for over 13,000 years; unique opportunity to hold worked flint pieces left by ancient tool makers; 37084 Alibates Rd., Potter County, TX 79036; tours by reservation only at 806-857-6680, offered most weekends at 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM; requires closed-toed shoes or hiking boots, proper hiking attire; not recommended for visitors with health concerns; no pets or smoking; tours canceled in inclement weather. [Source: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/alfl/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [NPS Quarries Tour](https://www.nps.gov/places/alibates-flint-quarries-guided-tour.htm), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60714-d3582648-Reviews-Alibates_Flint_Quarries_National_Monument-Fritch_Texas.html), [National Parks Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/2019/06/alibates-flint-quarries-national-monument-holding-history-your-hand)]

- **Visitor Center Experience** (30 minutes) - Explore interactive museum exhibits about flint quarrying history, watch an award-winning 10-minute film about the site's 13,000-year history of use by Indigenous peoples, browse the bookstore; 37084 Alibates Rd., Potter County, TX 79036; 9:00 AM-4:00 PM daily except Thanksgiving and Christmas. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/alfl/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/alfl/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/alibates-flint-quarries-national-monument/)]

- **Mesquite Trail Self-Guided Hike** (10 minutes) - Easy to moderate 0.25-mile trail on relatively flat terrain around a small mesa adjacent to the visitor center; view canyon scenery from the former LX Ranch property, spot local insects, reptiles, birds, Ice Age-era plants, and pieces of Alibates flint along the trail; 37084 Alibates Rd., Potter County, TX 79036; accessible during visitor center hours 9:00 AM-4:00 PM daily. [Source: [NPS Mesquite Trail](https://www.nps.gov/alfl/planyourvisit/mesquite.htm), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/alfl/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Alibates Heritage Garden and Butterfly Garden** (15 minutes) - Explore the educational interpretive garden featuring native plants including the "three sisters" (corn, beans, squash) that demonstrates xeriscaping, edible plants, and medicinal plants used by Native Americans; visit the certified Monarch Waystation butterfly garden supporting monarch butterfly migration; 37084 Alibates Rd., Potter County, TX 79036; accessible during visitor center hours 9:00 AM-4:00 PM daily. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/alfl/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/alfl/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Texas Highways](https://texashighways.com/travel-news/eternal-value-alibates-flint-quarries-national-monument/)]

- **Wildlife Viewing Along the Trail** (included in tour time) - Watch for roadrunners, mule deer, lizards, and various bird species along the quarries trail; particularly good opportunities for spotting wildlife in early morning or late afternoon during the ranger-guided tour; accessible only during guided tours by reservation at 806-857-6680. [Source: [National Parks Data](https://nationalparksdata.com/a-visitors-guide-to-alibates-flint-quarries-national-monument/), [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/alfl/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Palo Duro Canyon State Park** (half day to full day, 25 miles south of Amarillo, 30 minutes from Fritch) - Explore the second-largest canyon in the United States with scenic drives, hiking including the iconic 5.75-mile roundtrip Lighthouse Trail (2-4 hours), mountain biking, horseback riding, camping, and wildlife viewing; 11450 Park Rd 5, Canyon, TX 79015; 7:00 AM-10:00 PM daily; entrance fee required; recommend visiting early morning to avoid heat on exposed trails. [Source: [Texas Parks & Wildlife](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/palo-duro-canyon), [Visit Amarillo](https://www.visitamarillo.com/listing/palo-duro-canyon-state-park/621/), [AllTrails Lighthouse Trail](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/texas/the-lighthouse-trail), [American Southwest](https://www.americansouthwest.net/texas/palo-duro-canyon/lighthouse-trail.html)]

- **Cadillac Ranch** (30 minutes, 15 miles east of Amarillo) - Visit this iconic Route 66 art installation featuring ten vintage Cadillacs buried nose-down in the ground; visitors are encouraged to bring spray paint to add to the ever-changing graffiti art; 13651 I-40 Frontage Rd., Amarillo, TX 79124; open 24/7, no admission fee; approximately 1/4 mile walk from parking; best visited during daylight hours, early morning or late afternoon for fewer crowds and better lighting. [Source: [Visit Amarillo](https://www.visitamarillo.com/listing/cadillac-ranch/625/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g30165-d144342-Reviews-Cadillac_Ranch-Amarillo_Texas.html), [Traveler Lifes](https://www.travelerlifes.com/2025/05/cadillac-ranch.html)]

- **Panhandle-Plains Historical Museum** (2-4 hours, 20 minutes south of Amarillo in Canyon) - Visit the largest history museum in Texas featuring extensive exhibits on paleontology, dinosaurs, Native American artifacts, pioneer life, oil industry history, and regional art; located on the West Texas A&M University campus; 2503 4th Ave, Canyon, TX 79015; June-August: Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM-6:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM-6:00 PM; September-May: Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, closed Sundays and Mondays; admission: adults $12.50, seniors 65+ and military $10, children 4-12 $6; closed New Year's Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day. [Source: [Museum Website](https://www.panhandleplains.org/), [Visit Amarillo](https://www.visitamarillo.com/listing/panhandle-plains-historical-museum/504/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/panhandle-plains-historical-museum-canyon), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g55580-d106141-Reviews-Panhandle_Plains_Historical_Museum-Canyon_Texas.html)]

- **Lake Meredith National Recreation Area** (flexible, adjacent to Alibates) - Enjoy boating, fishing, swimming, camping, bird watching, horseback riding, and hiking at this large reservoir; headquarters at 419 E. Broadway, Fritch, TX 79036; 806-857-3151; headquarters open Monday-Friday 8:00 AM-4:30 PM; lake access open year-round; note that passport stamps for Lake Meredith are available at the Alibates Visitor Center. [Source: [NPS Lake Meredith](https://www.nps.gov/LAMR), [Lake Meredith Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/lamr/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Borger TX](https://www.borgertx.gov/265/Lake-Meredith-National-Recreation-Area)]
