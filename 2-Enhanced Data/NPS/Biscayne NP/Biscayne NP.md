# Biscayne NP (National Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-dante-fascell-visitor-center"></a>**Dante Fascell Visitor Center** (9700 SW 328th Street Sir Lancelot Jones Way, Homestead, FL 33033 (25.465332, -80.334324); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 786-335-3620) [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/bisc/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/bisc/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [NPS Passport Stamp](https://www.nps.gov/bisc/learn/virtual-passport-stamp.htm), [NPPlan Visitor Center](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/florida-national-parks/biscayne-national-park-park-at-a-glance/biscayne-national-park-dante-fascell-visitor-center/)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-snorkel-the-maritime-heritage-trail"></a>**Snorkel the Maritime Heritage Trail** (3.5 hours for guided tour) - Explore six historic shipwrecks along the underwater Maritime Heritage Trail including the Mandalay (1928 schooner) and Arratoon Apcar (1878 wreck), where shallow waters, coral reefs, and hurricanes made navigation treacherous before modern technology; snorkel around majestic sunken vessels now engulfed by colorful corals and surrounded by multitudes of fish and other wildlife; accessible by boat only from Dante Fascell Visitor Center, 9700 SW 328th Street, Homestead, FL 33033; tours operate daily weather permitting; $109 per person for 3.5-hour guided snorkel tour; 786-335-3620 [Source: [NPS Maritime Heritage Trail](https://www.nps.gov/bisc/learn/historyculture/maritime-heritage-trail.htm), [Biscayne Institute Snorkeling](https://www.biscaynenationalparkinstitute.org/snorkeling/snorkel-experience-2/), [Florida Rambler Snorkeling](https://www.floridarambler.com/southeast-florida-getaways/snorkel-biscayne-national-park/), [Our World Underwater](https://blog.owuscholarship.org/2019/08/traveling-the-maritime-heritage-trail-in-biscayne-national-park/)]

- <a id="activity-boca-chita-island-heritage-cruise"></a>**Boca Chita Island Heritage Cruise** (3.5 hours total with 1 hour on island) - Take a guided boat tour across Biscayne Bay to the most visited island in the park featuring a historic ornamental lighthouse, short hiking trail, and opportunities to learn about the park's ecosystems and history during the boat journey; departs from Dante Fascell Visitor Center, 9700 SW 328th Street, Homestead, FL 33033; tours at 9:15 AM and 1:15 PM daily; $83 per person; 786-335-3620 [Source: [Biscayne Institute Boca Chita](https://www.biscaynenationalparkinstitute.org/island-experiences/boca-chita-key-heritage-cruise/), [NPPlan Heritage Cruise](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/florida-national-parks/biscayne-national-park-park-at-a-glance/biscayne-national-park-guided-tours-and-excursions/biscayne-national-park-heritage-of-biscayne-cruise/), [Caplin News Boat Tours](https://caplinnews.fiu.edu/biscayne-national-park-boat-tours-stingray-boca-chita-key/), [National Parks Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/2016/02/boat-tours-boca-chita-key-biscayne-national-park)]

- <a id="activity-jones-lagoon-kayak-paddle-tour"></a>**Jones Lagoon Kayak/Paddle Tour** (3.5 hours for morning tour) - Paddle through the secluded, mangrove-fringed Jones Lagoon at the southern end of the park, exploring clear shallow waters in a remote unvisited area; encounter yellow stingrays, loggerhead turtles, juvenile bonnethead sharks and an otherworldly meadow of Cassiopeia (upside-down jellyfish); includes 30-minute boat ride to Hurricane Creek and Jones Lagoon; departs from Dante Fascell Visitor Center, 9700 SW 328th Street, Homestead, FL 33033; tours operate daily weather permitting; kayak or paddleboard options available; 786-335-3620 [Source: [Biscayne Institute Jones Lagoon](https://www.biscaynenationalparkinstitute.org/paddling/jones-lagoon-paddle-eco-adventure/), [Miami & Beaches Jones Lagoon](https://www.miamiandbeaches.com/things-to-do/parks-recreation/jones-lagoon), [Scho and Jo Things To Do](https://schoandjo.com/things-to-do-in-biscayne-national-park/), [Lost With Lydia Guide](https://www.lostwithlydia.com/destinations/usa/fl/How-to-Visit-Biscayne-National-Park/)]

- <a id="activity-visit-dante-fascell-visitor-center-walk-jetty-trail"></a>**Visit Dante Fascell Visitor Center & Walk Jetty Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - Explore the visitor center museum and art gallery featuring natural history exhibits with dioramas of birds, fish, and corals found in the park, watch short videos on park topics in the auditorium, then walk the easy 0.5-mile round-trip Jetty Trail along mangrove-lined shore (half boardwalk, half smooth gravel/sand surface) leading to the Colonial Bird Protection Area; Dante Fascell Visitor Center, 9700 SW 328th Street, Homestead, FL 33033; visitor center 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, trail 7:00 AM - 5:30 PM daily; free; 786-335-3620 [Source: [NPPlan Visitor Center](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/florida-national-parks/biscayne-national-park-park-at-a-glance/biscayne-national-park-dante-fascell-visitor-center/), [NPPlan Jetty Trail](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/florida-national-parks/biscayne-national-park-park-at-a-glance/biscayne-national-park-hiking-trails/biscayne-national-park-jetty-trail/), [Scho and Jo Things To Do](https://schoandjo.com/things-to-do-in-biscayne-national-park/), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/bisc/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- <a id="activity-snorkeling-at-coral-reefs"></a>**Snorkeling at Coral Reefs** (3.5 hours for guided tour) - Snorkel pristine coral reefs teeming with tropical fish, sea turtles, and colorful marine life in the largest marine sanctuary in the U.S. National Parks system; 95% of the park's nearly 173,000 acres are underwater in Biscayne Bay and the Atlantic Ocean; accessible by boat only from Dante Fascell Visitor Center, 9700 SW 328th Street, Homestead, FL 33033; tours operate daily weather permitting; $109 per person for 3.5-hour guided snorkel tour; 786-335-3620 [Source: [Biscayne Institute Snorkeling](https://www.biscaynenationalparkinstitute.org/snorkeling/), [Earth Trekkers Best Things](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-things-to-do-in-biscayne-national-park/), [National Park Posters Homestead](https://national-park-posters.com/blogs/national-park-posters/homestead-florida-your-gateway-to-biscayne-national-park), [Miami & Beaches Biscayne](https://www.miamiandbeaches.com/l/outdoor-experiences/biscayne-national-park/2128)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-stiltsville-guided-tour"></a>**Stiltsville Guided Tour** (2 hours) - Visit the surreal "village" of pastel-colored wooden houses built on stilts floating in the middle of Biscayne Bay, historically known for wild parties and speakeasies with stories straight out of a movie; accessible only by water via boat tour or private boat; tour departs from Coconut Grove (not from Dante Fascell Visitor Center); $70 per person; seasonal availability [Source: [Scho and Jo Things To Do](https://schoandjo.com/things-to-do-in-biscayne-national-park/), [Earth Trekkers Best Things](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-things-to-do-in-biscayne-national-park/)]

- <a id="gem-elliott-key-camping-and-hiking"></a>**Elliott Key Camping and Hiking** (Full day or overnight) - Explore the park's largest island at 7 miles long, accessible only by boat, featuring 40 first-come, first-served campsites ($25/night, $35 with boat docking; free May 1-September 30 but mosquitoes intense), a 1-mile loop trail near campground, and the 6-7 mile "Spite Highway" trail down the center of the island through maritime forest where rare Schaus' swallowtail butterflies can be spotted among mangroves, buttonwood, gumbo-limbo and torchwood trees; includes boat docks, restrooms, grills, showers, picnic tables, and potable water; boat access only from mainland Florida; open year-round; camping $25-35/night (waived May-September); 786-335-3620 [Source: [NPS Elliott Key](https://www.nps.gov/bisc/planyourvisit/elliottkey.htm), [Recreation.gov Elliott Key](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/10078979), [Scho and Jo Things To Do](https://schoandjo.com/things-to-do-in-biscayne-national-park/), [Florida Hikes Biscayne](https://floridahikes.com/biscayne-national-park/)]

- <a id="gem-full-day-snorkel-and-paddle-eco-adventure"></a>**Full-Day Snorkel and Paddle Eco-Adventure** (6-6.5 hours) - Combine snorkeling at one location with paddling through Jones Lagoon on an extended eco-adventure tour experiencing multiple park ecosystems in a single day; departs from Dante Fascell Visitor Center, 9700 SW 328th Street, Homestead, FL 33033; tours start at 10:00 AM and return around 4:30 PM; $199 per person; 786-335-3620 [Source: [Biscayne Institute Snorkel Paddle](https://www.biscaynenationalparkinstitute.org/paddling/snorkel-paddle-eco-adventure/), [NPPlan Snorkel Paddle](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/florida-national-parks/biscayne-national-park-park-at-a-glance/biscayne-national-park-guided-tours-and-excursions/biscayne-national-park-snorkel-and-paddle-eco-adventure/), [Lor Jor Explore Guide](https://www.lorjorexplore.com/blog/snorkeling-biscayne-national-park)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-everglades-national-park-ernest-coe-visitor-center"></a>**Everglades National Park - Ernest Coe Visitor Center** (Half day to full day, 30 minutes from Biscayne) - Explore the vast inland marsh of Everglades National Park featuring hiking trails, wildlife viewing, kayaking through mangroves, and the unique River of Grass ecosystem; only 20 miles of roadway separate Biscayne from the Everglades; Ernest Coe Visitor Center located at 40001 State Road 9336, Homestead, FL 33034; visitor center open 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily; $30 per vehicle entrance fee valid 7 days; 305-242-7700 [Source: [National Parks Homestead Gateway](https://www.npca.org/articles/715-homestead-named-gateway-to-everglades-and-biscayne-national-park), [Everywhere With Claire Itinerary](https://everywherewithclaire.com/biscayne-national-park-itinerary/), [NPS Everglades Directions](https://www.nps.gov/ever/planyourvisit/directions.htm), [Florida on Foot Homestead Entrance](https://floridaonfoot.com/exploring-everglades-national-park-homestead-entrance/)]

- <a id="nearby-coral-castle"></a>**Coral Castle** (1-1.5 hours, 15-20 minutes from Biscayne) - Explore the mysterious and fascinating stone structure built single-handedly by Edward Leedskalnin between 1923 and 1951, featuring intricate coral rock sculptures and architecture with no clear explanation of how one man moved the massive stones; hourly guided tours available; 28655 S. Dixie Highway, Homestead, FL 33033 (25.500833, -80.442778); Monday 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Thursday 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Friday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 7:00 PM, Sunday 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM, closed Tuesday-Wednesday; 305-248-6345 [Source: [Coral Castle Official](https://coralcastle.com/), [Coral Castle Hours](https://coralcastle.com/hours-location/), [TripAdvisor Coral Castle](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60739-d110106-Reviews-Coral_Castle-Homestead_Florida.html), [Visit Florida Coral Castle](https://www.visitflorida.com/listing/coral-castle/25784/)]

- <a id="nearby-fruit-spice-park"></a>**Fruit & Spice Park** (2-3 hours, 20-25 minutes from Biscayne) - Wander through the only tropical botanical garden of its kind in the United States, the Preston B. Bird/Mary Heinlein Fruit & Spice Park, featuring 37 acres of over 500 varieties of exotic fruits, vegetables, spices, herbs, and nuts from around the world; guided tours available daily at 11:00 AM, 1:30 PM, and 3:00 PM; 24801 SW 187th Ave, Homestead, FL 33031 (25.516667, -80.485833); 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily (no admission after 4:00 PM), closed Thanksgiving and Christmas; $15 adults, $8 children 6-11, free under 5; 305-247-5727 [Source: [Miami-Dade Fruit & Spice](https://www.miamidade.gov/parks/fruit-spice.asp), [TripAdvisor Fruit & Spice](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60739-d131917-Reviews-Fruit_Spice_Park-Homestead_Florida.html), [Visit Florida Fruit & Spice](https://www.visitflorida.com/listing/fruit-&-spice-park/27639/)]
