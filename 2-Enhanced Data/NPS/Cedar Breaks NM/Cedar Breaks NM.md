# Cedar Breaks NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Visitor Contact Station** (4730 UT-148, Brian Head, UT 84719 (37.635954, -112.845025); Late May to mid-October, daily 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.; 435-586-0787) [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Alltopsights](https://www.alltopsights.com/attraction-cedar-breaks-national-monument-utah-ut/), [NPS Contact](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/contacts.htm)]

**Note:** Call 435-586-0787 (mid-June through mid-October) or 435-586-9451 (administrative office, year-round) to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [NPS Contact](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/contacts.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Scenic Drive with Overlooks** (30-45 minutes) - The 5-mile road through Cedar Breaks offers easy access to five scenic overlooks (from south to north): Spectra Point Overlook, Point Supreme Overlook, Sunset Overlook, Chessman Overlook, and North Overlook, providing spectacular views of the amphitheater's colorful hoodoos and rock formations at 10,350 feet elevation; Cedar Breaks NM via UT-148, Brian Head, UT 84719; accessible when road is open, late May to mid-October. [Source: [Visit Cedar City](https://visitcedarcity.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/cedar-breaks/), [Frommers](https://www.frommers.com/destinations/bryce-canyon-national-park/side-trips/cedar-breaks-national-monument/), [NPS Point Supreme](https://www.nps.gov/places/point-supreme-overlook.htm)]

- **Spectra Point Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - 1.9-mile out-and-back hike with 380 feet elevation gain offering breathtaking views into the amphitheater with well-maintained trails lined with wildflowers; trailhead at Visitor Contact Station area, 4730 UT-148, Brian Head, UT 84719; accessible when road is open, late May to mid-October. [Source: [AllTrails Spectra Point](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/spectra-point-trail-cedar-breaks-national-monument), [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/planyourvisit/hike-a-trail.htm), [Road Trip Ryan](https://www.roadtripryan.com/go/t/utah/zion/cedar-breaks-national-monument)]

- **Ramparts Overlook Trail** (2-2.5 hours) - 3.7 to 3.8-mile out-and-back trail with 783-849 feet elevation gain, the most strenuous but dramatic trail in the monument offering spectacular views of hoodoo formations below at over 10,000 feet; trailhead at Visitor Contact Station area, 4730 UT-148, Brian Head, UT 84719; accessible when road is open, late May to mid-October. [Source: [AllTrails Ramparts](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/spectra-point-and-rampart-overlook-trails), [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/planyourvisit/hike-a-trail.htm), [American Southwest](https://www.americansouthwest.net/utah/cedar_breaks/spectra-point-ramparts-overlook-trail.html)]

- **Alpine Pond Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - 2-mile loop trail circling an alpine pond through wildflower meadows, particularly spectacular during July and August wildflower season, though with limited amphitheater views; trailhead along UT-148, Cedar Breaks NM; accessible when road is open, late May to mid-October. [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/planyourvisit/hike-a-trail.htm), [Road Trip Ryan](https://www.roadtripryan.com/go/t/utah/zion/cedar-breaks-national-monument)]

- **Wildflower Viewing** (30 minutes-2 hours depending on trails chosen) - Over 250 species of wildflowers bloom in the meadows and along trails, with peak bloom during the first two weeks of July coinciding with the annual Cedar Breaks Wildflower Festival; early bloomers like cushion phlox, kittentails, and aspen bluebells emerge in late June, while displays peak in mid-July with scarlet paintbrushes, Colorado columbines, little sunflowers, and elkweeds; trails throughout Cedar Breaks NM, particularly Alpine Pond Trail; best viewing July to August. [Source: [Visit Cedar City Wildflowers](https://visitcedarcity.com/blog/post/cedar-breaks-national-monument-wildflower-festival/), [Visit Utah Wildflowers](https://www.visitutah.com/articles/sunny-skies-epic-wildflowers-hoodoos-cedar-breaks), [NPS Wildflower Festival](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/learn/news/wildflower-festival-and-stargazing.htm)]

- **Stargazing Programs** (2 hours) - Free ranger-led star parties held every Saturday from May through Labor Day weekend (weather dependent) at Point Supreme Overlook, starting at approximately 9 p.m. with constellation tours around 10 p.m., featuring laser light tours and telescope viewing at this International Dark Sky Park at 10,350 feet elevation, the highest such programs in the national park system; Point Supreme Overlook, Cedar Breaks NM; summer season, weather dependent. [Source: [NPS Dark Sky](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/planyourvisit/experience-dark-night-skies.htm), [Visit Utah Stargazing](https://www.visitutah.com/articles/a-party-of-astronomic-proportions), [NPS Dark Sky Tours](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/planyourvisit/dark-sky-tours-2025.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Bartzen Viewpoint via Ramparts Trail** (3-3.5 hours) - The furthest viewpoint along the trail at 2.5 miles from the trailhead, this secluded spot is rarely visited and offers spectacular solitude with views into the amphitheater; accessed via Ramparts Trail from Visitor Contact Station area, 4730 UT-148, Brian Head, UT 84719; accessible when road is open, late May to mid-October. [Source: [Road Trip Ryan](https://www.roadtripryan.com/go/t/utah/zion/cedar-breaks-national-monument)]

- **Bartizan Arch Backcountry Route** (4-6 hours) - Advanced route to a unique triple arch at the western edge of Cedar Breaks, extending beyond the maintained Ramparts Trail with route finding, underbrush traversing, and steep loose footing along exposed cliffs; requires backcountry experience; accessed from Ramparts Trail, Cedar Breaks NM; accessible when road is open, late May to mid-October. [Source: [Hike Utah Bartizan](https://hike-utah.com/spectrapointrampartsbartizanarch/), [Beyond My Door](https://www.beyondmydoor.com/cedar-breaks-national-monument/)]

- **Rattlesnake Creek Trail - Ashdown Gorge Wilderness** (6-8 hours day hike, or overnight 2-day backpacking trip) - For advanced hikers with wayfinding and backcountry experience, this strenuous route comparable to Zion's Subway trail is a 10-12 mile trek dropping 3,000 feet through Ashdown Gorge Wilderness just outside the park boundary in Dixie National Forest, featuring beautiful waterfalls including Shooting Star Waterfall; trailhead access via Dixie National Forest roads near Cedar Breaks; best as overnight trip; permit requirements may apply. [Source: [AllTrails Rattlesnake](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/rattlesnake-creek-ashdown-gorge-trail), [Girl on a Hike](https://girlonahike.com/backpacking-ashdown-gorge-via.html), [Beyond My Door](https://www.beyondmydoor.com/cedar-breaks-national-monument/)]

- **Winter Activities: Cross-Country Skiing and Snowshoeing** (varies, 2-4 hours typical) - When the scenic drive closes (late November to mid-May), Cedar Breaks offers 7 miles of groomed trails plus 30 miles of marked and unmarked backcountry trails for cross-country skiing and snowshoeing, with access to a Winter Warming Yurt serving hot cocoa and hosting free guided snowshoe hikes every Saturday in January and February; Scenic Byway 148 (becomes groomed trail in winter), Cedar Breaks NM; late November through mid-May. [Source: [NPS Ski and Snowshoe](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/planyourvisit/ski-and-snowshoe.htm), [Visit Cedar City Winter](https://visitcedarcity.com/blog/post/experience-cedar-breaks-in-winter/), [NPS Winter Access](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/planyourvisit/winter-access.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Brian Head Resort Summer Activities** (2-4 hours) - Utah ski resort offering summer mountain biking with over 100 miles of downhill single track accessed via Giant Steps Express chairlift, scenic chairlift rides ($10 adults, $6 children 4-12), 18-hole disc golf course, and Vista Hiking Trail; located just 5 minutes/5 miles from Cedar Breaks; 329 S Village Way, Brian Head, UT 84719; lift-served activities open 9:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. seven days a week June 27 through August 10, then Friday-Sunday remainder of summer season. [Source: [Brian Head Summer Activities](https://www.brianhead.com/summer-activities/), [Visit Utah Brian Head Summer](https://www.visitutah.com/places-to-go/utah-ski-resorts/brian-head-resort/summer-in-brian-head), [Visit Cedar City Brian Head](https://visitcedarcity.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/brian-head/)]

- **Panguitch Lake Fishing and Recreation** (half-day to full-day, 3-8 hours) - Trophy trout fishing lake at 8,209 feet elevation with 10 miles of shoreline, known for rainbow, cutthroat, and tiger trout averaging 14-24 inches, offering year-round fishing including excellent ice fishing in winter; also features mountain biking, ATV riding, horseback riding, and water sports; located approximately 20 minutes from Cedar Breaks; Panguitch Lake area, accessed via scenic routes from Cedar Breaks; year-round, with multiple lodges and campgrounds available. [Source: [Bryce Canyon Country Panguitch](https://www.brycecanyoncountry.com/panguitch-lake/), [AllTrips Panguitch Lake](https://www.allbryce.com/lakes_rivers_falls/panguitch_lake.php), [Visit Cedar City Recreation](https://visitcedarcity.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/recreation-areas/)]

- **Utah Shakespeare Festival** (3-4 hours per performance) - Tony Award-winning theater festival presenting three Shakespearean productions plus contemporary plays, welcoming over 100,000 guests annually with matinee performances at 2:00 p.m., evening performances at 8:00 p.m., and free nightly Greenshow at 7:10 p.m. during summer; located 30 minutes from Cedar Breaks in Cedar City; Southern Utah University campus, 351 W Center St, Cedar City, UT 84720 (37.6769608, -113.0838172); runs every summer and fall season. [Source: [Utah Shakespeare Festival](https://www.bard.org/), [Visit Cedar City Shakespeare](https://visitcedarcity.com/shakespeare-festival/), [TripAdvisor Shakespeare](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60765-d627045-Reviews-Utah_Shakespeare_Festival-Cedar_City_Utah.html)]
