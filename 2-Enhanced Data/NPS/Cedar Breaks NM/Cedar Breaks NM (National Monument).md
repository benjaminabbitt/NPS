# Cedar Breaks NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Cedar Breaks Visitor Contact Station** - The NPS passport cancellation stamp is available at the newly opened Visitor Contact Station, which replaced the temporary information center in August 2024; 4730 UT-148, Brian Head, UT 84719 [37.6354, -112.8453]; Open June through October, daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (seasonal closure October through May due to snow); (435) 586-0787. [Source: [Cedar Breaks Information Center](https://www.nps.gov/places/cedar-breaks-information-center.htm), [Cedar Breaks Contact Us](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/contacts.htm), [Cedar Breaks Opening New Visitor Center](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/2024/08/cedar-breaks-national-monument-opening-new-visitor-center)]

**Note:** Call (435) 586-0787 to confirm current stamp locations and hours before visiting, as the monument experiences seasonal closures from October through May when Highway 148 is closed to automobiles.

## Key Activities

- **Point Supreme Overlook** (15-20 minutes) - Start your visit at the visitor center and nearby Point Supreme for a panoramic view of the 2,000-foot-deep natural amphitheater with its colorful hoodoos and rock formations; located at the central area of the monument along Highway 148; accessible whenever the scenic drive is open (late May to mid-November). [Source: [Cedar Breaks Scenic Drive](https://visitcedarcity.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/cedar-breaks/), [Point Supreme Overlook NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/point-supreme-overlook.htm)]

- **Complete Scenic Drive with All Five Overlooks** (30-45 minutes) - Drive the 6-mile Highway 148 through the monument, stopping at all five scenic overlooks from south to north: Spectra Point Overlook, Point Supreme Overlook, Sunset Overlook, Chessman Ridge Overlook, and North View Overlook, each offering unique perspectives of the amphitheater; Highway 148, Cedar Breaks National Monument; accessible late May to mid-November, closed in winter. [Source: [Cedar Breaks Scenic Drive Utah.com](https://www.utah.com/things-to-do/scenic-drives/cedar-breaks-scenic-drive/), [Visit Cedar City](https://visitcedarcity.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/cedar-breaks/)]

- **Sunset View Overlook** (15-20 minutes) - Drive north from Point Supreme to Sunset View for a closer view of the amphitheater and its colorful canyons, offering dramatic perspectives of the geological formations; located along Highway 148 north of the visitor center; accessible late May to mid-November. [Source: [Cedar Breaks Scenic Drive](https://visitcedarcity.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/cedar-breaks/), [Visit Utah](https://www.visitutah.com/places-to-go/parks-outdoors/cedar-breaks-national-monument)]

- **Chessman Ridge Overlook** (15-20 minutes) - View the hoodoos directly below that look like massive stone chess pieces, providing one of the most distinctive perspectives in the monument; located along Highway 148; accessible late May to mid-November. [Source: [Cedar Breaks Scenic Drive](https://visitcedarcity.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/cedar-breaks/), [Visit Utah](https://www.visitutah.com/places-to-go/parks-outdoors/cedar-breaks-national-monument)]

- **North View Overlook** (15-20 minutes) - Visit the northernmost overlook for your best look into the amphitheater, offering expansive views of the colorful rock formations below; located at the northern end of Highway 148; accessible late May to mid-November. [Source: [Cedar Breaks Scenic Drive](https://visitcedarcity.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/cedar-breaks/), [Visit Utah](https://www.visitutah.com/places-to-go/parks-outdoors/cedar-breaks-national-monument)]

- **Sunset Trail** (30-60 minutes) - Walk this ADA-accessible and pet-friendly 1-mile one-way paved trail between the visitor center and Sunset View Overlook, lined with wildflowers in summer; trailhead at Cedar Breaks Visitor Center, 4730 UT-148, Brian Head, UT 84719 [37.6354, -112.8453]; accessible June through October during daylight hours. [Source: [Hike a Trail NPS](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/planyourvisit/hike-a-trail.htm), [Road Trip Ryan](https://www.roadtripryan.com/go/t/utah/zion/cedar-breaks-national-monument)]

- **Alpine Pond Trail** (1-2 hours) - Hike this 2-mile loop trail that circles an alpine pond at 10,000 feet elevation, offering amphitheater views and passing through shaded forest and meadows, considered an easy to moderate hike with 203 feet elevation gain; trailhead located along Highway 148 near the campground; accessible June through October. [Source: [Alpine Pond Trail AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/alpine-pond-trail--3), [Hike a Trail NPS](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/planyourvisit/hike-a-trail.htm), [Traveling Mooses](https://travelingmooses.com/alpine-pond-loop/)]

- **Spectra Point Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - Hike this easy 1.7-mile round trip trail with only 353 feet elevation change to reach Spectra Point overlook, offering dramatic amphitheater views without the full commitment of the Ramparts Trail; trailhead at Spectra Point parking area on Highway 148 [37.6178, -112.8451]; accessible June through October. [Source: [Spectra Point AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/spectra-point-and-rampart-overlook-trails), [American Southwest](https://www.americansouthwest.net/utah/cedar_breaks/spectra-point-ramparts-overlook-trail.html)]

- **Ramparts Overlook Trail** (2-2.5 hours) - Complete the full 3.8-4 mile round trip hike to Ramparts Overlook, the most dramatic trail in the monument with 849 feet elevation gain, descending 430 feet from Spectra Point along the rim for stunning amphitheater views; trailhead at Spectra Point parking area on Highway 148 [37.6178, -112.8451]; accessible June through October; considered moderate. [Source: [Spectra Point and Ramparts AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/spectra-point-and-rampart-overlook-trails), [Grand Canyon Trust](https://www.grandcanyontrust.org/hikes/cpe-spectra-point-ramparts-overlook-trail/), [Road Trip Ryan](https://www.roadtripryan.com/go/t/utah/zion/cedar-breaks-national-monument)]

- **Wildflower Viewing** (variable, incorporated into overlooks and trails) - Experience the peak wildflower bloom during early July, with 260 species of wildflowers at 10,000 feet elevation including cushion phlox, kittentails, and aspen bluebells, some found nowhere else in the world; flower-filled meadows surround the amphitheater throughout the summer; throughout the monument along trails and overlooks; best viewing July 7-16 during annual Wildflower Festival. [Source: [Wildflower Festival NPS](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/planyourvisit/2025-wildflower-festival.htm), [Visit Utah Summertime](https://www.visitutah.com/articles/sunny-skies-epic-wildflowers-hoodoos-cedar-breaks), [Visit Cedar City](https://visitcedarcity.com/blog/post/cedar-breaks-national-monument-wildflower-festival/)]

- **Night Sky Programs and Stargazing** (2-3 hours) - Join free ranger-led stargazing parties on Friday and Saturday nights through mid-October to explore nebulae, constellations, and planets through the park's powerful telescopes at this protected Dark Sky park at over 10,000 feet elevation; Point Supreme Overlook; programs typically run from dusk until 2-3 hours later; free; reservations recommended. [Source: [Visit Utah](https://www.visitutah.com/places-to-go/parks-outdoors/cedar-breaks-national-monument), [Utah's Adventure Family](https://www.utahsadventurefamily.com/cedar-breaks-national-monument/), [Things to Do NPS](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Rattlesnake Creek Trail** (2-3 days) - Embark on this challenging 12.8-mile, two- to three-day backpacking hike in the Ashdown Gorge Wilderness that winds down into the amphitheater itself, offering a completely different perspective from below; trailhead at Rattlesnake Trail adjacent to the North Entrance of Cedar Breaks National Monument; accessible summer months; requires backcountry permit. [Source: [Things to Do NPS](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Brian Head Tourism](https://brianheadtown.utah.gov/places-to-go/)]

- **Ancient Bristlecone Pines** (30-45 minutes, incorporated into trails) - Discover ancient bristlecone pine trees that have been alive since the last millennium, some of the oldest living organisms on Earth, found along the trails including the Alpine Pond area; accessible along various trails June through October. [Source: [Visit Cedar City](https://visitcedarcity.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/cedar-breaks/), [Utah's Adventure Family](https://www.utahsadventurefamily.com/cedar-breaks-national-monument/)]

- **Winter Activities: Snowshoeing and Cross-Country Skiing** (2-4 hours) - Experience Cedar Breaks in winter when the monument is open for snowshoe hiking, cross-country skiing, and snowmobiling, with free guided snowshoe tours available; access via Highway 148 when snow conditions permit; winter day-use yurt staffed on weekends by volunteers offers free hot chocolate. [Source: [Visit Utah](https://www.visitutah.com/places-to-go/parks-outdoors/cedar-breaks-national-monument), [Tourist Secrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/utah/mysteries-of-utahs-cedar-breaks/), [Utah's Adventure Family](https://www.utahsadventurefamily.com/cedar-breaks-national-monument/)]

- **The Yurt Explorer Activities** (30-60 minutes) - Visit the winter yurt or summer visitor center for hands-on activities and Junior Ranger programs that provide interactive educational experiences about the monument's geology, ecology, and history; located at the visitor center area; accessible year-round with seasonal variations. [Source: [Tourist Secrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/utah/mysteries-of-utahs-cedar-breaks/)]

- **Wildflower Festival Ranger-Led Walks** (1-3 hours per walk) - Join ranger-led wildflower walks of varying length and difficulty during the annual festival (July 7-16), learning about the 260 species of wildflowers including rare species found nowhere else; multiple walks offered daily throughout the monument; free with park admission. [Source: [Wildflower Festival NPS](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/planyourvisit/2025-wildflower-festival.htm), [Wildflower Festival and Stargazing](https://www.nps.gov/cebr/learn/news/wildflower-festival-and-stargazing.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Brian Head Resort Summer Activities** (2-4 hours, 5 miles) - Enjoy mountain biking, the Brian Head Family Adventure Trail, Cliff Hanger Climbing Wall, Peak Shot Bungee Trampoline, Ridge Runner Zip Line, Avalanche Summer Tubing, Crooked Arrow Archery, and Navajo Vistas Disc Golf Course at this alpine resort at 10,000 feet elevation, just minutes from Cedar Breaks; 329 S Highway 143, Brian Head, UT 84719 [37.7024, -112.8493]; summer activities typically operate June through September, hours vary by activity; (866) 930-1010. [Source: [Brian Head Summer Activities](https://www.brianhead.com/summer-activities/), [Brian Head Utah Visit Utah](https://www.visitutah.com/places-to-go/cities-and-towns/brianhead)]

- **Navajo Lake Recreation Area** (2-4 hours, 14 miles) - Fish for rainbow, brook, German, and splake trout at this 3.5-mile-long lake at over 9,000 feet elevation where water dramatically disappears into sinkholes, or enjoy boating, swimming, camping, and hiking the Virgin River Rim Trail with panoramic views of Zion National Park; located on Highway 14 approximately 25 miles east of Cedar City and 14 miles from Cedar Breaks; open summer season (typically May through October), ice fishing in winter requires snowmobile access; boat rentals available at Navajo Lake Lodge. [Source: [Navajo Lake Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/273822), [Navajo Lake AllBryce](https://www.allbryce.com/lakes_rivers_falls/navajo_lake.php), [Visit Cedar City Recreation](https://visitcedarcity.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/recreation-areas/)]

- **Kolob Canyons (Zion National Park)** (2-3 hours, 42 miles/45 minutes) - Drive the scenic 5-mile Kolob Canyons Road through red rock canyons to reach overlooks of massive sandstone cliffs and finger canyons, offering a completely different geological perspective than the main Zion Canyon; located at Exit 40 on Interstate 15, 17 miles south of Cedar City; Kolob Canyons Visitor Center open daily 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (extended hours in summer); (435) 772-3256; requires Zion National Park entrance fee. [Source: [Kolob Canyons NPS](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/kolob-canyons.htm), [Visit Cedar City Kolob Guide](https://visitcedarcity.com/blog/post/kolob-canyons-a-quick-guide/), [My Utah Parks](https://www.myutahparks.com/road-trips/scenic-drives/kolob-canyon-scenic-drive/)]

- **Utah Shakespeare Festival** (3-4 hours including pre-show activities, 28 miles) - Experience world-class theater at this Tony Award-winning festival featuring Shakespeare and other classic plays in three grand theaters, with free nightly Greenshow performances, backstage tours, and play seminars; Southern Utah University campus, 351 W Center St, Cedar City, UT 84720 [37.6775, -113.0618]; performances June through October, matinees at 2:00 PM, evening shows at 8:00 PM, free Greenshow at 7:10 PM in summer; ticket prices vary; (800) 752-9849. [Source: [Utah Shakespeare Festival](https://www.bard.org/), [Visit Cedar City Shakespeare](https://visitcedarcity.com/shakespeare-festival/), [Visit Utah](https://www.visitutah.com/things-to-do/events/utah-shakespeare-festival)]

- **Dixie National Forest Scenic Byway (Highway 14)** (1-2 hours, connects to Cedar Breaks) - Drive the Markagunt High Plateau Scenic Byway along Highway 14, experiencing stunning views of red spires and cliffs across 170 miles of southern Utah forest, connecting Cedar City to Long Valley Junction via Navajo Lake; begins in Cedar City and connects to Highway 148 leading to Cedar Breaks; accessible spring through fall, some seasonal closures in winter. [Source: [Cedar City Attractions](https://www.visitutah.com/places-to-go/cities-and-towns/cedar-city), [Visit Cedar City](https://visitcedarcity.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/cedar-breaks/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 4-6 hours for a comprehensive visit including the scenic drive with all overlooks (30-45 minutes), one or two moderate hikes such as Alpine Pond Trail (1-2 hours) and Spectra Point (1-1.5 hours), wildflower viewing, and time at the visitor center for the cancellation stamp and exhibits (30-60 minutes). Full-day visitors adding the complete Ramparts Overlook hike and attending a stargazing program could spend 8-10 hours. [Source: [Visit Cedar City](https://visitcedarcity.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/cedar-breaks/), [Road Trip Ryan](https://www.roadtripryan.com/go/t/utah/zion/cedar-breaks-national-monument), [Visit Utah](https://www.visitutah.com/places-to-go/parks-outdoors/cedar-breaks-national-monument)]
