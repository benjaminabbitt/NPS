# El Morro NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **El Morro Visitor Center** (HC 61 Box 43, Ramah, NM 87321 (35.038380, -108.348320); Winter hours (October 19-May 23): Thursday-Monday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM (closed Tuesday-Wednesday), Summer hours (May 24-October 17): Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; 505-783-4226 x801)

**Note:** Call 505-783-4226 x801 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/elmo/planyourvisit/visitorcenter.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/elmo/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Inscription Loop Trail** (45-50 minutes) - A paved half-mile loop trail that takes visitors past the pool at the base of the 200-foot sandstone bluff and hundreds of inscriptions left by Spanish conquistadors, American pioneers, and pre-historical petroglyphs carved by Ancestral Puebloans; trailhead begins at El Morro Visitor Center, HC 61 Box 43, Ramah, NM 87321 (35.038380, -108.348320); Winter: Thursday-Monday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM (arrive by 4:00 PM), Summer: Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM (arrive by 4:00 PM); 505-783-4226 x801 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/elmo/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60800-d102820-Reviews-El_Morro_National_Monument-Ramah_New_Mexico.html), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/getaway-elmo.htm)]

- **Headland Trail** (2 hours) - A moderate 2-mile loop trail that includes the Inscription Loop Trail and continues climbing 200 feet to the mesa top, leading to the ruins of Atsinna Pueblo (which flourished from 1275-1350 CE with over 300 rooms) and offering panoramic views of the Zuni Mountains, El Malpais, and the high desert beyond; trailhead begins at El Morro Visitor Center, HC 61 Box 43, Ramah, NM 87321 (35.038380, -108.348320); Winter: Thursday-Monday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM (arrive by 3:00 PM), Summer: Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM (arrive by 3:00 PM); 505-783-4226 x801 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/elmo/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [Source](https://www.americansouthwest.net/new_mexico/el-morro/national-monument.html), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/elmo/learn/historyculture/atsinna.htm)]

- **Visitor Center Museum and Video** (30 minutes) - Explore exhibits covering over 700 years of human history in the El Morro area and watch a 15-minute introductory video about the site's cultural and natural heritage; park rangers and volunteers available to answer questions and provide orientation; El Morro Visitor Center, HC 61 Box 43, Ramah, NM 87321 (35.038380, -108.348320); Winter: Thursday-Monday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Summer: Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; 505-783-4226 x801 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/elmo/planyourvisit/visitorcenter.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/elmo/planyourvisit/indooractivities.htm)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (30-60 minutes) - Complete activities in the Junior Ranger book to learn about El Morro's important history and natural environment, then show a ranger to earn a badge; books available at El Morro Visitor Center, HC 61 Box 43, Ramah, NM 87321 (35.038380, -108.348320); Winter: Thursday-Monday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Summer: Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; 505-783-4226 x801 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/elmo/learn/kidsyouth/junior-ranger.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Atsinna Pueblo Hand and Toe Holds** (included in Headland Trail time) - While descending from the Atsinna Pueblo ruins, look closely for hand- and toe-holds that the Puebloans carved into the rock to bring them down to the pool at the base of the canyon; accessible via Headland Trail from El Morro Visitor Center, HC 61 Box 43, Ramah, NM 87321 (35.038380, -108.348320); Winter: Thursday-Monday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM (arrive by 3:00 PM), Summer: Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM (arrive by 3:00 PM); 505-783-4226 x801 [[Source](https://www.outdoorsy.com/guide/el-morro-national-monument)]

- **Lava Tube Cave Exploration** (2-4 hours) - Explore lava tube caves with fascinating geology and hidden ice formations using a free caving permit (available at El Morro Visitor Center or El Malpais Visitor Center) and proper equipment; permit valid for up to seven days and allows unguided exploration of currently accessible caves including Giant Ice and Lava Bomb; El Morro Visitor Center, HC 61 Box 43, Ramah, NM 87321 (35.038380, -108.348320); Winter: Thursday-Monday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Summer: Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; 505-783-4226 x801 for cave conditions [[Source](https://www.elmorro-nm.com/activities), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/elma/planyourvisit/permits.htm), [Source](https://home.nps.gov/elma/planyourvisit/caving.htm)]

- **Campground Dark Sky Viewing** (1-2 hours, evening) - Experience the monument's dark night skies from the nine-site campground, offering excellent stargazing opportunities away from light pollution; El Morro Campground (near Visitor Center), HC 61 Box 43, Ramah, NM 87321 (35.038380, -108.348320); open year-round, first-come first-served (no reservations), campground gates locked at sunset; 505-783-4226 x801 [[Source](https://www.outdoorsy.com/guide/el-morro-national-monument), [Source](https://thedyrt.com/camping/new-mexico/new-mexico-el-morro-national-monument)]

- **Picnic Area** (flexible timing) - Relax at the picnic area near the visitor center with four picnic tables and a grill, perfect for a meal before or after hiking; located near El Morro Visitor Center, HC 61 Box 43, Ramah, NM 87321 (35.038380, -108.348320); accessible during monument hours; 505-783-4226 x801 [[Source](https://www.outdoorsy.com/guide/el-morro-national-monument)]

## Also Nearby

- **Ancient Way Cafe** (1-2 hours, 2 miles east) - Dine at one of National Geographic's best restaurants in New Mexico, featuring astonishingly good, fresh, and locally-sourced food including lamb gyro, huevos rancheros, brisket, and more; located one mile east of El Morro entrance; 4018 Ice Caves Road, Ramah, NM 87321; Dine In: Thursday-Sunday 9:00 AM-3:00 PM, Grab and Go: Wednesday-Sunday 8:00 AM-4:00 PM; 505-783-4612 [[Source](https://www.elmorro-nm.com/ancient-way-cafe), [Source](https://www.yelp.com/biz/el-morro-rv-park-cabins-and-ancient-way-cafe-ramah), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g28481621-d2252000-Reviews-Ancient_Way_Cafe-El_Morro_New_Mexico.html)]

- **Ice Cave and Bandera Volcano** (1 hour, 25 miles east) - Explore a privately-operated lava tube ice cave with glowing ice formations and hike to Bandera Volcano crater via two self-guided trails (Ice Cave trail: 20 minutes round trip, Bandera Volcano trail: 40 minutes round trip); located halfway between El Malpais and El Morro on Highway 53; 12000 Ice Caves Road, Grants, NM 87020; Seasonal: March 1-October (closed for winter season), Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM when open; 505-783-4303 or 888-423-2283 [[Source](https://www.icecaves.com/), [Source](https://www.icecaves.com/attraction), [Source](https://www.newmexico.org/listing/ice-cave-and-bandera-volcano/9069/)]

- **El Malpais National Monument** (3-6 hours, 25 miles east) - Explore extensive lava flows, hiking trails including the historic 7.5-mile one-way Zuni-Acoma Trail, El Calderon cinder cone and lava tubes, scenic overlooks, and La Ventana Natural Arch (largest accessible natural arch in New Mexico); access from Grants, NM via Highway 53 or Highway 117; El Malpais Visitor Center: I-40 exit 85, Grants, NM; varying hours, contact 505-876-2783 for current information [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/elma/index.htm), [Source](https://www.frommers.com/destinations/el-malpais-and-el-morro-national-monuments/planning-a-trip/), [Source](https://sightdoing.net/roadtrip-albuquerque-el-morro-el-malpais/)]

- **Zuni Pueblo** (2-4 hours, 34 miles southwest) - Visit the largest of New Mexico's 19 pueblos, settled in 1699 and celebrated for producing silver jewelry, stone fetishes, paintings, and pottery; tour options include Middle Village walking tours, archaeology tours including ancestral villages like Hawikku, artist studio demonstrations, and historic farming village tours; Zuni Visitor & Arts Center, 1239 NM-53, Zuni, NM 87327; Monday-Friday 8:00 AM-5:00 PM (lobby 9:00 AM-5:00 PM), weekends by advanced notice for tours; 505-782-7238 [[Source](https://newmexiconomad.com/el-morro/), [Source](https://www.visitgallup.com/things-to-do/nearby-day-trips/zuni-pueblo/), [Source](http://zunitourism.com/about-zuni/visiting/), [Source](https://www.newmexico.org/listing/zuni-visitor-&-arts-center/6700/)]
