# San Antonio Missions NHP (National Historical Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-visitor-center-at-mission-san-jos"></a>**Visitor Center at Mission San José** (2202 Roosevelt Avenue, San Antonio, TX 78210 (29.362068, -98.478909); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 210-932-1001) [Source: [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/saan/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/saan/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [NPS Contact](https://www.nps.gov/saan/contacts.htm)]

**Note:** Two cancellation stamps are available when visiting San Antonio Missions - one for the park, and the other for El Camino Real de los Tejas National Historic Trail on which all the missions are located. Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [America's National Parks - Passport Locations](https://americasnationalparks.org/passport-to-your-national-parks/passport-cancellation-locations/)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-mission-san-jos-tour"></a>**Mission San José Tour** (1.5-2 hours) - Explore the "Queen of the Missions," featuring the iconic Rose Window sculpted in 1775, one of the finest examples of baroque architecture in North America; visitor center with rotating film "Gente de Razón" shown hourly 10:00 AM-4:00 PM; ranger-led tours daily at 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM; 6701 San Jose Drive, San Antonio, TX 78214 (29.3573, -98.4745); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/saan/planyourvisit/plan-your-visit.htm), [Visit San Antonio](https://www.visitsanantonio.com/things-to-do/san-antonio-missions/), [NPS Mission San José](https://www.nps.gov/saan/planyourvisit/sanjose.htm), [Texas Monthly - Rose Window](https://www.texasmonthly.com/the-culture/texas-primer-the-rose-window/)]

- <a id="activity-mission-concepci-n-tour"></a>**Mission Concepción Tour** (30 minutes) - Visit the oldest unrestored stone church in America, featuring original frescoes and the best-preserved mission; active Catholic parish with Mass schedule Sunday 10:00 AM (Bilingual) and 11:00 AM (Spanish); 807 Mission Rd, San Antonio, TX 78210 (29.391039, -98.491132); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Mission Concepción](https://www.nps.gov/saan/planyourvisit/concepcion.htm), [NPS Church Information](https://www.nps.gov/saan/planyourvisit/churchinfo.htm), [Waze Directions](https://www.waze.com/live-map/directions/us/tx/san-antonio/mission-concepcion)]

- <a id="activity-mission-san-juan-capistrano-tour"></a>**Mission San Juan Capistrano Tour** (30 minutes) - Discover the smaller, more intimate mission that served a primarily agricultural community; active Catholic parish with Mass Sunday 9:00 AM and 11:00 AM (Spanish); 9101 Graf Rd, San Antonio, TX 78214 (29.332458, -98.455431); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Mission San Juan](https://www.nps.gov/saan/planyourvisit/sanjuan.htm), [NPS Church Information](https://www.nps.gov/saan/planyourvisit/churchinfo.htm), [AllTopSights Mission San Juan](https://www.alltopsights.com/attraction-mission-san-juan-texas-tx/)]

- <a id="activity-mission-espada-tour"></a>**Mission Espada Tour** (30 minutes) - Explore the southernmost and most remote mission, featuring Spanish colonial architecture and peaceful grounds; 10040 Espada Rd, San Antonio, TX 78214 (29.318236, -98.451222); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Mission Espada](https://www.nps.gov/saan/planyourvisit/espada.htm), [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/usa/texas/san-antonio/attractions/mission-espada/a/poi-sig/1183534/362179), [AllTopSights Mission Espada](https://www.alltopsights.com/attraction-mission-espada-texas-tx/)]

- <a id="activity-mission-hike-bike-trail"></a>**Mission Hike & Bike Trail** (4-5 hours full trail, or shorter sections) - Walk or bike the 8-mile Mission Reach trail connecting all four missions along the San Antonio River; paved, relatively flat trail suitable for all ages; each mission approximately 3 miles apart; trail is out-and-back, not a loop; Trail access at all four missions; Daily during park hours [Source: [NPS Hike & Bike Trail](https://www.nps.gov/saan/planyourvisit/hikebike.htm), [AllTrails Mission Trail](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/texas/san-antonio-mission-trail), [San Antonio River Authority](https://www.sariverauthority.org/parks-trails/san-antonio-river-walk-mission-reach/)]

- <a id="activity-self-guided-audio-tours"></a>**Self-Guided Audio Tours** (varies by mission) - Free cell phone audio tours available at all missions providing historical context and stories; accessible during park operating hours; All four missions; Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [Visit San Antonio](https://www.visitsanantonio.com/things-to-do/san-antonio-missions/), [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/saan/planyourvisit/plan-your-visit.htm)]

- <a id="activity-church-service-attendance"></a>**Church Service Attendance** (1 hour) - Attend an active Catholic Mass at any of the four missions, which continue to serve as parish churches with regular services, weddings, and baptisms; all churches open to visitors during regular park hours except during special services; Various times at all four missions; See church schedules above [Source: [NPS Church Information](https://www.nps.gov/saan/planyourvisit/churchinfo.htm), [Visit San Antonio](https://www.visitsanantonio.com/things-to-do/san-antonio-missions/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-espada-aqueduct"></a>**Espada Aqueduct** (15-20 minutes) - Visit the oldest Spanish aqueduct in the United States, built 1740-1745 and still functioning today; part of the historic acequia (irrigation) system; excellent photo opportunity; 9045 Espada Rd, San Antonio, TX 78214 (29.33288, -98.46158); Daily 9:00 AM-4:30 PM [Source: [NPS Espada Aqueduct](https://www.nps.gov/places/espada-aqueduct.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60956-d10079192-Reviews-The_Espada_Aqueduct-San_Antonio_Texas.html), [SATXtoday](https://satxtoday.6amcity.com/parks-and-recreation/espada-aqueduct-and-dam-in-san-antonio-tx), [HMDB Historical Marker](https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=195503)]

- <a id="gem-mission-reach-paddling"></a>**Mission Reach Paddling** (3-5 hours) - Paddle the 8-mile Mission Reach trail on the San Antonio River; stand-up paddleboards and kayaks allowed on certain sections; experience the missions from a unique water perspective; Various access points along Mission Reach; Check water levels and conditions [Source: [TPWD Mission Reach Paddling Trail](https://tpwd.texas.gov/boating/paddling-trails/south-texas-plains/mission-reach/), [Visit San Antonio](https://www.visitsanantonio.com/blog/post/ultimate-guide-to-river-walk-neighborhoods/)]

- <a id="gem-rose-window-photography"></a>**Rose Window Photography** (30 minutes) - Capture photos of the famous Rose Window at Mission San José, sculpted in 1775 and considered one of the most photographed windows in the world; located on the south wall of the church sacristy; morning or late afternoon light recommended for dramatic side-lighting; Mission San José, 6701 San Jose Drive, San Antonio, TX 78214; Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [Mission San José Rose Window](https://missionsanjosechurch.org/rose-window), [Texas Monthly](https://www.texasmonthly.com/the-culture/texas-primer-the-rose-window/), [Texas History Portal](https://texashistory.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metapth460003/)]

- <a id="gem-mission-san-juan-peaceful-exploration"></a>**Mission San Juan Peaceful Exploration** (45 minutes) - Experience the most tranquil and authentic mission atmosphere at Mission San Juan, away from crowds; offers excellent photo opportunities and a more contemplative visit than the busier missions; 9101 Graf Rd, San Antonio, TX 78214; Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [Journée Mondiale - Hidden Gems](https://www.journee-mondiale.com/en/hidden-gems-of-san-antonio-4-stunning-mission-ruins-that-rival-the-alamo-and-why-youve-never-heard-of-them/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-japanese-tea-garden"></a>**Japanese Tea Garden** (1 hour) - Stroll through this beautiful year-round garden featuring koi ponds, a 60-foot waterfall, stone bridges, and lush landscapes in the former quarry; restaurant Jingu House offers refreshments; free admission; located in Brackenridge Park near San Antonio Zoo; 3853 N St Mary's St, San Antonio, TX 78212 (29.4607, -98.4772); Daily 7:00 AM-5:00 PM; 210-559-3148 [Source: [City of San Antonio](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/Parks/Parks-Facilities/Buildings-Centers/Japanese-Tea-Garden), [Visit San Antonio](https://www.visitsanantonio.com/listing/japanese-tea-garden/3058/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60956-d108031-Reviews-Japanese_Tea_Gardens-San_Antonio_Texas.html), [HMDB Historical Marker](https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=214787)]

- <a id="nearby-san-antonio-zoo"></a>**San Antonio Zoo** (3-4 hours) - Explore one of the nation's largest zoos with diverse animal exhibits; located in Brackenridge Park; general admission $31.99 adults, $27.99 children ages 3-11, under 3 free; discounts for military/seniors; recommend purchasing tickets online in advance; free parking during zoo hours; 3903 N St Mary's St, San Antonio, TX 78212; Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, last admission 4:00 PM [Source: [San Antonio Zoo](https://sazoo.org/visit/), [Rosct Guide](https://www.rosct.com/usa/texas/san-antonio/san-antonio-zoo/1816/), [San Antonio Zoo Guide](https://www.sanantoniothingstodo.com/san-antonio-zoo-texas-hours-tickets-prices-coupons-maps/)]

- <a id="nearby-witte-museum"></a>**Witte Museum** (2-3 hours) - Visit San Antonio's largest museum featuring natural science, Texas history, and regional culture exhibits; free admission Tuesdays 3:00 PM-6:00 PM (reservations encouraged); general admission $17 adults, $16 seniors/teens 13-18, $11 children 4-12, under 3 free; located in Brackenridge Park; 3801 Broadway St, San Antonio, TX 78209; Monday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM, Tuesday 10:00 AM-6:00 PM, Wednesday-Saturday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM-5:00 PM; closed third Monday in October, Thanksgiving, Christmas, Easter; 210-357-1900 [Source: [Witte Museum](https://www.wittemuseum.org/plan-your-visit/), [Tour Texas](https://www.tourtexas.com/attractions/the-witte-museum-hours-admission-prices), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60956-d103611-Reviews-Witte_Museum-San_Antonio_Texas.html)]

- <a id="nearby-natural-bridge-caverns"></a>**Natural Bridge Caverns** (3-4 hours including tour and surface attractions) - Explore Texas's largest commercial caverns on a 75-minute guided Discovery Tour; additional surface attractions include maze, zip lines, and ropes course; Discovery Tour $28-32 depending on date (book online for discount); tours depart every 10-30 minutes; located 30 minutes north of San Antonio in Texas Hill Country; 26495 Natural Bridge Caverns Rd, San Antonio, TX 78266 (29.6923121, -98.3427751); Daily 10:00 AM-4:00 PM (hours vary, extended in spring/summer), final tour 4:00 PM; closed New Year's Day, Easter, Thanksgiving, Christmas [Source: [Natural Bridge Caverns](https://naturalbridgecaverns.com/), [Tour Texas](https://www.tourtexas.com/destinations/natural-bridge-caverns-san-antonio-hours-ticket-prices), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60956-d169799-Reviews-Natural_Bridge_Caverns-San_Antonio_Texas.html), [Apple Maps](https://maps.apple.com/place?address=26495+Natural+Bridge+Caverns+Road)]
