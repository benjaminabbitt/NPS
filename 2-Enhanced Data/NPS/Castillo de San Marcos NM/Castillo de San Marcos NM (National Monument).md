# Castillo de San Marcos NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Castillo de San Marcos Visitor Center** - Cancellation stamps are available at the fort entrance (1 South Castillo Drive, Saint Augustine, FL 32084 [29.8978, -81.3116]; Open daily 9:00 AM - 5:15 PM except Thanksgiving and Christmas Day; 904-829-6506) [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/casa/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Contact Info](https://www.nps.gov/casa/contacts.htm), [Cancellation Locations PDF](https://americasnationalparks.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Cancellations-07-24.pdf)]

**Note:** Call 904-829-6506 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Self-Guided Fort Tour** (1-2 hours) - Explore the oldest masonry fortification in the continental United States at your own pace using a free brochure or the NPS mobile app; walk through historic storerooms surrounding the central courtyard featuring exhibits on the fort's 300+ year history, visit the parade ground, and access the gun deck for panoramic views of Matanzas Bay and downtown St. Augustine; 1 South Castillo Drive, Saint Augustine, FL 32084 [29.8978, -81.3116]; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:15 PM; $15 adults 16+, free for ages 15 and under [Source: [NPS Self-Guided Walking Tour](https://www.nps.gov/casa/planyourvisit/self-guided-walking-tour.htm), [Visit St. Augustine](https://www.visitstaugustine.com/thing-to-do/castillo-de-san-marcos), [Trolley Tours Guide](https://www.trolleytours.com/st-augustine/castillo-de-san-marcos), [NPPlan Touring Guide](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/florida-national-parks/at-a-glance-castillo-de-san-marcos-national-monument/touring-the-fort-castillo-de-san-marcos-national-monument/)]

- **Museum Exhibits in Historic Storerooms** (30-45 minutes) - View exhibits highlighting various chapters of the fort's long history displayed in original bombproof storerooms surrounding the central plaza, including displays on Spanish colonial life, military history, and the fort's construction using coquina stone; 1 South Castillo Drive, Saint Augustine, FL 32084 [29.8978, -81.3116]; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:15 PM [Source: [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/facility/10088440), [Trolley Tours](https://www.trolleytours.com/st-augustine/castillo-de-san-marcos), [NPPlan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/florida-national-parks/at-a-glance-castillo-de-san-marcos-national-monument/)]

- **Gun Deck Views** (15-30 minutes) - Climb stairs to the upper gun deck to see historic cannons and artillery pieces, take in spectacular views of Matanzas Bay and the historic St. Augustine waterfront, and explore the Sentry Box and Bell Tower; note that there is no handicap access to the gun deck; 1 South Castillo Drive, Saint Augustine, FL 32084 [29.8978, -81.3116]; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:15 PM [Source: [VoiceMap Self-Guided Tour](https://voicemap.me/tour/st-augustine-florida/castillo-de-san-marcos-a-self-guided-tour-of-st-augustine-s-old-fort), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g34599-d13555384-Reviews-Castillo_de_San_Marcos_National_Monument-St_Augustine_Florida.html), [Peachy Keenes](https://peachykeenes.com/castillo-de-san-marcos-with-kids/)]

- **Cannon Firing Demonstrations** (20-30 minutes per demonstration) - Watch costumed historians perform cannon and weaponry demonstrations on weekends, showcasing historic military life at the fort; demonstrations occur on Saturdays and Sundays at 10:30 AM, 11:30 AM, 1:30 PM, 2:30 PM, and 3:30 PM, weather and staff permitting; 1 South Castillo Drive, Saint Augustine, FL 32084 [29.8978, -81.3116]; Weekends only at scheduled times [Source: [Visit St. Augustine](https://www.visitstaugustine.com/thing-to-do/castillo-de-san-marcos), [U.S. News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/St_Augustine_FL/Things_To_Do/Castillo_de_San_Marcos_National_Monument_63358/), [Viator](https://www.viator.com/St-Augustine-attractions/Castillo-de-San-Marcos-National-Monument/overview/d823-a1948)]

- **Ranger-Led Interpretive Talks** (20-30 minutes per talk) - Attend ranger presentations on the fort's history and culture, offered throughout the day at 10:30 AM, 11:30 AM, 1:30 PM, 2:30 PM, and 3:30 PM Monday through Friday, weather and staff permitting; topics vary and schedules are posted daily in the courtyard and at the entrance; 1 South Castillo Drive, Saint Augustine, FL 32084 [29.8978, -81.3116]; Weekdays at scheduled times [Source: [Fun 4 Auggie Kids](https://fun4auggiekids.com/calendar/icalrepeat.detail/2025/01/02/216976/52/castillo-de-san-marcos-national-monument-ranger-programs), [Trolley Tours](https://www.trolleytours.com/st-augustine/castillo-de-san-marcos), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g34599-d13555384-Reviews-Castillo_de_San_Marcos_National_Monument-St_Augustine_Florida.html)]

- **Park Film** (25 minutes) - Watch a comprehensive film showcasing the history of the fort along with cannon and musket firing sequences, shown hourly throughout the day in the fort's theater; 1 South Castillo Drive, Saint Augustine, FL 32084 [29.8978, -81.3116]; Daily during operating hours [Source: [Visit St. Augustine](https://www.visitstaugustine.com/thing-to-do/castillo-de-san-marcos), [NPPlan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/florida-national-parks/at-a-glance-castillo-de-san-marcos-national-monument/), [National Park Obsessed](https://nationalparkobsessed.com/visit-to-castillo-de-san-marcos-national-monument/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Junior Ranger Program** (30-45 minutes) - Participate in the free Junior Ranger activity where children receive an age-appropriate book at the entrance, complete 6 pages including one in Spanish, and earn a Junior Ranger pin, diploma, and swearing-in ceremony; 1 South Castillo Drive, Saint Augustine, FL 32084 [29.8978, -81.3116]; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:15 PM [Source: [NPS Junior Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/casa/learn/kidsyouth/beajuniorranger.htm), [Coast2Coast With Kids](https://coast2coastwithkids.com/5-ways-to-explore-the-castillo-de-san-marcos-national-monument-in-st-augustine-florida/), [Junior Ranger Project Blog](http://juniorrangerproject.blogspot.com/2013/06/update-castillo-de-san-marcos-national.html)]

- **Lower Level Exploration** (20-30 minutes) - Walk through the often-overlooked lower level barracks to discover sleeping quarters, prison cells, storage rooms, and a chapel used by Spanish soldiers, including a fireplace taller than most people and historic graffiti on the walls; 1 South Castillo Drive, Saint Augustine, FL 32084 [29.8978, -81.3116]; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:15 PM [Source: [Coast2Coast With Kids](https://coast2coastwithkids.com/5-ways-to-explore-the-castillo-de-san-marcos-national-monument-in-st-augustine-florida/)]

- **Artillery Counting Activity** (15-20 minutes) - Engage in this overlooked self-guided activity by counting the number of artillery pieces on display throughout the fort, a fun way to explore every corner while learning about historic weaponry; 1 South Castillo Drive, Saint Augustine, FL 32084 [29.8978, -81.3116]; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:15 PM [Source: [Coast2Coast With Kids](https://coast2coastwithkids.com/5-ways-to-explore-the-castillo-de-san-marcos-national-monument-in-st-augustine-florida/)]

- **Re-enactor Presentations** (15-20 minutes) - Encounter period-dressed re-enactors around the fort who give historical weapons demonstrations and presentations about the life and experiences of Spanish colonists who lived at the Castillo; 1 South Castillo Drive, Saint Augustine, FL 32084 [29.8978, -81.3116]; Primarily weekends, weather and staff permitting [Source: [Coast2Coast With Kids](https://coast2coastwithkids.com/5-ways-to-explore-the-castillo-de-san-marcos-national-monument-in-st-augustine-florida/)]

## Also Nearby

- **St. Augustine Pirate & Treasure Museum** (1-2 hours, across the street) - Explore over 800 authentic pirate artifacts at this interactive museum located directly across from Castillo de San Marcos, featuring treasure displays, historical weapons, and immersive exhibits on the golden age of piracy; 12 S Castillo Drive, St. Augustine, FL 32084; Daily 10:00 AM - 7:00 PM except Christmas; Adults vary, children 4 and under free [Source: [Pirate Museum Visit Page](https://www.thepiratemuseum.com/visit), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g34599-d1965733-Reviews-St_Augustine_Pirate_Treasure_Museum-St_Augustine_Florida.html), [Trolley Tours](https://www.trolleytours.com/st-augustine/pirate-treasure-museum), [U.S. News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/St_Augustine_FL/Things_To_Do/St_Augustine_Pirate_Treasure_Museum_63361/)]

- **Colonial Quarter** (45 minutes - 1 hour, steps away) - Journey back in time to the 17th century at this living history museum featuring blacksmithing demonstrations, musket drills, and a 35-foot watchtower replica with interactive exhibits on colonial life; 14 S Castillo Drive (accessible from 33 St. George Street), St. Augustine, FL 32084 [29.8966, -81.3131]; Monday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, tours at 10:30 AM, 12:00 PM, 1:30 PM, and 3:00 PM; Closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day [Source: [Colonial Quarter Website](https://colonialquarter.com/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/colonial-quarter-st-augustine-2), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g34599-d3971799-Reviews-Colonial_Quarter-St_Augustine_Florida.html), [Trolley Tours](https://www.trolleytours.com/st-augustine/colonial-quarter)]

- **St. Augustine Lighthouse & Maritime Museum** (2-3 hours, 10 minutes drive) - Climb 219 steps to the top of this historic 165-foot lighthouse for spectacular views of the city and ocean, then explore maritime exhibits and the museum grounds; morning visits recommended for cooler, less crowded conditions; 100 Red Cox Road, Saint Augustine, FL 32080 [29.8854, -81.2883]; Daily 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM (extended to 7:00 PM in summer and holidays), closed Thanksgiving and Christmas; 904-829-0745 [Source: [Lighthouse Website](https://www.staugustinelighthouse.org/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/st-augustine-lighthouse-and-maritime-museum-saint-augustine), [Visit St. Augustine](https://www.visitstaugustine.com/thing-to-do/st-augustine-lighthouse-and-museum), [U.S. News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/St_Augustine_FL/Things_To_Do/St_Augustine_Lighthouse_Maritime_Museum_63359/)]

- **Fountain of Youth Archaeological Park** (1-2 hours, 5 minutes drive) - Explore 15 waterfront acres at the legendary site where Ponce de León purportedly landed in 1513, featuring archaeological exhibits, planetarium shows, native Timucuan village, and the famous spring; 11 Magnolia Avenue, St. Augustine, FL 32084 [29.9072, -81.3154]; Daily 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM, last tickets at 5:00 PM, closed Christmas (early closing on Christmas Eve); Adults $22.95, Seniors $20.95, Children 6-12 $9.95, Under 5 free; 904-829-3168 [Source: [Fountain of Youth Website](https://www.fountainofyouthflorida.com/), [Visit Page](https://www.fountainofyouthflorida.com/visit/), [Visit St. Augustine](https://www.visitstaugustine.com/thing-to-do/fountain-youth), [U.S. News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/St_Augustine_FL/Things_To_Do/Ponce_de_Leons_Fountain_of_Youth_Archaeological_Park_63365/)]

- **St. Augustine Alligator Farm Zoological Park** (3-4 hours, 12 minutes drive) - Visit every species of crocodilian in the world at this 125-year-old zoological park, featuring alligator shows, zip-lining, and exceptional bird rookery photography opportunities during nesting season (Valentine's Day through July); 999 Anastasia Blvd., Saint Augustine, FL 32084; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (extended to 6:00 PM Memorial Day - Labor Day); Adults 12+ $35.99, Children 3-11 $20.99; 904-824-3337 [Source: [Alligator Farm Website](https://alligatorfarm.com/tickets-info/location-hours/), [Visit St. Augustine](https://www.visitstaugustine.com/thing-to-do/st-augustine-alligator-farm), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/st-augustine-alligator-farm-zoological-park-saint-augustine), [U.S. News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/St_Augustine_FL/Things_To_Do/St_Augustine_Alligator_Farm_Zoological_Park_65818/)]

- **St. Augustine Beach** (30-45 minutes relaxation time, 12 minutes drive) - Relax at this public beach approximately 5 miles from downtown, offering sandy shores, ocean views, and a break from historic site touring; St. Augustine Beach, FL 32080; Open daily; Free [Source: [Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Saint-Augustine/Saint-Augustine-Beach), [Trippy Distance](https://www.trippy.com/distance/St-Augustine-Beach-to-Saint-Augustine), [U.S. News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/St_Augustine_FL/Things_To_Do/St_Augustine_Beach_63366/)]
