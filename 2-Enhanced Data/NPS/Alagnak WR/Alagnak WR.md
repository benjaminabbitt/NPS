# Alagnak WR (Wild River)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **King Salmon Interagency Visitor Center** - The NPS passport cancellation stamp for Alagnak WR is available at this interagency facility next to the King Salmon Airport; 1000 Silver Street, Building 603, King Salmon, AK 99613 (located across from the Southwest Alaska Vocational & Education Center on King Salmon Air Force Base); Open year-round 8:00 AM–5:00 PM; 907-246-4250. [Source: [NPS Alagnak Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/alag/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [NPS Alagnak Contacts](https://www.nps.gov/alag/contacts.htm)] [f0eee3]

**Note:** Alagnak Wild River is accessible only by floatplane from King Salmon. The passport stamp is located at the visitor center in King Salmon, not within the wild river corridor itself. For those unable to visit in person, a virtual stamp option is available by downloading the image from the NPS website. Call to confirm current stamp availability before visiting. [Source: [Alaska.org NPS Passport Tips](https://www.alaska.org/advice/tips-for-national-park-passport-stampers-visiting-alaska)]

## Key Activities

- **Multi-Day River Float Trip (5-6 days)** - Float the 67-76 miles of designated wild river through pristine wilderness, experiencing Class I to III rapids while fishing for all five Pacific salmon species, rainbow trout, arctic char, arctic grayling, and northern pike; trips typically begin at Nonvianuk Lake (67 miles) or Kukaklek Lake (75 miles) accessible via floatplane from King Salmon, with takeout on the lower 30 miles of river; no NPS facilities exist along the river. The 6-day adventure covers approximately 76 miles total, with the upper section spanning 11-19 miles (depending on put-in), a 15-mile braided middle section ideal for wading and fishing on gravel bars, and a final 41-mile forested lower section with good tent sites. [Source: [NPS Alagnak Rafting](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/raft-or-kayak-the-alagnak-river.htm), [Alaska.org Alagnak River](https://www.alaska.org/detail/alagnak-river), [Kenai Backcountry Adventures](https://kenaibackcountryadventures.com/adventures/alagnak-wild-river)] [97551f]

- **Lodge-Based Fly Fishing (9-10 hours daily)** - Stay at one of several fishing lodges on private land within the river corridor and enjoy daily guided fishing trips; lodges typically offer 9-10 hours of guided fishing per day with boats departing as early as 7:00 AM; 3-7 night packages available ranging from $5,000-$10,500 per person depending on season and duration. [Source: [Angler's Alibi](https://www.anglersalibi.com/the-alagnak-river.html), [MyAlaskanFishingTrip](https://www.myalaskanfishingtrip.com/destinations/visit/alagnak-river), [Alaska Rainbow Adventures](https://www.akrainbow.com/)] [1b09fe]

- **Wildlife Viewing (ongoing during float trips)** - Observe brown bears feasting on spawning salmon, moose browsing along riverbanks in wet sedge tundra, bald eagles perched in spruce trees, river otters, beavers, and ospreys while floating down the river; wildlife viewing is integrated into multi-day river trips with dawn and dusk offering the best opportunities; July through November is prime time for bears concentrating along salmon spawning streams. [Source: [NPS Alagnak Rafting](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/raft-or-kayak-the-alagnak-river.htm), [Rivers.gov Alagnak](https://www.rivers.gov/apps/river/alagnak)] [00a3f8]

## Hidden Gems

- **Tundra Hiking from Headwater Lakes (2-4 hours)** - Explore the tundra landscape on foot with hikes accessible from the upper section between the headwater lakes (Kukaklek and Nonvianuk) and the confluence; this 11-19 mile section offers dry tundra suitable for exploration beyond the river corridor itself. [Source: [Alaska.org Alagnak River](https://www.alaska.org/detail/alagnak-river)] [f2205a]

- **Braided Channel Exploration (flexible timing within multi-day trips)** - Wade and explore the 15-mile middle section where the river frequently changes channels through braided waterways (giving the river its Yup'ik name meaning "making mistakes"); this section provides excellent opportunities for fishing from gravel bars and experiencing the dynamic river morphology. [Source: [Alaska.org Alagnak River](https://www.alaska.org/detail/alagnak-river), [NPS Alagnak Rafting](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/raft-or-kayak-the-alagnak-river.htm)] [f98a38]

## Also Nearby

- **Brooks Falls Bear Viewing at Katmai National Park (4-6 hours viewing time, full day trip)** - Witness brown bears fishing for salmon at the iconic Brooks Falls, one of Alaska's most famous wildlife spectacles; located 30 miles from King Salmon via 30-minute floatplane flight or 45-minute water taxi ($450 round-trip per person); all visitors must attend a mandatory 20-minute bear safety briefing; viewing platform time at the falls is limited to 30 minutes during peak season, but unlimited time allowed at other platforms along Brooks River; full day trips from Anchorage depart 7:00-8:00 AM and return 6:30-7:00 PM. [Source: [Alaska Itinerary Brooks Falls](https://alaskaitinerary.com/bear-viewing-brooks-falls/), [Katmai Air](https://katmaiair.com/bear-viewing/brooks-falls-day-tours/), [NPS Katmai Nearby Attractions](https://www.nps.gov/katm/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm)] [d31784]

- **Aniakchak National Monument Flightseeing (4-5 hours including flight time)** - Explore one of the most remote and least-visited units in the National Park System, featuring a massive volcanic caldera; accessible via 90-minute floatplane flight from King Salmon (150 miles); comprehensive flightseeing tours typically take 4-5 hours and can include both the Monument and Preserve; the caldera contains Surprise Lake and the Aniakchak River's dramatic exit through "The Gates." [Source: [Travel Alaska Aniakchak](https://www.travelalaska.com/destinations/parks-public-lands/aniakchak-national-monument-preserve), [Bush Plane Parks Alaska](https://sites.google.com/site/bushplaneparksalaska/home/alaska-fly-in-parks/aniakchak), [NPS Aniakchak Directions](https://www.nps.gov/ania/planyourvisit/directions.htm)] [75dae7]

- **McNeil River State Game Sanctuary Bear Viewing (4 days on-site, permit required)** - Experience the world's largest known gathering of brown bears (up to 74 observed during peak salmon runs in late July); accessible via floatplane from King Salmon; highly competitive permit lottery by Alaska Department of Fish and Game allows only 10 visitors per day for 4-day periods between June 7 and August 25; applications must be received by March 1. [Source: [Alaska.org King Salmon](https://www.alaska.org/destination/king-salmon), [Alaska DFG McNeil River](https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/index.cfm?adfg=viewinglocations.mcneilriver)] [8e84c2]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 5-6 days for a comprehensive multi-day float trip, or 3-7 days for a lodge-based fishing experience. Day visitors typically spend 1-2 hours at the King Salmon Visitor Center for exhibits and stamp collection only, as the wild river corridor itself requires multi-day commitment. [Source: [Alaska.org Alagnak River](https://www.alaska.org/detail/alagnak-river), [Angler's Alibi](https://www.anglersalibi.com/the-alagnak-river.html)]
