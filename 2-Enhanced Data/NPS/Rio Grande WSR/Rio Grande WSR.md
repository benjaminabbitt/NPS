# Rio Grande WSR (Wild and Scenic River)

**Note:** Rio Grande Wild and Scenic River encompasses multiple sections managed by NPS and BLM. This entry focuses on sections accessible through Big Bend National Park in Texas, relevant to NM/TX/OK trip planning.

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Rio Grande Village Visitor Center** (0.25 miles north of Rio Grande Village, Big Bend National Park, TX; Seasonally November-April: 9:00 AM-4:30 PM daily, closed for lunch; 432-477-2251; Big Bend NP entrance fee applies: $30 per vehicle) [[Source](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/10110961), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/big-bend-visitor-centers.htm)]

- **Panther Junction Visitor Center** (Headquarters, Big Bend National Park, TX; Daily 8:00 AM-5:00 PM, hours may vary seasonally; 432-477-2251; Big Bend NP entrance fee applies) [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/big-bend-visitor-centers.htm), [Source](https://schoandjo.com/big-bend-national-park-visitor-centers/)]

**Note:** Additional stamps available at Chisos Basin, Castolon, and Persimmon Gap Visitor Centers within Big Bend NP. Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [[Source](http://nostalgia.esmartkid.com/bigbendpassport.html)]

## Key Activities

- **Santa Elena Canyon Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - Hike 1.7 miles into one of Big Bend's most dramatic canyons where the Rio Grande runs between sheer limestone cliffs rising 1,500 feet; cross Terlingua Creek and descend along the river surrounded by lush riparian vegetation and towering vertical walls; Santa Elena Canyon trailhead, western Big Bend; Dawn to dusk; Free with park admission [[Source](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/texas/big-bend-national-park), [Source](https://thestromads.com/things-to-do-in-big-bend-national-park/)]

- **Boquillas Canyon Trail** (1 hour) - Easy 1.4-mile round-trip hike into one of the most scenic Rio Grande canyons featuring towering limestone walls, sandy beaches, and opportunities to spot wild burros; chance to hear the Singing Mexican, a local musician who serenades visitors from across the river; Boquillas Canyon trailhead, eastern Big Bend; Dawn to dusk; Free with park admission [[Source](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/texas/big-bend-national-park), [Source](https://thestromads.com/things-to-do-in-big-bend-national-park/)]

- **Hot Springs Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - Hike to natural 105°F spring tucked along the Rio Grande accessed via narrow dirt road; trail delivers incredible views over the river to distant mountains, especially beautiful in spring when flowers bloom; includes ruins of J.O. Langford's historic bathhouse and trading post; Hot Springs Historic District, eastern Big Bend; Dawn to dusk; Free with park admission [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/river_hikes.htm), [Source](https://thestromads.com/things-to-do-in-big-bend-national-park/)]

- **Rio Grande Rafting/Kayaking Day Trips** (4-8 hours) - Paddle through stunning canyons on the Rio Grande with options for guided tours or private trips; popular sections include Santa Elena Canyon, Mariscal Canyon, and Boquillas Canyon; river permit not required for private boaters but Big Bend NP entrance fee applies; Various put-in points throughout Big Bend; Year-round, water levels permitting; Park admission, commercial tours $100-200 per person [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/river-trips.htm), [Source](https://thestromads.com/things-to-do-in-big-bend-national-park/)]

- **Rio Grande Multi-Day Float Trips** (2-7 days) - Extended river journeys through remote canyons including Lower Canyons (83 miles, 4-7 days), Mariscal Canyon (14 miles, 1-2 days), and Boquillas Canyon (33 miles, 2-3 days); experience desert solitude, camp on sandy beaches, and navigate Class I-III rapids; River access points throughout Big Bend; Year-round, best March-May and September-November; Park admission, backcountry camping permit recommended [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/river-trips.htm), [Source](https://www.hikingbigbend.com/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Rio Grande Village Nature Trail** (45 minutes-1 hour) - Unlike any other Big Bend hike, this trail features a marsh habitat boardwalk over a pond filled with fish, turtles, and frogs, leading to a ridge with stunning views of the Rio Grande, Sierra del Carmen, and Chisos Mountains; Rio Grande Village area; Dawn to dusk; Free with park admission [[Source](https://thestromads.com/things-to-do-in-big-bend-national-park/), [Source](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-hikes-in-big-bend-national-park/)]

- **River Road** (3-5 hours) - Remote 51-mile backcountry drive following the Rio Grande to isolated campsites and historic sites; offers spectacular river views and access to primitive hot springs; requires high-clearance or four-wheel drive vehicle; River Road from Castolon to Rio Grande Village; Dawn to dusk; Free with park admission [[Source](https://thestromads.com/things-to-do-in-big-bend-national-park/), [Source](https://www.planetware.com/texas/top-rated-hikes-in-big-bend-national-park-us-tx-82.htm)]

- **Singing Mexican at Boquillas Canyon** (flexible timing) - Listen for local musicians from the Mexican village of Boquillas del Carmen who often serenade hikers from across the river at Boquillas Canyon trailhead; spontaneous cultural experience unique to this border location; Boquillas Canyon area; Varies, often mornings and afternoons; Free (tips appreciated) [[Source](https://thestromads.com/things-to-do-in-big-bend-national-park/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Chisos Basin Trails** (2-6 hours, within Big Bend NP) - Explore iconic mountain trails including Window Trail (5.6 miles, 3-4 hours) offering sunset views through a natural rock window, and Lost Mine Trail (4.8 miles, 3-4 hours) with panoramic views of surrounding peaks; Chisos Basin area, central Big Bend; Dawn to dusk; Free with park admission [[Source](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/texas/big-bend-national-park), [Source](https://schoandjo.com/top-things-to-do-in-big-bend/)]

- **Boquillas del Carmen, Mexico** (Half-day) - Cross the international border via rowboat ferry ($5 round-trip) to this authentic Mexican village offering restaurants, shops, and cultural experiences; requires valid passport; Boquillas Crossing port of entry; Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM-6:00 PM (U.S. hours), village open daily; Ferry $5, village free [[Source](https://schoandjo.com/top-things-to-do-in-big-bend/), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/boquillas.htm)]

- **Terlingua Ghost Town** (1-2 hours, 30 miles west) - Explore abandoned mercury mining town with historic ruins, quirky art installations, and restaurants serving Tex-Mex; famous for annual chili cook-off; Terlingua, TX 79852; Accessible 24/7, restaurants vary; Free [[Source](https://schoandjo.com/top-things-to-do-in-big-bend/), [Source](https://visitbigbend.com)]

- **McDonald Observatory** (3-4 hours, 90 miles north) - Tour one of the world's premier astronomical research facilities and attend star parties with telescope viewing under some of the darkest skies in North America; 3640 Dark Sky Drive, Fort Davis, TX 79734; Visitor center daily 10:00 AM-5:30 PM, star parties Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday evenings; Tours $8-12, star parties $18 [[Source](https://mcdonaldobservatory.org)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 1-2 hours for a single canyon hike and passport stamps at visitor centers. Half-day (4-5 hours) recommended for multiple canyon hikes and Hot Springs. Full day or multi-day for rafting/kayaking trips. Big Bend National Park typically requires 2-4 days to explore thoroughly.
