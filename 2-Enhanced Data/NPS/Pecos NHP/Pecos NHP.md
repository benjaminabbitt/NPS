# Pecos NHP (National Historical Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-pecos-national-historical-park-visitor-center"></a>**Pecos National Historical Park Visitor Center** (P.O. Box 418, Pecos, NM 87552; Memorial Day-Labor Day: 8:00 AM-4:30 PM daily, Labor Day-Memorial Day: 8:30 AM-4:00 PM daily, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 505-757-7241; Free admission) [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/peco/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/peco/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** National Park Passport stamps available in visitor center. Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-ancestral-sites-trail"></a>**Ancestral Sites Trail (Pueblo and Mission Ruins)** (45 minutes-1 hour) - Easy 1.25-mile self-guided loop trail from visitor center winding through ruins of Pecos Pueblo (inhabited 1100-1838 AD) and two Spanish Colonial mission churches, with interpretive signs explaining 700 years of history; Trailhead at visitor center, P.O. Box 418, Pecos, NM 87552; Summer: 8:00 AM-4:30 PM, Winter: 8:30 AM-4:00 PM; Free admission [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/peco/index.htm), [Source](https://upgradedpoints.com/travel/pecos-national-historical-park-travel-guide/), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60930-d102446-Reviews-Pecos_National_Historical_Park-Pecos_New_Mexico.html)]

- <a id="activity-glorieta-pass-battlefield-trail"></a>**Glorieta Pass Battlefield Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - Moderate 2.25-mile loop trail telling the story of the Battle of Glorieta Pass (March 26-28, 1862), often called the "Gettysburg of the West"; Glorieta Pass Battlefield unit, gate code required from visitor center; Summer: 8:00 AM-4:30 PM, Winter: 8:30 AM-4:00 PM; Free admission [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/peco/index.htm), [Source](https://ironbrigader.com/2020/07/14/visiting-the-glorieta-pass-battlefield-at-pecos-national-historical-park/), [Source](https://discoveringhiddengems.com/pecos-national-historical-park-worth-visiting/)]

- <a id="activity-visitor-center-museum"></a>**Visitor Center Museum** (30-45 minutes) - Explore exhibits covering 12,000 years of history including Puebloan culture, Spanish colonization, Santa Fe Trail, Civil War, and 20th century ranching; watch introductory film and view artifacts; Visitor center, P.O. Box 418, Pecos, NM 87552; Summer: 9:00 AM-4:00 PM, Winter: 9:00 AM-4:00 PM; Free admission [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/peco/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Source](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/pecos-national-historical-park)]

- <a id="activity-ranger-led-programs"></a>**Ranger-Led Programs** (30-60 minutes) - Join ranger-led talks and tours explaining Puebloan architecture, Spanish missions, and Civil War history; Check visitor center for daily schedule; Visitor center and trail areas; Seasonal, check schedule; Free [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/peco/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- <a id="activity-civil-war-encampment"></a>**Civil War Encampment (Annual Event)** (2-4 hours, March) - Attend annual living history event each March commemorating the Battle of Glorieta Pass with guest speakers, living history camps, demonstrations, and hands-on activities; Park grounds; March 26-28 annually (check website for exact dates); Free [[Source](https://discoveringhiddengems.com/pecos-national-historical-park-worth-visiting/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-forked-lightning-ranch-tour"></a>**Forked Lightning Ranch Tour** (1-2 hours) - Tour this historic 1920s cattle ranch established by rodeo producer Tex Austin and later owned by Texas oilman Buddy Fogelson and actress Greer Garson, who hosted Hollywood elite; five historic buildings available for viewing; Ask at visitor center for tour availability, Forked Lightning Ranch unit; Tours by arrangement only; Free [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/peco/learn/historyculture/the-forked-lightning-ranch.htm), [Source](https://wnpa.org/plan-your-trip/trip-ideas/park-stories/plan-your-trip-to-pecos-national-historical-park-1), [Source](https://www.santafe.org/blog/post/pecos-national-historical-park-past-persists/)]

- <a id="gem-wildlife-viewing"></a>**Wildlife Viewing** (1-2 hours, dawn/dusk best) - Watch for mountain bluebirds, hummingbirds, golden eagles, mule deer, elk, and occasionally black bears in the park's varied habitats; Throughout park, especially along trails; Dawn and dusk year-round [[Source](https://villageofpecos.com/things-to-do/)]

- <a id="gem-photography-opportunities"></a>**Photography Opportunities** (flexible timing) - Capture dramatic photos of pueblo ruins against mountain backdrops, mission church walls, and sweeping valley views from the battlefield trail; Throughout park; Best light early morning and late afternoon [[Source](https://www.legendsofamerica.com/nm-pecospark/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-santa-fe-plaza-and-historic-district"></a>**Santa Fe Plaza and Historic District** (2-4 hours, 25 miles west) - Explore the heart of America's oldest state capital with art galleries, museums, Palace of the Governors (oldest continuously occupied public building in the U.S.), and authentic Southwestern restaurants; Downtown Santa Fe, 63 Lincoln Ave, Santa Fe, NM 87501; Shops and galleries generally 10:00 AM-5:00 PM, restaurants vary; Free to explore plaza [[Source](https://farolito.com/blog/pecos-national-historical-park/), [Source](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/usa/new-mexico/santa-fe-region/attractions/pecos-national-historical-park/a/poi-sig/1110378/1324599)]

- <a id="nearby-pecos-wilderness-area"></a>**Pecos Wilderness Area** (Half-day to full-day, adjacent north) - Hike trails ranging from easy riverside walks to challenging mountain ascents in this 223,000-acre wilderness with alpine lakes, 12,000-foot peaks, and trout fishing; Access via NM Highway 63 north of Pecos; Campgrounds in Santa Fe National Forest; Trails open year-round, best June-October [[Source](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/gateways/2855), [Source](https://villageofpecos.com/things-to-do/)]

- <a id="nearby-monastery-of-christ-in-the-desert"></a>**Monastery of Christ in the Desert** (2-3 hours, 1.5 hours north via scenic drive) - Visit this remote Benedictine monastery in the Chama River Canyon where monks live a traditional monastic life; attend prayers or simply enjoy the peaceful setting; Forest Road 151, Abiquiú, NM; Grounds open during daylight hours, prayer services several times daily; Free (donations welcome) [[Source](https://www.legendsofamerica.com/nm-pecospark/)]

- <a id="nearby-pecos-river-and-pecos-canyon-state-park"></a>**Pecos River and Pecos Canyon State Park** (1-2 hours, adjacent) - Fish for trout in the Pecos River, picnic, and watch for wildlife including birds and elk in this scenic canyon setting; Pecos River Picnic Area, off NM Highway 63; Dawn to dusk; Free [[Source](https://villageofpecos.com/things-to-do/)]
