# Harry S Truman NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

### Main Site - Visitor Center
**Address:** 223 N Main St, Independence, Missouri 64050
**Geocoordinates:** (39.0936143, -94.4154897)
**Hours:** Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; Closed Monday-Tuesday
**Phone:** (816) 254-9929

**Note:** The visitor center is located in the historic Independence Fire Station No. 1 and serves as the ticketing station for Truman Home tours. Passport stamps are available here. Call to confirm current stamp locations and tour availability before visiting, as the number of tours offered each day can vary depending on staffing [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/hstr/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60849-d3412183-Reviews-Harry_S_Truman_National_Historic_Site-Independence_Missouri.html)].

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-guided-tour-of-the-truman-home"></a>**Guided Tour of the Truman Home** (30 minutes) - Ranger-guided tour of the historic Victorian-era home at 219 N Delaware St where Harry and Bess Truman lived from their marriage in 1919 until their deaths, featuring thousands of original artifacts on display; tours limited to 8 people, starting every half-hour from 9:00 AM (last tour at 4:30 PM); free admission, first-come first-served ticketing at visitor center (tickets may sell out by early afternoon on weekends, holidays, and during summer); 219 N Delaware St, Independence, Missouri 64050 (39.0937292, -94.4233448); Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM-4:30 PM; Closed Monday-Tuesday [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/hstr/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Source](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/harry-s-truman-national-historic-site/), [Source](https://www.independencemo.gov/visitors/our-history-and-culture/book-tour-historic-homes-independence-mo/truman-home-visitor-center)]

- <a id="activity-visitor-center-experience"></a>**Visitor Center Experience** (30-45 minutes) - Explore exhibits of objects from the Truman home, watch an audio/visual program about Harry Truman's home life, browse the bookstore, and obtain passport stamps; 223 N Main St, Independence, Missouri 64050 (39.0936143, -94.4154897); Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; Closed Monday-Tuesday; (816) 254-9929 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/hstr/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60849-d3412183-Reviews-Harry_S_Truman_National_Historic_Site-Independence_Missouri.html)]

- <a id="activity-self-guided-tour-of-the-noland-home"></a>**Self-Guided Tour of the Noland Home** (20-30 minutes) - Explore the first floor of the home where President Truman's favorite aunt and cousins lived, learning about Harry and Bess's courtship and life in the Truman neighborhood; self-guided tour (no ranger required); 216 N Delaware St, Independence, Missouri 64050; Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM-4:30 PM; Closed Monday-Tuesday; free admission [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/hstr/planyourvisit/noland-home.htm), [Source](https://www.independencemo.gov/visitors/our-history-and-culture/book-tour-historic-homes-independence-mo/truman-home-visitor-center)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-truman-farm-home-in-grandview"></a>**Truman Farm Home in Grandview** (30-minute tour) - Tour the farmhouse at 12301 Blue Ridge Blvd where Harry Truman worked as a young man from 1906-1917 on his family's former 600-acre farm; admission includes tour of main Truman Home in Independence; 12301 Blue Ridge Blvd, Grandview, Missouri 64030 (approximately 15 miles southwest of Independence); seasonal hours: Memorial Day-Labor Day, Friday-Sunday 9:30 AM-4:00 PM (closed noon hour for lunch); $4 for ages 16+, free for 15 and under; (816) 254-2720; note: stairs throughout make upper floors inaccessible for strollers and wheelchairs [[Source](https://kcparent.com/truman-farm-home-grandview-mo/), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/hstr/learn/historyculture/truman-farm-home-photo-tour.htm), [Source](https://www.visitmo.com/things-to-do/harry-s-truman-national-historic-site-truman-farm-home)]

- <a id="gem-second-floor-photo-tour"></a>**Second Floor Photo Tour** (included in visit) - While the second floor of the Truman Home remains closed to preserve the family's privacy per Bess Truman's will, a photo tour of the closed rooms including Harry and Bess's bedroom is available; included with visitor center experience; 223 N Main St, Independence, Missouri 64050; Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [[Source](https://www.npca.org/parks/harry-s-truman-national-historic-site), [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_S._Truman_National_Historic_Site)]

- <a id="gem-walking-tour-of-truman-neighborhood"></a>**Walking Tour of Truman Neighborhood** (30-45 minutes) - Self-guided walk around the historic neighborhood including views of the two adjacent homes of Mrs. Truman's brothers (part of the NHS but not open for interior tours); free; accessible during daylight hours [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/hstr/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-harry-s-truman-presidential-library-museum"></a>**Harry S. Truman Presidential Library & Museum** (2 hours) - Comprehensive presidential library featuring immersive exhibits on Truman's presidency, WWII, the Cold War, civil rights, and the Korean War; recently renovated with modern interactive displays; 500 W US Hwy 24, Independence, Missouri 64050 (39.1034078, -94.4212535) (approximately 1.5 miles from NHS visitor center); Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday Noon-5:00 PM (April-December), closed Sundays January-March, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; admission varies (discounts for seniors, students, veterans, active military) [[Source](https://www.trumanlibrary.gov/museum/hours), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60849-d144809-Reviews-Harry_S_Truman_Presidential_Library_Museum-Independence_Missouri.html), [Source](https://www.independencemo.gov/visitors/our-history-and-culture/harry-s-truman/harry-s-truman-presidential-library-museum)]

- <a id="nearby-national-frontier-trails-museum"></a>**National Frontier Trails Museum** (1-1.5 hours) - Museum documenting the Oregon, California, and Santa Fe trails with covered wagons, trail artifacts, and award-winning film; temporarily located at 416 W Maple Ave, Independence, Missouri 64050 (approximately 0.5 miles from NHS); Thursday-Saturday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM; recommended to call ahead to confirm hours; group tours by appointment [[Source](https://www.independencemo.gov/government/city-departments/parks-recreation-and-tourism/national-frontier-trails-museum/planning-visit), [Source](https://gowandering.com/places/national-frontier-trails-museum), [Source](https://www.visitmo.com/things-to-do/national-frontier-trails-museum-independence)]

- <a id="nearby-1859-jail-museum"></a>**1859 Jail Museum** (45 minutes-1 hour) - Historic jail and marshal's house where Frank James was held, featuring thick limestone cells and exhibits on frontier justice and the Civil War era; includes one-room schoolhouse; 217 N Main St, Independence, Missouri 64050 (1 block from NHS visitor center); Monday-Saturday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM, Sunday 1:00-4:00 PM (confirm hours as sources vary); $6 adults, $5 seniors (65+), $3 children 6-16, free under 6 [[Source](https://www.jchs.org/1859jailmuseum), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60849-d274485-Reviews-1859_Jail_Museum-Independence_Missouri.html), [Source](https://www.uponarriving.com/1859-jail/)]

- <a id="nearby-bingham-waggoner-mansion-estate"></a>**Bingham-Waggoner Mansion & Estate** (1-1.5 hours) - 26-room, 3-story Victorian mansion with 95% original contents from the last family who lived there for 97 years; tour begins with 10-minute video in Carriage House; 313 W Pacific Ave, Independence, Missouri 64050 (approximately 0.8 miles from NHS); Thursday-Saturday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM (last tour 3:00 PM), Sunday 1:00-4:00 PM (last tour 3:00 PM), Monday-Wednesday by reservation; $15 adults (April-December), $10 adults (February-March), $5 children 6-12, free under 6; closed October 30-November 16, November 27-28, December 24-25, December 31; (816) 461-3491 [[Source](https://www.bwestate.net/visit), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60849-d4742860-Reviews-Bingham_Waggoner_Mansion_Estate-Independence_Missouri.html), [Source](https://www.independencemo.gov/visitors/our-history-and-culture/book-tour-historic-homes-independence-mo/bingham-waggoner-mansion-and-estate)]
