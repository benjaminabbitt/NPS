# Harry S Truman NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Truman Home Visitor Center** (223 North Main Street, Independence, MO 64050 (39.091, -94.415); Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed Monday-Tuesday; 816-254-9929) - Stamps are typically available at the visitor center, located in the historic 1928 Independent Fire Station No. 1. Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [Harry S Truman NHS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/hstr/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/hstr/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

**Note:** Call 816-254-9929 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Guided Tour of Truman Home** (30 minutes) - Take a guided tour (limited to 8 people per tour) of the Victorian home at 219 North Delaware Street where Harry S. Truman lived with his wife Bess from 1919 until his death in 1972, featuring thousands of original artifacts from the Truman family; 219 North Delaware Street, Independence, MO 64050 (39.097, -94.418); tours run every 30 minutes from 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM (last tour), Wednesday-Sunday, closed Monday-Tuesday; free admission but tickets must be obtained same-day at the visitor center (223 N Main Street) and often sell out by early afternoon on weekends and during summer. [Source: [Truman Home Tours](https://www.independencemo.gov/visitors/our-history-and-culture/harry-s-truman/harry-s-truman-home), [Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/hstr/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Noland Home Self-Guided Tour** (15-30 minutes) - Visit the home of Harry's cousins to learn about the courtship of Harry and Bess Truman and life in the Truman neighborhood, with photography allowed inside; across the street from the Truman Home, Independence, MO 64050; Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM, closed Monday-Tuesday; free admission. [Source: [The Noland Home](https://www.nps.gov/hstr/planyourvisit/noland-home.htm), [Lake Travel Guide](https://www.lake.com/parks/harry-s-truman-national-historic-site/)]

- **Truman Historic Walking Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - Follow the 2.7-mile self-guided walking trail that includes 43 stops marked by plaques featuring important places in President Truman's life, including landmarks and homes of Truman's friends and associates; begins at Truman Home Visitor Center at Main Street and Truman Road, Independence, MO 64050; accessible during daylight hours; free. Pick up printed brochures at the Truman Home Visitor Center. [Source: [Truman Historic Walking Trail](https://www.independencemo.gov/visitors/our-history-and-culture/walking-trails/truman-historic-walking-trail), [Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/hstr/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Visitor Center Exhibits** (20-30 minutes) - Explore exhibits in the historic 1928 fire station featuring information about the 33rd President, watch an informational video, and browse the park store; 223 North Main Street, Independence, MO 64050 (39.091, -94.415); Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed Monday-Tuesday; free admission; 816-254-9929. [Source: [Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/hstr/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60849-d3412183-Reviews-Harry_S_Truman_National_Historic_Site-Independence_Missouri.html)]

- **George and Frank Wallace Homes** (15-20 minutes each) - Explore the two small bungalow homes that once belonged to Bess Wallace Truman's brothers, offering a window into the community and family ties that shaped Truman's life; located in the Truman neighborhood, Independence, MO 64050; Wednesday-Sunday during park hours; free admission. [Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_S._Truman_National_Historic_Site), [Lake Travel Guide](https://www.lake.com/parks/harry-s-truman-national-historic-site/)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (30-45 minutes) - Complete a Junior Ranger booklet at the visitor center and earn a badge; available at the Truman Home Visitor Center, 223 North Main Street, Independence, MO 64050 (39.0936029, -94.4152395); Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; free. [Source: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60849-d3412183-Reviews-Harry_S_Truman_National_Historic_Site-Independence_Missouri.html)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Truman Farm Home Self-Guided Tour** (45 minutes - 1 hour) - Visit the 1894 farmhouse on a 10-acre remnant of what was once a 600-acre farm where Harry Truman worked as a young man from 1906-1917, explore the grounds including the house, garage, chicken coop, reconstructed smokehouse, and privy with an available audio tour; 12301 Blue Ridge Boulevard, Grandview, MO 64030 (38.889, -94.528); grounds open year-round for self-guided tours, Wednesday-Sunday, closed Monday-Tuesday; free admission; 19 miles southwest of Independence (about 25 minutes drive); 816-254-9929. [Source: [Truman Farm Home](https://www.nps.gov/places/truman-farm-home.htm), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_S._Truman_Farm_Home), [Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/hstr/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Virtual Tour Experience** - Access the virtual tour of the first floor and video tour of the second floor of the Truman Home (the upstairs is not open to visitors due to the fragile structure); available online; free. [Source: [Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/hstr/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)]

- **Dear Bess/Dear Harry Podcast** - Listen to "A Great Love Story in Letters" through the park's podcast series featuring the correspondence between Harry and Bess Truman; available online; free. [Source: [Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/hstr/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **NPS Wellness Challenge Walking Paths** - Take part in three physical challenges at Harry S Truman NHS by following marked walking paths in Truman's footsteps; trails begin at the visitor center; accessible during daylight hours; free. [Source: [NPS Wellness Challenge](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/the-nps-wellness-challenge-at-harry-s-truman-national-historic-site.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Harry S. Truman Presidential Library & Museum** (2-3 hours) - Explore one of 15 presidential libraries in the country featuring thousands of objects including the famous "The Buck Stops Here!" sign, exhibits about Truman's leadership through World War II and the post-war era, and visit the courtyard where President and Mrs. Truman are buried; 500 West US Highway 24, Independence, MO 64050 (39.103, -94.421); Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday (April-December) 12:00 PM - 5:00 PM, closed Sundays January-March and on Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; admission charged; 1-800-833-1225 or 816-268-8200; 1 mile from Truman NHS. [Source: [Truman Library Hours](https://www.trumanlibrary.gov/museum/hours), [Independence MO Gov](https://www.independencemo.gov/visitors/our-history-and-culture/harry-s-truman/harry-s-truman-presidential-library-museum)]

- **National Frontier Trails Museum** (1.5-2 hours) - Discover the history of the Oregon, Santa Fe, and California Trails with authentic covered wagons, trail artifacts, original diaries and letters, an award-winning film, and the nation's largest public collection of rare books about the trails in the Merrill J. Mattes Research Library; 416 W. Maple Ave., Independence, MO 64050 (39.093189, -94.4208614); hours vary seasonally (May-September: Monday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM); modest admission charged; 816-325-7575; 0.3 miles from Truman NHS. [Source: [National Frontier Trails Museum](https://www.independencemo.gov/government/city-departments/parks-recreation-and-tourism/national-frontier-trails-museum), [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/000/national-frontier-trails-museum.htm)]

- **1859 Jail Museum** (45 minutes - 1 hour) - Tour the historic frontier jail where Frank James (Jesse James' brother) was imprisoned for 112 days in 1882, explore the Marshall's home, jail cells, schoolhouse, and surrounding grounds with exhibits of handmade weapons; 217 Main Street, Independence, MO 64050 (39.0934837, -94.4153572); Monday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM; general admission $6; located on Independence Square, 0.2 miles from Truman NHS. [Source: [1859 Jail Museum](https://www.jchs.org/1859jailmuseum), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60849-d274485-Reviews-1859_Jail_Museum-Independence_Missouri.html)]

- **Bingham-Waggoner Mansion & Estate** (1 hour) - Tour the 26-room, 3-story mansion with 95% original contents from the Waggoner family who lived there for 97 years, situated on 19+ acres at the beginning of the Santa Fe, Oregon, and California Trails, once occupied by artist/politician George Caleb Bingham; includes 10-minute introductory video in the Carriage House; 313 W Pacific Ave, Independence, MO 64050 (39.086468, -94.4183654); Thursday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM (last tour 3:00 PM), Sunday 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM (last tour 3:00 PM), Monday-Wednesday by reservation; adults $15 (April-December) or $10 (February-March), children 6-12 $5, under 6 free; 816-461-3491; 0.8 miles from Truman NHS. [Source: [Bingham-Waggoner Estate](https://www.bwestate.net/visit), [Independence MO Gov](https://www.independencemo.gov/visitors/our-history-and-culture/book-tour-historic-homes-independence-mo/bingham-waggoner-mansion-and-estate)]

- **Vaile Mansion** (1 hour) - Tour the Gothic-like 31-room Second Empire Victorian mansion built in 1881 featuring nine marble fireplaces, spectacular painted ceilings, era-appropriate furnishings, and Level 1 Arboretum grounds (one of only 2 combination historical mansions and arboretums in the country); 1500 North Liberty Street, Independence, MO 64050 (39.1075801, -94.4171372); Thursday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM (April 1 - October 31 for regular tours; November 28 - December 30 for holiday tours, closed December 23-25); tickets purchased at door, no reservations needed; 816-503-6700; 1.2 miles from Truman NHS. [Source: [Vaile Mansion](https://www.vailemansion.org/visit), [Independence MO Gov](https://www.independencemo.gov/visitors/our-history-and-culture/book-tour-historic-homes-independence-mo/vaile-victorian-mansion)]

- **Clinton's Soda Fountain** (30 minutes) - Step back in time at this authentic soda fountain on Independence Square where 14-year-old Harry Truman worked his first job; enjoy phosphates (the predecessor to milkshakes), ice cream including "Harry's Favorite" (butterscotch sundae with chocolate ice cream), candy, pastries, and coffee in a historic setting with marble bar and solid wood framing; 100 W Maple Ave, Independence, MO 64050 (39.0929667, -94.4158284); Monday-Thursday 11:00 AM - 6:00 PM (or 12:00 PM - 8:00 PM per Instagram), Friday-Saturday 11:00 AM - 8:00 PM (or 12:00 PM - 9:00 PM per Instagram), closed Sunday; call ahead to confirm hours; 0.2 miles from Truman NHS. [Source: [Clinton's Soda Fountain](https://www.clintonssodafountain.com/), [Independence MO Gov](https://www.independencemo.gov/visitors/our-history-and-culture/harry-s-truman/clintons-soda-fountain), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/clintons-soda-fountain-independence)]
