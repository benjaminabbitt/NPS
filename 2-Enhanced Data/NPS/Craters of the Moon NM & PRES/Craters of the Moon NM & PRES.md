# Craters of the Moon NM (National Monument) & PRES (Preserve)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Robert Limbert Visitor Center** (1266 Craters Loop Road, Arco, ID 83213 [43.47262820, -113.56689260]; Hours vary by season - Summer: daily 8:00 AM-6:00 PM starting late May, Winter: Thursday-Monday 9:00 AM-4:30 PM, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 208-527-1335) [Source: [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/crmo/planyourvisit/visitor-center.htm), [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/crmo/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** Call 208-527-1335 to confirm current stamp locations and hours before visiting, as seasonal schedules vary.

## Key Activities

- **Loop Road Scenic Drive** (30 minutes) - Drive the 7-mile paved loop road through the volcanic landscape, open May-November (closes due to snow November-May); accessible from visitor center; open 24 hours when road is clear [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/craters-of-the-moon/), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/crmo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Devil's Orchard Nature Trail** (15-45 minutes) - Walk a flat, paved 0.5-mile wheelchair-accessible loop through ancient pahoehoe lava flows with interpretive signs; located along Loop Road; accessible 24 hours [Source: [America from the Road](https://americafromtheroad.com/devils-orchard-nature-trail/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/idaho/devils-orchard), [NPS Devils Orchard](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hike-devils-orchard.htm)]

- **Inferno Cone Hike** (30-45 minutes round trip) - Climb a short but steep 0.4-mile trail with 164-foot elevation gain to reach panoramic 360-degree views of the Great Rift, Snake River Plain, and Pioneer Mountains; located along Loop Road; accessible during daylight hours [Source: [NPS Inferno Cone](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/crmo_infernocone.htm), [Hikespeak](https://www.hikespeak.com/trails/craters-of-the-moon-inferno-cone-trail/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/idaho/inferno-cone-viewpoint)]

- **Cave Exploration - Indian Tunnel & Dewdrop Cave** (1.5-2 hours) - Explore lava tube caves via the 1.6-mile Caves Trail (free permit required from visitor center); Indian Tunnel is 800 feet long and 30 feet tall with skylights providing natural light; bring flashlight and closed-toe shoes; located along Loop Road [Source: [NPS Caves](https://www.nps.gov/crmo/learn/nature/cave.htm), [Hikespeak Caves Trail](https://www.hikespeak.com/trails/craters-of-the-moon-caves-trail/), [Nature Impacts Us](https://www.natureimpactsus.com/new-blog/2023/10/28/exploring-craters-of-the-moon-national-monument)]

- **Broken Top Loop Trail** (40-60 minutes) - Hike the 1.8-mile loop with 242-foot elevation gain across loose cinders and rugged lava to observe diverse volcanic features; wear sturdy shoes; located along Loop Road [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/idaho/broken-top-loop-trail), [NPS Broken Top](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/crmo_brokentop.htm), [Free Roaming Hiker](https://freeroaminghiker.com/broken-top-loop/)]

- **North Crater Flow Trail** (15-30 minutes) - Walk a paved, wheelchair-accessible 0.3-mile loop on a raised boardwalk through 2,100-year-old pahoehoe lava flows; located along Loop Road [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/idaho/north-crater-flow-trail), [NPS North Crater Flow](https://www.nps.gov/places/northcraterflowtrail.htm)]

- **Tree Molds Trail** (45-60 minutes) - Hike a 2-mile out-and-back trail through limber pine stands to the edge of Blue Dragon Flow, where ancient trees left molds in cooling lava; mostly flat and kid-friendly but minimal shade; located along Loop Road [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/idaho/tree-molds-trail), [NPS Tree Molds](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/crmo_treemoldstrail.htm), [Free Roaming Hiker](https://freeroaminghiker.com/tree-molds/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Dark Sky Stargazing** (2-3 hours) - Experience some of the darkest skies in the country at this designated International Dark Sky Park; best after visitor center closes; accessible year-round weather permitting [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/crmo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Visit Idaho](https://visitidaho.org/travel-tips/a-family-adventure-at-craters-of-the-moon/)]

- **Wilderness Trail to Echo Crater** (3-4 hours one-way, 8 miles round trip) - Hike through remote backcountry past lava trees, cinder cones, and open wilderness to Echo Crater, the recommended backcountry camping location with protection from sun and wind near limber pines (free backcountry permit required); trailhead at Broken Top Loop parking area [Source: [Backpacker](https://www.backpacker.com/trips/boise-id-walk-the-moon-craters-of-the-moon-national-monument/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/idaho/craters-of-the-moon-wilderness-trail), [NPS Backpacking](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/crmo-backpacking.htm)]

- **Early Morning Wildlife Viewing** (1-2 hours before 8:00 AM) - Visit before the visitor center opens to hike trails in solitude and spot deer and small animals crossing roads without crowds; Loop Road accessible 24 hours (when open seasonally) [Source: [Gypsy with a Day Job](https://gypsywithadayjob.com/craters-of-the-moon/)]

- **Winter Cross-Country Skiing & Snowshoeing** (2-4 hours) - Explore the volcanic landscape on skis or snowshoes when the Loop Road closes to vehicles (November-May); bring own equipment; accessible during daylight hours [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/crmo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Visit Idaho](https://visitidaho.org/things-to-do/hiking-backpacking/craters-of-the-moon/)]

- **Spatter Cones** (15-30 minutes) - Observe unique volcanic formations created when lava spewed from vents and landed around them, forming cone-like structures; accessible via short trail along Loop Road [Source: [Visit Idaho](https://visitidaho.org/travel-tips/a-family-adventure-at-craters-of-the-moon/)]

## Also Nearby

- **EBR-I (Experimental Breeder Reactor) Atomic Museum** (1-1.5 hours, 18 miles southeast of Arco) - Tour the world's first electricity-generating nuclear power plant and U.S. National Historic Landmark with free admission and guided/self-guided tours; U.S. Highway 20, 50 miles west of Idaho Falls; open Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; 208-526-0050 [Source: [Idaho National Laboratory](https://inl.gov/news-release/experimental-breeder-reactor-i-atomic-museum-opens-may-23-for-2025-season/), [Atomic Archive](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/atomic-tourist/ebr.html)]

- **Wild Rose (Carey) Hot Springs** (1-2 hours, 34 miles from Arco) - Soak in natural hot springs with two pools set against lava rock; unmarked pull-off on Highway 20, 10 miles from Carey toward Craters of the Moon (43.364077, -113.789443); open year-round, day-use only (no camping), free; no facilities [Source: [Top Hot Springs](https://www.tophotsprings.com/wild-rose-hot-springs-idaho/), [Arbours Abroad](https://www.arboursabroad.com/wild-rose-hot-springs-idaho/), [Ultimate Hot Springs Guide](https://www.ultimatehotspringsguide.com/carey-hot-springs.html)]

- **Big Southern Butte** (3-5 hours, 25 miles east of Arco) - Hike one of the world's largest volcanic domes rising 2,500 feet above the lava plain for spectacular panoramic summit views; accessed via gravel roads (best by ATV/UTV), 7,550-foot summit elevation; open mid-May through October depending on snow; call Upper Snake Field Office at 208-524-7500 for current conditions (43°24'3.60"N, 113°1'22.8"W) [Source: [BLM Big Southern Butte](https://www.blm.gov/visit/big-southern-butte), [Idaho: A Climbing Guide](https://www.idahoaclimbingguide.com/bookupdates/big-southern-butte/)]

- **Pickle's Place Restaurant** (1 hour meal, in Arco) - Enjoy iconic fried pickles and full menu at this themed restaurant; 440 South Front Street, Arco, ID 83213; 208-527-9944 [Source: [Pickle's Place Contact](https://picklesplacerestaurant.com/contact), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/pickles-place-arco)]
