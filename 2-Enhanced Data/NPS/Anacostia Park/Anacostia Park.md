# Anacostia Park

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Kenilworth Aquatic Gardens Visitor Center** (1550 Anacostia Avenue NE, Washington, DC 20019; Winter: 8:00 AM-4:00 PM, Summer: 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; 771-208-1571)
- **Frederick Douglass National Historic Site Visitor Center** (1411 W Street SE, Washington, DC 20020; April 16-October 15: 9:00 AM-5:00 PM Tues-Sat, October 16-April 15: 9:00 AM-4:00 PM Tues-Sat; 771-208-1499)

**Note:** National Capital Parks-East, which includes Anacostia Park, does not have a dedicated visitor center. Passport cancellation stamps for Anacostia Park can be collected at either Kenilworth Aquatic Gardens or Frederick Douglass National Historic Site visitor centers. Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [National Capital Parks-East Passport Stamps](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/passport-stamps.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Roller Skating at Anacostia Pavilion** (1-3 hours) - The only roller skating rink owned and operated by the National Park Service, featuring free skate rentals with government-issued ID during summer months (Memorial Day through Labor Day), DJ music on summer weekends, and special Late Skate events on the last Saturday of each month from June to October; 1500 Anacostia Drive SE, Washington, DC; Open daily 6:00 AM-10:00 PM (last skate rentals distributed 30 minutes before closing); 771-208-1571 [Source: [NPS Roller Skating](https://www.nps.gov/anac/planyourvisit/roller-skating.htm), [NPS Anacostia Roller Skating Pavilion](https://www.nps.gov/places/000/anacostia-roller-skating-pavilion.htm)]

- **Kayaking/Canoeing the Anacostia Water Trail** (2-3 hours) - Paddle the nine-mile stretch of the gentle-flowing Anacostia River with forested shoreline views; guided interpretive tours available for 1-2 hours; public boat launch at north end of Anacostia Park off Anacostia Drive SE; kayak and canoe rentals available at Ballpark Boathouse, $15-$25 per hour with 2-hour or 4-hour pass options; 771-208-1571 [Source: [NPS Anacostia Water Trail](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/paddle-the-anacostia-water-trail.htm), [Ballpark Boathouse](https://boatingindc.com/ballpark-boathouse/)]

- **Biking/Walking the Anacostia Riverwalk Trail** (1-3 hours) - Explore the 3.5-mile paved trail through Anacostia Park (part of the larger 25-mile trail system when complete, with 12-16 miles currently open); 10-12 foot wide trail suitable for cyclists, runners, skaters, and walkers with gentle grades (5% or less); connects to Kenilworth Aquatic Gardens, River Terrace Park, and Nationals Park; free and open year-round during daylight hours; pets welcome on 6-foot or shorter leash; Anacostia Drive SE, Washington, DC 20020 [Source: [NPS Anacostia Riverwalk Trail](https://www.nps.gov/anac/planyourvisit/anacostia-riverwalk-trail.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/washington-dc/anacostia-riverwalk-trail), [TrailLink](https://www.traillink.com/trail/anacostia-river-trail/)]

- **Fishing the Anacostia River** (1-4 hours) - Fish from the banks of the Anacostia River for sunfish, blue catfish, largemouth bass, and catfish species; practice catch-and-release recommended due to fish consumption advisories; Washington D.C. fishing license required through Department of Energy and Environment; good fishing areas include Diamond Teague Park and along Anacostia Park streambeds; 771-208-1571 [Source: [NPS Fishing the Anacostia River](https://www.nps.gov/anac/planyourvisit/fishing.htm), [Anacostia Watershed Society Recreation](https://www.anacostiaws.org/events-and-recreation/recreation-in-our-watershed.html)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Picnicking at Anacostia Pavilions** (1-2 hours) - Enjoy riverside picnicking at cluster of three pavilions with grills along Nicholson Road (first come, first served) or reserve the single covered pavilion at River Terrace Park near intersection of Benning Road and Anacostia Avenue ($50 permit fee); random picnic tables scattered along Anacostia Drive; bring your own charcoal grill for tables without grills; best wildlife viewing at dawn and dusk; 771-208-1453 for permits and reservations [Source: [NPS Permits & Reservations](https://www.nps.gov/anac/planyourvisit/permitsandreservations.htm), [NPPlan Anacostia Park Picnic Areas](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/washington-d-c-national-parks/national-capital-parks-east-park-at-a-glance/anacostia-park-park-at-a-glance/anacostia-park-picnic-areas/)]

- **Late Skate Events** (6-8 hours) - Special extended skating events held on the last Saturday of the month from June to October (August 23rd, September 27th, October 25th in 2025) featuring free admission and skate rentals, music, dancing, games, free boat tours by Anacostia Watershed Society passing under old railroad bridges and visiting wild areas along the river, and complimentary shuttle service between nearby transit locations; 12:00 PM-10:00 PM; 1500 Anacostia Drive SE, Washington, DC; 771-208-1571 [Source: [NPS Roller Skating](https://www.nps.gov/anac/planyourvisit/roller-skating.htm), [National Parks Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/2024/09/dc-park-people-who-love-roller-skate-and-fish)]

## Also Nearby

- **Kenilworth Aquatic Gardens** (2-3 hours, 0.5 miles) - Visit the only National Park Service site dedicated to cultivated aquatic plants, featuring rare waterlilies and lotus flowers in cultivated ponds and the largest remaining tidal marsh in Washington D.C.; best viewed early morning 8:30 AM-11:00 AM before heat closes blooms; peak season late June through mid-August with peak bloom mid-July during annual Lotus & Water Lily Festival; 1550 Anacostia Avenue NE, Washington, DC 20019; grounds open daily 8:00 AM-4:00 PM, visitor center Winter: 8:00 AM-4:00 PM, Summer: 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; 771-208-1571 [Source: [NPS Kenilworth Park & Aquatic Gardens](https://www.nps.gov/keaq/), [Friends of Kenilworth Aquatic Gardens](https://kenaqgardens.org/explore/directions-hours/), [Not Bored in DC](https://notboredindc.com/kenilworth-aquatic-gardens/)]

- **Frederick Douglass National Historic Site** (1.5-2 hours, 1.5 miles) - Tour Cedar Hill, the preserved home of abolitionist and statesman Frederick Douglass, a registered national historic site; guided tours of historic home available at scheduled times throughout the day; reservations required for groups of 10 or more; 1411 W Street SE, Washington, DC 20020; April 16-October 15: 9:00 AM-5:00 PM Tues-Sat, October 16-April 15: 9:00 AM-4:00 PM Tues-Sat; closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day; 771-208-1499 [Source: [NPS Frederick Douglass National Historic Site](https://www.nps.gov/frdo/), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/frdo/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- **U.S. National Arboretum** (2-4 hours, 10 minutes from Capitol Building) - Explore 446 acres featuring the National Bonsai & Penjing Museum (10:00 AM-4:00 PM daily), National Capitol Columns, azalea collections, and extensive trails; free admission and parking; two entrances at 3501 New York Avenue NE (closes 2:00 PM weekdays, 4:30 PM weekends) and 24th & R Streets NE off Bladensburg Road (open 8:00 AM-5:00 PM daily); 3501 New York Avenue NE, Washington, DC 20002; open daily 8:00 AM-5:00 PM except December 25, last admittance 4:30 PM [Source: [USDA National Arboretum Hours](https://www.usna.usda.gov/visit/hours-and-directions/), [Washington.org Arboretum Guide](https://washington.org/visit-dc/guide-us-national-arboretum)]

- **Anacostia Community Museum** (1-2 hours, 1 mile from Anacostia Metro) - Explore this Smithsonian Institution museum examining historical and contemporary social issues impacting urban communities through rotating exhibitions; free admission and on-site parking; free round-trip shuttle picks up at National Mall and Anacostia Metro station during summer; 1901 Fort Place SE, Washington, DC 20020; open daily 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [Smithsonian Anacostia Community Museum](https://anacostia.si.edu/), [Smithsonian Institution Museums](https://www.si.edu/museums/anacostia-community-museum)]

**Total Recommended Time:** 3-6 hours for Anacostia Park activities (2-3 hours for a combination of trail walking/biking and one featured activity such as roller skating or kayaking, or up to 6 hours for Late Skate events) [Source: [AllTrails Anacostia Riverwalk Trail](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/washington-dc/anacostia-riverwalk-trail), [NPS Anacostia Water Trail](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/paddle-the-anacostia-water-trail.htm)]
