## Key Activities

- **Ebey's Landing Bluff Trail** (2-3 hours) - The most popular hike in the state, featuring dramatic sweeping views of the Olympic Mountains and Puget Sound; moderate 3.5-5.5 mile loop trail with 300-328 feet elevation gain, offering stunning coastal bluff scenery and potential gray whale sightings in spring/summer; Ebey's Landing Rd, Coupeville, WA; dawn to dusk [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/washington/ebeys-landing-trail), [Washington Trails Association](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/ebeys-landing), [The Froggy Adventures](https://thefroggyadventures.com/ebeys-landing-hike/)]

- **Island County Historical Museum** (1-2 hours) - Explore two floors of exhibits featuring prehistory, Native American history including three rare dugout canoes, maritime heritage, and local artifacts including Whidbey Island's first car (1907 Holsman); 908 NW Alexander St, Coupeville, WA 98239; Monday-Saturday 10am-4pm, Sunday 11am-4pm; free admission by donation; 360-678-3310 [Source: [Island County Historical Museum](https://www.islandhistory.org/), [Whidbey and Camano Islands](https://whidbeycamanoislands.com/island-county-historical-museum/), [AAA](https://www.aaa.com/travelinfo/washington/coupeville/attractions/island-county-historical-museum-501498.html)]

- **Historic Downtown Coupeville Walking Tour** (1-2 hours) - Self-guided tour of 65 historic locations in Washington State's second oldest town, featuring colorful anecdotes about early settlers and buildings dating before statehood; pick up free walking tour guide at Chamber of Commerce (905 NW Alexander St; 11am-4pm) or Museum (908 NW Alexander St; Monday-Saturday 10am-4pm, Sunday 11am-4pm) [Source: [Downtown Coupeville](https://www.ebeysreserve.com/downtown-coupeville), [NPS Walking Tour PDF](https://www.nps.gov/ebla/planyourvisit/upload/Walking-Tour-Map-for-Web-2.pdf), [Visitor Information](https://www.ebeysreserve.com/visitor-information)]

- **Fort Casey Historical State Park and Admiralty Head Lighthouse** (2-3 hours) - Explore World War I gun batteries with underground passages (bring flashlight), climb the lighthouse tower (limited to 5 visitors at a time), learn history from docents, and enjoy beach access; 1280 Engle Rd, Coupeville, WA 98239; Lighthouse open noon-4pm daily June-August (call 360-678-4519 for other months); 45-minute guided battery tours start at 1:30pm Memorial Day through Labor Day; Discover Pass required [Source: [Fort Casey State Park](https://parks.wa.gov/find-parks/state-parks/fort-casey-historical-state-park), [NPS Fort Casey](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/ebey-s-reserve-fort-casey-admiralty-head-lighthouse.htm), [Lighthouse Friends](https://www.lighthousefriends.com/light.asp?ID=31)]

- **Pratt Loop Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - Less crowded trail cutting through Robert Pratt Preserve along agricultural land edges, passing old sheep barn and machine shed with potential wildlife encounters; trailhead at Ebey's Landing State Park parking lot [Source: [Nature Conservancy](https://www.nature.org/en-us/get-involved/how-to-help/places-we-protect/ebeys-landing/), [Explore N Borgen](https://explorenborgen.com/ebeys-landing/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Jacob and Sarah Ebey's Blockhouse and Cemetery** (30-45 minutes) - Preserved historical homestead accessible via less-crowded trail from Prairie Overlook parking area; hike to one of Washington's oldest structures and the cemetery where Isaac Ebey is buried; 1784 W Rebecca Dr parking area; accessible year-round for exterior viewing [Source: [Hiking Shenandoah Blog](https://www.hikingshenandoah.com/2017/01/ebeys-landing.html), [State Parks](https://www.ebeysreserve.com/state-parks)]

- **Ferry House** (30 minutes) - One of the oldest structures in Washington State, built in 1866, now owned by the National Park Service; accessible from beach trail at Ebey's Landing; dawn to dusk [Source: [Seattle's Child](https://www.seattleschild.com/parent-review-a-hiking-day-trip-to-ebeys-landing/)]

- **Perego's Lake Bird Watching** (30-60 minutes) - Birds-eye view from the bluff trail overlooking Perego's Lake, a birdwatcher's haven; also home to globally rare golden paintbrush plants found at fewer than a dozen sites; best viewing from Ebey's Landing Bluff Trail; dawn to dusk [Source: [State Parks](https://www.ebeysreserve.com/state-parks), [Travel with the One You Love](https://travelwiththeoneyoulove.com/hiking-ebeys-landing-on-whidbey-island-bluff-trail-review/)]

- **Penn Cove Mussel Dining** (1-2 hours) - Sample locally-harvested Penn Cove mussels at historic waterfront restaurants in downtown Coupeville; Coupeville is famous for its mussel aquaculture; various downtown restaurants; hours vary by establishment [Source: [Things to Do in Coupeville](https://smalltownwashington.com/things-to-do-in-coupeville-washington/), [State of WA Tourism](https://stateofwatourism.com/1-day-on-whidbey-island/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Deception Pass State Park** (2-4 hours, 15 miles north) - Washington's most visited state park featuring the iconic Deception Pass Bridge, Goose Rock summit hike with sweeping views, nearly 10 miles of Pacific Northwest Trail, white water kayaking, crabbing, fishing, and jet boat wildlife tours (April-September); 41229 State Route 20, Oak Harbor, WA 98277; Summer 6:30am-dusk, Winter 8am-dusk; Discover Pass required [Source: [Deception Pass State Park](https://parks.wa.gov/find-parks/state-parks/deception-pass-state-park), [Complete Guide](https://stateofwatourism.com/deception-pass-state-park/), [Whidbey and Camano Islands](https://whidbeycamanoislands.com/deception-pass-state-park/)]

- **Greenbank Farm** (1-2 hours, 10 miles south) - Historic farm with converted barns featuring wine tastings, shopping, art shows, and huge meadow for off-leash dogs; hiking trails offer views of water on both sides of Whidbey Island; 765 Wonn Rd, Greenbank, WA 98253; hours vary by season and tenant [Source: [Things to Do on Whidbey Island](https://www.ordinary-adventures.com/2021/09/things-to-do-on-whidbey-island/), [State of WA Tourism](https://stateofwatourism.com/1-day-on-whidbey-island/)]

- **Langley Village** (1-2 hours, 18 miles south) - Charming waterfront village with tightly-knit downtown filled with art galleries, bakeries, boutiques featuring handmade jewelry, clothing, home goods, and books; beach access via stairs on 1st Street; Langley, WA 98260; business hours vary [Source: [PlanetWare](https://www.planetware.com/washington/top-rated-things-to-do-in-whidbey-island-us-wa-182.htm), [State of WA Tourism](https://stateofwatourism.com/1-day-on-whidbey-island/)]

- **Pacific Northwest Naval Air Museum** (1-1.5 hours, 12 miles north in Oak Harbor) - Aviation history museum featuring the story of Naval Air Station Whidbey Island with up-close viewing of PBY-Catalina 5A aircraft; 270 SE Pioneer Way, Oak Harbor, WA 98277; check website for current hours [Source: [Things to Do on Whidbey Island](https://www.ordinary-adventures.com/2021/09/things-to-do-on-whidbey-island/), [PlanetWare](https://www.planetware.com/washington/top-rated-things-to-do-in-whidbey-island-us-wa-182.htm)]
