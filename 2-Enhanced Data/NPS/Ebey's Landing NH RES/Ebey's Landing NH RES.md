## <a id="activities"></a>Key Activities

- <a id="activity-bluff-trail"></a>**Ebey's Landing Bluff Trail** (2-3 hours) - The most popular trail in the Reserve, this 3.6-mile loop features spectacular coastal views along dramatic bluffs overlooking the Strait of Juan de Fuca and Olympic Mountains. The moderately challenging route includes beach walking (tide-dependent), prairie overlook, and historical sites. The trail can be accessed from either the Prairie Overlook trailhead near Sunnyside Cemetery (162 Cemetery Road, Coupeville, WA 98239) or from the seaside parking lot at the end of Ebey's Landing Road. Available dawn to dusk year-round, though trails may be muddy in winter/early spring. [[Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/washington/ebeys-landing-trail), [Source](https://thefroggyadventures.com/ebeys-landing-hike/), [Source](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/ebeys-landing), [Source](https://explorenborgen.com/ebeys-landing/)]

- <a id="activity-ebey-house"></a>**Jacob & Sarah Ebey House Tour** (0.5-1 hour) - Tour the historic 1850s pioneer homestead of Isaac Ebey, accessible via a quarter-mile walk from The Cottage at Sunnyside through scenic prairie farmland. The house and blockhouse showcase 19th-century pioneer life with volunteer docents available to answer questions. Free admission. 162 Cemetery Road, Coupeville, WA 98239 (48.2182, -122.6835); Memorial Day through Labor Day, Friday-Sunday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM; staffed by volunteers. [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/ebey-s-reserve-the-jacob-sarah-ebey-house.htm), [Source](https://www.heraldnet.com/life/a-landmark-steeped-in-19th-century-history-reopens-on-whidbey/), [Source](https://whidbeycamanoislands.com/jacob-and-sarah-ebey-house/)]

- <a id="activity-fort-casey"></a>**Fort Casey Historical State Park & Gun Batteries** (2-3 hours) - Explore historic military fortifications including Battery Worth with two 10-inch disappearing carriage guns and Battery Trevor with rapid-fire pedestal guns. Walk atop gun platforms and explore underground bunkers (bring flashlight). Guided 45-minute battery tours available Memorial Day through September 7, starting at 1:30 PM. The park features 1.8 miles of hiking trails and 10,000+ feet of saltwater shoreline. 1280 Engle Road, Coupeville, WA 98239 (48.15753, -122.67705); Park opens 8:00 AM, closes at dusk; Discover Pass required ($10 day/$30 annual). [[Source](https://parks.wa.gov/find-parks/state-parks/fort-casey-historical-state-park), [Source](https://explorewashingtonstate.com/exploring-fort-casey-on-whidbey-island/), [Source](https://whidbeycamanoislands.com/activities/fort-casey-state-park/)]

- <a id="activity-admiralty-light"></a>**Admiralty Head Lighthouse** (0.5-1 hour) - Climb the lighthouse tower and explore exhibits about Pacific Northwest maritime history. Knowledgeable docents share stories about the lighthouse's history. Lower level features interesting displays. Located within Fort Casey State Park at 1280 Engle Road, Coupeville, WA 98239 (48.15753, -122.67705); May (Thursday-Monday), June-August (Daily), September (Friday-Monday), October (Saturday-Sunday), November (Thanksgiving weekend only), December (weekends and Dec 26-30), closed January-February; 11:00 AM-4:00 PM when open. [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/ebey-s-reserve-fort-casey-admiralty-head-lighthouse.htm), [Source](https://parks.wa.gov/find-parks/state-parks/fort-casey-historical-state-park), [Source](https://www.lighthousefriends.com/light.asp?ID=31)]

- <a id="activity-coupeville"></a>**Historic Coupeville Walking Tour** (1-2 hours) - Self-guided tour through one of the oldest towns in Washington State, featuring 65 historic locations along the waterfront and downtown. Pick up tour maps at the Island County Historical Museum or Coupeville Chamber of Commerce. Tour can be customized to any length. Historic Coupeville Wharf and downtown area; 905 NW Alexander Street, Coupeville, WA 98239 (for maps); self-paced, available anytime. [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/ebla/planyourvisit/upload/Walking-Tour-Map-for-Web-2.pdf), [Source](https://www.ebeysreserve.com/downtown-coupeville)]

- <a id="activity-historical-museum"></a>**Island County Historical Museum** (1.5-2 hours) - Explore diverse exhibits spanning Ice Age prehistory through modern times, including rare dugout canoes, Native American artifacts, maritime history, and the first automobile on Whidbey Island (1902 Holsman). Features Colombian Mammoth remains and special rotating exhibits. Admission by donation. 908 NW Alexander Street, Coupeville, WA 98239 (48.2211516, -122.6887935); Monday-Saturday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM, Sunday 11:00 AM-4:00 PM. [[Source](https://www.islandhistory.org/), [Source](https://whidbeycamanoislands.com/island-county-historical-museum/)]

- <a id="activity-prairie-ridge"></a>**Ebey's Prairie Ridge Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - Easy walk between active farm fields with spectacular views of Admiralty Inlet and the Olympic Mountains. This less-traveled trail showcases the Reserve's working agricultural landscape. Trailhead at Prairie Overlook near Sunnyside Cemetery, 162 Cemetery Road, Coupeville, WA 98239 (48.2182, -122.6835); dawn to dusk year-round. [[Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/washington/ebey-s-prairie-ridge-trail-to-bluff-overlook), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/ebla/planyourvisit/hiking-and-trails.htm)]

## <a id="hidden"></a>Hidden Gems

- <a id="hidden-pratt-loop"></a>**Pratt Loop Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - Lesser-traveled trail along prairie edges to the old Pratt sheep barn and machine shed, then through woods where quail and rabbits rustle. A birdwatcher's haven featuring views of Perego's Lake/Lagoon, with coastal seabirds including scoters, hooded mergansers, and harlequin ducks. Trailhead near Sunnyside area; dawn to dusk year-round. [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60794-d292905-Reviews-Ebey_s_Landing_National_Historical_Reserve-Coupeville_Whidbey_Island_Washington.html), [Source](https://explorenborgen.com/ebeys-landing/)]

- <a id="hidden-old-growth"></a>**Old-Growth Douglas Fir Forest Walk** (30-45 minutes) - Discover gnarled, wind-shaped Douglas fir trees estimated to be over 200 years old along the edges of the Bluff Trail. This mature lowland forest section is often overlooked by hikers focused on coastal views. Access via Ebey's Landing Bluff Trail; dawn to dusk year-round. [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60794-d292905-Reviews-Ebey_s_Landing_National_Historical_Reserve-Coupeville_Whidbey_Island_Washington.html)]

- <a id="hidden-golden-paintbrush"></a>**Golden Paintbrush Viewing** (15-30 minutes, seasonal) - Observe a robust population of golden paintbrush, a globally rare plant known to exist in fewer than a dozen sites worldwide. Best viewed in spring/early summer. Prairie areas near Ebey's Landing Trail; dawn to dusk, spring through early summer peak season. [[Source](https://www.nature.org/en-us/get-involved/how-to-help/places-we-protect/ebeys-landing/)]

- <a id="hidden-ferry-house"></a>**Ferry House** (15-20 minutes) - View one of Washington State's oldest structures, built in 1866 and now owned by the National Park Service. Exterior viewing only, but significant for its historical importance. Located in Coupeville near the waterfront; exterior viewing anytime. [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60794-d292905-Reviews-Ebey_s_Landing_National_Historical_Reserve-Coupeville_Whidbey_Island_Washington.html)]

- <a id="hidden-fort-ebey"></a>**Fort Ebey Bluff Trail** (2-3 hours) - Multi-use wooded trail network less crowded than main Ebey's Landing Trail, featuring bluff views and possibilities for solitude during off-season. Connects to a large trail system and offers a wooded public campground (seasonal). Fort Ebey State Park area, north of Coupeville; dawn to dusk year-round; Discover Pass required. [[Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/washington/fort-ebey-bluff-trail), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60794-d292905-Reviews-Ebey_s_Landing_National_Historical_Reserve-Coupeville_Whidbey_Island_Washington.html)]

## <a id="nearby"></a>Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-deception-pass"></a>**Deception Pass State Park** (3-4 hours, 20 miles/28 minutes north) - Washington's most popular state park featuring the iconic Deception Pass Bridge connecting Whidbey and Fidalgo Islands. Highlights include dramatic coastline cliffs, the 5-mile Lighthouse Point trail via Bowman Bay and Rosario Beach, the Maiden of Deception Pass story pole, and seasonal jet boat tours (April-September) to see eagles, seals, porpoises, and whales. Arrive before 11 AM for parking. 41020 State Route 20, Oak Harbor, WA (48.3062735, -122.6438068); opens 6:30 AM summer/8:00 AM winter, closes at dusk; Discover Pass required ($10 day/$30 annual). [[Source](https://parks.wa.gov/find-parks/state-parks/deception-pass-state-park), [Source](https://stateofwatourism.com/deception-pass-state-park/), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g58643-d141364-Reviews-Deception_Pass_State_Park-Oak_Harbor_Whidbey_Island_Washington.html), [Source](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Coupeville/Deception-Pass-State-Park)]

- <a id="nearby-greenbank-farm"></a>**Greenbank Farm** (1-2 hours, 13 minutes south) - Historic farm at Whidbey Island's midpoint featuring walking trails, master gardens (always open), wine shop, cheese shop, restaurant, off-leash dog area, and scenic views of Cascades, Olympics, and Puget Sound. Great midday stop while touring the island. 765 Wonn Road, Greenbank, WA (48.1054058, -122.5726837); Monday-Sunday 11:00 AM-5:00 PM. [[Source](https://portoc.org/greenbank-farm-2/), [Source](https://whidbeycamanoislands.com/greenbank-farm/), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g58505-d2269060-Reviews-Greenbank_Farm-Greenbank_Whidbey_Island_Washington.html)]
