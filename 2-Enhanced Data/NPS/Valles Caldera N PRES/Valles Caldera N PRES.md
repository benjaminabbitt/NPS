# Valles Caldera N PRES (National Preserve)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Valle Grande Visitor Center** - Located at the preserve's main entrance along Highway 4; 39201 Highway 4, Jemez Springs, NM 87025 (35.856754, -106.491015); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; Phone: 505-670-1612 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/vall/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/vall/contacts.htm)]

**Note:** Call 505-670-1612 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Jemez River Trail** (2-3 hours) - The highest-rated trail in the preserve (4.8 stars from 470 reviews), this 3.4-mile route features a moderate 246-foot elevation gain and follows the Jemez River through meadows and forest; Trailhead accessed via backcountry permit from Valle Grande Visitor Center; Daylight hours during permit window [[Source](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/new-mexico/valles-caldera-national-preserve), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/vall/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)]

- **Coyote Call Trail Loop** (1.5-2 hours) - The most popular trail with 4.5 stars from 252 reviews, this accessible loop offers excellent wildlife viewing opportunities and valley vistas; Valle Grande area, accessible from Highway 4; Daily during daylight hours [[Source](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/new-mexico/valles-caldera-national-preserve)]

- **Wildlife Viewing - Elk Watching** (1-2 hours) - View the second-largest elk herd in New Mexico (approximately 3,000 elk) during dawn or dusk for best viewing; particularly spectacular during fall rutting season (September-October) when bull elk bugling can be heard throughout the caldera; Valle Grande meadows visible from Highway 4 pullouts; Dawn/dusk year-round, peak during fall [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/vall/planyourvisit/wildlife-observing.htm), [Source](https://wildlife.dgf.nm.gov/wildlife-for-everyone-experiencing-the-elk-rut-in-the-valles-caldera/)]

- **Valle Grande Scenic Overlooks** (30 minutes) - Stop at multiple pullouts along Highway 4 to view the 14-mile-wide volcanic caldera formed 1.25 million years ago, with expansive meadows and mountain vistas; Highway 4 corridor; Open 24/7 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/vall/index.htm)]

- **Ranger-Led Programs** (1-2 hours) - Join morning or afternoon guided hikes and educational programs offered June 1-October 1, plus bi-monthly night sky programs at this International Dark Sky Park; Valle Grande Visitor Center; Check schedule at visitor center [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/vall/planyourvisit/ranger-led-activities.htm), [Source](https://albuquerque.com/make-a-road-trip-to-unforgettable-valles-caldera/)]

- **La Garita Summit Trail** (4-5 hours) - A challenging 7.6-mile out-and-back hike with 1,800-foot elevation gain reaching the caldera's north rim, offering what many consider the best views in the preserve; Accessed via backcountry permit; Daylight hours [[Source](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/new-mexico/valles-caldera-national-preserve), [Source](https://www.newmexicomagazine.org/blog/post/valles-caldera-hiking-trails/)]

- **El Cajete Trail** (4-5 hours) - A 9.4-mile loop featuring wildflowers in spring, leaving from the Cabin District and climbing to El Cajete Meadow on a high mesa; Cabin District trailhead (4 miles from visitor center); Daylight hours with backcountry permit [[Source](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/new-mexico/valles-caldera-national-preserve), [Source](https://www.newmexicomagazine.org/blog/post/valles-caldera-hiking-trails/)]

- **Fly Fishing** (3-4 hours) - Fish pristine streams in Valle Toledo and San Antonio Creek for rainbow and German brown trout using only artificial flies or lures with single barbless hooks (no wading allowed); Requires New Mexico fishing license, HMAV stamp, and preserve fishing permit (annual or 7-day); Backcountry areas; Year-round when streams not frozen [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/vall/planyourvisit/fishing.htm), [Source](https://www.losamigosdevallescaldera.org/post/valles-caldera-2024-2025-fishing-season-open)]

- **Mountain Biking** (2-5 hours depending on route) - Ride trails ranging from the 8-mile Rito de Los Indios (most popular) to the challenging 15.9-mile Redondito trail; routes cover 22-38 miles with elevation gains up to 3,800 feet at altitudes above 8,500 feet; Backcountry trails; Daylight hours with backcountry vehicle permit [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/vall/planyourvisit/mountain-biking.htm), [Source](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/new-mexico/valles-caldera-national-preserve/mountain-biking)]

- **Stargazing Programs** (2-3 hours) - Experience New Mexico's newest International Dark Sky Park (designated 2021) with year-round ranger-led programs, telescope viewing, full moon hikes, meteor shower watch parties, and astrophotography workshops; Valle Grande overlooks and visitor center area; Evening programs, check schedule [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/vall/planyourvisit/night-sky-viewing.htm), [Source](https://darksky.org/places/valles-caldera-national-preserve-dark-sky-park/)]

- **Backcountry Vehicle Tours** (4-6 hours) - Drive into the preserve's backcountry areas with a timed-entry permit (maximum 45 vehicles per day) to access remote trails, fishing spots, and wildlife viewing areas; Entrance Station for permit pickup; 6-hour entry windows, must return 30 minutes before park closure [[Source](https://www.recreation.gov/timed-entry/10089849), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/vall/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- **Cross-Country Skiing and Snowshoeing** (2-4 hours) - Explore groomed trails in Valle Grande area during winter months (mid-December to mid-March); snowshoe rentals available at Entrance Station; Valle Grande Staging Area; Fridays-Sundays 9:00 AM-5:00 PM (expanded holiday schedule Dec 26-Jan 6) [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/vall/planyourvisit/winter-activities.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/cross-country-skiing-at-vall.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **History Grove** (1-2 hours) - Hike through a 125-acre old-growth forest featuring ponderosa pines and Douglas firs between 250-400 years old, located about 4.5 miles from the main gate; Backcountry area, 4.5 miles from entrance; Daylight hours with backcountry access [[Source](https://www.travelawaits.com/2677727/valles-caldera-best-things-to-do/)]

- **The Cabin District** (1-2 hours) - Visit five historic buildings including the Ranch Foreman's Cabin where Netflix filmed the Longmire series; take a self-guided tour exploring structures from the preserve's ranching history; 4 miles from visitor center; 30-minute self-guided tour available during daylight [[Source](https://www.travelawaits.com/2677727/valles-caldera-best-things-to-do/), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/take-a-self-guided-tour-of-the-cabin-district.htm)]

- **Hidden Valley Trail and "The Missing" Cabin** (2-3 hours) - Hike this scenic 3.5-mile out-and-back trail that passes the cabin location used in Ron Howard's 2003 film "The Missing," considered one of the most beautiful trails in the preserve; Hidden Valley trailhead; Daylight hours [[Source](https://www.travelawaits.com/2677727/valles-caldera-best-things-to-do/)]

- **Valle San Antonio** (2-3 hours) - Explore this serene valley known for lush greenery, tranquil streams, and excellent birdwatching opportunities away from the more popular Valle Grande area; Backcountry access required; Daylight hours [[Source](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/new-mexico/hidden-wonders-of-new-mexicos-valles-caldera-national-preserve/)]

- **Sulphur Creek Geothermal Trail** (1-2 hours) - Follow the Sulphur Creek trail through an old sulphur mining area, skirting Valle Seco and passing three geothermal ponds with active thermal features; self-guided geology tour available; Sulphur Springs area; 1-2 hours for self-guided tour [[Source](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/new-mexico/hidden-wonders-of-new-mexicos-valles-caldera-national-preserve/), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/take-a-self-guided-geology-tour-of-sulphur-springs.htm)]

- **Cerro La Jara Loop Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - Walk around a small volcanic dome on this flat, accessible trail perfect for all ages, offering beautiful grassland landscapes with fewer crowds than main trails; Backcountry area; Daylight hours [[Source](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/new-mexico/valles-caldera-national-preserve)]

## Also Nearby

- **Bandelier National Monument** (3-4 hours, 30-45 minutes from Valles Caldera) - Explore ancestral Puebloan cliff dwellings, with the 1.2-mile Main Loop Trail providing access to pueblo homes, kivas, rock paintings, and petroglyphs, plus 70+ miles of additional hiking trails; 15 Entrance Road, Los Alamos, NM 87544; Daily, hours vary by season [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/band/planyourvisit/directions.htm), [Source](https://roadtrippers.com/magazine/visiting-bandelier-national-monument/)]

- **Jemez Historic Site** (1 hour, 15 minutes south on Highway 4) - Walk through 500-year-old pueblo ruins and Spanish mission on a 1,400-foot interpretive trail at this state historic site; 18078 NM-4, Jemez Springs, NM 87025; Wednesday-Sunday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM; Adults $7, children 16 and under free [[Source](https://nmhistoricsites.org/jemez), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g47073-d126835-Reviews-Jemez_Historic_Site-Jemez_Springs_New_Mexico.html)]

- **Spence Hot Springs** (1-1.5 hours, 20 minutes south) - Hike a moderate 0.7-mile trail (20-30 minutes each way) to natural hot spring pools at 95-100°F with scenic views of Jemez Canyon; Spence Hot Springs Trailhead off Highway 4, 7 miles north of Jemez Springs village; Open 24/7, best at dawn/dusk to avoid crowds [[Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/spence-hot-springs-trail), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g47073-d103746-Reviews-Spence_Hot_Springs-Jemez_Springs_New_Mexico.html)]

- **Soda Dam** (30 minutes, 15 minutes south) - View this unique natural travertine formation created by mineral-rich hot springs depositing calcium carbonate over thousands of years, easily accessible roadside stop; Highway 4, 6 miles north of Jemez Springs; Open 24/7 [[Source](https://jemezsprings.com/things-to-do/attractions/soda-dam-experience-this-wonder-in-jemez-springs-new-mexico/), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g47073-Activities-Jemez_Springs_New_Mexico.html)]

- **Battleship Rock** (30 minutes, 12 minutes south) - Photograph this 200-foot-tall volcanic rock formation created over 5 million years ago, a dramatic landmark along the Jemez River; Highway 4, 6 miles north of Jemez Springs; Open 24/7 [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g47073-Activities-Jemez_Springs_New_Mexico.html)]

- **Fenton Lake State Park** (1-2 hours, 10 minutes north) - Fish for rainbow and German brown trout at this high-elevation lake (7,900 feet) with wheelchair-accessible fishing platforms; winter ice fishing available; 455 Fenton Lake Road, Jemez Springs, NM 87025; Open 24 hours daily [[Source](https://www.emnrd.nm.gov/spd/find-a-park/fenton-lake-state-park/), [Source](https://www.newmexico.org/listing/fenton-lake-state-park/243/)]

- **Jemez Springs Bath House** (1-2 hours, 20 minutes south) - Soak in mineral hot springs at this village-owned bathhouse offering private treatment rooms with cool or hot soaks and licensed massage therapy; Village of Jemez Springs; Hours vary, call ahead; Starting at $12 for 25-minute soak [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g47073-Activities-Jemez_Springs_New_Mexico.html)]

- **Los Alamos Museums** (2-3 hours, 40 minutes northeast) - Visit the Bradbury Science Museum (free admission) featuring Manhattan Project history and atomic bomb replicas, and the Los Alamos History Museum ($5 admission) covering ancient Puebloans through the Manhattan Project; Los Alamos, NM (38 miles via NM 4); Museum hours vary [[Source](https://wanderlog.com/drive/between/59812/60021/los-alamos-to-jemez-springs-drive), [Source](https://www.trippy.com/distance/Jemez-Springs-to-Los-Alamos-NM)]
