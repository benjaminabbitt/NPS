# Theodore Roosevelt Island

## Key Activities

- <a name="activity-memorial-plaza"></a>**Memorial Plaza** (15-20 minutes) - The focal point of the island featuring a 17-foot-tall bronze statue of President Theodore Roosevelt, surrounded by four 21-foot-tall granite tablets inscribed with his most famous quotations on nature, manhood, youth, and the state, located in a clearing on the island's northern side with two pools with fountains and a water-filled moat crossed by pedestrian bridges; Theodore Roosevelt Island, George Washington Memorial Parkway, Arlington, VA (38.895073, -77.061859); Open daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/this/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Simply Awesome Trips](https://www.simplyawesometrips.com/visit-theodore-roosevelt-island/), [The Travel](https://www.thetravel.com/what-to-know-about-theodore-roosevelt-island/)]

- <a name="activity-woods-trail"></a>**Woods Trail** (10-15 minutes) - The shortest trail at 0.3 miles, passing through the middle of the island including the Memorial Plaza, offering an easy walk through forested areas; Theodore Roosevelt Island (38.895073, -77.061859); Open daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM [Source: [NPS Trails](https://www.nps.gov/this/planyourvisit/trails.htm), [Fun in Fairfax VA](https://www.funinfairfaxva.com/explore-the-surprises-of-theodore-roosevelt-island-hike/)]

- <a name="activity-upland-trail"></a>**Upland Trail** (20-25 minutes) - A 0.75-mile trail through the interior wooded uplands of the island, passing near the former John Mason mansion site where visitors can explore the burnt remains of the historic structure; Theodore Roosevelt Island (38.895073, -77.061859); Open daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM [Source: [NPS Trails](https://www.nps.gov/this/planyourvisit/trails.htm), [The Travel](https://www.thetravel.com/what-to-know-about-theodore-roosevelt-island/)]

- <a name="activity-swamp-trail"></a>**Swamp Trail** (45-60 minutes) - A 1.5-mile loop trail encircling the perimeter of the island, the longest trail on the island, passing through swampy woods and cattail marsh with part pea gravel and part boardwalk surfaces, excellent for birdwatching and wildlife viewing; Theodore Roosevelt Island (38.895073, -77.061859); Open daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM [Source: [NPS Trails](https://www.nps.gov/this/planyourvisit/trails.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/washington-dc/theodore-roosevelt-island--2), [District Fray](https://districtfray.com/articles/wanderlust-theodore-roosevelt-island/)]

- <a name="activity-wildlife-viewing"></a>**Wildlife Viewing and Birdwatching** (30-60 minutes) - The forested island acts as a migrant trap hosting diverse bird life during spring and fall migration, with ornithologists identifying over 200 species including woodpeckers, ducks, herons, rails, waders, shorebirds, Marsh Wren, Swamp Sparrow, Wood Ducks, and Prothonotary Warblers, plus excellent winter viewing habitat; trails throughout the island (38.895073, -77.061859); Open daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM [Source: [Virginia DWR](https://dwr.virginia.gov/vbwt/sites/theodore-roosevelt-island/), [Birders Guide to Maryland and DC](https://birdersguidemddc.org/site/theodore-roosevelt-island/), [The Travel](https://www.thetravel.com/what-to-know-about-theodore-roosevelt-island/)]

- <a name="activity-ranger-programs"></a>**Ranger-led Programs** (45 minutes) - Guided tours with rangers or NPS volunteers providing educational programs and the inside story on Theodore Roosevelt Island; Theodore Roosevelt Island (38.895073, -77.061859); Check NPS website for current schedule [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/this/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Viator](https://www.viator.com/Washington-DC-attractions/Theodore-Roosevelt-Island/overview/d657-a22971)]

- <a name="activity-junior-ranger"></a>**Junior Ranger Program** (30-45 minutes) - Kids can complete activity booklet to earn a Junior Ranger badge while learning about Theodore Roosevelt's conservation legacy; Available at the island or online; Theodore Roosevelt Island (38.895073, -77.061859); Open daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/this/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a name="hidden-grandmother-tree"></a>**The Grandmother Tree** (10 minutes) - A special elegant tree with limbs that appear to be hugging the air, located along one of the island's trails, offering a unique photo opportunity and moment of natural beauty; Theodore Roosevelt Island trails (38.895073, -77.061859); Open daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM [Source: [The Travel](https://www.thetravel.com/what-to-know-about-theodore-roosevelt-island/)]

- <a name="hidden-rare-plants"></a>**Rare Native Plant Species** (20-30 minutes) - The island's isolation has led to the presence of several rare plant species including American Holly thriving in the shady understory, with more than 35,000 indigenous species of plants providing important food and shelter for local wildlife, best viewed in spring and early summer when wildflowers are spectacular; Throughout island trails (38.895073, -77.061859); Open daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM [Source: [The Travel](https://www.thetravel.com/what-to-know-about-theodore-roosevelt-island/), [Mighty Travels](https://www.mightytravels.com/2024/05/uncovering-hidden-gems-7-unforgettable-off-the-beaten-path-experiences-in-washington-dc/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/washington-dc/theodore-roosevelt-island--2)]

- <a name="hidden-kayak-launch"></a>**Canoe and Kayak Launch** (1-2 hours) - Lesser-known watercraft access points allowing visitors to explore the island's floodplain and marshes from the Potomac River waterways, offering unique perspectives of the island and wildlife; Theodore Roosevelt Island shoreline (38.895073, -77.061859); Open daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM [Source: [The Travel](https://www.thetravel.com/what-to-know-about-theodore-roosevelt-island/), [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/this/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- <a name="hidden-olmsted-design"></a>**Frederick Law Olmsted Landscape Design** (varies) - The entire island was designed by Frederick Law Olmsted, America's first landscape architect, with the Civilian Conservation Corps transforming this former farmland from a tangled swamp into a "real forest" designed to mimic the natural forest that once covered the island after it was purchased in 1931, creating a living memorial to the conservation president; Throughout the island (38.895073, -77.061859); Open daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM [Source: [The Travel](https://www.thetravel.com/what-to-know-about-theodore-roosevelt-island/), [8 Must-See Monuments](https://washington.org/visit-dc/must-see-memorials-monuments-national-mall)]

## Also Nearby

- <a name="nearby-arlington-cemetery"></a>**Arlington National Cemetery** (1-2 hours, 2 miles) - America's most famous military cemetery featuring the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier with its Changing of the Guard ceremony, John F. Kennedy's gravesite with eternal flame, and memorials to service members from every American conflict; Arlington National Cemetery, Arlington, VA 22211 (38.8783, -77.0687); Open daily 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (October-March) or 8:00 AM - 7:00 PM (April-September) [Source: [Stay Arlington](https://www.stayarlington.com/directory/theodore-roosevelt-island/)]

- <a name="nearby-marine-memorial"></a>**Marine Corps War Memorial (Iwo Jima Memorial)** (30 minutes, 2 miles) - Iconic bronze sculpture depicting six Marines raising the American flag on Mount Suribachi during WWII, one of the most recognizable war memorials in America with spectacular views of Washington DC; US Marine Memorial Circle, Arlington, VA 22209 (38.8909, -77.0708); Open 24 hours [Source: [Stay Arlington](https://www.stayarlington.com/directory/theodore-roosevelt-island/)]

- <a name="nearby-pentagon-memorial"></a>**National 9/11 Pentagon Memorial** (45 minutes, 3 miles) - Outdoor memorial featuring 184 illuminated benches representing each victim of the September 11, 2001 terrorist attack on the Pentagon, arranged by age with each bench uniquely positioned over a pool of flowing water; 1 N Rotary Rd, Arlington, VA 22202 (38.8613, -77.0526); Open 24 hours [Source: [Stay Arlington](https://www.stayarlington.com/directory/theodore-roosevelt-island/)]

- <a name="nearby-georgetown"></a>**Georgetown Historic District** (1-3 hours, 1 mile) - Historic neighborhood featuring cobblestone streets, Federal-style architecture, upscale shopping on M Street and Wisconsin Avenue, waterfront dining, and Georgetown University campus; Georgetown, Washington, DC (38.9072, -77.0719); Always accessible, individual shop/restaurant hours vary [Source: [Stay Arlington](https://www.stayarlington.com/directory/theodore-roosevelt-island/)]

- <a name="nearby-lincoln-memorial"></a>**Lincoln Memorial** (30-45 minutes, 2 miles) - Iconic marble memorial featuring a 19-foot seated statue of Abraham Lincoln, with inscriptions of his Gettysburg Address and Second Inaugural Address, anchoring the western end of the National Mall with views across the Reflecting Pool to the Washington Monument; 2 Lincoln Memorial Cir NW, Washington, DC 20002 (38.8893, -77.0502); Open 24 hours, staffed daily 9:30 AM - 10:00 PM [Source: [Washington.org](https://washington.org/visit-dc/must-see-memorials-monuments-national-mall)]

- <a name="nearby-gravelly-point"></a>**Gravelly Point Park** (30-60 minutes, 3 miles via Mount Vernon Trail) - Popular spot offering unique views of planes taking off and landing at Reagan National Airport, with picnic areas and connection to the Mount Vernon Trail for biking and walking; George Washington Memorial Parkway, Arlington, VA 22202 (38.8594, -77.0376); Open daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM [Source: [Stay Arlington](https://www.stayarlington.com/directory/theodore-roosevelt-island/)]

- <a name="nearby-lady-bird-park"></a>**Lady Bird Johnson Park** (30-45 minutes, 1 mile) - Island park featuring thousands of bright yellow daffodils and hundreds of tulips in spring, offering scenic walking paths and views of the Potomac River and DC monuments; George Washington Memorial Parkway, Arlington, VA (38.8819, -77.0528); Open daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM [Source: [Washington.org](https://washington.org/visit-va/things-do-arlington-waterfront)]

- <a name="nearby-roaches-run"></a>**Roaches Run Waterfowl Sanctuary** (45-60 minutes, 2 miles) - Popular birdwatching spot along the Potomac River with observation areas for viewing osprey, green heron, red-winged blackbirds, and mallards, offering a peaceful natural setting near Reagan National Airport; George Washington Memorial Parkway, Arlington, VA (38.8597, -77.0430); Open daily dawn to dusk [Source: [Washington.org](https://washington.org/visit-va/things-do-arlington-waterfront)]

## Review / Personal Notes

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 1.5 to 2 hours for a complete island visit including the Memorial Plaza, at least one trail hike (preferably the Swamp Trail for the full perimeter experience), and time for wildlife viewing; serious birdwatchers may wish to allow up to 3 hours during migration seasons. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/this/planyourvisit/index.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/washington-dc/theodore-roosevelt-island--2), [Viator](https://www.viator.com/Washington-DC-attractions/Theodore-Roosevelt-Island/overview/d657-a22971), [Simply Awesome Trips](https://www.simplyawesometrips.com/visit-theodore-roosevelt-island/)]

**Note:** The island requires no entrance fee and is accessible via a footbridge from a parking lot on the northbound side of George Washington Memorial Parkway in Arlington, Virginia. No cars or bicycles are permitted on the island. Restrooms are closed during winter (typically October-April) with a portable toilet available near the restroom building. Dogs are allowed on leash. [Source: [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/this/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]
