# Theodore Roosevelt Island N MEM (National Memorial)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **George Washington Memorial Parkway Headquarters** (700 George Washington Memorial Parkway, McLean, VA 22101 [38.9617705, -77.146267]; Weekdays 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM; 703-289-2500) - Theodore Roosevelt Island is part of the George Washington Memorial Parkway system, and passport stamps are available at the parkway headquarters. [Source: [NPS George Washington Memorial Parkway Passport Stamps](https://www.nps.gov/gwmp/planyourvisit/passport_stamps.htm)]

**Note:** There is no visitor center on Theodore Roosevelt Island itself. Stamps may also be available at other George Washington Memorial Parkway locations including Arlington House Visitor Center. Call 703-289-2500 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [NPS Theodore Roosevelt Island](https://www.nps.gov/this/)]

## Key Activities

- **Swamp Trail Loop (45-60 minutes)** - This 1.5-mile loop trail takes you around the perimeter of the island through diverse ecosystems including swampy bottomlands and upland forests, featuring a boardwalk section through marshes with views of Georgetown and the Potomac River; parking lot off northbound George Washington Memorial Parkway (Theodore Roosevelt Island, VA [38.895073, -77.061859]; Daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM). [Source: [NPS Theodore Roosevelt Island Trails](https://www.nps.gov/this/planyourvisit/trails.htm), [Simply Awesome Trips](https://www.simplyawesometrips.com/visit-theodore-roosevelt-island/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/washington-dc/theodore-roosevelt-island--2)]

- **Theodore Roosevelt Memorial Plaza (15-30 minutes)** - Visit the 17-foot bronze statue of Theodore Roosevelt on the island's highest point, surrounded by granite monoliths inscribed with his quotes about nature, manhood, youth, and the state, completed in 1967; accessible via footbridge from parking area; Theodore Roosevelt Island, VA [38.895073, -77.061859]; Daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM. [Source: [NPS Theodore Roosevelt Memorial](https://www.nps.gov/places/000/theodore-roosevelt-memorial.htm), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodore_Roosevelt_Island), [Washington.org](https://washington.org/find-dc-listings/theodore-roosevelt-island)]

- **Woods Trail and Upland Trail (30-45 minutes combined)** - Explore shorter woodland trails (Woods Trail 0.3 miles, Upland Trail 0.75 miles) through the island's diverse habitats with shade and peaceful forest settings; Theodore Roosevelt Island, VA [38.895073, -77.061859]; Daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM. [Source: [NPS Theodore Roosevelt Island Trails](https://www.nps.gov/this/planyourvisit/trails.htm), [District Fray](https://districtfray.com/articles/wanderlust-theodore-roosevelt-island/)]

- **Complete Island Loop Trail (1-1.5 hours)** - Walk the combined 1.7-mile trail system connecting all paths on the island, easy terrain with 55 feet elevation gain, suitable for all ages; Theodore Roosevelt Island, VA [38.895073, -77.061859]; Daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM. [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/washington-dc/theodore-roosevelt-island--2), [Go Hike Virginia](https://gohikevirginia.com/theodore-roosevelt-island/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Birdwatching (1-2 hours)** - Over 200 bird species have been identified on the island due to its diverse habitats and isolation, making it a prime location for ornithologists and bird enthusiasts to observe waterfowl, songbirds, and raptors; Theodore Roosevelt Island, VA [38.895073, -77.061859]; Daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM. [Source: [Mighty Travels](https://www.mightytravels.com/2024/05/uncovering-hidden-gems-7-unforgettable-off-the-beaten-path-experiences-in-washington-dc/)]

- **The Grandmother Tree (15-20 minutes)** - Seek out this special tree known for its elegant branches that appear to hug the air, a unique natural feature mentioned by visitors exploring the island's trails; Theodore Roosevelt Island, VA [38.895073, -77.061859]; Daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM. [Source: [Mighty Travels](https://www.mightytravels.com/2024/05/uncovering-hidden-gems-7-unforgettable-off-the-beaten-path-experiences-in-washington-dc/)]

- **John Mason Mansion Ruins (20-30 minutes)** - The Upland Trail passes near the burnt remains of John Mason's historic mansion, offering a glimpse into the island's 18th-century history when it was a private estate; Theodore Roosevelt Island, VA [38.895073, -77.061859]; Daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM. [Source: [Mighty Travels](https://www.mightytravels.com/2024/05/uncovering-hidden-gems-7-unforgettable-off-the-beaten-path-experiences-in-washington-dc/), [Fun in Fairfax VA](https://www.funinfairfaxva.com/explore-the-surprises-of-theodore-roosevelt-island-hike/)]

- **Kayaking and Canoeing Access (1-3 hours)** - Lesser-known canoe and kayak launches allow exploration of the island's floodplain and marshes from the Potomac River waterways, offering unique perspectives of the island's natural features; Theodore Roosevelt Island, VA [38.895073, -77.061859]; Daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM. [Source: [Mighty Travels](https://www.mightytravels.com/2024/05/uncovering-hidden-gems-7-unforgettable-off-the-beaten-path-experiences-in-washington-dc/)]

- **Seasonal Wildflowers and Winter Views (30-60 minutes)** - Visit in late spring to see wildflowers covering the ground, or in winter/early spring when minimal foliage allows views of the Kennedy Center, Watergate Hotel, and Washington Harbor through the trees; Theodore Roosevelt Island, VA [38.895073, -77.061859]; Daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM. [Source: [Fun in Fairfax VA](https://www.funinfairfaxva.com/explore-the-surprises-of-theodore-roosevelt-island-hike/)]

- **Rare Plant Species Observation (30-60 minutes)** - The island's isolation has preserved over 35,000 indigenous plant species including American Holly thriving in the shady understory, providing important habitat for local wildlife; Theodore Roosevelt Island, VA [38.895073, -77.061859]; Daily 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM. [Source: [Mighty Travels](https://www.mightytravels.com/2024/05/uncovering-hidden-gems-7-unforgettable-off-the-beaten-path-experiences-in-washington-dc/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Georgetown Historic District and C&O Canal (2-4 hours)** - Explore the historic Georgetown neighborhood with cobblestone streets, shops, restaurants, and the Chesapeake & Ohio Canal National Historical Park featuring the towpath trail and visitor center with free ranger-led walking tours at 11:00 AM and 2:30 PM; across the Potomac River from Theodore Roosevelt Island; 1057 Thomas Jefferson Street NW, Washington, DC [38.9037, -77.0632]; Visitor Center: Wednesday-Sunday 9:30 AM - 4:30 PM, Trail: Open daily. [Source: [Georgetown DC C&O Canal](https://www.georgetowndc.com/neighborhood/co-canal/), [NPS Georgetown Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/choh/planyourvisit/georgetownvisitorcenter.htm), [C&O Canal Trust](https://www.canaltrust.org/pyv_events/georgetown-walking-tours/)]

- **John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts (1-2 hours for tour)** - Visit this premier performing arts center with free daily tours (weekdays 10:00 AM - 4:30 PM, weekends 10:00 AM - 12:30 PM, 75 minutes) exploring grand halls and theaters, plus free Millennium Stage performances at 6:00 PM Wednesday-Saturday; visible from Theodore Roosevelt Island across the Potomac; 2700 F Street NW, Washington, DC [38.8957, -77.0555]; Daily 10:00 AM - Midnight. [Source: [Free Tours By Foot](https://freetoursbyfoot.com/the-kennedy-center/), [Kennedy Center](https://www.kennedy-center.org/), [Washington.org Kennedy Center Guide](https://washington.org/dc-guide-to/john-f-kennedy-center-washington-dc)]

- **Arlington National Cemetery (2-4 hours)** - Tour this iconic military cemetery featuring the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier with Changing of the Guard ceremony, John F. Kennedy's gravesite, and Welcome Center with exhibits; approximately 2 miles south of Theodore Roosevelt Island along the George Washington Memorial Parkway; Arlington, VA [38.8783, -77.0687]; Daily 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Tours: 8:30 AM - 4:00 PM. [Source: [Arlington National Cemetery](https://www.arlingtoncemetery.mil/visit), [Washington.org Arlington Cemetery Guide](https://washington.org/DC-guide-to/arlington-national-cemetery), [Arlington Cemetery Welcome Center](https://www.arlingtoncemetery.mil/Explore/Welcome-Center)]

- **U.S. Marine Corps War Memorial (Iwo Jima Memorial) (30-45 minutes)** - View the iconic bronze statue depicting the raising of the American flag at Iwo Jima, located near Arlington National Cemetery with 24/7 access and sunset parades in summer; 1.1 miles from Theodore Roosevelt Island; Marshall Drive, Arlington, VA [38.8908, -77.0701]; Open 24 hours, free admission. [Source: [TripAdvisor Theodore Roosevelt Island Nearby](https://www.tripadvisor.com/AttractionsNear-g30242-d482047-Theodore_Roosevelt_Island_Park-Arlington_Virginia.html)]
