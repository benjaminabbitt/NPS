## Key Activities

- **Ranger-Led Home Tour** (45-60 minutes) - Guided tour through the preserved historic home where civil rights leader Medgar Evers lived with his family at the time of his assassination on June 12, 1963; visitors can see the children's rooms and evidence of the tragic assassination; the home is preserved as a museum documenting the Civil Rights Movement; 2332 Margaret Walker Alexander Drive, Jackson, MS 39213; Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM-4:00 PM (closed noon-1:00 PM for lunch); groups of 10 or more should reserve in advance, maximum group size 30 people, reservations required for groups larger than 15; 601-345-7211. [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/memy/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g43833-d286679-Reviews-Medgar_Evers_Home-Jackson_Mississippi.html), [Visit Jackson](https://www.visitjackson.com/blog/medgar-evers-home-extends-days-hours/)]

- **Self-Guided Neighborhood Exploration** (15-30 minutes) - Walk through the historic neighborhood surrounding the Evers home, viewing the exterior and learning about the context of the Civil Rights era; visitors should respect the privacy of nearby residents; 2332 Margaret Walker Alexander Drive, Jackson, MS 39213; accessible during daylight hours. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/memy/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Myrlie's Garden Visit** (15-30 minutes) - Visit the memorial garden featuring picnic tables, shaded pavilion, educational exhibits, and community garden with accessible parking and sidewalks; offers a peaceful space for reflection on the Evers' legacy; Jackson, MS; open sunrise to sunset daily. [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/memy/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Mississippi Civil Rights Collection at Tougaloo College** (1-2 hours) - The Lillian Pierce Benbow Room of Special Collections in Coleman Library contains thousands of documents, tapes, photographs, and artifacts documenting the civil rights struggle, including original papers, photographs and memorabilia of movement leaders including Fannie Lou Hamer, Medgar Evers and Martin Luther King Jr.; 500 West County Line Road, Jackson, MS; 601-977-7905 or 1-888-42GALOO. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/memy/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Civil Rights Trail](https://civilrightstrail.com/attraction/tougaloo-college/), [Visit Jackson](https://www.visitjackson.com/blog/tougaloo-college/)]

- **Woodworth Chapel at Tougaloo College** (20-30 minutes) - Historic 1901 chapel built mostly with student labor that hosted many state and national Civil Rights leaders including Medgar Evers, Fannie Lou Hamer, and Dr. Martin Luther King Jr.; the college served as a refuge for activists and Freedom Riders during the Civil Rights Movement; 500 West County Line Road, Jackson, MS; 601-977-7905. [Source: [Civil Rights Trail](https://civilrightstrail.com/attraction/tougaloo-college/), [Visit Jackson](https://www.visitjackson.com/blog/tougaloo-college/)]

- **Margaret Walker Center at Jackson State University** (30-60 minutes) - Cultural and educational center documenting African American history and literature, part of the broader civil rights heritage sites in Jackson; contact for current hours and programming; Jackson, MS. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/memy/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **COFO Civil Rights Education Center at Jackson State University** (30-60 minutes) - Education center focused on the Council of Federated Organizations' role in the Mississippi Civil Rights Movement; contact for current hours and visitor information; Jackson, MS. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/memy/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]
