## Key Activities {#activities}

- <span id="activity-guided-tour">**Ranger-Led Guided Home Tour**</span> (45 minutes to 1 hour) - Tour the preserved home where civil rights leader Medgar Evers lived with his family at the time of his assassination in 1963. The home retains visible evidence of the shooting, including the bullet hole through the front window and wall. Knowledgeable National Park Service guides provide comprehensive oral history of the Evers family and the civil rights movement. Tours are available throughout operating hours; groups of 10 or more should call ahead, and groups larger than 15 require reservations (maximum capacity 30 visitors). The home features an accessible ramp, though visitors using wheelchairs may need to transfer to smaller chairs to navigate the compact dwelling. No restrooms are available on site. 2332 Margaret Walker Alexander Drive, Jackson, MS 39213 (32.340878, -90.212145); Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM-4:00 PM with closure from noon-1:00 PM for lunch; 601-345-7211. [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/memy/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g43833-d286679-Reviews-Medgar_Evers_Home-Jackson_Mississippi.html), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/medgar-and-myrlie-evers-home-national-monument/)

- <span id="activity-myrlies-garden">**Myrlie's Garden**</span> (30 minutes to 1 hour) - Visit the commemorative garden featuring picnic tables, shaded pavilion, educational exhibits, and community garden. The garden offers accessible parking and paved sidewalks. Open sunrise to sunset year-round. 2332 Margaret Walker Alexander Drive, Jackson, MS 39213 (32.340878, -90.212145); sunrise to sunset daily; 601-345-7211. [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/memy/planyourvisit/hours.htm)

- <span id="activity-junior-ranger">**Junior Ranger Activities**</span> (30 minutes to 1 hour) - Participate in Junior Ranger programs available during special events and commemoration periods. Contact the monument for current program schedules. 601-345-7211. [NPS Junior Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/memy/learn/kidsyouth/junior-ranger.htm)

## Hidden Gems {#hidden}

- <span id="hidden-smith-robertson">**Smith Robertson Museum and Cultural Center**</span> (1-2 hours) - Housed in the first public school for Black students in Jackson (named for Smith Robertson, a former slave who became Jackson's first Black city alderman), this museum features permanent exhibits including "From Africa to Mississippi," "The Medgar Wiley Evers Retrospective Exhibition," and civil rights exhibits. The museum is a comprehensive depository of artifacts celebrating African-American history and culture. 528 Bloom Street, Jackson, MS 39202 (32.3054071, -90.1867736); Monday-Friday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Saturday 10:00 AM-1:00 PM, closed Sundays; $7 adults 18-59, $5 seniors 60+, $6 military, $5 college students with ID, $4 children 4-17; 601-960-1457. [Smith Robertson Museum](https://jacksonms.gov/smith-robertson-museum/), [Visit Jackson](https://www.visitjackson.com/directory/smith-robertson-museum-and-cultural-center/)

- <span id="hidden-civil-rights-tour">**Jackson Civil Rights Trail**</span> (2-4 hours self-guided) - Follow the Mississippi Freedom Trail markers throughout Jackson, visiting sites including the Greyhound Bus Station, Mississippi State Capitol, Council of Federated Organizations Civil Rights Education Center, Jackson State University, the historic Woolworth's sit-in site, Farish Street Historic District (with Big Apple Inn, frequented by Medgar Evers), Lynch Street Corridor centered around the M.W. Stringer Masonic Lodge that sheltered NAACP leader Medgar Evers, and Tougaloo College (recognized as "The Cradle of the Civil Rights Movement in Mississippi"). Various locations throughout Jackson, MS; various hours. [Visit Jackson Civil Rights Tour](https://www.visitjackson.com/things-to-do/civil-rights-history/), [US Civil Rights Trail](https://civilrightstrail.com/attraction/medgar-evers-home/)

- <span id="hidden-medgar-evers-statue">**Medgar Evers Statue and Library**</span> (15-30 minutes) - Visit the life-sized bronze statue of Medgar Evers and the city library renamed in his memory, located in Evers' former neighborhood on a street also renamed in his honor. Jackson, MS; library hours vary, call ahead. [Visit Jackson Civil Rights History](https://www.visitjackson.com/things-to-do/civil-rights-history/)

## Also Nearby {#nearby}

- <span id="nearby-mcrm">**Mississippi Civil Rights Museum**</span> (2-3 hours, 5 miles from monument) - One of the premier civil rights museums in the nation, featuring eight interactive galleries documenting the struggle for civil rights in Mississippi between 1945 and 1970. The museum shares stories of the Mississippi Civil Rights Movement that changed the nation. Located adjacent to the Museum of Mississippi History in downtown Jackson. 222 North Street, Jackson, MS 39201 (32.3005771, -90.1794839); Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM-5:00 PM (free admission on Sundays), closed Mondays and major holidays; $15 adults, $8 youth (ages 4-22), $13 seniors (60+) or military, free for children under 3; 601-576-6800. [Mississippi Civil Rights Museum](https://mcrm.mdah.ms.gov/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g43833-d13382408-Reviews-Mississippi_Civil_Rights_Museum-Jackson_Mississippi.html)

- <span id="nearby-welty-house">**Eudora Welty House and Garden**</span> (1-1.5 hours, 3 miles from monument) - Tour the home where Pulitzer Prize-winning author Eudora Welty lived and wrote for seventy-six years. This National Historic Landmark offers guided house tours and self-guided garden experiences. Reservations recommended. 1119 Pinehurst Street, Jackson, MS 39202 (32.3170051, -90.1703183); Guided tours Tuesday-Friday at 9:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 3:00 PM, and Saturday at 1:00 PM and 3:00 PM; Self-guided garden experiences Tuesday-Friday 8:30 AM-4:00 PM, Saturday 12:30 PM-4:00 PM; Admission charged for guided tours; 601-353-7762. [Eudora Welty House](https://welty.mdah.ms.gov/visit), [Visit Jackson](https://www.visitjackson.com/directory/eudora-welty-house-and-garden/)

- <span id="nearby-ms-museum-art">**Mississippi Museum of Art**</span> (2-3 hours, 4 miles from monument) - The largest art museum in Mississippi, featuring permanent collections and rotating exhibitions, plus the Art Garden outdoor space. Free exhibitions available alongside ticketed Barksdale Galleries. 380 S Lamar Street, Jackson, MS 39201 (32.2966456, -90.1868192); Tuesday-Thursday 11:00 AM-7:00 PM, Friday-Saturday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM-5:00 PM, closed Mondays; $15 adults, $13 seniors (65+), $10 youth (6-17) and college students with ID, free for children 5 and under and on first Saturday of each month; 601-960-1515. [Mississippi Museum of Art](https://www.msmuseumart.org/tickets-hours/), [Visit Jackson](https://www.visitjackson.com/directory/mississippi-museum-of-art/)

- <span id="nearby-natchez-trace">**Natchez Trace Parkway**</span> (Half-day to full day, adjacent to Jackson) - A 444-mile scenic drive through three states with hiking, biking, horseback riding, and camping. Near Jackson, explore the Cypress Swamp boardwalk trail. The parkway passes along the Ross Barnett Reservoir before reaching Jackson. Maximum speed limit 50 mph, strictly enforced by park rangers. Free admission. Multiple access points near Jackson, MS; open 24 hours; 800-305-7417. [Natchez Trace Parkway NPS](https://www.nps.gov/natr/), [Scenic Trace](https://www.scenictrace.com/)

- <span id="nearby-vicksburg">**Vicksburg National Military Park**</span> (3-4 hours, 45 miles west/45-60 minutes from Jackson) - Relive one of the most crucial battles of the Civil War at this military park featuring a 16-mile driving tour, visitor center with 20-minute film, and extensive monuments and markers. Stop at the visitor center first to understand the historical context. 3201 Clay Street, Vicksburg, MS 39183 (32.3440605, -90.8514451); Daily 8:00 AM-5:00 PM (Visitor Center hours vary seasonally: April-October 8:00 AM-5:00 PM, rest of year 8:30 AM-4:30 PM), closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; $20 per vehicle, America the Beautiful Senior Pass accepted; 601-636-0583. [NPS Vicksburg](https://www.nps.gov/vick/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Visit Jackson](https://www.visitjackson.com/things-to-do/)
