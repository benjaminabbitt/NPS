# Chaco Culture NHP (National Historical Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-visitor-center"></a>**Visitor Center** (1808 County Road 7950, Nageezi, NM 87037 (36.0496541, -107.9482269); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 505-786-7014)

**Note:** Call 505-786-7014 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/chcu/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/chcu/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [NPS Contact](https://www.nps.gov/chcu/contacts.htm)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-pueblo-bonito-trail"></a>**Pueblo Bonito Trail (45 minutes)** - Walk through the largest and most famous great house at Chaco, featuring over 600 rooms and 40 kivas. This 0.6-mile roundtrip graveled trail climbs several short, steep rises as it loops through the ancient structure; 1808 County Road 7950, Nageezi, NM 87037; accessible during park hours (sunrise to sunset) [Source: [NPS Pueblo Bonito](https://www.nps.gov/chcu/planyourvisit/pueblo-bonito.htm), [The Trek Planner](https://thetrekplanner.com/pueblo-bonito-chaco-culture-national-historical-park-new-mexico/)]

- <a id="activity-chetro-ketl-trail"></a>**Chetro Ketl Trail (30-60 minutes)** - Explore one of Chaco's largest great houses, second only to Pueblo Bonito in size. The 0.6-mile trail provides access to the impressive architectural remains and offers views of the plaza and kivas; 1808 County Road 7950, Nageezi, NM 87037; accessible during park hours (sunrise to sunset) [Source: [AllTrails Chetro Ketl](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/chetro-ketl-trail), [GJ Hikes](https://www.gjhikes.com/2013/10/chetro-ketl.html), [Budget Savvy Travelers](https://www.thebudgetsavvytravelers.com/chaco-culture-national-historical-park/)]

- <a id="activity-casa-rinconada-trail"></a>**Casa Rinconada Trail (45 minutes)** - Visit the largest great kiva in Chaco Canyon, an impressive ceremonial structure measuring 63 feet in diameter. The trail through Casa Rinconada and nearby villages is 0.5 mile roundtrip; 1808 County Road 7950, Nageezi, NM 87037; accessible during park hours (sunrise to sunset) [Source: [NPS Casa Rinconada](https://www.nps.gov/places/casa-rinconada.htm), [Budget Savvy Travelers](https://www.thebudgetsavvytravelers.com/chaco-culture-national-historical-park/)]

- <a id="activity-pueblo-alto-loop-trail"></a>**Pueblo Alto Loop Trail (2-4 hours)** - Hike this 5.1-mile loop with 270 feet of elevation change for elevated views of Pueblo Bonito and access to multiple archaeological sites on the mesa top. The trail is easy and fairly level, though hot and exposed in summer. Many hikers just do the first part to the Pueblo Bonito overlook (2 miles roundtrip); 1808 County Road 7950, Nageezi, NM 87037; accessible during park hours (sunrise to sunset) [Source: [American Southwest Pueblo Alto](https://www.americansouthwest.net/new_mexico/chaco_culture/pueblo-alto-trail.html), [NPS Hiking Trails](https://www.nps.gov/chcu/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)]

- <a id="activity-una-vida-trail"></a>**Una Vida Trail (30-45 minutes)** - Discover one of Chaco's often-overlooked great houses located near the visitor center. The 1-mile roundtrip trail includes petroglyphs and begins at the northeast corner of the visitor center parking lot; 1808 County Road 7950, Nageezi, NM 87037; accessible during park hours (sunrise to sunset) [Source: [American Southwest Scenic Drive](https://www.americansouthwest.net/new_mexico/chaco_culture/scenic_drive.html), [Discovering Hidden Gems](https://discoveringhiddengems.com/chaco-culture-national-historical-park/)]

- <a id="activity-visitor-center-and-museum"></a>**Visitor Center and Museum (30-60 minutes)** - View exhibits about Chacoan culture, architecture, and astronomy. The center provides essential orientation to the site and its cultural significance. Stop here before exploring the ruins; 1808 County Road 7950, Nageezi, NM 87037; 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily [Source: [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/chcu/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Western National Parks](https://wnpa.org/about-wnpa/newsroom/five-must-do-activities-at-chaco-culture-national-historical-park-3)]

- <a id="activity-canyon-loop-drive-by-bicycle"></a>**Canyon Loop Drive by Bicycle (2-3 hours)** - Bike the nine-mile paved Canyon Loop Drive to experience all the natural beauty and history at Chaco. The road is nearly level and open from sunrise to sunset, with bike racks located at the visitor center and parking areas at the Chacoan sites; 1808 County Road 7950, Nageezi, NM 87037; sunrise to sunset [Source: [Western National Parks](https://wnpa.org/about-wnpa/newsroom/five-must-do-activities-at-chaco-culture-national-historical-park-3), [Discovering Hidden Gems](https://discoveringhiddengems.com/chaco-culture-national-historical-park/)]

- <a id="activity-night-sky-programs"></a>**Night Sky Programs (1.5-2.5 hours)** - Attend ranger-led stargazing programs to learn about celestial observations and astronomical practices of the ancestral Puebloans. Chaco is an International Dark Sky Park with incredibly dark and clear night skies. Programs offered April-October, typically on Fridays and Saturdays. Special events include full moon walks through Pueblo Bonito and bi-annual Star Parties in May and September; 1808 County Road 7950, Nageezi, NM 87037; call 505-786-7014 for schedule [Source: [NPS Night Sky](https://www.nps.gov/chcu/planyourvisit/nightsky.htm), [Visit Four Corners Stargazing](https://visitfourcorners.com/a-beginners-guide-to-stargazing-in-chaco-culture-national-historical-park/), [NPS Dark Sky Park](https://www.nps.gov/chcu/learn/nature/darkskypark.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-pe-asco-blanco-trail-with-supernova-pictograph"></a>**Peñasco Blanco Trail with Supernova Pictograph (4-6 hours)** - Hike the longest backcountry trail in Chaco Canyon, a 7.5-mile roundtrip journey to reach the remote Peñasco Blanco great house and the famous Supernova Pictograph, which may depict the 1054 AD supernova. The trail features numerous petroglyphs along the cliff base. Requires free backcountry permit at trailhead; hot summer sun and soft sand make this challenging; carry plenty of water; 1808 County Road 7950, Nageezi, NM 87037; sunrise to sunset [Source: [NPS Peñasco Blanco Trail](https://www.nps.gov/chcu/planyourvisit/penascoblancotrail.htm), [Pine Coast Hiking](https://pinecoasthiking.com/penasco-blanco-trail/), [AllTrails Peñasco Blanco](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/penasco-blanco-ruins-trail), [GJ Hikes](https://www.gjhikes.com/2015/01/penasco-blanco.html), [As They Are](https://astheyare.org/2023/09/03/chaco-culture-national-historical-park-hiking-to-a-supernova/)]

- <a id="gem-wijiji-trail"></a>**Wijiji Trail (1-1.5 hours)** - Explore the easternmost great house via this easy 4.1-mile trail with minimal elevation gain (101 feet). Wijiji is less visited than the main ruins and offers a peaceful walk along the canyon floor. Requires free backcountry permit at trailhead; 1808 County Road 7950, Nageezi, NM 87037; sunrise to sunset [Source: [AllTrails Wijiji](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/wijiji-trail), [NPS Wijiji Trail](https://www.nps.gov/chcu/planyourvisit/wijiji-trail.htm), [American Southwest Wijiji](https://www.americansouthwest.net/new_mexico/chaco_culture/wijiji-trail.html)]

- <a id="gem-tsin-kletsin-trail"></a>**Tsin Kletsin Trail (2-4 hours)** - Climb to a mesa-top great house for panoramic views of Chaco Canyon. The trail covers 3-4.1 miles (depending on loop choice) with 450 feet of elevation gain. The hike to just Tsin Kletsin takes 2-3 hours, while the full loop takes 3-4 hours. Requires free backcountry permit at trailhead; 1808 County Road 7950, Nageezi, NM 87037; sunrise to sunset [Source: [AllTrails Tsin Kletsin](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/tsin-kletzin-trail), [DesertUSA Chaco](https://www.desertusa.com/chaco/chaco_todo.html), [NPS Hiking Trails](https://www.nps.gov/chcu/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)]

- <a id="gem-sunrise-photography-at-the-ruins"></a>**Sunrise Photography at the Ruins (30-60 minutes)** - Photograph the great houses during sunrise when early light makes the sandstone glow. The golden hour provides stunning photo opportunities at Pueblo Bonito and other major sites; 1808 County Road 7950, Nageezi, NM 87037; sunrise [Source: [Discovering Hidden Gems](https://discoveringhiddengems.com/chaco-culture-national-historical-park/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-aztec-ruins-national-monument"></a>**Aztec Ruins National Monument (1 hour, 50 miles/1 hour drive from Chaco)** - Explore ancient ruins built by the Ancestral Puebloans in the 1100s, featuring ceremonial, public, and storage structures as well as the Great Kiva - the oldest and largest reconstructed kiva in North America. Located near Farmington on the San Juan and Animas Rivers, this intimate site contrasts nicely with the scale of Chaco; 725 Ruins Road, Aztec, NM 87410 (approximately 36.8364, -107.9986); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily; 505-334-6174 [Source: [Farmington Day Trips](https://www.newmexico.org/places-to-visit/regions/northwest/farmington/farmington-days-trips/), [NPS Aztec Ruins](https://www.nps.gov/azru/index.htm), [NPS Aztec Hours](https://www.nps.gov/azru/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Yelp Aztec Ruins](https://www.yelp.com/biz/aztec-ruins-national-monument-aztec)]

- <a id="nearby-salmon-ruins-and-heritage-park"></a>**Salmon Ruins and Heritage Park (1.5-2.5 hours, 60 miles/1.5 hours drive from Chaco)** - Visit an 800-year-old Chacoan outlier featuring a cultural museum, Heritage Park with reconstructed dwellings, and the San Juan County Archaeological Research Center. Located just west of Bloomfield along the San Juan River; 6131 Highway 64, Bloomfield, NM 87413 (approximately 36.7237, -107.9914); Summer (April-October) Monday-Sunday 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Winter (November-March) Monday-Saturday 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM - 5:00 PM, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 505-632-2013 [Source: [Salmon Ruins](https://www.salmonruins.com/), [Farmington Salmon Ruins](https://farmingtonnm.org/listings/salmon-ruins), [New Mexico Nomad](https://newmexiconomad.com/salmon-ruins-and-heritage-park/), [Passport to Eden](https://passporttoeden.com/salmon-ruins/)]

- <a id="nearby-bisti-de-na-zin-wilderness"></a>**Bisti/De-Na-Zin Wilderness (3-4 hours, 63 miles/3.25 hours drive from Chaco)** - Explore roughly 60 square miles of remote badlands featuring otherworldly rock formations, hoodoos, and petrified wood. Listed by National Geographic Traveler as a must-visit adventure destination, this area offers unique desert hiking and photography opportunities. No facilities or marked trails; wilderness hiking experience recommended; Bisti Wilderness parking area off Highway 371, approximately 36 miles south of Farmington (approximately 36.2857, -108.2857); open 24 hours [Source: [Rome2Rio Chaco to Bisti](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Chaco-Culture-National-Historical-Park/Bisti-De-Na-Zin-Wilderness), [Visit Four Corners Chaco](https://visitfourcorners.com/chaco-canyon-center-of-culture-and-mystery/), [New Mexico Nomad Bisti](https://newmexiconomad.com/bisti/)]
