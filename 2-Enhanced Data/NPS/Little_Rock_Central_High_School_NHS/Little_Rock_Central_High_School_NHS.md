# Little Rock Central High School NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Visitor Center** - The stamp is located inside the visitor center near the information desk; ask a ranger or volunteer for assistance if needed; 2120 W Daisy L Gatson Bates Drive, Little Rock, AR 72202 (34.738196, -92.297417); Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM, closed Sunday-Monday; 501-374-1957 [Source: [Stamp Your Park Passport](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/stamp-your-park-passport.htm), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/little-rock-central-high-school-national-historic-site/), [Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/chsc/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Contact Us](https://www.nps.gov/chsc/contacts.htm)]

**Note:** Call 501-374-1957 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. The site offers multiple stamps including the main stamp featuring the ornate architectural façade of the school, a passport cancellation stamp with the date, and a Junior Ranger badge stamp.

## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center Exhibits** (30-60 minutes) - Interactive exhibits, touch screens, tactile displays, and living history panels documenting the 1957 desegregation crisis and the Little Rock Nine; designed with accessibility in mind; 2120 W Daisy L Gatson Bates Drive, Little Rock, AR 72202 (34.738196, -92.297417); Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM; free admission [Source: [Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/chsc/planyourvisit/index.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60766-d106709-Reviews-Little_Rock_Central_High_School_National_Historic_Site-Little_Rock_Arkansas.html), [Plan Like a Park Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/plan-like-a-park-ranger-top-10-tips-for-visiting-little-rock-central-high-school-nhs.htm)]

- **Ranger-Led School Tours** (approximately 60-90 minutes) - Free guided tours exploring overlooked details, first-hand anecdotes, and thoughtful reflections surrounding Central High School, the Little Rock Nine, and school desegregation in the United States; tours include immersive role-playing where visitors walk the path as one of the "nine" with the ranger acting as an antagonizer to recreate the historic experience; must be reserved in advance online; 2120 W Daisy L Gatson Bates Drive, Little Rock, AR 72202 (34.738196, -92.297417); Tuesday-Saturday at 1:00 PM; reservations required; 501-374-1957 [Source: [Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/chsc/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [Feed The Malik](https://www.feedthemalik.com/post/tour-central-high-school-little-rock-things-to-do), [Plan Like a Park Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/plan-like-a-park-ranger-top-10-tips-for-visiting-little-rock-central-high-school-nhs.htm)]

- **Commemorative Garden** (15-30 minutes) - Walk through the garden and learn about the sacrifices and struggles that paved the way for educational opportunities worldwide; features the Elizabeth Eckford Bus Bench and grounds of Little Rock Central High School; open daily 7:00 AM until sunset; 2120 W Daisy L Gatson Bates Drive, Little Rock, AR 72202 (34.738196, -92.297417); free admission [Source: [Plan Like a Park Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/plan-like-a-park-ranger-top-10-tips-for-visiting-little-rock-central-high-school-nhs.htm), [Half Day Itinerary](https://www.nps.gov/tripideas/central-high-half-day.htm), [Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/chsc/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (30-60+ minutes) - Activity booklet guides visitors throughout the park with information about the site; completion time varies by age and activities chosen; earn a Junior Ranger badge stamp upon completion; available at visitor center; 2120 W Daisy L Gatson Bates Drive, Little Rock, AR 72202 (34.738196, -92.297417); Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM [Source: [Plan Like a Park Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/plan-like-a-park-ranger-top-10-tips-for-visiting-little-rock-central-high-school-nhs.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Multiple Passport Stamps Collection** (5-10 minutes) - Collect several unique stamps including the main architectural façade stamp, dated cancellation stamp, and Junior Ranger badge stamp; all available at the visitor center information desk; 2120 W Daisy L Gatson Bates Drive, Little Rock, AR 72202 (34.738196, -92.297417); Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/little-rock-central-high-school-national-historic-site/)]

- **Optimal Visit Timing** (planning tip) - Avoid visiting during 8:30-9:00 AM and 3:30-4:00 PM on weekdays to miss peak student pickup/drop-off congestion; call 501-374-1957 well in advance to reserve the 1:00 PM ranger-led interpretive program; 2120 W Daisy L Gatson Bates Drive, Little Rock, AR 72202 (34.738196, -92.297417) [Source: [Plan Like a Park Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/plan-like-a-park-ranger-top-10-tips-for-visiting-little-rock-central-high-school-nhs.htm)]

- **Elizabeth Eckford Bus Bench** (5-10 minutes) - Historic landmark within the Commemorative Garden marking a significant moment in civil rights history; part of the outdoor experience; 2120 W Daisy L Gatson Bates Drive, Little Rock, AR 72202 (34.738196, -92.297417); open daily 7:00 AM until sunset [Source: [Plan Like a Park Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/plan-like-a-park-ranger-top-10-tips-for-visiting-little-rock-central-high-school-nhs.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Clinton Presidential Center** (2-3 hours, 2.5 miles) - Presidential library and museum featuring exhibits about President Bill Clinton's administration, replicas of the Oval Office and Cabinet Room, and temporary exhibitions; located on the banks of the Arkansas River; 1200 President Clinton Ave, Little Rock, AR 72201 (34.7464097, -92.2583824); Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM-5:00 PM, closed New Year's Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas; adults $12, children 6-17 $7, discounts available; 501-370-8000 [Source: [Hours and Admission](https://www.clintonlibrary.gov/about-us/hours-and-admission), [U.S. News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/Little_Rock_AR/Things_To_Do/Clinton_Presidential_Center_65420/)]

- **Museum of Discovery** (2 hours, 2.3 miles) - Interactive science museum in the historic River Market district with hands-on exhibits; 500 President Clinton Ave, Suite 150, Little Rock, AR 72201 (34.7476539, -92.2650471); Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM-5:00 PM, closed Mondays during school year (open summer Mondays and most Monday holidays); 501-396-7050 [Source: [Hours and Location](https://museumofdiscovery.org/hours-and-location/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60766-d581027-Reviews-Museum_Of_Discovery-Little_Rock_Arkansas.html)]

- **Historic Arkansas Museum** (2 hours, 2.6 miles) - Features historic city block with the oldest building in Little Rock and an 1850s farmstead; galleries showcase Arkansas art and history; tours on the hour; 200 E 3rd St, Little Rock, AR 72201 (34.746001, -92.269196); Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM-5:00 PM, closed Mondays and major holidays; galleries free, grounds tours $2.50 adults, $1.50 seniors 65+, $1.00 under 18; 501-324-9351 [Source: [Arkansas Heritage](https://www.arkansasheritage.com/historic-arkansas-museum/historic-arkansas-museum-home), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60766-d213931-Reviews-Historic_Arkansas_Museum-Little_Rock_Arkansas.html), [TripHobo](https://www.triphobo.com/places/little-rock-united-states/historic-arkansas-museum)]

- **River Market District** (1-2 hours, 2.4 miles) - Year-round indoor market with 10,000 square feet of owner-operated shops and food stalls; adjacent to the Arkansas River Trail and Riverfront Park; downtown Little Rock; various hours depending on vendors [Source: [Attractions Nearby](https://www.nps.gov/chsc/planyourvisit/attractions-nearby.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g60766-Activities-Little_Rock_Arkansas.html)]

- **Big Dam Bridge and Arkansas River Trail** (1-3 hours, 8 miles west) - The longest pedestrian and bicycle bridge in North America at 4,226 feet; part of an 88-mile multi-use loop trail system; ideal for walking, cycling, and river views; open daily dawn to dusk; free [Source: [Attractions Nearby](https://www.nps.gov/chsc/planyourvisit/attractions-nearby.htm), [Pip and the City](https://pipandthecity.com/best-things-to-do-in-little-rock/)]
