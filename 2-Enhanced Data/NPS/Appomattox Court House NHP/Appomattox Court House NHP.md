# Appomattox Court House NHP (National Historical Park)

- **Appomattox Court House Visitor Center** - 111 National Park Dr, Appomattox, VA 24522 (37.3798843, -78.7985256); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; (434) 352-8987 [Sources: [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/apco/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Appomattox+Court+House+National+Historical+Park/@37.3798843,-78.7985256,17z)]

## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center Film and Museum** (30-45 minutes) - Watch a 17-minute film covering Lee's departure from Petersburg, Grant's pursuit, and the surrender at Appomattox Court House, then explore museum exhibits featuring personal artifacts including the pencil Lee used to sign the surrender documents and part of the first Flag of Truce; 111 National Park Dr, Appomattox, VA 24522 (37.3798843, -78.7985256); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, film shown on the hour and half-hour [Sources: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60723-d1546483-Reviews-Appomattox_Court_House_National_Historical_Park-Appomattox_Virginia.html), [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/apco/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **McLean House Tour** (20-30 minutes) - Tour the reconstructed McLean House where General Robert E. Lee surrendered to General Ulysses S. Grant on April 9, 1865, ending the Civil War; the house is a short walk from the main parking lot; 111 National Park Dr, Appomattox, VA 24522 (37.3798843, -78.7985256); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Sources: [Virginia.org McLean House](https://www.virginia.org/listing/mclean-house-appomattox-court-house-national-historical-park/209/), [Frommers](https://www.frommers.com/destinations/charlottesville/attractions/appomattox-court-house-national-historical-park/)]

- **Historic Village Walking Tour** (1-1.5 hours) - Self-guided walking tour through the restored 1865 village featuring Clover Hill Tavern (1819, where Union soldiers printed over 30,000 parole documents), Meeks Store (1852, general store and post office), the reconstructed courthouse (1964, original burned 1892), county jail (1850), and other period structures; pick up a map and self-guided tour booklet at visitor center; 111 National Park Dr, Appomattox, VA 24522 (37.3798843, -78.7985256); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Sources: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/apco/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [NPS Historic Structures](https://home.nps.gov/apco/learn/historyculture/historic-structures-at-appomattox-court-house.htm), [NPS Self-Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/apco/planyourvisit/appomattox-court-house-walking-tour.htm/index.htm)]

- **Living History Programs** (10-15 minutes each) - Attend talks presented by staff portraying people who lived during 1865, sharing firsthand experiences around the time of surrender; presentations typically offered at 50 minutes after the hour; 111 National Park Dr, Appomattox, VA 24522 (37.3798843, -78.7985256); April-October, check schedule for specific times [Sources: [NPS Things to Know](https://home.nps.gov/apco/planyourvisit/things2know.htm), [Virginia.org McLean House](https://www.virginia.org/listing/mclean-house-appomattox-court-house-national-historical-park/209/)]

- **Stage Road Trail** (30 minutes one-way) - Hike the 0.9-mile historic Lynchburg-Richmond Stage Road from the Confederate Cemetery to the Appomattox River, passing through the historic village; 111 National Park Dr, Appomattox, VA 24522 (37.3798843, -78.7985256); Daily dawn-dusk [Sources: [NPS Hiking Trails](https://www.nps.gov/apco/planyourvisit/hiking-trails.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/virginia/appomattox-court-house-national-historical-park)]

- **Grant's Approach Trail** (45 minutes loop) - Walk the 1.4-mile loop trail following Grant's route to Appomattox, rated easy; 111 National Park Dr, Appomattox, VA 24522 (37.3798843, -78.7985256); Daily dawn-dusk [Sources: [NPS Hiking Trails](https://www.nps.gov/apco/planyourvisit/hiking-trails.htm), [NPPlan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/virginia-national-parks/appomattox-court-house-national-historical-park-park-at-a-glance/appomattox-court-house-national-historical-park-hiking-trails/)]

- **Tobacco Prizery Nature Trail** (40 minutes loop) - Explore the 1.2-mile loop trail offering nature interpretation and pollinator gardens; 111 National Park Dr, Appomattox, VA 24522 (37.3798843, -78.7985256); Daily dawn-dusk [Sources: [NPS Hiking Trails](https://www.nps.gov/apco/planyourvisit/hiking-trails.htm), [NPPlan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/virginia-national-parks/appomattox-court-house-national-historical-park-park-at-a-glance/appomattox-court-house-national-historical-park-hiking-trails/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Lee's Headquarters Site** (10-15 minutes) - Visit the marked site of General Lee's final headquarters, located at a pullout along Virginia Route 24, approximately 1.25 miles northeast of the visitor center; offers a quieter, more contemplative stop away from the main village; Virginia Route 24, Appomattox, VA 24522; Daily dawn-dusk [Sources: [Stone Sentinels](https://stonesentinels.com/less-known/appomattox-court-house/lees-hq/), [NPS Virtual Tour](https://www.nps.gov/apco/learn/virtual-tour-of-appomattox-court-house.htm)]

- **Confederate Cemetery** (15-20 minutes) - Pay respects at this cemetery containing the remains of 18 Confederates and one Union soldier who died in General Lee's last battles, maintained by the Appomattox Chapter UDC; located west of the village along Virginia Route 24, approximately 500 yards from the village entrance (37° 22.548′ N, 78° 48.188′ W); Virginia Route 24, Appomattox, VA 24522; Daily dawn-sunset [Sources: [Stone Sentinels Confederate Cemetery](https://stonesentinels.com/less-known/appomattox-court-house/confederate-cemetery/), [HMDB](https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=272144), [NPS Places Beyond Village](https://www.nps.gov/apco/learn/historyculture/places-beyond-the-village.htm)]

- **Wildlife and Bird Watching** (flexible, 30-60 minutes) - Explore the park's 1,700 acres of woodlands and open meadows beyond the village core where Confederate and Union armies camped; watch for red-bellied, downy, and hairy woodpeckers, spring and fall neotropical migrants including black-throated green and Blackburnian warblers, blue-headed vireos, and blue-gray gnatcatchers; white-tailed deer graze in fields during early morning or dusk, and northern harriers hunt for rodents in winter; walk along the Appomattox River banks for best viewing; 111 National Park Dr, Appomattox, VA 24522 (37.3798843, -78.7985256); Daily dawn-dusk [Sources: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60723-d1546483-Reviews-Appomattox_Court_House_National_Historical_Park-Appomattox_Virginia.html), [Virginia DWR](https://dwr.virginia.gov/vbwt/sites/appomattox-court-house-national-historical-park/)]

- **Lee's Headquarters Loop Trail** (10 minutes) - Short 0.3-mile loop trail at the site of Lee's final headquarters, offering a quick easy walk in a historically significant location; Virginia Route 24, Appomattox, VA 24522; Daily dawn-dusk [Sources: [NPS Hiking Trails](https://www.nps.gov/apco/planyourvisit/hiking-trails.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/virginia/appomattox-court-house-national-historical-park)]

- **Baines Books and Coffee** (30-60 minutes, 3 miles) - Browse an eclectic selection of books, vinyl, and musical instruments in a charming historical building in downtown Appomattox; enjoy beverages and snacks; Main Street, Appomattox, VA 24522; Check locally for hours [Sources: [Historic Appomattox](https://historicappomattox.com/experience-appomattox-tourism/), [Only In Your State](https://www.onlyinyourstate.com/trip-ideas/virginia/things-to-do-in-appomattox-va)]
