# Waco Mammoth National Monument

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Waco Mammoth National Monument Visitor Center** (6220 Steinbeck Bend Drive, Waco, TX 76708; Monday-Sunday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 254-750-7946; $5 per adult, children 5 and under free) [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/waco/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/waco/planyourvisit/fees.htm)]

**Note:** Stamps available at visitor center. Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Guided Fossil Tour** (45 minutes-1 hour) - Join ranger-led tour departing every 30 minutes along 300-yard paved path to the Dig Shelter where you view in situ Columbian mammoth fossils, including a nursery herd of female mammoths and a bull mammoth, plus ancient camel and saber-toothed cat remains from 67,000 years ago; Tours depart from visitor center, 6220 Steinbeck Bend Drive, Waco, TX 76708; Tours run continuously Monday-Sunday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, no reservations needed; $5 per adult [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/waco/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g56521-d7283675-Reviews-Waco_Mammoth_National_Monument-Waco_Texas.html), [Source](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/waco-mammoth-national-monument)]

- **Dig Shelter Fossil Viewing** (included in guided tour) - View one of the nation's only recorded discoveries of a nursery herd of Pleistocene mammoths in their primary context, with fossils left in place where they were discovered; Dig Shelter accessed via guided tour; During tour hours [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/waco/index.htm), [Source](https://www.onlyinyourstate.com/texas/waco-mammoth-tx/)]

- **Visitor Center Exhibits** (15-30 minutes) - Explore exhibits about Ice Age Texas, the Columbian mammoth, and the paleontological discovery process; view orientation video before your tour; Visitor center building, 6220 Steinbeck Bend Drive; Monday-Sunday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; Included with admission [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/waco/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Source](https://www.frommers.com/destinations/waco/attractions/waco-mammoth-national-monument)]

- **Nature Trails** (30 minutes-1 hour) - Walk self-guided trails through the wooded bottomland along the Bosque River observing wildlife and native plants; trail system connects to dig shelter path; Trails accessible from visitor center; Dawn to dusk; Free (or included with admission) [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/waco/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Source](https://texashillcountry.com/waco-mammoth-national-monument/)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (30-45 minutes) - Complete activity booklet during your visit to earn Junior Paleontologist badge; pick up booklet at visitor center; Available during operating hours; Free with admission [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/waco/learn/kidsyouth/index.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Photography at Dig Shelter** (included in tour time) - Capture unique photos of in situ Ice Age fossils through the viewing windows and from designated photo spots inside the climate-controlled shelter; During guided tour; Tour hours [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g56521-d7283675-Reviews-Waco_Mammoth_National_Monument-Waco_Texas.html)]

- **Picnic Area** (flexible timing) - Enjoy a picnic in shaded areas near the visitor center before or after your tour; Picnic area adjacent to visitor center parking; Dawn to dusk; Free [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/waco/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

- **Educational Programs and Special Events** (varies) - Attend seasonal ranger programs, fossil day events, and educational presentations about Ice Age megafauna and paleontology; Check visitor center or website for schedule; Various times throughout year; Free with admission [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/waco/planyourvisit/calendar.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Magnolia Market at the Silos** (2-3 hours, 6 miles northeast) - Visit Chip and Joanna Gaines' famous shopping destination featuring home goods, bakery, food trucks, and gardens at the former HGTV Fixer Upper headquarters; 601 Webster Avenue, Waco, TX 76706; Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM-6:00 PM, closed Sunday; Free admission [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g56521-Activities-Waco_Texas.html)]

- **Dr Pepper Museum** (1.5-2 hours, 7 miles northeast) - Explore three floors of exhibits in the 1906 Artesian Bottling Works building where Dr Pepper was first bottled, including interactive displays and a working soda fountain; 300 S 5th Street, Waco, TX 76701; Monday-Saturday 10:00 AM-5:15 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM-5:15 PM; $13 adults [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g56521-Activities-Waco_Texas.html), [Source](https://www.drpeppermuseum.com)]

- **Texas Ranger Hall of Fame and Museum** (1-2 hours, 8 miles northeast) - Discover the history of the legendary Texas Rangers law enforcement agency through exhibits, artifacts, and multimedia presentations; 100 Texas Ranger Trail, Waco, TX 76706; Daily 10:00 AM-5:00 PM; $8 adults [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g56521-Activities-Waco_Texas.html)]

- **Cameron Park and Cameron Park Zoo** (2-4 hours, 7 miles northeast) - Explore one of the largest municipal parks in Texas with 20+ miles of trails through limestone cliffs and woodlands, plus a nationally ranked zoo; 1300 Herring Avenue and 1701 N 4th Street, Waco, TX; Park dawn to dusk, Zoo daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; Park free, Zoo $13 adults [[Source](https://www.cityofwaco.com/departments/parks-recreation), [Source](https://www.cameronparkzoo.com)]
