# César E. Chávez National Monument (NM)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Visitor Center** (29700 Woodford-Tehachapi Road, Keene, CA 93531 [35.2234505, -118.5590097]; Daily 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM except major holidays including Easter Sunday, Thanksgiving Day and the Friday after, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve, and New Year's Day; 661-823-6134)

**Note:** Call (661) 823-6134 to confirm current stamp locations and hours before visiting. [Source: [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/cech/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/cech/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center Exhibits** (30-60 minutes) - Explore César Chávez's preserved office with original furnishings and artifacts from his time at La Paz (1970-1988), view exhibits detailing the farmworker movement and United Farm Workers history, and watch a 12-minute video about the national monument; 29700 Woodford-Tehachapi Road, Keene, CA 93531; Daily 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM [Source: [National Chavez Center](https://chavezfoundation.org/national-chavez-center), [NPS Top 10 Tips](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/top10cech.htm/index.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g32550-d3733202-Reviews-Cesar_E_Chavez_National_Monument-Keene_California.html)]

- **Memorial Garden** (20-30 minutes) - Visit the peaceful memorial garden featuring the gravesites of César and Helen Chávez along with their two dogs, view the fountain honoring five workers, admire the stonework version of the Huelga flag, and explore year-round blooming roses (peak April-October); 29700 Woodford-Tehachapi Road, Keene, CA 93531; Daily 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM [Source: [NPS Top 10 Tips](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/top10cech.htm/index.htm), [California Through My Lens](https://californiathroughmylens.com/cesar-chavez-monument/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g32550-d3733202-Reviews-Cesar_E_Chavez_National_Monument-Keene_California.html)]

- **Garden of the Southwest** (15-20 minutes) - Explore native plants and vegetation from César's birthplace in Arizona's Sonoran Desert, including cacti and native grasses; 29700 Woodford-Tehachapi Road, Keene, CA 93531; Daily 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM [Source: [NPS Top 10 Tips](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/top10cech.htm/index.htm)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (30-45 minutes) - Complete activities in the junior ranger booklet to earn a badge while learning about César Chávez and the farmworker movement; available at Visitor Center, 29700 Woodford-Tehachapi Road, Keene, CA 93531; Daily 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/cesar-chavez-national-monument/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Delano Driving Tour** (1-2 hours) - Use the free NPS mobile app to take a self-guided driving tour of significant farmworker movement sites in Delano, California, including Forty Acres, the early headquarters of the United Farm Workers; located approximately 45 minutes north of the monument in Delano, CA; available 24/7 via mobile app [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/cesar-chavez-national-monument/), [Visit California](https://www.visitcalifornia.com/experience/cesar-e-chavez-national-monument/)]

- **Virtual Photo Tours** (20-30 minutes) - Explore digital tours of the monument and the Nuestra Señora Reina de la Paz site through Google Arts & Culture; accessible online anytime [Source: [Google Arts & Culture](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/virtual-tour-of-the-c%C3%A9sar-e-ch%C3%A1vez-national-monument-outdoors-national-park-service/ggVxJtdNDZH-IA?hl=en), [National Chavez Center](https://chavezfoundation.org/national-chavez-center)]

## Also Nearby

- **Tehachapi Loop** (30-60 minutes, 3 miles) - Watch freight trains navigate this National Historic Civil Engineering Landmark, a marvel of 1870s railroad engineering where trains pass over themselves in a spiral; viewing platform with parking at Woodford-Tehachapi Road near coordinates 35.197076, -118.53901, approximately 3.2 miles east of Keene exit; open 24/7, averages 36 trains daily with typical wait times of 20-60 minutes [Source: [TripAdvisor Tehachapi Loop](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g33163-d1576333-Reviews-Tehachapi_Loop-Tehachapi_California.html), [Railfan Guide](https://www.railfanguides.us/ca/tehachapi/index.htm), [Tehachapi News](https://www.tehachapinews.com/visitor-guide/visitor-guide-loop-overlook-completed/article_d03dd278-eb86-11ec-bf95-47ad1db95d7b.html)]

- **Kern County Museum** (2-3 hours, 32 miles) - Explore over 56 historic buildings on 16 acres featuring thousands of artifacts, including Pioneer Village, Black Gold oil exhibition, and the Lori Brock Children's Discovery Center; 3801 Chester Ave, Bakersfield, CA 93301; Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM (no admission after 3:30 PM), closed Monday, Tuesday, and federal holidays; Adults $10, Seniors $9, Students $9, Children $7; 661-437-3330 [Source: [Kern County Museum](https://kerncountymuseum.org/hours-and-admission/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g32037-d587655-Reviews-Kern_County_Museum-Bakersfield_California.html)]

- **California Living Museum (CALM)** (2-3 hours, 40 miles) - Visit California's premier native zoo and botanical garden featuring over 80 species of medically unreleasable California native animals and plants on 14 acres; 10500 Alfred Harrell Highway, Bakersfield, CA 93306; Tuesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM, closed Easter, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day; Adults $10, Seniors (60+) $7, Children (up to 12) $6, Under 3 free, Military/Veterans free; 661-546-8940 [Source: [CALM Zoo](https://calmzoo.org/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g32037-d2154125-Reviews-California_Living_Museum-Bakersfield_California.html), [Mentors Moving Guide](https://mentorsmoving.com/blog/calm-bakersfield-zoo-complete-guide/)]
