# Chickasaw NRA (National Recreation Area)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-travertine-nature-center"></a>**Travertine Nature Center** (1504 Perimeter Rd, Sulphur, OK 73086 (34.50399, -96.94965); 9am-5pm Memorial Day-Labor Day, 9am-4:30pm Labor Day-Memorial Day, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; (580) 622-7234) [Source: [NPS Passport Stamps](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/passport_stamps.htm), [Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Travertine Nature Center](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/travertine-information-and-nature-center.htm), [Coordinates](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/gps.htm)]

**Note:** Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-little-niagara-falls-swimming"></a>**Little Niagara Falls Swimming** (1-3 hours) - Cool off in a natural swimming pool formed by a scenic waterfall with 62-64°F year-round water temperatures, perfect for hot days; jumping from the falls is popular; water shoes recommended; arrive late afternoon to avoid noon crowds; Little Niagara (34.50523, -96.95246); accessible during daylight hours [Source: [Swimming](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/swimming.htm), [Get Lost In The USA](https://getlostintheusa.com/chickasaw-national-recreation-area/), [Naturally Oklahoma](https://naturallyoklahoma.com/chickasaw-national-recreation-area/), [Coordinates](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/gps.htm)]

- <a id="activity-travertine-creek-trail"></a>**Travertine Creek Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - Easy 1.5-mile trail (2.8 miles out-and-back) following Travertine Creek from the Nature Center to Pavilion Springs, passing Sycamore Falls and connecting to Little Niagara Falls; Travertine Nature Center parking area (34.50399, -96.94965); accessible during daylight hours [Source: [Hiking Trails](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/trails.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/oklahoma/travertine-creek-trail), [Get Lost In The USA](https://getlostintheusa.com/chickasaw-national-recreation-area/), [Coordinates](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/gps.htm)]

- <a id="activity-bison-viewing"></a>**Bison Viewing** (30 minutes-1 hour) - View a small herd of bison in fenced pastures on both sides of U.S. Route 177; walk the Veterans Trail along the fence line for best viewing; dawn and dusk offer the best wildlife viewing opportunities; Bison Pasture Viewpoint (34.49871, -96.97119); accessible during daylight hours [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/chickasaw-national-recreation-area/), [Get Lost In The USA](https://getlostintheusa.com/chickasaw-national-recreation-area/), [Naturally Oklahoma](https://naturallyoklahoma.com/chickasaw-national-recreation-area/), [Coordinates](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/gps.htm)]

- <a id="activity-veterans-lake-trail"></a>**Veterans Lake Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - Paved 3-mile loop trail circling Veterans Lake, suitable for hiking, walking, biking, strollers, and rollerblades; features uphill and level sections; provides access to fishing spots; minimal shade so bring water; Veterans Lake Parking Area (34.49143, -96.97682); accessible during daylight hours [Source: [Hiking Trails](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/trails.htm), [Chickasaw Country](https://www.chickasawcountry.com/article/hit-trails-chickasaw-country), [Get Lost In The USA](https://getlostintheusa.com/chickasaw-national-recreation-area/), [Coordinates](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/gps.htm)]

- <a id="activity-lake-of-the-arbuckles-fishing-and-boating"></a>**Lake of the Arbuckles Fishing and Boating** (2-4 hours) - Fish for bass, catfish, white bass, crappie, bluegill, and perch in 2,350 acres of crystal clear water with 36 miles of shoreline; rated best bass fishing in Oklahoma; boat ramps at Buckhorn, the Point, and Guy Sandy campgrounds; Oklahoma fishing license required (available at Travertine Nature Center); boat permits required; Lake of the Arbuckles, 6 miles southwest of Sulphur; accessible year-round [Source: [Fishing](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/fishing.htm), [Boating](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/boating.htm), [Lake of the Arbuckles](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/loa.htm), [Chickasaw Country](https://www.chickasawcountry.com/nature-outdoors/lake-arbuckles)]

- <a id="activity-antelope-and-buffalo-springs-trail"></a>**Antelope and Buffalo Springs Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - Easy 1.2-mile trail from Travertine Nature Center to freshwater springs; relatively level terrain through scenic areas; less crowded than main attractions; Travertine Nature Center parking area (34.50399, -96.94965); accessible during daylight hours [Source: [Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Get Lost In The USA](https://getlostintheusa.com/chickasaw-national-recreation-area/), [Coordinates](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/gps.htm)]

- <a id="activity-travertine-nature-center-exhibits"></a>**Travertine Nature Center Exhibits** (30 minutes-1 hour) - Explore interpretive displays and live animals in the main visitor center; obtain fishing licenses and park information; exhibits about park history and natural features; 1504 Perimeter Rd, Sulphur, OK 73086 (34.50399, -96.94965); 9am-5pm Memorial Day-Labor Day, 9am-4:30pm Labor Day-Memorial Day [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60771-d117491-Reviews-Chickasaw_National_Recreation_Area-Sulphur_Oklahoma.html), [Naturally Oklahoma](https://naturallyoklahoma.com/travertine-nature-center/), [Coordinates](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/gps.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-vendome-well"></a>**Vendome Well** (15-30 minutes) - Sample fresh mineral water from a flowing artesian well; water is 9,000 years old according to carbon-14 dating; connected to Lincoln Bridge via gravel trails; Flower Park/Vendome Well (34.5059, -96.97273); accessible during daylight hours [Source: [Vendome Well](https://www.nps.gov/chic/learn/nature/vendome_well.htm), [Chickasaw Country](https://www.chickasawcountry.com/article/5-ways-explore-chickasaw-national-recreation-area), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/chickasaw-national-recreation-area/), [Coordinates](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/gps.htm)]

- <a id="gem-bromide-hill-overlook-trail"></a>**Bromide Hill Overlook Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - Moderate 2.9-mile out-and-back trail with 288 ft elevation gain; winds through forest past large rock formations and follows Rock Creek with beautiful water views; steep final tenth of a mile features historic CCC stonework; Bromide Pavilion parking area (34.49662, -96.98248); accessible during daylight hours [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/oklahoma/bromide-hill-trail), [Hiking Project](https://www.hikingproject.com/trail/7042911/bromide-hill-overlook), [Coordinates](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/gps.htm)]

- <a id="gem-sycamore-falls"></a>**Sycamore Falls** (15-30 minutes) - Multiple small cascading waterfalls along Travertine Creek; less visited than Little Niagara Falls; accessible via Travertine Creek Trail; near Travertine Creek Trail (34.50399, -96.94965 area); accessible during daylight hours [Source: [Get Lost In The USA](https://getlostintheusa.com/chickasaw-national-recreation-area/), [Hiking Trails](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/trails.htm)]

- <a id="gem-buckhorn-area-boat-ramp-sunset-viewing"></a>**Buckhorn Area Boat Ramp Sunset Viewing** (30 minutes-1 hour) - Gorgeous sunset viewing spot with picnic tables and swimming areas at Lake of the Arbuckles; quieter than main recreation areas; Buckhorn Ranger Station (34.43797, -97.00823); accessible during daylight hours [Source: [Get Lost In The USA](https://getlostintheusa.com/chickasaw-national-recreation-area/), [Coordinates](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/gps.htm)]

- <a id="gem-veterans-lake-fishing"></a>**Veterans Lake Fishing** (1-3 hours) - Quieter fishing experience than Lake of the Arbuckles; good for anglers testing their skills; paved trail access around lake; Veterans Lake Fishing Dock (34.49223, -96.98662); accessible during daylight hours [Source: [Get Lost In The USA](https://getlostintheusa.com/chickasaw-national-recreation-area/), [Coordinates](https://www.nps.gov/chic/planyourvisit/gps.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-turner-falls-park"></a>**Turner Falls Park** (2-4 hours, 20 minutes from Sulphur) - Oklahoma's tallest waterfall at 77 feet dropping into a natural swimming pool; hiking trails and three caves to explore; I-35 & Highway 77, Davis, OK 73030 (34.4263122, -97.1549625); 7am-10pm daily (seasonal variations: summer 6am-8:30pm, winter 7am-6pm), closed Thanksgiving and Christmas; admission: summer weekday $16 adults/$9 children 6-12, summer weekend $20 adults/$16 children, winter rates lower; (580) 369-2988 [Source: [Turner Falls Park](https://www.turnerfallspark.com/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g51303-d117492-Reviews-Turner_Falls_Park-Davis_Oklahoma.html), [Information Center](https://www.turnerfallspark.com/information-center/)]

- <a id="nearby-chickasaw-cultural-center"></a>**Chickasaw Cultural Center** (2-3 hours, 10 minutes from Sulphur) - 109-acre cultural center featuring exhibits, traditional village, honor garden, and history center; outdoor grounds free, admission charged for exhibit center; 867 Cooper Memorial Dr., Sulphur, OK 73086 (34.4930196, -96.9957912); Tuesday-Saturday 10am-5pm, closed Sunday-Monday and holidays; admission: $7 adults, $6 students/seniors/military, free for Chickasaw citizens and children 12 and under; (580) 622-7130 [Source: [Hours & Admission](https://www.chickasawculturalcenter.com/visit/hours-and-admission/), [Chickasaw Nation](https://chickasaw.net/Our-Nation/Locations/Chickasaw-Cultural-Center.aspx), [Directions](https://www.chickasawculturalcenter.com/visit/directions-and-parking/)]

- <a id="nearby-arbuckle-wilderness-park"></a>**Arbuckle Wilderness Park** (1-1.5 hours, 25 minutes from Sulphur) - Drive-through exotic animal safari park with over 400 acres featuring camels, llamas, deer, buffalo, yaks, zebras, and more; includes walk-through area with petting zoo, birds, and reptiles; feed animals from your car with purchased food; 6132 Kay Starr Trl., Davis, OK 73030 (I-35 exit 51); Monday-Friday 9am-5pm, Saturday-Sunday 9am-5:30pm (seasonal variations); admission approximately $18.95 per adult, group rates available [Source: [Arbuckle Wilderness Park](https://arbucklewildernesspark.com/), [Chickasaw Country](https://www.chickasawcountry.com/things-do/arbuckle-wilderness-park), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g51303-d117495-Reviews-Arbuckle_Wilderness-Davis_Oklahoma.html)]
