## Key Activities

- **Biking the C&O Canal Towpath** (184.5 miles total; 3-5 days for full Georgetown to Cumberland route; day sections: 2-6 hours depending on distance) - The towpath runs 184.5 miles from Georgetown, D.C. to Cumberland, Maryland along a relatively flat, crushed limestone and dirt/gravel trail with a maximum grade of 1.25%, suitable for riders of all skill levels; most cyclists complete the entire route in 3-5 days with 50-60 mile segments, though experienced riders can do sections in a day; for casual riders, plan 2-4 hours for shorter segments of 10-20 miles; 11710 MacArthur Blvd, Potomac, MD 20854 (Great Falls access) or 1057 Thomas Jefferson St NW, Washington, DC 20007 (Georgetown access); sunrise to sunset [Source: [Bike Washington](http://bikewashington.org/canal/), [Hike Bike Travel](https://hikebiketravel.com/co-canal-bike-trail/), [Best Rides DC](https://www.bestridesdc.com/journal/riding-the-iconic-c-and-o-towpath)]

- **Billy Goat Trail Section A** (3.7-mile loop including return via towpath; 2-3 hours) - The most popular and challenging trail that Great Falls has to offer, this 1.7-mile rocky trail along Mather Gorge features steep climbs including a 50-foot rock face ascent with spectacular views of Great Falls and the Potomac River; combined with the return via the C&O Canal towpath creates a 3.7-mile loop; considered moderately difficult with scrambling over boulders required; starts from Great Falls Tavern; 11710 MacArthur Blvd, Potomac, MD 20854; 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM (visitor center hours, trail accessible dawn to dusk) [Source: [C&O Canal Trust Billy Goat](https://www.canaltrust.org/pyv/billy-goat-trail-a/), [AllTrails Billy Goat](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/maryland/billy-goat-loop-and-great-falls-overlook), [District Fray](https://districtfray.com/articles/hike-billy-goat-trail/)]

- **Great Falls Overlook via Olmsted Island Boardwalk** (0.25 miles one way, 1.2 miles round trip from visitor center; 45 minutes round trip) - Handicap-accessible wooden boardwalk extending to a viewing platform over the main falls where the Potomac River drops over 60 feet; best visited at sunrise or after heavy regional rains for most dramatic views; dogs not permitted; $15 entrance fee for cars; 11710 MacArthur Blvd, Potomac, MD 20854; 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM (visitor center), boardwalk accessible dawn to dusk [Source: [NPS Olmsted Island](https://www.nps.gov/places/olmsted-island-and-overlook-trail.htm), [C&O Canal Trust Olmsted](https://www.canaltrust.org/pyv/olmsted-island/), [TripAdvisor Olmsted](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g41328-d14789342-Reviews-Olmsted_Island-Potomac_Montgomery_County_Maryland.html)]

- **Mule-Drawn Canal Boat Rides** (1 hour round trip; April-October, schedules vary seasonally) - One-hour guided canal boat excursions pulled by mules departing from Great Falls Tavern with Park Rangers providing historical interpretation; adults $8, children $5, seniors $6; tickets sold first-come, first-served at visitor center; groups of 10+ can make advance reservations; Georgetown location currently on hiatus for restoration; 11710 MacArthur Blvd, Potomac, MD 20854; call 301-767-3714 for current schedule and reservations [Source: [NPS Canal Boat](https://www.nps.gov/choh/planyourvisit/great-falls-canal-boat-rides.htm), [NPPlan Boat Ride](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/maryland-national-parks/chesapeake-and-ohio-canal-national-historical-park-park-at-a-glance/chesapeake-and-ohio-canal-national-historical-park-canal-boat-rides/chesapeake-and-ohio-canal-national-historical-park-mule-drawn-canal-boat-ride-at-great-falls-tavern/), [C&O Canal Trust Boat](https://www.canaltrust.org/discoverypoi/mule-drawn-canal-boat-ride/)]

- **Paw Paw Tunnel Trail** (4.8 miles round trip with tunnel; 2-2.5 hours; tunnel: 3,118 feet / 0.6 miles through) - Moderate hike featuring the longest man-made structure on the C&O Canal, a 3,118-foot brick tunnel built in 1850 with over six million bricks; tunnel is unlit so headlamp/flashlight required; can walk/bike through tunnel (dismount recommended due to narrow towpath and uneven surface) or hike over via Tunnel Hill Trail for views of Potomac River; tunnel open dawn to dusk year-round; near Paw Paw, WV at mile marker 155; dawn to dusk [Source: [AllTrails Paw Paw](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/maryland/paw-paw-tunnel-trail), [TripAdvisor Paw Paw](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g41098-d1570066-Reviews-C_O_Canal_Paw_Paw_Tunnel-Cumberland_Maryland.html), [GAP Trail](https://gaptrail.org/amenities/paw-paw-tunnel/)]

- **Kayaking and Canoeing the Potomac River** (2-7 hours depending on route) - Paddle the Potomac River alongside the canal with access via multiple boat ramps; popular routes include Snyder's Landing to Shepherdstown (3-4 hours average), 5.5-mile sections near Brunswick (2 hours), and longer all-day excursions up to 17.5 miles (7 hours for strong paddlers); features both flatwater and whitewater sections; Class II-III rapids near Harpers Ferry, Class IV-V rapids at Great Falls (expert only); life jackets required by Maryland law; various access points including Taylor's Landing (Mile 81), Snyder's Landing (Mile 77), Shepherdstown (Mile 72.75); sunrise to sunset [Source: [NPS Boating](https://www.nps.gov/choh/planyourvisit/boating.htm), [C&O Canal Trust Paddling](https://www.canaltrust.org/plan/things-to-do-co-canal/boating-paddling/), [River Trail](https://www.rivertrail.com/paddling/potomac-river-kayaking/)]

- **Fishing along the C&O Canal** (half day to full day) - Fish any accessible area of the Potomac River and watered sections of the canal; popular spots include Fletcher's Cove, McMahon's Mill, and locations throughout Washington County section; species available year-round; requires valid state fishing license (Maryland, Virginia, or DC depending on location); no NPS permit required but state regulations apply; Maryland owns the Potomac to the Virginia bank, so Maryland regulations apply; various access points along 184.5-mile towpath; dawn to dusk [Source: [NPS Fishing](https://www.nps.gov/choh/planyourvisit/fishing.htm), [NPPlan Fishing](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/maryland-national-parks/chesapeake-and-ohio-canal-national-historical-park-park-at-a-glance/chesapeake-and-ohio-canal-national-historical-park-fishing/), [PRFC Fishing](https://prfc.us/fishing_potomac.html)]

- **Monocacy Aqueduct** (30 minutes to 1 hour) - Visit the largest aqueduct on the C&O Canal, a 516-foot, seven-arched structure built 1829-1833; easily accessible from parking lot at western end of Mouth of Monocacy Road with historical markers, picnic tables, boat ramp, and restrooms; excellent for photos and short walks along the towpath; Near Dickerson, MD at Mile 42; dawn to dusk, parking gates lock after dusk [Source: [NPS Monocacy](https://www.nps.gov/choh/learn/historyculture/themonocacyaqueduct.htm), [C&O Canal Trust Monocacy](https://www.canaltrust.org/pyv/monocacy-aqueduct/), [Wikipedia Monocacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monocacy_Aqueduct)]

- **Rock Climbing at Carderock** (half day to full day; 3-6 hours typical) - Top-rope climbing on cliffs up to 80 feet high along the Potomac River Gorge; most popular climbing spot in the region with routes ranging from 5.0 to 5.14 difficulty (most 5.5-5.9); route lengths 25-75 feet; top-roping is the norm, no drilling of anchors permitted; suitable for beginners to advanced climbers; Carderock Recreation Area along C&O Canal; 7:00 AM to dark [Source: [C&O Canal Trust Carderock](https://www.canaltrust.org/pyv/carderock-climbing-area/), [Mountain Project Carderock](https://www.mountainproject.com/area/106027973/carderock), [SummitPost Great Falls](https://www.summitpost.org/great-falls-carderock-potomac-river-gorge/283338)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Canal Quarters Lockhouse Overnight Stays** (overnight experience) - Stay overnight in one of seven restored historic lockhouses furnished to different time periods (1830s-1950s); three fully modernized with electricity, heat/AC, and modern bathrooms (Lockhouses 6, 10, 21); one with electricity only (Lockhouse 49); three rustic without electricity, heat, or water (Lockhouses 22, 25, 28); accommodates up to 8 guests; cookware and dishes provided, guests supply linens; unique opportunity to experience life as canal lockkeepers; book through C&O Canal Trust; various locations along towpath; 24-hour stays [Source: [Canal Quarters](http://www.canaltrust.org/programs/canal-quarters/), [NPPlan Lockhouses](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/maryland-national-parks/chesapeake-and-ohio-canal-national-historical-park-park-at-a-glance/chesapeake-and-ohio-canal-national-historical-park-lockhouse-rentals/), [Matador Network](https://matadornetwork.com/read/stay-overnight-gorgeous-lockhouses-along-marylands-historic-canal/)]

- **Swain's Lock** (1-2 hours) - Quiet, lesser-known spot along the canal perfect for peaceful walks or picnics; historic lockhouse operated by the Swains family until 2006, now available for overnight stays furnished in circa-1916 style; offers glimpse into canal's quieter history away from major tourist hubs; Mile 16.6 near Potomac, MD; dawn to dusk [Source: [C&O Canal Trust Hidden Gems](https://www.canaltrust.org/itinerary/hidden-gems-along-the-co-canal/), [Visit Montgomery Swains](https://visitmontgomery.com/directory/canal-quarters-lockhouse-22/)]

- **Georgetown Lime Kilns** (30 minutes) - Two remaining lime kilns in Foggy Bottom saved for historic preservation; lime production was big business in Georgetown until 1907; these structures represent the Mall's industrial heritage and the C&O Canal's role in commercial transportation; often overlooked by visitors focused on the canal itself; Georgetown, Washington, DC; accessible 24/7 [Source: [Washingtonian Hidden Gems](https://www.washingtonian.com/2020/01/26/92-hidden-gems-attractions-in-washington/)]

- **Historic Lockkeeper's Toll House** (15-30 minutes) - The Mall's oldest structure, reopened after more than 40 years as a storage shed; served as toll plaza for C&O Canal barges from 1835 to 1855; small but historically significant building offering insight into the canal's commercial operations; Constitution Avenue NW and 17th Street NW, Washington, DC; exterior accessible 24/7 [Source: [Washingtonian Hidden Gems](https://www.washingtonian.com/2020/01/26/92-hidden-gems-attractions-in-washington/)]

- **Canal Discoveries - Western Section** (varies by site) - The western section of the C&O Canal from Hancock to Cumberland offers many remote points of interest less frequently visited than popular eastern hubs; C&O Canal Trust's "Canal Discoveries" highlights 48 hidden gems with unique histories along the 184.5-mile towpath; includes lesser-known locks, structures, and natural features; various locations west of Hancock; dawn to dusk [Source: [C&O Canal Trust Hidden Gems](https://www.canaltrust.org/itinerary/hidden-gems-along-the-co-canal/), [C&O Canal Trust Things to Do](https://www.canaltrust.org/category/things-to-do/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Harpers Ferry National Historical Park** (3-4 hours minimum, full day recommended; 30-45 minutes from Brunswick section of C&O Canal) - Historic town at the confluence of the Potomac and Shenandoah Rivers with Civil War history, museums, exhibits, and hiking trails including connection to Appalachian Trail; spectacular views from Maryland Heights (1,200-foot climb); 171 Shoreline Dr, Harpers Ferry, WV 25425; Daily sunrise-sunset, museums and visitor center 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM except Thanksgiving, Dec 25, Jan 1 [Source: [NPS Harpers Ferry Hours](https://www.nps.gov/hafe/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [TripAdvisor Harpers Ferry](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60722-d143167-Reviews-Harpers_Ferry_National_Historical_Park-Harpers_Ferry_West_Virginia.html), [NPS Nearby Attractions](https://www.nps.gov/hafe/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm)]

- **Antietam National Battlefield** (3-6 hours; 30-40 minutes from Williamsport section of C&O Canal) - One of the most pristine Civil War battlefields in the nation with over 10,000 acres preserved; 8.5-mile driving tour route with key battle points; visitor center with exhibits (1.5 hours); driving tour without stops (2 hours); thorough visit with walking battlefield trails (4-6 hours); 5831 Dunker Church Road, Sharpsburg, MD 21782; Visitor center daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM except Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day; 301-432-5124 [Source: [NPS Antietam Hours](https://www.nps.gov/anti/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [TripAdvisor Antietam](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g60720-d146725-t3187238-How_much_time_should_I_allot_to_visit_this_place.html), [NPS Antietam Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/anti/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

- **Frederick, Maryland Historic District** (2-4 hours; 20-30 minutes from Monocacy Aqueduct) - Charming historic downtown with shops, restaurants, breweries, and Civil War history including nearby Monocacy National Battlefield; main street community with outdoor recreation access to C&O Canal; downtown Frederick, MD; varies by establishment [Source: [Visit Frederick C&O Canal](https://www.visitfrederick.org/blog/stories/post/outdoor-recreation-c-o-canal/), [C&O Canal Trust Locales](https://www.canaltrust.org/2020/10/must-see-locales-within-5-miles-of-the-canal/)]

**Total Recommended Time:** 6-8 hours for a comprehensive Great Falls area visit including Great Falls Tavern Visitor Center (1 hour), Olmsted Island Overlook (1 hour), Billy Goat Trail Section A (3 hours), and mule-drawn canal boat ride (1 hour), plus additional time for exhibits and lunch. For towpath biking, allocate 3-5 days for the full Georgetown to Cumberland route (184.5 miles), or 2-6 hours for shorter day sections. Individual visitor centers and shorter trail segments can be visited in 2-3 hours.
