# Andrew Johnson NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Andrew Johnson NHS Visitor Center** (101 N. College St., Greeneville, TN 37743; Summer hours April-November: Daily 9:00 AM-4:00 PM; Winter hours December-March: Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM-4:00 PM; 423-638-3551)

**Note:** Call 423-638-3551 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. The site also offers virtual passport stamps through the NPS virtual passport program [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/anjo/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/anjo/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [NPS Virtual Stamp](https://www.nps.gov/anjo/planyourvisit/virtual-passport-stamp.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center & Museum Experience** (30 minutes) - Begin your visit at the Visitor Center which houses the Presidential museum, a 13.5-minute orientation film "Defender of the Constitution," and Andrew Johnson's original 1830s Tailor Shop preserved in the Memorial Building; 101 N. College St., Greeneville, TN 37743; Summer hours April-November: Daily 9:00 AM-4:00 PM; Winter hours December-March: Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM-4:00 PM; 423-638-3551 [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/anjo/planyourvisit/index.htm), [NPPlan Visitor Center](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/tennessee/andrew-johnson-national-historic-site-park-at-a-glance/andrew-johnson-national-historic-site-visitor-center/), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/anjo/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- **Andrew Johnson Homestead Guided Tour** (45 minutes) - Ranger-led tours of the nine-room home where the Johnson family lived from 1851, featuring original furniture and detailed interpretation of his life and presidency; 209 S. Main St., Greeneville, TN 37743; Tours at 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 2:00 PM, and 3:00 PM (limited to 10 visitors, first-come/first-served ticketing at Visitor Center, tickets available until 15 minutes before tour time); 423-638-3551 [Source: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/anjo/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [NPPlan Homestead](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/tennessee/andrew-johnson-national-historic-site-park-at-a-glance/andrew-johnson-national-historic-site-johnson-homestead-tour/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60719-d293545-Reviews-Andrew_Johnson_National_Historic_Site-Greeneville_Tennessee.html)]

- **Early Johnson Home Visit** (15 minutes) - Self-guided visit to the unfurnished home where Andrew and Eliza's family lived from the 1830s until 1851, featuring exhibit panels about Johnson's early life and political beginnings; across from Visitor Center at 101 N. College St., Greeneville, TN 37743; open during park hours; 423-638-3551 [Source: [NPPlan Early Home](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/tennessee/andrew-johnson-national-historic-site-park-at-a-glance/andrew-johnson-national-historic-site-early-johnson-home/), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/anjo/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- **Andrew Johnson National Cemetery** (15-30 minutes) - Visit the scenic hilltop cemetery where President Johnson, his wife, and descendants are buried; this active veteran cemetery offers peaceful grounds with beautiful views (note: one mile from Visitor Center, steep terrain with stairs); Signal Hill, Greeneville, TN 37743; open daily dawn to dusk [Source: [NPPlan Cemetery](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/tennessee/andrew-johnson-national-historic-site-park-at-a-glance/andrew-johnson-national-historic-site-andrew-johnson-national-cemetery/), [NPS Cemetery](https://www.nps.gov/places/andrew-johnson-national-cemetery.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60719-d10068316-Reviews-Andrew_Johnson_National_Cemetery-Greeneville_Tennessee.html)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Cemetery Walking Tour** (90 minutes) - Extended ranger-led walking tour exploring the history and notable burials in the National Cemetery, involving walking on uneven ground and stairs; departs from cemetery location; check with Visitor Center at 423-638-3551 for availability and scheduling [Source: [NPS Cemetery Walking Tour](https://www.nps.gov/anjo/learn/news/cemetery-walking-tour.htm)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (30-60 minutes) - Kids can become Junior Rangers by completing an activity book available at the Visitor Center, exploring the site's history through hands-on activities; available at Visitor Center during operating hours [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/andrew-johnson-national-historic-site/), [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/anjo/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Cell Phone Audio Tours** (variable) - Self-guided audio tours accessible via cell phone provide additional interpretation at various locations throughout the site; dial numbers posted at locations throughout the park [Source: [NPS Cell Phone Tours](https://www.nps.gov/anjo/planyourvisit/cell-phone-tours.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **David Crockett Birthplace State Park** (varies, currently closed) - Visit the birthplace site of the legendary frontiersman Davy Crockett with historical exhibits and nature trails along the Nolichucky River (note: park closed due to Hurricane Helene damage as of 2024); 1245 Davy Crockett Park Rd., Limestone, TN 37681, approximately 10 miles east of Greeneville (15-20 minutes drive); check [Tennessee State Parks](https://tnstateparks.com/parks/david-crockett-birthplace) for reopening status [Source: [TN State Parks](https://tnstateparks.com/parks/david-crockett-birthplace), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Crockett_Birthplace_State_Park)]

- **Greene County History Museum** (1-3 hours) - Explore twelve galleries covering Greene County's rich history in this free, self-guided museum with three accessible levels; 101 W. McKee St., Greeneville, TN 37743; Tuesday-Saturday 11:00 AM-4:00 PM; 423-636-1558 [Source: [Museum Website](https://www.greenevillegreenecountyhistorymuseum.com/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60719-d10748563-Reviews-Greene_County_History_Museum-Greeneville_Tennessee.html)]

- **City Garage Car Museum** (1-2 hours) - View over 30 classic cars and trucks dating from 1901 to modern era, including a 1981 DeLorean from Back to the Future; 210 S. Main St., Greeneville, TN 37743; Friday-Saturday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM; 423-470-0841; check for admission fees [Source: [Claiborne County Sports Zone](https://claibornecountysportszone.com/2022/07/greenevilles-hidden-gem-city-garage-car-museum/), [I May Roam](https://imayroam.com/2019/04/15/greeneville-tennessee/)]

- **Historic Jonesborough** (2-4 hours) - Explore Tennessee's oldest town (established 1779) featuring the historic district on the National Register, Chester Inn (1797), International Storytelling Center, and charming downtown shops and restaurants; approximately 33 miles east of Greeneville (35-45 minutes drive) [Source: [Road Runner Travel](https://www.roadrunner.travel/tours/tennessees-oldest-town-jonesborough-tn/), [Jonesborough Official](https://jonesborough.com/), [TN Vacation](https://www.tnvacation.com/articles/jonesborough-tennessees-oldest-town-has-vibrant-story-tell)]
