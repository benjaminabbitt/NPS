# Cabrillo NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Cabrillo National Monument Visitor Center** (1800 Cabrillo Memorial Dr, San Diego, CA 92106 (32.673543, -117.242519); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, tidepools 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM; 619-523-4285) [Source: [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/cabr/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/cabrillo-national-monument-san-diego), [GPS Coordinates](https://www.alltopsights.com/attraction-cabrillo-national-monument-california-ca/)]

**Note:** Multiple passport stamps available including bonus lighthouse stamps. [Source: [California Passport Stamps](http://nostalgia.esmartkid.com/capassport.html)]

## Key Activities

- **Explore Tidepools** (1-2 hours) - Discover colorful anemones, sea stars, limpets, crabs and fish in some of San Diego's best tidepools; tidepool area open 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM; best at low tide [Source: [Roadtripping California](https://roadtrippingcalifornia.com/cabrillo-national-monument-san-diego/), [SanDiegoing](https://sandiegoing.com/the-ultimate-guide-for-cabrillo-national-monument-newbies/)]

- **Visit Old Point Loma Lighthouse** (30-45 minutes) - Tour the historic 1855 lighthouse with panoramic views of San Diego Bay and city [Source: [CNMF](https://www.cnmf.org/visit-the-park), [Roadtripping California](https://roadtrippingcalifornia.com/cabrillo-national-monument-san-diego/)]

- **Hike Bayside Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - Walk 2.5 miles round-trip with 350 feet elevation gain offering bay views [Source: [Roadtripping California](https://roadtrippingcalifornia.com/cabrillo-national-monument-san-diego/)]

- **Whale Watch** (30 minutes to 1 hour) - View gray whales from Whale Overlook during migration December-March, best in January mornings [Source: [SanDiegoing](https://sandiegoing.com/the-ultimate-guide-for-cabrillo-national-monument-newbies/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Hike Coastal Trail** (1 hour) - Walk this 1-mile easy to moderate trail to tidepool access area with coastal views [Source: [Roadtripping California](https://roadtrippingcalifornia.com/cabrillo-national-monument-san-diego/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Point Loma area** - Various San Diego attractions within 15-30 minutes in greater San Diego area
