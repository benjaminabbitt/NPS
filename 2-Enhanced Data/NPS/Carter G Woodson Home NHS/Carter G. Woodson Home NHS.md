## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-ranger-led-home-tour"></a>**Ranger-Led Home Tour** (45 minutes) - When the site reopens, guided tours will explore the three-floor townhouse where Dr. Carter G. Woodson lived and worked from 1922 to 1950, establishing Negro History Week in 1926 (now Black History Month) and serving as headquarters for the Association for the Study of African American Life and History; tours offered Thursday-Saturday at 9:00 AM, 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 2:00 PM, 3:00 PM, and 4:00 PM; maximum 20 visitors per tour; 1538 9th Street NW, Washington, DC 20001 (38.9107953, -77.0241986); advance reservations recommended via recreation.gov ($1.00 per person fee); (202) 690-5185 [Source: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/cawo/planyourvisit/guided-tours.htm), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g28970-d12456556-Reviews-Carter_G_Woodson_Home_National_Historic_Site-Washington_DC_District_of_Columbia.html)]

- <a id="activity-interpretive-galleries"></a>**Interpretive Galleries** (15-20 minutes) - When reopened, visit the introductory gallery providing context on Woodson's life and work, and a final gallery exploring his legacy and the continued importance of Black History Month; located in the temporary visitor center at 1540 9th Street NW (next door to the historic home); Thursday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/cawo/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-carter-g-woodson-memorial-park-visit"></a>**Carter G. Woodson Memorial Park Visit** (10-15 minutes) - View the statue of Dr. Woodson located just steps from the historic home, providing a peaceful outdoor spot to reflect on his contributions; open 24/7; adjacent to 1538 9th Street NW, Washington, DC 20001 [Source: [TripAdvisor Carter G. Woodson Memorial Park](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g28970-d15546574-Reviews-Carter_G_Woodson_Memorial_Park-Washington_DC_District_of_Columbia.html)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-shaw-neighborhood-walking-tour"></a>**Shaw Neighborhood Walking Tour** (90 minutes) - Self-guided exploration of Dr. Woodson's historic Shaw community featuring sites from his era including Little Ethiopia (9th Street NW) with authentic Ethiopian restaurants, historic architecture, and the cultural landscape that surrounded Woodson during his residence; start at Carter G. Woodson Home, walk through Shaw neighborhood bounded by Florida Ave, 7th St NW, P St NW, and 11th St NW; free and accessible 24/7 [Source: [NPS Things to Do - Woodson Neighborhood](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/passport-stamps.htm), [Shaw Neighborhood Guide](https://washington.org/dc-neighborhoods/shaw)]

- <a id="gem-blagden-alley-dc-alley-museum"></a>**Blagden Alley DC Alley Museum** (30-45 minutes) - Explore this outdoor street art museum featuring colorful murals painted on garage doors and building walls, including tributes to Sun Ra and Erykah Badu, located in historic alleys that reflect Shaw's alley culture from Woodson's era; Blagden Alley NW (between M and N Streets, 9th and 10th Streets NW), Washington, DC; open 24/7, free; best visited weekday mornings for quiet exploration [Source: [DC Alley Museum](https://www.dcalleymuseum.com/), [Washington.org Alleys](https://washington.org/visit-dc/alleys-and-offshoots)]

- <a id="gem-ledroit-park-heritage-trail"></a>**LeDroit Park Heritage Trail** (90 minutes) - Self-guided walking tour featuring 16 interpretive signs chronicling the history of this historic African American neighborhood where Duke Ellington grew up and which Woodson would have known, featuring unique 19th-century houses in Second Empire, Gothic Revival, and Italianate styles; trail starts at 6th Street and Florida Avenue NW, Washington, DC; open 24/7, free [Source: [LeDroit Park Heritage Trail](http://leftforledroit.com/category/history/ledroit-park-bloomingdale-heritage-trail/), [NPS LeDroit Park](https://www.nps.gov/places/ledroit-park-historic-district.htm)]

- <a id="gem-u-street-music-history-walking-tour"></a>**U Street Music History Walking Tour** (2 hours) - Guided tour led by local musician Ken Avis exploring the Greater U Street area once known as Black Broadway, featuring murals of notable history makers like Duke Ellington and Marvin Gaye, and learning about the Jazz and Cultural Renaissance that flourished during Woodson's lifetime; meets at Howard Theatre, 620 T Street NW, Washington, DC 20001; various scheduled times, check availability; fee varies [Source: [Free Tours by Foot U Street](https://freetoursbyfoot.com/u-street-walking-tour/), [DC Jazz Festival Walking Tour](https://www.dcjazzfest.org/event-details/walkingtour)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-african-american-civil-war-museum"></a>**African American Civil War Museum** (1 hour) - Explore exhibits documenting the role of African Americans in the Civil War, including the United States Colored Troops, with a museum dedicated to sharing unknown stories of African American Civil War history; located 0.5 miles from Carter G. Woodson Home; 1925 Vermont Ave NW, Washington, DC 20001 (38.9165003, -77.0258099); Monday closed, Tuesday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM; free admission; (202) 667-2667 [Source: [AACWM Visit Us](https://afroamcivilwar.org/visit-the-aacwm/), [NPS African American Civil War Memorial](https://www.nps.gov/afam/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

- <a id="nearby-ben-s-chili-bowl"></a>**Ben's Chili Bowl** (30-60 minutes) - Visit this iconic DC landmark and civil rights meeting place operating since 1958, famous for the original half-smoke; frequented by both police and protesters during the 1968 riots, and visited by President Obama; located 0.3 miles from Carter G. Woodson Home; 1213 U Street NW, Washington, DC 20009; Monday-Thursday 11:00 AM - 11:00 PM, Friday-Saturday 11:00 AM - 4:00 AM, Sunday 11:00 AM - 11:00 PM [Source: [Ben's Chili Bowl](https://benschilibowl.com/), [Washington.org Ben's Chili Bowl](https://washington.org/find-dc-listings/bens-chili-bowl)]

- <a id="nearby-howard-theatre"></a>**Howard Theatre** (2-4 hours for shows) - Experience live performances at this historic venue opened in 1910 as the first theater in the USA constructed for Black artists and audiences, where legends like Ella Fitzgerald, Duke Ellington, and Marvin Gaye performed; located 0.2 miles from Carter G. Woodson Home; 620 T Street NW, Washington, DC 20001; Monday-Saturday 6:00 PM - 3:00 AM (hours vary by event), Sunday closed; ticket prices vary; (202) 803-2899 [Source: [The Howard Theatre](https://www.unionstagepresents.com/the-howard/), [Washington.org Howard Theatre](https://washington.org/find-dc-listings/howard-theatre)]

- <a id="nearby-lincoln-theatre"></a>**Lincoln Theatre** (2-4 hours for shows) - Attend concerts, comedy shows, or theatrical productions at this historic venue on "Washington's Black Broadway" that served the city's African American community during segregation; located 0.1 miles from Carter G. Woodson Home; 1215 U Street NW, Washington, DC 20009; box office Monday-Friday 11:30 AM - 6:00 PM, Saturday-Sunday open only for performances; ticket prices vary; (202) 328-6000 [Source: [Lincoln Theatre](https://www.thelincolndc.com/), [Washington.org Lincoln Theatre](https://washington.org/find-dc-listings/lincoln-theatre)]

- <a id="nearby-9-30-club"></a>**9:30 Club** (3-5 hours for shows) - See live music at this legendary concert venue rated one of the 10 best in the United States by Rolling Stone, anchoring the U Street Corridor since 1996; located 0.3 miles from Carter G. Woodson Home; 815 V Street NW, Washington, DC 20001; Monday-Thursday 12:00 PM - 7:00 PM, Friday 12:00 PM - 7:00 PM, Saturday 6:00 PM - 11:00 PM, Sunday 6:00 PM - 8:30 PM (show times vary); ticket prices vary; (202) 265-0930 [Source: [9:30 Club](https://www.930.com/), [Washington.org 9:30 Club](https://washington.org/find-dc-listings/930-club)]

- <a id="nearby-national-museum-of-african-american-history-and-culture"></a>**National Museum of African American History and Culture** (4-6 hours) - Comprehensive museum dedicated to African American culture, art, and history, with extensive exhibits that complement and expand upon Woodson's legacy; located 1.5 miles from Carter G. Woodson Home; 1400 Constitution Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20560 (38.891068, -77.032928); Monday 12:00 PM - 5:30 PM, Tuesday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 5:30 PM, closed December 25; free admission with timed-entry passes required (released 30 days in advance); closest Metro: Federal Triangle and Smithsonian [Source: [NMAAHC Plan Your Visit](https://nmaahc.si.edu/visit/plan-your-visit), [Washington.org NMAAHC Guide](https://washington.org/visit-dc/guide-to-smithsonian-national-museum-african-american-history-culture)]
