# Amistad NRA (National Recreation Area)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-amistad-nra-visitor-center"></a>**Amistad NRA Visitor Center** (10477 Highway 90 West, Del Rio, TX 78840 (29.466018, -100.98844); 8:00 AM-4:30 PM daily; 830-775-7491)

**Note:** Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-visitor-center-experience"></a>**Visitor Center Experience** (30-60 minutes) - Watch the park's 35-minute film about Amistad Reservoir, explore exhibits on the area's natural and cultural history, and talk with park rangers; 10477 Highway 90 West, Del Rio, TX 78840; 8:00 AM-4:30 PM daily; 830-775-7491 [[Source: NPS](https://home.nps.gov/places/visitor-center-amistad.htm), [Source: TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60717-d10168741-Reviews-Amistad_National_Recreation_Area_Visitors_Center-Del_Rio_Texas.html)]

- <a id="activity-boating-and-water-sports"></a>**Boating and Water Sports** (half-day to full-day, 4-8 hours) - Navigate the 850 miles of shoreline and 68,000 acres of Lake Amistad, with access to the Rio Grande (74 miles), Devils River (25 miles), and Pecos River (14 miles); multiple boat launches throughout the recreation area; dawn to dusk [[Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/amis/planyourvisit/boating.htm), [Source: BoatUS](https://www.boatus.com/expert-advice/expert-advice-archive/2014/april/get-friendly-with-amistad)]

- <a id="activity-fishing"></a>**Fishing** (half-day to full-day, 4-8+ hours) - World-class fishing for largemouth, smallmouth, and striped bass in one of Texas's premier bass fishing destinations; available throughout the reservoir; year-round [[Source: TPWD](https://tpwd.texas.gov/fishboat/fish/recreational/lakes/amistad/), [Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/amis/planyourvisit/fishing.htm)]

- <a id="activity-kayaking-canoeing-short-trails"></a>**Kayaking/Canoeing Short Trails** (3 hours) - Paddle the Parida Cave Trail, Evans Creek from Spur 406, or San Pedro Canyon from Spur 454 to explore protected coves and side canyons; various launch areas including Spur 406 and Spur 454; dawn to dusk [[Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/amis/planyourvisit/paddling.htm), [Source: Tour Texas](https://www.tourtexas.com/destinations/del-rio-events)]

- <a id="activity-hiking-sunrise-trail"></a>**Hiking Sunrise Trail** (1-2 hours) - Walk the 2.1-mile trail connecting the Visitor Center and Spur 454 through the old Brite Ranch; trailhead at 10477 Highway 90 West, Del Rio, TX 78840; dawn to dusk [[Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/amis/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [Source: AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/texas/amistad-national-recreation-area)]

- <a id="activity-scuba-diving"></a>**Scuba Diving** (half-day, 4-6 hours) - Explore underwater caves and two boat wrecks at Scuba Cove at Diablo East with 25-30 feet visibility November through April; Diablo East, Del Rio, TX; dawn to dusk [[Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/amis/planyourvisit/scuba-diving.htm), [Source: TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g60717-d293124-r357951633-Amistad_National_Recreation_Area-Del_Rio_Texas.html)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-rock-art-viewing-at-panther-cave"></a>**Rock Art Viewing at Panther Cave** (4-6 hours including boat travel) - View 4,000-year-old Lower Pecos rock art pictographs stretching over 100 feet along rock shelter walls, accessible by boat from Pecos River boat ramp traveling 7 miles down the Rio Grande; Pecos River boat ramp, Del Rio, TX; dawn to dusk; call 830-775-7491 to confirm current lake levels before visiting [[Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/amis/learn/historyculture/howtorockart.htm), [Source: San Antonio Report](https://sanantonioreport.org/accessible-only-by-boat-panther-cave-offers-another-example-of-lower-pecos-rock-art/)]

- <a id="gem-kayaking-devils-river-multi-day-trip"></a>**Kayaking Devils River Multi-Day Trip** (16 miles roundtrip, overnight camping recommended) - Paddle to Indian Springs through one of the most ecologically intact rivers in Texas with dramatic canyon scenery; launch from Devils River access points; dawn to dusk; plan for overnight due to frequent headwinds [[Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/amis/planyourvisit/paddling.htm), [Source: TPWD](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/devils-river)]

- <a id="gem-governors-landing-overlook"></a>**Governors Landing Overlook** (30-60 minutes) - Enjoy stunning views of the Chihuahuan Desert landscape and Lake Amistad from this scenic overlook with covered picnic tables and a short hike down to swimming areas; Governors Landing Road, Del Rio, TX; dawn to dusk [[Source: Hipcamp](https://www.hipcamp.com/en-US/campground/united-states/texas/governors-landing-campground-lx2zc2mr), [Source: The Dyrt](https://thedyrt.com/camping/texas/texas-governor-s-landing)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-seminole-canyon-state-park-fate-bell-shelter-rock-art-tour"></a>**Seminole Canyon State Park - Fate Bell Shelter Rock Art Tour** (90 minutes, 45 minutes from Del Rio) - Take a guided tour to view ancient pictographs with a 2-mile roundtrip hike 200 feet down to the canyon floor; tours at 10 AM and 3 PM Wednesday-Sunday; US 90 W, Comstock, TX 78837; park open 7 days/week, tours Wednesday-Sunday; 432-292-4464; $8 adults, $5 ages 5-12 [[Source: TPWD](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/seminole-canyon), [Source: Austin Travels](https://austintravels.com/trips/seminole-canyons-world-class-rock-art-is-a-lens-to-history/)]

- <a id="nearby-whitehead-memorial-museum"></a>**Whitehead Memorial Museum** (1-2 hours, 10 minutes from Amistad Visitor Center) - Explore the early history of Del Rio and Val Verde County, including a replica of Judge Roy Bean's "Jersey Lilly" saloon; 1308 S Main St, Del Rio, TX 78840; Tuesday-Saturday 10 AM-6 PM, Sunday 1-5 PM, closed Monday; 830-774-7568 [[Source: Official Website](https://whiteheadmuseum.org/index.html), [Source: Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/whitehead-memorial-museum-del-rio)]

- <a id="nearby-val-verde-winery"></a>**Val Verde Winery** (20-30 minutes, 10 minutes from Amistad Visitor Center) - Tour and taste wines at Texas's oldest continuously operating winery since 1883, featuring award-winning Don Luis Tawny Port; 100 Qualia Dr, Del Rio, TX 78840; Tuesday-Saturday 12-7 PM, Sunday 1-5 PM, closed Monday; 830-775-9714; free tours and tastings [[Source: Official Website](https://valverdewinery.com/), [Source: Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/val-verde-winery-del-rio)]

- <a id="nearby-devils-river-state-natural-area"></a>**Devils River State Natural Area** (full day, 65 miles from Del Rio) - Hike over 25 miles of trails, swim, fish, and paddle one of Texas's most ecologically intact rivers with wilderness camping; 21715 Dolan Creek Rd, Del Rio, TX 78840; gate hours Friday-Monday 8 AM-4 PM, closed Tuesday-Thursday; 830-395-2133; reservations required [[Source: TPWD](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/devils-river), [Source: Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/devils-river-state-natural-area-del-rio)]
