# Fort Larned NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Fort Larned NHS Visitor Center** (1767 K-156 Hwy, Larned, KS 67550 (38.1830, -99.2182); Daily 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM, staff begin closing at 4:30 PM, parking lot locked at 5:00 PM, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 620-285-6911) - National Park Service passport cancellation stamps available at visitor center in stone barracks building; listed as part of Santa Fe National Historic Trail locations; visitor center includes bookstore featuring books on Army life, Plains Indians, Santa Fe Trail, historic toys and souvenirs; FREE admission. [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Fort Larned Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/fort-larned-visitor-center.htm), [America's National Parks](https://americasnationalparks.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Cancellations-07-24.pdf)]

**Note:** Site located approximately 5.5 miles west of Larned, Kansas. Download NPS App before visiting for self-guided cell phone tours. FREE admission and activities.

## Key Activities

- **Ranger-Guided Tours of Nine Restored Buildings** (1 hour) - Join approximately one-hour guided tour through nine fully furnished buildings restored to 1860s-1870s historic appearance including barracks, officers' quarters, blockhouse, hospital, commissary, shops; check in at visitor center for next tour time; best-preserved Indian Wars military post on Santa Fe Trail; Fort grounds; Daily 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM; FREE [Source: [WNPA Fort Larned](https://wnpa.org/explore-parks/fort-larned), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/fort-larned-national-historic-site/), [Fort Tours](https://www.forttours.com/pages/fortlarned.asp)]

- **Self-Guided Cell Phone Tour** (1-2 hours) - Download NPS App and use cell phone for comprehensive self-guided tour of fort buildings and grounds; "ranger in your pocket" experience; explore at own pace; detailed information about each building and fort history; fort grounds; Daily 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM; FREE [Source: [WNPA Fort Larned](https://wnpa.org/explore-parks/fort-larned), [Fort Tours](https://www.forttours.com/pages/fortlarned.asp)]

- **Living History Demonstrations** (30-60 minutes) - Watch rangers and volunteers in period dress bring fort's past to life with reenactments, educational programs, and weapons demonstrations; see blacksmith at work in working forge; experience how soldiers and families lived on frontier during Indian Wars era; largest living history events in Western Kansas; primarily summer and major holidays; fort grounds; seasonal schedules; FREE [Source: [NPS Fort Larned](https://www.nps.gov/fols/), [WNPA Fort Larned](https://wnpa.org/explore-parks/fort-larned), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/fort-larned-national-historic-site/)]

- **Santa Fe Trail Interpretation** (included in visit) - Learn about Fort Larned's role as "Guardians of the Santa Fe Trail" from 1859-1878; understand fort's protection of vital transportation route; see sandstone buildings that sheltered troops; explore connection to westward expansion and frontier commerce; integrated throughout site; Daily 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM; FREE [Source: [NPS Santa Fe Trail](https://www.nps.gov/fols/learn/historyculture/santa-fe-trail.htm), [NPS Fort Larned Place](https://www.nps.gov/places/fort-larned-national-historic-site-the-santa-fe-trail.htm), [WNPA Fort Larned](https://wnpa.org/explore-parks/fort-larned)]

- **Visitor Center Museum and Exhibits** (30-45 minutes) - Explore exhibits in stone barracks visitor center covering fort history, Plains Indians, Army life, Santa Fe Trail; browse extensive bookstore collection; excellent orientation before touring grounds; stone barracks building; Daily 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM; FREE [Source: [NPS Fort Larned Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/fort-larned-visitor-center.htm), [AAA](https://www.aaa.com/travelinfo/kansas/larned/attractions/fort-larned-national-historic-site-511082.html)]

- **Historic Grounds Exploration** (1-2 hours) - Walk expansive fort grounds at own pace; see complete authentic 1860s-1870s army post layout; view officer and enlisted housing, administration buildings, support structures; photograph impressive sandstone architecture; understand military post operations; fort grounds; Daily 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM; FREE [Source: [Travel Kansas](https://www.travelks.com/listing/fort-larned-national-historic-site/1518/), [NPCA](https://www.npca.org/parks/fort-larned-national-historic-site)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Visible Santa Fe Trail Wagon Ruts** (15-30 minutes) - Discover still-visible wagon ruts left by traders and settlers who journeyed along famous Santa Fe Trail; tangible connection to thousands who passed through; powerful historical artifact; fort grounds and surrounding areas; accessible during daylight; FREE [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/fort-larned-national-historic-site/), [WNPA Fort Larned](https://wnpa.org/explore-parks/fort-larned)]

- **Long-Lost Structures Natural Area** (30-45 minutes) - Explore natural areas around fort to discover remnants of long-lost structures that made fort's operation possible; archaeological evidence of support buildings, wells, and infrastructure; often overlooked by visitors focused on main buildings; various locations around fort; accessible during park hours; FREE [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/fort-larned-national-historic-site/), [WNPA Fort Larned](https://wnpa.org/explore-parks/fort-larned)]

- **Blacksmith Shop Working Forge** (15-30 minutes during demonstrations) - Watch period-dressed blacksmith demonstrate frontier metalworking techniques in working forge; see tools and horseshoes being made as they were in 1860s-1870s; hands-on history; blacksmith shop building; during living history events; FREE [Source: [WNPA Fort Larned](https://wnpa.org/explore-parks/fort-larned), [Travel Kansas](https://www.travelks.com/places-to-visit/cities/larned/)]

- **Indian Wars Era Complete Fort Experience** (integrated throughout) - Experience only complete and authentic Indian Wars era army post from 1860s-1870s; best-preserved military post on Santa Fe Trail; tumultuous history of westward expansion and Native American conflicts; nine restored buildings tell complete story; entire site; Daily 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM; FREE [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/fort-larned-national-historic-site/), [WNPA Fort Larned](https://wnpa.org/explore-parks/fort-larned)]

## Also Nearby

- **Santa Fe Trail Center Museum and Research Library** (1-2 hours, 4 miles east) - Visit comprehensive museum telling complete Santa Fe Trail story; research library with extensive archives; exhibits on trail commerce, travelers, and frontier life; complements fort visit perfectly; 1349 K-156, Larned, KS 67550; hours vary; admission charged [Source: [Santa Fe Trail Center](https://santafetrailcenter.org/), [NPS Santa Fe Trail Center](https://www.nps.gov/places/santa-fe-trail-center.htm), [NPS Nearby Attractions](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm)]

- **Coon Creek Crossing Marker** (15-20 minutes, 1.5 miles southwest of Garfield) - Visit marker commemorating last stream forded on wet route by Santa Fe-bound travelers; historical waypoint on trail; Garfield area; accessible during daylight; FREE [Source: [NPS Nearby Attractions](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm), [Travel Kansas](https://www.travelks.com/places-to-visit/cities/larned/)]

- **Sibley's Camp** (20-30 minutes) - See 1825 Santa Fe Trail survey team campsite; early trail exploration history; historical marker and interpretation; location near Larned; accessible during daylight; FREE [Source: [NPS Nearby Attractions](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm)]

- **Pawnee County All Veterans Memorial** (20-30 minutes, Larned) - Pay respects at comprehensive veterans memorial honoring all service branches; local history connection; Larned, KS; accessible 24/7; FREE [Source: [Pawnee County Landmarks](https://www.pawneecountykansas.com/195/Local-Landmarks), [Larned Chamber](http://www.larnedks.org/play/historical-attractions)]

- **Pawnee County Historical Homes** (30-60 minutes, Larned) - Tour historic homes showcasing frontier and early 20th century Kansas architecture and life; Larned area; hours vary; admission may vary [Source: [Pawnee County Landmarks](https://www.pawneecountykansas.com/195/Local-Landmarks), [Larned Chamber](http://www.larnedks.org/play/historical-attractions)]

---

**Total Recommended Time at Fort Larned NHS (National Historic Site):** Minimum 1.5-2 hours for visitor center and ranger-guided tour of nine buildings; 2.5-3 hours recommended for comprehensive experience including self-guided grounds exploration, wagon ruts, and demonstrations. Add time during living history events (primarily summer and major holidays). Best-preserved Indian Wars military post on Santa Fe Trail. Parking lot locked at 5:00 PM. [Source: [WNPA Fort Larned](https://wnpa.org/explore-parks/fort-larned), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/fort-larned-national-historic-site/), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]
