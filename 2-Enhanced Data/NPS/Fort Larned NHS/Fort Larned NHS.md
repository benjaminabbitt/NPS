# Fort Larned NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Fort Larned Visitor Center** (1767 KS Hwy 156, Larned, KS 67550; 38.1881528, -99.1018604; Daily 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM; Phone: 620-285-6911) - The visitor center is housed in a modern building adjacent to the historic fort complex. Passport stamps available during operating hours. The site is closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day. [Source: [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** There is no entrance fee. Call 620-285-6911 to confirm current hours, especially during severe winter weather when the site may close or delay opening.

## Key Activities

- **Self-Guided Fort Tour** (1.5-2 hours) - Explore nine original buildings and one reconstructed building, all restored to their 1860s-1870s appearance. Buildings include the officers' quarters, barracks, commissary storehouse, quartermaster storehouse, and blockhouse. Use the NPS Mobile App for audio narration as you walk through the nation's best-preserved Indian Wars-era fort on the Santa Fe Trail. 1767 KS Hwy 156, Larned, KS 67550; Daily 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/index.htm), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Visitor Center Exhibits** (30-45 minutes) - The visitor center features museum exhibits about the Santa Fe Trail, the fort's role in protecting travelers and mail coaches, and interactions between the U.S. military and Plains Indian tribes during the 1860s-1870s. Interpretive displays cover daily life of soldiers, the trail's commerce, and the region's history. 1767 KS Hwy 156, Larned, KS 67550; Daily 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM. [Source: [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

- **Living History Events** (2-4 hours, special weekends) - Experience frontier military life through costumed interpreters demonstrating period activities, weapons demonstrations, cavalry drills, and camp life presentations. Fort Larned hosts several of the largest living history events in Western Kansas. Check the park calendar for dates. 1767 KS Hwy 156, Larned, KS 67550; Special event weekends only. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Weapons Demonstrations** (30-45 minutes, special events) - Watch demonstrations of 19th-century military firearms and artillery during special event weekends. Rangers and volunteers demonstrate loading, firing, and maintenance of period weapons while explaining their role in frontier defense. Special event weekends only. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Santa Fe Trail Ruts** (30-45 minutes) - View visible wagon ruts from the historic Santa Fe Trail, one of the most significant trade routes in American history. The trail passed directly by the fort, which was established in 1859 to protect travelers. Interpretive signs explain the trail's significance. On-site near fort buildings; accessible during park hours. [Source: [NPS History](https://www.nps.gov/fols/learn/historyculture/index.htm)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (30-60 minutes) - Complete activities in the Junior Ranger booklet to learn about frontier military life, the Santa Fe Trail, and the fort's history. Earn a Junior Ranger badge upon completion. Available at the visitor center during operating hours. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Detached Blockhouse** (15-20 minutes) - Visit the reconstructed blockhouse located away from the main fort complex, providing a strategic defensive position. This two-story structure offers views of the surrounding prairie and demonstrates frontier military defensive architecture. Short walk from main fort; accessible during park hours. [Source: [NPS History](https://www.nps.gov/fols/learn/historyculture/index.htm)]

- **Prairie Landscape Walk** (20-30 minutes) - Take a contemplative walk around the fort grounds to experience the shortgrass prairie ecosystem that soldiers and travelers encountered. The relatively unchanged landscape helps visitors understand the isolation and environment of frontier military posts. Fort grounds; accessible during park hours. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Officers' Quarters Period Rooms** (20-30 minutes) - Explore the furnished officers' quarters featuring period furniture and artifacts that recreate living conditions for fort officers and their families. The attention to historical accuracy provides insight into the more comfortable side of frontier military life. Within fort complex; Daily 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Santa Fe Trail Center** (1.5-2 hours, 6 miles east in Larned) - A museum dedicated to the Santa Fe Trail featuring exhibits on Native Americans, traders, soldiers, and settlers who shaped the trail's history. Includes a recreated sod house and dugout. 1349 K-156, Larned, KS 67550; Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (seasonal hours vary); Admission charged. [Source: [Santa Fe Trail Center](https://www.santafetrailcenter.org/)]

- **Fort Dodge** (45 minutes, 35 miles west) - Site of another historic military post established in 1865 to protect the Santa Fe Trail. Now the site of the Kansas Soldiers' Home, some original buildings remain. Fort Dodge, KS; exterior viewing accessible. [Source: [Kansas Historical Society](https://www.kshs.org/)]

- **Pawnee Rock State Historic Site** (30 minutes, 20 miles northeast) - A famous Santa Fe Trail landmark where travelers carved their names into sandstone. The rock was significantly reduced by quarrying but remains an important historical site with interpretive displays. Near Pawnee Rock, KS; Daily dawn to dusk; Free admission. [Source: [Kansas State Parks](https://www.kshs.org/p/pawnee-rock-state-historic-site/16891)]

- **Quivira National Wildlife Refuge** (45-60 minutes, 25 miles southeast) - A 22,135-acre refuge featuring wetlands, grasslands, and salt marshes providing habitat for migrating waterfowl and shorebirds. Excellent birding, especially during spring and fall migrations with whooping cranes passing through. 1434 NE 80th St, Stafford, KS 67578; Daily sunrise to sunset; Free admission. [Source: [USFWS Quivira](https://www.fws.gov/refuge/quivira)]

## Total Recommended Time

**Total recommended visit time for Fort Larned NHS: 2-3 hours**

This includes 1.5-2 hours for the self-guided tour of the nine original buildings and reconstructed blockhouse, 30-45 minutes for the visitor center exhibits, and time to view Santa Fe Trail ruts on the grounds. For special living history event weekends, plan 3-4 hours to fully experience demonstrations and activities. The site is free to enter and open daily except major holidays.

[Sources: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/index.htm), [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]
