# Fort Larned NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-fort-larned-nhs-visitor-center"></a>**Fort Larned NHS Visitor Center** - Located in one of the fort's barracks buildings; 1767 KS-156, Larned, KS 67550 (38.1881528, -99.1018604); daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 620-285-6911 [Source: [NPS Fort Larned Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/fort-larned-national-historic-site/)]

**Note:** Call 620-285-6911 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-self-guided-fort-exploration"></a>**Self-Guided Fort Exploration** (1.5-2 hours) - Explore nine original stone structures from the 1860s including the post hospital, bakery, enlisted men's barracks, mess hall, officers quarters, storehouses, and a reconstructed blockhouse; visitors can walk through all buildings and use a cell phone tour for "a ranger in your pocket" narration throughout the fort and grounds; 1767 KS-156, Larned, KS 67550 (38.1881528, -99.1018604); daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM, parking lot gate locked at 5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Fort Larned](https://www.nps.gov/fols/), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/fort-larned-national-historic-site/), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60810-d534161-Reviews-Fort_Larned_National_Historic_Site-Larned_Kansas.html)]

- <a id="activity-guided-ranger-tour"></a>**Guided Ranger Tour** (1 hour) - Join a ranger-led tour exploring the fort's history and buildings; simply check in at the visitor center to see when the next tour is available (large groups and school groups must call ahead for reservations); 1767 KS-156, Larned, KS 67550; daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM; 620-285-6911 [Source: [NPS Fort Larned Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [NationalParksData.com](https://nationalparksdata.com/a-visitors-guide-to-fort-larned-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="activity-blacksmith-shop-demonstrations"></a>**Blacksmith Shop Demonstrations** (30 minutes) - Watch the blacksmith use fire, anvil, and tools to turn iron into everyday objects from the 1860s; demonstrations available late spring through early fall, best on weekends but also some weekdays (call ahead to confirm schedule); during special events (Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day weekends) demonstrations scheduled at 10:00 AM; 1767 KS-156, Larned, KS 67550; 620-285-6911 [Source: [NPS Visit Fort Larned Blacksmith](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/visit-the-fort-larned-blacksmith.htm), [Schedule of Labor Day weekend festivities](https://greatbendpost.com/posts/1246b26c-3e59-41ce-a05e-eaf169f65bbc)]

- <a id="activity-history-nature-trail"></a>**History & Nature Trail** (30-45 minutes) - Walk a one-mile loop with half on a mowed path and half on gravel road through the fort; use your cell phone to access the audio tour along the trail; 1767 KS-156, Larned, KS 67550; daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM [Source: [NPS Fort Larned Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/fort-larned-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="activity-visitor-center-exhibits"></a>**Visitor Center Exhibits** (30 minutes) - View exhibits about Fort Larned's role protecting the Santa Fe Trail and learn about frontier life in the 1860s-70s; browse the park store with toys, collectibles, apparel, and extensive book collection; 1767 KS-156, Larned, KS 67550 (38.1881528, -99.1018604); daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM; 620-285-6911 [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/fort-larned-national-historic-site/), [NPS Fort Larned](https://www.nps.gov/fols/)]

- <a id="activity-living-history-events"></a>**Living History Events** (varies, full day recommended) - Experience the largest living history events in western Kansas with rangers and volunteers in period dress bringing the fort's past to life through reenactments, demonstrations, and weapons demonstrations; special events occur on Memorial Day, July 4th, and Labor Day weekends; 1767 KS-156, Larned, KS 67550; 620-285-6911 [Source: [WNPA Fort Larned](https://wnpa.org/explore-parks/fort-larned), [NPS Fort Larned Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-santa-fe-trail-wagon-ruts-site"></a>**Santa Fe Trail Wagon Ruts Site** (1 hour round trip + exploration time) - See real wagon ruts left by countless traders and settlers who traveled the legendary Santa Fe Trail; requires a 5.5-mile one-way trip on gravel roads from the main fort, so check road conditions with a park ranger before setting out; accessible via gravel road from fort; check with rangers for current conditions; 620-285-6911 [Source: [NPS Fort Larned Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [NPS Santa Fe Trail Ruts](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/santa-fe-trail-ruts.htm)]

- <a id="gem-long-lost-structures-exploration"></a>**Long-Lost Structures Exploration** (30-60 minutes) - Explore Fort Larned's natural areas to discover remnants of long-lost structures that once made the fort's operation possible; park rangers can provide guidance on where to find these features; 1767 KS-156, Larned, KS 67550; daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM; 620-285-6911 [Source: [NPS Fort Larned Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- <a id="gem-historical-garden"></a>**Historical Garden** (15-30 minutes) - Visit the historical garden for birding opportunities and during special event weekends, pick vegetables from the period-appropriate garden; 1767 KS-156, Larned, KS 67550; available during special events [Source: [NPS Fort Larned Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- <a id="gem-annual-candlelight-tour"></a>**Annual Candlelight Tour** (1.5 hours, reservations required) - Experience the fort after dark with only lanterns and candles lighting the buildings, recreating how the fort appeared in the 1800s before electricity; held annually on the 2nd Saturday in October since 1976 with a different theme each year; tours run from 7:30 PM to 10:15 PM every 15 minutes (12 total tours, 20 people per tour); reservations required by phone at 620-285-6911 or in person at visitor center starting late September [Source: [NPS Candlelight Tour](https://www.nps.gov/fols/planyourvisit/candlelight-tour.htm), [Great Bend Post](https://greatbendpost.com/posts/c380fdcf-594e-4b79-b35a-51a2264bca94)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-santa-fe-trail-center-museum-research-library"></a>**Santa Fe Trail Center Museum & Research Library** (1.5-2 hours, 4 miles east) - Explore ten buildings including sod and dugout houses, one-room schoolhouse, limestone cooling house, Santa Fe Railroad Depot, Escue Chapel, and buildings dedicated to historic farm equipment and autos; learn about the historic Santa Fe Trail commerce route and westward expansion's effect on central Kansas; 1349 K-156 Hwy, Larned, KS 67550; Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, closed Sunday-Monday (Monday tours available by advance arrangement), closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day; 620-285-2054 [Source: [Santa Fe Trail Center](https://santafetrailcenter.org/), [Santa Fe Trail Center Visit Info](https://santafetrailcenter.org/your-visit/)]

- <a id="nearby-pawnee-rock-state-historic-site"></a>**Pawnee Rock State Historic Site** (30-45 minutes, 10-16 miles east) - Walk the grounds of this sandstone outcropping that served as a landmark and halfway point on the Santa Fe Trail; American Indians used it as a vantage point to spot bison herds and approaching wagon trains; enjoy 360-degree views from this historic landmark with interpretive signs and a granite monument; 631 N Centre St, Pawnee Rock, KS 67567; daily 7:00 AM-8:00 PM; free admission [Source: [NPS Pawnee Rock](https://www.nps.gov/places/pawnee-rock-state-historic-site.htm), [Travel KS](https://www.travelks.com/listing/pawnee-rock-state-historic-site/972/)]

- <a id="nearby-cheyenne-bottoms-wildlife-area"></a>**Cheyenne Bottoms Wildlife Area** (2-3 hours, approximately 30 miles northeast via Great Bend) - Visit the largest marsh in the inland United States and one of the most important stopovers for migrating shorebirds in the Western Hemisphere; explore 19,857 acres of wetlands with viewing opportunities and visit the Kansas Wetlands Education Center; Kansas Wetlands Education Center hours: Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM-5:00 PM (closed Mondays November 1-March 31); 620-566-1456 [Source: [KDWP Cheyenne Bottoms](https://ksoutdoors.com/KDWP-Info/Locations/Wildlife-Areas/Public-Wildlife-Areas-in-Southwest-Kansas/Cheyenne-Bottoms), [NPS Cheyenne Bottoms](https://www.nps.gov/places/cheyenne-bottoms-wildlife-area.htm)]

- <a id="nearby-quivira-national-wildlife-refuge"></a>**Quivira National Wildlife Refuge** (2-3 hours, 33 miles east) - Explore 22,135 acres featuring a unique combination of rare inland salt marsh and sand prairie; over 340 species of birds documented at this Wetland of Global Importance; drive the auto tour route through diverse habitats; headquarters near Stafford, KS; open 1.5 hours before sunrise to 1.5 hours after sunset; 620-410-4011 [Source: [USFWS Quivira](https://www.fws.gov/refuge/quivira), [NPS Pawnee Rock](https://www.nps.gov/places/pawnee-rock-state-historic-site.htm)]
