# Black Canyon of the Gunnison NP (National Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-south-rim-visitor-center"></a>**South Rim Visitor Center** (9800 Highway 347, Montrose, CO 81401; 38.5752, -107.6836; Daily 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, may have reduced winter hours; Phone: 970-641-2337) - Primary visitor center with exhibits on canyon geology, films, bookstore, and ranger programs. Located at the park's main entrance on the South Rim. Primary stamp location. [Source: [NPS Black Canyon](https://www.nps.gov/blca/index.htm)]

**Note:** The North Rim is accessed via a different route and has a ranger station (seasonal). Call 970-641-2337 for current hours and stamp availability.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-south-rim-drive"></a>**South Rim Drive** (2-4 hours) - Seven-mile scenic drive along the South Rim with 12 viewpoints offering dramatic views into one of North America's steepest and deepest canyons. Pull-offs at each overlook allow visitors to experience views of the 2,000+ foot canyon walls and Gunnison River below. Start at Tomichi Point near the visitor center and end at High Point. Year-round access (weather permitting). [Source: [Earth Trekkers South Rim Guide](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/south-rim-drive-road-black-canyon-gunnison/)]

- <a id="activity-gunnison-point-overlook"></a>**Gunnison Point Overlook** (15-30 minutes) - Most popular viewpoint located directly behind the South Rim Visitor Center. Short downhill walk leads to panoramic views of the canyon depths with the Gunnison River 1,800 feet below. Accessible year-round. [Source: [NPS South Rim](https://www.nps.gov/blca/planyourvisit/southrim.htm)]

- <a id="activity-painted-wall-view-at-cedar-point"></a>**Painted Wall View at Cedar Point** (30-45 minutes) - Easy 0.2-mile Cedar Point Nature Trail leads to two viewpoints including Colorado's tallest cliff (2,250 feet). Best view of the distinctive pink and white pegmatite veins that give the Painted Wall its name. South Rim Drive, Milepost 4.5. [Source: [American Southwest Viewpoints](https://www.americansouthwest.net/colorado/black_canyon_gunnison/viewpoints.html)]

- <a id="activity-pulpit-rock-overlook"></a>**Pulpit Rock Overlook** (20-30 minutes) - 134-yard trail leads to viewing area with excellent views of the Gunnison River below. Two miles along South Rim Road from the visitor center. [Source: [Earth Trekkers Guide](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/things-to-do-south-rim-of-black-canyon-of-the-gunnison/)]

- <a id="activity-warner-point-nature-trail"></a>**Warner Point Nature Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - 1.5-mile round-trip hike at the end of South Rim Road. Trail winds through pinyon-juniper woodland with views of the San Juan Mountains and Uncompahgre Valley, ending at Warner Point with excellent canyon views. Moderate difficulty. [Source: [WNPA Trails](https://wnpa.org/hike-these-awesome-trails-of-black-canyon-of-the-gunnison/)]

- <a id="activity-rim-rock-nature-trail"></a>**Rim Rock Nature Trail** (45 minutes - 1 hour) - One-mile interpretive trail following the canyon's edge from Campground Loop C to the South Rim Visitor Center, or accessible from Tomichi Point. Excellent views of the sheer canyon walls and Gunnison River below. [Source: [WNPA Trails](https://wnpa.org/hike-these-awesome-trails-of-black-canyon-of-the-gunnison/)]

- <a id="activity-oak-flat-loop-trail"></a>**Oak Flat Loop Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - 2-mile loop trail descending below the rim into a Gambel oak forest. Some of the best views in the park with multiple viewpoints along the route. Moderate difficulty with some steep sections. Trailhead near the visitor center. [Source: [We're in the Rockies](https://wereintherockies.com/black-canyon-of-the-gunnison/)]

- <a id="activity-north-rim-drive"></a>**North Rim Drive** (2-3 hours) - More remote experience with 6 overlooks including Chasm View, the closest you can get to the canyon's narrowest point (1,100 feet wide). Ranger station at North Rim Campground (seasonal). Access via separate gravel road from Crawford; road closed in winter. [Source: [NPS Black Canyon](https://www.nps.gov/blca/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-stargazing"></a>**Stargazing** (1-3 hours) - International Dark Sky Park designation makes this one of the best night sky viewing locations in Colorado. Ranger-led astronomy programs offered seasonally. Year-round after dark. [Source: [NPS Black Canyon](https://www.nps.gov/blca/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-inner-canyon-routes"></a>**Inner Canyon Routes** (4-8 hours) - Several unmaintained routes descend to the Gunnison River for experienced adventurers. Gunnison Route is the most popular. Requires free wilderness permit from visitor center. Class III terrain with exposure; not for beginners. [Source: [NPS Black Canyon](https://www.nps.gov/blca/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-east-portal-road"></a>**East Portal Road** (1-2 hours) - Steep 16% grade descent (2,000 feet in 5 miles) to the Gunnison River at the base of the canyon. Access to fishing and river-level views. Open seasonally; no RVs or trailers. [Source: [NPS Black Canyon](https://www.nps.gov/blca/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-fishing"></a>**Fishing** (2-4 hours) - Gold Medal trout fishing in the Gunnison River accessible via East Portal or inner canyon routes. Rainbow and brown trout. Colorado fishing license required. Year-round (access weather dependent). [Source: [NPS Black Canyon](https://www.nps.gov/blca/index.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-cross-fissures-view"></a>**Cross Fissures View** (20-30 minutes) - 357-yard path leads to viewpoint where you can see where two side canyons intersect. Less crowded than other overlooks. South Rim Drive. [Source: [Earth Trekkers Guide](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/south-rim-drive-road-black-canyon-gunnison/)]

- <a id="gem-chasm-view-on-north-rim"></a>**Chasm View on North Rim** (30-45 minutes) - The narrowest point of the canyon (1,100 feet rim to rim) is best viewed from this North Rim overlook. Includes views down to the river 1,800 feet below. Requires North Rim access. [Source: [American Southwest Viewpoints](https://www.americansouthwest.net/colorado/black_canyon_gunnison/viewpoints.html)]

- <a id="gem-high-point"></a>**High Point** (20-30 minutes) - The highest overlook at the end of South Rim Drive. Excellent panoramic views of the canyon and surrounding landscape. Less crowded than viewpoints closer to the visitor center. [Source: [Alicia Marie Travels](https://aliciamarietravels.com/black-canyon-of-the-gunnison-south-rim-viewpoints-and-overlooks-a-complete-guide/)]

- <a id="gem-tomichi-point-at-sunrise"></a>**Tomichi Point at Sunrise** (30-45 minutes) - First overlook on South Rim Drive, before the visitor center. East-facing for excellent sunrise photography with morning light illuminating the canyon walls. [Source: [Earth Trekkers Guide](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/things-to-do-south-rim-of-black-canyon-of-the-gunnison/)]

- <a id="gem-rock-climbing"></a>**Rock Climbing** (full day) - The Painted Wall and other canyon walls offer world-class technical climbing for experienced climbers. Multi-day big-wall routes available. Free permit required. [Source: [NPS Black Canyon](https://www.nps.gov/blca/index.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-curecanti-national-recreation-area"></a>**Curecanti National Recreation Area** (2-6 hours, adjacent) - Three reservoirs on the Gunnison River upstream from Black Canyon. Boating, fishing, camping, and boat tours into the upper Black Canyon (Morrow Point Boat Tours). Elk Creek Visitor Center off US 50. [Source: [NPS Curecanti](https://www.nps.gov/cure/index.htm)]

- <a id="nearby-montrose"></a>**Montrose** (variable, 8 miles) - Gateway town with lodging, dining, and services. Museum of the Mountain West offers historic buildings and artifacts. Montrose, CO on US 50. [Source: [Centennial RV Guide](https://www.centennialrv550.com/black-canyon-of-the-gunnison)]

- <a id="nearby-crawford-state-park"></a>**Crawford State Park** (2-4 hours, 10 miles north) - Reservoir with swimming, fishing, and camping. Alternative camping option if park campgrounds are full. North of park via CO-92. [Source: [NPS Black Canyon](https://www.nps.gov/blca/index.htm)]

- <a id="nearby-colorado-national-monument"></a>**Colorado National Monument** (half-day, 65 miles northwest) - Dramatic red rock canyons and formations near Grand Junction. Rim Rock Drive scenic road. Grand Junction, CO. [Source: [NPS Colorado NM](https://www.nps.gov/colm/index.htm)]

## Total Recommended Time

**Total recommended visit time for Black Canyon of the Gunnison NP: 1-2 days**

A half-day allows time for the South Rim Drive and several overlooks. A full day adds hiking trails (Warner Point, Oak Flat Loop) and stargazing. Two days enables exploration of both rims and fishing at East Portal. The South Rim is the most accessible and has the most facilities; the North Rim offers a more remote experience but requires a separate 2-hour drive.

[Sources: [NPS Black Canyon](https://www.nps.gov/blca/index.htm), [Earth Trekkers Guide](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/south-rim-drive-road-black-canyon-gunnison/), [WNPA Trails](https://wnpa.org/hike-these-awesome-trails-of-black-canyon-of-the-gunnison/)]
