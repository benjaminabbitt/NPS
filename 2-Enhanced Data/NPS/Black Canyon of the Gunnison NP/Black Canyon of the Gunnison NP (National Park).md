# Black Canyon of the Gunnison NP (National Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **South Rim Visitor Center** (9800 Highway 347, Montrose, CO [38.5550, -107.6866]; Open year-round except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 970-641-2337) [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/blca/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/blca/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/10050758)]

- **North Rim Ranger Station** (7295 Black Canyon Road, Crawford, CO; Seasonal - typically mid-May through mid-November; 970-641-2337) [Source: [NPS North Rim](https://www.nps.gov/blca/planyourvisit/northrim.htm), [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/10318698)]

**Note:** Call ahead at 970-641-2337 to confirm current stamp locations and seasonal availability, especially for the North Rim Ranger Station which closes in winter.

## Key Activities

- **South Rim Road Scenic Drive** (2-4 hours) - The main 7-mile scenic drive features 12 viewpoints including Tomichi Point, Pulpit Rock View, Cross Fissures View, Rock Point, Devil's Lookout, Chasm View, Painted Wall View (featuring Colorado's tallest cliff at 2,250 feet), Cedar Point, Dragon Point, Sunset View, and High Point; some overlooks are right next to parking lots while others require short walks; South Rim Road, Montrose, CO [38.5550, -107.6866]; Late April-mid November for full road access, Gunnison Point area accessible year-round; 970-641-2337 [Source: [Earth Trekkers South Rim Drive](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/south-rim-drive-road-black-canyon-gunnison/), [Live That Adventure](https://livethatadventure.com/south-rim-road-the-best-way-to-visit-black-canyon-of-gunnison-np/), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g143014-d145028-Reviews-South_Rim_Road-Black_Canyon_Of_The_Gunnison_National_Park_Colorado.html)]

- **Warner Point Nature Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - The most popular trail in the park, this 1.5-1.6 mile roundtrip hike gains 420 feet and leads to Warner Point overlook with panoramic views of the canyon and San Juan Mountains; trailhead at High Point parking area at the end of South Rim Road, 6 miles west of South Rim Visitor Center [38.5550, -107.6866]; Year-round but best late April-November; 970-641-2337 [Source: [AllTrails Warner Point](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/colorado/warner-point-nature-trail), [Noah Hawaii Photography](https://noahlangphotography.com/blog/warner-point-nature-trail-black-canyon-of-the-gunnison-national-park), [10Adventures](https://www.10adventures.com/hikes/bcgnp/warner-point-trail/)]

- **Rim Rock Nature Trail** (30-45 minutes) - An easy 1-mile trail following the canyon's edge with excellent views of sheer walls and the Gunnison River below; connects South Rim Campground and South Rim Visitor Center; rocky with stairs and steep drop-offs; Trailhead near South Rim Visitor Center, 9800 Highway 347, Montrose, CO [38.5550, -107.6866]; Year-round; 970-641-2337 [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/colorado/black-canyon-of-the-gunnison-national-park), [NPS Hiking Trails](https://www.nps.gov/blca/planyourvisit/hikingtrails.htm)]

- **Oak Flat Loop Trail** (45 minutes - 1 hour) - A moderate 1.4-mile loop that leads away from the rim through desert brush before reaching a canyon overlook; less crowded than rim trails, offering more solitude; features a hidden climbing wall; South Rim area, access from South Rim Road [38.5550, -107.6866]; Best April-November; 970-641-2337 [Source: [We're in the Rockies](https://wereintherockies.com/black-canyon-of-the-gunnison/), [Travels and Curiosities](https://www.travelsandcuriosities.com/cool-things-to-do-at-black-canyon-of-the-gunnison-national-park)]

- **East Portal Road Drive** (2-3 hours roundtrip) - A dramatic 5-mile descent dropping 2,000 feet to the canyon bottom with 16% grade and hairpin turns; the only vehicle access to the Gunnison River within the park; takes 45 minutes one-way; vehicles over 22 feet prohibited; Entrance just beyond South Rim entrance station off Highway 347 [38.5550, -107.6866]; Late April-mid November; 970-641-2337 [Source: [Parked in Paradise](https://www.parkedinparadise.com/east-portal-road/), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/drive-east-portal.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g143014-d3559738-Reviews-East_Portal_Road-Black_Canyon_Of_The_Gunnison_National_Park_Colorado.html)]

- **Cedar Point Nature Trail** (15-30 minutes) - An easy 0.2-0.3 mile trail ending at two incredible canyon viewpoints including views of the Painted Wall, Colorado's tallest cliff at 2,250 feet; South Rim Road [38.5550, -107.6866]; Best April-November; 970-641-2337 [Source: [We're in the Rockies](https://wereintherockies.com/black-canyon-of-the-gunnison/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/colorado/black-canyon-of-the-gunnison-national-park)]

- **Painted Wall Overlook** (10-15 minutes) - Short 200-yard walk from parking to view Colorado's tallest cliff rising 2,247 feet from the Gunnison River; particularly beautiful at sunrise; Located off South Rim Road, about 200 yards from parking area [38.5550, -107.6866]; Best April-November, stunning at sunrise; 970-641-2337 [Source: [Great American Hikes](https://www.greatamericanhikes.com/post/view-the-painted-wall-in-black-canyon-of-the-gunnison-national-park), [Parked in Paradise](https://www.parkedinparadise.com/painted-wall/), [American Southwest](https://www.americansouthwest.net/colorado/black_canyon_gunnison/viewpoints.html)]

- **Fishing on Gunnison River** (Half day to full day) - Gold Medal water for trophy rainbow and brown trout; artificial flies and lures only (no bait); requires 90-minute descent via inner canyon routes and 2-hour return hike; best during salmonfly hatch early-mid June; must register at visitor center and sign waiver; East Portal area and inner canyon routes from South Rim [38.5550, -107.6866]; Year-round, best early-mid June; 970-641-2337 [Source: [NPS Fishing](https://www.nps.gov/blca/planyourvisit/fishing.htm), [DIY Fly Fishing](https://diyflyfishing.com/black-canyon-gunnison-national-park-colorado/), [Black Canyon Anglers](https://blackcanyonanglers.com/fly-fishing/)]

- **Rock Climbing** (Full day to multi-day) - Expert-level climbing on routes 200-1,000+ feet long on some of North America's steepest cliffs; free permits required; raptor closures March 15-July 15; peak seasons mid-April to early June and mid-September to early November; must register at visitor center; Inner canyon walls accessed from South Rim and North Rim [38.5550, -107.6866]; Mid-April to early June and mid-September to early November (peak seasons); 970-641-2337 [Source: [NPS Rock Climbing](https://www.nps.gov/blca/planyourvisit/rockclimbing.htm), [Mountain Trip](https://mountaintrip.com/tellurides-local-mountain-guides/black-canyon-rock-climbing/), [Mountain Project](https://www.mountainproject.com/area/105744397/black-canyon-of-the-gunnison)]

## Hidden Gems

- **North Rim Road and Viewpoints** (2-3 hours) - Far less crowded than South Rim, this scenic road features 6 overlooks including Kneeling Camel, Island Peaks, Chasm View, and Green Mountain; offers quieter, more remote experience with historic overlooks; North Rim Road accessed from Crawford, CO via Black Canyon Road (last 7 miles unpaved) [38.571615, -107.7272721]; Mid-May through mid-November; 970-641-2337 [Source: [Live That Adventure North Rim](https://livethatadventure.com/north-rim-scenic-road-of-black-canyon-of-gunnison-np/), [Travels and Curiosities](https://www.travelsandcuriosities.com/cool-things-to-do-at-black-canyon-of-the-gunnison-national-park)]

- **Chasm View Nature Trail (North Rim)** (30-45 minutes) - A hidden gem short 0.3-0.5 mile interpretive loop offering excellent views of The Narrows, the narrowest section of the canyon; rarely crowded; North Rim Road [38.571615, -107.7272721]; Mid-May through mid-November; 970-641-2337 [Source: [We're in the Rockies](https://wereintherockies.com/black-canyon-of-the-gunnison/), [Travels and Curiosities](https://www.travelsandcuriosities.com/cool-things-to-do-at-black-canyon-of-the-gunnison-national-park)]

- **Deadhorse Trail (North Rim)** (3-4 hours) - A 5-mile roundtrip hike through sagebrush and oak to scenic views of Deadhorse Gulch; features inconspicuous unnamed viewpoints at unexpected spots, including one of the best quiet sunset locations; Deadhorse Trailhead, North Rim Road [38.571615, -107.7272721]; Mid-May through mid-November; 970-641-2337 [Source: [Travels and Curiosities](https://www.travelsandcuriosities.com/cool-things-to-do-at-black-canyon-of-the-gunnison-national-park), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/colorado/black-canyon-of-the-gunnison-national-park)]

- **North Vista Trail to Exclamation Point** (2-3 hours) - A 3-mile roundtrip hike to Exclamation Point with dramatic canyon views, or continue to 7-mile Green Mountain for extended exploration; North Rim Trailhead [38.571615, -107.7272721]; Mid-May through mid-November; 970-641-2337 [Source: [We're in the Rockies](https://wereintherockies.com/black-canyon-of-the-gunnison/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/colorado/black-canyon-of-the-gunnison-national-park)]

- **Cross-Country Skiing and Snowshoeing** (Half day to full day) - South Rim Road is groomed for cross-country skiing just past the Visitor Center, allowing a 6-mile trek with access to all overlook points; snowshoeing available all along the South Rim with beautiful snowy canyon views; South Rim Road from Visitor Center [38.5550, -107.6866]; December-March; 970-641-2337 [Source: [Travels and Curiosities](https://www.travelsandcuriosities.com/cool-things-to-do-at-black-canyon-of-the-gunnison-national-park), [My Colorado Parks](https://www.mycoloradoparks.com/things-to-do/itineraries/black-canyon-2-day-itinerary/)]

- **Stargazing and Astronomy Programs** (2-3 hours) - Remote location with undisturbed dark skies ideal for stargazing and night photography, especially during new moon phases; ranger-led astronomy programs offered year-round; South Rim Visitor Center area [38.5550, -107.6866]; Year-round, best during new moon; 970-641-2337 [Source: [Travels and Curiosities](https://www.travelsandcuriosities.com/cool-things-to-do-at-black-canyon-of-the-gunnison-national-park), [National Parks Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/park/subpage/rock-climbing-black-canyon-gunnison-national-park)]

## Also Nearby

- **Curecanti National Recreation Area and Blue Mesa Reservoir** (Half day to full day, 16 miles east) - The largest body of water in Colorado featuring year-round fishing for lake trout and kokanee salmon, boating, windsurfing, waterskiing, ice fishing, and hiking trails including the popular Dillon Pinnacles trail; 5,000-acre archaeological district; no entrance fee; Elk Creek Visitor Center: 102 Elk Creek, Gunnison, CO 81230 [38.46574610, -107.16667990]; Visitor Center open Mon-Fri 9am-12pm and 12:30pm-4pm; 970-641-2337 [Source: [Gunnison Crested Butte Tourism](https://gunnisoncrestedbutte.com/visit/places-to-go/parks-and-outdoors/curecanti-and-blue-mesa-reservoir/), [NPS Curecanti](https://www.nps.gov/cure/), [Uncover Colorado](https://www.uncovercolorado.com/national-lands/curecanti-national-recreation-area/)]

- **Gunnison Gorge National Conservation Area Rafting** (Full day to 2-day trip, 10 miles north of Montrose) - 13.5-14 mile rafting adventure through Gold Medal waters with Class III/III+ rapids; requires hiking down steep 1-mile Chukar Trail to put-in; limited to two groups of nine per day for wilderness experience; 2-day trips commonly offered; BLM Uncompahgre Field Office, 2465 South Townsend Avenue, Montrose, CO; Call for hours; Contact BLM office [Source: [Wilderness Aware](https://www.inaraft.com/rivers/gunnison/2day-trip/), [River Bent](https://riverbent.com/paddling-gunnison-gorge-colorado/), [BLM Gunnison Gorge](https://www.blm.gov/programs/national-conservation-lands/colorado/gunnison-gorge-nca)]

- **Ute Indian Museum** (1.5-2 hours, Montrose - 14 miles from park) - Museum celebrating the history and culture of the Ute people, Colorado's longest continuous residents; 17253 Chipeta Rd, Montrose, CO 81403 [38.4419, -107.8539]; Mon-Sat 9am-4:30pm, Sun 11am-4pm; 970-249-3098; Adult admission $12.50 [Source: [Uncover Colorado Ute Museum](https://www.uncovercolorado.com/activities/ute-indian-museum/), [History Colorado](https://www.historycolorado.org/ute-indian-museum), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/ute-indian-museum-montrose)]

- **Museum of the Mountain West** (1.5 hours, Montrose - 16 miles from park) - Living history museum featuring Colorado's history from the 1800s to 1930s with authentic buildings and artifacts; guided tours by trained docents; 68169 E. Miami Rd, Montrose, CO 81401 [38.4780, -107.8150]; Mon-Sat 8:30am-4:30pm, closed Sunday; 970-240-3400; Adult admission $12.50 [Source: [Museum of the Mountain West](https://museumofthemountainwest.org/), [Uncover Colorado](https://www.uncovercolorado.com/museums/museum-mountain-west/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g33559-d1539380-Reviews-Museum_of_the_Mountain_West-Montrose_Colorado.html)]

- **Gunnison Gorge National Conservation Area Hiking and Fishing** (Half day to full day, 10 miles north of Montrose) - Gold Medal fishing waters and scenic hiking trails in the conservation area adjacent to the national park; additional access point for Gunnison River fishing; 2465 South Townsend Avenue, Montrose, CO (BLM office); Call for current conditions; Contact BLM [Source: [Visit Montrose](https://visitmontrose.com/), [BLM Gunnison Gorge](https://www.blm.gov/programs/national-conservation-lands/colorado/gunnison-gorge-nca)]
