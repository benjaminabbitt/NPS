# Charles Pinckney NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-charles-pinckney-nhs-visitor-center"></a>**Charles Pinckney NHS Visitor Center**
  **Address:** 1254 Long Point Road, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464
  **Geocoordinates:** (32.8467455, -79.8278953)
  **Hours:** Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed Monday-Tuesday and New Year's Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas
  **Phone:** (843) 881-5516 or (843) 883-3123

**Important Notes:**
- The Visitor Center is temporarily closed until Spring 2025 for renovations. During this closure, the grounds remain open Wednesday-Sunday from 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM. [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/chpi/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-visitor-center-exhibits"></a>**Visitor Center Exhibits** (30-60 minutes) - Explore exhibits about Charles Pinckney, the Constitution, Lowcountry plantation life, enslaved labor, and Gullah culture; includes a small store and museum with photos and information on the Gullah-Geechee National Heritage Corridor; 1254 Long Point Road, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464; Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (closed until Spring 2025). [[Source](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/charles-pinckney-national-historic-site/)], [[Source](https://www.npca.org/articles/1178-how-charles-pinckney-changed-my-view-of-national-parks)]

- <a id="activity-park-films"></a>**Park Films** (20-50 minutes total) - Watch educational films including "The Birth of the Constitution" (24 minutes), "Forgotten Founder" (17 minutes), and "Founding Brothers: Sides of Slavery" (11 minutes) that provide an overview of Pinckney's life and contributions; visitor center; Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (closed until Spring 2025). [[Source](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/charles-pinckney-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="activity-half-mile-nature-trail"></a>**Half-Mile Nature Trail** (30-45 minutes) - Walk a well-maintained loop trail starting on an old road lined with cedar trees, passing towering magnolia trees, ancient live oaks, and wax myrtle; trail follows part of the roadway that once connected Snee Farm to US 17; includes exhibits describing archaeological excavations of slave quarters and outbuildings; walk along the banks of a wooded tidal marsh leading to a boardwalk overlooking a branch of the Wampacheone Creek; 1254 Long Point Road, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464; grounds open Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM (5:00 PM when visitor center reopens). [[Source](https://www.sctrails.net/trails/trail/charles-pinckney-national-historic-site)], [[Source](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/charles-pinckney-national-historic-site/)], [[Source](https://www.npca.org/articles/1178-how-charles-pinckney-changed-my-view-of-national-parks)]

- <a id="activity-ranger-led-programs"></a>**Ranger-Led Programs** (1 hour) - Join ranger-led programs offered Saturdays and Sundays at 11:00 AM with approximately 1 hour duration and maximum walking distance of ½ mile; visitor center area; Saturdays and Sundays at 11:00 AM. [[Source](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/charles-pinckney-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="activity-junior-ranger-program"></a>**Junior Ranger Program** (30-60 minutes) - Complete the Junior Ranger program activities and earn a badge; program duration varies by age of participant; visitor center; Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (when visitor center reopens). [[Source](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/charles-pinckney-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="activity-ornamental-gardens-and-historic-grounds-walk"></a>**Ornamental Gardens and Historic Grounds Walk** (20-30 minutes) - Stroll through ornamental gardens under Spanish moss-draped live oaks and beautiful camellias while reading wayside exhibits about the plantation's history; see a stone cenotaph commemorating Colonel Charles Pinckney, the father of governor Pinckney; 1254 Long Point Road, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464; grounds open Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM (5:00 PM when visitor center reopens). [[Source](https://travelerofcharleston.com/plan-a-visit-to-the-charles-pinckney-site/)], [[Source](https://www.npca.org/articles/1178-how-charles-pinckney-changed-my-view-of-national-parks)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-archaeological-sites-of-slave-quarters"></a>**Archaeological Sites of Slave Quarters** (15-30 minutes) - View the archaeological foundations of three slave houses and remains of plantation structures; exhibits along the nature trail describe archaeological excavations of slave quarters and outbuildings; see self-guided tours including artifacts unearthed by archaeologists on the property; along the ½ mile nature trail; grounds open Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM (5:00 PM when visitor center reopens). [[Source](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/charles-pinckney-national-historic-site/)], [[Source](https://www.npca.org/articles/1178-how-charles-pinckney-changed-my-view-of-national-parks)]

- <a id="gem-museum-collection-artifacts"></a>**Museum Collection Artifacts** (15-20 minutes) - View the museum's collection of over 173,000 objects including slave-made pottery and 18th and 19th-century tableware from plantation life; visitor center; Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (closed until Spring 2025). [[Source](https://www.npca.org/articles/1178-how-charles-pinckney-changed-my-view-of-national-parks)]

- <a id="gem-constitution-week-naturalization-ceremony"></a>**Constitution Week Naturalization Ceremony** (1-2 hours, September only) - Attend the annual naturalization ceremony hosted by the park every September during Constitution Week; a unique opportunity to witness new citizens being sworn in at a historic site connected to the Constitution's creation; visitor center area; specific date in September varies annually. [[Source](https://www.npca.org/articles/1178-how-charles-pinckney-changed-my-view-of-national-parks)]

- <a id="gem-picnicking-on-historic-grounds"></a>**Picnicking on Historic Grounds** (flexible timing) - Bring a picnic and enjoy lunch on the historic grounds surrounded by live oaks and marsh views; picnic areas available on grounds; grounds open Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM (5:00 PM when visitor center reopens). [[Source](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/charles-pinckney-national-historic-site/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-boone-hall-plantation-gardens"></a>**Boone Hall Plantation & Gardens** (2-3 hours, adjacent) - Visit one of America's oldest working plantations dating back to at least the 1680s, located just across Long Point Road from Charles Pinckney NHS; tour includes entrance via the famous Avenue of Oaks, covered wagon tours, "Exploring The Gullah Culture" live presentations, guided tours of the plantation home's first floor, Black History In America Exhibit in nine original slave cabins, outdoor gardens, and stable tours; 1235 Long Point Road, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464; Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM - 5:00 PM; Adults $30, Seniors/Military/AAA $27, Child (6-12) $12, Children 5 and under free. [[Source](https://boonehallplantation.com/)], [[Source](https://www.visit-historic-charleston.com/boone-hall-plantation.html)]

- <a id="nearby-patriots-point-naval-maritime-museum"></a>**Patriots Point Naval & Maritime Museum (USS Yorktown)** (3-4 hours, 15 minutes) - Explore two historic ships including the aircraft carrier USS Yorktown and destroyer USS Laffey, 27 historic aircraft, and the three-acre Vietnam Experience; 40 Patriots Point Road, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464 (near the Ravenel Bridge); daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (ticket office closes at 4:00 PM); Adults (12+) $27, Seniors (62+) $19, Child (6-11) $16, Children under 6 free, Military in uniform free. [[Source](https://www.patriotspoint.org/)], [[Source](https://www.patriotspoint.org/visit/hours-operation)]

- <a id="nearby-fort-sumter-national-monument"></a>**Fort Sumter National Monument** (2-2.5 hours including ferry, 25 minutes to Patriots Point departure) - Take a ferry tour to historic Fort Sumter where the Civil War began; tour includes approximately 30 minutes each way on the ferry plus one hour exploring the fort; departs from Patriots Point (40 Patriots Point Road, Mt. Pleasant, SC 29464); seasonal departure times from Patriots Point: March 1 - November 30 at 10:45 AM & 1:30 PM, December & January-February at 12:30 PM; arrive 30 minutes early; tour prices vary. [[Source](https://www.fortsumtertours.com/)], [[Source](https://www.visit-historic-charleston.com/fort-sumter.html)]

- <a id="nearby-fort-moultrie"></a>**Fort Moultrie** (1-2 hours, 20 minutes) - Explore this historic fort on Sullivan's Island, part of Fort Sumter and Fort Moultrie National Historical Park, with exhibits spanning over 200 years of coastal defense history; 1214 Middle Street, Sullivan's Island, SC 29482; daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Visitor Center 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM), closed New Year's Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas; Adults $3, Seniors (62+) $1, Children 15 and under free. [[Source](https://www.visit-historic-charleston.com/fort-moultrie.html)], [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g54454-d143134-Reviews-Fort_Moultrie-Sullivan_s_Island_South_Carolina.html)]

- <a id="nearby-shem-creek-park-and-boardwalk"></a>**Shem Creek Park and Boardwalk** (30-60 minutes, 10 minutes) - Walk the 2,200-foot boardwalk stretching from Coleman Boulevard to the mouth of the creek at Charleston harbor with floating dock space; popular spot for viewing shrimp boats and waterfront dining; Mount Pleasant, SC; open daily dawn to dusk. [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g54354-Activities-Mount_Pleasant_South_Carolina.html)]

- <a id="nearby-isle-of-palms-beach"></a>**Isle of Palms Beach** (2-4 hours, 20 minutes) - Enjoy a nearby beach destination with swimming, sunbathing, and water activities; Isle of Palms County Park offers amenities including seasonal waterpark facilities; Isle of Palms, SC; beach access open daily; county park hours and fees vary seasonally. [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g54354-Activities-Mount_Pleasant_South_Carolina.html)]
