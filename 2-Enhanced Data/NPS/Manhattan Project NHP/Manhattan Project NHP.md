# Manhattan Project NHP (National Historical Park)

**Note:** Manhattan Project NHP has three geographically separate units in Los Alamos NM, Oak Ridge TN, and Hanford WA. This entry focuses on the Los Alamos unit relevant to NM/TX/OK trip planning.

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Manhattan Project Los Alamos Visitor Center** (475 20th Street, Los Alamos, NM 87544; Friday-Monday 10:00 AM-3:00 PM, closed Tuesday-Thursday; 505-661-6277; Free admission) [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/mapr/planyourvisit/losalamos-visitorcenter.htm), [Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60730-d10380201-Reviews-Manhattan_Project_National_Historical_Park-Los_Alamos_New_Mexico.html)]

**Note:** Stamps available in visitor center; pre-printed slips available outside if center is closed. Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Bathtub Row Walking Tour** (30-45 minutes) - Walk among historic homes built for Los Alamos Ranch School and adapted for Manhattan Project scientists; named because they were the only homes in town with bathtubs during WWII; Bathtub Row, accessible from visitor center; Self-guided tour available during daylight hours; Free [[Source](https://www.visitlosalamos.org/Recreation/Los-Alamos-Gateway-To-Three-National-Parks/Manhattan-Project-National-Historical-Park), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/mapr/planyourvisit/losalamos-things2do.htm)]

- **Bradbury Science Museum** (1-2 hours) - Explore over 60 interactive exhibits tracing the Manhattan Project history and Los Alamos National Laboratory's ongoing research projects; 1350 Central Avenue, Los Alamos, NM 87544; Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday-Monday 1:00 PM-5:00 PM; Free [[Source](https://www.visitlosalamos.org/Recreation/Los-Alamos-Gateway-To-Three-National-Parks/Manhattan-Project-National-Historical-Park), [Source](https://www.lanl.gov/about/history-innovation/mapr/sites)]

- **Los Alamos History Museum** (1 hour) - View exhibits on the Manhattan Project and Los Alamos community, including sculptures of Dr. J. Robert Oppenheimer and Gen. Leslie Groves; 1050 Bathtub Row, Los Alamos, NM 87544; Monday-Friday 9:00 AM-4:00 PM, Saturday-Sunday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM; Free [[Source](https://www.visitlosalamos.org/Recreation/Los-Alamos-Gateway-To-Three-National-Parks/Manhattan-Project-National-Historical-Park)]

- **Fuller Lodge** (30 minutes) - Visit this 1928 building originally built as dining hall for Los Alamos Ranch School, later used during Manhattan Project; now houses art center and visitor information; 2132 Central Avenue, Los Alamos, NM 87544; Gallery hours vary; Free [[Source](https://www.lanl.gov/about/history-innovation/mapr/sites)]

- **Guided Walking Tours** (1.5-2 hours) - Join ranger-led tours exploring Manhattan Project sites; some locations only accessible on guided tours; Depart from visitor center; Seasonal schedule, reservations recommended; Free [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/mapr/planyourvisit/losalamos-guided-tours.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/mapr/planyourvisit/losalamos-tours.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Guest Cottage** (15 minutes) - View Gen. Leslie Groves' favorite Los Alamos lodging where he stayed during Manhattan Project visits; Part of Bathtub Row area; Exterior viewing during daylight hours [[Source](https://www.lanl.gov/about/history-innovation/mapr/sites)]

- **Ice House Memorial** (15 minutes) - See memorial containing original stone from the building where scientists assembled nuclear components of the Trinity gadget; Memorial site in Los Alamos; Accessible during daylight hours [[Source](https://www.lanl.gov/about/history-innovation/mapr/sites)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (1-2 hours) - Complete activities throughout park sites to become a Manhattan Project Junior Ranger; Pick up book at visitor center; Complete during park hours [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/mapr/planyourvisit/losalamos-visitorcenter.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Bandelier National Monument** (Half-day, 12 miles south) - Explore ancestral Puebloan cliff dwellings and canyon trails; 15 Entrance Road, Los Alamos, NM 87544; Daily, hours vary seasonally [[Source](https://www.visitlosalamos.org/Recreation/Los-Alamos-Gateway-To-Three-National-Parks/Manhattan-Project-National-Historical-Park)]

- **Valles Caldera National Preserve** (Half-day to full-day, 16 miles west) - Hike trails and view wildlife in massive volcanic caldera; 39201 Highway 4, Jemez Springs, NM 87025; Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [[Source](https://www.visitlosalamos.org/Recreation/Los-Alamos-Gateway-To-Three-National-Parks/Manhattan-Project-National-Historical-Park)]

- **Santa Fe** (2-4 hours, 35 miles south) - Explore art galleries, museums, historic plaza, and Southwestern cuisine in New Mexico's capital; Downtown Santa Fe; Various hours [[Source](https://www.visitlosalamos.org)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 3-4 hours for key Los Alamos sites including visitor center, Bradbury Science Museum, Los Alamos History Museum, and Bathtub Row walking tour. Full-day (6-8 hours) recommended to include guided tours and all museum exhibits.
