# Gauley River NRA (National Recreation Area)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Canyon Rim Visitor Center** (162 Visitor Center Rd., Lansing, WV 25862 (37.9270, -81.1525); Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; 304-574-2115) - Primary visitor contact station serving New River Gorge National Park & Preserve, Bluestone National Scenic River, and Gauley River National Recreation Area; NPS passport cancellation stamps available; located at the New River Gorge Bridge, just north of Fayetteville. [Source: [Canyon Rim Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/neri/planyourvisit/crvc.htm), [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/258783), [NPS GARI](https://www.nps.gov/gari/)]

**Note:** Call 304-574-2115 or email neri_interpretation@nps.gov to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. Administrative address: P.O. Box 246, Glen Jean, WV 25846-0246.

## Key Activities

- **Upper Gauley River Whitewater Rafting** (full day, 6-8 hours) - Experience 11 miles of intense, technical Class V rapids including legendary Pillow Rock, Iron Ring, and Lost Paddle; consistently ranked top five whitewater rivers in world; drops 668 feet over 24 miles; recommended for highly experienced rafters; available during Gauley Season (early September - mid-October) when Summersville Dam releases; multiple outfitters available; prices vary by outfitter [Source: [NPS Whitewater](https://www.nps.gov/gari/planyourvisit/whitewater.htm), [Adventures on the Gorge](https://www.adventuresonthegorge.com/whitewater-rafting/upper-gauley), [WV Tourism](https://wvtourism.com/plan-a-whitewater-rafting-adventure-this-gauley-season-in-west-virginia/)]

- **Lower Gauley River Rafting** (full day, 6-8 hours) - Raft slightly less difficult but still thrilling section with over 50 Class III-V rapids feeling like a watery roller coaster; 13 miles of continuous action; suitable for intermediate to advanced rafters; available during Gauley Season dam releases September-October; multiple outfitters; prices vary [Source: [NPS Whitewater](https://www.nps.gov/gari/planyourvisit/whitewater.htm), [Gauley River Adventures](https://www.gauley.com/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g59234-d3330369-Reviews-New_Gauley_River_Adventures-Lansing_West_Virginia.html)]

- **Marathon Gauley Trip** (full day, 10-12 hours) - Raft all 26 miles of Upper and Lower Gauley in one epic day; combines most intense rapids with non-stop whitewater action; for experienced rafters only; available during Gauley Season; outfitter required; prices vary [Source: [ACE Raft](https://aceraft.com/new-river-gorge/gauley-river-national-recreation-area/), [Gauley River Rapids](https://www.gauleyriverrapids.com/)]

- **Fishing** (2-4 hours) - Fish for trout, smallmouth bass, walleye, and muskellunge in Gauley River and tributaries; prime fishing in calmer sections and tailwaters below Summersville Dam; WV fishing license required; various access points along river; year-round; FREE with valid license [Source: [ACE Raft](https://aceraft.com/new-river-gorge/gauley-river-national-recreation-area/), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/gari/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Patterson Trail Hike** (2-3 hours) - Hike popular moderate trail offering scenic overlooks with panoramic views of Gauley River and Summersville Lake; beautiful vistas reward the effort; various trailheads; open dawn to dusk; FREE [Source: [Tourist Secrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/gauley-river-national-recreation-area-west-virginias-whitewater/), [AllTrails GARI](https://www.alltrails.com/lists/gauley-river-national-recreation-area)]

- **Rock Climbing** (half day to full day) - Experience fast-growing sport in area with various climbing routes along gorge walls; multiple difficulty levels; proper equipment required; various access points; dawn to dusk; FREE [Source: [ACE Raft](https://aceraft.com/new-river-gorge/gauley-river-national-recreation-area/), [NPS GARI](https://www.nps.gov/gari/index.htm)]

- **Gauley Tailwaters Camping** (overnight) - Primitive camping at NPS Gauley Tailwaters campground; experience riverside camping near world-class whitewater; basic facilities; register at information board; P.O. Box 246, Glen Jean, WV 25846; year-round; FREE [Source: [NPS Camping](https://www.nps.gov/gari/planyourvisit/camping.htm), [ACE Raft](https://aceraft.com/new-river-gorge/gauley-river-national-recreation-area/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Two-Day Gauley Overnight Trip** (2 days/1 night) - Combine best rapids of Upper and Lower Gauley with night under stars; riverside camping between sections; includes meals and camping gear; ultimate Gauley experience; available during Gauley Season; outfitter required; prices vary [Source: [WV Tourism](https://wvtourism.com/plan-a-whitewater-rafting-adventure-this-gauley-season-in-west-virginia/), [Gauley Adventures](https://www.gauley.com/)]

- **Carnifex Ferry Battlefield Trails** (1-2 hours) - Hike trails at nearby Carnifex Ferry Battlefield State Park leading to Gauley River overlooks and, for adventurous hikers, all the way down to river; commemorates 1861 Civil War battle; 20 minutes from northern rim; dawn to dusk; state park fees may apply [Source: [Tourist Secrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/gauley-river-national-recreation-area-west-virginias-whitewater/), [Ferry Cabins](https://www.ferrycabins.com/en/1725136/what-to-do)]

- **Off-Season River Exploration** (varies) - Explore calmer Gauley River during non-release periods (late October - August) when river flows naturally; fishing, kayaking for experts, wildlife viewing opportunities; peaceful alternative to Gauley Season crowds; various access points; FREE [Source: [NPS GARI](https://www.nps.gov/gari/index.htm), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/gari/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Wildlife Viewing and Photography** (1-3 hours) - Observe diverse wildlife including black bears, white-tailed deer, wild turkeys, and numerous bird species along river corridors and forested areas; bring binoculars and camera; best at dawn/dusk; year-round; FREE [Source: [WV Tourism](https://wvtourism.com/places-to-go/parks-public-lands/national-parks/gauley-river-national-recreation-area/), [ACE Raft](https://aceraft.com/new-river-gorge/gauley-river-national-recreation-area/)]

## Also Nearby

- **New River Gorge Bridge and Bridge Walk** (1-2 hours, 15 miles) - Walk beneath the 3,030-foot single arch bridge spanning 876 feet above New River on guided catwalk tour; iconic landmark completed 1977; breathtaking views; Bridge Walk reservations required; 162 Visitor Center Rd., Lansing, WV 25862; tours run daily seasonally; admission charged [Source: [NPS Nearby Attractions](https://www.nps.gov/gari/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm), [ACE Raft Guide](https://aceraft.com/new-river-gorge/)]

- **Summersville Lake** (2-4 hours, 10 miles north) - Visit West Virginia's largest lake above Gauley River NRA; swimming, boating, fishing, scuba diving in crystal-clear waters; marina and boat rentals available; Summersville Lake, WV; hours vary; fees for some activities [Source: [ACE Raft](https://aceraft.com/new-river-gorge/gauley-river-national-recreation-area/), [NPS Nearby Attractions](https://www.nps.gov/gari/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm)]

- **Fayetteville - "America's Coolest Small Town"** (varies, 12 miles) - Explore hub town for outdoor recreation featuring local restaurants, breweries, outdoor outfitters, shops; base for Gauley and New River adventures; Fayetteville, WV 25840; business hours vary [Source: [ACE Raft](https://aceraft.com/new-river-gorge/gauley-river-national-recreation-area/), [NPS Nearby](https://www.nps.gov/gari/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm)]

- **New River Gorge National Park & Preserve** (half day to full day, adjacent) - Explore 70,000+ acre park featuring New River (one of oldest rivers in world), endless rock climbing, hiking trails, scenic overlooks, and whitewater rafting on New River; Canyon Rim Visitor Center serves as gateway; Glen Jean, WV; park open 24/7; FREE [Source: [NPS NERI](https://www.nps.gov/neri/), [ACE Raft Guide](https://aceraft.com/new-river-gorge/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time at Gauley River NRA (National Recreation Area):** Full day minimum (6-8 hours) for single whitewater rafting trip; 2-3 days ideal for multiple river sections, hiking, camping, and exploring nearby attractions. **Peak Season: Gauley Season (early September - mid-October)** when Summersville Dam releases create legendary whitewater; off-season offers peaceful fishing and hiking.
