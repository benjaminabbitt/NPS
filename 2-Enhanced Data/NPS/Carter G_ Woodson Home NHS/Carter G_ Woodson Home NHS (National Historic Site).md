## Key Activities

**When the site reopens:**

- **Guided Tours of Historic Home** (30-45 minutes) - Ranger-led tours of Carter G. Woodson's three-story Victorian rowhouse where he lived and worked for nearly three decades; tours will be offered every hour (except noon) on Thursdays, Fridays, and Saturdays, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; 1538 9th Street NW, Washington, DC 20001 [38.9115, -77.0253]; (202) 690-5185 [Source: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/cawo/planyourvisit/guided-tours.htm), [Emancipation DC](https://emancipation.dc.gov/event/tour-carter-g-woodson-home-national-historic-site)]

- **Introductory Gallery** (10-15 minutes) - Interpretive gallery providing context before touring the historic home; same address and hours as above [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g28970-d12456556-Reviews-Carter_G_Woodson_Home_National_Historic_Site-Washington_DC_District_of_Columbia.html)]

- **Legacy Gallery** (15-20 minutes) - Final gallery exploring Woodson's lasting impact as the "Father of Black History" and founder of Black History Month; same address and hours as above [Source: [National Park Getaway](https://www.nps.gov/articles/getaway-cawo.htm)]

- **Shaw Neighborhood Walking Tours** (1-2 hours) - NPS-led walking tours of Dr. Woodson's historic Shaw community neighborhood; offered periodically; reservations and schedule info at (202) 426-5961 or woodsonnhs@nps.gov [Source: [Emancipation DC](https://emancipation.dc.gov/event/tour-carter-g-woodson-home-national-historic-site)]

- **Educational Programs** (varies) - Interpretive and educational programs throughout the year when site is open; schedule varies seasonally [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/cawo/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Bookstore** (15-20 minutes) - Browse books related to African American history and Carter G. Woodson's work when site reopens; 1538 9th Street NW, Washington, DC 20001 [38.9115, -77.0253]; same hours as site [Source: [NPS Bookstore](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/carter-g-woodson-home-nhs-bookstore.htm)]

- **Woodson's Office** (included in tour) - See the workspace where Woodson published the Journal of Negro History and Negro History Bulletin and managed the Association for the Study of African American Life and History; part of guided tour [Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carter_G._Woodson_Home_National_Historic_Site)]

## Also Nearby

- **Howard Theatre** (1-2 hours, 0.5 miles) - Historic theater restored in 2007 where Ella Fitzgerald and Marvin Gaye performed; now hosts jazz, rock, and blues acts; 620 T Street NW, Washington, DC 20001 [38.9174, -77.0211]; check website for show times [Source: [Washington.org Shaw](https://washington.org/visit-dc/things-see-and-do-shaw-neighborhood-washington)]

- **Ben's Chili Bowl** (30-60 minutes, 0.3 miles) - Iconic restaurant serving chili dogs since 1958; a Shaw neighborhood landmark; 1213 U Street NW, Washington, DC 20009 [38.9169, -77.0285]; Daily 6 AM - 2 AM [Source: [Washington.org Shaw](https://washington.org/visit-dc/things-see-and-do-shaw-neighborhood-washington)]

- **Thurgood Marshall Center** (30-45 minutes, 0.4 miles) - Built in 1912 as local YMCA, designed by one of nation's first Black architects; where Justice Marshall wrote portions of Brown v. Board opinion; 1816 12th Street NW, Washington, DC 20009 [38.9174, -77.0273]; Hours vary [Source: [Washington.org Shaw](https://washington.org/visit-dc/things-see-and-do-shaw-neighborhood-washington)]

- **DC Alley Museum** (20-30 minutes, 0.6 miles) - Collection of murals by DC artists on garage doors along Blagden Alley; free outdoor gallery; Blagden Alley NW (between 9th & 10th, M & N Streets), Washington, DC 20001 [38.9065, -77.0259]; 24/7 [Source: [Washington.org Shaw](https://washington.org/visit-dc/things-see-and-do-shaw-neighborhood-washington)]

- **Ethiopian Restaurants on U Street** (1-2 hours, 0.3 miles) - Experience authentic Ethiopian cuisine at multiple restaurants along U Street corridor; 13th and 9th Streets NW area, Washington, DC; Hours vary by restaurant [Source: [Washington.org Shaw](https://washington.org/visit-dc/things-see-and-do-shaw-neighborhood-washington)]
