# Bering Land Bridge N PRES (National Preserve)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-bering-land-bridge-heritage-center"></a>**Bering Land Bridge Heritage Center** - Located in the Sitnasuak Building in Nome, featuring exhibits on Inupiaq culture, interactive displays about the preserve's landscapes and wildlife, and a bookstore with ranger programs in summer; 214 Front Street, Nome, AK 99762 (64.5011, -165.4064); Recently opened September 2025, contact Katie Cullen at 907-759-8020 for hours; General preserve information: 907-443-2522 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bela/planyourvisit/bering-land-bridge-visitor-center.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/bela/contacts.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/bela/learn/news/grand-opening.htm)]

**Note:** Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. Bering Land Bridge National Preserve is one of America's most remote national preserves with no roads leading into, within, or surrounding it. The visitor center in Nome is the most accessible location for obtaining stamps.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-serpentine-hot-springs-visit"></a>**Serpentine Hot Springs Visit** (2 hours minimum ground time, full day or overnight recommended) - Spend the day hiking among huge granite tors encircling the hot springs area, then soak in the geothermal hot springs which produce water at temperatures of 140-170°F; accessible by air taxi from Nome or Kotzebue landing on unmaintained gravel airstrip, or by hiking/biking 30 miles from end of Kougarok Road, or snowmobiling 100 miles from Nome; facilities include bathhouse with soaking tub and changing rooms, bunkhouse with bunks and kitchen for overnight stays, and outhouse; staying overnight allows exploration 3-5 miles from the airstrip [[Source](https://home.nps.gov/bela/planyourvisit/shs_travel.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/bela/learn/nature/serpentine-hot-springs.htm), [Source](https://www.alaska.org/detail/serpentine-hot-springs)]

- <a id="activity-granite-tors-hiking"></a>**Granite Tors Hiking** (2-4 hours) - Hike among impressive granite formations surrounding Serpentine Hot Springs, which are the remains of magma trapped underground millions of years ago that became exposed by wind, rain and glacial movement; each tor has its own name given by the native Inupiat; accessible from Serpentine Hot Springs area [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bela/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Source](https://www.alaska.org/detail/serpentine-hot-springs), [Source](http://packrafting.blogspot.com/2017/05/kobuk-valley-national-park-june-2016.html)]

- <a id="activity-wildlife-and-bird-watching"></a>**Wildlife and Bird Watching** (2-4 hours) - View over 112 migratory bird species including plovers, jaegers, and gyrfalcons during spring and early to mid-summer (large flocks move on by end-July); observe reindeer, muskoxen, moose, and occasionally polar bears on land; watch for bowhead and beluga whales and walrus offshore; accessible throughout the preserve [[Source](https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/index.cfm?adfg=viewinglocations.beringbridge), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/bela/planyourvisit/wildlife-viewing.htm), [Source](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/bering-land-bridge-national-preserve)]

- <a id="activity-hiking-and-backpacking"></a>**Hiking and Backpacking** (varies, multi-day trips common) - Explore 2.7 million acres of roadless wilderness with no formal marked trails; hike from the end of Kougarok Road (one of three roads leading from Nome) or arrange drop-off by bush plane; several adventure tour companies offer multi-day hiking and backpacking trips; winter access by snowmobile or dog sled [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bela/planyourvisit/hiking-and-backpacking.htm), [Source](https://www.travelalaska.com/destinations/parks-public-lands/bering-land-bridge-national-preserve)]

- <a id="activity-junior-ranger-programs"></a>**Junior Ranger Programs** (over 1 hour) - Participate in weekly summer programs for toddlers and school-age children at the Bering Land Bridge Heritage Center in Nome; children learn about Arctic and Subarctic survival through team building games, crossword puzzles, and emergency equipment education [[Source](https://knom.org/2023/07/10/youth-in-nome-become-jr-park-rangers-after-jr-ranger-program-with-the-bering-land-bridge-national-preserve/), [Source](https://www.alaska.org/detail/bering-land-bridge-national-preserve-visitor-center)]

- <a id="activity-flightseeing-tours"></a>**Flightseeing Tours** (varies, customizable) - Take scenic flights over the preserve from Nome or Kotzebue; Golden Eagle Outfitters offers tours visiting all five remote Alaska parks including Bering Land Bridge in about 8 hours; flights can include drop-offs at locations like Serpentine Hot Springs or flyovers of geological features; fully customizable based on interests [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bela/planyourvisit/flying.htm), [Source](https://www.alaska.org/detail/golden-eagle-outfitters-flightseeing-air-taxi), [Source](https://www.valerievalise.com/how-to-visit-bering-land-bridge-national-preserve/)]

- <a id="activity-fishing"></a>**Fishing** (2-4 hours) - Fish in the preserve's waters for Arctic grayling, salmon, and other species; popular activity combined with camping at the bunkhouse near Serpentine Hot Springs [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bela/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Source](https://www.travelalaska.com/destinations/parks-public-lands/bering-land-bridge-national-preserve)]

- <a id="activity-ranger-guided-hikes"></a>**Ranger-Guided Hikes** (varies) - Join occasional guided hikes led by National Park Rangers during summer months; check with the Heritage Center in Nome for current schedule [[Source](https://www.travelalaska.com/destinations/parks-public-lands/bering-land-bridge-national-preserve)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-espenberg-maar-lakes-exploration"></a>**Espenberg Maar Lakes Exploration** (multi-day expedition) - Visit the four largest and northernmost maar lakes in the world, formed by volcanic explosions 17,500 to 200,000 years ago; Devil Mountain Maar Lake is nearly 5 miles long, 3.7 miles wide, and 328 feet deep; accessible by float plane (lake landings possible), non-motorized vehicle, on foot, or winter snowmachine; most visitors see these only from the air due to extreme remoteness; activities include scenic viewing, photography, and hiking; some expeditions like hiking to Devil Mountain Lakes may take as long as two weeks [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bela/planyourvisit/maar-lakes.htm), [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bering_Land_Bridge_National_Preserve), [Source](https://www.usgs.gov/geology-and-ecology-of-national-parks/geology-bering-land-bridge-national-preserve)]

- <a id="gem-lost-jim-lava-flow"></a>**Lost Jim Lava Flow** (2-4 hours if accessible) - Explore one of Alaska's youngest lava flows at only 1,000 to 2,000 years old, produced from around 75 vents with the largest being Lost Jim Cone at about 75 feet high; accessible by bush plane or extended backpacking trip [[Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bering_Land_Bridge_National_Preserve), [Source](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/bering-land-bridge-national-preserve)]

- <a id="gem-inupiat-village-cultural-visits"></a>**Inupiat Village Cultural Visits** (2-4 hours) - Visit nearby Inupiat Eskimo villages to learn about Native subsistence practices, reindeer herding, and the relationship between Alaska's indigenous peoples and wildlife; opportunities to experience authentic cultural traditions in one of America's most remote regions [[Source](https://www.npca.org/parks/bering-land-bridge-national-preserve), [Source](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/bering-land-bridge-national-preserve)]

- <a id="gem-winter-activities"></a>**Winter Activities** (varies) - Access the preserve during winter via ski-planes, dog sled, or snowmobile for a truly unique Arctic wilderness experience; the hot springs remain accessible and provide a dramatic contrast in the frozen landscape [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bela/planyourvisit/index.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/bela/planyourvisit/hiking-and-backpacking.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-pilgrim-hot-springs"></a>**Pilgrim Hot Springs** (2-3 hours) - Soak in a subarctic oasis full of tundra trees and bubbling hot springs located about 60 miles northeast of Nome; Mile 53 Kougarok Highway (approximately 53 miles from Nome, then 7 miles on gravel road), Nome, AK 99762; open mid-June to September (weather permitting); obtain no-fee recreational use permit before visiting from Nome Visitors Center, Aurora Inn, or Bering Straits Native Corporation offices; requires four-wheel drive vehicle and rough road conditions; no cell service; contact: 907-443-4277 or pilgrim@kawerak.org [[Source](https://www.pilgrimhotsprings.com/), [Source](https://www.alaska.org/detail/pilgrim-hot-springs), [Source](https://www.visitnomealaska.com/businesses/pilgrim-hot-springs)]

- <a id="nearby-carrie-m-mclain-memorial-museum"></a>**Carrie M. McLain Memorial Museum** (1-2 hours) - Explore exhibits on Nome's history, Bering Strait cultures, gold rush heritage, and Alaska Native artwork with interactive displays and immersive environments; 100 W 7th Avenue inside the Richard Foster Building, Nome, AK 99762; Monday-Thursday 12pm-7pm, Friday-Saturday 12pm-6pm, Sunday closed; admission: Nome residents free with card, non-residents $4 adults/$3 youth and elders, free for museum members and youth under 5, free every Friday; 907-443-6630 [[Source](https://www.nomealaska.org/department/index.php?structureid=12), [Source](https://www.alaska.org/detail/carrie-m.-mclain-memorial-museum)]

- <a id="nearby-iditarod-trail-sled-dog-race-finish-line"></a>**Iditarod Trail Sled Dog Race Finish Line** (varies, race viewing 1-2 hours, event atmosphere all week) - Stand by the historic burled arch on Front Street as mushers complete the 1,049-mile race from Anchorage; watch teams come along the Bering Sea down Front Street; race occurs every March with mushers arriving in Nome approximately 8-15 days after start; peak finish viewing typically March 16-23; Nome's population grows by approximately 1,000 during the event creating a "Mardi Gras of the North" atmosphere; Front Street, Nome, AK; book accommodations a year in advance [[Source](https://www.visitnomealaska.com/iditarod), [Source](https://www.travelalaska.com/explore-alaska/articles/all-about-iditarod), [Source](https://www.alaska.org/detail/the-iditarod-how-to-experience-alaskas-biggest-winter-event)]

- <a id="nearby-nome-road-system-wildlife-viewing"></a>**Nome Road System Wildlife Viewing** (half day to full day) - Explore three roads leading from Nome (Nome-Teller Highway, Council Road, and Kougarok Road) where abundant wildflowers and tundra plants blanket the landscape; observe resident herds of wild muskox near the cemetery or airport, plus moose, bears, foxes, and owls; roads provide access to 14 rivers for fishing Arctic grayling, salmon, and pike; accessible by vehicle from Nome [[Source](https://www.thecrazytourist.com/15-best-things-to-do-in-nome-ak/), [Source](https://www.ottsworld.com/blogs/things-to-do-in-nome-alaska/), [Source](https://www.alaska.org/destination/nome)]

- <a id="nearby-anvil-city-square-and-gold-pan-monument"></a>**Anvil City Square and Gold Pan Monument** (30 minutes) - Visit a green space featuring the world's largest gold pan measuring 21 feet in diameter, dedicated to Nome's gold rush history; view statues including "the three Swedes" who discovered gold in Nome; Front Street area, Nome, AK; accessible year-round [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g60732-Activities-Nome_Alaska.html), [Source](https://www.travelalaska.com/explore-alaska/articles/7-things-do-nome)]

## Review / Personal Notes

---

**Total Recommended Time:** For visitors focusing on the Heritage Center in Nome only: 1-2 hours. For those visiting Serpentine Hot Springs with hiking: full day (6-8 hours) or overnight stay recommended to fully experience the area. Multi-day backpacking expeditions to remote features like the Espenberg Maar Lakes require 2-14 days depending on destination and activities. Most visitors combine a Heritage Center visit in Nome with either a day trip to Serpentine Hot Springs or a flightseeing tour.
