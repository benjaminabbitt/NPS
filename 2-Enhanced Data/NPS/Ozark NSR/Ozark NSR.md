## Key Activities {#key-activities}

- <a id="activity-float-current"></a>**Float Trip on Current River** (6-8 hours) - One of the most popular activities at Ozark NSR is floating the crystal-clear, spring-fed Current River by canoe, kayak, or tube. Popular sections include Akers Ferry to Pulltite Spring (10 miles) and Cedar Grove to Akers Ferry (8 miles). Float trips generally average about 6 hours but can take up to 8 hours if you stop frequently to explore; you'll average about 2 miles per hour plus any stops. Multiple outfitters provide rentals and shuttle services. The water stays cold year-round (57-60°F). Various put-in and take-out locations along Current River (coordinates vary by access point); Memorial Day-Labor Day peak season, spring and fall also popular; contact local outfitters. [Source: [NPS Float Times](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/planyourvisit/floattimes.htm), [Missouri Scenic Rivers](https://missouriscenicrivers.com/jacks-fork-float-schedule), [417 Magazine](https://www.417mag.com/outdoors/waterways/best-missouri-float-trips/jacks-fork-river-current-river-missouri/)]

- <a id="activity-float-jacks"></a>**Float Trip on Jacks Fork River** (6-8 hours) - Float the scenic Jacks Fork, a major tributary of the Current River. Popular routes include Alley Spring to Eminence (6 miles) and Alley Spring to Two Rivers (15 miles). The river features beautiful bluffs, clear water, and excellent swimming holes. Same timing as Current River floats: generally 6 hours, up to 8 hours with frequent stops. Various put-in and take-out locations along Jacks Fork River (coordinates vary by access point); Memorial Day-Labor Day peak season; contact local outfitters. [Source: [Missouri Scenic Rivers](https://missouriscenicrivers.com/jacks-fork-float-schedule), [Jacks Fork Canoe](https://www.jacksforkcanoe.com/), [Windy's Floats](https://windysfloats.com/jacks-fork-river-floats/)]

- <a id="activity-big-spring"></a>**Big Spring** (1-2 hours) - Visit the largest spring in Missouri and one of the largest in the world, with an average daily discharge of 276 million gallons. A short, easy paved trail leads from the parking area to viewing areas of the spring. The site includes a historic lodge building (now dining lodge), picnic areas, and river access. Incredibly scenic and accessible for all abilities. Highway 103, 3 miles south of Van Buren, MO (36.9625, -90.9830); Open 24/7, Entrance Station staffed Memorial Day-Labor Day 8:30 AM-5:00 PM daily; 573-323-4236. [Source: [NPS Big Spring](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/planyourvisit/big-spring.htm), [Wikipedia Big Spring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Spring_(Missouri)), [See The Ozarks](https://seetheozarks.com/van-buren/big-spring/)]

- <a id="activity-alley-spring"></a>**Alley Spring and Mill** (1-2 hours) - Visit the historic three-story red roller mill built in 1894, now a museum, alongside Missouri's 7th largest spring (81 million gallons per day). Walk the grounds, tour the mill building (when open), photograph the iconic mill, and explore the spring area. Short trails lead around the mill and spring area. 12185 Alley Hollow Road, Eminence, MO 65466 (37.1582, -91.4476); Dawn to dusk daily, mill building with exhibits open spring-fall; 573-226-3945. [Source: [Visit MO](https://www.visitmo.com/things-to-do/alley-mill-national-park-service), [MDC Alley Spring](https://mdc.mo.gov/discover-nature/places/natural-areas/alley-spring), [NPS Alley Spring](https://www.nps.gov/places/alley-spring-and-mill.htm)]

- <a id="activity-blue-spring"></a>**Blue Spring Trail** (1 hour) - Hike the short, easy trail (under 1 mile round trip, completely flat) to Blue Spring, Missouri's sixth largest and deepest spring at 310 feet. The water is so blue and clear it's been called "the most beautiful thing in all of Missouri." Swimming is strictly prohibited to preserve the spring's pristine beauty. The water stays a consistent 57°F year-round and discharges an average of 90 million gallons daily. From Eminence, take Highway 106 east approximately 14-16 miles, turn right (south) on County Road 535, follow 3 miles to parking area (coordinates not published to protect the site); Open 24/7; no phone. [Source: [NPS Blue Spring](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/blue-spring.htm), [For the Love of Wanderlust](https://fortheloveofwanderlust.com/blue-spring-gem-ozarks/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/missouri/ozark-national-scenic-riverway)]

- <a id="activity-round-spring"></a>**Round Spring** (1 hour) - Visit this scenic spring site on the Current River. Round Spring was a Missouri State Park from 1932-1964 before becoming part of Ozark NSR. The spring area features a campground and river access. Note: Round Spring Cave tours are currently suspended for the 2025 season; when operating, tours run daily at 10 AM, 12 PM, and 2 PM (60-90 minutes, limited to 15 participants). 13209 Round Spring Campground Rd, 13 miles north of Eminence on Highway 19 (37.2838, -91.4067); Open 24/7, Ranger Station hours vary; 573-858-3297. [Source: [NPS Round Spring Cave](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/planyourvisit/round-spring-and-round-spring-cave.htm), [Round Spring Cave Tour](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/round-spring-guided-cave-tour.htm), [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/234045)]

- <a id="activity-cave-spring"></a>**Cave Spring Loop Trail** (2-2.5 hours) - Hike this moderately difficult 4.6-mile loop trail that begins at Devils Well, traverses oak-pine forest ridges and limestone glades, winds through hollows, crosses intermittent streams, and brings hikers down to Cave Spring on the Current River. The 'Cliff' segment features spectacular views from a high limestone bluff overlooking the Current River. Some steep sections with short challenging scrambles. Trailhead at Devils Well parking area, 15 miles south of Eminence on Highway 19, then follow signs east on KK (coordinates vary along loop); Open 24/7; no phone. [Source: [NPS Cave Spring Trail](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/cave-spring-trail.htm), [AllTrails Cave Spring Loop](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/missouri/cave-spring-loop-trail), [Hiking Project](https://www.hikingproject.com/trail/7031431/cave-spring-trail)]

- <a id="activity-swimming"></a>**Swimming and Picnicking** (2-4 hours) - Enjoy swimming in the clear, cold spring-fed waters at designated areas throughout the park. Popular swimming spots include near Big Spring, Alley Spring, Round Spring, and various river access points. Rocky Falls (see Also Nearby section) is particularly popular. Bring a swimsuit and towel; water temperatures stay cold (57-60°F) year-round. Various locations throughout the park; Open 24/7 during appropriate weather; no phone. [Source: [Visit MO Things to Do](https://www.visitmo.com/things-to-do/ozark-national-scenic-riverways), [NPS Ozark](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/)]

## Hidden Gems {#hidden-gems}

- <a id="gem-klepzig"></a>**Klepzig Mill** (1-2 hours) - Explore this hidden historic site featuring an early 20th century turbine sawmill built by Walter Klepzig in 1928 along Rocky Creek. The site includes mill ruins, a cement spillway, and various old foundations in a scenic, off-the-beaten-path location. Accessible via a short hike from the end of pavement on County Road NN, or as a 3-mile hike from Rocky Falls along the Ozark Trail (6 miles total out-and-back). No facilities, easy to miss in summer vegetation. From Eminence or Winona, take Highway H east to Highway NN, turn left on County Road 522, Klepzig Mill less than 1 mile on right (approximate coordinates 37.15, -91.32); Open 24/7; no phone. [Source: [NPS Klepzig Mill](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/learn/historyculture/klepzig-mill.htm), [Midwest Hiker](https://midwesthiker.com/2019/11/29/klepzig-mill/), [Only In Your State](https://www.onlyinyourstate.com/nature/usa/klepzig-mill-trail-mo)]

- <a id="gem-devils-well"></a>**Devils Well** (30 minutes - 1 hour) - Visit this remarkable sinkhole cave containing Missouri's largest underground lake. From the parking area, a short, well-marked trail leads to a spiral staircase descending into the mouth of the sinkhole, where you can peer into a water-filled cavern 100 feet below. The underground lake's surface area is larger than a football field. A 4.6-mile loop trail connects Devils Well with Cave Spring and the Current River. From Eminence, travel south on Highway 19 about 15 miles, follow signs on KK east of Akers to gravel access road leading to Devils Well (approximate coordinates 37.20, -91.35); Open during daylight hours; no phone. [Source: [NPS Devils Well](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/planyourvisit/devils-well.htm), [Tourist Secrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/missouri/mysteries-of-missouris-devils-well/), [AllTrails Devils Well](https://www.alltrails.com/poi/us/missouri/salem/devil-s-well)]

- <a id="gem-jam-up"></a>**Jam-Up Cave** (accessible during float trips) - Accessible only by river, Jam-Up Cave is located approximately 2 miles downriver on the Current River (near river mile 89). This cave offers an adventurous stop during float trips for those who want to explore beyond the main attractions. No land-based access; must be reached via canoe, kayak, or other watercraft. Current River, approximately 2 miles downriver from reference point (river mile 89, coordinates vary); Accessible during river floating hours; no phone. [Source: [Wikipedia Ozark NSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozark_National_Scenic_Riverways), [Eminence Cottages](https://www.eminencecottagescamp.com/ThingsToDo/ozark-national-scenic-riverways.html)]

- <a id="gem-powder-mill"></a>**Powder Mill - Ozark Trail Crossing** (1-2 hours) - Visit where the Ozark Trail crosses the Current River on Highway 106 at Powder Mill. Access the trail to Blue Spring from either Powder Mill or the Blue Spring picnic area trailhead. This lesser-known crossing point provides access to scenic hiking along the Ozark Trail and connections to Blue Spring. Highway 106 at Powder Mill crossing (approximate coordinates 37.18, -91.20); Open 24/7; no phone. [Source: [NPS Caves and Karst](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/learn/nature/caves.htm), [Peak Visor](https://peakvisor.com/park/ozark-national-scenic-riverways.html)]

## Also Nearby {#also-nearby}

- <a id="nearby-rocky-falls"></a>**Rocky Falls Shut-Ins** (1-2 hours, 20 miles from Van Buren) - Visit this stunning 40-foot waterfall cascading over vibrant pink rhyolite rock into a clear swimming hole. The falls are formed where volcanic rock has "shut in" Rocky Creek, creating natural water park conditions. Popular for swimming in spring and summer; best waterfall views after rain. Easily accessible with parking lot close to falls, includes bathrooms and well-maintained trails. Visit in spring/fall before summer crowds. From Van Buren, take Highway 60 west 20 miles to Winona, then north on Highway 19 1 mile, right on H Highway several miles to Highway NN. County Rd NN-526, Winona, MO 65588 (approximate coordinates 37.10, -91.35); Open year-round 24/7; no phone. [Source: [NPS Rocky Falls](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/learn/nature/rocky-falls.htm), [See The Ozarks](https://seetheozarks.com/eminence/rocky-falls/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g20160185-d273991-Reviews-Rocky_Falls_Shut_ins-Winona_Township_Missouri.html)]

- <a id="nearby-peck-ranch"></a>**Peck Ranch Conservation Area - Elk Viewing** (2-3 hours, 13 miles from Van Buren) - Experience one of Missouri's most unique wildlife viewing opportunities at this 23,048-acre conservation area where approximately 140 adult elk and 40-50 calves roam. Take the self-guided driving tour beginning at the office (marked along the way) for the best chances of spotting elk during sunrise or sunset hours. About 70% of Missouri's elk herd is typically found here. Roads require adequate ground clearance; some may be impassable during high water. Twin Pines Conservation Education Center in Winona (573-325-1381) provides maps and information. From Van Buren, take Highway 60 west 13 miles to Highway P, then north 3 miles to end of blacktop. Near Winona in Shannon County, entrances off Route 19 and Route 60 (approximate coordinates 37.08, -91.28); Open sunrise to sunset daily except during managed hunts and when roads closed for weather; no entrance fee. [Source: [MDC Peck Ranch](https://mdc.mo.gov/discover-nature/places/peck-ranch-conservation-area), [Visit MO Elk Tours](https://www.visitmo.com/articles/elk-tours-an-outdoor-experience-in-southeast-missouri), [See The Ozarks](https://seetheozarks.com/van-buren/peck-ranch-conservation-area/), [KSMU Radio](https://www.ksmu.org/news/2019-09-23/to-hear-the-otheworldly-bugle-of-an-elk-take-a-fall-drive-through-peck-ranch)]

## Review / Personal Notes

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 2-3 days for a comprehensive visit. This includes one full day for a float trip on the Current River or Jacks Fork (6-8 hours), plus 1-2 days to visit the major springs (Big Spring, Alley Spring, Blue Spring, Round Spring - totaling 4-6 hours), hike Cave Spring Loop Trail (2-2.5 hours), explore hidden gems like Devils Well and Klepzig Mill (2-3 hours), and allow time for swimming and picnicking (2-4 hours). If adding Rocky Falls and Peck Ranch elk viewing, extend to 3-4 days. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/planyourvisit/index.htm), [Roadtrippers](https://roadtrippers.com/magazine/ozark-national-scenic-riverways-nps-crush/), timing estimates compiled from activity sources cited above]
