## Key Activities

- **Float the Current River or Jacks Fork** (4-8 hours for day trips, multi-day options available) - Canoe, kayak, or tube two of the finest spring-fed floating rivers in America through crystal-clear, cold water; popular day float from Alley Springs to Eminence on Jacks Fork is approximately 7 miles (4 hours); authorized outfitters provide equipment rentals and shuttle services; float season best May-September; multiple access points throughout the park; contact outfitters for current river conditions and rental rates [Source: [Floating](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/planyourvisit/floating.htm), [Estimated Float Times](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/planyourvisit/floattimes.htm), [Things to Do](https://www.visitmo.com/things-to-do/ozark-national-scenic-riverways), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozark_National_Scenic_Riverways)]

- **Visit Big Spring** (30-45 minutes) - Marvel at one of the largest freshwater springs in North America producing an astounding 280-286 million gallons of water per day from the largest single spring outlet in the Ozarks; short walking trails provide views of the brilliant blue spring and access to the Current River; Big Spring Historic District listed on National Register of Historic Places; 404 Watercress Drive, Van Buren, MO 63965 (located 3 miles south of Van Buren on MO Route 103); accessible during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [Big Spring](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/planyourvisit/big-spring.htm), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozark_National_Scenic_Riverways), [Tourist Secrets Big Spring](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/missouri/big-spring-natures-hidden-gem-in-the-ozarks/), [6 Must See Spots](https://midwestnomads.com/2021/08/20/6-must-see-spots-in-missouris-ozark-scenic-riverways/)]

- **Explore Alley Spring and Historic Mill** (45 minutes - 1 hour) - Photograph one of the most iconic scenes in the Ozarks at the stunning red 1894 three-story grist mill set against turquoise spring waters producing 81 million gallons per day; tour the restored mill that ground corn and wheat for local farming community; visit nearby one-room schoolhouse; considered the historical and cultural gem of the park; 12185 Alley Hollow Road, Eminence, MO 65466 (37.1489, -91.4622); grounds accessible during daylight hours, mill tour hours vary; FREE [Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozark_National_Scenic_Riverways), [Tourist Secrets Alley Spring](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/missouri/mystery-of-missouris-alley-spring/), [Roadtrippers](https://roadtrippers.com/magazine/ozark-national-scenic-riverways-nps-crush/), [Top 10 Ozark NSR](https://fortheloveofwanderlust.com/top-10-ozark-national-scenic-riverways/)]

- **Round Spring and Cave Tour** (1-1.5 hours including cave tour) - Visit Round Spring producing 26 million gallons daily about 15 miles north of Eminence off Highway 19; take limited-access ranger-led cave tours (summer only, twice daily, maximum 15 people) of Round Spring Cave discovered during Civil War and formerly a commercial tourist cave; 13209 Round Spring Campground Road, Eminence, MO 65466; spring accessible during daylight hours, cave tours summer only; cave tour requires reservations; 573-858-3297 [Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozark_National_Scenic_Riverways), [Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/planyourvisit/visitor-centers.htm), [6 Must See Spots](https://midwestnomads.com/2021/08/20/6-must-see-spots-in-missouris-ozark-scenic-riverways/)]

- **Hike to Rocky Falls** (2-3 hours including swimming) - Trek to this stunning 40-foot waterfall cascading over vibrant rhyolite rock into a clear, inviting swimming hole perfect for water play; accessible by car near Eminence then short walk; can also hike 3 miles from Klepzig Mill through scenic mill mountain area; located off Highway NN east of Eminence; accessible during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [6 Must See Spots](https://midwestnomads.com/2021/08/20/6-must-see-spots-in-missouris-ozark-scenic-riverways/), [Nearby Attractions](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm), [Area Attractions](https://www.thelandingcurrentriver.com/attractions.php)]

- **Powder Mill Trail Hike** (2-3 hours for 5-mile round trip) - Follow the Ozark Trail for 3-5 miles beginning at Powder Mill Trailhead near Powder Mill campground; option for 5-mile round trip from Powder Mill Trailhead to Blue Spring Trailhead and back; moderate difficulty; accessible during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [Fall Hiking Series](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/learn/news/fall-hiking-series-to-begin-september-23-at-ozark-national-scenic-riverways.htm), [Hiking in the Ozarks](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/planyourvisit/hiking-in-the-ozarks.htm)]

- **Fishing** (2-4 hours or all day) - Fish the Current River and Jacks Fork for smallmouth bass, goggle-eye, and other species in crystal-clear spring-fed waters; bank fishing or wading available at numerous access points; Missouri fishing license required; accessible year-round; license required [Source: [Things to Do](https://www.visitmo.com/things-to-do/ozark-national-scenic-riverways), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozark_National_Scenic_Riverways)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Devil's Well** (30-45 minutes) - Discover one of the wildest hidden gems in the park - a massive underground lake sitting inside a collapsed cave system with water over 100 feet deep that eventually resurfaces at Cave Spring before flowing into the Current River; viewing platform provides safe overlook into the depths; location requires local inquiry for directions; accessible during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [Roaming Nelsons Wonders](https://roamingnelsons.com/ozark-nsr/), [Top 10 Ozark NSR](https://fortheloveofwanderlust.com/top-10-ozark-national-scenic-riverways/)]

- **Blue Spring** (30-45 minutes) - View this incredibly deep spring so profound that the Statue of Liberty would be fully submerged if placed at the bottom; averages 90 million gallons of water discharge daily; accessed via trail from Powder Mill area; accessible during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [Roaming Nelsons Wonders](https://roamingnelsons.com/ozark-nsr/)]

- **Stegall Mountain Hike** (2-3 hours for 5-mile round trip) - Hike 2.5 miles on the Ozark Trail from Rocky Falls to the summit of Stegall Mountain for panoramic Ozarks views, or complete the 5-mile round trip; moderate to strenuous difficulty; accessible during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [Fall Hiking Events](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/learn/news/fall-hiking-events-begin-september-17-at-ozark-national-scenic-riverways.htm)]

- **Klepzig Mill Excursion** (1.5-2 hours) - Enjoy 3 miles of beautiful, relatively flat hiking through scenic mill mountain area on this lesser-known trail; can combine with Rocky Falls visit; accessible during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [Fall Hiking Series](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/learn/news/fall-hiking-series-to-begin-september-23-at-ozark-national-scenic-riverways.htm)]

- **Peck Ranch Wildlife Area** (1-2 hours) - Visit this area home to Missouri's wild elk herd restoration as well as white-tailed deer, wild turkeys, songbirds, and many other native species; excellent wildlife photography opportunities especially at dawn and dusk; accessible during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [Nearby Attractions](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm), [Area Attractions](https://www.thelandingcurrentriver.com/attractions.php)]

- **Fall Ranger-Led Hiking Series** (2-4 hours, seasonal) - Join National Park rangers for guided hikes exploring trails throughout the park; most hikes on Saturdays beginning at 10:00 AM; September through November; check park website for current schedule; FREE [Source: [Fall Hiking Series 2024](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/learn/news/2024-09-27-fall-hiking-series.htm), [National Parks Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/2023/09/fall-hiking-series-coming-ozark-national-scenic-riverways)]

## Also Nearby

- **Echo Bluff State Park with Creekside Grill** (1-2 hours, adjacent to Ozark NSR near Eminence) - Missouri's newest state park offering additional hiking, lodging, and dining at the Creekside Grill restaurant with excellent food; seamlessly connected to Ozark National Scenic Riverways; near Eminence, MO; hours vary; restaurant charges apply [Source: [Restaurants](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/planyourvisit/restaurants.htm), [6 Must See Spots](https://midwestnomads.com/2021/08/20/6-must-see-spots-in-missouris-ozark-scenic-riverways/)]

- **Eminence, Missouri Restaurants and Shops** (1-2 hours, gateway town) - Visit the primary gateway town to Ozark NSR featuring J&B's Family Restaurant, Dairy Shack, and Dos Rios for great food choices, plus outfitters and local shops; Eminence, MO 65466; hours vary by establishment [Source: [Restaurants](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/planyourvisit/restaurants.htm), [Visit Eminence](https://visiteminence.com/scenic-riverways/)]

- **Van Buren, Missouri** (30 minutes - 1 hour, 3 miles from Big Spring) - Small town with additional dining, lodging, and services; convenient base for exploring Big Spring and Current River; Van Buren, MO 63965; hours vary [Source: [Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

- **The Landing - Blue Heron Restaurant** (1-2 hours for meal) - Enjoy meals with sweeping views of the Current River; also offers lodging and float trip services; located along Current River; hours vary seasonally; 573-226-3568 [Source: [The Landing](https://www.thelandingcurrentriver.com/), [Restaurants](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/planyourvisit/restaurants.htm)]

**Total Recommended Time at Ozark NSR (National Scenic Riverways):** Minimum 4-6 hours for single-day visit including one spring, short float, and hiking; 2-3 days recommended for comprehensive experience with multiple springs, full-day float, hiking, and cave tour; multi-day camping float trips available from overnight to 5 nights.
