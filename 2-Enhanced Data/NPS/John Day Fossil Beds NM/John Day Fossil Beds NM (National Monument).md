# John Day Fossil Beds NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

John Day Fossil Beds National Monument has four passport stamp locations across its three geographically separated units:

- **Thomas Condon Paleontology Center** (32651 Highway 19, Kimberly, OR 97848 [44.548611, -119.634444]; Open daily 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM, may vary seasonally; 541-987-2333) - Located in the Sheep Rock Unit, this serves as the main visitor center and research facility with a fossil gallery, paleontology lab, and museum exhibits. [Source: [NPS Thomas Condon Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/joda/planyourvisit/tcpv.htm)]

- **Cant Ranch Historic District** (Sheep Rock Unit, accessed via Highway 19 near Kimberly, OR [44.548611, -119.634444]; Open Monday-Thursday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM during summer months, closed fall/winter/spring or by appointment; 541-987-2333) - Historic ranch house and museum with walking tours offered seasonally, subject to staffing availability. [Source: [NPS Cant Ranch](https://www.nps.gov/joda/learn/historyculture/cant-ranch.htm), [National Park Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/2025/10/day-park-john-day-fossil-beds-national-monument)]

- **Painted Hills Unit** (Bear Creek Rd, Mitchell, OR 97750 [44.661522, -120.273073]; Trails and overlooks open 24/7, ranger station hours vary; 541-987-2333) - Inside the ranger station is a small bookstore where you can purchase books and souvenirs as well as stamp your passport. Located 9 miles northwest of Mitchell. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/joda/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Clarno Unit** (Oregon Route 218, 18 miles west of Fossil, OR [44.9135, -120.4102]; Trails and overlooks open 24/7; 541-987-2333) - The least visited unit with picnic area and trail access. Located 18 miles west of Fossil along Oregon Route 218. [Source: [NPS Clarno Unit](https://www.nps.gov/joda/planyourvisit/clarno.htm)]

**Note:** Call 541-987-2333 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. The outdoor areas, trails, and overlooks in all three units are open at all times, but visitor center hours may vary seasonally.

## Key Activities

- **Blue Basin Overlook Trail** (3 hours) - Challenging 3-mile loop trail that circles all around the basin, climbing quite high above the blue-green badlands with spectacular John Day River Valley views from the top; Located at Sheep Rock Unit off Highway 19; Open daylight hours year-round. The trail ascends significantly and offers panoramic vistas of the geological formations. [Source: [American Southwest](https://www.americansouthwest.net/oregon/john-day-fossil-beds/blue-basin-trail.html), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/oregon/island-in-time-john-day-fossil-beds-sheep-rock-unit)]

- **Island in Time Trail** (30-60 minutes) - Easy, family-friendly 1.2-mile out-and-back trail following the canyon floor through blue-green badlands with interpretive signs and fossil replicas, ascending 200 feet in elevation; Located at Blue Basin area, Sheep Rock Unit; Open daylight hours year-round. This is the most accessible trail for families and provides an excellent introduction to the area's paleontology. [Source: [American Southwest](https://www.americansouthwest.net/oregon/john-day-fossil-beds/blue-basin-trail.html), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/oregon/island-in-time-john-day-fossil-beds-sheep-rock-unit)]

- **Thomas Condon Paleontology Center Museum** (45-90 minutes) - Museum and research facility featuring fossil gallery covering over 40 million years, working paleontology lab with large bay windows to watch scientists preparing fossils, 20-minute park film "Layers of Life: Stories of Ancient Oregon," and interactive exhibits; 32651 Highway 19, Kimberly, OR; Open daily 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM (may vary seasonally); Free admission. [Source: [NPS Thomas Condon Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/joda/planyourvisit/tcpv.htm), [NPS Top Tips](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/top-tips-for-visiting-john-day-fossil-beds.htm)]

- **Painted Cove Trail** (15-30 minutes) - Popular 0.25-mile loop trail showcasing the most vibrant striped hills with red, gold, and black bands of ancient volcanic ash and mudstone; Located at Painted Hills Unit off Bear Creek Road; Open 24/7; Wheelchair accessible. One of the most photographed spots in Oregon. [Source: [The Outbound](https://www.theoutbound.com/oregon/chillin/walk-the-painted-cove-loop-trail), [Krista's Compass](https://kristascompass.com/best-things-to-do-in-painted-hills-oregon/)]

- **Painted Hills Overlook Trail** (15-20 minutes) - Easy 0.6-mile out-and-back trail offering elevated panoramic views of the colorful hills; Located at Painted Hills Unit; Open 24/7. Best lighting for photography is late afternoon or early morning. [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/oregon/painted-hills-overlook), [Oregon Essential](https://oregonessential.com/painted-hills-oregon/)]

- **Carroll Rim Trail** (45-60 minutes) - The longest trail at Painted Hills, this 1.6-mile roundtrip hike climbs to a rim viewpoint overlooking the painted hills and surrounding valleys; Located at Painted Hills Unit; Open 24/7. Offers the best panoramic perspective of the entire Painted Hills area. [Source: [Oregon Hikers](https://www.oregonhikers.org/field_guide/Painted_Hills-Carroll_Rim_Hike), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/oregon/carroll-rim-trail)]

- **Leaf Hill Trail** (10-20 minutes) - Short 0.25 to 0.5-mile trail showcasing fossil leaf beds and colorful clay formations; Located at Painted Hills Unit; Open 24/7. Named for the abundant fossilized leaves found in the area. [Source: [National Park Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/2023/05/trails-ive-hiked-painted-hills-unit-trails), [We Dream of Travel](https://www.wedreamoftravel.com/painted-hills-oregon/)]

- **Trail of Fossils** (15-30 minutes) - Short 0.2 to 0.25-mile loop where interpretive signs point out actual plant fossils from 44 million years ago embedded in the rock formations; Located at Clarno Unit off Oregon Route 218; Open 24/7. One of the few places visitors can see fossils in situ without entering a museum. [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/oregon/geologic-time-trail-and-trail-of-fossils), [NPS Clarno Unit Trails](https://www.nps.gov/joda/planyourvisit/clarno-unit-trails.htm)]

- **Geologic Time Trail** (20-30 minutes) - Half-mile trail exploring the Clarno Palisades with interpretive signs explaining the area's 44-million-year-old geology, leading to a natural arch; Located at Clarno Unit; Open 24/7. Often combined with Trail of Fossils for a comprehensive Clarno Unit experience. [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/oregon/geologic-time-trail-and-trail-of-fossils), [Wander Filled Life](https://wanderfilledlife.com/john-day-fossil-beds/)]

- **Ranger-Led Programs** (30-90 minutes, seasonal) - Free programs historically including indoor and outdoor talks, Blue Basin hikes, Cant Ranch walking tours, and astronomy programs at Painted Hills Unit; Various locations across all three units; Schedule varies by season, most do not require reservations; Call 541-987-2333 for current schedule. Programs subject to staffing availability. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/joda/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Junior Ranger and Junior Paleontologist Programs** (1-2 hours) - Self-guided activity books available at the Thomas Condon Visitor Center front desk for visitors of all ages to complete during their visit and earn a badge; Thomas Condon Paleontology Center; Available during visitor center hours; Free. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/joda/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Picture Gorge Scenic Drive** (15-30 minutes) - Drive through the scenic canyon on Highway 19 with Native American pictographs visible on cliff walls, located at the south end of the Sheep Rock Unit; Highway 19 between Dayville and Kimberly; Accessible 24/7; Pull-out viewing area at east entrance. The pictographs are centuries old and represent at least six panels of rock art. [Source: [NPS Picture Gorge](https://www.nps.gov/joda/learn/nature/picture-gorge.htm), [His and Hers Photo](https://hisandhersphoto.com/Heritage/hhrockartm_005.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Foree Area Trails** (30-45 minutes total for both trails) - Two brief trails highlighting blue-green claystones colored by weathering celadonite mineral: Story in Stone Trail (0.3-mile loop, 10-15 minutes) through dramatic pillars of Turtle Cove formation, and Flood of Fire Trail (0.4-mile roundtrip, 15-20 minutes) climbing to a viewpoint overlooking colorful rock formations; Located in Sheep Rock Unit between Thomas Condon Center and Blue Basin; Open daylight hours year-round. These trails are often overlooked by visitors rushing to Blue Basin. [Source: [Wander Filled Life](https://wanderfilledlife.com/john-day-fossil-beds/), [National Park Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/park/subpage/hiking-john-day-fossil-beds-national-monument)]

- **Mascall Overlook** (10-15 minutes) - Quick roadside stop with sweeping views of John Day Valley, Picture Gorge, Strawberry Mountain Range, and the Mascall and Rattlesnake formations from 150 feet elevation; Located just off Highway 26 heading east toward John Day from Sheep Rock Unit; Open 24/7; Picnic table and pit toilet available. Only a couple hundred feet from parking area, this viewpoint offers dramatic vistas often missed by those focused only on the main units. [Source: [NPS Sheep Rock Unit](https://www.nps.gov/joda/planyourvisit/sr-unit.htm), [National Park Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/2020/04/3-days-john-day-fossil-beds-national-monument)]

- **Clarno Palisades Natural Arch** (30-45 minutes) - Hike along the palisades to discover a curious natural arch formation in the rock roof overhead via the Geologic Time Trail; Located at Clarno Unit; Open 24/7. The arch is a hidden feature not prominently advertised but rewarding for those who complete the trail. [Source: [Wander Filled Life](https://wanderfilledlife.com/john-day-fossil-beds/)]

- **Cant Ranch Historic Buildings** (30-60 minutes) - While the ranch house museum has limited hours, visitors can walk the property year-round to see historic outbuildings and learn how this family thrived in the arid climate of eastern Oregon in the early 1900s; Located in Sheep Rock Unit off Highway 19; Grounds open 24/7, building tours Monday-Thursday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM summer months only; Free. [Source: [Hot Flash Packer](https://thehotflashpacker.com/john-day-fossil-beds-national-monument/), [NPS Cant Ranch](https://www.nps.gov/joda/learn/historyculture/cant-ranch.htm)]

- **Picture Gorge Pictographs** (15-30 minutes) - Six panels of centuries-old Native American rock art on canyon walls at the south end of Sheep Rock Unit, viewable from Highway 19 pull-out at east entrance; Highway 19 through Picture Gorge; Accessible 24/7; No parking in gorge, use designated pull-out. Art is of undetermined origin but at least centuries old, with some panels well-preserved despite historical vandalism. [Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Day_Fossil_Beds_National_Monument), [His and Hers Photo](https://hisandhersphoto.com/Heritage/hhrockartm_005.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Wheeler High School Fossil Beds** (1-3 hours, 18 miles west) - Oregon's only legally accessible public fossil dig site where visitors can keep fossils they find, primarily 33-million-year-old fossilized leaves and branches of deciduous trees; 600 E B St, Fossil, OR 97830; Open daylight hours year-round; Fees as of 2023: individuals $5, family of four $15, groups of 20 or less $25 (donation box); Tools helpful but not required. Located 18 miles from Clarno Unit. For group reservations call Wheeler High School at 541-763-0907. [Source: [Oregon Paleo Lands Center](https://www.oregonpaleolandscenter.com/wheeler-high-school-fossil-beds), [Oregon Discovery](https://oregondiscovery.com/wheeler-high-school-fossil-beds), [That Oregon Life](https://thatoregonlife.com/2025/11/wheeler-high-fossil-beds)]

- **Kam Wah Chung State Heritage Site & Museum** (1-2 hours, 40 miles east) - National Historic Landmark Chinese medical clinic, general store, community center and residence from 1865 providing an incomparable glimpse into Chinese immigrant history in eastern Oregon; 125 NW Canton Street, John Day, OR 97845; Tours offered Thursday-Monday May to October, advance booking recommended, call 541-575-2800; Day-use fee $5 per vehicle. Located in downtown John Day, approximately 40 miles from Sheep Rock Unit. [Source: [Oregon State Parks](https://stateparks.oregon.gov/index.cfm?do=park.profile&parkId=5), [Travel Oregon](https://traveloregon.com/things-to-do/culture-history/historic-sites-oregon-trail/kam-wah-chung-state-heritage-site-museum/), [Atlas Obscura](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/kam-wah-chung-co-museum)]

- **Priest Hole Recreation Site - John Day River** (30 minutes swimming/relaxing, 12 miles from Painted Hills) - Local favorite swimming hole on the John Day Wild and Scenic River with crystal-clear water perfect for swimming and fishing; Located 12 miles from Mitchell via Burnt Ranch Road; Open 24/7; Free camping with 14 drive-in campsites, fire rings, picnic tables, two toilets; No potable water or garbage service. The Painted Hills are 6 miles away, making this an ideal relaxation spot after hiking. AWD vehicle recommended for gravel road access. Watch for rattlesnakes. [Source: [BLM](https://www.blm.gov/visit/priest-hole-recreation-site), [Bend Bulletin](https://www.bendbulletin.com/explore/swim-in-the-high-desert-this-summer/article_ef4b1c6e-4ad6-11ef-b9fa-7f8485efabb8.html), [Oregon Natural Desert Association](https://onda.org/swim-in-the-desert-this-summer/)]

- **Sumpter Valley Dredge State Heritage Area** (1-2 hours, 90 miles southeast) - Historic gold dredge from 1935, the last of three dredges that extracted $10-12 million in gold from Sumpter Valley between 1913-1954, featuring 72 one-ton buckets and interpretive exhibits about eastern Oregon's gold mining history; Sumpter, OR (25 miles from Austin Junction via State Route 7, then 4 miles on Route 410); Open seasonally, call ahead for hours; Day-use fee. Oregon Parks acquired and opened this National Register site to the public in 1994. [Source: [Oregon State Parks](https://stateparks.oregon.gov/index.cfm?do=park.profile&parkId=174), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumpter_Valley_Gold_Dredge), [Blue Mountain Eagle](https://bluemountaineagle.com/2019/08/01/journey-sumpter-valley-dredge-a-monument-to-area-mining/)]

**Total Recommended Time:** 6-8 hours for a single unit visit (Sheep Rock or Painted Hills), 2-3 days to thoroughly explore all three units. Most visitors spend a few hours at one or two units.
