# Badlands NP (National Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Ben Reifel Visitor Center** (25216 SD-240, Interior, SD 57750; Summer: 7:00 AM - 7:00 PM, Spring/Fall: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Winter: 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM daily; 605-433-5361) [Source: [NPS Ben Reifel Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/visit-ben-reifel.htm), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/badl/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- **White River Visitor Center** (South Unit on SD Highway 27, approximately 20 miles south of Scenic, SD; Open seasonally May through October; 605-433-5361 or 605-455-2878) [Source: [NPS White River Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/white-river-vc.htm), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/badl/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

**Note:** Call 605-433-5361 to confirm current stamp locations and seasonal hours before visiting. Self-serve passport stamping station available at the information desk at Ben Reifel Visitor Center.

## Key Activities

- **Drive the Badlands Loop Road (Highway 240)** (1-3 hours depending on stops) - Experience the 39-mile scenic byway featuring nearly 30 overlooks and trailheads including Big Badlands Overlook, Pinnacles Overlook (best for sunset), Yellow Mounds, and Roberts Prairie Dog Town, with opportunities to spot bison, bighorn sheep, pronghorn, and prairie dogs; road accessible from Interstate 90 at exits 110 or 131; open 24 hours daily year-round (subject to winter weather closures); free with park admission; 605-433-5361 [Source: [NPS Badlands Loop Road](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/badl-loop-road.htm), [Black Hills Vacations](https://www.blackhillsvacations.com/badlands-loop-road/), [AllTrips](https://www.allblackhills.com/badlands_national_park/badlands_loop_scenic_byway.php)]

- **Hike the Door Trail and Window Trail** (Door Trail: 30-60 minutes, Window Trail: 15-20 minutes) - Walk the 0.75-mile Door Trail to "The Door," a natural break in the Badlands Wall offering panoramic views of eroded buttes and spires, followed by the easy 0.25-mile Window Trail to a natural window framing spectacular badlands formations; Door Trailhead located along Badlands Loop Road, mile marker 12; trails open dawn to dusk daily; free with park admission; 605-433-5361 [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hiking-badl.htm), [Door Trail Hikespeak](https://www.hikespeak.com/trails/door-trail-badlands/), [AllTrails Door Trail](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/south-dakota/the-door-trail)]

- **Explore Ben Reifel Visitor Center and Fossil Preparation Lab** (45-60 minutes) - Browse museum exhibits, watch the 20-minute orientation film "Land of Stone and Light," and observe park paleontologists actively preparing fossils discovered in the park through viewing windows into the working Fossil Preparation Lab; 25216 SD-240, Interior, SD 57750; Summer: 7:00 AM - 7:00 PM, Spring/Fall: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Winter: 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM daily; Fossil Lab open June-September 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM; free with park admission; 605-433-5361 [Source: [NPS Ben Reifel Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/visit-ben-reifel.htm), [NPS Fossil Preparation Lab](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/badl-fossil-prep-lab.htm), [Badlands Info](https://www.badlandsinfo.com/things-to-do-in-the-park/visitor-centers/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Drive Sage Creek Rim Road for Bison Viewing** (1-2 hours) - Explore this scenic gravel road through the Sage Creek Wilderness Area offering panoramic views, frequent bison sightings in their natural habitat, prairie dog colonies, and minimal traffic away from the main loop; western end of Badlands Loop Road near Pinnacles Overlook entrance; open year-round (avoid during thunderstorms; may close in winter); free with park admission; 605-433-5361 [Source: [NPS Sage Creek Rim Road](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/sage-creek-rim-road.htm), [MustDoThings](https://mustdothings.com/things-to-do/badlands-national-park-drive-sage-creek-rim-road/), [The Outbound](https://www.theoutbound.com/south-dakota/photography/drive-the-sage-creek-rim-road)]

- **Become a Badlands Junior Ranger** (2 hours for booklet, or 30-45 minutes for ranger-led program) - Complete age-appropriate activities in a Junior Ranger booklet while exploring the park and watch the park film or attend a ranger-led program (summer only), then share answers with a ranger to earn an official Junior Ranger badge; also available: Night Explorer patch, Junior Paleontologist badge, and Historic Preservation badge; pick up booklets at Ben Reifel Visitor Center, 25216 SD-240, Interior, SD 57750; visitor center hours vary by season; free; 605-433-5361 [Source: [NPS Junior Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/junior-ranger-booklet-badl.htm), [NPS Ranger Programs](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/badl-ranger-programs.htm), [Peachy Keenes](https://peachykeenes.com/badlands-junior-ranger/)]

- **Hike the Medicine Root Loop Trail** (1-1.5 hours for 4 miles) - Trek this moderate rolling trail through mixed-grass prairie with distant views of the Badlands formations, connecting with Castle Trail and often combined with Saddle Pass Trail for varied loop options; trailhead accessible from Castle Trail or Saddle Pass Trailhead along Badlands Loop Road; open dawn to dusk daily; free with park admission; watch for cactus and bring water (limited shade); 605-433-5361 [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hiking-badl.htm), [AllTrails Medicine Root Loop](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/south-dakota/medicine-root-and-castle-trail-loop), [Simply Awesome Trips](https://www.simplyawesometrips.com/loop-hike-badlands-national-park/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Wall Drug** (45-60 minutes, 8 miles north) - Visit this iconic roadside attraction featuring free ice water, 5¢ coffee, Western art, animatronic displays, shopping, dining, and a sprawling collection of quirky Americana across multiple storefronts; 510 Main Street, Wall, SD 57790 (8 miles/15 minutes north of Badlands); open daily 7:00 AM - 9:00 PM (some stores and café may close earlier); free admission; 605-279-2175 [Source: [Wall Drug](https://www.walldrug.com/about-us/city-of-wall/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g54848-d145078-Reviews-Wall_Drug-Wall_South_Dakota.html), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wall_Drug)]

- **Minuteman Missile National Historic Site** (30 minutes for tour, plus visitor center time, 4 miles north) - Take a guided 30-minute underground tour of the Delta-01 Launch Control Facility (reservations required, limited to 6 people) and visit the Delta-09 Missile Silo to learn about Cold War history and America's nuclear deterrent program; Visitor Center at Exit 131 off I-90, Delta-09 at Exit 116 (6 miles from Wall); Visitor Center open year-round Tuesday-Saturday 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM; advanced tour reservations required at 866-601-5129; free admission, $10 tour fee; 605-433-5552 [Source: [NPS Minuteman Missile](https://www.nps.gov/mimi/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/mimi/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g54756-d293554-Reviews-Minuteman_Missile_National_Historic_Site-Philip_South_Dakota.html)]

- **Custer State Park Wildlife Loop Road** (1.5-2 hours for 18-mile scenic drive, 70 miles/1 hour 15 minutes southwest) - Drive this renowned 18-mile scenic byway renowned for abundant wildlife including bison herds, elk, pronghorn, deer, bighorn sheep, and burros roaming freely through diverse landscapes of forests, grasslands, and granite outcrops; access from multiple entrances off US Highway 16A in Custer State Park; open year-round 24 hours; $20 per vehicle entrance fee (7-day pass); 605-255-4515 [Source: [Travel South Dakota](https://www.travelsouthdakota.com/trip-ideas/wildlife-loop-state-scenic-byway), [Custer Resorts](https://www.custerresorts.com/activities/scenic-drives/wildlife-loop), [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/drive/between/58728/59699/custer-to-badlands-national-park-drive)]

**Total Recommended Time at Badlands NP (National Park):** 2-3 hours for a quick visit (Loop Road drive with minimal stops); 4-6 hours for a half-day visit (Loop Road with several hikes and Visitor Center); 1-2 days to explore thoroughly including backcountry areas, multiple trails, and ranger programs.
