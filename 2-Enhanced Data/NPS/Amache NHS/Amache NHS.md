# Amache NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Amache Museum** (205 E. Goff Ave., Granada, CO 81041 (38.0624519, -102.3087324); Winter hours: Monday-Friday 8:00 AM to 3:30 PM, closed weekends and major holidays; 719-936-7600)

**Note:** Passport stamps are available at the Amache Museum in Granada, operated by the Amache Preservation Society. Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/amch/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Self-Guided Auto Tour** (1.5 hours) - Drive through the historic site following gravel roads with interpretive signs at key locations including the reconstructed barrack, mess hall, guard tower, and water tower placed on their original locations, plus building foundations and the camp cemetery; CO-Rd 23 5/10, Granada, CO 81041 (2 miles west of Granada); open sunrise to sunset daily; 719-936-7600 [Source: [Amache.org Visitor Information](https://amache.org/visitor-information/), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g33448-d2205413-Reviews-Camp_Amache-Granada_Colorado.html), [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/amch/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

- **Walking Trail** (30-60 minutes) - Short walking trail with picnic tables maintained by the Amache Preservation Society near the main entrance kiosk, featuring the reconstructed Honor Roll Memorial listing WWII military service members from the camp; CO-Rd 23 5/10, Granada, CO 81041; open sunrise to sunset daily; 719-936-7600 [Source: [Amache.org Visitor Information](https://amache.org/visitor-information/), [Beyond My Door](https://www.beyondmydoor.com/amache-national-historic-site/)]

- **Amache Museum Tour** (30 minutes) - View exhibits and culturally significant collections about the Granada Relocation Center, including artifacts recovered from the camp site and educational materials; 205 E. Goff Ave., Granada, CO 81041; Winter hours: Monday-Friday 8:00 AM to 3:30 PM, closed weekends and major holidays; contact amache@usa.com or john.hopper@granadaschools.org [Source: [Amache.org Visitor Information](https://amache.org/visitor-information/), [TripAdvisor Museum Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g33448-d6673966-Reviews-Amache_Museum-Granada_Colorado.html), [Uncover Colorado](https://www.uncovercolorado.com/activities/amache-museum/)]

- **Cemetery Visit** (15-30 minutes) - Pay respects at one of only three surviving Japanese American incarceration camp cemeteries, featuring names of 148 persons including 31 Nisei soldiers killed in action and 117 who died at Amache; located within the historic site on CO-Rd 23 5/10; open sunrise to sunset daily; 719-936-7600 [Source: [Beyond My Door](https://www.beyondmydoor.com/amache-national-historic-site/), [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/amch/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Educational Presentation** (1 hour) - Attend an hour-long presentation by the Amache Preservation Society covering the detailed history of the camp and its impact, available by appointment during the school year; 205 E. Goff Ave., Granada, CO 81041; contact amache@usa.com or john.hopper@granadaschools.org to schedule [Source: [Amache.org Visitor Information](https://amache.org/visitor-information/), [The Great High Prairie](https://thegreathighprairie.com/camp-amache-national-historic-site/)]

- **Self-Guided Audio Tour** (1.5 hours) - Download the audio driving tour from the Amache Preservation Society website for enhanced interpretation as you explore the site, providing deeper context about what each location was used for during the incarceration period; available at amache.org; CO-Rd 23 5/10, Granada, CO 81041; open sunrise to sunset daily [Source: [Amache.org Driving Tour](https://amache.org/driving-tour-map-podcasts/), [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/amch/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

## Also Nearby

Rd. 24, Hasty, CO 81044; park open daily 5:00 AM to 10:00 PM, office Monday-Friday 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM; 719-829-1801 [Source: [Colorado Parks and Wildlife](https://cpw.state.co.us/state-parks/john-martin-reservoir-state-park), [Colorado.com](https://www.colorado.com/state-parks/john-martin-reservoir-state-park)]
