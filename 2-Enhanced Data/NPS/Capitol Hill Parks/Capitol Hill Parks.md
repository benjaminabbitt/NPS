# Capitol Hill Parks

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-frederick-douglass-national-historic-site-visitor-center"></a>**Frederick Douglass National Historic Site Visitor Center** (1411 W St SE, Washington, DC 20020 (38.8639, -76.9854); April 1-October 31: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, November 1-March 31: 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM daily) [Source: [NPS Frederick Douglass Hours](https://www.nps.gov/frdo/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Frederick Douglass Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/frederick-douglass-nhs-visitor-center.htm/index.htm)]

- <a id="stamp-kenilworth-aquatic-gardens-visitor-center"></a>**Kenilworth Aquatic Gardens Visitor Center** (1550 Anacostia Ave NE, Washington, DC 20019 (38.9133, -76.9436); April 1-October 31: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, November 1-March 31: 8:00 AM - 3:30 PM, closed January 1, Thanksgiving, and December 25) [Source: [NPS Kenilworth Hours](https://www.nps.gov/keaq/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Friends of Kenilworth](https://kenaqgardens.org/explore/directions-hours/)]

- <a id="stamp-mary-mcleod-bethune-council-house-nhs"></a>**Mary McLeod Bethune Council House NHS** (1318 Vermont Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20005 (38.9088, -77.0314); Thursday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, tours at top of each hour) [Source: [NPS Mary McLeod Bethune Hours](https://www.nps.gov/mamc/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

**Note:** Capitol Hill Parks has no central visitor center. Passport stamps available at three separate National Capital Parks - East locations listed above. Capitol Hill Parks encompasses urban green spaces east of U.S. Capitol including Lincoln, Stanton, Marion, and Folger Parks, plus 59 circles, triangles, and squares. [Source: [NPS Capitol Hill Parks](https://www.nps.gov/cahi/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Hill_Parks)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-visit-lincoln-park-monuments"></a>**Visit Lincoln Park Monuments** (30-60 minutes) - View statues of Abraham Lincoln (Emancipation Memorial, unveiled 1876) and Mary McLeod Bethune (first monument honoring a Black woman in DC public park); explore 7-acre park, largest on Capitol Hill, between 11th and 13th Streets NE/SE off East Capitol St NE; open 5:00 AM - midnight; free [Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Hill_Parks), [DC Moms Best Parks](https://dcmoms.com/in-around-dc/best-parks-on-capitol-hill/)]

- <a id="activity-explore-stanton-park"></a>**Explore Stanton Park** (20-30 minutes) - See statue of Revolutionary War General Nathanael Greene; enjoy playground area for young children and green space; excellent location for cherry blossom photos in spring; located off Maryland Avenue NE between 4th and 6th Streets NE near Union Station; open 5:00 AM - midnight; free [Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Hill_Parks), [DC Moms Best Parks](https://dcmoms.com/in-around-dc/best-parks-on-capitol-hill/), [Compass Coffee Spring Guide](https://www.compasscoffee.com/blogs/the-daily-grind/the-ultimate-guide-to-spring-in-dc-cherry-blossoms-dc)]

- <a id="activity-view-ulysses-s-grant-memorial"></a>**View Ulysses S. Grant Memorial** (15-30 minutes) - Experience dramatic bronze sculptures at base of Capitol Hill in front of U.S. Capitol; one of largest equestrian statues in world; part of Capitol Hill Parks jurisdiction [Source: [Historians.org](https://www.historians.org/perspectives-article/washingtons-parks-memorials-and-historic-sites/)]

- <a id="activity-walk-capitol-hill-parks-route"></a>**Walk Capitol Hill Parks Route** (1-2 hours) - Stroll through network of urban parks connecting Lincoln Park and Stanton Park via paved walkways; approximately 15 minutes walking between parks, 1 hour for full route with stops; benches throughout for rest; great for running, biking, picnics [Source: [Awkward Together Capitol Hill Guide](https://www.awkwardtogether.com/capitol-hill-neighborhood-guide/), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/cahi/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-attend-summer-concerts-in-lincoln-park"></a>**Attend Summer Concerts in Lincoln Park** (1-2 hours, seasonal) - Enjoy free outdoor concerts held during summer months in Lincoln Park's open green spaces; bring blanket for picnic-style seating [Source: [DC Moms Best Parks](https://dcmoms.com/in-around-dc/best-parks-on-capitol-hill/)]

- <a id="gem-discover-59-circles-and-triangles"></a>**Discover 59 Circles and Triangles** (varies) - Explore smaller green spaces including Seward Square, Twining Square, Maryland Avenue Triangles, and Pennsylvania Avenue Medians designed by Pierre L'Enfant; scattered throughout Capitol Hill neighborhood offering quiet urban respites [Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Hill_Parks), [NPPlan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/washington-d-c-national-parks/national-capital-parks-east-park-at-a-glance/capitol-hill-parks/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-u-s-capitol-building"></a>**U.S. Capitol Building** (5 minutes walking) - Tour historic seat of U.S. Congress with advance reservations; explore Capitol Visitor Center; free admission; immediately adjacent to Capitol Hill Parks [Source: [Solo Excursion Capitol Hill](https://soloexcursion.com/washington/things-to-do-in-capitol-hill/)]

- <a id="nearby-eastern-market"></a>**Eastern Market** (10 minutes walking) - Visit historic public market operating since 1873 with fresh food vendors, flea market on weekends, and local crafts; located at Eastern Market Metro station within Capitol Hill Parks boundary [Source: [Solo Excursion Capitol Hill](https://soloexcursion.com/washington/things-to-do-in-capitol-hill/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Hill_Parks)]

- <a id="nearby-library-of-congress"></a>**Library of Congress** (10 minutes walking) - Explore world's largest library with stunning architecture, free exhibits, and reading rooms; advance registration for research areas; immediately west of Capitol Hill [Source: [Solo Excursion Capitol Hill](https://soloexcursion.com/washington/things-to-do-in-capitol-hill/)]
