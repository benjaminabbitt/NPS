# Nicodemus NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

### Main Site
- <a id="stamp-township-hall-nps-visitor-center"></a>**Township Hall / NPS Visitor Center** (304 Washington Ave., Nicodemus, KS 67625; (39.3903886, -99.6178726); Thursday-Monday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Closed Tuesday-Wednesday; 785-839-4233)

Nicodemus NHS serves as the Midwest Regional Stamp location within the Passport to Your National Parks program. Cancellation stamps are available at the Township Hall visitor center during operating hours. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/nico/planyourvisit/index.htm), [America's National Parks - Passport Cancellation Locations](https://americasnationalparks.org/passport-to-your-national-parks/passport-cancellation-locations/)]

**Note:** Call 785-839-4233 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-township-hall-visitor-center-exhibits"></a>**Township Hall Visitor Center Exhibits** (1-1.5 hours) - View historic exhibits, interpretive displays, and short films about Nicodemus and African Americans in the West; 304 Washington Ave., Nicodemus, KS 67625; Thursday-Monday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Closed Tuesday-Wednesday; 785-839-4233 [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/nico/planyourvisit/index.htm), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/nicodemus-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="activity-self-guided-walking-tour-of-historic-buildings"></a>**Self-Guided Walking Tour of Historic Buildings** (30-60 minutes) - Explore the exteriors of five historic buildings including the Township Hall, St. Francis Hotel, First Baptist Church, African Methodist Episcopal Church, and District Number 1 School; walking tour brochure available at visitor center; self-guided tours available during daylight hours [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/nico/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Western National Parks Association](https://wnpa.org/plan-your-trip/trip-ideas/12-hours-at-nicodemus-national-historic-site)]

- <a id="activity-ranger-guided-tour"></a>**Ranger-Guided Tour** (1-2 hours) - Take a guided tour with knowledgeable park rangers who provide stories and historical context about the town and its buildings; book in advance by calling 785-839-4233; Thursday-Monday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Articles](https://www.nps.gov/articles/nicodemus.htm), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g4185924-d325031-Reviews-Nicodemus_National_Historic_Site-Nicodemus_Kansas.html)]

- <a id="activity-ame-church-interior-visit"></a>**AME Church Interior Visit** (15-30 minutes) - View the interior of the African Methodist Episcopal Church, which is fully accessible to the public with exhibits and artifacts on display; 304 Washington Ave., Nicodemus, KS 67625; Thursday-Monday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [NPS AME Church](https://www.nps.gov/places/ame-church.htm), [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/kansas-historic-grows-acquisition-church-210421513.html)]

- <a id="activity-junior-ranger-program"></a>**Junior Ranger Program** (30-60 minutes) - Complete activities in the Junior Ranger booklet to learn about Nicodemus history and earn a badge; available at visitor center; Thursday-Monday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/nicodemus-national-historic-site/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-nicodemus-historical-society-museum"></a>**Nicodemus Historical Society & Museum** (1-2 hours) - Visit the community-run museum operated by descendants, featuring personal family stories and offering tours to cemeteries, homesteads, and historic school sites in the township; 611 S. 5th St., Nicodemus, KS 67625; Tuesday-Saturday 12:00 PM - 5:00 PM; 785-839-4280 [Source: [Nicodemus Historical Society](https://www.nicodemushistoricalsociety.org/), [Museums Database](https://museumsdatabase.com/museums/view/11775)]

- <a id="gem-emancipation-homecoming-celebration"></a>**Emancipation/Homecoming Celebration** (full day, late July weekend) - Experience the annual celebration when hundreds of descendants return to Nicodemus for speeches, bands, games, sports, and food during the last weekend of July; this multi-day event celebrates freedom and community heritage; dates vary, check NPS website [Source: [NPS Articles](https://www.nps.gov/articles/nicodemus.htm), [Travel Kansas](https://www.travelks.com/places-to-visit/cities/nicodemus/)]

- <a id="gem-roadside-park"></a>**Roadside Park** (30 minutes) - Visit the small park with playground and picnic area located south of Highway 24, offering a quiet spot to reflect on the historic landscape; open daily during daylight hours [Source: [NPS Articles](https://www.nps.gov/articles/nicodemus.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-graham-county-auto-and-art-museum"></a>**Graham County Auto and Art Museum** (1-2 hours, 10 miles) - Explore this 8,000-square-foot museum featuring vintage cars from the 1920s-1960s, including the hearse that carried JFK's body, plus original artwork from local artists; Highway 283 & Highway 24, Hill City, KS; Thursday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM - 4:00 PM; $5 admission [Source: [Travel Kansas](https://www.travelks.com/listing/graham-county-auto-and-art-museum/44782/), [KWCH News](https://www.kwch.com/2021/09/15/hearse-that-carried-body-jfk-featured-hill-city-museum/)]

- <a id="nearby-webster-state-park"></a>**Webster State Park** (1-4 hours, approximately 30 miles east) - Enjoy fishing, boating, camping, and wildlife viewing at this 8,018-acre lake in the rolling chalk hills region; excellent birdwatching with Bald and Golden Eagles during fall/winter; features Kansas's only state park Pickleball Court and new disc golf course; open year-round [Source: [Northwest Kansas Travel Council](https://www.northwestkansas.org/attractions/state-parks), [Kansas State Parks](https://www.travelks.com/things-to-do/parks-and-nature/state-parks/webster/)]

- <a id="nearby-cedar-bluff-state-park-reservoir"></a>**Cedar Bluff State Park & Reservoir** (2-6 hours, approximately 60 miles southwest) - Hike the 5-mile Agave Ridge Trail, view historic Threshing Machine Canyon with names carved into sandstone bluffs from 1867, and enjoy water recreation at this 4,000-acre reservoir; open year-round [Source: [Kansas State Parks](https://www.travelks.com/things-to-do/parks-and-nature/state-parks/cedar-bluff/), [Legends of Kansas](https://legendsofkansas.com/cedar-bluff-reservoir-kansas/)]

- <a id="nearby-solomon-heritage-alliance-information-kiosks"></a>**Solomon Heritage Alliance Information Kiosks** (15-30 minutes per kiosk, various locations) - Visit informational kiosks in 24 small towns including nearby Penokee, Bogue, Morland, and Hill City, each telling the distinctive story of their community along Highway 24; accessible 24/7 [Source: [Osborne County Heritage](http://genealogytrails.com/kan/osborne/county_solomon_heritage_alliance.html), [Rural Kansas Tourism](http://www.getruralkansas.org/Morland/115Explore/828.shtml)]
