# Glacier Bay NP & PRES (National Park and Preserve)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Glacier Bay National Park Visitor Center** (179 Bartlett Cove Road, Glacier Bay Lodge, Gustavus, AK 99826 [58.4537, -135.8825]; Mid-May to mid-September, daily; 907-697-2230) - Located on the second floor of Glacier Bay Lodge in Bartlett Cove, 10 miles from Gustavus. The visitor center includes exhibits, park information, trip planning, bookstore, reading area, hydrophone kiosk, interpretive programs, park films, and brochures. [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [NPS Stop by Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/glba-visitor-center.htm), [Anyplace America](https://www.anyplaceamerica.com/directory/ak/hoonah-angoon-census-area-02105/locales/glacier-bay-lodge-1402591/)]

- **On Cruise Ships** - Park rangers board cruise ships at Bartlett Cove and bring along the national park stamp. There will be a desk set up with national park souvenirs with the passport stamp nearby. Rangers and Alaska Geographic park store staff board cruise ships entering Glacier Bay. [Source: [Cruise Critic](https://boards.cruisecritic.com/topic/1569020-national-park-passport-stamps-glacier-bay-national-park/), [My Perfect Alaska Cruise](https://myperfectalaskacruise.com/alaska-travel/alaska-national-park-passport-stamps/), [Flashpacking America](https://www.flashpackingamerica.com/alaska-travel/glacier-bay-national-park-stamp/)]

**Note:** Call 907-697-2230 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting, especially regarding seasonal hours.

## Key Activities

- **Glacier Bay Day Boat Tour** (8 hours) - The signature experience: an all-day cruise aboard a 150-passenger catamaran traveling 130 miles through the West Arm of Glacier Bay to the faces of Margerie and Grand Pacific glaciers, two towering masses of ice and snow rising 245 feet above the ocean. Tour includes lunch, beverages (coffee, tea, water, lemonade), and narration by a National Park Service ranger. Passengers board at 6:50am, depart at 7:15am, and return around 3:30pm daily. Wildlife viewing opportunities include humpback whales, sea lions (300+ may cluster on South Marble Island), bears, seals, eagles, puffins, cormorants, and sea otters; 179 Bartlett Cove Road, Glacier Bay Lodge, Gustavus, AK 99826; Daily during operating season (mid-May to mid-September); Adults $265.60, Children (3-12) $138.76. [Source: [NPS Tour Boat](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/glacier-bay-tour-boat.htm), [Visit Glacier Bay](https://www.visitglacierbay.com/tours/glacier-bay-day-tour/), [Alaska.org](https://www.alaska.org/destination/glacier-bay-national-park/day-tours)]

- **Whale Watching Tours** (3.5 hours) - Guided boat tours to Point Adolphus and Icy Strait, one of the most famous humpback whale-watching areas in the world. Tours also offer opportunities to see orcas, sea lions, and other marine wildlife. Tours typically depart at 8:30am (returning at noon), 10:00am (returning at 1:30pm), or 12:30pm (returning at 4:00pm); Departs from Gustavus area; Seasonal. [Source: [Glacier Bay Alaska](https://glacierbayalaska.com/experience-alaska/whale-watching/), [Visit Glacier Bay](https://www.visitglacierbay.com/things-to-do/whale-watching/), [TAZ Whale Watching](https://www.tazwhalewatching.com/)]

- **Forest Loop Trail** (0.5-1.5 hours) - Easy 1-mile loop trail through the temperate rainforest and beach environments of Bartlett Cove. Features dirt, gravel, and boardwalk surfaces with benches and viewing platforms in the spruce/hemlock forest. Trail starts at Glacier Bay Lodge and includes pond-studded areas with two viewing decks overlooking ponds; Trailhead at Glacier Bay Lodge, 179 Bartlett Cove Road, Gustavus, AK 99826; Open year-round, daylight hours. [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [NPS Exploring Bartlett Cove](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/exploring-bartlett-cove.htm), [Trail Cooking](https://trailcooking.com/2021/06/29/glacier-bay-national-park-bartlett-cove-forest-trail/)]

- **Bartlett River Trail** (4-5 hours) - Easy to moderate 4-mile round-trip trail following an intertidal lagoon through spruce/hemlock forest to the river estuary. Wildlife includes coyotes, moose, bears, river otters, migrating waterfowl, and harbor seals attracted by salmon runs; Trailhead at Bartlett Cove, accessible from Glacier Bay Lodge; Open year-round, daylight hours. [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)]

- **Sea Kayaking - Day Tours** (4-6 hours on water) - Guided or self-guided kayaking in Bartlett Cove and surrounding waters. Day trips include gear, guides, and lunch. Popular for exploring hidden coves and viewing marine wildlife up close; Departs from Bartlett Cove; Seasonal (mid-May to mid-September). [Source: [Visit Glacier Bay Kayaking](https://www.visitglacierbay.com/things-to-do/guided-kayaking-rentals/), [Spirit Walker Expeditions](https://seakayakalaska.com/), [NPS Kayaking](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/kayaking.htm)]

- **Flightseeing Tours** (1-2 hours) - Scenic flights by seaplane offering spectacular aerial views of glaciers, mountains, fjords, and wildlife throughout the park. Tours can be customized to fit various budgets and timelines. Charter planes accommodate up to 5 people; Departs from Gustavus; Year-round with weather permitting; $1,250 (1 hour), $1,500 (1.5 hours), $1,750 (2 hours) for charter. [Source: [NPS Flightseeing](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/flightseeing.htm), [Visit Glacier Bay Flightseeing](https://www.visitglacierbay.com/things-to-do/flightseeing/), [Glacier Bay Alaska](https://glacierbayalaska.com/flightseeing-tours/)]

- **Fishing Charters** (8-9 hours) - Saltwater fishing charters in Icy Strait and the Outer Coast targeting halibut, all five species of Pacific salmon (king and coho especially), lingcod, and rockfish. Travel time to fishing grounds is less than 20 minutes from Gustavus; Departs from Gustavus area; Seasonal (mid-May to September). [Source: [Glacier Bay Alaska Fishing](https://glacierbayalaska.com/alaska-fishing/salt-water-fishing/), [Gustavus Visitor Association](https://www.gustavusak.com/things-to-do/fishing-and-whale-watching/), [Fairweather Adventures](https://www.fairweatheradventures.net/fishing/)]

- **Visitor Center Exhibits and Programs** (1-2 hours) - Explore exhibits about natural and cultural history, including displays about geography, an underwater hydrophone listening station where you can hear marine life, Alaska Geographic bookstore, quiet reading area, and auditorium for park films. Ranger-led walks and programs operate daily throughout summer in partnership with tribal programs; 179 Bartlett Cove Road, second floor of Glacier Bay Lodge, Gustavus, AK 99826; Mid-May to mid-September, daily; 907-697-2230. [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [NPS Exploring Bartlett Cove](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/exploring-bartlett-cove.htm)]

- **Tlingit Trail and Huna Tribal House** (0.5-1 hour) - Easy 0.5-mile one-way shoreline forest walk featuring a traditional dugout canoe, whale skeleton ("Snow"), native plants, and the Huna Tribal House displaying Lingit culture and formline design; Trailhead at Bartlett Cove; Open year-round, daylight hours. [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [NPS Exploring Bartlett Cove](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/exploring-bartlett-cove.htm)]

- **Beach Walking and Shore Exploration** (1 hour to full day) - Walk along the rocky and sandy beaches revealing intertidal life at low tide, with opportunities for birdwatching and viewing whales and sea otters from shore. Can walk up to 6 miles one way to Point Gustavus; Accessible from Bartlett Cove area; Open year-round. [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Bartlett Lake Trail** (7-8 hours) - Moderate, less-maintained 8-mile round-trip trail that branches off from Bartlett River Trail. Climbs a moraine with moss-covered boulders and lichen-covered trees before reaching a remote lake. Offers exceptional solitude and opportunities to hear loons calling. Requires water, lunch, and rain gear; Trailhead at Bartlett Cove, branches from Bartlett River Trail; Open year-round, daylight hours. [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)]

- **Point Gustavus Beach Walk for Whale Watching** (30-minute walk each way, plus viewing time) - Often missed by tourists who don't know to ask for it, this beach walk from Gustavus leads to Point Gustavus, which sits just north of one of the most famous humpback whale-watching areas in the world. During peak season (usually July), visitors can expect to see many whales right from the shore without needing to book a boat tour; Accessible from Gustavus; Best in July. [Source: [Dirt In My Shoes Secret Hike](https://www.dirtinmyshoes.com/secret-hike-glacier-bay-national-park/), [Bright Standards](https://brightstandards.com/glacier-bay-national-park-things-to-do/)]

- **Multi-Day Sea Kayaking Expeditions** (5-8 days) - Extended kayaking trips into the remote East or West Arms of Glacier Bay, with drop-off and pick-up service provided by the day boat. Trips involve paddling 5-6 miles per day (up to 5 hours of paddling) and camping on remote beaches where you may be the only people for miles. East Arm trips are 6 days/5 nights (Spirit Walker) or 8 days (Alaska Mountain Guides); West Arm trips are 5-7 days; Departs from Bartlett Cove via tour boat drop-off; Mid-May to mid-September; Camper drop-off/pick-up: Adults $146.08, Children $76.32. [Source: [Spirit Walker East Arm](https://seakayakalaska.com/trips/glacier-bay-east-arm-kayaking-trip/), [Alaska Mountain Guides East Arm](https://alaskamountainguides.com/tour-page/glacier-bay-east-arm/), [Alaska Mountain Guides Escape](https://alaskamountainguides.com/tour-page/glacier-bay-escape/), [NPS Kayaking](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/kayaking.htm)]

- **Backcountry Camping Permits and Remote Exploration** (Variable, 1-7+ days) - Secluded backcountry camping experiences requiring permits to keep the park pristine. Visitors can be dropped off in the backcountry by the park's concession-operated tour boat and picked up on a later date. Remote campsites and cabins offer a wilderness experience where you'll feel completely alone; Permits issued at Visitor Information Station at the public-use dock in Bartlett Cove; 907-697-2627; Mid-May to mid-September. [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Bright Standards](https://brightstandards.com/glacier-bay-national-park-things-to-do/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Hoonah and Icy Strait Point** (30-60 minutes by boat from Gustavus, potential ferry service) - Alaska's largest Tlingit village centered around a restored 1912 salmon cannery that now houses a museum, local arts and crafts shops, restaurants, and historic cannery displays. Home to the world's largest ZipRider with six 5,330-foot ziplines dropping 1,330 feet at speeds up to 60 mph. Cultural tours showcase Tlingit dance, music, arts, and history, including opportunities to tour in a traditional hand-carved Tlingit dugout canoe. Bear viewing tours explore Chichagof Island, which has the highest concentration of brown bears in the world. Tours typically last 3.5 hours; Hoonah, AK; Year-round, cruise ship season May-September. [Source: [Travel Alaska Hoonah](https://www.travelalaska.com/destinations/cities-towns/hoonah-icy-strait-point), [Alaska.org Hoonah](https://www.alaska.org/destination/hoonah-icy-strait), [Alaska Magazine Icy Strait](https://alaskamagazine.com/travel/southeast-alaska/icy-strait-point/), [Icy Strait Point](https://icystraitpoint.com/)]

- **Bear Viewing and Fly Fishing Tours with NatVenture** (4-6 hours) - Specialized fly fishing, sea kayaking, packrafting, and bear watching excursions in the Gustavus area and Icy Strait. Offers access to pristine fishing spots and bear viewing locations; Operates from Gustavus and Icy Strait Point; Seasonal. [Source: [NatVenture Tours](https://www.natventuretours.com), [Travel Alaska Gustavus](https://www.travelalaska.com/destinations/cities-towns/glacier-bay-gustavus)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 3-4 days for a comprehensive experience including the day boat tour, hiking trails, kayaking, and wildlife viewing. This timeframe allows visitors to take the signature 8-hour glacier tour, explore multiple hiking trails, enjoy kayaking excursions, and experience the diverse wildlife and glaciers that make Glacier Bay unique. Single-day visits via cruise ship are possible but provide a limited experience.
