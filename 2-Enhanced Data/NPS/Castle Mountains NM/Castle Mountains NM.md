# Castle Mountains NM (National Monument)

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-scenic-driving-through-joshua-tree-forest"></a>**Scenic Driving Through Joshua Tree Forest** (1-2 hours) - The monument is accessible only via dirt roads; high clearance 4WD vehicles are recommended for exploring the spectacular Joshua tree forest and desert grassland; wandering along dirt roads allows visitors to enjoy the natural landscape; enter via Walking Box Ranch Road off NV-164 west of Searchlight; accessible year-round but best in spring or fall [Source: [NPS Castle Mountains FAQs](https://www.nps.gov/camo/faqs.htm), [Visit California Castle Mountains](https://www.visitcalifornia.com/experience/castle-mountains-national-monument/), [National Park Units](https://www.nationalparkunits.com/castle-mountains-national-monument/)]

- <a id="activity-wildlife-viewing"></a>**Wildlife Viewing** (variable time) - The monument is home to desert bighorn sheep, golden eagles, mountain lions, coyotes, bobcats, roadrunners, Gila Monsters, and jack rabbits; a herd of desert bighorn sheep lives on the steep, rocky slopes of the Castle Mountains; best viewing opportunities in early morning or late afternoon [Source: [Lake.com Castle Mountains](https://www.lake.com/parks/castle-mountains-national-monument/), [Castle Mountains Outdoorsy](https://www.outdoorsy.com/guide/castle-mountains-national-monument), [NPS Brochure](https://npshistory.com/publications/camo/brochures/2016-2.pdf)]

- <a id="activity-wildflower-viewing"></a>**Wildflower Viewing** (1-2 hours, March-April) - After substantial spring rains, the carpet of wildflowers can be spectacular from March through April; the desert bloom is not an annual event and depends on storms aligning properly; the monument features some of the finest native desert grassland in the Mojave Desert [Source: [NPS Castle Mountains Wildflowers](https://www.nps.gov/camo/where-the-wildflowers-are.htm), [Plan Your Visit Castle Mountains](https://www.nps.gov/camo/planyourvisit/index.htm), [Aspen's Tracks](https://aspenstracks.com/beautiful-spectacular-castle-mountains-national-monument/)]

- <a id="activity-backcountry-camping"></a>**Backcountry Camping** (overnight) - Backcountry camping is allowed within the monument; no facilities, developed campgrounds, or established trails exist; visitors can drive in and set off on a hike or camp as they like; dispersed camping available throughout the monument [Source: [NPS Castle Mountains FAQs](https://www.nps.gov/camo/faqs.htm), [Hipcamp Castle Mountains](https://hipcamp.com/en-US/discover/california/castle-mountains-national-monument), [Castle Mountains Outdoorsy](https://www.outdoorsy.com/guide/castle-mountains-national-monument)]

- <a id="activity-photography-of-castle-peaks-and-desert-landscapes"></a>**Photography of Castle Peaks and Desert Landscapes** (1-2 hours) - Hart Peak (5,543 feet) is the prominent feature in the Castle Mountains skyline; the dramatic mountain peaks, Joshua tree forests, and desert landscapes provide excellent photography subjects; no developed trails, but visitors can explore freely [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/guide-to-exploring-castle-mountains-national-monument/), [Federal Register Establishment](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2016/02/18/2016-03540/establishment-of-the-castle-mountains-national-monument)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-hart-mining-town-ghost-town-site"></a>**Hart Mining Town Ghost Town Site** (1-2 hours) - Beneath the shadow of Hart Peak lie rich cultural and historic resources, including the historic gold mining ghost town of Hart; in 1907, brothers Bert and Clark Hitt found rich gold ore, and with James Hart founded the town at the base of Hart Peak; by 1908, the town had 1,500 residents, telephone lines and a weekly newspaper, but mining declined and by 1920, the town had disappeared; the site now contains archeological remains and mining history [Source: [Press Enterprise Castle Mountains](https://www.pressenterprise.com/2016/03/28/castle-mountains-monument-a-glimpse-at-old-west-history/), [Federal Register Establishment](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2016/02/18/2016-03540/establishment-of-the-castle-mountains-national-monument)]

- <a id="gem-walking-box-ranch-historic-site"></a>**Walking Box Ranch Historic Site** (30 minutes-1 hour) - On the outskirts of the monument is Walking Box Ranch, purchased in 1931 by silent film stars Rex Bell and Clara Bow; the Bureau of Land Management now owns the historic property, which includes the original home, barn and corrals; 2WD high clearance vehicles must use Walking Box Ranch Road for entry and exit to access the monument [Source: [Press Enterprise Castle Mountains](https://www.pressenterprise.com/2016/03/28/castle-mountains-monument-a-glimpse-at-old-west-history/), [Wikipedia Castle Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castle_Mountains_National_Monument)]

- <a id="gem-native-american-archaeological-sites"></a>**Native American Archaeological Sites** (variable time) - The monument contains significant Native American archeological sites and cultural resources protected within the landscape; visitors should respect all cultural resources and not disturb artifacts; specific locations are not publicized to protect these sensitive sites [Source: [Federal Register Establishment](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2016/02/18/2016-03540/establishment-of-the-castle-mountains-national-monument), [Press Enterprise Castle Mountains](https://www.pressenterprise.com/2016/03/28/castle-mountains-monument-a-glimpse-at-old-west-history/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-grapevine-canyon-petroglyphs"></a>**Grapevine Canyon Petroglyphs** (1 hour, 15 miles west) - Located in Avi Kwa Ame NM (National Monument) near Laughlin, Nevada; over 700 petroglyphs created between 1100 and 1900 AD in a scenic canyon; easy 0.5-mile walk in sandy wash to reach petroglyphs; Grapevine Canyon Trailhead, Grapevine Canyon Road, Searchlight, NV 89046; accessed via Christmas Tree Pass Road, 2 miles north from Highway 163, then left turn into Grapevine Canyon; open year-round, best in winter mornings; free admission [Source: [Tripadvisor Grapevine Canyon](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g45964-d109788-Reviews-Grapevine_Canyon-Laughlin_Nevada.html), [AllTrails Grapevine Canyon](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/nevada/grapevine-canyon-trail), [Desert USA Grapevine](https://www.desertusa.com/thingstodo/du_ttd_grapevine.html)]

- <a id="nearby-christmas-tree-pass-scenic-drive"></a>**Christmas Tree Pass Scenic Drive** (1 hour without stops, 16 miles) - Scenic drive through Avi Kwa Ame NM (National Monument) from Laughlin to Searchlight; 16.3-mile mostly gravel road through the Newberry Mountains, Mojave Desert, and along the Colorado River; winds through rugged canyons and Joshua tree forests with views of the Colorado River; begins on NV Highway 163 outside Laughlin; high-clearance vehicle recommended but regular cars can navigate slowly; leads to Grapevine Canyon petroglyph site [Source: [Exploring Nevada Christmas Tree Pass](https://www.exploringnevada.com/scenic-drives/christmas-tree-pass.php), [Tripadvisor Christmas Tree Pass](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g45964-d3971142-Reviews-Christmas_Tree_Pass-Laughlin_Nevada.html), [AllTrails Christmas Tree Pass](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/nevada/christmas-tree-pass)]

- <a id="nearby-don-laughlin-s-classic-car-museum"></a>**Don Laughlin's Classic Car Museum** (1-2 hours, approximately 40 miles) - Features over 80 of the world's most distinctive vintage and rare automobiles in approximately 30,000 square feet of exhibition space; located inside the Don Laughlin Riverside Resort Hotel and Casino; 1650 S Casino Drive, Laughlin, NV 89029 (35.1673428, -114.5717361); Monday-Thursday 10am-8pm, Friday-Saturday 10am-9pm, Sunday 10am-8pm; free with King of Clubs Players' Club Card or $5 without; 702-298-2535 [Source: [Visit Laughlin](https://www.visitlaughlin.com/listing/don-laughlins-classic-car-museum/36963/), [Tripadvisor Don Laughlin Museum](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g45964-d283845-Reviews-Don_Laughlin_s_Classic_Car_Museum-Laughlin_Nevada.html), [Riverside Resort](https://www.riversideresort.com/don-laughlins-classic-car-museum/)]

- <a id="nearby-searchlight-museum"></a>**Searchlight Museum** (30 minutes-1 hour, approximately 20 miles) - Focuses on the history of Searchlight, a typical mining town that once had a larger population than Las Vegas; covers the mining history and community development; 200 Michael Wendell Way, Searchlight, NV 89046 (35.4681811, -114.9067382); Monday-Friday 9am-5pm, Saturday 9am-1pm (call ahead to confirm current hours: 702-297-1682); free admission; full walk-through takes 30 minutes to 1 hour [Source: [Clark County Searchlight Museum](https://www.clarkcountynv.gov/government/departments/parks___recreation/cultural_division/musuems/searchlight-musuem), [Tripadvisor Searchlight Museum](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g45997-d7201453-Reviews-Searchlight_Historical_Museum-Searchlight_Nevada.html), [Searchlight Museum Location](https://www.searchlightmuseum.org/index.php/home/location)]
