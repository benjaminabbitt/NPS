# Pu'ukohola Heiau NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations {#stamps}

- <span id="stamp-visitor-center">**Visitor Center**</span> (62-3601 Kawaihae Road, Kawaihae, HI 96743 (20.027285, -155.821541); Daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM; 808-882-7218)

**Note:** Call (808) 882-7218 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/puhe/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/puukohola-heiau-national-historic-site-waimea-2), [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/hi0866/)]

## Key Activities {#activities}

- <span id="activity-trail">**Interpretive Trail Walk**</span> (30-60 minutes) - Self-guided walk on a half-mile paved trail that passes Pu'ukoholā Heiau (the main temple), Mailekini Heiau, Pelekane (royal courtyard), viewpoint of submerged Hale O Kapuni heiau, and John Young's Homestead; 62-3601 Kawaihae Road, Kawaihae, HI 96743 (20.027285, -155.821541); Daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/hawaii/hawaii/puukohola-heiau-national-historical-site-trail), [GoHawaii](https://www.gohawaii.com/islands/hawaii-big-island/regions/kohala/puukohola-heaiu-national-historic-site), [US News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/Big_Island_HI/Things_To_Do/Puukohola_Heiau_National_Historic_Site_66225/)]

- <span id="activity-audio-tour">**Smartphone Audio Tour**</span> (27 minutes) - Free NPS audio tour narrated by park rangers providing interpretive information about the heiau and Hawaiian history; accessible through smartphone browser or NPS app; accompanies the interpretive trail; 62-3601 Kawaihae Road, Kawaihae, HI 96743 (20.027285, -155.821541); Daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM [Source: [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/259175), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/hawaii/hawaii/puukohola-heiau-national-historical-site-trail), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/puukohola-heiau-national-historic-site/)]

- <span id="activity-visitor-center">**Visitor Center Exhibits**</span> (20-30 minutes) - Open-air visitor center with informative displays, museum exhibits, videos about the site's history, and park store; 62-3601 Kawaihae Road, Kawaihae, HI 96743 (20.027285, -155.821541); Daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60942-d108756-Reviews-Pu_ukohola_Heiau-Kawaihae_Island_of_Hawaii_Hawaii.html), [GoHawaii](https://www.gohawaii.com/islands/hawaii-big-island/regions/kohala/puukohola-heaiu-national-historic-site)]

- <span id="activity-whale-watching">**Seasonal Whale Watching**</span> (Variable) - View humpback whales migrating through Pelekane Bay during peak season (November-April); best viewing from the interpretive trail along the shoreline; 62-3601 Kawaihae Road, Kawaihae, HI 96743 (20.027285, -155.821541); November-April, Daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM [Source: [National Parks Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/2023/02/ancient-temples-and-migrating-whales-puukohola-heiau-national-historic-site), [Love Big Island](https://www.lovebigisland.com/pololu-valley/)]

- <span id="activity-cultural-programs">**Cultural Demonstrations**</span> (Variable) - Throughout the year, park offers traditional Hawaiian cultural activities including lei making, kukui nut top, mini feather kahili, and live Hawaiian mele (music); schedule varies; 62-3601 Kawaihae Road, Kawaihae, HI 96743 (20.027285, -155.821541); Check with visitor center at 808-882-7218 [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60942-d108756-Reviews-Pu_ukohola_Heiau-Kawaihae_Island_of_Hawaii_Hawaii.html), [Hawaii Activities](https://www.hawaiiactivities.com/travelguide/puukohola-heiau/)]

- <span id="activity-festival">**Ho'oku'ikahi Annual Hawaiian Cultural Festival**</span> (2 days, mid-August) - Annual establishment day celebration featuring 6 AM ho'okupu (gift-giving) ceremony, cultural demonstrations including ulana lauhala (lauhala weaving), canoe rides, kuiki (quilting), Hawaiian games, lei haku and lei wili (lei-making), ulana niu (coconut frond weaving), kapa making, hulu ali'i (chiefly featherwork), kaula (cordage), and live Hawaiian mele; festivities 9 AM-3 PM; 62-3601 Kawaihae Road, Kawaihae, HI 96743 (20.027285, -155.821541); Mid-August annually; Free admission [Source: [NPS Festival Page](https://www.nps.gov/puhe/festival.htm), [Big Island Video News 2025](https://www.bigislandvideonews.com/2025/08/08/puukohola-heiau-establishment-day-hawaiian-cultural-festival-set/), [West Hawaii Today](https://www.westhawaiitoday.com/2025/08/14/features/community-connection/hawaiian-cultural-festival-to-celebrate-puukohola-heiau-national-historic-site/)]

## Hidden Gems {#hidden-gems}

- <span id="hidden-mailekini">**Mailekini Heiau**</span> (Included in trail) - Older temple site predating Pu'ukoholā, meaning "many maile vines"; converted by John Young into a fort during Kamehameha's reign, equipped with as many as 21 guns to protect the bay from naval attack; visible from interpretive trail; 62-3601 Kawaihae Road, Kawaihae, HI 96743 (20.027285, -155.821541); Daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM [Source: [GoHawaii](https://www.gohawaii.com/islands/hawaii-big-island/regions/kohala/puukohola-heaiu-national-historic-site), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/puukohola-heiau-national-historic-site/), [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/hale-o-kapuni-heiau.htm)]

- <span id="hidden-shark-temple">**Hale o Kapuni Heiau (Shark Temple)**</span> (Viewing from shore) - Submerged temple dedicated to shark gods, now completely underwater; remnants last visible during low tide in 1950s; can be spotted from shoreline with careful observation; local lore tells of chiefs watching sharks circle and devour offerings; located offshore below Mailekini Heiau in Pelekane Bay; 62-3601 Kawaihae Road, Kawaihae, HI 96743 (20.027285, -155.821541); Daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM [Source: [NPS Hale o Kapuni](https://www.nps.gov/places/hale-o-kapuni-heiau.htm), [Big Island Hikes](https://bigislandhikes.com/puukohola-heiau-historic-site), [Hawaii Guide](https://www.hawaii-guide.com/big-island/sights/puukohola-heiau)]

- <span id="hidden-shark-viewing">**Shark Viewing in Pelekane Bay**</span> (Variable) - Most days visitors can observe sharks returning to the bay where the submerged Hale o Kapuni Heiau is located; best viewing from the shoreline trail; 62-3601 Kawaihae Road, Kawaihae, HI 96743 (20.027285, -155.821541); Daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60942-d108756-Reviews-Pu_ukohola_Heiau-Kawaihae_Island_of_Hawaii_Hawaii.html)]

- <span id="hidden-john-young">**John Young's Homestead Site**</span> (Brief viewing) - Site of the homestead of John Young (Olohana), Kamehameha's western advisor who helped convert Mailekini into a fort; accessible from the interpretive trail though not within close walking distance of the visitor center; 62-3601 Kawaihae Road, Kawaihae, HI 96743 (20.027285, -155.821541); Daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM [Source: [NPS John Young Homestead](https://www.nps.gov/places/john-young-homestead.htm), [NPS Homestead History](https://www.nps.gov/puhe/learn/historyculture/homestead.htm), [US News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/Big_Island_HI/Things_To_Do/Puukohola_Heiau_National_Historic_Site_66225/)]

- <span id="hidden-pelekane">**Pelekane (Royal Courtyard)**</span> (Included in trail) - Royal courtyard near the ocean, only a short walking distance from Pu'ukoholā Heiau; significant historical site where Hawaiian royalty gathered; 62-3601 Kawaihae Road, Kawaihae, HI 96743 (20.027285, -155.821541); Daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM [Source: [NPS Hawai'i Island Article](https://www.nps.gov/articles/puheiau.htm), [GoHawaii](https://www.gohawaii.com/islands/hawaii-big-island/regions/kohala/puukohola-heaiu-national-historic-site)]

## Also Nearby {#nearby}

- <span id="nearby-spencer">**Spencer Beach Park**</span> (1-3 hours, adjacent) - Family-friendly white sand beach immediately south of Pu'ukoholā Heiau offering swimming, snorkeling, paddleboarding, kayaking, picnicking, and camping; lifeguards on weekends and holidays 9:30 AM-4:45 PM; 62-3461 Kawaihae Road, Waimea, HI 96743 (20.024703, -155.822372); Daily 6:00 AM-9:00 PM [Source: [Spencer Beach Park County](https://www.parks.hawaiicounty.gov/Home/Components/FacilityDirectory/FacilityDirectory/627/1912), [To-Hawaii.com](https://www.to-hawaii.com/big-island/beaches/spencerbeachpark.php), [Big Island Guide](https://bigislandguide.com/samuel-m-spencer-beach-park)]

- <span id="nearby-lapakahi">**Lapakahi State Historical Park**</span> (1-1.5 hours, 12 miles north/~20 min drive) - Ancient Hawaiian fishing village site with 1.1-mile Lapakahi Village Interpretive Trail featuring 19 numbered sites including restored hale (houses), lava stone walls, burial sites, and traditional Hawaiian games; also offers snorkeling and beach activities; Highway 270 near mile marker 14, North Kohala (20.174879, -155.89559); Daily 8:00 AM-3:30 PM (gates lock at 4:00 PM), closed state holidays; Free admission [Source: [DLNR State Parks](https://dlnr.hawaii.gov/dsp/parks/hawaii/lapakahi-state-historical-park/), [Big Island Hikes](https://bigislandhikes.com/lapakahi-state-historical-park), [GoHawaii](https://www.gohawaii.com/islands/hawaii-big-island/regions/kohala/lapakahi-state-historical-park)]

- <span id="nearby-pololu">**Pololu Valley Lookout**</span> (1.5-2 hours, ~25 miles north/~45 min drive) - Scenic overlook at the end of Highway 270 offering dramatic views of the black sand beach valley; 0.9-mile moderately challenging trail descends to valley floor (20 min down, 30 min up); arrive before 9 AM for parking; 52-5088 Akoni Pule Highway, Kapaau, HI (20.204, -155.734); Open year-round 24/7 [Source: [GoHawaii](https://www.gohawaii.com/islands/hawaii-big-island/regions/kohala/pololu-valley-lookout), [Love Big Island](https://www.lovebigisland.com/pololu-valley/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/hawaii/hawaii/pololu-trail)]

- <span id="nearby-hapuna">**Hapuna Beach State Recreation Area**</span> (2-4 hours, 2.3 miles south/~5 min drive) - One of Hawaii's largest and most beautiful white sand beaches on the Kohala Coast; excellent for swimming, bodyboarding, and sunbathing; lifeguards on duty; parking fee applies; Queen Ka'ahumanu Highway (Highway 19) south of Kawaihae; Daily hours vary by season [Source: [DLNR State Parks](https://dlnr.hawaii.gov/dsp/parks/hawaii/hapuna-beach-state-recreation-area/), [GoHawaii](https://www.gohawaii.com/islands/hawaii-big-island/regions/kohala/hapuna-beach-state-park), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hapuna_Beach_State_Recreation_Area)]

- <span id="nearby-waimea">**Waimea (Kamuela) Town**</span> (2-3 hours, 9.1 miles east/~17 min drive) - Historic paniolo (Hawaiian cowboy) town known as a food lover's paradise; home to Parker Ranch (one of the largest cattle ranches in the US), art galleries, local craft stores, and restaurants; numerous attractions and cultural sites; Waimea, HI; Hours vary by establishment [Source: [Love Big Island Waimea](https://www.lovebigisland.com/waimea-kamuela-hawaii/highlights/), [Expedia Kawaihae](https://www.expedia.com/Kawaihae.dx11858)]
