# Cape Hatteras NS (National Seashore)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Hatteras Island Visitor Center** (46375 Lighthouse Road, Buxton, NC 27920 (35.2519, -75.5267); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM year-round; 252-475-9042) [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/caha/planyourvisit/visitor-centers.htm), [NPS Get Passport Stamp](https://www.nps.gov/caha/planyourvisit/get-a-passport-stamp.htm)]

- **Bodie Island Visitor Center** (8180 Bodie Island Lighthouse Road, Nags Head, NC (35.8181, -75.5639); rangers on site, building closed due to January 2025 fire; 252-475-9501) [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/caha/planyourvisit/visitor-centers.htm)]

- **Ocracoke Island Visitor Center** (Near Ocracoke Lighthouse, Ocracoke, NC (35.1115, -75.9877); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM year-round; phone not listed) [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/caha/planyourvisit/visitor-centers.htm)]

**Note:** Bodie Island Lighthouse climbing season starts April 18, 2025 (7 days/week, climbs every 20 minutes 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM); lighthouse climbing not affected by visitor center closure. Call 252-475-9054 for lighthouse tickets. [Source: [Recreation.gov Bodie Island](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/facility/252034)]

## Key Activities

- **Surf World-Class Beaches** (2-4 hours) - Experience best surfing and fishing on East Coast across 70+ miles from South Nags Head to Ocracoke Inlet covering 30,000 acres; major tournaments like Wave Jam and ESA championships held here; year-round conditions [Source: [OuterBanks.com](https://www.outerbanks.com/cape-hatteras-national-seashore.html), [NPS Surfing](https://www.nps.gov/caha/planyourvisit/surfing.htm)]

- **Climb Bodie Island Lighthouse** (45 minutes) - Ascend historic 1872 lighthouse for panoramic Outer Banks views; seasonal climbing April 18 - October 13, 2025; ticket required [Source: [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/facility/252034), [NPS Bodie Island](https://www.nps.gov/caha/planyourvisit/bils.htm)]

- **Fish Year-Round** (2-8 hours) - Cast for prized red drum during spring and fall migrations, plus year-round offshore and inshore fishing; shoulder seasons (late spring, fall) best for migrating species [Source: [OuterBanks.com](https://www.outerbanks.com/cape-hatteras-national-seashore.html), [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/caha/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Participate in Water Sports** (2-4 hours) - Try kiteboarding, windsurfing, ocean kayaking, body boarding, skimboarding, canoeing, boating, biking, and birdwatching along the seashore [Source: [OuterBanks.org](https://www.outerbanks.org/things-to-do/attractions/cape-hatteras-national-seashore/), [NPS Beach Activities](https://www.nps.gov/caha/planyourvisit/beachactivities.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Ghost Crab Hunting** (1-2 hours evening) - Search beaches with flashlight after dark to spot iridescent ghost crabs thriving at night; unique nighttime activity especially popular on Hatteras and Ocracoke Islands [Source: [Surf or Sound](https://www.surforsound.com/experiences/national-seashore/)]

- **Beach Fires** (evening) - Enjoy small beach fires (under 3 feet diameter) 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM November 16 - April 30 anywhere, or May 1 - November 15 only in villages, Coquina Beach, or Ocracoke Day Use Area [Source: [NPS Beach Activities](https://www.nps.gov/caha/planyourvisit/beachactivities.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Roanoke Island** (30 minutes north, Manteo) - Visit Fort Raleigh NHS, North Carolina Aquarium, and historic downtown Manteo [Source: [OuterBanks.com](https://www.outerbanks.com/cape-hatteras-national-seashore.html)]

- **Wright Brothers National Memorial** (45 minutes north, Kill Devil Hills) - See site of first powered flight December 17, 1903; visitor center and monument [Source: [Visit OBX](https://www.visitocracokenc.com/national-park-service/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** Full day to multi-day; day trip 6-8 hours for beaches and lighthouse, multi-day to explore all three districts and islands. [Source: [US News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/Outer_Banks_NC/Things_To_Do/Cape_Hatteras_National_Seashore_61659/)]
