# Cape Hatteras NS (National Seashore)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-ocracoke-island-visitor-center"></a>**Ocracoke Island Visitor Center** (Near Ocracoke Lighthouse, Ocracoke, NC (35.1115, -75.9877); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM year-round; phone not listed) [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/caha/planyourvisit/visitor-centers.htm)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-surf-world-class-beaches"></a>**Surf World-Class Beaches** (2-4 hours) - Experience best surfing and fishing on East Coast across 70+ miles from South Nags Head to Ocracoke Inlet covering 30,000 acres; major tournaments like Wave Jam and ESA championships held here; year-round conditions [Source: [OuterBanks.com](https://www.outerbanks.com/cape-hatteras-national-seashore.html), [NPS Surfing](https://www.nps.gov/caha/planyourvisit/surfing.htm)]

- <a id="activity-climb-bodie-island-lighthouse"></a>**Climb Bodie Island Lighthouse** (45 minutes) - Ascend historic 1872 lighthouse for panoramic Outer Banks views; seasonal climbing April 18 - October 13, 2025; ticket required [Source: [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/facility/252034), [NPS Bodie Island](https://www.nps.gov/caha/planyourvisit/bils.htm)]

- <a id="activity-fish-year-round"></a>**Fish Year-Round** (2-8 hours) - Cast for prized red drum during spring and fall migrations, plus year-round offshore and inshore fishing; shoulder seasons (late spring, fall) best for migrating species [Source: [OuterBanks.com](https://www.outerbanks.com/cape-hatteras-national-seashore.html), [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/caha/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- <a id="activity-participate-in-water-sports"></a>**Participate in Water Sports** (2-4 hours) - Try kiteboarding, windsurfing, ocean kayaking, body boarding, skimboarding, canoeing, boating, biking, and birdwatching along the seashore [Source: [OuterBanks.org](https://www.outerbanks.org/things-to-do/attractions/cape-hatteras-national-seashore/), [NPS Beach Activities](https://www.nps.gov/caha/planyourvisit/beachactivities.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-ghost-crab-hunting"></a>**Ghost Crab Hunting** (1-2 hours evening) - Search beaches with flashlight after dark to spot iridescent ghost crabs thriving at night; unique nighttime activity especially popular on Hatteras and Ocracoke Islands [Source: [Surf or Sound](https://www.surforsound.com/experiences/national-seashore/)]

- <a id="gem-beach-fires"></a>**Beach Fires** (evening) - Enjoy small beach fires (under 3 feet diameter) 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM November 16 - April 30 anywhere, or May 1 - November 15 only in villages, Coquina Beach, or Ocracoke Day Use Area [Source: [NPS Beach Activities](https://www.nps.gov/caha/planyourvisit/beachactivities.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-roanoke-island"></a>**Roanoke Island** (30 minutes north, Manteo) - Visit Fort Raleigh NHS, North Carolina Aquarium, and historic downtown Manteo [Source: [OuterBanks.com](https://www.outerbanks.com/cape-hatteras-national-seashore.html)]

- <a id="nearby-wright-brothers-national-memorial"></a>**Wright Brothers National Memorial** (45 minutes north, Kill Devil Hills) - See site of first powered flight December 17, 1903; visitor center and monument [Source: [Visit OBX](https://www.visitocracokenc.com/national-park-service/)]
