# Great Smoky Mountains NP (National Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Sugarlands Visitor Center** (1420 Fighting Creek Gap Road, Gatlinburg, TN 37738; 35.6886, -83.5342; Daily 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, extended summer hours; Phone: 865-436-1200) - Main Tennessee-side visitor center with museum, film, ranger programs, and bookstore. Primary stamp location for park's Tennessee entrance. [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

- **Oconaluftee Visitor Center** (1194 Newfound Gap Road, Cherokee, NC 28719; 35.5138, -83.3067; Daily 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, extended summer hours; Phone: 828-497-1904) - Main North Carolina-side visitor center, 2 miles north of Cherokee on US 441. Features Mountain Farm Museum with historic buildings. [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

- **Cades Cove Visitor Center** (Cades Cove Loop Road, Townsend, TN; 35.5963, -83.7975; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM seasonal, check for closures; Phone: 865-436-1200) - Located mid-point of the 11-mile Cades Cove Loop Road. Adjacent to Cable Mill historic area. [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

- **Clingmans Dome Visitor Contact Station** (Clingmans Dome Road; 35.5628, -83.4985; April-November, hours vary; seasonal closure) - Highest point in Tennessee. Stamp available when station is open. Road closed December-March. [Source: [NPS Clingmans Dome](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/clingmansdome.htm)]

**Note:** Park entrance is free (no entrance fee). Parking tags ($5-$15/day) required at some trailheads. Call 865-436-1200 for current hours and conditions.

## Key Activities

- **Cades Cove Loop Road** (2-4 hours) - Drive or bike the 11-mile one-way scenic loop through an isolated valley surrounded by mountains. View historic churches, cabins, and grist mill dating from 1800s settlement. Excellent wildlife viewing for deer, black bears, and wild turkeys. Very popular; arrive early to avoid crowds. Bicycles only on Wednesdays and Saturdays until 10:00 AM (May-September). Townsend entrance; open year-round, sunrise to sunset. [Source: [NPS Cades Cove](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/cadescove.htm)]

- **Clingmans Dome** (1-1.5 hours) - Hike the steep 0.5-mile paved trail to the highest point in Tennessee (6,643 feet) and the Great Smoky Mountains. The observation tower offers 360-degree panoramic views on clear days. Visitor contact station at parking area. Clingmans Dome Road off Newfound Gap Road; April-November, road closed in winter. [Source: [NPS Clingmans Dome](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/clingmansdome.htm)]

- **Newfound Gap Road (US 441)** (1-1.5 hours driving) - The only road crossing the park through the mountains, reaching 5,048 feet at Newfound Gap on the Tennessee-North Carolina state line. Stunning mountain vistas, overlooks, and access to Clingmans Dome Road. Connects Gatlinburg, TN to Cherokee, NC (33 miles). Open year-round but may close for weather. [Source: [NPS Roads](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/driving.htm)]

- **Laurel Falls Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - One of the park's most popular trails, this 2.6-mile round-trip paved path leads to an 80-foot waterfall. Moderate difficulty with steady uphill grade. Parking fills early; arrive before 8:00 AM on weekends. Fighting Creek Gap Road, 3.5 miles from Sugarlands; open year-round. [Source: [NPS Waterfalls](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/waterfalls.htm)]

- **Alum Cave Trail to Mount LeConte** (5-8 hours) - Challenging 10.2-mile round-trip hike to the third-highest peak (6,593 feet). Trail passes through old-growth forest, Arch Rock, and Alum Cave Bluffs before climbing to LeConte Lodge. Alum Cave Bluffs (5 miles round-trip) is a popular turnaround for day hikers. Alum Cave parking area off Newfound Gap Road. [Source: [NPS Day Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/dayhikes.htm)]

- **Chimney Tops Trail** (2-3 hours) - Strenuous 3.8-mile round-trip trail with 1,400 feet elevation gain to twin rock formations offering panoramic views. Currently ends at new viewing area (summit closed for rehabilitation). Chimney Tops parking area off Newfound Gap Road; fills early. [Source: [NPS Day Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/dayhikes.htm)]

- **Roaring Fork Motor Nature Trail** (1-2 hours) - One-way 5.5-mile scenic driving loop through old-growth forest featuring historic cabins, a working tub mill, and trailheads to waterfalls. Access to Grotto Falls and Rainbow Falls trailheads. Closed in winter. Gatlinburg entrance; seasonal. [Source: [NPS Roaring Fork](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/roaringfork.htm)]

- **Grotto Falls Trail** (2-3 hours) - Moderate 2.6-mile round-trip hike to a 25-foot waterfall you can walk behind. Trail passes through old-growth hemlock forest. Trailhead on Roaring Fork Motor Nature Trail. Popular; arrive early. [Source: [NPS Waterfalls](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/waterfalls.htm)]

- **Rainbow Falls Trail** (4-5 hours) - Strenuous 5.4-mile round-trip hike to an 80-foot waterfall, the highest single-drop waterfall accessible by hiking trail. Best after rain. Trailhead on Cherokee Orchard Road. [Source: [NPS Waterfalls](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/waterfalls.htm)]

- **Mountain Farm Museum at Oconaluftee** (30-45 minutes) - Open-air museum featuring authentic log structures relocated from throughout the park, including farmhouse, barn, apple house, springhouse, and blacksmith shop. Living history demonstrations seasonal. Adjacent to Oconaluftee Visitor Center; free with park admission. [Source: [NPS Mountain Farm Museum](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/learn/historyculture/mountain-farm-museum.htm)]

- **Cable Mill Historic Area at Cades Cove** (1-1.5 hours) - Working grist mill (seasonal), barn, blacksmith shop, and other historic structures. Rangers demonstrate pioneer skills and milling. Located at Cades Cove Visitor Center stop on loop road. Open March-November. [Source: [NPS Cades Cove](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/cadescove.htm)]

- **Wildlife Viewing** (variable) - Home to approximately 1,500 black bears, plus elk (reintroduced 2001), white-tailed deer, wild turkeys, and over 200 bird species. Best viewing at Cades Cove (early morning/evening) and Cataloochee Valley for elk. Throughout park; dawn and dusk best. [Source: [NPS Wildlife](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/learn/nature/wildlife.htm)]

- **Fishing** (2-4 hours) - Nearly 2,900 miles of streams with brook, rainbow, and brown trout. Tennessee or North Carolina license required. Catch-and-release only in many areas. Throughout park; year-round. [Source: [NPS Fishing](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/fishing.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Cataloochee Valley** (half-day) - Remote valley on park's east side with historic buildings and the best place to view elk, especially at dawn and dusk. Less crowded than Cades Cove. Challenging access via narrow gravel road. Cataloochee Road off I-40; open year-round (road may close in weather). [Source: [NPS Cataloochee](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/cataloochee.htm)]

- **Abrams Falls** (3-4 hours) - Moderate 5-mile round-trip hike to a 20-foot-high, 100-foot-wide waterfall with powerful cascade. Less crowded than Laurel Falls. Trailhead at Cades Cove (stop #10). [Source: [NPS Waterfalls](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/waterfalls.htm)]

- **Little River Road** (1-1.5 hours driving) - Scenic 18-mile drive following the Little River with numerous pullouts, swimming holes, and the Elkmont Historic District. Connects Sugarlands to Townsend. Open year-round. [Source: [NPS Roads](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/driving.htm)]

- **Elkmont Historic District** (1-2 hours) - Remains of an early 20th-century resort community now being preserved by NPS. Explore old cottages and learn about the Wonderland Club era. Elkmont Road off Little River Road. [Source: [NPS Elkmont](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/learn/historyculture/elkmont.htm)]

- **Synchronous Fireflies** (2-3 hours, late May-early June) - One of only a few places in the world where synchronous fireflies display, lighting up together. Lottery system for vehicle passes during peak viewing period. Elkmont area; limited viewing nights annually. [Source: [NPS Fireflies](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/learn/nature/fireflies.htm)]

- **Gregory Bald** (7-9 hours) - Challenging 11.3-mile round-trip hike to a grassy bald with stunning views and peak azalea bloom in mid-June. One of the park's most rewarding hikes. Trailhead at Cades Cove. [Source: [NPS Day Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/dayhikes.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Gatlinburg** (variable, adjacent) - Mountain resort town at park's main Tennessee entrance with shops, restaurants, attractions including Ripley's Aquarium of the Smokies, Ober Gatlinburg ski area/tramway, and Great Smoky Arts & Crafts Community (8-mile loop of artisan shops). Downtown Gatlinburg, TN; open year-round. [Source: [Visit Gatlinburg](https://www.gatlinburg.com/)]

- **Pigeon Forge** (variable, 5 miles north) - Tourism hub featuring Dollywood theme park (Tennessee's #1 attraction), Dolly Parton's Stampede dinner show, go-karts, mini-golf, outlet shopping, and museums including Titanic Museum. Pigeon Forge, TN; open year-round. [Source: [Visit Pigeon Forge](https://www.mypigeonforge.com/)]

- **Cherokee, NC** (1-3 hours, adjacent) - Gateway town on park's North Carolina entrance, home to the Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians. Visit the Museum of the Cherokee Indian, Oconaluftee Indian Village living history site, and Qualla Arts & Crafts Mutual. Harrah's Cherokee Casino nearby. Cherokee, NC; open year-round. [Source: [Visit Cherokee](https://visitcherokeenc.com/)]

- **Blue Ridge Parkway** (variable, connects to park) - "America's Favorite Drive" connects Great Smoky Mountains to Shenandoah National Park via 469 miles of scenic driving along the Blue Ridge Mountains. Access at Cherokee, NC (milepost 469). Open year-round (sections may close for weather). [Source: [NPS Blue Ridge Parkway](https://www.nps.gov/blri/index.htm)]

## Total Recommended Time

**Total recommended visit time for Great Smoky Mountains NP: 2-5 days**

For a day trip, focus on Newfound Gap Road, Clingmans Dome, and one major activity (Cades Cove OR a waterfall hike). Two days allows Cades Cove plus multiple hikes. Three or more days enables exploration of Cataloochee, backcountry trails, and nearby attractions. The park is the most visited in the National Park System (12+ million annual visitors), so arrive early at popular spots or visit during shoulder seasons.
