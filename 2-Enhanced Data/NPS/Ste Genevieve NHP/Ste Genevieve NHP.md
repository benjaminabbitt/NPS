# Ste Genevieve NHP (National Historical Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations {#stamps}

- <a id="stamp-welcome-center"></a>**Ste. Genevieve Welcome Center** (66 South Main Street, Ste. Genevieve, MO 63670 (37.9814, -90.0418); Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed Thanksgiving, December 25, and New Year's Day; 573-880-7189) - The primary visitor contact point and NPS passport stamp location. The Welcome Center features a 10-minute introductory video about the town and its French Colonial history, exhibits with a detailed diorama, and park staff available to answer questions and provide maps. Tour tickets for ranger-guided house tours are available here on a first-come, first-served basis. [Source: [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/stge/ste-genevieve-nhp-visitor-center.htm), [NPS Operating Hours](https://home.nps.gov/stge/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

**Note:** Call 573-880-7189 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities {#activities}

- <a id="activity-green-tree-tavern"></a>**Green Tree Tavern (Janis-Ziegler House) Ranger-Guided Tour** (1 hour) - Free ranger-guided tour of the oldest verified vertical log building in Ste. Genevieve, officially dated to 1790 by dendrochronology. This poteaux-sur-sol (post-on-sill) French Colonial structure has served as a tavern, inn, tobacco shop, and Masonic Lodge. Tours are offered daily depending on staffing and weather, limited to 15 individuals with free tickets required from the Welcome Center on a first-come, first-served basis; located in Ste. Genevieve's historic district; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM. [Source: [NPS Green Tree Tavern](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/visit-green-tree-tavern.htm), [Wikipedia Green Tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Janis_House), [NPS FAQ](https://www.nps.gov/stge/planyourvisit/faq.htm)]

- <a id="activity-jb-valle-house"></a>**Jean Baptiste Vallé House Ranger-Guided Tour** (1 hour) - Free ranger-guided tour of one of the three historic houses managed by the NPS in Ste. Genevieve. Tours are offered daily depending on staffing and weather, limited to 15 individuals with free tickets required from the Welcome Center. The house is located at 99 S. Main Street, Ste. Genevieve, MO 63670; gardens open sunrise to sunset; house tours Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM. Note: seven steps to enter. [Source: [NPS Jean Baptiste Vallé House](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/visit-the-jean-baptiste-valle-house.htm), [NPS Places JB Vallé](https://www.nps.gov/places/jean-baptiste-valle-house.htm)]

- <a id="activity-bauvais-amoureux"></a>**Bauvais-Amoureux House Tour** (1 hour) - Tour one of only three remaining poteaux-en-terre (post-in-ground) houses in Ste. Genevieve and only five known to remain in the entire United States. Built in 1792 for Jean Baptiste Bauvais, this vertical log construction house demonstrates rare French Colonial architecture. Free ranger-led tours available; tour tickets from Welcome Center; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM. [Source: [NPS Bauvais-Amoureux](https://www.nps.gov/places/bauvais-amoureux-house.htm), [JNPA Blog](https://jnpa.blog/tag/bauvais-amoureux-house/)]

- <a id="activity-bolduc-house"></a>**Bolduc House Museum & Centre for French Colonial Life Tour** (1.5 hours) - Guided hourly tours of the Louis Bolduc House (c. 1794) and Bolduc-LeMeilleur House (c. 1820), both furnished with original artifacts representing French Colonial life. The Centre for French Colonial Life Museum features exhibits about the region's history. Tours available every hour; 198 Market Street, Ste. Genevieve, MO 63670 (37.9799, -90.0444); Monday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday Noon - 5:00 PM; 573-883-3105; Adult $10, Student $5, Military free with ID. [Source: [TripAdvisor Bolduc House](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g44888-d3610251-Reviews-Bolduc_House_Museum-Sainte_Genevieve_Missouri.html), [Visit MO Bolduc](https://www.visitmo.com/things-to-do/bolduc-house-museum), [Visit Ste Gen Bolduc](https://visitstegen.com/2020/01/bolduc-lemeilleur-houses/)]

- <a id="activity-felix-valle"></a>**Felix Vallé House State Historic Site Tour** (45 minutes) - Tour the 1818 Federal-style limestone building that served as both a mercantile store and residence, restored and furnished to reflect the 1830s. Tours offered at 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 2:00 PM, and 3:00 PM; 198 Merchant Street, Ste. Genevieve, MO 63670 (37.9799, -90.0444); April-October Daily 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM, November-March Thursday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM; 573-883-7102. [Source: [MO State Parks Felix Vallé](https://mostateparks.com/park/felix-valle-house-state-historic-site), [Lewis & Clark Trail Felix Vallé](https://www.lewisandclark.travel/listing/felix-valle-state-historic-site/)]

- <a id="activity-self-guided-walk"></a>**Self-Guided Historic District Walking Tour** (1.5 hours) - Explore the National Historic Landmark District featuring the largest concentration of French Colonial architecture in North America. Pick up a walking tour map at the Welcome Center to explore historic homes, restaurants, shops, a candy store, ice cream parlor, and winery throughout downtown. The walking tour includes views of vertical log construction buildings and the opportunity to walk the levee trail near the Mississippi River; maps available at 66 South Main Street; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM. [Source: [Great River Road Ste Gen](https://www.greatriverroad.com/ste-genevieve-attractions), [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/stge/ste-genevieve-nhp-visitor-center.htm)]

## Hidden Gems {#hidden}

- <a id="hidden-memorial-cemetery"></a>**Sainte Genevieve Memorial Cemetery** (30 minutes) - Walk through Missouri's oldest cemetery, established in 1787 with the first documented burial in 1793. The cemetery is divided into three sections for Catholic burials, Lutherans, and other Protestants, and serves as the final resting place of many of Missouri's early pioneers. Recently restored through federal, state, and local partnerships; 35 N. Fifth Street, Sainte Genevieve, MO 63670 (37.9814, -90.0418); Daily; Free. [Source: [NPS Memorial Cemetery](https://www.nps.gov/places/memorial-cemetery.htm), [Visit Ste Gen Cemetery](https://visitstegen.com/places/ste-genevieve-memorial-cemetery/), [Wikipedia Memorial Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ste._Genevieve_Memorial_Cemetery)]

- <a id="hidden-linden-house"></a>**Linden "Living History House"** (1 hour) - Experience interactive living history programs at this historic property operated by the Centre for French Colonial Life. Costumed interpreters demonstrate colonial life activities on Saturdays from the last weekend in March through the first weekend in November; part of the Bolduc House Museum campus, 198 Market Street, Ste. Genevieve, MO 63670; Saturdays 11:00 AM - 4:00 PM (late March - early November). [Source: [TripAdvisor Bolduc House](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g44888-d3610251-Reviews-Bolduc_House_Museum-Sainte_Genevieve_Missouri.html)]

- <a id="hidden-jour-de-fete"></a>**Jour de Fete Festival** (Full day) - If visiting the second full weekend in August, experience this premier Midwest arts and crafts festival with over 200 vendor booths, French Marine Encampment, Birds of Prey exhibits from the World Bird Sanctuary, antique steam engines and tractors, colonial crafts demonstrations, and free admission to Felix Vallé State Historic Site and Beauvais-Amoureux House. This festival was created in the 1960s to revitalize downtown Ste. Genevieve and celebrates the town's French Colonial heritage; Historic District, Ste. Genevieve, MO; Second full weekend of August annually; Free admission to festival grounds. [Source: [Jour de Fete Official Site](https://www.saintegenevievejourdefete.com/), [Great River Road Jour de Fete](https://www.greatriverroad.com/art-events/jour-de-fete), [Missouri Life Jour de Fete](https://missourilife.com/ste-genevieves-festival-with-a-french-flair/)]

## Also Nearby {#nearby}

- <a id="nearby-pickle-springs"></a>**Pickle Springs Natural Area - Trail Through Time** (1.5 hours, 25 miles southwest) - Hike a 2-mile loop trail featuring waterfalls, sandstone arches, rock shelters, towering bluffs, canyons, and impressive rock outcrops. This 256.5-acre natural area showcases unique geological formations in Ste. Genevieve County; Dorlac Road, New Offenburg, MO 63670 (37.8019, -90.3015); Daily dawn to dusk; Free; From Ste. Genevieve, travel west on Highway 32, turn right on Highway AA for 1.7 miles to Dorlac Road. [Source: [MDC Pickle Springs](https://mdc.mo.gov/discover-nature/places/pickle-springs-natural-area), [AllTrails Pickle Springs](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/missouri/trail-through-time-pickle-springs-natural-area), [Travel Bug Life Pickle Springs](https://www.thetravelbuglife.com/pickle-springs-natural-area/)]

- <a id="nearby-hawn-state-park"></a>**Hawn State Park** (3-4 hours, 30 minutes drive) - Explore 4,956 acres of scenic hiking trails ranging from 2 to 10 miles through hills of pine and oak trees, clear sand-bottomed streams, and sandstone canyons and cliffs. Activities include mountain biking, geocaching, orienteering, skiing, hiking, and camping. Trails include Whispering Pines, White Oak, and Pickle Creek; 12096 Park Drive, Ste. Genevieve, MO 63670 (37.8217, -90.2362); Daily; 573-883-3603; Free entry, camping fees apply. [Source: [MO State Parks Hawn](https://mostateparks.com/park/hawn-state-park), [Visit MO Hawn](https://www.visitmo.com/things-to-do/hawn-state-park), [Visit Ste Gen Hawn](https://visitstegen.com/2016/06/hawn-state-park/)]

- <a id="nearby-crown-ridge"></a>**Crown Ridge Tiger Sanctuary** (1 hour, 15 minutes from downtown) - Visit this non-profit sanctuary providing lifelong care for unwanted and abused big cats. General tours offer up-close views of majestic tigers and other big cats, with specialty VIP tiger feeding tours available by reservation. General tours Thursday-Sunday at 11:00 AM, 1:00 PM, and 3:00 PM; Monday-Wednesday by reservation only; 19620 Crown Ridge Road, Ste. Genevieve, MO 63670 (37.9814, -90.0418); Thursday-Sunday 11:00 AM - 3:45 PM; 573-883-9909; Tour fees apply. [Source: [TripAdvisor Crown Ridge](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g44888-d4975267-Reviews-Crown_Ridge_Tiger_Sanctuary-Sainte_Genevieve_Missouri.html), [Crown Ridge Official Site](https://crownridgetigers.com/), [Visit MO Crown Ridge](https://www.visitmo.com/things-to-do/crown-ridge-tiger-sanctuary)]

- <a id="nearby-charleville-winery"></a>**Charleville Brewery & Winery** (1-1.5 hours, 15 minutes from downtown) - Tour and taste distinctive handcrafted wines and beers at this vineyard, winery, and microbrewery established in 2003. Guided tours available Wednesday-Sunday showcase the brewing process, followed by sampling various beers and wines on the patio with breathtaking views above Saline Creek Valley; 16937 Boyd Road, Sainte Genevieve, MO 63670 (37.9814, -90.0418); Wednesday-Thursday 11:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Friday 11:00 AM - 8:00 PM, Saturday 11:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Sunday 11:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Closed Monday-Tuesday; 573-568-8165. [Source: [Charleville Official Site](https://charlevillemo.com/), [Yelp Charleville](https://www.yelp.com/biz/charleville-brewery-and-winery-ste-genevieve), [TripAdvisor Charleville](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g44888-d8134675-Reviews-Charleville_Vineyard_Winery-Sainte_Genevieve_Missouri.html)]
