# Missouri NRR (National Recreational River)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-headquarters"></a>**Missouri National Recreational River Headquarters** (508 East 2nd Street, Yankton, SD 57078 (42.871109, -97.397281); 8:00 AM - 4:30 PM Monday through Friday, closed federal holidays; 605-665-0209 ext. 21) - The visitor contact station inside the headquarters building provides passport stamps during business hours [Source: [NPS Headquarters](https://www.nps.gov/places/missouri-national-recreational-river-headquarters.htm), [Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/mnrr/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

- <a id="stamp-ponca"></a>**Missouri National Recreational River Resource and Education Center at Ponca State Park** (88090 Spur 26 E, Ponca, NE 68770 (42.594041, -96.709481); Monday through Saturday 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM, extended hours in summer; 402-755-2284) - Resource and education center featuring exhibits about the river [Source: [NPS Resource Center](https://www.nps.gov/places/missouri-national-recreational-river-resource-and-education-center.htm), [Ponca State Park](https://www.nps.gov/places/ponca-state-park.htm)]

- <a id="stamp-lewis-clark"></a>**Lewis and Clark Visitor Center** (55245 Nebraska Highway 121, Crofton, NE 68730 (42.849226, -97.477055); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily warm season, Monday through Friday 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM October through February, closed federal holidays; 402-667-2546) - Visitor center at Gavins Point Dam with exhibits on Missouri River basin and Lewis and Clark expedition [Source: [Lewis and Clark Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/places/lewis-and-clark-visitor-center.htm), [Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/mnrr/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

**Note:** Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-kayaking"></a>**Kayaking and Canoeing** (Half-day to full-day trips, variable duration based on distance; 14-30 miles per day possible) - Experience the Missouri River as Lewis and Clark did by paddling the two free-flowing stretches totaling 100 miles; access available at public launch sites with local outfitters providing canoe/kayak rentals; free kayak clinics offered at Lake Yankton every Wednesday and most Sundays 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM July through early September at the non-motorized boat launch near Chief White Crane State Recreation Area off Toe Road (kayaks, paddles, life jackets provided, minimum age 10 for solo paddling, 5 with adult in tandem, reservations at 605-665-0209 ext. 21) [Source: [Canoeing and Kayaking](https://www.nps.gov/mnrr/planyourvisit/canoeing-and-kayaking.htm), [Kayak Clinics](https://www.yankton.net/community/article_f6a13cb4-d7f4-4568-8ca7-8d1b02b8e379.html), [Trip Planning](https://missouririverwatertrail.org/paddling-missouri-river/trip-planning-and-distances)]

- <a id="activity-fishing"></a>**Fishing** (2-4 hours per session) - Popular activity for walleye, catfish, paddlefish, sauger on the Missouri River with excellent opportunities in lower Verdigre Creek (8 miles) and lower Niobrara River (20 miles); access available at multiple river access points detailed in Missouri River Outdoor Recreation Guide [Source: [Outdoorsy Guide](https://www.outdoorsy.com/guide/missouri-national-recreational-river), [River Access Points](https://www.nps.gov/mnrr/planyourvisit/riveraccpts.htm)]

- <a id="activity-mulberry-bend"></a>**Mulberry Bend Overlook Trail** (30 minutes to 1 hour) - A 1.2-mile loop trail with 150 feet elevation gain, featuring paved, wheelchair-accessible path to scenic overlooks of the Missouri River; short paved trail rises 60 feet to two overlooks with wayside information; picnic table and vault toilet available near parking lot; Nebraska Highway 15, Newcastle, NE 68757 (42.714367, -96.952191) [Source: [Visit Mulberry Bend](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/visit-mulberry-bend-overlook.htm), [AllTrails Mulberry Bend](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/nebraska/mulberry-bend-overlooks)]

- <a id="activity-birdwatching"></a>**Birdwatching and Wildlife Viewing** (1-3 hours) - Over 250 bird species identified within the riverway including American bald eagles (increasing population), endangered least tern, and threatened piping plover; excellent viewing at scenic overlooks in Ponca State Park and Niobrara State Park where the Niobrara and Missouri rivers collide; deer often visible along riverbanks [Source: [Wikipedia MNRR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri_National_Recreational_River), [NPCA](https://www.npca.org/parks/missouri-national-recreational-river)]

- <a id="activity-hiking-biking"></a>**Hiking and Biking Trails** (1-4 hours depending on trail) - Multiple trails available through collaboration with federal, state, and local agencies; Yankton Trail System offers more than 30 miles of trails; Ponca State Park and Niobrara State Park maintain multi-purpose trails, scenic overlooks, and campgrounds; Niobrara State Park offers 14 miles of hiking trails [Source: [NPS Hiking & Biking](https://www.nps.gov/mnrr/hiking.htm), [Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/mnrr/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- <a id="activity-ranger-programs"></a>**Ranger-Guided Excursions** (2-3 hours per program) - Scheduled often during summer months, providing educational experiences about the river's natural and cultural history [Source: [Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/mnrr/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-paddlefish-archery"></a>**Paddlefish Archery** (4-8 hours per outing, June 1-30, sunrise to sunset) - Highly challenging bow and arrow fishing for paddlefish in designated Missouri River area from Gavins Point Dam downstream to mouth of Big Sioux River at mile marker 734; Nebraska regulations allow archery-only season during June [Source: [Nebraska Paddlefish Regulations](https://www.eregulations.com/nebraska/fishing/paddlefish-regulations), [Rivers and Trails](https://rivers.gov/river/missouri-nebraska-south-dakota)]

- <a id="gem-bow-creek"></a>**Bow Creek Recreation Area Primitive Camping** (Overnight camping) - Free primitive camping at five established campsites accessible from the river with tent pads and fire rings; Leave No Trace camping along Bow Creek and Missouri River about 2 miles northeast of Wynot, Nebraska; access via series of gravel roads following brown directional signs; call 605-665-0209 to leave itinerary for safety; dead and down wood collection allowed, motorized vehicles and horses prohibited [Source: [Bow Creek Recreation Area](https://www.nps.gov/mnrr/planyourvisit/bow-creek-recreation-area.htm), [Recreation.gov Bow Creek](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/10074384)]

- <a id="gem-goat-island"></a>**Goat Island** (Future development) - Located just downstream of Wynot, Nebraska and upstream of Vermillion, South Dakota; under development planning with possible future camping, trails, hunting, birding, and recreational opportunities [Source: [Wikipedia MNRR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri_National_Recreational_River)]

- <a id="gem-historic-sites"></a>**National Register Historic Sites** (1-2 hours each site) - Four sites listed in National Register of Historic Places: Ponca Agency, Spirit Mound, Old Baldy, and Fort Randall; natural textures and colors featuring meandering channels, shifting sandbars, and sheer chalkstone bluffs representing remnant examples of diverse shallow-water habitat [Source: [Wikipedia MNRR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri_National_Recreational_River)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-spirit-mound"></a>**Spirit Mound Historic Prairie** (1-2 hours, 5 miles north of Vermillion, SD) - Historic site visited by Lewis and Clark in 1804 featuring a moderate hiking trail to the summit with panoramic views of the Missouri River valley; 31148 SD Hwy 19, Vermillion, SD 57069 (42.86761, -96.95443); 605-987-2263 [Source: [SD GFP Spirit Mound](https://gfp.sd.gov/parks/detail/spirit-mound-historic-prairie/), [Spirit Mound Official](https://www.spiritmound.org/)]

- <a id="nearby-ponca-state-park"></a>**Ponca State Park** (Half-day to full-day, adjacent to river) - 2,100-acre state park with stunning overlooks, campgrounds, horseback trail rides, swimming pool, and boat ramps; eastern gateway to 59-mile section of Missouri National Recreational River; 88090 Spur 26 E, Ponca, NE 68770 (42.594041, -96.709481); 402-755-2284 [Source: [Ponca State Park NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/ponca-state-park.htm), [Nebraska Ponca](https://outdoornebraska.gov/location/ponca/)]

- <a id="nearby-niobrara-state-park"></a>**Niobrara State Park** (Half-day to full-day, near downstream terminus of western stretch) - State park at confluence of Missouri and Niobrara rivers offering 20 cabins, camping, horseback trail rides, fishing, boat ramps, swimming pool, and 14 miles of hiking trails; 89261 522 Ave., Niobrara, NE 68760 (42.752937, -98.065896); 402-857-3373; summer hours daily 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM, off season Monday through Friday 8:00 AM - 12:00 PM and 12:30 PM - 4:30 PM [Source: [Niobrara State Park](https://visitnebraska.com/niobrara/niobrara-state-park), [Nebraska Niobrara](https://outdoornebraska.gov/location/niobrara/)]

- <a id="nearby-gavins-point"></a>**Gavins Point Dam and Lewis and Clark Lake** (Half-day to full-day, 4 miles west of Yankton, SD) - 25-mile-long lake with 90 miles of shoreline and 27 recreation areas offering camping, fishing, boating, water-skiing, swimming, hiking, photography, biking; Lewis & Clark Visitor Center atop Calumet Bluff with spectacular views and exhibits; paved trail from Yankton to Gavins Point area; winter activities include ice fishing, cross-country skiing, snowmobiling, sledding; bald eagles common during migration and winter, waterfowl by thousands in spring and fall [Source: [Lewis Clark Trail](https://www.lewisandclark.travel/listing/lewis-and-clark-visitors-center-at-gavins-point-dam/), [SD Travel Lewis Clark](https://www.travelsouthdakota.com/yankton/national-state-parks/state-recreation-area/lewis-and-clark-state-recreation-area)]

- <a id="nearby-fort-randall"></a>**Fort Randall Dam and Lake Francis Case** (Half-day to full-day, near Pickstown, SD) - Army Corps of Engineers visitor center open Memorial Day to Labor Day, Thursday through Monday 8:00 AM - 5:30 PM with interactive exhibits on dam construction, Missouri River ecology, local history, paleontology, and Lewis and Clark expedition (free admission); Lake Francis Case offers camping, fishing (walleye, smallmouth bass, crappie, northern pike, catfish), hunting, boating, sailing, swimming, water skiing, birdwatching, and photography; Fort Randall Eagle Roost listed as National Natural Landmark in 1967 with significant eagle viewing opportunities; Randall Creek Recreation Area located 1 mile west of Pickstown off US Highway 281 (vehicle traffic and camping prohibited November 15 through third Friday in March due to bald eagle roosting) [Source: [Fort Randall Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/places/fort-randall-dam-visitor-center.htm), [SD Missouri River Fort Randall](https://www.sdmissouririver.com/directory/view/fort-randall-dam-visitor-center/)]

- <a id="nearby-karl-mundt"></a>**Karl E. Mundt National Wildlife Refuge** (2-4 hours, near Pickstown, SD) - Wildlife refuge offering birdwatching and nature viewing opportunities along the Missouri River [Source: [Nearby Attractions](https://www.nps.gov/mnrr/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm)]
