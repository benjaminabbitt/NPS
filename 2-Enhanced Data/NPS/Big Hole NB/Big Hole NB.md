# Big Hole NB (National Battlefield)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Big Hole National Battlefield Visitor Center** (16425 Highway 43 West, Wisdom, MT 59761 (45.640789, -113.643300); Summer hours: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM daily, Fall hours: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, Winter/Spring hours: 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 406-689-3155) [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/biho/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Contact](https://www.nps.gov/biho/contacts.htm), [Yelp Big Hole](https://www.yelp.com/biz/big-hole-national-battlefield-wisdom)]

**Note:** Call 406-689-3155 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. Park grounds are open sunrise to sunset daily year-round.

## Key Activities

- **Watch Orientation Film and Explore Museum Exhibits** (45-60 minutes) - View the award-winning 26-minute film titled "Weet'uciklitukt: There's No Turning Back, Battle at Big Hole" providing an introduction to the Nez Perce Flight of 1877 and the battle that took place on August 9-10, 1877, shown throughout the day with close-captioning, then explore museum exhibits featuring photographs, quotations, and belongings of battle participants and non-combatants; Big Hole National Battlefield Visitor Center, 16425 Highway 43 West, Wisdom, MT 59761; Summer hours: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM daily, Fall hours: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, Winter/Spring hours: 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; free admission; 406-689-3155 [Source: [Visit Montana](https://visitmt.com/listing/big-hole-national-battlefield-34), [Visit Bitterroot Valley](https://www.visitbitterrootvalley.com/location/big-hole-national-battlefield/), [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/258655)]

- **Hike the Nez Perce Camp Trail** (45-60 minutes) - Walk the 1.6-mile round-trip trail with no elevation gain leading to the site where the Nez Perce camp was located on the morning of August 9, 1877, when gun shots shattered a chilly dawn on a sleeping camp, with interpretive markers explaining the battle's events; trailhead at lower parking area (separate from visitor center), 16425 Highway 43 West, Wisdom, MT 59761; open year-round sunrise to sunset daily, trail guides available for $1.00 at trailheads during summer and fall; free admission; 406-689-3155 [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/biho/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [Outdoor Project](https://www.outdoorproject.com/united-states/montana/big-hole-national-battlefield), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/big-hole-national-battlefield/)]

- **Hike the Siege Area Trail** (45-60 minutes) - Explore the 1.2-mile round-trip trail with 50 feet of elevation gain to the site where the Nez Perce warriors fought a delaying action against the U.S. Army's 7th Infantry Regiment, pinning down soldiers and volunteers during the battle; trailhead at lower parking area, 16425 Highway 43 West, Wisdom, MT 59761; open year-round sunrise to sunset daily, trail guides available for $1.00 at trailheads during summer and fall; free admission; 406-689-3155 [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/biho/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [Outdoor Project](https://www.outdoorproject.com/united-states/montana/big-hole-national-battlefield), [Visit National Parks](https://www.visitnationalparks.us/park/biho)]

- **Hike the Howitzer Capture Site Trail** (30-45 minutes) - Walk the 0.8-mile round-trip trail with 320 feet of elevation gain to the location where Nez Perce warriors captured the Army's mountain howitzer during the battle, demonstrating their tactical prowess during the engagement; trailhead at lower parking area, 16425 Highway 43 West, Wisdom, MT 59761; open year-round sunrise to sunset daily, trail guides available for $1.00 at trailheads during summer and fall; free admission; 406-689-3155 [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/biho/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [Outdoor Project](https://www.outdoorproject.com/united-states/montana/big-hole-national-battlefield), [AllTrails Big Hole](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/montana/big-hole-national-battlefield-trail)]

- **Join Ranger-Guided Tours and Programs** (1-1.5 hours) - Participate in ranger-guided activities held daily during summer months, including ranger-guided tours each weekend from late June through Labor Day starting at 10:00 AM and ending by 11:30 AM, with hikes ranging from 1.2 to 2 miles covering the battle's history and significance; meet at Big Hole National Battlefield Visitor Center, 16425 Highway 43 West, Wisdom, MT 59761; summer weekends, late June through Labor Day; free with admission; check calendar for specific dates; 406-689-3155 [Source: [NPS Ranger Programs](https://www.nps.gov/biho/planyourvisit/rangerprograms.htm), [Montana Standard Summer Events](https://mtstandard.com/news/local/history-and-remembrance-things-to-do-at-the-big-hole-national-battlefield-this-summer/collection_6f87f9cf-a19f-512d-bf47-20e14f850c9d.html), [AllTrips Big Hole](https://www.explorethebitterroot.com/history_museums/big_hole_battlefield.php)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Attend Tipi Demonstration and Speaker Series** (1-2 hours per program) - Watch demonstrations of traditional tipi construction and attend speaker series lectures covering topics related to the Nez Perce War, Native American culture, and the battle's historical context, offered during summer months; Big Hole National Battlefield Visitor Center, 16425 Highway 43 West, Wisdom, MT 59761; summer months, check calendar for specific dates and times; free admission; 406-689-3155 [Source: [Visit Montana](https://www.visitmt.com/listings/general/national-historic-site/big-hole-national-battlefield), [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/biho/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [AllTrips Big Hole](https://www.explorethebitterroot.com/history_museums/big_hole_battlefield.php)]

- **Participate in Annual Commemoration Events** (2-3 hours) - Attend annual commemoration programs honoring the Nez Perce and soldiers who fought and died at Big Hole, featuring pipe ceremony, traditional drum circle, and cultural presentations that are free and open to the public (photography and filming not allowed during ceremonies); Big Hole National Battlefield, 16425 Highway 43 West, Wisdom, MT 59761; held around the August 9-10 anniversary dates; free admission; check calendar for specific dates; 406-689-3155 [Source: [Montana Standard Summer Events](https://mtstandard.com/news/local/history-and-remembrance-things-to-do-at-the-big-hole-national-battlefield-this-summer/collection_6f87f9cf-a19f-512d-bf47-20e14f850c9d.html), [Big Hole River Foundation](https://www.bhrf.org/newsandevents/the-legacy-of-the-big-hole-battlefield)]

- **Complete the Junior Ranger Program** (1-2 hours) - Pick up a free Junior Ranger activity booklet at the Visitor Center, complete activities while exploring the park and learning about the Nez Perce War, then share answers with a ranger to receive a Junior Ranger badge; Big Hole National Battlefield Visitor Center, 16425 Highway 43 West, Wisdom, MT 59761; Summer hours: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM daily, Fall hours: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, Winter/Spring hours: 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily; free; 406-689-3155 [Source: [NPS Kids & Youth](https://www.nps.gov/biho/learn/kidsyouth/index.htm), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/big-hole-national-battlefield/)]

- **Snowshoe the Battlefield Trails in Winter** (1-2 hours) - Experience the battlefield trails on foot or by snowshoeing during winter months when the landscape is covered in snow, offering a unique perspective on the challenging conditions the Nez Perce faced during their 1877 flight; trails accessible from lower parking area, 16425 Highway 43 West, Wisdom, MT 59761; winter months, sunrise to sunset daily; free admission; bring your own snowshoes; 406-689-3155 [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/biho/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Visit Montana](https://www.visitmt.com/listings/general/national-historic-site/big-hole-national-battlefield)]

## Also Nearby

- **Bannack State Park Ghost Town** (3-4 hours for visit) - Explore Montana's best-preserved ghost town and first territorial capital with over 60 structures from the 1862 gold rush era available for self-guided touring using a $2 photo tour pamphlet, including the gallows, schoolhouse, hotel, and Masonic Hall, plus ranger programs on summer weekends; 4200 Bannack Road, Dillon, MT 59725 (45.162228, -112.999454) (approximately 65 miles southeast, 1 hour 15 minutes from Big Hole); town site hours: Memorial Day through Labor Day 8:00 AM - 9:00 PM daily, rest of year 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, Visitor Center open weekends 11:00 AM - 5:00 PM in May or by appointment, seven days a week 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM late May through September; Montana resident vehicle $8, non-resident vehicle $10 [Source: [Montana FWP Bannack](https://fwp.mt.gov/stateparks/bannack-state-park), [TripAdvisor Bannack](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g45145-d107268-Reviews-Bannack_State_Park-Dillon_Montana.html), [My Itchy Travel Feet](https://myitchytravelfeet.com/relive-gold-rush-history-bannack-ghost-town/), [Yelp Bannack](https://www.yelp.com/biz/bannack-state-park-dillon)]

- **Jackson Hot Springs Lodge** (1-2 hours for soaking) - Soak in naturally heated geothermal pool waters at this historic hot springs resort that has welcomed travelers since 1911, featuring an outdoor pool and bar/restaurant service; 108 Jardine Avenue, Jackson, MT 59736 (45.367900, -113.408892) (approximately 15 miles north, 20 minutes from Big Hole); Pool Hours: Thursday-Sunday 8:00 AM - 11:00 PM, Restaurant Hours: Thursday-Sunday 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM, closed Monday-Wednesday; pool fees apply; 406-834-3151 [Source: [Jackson Hot Springs](https://jacksonhotspringslodge.com/), [Yelp Jackson Hot Springs](https://www.yelp.com/biz/jackson-hot-springs-lodge-jackson), [Southwest Montana Jackson Hot Springs](https://southwestmt.com/listings/jackson-hot-springs-lodge/)]

- **Big Hole River Fly Fishing** (half-day to full-day) - Cast for rainbow trout, brown trout, cutthroat trout, brook trout, and native fluvial Arctic grayling in one of Montana's most scenic fly fishing rivers featuring slower-moving water with grassy islands and deeply cut banks perfect for dry fly fishing, with numerous public fishing access sites including Fish Trap Fishing Access near Wisdom; Big Hole River near Wisdom, MT (multiple access points along Highway 43); open year-round sunrise to sunset, best fishing May-October; Montana fishing license required; check Montana Fish, Wildlife & Parks for current regulations [Source: [Montana Angler Big Hole](https://www.montanaangler.com/montana-fly-fishing-rivers/big-hole-river-fishing-guides), [DIY Fly Fishing Big Hole](https://diyflyfishing.com/big-hole-river-montana/), [Big Sky Fishing Big Hole](https://www.bigskyfishing.com/rivers/southwest-montana/big-hole-river/), [Big Hole River Foundation Access Sites](https://www.bhrf.org/newsandevents/uazv7ezlhk3cbfhixb3ida7p6ej2op)]

- **View Historic Beaverslide Hay Stackers** (15-30 minutes) - See the unique wooden hay-stacking structures invented in 1908 in the Big Hole Valley, featuring 30-foot wooden frames supporting inclined planes that allow horse teams to pull hay loads to 20 feet before dropping them into square pens, with the highest concentration of operational beaverslides still used each summer in late July; viewable throughout Big Hole Valley along Montana Highway 278, with interpretive marker at highway pull-off on MT-278 between Wisdom and Jackson; visible year-round from roadside, operational in late July during haying season; free viewing from public roads [Source: [Montana Historic Landscapes Beaverslide](https://montanahistoriclandscape.com/tag/beaverslide-hay-stacker/), [HMDB Beaverslide Marker](https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=210114), [Southwest Montana Valley of 10,000 Haystacks](https://southwestmt.com/specialfeatures/this-is-montana/historical-geologic-highway-signage/valley-of-10000-haystacks-the-big-hole-basin/), [NBC Montana Big Hole Barns](https://nbcmontana.com/news/montana-moment/best-of-montana-moment-barns-of-the-big-hole-land-of-10000-haystacks)]
