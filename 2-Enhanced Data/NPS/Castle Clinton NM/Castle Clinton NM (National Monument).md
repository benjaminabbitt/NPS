# Castle Clinton NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Castle Clinton Visitor Center** - The NPS passport cancellation stamp is located inside the fort; Battery Park, New York, NY 10004 (40.703636, -74.017288); Open daily 7:45 AM – 5:00 PM (closed Thanksgiving and Christmas Day); 212-344-7220 [[NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/cacl/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/castle-clinton-national-monument/)]

**Note:** Call 212-344-7220 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Free Ranger-Guided Tours** (20-30 minutes) - Free guided tours led by knowledgeable Park Rangers exploring the fort's fascinating history as a defensive fortification, entertainment venue, immigration depot, and aquarium; tours available inside Castle Clinton; daily at 10:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 2:00 PM, and 3:30 PM [[NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/cacl/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [NPS Event Details](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/event-details.htm?id=0D25A738-155D-451F-67D31ADD6827D16D), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/castle-clinton-national-monument/)]

- **Indoor Exhibits** (10 minutes) - One-room indoor exhibit featuring images, dioramas, and three detailed models showing how the structure evolved and connected to the surrounding land throughout its history; located inside the fort; open during monument hours 7:45 AM – 5:00 PM daily [[TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60763-d105811-Reviews-Castle_Clinton_National_Monument-New_York_City_New_York.html), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/castle-clinton-national-monument/)]

- **Educational Film** (20 minutes) - Film detailing the interesting history of Castle Clinton, including its service as a fort, entertainment center, immigration depot, and aquarium, shown on repeat; viewed inside the fort; available during monument operating hours [[NYC Tourism](https://www.nyctourism.com/attractions-tours/castle-clinton-national-monument/), [Outdoorsy](https://www.outdoorsy.com/guide/castle-clinton-national-monument)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (30 minutes) - Complete activity booklet to become a Junior Ranger and learn about early life in NYC; booklet available at Castle Clinton or can be completed while waiting for or riding the Statue of Liberty ferry; available during monument operating hours [[NPS Junior Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/cacl/learn/kidsyouth/beajuniorranger.htm), [Kids & Passports](https://www.kidsandpassports.com/10-junior-ranger-badges-to-earn-in-new-york-city/)]

- **Self-Guided Exploration** (15-20 minutes) - Walk through the circular reddish brownstone fort completed in 1811, exploring the historic structure and Battery Park surroundings; Castle Clinton, Battery Park, New York, NY 10004; open daily 7:45 AM – 5:00 PM [[Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/castle-clinton-national-monument/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60763-d105811-Reviews-Castle_Clinton_National_Monument-New_York_City_New_York.html)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Music at Castle Clinton Concert Series** (varies) - Annual free concert series occasionally featuring evening performances in the historic fort setting; Castle Clinton, Battery Park, New York, NY 10004; schedule varies seasonally [[Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/castle-clinton-national-monument/), [NYC Tourism](https://www.nyctourism.com/attractions-tours/castle-clinton-national-monument/)]

- **Gift Shop and Bookstore** (10-15 minutes) - Less crowded souvenir shop inside the fort offering Castle Clinton themed items, Liberty and Ellis Island souvenirs, books celebrating New York-based national monuments, and especially robust selection of Stonewall items; inside Castle Clinton; open during monument hours [[Statue of Liberty Tour Blog](https://www.statueoflibertytour.com/blog/visit-castle-clinton-downtown-nycs-mysterious-fort/)]

## Also Nearby

- **9/11 Memorial & Museum** (2-3 hours) - Museum documenting the September 11 attacks with artifacts, exhibitions, and memorial spaces honoring victims; 180 Greenwich Street, New York, NY 10006 (40.7094, -74.0133); Museum open Wednesday-Monday 9 AM – 7 PM (last entry 5:30 PM), outdoor Memorial open daily 8 AM – 8 PM [[9/11 Memorial](https://www.911memorial.org/visit/visit-museum-1), [The Tour Guy](https://thetourguy.com/travel-blog/usa/new-york-city/911-memorial/how-to-visit-ground-zero-911-memorial-museum/)]

- **SeaGlass Carousel** (5-10 minutes) - Unique carousel featuring 30 giant glowing fiberglass fish creating an immersive underwater experience with music and LED lights, homage to the first New York Aquarium formerly at the Battery; 7 State Street, The Battery Park, New York, NY 10004 (40.7025, -74.0139); Thursday 11:00 AM – 7:00 PM, Friday-Saturday 11:00 AM – 8:00 PM, Sunday 11:00 AM – 7:00 PM, Monday-Wednesday closed; $5 per person [[SeaGlass Carousel](https://www.seaglasscarousel.nyc/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/seaglass-carousel-new-york)]

- **Museum of Jewish Heritage** (2-3 hours) - Living memorial to the Holocaust featuring exhibitions about Jewish life before, during, and after the Holocaust, interactive displays, and memorial garden with Andy Goldsworthy's Garden of Stones sculpture; 36 Battery Place, New York, NY 10280 (40.703564, -74.016678); Sunday and Wednesday 10 AM – 5 PM, Thursday 10 AM – 8 PM, Friday 10 AM – 5 PM, closed Monday, Tuesday, Saturday; free admission Thursdays 4-8 PM [[Museum of Jewish Heritage](https://mjhnyc.org/visitor-information/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/museum-of-jewish-heritage-a-living-memorial-to-the-holocaust-new-york)]

- **One World Observatory** (1-2 hours) - Observation deck atop One World Trade Center offering panoramic views of New York City, interactive exhibits, and Forever Theater film; 285 Fulton Street, New York, NY 10048 (40.712742, -74.013382); daily 9 AM – 9 PM (last admission 1 hour before closing); approximately 15 minutes from Castle Clinton [[One World Observatory](https://www.oneworldobservatory.com/plan-your-visit/), [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/world-trade-center/)]

- **South Street Seaport Museum** (1-2 hours) - Maritime museum featuring exhibitions, historic ships at Pier 16, and letterpress shop exploring New York's history as a port city; 12 Fulton Street, New York, NY 10038 (40.706322, -74.002640); Wednesday-Sunday 11 AM – 5 PM, closed Monday-Tuesday; pay-what-you-wish admission; approximately 15-20 minutes walk from Castle Clinton [[South Street Seaport Museum](https://southstreetseaportmuseum.org/admission/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/south-street-seaport-museum-new-york-2)]

- **Brooklyn Bridge Walk** (30-60 minutes) - Walk across the iconic Brooklyn Bridge pedestrian pathway with views of Manhattan and Brooklyn skylines; Brooklyn Bridge, Manhattan side entrance near City Hall; open 24/7; approximately 1 mile walk from Battery Park to bridge entrance, bridge crossing is 1.1 miles [[Free Tours by Foot](https://freetoursbyfoot.com/walking-the-brooklyn-bridge/), [Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Battery-Park/Brooklyn-Bridge)]
