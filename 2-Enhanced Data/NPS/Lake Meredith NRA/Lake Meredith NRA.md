# Lake Meredith NRA (National Recreation Area)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Lake Meredith National Recreation Area Headquarters** (419 E Broadway St, Fritch, TX 79036 (35.6432673, -101.5862787); Monday-Friday 9:00 AM-4:00 PM; 806-857-3151) - Call to confirm current stamp location before visiting [Source: [NPS Contact Info](https://www.nps.gov/lamr/contacts.htm), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/lake-meredith-national-recreation-fritch)]

**Note:** Lake Meredith was featured in the 2015 Passport Stamp Series. Contact park headquarters to confirm exact stamp location. [Source: [NPS 2015 Stamps](https://www.nps.gov/lamr/planyourvisit/2015-passport-stamps.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Boating** (2-4 hours) - Boating is one of the most popular water sports at Lake Meredith, with five boat launch ramps and one marina available; boat ramps at Sanford-Yake, Cedar Canyon, and Blue West are open for public use; no fee for boating but boat launch fees are $4 for one day, $10 for 3 days, $40 for the year; rentals available starting at $275; Lake Meredith Marina, Fritch, TX; open 24/7 [Source: [NPS Boating](https://www.nps.gov/lamr/planyourvisit/boating.htm), [Outdoorsy](https://www.outdoorsy.com/guide/lake-meredith-national-recreation-area), [Lake Boat Rental](https://www.lakeboatrental.com/lakes/texas/lake-meredith)]

- **Fishing** (2-4 hours) - Lake Meredith has the best walleye fishing in Texas, with peak season from April through June; also features bass, catfish, crappie, sunfish, and panfish; fishing available year-round; various boat launch locations; open 24/7 [Source: [TPWD Fishing](https://tpwd.texas.gov/fishboat/fish/recreational/lakes/meredith/), [NPS Fishing](https://www.nps.gov/lamr/planyourvisit/fishing.htm)]

- **Swimming** (1-2 hours) - Several designated swimming areas roped off where watercraft are prohibited for safety; most campgrounds have designated swimming areas nearby; free access; various locations throughout recreation area; open during daylight hours [Source: [Travel.com](https://travel.com/regions/north-america/united-states/texas/lake-meredith-national-recreation-area-texas-best-things-to-do-top-picks/), [Outdoorsy](https://www.outdoorsy.com/guide/lake-meredith-national-recreation-area)]

- **Harbor Bay Trail** (3-4 hours) - Moderate to hard trail covering 2-8 miles depending on loop chosen; features diverse habitats including wetlands, rocky areas, marsh, short grass prairie, and shoreline; trail runs from Harbor Bay to Meredith Way (5.23 miles total) with several steep slopes and switchbacks; trailhead at Harbor Bay; open 24/7 [Source: [Boondocker's Bible](https://www.boondockersbible.com/nps/lake-meredith-national-recreation-area/), [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/lamr/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)]

- **South Turkey Creek Trail** (3-4 hours) - Easy to moderate trail covering 6.17 miles; offers the best lake views of all trails; starts at Harbor Bay; open 24/7 [Source: [Boondocker's Bible](https://www.boondockersbible.com/nps/lake-meredith-national-recreation-area/), [Western National Parks](https://wnpa.org/a-gem-in-the-texas-panhandle/)]

- **Mullinaw Trail** (2-3 hours) - Easy moderate trail covering 4.3 miles; mostly flat landscape with very few slopes or hills; follows the Canadian River; particularly beautiful in fall; trailhead at Mullinaw Campground; open 24/7 [Source: [Boondocker's Bible](https://www.boondockersbible.com/nps/lake-meredith-national-recreation-area/), [Western National Parks](https://wnpa.org/a-gem-in-the-texas-panhandle/)]

- **Davis Canyon Trail** (3-4 hours) - Moderate 6.4 mile trail with several slopes; excellent birding opportunities; open 24/7 [Source: [Boondocker's Bible](https://www.boondockersbible.com/nps/lake-meredith-national-recreation-area/), [Western National Parks](https://wnpa.org/a-gem-in-the-texas-panhandle/)]

- **Camping** (overnight) - Lake Meredith offers 11-13 different campgrounds with comfortable campsites; camping and visitation are free; stay up to 14 days; locations include Mullinaw, Rosita Flats OHV Area, Plum Creek (Upper, Middle, Lower), Blue Creek, and Bugbee; most sites offer scenic lookouts, wooded areas, or lakefront access; quiet hours 10 PM-6 AM; campgrounds open 24/7 [Source: [NPS Camping](https://www.nps.gov/lamr/planyourvisit/camping.htm), [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60714-d277425-Reviews-Lake_Meredith_National_Recreation_Area-Fritch_Texas.html)]

- **Alibates Flint Quarries Ranger-Guided Tour** (2 hours) - Tour of ancient flint quarries with 13,000+ years of human history; covers approximately 2 miles with 170 feet elevation gain; reservations required; tours offered at 10 AM and 1 PM April-October (call to confirm year-round); accessed only via ranger-guided tour; Alibates Flint Quarries Visitor Center, P.O. Box 1460, Fritch, TX 79036; call 806-857-6680 for reservations [Source: [NPS Alibates Tours](https://www.nps.gov/alfl/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/texas/alibates-flint-quarries-national-monument), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/alibates-flint-quarries-national-monument/)]

- **Scuba Diving** (2-4 hours) - Lake Meredith offers scuba diving opportunities with clear waters and diverse underwater landscapes; various access points; open during daylight hours [Source: [Travel.com](https://travel.com/regions/north-america/united-states/texas/lake-meredith-national-recreation-area-texas-best-things-to-do-top-picks/), [National Park Foundation](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/lake-meredith-national-recreation-area)]

- **Canoeing & Kayaking** (2-4 hours) - Peaceful paddling opportunities on the lake; rentals available; various launch points throughout recreation area; open during daylight hours [Source: [NPS Kayaking](https://www.nps.gov/lamr/planyourvisit/kayaking.htm)]

- **Wildlife Viewing** (1-2 hours) - View birds, coyotes, deer, raccoons, skunks, and porcupines; over 300 bird species migrate through the area; best wildlife viewing in morning and evening hours; Panhandle Plains Wildlife Trail winds through areas along Cas Johnson Road to McBride Canyon and Blue West Road to Blue West; open 24/7 [Source: [NPS Wildlife](https://www.nps.gov/lamr/learn/nature/wildlife.htm), [NPS Wildlife Viewing](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/wildlife-viewing-at-lake-meredith.htm)]

- **Off-Road Vehicles** (2-4 hours) - Two designated off-roading areas: Blue Creek and Rosita Flat; open during daylight hours [Source: [Outdoorsy](https://www.outdoorsy.com/guide/lake-meredith-national-recreation-area), [Western National Parks](https://wnpa.org/a-gem-in-the-texas-panhandle/)]

- **Hunting** (4-8 hours) - Year-round hunting subject to state laws and federal restrictions; primary game includes whitetail deer, mule deer, turkey, dove, quail, geese, and ducks; contact park headquarters for current regulations; 806-857-3151 [Source: [NPS Hunting](https://www.nps.gov/lamr/planyourvisit/hunting.htm), [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60714-d277425-Reviews-Lake_Meredith_National_Recreation_Area-Fritch_Texas.html)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Spring Canyon Birdwalk** (1-2 hours) - Special birding area with boardwalk and dock devoted to birdwatching; features marshes, ponds, and beautiful views of Canadian River Valley; over 230 species recorded including painted bunting (nests in McBride Canyon), wild turkey, red-headed woodpeckers (summer), mountain bluebirds and American tree sparrows (winter); designated as significant birding area by National Audubon Society; located below Sanford Dam with vessel launch at Sanford-Yake Campground; open 24/7 [Source: [Birding Hotspots](https://birdinghotspots.org/hotspot/L2606162), [Western National Parks](https://wnpa.org/a-gem-in-the-texas-panhandle/), [Great Outdoors](https://destinations.thegreatoutdoors.io/listing/birdwatching-at-spring-canyon/)]

- **Blue West Scenic Overlook** (30 minutes-1 hour) - Breathtaking views slightly off the beaten path; accessed via Blue West Road; excellent for photography and sunset viewing; open 24/7 [Source: [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60714-d277425-Reviews-or10-Lake_Meredith_National_Recreation_Area-Fritch_Texas.html)]

- **Mesquite Trail** (10 minutes) - Short 0.3-mile interpretive trail near Visitor Center; quick nature walk showcasing local flora; starts next to Visitor Center at 419 E Broadway St, Fritch, TX; open 24/7 [Source: [Presidential Adventures](https://theadventuringheidi.com/2023/03/01/presidential-adventure-23-day-2-alibates-flint-quarries-national-monument-lake-meredith-national-recreation-area-palo-duro-canyon-route-66/)]

- **Shoreline Hiking & Biking Trails** (1-3 hours) - Created when lake water levels dropped in 2013; trails along shoreline, on top of mesas, and through wetlands; less crowded than main trails; various access points; open 24/7 [Source: [Western National Parks](https://wnpa.org/a-gem-in-the-texas-panhandle/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Palo Duro Canyon State Park** (4-8 hours, 50 miles) - Second largest canyon in the United States; offers biking, hiking, bird watching, horseback riding, and camping; day use fee $8 per person 13+; park hours 7:00 AM-10:00 PM (gate closes automatically); 11450 Park Rd 5, Canyon, TX 79015 (34.9826, -101.6801); 806-488-2227; best visited early morning or late evening due to summer heat (90-115°F) [Source: [TPWD Palo Duro](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/palo-duro-canyon), [Visit Amarillo](https://www.visitamarillo.com/listing/palo-duro-canyon-state-park/621/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/palo-duro-canyon-state-park-canyon-3)]

- **Cadillac Ranch** (30 minutes-1 hour, 40 miles) - Iconic Route 66 art installation featuring 10 half-buried Cadillacs; visitors can spray paint the cars; free admission; open 24/7; best visited early morning or late afternoon for photos and smaller crowds; bring spray paint; 13651 I-40 Frontage Rd, Amarillo, TX 79124 (35.1872, -101.9871); exit I-40 at exit 60, then frontage road south side east for 1 mile [Source: [Route 66 Road Trip](https://www.route66roadtrip.com/cadillac-ranch-route-66-amarillo-texas.htm), [Wanderlust in Real Life](https://wanderlustinreallife.com/cadillac-ranch-guide-amarillo-texas/), [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g30165-d144342-Reviews-Cadillac_Ranch-Amarillo_Texas.html)]

- **Don Harrington Discovery Center** (2-3 hours, 40 miles) - Interactive science center and planetarium with hands-on exhibits and Space Theater; admission $11 adults, $8 children/students/seniors/military, free for children under 2; Tuesday-Saturday 9:30 AM-4:30 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM-4:30 PM, closed Monday; 1200 Streit Drive, Amarillo, TX 79106 (35.2012, -101.8346); 806-355-9547 [Source: [Discovery Center](https://discoverycentercollective.org/don-harrington-discovery-center/), [Visit Amarillo](https://www.visitamarillo.com/listing/don-harrington-discovery-center/495/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/don-harrington-discovery-center-amarillo)]

- **Buffalo Lake National Wildlife Refuge** (2-3 hours, 35 miles) - Small reservoir popular for wildlife viewing, hiking, fishing, and photography; best viewing early morning and evening October-March; free admission; open 8:00 AM-6:00 PM (October-March), extended to 8:00 PM during summer; entrance 1.5 miles south of Umbarger on FM 168 (10 miles west of Canyon) (34.9177, -101.9318); best visited October-March [Source: [USFWS Buffalo Lake](https://www.fws.gov/refuge/buffalo-lake/visit-us), [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g55580-d10344368-Reviews-Buffalo_Lake_National_Wildlife_Refuge-Canyon_Texas.html)]

**Total Recommended Time:** 1-3 days depending on activities - A quick visit to get passport stamp and see scenic overlooks can be done in 2-3 hours; a full day allows for one water activity (boating/fishing/swimming) plus one hiking trail; 2-3 days recommended for visitors wanting to experience multiple water activities, hike several trails, camp overnight, and take the Alibates Flint Quarries tour. Many visitors camp for up to 14 days to fully explore the area.
