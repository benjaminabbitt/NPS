## Key Activities

- **Self-Guided Walking Tour** (30-60 minutes) - Explore the archaeological site of New Philadelphia on a quarter-mile one-way walking path with interpretive signs and markers providing information about the town layout and its significance; the trail is level natural surface with mown grass, uneven and sometimes muddy in places; 29766 306 Lane, Barry, Illinois 62312 (39.70, -91.04); open year-round, 7 days a week, daytime visits recommended [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/neph/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/neph/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [New Philadelphia AR Tour](http://newphiladelphiail.org/explore-new-philadelphia/augmented-reality/)]

- **Augmented Reality Cell Phone Tour** (30-60 minutes) - Download the free New Philadelphia AR App at the Information Kiosk using free WiFi (password: Frank1836) and follow the quarter-mile trail to 11 stops where animated buildings, people, and farm animals appear on your mobile device's camera, bringing the 19th-century town to life with audio narration and sound effects; includes stops at the old school house, post office, and homes; 29766 306 Lane, Barry, Illinois 62312 (39.70, -91.04); open year-round, 7 days a week [Source: [New Philadelphia AR App](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.amakawa.newphiladelphia), [Cultural Heritage Informatics](https://chi.anthropology.msu.edu/2015/11/new-philadelphia-app/), [NPS AR Tour](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/augmented-reality-tour.htm), [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/neph/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

- **Information Kiosk Exhibits** (15-30 minutes) - View pavilion exhibits with signs telling the story of Free Frank McWorter, his wife Lucy, and others who lived in this multiracial frontier town; includes information about the town's role as an Underground Railroad station; 29766 306 Lane, Barry, Illinois 62312 (39.70, -91.04); open year-round, 7 days a week; free WiFi available [Source: [NPS New Philadelphia](https://www.nps.gov/neph/), [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/neph/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Underground Railroad Connection** (included in walking tour) - Learn about New Philadelphia's role as a station on the Underground Railroad before the Civil War, helping escaped slaves move to Canada; this lesser-known aspect of the town's history is documented through historical records and interpretive signage; 29766 306 Lane, Barry, Illinois 62312 (39.70, -91.04); open year-round, 7 days a week [Source: [New Philadelphia Town Site](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g35648-d8681270-Reviews-New_Philadelphia_Town_Site-Barry_Illinois.html), [Great River Road](https://www.greatriverroad.com/tristate-attractions-1/new-philadelphia-town-site)]

- **Integrated Frontier Community History** (included in exhibits) - Discover the unique story of a multiracial town where Black and white villagers lived side by side and children attended one integrated public school in the 1800s, though the town's cemetery remained segregated; census records and historical documents reveal this rare social experiment on the Illinois frontier; Information Kiosk, 29766 306 Lane, Barry, Illinois 62312 (39.70, -91.04); open year-round, 7 days a week [Source: [New Philadelphia Town Site](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g35648-d8681270-Reviews-New_Philadelphia_Town_Site-Barry_Illinois.html), [University of Illinois Historic Landscapes](http://faculty.las.illinois.edu/cfennell/NP/)]

- **Jim's Journey: The Huck Finn Freedom Center** (45-60 minutes, 27 miles west) - Visit the country's first memorial to Jim from "Adventures of Huckleberry Finn" and the only African American history museum in Northeast Missouri, paying homage to Jim's real-life prototype Daniel Quarles, an enslaved person on Samuel Clemens' uncle's farm; Hannibal, Missouri (specific address not widely published); seasonally open Friday-Saturday 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM (Memorial Day through Labor Day weekends, closed holiday Mondays); free admission, donations welcome; call (217) 617-1507 for private tour arrangements [Source: [Jim's Journey](https://www.jimsjourney.org/visit), [Visit Hannibal](https://visithannibal.com/explore/the-huck-finn-freedom-center/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g44460-d7116647-Reviews-Jim_s_Journey_The_Huck_Finn_Freedom_Center-Hannibal_Missouri.html)]

- **Lincoln Home National Historic Site** (1-2 hours, 77 miles east) - Tour the only home Abraham and Mary Lincoln owned, beautifully restored to its 1860 appearance where Lincoln lived for 17 years before leaving for Washington to become the 16th President; explore four blocks of restored homes with exhibits about Lincoln's neighbors and daily life, watch films at the Visitor's Center, and attend presentations by Mary Todd Lincoln and Abraham Lincoln impersonators; 413 S. 8th Street, Springfield, Illinois 62701; daily 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM, closed January 1, Thanksgiving, December 25; free admission; 217-492-4241 [Source: [NPS Lincoln Home Hours](https://www.nps.gov/liho/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Visit Springfield](https://www.visitspringfieldillinois.com/LocationDetails/?id=Lincoln-Home-National-Historic-Site), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60887-d143197-Reviews-Lincoln_Home_National_Historic_Site-Springfield_Illinois.html)]

- **Pike County Courthouse** (15-30 minutes, 20 miles southeast) - View breathtaking Gothic Revival architecture based on an octagonal (24-sided) floor plan; built in 1894 and listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1980, this historic courthouse draws visitors from across the region; Pittsfield, Illinois; viewable from exterior anytime, interior hours vary [Source: [Pike County IL Attractions](https://www.greatriverroad.com/pike-county-il-attractions), [See Quincy](https://seequincy.com/things-to-do/road-trips/pike-county/)]

- **Abraham Lincoln Heritage Sites in Pike County** (1-2 hours, 20 miles southeast) - Take Abe Lincoln's Talking House Tour, a self-guided tour of eleven homes with Abraham Lincoln significance in Pittsfield by tuning your radio to the station listed on house markers; Lincoln spent considerable time in Pike County between 1838 and 1858 before his presidency; various locations in Pittsfield, Illinois; accessible anytime [Source: [Pike County Attractions](https://www.greatriverroad.com/pike-county-il-attractions), [Heartland Lodge](https://www.heartlandlodge.com/pike-county-il-top-ten-vacation-ideas/)]
