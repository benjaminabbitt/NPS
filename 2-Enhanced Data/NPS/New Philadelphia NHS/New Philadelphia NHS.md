# New Philadelphia NHS (National Historic Site)

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-augmented-reality"></a>**Augmented Reality (AR) Walking Tour** (45 minutes to 1 hour) - A self-guided tour using a free mobile app (available on Apple App Store and Google Play as "New Phil AR Tour") that guides visitors along a 1/4-mile level walking path with 11 interpretive stops. The path features mown grass surface (sometimes uneven and muddy) where visitors can view virtually reconstructed portions of the historic town with audio narration and see animated historical figures and farm animals providing insights into 19th-century daily life at New Philadelphia. WiFi password "Frank1836" is provided on-site. Begin at the Information Kiosk at the south end of the parking lot. [Source: [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/neph/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [New Philadelphia AR](http://newphiladelphiail.org/explore-new-philadelphia/augmented-reality/), [NPS AR Tour](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/augmented-reality-tour.htm)]

- <a id="activity-visitor-center-exhibits-and-information-kiosk"></a>**Visitor Center Exhibits and Information Kiosk** (30-45 minutes) - Explore interpretive panels at the main information kiosk explaining the landscape, history of Frank McWorter and New Philadelphia, and the site's significance as the first African American-founded and registered town in the United States. The kiosk features QR code posts for additional AR experiences and information about the town's integrated community, Underground Railroad connections, and McWorter family's freedom-buying achievements [Source: [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/neph/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

- <a id="activity-guided-tours"></a>**Guided Tours** (1-2 hours) - The New Philadelphia Association offers guided tours of the historic site by appointment. Contact the New Philadelphia Association at 1-833-582-9622 to arrange group tours with trained guides who can provide deeper historical context and stories about the town's founding and significance [Source: [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/neph/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

- <a id="activity-landscape-interpretation"></a>**Landscape Interpretation** (15-30 minutes) - Stand at the main overlook and use the interpretive panels and guideposts to read the landscape and understand where key buildings, streets, and community spaces were located. The 144-lot original town was arranged in a 12x12 square grid with 22 named crisscrossing streets [Source: [NPS Places](https://www.nps.gov/places/new-philadelphia-nhs.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-lucy-s-butterfly-garden-and-pollinator-plot"></a>**Lucy's Butterfly Garden and Pollinator Plot** (15-30 minutes) - Located on the grounds of New Philadelphia NHS, these garden areas honor Lucy McWorter (Frank's wife) and serve as both naturalistic spaces and educational features about native plants and pollinator species that would have been present in the 1830s frontier landscape. The gardens provide a quiet contemplative space while reinforcing the agricultural heritage of the site [Source: [NPS Places](https://www.nps.gov/places/new-philadelphia-nhs.htm)]

- <a id="gem-frank-mcworter-s-freedom-story-archaeological-sites"></a>**Frank McWorter's Freedom Story Archaeological Sites** (Variable with guided tour) - Explore specific locations where archaeological evidence has revealed information about how McWorter's family lived, worked, and contributed to the community. These sites demonstrate the community's diverse occupations including cabinetmakers, shoemakers, a wheelwright, carpenter, physician, teachers, ministers, merchants, and blacksmiths. The site served as both a stagecoach stop and postal station [Source: [NPS History Lesson](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/-h-our-lesson-from-freedom-seeker-to-town-founder-free-frank-mcworter-and-new-philadelphia.htm)]

- <a id="gem-underground-railroad-station-history"></a>**Underground Railroad Station History** (With guided tour or research) - Learn about New Philadelphia's role as an Underground Railroad station near the Missouri border. McWorter's cabin reportedly contained a cellar where freedom seekers could hide on their journey north, making this integrated community a crucial waystation on the path to freedom. This lesser-known aspect of the site connects to the broader story of resistance and liberation [Source: [NPS History Culture](https://home.nps.gov/neph/learn/historyculture/index.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-mark-twain-cave-complex"></a>**Mark Twain Cave Complex** (27 miles west in Hannibal, Missouri; 60 minutes driving; 1-1.5 hours on-site) - The oldest continuously operating show cave in Missouri (since 1886), featuring a 55-60 minute guided cave tour through electrically lit passages. The cave covers 3 miles with four entrances and maintains a year-round temperature of 52°F. Located at 300 Cave Hollow Rd, Hannibal, MO 63401; open daily 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. The complex also includes the Mark Twain Boyhood Home & Museum and related attractions celebrating the author's connection to the region [Source: [Mark Twain Cave](https://www.marktwaincave.com/), [Visit Hannibal](https://visithannibal.com/explore/mark-twain-cave-complex/)]

- <a id="nearby-quincy-underground-railroad-museum"></a>**Quincy Underground Railroad Museum (Dr. Richard Eells House)** (36 miles east in Quincy, Illinois; 40 minutes driving; 1.5-2 hours on-site) - One of the earliest and most significant documented Underground Railroad sites in the United States, this museum occupies the oldest standing two-story brick house in Quincy. The home was a station on the Underground Railroad in the 1840s, and Dr. Richard Eells is credited with helping several hundred enslaved people reach freedom. The museum features tours, video presentations, galleries explaining abolition history, and information about a nine-stop tour of Underground Railroad locations around Quincy including hidden passageways and secret rooms. The site emphasizes complementary African American freedom history [Source: [Quincy Underground Railroad Museum](https://www.quincyundergroundrailroad.org/)]

- <a id="nearby-pike-county-historic-museum"></a>**Pike County Historic Museum (East School Museum)** (15 miles south in Pittsfield, Illinois; 20-30 minutes driving; 1-2 hours on-site) - Located in a renovated 1866 East Ward School (listed on the National Register of Historic Places), this museum showcases local history with memorabilia including a dray wagon, printing press, wedding dresses, furniture, dishes, local histories, photographs, and documents. Free admission with donations appreciated. Hours: Saturday and Sunday noon to 4:00 PM (May through October); closed winters but available by appointment. Address: 400 E Jefferson St, Pittsfield, IL 62363; Phone: 217-285-6335 [Source: [Great River Road](https://www.greatriverroad.com/tristate-attractions-1/pike-county-historic-museum)]

- <a id="nearby-pike-county-all-wars-museum"></a>**Pike County All Wars Museum** (15 miles south in Pittsfield, Illinois; 20-30 minutes driving; 1-1.5 hours on-site) - Located at 320 N Illinois St, Pittsfield, IL, this museum focuses on the history of Pike County residents who served in military conflicts. During the Civil War alone, 3,124 troops from Pike County's nearly 30,000 citizens served, making this an important regional military history resource [Source: [Pike County Historical Society](https://pikecountyilhistoricalsociety.org/)]

## Total Recommended Time

**Total recommended visit time for New Philadelphia NHS: 2-3 hours**

This estimate includes 45 minutes to 1 hour for the self-guided augmented reality walking tour with 11 interpretive stops, 30-45 minutes exploring the visitor center exhibits and information kiosk, 15-30 minutes for the butterfly garden and pollinator plot, and additional time for landscape interpretation and reflection. A comprehensive visit that includes a guided tour option would extend to 3-4 hours. The site is open every day of the year with free admission, though most services and stamp locations are in nearby Barry, 4 miles west.

[Sources: [NPS Places](https://www.nps.gov/places/new-philadelphia-nhs.htm), [NPS Passport Cancellation Stations](https://www.nps.gov/neph/planyourvisit/nps-passport-cancellation-stations.htm), [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/neph/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [NPS History Culture](https://home.nps.gov/neph/learn/historyculture/index.htm), [NPS AR Tour](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/augmented-reality-tour.htm), [New Philadelphia AR](http://newphiladelphiail.org/explore-new-philadelphia/augmented-reality/), [NPS History Lesson](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/-h-our-history-lesson-from-freedom-seeker-to-town-founder-free-frank-mcworter-and-new-philadelphia.htm)]
