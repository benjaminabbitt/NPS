# Blackstone River Valley NHP (National Historical Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Old Slater Mill Visitor Center** (67 Roosevelt Ave, Pawtucket, RI 02860 [41.8775, -71.3825]; Friday-Monday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM; 401-725-8638) - Main visitor center with outdoor stamp station accessible even when closed [Source: [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Passport](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/planyourvisit/passport.htm)]
- **River Bend Farm Visitor Center** (287 Oak Street, Uxbridge, MA 01569 [42.0939, -71.6238]; Daily 8:30 AM-4:00 PM except Thanksgiving and Christmas; 508-278-7604) - Gateway to Blackstone River and Canal Heritage State Park [Source: [Blackstone Heritage Corridor](https://blackstoneheritagecorridor.org/exploring-the-blackstone-river-valley/heritage-centers/river-bend-farm/)]

**Note:** The park has stamp locations throughout the valley. Old Slater Mill features an outdoor stamp station for access outside visitor center hours. Call ahead to confirm current stamp availability at (401) 725-8638. [Source: [NPS Passport](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/planyourvisit/passport.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Old Slater Mill Guided Tours** (30-45 minutes) - Join a Park Ranger for guided tours of America's first cotton-spinning mill at 10:30 AM, 12:30 PM, and 2:30 PM Thursday-Sunday, exploring the legacy of the Industrial Revolution; 67 Roosevelt Ave, Pawtucket, RI 02860 [41.8775, -71.3825]; Thursday-Sunday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM; 401-725-8638 [Source: [NPS Slater Mill Tours](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/ranger-guided-tours-slater-mill.htm), [Visit Rhode Island](https://www.visitrhodeisland.com/listing/old-slater-mill-national-historic-landmark/8527/)]

- **Blackstone River Bikeway** (2-6.5 hours) - Bike along 18+ miles of paved, flat trails featuring historic canal towpaths and scenic river views, with completed sections including Worcester to Millbury (2.5 miles, 2-2.5 hours), Uxbridge to Woonsocket (5 miles), and Woonsocket to Valley Falls (11 miles); access points throughout the valley; dawn to dusk [Source: [NPS Bikeway](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/blackstone-river-greenway-and-bikeway.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/rhode-island/blackstone-river-bikeway-trail), [TrailLink](https://www.traillink.com/trail/blackstone-river-greenway/)]

- **River Bend Farm Trails** (1-2 hours) - Walk or bike 3.6 miles of multi-use trails winding through wooded corridors along the historic Blackstone Canal towpath, with restored barn exhibits on valley life; 287 Oak Street, Uxbridge, MA 01569 [42.0939, -71.6238]; Daily 8:30 AM-4:00 PM; 508-278-7604 [Source: [Mass.gov State Park](https://www.mass.gov/locations/blackstone-river-and-canal-heritage-state-park), [Blackstone Heritage Corridor](https://blackstoneheritagecorridor.org/exploring-the-blackstone-river-valley/heritage-centers/river-bend-farm/)]

- **Ranger-Led Walking Tours** (1 hour) - Join Park Rangers for guided walks at varying Blackstone Valley locations during June, July, and August, plus Thursday evening Ranger Walkabouts at 6:30 PM; schedule varies by location and season; 401-725-8638 [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [NPS Top 10 Tips](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/top-10-tips-for-visiting-blackstone-river-valley-nhp.htm)]

- **Blackstone Valley Explorer Riverboat Tours** (50 minutes) - Cruise on a 40-passenger tour boat exploring the history, ecology, and culture of the Blackstone River Valley with binoculars and nature guides provided, departing at 1:00 PM, 2:00 PM, and 3:00 PM on Sunday afternoons plus Saturday evening cruises; May 25-October 26; Central Falls, RI; $10 pre-booked online [Source: [Explorer River Tours](https://tourblackstone.com/listing/explorer-river-tours/1478/), [River Tour Blackstone](https://www.rivertourblackstone.com/site/), [Visit Rhode Island](https://www.visitrhodeisland.com/listing/explorer-river-tours/8703/)]

- **Paddling the Blackstone** (2-4 hours) - Canoe, kayak, or paddleboard along the Blackstone River with multiple access points throughout the valley; various locations; dawn to dusk [Source: [NPS Paddling](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/planyourvisit/outdooractivities.htm)]

- **Junior Angler Fishing Activities** (2 hours) - Join park staff Sunday afternoons in June and July for free family fishing activities to catch fish, learn about the river ecosystem, and earn a Junior Angler badge; specific locations announced; 401-725-8638 [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **King Philip's Trail to Lookout Rock** (2-3 hours) - Hike this peaceful trail through wooded paths and open areas along the Blackstone River and Canal State Park to Lookout Rock, one of the most spectacular viewpoints in the valley overlooking the Blackstone River winding through Northbridge and Uxbridge; River Bend Farm area, Uxbridge, MA; dawn to dusk [Source: [AllTrails State Park](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/massachusetts/blackstone-river-and-canal-state-park), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g41936-d13335992-Reviews-Blackstone_River_Valley_National_Historical_Park-Whitinsville_Massachusetts.html), [Earth of Hearts](https://earthofhearts.com/blackstone-river-canal-heritage-state-park/)]

- **Historic Canal Locks Exploration** (1-2 hours) - Discover remnants of the original Blackstone Canal including intact granite locks along trails like Goat Hill Trail, with stonewalls, rock ledges, and canal features; Blackstone River and Canal Heritage State Park, Uxbridge, MA; dawn to dusk [Source: [Mass.gov Walking Tour](https://www.mass.gov/doc/towpath-walking-tour/download), [State Parks Info](https://stateparks.info/where-history-flows-exploring-the-blackstone-river-and-canal-heritage-state-park/)]

- **Kelly House Transportation Museum** (30-45 minutes) - Visit the Captain Wilbur Kelly House along the Blackstone River Bikeway to explore exhibits on transportation modes in the Blackstone Valley from the late 18th through early 20th centuries, including the river, canal, and railroad; along Blackstone River Bikeway; check local hours [Source: [Blackstone Heritage Corridor](https://blackstonevalleycorridor.org/explore/visitorcenters/), [Harvard Magazine](https://www.harvardmagazine.com/2020/04/h2-blackstone-river-valley)]

## Also Nearby

- **Museum of Work and Culture** (1.5-2 hours, 5 miles from Pawtucket) - Explore nine immersive exhibits across two floors highlighting the lives of French-Canadian immigrants who worked in the textile mills, operated by the Rhode Island Historical Society; 42 South Main Street, Woonsocket, RI 02895 [42.0029, -71.5148]; Tuesday-Friday 9:30 AM-4:00 PM, Saturday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM-4:00 PM, closed Monday; Adults $8, Seniors/Students $6; 401-769-9675 [Source: [Museum of Work & Culture](https://www.rihs.org/locations/museum-of-work-culture/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60741-d105965-Reviews-Museum_of_Work_and_Culture-Woonsocket_Rhode_Island.html)]

- **Roger Williams Park Zoo** (3-4 hours, 7 miles from Pawtucket) - Visit one of the nation's oldest zoos (opened 1872) featuring over 100 species including African elephants, Masai giraffes, snow leopards, red pandas, and Komodo dragons across 40 acres of naturalistic woodlands; 1000 Elmwood Avenue, Providence, RI 02907; check website for seasonal hours and admission prices [Source: [Roger Williams Park Zoo](https://www.rwpzoo.org/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60946-d105259-Reviews-Roger_Williams_Park_Zoo-Providence_Rhode_Island.html)]

- **WaterFire Providence** (2-3 hours, 7 miles from Pawtucket) - Experience the iconic WaterFire installation by Barnaby Evans featuring small bonfires lit in iron braziers along the Woonasquatucket, Moshassuck, and Providence rivers, accompanied by music and cultural performances; downtown Providence rivers; select evenings late spring through early autumn; free admission [Source: [Visit Providence](https://www.goprovidence.com/things-to-do/waterfire/), [TripAdvisor Roger Williams Park](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60946-d531789-Reviews-Roger_Williams_Park-Providence_Rhode_Island.html)]

- **Blackstone Canal District Walking Tour, Worcester** (2 hours, 15 miles from Uxbridge) - Take a self-guided tour of eleven historic buildings reflecting the history of Worcester's Canal District along the Blackstone Canal; downtown Worcester, MA; 1.3 miles; dawn to dusk [Source: [GPS My City](https://www.gpsmycity.com/tours/canal-district-walking-tour-5312.html), [Canal District](http://thecanaldistrict.com/historical-tour/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 4-6 hours for core park experience including Old Slater Mill tour (45 minutes), visitor center exploration (30-60 minutes), and either a bikeway ride (2-3 hours) or riverboat tour (1 hour with travel). Full-day visits (8-10 hours) can include additional activities like hiking to Lookout Rock and exploring canal locks.
