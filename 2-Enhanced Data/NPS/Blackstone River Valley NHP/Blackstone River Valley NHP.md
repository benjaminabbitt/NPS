# Blackstone River Valley NHP (National Historical Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Old Slater Mill Visitor Center** (67 Roosevelt Avenue, Pawtucket, RI 02860; 41.8784, -71.3839; Hours vary by season, check website; Phone: 401-725-8638) - Primary visitor center at the birthplace of America's Industrial Revolution. Exhibits on textile manufacturing history and early water-powered mills. Passport stamps available. [Source: [NPS Blackstone River Valley](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/index.htm)]

- **Bancroft Memorial Library** (50 Hopedale Street, Hopedale, MA 01747; 42.1301, -71.5412; Hours vary by day, check website; Phone: 508-634-2209) - Alternative stamp location in the Hopedale model industrial community. Library contains archives related to Draper Corporation manufacturing history. [Source: [NPS Blackstone River Valley](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

**Note:** Call 401-725-8638 for current hours as visitor center schedules vary seasonally.

## Key Activities

- **Old Slater Mill Tour** (1-1.5 hours) - Guided tour of the 1793 mill where Samuel Slater introduced water-powered cotton spinning to America. See working textile machinery demonstrations and learn about the birth of American industry. 67 Roosevelt Avenue, Pawtucket, RI; seasonal hours. [Source: [NPS Blackstone River Valley](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/index.htm)]

- **Self-Guided Mill Village Walking Tours** (1-2 hours each) - Digital walking tours exploring historic mill villages throughout the Blackstone River Valley. Use your smartphone to access audio content and historical photos at your own pace. Multiple villages including Slatersville, Hopedale, and Whitinsville. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Ranger-Led Programs** (1-2 hours) - Join rangers for guided hikes, mill tours, and educational programs. Ranger walkabouts at historic sites like Kelly House and Kelly Mill explore industrial heritage. Check park calendar for current schedule. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Blackstone River Bikeway** (2-6 hours) - Multi-use path following the river corridor with access to historic sites, mills, and villages. Sections in both Rhode Island and Massachusetts. Perfect for biking or walking to experience the industrial landscape. [Source: [NPS Blackstone River Valley](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/index.htm)]

- **Kayaking the Blackstone River** (2-4 hours) - Paddle the river that powered America's Industrial Revolution. Various launch points along the 48-mile river. See historic mill buildings from the water. Bring your own kayak or canoe. [Source: [NPS Blackstone River Valley](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Blackstone River State Park** (2-4 hours) - Rhode Island state park along the river with hiking trails, picnic areas, and access to the bikeway. Captain Wilbur Kelly House Museum features exhibits on local history. Lincoln, RI. [Source: [NPS Blackstone River Valley](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/index.htm)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (1-2 hours) - Activity boards and educational materials for young visitors. Complete activities at park sites to earn a Junior Ranger badge. Available at visitor centers. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Passport Book Exploration** (half-day to full day) - Collect stamps at designated locations throughout the Blackstone River Valley. Multiple stamp sites at historic mills, museums, and visitor centers spanning Rhode Island and Massachusetts. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Movie Nights at Old Slater Mill** (2-3 hours) - Special events featuring outdoor films at the historic mill site. Unique setting combining industrial heritage with community entertainment. Check park calendar for schedule. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Hopedale Model Community** (1-2 hours) - Explore the utopian industrial community founded in 1842 by Adin Ballou. Planned village with company housing, gardens, and the Draper Corporation mill complex. Hopedale, MA. [Source: [NPS Blackstone River Valley](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/index.htm)]

- **Whitinsville Village** (1-2 hours) - Well-preserved mill village in Massachusetts with company housing, churches, and the historic Whitin Machine Works complex. Self-guided walking tour available. Whitinsville, MA. [Source: [NPS Blackstone River Valley](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/index.htm)]

- **B.A.R.K. Ranger Program** (1-2 hours) - Volunteer program for visitors with dogs to become certified B.A.R.K. Rangers while exploring pet-friendly areas of the park. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Slatersville Historic District** (1-2 hours) - First planned mill village in America (1807). Walk through the company-built village with worker housing, church, and original mill site. North Smithfield, RI. [Source: [NPS Blackstone River Valley](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/index.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Providence, Rhode Island** (variable, 5 miles south) - State capital with historic Benefit Street, RISD Museum, Federal Hill Italian district, and WaterFire art installation. Downtown Providence, RI. [Source: Visit Rhode Island]

- **Worcester, Massachusetts** (variable, 15 miles north) - Second-largest city in New England with Worcester Art Museum, Higgins Armory Collection, and dining scene. Worcester, MA. [Source: Visit Worcester]

- **Roger Williams National Memorial** (30-60 minutes, 6 miles south) - NPS site commemorating the founder of Rhode Island and the principle of religious freedom. Downtown Providence, RI. [Source: [NPS Roger Williams](https://www.nps.gov/rowi/index.htm)]

- **Purgatory Chasm State Reservation** (1-2 hours, 20 miles west) - Unique granite chasm with rock scrambling and hiking trails. Sutton, MA. [Source: Massachusetts DCR]

## Total Recommended Time

**Total recommended visit time for Blackstone River Valley NHP: Half-day to full day**

A half-day allows time to tour Old Slater Mill and explore one mill village. A full day enables visiting multiple mill villages across Rhode Island and Massachusetts, plus kayaking or biking along the river corridor. The park's dispersed sites span two states, so allow extra time for driving between locations.

[Sources: [NPS Blackstone River Valley](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/index.htm), [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/planyourvisit/index.htm), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/blrv/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]
