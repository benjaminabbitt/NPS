# Cape Lookout NS (National Seashore)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Harkers Island Visitor Center** (131 Charles Street, Harkers Island, NC 28531 (34.6953, -76.5569); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily; 252-728-2250) [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/calo/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60757-d6780840-Reviews-Harkers_Island_Visitor_Center-Harkers_Island_North_Carolina.html)]

- **Light Station Visitor Center** (Great Island, South Core Banks; accessible only by ferry or private boat; hours vary; no phone) [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/calo/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

- **Beaufort Visitor Information Center** (701 Front Street, 1st floor Town Hall, Beaufort, NC (34.7185, -76.6608); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily; phone not listed) [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/calo/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

**Note:** Five cancellation stamps total available at Harkers Island VC, Light Station VC, Beaufort VC, and Portsmouth Village (Theodore and Annie Salter House, US Life-Saving Station). No entrance fee. Ferry required to reach most stamp locations. [Source: [NPS Passport Stamps](https://www.nps.gov/calo/planyourvisit/passports.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Take Ferry to Islands** (30-60 minutes one-way) - Board commercial ferry from Beaufort (every 30 minutes, 8:30 AM - 5:30 PM) or Harkers Island (hourly, 8:15 AM - 5:45 PM); park accessible only by ferry or private boat; ferries operate April-December seasonally [Source: [Island Express Ferry](https://www.islandexpressferryservices.com/ferry-transportation/beaufort-cape-lookout/), [NPS Ferry List](https://www.nps.gov/calo/planyourvisit/ferry.htm)]

- **View Shackleford Banks Wild Horses** (1-2 hours) - Watch restored wild horse herd roaming freely; Horse Sense and Survival Tours offered monthly June-November (physically strenuous); best viewing at dawn/dusk [Source: [OuterBanks.com](https://www.outerbanks.com/cape-lookout-national-seashore.html), [NC Tripping](https://www.nctripping.com/cape-lookout-national-seashore-nc/)]

- **Shell Hunt at "The Point"** (2-3 hours) - Collect whelks, Florida Fighting conchs, sand dollars, helmet conchs, and rare Scotch Bonnets at some of East Coast's best shelling beaches; South Core Banks at Cape Lookout Point offers prime collecting [Source: [OuterBanks.com](https://www.outerbanks.com/cape-lookout-national-seashore.html), [NC Tripping](https://www.nctripping.com/cape-lookout-national-seashore-nc/)]

- **Climb Cape Lookout Lighthouse** (30-45 minutes) - Ascend iconic black-and-white diamond pattern lighthouse for panoramic views; small fee required; summer season only [Source: [Morehead.com](https://www.morehead.com/cape-lookout-national-seashore.html)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Fish and Bird Year-Round** (2-8 hours) - Cast in pristine waters or observe diverse bird species in undeveloped coastal environment; excellent fishing and birding opportunities across all islands [Source: [OuterBanks.com](https://www.outerbanks.com/cape-lookout-national-seashore.html)]

- **Camp on Remote Islands** (overnight) - Experience primitive beach camping on undeveloped barrier islands accessible only by boat; advance reservations recommended [Source: [NC Tripping](https://www.nctripping.com/cape-lookout-national-seashore-nc/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Beaufort Historic District** (30 minutes) - Explore North Carolina's third-oldest town with historic homes, maritime museum, and waterfront dining; downtown Beaufort adjacent to ferry terminal [Source: [Beaufort NC](https://www.beaufort-nc.com/cape-lookout-ferry.html)]

- **Morehead City** (30 minutes) - Visit coastal town with restaurants, shopping, and additional water access points [Source: [Morehead.com](https://www.morehead.com/cape-lookout-national-seashore.html)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** Full day minimum; plan 2-3 hours exploring shoreline plus ferry time; multi-day for camping and comprehensive exploration. Best visits in fall and spring when climate is moderate, fishing and shelling exceptional, and mosquitoes low. [Source: [OuterBanks.com](https://www.outerbanks.com/cape-lookout-national-seashore.html), [NC Tripping](https://www.nctripping.com/cape-lookout-national-seashore-nc/)]
