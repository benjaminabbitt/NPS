# Big Cypress N PRES (National Preserve)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- Oasis Visitor Center (52105 Tamiami Trail E, Ochopee, FL 34141 (25.8573844, -81.0335442); Open 7 days a week, 9:00 AM-4:30 PM, closed December 25; (239) 695-4111) [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bicy/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Source](https://americasnationalparks.org/passport-to-your-national-parks/passport-cancellation-locations/)]
- Big Cypress Swamp Welcome Center/Nathaniel P. Reed Visitor Center (33100 Tamiami Trail E, Ochopee, FL 34141 (26.0485819, -81.0754657); Open 7 days a week, 9:00 AM-4:30 PM, closed December 25) [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bicy/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

**Note:** Call ahead to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. Both visitor centers have NPS Passport cancellation stamps available. The Oasis Visitor Center also has stamps for the Florida National Scenic Trail.

## Key Activities

- Loop Road Scenic Drive (2-4 hours) - A 24-mile mostly unpaved gravel road through cypress swamp offering excellent wildlife viewing opportunities; drive slowly (5-10 mph) to spot alligators, birds, and other wildlife along canals and through dwarf cypress forests; entrance accessible from Tamiami Trail (US-41); open daily [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bicy/planyourvisit/scenic-drives.htm), [Source](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/florida-national-parks/big-cypress-national-preserve-park-at-a-glance/big-cypress-national-preserve-scenic-drives/), [Source](https://www.floridarambler.com/everglades-getaways/big-cypress-national-preserve/)]

- Turner River Paddling Trail (5-10 hours for full trail) - An 8.5-mile canoe/kayak trail from Turner River Launch to Chokoloskee passing through cypress domes, mangrove estuaries, and sawgrass prairie with opportunities to see otters, manatees, alligators, and wading birds; shorter sections available as out-and-back trips; Turner River Launch accessible from Tamiami Trail; November-March (typically closes mid-February due to low water) [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/places/turner-river-paddling-trail.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/bicy/planyourvisit/canoeing-and-kayaking.htm), [Source](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/florida-national-parks/big-cypress-national-preserve-park-at-a-glance/big-cypress-national-preserve-paddling/big-cypress-national-preserve-turner-river-paddle-trip/)]

- Kirby Storter Boardwalk (20-30 minutes) - One-mile round-trip boardwalk journey into a cypress strand ending at a gator hole; excellent for wildlife viewing including birds, snakes, fish, turtles, and alligators; Kirby Storter Roadside Park, Tamiami Trail between visitor centers; open daily from one hour before sunrise to one hour after sunset [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bicy/planyourvisit/kirby-storter-roadside-park.htm), [Source](https://www.miamiscapes.com/big-cypress-kirby-storter.html), [Source](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/florida-national-parks/big-cypress-national-preserve-park-at-a-glance/big-cypress-national-preserve-picnic-areas/big-cypress-national-preserve-kirby-storter-roadside-park/)]

- Oasis Visitor Center and Boardwalk (30-60 minutes) - Exhibits about Big Cypress ecology and wildlife, short boardwalk trail for wildlife viewing, and bookstore; 52105 Tamiami Trail E, Ochopee, FL 34141 (25.8573844, -81.0335442); 9:00 AM-4:30 PM daily except December 25; (239) 695-4111 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bicy/planyourvisit/oasis-visitor-center.htm), [Source](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/florida-national-parks/big-cypress-national-preserve-park-at-a-glance/big-cypress-national-preserve-visitor-centers/big-cypress-national-preserve-oasis-visitor-center/)]

- Big Cypress Swamp Welcome Center (30-45 minutes) - Visitor center with exhibits, orientation film, and information about the preserve; 33100 Tamiami Trail E, Ochopee, FL 34141 (26.0485819, -81.0754657); 9:00 AM-4:30 PM daily except December 25 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bicy/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Source](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/florida-national-parks/big-cypress-national-preserve-park-at-a-glance/big-cypress-national-preserve-visitor-centers/big-cypress-national-preserve-big-cypress-swamp-welcome-center/)]

- Turner River Road Scenic Drive (1-2 hours) - A 17-mile drive through open prairies and popular wading bird feeding areas; entrance from Tamiami Trail; open daily [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bicy/planyourvisit/scenic-drives.htm), [Source](https://www.floridarambler.com/everglades-getaways/big-cypress-national-preserve/)]

- Deep Lake Trail (1 hour) - One-mile moderate hike to Deep Lake through cypress and hardwood habitats; trailhead off US-41; best during dry season (November-April) [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bicy/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [Source](https://www.floridarambler.com/everglades-getaways/big-cypress-national-preserve/)]

- Tree Snail Hammock Trail (15-20 minutes) - Quarter-mile short nature trail showcasing tropical hardwood hammock habitat; off Loop Road; open daily [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bicy/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [Source](https://www.floridarambler.com/everglades-getaways/big-cypress-national-preserve/)]

- H.P. Williams Roadside Park Boardwalk (10-15 minutes) - Short 200-meter boardwalk for quick wildlife viewing and photography; H.P. Williams Roadside Park, Tamiami Trail; open daily [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bicy/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)]

- Wildlife Viewing along Tamiami Trail (flexible, 1-4 hours) - Drive the main corridor (US-41) through the preserve with numerous pullouts for wildlife observation; commonly see alligators, wading birds (egrets, herons), Florida deer, and other species; 90 minutes west of Miami on Tamiami Trail [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bicy/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Source](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/florida/hidden-gems-of-floridas-big-cypress-national-preserve/)]

- Swamp Buggy Tours (30 minutes to 4 hours depending on tour) - Guided tours into backcountry areas not accessible on foot, traveling 6-8 miles into the preserve to see wildlife including alligators, birds, and other species; various operators along Tamiami Trail; best November-April; reservations recommended [[Source](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/florida-national-parks/big-cypress-national-preserve-park-at-a-glance/big-cypress-national-preserve-guided-tours/big-cypress-national-preserve-swamp-buggy-tour/), [Source](https://www.bigcypressswampbuggytours.com/tour)]

- Stargazing (1-2 hours) - Big Cypress is an International Dark Sky Site with minimal light pollution offering exceptional night sky viewing; dispersed camping areas or visitor center areas after hours; year-round, best on clear nights [[Source](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/florida/hidden-gems-of-floridas-big-cypress-national-preserve/), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/bicy/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- Gator Hook Trail (3-4 hours for 5-mile out-and-back) - Historic tram road built by loggers running through cypress swamp; typically a wet walk with knee-to-thigh-deep water during wet season, dry walking during dry season (November-April); trailhead 2 miles south of Tamiami Trail on Loop Road [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bicy/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [Source](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/florida/hidden-gems-of-floridas-big-cypress-national-preserve/), [Source](https://www.floridarambler.com/everglades-getaways/big-cypress-national-preserve/)]

- Halfway Creek Paddling (3.5 hours) - More secluded paddling experience from Halfway Creek to Turner River into Everglades City with opportunities to see otters and manatees; launch from Halfway Creek access point; November-March [[Source](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/florida/hidden-gems-of-floridas-big-cypress-national-preserve/), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/bicy/planyourvisit/canoeing-and-kayaking.htm)]

- Bear Island Backcountry Unit (flexible, half to full day) - Remote section of Big Cypress located north of I-75/Alligator Alley offering more isolated wilderness experience; accessible via designated access points; backcountry permit required; best during dry season [[Source](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/florida/hidden-gems-of-floridas-big-cypress-national-preserve/)]

- Ochopee Post Office (5-10 minutes) - Smallest post office in the United States, in continuous use since 1953 after fire destroyed original building; quirky roadside attraction and photo opportunity; 40001 Tamiami Trail E, Ochopee, FL; postal hours vary [[Source](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/florida/hidden-gems-of-floridas-big-cypress-national-preserve/)]

- Dispersed Backcountry Camping (overnight) - Unlike Everglades National Park, Big Cypress permits dispersed camping for backpackers and hikers anywhere south of I-75/Alligator Alley; backcountry permit required; best November-April [[Source](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/florida/hidden-gems-of-floridas-big-cypress-national-preserve/)]

- Florida National Scenic Trail Section (6-8 hours for 30-mile section or shorter segments) - 30-mile section of the Florida Trail beginning at mile 63 where it intersects I-75/Alligator Alley and ending at Big Cypress Oasis Visitor Center; can be done in segments; trail often underwater during wet season; best November-April [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bicy/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [Source](https://www.floridarambler.com/everglades-getaways/big-cypress-national-preserve/)]

## Also Nearby

- Fakahatchee Strand Preserve State Park - Big Cypress Bend Boardwalk (30-45 minutes, 7 miles west) - A 2,500-foot boardwalk through pristine swamp featuring 500-year-old bald cypress trees, rare wild royal palms, massive strangler figs, and 47 species of native orchids including the elusive Ghost Orchid; recognized as the orchid capital of the United States; 27020 Tamiami Trail E, Naples, FL; open year-round; $3 donation suggested [[Source](https://orchidswamp.org/plan-your-visit/the-boardwalk/), [Source](https://www.floridastateparks.org/parks-and-trails/fakahatchee-strand-preserve-state-park), [Source](https://www.floridarambler.com/everglades-getaways/fakahatchee-strand-preserve-state-park/)]

- Clyde Butcher Big Cypress Gallery (30-60 minutes, 0.5 miles east of Oasis Visitor Center) - Famous large format black and white landscape photography gallery featuring Everglades and Big Cypress images; also offers swamp walk eco-tours behind the gallery; 52388 Tamiami Trail E, Ochopee, FL 34141; open 7 days a week, 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [[Source](https://clydebutcher.com/big-cypress-gallery/), [Source](https://www.paradisecoast.com/listing/clyde-butchers-big-cypress-gallery)]

- Skunk Ape Research Headquarters (20-30 minutes, 15 miles south of I-75) - Quirky roadside attraction dedicated to Florida's legendary Bigfoot-like creature; features reptile and bird exhibits with large alligators, constrictors, and exotic birds; gift shop with Skunk Ape memorabilia; 40904 Tamiami Trail E, Ochopee, FL 34141 (25.8938584, -81.278599); open 7 days a week, 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; $5 admission per person, children 5 and under free; (239) 695-2275 [[Source](https://www.yelp.com/biz/skunk-ape-research-headquarters-ochopee), [Source](https://www.roadsideamerica.com/story/13341), [Source](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/skunk-ape-research-headquarters)]

- Everglades National Park - Shark Valley (2-3 hours, 20 miles east/25 minutes) - 15-mile paved loop trail accessible by bicycle, tram tour, or walking with observation tower at midpoint offering panoramic Everglades views; excellent wildlife viewing including alligators and wading birds; 36000 SW 8th St, Miami, FL; open daily, hours vary seasonally [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g143024-i1654-k5020730-Shark_Valley_and_Big_Cypress_National_Preserve-Everglades_National_Park_Florida.html), [Source](https://www.sharkvalleytramtours.com/everglades-national-park-itinerary/)]

- Everglades City and Ten Thousand Islands (1-2 hours for boat tour, 35-45 minutes west) - Gateway to Ten Thousand Islands archipelago offering boat tours, kayaking, fishing charters, and eco-tours to see manatees (spotted 80% of the time), dolphins, osprey, and bald eagles; Gulf Coast Visitor Center and various tour operators in Everglades City, FL; tours and activities available year-round [[Source](https://visitevergladescity.com/the-ten-thousand-islands-of-florida/), [Source](https://www.visitflorida.com/places-to-go/southwest/everglades-city/), [Source](https://evergladesnationalparkadventures.com/attractions/ten-thousand-islands-boat-tour/)]

**Total Recommended Time:** 6-8 hours for a comprehensive visit including Loop Road scenic drive (2-4 hours), Kirby Storter Boardwalk (30 minutes), one visitor center (30-60 minutes), wildlife viewing stops along Tamiami Trail (1-2 hours), and short trails (1-2 hours total). A full day allows for more extensive exploration including longer hikes or paddling activities. Best visited November-April during dry season when wildlife is more concentrated and trails are accessible.
