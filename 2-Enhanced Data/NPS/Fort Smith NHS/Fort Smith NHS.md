# Fort Smith NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

Fort Smith NHS has multiple cancellation stamps available at the front desk of the visitor center during normal operating hours:

- **Fort Smith National Historic Site stamp** - Available at front desk; 301 Parker Ave, Fort Smith, AR 72901; Daily 9:00 AM–5:00 PM (closed Christmas, New Year's Day, Thanksgiving); (479) 783-3961 [Source: [NPS Park Passport Stamps](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/stamps-fort-smith.htm)]

- **Trail of Tears National Historic Trail stamp** - Fort Smith NHS is a designated site on the Trail of Tears; stamp available at front desk; same location and hours as above [Source: [NPS Park Passport Stamps](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/stamps-fort-smith.htm)]

- **Butterfield Overland National Historic Trail stamp** - Fort Smith NHS is a designated site on the Butterfield Overland Trail; stamp available at front desk; same location and hours as above [Source: [NPS Park Passport Stamps](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/stamps-fort-smith.htm)]

- **Junior Ranger stamp** - Available at front desk during normal operating hours; same location and hours as above [Source: [NPS Park Passport Stamps](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/stamps-fort-smith.htm)]

**Note:** Fort Smith is featured on the 2001 Passport Stamp Set and has an official Passport To Your National Parks® Single Sticker. Call (479) 783-3961 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [NPS Park Passport Stamps](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/stamps-fort-smith.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center Exhibits and Courtroom Tour** (1.5–2 hours) - Explore the historic barracks/courthouse/jail building featuring exhibits on military history (1817-1871), Judge Isaac C. Parker's restored courtroom, the original "Hell on the Border" jail with two jail sections, displays on the Trail of Tears and Native American removal, U.S. Deputy Marshals and outlaws, and interactive exhibits including the American Indian language exhibit. The building complex includes hands-on activities and children can explore reconstructed jail cells; 301 Parker Ave, Fort Smith, Arkansas 72901; 9:00 AM–5:00 PM daily [Sources: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fosm/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Arkansas.com](https://www.arkansas.com/fort-smith/landmarks/fort-smith-national-historic-site)]

- **Commissary Building Tour** (30–45 minutes) - Visit the oldest standing building in Fort Smith (constructed 1838), originally built by the U.S. military to store food and supplies. Now features exhibits on the building's evolution through military, federal court, and modern era periods. The second floor housed Judge Parker's office from 1875-1890 during his tenure as federal judge; 301 Parker Ave, Fort Smith, Arkansas 72901; 9:00 AM–5:00 PM daily [Source: [NPS Commissary](https://www.nps.gov/places/000/fort-smith-commissary.htm)]

- **Arkansas River Trail and Trail of Tears Overlook Walk** (1–1.5 hours) - Hike the scenic 3/4-mile paved walking trail beginning from the parking lot and following the Arkansas River to Belle Point. The trail features wayside exhibit panels telling the story of the five civilized tribes (Cherokee, Choctaw, Chickasaw, Muscogee/Creek, and Seminole) forcibly removed to Indian Territory. The path includes a small loop around the foundation remains of the First Fort Smith (1817-1824), offering river views and the confluence of the Poteau and Arkansas rivers. Trail is paved and accessible for all ages and skill levels; begins at parking lot (3rd Street and Garland Avenue), Fort Smith, Arkansas; 9:00 AM–5:00 PM daily [Sources: [NPS Trail of Tears](https://www.nps.gov/places/fort-smith-national-historic-site-trail-of-tears.htm), [ARK-OK-HOMER](https://arkokhomer.org/west-ark/fort-smith-national-historic-site-river-walk/)]

- **Gallows and First Fort Site Exploration** (20–30 minutes) - View the reconstructed gallows used by Judge Parker's federal court and explore the stone footings of the original First Fort Smith (1817-1824) with interpretive markers. This self-guided outdoor exploration provides historical context about frontier military operations and federal justice on the Indian Territory border; grounds accessible during daylight hours [Source: [Discover Fort Smith](https://www.discoverfortsmith.com/listing/fort-smith-national-historic-site/183/)]

- **Junior Ranger Program Completion** (45–60 minutes) - Complete the Fort Smith Junior Ranger activity booklet featuring mazes, crosswords, drawings, and educational activities designed for children of all ages. Upon completion, return your booklet to a ranger at the visitor center desk and take the official Junior Ranger pledge to earn a badge. Booklets are available at the visitor center information desk or by request via phone (479-783-3961) or email; 301 Parker Ave, Fort Smith, Arkansas 72901; 9:00 AM–5:00 PM daily [Source: [NPS Junior Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/become-a-fort-smith-junior-ranger.htm)]

- **Ranger-Guided Tour** (45 minutes–1 hour) - Participate in an interpretive ranger-led tour through the museum's two jails, courtroom, and exhibits with educational methods tailored to engage visitors. Rangers provide historical context and stories of frontier life, the federal court's impact on Indian Territory, and law enforcement history. Tour availability varies; inquire at visitor center desk; 301 Parker Ave, Fort Smith, Arkansas 72901; 9:00 AM–5:00 PM daily [Source: [NPS Ranger Guided](https://www.nps.gov/fosm/learn/education/rangerguided.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Commemorative Video Screening** (10–15 minutes) - Watch the park's orientation video in an air-conditioned auditorium with comfortable seating at the visitor center. This concise introduction to Fort Smith's history, significance, and layout is an excellent way to orient yourself before exploring the grounds; 301 Parker Ave, Fort Smith, Arkansas 72901; available during visitor center hours [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60816-d298398-Reviews-Fort_Smith_National_Historic_Site-Fort_Smith_Arkansas.html)]

- **B.A.R.K. Ranger Program** (varies) - Bring your dog to explore the park trails and grounds while following BARK Ranger rules (Bag your pet's waste, Always use a leash, Respect wildlife, Know where you can go); complete the Fort Smith BARK Ranger booklet available at the visitor center to earn an official BARK Ranger leash tag for your pet; bag dispensers and trash bins along walking trails; pets welcome on outdoor grounds but only service animals allowed inside visitor center; remember to bring water for both you and your pet and test pavement temperature before walking; 301 Parker Ave, Fort Smith, Arkansas 72901; Grounds open daily 24 hours; (479) 783-3961 [Source: [NPS Become a Fort Smith BARK Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/bark-ranger-fort-smith.htm)]

- **Sunrise or Sunset at Belle Point** (variable) - Experience the Trail of Tears Overlook during golden hour for exceptional photography and reflection. The confluence of the Poteau and Arkansas rivers creates a picturesque setting particularly stunning during early morning or late afternoon light; accessible during daylight hours from the 3/4-mile paved trail; parking at 3rd Street and Garland Avenue, Fort Smith, Arkansas [Source: [TripAdvisor Photos](https://www.tripadvisor.com/LocationPhotoDirectLink-g60816-d298398-i160116882-Fort_Smith_National_Historic_Site-Fort_Smith_Arkansas.html)]

- **Hands-On Jail Cell Experience** (10–15 minutes) - Children and adults can step into and explore reconstructed jail cells within the visitor center building, experiencing a tangible connection to frontier justice and incarceration practices. This interactive element provides unique educational context missing from typical museum exhibits; 301 Parker Ave, Fort Smith, Arkansas 72901; 9:00 AM–5:00 PM daily [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60816-d298398-Reviews-Fort_Smith_National_Historic_Site-Fort_Smith_Arkansas.html)]

## Also Nearby

- **U.S. Marshals Museum** (1–2 hours, 0.5 miles) - A newly opened museum (opened July 2023) beautifully situated on the Arkansas River banks featuring five immersive galleries educating visitors about America's oldest federal law enforcement agency established in 1789. Exhibits cover the history, evolution, and contemporary role of the U.S. Marshals with interactive and educational displays. The museum café operates Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM–5:00 PM. Adult admission $15, seniors and students discounted, children under 5 free. Group rates available; 101 N. 3rd Street, Fort Smith, Arkansas 72901; Tuesday–Saturday 10:00 AM–5:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM–5:00 PM, closed Mondays; wheelchair accessible [Sources: [US Marshals Museum](https://usmmuseum.org/plan-your-visit/), [Arkansas.com](https://www.arkansas.com/fort-smith/landmarks/us-marshals-museum)]

- **Chaffee Barbershop and Military Museum** (1.5–2 hours, 2 miles) - Visit the historic barbershop where Elvis Presley received his iconic G.I. buzz cut on March 25, 1958, dubbed "the haircut heard 'round the world"; the barbershop is restored to its 1950s charm with memorabilia from that day including the camera that captured the moment, news footage, and a letter from the National Archives; also houses a military museum with photos and artifacts documenting the 70-year history of Fort Chaffee, including its use for German prisoners of war, Cuban refugees, and relocated Vietnamese citizens; 7313 Terry Street, Chaffee Crossing Historic District, Fort Smith, AR; Monday–Saturday 9:00 AM–4:00 PM (closed Sundays, phone number on door if locked); free admission (donations welcomed); plan 30 minutes for barbershop, 2 hours to explore all exhibits thoroughly; (479) 242-2724 [Sources: [Discover Fort Smith Chaffee Barbershop](https://www.discoverfortsmith.com/listing/chaffee-barbershop-and-military-museum/160/), [Fort Smith Experience Elvis Barbershop](https://fortsmith.org/elvis-barbershop-museum), [TripAdvisor Chaffee Barbershop](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60816-d7242145-Reviews-Chaffee_Barbershop_Museum-Fort_Smith_Arkansas.html), [Discover Fort Smith Chaffee Museum Blog](https://www.discoverfortsmith.com/blog/post/chaffee-museum/), [Atlas Obscura](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/chaffee-barbershop-museum)]

- **Ben Geren Regional Park** (2–4 hours, 5 miles) - Arkansas's largest county park spanning 1,300+ acres offering diverse recreational activities: disc golf course, walking trails, mountain biking trails, fitness trails, tennis courts, softball and soccer fields. The park also features Parrot Island Waterpark (wave pool, lazy river, aquatic play area, water slides, surfing machine) and the 27-hole Ben Geren Golf Course. Excellent option for active visitors seeking outdoor recreation beyond the historic site; various facilities with extended hours; Ben Geren Regional Park, Fort Smith, Arkansas [Source: [Arkansas.com](https://www.arkansas.com/fort-smith/things-to-do)]

- **Janet Huckabee Arkansas River Valley Nature Center** (1.5–2 hours, 3 miles) - Set on over 170 acres with Wells Lake (a 12-acre body of water built in the 1940s), woodlands, wetlands, and rolling meadows highlighting unique ecosystems of western Arkansas and River Valley communities; offers more than four miles of trails (two ADA-compliant), archery range, fishing docks (regularly stocked with catfish and winter rainbow trout), and hands-on nature exhibits including a 1,200-gallon aquarium with Arkansas native fish species; poles and tackle boxes available as loaners at front desk (bring your own bait); fishing license required for ages 16+, available for purchase at center; 8300 Wells Lake Road, Fort Smith, AR (Fort Chaffee District); Tuesday–Saturday 8:30 AM–4:30 PM (closed Sundays and Mondays); free admission [Sources: [TripAdvisor Janet Huckabee Nature Center](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60816-d6716371-Reviews-Janet_Huckabee_Arkansas_River_Valley_Nature_Center-Fort_Smith_Arkansas.html), [Fort Smith Experience Nature Center](https://fortsmith.org/arkansas-river-valley-nature-center), [Discover Fort Smith Nature Center Blog](https://www.discoverfortsmith.com/blog/post/fort-smith-nature-center/), [AGFC Janet Huckabee Nature Center](https://www.agfc.com/things-to-do/nature-centers/fort-smith/), [Arkansas.com](https://www.arkansas.com/fort-smith/things-to-do)]

- **Lake Fort Smith State Park** (3–4 hours, approximately 30 miles north) - Located in the scenic Boston Mountain Valley of the Ozarks in Mountainburg, AR (8 miles north of Mountainburg on U.S. 71, or Exit 29 off I-540); western terminus of the 240-mile Ozark Highlands Trail; offers camping, fishing, kayaking, hiking (5 trails including 5.3-mile Shepherd Springs Loop with 4.5-star rating), and mountain biking; features 30 campsites, 10 cabins, swimming pool (open Memorial Day–Labor Day), marina with boat rentals, pavilion, picnic sites, and group facilities; visitor center (open daily 8 AM–5 PM with extended hours May 25–Sept 1: Friday & Saturday 8 AM–8 PM) houses replica pioneer log cabin and covered wagon with exhibits on area's early history; park interpreters lead nature hikes and lake tours year-round; swimming, water skiing, stand-up paddle boarding, and jet skiing not allowed on lake but pool available with lifeguards; closed New Year's Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day [Sources: [Arkansas State Parks Lake Fort Smith](https://www.arkansasstateparks.com/parks/lake-fort-smith-state-park), [AllTrails Lake Fort Smith](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/arkansas/lake-fort-smith-state-park), [TripAdvisor Lake Fort Smith](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g31803-d145940-Reviews-Lake_Fort_Smith_State_Park-Mountainburg_Arkansas.html), [Arkansas.com](https://www.arkansas.com/fort-smith/things-to-do)]

- **Fort Smith Trolley Museum** (1–1.5 hours, 2 miles downtown) - Collection of historic railcars including Frisco 4003 Steam Locomotive and a working 1926 electric Birney Streetcar that takes passengers on a 3/4 mile tour down a stretch of track between historic Garrison Avenue and the National Cemetery in downtown Fort Smith; 100 S 4th St, Fort Smith, AR 72901; Summer Hours (May 1–Oct 31): Monday–Saturday 10 AM–5 PM, Sunday 1 PM–5 PM; Winter Hours (Nov 1–Apr 30): Saturday 10 AM–5 PM, Sunday 1 PM–5 PM (weekday tours available by appointment); Trolley Rides: Adults $4, Children $2; (479) 719-7500 during operating hours [Sources: [Fort Smith Trolley Museum Website](https://www.fstrolley.org/), [Discover Fort Smith Trolley Museum](https://www.discoverfortsmith.com/listing/fort-smith-trolley-museum/186/), [TripAdvisor Fort Smith Trolley Museum](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60816-d666210-Reviews-Fort_Smith_Trolley_Museum-Fort_Smith_Arkansas.html), [Discover Fort Smith](https://www.discoverfortsmith.com/play/)]

- **The Unexpected Murals Project** (1–2 hours walking tour, downtown Fort Smith) - Self-guided walking tour through downtown Fort Smith featuring more than 30 large murals painted by internationally acclaimed and local artists on downtown buildings. The city's thriving public art scene offers photo opportunities and cultural exploration; downtown Fort Smith, Arkansas [Source: [Discover Fort Smith](https://www.discoverfortsmith.com/play/)]

- **Fort Smith Regional Art Museum (RAM)** (1.5–2 hours, 0.5 miles downtown) - Modern 16,000-square-foot facility offering fine art exhibitions in five galleries featuring world-class artists, important traveling exhibitions, extensive permanent collection, and art by emerging artists; includes local, regional, and national art exhibitions, mid-century modern architecture, sculpture garden, and murals; offers opening receptions, galas, members-only events, lectures, artist-led workshops, and children's art camps; plan 1 hour for a quick look at everything or 2 hours to truly absorb the artists' works; 1601 Rogers Avenue, Fort Smith, AR 72901; Regular Hours: Tuesday–Saturday 11 AM–5 PM, Sunday 1 PM–5 PM; Summer Hours: Tuesday–Saturday 11 AM–6 PM, Sunday 1 PM–5 PM; free admission [Sources: [Fort Smith Regional Art Museum Website](https://fsram.org/visit/), [Discover Fort Smith RAM Blog](https://www.discoverfortsmith.com/blog/post/regional-art-museum/), [TripAdvisor Fort Smith Regional Art Museum](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60816-d10449004-Reviews-Fort_Smith_Regional_Art_Museum-Fort_Smith_Arkansas.html), [Arkansas.com](https://www.arkansas.com/fort-smith/landmarks/fort-smith-regional-art-museum)]
