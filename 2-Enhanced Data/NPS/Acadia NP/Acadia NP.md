## Key Activities

- **Watch Sunrise from Cadillac Mountain** (1.5-2 hours including drive and viewing time) - Experience viewing the sunrise from the summit of Cadillac Mountain, the highest point on the U.S. Atlantic coast at 1,530 feet, where from October 7 to March 6 you can witness the first sunrise in the country; arrive roughly an hour before sunrise (sunrise times range from 4:30 AM to 6:00 AM depending on season), with a 3.5-mile, 10-12 minute drive from the base to summit or 1-2 hours if hiking; Cadillac Summit Road, Park Loop Road, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 (parking lot typically fills 30-45 minutes before sunrise); vehicle reservations required during peak season ($6); 207-288-3338 [Source: [NPS Cadillac Mountain](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/cadillac-mountain.htm), [TripAdvisor Cadillac](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g143010-d108269-Reviews-Cadillac_Mountain-Acadia_National_Park_Mount_Desert_Island_Maine.html), [Parkcation Cadillac Sunrise](https://www.parkcation.com/nationalparks/acadia/cadillacmountain/sunrise/), [Just Go Travel Cadillac](https://www.justgotravelstudios.com/blogs/just-go-travel-blog/cadillac-mountain-sunrise)]

- **Hike the Jordan Pond Path Loop** (1.5-2 hours) - Walk a 3.3-mile loop around pristine Jordan Pond with outstanding views of the Bubbles (two rounded mountains) reflected in the water, featuring level crushed stone path on the eastern side and a more challenging rocky boulder section on the western side with stepping stones and wooden boardwalk through forested areas; trailhead at Jordan Pond North parking lot, Park Loop Road, approximately 3.7 miles south of Cadillac Mountain Summit Road turnoff, Bar Harbor, ME 04609; open year-round dawn to dusk; free with park admission; 207-288-3338 [Source: [NPS Jordan Pond Path](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hike-jordan-pond-path.htm), [Maine Trail Finder Jordan Pond](https://www.mainetrailfinder.com/trails/trail/acadia-national-park-jordan-pond-path), [AllTrails Jordan Pond](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/maine/jordan-pond-path), [Joe's Guide Jordan Pond](https://www.citrusmilo.com/acadiaguide/jordanpond.php)]

- **Explore Ocean Path Trail to Thunder Hole** (1.5-2 hours for full trail) - Hike the 2.2-mile one-way (4.4 miles round trip) Ocean Path trail offering spectacular coastal views from Sand Beach to Otter Point, passing Thunder Hole (an underwater sea cave producing thundering crashes 2 hours before high tide with high winds), Monument Cove, and Otter Cliff; trailhead at Sand Beach upper parking lot, Park Loop Road less than half a mile south of NPS entrance booths, Bar Harbor, ME 04609; open year-round dawn to dusk; free with park admission; 207-288-3338 [Source: [NPS Ocean Path](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hike-ocean-path-trail.htm), [Maine Trail Finder Ocean Path](https://www.mainetrailfinder.com/trails/trail/acadia-national-park-ocean-path), [AllTrails Ocean Path](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/maine/ocean-path-trail--2), [Joe's Guide Ocean Path](https://www.citrusmilo.com/acadiaguide/oceanpath.php)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Discover Sieur de Monts and Wild Gardens of Acadia** (1-1.5 hours) - Explore the historic Sieur de Monts Spring area featuring the Nature Center with natural history exhibits, Wild Gardens of Acadia showcasing over 300 labeled native plant species in typical island habitats (managed by Bar Harbor Garden Club), the historic spring house, Great Meadow Wetland, and access to multiple memorial paths; located 2 miles south of Bar Harbor near intersection of Park Loop Road and Route 3, Bar Harbor, ME 04609; Wild Gardens open year-round, Nature Center open weekends in May, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM June-August, 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM September-early October; free with park admission; 207-288-3338 [Source: [NPS Sieur de Monts](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/sieur-de-monts.htm), [NPS Nature Center](https://www.nps.gov/places/sieur-de-monts-nature-center.htm), [Acadia Magic Wild Gardens](https://acadiamagic.com/wild-gardens.html), [Outdoor Project Sieur de Monts](https://www.outdoorproject.com/united-states/maine/sieur-de-monts-nature-center-wild-gardens)]

- **Hike the Beehive Loop Trail** (1-2 hours) - Challenge yourself on one of Acadia's most popular and thrilling hikes featuring iron rungs, ladders, and exposed cliff scrambling up a 1.4-mile loop with 450 feet of elevation gain to the top of the Beehive, offering incredible coastal views; not recommended in wet weather or for those with fear of heights; trailhead starts on Bowl Trail across Park Loop Road from Sand Beach parking lot, Bar Harbor, ME 04609; open seasonally (check for closures during peregrine falcon nesting); free with park admission; pets not permitted on rung and ladder trails; 207-288-3338 [Source: [NPS Beehive Loop](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hike-beehive-loop.htm), [AllTrails Beehive](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/maine/the-beehive-loop-trail), [Maine Trail Finder Beehive](https://www.mainetrailfinder.com/trails/trail/acadia-national-park-beehive-loop), [Joe's Guide Beehive](https://www.citrusmilo.com/acadiaguide/thebeehive.php)]

- **Visit Schoodic Peninsula** (3-4 hours for half-day visit) - Explore the quieter, less crowded section of Acadia National Park located about 1 hour from Bar Harbor featuring a scenic 6-mile one-way loop road with ocean views, Schoodic Point (rocky barren southern tip with majestic Atlantic views), over 8 miles of hiking trails, 8 miles of bike paths, excellent tidepooling, and outstanding stargazing opportunities; entrance near Winter Harbor, ME, approximately 20 miles east of Ellsworth via Schoodic National Scenic Byway; open year-round dawn to dusk; free with park admission; 207-288-3338 [Source: [NPS Schoodic Peninsula](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/schoodic.htm), [Joe's Guide Schoodic](https://www.citrusmilo.com/acadiaguide/schoodicpeninsula.php), [Adventure Detour Schoodic](https://theadventuredetour.com/quiet-side-of-acadia-schoodic-peninsula/), [Earth Trekkers Acadia Itinerary](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/acadia-national-park-itinerary/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Abbe Museum** (1-1.5 hours) - Discover Maine's Wabanaki art, history, and culture at this Smithsonian Affiliate museum featuring 70,000+ collections including the largest and best-documented collection of Maine Native American basketry in any museum, with nationally recognized artworks by Wabanaki artists; 26 Mt Desert Street, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 (approximately 5 minutes from park entrance); Monday-Friday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM (no admission sold after 4:30 PM), open seasonally May-October, closes October 31; $10 adults, $7 seniors, $5 children (11-17), free for children 10 and under and those with Tribal ID or Military ID; 207-288-3519 [Source: [Abbe Museum](https://www.abbemuseum.org/), [Abbe Museum Hours](https://www.abbemuseum.org/two-locations), [TripAdvisor Abbe](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60709-d259749-Reviews-Abbe_Museum-Bar_Harbor_Mount_Desert_Island_Maine.html)]

- **Bass Harbor Head Lighthouse** (30-45 minutes) - Visit one of Maine's most photographed lighthouses located within Acadia National Park in the southwest portion of Mount Desert Island, featuring a scenic walking path down cliff stairs to ocean-side observation area and panoramic ocean views (lighthouse interior not open to public as it's a private coast guard residence); Lighthouse Road off Route 102A, Bass Harbor, ME 04653 (approximately 45 minutes from Bar Harbor); grounds open 9:00 AM to sunset year-round; free parking and admission; best visited before 7:00 AM or after 3:00 PM to avoid crowds; 207-288-3338 [Source: [NPS Bass Harbor](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/bass-harbor-head-light-station.htm), [AllTrips Bass Harbor](https://www.acadianationalpark.com/bar_harbor_maine_attractions/bass_harbor_head_lighthouse.php), [Joe's Guide Bass Harbor](https://www.citrusmilo.com/acadiaguide/bassharborheadlight.php), [TripAdvisor Bass Harbor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g40504-d103840-Reviews-Bass_Harbor_Head_Lighthouse-Bass_Harbor_Tremont_Mount_Desert_Island_Maine.html)]

- **Isle au Haut via Mail Boat from Stonington** (Full day trip: 8-10 hours including ferry) - Experience the most remote part of Acadia National Park by taking the Isle au Haut Boat Services mail boat (45-minute to 1-hour ferry ride) from Stonington to explore 18 miles of hiking trails and pristine wilderness; day-trippers departing on the 10:00 AM ferry to Duck Harbor get approximately 4 hours on the island (arrives 11:15 AM, returns 4:45 PM, back in Stonington 6:15 PM); Isle au Haut Boat Services departs from Seabreeze Avenue, Stonington, ME 04681 (approximately 1 hour 15 minutes from Bar Harbor); Duck Harbor landing operates mid-June through Columbus Day, Town Landing year-round; ferry fee applies; arrive at least 1 hour before departure as tickets are first-come, first-served with limited capacity; 207-367-5193 [Source: [NPS Isle au Haut](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/isle-au-haut.htm), [Joe's Guide Isle au Haut](https://www.citrusmilo.com/acadiaguide/isleauhaut.php), [Isle au Haut Boat Services](https://isleauhautferryservice.com/), [Earth Trekkers Isle au Haut](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/isle-au-haut/)]
