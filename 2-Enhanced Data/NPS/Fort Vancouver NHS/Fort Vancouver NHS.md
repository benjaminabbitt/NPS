# Fort Vancouver NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

Fort Vancouver NHS has two separate locations where cancellation stamps can be obtained:

### Main Site (Vancouver, Washington)
- **Fort Vancouver Visitor Center** (1501 E Evergreen Blvd., Vancouver, WA 98661; 45.6262017, -122.6566331; Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM-4:00 PM; (360) 816-6230) - The primary visitor center houses the main cancellation stamp. Open Tuesday through Saturday with seasonal variations: summer hours 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, winter hours 9:00 AM-4:00 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday. [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/fova/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/fova/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Visit Vancouver WA](https://www.visitvancouverwa.com/trip-planning/visitor-center/)]

### Oregon City Unit
- **McLoughlin House/Barclay House** (713 Center St., Oregon City, OR 97045; 45.3572559, -122.6057498; Friday-Saturday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM; (503) 656-5151) - Part of Fort Vancouver NHS, this historic home in Oregon City offers a separate cancellation stamp. Free tours available Friday-Saturday. Visit the adjacent Barclay House for tours and the gift shop. Seasonally closed after the first Saturday in December, reopening mid-February. [Source: [Yelp McLoughlin House](https://www.yelp.com/biz/mcloughlin-house-oregon-city), [NPS McLoughlin House](https://www.nps.gov/fova/planyourvisit/placestogo-mcloughlin-house.htm), [McLoughlin Memorial Association](https://www.mcloughlinhouse.org)]

**Note:** Call ahead to confirm current stamp locations and hours. Park grounds are open daily dawn to dusk. All facilities closed on Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve, and New Year's Day.

## Key Activities

- **Reconstructed Fort Vancouver Tour** (1 hour) - Explore the reconstructed Hudson's Bay Company fur trading post from the 1840s, including the palisade walls, Chief Factor's House, bakehouse, fur storage, blacksmith shop, kitchen, and jail. Watch costumed interpreters demonstrate period crafts and skills. Self-guided tours available, or join ranger-led tours offered daily; 1501 E Evergreen Blvd., Vancouver, WA 98661; Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM-4:00 PM; (360) 816-6230 [Source: [Visit Vancouver WA Fort Vancouver](https://www.visitvancouverwa.com/things-to-do/historic-sites-and-museums/fort-vancouver/), [Fort Tours](https://www.forttours.com/pages/fortvancouver.asp), [NPS Tours](https://www.nps.gov/fova/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)]

- **Living History Demonstrations** (varies, typically 30-60 minutes per demonstration) - Watch blacksmiths forge tools and metalwork using 1840s techniques, observe period cooking demonstrations in the kitchen, and participate in hands-on educational activities. Demonstrations occur throughout the day when the fort is open; 1501 E Evergreen Blvd., Vancouver, WA 98661; Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM-4:00 PM; (360) 816-6230 [Source: [TripAdvisor Fort Vancouver](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60820-d143143-Reviews-Fort_Vancouver_National_Historic_Site-Vancouver_Washington.html), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fova/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Visitor Center Exhibits** (30-45 minutes) - Explore hands-on exhibits about the fur trade era, Hudson's Bay Company operations, and the Columbia River region's history. Watch the orientation film about Fort Vancouver's significance. Features rotating displays of artifacts and artwork; 1501 E Evergreen Blvd., Vancouver, WA 98661; Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM-4:00 PM; (360) 816-6230 [Source: [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/fova/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/fort-vancouver-national-historic-site/)]

- **Pearson Air Museum** (45-60 minutes) - Discover aviation history at one of the oldest operating airfields in the United States. View historic aircraft from the early 20th century, learn about World War I spruce lumber production, and see exhibits about the famous 1937 transpolar flight that landed at Pearson Field; 1115 E 5th Street, Vancouver, WA 98661 (45.6239356, -122.6562831); Tuesday-Saturday 8:00 AM-5:00 PM; Free admission; (360) 816-6230 [Source: [NPS Pearson Air Museum](https://www.nps.gov/fova/planyourvisit/pearsonairmuseum.htm), [Visit Vancouver WA Pearson](https://www.visitvancouverwa.com/things-to-do/historic-sites-and-museums/pearson-field-and-museum/), [TripAdvisor Pearson](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60820-d7613540-Reviews-Pearson_Air_Museum-Vancouver_Washington.html)]

- **Vancouver Barracks Walking Tour** (45-90 minutes self-guided) - Use the cellphone self-guided tour to explore the historic military parade grounds and learn about the U.S. Army's presence from 1849-2011. See historic buildings and interpretive displays about military history; Parade grounds at Fort Vancouver NHS, Vancouver, WA 98661; Dawn to dusk daily; Free [Source: [The Columbian Self-Guided Tour](https://www.columbian.com/news/2024/jun/20/find-hidden-stories-of-familiar-grounds-with-fort-vancouvers-self-guided-tour/), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fova/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Historic Fort Vancouver Garden** (30-45 minutes) - Stroll through the beautifully maintained garden set in English-style paths and patterns, featuring fresh produce, colorful flowers, and lush greenery during summer months. Located adjacent to the reconstructed fort; Fort Vancouver NHS, Vancouver, WA 98661; Dawn to dusk daily; Free [Source: [Visit Vancouver WA](https://www.visitvancouverwa.com/things-to-do/historic-sites-and-museums/fort-vancouver/), [TripAdvisor Garden](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60820-d15007102-Reviews-Fort_Vancouver_National_Historic_Site_Garden-Vancouver_Washington.html)]

- **Officers Row Walk** (30-60 minutes) - Walk along the historic Officers Row featuring 22 restored Victorian-era homes from the 1880s, listed on the National Historic Register. View the architecture and learn about military officers who lived here; Officers Row, Vancouver, WA 98661; Exterior viewing available daily; Free [Source: [Visit Vancouver WA Officers Row](https://www.visitvancouverwa.com/things-to-do/historic-sites-and-museums/officers-row/), [USA Vancouver](https://usavancouver.com/officers-row-fort-vancouver/)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (1-2 hours) - Children can participate in this interactive educational program and earn up to three Junior Ranger badges by completing activity booklets and attending ranger programs; Fort Vancouver Visitor Center, 1501 E Evergreen Blvd., Vancouver, WA 98661; Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM-4:00 PM; Free; (360) 816-6230 [Source: [Visit Vancouver WA](https://www.visitvancouverwa.com/things-to-do/historic-sites-and-museums/fort-vancouver/), [Dinkum Tribe](https://dinkumtribe.com/fort-vancouver-national-park/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Marshall House Tour** (45-60 minutes) - Visit the ornate Queen Anne Victorian mansion built in 1886, home to General George C. Marshall from 1936-1938. Marvel at stained glass windows, decorative woodwork, and the distinctive round turret. Marshall lived here while commanding the Vancouver Barracks and supervising regional Civilian Conservation Corps camps. This is where he and Mrs. Marshall welcomed the Russian crew of the 1937 transpolar flight from Europe. Portions open for tours and special events; 1301 Officers Row, Vancouver, WA 98661 (45.6276053, -122.6573559); Check with visitor center for tour availability; (360) 816-6230 [Source: [NPS Marshall House](https://www.nps.gov/articles/marshallhouse.htm), [Clark County Marshall House](https://clark.wa.gov/community-planning/marshall-house), [TripAdvisor Marshall House](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60820-d282097-Reviews-George_C_Marshall_House-Vancouver_Washington.html)]

- **McLoughlin House Historic Tours** (45 minutes) - Tour the 1846 home of Dr. John McLoughlin, the "Father of Oregon," who served as Chief Factor of Fort Vancouver. Free guided tours explore the life of this influential figure in Pacific Northwest history. Visit the adjacent Barclay House with its gift shop and interpretive displays; 713 Center St., Oregon City, OR 97045 (45.3572559, -122.6057498); Friday-Saturday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM, last tour at 3:00 PM; Free; (503) 656-5151 [Source: [Yelp McLoughlin House](https://www.yelp.com/biz/mcloughlin-house-oregon-city), [TripAdvisor McLoughlin](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g52007-d294419-Reviews-McLoughlin_House-Oregon_City_Clackamas_County_Oregon.html), [Travel Oregon McLoughlin](https://traveloregon.com/things-to-do/culture-history/historic-sites-oregon-trail/mcloughlin-house/)]

- **Waterfront Renaissance Trail** (30-60 minutes for section near fort) - Walk or bike along this paved 5-mile pedestrian path stretching along the Columbia River waterfront. The trail connects Esther Short Park with Wintler Community Park and offers scenic river views and access to Fort Vancouver; Vancouver Waterfront, Vancouver, WA; Open daily dawn to dusk; Free [Source: [PlanetWare Vancouver WA](https://www.planetware.com/washington/top-rated-attractions-things-to-do-in-vancouver-wa-us-wa-145.htm), [Jaunty Everywhere Vancouver](https://www.jauntyeverywhere.com/local-secrets-20-things-vancouver-wa/)]

- **Lantern-Lit Fort Tours** (1-1.5 hours) - Experience the fort after dark during special evening programs with lantern-lit tours of the historic buildings. These atmospheric tours bring 1840s fort life to vivid detail. Check the park calendar for scheduled dates; Fort Vancouver NHS, 1501 E Evergreen Blvd., Vancouver, WA 98661; Special events only - check calendar; (360) 816-6230 [Source: [Visit Vancouver WA](https://www.visitvancouverwa.com/things-to-do/historic-sites-and-museums/fort-vancouver/)]

- **Spruce Mill Trail Dog Walk** (45-60 minutes) - Enjoy a dog-friendly trail exploring World War I history through a self-guided tour. Learn about the spruce lumber production that supported the war effort while walking your canine companion; Fort Vancouver NHS grounds, Vancouver, WA; Dawn to dusk daily; Free [Source: [Visit Vancouver WA](https://www.visitvancouverwa.com/things-to-do/historic-sites-and-museums/fort-vancouver/), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fova/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Columbia River Gorge Waterfalls** (2-4 hours, 30 miles east) - Explore the stunning Columbia River Gorge National Scenic Area with its spectacular waterfalls including the iconic 620-foot Multnomah Falls, Latourell Falls, Bridal Veil Falls, and Horsetail Falls. Visit Crown Point Vista House, a century-old observatory perched 733 feet above the Columbia River. The Gorge is approximately 30 miles (30-45 minute drive) east of Vancouver via I-84; Columbia River Gorge National Scenic Area; Open daily year-round, individual site hours vary; Northwest Forest Pass or fee required at some locations [Source: [Travel Portland Columbia Gorge](https://www.travelportland.com/region/columbia-river-gorge/), [Road is Calling Gorge Drive](https://www.roadiscalling.com/places-to-visit-on-columbia-river-gorge-drive/), [Travel Oregon Gorge](https://traveloregon.com/places-to-go/regions/columbia-river-gorge/)]

- **Ridgefield National Wildlife Refuge** (1-3 hours, 15 miles north) - Explore 5,200 acres of marshes, lakes, and grasslands teeming with wildlife. Excellent for birdwatching, wildlife photography, and nature walks. Popular during waterfowl migration seasons. Located approximately 15-20 miles north of Vancouver; Ridgefield NWR, Ridgefield, WA; Open daily dawn to dusk; Free [Source: [PlanetWare Vancouver WA](https://www.planetware.com/washington/top-rated-attractions-things-to-do-in-vancouver-wa-us-wa-145.htm)]

- **Esther Short Park** (1 hour, 2 miles from fort) - Visit Washington State's oldest public square, a vibrant 5-acre park in downtown Vancouver featuring fountains, public art, Saturday farmers markets (March-October), and community events. Beautiful setting for picnics and relaxation; 605 Esther St., Vancouver, WA 98660; Open daily; Free [Source: [PlanetWare Vancouver WA](https://www.planetware.com/washington/top-rated-attractions-things-to-do-in-vancouver-wa-us-wa-145.htm), [Jaunty Everywhere](https://www.jauntyeverywhere.com/local-secrets-20-things-vancouver-wa/)]

- **Lacamas Lake Park** (2-3 hours, 15 miles east in Camas) - Enjoy over 12 miles of scenic trails, plus opportunities for kayaking and standup paddleboarding on the lake. Features picturesque Round Lake with its distinctive "heritage tree" growing in the middle, and beautiful Lacamas Creek with small waterfalls. Located in nearby Camas, WA; Camas, WA; Open daily dawn to dusk; Free [Source: [PlanetWare Vancouver WA](https://www.planetware.com/washington/top-rated-attractions-things-to-do-in-vancouver-wa-us-wa-145.htm)]

- **Beacon Rock State Park** (2-3 hours, 35 miles east) - Hike to the top of the 848-foot volcanic plug via a trail with switchbacks and bridges offering panoramic views of the Columbia River Gorge. This prominent landmark can be seen for miles. One of the most popular hikes in the Gorge area. Located approximately 35 miles (40-minute drive) east of Vancouver; Beacon Rock State Park, Skamania, WA; Open daily dawn to dusk; Discover Pass required; (509) 427-8265 [Source: [PlanetWare Vancouver WA](https://www.planetware.com/washington/top-rated-attractions-things-to-do-in-vancouver-wa-us-wa-145.htm)]

- **Vancouver Lake Regional Park** (2-3 hours, 10 miles west) - Enjoy a nearly 3,000-acre lake west of Vancouver offering boat ramps, picnic areas, playground equipment, swimming beaches, and water sports. Popular for windsurfing, sailing, and fishing. Beautiful sunset views; 6801 NW Lower River Rd., Vancouver, WA 98660; Open daily dawn to dusk; Free [Source: [PlanetWare Vancouver WA](https://www.planetware.com/washington/top-rated-attractions-things-to-do-in-vancouver-wa-us-wa-145.htm)]
