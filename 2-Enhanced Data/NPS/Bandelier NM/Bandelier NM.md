# Bandelier NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Bandelier National Monument Visitor Center** (15 Entrance Road, Los Alamos, NM 87544 (35.778175, -106.269608); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; 505-672-3861 x0)

**Note:** Call ahead to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [NPS Bandelier Contact](https://www.nps.gov/band/contacts.htm), [Latlong.net](https://www.latlong.net/place/bandelier-national-monument-los-alamos-nm-usa-23695.html)]

## Key Activities

- **Main Pueblo Loop Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - A paved 1.4-mile loop trail through excavated archaeological sites in Frijoles Canyon featuring ancient Puebloan dwellings, kivas, and ladders to climb into small human-carved cavates; 15 Entrance Road, Los Alamos, NM 87544 (35.778175, -106.269608); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Main Loop Trail](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hike-the-pueblo-loop-trail.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g60730-d146366-t1235339-Hi_can_you_give_an_indication_of_how_long_the.html), [AllTrails Main Ruin Loop](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/main-ruin-loop-trail)]

- **Alcove House Trail** (45 minutes-1.5 hours) - A 1.2-mile round-trip hike extending from the Main Loop to Alcove House (formerly Ceremonial Cave), requiring climbing four steep ladders totaling 140 feet above the canyon floor to reach the alcove; 15 Entrance Road, Los Alamos, NM 87544 (35.778175, -106.269608); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM (check current accessibility status before visiting) [Source: [NPS Alcove House](https://www.nps.gov/band/planyourvisit/alcove-house.htm), [AllTrails Alcove House](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/alcove-house-trail), [Discover All Corners](https://discoverallcorners.com/alcove-house-trail-bandelier-national-monument/)]

- **Tsankawi Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - A 1.5-mile loop trail with 200 feet of elevation change through a detached, less-visited section of the monument featuring three wooden ladders, cavates, petroglyphs, and the ancient village of Tsankawi with panoramic views; Located approximately 12 miles from main monument area on Highway 4; Daily sunrise to sunset [Source: [NPS Tsankawi](https://www.nps.gov/band/planyourvisit/tsankawi.htm), [TripAdvisor Tsankawi](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60730-d1210615-Reviews-Tsankawi-Los_Alamos_New_Mexico.html), [American Southwest](https://www.americansouthwest.net/new_mexico/bandelier/tsankawi-trail.html)]

- **Visitor Center Experience** (30 minutes-1 hour) - Explore exhibits featuring Ancestral Pueblo pottery, tools, artifacts of daily life, and orientation displays about the monument's inhabitants; 15 Entrance Road, Los Alamos, NM 87544 (35.778175, -106.269608); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; 505-672-3861 x0 [Source: [NPS Bandelier](https://www.nps.gov/band), [SAH Archipedia](https://sah-archipedia.org/buildings/NM-01-043-0049-01)]

- **Falls Trail (Upper Section)** (1-2 hours) - A trail descending through forest past waterfalls (currently only the Upper Falls section is accessible); 15 Entrance Road, Los Alamos, NM 87544 (35.778175, -106.269608); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Falls Trail](https://www.nps.gov/band/planyourvisit/falls-trail.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g60730-d146366-t1235339-Hi_can_you_give_an_indication_of_how_long_the.html)]

- **Ranger-Led Programs** (1-2 hours) - Guided tours of the Main Pueblo Loop Trail, backcountry hikes, stargazing programs, and full moon walks offered from June through September (subject to staff availability); 15 Entrance Road, Los Alamos, NM 87544 (35.778175, -106.269608); Schedule varies by season; 505-672-3861 x0 [Source: [NPS Ranger Programs](https://www.nps.gov/band/planyourvisit/ranger-programs.htm), [NPS Interpretive Programs](https://www.nps.gov/band/planyourvisit/interpprog.htm)]

- **Frijolito Loop Trail** (3-4 hours) - A backcountry day hike starting from the visitor center exploring diverse terrains including steep ascents, mesas, and canyon rims with views of Frijoles Canyon and access to Frijolito Pueblo archaeological site dating to the mid-1400s; Trailhead at visitor center area, 15 Entrance Road, Los Alamos, NM 87544 (35.778175, -106.269608); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [ProTrails Frijolito](http://www.protrails.com/trail/248/bandelier-national-monument-frijolito-loop-trail-frijolito-ruins), [American Southwest](https://www.americansouthwest.net/new_mexico/bandelier/frijolito-loop-trail.html), [NPS Longer Hikes](https://www.nps.gov/band/planyourvisit/longer-hikes.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Painted Cave** (Full day/overnight backpack recommended) - A remote backcountry destination featuring ancient rock art on cave walls; accessible via a challenging 22+ mile round-trip hike from the visitor center or 12.3-mile trail from Dome Trailhead (note: Cochiti Reservation access road is closed); check at visitor center for current trail conditions; 15 Entrance Road, Los Alamos, NM 87544 (35.778175, -106.269608); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM for visitor center; 505-672-3861 x0 [Source: [NPS Painted Cave](https://www.nps.gov/band/planyourvisit/painted-cave.htm), [AllTrails Painted Cave](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/painted-cave), [Tourist Secrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/new-mexico/mysteries-of-bandeliers-ancient-ruins/)]

- **Stone Lions Shrine** (Full day hike or overnight) - A sacred shrine featuring two roughly carved mountain lion figures, each about 6 feet long and 2 feet high, still significant to nearby pueblos with evidence of recent ceremonial activity; accessible via backcountry trails from visitor center or connecting trails from Frijolito Loop; 15 Entrance Road, Los Alamos, NM 87544 (35.778175, -106.269608); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM for visitor center; 505-672-3861 x0 [Source: [American Southwest Hiking](https://www.americansouthwest.net/new_mexico/bandelier/hiking.html), [AllTrails Stone Lions Loop](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/middle-alamo-trail-stone-lions-trail-and-frijoes-rim-trail-loop), [Wikivoyage Bandelier](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Bandelier_National_Monument)]

- **Night Sky and Astronomy Programs** (2-3 hours) - Ranger-led stargazing and astronomy interpretive programs offered after dark during fall and summer months, plus full moon walks; register in advance through park; 15 Entrance Road, Los Alamos, NM 87544 (35.778175, -106.269608); Schedule varies by season; 505-672-3861 x0 [Source: [Western National Parks](https://wnpa.org/five-must-do-activities-at-bandelier-national-monument/), [NPS Ranger Programs](https://www.nps.gov/band/planyourvisit/ranger-programs.htm)]

- **White Rock Canyon Boating** (Half day) - A less-visited water-based activity allowing exploration of the monument from White Rock Canyon on the Rio Grande; requires advance planning and permits through NPS; contact park for details; 15 Entrance Road, Los Alamos, NM 87544 (35.778175, -106.269608); 505-672-3861 x0 [Source: [Western National Parks](https://wnpa.org/five-must-do-activities-at-bandelier-national-monument/), [Passion and Passport](https://apassionandapassport.com/bandelier-national-monument-trails-new-mexico/)]

- **Winter Cross-Country Skiing** (Varies by trail and conditions) - Cross-country skiing opportunities on monument trails during winter months when snow conditions permit; 15 Entrance Road, Los Alamos, NM 87544 (35.778175, -106.269608); Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM for visitor center; 505-672-3861 x0 [Source: [Passion and Passport](https://apassionandapassport.com/bandelier-national-monument-trails-new-mexico/), [Casa Escondida](https://casaescondida.com/blog/bandelier-national-monument/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Valles Caldera National Preserve** (2-4 hours for popular trails) - A massive volcanic caldera 18 miles west of Bandelier via NM-4 offering spectacular hiking trails including the 2.9-mile Coyote Call Trail Loop and 3.4-mile Jemez River Trail, plus mountain biking, bird watching, and ranger programs June-October; 090 State Road 4, Jemez Springs, NM 87025; Daily sunrise to sunset; approximately 25-30 minutes drive from Bandelier [Source: [Valles Caldera Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/vall/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [AllTrails Valles Caldera](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/new-mexico/valles-caldera-national-preserve), [New Mexico Magazine](https://www.newmexicomagazine.org/blog/post/valles-caldera-hiking-trails/), [Valles Caldera Directions](https://www.nps.gov/vall/planyourvisit/directions.htm)]

- **Bradbury Science Museum** (1-2 hours) - A free museum in Los Alamos featuring over 40 interactive exhibits on the Manhattan Project history, Los Alamos National Laboratory research, and defense work; 1350 Central Ave., Los Alamos, NM 87544 (35.8816651, -106.2986491); Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM-5:00 PM, closed Monday; 505-667-4444; approximately 15-20 minutes drive from Bandelier [Source: [Bradbury Science Museum](https://visitlosalamos.org/science-museum), [NPS Bradbury](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/los-alamos-explore-the-bradbury-science-museum.htm), [New Mexico Tourism](https://www.newmexico.org/listing/bradbury-science-museum/2621/)]

- **Manhattan Project National Historical Park** (1-2 hours) - Los Alamos visitor center and historic sites related to the development of the atomic bomb with special bus tours to restricted lab areas available; 475 20th Street, Los Alamos, NM 87544 (35.8809693, -106.3021986); Friday-Monday 10:00 AM-3:00 PM, closed Tuesday-Thursday; 505-661-6277; Free admission; approximately 15-20 minutes drive from Bandelier [Source: [NPS Manhattan Project LA](https://www.nps.gov/mapr/planyourvisit/losalamos-visitorcenter.htm), [Visit Los Alamos Manhattan Project](https://www.visitlosalamos.org/Recreation/Los-Alamos-Gateway-To-Three-National-Parks/Manhattan-Project-National-Historical-Park)]

- **Los Alamos History Museum** (1-2 hours) - Museum campus on Bathtub Row featuring exhibits on Ancient Puebloans, homesteaders, Ranch School boys, and Manhattan Project scientists with guided walking tours; 1050 Bathtub Row, Los Alamos, NM 87544; Monday-Friday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Saturday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM, closed Sunday; 505-709-7794; approximately 15-20 minutes drive from Bandelier [Source: [Los Alamos Historical Society](https://losalamoshistory.org/plan-your-visit/), [Visit Los Alamos History Museum](https://www.visitlosalamos.org/History-in-Los-Alamos/Los-Alamos-History-Museum), [NPS History Museum](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/los-alamos-visit-the-history-museum-campus.htm)]

- **Puye Cliff Dwellings** (2-3 hours) - Ancestral Puebloan cliff dwellings on Santa Clara Pueblo land featuring guided tours of cavates and mesa-top pueblo ruins; approximately 20-25 miles from Bandelier (30-40 minutes drive); contact for current hours and tour schedules at puyecliffdwellings.com [Source: [Puye vs Bandelier](https://diytravelhq.com/puye-cliff-dwellings-or-bandelier/), [Four Kachinas Puye](https://fourkachinas.com/blog/puye-cliff-dwellings/), [TripAdvisor Forum](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g60958-i228-k623041-Bandelier_vs_Puye_Cliff_Dwellings-Santa_Fe_New_Mexico.html)]

- **Jemez Mountain Trail National Scenic Byway** (3 hours-full day for complete 163-mile loop) - A spectacular 163-mile scenic drive through the Jemez Mountains passing directly through Bandelier via NM-4, climbing to 9,000 feet elevation with views of Valles Caldera, waterfalls, hot springs, and historic sites; route accessible from Los Alamos via State Road 501 to State Road 4; allow 3 hours for driving only or full day for stops [Source: [National Scenic Byway Foundation](https://nsbfoundation.com/nb/jemez-mountain-trail-national-scenic-byway/), [New Mexico Tourism Byway](https://www.newmexico.org/places-to-visit/scenic-byways/jemez-mountain-trail-national/), [Visit Los Alamos Byway](https://visitlosalamos.org/jemez-mountain-scenic-byway)]

- **Santa Fe Plaza and Historic District** (3-4 hours minimum) - The historic heart of Santa Fe featuring art galleries, shops, Georgia O'Keeffe Museum, Canyon Road galleries, and the Palace of the Governors dating to 1610; Santa Fe, NM (Plaza at center of downtown); varies by attraction; approximately 45-50 minutes drive south from Bandelier [Source: [Santa Fe Day Trips](https://fourkachinas.com/blog/santa-fe-day-trips/), [Rome2Rio Santa Fe to Bandelier](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Santa-Fe-NM-USA/Bandelier-National-Monument), [Travel with Mike & Dee](https://deandrandmiketravel.com/onedayatbandelier/)]
