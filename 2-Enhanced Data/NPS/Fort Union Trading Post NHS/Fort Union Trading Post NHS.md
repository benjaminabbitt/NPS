# Fort Union Trading Post NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-bourgeois-house"></a>**Bourgeois House Visitor Center** (15550 Highway 1804, Williston, ND 58801 (47.999443, -104.04058); Summer (Memorial Day-Labor Day): 8:00am-8:00pm, Winter (Labor Day-Memorial Day): 9:00am-5:30pm, Central Time, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 701-572-9083) - NPS passport cancellation stamps are available at the visitor center located in the reconstructed Bourgeois House, which was the home of the head trader. The site is fee-free and no reservations are required. [Source: [Operating Hours & Seasons - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/fous/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [America's National Parks - Passport Cancellation Locations](https://americasnationalparks.org/passport-to-your-national-parks/passport-cancellation-locations/), [Fort Union Trading Post - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/fous/index.htm)]

**Note:** Call 701-572-9083 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-self-guided-tour"></a>**Self-Guided Walking Tour** (30 minutes-2 hours) - Explore the reconstructed fur trading post at your own pace, traversing the fort's courtyard and reconstructed buildings including the Trade House, Clerk's Office, and Southwest Bastion. "Expect at least 1/2 hour to tour at a fast speed, 2 hours to tour at a leisurely speed." The site features ghosted structures and building outlines showing the original fort layout, with interpretive displays illustrating how American Indians, Métis, and fort employees lived and worked together; 15550 Highway 1804, Williston, ND 58801 (47.999443, -104.04058); open during park hours (Summer: 8:00am-8:00pm, Winter: 9:00am-5:30pm, Central Time); 701-572-9083 [Source: [Tour Fort Union Trading Post - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/tour-fort-union-trading-post.htm), [TripAdvisor FAQ - Time Needed](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g60819-d1468612-t5957149-Approximately_how_much_time_is_needed_for_this.html)]

- <a id="activity-trade-house"></a>**Trade House Experience** (5-30 minutes) - Visit the Trade House during summer and early fall to learn why Fort Union was the most important trading post on the Upper Missouri River and understand historical trading transactions. The Trade House features period-costumed interpreters who demonstrate different goods that were traded and explain pricing in furs. "Trade Talks can range from 5 to 30 minutes (or more) depending on visitor interest"; 15550 Highway 1804, Williston, ND 58801 (47.999443, -104.04058); open Memorial Day-Labor Day, 10:00am-5:45pm; 701-572-9083 [Source: [Plan Like a Park Ranger - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/plan-like-a-park-ranger-top-10-tips-for-visiting-fort-union-trading-post-nhs.htm), [Visit Fort Union's Trade House - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/fort-union-trade-house.htm)]

- <a id="activity-museum-exhibits"></a>**Museum Exhibits at Bourgeois House** (30-45 minutes) - View archaeological artifacts recovered from excavations between 1968 and 1988, including over one million objects such as glass beads (the country's largest collection), shell casings, buttons, hand-forged tools, trade beads, animal bones, building hardware, blacksmith tools, and pottery fragments. Exhibits include hands-on animal pelts display, examples of furs that were traded, and a closed-caption video documenting life at Fort Union; Bourgeois House Visitor Center, 15550 Highway 1804, Williston, ND 58801 (47.999443, -104.04058); Summer: 8:00am-8:00pm, Winter: 9:00am-5:30pm, Central Time; 701-572-9083 [Source: [Fort Union Collections - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/fous/learn/historyculture/collections.htm), [Bourgeois House Visitor Center - WhichMuseum](https://whichmuseum.com/museum/bourgeois-house-visitor-center-fort-union-trading-post-national-historic-site-williston-29932), [Go Wandering - Fort Union](https://gowandering.com/places/fort-union-trading-post-historic-site)]

- <a id="activity-missouri-river-trail"></a>**Missouri River Trail** (20-30 minutes) - A short hiking trail covering "approximately 2,000 feet or 2/5th of a mile roundtrip" that takes visitors through river bottoms to the current Missouri River location near the confluence with the Yellowstone River. The trail offers scenic views and connects visitors to the natural setting that made this location ideal for fur trading; trailhead at Fort Union Trading Post, 15550 Highway 1804, Williston, ND 58801 (47.999443, -104.04058); open during park hours; 701-572-9083 [Source: [Plan Like a Park Ranger - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/plan-like-a-park-ranger-top-10-tips-for-visiting-fort-union-trading-post-nhs.htm)]

- <a id="activity-junior-trader"></a>**Junior Trader Program** (30-45 minutes) - An age-inclusive activity where visitors of all ages complete a booklet of activities at the Visitor Center, then get sworn in as Junior Traders and trade with rangers. This hands-on program helps visitors understand the trading process and life at Fort Union; Bourgeois House Visitor Center, 15550 Highway 1804, Williston, ND 58801 (47.999443, -104.04058); available during visitor center hours; 701-572-9083 [Source: [Plan Like a Park Ranger - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/plan-like-a-park-ranger-top-10-tips-for-visiting-fort-union-trading-post-nhs.htm), [Fort Union - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/fous/)]

- <a id="activity-rendezvous"></a>**Fort Union Rendezvous** (4-day event, full days) - An annual 4-day living history event typically held the third weekend in June (10:00am-5:00pm Central Time). Features reenactments of fur trade era events including black powder shooting demonstrations, trade negotiations, traditional skills demonstrations (blacksmithing, pottery, spinning and weaving, frontier cooking), historical vendors, and interactive activities. Thursday is designated as Kids Day (10:00am-2:00pm) with educational activities designed for children. Admission and parking are free; 15550 Highway 1804, Williston, ND 58801 (47.999443, -104.04058); third weekend in June, 10:00am-5:00pm; 701-572-9083 [Source: [40th Annual Rendezvous 2023 - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/fous/learn/news/40th-annual-fort-union-rendezvous-set-for-june-15-18-2023.htm), [41st Annual Rendezvous 2024 - The Roundup](https://www.roundupweb.com/story/2024/06/12/news/41st-annual-fort-union-rendezvous-set-for-june-13-16/20756.html)]

- <a id="activity-indian-arts"></a>**Indian Arts Festival** (2-day event, full days) - Annual celebration typically held the first weekend in August (10:00am-5:00pm) commemorating American Indian history and culture through music, traditional crafts, lectures by tribal historians and elders, dancing, drumming, singing, games, demonstrations, and children's activities. Past events have featured scheduled activities including buffalo hide talks, flint knapping demonstrations, Native film presentations, traditional singing and dance, moon calendar presentations, cannon/black powder firing demonstrations, and ongoing beadwork, quillwork, and storytelling. Admission and parking are free; 15550 Highway 1804, Williston, ND 58801 (47.999443, -104.04058); first weekend in August, 10:00am-5:00pm; 701-572-9083 [Source: [Indian Arts Festival 2022 - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/fous/learn/news/indian-arts-festival-scheduled-for-august-6-7-2022.htm), [Indian Arts Festival - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/fous/planyourvisit/indian-arts-festival.htm), [Williston Herald - Indian Arts Festival](https://www.willistonherald.com/news/indian-arts-festival-august-5-6/article_a1aeb25e-1c3d-11ee-8a3f-5b28562548d0.html)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="hidden-living-history"></a>**Living History Weekend** (1 day, 6 hours) - Traditionally held on Labor Day weekend Saturday (10:00am-4:00pm Central Time), this event features Fort Union Muzzleloaders, staff, and reenactors who transform into historical figures such as laborers, clerks, traders, and Métis women, living within the reconstructed fort. Artisan demonstrations include blacksmithing, spinning and weaving, historical medical tools and practices, hide preparation and tanning, tailoring, tinsmithing, glass plate photography, making moccasins, black powder shooting, and kid's activities. Last Bell Tours on Saturday begin at 8:00pm CST. Admission and parking are free; 15550 Highway 1804, Williston, ND 58801 (47.999443, -104.04058); Labor Day weekend Saturday, 10:00am-4:00pm CST; 701-572-9083 [Source: [Living History Weekend 2022 - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/fous/learn/news/living-history-weekend-announced-for-september-3-4-2022.htm), [Living History Day 2024 - Minot Daily News](https://www.minotdailynews.com/news/local-news/2024/08/living-history-day-set-at-fort-union/)]

- <a id="hidden-confluence"></a>**Missouri-Yellowstone River Confluence Trail** (30-45 minutes) - Explore the natural setting near the confluence of the Missouri and Yellowstone Rivers, where the fort's strategic location made it the center of Upper Missouri fur trade. This lesser-known trail offers excellent opportunities for wildlife viewing and understanding the geographical significance that drew traders to this location. The confluence area was a crucial meeting point for indigenous peoples and fur traders; near Fort Union Trading Post, 15550 Highway 1804, Williston, ND 58801 (47.999443, -104.04058); accessible during park hours; 701-572-9083 [Source: [Fort Union - National Park Units](https://www.nationalparkunits.com/fort-union-trading-post-national-historic-site/), [Lewis and Clark Trail - Fort Union](https://www.lewisandclark.travel/listing/fort-union-trading-post-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="hidden-remote-location"></a>**Remote Northern Plains Experience** (variable) - Fort Union Trading Post straddles the Montana-North Dakota border and "isn't really anywhere close to anything," making it a truly off-the-beaten-path destination. The site offers an authentic experience of the remote northern plains environment with minimal modern development visible, allowing visitors to experience the isolation and frontier atmosphere similar to what 19th-century traders encountered. Bring mosquito repellent due to proximity to the rivers; 15550 Highway 1804, Williston, ND 58801 (47.999443, -104.04058); 701-572-9083 [Source: [Fort Union - National Park Units](https://www.nationalparkunits.com/fort-union-trading-post-national-historic-site/), [Plan Like a Park Ranger - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/plan-like-a-park-ranger-top-10-tips-for-visiting-fort-union-trading-post-nhs.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-fort-buford"></a>**Fort Buford State Historic Site** (1-1.5 hours, 3 miles) - Historic military fort built in 1866 near the confluence of the Missouri and Yellowstone Rivers, known as the site of Sitting Bull's surrender. Includes restored buildings and outdoor interpretive signs. Site grounds with interpretive signs are open year-round, with buildings open May 1-September 30: 9:00am-5:00pm daily, October 1-April 30: by appointment; 15349 39th Ln NW, Williston, ND 58801 (47.987658, -104.000413); 3 miles from Fort Union Trading Post; 701-572-9034 [Source: [Fort Buford State Historic Site - SHSND](https://www.history.nd.gov/historicsites/buford/), [Fort Buford - Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/fort-buford-state-historic-site-williston)]

- <a id="nearby-mycic"></a>**Missouri-Yellowstone Confluence Interpretive Center** (1-1.5 hours, 3 miles) - Museum and interpretive center less than half a mile from Fort Buford showcasing prehistoric, natural, tribal, and pioneer history of the area. Main gallery features artifacts and information on Lewis and Clark's 1805-1806 visit, the fur trade, Fort Buford, and development of irrigation and electricity in North Dakota. Includes 20-minute film. $5.00 admission provides access to both the center and Fort Buford buildings. Open October 1-April 30: 9:00am-5:00pm Tuesday-Saturday, May 1-September 30: 9:00am-5:00pm daily; near Fort Buford State Historic Site, Williston, ND (approximately 47.987658, -104.000413); 3 miles from Fort Union Trading Post; 701-572-9034 [Source: [Missouri-Yellowstone Confluence Interpretive Center - SHSND](https://www.history.nd.gov/historicsites/mycic/), [MYCIC - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/missouri-yellowstone-confluence-interpretive-center.htm)]

- <a id="nearby-theodore-roosevelt"></a>**Theodore Roosevelt National Park - South Unit** (3-4 hours minimum, 83 miles) - Stunning badlands scenery with scenic drive, hiking trails, and wildlife viewing including bison, wild horses, and prairie dogs. Located approximately 2 hours southeast of Fort Union Trading Post. Features the 36-mile Scenic Loop Drive with overlooks, short nature trails, and longer backcountry hikes. Visitor center and museum in Medora. Open year-round, visitor center hours vary seasonally; South Unit Visitor Center: Interstate 94, Exit 24, Medora, ND 58645 (approximately 46.9133, -103.5338); 83 miles from Fort Union Trading Post, approximately 2 hours drive; 701-623-4466 [Source: [Theodore Roosevelt NP - Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/fort-union-trading-post-national-historic-park/), [North Dakota Historic Sites - NDTourism](https://www.ndtourism.com/articles/visit-theodore-roosevelt-national-park-and-north-dakota-historic-sites)]

- <a id="nearby-williston-arc"></a>**Williston Area Recreation Center (ARC)** (2-3 hours, 25 miles) - Indoor recreation facility featuring Flow Rider wave machine for simulating surfing or wakeboarding, indoor waterpark, golf simulator, and batting cages. Family-friendly entertainment option for active recreation; Williston, ND; 25 minutes from Fort Union Trading Post [Source: [Williston - NDTourism](https://www.ndtourism.com/cities/williston), [Great Plains Travel - Williston](https://greatplainstravel.com/things-to-do-williston/)]

- <a id="nearby-frontier-museum"></a>**Frontier Museum** (1-1.5 hours, 25 miles) - Local history museum in Williston featuring a rural church, two-story house, restored Great Northern depot, country school, and displays from the 1920s showcasing pioneer life in northwestern North Dakota; Williston, ND; 25 minutes from Fort Union Trading Post [Source: [Williston - Lewis and Clark Trail](https://www.lewisandclark.travel/listing/williston/), [Things to Do in Williston - Ryan Honda](https://www.ryanhonda.net/williston-guide/things-to-do-in-williston/)]

- <a id="nearby-lake-sakakawea"></a>**Lake Sakakawea** (half day to full day, 30 miles) - One of the largest man-made reservoirs in the United States, offering boating, fishing, camping, and water recreation. Multiple access points and marinas available with opportunities for walleye, northern pike, and salmon fishing. Scenic reservoir with numerous bays and recreational areas; near Williston, ND; approximately 30 minutes from Fort Union Trading Post [Source: [Williston - NDTourism](https://www.ndtourism.com/cities/williston), [Great Plains Travel - Williston](https://greatplainstravel.com/things-to-do-williston/)]
