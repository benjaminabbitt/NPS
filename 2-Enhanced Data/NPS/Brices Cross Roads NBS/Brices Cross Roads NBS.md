# Brices Cross Roads NBS (National Battlefield Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-natchez-trace-visitor-center"></a>**Natchez Trace Parkway Visitor Center** (2680 Natchez Trace Parkway, Tupelo, MS 38804 (34.3304166, -88.709833); 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM Central Time daily, closed Thanksgiving, December 25, and January 1; 1-800-305-7417) - The NPS passport cancellation stamps for Brices Cross Roads National Battlefield Site (along with Natchez Trace Parkway, Natchez Trace National Scenic Trail, and Tupelo National Battlefield) are available here at Milepost 266, approximately 15 miles south of the battlefield site [Source: [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/brcr/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/brcr/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NatchezTraceTravel](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-mississippi/tupelo-ms/142-brices-cross-roads-national-battlefield-site.html)]

**Note:** Call 1-800-305-7417 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. There are no visitor facilities at the actual one-acre battlefield monument site.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-self-guided-battlefield-driving-tour"></a>**Self-Guided Battlefield Driving Tour** (3-4 hours) - Follow the roughly 8-mile driving tour with 10 designated stops including First Shots of the Battle, Cavalry Skirmish locations, Federal and Confederate battle lines, the National Battlefield Monument, Log Cabin Knoll, Tishomingo Creek Bridge, James Jourdan's Grave, and United States Colored Troops position on White House Ridge; tour starts at Mississippi's Final Stands Interpretive Center, 607 Grisham St, Baldwyn, MS 38824 (34.50448, -88.65758); open year-round during daylight hours; free; many sites wheelchair accessible but signs can be difficult to locate; pets allowed on 6-foot leashes [Source: [NPS Exploring the Battlefield](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/exploring-the-brices-cross-roads-battlefield.htm), [NPPlan Driving Tour](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/mississippi-national-parks/brices-cross-roads-national-battlefield-site-park-at-a-glance/brices-cross-roads-national-battlefield-site-battlefield-driving-tour/), [NationalParksData](https://nationalparksdata.com/a-guide-to-visiting-brices-cross-roads-national-battlefield-site/)]

- <a id="activity-visit-mississippi-final-stands-interpretive-center"></a>**Visit Mississippi's Final Stands Interpretive Center** (1 hour) - Explore exhibits on the Battle of Brice's Cross Roads and the Battle of Tupelo featuring a battlefield model with over 1,500 finely painted miniatures showing final stages of each battle, watch a 25-minute film about the battles shown on demand, and view Civil War artifacts and information panels; 607 Grisham St, Baldwyn, MS 38824 (34.50448, -88.65758); Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM, closed Sunday and Monday; admission $7 adults, children under 7 free; 662-365-3969 [Source: [NPS Mississippi Final Stands](https://www.nps.gov/places/msfinalstands.htm), [Visit Mississippi](https://visitmississippi.org/things-to-do/civil-war/brices-cross-roads-national-battlefield-site/), [Backroad Planet](https://backroadplanet.com/brices-crossroads-tupelo-national-battlefield/), [American Battlefield Trust](https://www.battlefields.org/visit/heritage-sites/brices-crossroads-battlefield-visitor-and-interpretive-center)]

- <a id="activity-visit-the-one-acre-memorial-site"></a>**Visit the One-Acre Memorial Site** (20-30 minutes) - View the grassy commemorative park with flagpole, memorial monument, and two cannons marking the location where the Brice family house once stood at the crossroads; located on Mississippi Highway 370, approximately 6 miles west of Baldwyn, MS (34.506, -88.659); open 24 hours daily; free; no visitor facilities on-site [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/brcr/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brices_Cross_Roads_National_Battlefield_Site), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/brices-cross-roads-national-battlefield-site/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g43668-d1459371-Reviews-Brice_s_Crossroads_National_Battlefield-Baldwyn_Mississippi.html)]

- <a id="activity-explore-old-bethany-cemetery"></a>**Explore Old Bethany Cemetery** (15-20 minutes) - Visit the historic cemetery adjacent to the battlefield site that predates the Civil War, containing graves of more than 90 Confederate soldiers killed in the battle (96-98 headstones mark a mass grave with approximately 30 soldiers), plus see the site where the original 1852 Bethany A.R.P. Church stood (which was used as a hospital after the battle) and view the historical marker at the new church built in 1956 across the street; located adjacent to the National Battlefield Site on Highway 370, approximately 6 miles west of Baldwyn, MS; open dawn to dusk daily; free [Source: [NPPlan Bethany Cemetery](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/mississippi-national-parks/brices-cross-roads-national-battlefield-site-park-at-a-glance/brices-cross-roads-national-battlefield-site-battlefield-driving-tour/brices-cross-roads-national-battlefield-site-bethany-associate-reformed-presbyterian-church-cemetery/), [Lake.com](https://www.lake.com/parks/brices-cross-roads-national-battlefield-site/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-hike-battlefield-trails"></a>**Hike Battlefield Trails** (30-60 minutes) - Walk two short battlefield interpretive trails at tour stops along the driving route including the trail at Mississippi Counties Memorial; trails provide opportunities to explore battle positions and terrain features; trailheads along the driving tour route starting from 607 Grisham St, Baldwyn, MS; open dawn to dusk; free [Source: [NPS Exploring the Battlefield](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/exploring-the-brices-cross-roads-battlefield.htm), [NationalParksData](https://nationalparksdata.com/a-guide-to-visiting-brices-cross-roads-national-battlefield-site/)]

- <a id="gem-view-mississippi-state-historical-markers"></a>**View Mississippi State Historical Markers** (included in driving tour time) - Read detailed historical markers placed throughout the battlefield by the Mississippi Department of Archives and History and maintained by the Brice's Cross Roads National Battlefield Commission, providing in-depth context about troop movements and key moments of the June 10, 1864 battle; markers located at various stops along the 8-mile driving tour route; accessible 24 hours; free; note that most land beyond markers is private property [Source: [NPPlan Historical Markers](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/mississippi-national-parks/brices-cross-roads-national-battlefield-site-park-at-a-glance/brices-cross-roads-national-battlefield-site-battlefield-driving-tour/brices-cross-roads-national-battlefield-site-mississippi-state-historical-markers/), [NPS Exploring the Battlefield](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/exploring-the-brices-cross-roads-battlefield.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-tupelo-national-battlefield"></a>**Tupelo National Battlefield** (15-20 minutes) - Visit the one-acre monument commemorating the July 1864 Battle of Tupelo/Harrisburg where Union forces successfully defended against Confederate General Nathan Bedford Forrest's troops, featuring monuments, interpretive signage, and a cannon; located on Main Street (Highway 6/278), Tupelo, MS (15 miles south of Brices Cross Roads); grounds open dawn to dusk daily; free admission; also interpreted at Mississippi's Final Stands Interpretive Center; 1-800-305-7417 [Source: [NPS Tupelo Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/tupe/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Tupelo Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/tupe/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [American Battlefield Trust](https://www.battlefields.org/visit/battlefields/tupelo-battlefield)]

- <a id="nearby-elvis-presley-birthplace"></a>**Elvis Presley Birthplace & Museum** (2 hours) - Tour the modest two-room house where Elvis was born on January 8, 1935, built by his father for $180, and explore the 15-acre Elvis Presley Park including a museum chronicling his life, the "Elvis at 13" statue, his childhood Assembly of God Church (where grounds can be explored anytime), and various exhibits; 306 Elvis Presley Dr, Tupelo, MS 38804 (approximately 15 miles/30 minutes south of Brices Cross Roads); Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM; admission $17 for birthplace and museum combo ($8 house only), $5 ages 7-12, under 7 free [Source: [Elvis Birthplace Visit](https://elvispresleybirthplace.com/visit/), [Visit Tupelo](https://www.tupelo.net/directory/elvis-presley-birthplace-and-museum/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/elvis-presley-birthplace-and-museum-tupelo), [NatchezTraceTravel](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-mississippi/tupelo-ms/tupelo-mississippi.html)]

- <a id="nearby-natchez-trace-parkway-headquarters"></a>**Natchez Trace Parkway Headquarters & Visitor Center** (30-45 minutes) - Learn about the 444-mile scenic parkway following the historic Natchez Trace trail, view exhibits on the region's history and natural features, and obtain information about points of interest along the parkway; 2680 Natchez Trace Parkway, Tupelo, MS 38804 (34.3304166, -88.709833) at Milepost 266 (15 miles south of Brices Cross Roads); 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM Central Time daily, closed Thanksgiving, December 25, and January 1; free admission; 1-800-305-7417 [Source: [Visit Tupelo Natchez Trace](https://www.tupelo.net/directory/natchez-trace-parkway-visitor-center/), [NatchezTraceTravel](https://www.natcheztracetravel.com/natchez-trace-mississippi/tupelo-ms/139-tupelo-visitors-center.html)]
