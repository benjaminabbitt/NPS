# William Howard Taft NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

### Main Site
**Address:** 2038 Auburn Avenue, Cincinnati, Ohio 45219
**Geocoordinates:** (39.119899, -84.508528)
**Hours:** Thursday-Sunday: 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM; Closed Monday-Wednesday
**Phone:** Contact visitor center for current information

**Note:** National Park passport cancellation stamps are available at the visitor center front desk. Staff can help locate any special bonus stamps that might not be on display. [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/wiho/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [The Roaming Monk](https://roamingmonk.com/william-howard-taft-national-historic-site/)]

The site announced reduced operational hours in fall 2024, with interior facilities closed Monday-Wednesday, though exterior park grounds remain open daily. [Source: [NPS News Release](https://www.nps.gov/wiho/learn/news/wiho-reduced-hours-fall-2024.htm)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-guided-house-tour"></a>**Guided House Tour** (30 minutes) - Ranger-led tours of the first floor period rooms showcase William Howard Taft's birthplace and boyhood home with authentic furnishings and family artifacts; tours depart at the top and bottom of every hour on Thursday-Friday, with final departure at 3:30 PM; on Saturday-Sunday, tours offered at 9 AM, 10 AM, 1 PM, and 2 PM; 2038 Auburn Avenue, Cincinnati, Ohio 45219; Thursday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM [Source: [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/wiho/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [NPS Touring the Taft Family House](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/tour-taft-house.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60993-d137349-Reviews-William_Howard_Taft_National_Historic_Site-Cincinnati_Ohio.html)]

- <a id="activity-biographical-film-education-center-exhibits"></a>**Biographical Film & Education Center Exhibits** (15-20 minutes) - Watch an informative 14-15 minute biographical film about Taft and his family, then explore Gallery I featuring a life-size animatronic figure of Charles P. Taft II (William's son) who discusses "quite a family" and Taft's unique distinction as both 27th President and 10th Chief Justice; 2038 Auburn Avenue, Cincinnati, Ohio 45219; Thursday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM [Source: [Best Western Guide](https://www.bestwestern.com/en_US/hotels/destinations/united-states/great-lakes/ohio/things-to-do/outdoor-adventure/nat-parks/william-howard-taft.html), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60993-d137349-Reviews-William_Howard_Taft_National_Historic_Site-Cincinnati_Ohio.html)]

- <a id="activity-self-guided-second-floor-exhibits"></a>**Self-Guided Second Floor Exhibits** (20-30 minutes) - Explore museum-style exhibits on the second floor showcasing Taft's political career, personal memorabilia, and his impact on American history; includes detailed information about his presidency and Supreme Court tenure; 2038 Auburn Avenue, Cincinnati, Ohio 45219; Thursday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM [Source: [Road Trips & Coffee](https://www.roadtripsandcoffee.com/william-howard-taft-national-historic-site/), [NPS Field Trips](https://www.nps.gov/wiho/learn/education/classrooms/fieldtrips.htm)]

- <a id="activity-junior-ranger-program"></a>**Junior Ranger Program** (30-45 minutes) - Complete activities from the Junior Ranger booklet available online or at the visitor center; activities teach about Taft's life, the presidency, and Cincinnati history; suitable for all ages; earn a Junior Ranger badge upon completion; 2038 Auburn Avenue, Cincinnati, Ohio 45219; Thursday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM [Source: [NPS Junior Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/wiho/learn/kidsyouth/beajuniorranger.htm), [NPS Junior Ranger Program](https://www.nps.gov/wiho/learn/kidsyouth/junior-ranger.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-mount-adams-greek-revival-architecture"></a>**Mount Adams Greek Revival Architecture** (15-20 minutes) - The Taft birthplace sits atop one of Cincinnati's most prominent hilltops in the historic Mount Adams neighborhood; the two-story Greek Revival house itself is architecturally significant and provides panoramic views of the area; the site is described as a "hidden gem" that many Cincinnati residents don't even know exists; 2038 Auburn Avenue, Cincinnati, Ohio 45219; grounds accessible daily [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60993-d137349-Reviews-William_Howard_Taft_National_Historic_Site-Cincinnati_Ohio.html), [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/161150/william-howard-taft-national-historic-site)]

- <a id="gem-national-park-bookstore"></a>**National Park Bookstore** (10-15 minutes) - Browse a well-curated selection of books about presidential history, the Taft family, Cincinnati history, and the Gilded Age; staff are knowledgeable and can recommend titles for deeper learning; located in the Taft Education Center; 2038 Auburn Avenue, Cincinnati, Ohio 45219; Thursday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM [Source: [Best Western Guide](https://www.bestwestern.com/en_US/hotels/destinations/united-states/great-lakes/ohio/things-to-do/outdoor-adventure/nat-parks/william-howard-taft.html)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-cincinnati-art-museum"></a>**Cincinnati Art Museum** (2-3 hours, 1.2 miles) - Features over 73,000 works of art spanning 6,000 years including American, European, African, and Asian collections; general admission and parking are free; located in Eden Park with beautiful architecture and grounds; 953 Eden Park Drive, Cincinnati, Ohio 45202; Tuesday-Sunday 11:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Thursday until 8:00 PM) [Source: [TripAdvisor Eden Park](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60993-d138527-Reviews-Eden_Park-Cincinnati_Ohio.html), [Visit Cincy Mount Adams](https://www.visitcincy.com/blog/post/mount-adams-neighborhood-guide-cincinnati/)]

- <a id="nearby-krohn-conservatory"></a>**Krohn Conservatory** (45-60 minutes, 1.3 miles) - Art Deco greenhouse built in 1933 containing over 3,500 plant species from around the world; features seasonal floral shows and a stunning butterfly garden; located in Eden Park; 1501 Eden Park Drive, Cincinnati, Ohio 45202; Tuesday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [TripAdvisor Eden Park](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60993-d138527-Reviews-Eden_Park-Cincinnati_Ohio.html), [Mt. Adams Cincy](https://www.mtadamscincy.com/attractions/)]

- <a id="nearby-holy-cross-immaculata-church"></a>**Holy Cross-Immaculata Church** (20-30 minutes, 0.4 miles) - Historic 1859 church perched on a bluff overlooking the Ohio River and downtown Cincinnati; famous for its Good Friday pilgrimage with 85 steps; beautiful architecture and stunning city views; 30 Guido Street, Cincinnati, Ohio 45202; accessible during service times and special events [Source: [Visit Cincy Mount Adams](https://www.visitcincy.com/blog/post/mount-adams-neighborhood-guide-cincinnati/), [Mt. Adams Cincy](https://www.mtadamscincy.com/attractions/)]

- <a id="nearby-national-underground-railroad-freedom-center"></a>**National Underground Railroad Freedom Center** (2.5-3 hours, 3.5 miles) - Powerful museum documenting the history of the Underground Railroad and ongoing struggles for freedom; three floors of exhibits including artifacts, interactive displays, and a preserved slave pen; visitors recommend allowing at least 3 hours; 50 East Freedom Way, Cincinnati, Ohio 45202; Wednesday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM; closed Monday-Tuesday [Source: [Freedom Center Plan Your Visit](https://freedomcenter.org/visit/plan-your-visit/), [US News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/Cincinnati_OH/Things_To_Do/National_Underground_Railroad_Freedom_Center_62948/)]

- <a id="nearby-american-sign-museum"></a>**American Sign Museum** (1-1.5 hours, 4.2 miles) - Unique museum showcasing vintage and historic advertising signs from the 19th century to present; features neon signs, painted signs, and electric signs in a visually stunning display; recommended for photography enthusiasts and design lovers; 1330 Monmouth Avenue, Cincinnati, Ohio 45225; Wednesday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM; closed Monday-Tuesday [Source: [American Sign Museum Visit](https://www.americansignmuseum.org/visit/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/american-sign-museum-cincinnati)]

- <a id="nearby-cincinnati-museum-center-at-union-terminal"></a>**Cincinnati Museum Center at Union Terminal** (3-4 hours, 4.8 miles) - Magnificent Art Deco train station housing three museums: Cincinnati History Museum, Museum of Natural History & Science, and the Children's Museum; features the world's largest half-dome and stunning architecture; admission includes all three museums; 1301 Western Avenue, Cincinnati, Ohio 45203; daily 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [Cincinnati Museum Center Hours](https://www.cincymuseum.org/hours/), [US News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/Cincinnati_OH/Things_To_Do/Cincinnati_Museum_Center_at_Union_Terminal_62946/)]

- <a id="nearby-findlay-market"></a>**Findlay Market** (1-2 hours, 3.8 miles) - Ohio's oldest continuously operated public market (since 1855); over 50 year-round indoor merchants selling meat, produce, cheese, flowers, and specialty foods plus weekend outdoor farmers market; best visited on Saturday mornings for the full bustling market experience; 1801 Race Street, Cincinnati, Ohio 45202; Tuesday-Friday 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Saturday 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Sunday 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM [Source: [US News Travel Findlay Market](https://travel.usnews.com/Cincinnati_OH/Things_To_Do/Findlay_Market_62949/)]
