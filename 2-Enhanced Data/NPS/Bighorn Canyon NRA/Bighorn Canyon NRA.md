# Bighorn Canyon NRA (National Recreation Area)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Cal S. Taggart Bighorn Canyon Visitor Center** (20 US Hwy 14A, Lovell, WY 82431 (44.83752, -108.37672); Wednesday-Sunday 9 AM-4 PM; 307-548-5406) - South District visitor center open year-round with varying seasonal hours [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/planyourvisit/bighorn-canyon-visitor-center.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

- **Yellowtail Dam Visitor Center** (Rte 210, Fort Smith, MT (45.3127372, -107.937055); Memorial Day to Labor Day, daily 8:30 AM-5 PM; 406-666-9961) - North District visitor center located near the top of the dam [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/planyourvisit/yellowtail-dam-visitor-center.htm), [Source](https://southeastmontana.com/listing/yellowtail-dam-visitor-center)]

**Note:** Call visitor centers to confirm current stamp locations and hours before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Scenic Boat Tour** (2 hours) - Explore 16 miles up Bighorn Canyon with views of wildlife including bighorn sheep, wild horses, and over 230 species of birds; departs from Horseshoe Bend Marina (14 miles from Lovell, WY off Highway 37) at 10 AM and 2 PM daily, with sunset cruises Friday and Saturday evenings at 6:30 PM; 307-548-7230 [[Source](https://www.hiddentreasurecharters.com/bighorn-canyon-scenic-boat-tour/), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/planyourvisit/marinas.htm)]

- **Devil Canyon Overlook** (30 minutes-1 hour) - View the Bighorn River 1,000 feet below from a paved path with multiple viewpoints and informational signs; turn north onto Hwy 37 from 14A near Lovell, WY, and remain on Hwy 37 for approximately 15 miles to paved parking lot [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/planyourvisit/devil-canyon.htm), [Source](https://theadventuringheidi.com/2022/09/06/a-quickie-in-bighorn-canyon-national-recreation-area/)]

- **Fishing at Bighorn Lake and River** (2-8 hours) - World-renowned fishing for 38 species including trout, crappie, smallmouth bass, and walleye; public access at Afterbay Dam (River Launch), 3 Mile (Lind Ranch), and 13 Mile (Bighorn Access); best shoreline fishing at the buoy line, campgrounds, and Historic Big Horn Headgate [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/planyourvisit/fishing.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/planyourvisit/bighorn-river-in-montana.htm)]

- **Guided Kayaking Tours** (2-4 hours) - Free ranger-led kayaking tours on Bighorn Lake; check with visitor centers for upcoming tour times; self-guided trips range from 4-6 miles (half day) to 35-38 miles (five days) [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Source](https://rovingvails.com/get-outdoors/bighorn-canyon-national-recreation-area-best-place-to-visit-in-montana/)]

- **Boating and Water Recreation** (half day or full day) - Kayaking, canoeing, or boating on Bighorn Lake's 12,700 surface acres with 191 miles of shoreline; two marinas: Ok-A-Beh Marina (North District) and Horseshoe Bend Marina (South District); boat rentals available at 9 AM or 12:30 PM (half-day) and full-day [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/planyourvisit/boating.htm), [Source](https://www.hiddentreasurecharters.com/our-rentals/)]

- **Sullivan's Knob Trail** (30 minutes-1 hour) - 0.75-0.9 mile loop trail with 203-300 feet elevation gain approaching the canyon edge; located about 0.8 miles north of Devil Canyon turnoff; relatively flat throughout with big payoff for little effort [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/planyourvisit/sullivans-knob-trail.htm), [Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/montana/sullivans-knob-trail)]

- **Hiking Trails** (1-3 hours) - Over 17 miles of trails ranging from short walks to dramatic overlooks; North District has 3 trails and South District has 12 trails; popular trails include Upper Layout Creek (4.4 miles round trip, 2.5-3 hours) and Hillsboro historic ranch walk (1.4 miles, 1 hour) [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [Source](https://wanderfilledlife.com/bighorn-canyon/)]

- **Pryor Mountain Wild Horse Range** (2-4 hours) - View wild mustangs along the paved Route 37 which parallels Bighorn Canyon; best viewing late spring through early fall (June-September) when roads are accessible; Pryor Mountain Wild Mustang Center in Lovell open Monday-Friday 10 AM-5 PM year-round [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/learn/nature/pryor-mountain-wild-horse-range.htm), [Source](https://southeastmontana.com/listing/pryor-mountains-wild-horse-range)]

- **Historic Ranch Tours** (1-2 hours each) - Four historic ranches including Ewing-Snell Ranch and Mason-Lovell Ranch offer self-guided tours and ranger-led tours when staffing allows during summer; contact visitor center at 307-548-5406 at least 48 hours in advance for accessibility arrangements [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/learn/historyculture/historic-ranches.htm), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/planyourvisit/historic-ranch-tours.htm)]

- **Wildlife Viewing** (1-3 hours) - Observe 231 species of birds, bighorn sheep, black bear, mule deer, mountain lions, pronghorn, coyotes, beavers, and marmots in varied climates throughout the recreation area [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Source](https://rovingvails.com/get-outdoors/bighorn-canyon-national-recreation-area-best-place-to-visit-in-montana/)]

- **Camping** (overnight) - Seven designated campgrounds with four drive-in campgrounds open year-round on a first-come, first-serve basis; free camping; popular campgrounds include Horseshoe Bend, Barry's Landing ($18/night mid-April through October), and Trail Creek [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/planyourvisit/campgrounds.htm), [Source](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/247536)]

- **Swimming** (1-2 hours) - Swimming permitted throughout the recreation area; lifeguarded areas at Horseshoe Bend and Ok-A-Beh recommended [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Natural Arch near Devil Canyon** (30 minutes-1 hour) - Lesser-known natural arch south of Devil Canyon Overlook parking lot offering stunning photo opportunities; information limited but worth seeking out for photographers [[Source](https://theadventuringheidi.com/2022/09/06/a-quickie-in-bighorn-canyon-national-recreation-area/)]

- **Sykes Mountain Trail** (2-3 hours) - Quiet hiking trail offering solitude despite the park's beauty; few visitors know about the hiking opportunities on land, making this a peaceful alternative to water-based activities [[Source](https://travelwyoming.com/places-to-go/destinations/national-historic-sites-recreation-areas/bighorn-canyon-national-recreation-area/)]

- **Boat-in Campsites** (overnight) - Remote camping accessible only by boat; offers complete solitude and stunning canyon views; significantly less crowded than Yellowstone (250,000 visitors vs. 4 million annually) [[Source](https://myitchytravelfeet.com/explore-bighorn-canyon/)]

- **Afterbay Fishing Spots** (2-4 hours) - First three miles below Afterbay Dam is a wade-angler's paradise with iconic spots like "Breakfast Hole," "Hot-Dog Run," and "Red Cliffs"; special regulations allow live bait within 200 yards of dam [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/planyourvisit/fishing-the-afterbay.htm), [Source](https://flyfishingbozeman.com/montana-fly-fishing-rivers/bighorn-river-montana)]

- **Penn's Cabin via 4WD** (3-4 hours round trip) - Accessible via rutted dirt road through the forest; good horse viewing spot for wild mustangs; requires four-wheel drive vehicle [[Source](https://www.visitmt.com/listings/general/blm-recreation-area/pryor-mountains-wild-horse-range)]

## Also Nearby

- **Medicine Wheel National Historic Landmark** (2-3 hours, 25 miles east of Lovell) - Sacred Native American site at 9,640 feet elevation on Medicine Mountain; 3-mile round-trip walk from parking lot to the wheel; open mid-June through mid-September (access road opens July 1); take Medicine Wheel Passage 32 miles east of Lovell on US Highway 14A, then 1.5 miles northwest on Forest Road 12 [[Source](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/bighorn/natural-resources/arch-cultural/medicine-wheel-medicine-mountain-national-historic), [Source](https://travelwyoming.com/listing/medicine-wheel-national-historic-landmark/738/)]

- **Porcupine Falls** (1-2 hours, 35 miles east of Lovell) - Short, steep 0.8-mile trail with 360 feet elevation gain to beautiful waterfall in Bighorn National Forest; best in late spring through early fall; from Lovell take Hwy 14A east for 35 miles, turn north onto FSR 14 for 10 miles to FSR 146; no admission fee, open dawn to dusk [[Source](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/bighorn/recreation/porcupine-falls-trail-135), [Source](https://waterfallguides.com/wy-porcupine-falls/)]

- **Little Bighorn Battlefield National Monument** (2-4 hours, 58 miles northeast) - Site of the 1876 Battle of the Little Bighorn; open year-round with seasonal hours (8 AM-4:30 PM winter, 8 AM-6 PM spring/fall, 8 AM-9 PM summer); visitor center currently closed for renovations but contact station open Friday-Sunday 8 AM-4 PM; Interstate 90 Frontage Rd, Crow Agency, MT [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/libi/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://www.visittheusa.com/experience/insiders-guide-little-bighorn-battlefield-national-monument)]

- **Cody, Wyoming** (1 full day, 46 miles west) - "Rodeo Capital of the World" with nightly rodeos June-August, Buffalo Bill Center of the West (world-class museum complex, 2-day entry), Buffalo Bill Dam, and downtown historic Irma Hotel; diverse activities including hiking, fishing, river tubing, and horseback riding [[Source](https://centerofthewest.org), [Source](https://travelwyoming.com/places-to-go/cities/cody/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 2-3 days for a comprehensive experience including major activities, scenic drives, and wildlife viewing; minimum 1 day for Devil Canyon Overlook, visitor center, and one water activity [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/bica/planyourvisit/itineraries.htm), [Source](https://myitchytravelfeet.com/explore-bighorn-canyon/)]
