# Cape Henry MEM (Memorial)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-old-cape-henry-lighthouse"></a>**Old Cape Henry Lighthouse** (583 Atlantic Avenue, Fort Story, Virginia Beach, VA 23459 (36.9262, -76.0083); 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM daily, extended hours March 16 - October 31: 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed December 24-25, 31, January 1-2, and Thanksgiving; 757-422-9421) [Source: [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/came/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Preservation Virginia](https://preservationvirginia.org/historic-sites/cape-henry-lighthouse/)]

**Note:** NPS passport stamp available in lighthouse gift shop operated by Preservation Virginia. Memorial marks English settlers' April 26, 1607 arrival (13 years before Plymouth Rock). Military base entry requires REAL ID for visitors 18+, valid vehicle registration, and insurance. Cannot drive personal vehicle onto base without official access - park near gate and take free shuttle. Security processing can take 1 hour; arrive 2+ hours before closing. Parking fills by 11 AM April-November. $8 admission; discounts available. [Source: [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/came/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Visit Virginia Beach](https://www.visitvirginiabeach.com/experiences/attractions/cape-henry-lighthouse/)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-view-cape-henry-memorial-cross"></a>**View Cape Henry Memorial Cross** (15-30 minutes) - See 1935 granite cross marking first English landing site in Virginia; self-guided tours daily with historic interpreters discussing area history, lighthouse, military, and STEM topics [Source: [Visit Virginia Beach Cape Henry Memorial](https://www.visitvirginiabeach.com/listing/cape-henry-memorial-cross/34/), [Cape Henry Memorial Tours](https://www.expedia.com/Cape-Henry-Memorial-Fort-Story.d6093281.Vacation-Attraction)]

- <a id="activity-climb-old-cape-henry-lighthouse"></a>**Climb Old Cape Henry Lighthouse** (30-45 minutes) - Ascend America's first federally funded lighthouse built 1792; open 7 days/week, weather permitting; within active Fort Story military base; $8 admission [Source: [Preservation Virginia](https://preservationvirginia.org/historic-sites/cape-henry-lighthouse/), [Visit Virginia Beach](https://www.visitvirginiabeach.com/experiences/attractions/cape-henry-lighthouse/)]

- <a id="activity-visit-st-theresa-chapel"></a>**Visit St. Theresa Chapel** (15-20 minutes) - Tour historic chapel near memorial; open for visitation [Source: [Visit Virginia Beach](https://www.visitvirginiabeach.com/experiences/attractions/cape-henry-lighthouse/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-explore-first-landing-chapel"></a>**Explore First Landing Chapel** (15-20 minutes) - Discover historic chapel commemorating first landing; adjacent to memorial cross [Source: [Visit Virginia Beach](https://www.visitvirginiabeach.com/experiences/attractions/cape-henry-lighthouse/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-first-landing-state-park"></a>**First Landing State Park** (15 minutes, adjacent) - Experience 19 miles of trails through cypress swamp, salt marsh, maritime forest, freshwater wetlands, dunes, and bay shoreline; boating, swimming, nature programs, hiking, biking, picnicking, boat launch, camping, cabin rentals; open daily 9:00 AM - 10:00 PM Memorial Day Weekend - Labor Day [Source: [First Landing State Park](https://www.dcr.virginia.gov/state-parks/first-landing), [Visit Virginia Beach](https://www.visitvirginiabeach.com/explore/outdoor-activities/parks/first-landing-state-park/)]

- <a id="nearby-virginia-beach-oceanfront"></a>**Virginia Beach Oceanfront** (20 minutes) - Visit 3-mile boardwalk with restaurants, shops, entertainment, and beach activities; year-round destination [Source: [Visit Virginia Beach](https://www.visitvirginiabeach.com/listing/cape-henry-memorial-cross/34/)]
