## Key Activities

- **Wild Horse Viewing (2-4 hours)** - See the famous wild horses of Assateague roaming freely across the island, best viewed from late April through mid-October with horses visible year-round along main roads, near campgrounds, and throughout the Maryland district from the north end down to the Virginia state line; must maintain 40 feet distance; 7206 National Seashore Lane, Berlin, MD 21811; Daily 24/7 access (park open year-round) [Source: [Assateague's Wild Horses](https://www.nps.gov/asis/learn/nature/horses.htm), [See the Wild Horses](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/see-the-wild-horses-in-the-maryland-district.htm), [Safety Tips](https://www.nps.gov/asis/learn/nature/safety-tips-for-viewing-the-wild-horses.htm)]

- **Beach Swimming and Sunbathing (2-6 hours)** - Enjoy swimming, surfing, and relaxing on two miles of pristine Atlantic Ocean beaches with sandy shores and coastal bays; Assateague Island NS, Berlin, MD 21811 and Chincoteague, VA 23336; Daily sunrise to sunset [Source: [Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/asis/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/asis/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Kayaking and Canoeing (2-4 hours)** - Paddle through coastal bays, salt marshes, and waterways with ranger-led trips lasting 2 hours and wildlife tours lasting 2-2.5 hours; rentals available from 2 hours to a full week; backcountry kayaking trips to campsites 5 miles down the island take approximately 4 hours one way; Bayside areas and launch points throughout park; Hours vary by rental company and season [Source: [Kayaking and Canoeing](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/maryland-national-parks/assateague-island-national-seashore-park-at-a-glance/assateague-island-national-seashore-kayaking-and-canoeing/), [Assateague Island Adventure](https://paddling.com/paddle/trips/assateague-island-adventure)]

- **Life of the Marsh Trail (0.5-1 hour)** - Walk this 0.5-mile boardwalk trail with minimal elevation through marsh habitat offering stunning views of Sinepuxent Bay, excellent for birdwatching and wildlife spotting; Near Assateague Island Visitor Center, 7206 National Seashore Lane, Berlin, MD 21811; Daily dawn to dusk [Source: [Life of the Marsh Trail](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/maryland/life-of-the-marsh-trail--2), [Maryland Hiking Trails](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/maryland-national-parks/assateague-island-national-seashore-park-at-a-glance/assateague-island-national-seashore-hiking-trails/assateague-island-national-seashore-maryland-hiking-trails/assateague-island-national-seashore-life-of-the-marsh-trail/)]

- **Life of the Forest Trail (20-30 minutes)** - Hike this short trail winding through vibrant maritime forest teeming with wildlife, leading to a scenic boardwalk section with views of Sinepuxent Bay; Near Assateague Island Visitor Center, 7206 National Seashore Lane, Berlin, MD 21811; Daily dawn to dusk [Source: [Life of the Forest Trail](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/maryland-national-parks/assateague-island-national-seashore-park-at-a-glance/assateague-island-national-seashore-hiking-trails/assateague-island-national-seashore-maryland-hiking-trails/assateague-island-national-seashore-life-of-the-forest-trail/), [Take a Hike!](https://www.nps.gov/asis/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)]

- **Birdwatching (2-4 hours)** - Observe over 300 species of birds annually along the Atlantic Flyway including shorebirds, eagles, herons, egrets, oystercatchers, pelicans, skimmers, terns, and gulls; best during spring and fall migrations with August-September optimal for shorebirds; trails throughout park and especially Chincoteague National Wildlife Refuge; Daily dawn to dusk [Source: [Birds](https://www.nps.gov/asis/learn/nature/birds.htm), [Birdwatching Assateague](https://www.assateagueisland.com/birdwatching/birdwatching.htm), [How to See and Photograph Wildlife](https://www.nps.gov/asis/planyourvisit/how-to-see-and-photograph-wildlife.htm)]

- **Surf Fishing (4-8 hours)** - Fish along the Atlantic coastline for striped bass, bluefish, flounder, and other species; no saltwater license required for surf fishing on the coast, though after-hours permit required on Virginia end; state seasons and regulations apply; Beach access points throughout park; Daily 24/7 [Source: [Surf Fishing](https://www.nps.gov/asis/planyourvisit/surf-fishing.htm), [Permits & Reservations](https://www.nps.gov/asis/planyourvisit/permitsandreservations.htm)]

- **Over-Sand Vehicle (OSV) Beach Driving (4+ hours)** - Drive on designated beach sections with 4WD vehicles by permit only (Standard OSV $110, Bullpen camping permit $200); permits good for one year; vehicles limited to under 10,000 pounds, under 26 feet long, and 8 feet wide with 7 inches ground clearance; Maryland allows 145 vehicles maximum, Virginia section closed March 15-August 31 for bird nesting; Maryland and Virginia beach access points; Permit allows 24/7 access [Source: [Over Sand Vehicle Use](https://www.nps.gov/asis/planyourvisit/osv.htm), [OSV Permits](https://www.recreation.gov/vehiclepermits/3332403), [Beach Driving](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/maryland-national-parks/assateague-island-national-seashore-park-at-a-glance/assateague-island-national-seashore-beach-driving/)]

- **Camping - Oceanside and Bayside (Overnight)** - Camp at oceanside or bayside campgrounds with access to beaches, salt marshes, maritime forests, and coastal bays; reservations required for regular campgrounds, 7-day passes available; Assateague Island National Seashore Campground, Berlin, MD; Reservations and hours vary [Source: [Camping At Assateague](https://www.nps.gov/asis/planyourvisit/marylandcamping.htm), [Campgrounds](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/232507)]

- **Crabbing (2-4 hours)** - Catch blue crabs in coastal waters with maximum of one bushel of hard crabs per day per person; state seasons, limits and regulations apply; inquire at visitor centers for current seasons; Bayside waters throughout park; Seasonal, check with visitor centers [Source: [Crabbing](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/crabbing-at-assateague-island.htm), [Crabbing](https://home.nps.gov/asis/planyourvisit/crabbing.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Little Beach (2-4 hours)** - Access this true hidden gem beach only by boat for perfect solitude away from crowds, offering secluded shoreline and pristine natural beauty; Accessible by kayak or small boat from mainland; Dawn to dusk [Source: [Hidden Beaches](https://chincoteaguetourist.com/guides/hidden-beaches-in-assateague-island/)]

- **Swan Cove Trail (1-2 hours)** - Hike this lesser-known trail leading to a secluded beach area that remains quiet compared to tourist-heavy parts of the island, offering peaceful wildlife viewing; Part of Chincoteague National Wildlife Refuge trail system; Daily dawn to dusk [Source: [Hidden Beaches](https://chincoteaguetourist.com/guides/hidden-beaches-in-assateague-island/)]

- **Backcountry Camping (Overnight)** - Camp at remote backcountry sites accessible only by backpacking or hand-paddled watercraft (no motorized transport); same-day permits only ($10 per person plus $25 vehicle fee for 7-day pass), obtained at Maryland Ranger Station or Toms Cove Visitor Center at least 2-8 hours before sunset depending on site; spring and fall best seasons; Tingles Island and other backcountry sites; Permit required, obtain day-of only [Source: [Backcountry Camping](https://www.nps.gov/asis/planyourvisit/backcountry-camping.htm), [Permits & Reservations](https://www.nps.gov/asis/planyourvisit/permitsandreservations.htm)]

- **Shell Collecting at Tom's Cove Spit (1-2 hours)** - Search for shells at the tip of the Tom's Cove spit of land, the most productive area for beachcombing on the island; Southern end of Virginia district, Tom's Cove area; Daily dawn to dusk [Source: [Hidden Activities](https://chincoteaguetourist.com/guides/hidden-beaches-in-assateague-island/)]

- **Western Side Island Exploration (2-4 hours)** - Explore the most secluded western side of the island via kayak or boat for quiet nature experiences away from main tourist areas; Bayside access points; Dawn to dusk [Source: [Hidden Beaches](https://chincoteaguetourist.com/guides/hidden-beaches-in-assateague-island/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Ocean City Boardwalk (3-5 hours, 15 miles)** - Walk nearly three miles of one of America's "Best Boardwalks" featuring Trimper's Rides (operating since 1893 with 100+ rides), Jolly Roger amusement park, Ocean City Life-Saving Station Museum, Ripley's Believe It or Not! Museum, and classic boardwalk foods including Thrashers Fries, Fisher's Popcorn, and Dumser's Ice Cream; stretches from the inlet to 27th Street; Boardwalk, Ocean City, MD 21842; Daily, hours vary by attraction [Source: [Ocean City Boardwalk](https://www.ococean.com/things-to-do/attractions/boardwalk/), [Boardwalk Guide](https://www.oceancity.com/boardwalk/), [Best Things to Do](https://www.touropia.com/things-to-do-in-ocean-city-md/)]

- **Berlin, Maryland Historic Downtown (2-3 hours, 8 miles)** - Explore "Coolest Small Town in America" and USA Today's Best Town for Shopping with over 60 retail shops, galleries, antiques, 15+ dining establishments, and 47 structures on the National Historic Register; designated Maryland Main Street Community and Arts & Entertainment District; features 2nd Fridays with live music and wine tastings, plus Farmers Market Sundays 9-1 May-Sept; Main Street, Berlin, MD 21811; Shop hours typically 10:00 AM-5:00 PM, varies by business [Source: [Berlin Main Street](https://berlinmainstreet.com/), [Main Street Berlin](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60727-d1992987-Reviews-Main_Street_Berlin-Berlin_Maryland.html), [Things to Do](https://vacationidea.com/maryland/best-things-to-do-in-berlin-md.html)]

- **Museum of Chincoteague Island (1-2 hours, 12 miles)** - Visit museum featuring the 1st Order Fresnel Lens from Assateague Island Lighthouse, famous Chincoteague ponies Misty and Stormy, historic vessel models, shipwreck artifacts, and decoy carver's workshop; 7125 Maddox Boulevard, Chincoteague, VA 23336; March-early December, 10:00 AM-4:00 PM; 757-336-6117 [Source: [Chincoteague Island](https://travel.com/regions/north-america/united-states/virginia/chincoteague-island-virginia-best-things-to-do-top-picks/), [Things To Do](https://www.chincoteague.com/things-to-do/)]

- **Island Theatre and Misty Hoof Prints (30 minutes, 12 miles)** - See the historic Island Theatre with its classic marquee on Main Street where famous pony Misty's actual hoof prints are preserved in cement from the 1961 movie premiere; Main Street, Chincoteague, VA 23336; Exterior viewing anytime, theater hours vary [Source: [Things To Do](https://www.chincoteague.com/things-to-do/), [Chincoteague Attractions](https://travel.com/regions/north-america/united-states/virginia/chincoteague-island-virginia-best-things-to-do-top-picks/)]

- **Assateague Lighthouse (30-60 minutes, 12 miles)** - Climb 175 steps to breathtaking panoramic views in this historic lighthouse with tours including the keeper's house and oil house; free admission with suggested donation for restoration; children 2-12 must be accompanied by adult; Beach Road, Chincoteague, VA 23336; April-November, Fridays & Saturdays 9:00 AM-3:00 PM (9:00 AM-3:30 PM through Labor Day, Saturdays only after Labor Day until Thanksgiving); 757-336-2872 [Source: [Assateague Lighthouse Tours](https://www.assateagueisland.com/lighthouse/lighthouse_info.htm), [Assateague Lighthouse](https://www.nps.gov/asis/learn/historyculture/assateague-lighthouse.htm), [Lighthouse Info](https://chincoteaguetraveler.com/assateague-lighthouse-is-now-open-for-tours-again/)]

- **NASA Wallops Visitor Center (45 minutes-1 hour, 10 miles)** - Explore FREE exhibits about aeronautics, orbital and sub-orbital rockets, scientific balloons, current missions, and Wallops history; features auditorium, Science on the Sphere Theater, observation deck, and Launch Viewing Area located 7 miles from launch range; Route 175, Building J-17, Wallops Island, VA 23337; Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM; 757-824-1344 [Source: [Wallops Visitor Center](https://www.nasa.gov/wallops/visitor-center/), [NASA Wallops](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g58283-d498110-Reviews-NASA_Wallops_Visitor_Center-Wallops_Island_Virginia.html), [Plan Your Visit](https://www.nasa.gov/wallops/visitor-center/plan-your-visit/)]

- **Chincoteague Pony Swim (All day event, 12 miles)** - Witness the famous annual tradition dating back to 1925 where wild ponies swim across the channel between Assateague and Chincoteague islands, drawing thousands of visitors; Last Wednesday of July; Memorial Park, Chincoteague, VA 23336; Event typically morning through afternoon [Source: [Things To Do](https://www.chincoteague.com/things-to-do/), [Chincoteague Island](https://travel.com/regions/north-america/united-states/virginia/chincoteague-island-virginia-best-things-to-do-top-picks/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** A full day (6-8 hours) is recommended for first-time visitors to experience the main attractions including wild horse viewing, beach activities, nature trails, and visitor centers. Visitors could easily spend an entire weekend exploring both Maryland and Virginia districts with camping, kayaking, and extended activities. One visitor spent approximately 5 hours on the Maryland side and 3 hours on the Virginia side for an 8-hour comprehensive day trip. The $25 seven-day entrance pass allows multiple visits to explore different areas and activities.
