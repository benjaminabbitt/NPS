# Bryce Canyon NP (National Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Bryce Canyon Visitor Center** (Highway 63, PO Box 640201, Bryce, UT 84764 (37.6383, -112.1667); Summer 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM daily, reduced hours other seasons 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 435-834-5322) [Source: [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/brca/planyourvisit/tourvisitor.htm), [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/brca/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Contact](https://www.nps.gov/brca/contacts.htm)]

**Note:** Call 435-834-5322 to confirm current stamp location and hours before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Hike Queens Garden/Navajo Loop Combination** (1-2 hours) - Take the most popular hike combining open views and unique hoodoos of the Queen's Garden Trail with iconic switchbacks and towering canyon walls of the Navajo Loop; 2.9 miles with 600 feet elevation change; trailhead at Sunset Point; open year-round weather permitting [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/brca/planyourvisit/day-hikes.htm), [Visit Utah](https://www.visitutah.com/places-to-go/parks-outdoors/bryce-canyon/adventure-guide), [We're in the Rockies](https://wereintherockies.com/bryce-canyon-itinerary/)]

- **Drive the Scenic Highway 63** (2-3 hours with stops) - Travel the 18-mile scenic drive from park entrance to Rainbow Point with stunning viewpoints including Inspiration Point, Bryce Point, and Rainbow Point; takes 30-40 minutes without stops, 2-3 hours to properly enjoy viewpoints; Highway 63 through park; open year-round except when closed by snow [Source: [We're in the Rockies](https://wereintherockies.com/bryce-canyon-itinerary/), [NPS Plan Visit](https://www.nps.gov/brca/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- **Walk the Rim Trail** (30 minutes to all day) - Stroll the 5.5-mile trail from Bryce Point to Fairyland Point with the easiest paved section from Sunset to Sunrise Point; accessible year-round; free with park admission [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/brca/planyourvisit/day-hikes.htm), [Visit Utah](https://www.visitutah.com/places-to-go/parks-outdoors/bryce-canyon/adventure-guide)]

- **Attend a Star Party** (2-3 hours evening) - Experience world-class stargazing at this International Dark Sky Park; ranger-led programs available seasonally; check visitor center for schedule; 435-834-5322 [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/brca/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [More Than Just Parks](https://morethanjustparks.com/things-to-do-bryce-canyon-national-park/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Hike Fairyland Loop Trail** (4-5 hours) - Explore this moderate to strenuous 7.8-mile trail through magical landscape of hoodoos including Tower Bridge rock formation and China Wall, offering solitude and stunning views; trailhead at Fairyland Point; 1,545 feet elevation gain [Source: [MaxTour Hidden Trails](https://www.maxtour.co/hidden-hiking-trails-of-bryce-canyon/), [AllTrails Hidden Gems](https://www.alltrails.com/guides/us/utah/hidden-gems-in-bryce-canyon-national-park)]

- **Visit Mossy Cave Trail** (30-45 minutes) - Discover this short, family-friendly hike at the park's northern edge leading to a delicate waterfall and hidden alcove with lush moss, providing surprising contrast to surrounding desert; located outside main park area on Highway 12 [Source: [Bryce Canyon Country Secret Spots](https://www.brycecanyoncountry.com/secret-hiking-spots/), [KrisSid Hidden Gems](https://krissiddesignsstore.com/bryce-canyons-hidden-gems-unveiling-natures-best-kept-secrets/)]

- **Explore Bristlecone Loop Trail** (1 hour) - Walk through forest of ancient bristlecone pines on this short family-friendly trail at Rainbow Point that comes alive after sunset as an International Dark Sky Park location; 1-mile loop [Source: [Bryce Canyon Country Secret Spots](https://www.brycecanyoncountry.com/secret-hiking-spots/), [AllTrails Hidden Gems](https://www.alltrails.com/guides/us/utah/hidden-gems-in-bryce-canyon-national-park)]

## Also Nearby

- **Kodachrome Basin State Park** (3-4 hours, 30 minutes southeast) - Explore red mountains, eroded buttes, and several arches including Angels Palace Trail and Shakespeare Arch; Kodachrome Basin Road, Cannonville, UT 84718; open year-round; $10 per vehicle day use [Source: [Bryce Canyon Country](https://www.brycecanyoncountry.com/), [Under Canvas](https://www.undercanvas.com/blog/locals-list-bryce-canyon-and-nearby-areas/)]

- **Red Canyon** (1-2 hours, 15 minutes west) - Visit dramatic red rock formations with fewer crowds than Bryce; Highway 12 west of Bryce; open year-round; free admission [Source: [Bryce Canyon Country](https://www.brycecanyoncountry.com/), [Under Canvas](https://www.undercanvas.com/blog/locals-list-bryce-canyon-and-nearby-areas/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time at Bryce Canyon NP (National Park):** 1-2 days is perfect allowing time for the best hike (Queens Garden/Navajo Loop), scenic drive, viewpoints, and recreational activities.
