## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-appalachian-trail-conservancy-visitor-center-experience"></a>**Appalachian Trail Conservancy Visitor Center Experience** (0.5-1 hour) - Explore the headquarters and psychological halfway point of the AT, featuring exhibits including a 10-foot 3D map of the entire East Coast with miniature replica trail shelter, peruse over 30,000 photos of hikers on the AT since 1979, visit the gift shop, and get hiking advice from friendly staff and volunteers; 799 Washington Street, Harpers Ferry, WV 25425 (39.3253925, -77.7404426); Tuesday & Wednesday: 10:00 AM-4:00 PM, Thursday-Monday: 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [Harpers Ferry Visitor Center](https://appalachiantrail.org/experience/visit-us/harpers-ferry-visitor-center/), [C&O Canal Trust](https://www.canaltrust.org/pyv/appalachian-trail-conservancy-headquarters-and-visitor-center/)]

- <a id="activity-maryland-heights-trail"></a>**Maryland Heights Trail** (3-4 hours) - Strenuous 6.5-mile loop or 4.5-mile out-and-back hike along the Appalachian Trail with spectacular overlook views of Harpers Ferry and the confluence of the Potomac and Shenandoah Rivers, plus Civil War fortification ruins along the ridge; trailhead accessible from Harpers Ferry, WV [Source: [NPS Harpers Ferry](https://www.nps.gov/hafe/planyourvisit/maryland-heights-trail.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/west-virginia/maryland-heights-via-overlook-cliffs-and-combined-trail)]

- <a id="activity-mcafee-knob-day-hike"></a>**McAfee Knob Day Hike** (4-6 hours) - The most photographed spot on the Appalachian Trail, this moderate 7.9-mile round-trip hike features a steady gradual climb with 1,830 feet elevation gain to a dramatic cliff overlook; VA 311 parking lot near Catawba, VA [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/virginia/mcafee-knob-appalachian-trail), [Visit Roanoke](https://www.visitroanokeva.com/things-to-do/outdoor-adventure/appalachian-trail/mcafee-knob/)]

- <a id="activity-dragon-s-tooth-trail"></a>**Dragon's Tooth Trail** (2.5-3.5 hours) - Exciting 4.6-mile round-trip hike with 1,200+ feet elevation gain to a 35-foot-tall rock spire along the AT in Jefferson National Forest; can be technical at times with rock scrambling; Dragon's Tooth Trailhead, Catawba, VA [Source: [Go Hike Virginia](https://gohikevirginia.com/dragons-tooth-hike/), [Visit Roanoke](https://www.visitroanokeva.com/things-to-do/outdoor-adventure/appalachian-trail/dragons-tooth/)]

- <a id="activity-max-patch-loop-hike"></a>**Max Patch Loop Hike** (0.5-1 hour) - Easy 1.5-mile family-friendly loop across a massive 300-acre grassy bald at 4,600+ feet elevation with 360-degree views, a favorite spot for thru-hikers without tourist crowds; Max Patch parking area near Hot Springs, NC [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/north-carolina/max-patch-loop), [Asheville Trails](https://ashevilletrails.com/appalachian-trail-north-carolina/max-patch/)]

- <a id="activity-blood-mountain-summit-hike"></a>**Blood Mountain Summit Hike** (3-4 hours) - Moderate 4.6-mile round-trip climb to the highest peak in Georgia on the AT at 4,458 feet, featuring panoramic views and the historic Blood Mountain Shelter; Byron Reece Trailhead near Neels Gap, GA [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/georgia/blood-mountain-via-appalachian-trail), [Atlanta Trails](https://www.atlantatrails.com/hiking-trails/wooded-serenity-the-appalachian-trail-at-blood-mountain/)]

- <a id="activity-grayson-highlands-wild-ponies-hike"></a>**Grayson Highlands Wild Ponies Hike** (1-2 hours) - Moderate 2.3-2.5 mile out-and-back hike from Massie Gap through rhododendron along the AT to Wilburn Ridge, where over 100 wild ponies roam freely at 5,000+ feet elevation with expansive mountain views; Massie Gap parking area, Grayson Highlands State Park, VA [Source: [Go Hike Virginia](https://gohikevirginia.com/wild-ponies-grayson-highlands-state-park/), [Grayson Highlands State Park](https://www.dcr.virginia.gov/state-parks/grayson-highlands)]

- <a id="activity-clingmans-dome-via-appalachian-trail"></a>**Clingmans Dome via Appalachian Trail** (0.5-1 hour for short trail, full day for long sections) - Visit the highest point on the entire Appalachian Trail at 6,643 feet; the paved 1.3-mile round-trip Observation Tower Trail takes 30-60 minutes, or hike 8.3 miles one-way from Newfound Gap for a challenging full-day trek; Great Smoky Mountains National Park, TN/NC [Source: [Clingmans Dome](https://www.clingmansdome.com/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/north-carolina/clingmans-dome-observation-tower-trail)]

- <a id="activity-franconia-ridge-loop"></a>**Franconia Ridge Loop** (7-10 hours) - One of the most spectacular hikes in the White Mountains, this strenuous 9.3-mile loop with 3,809 feet elevation gain traverses Mount Lafayette, Mount Lincoln, and Little Haystack along the AT with stunning above-treeline ridge walking and views of the Presidential Range; Lafayette Place parking area, Franconia Notch State Park, NH [Source: [The Researched Traveler](https://theresearchedtraveler.com/franconia-ridge-loop/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-hampshire/mount-lafayette-and-franconia-ridge-trail-loop)]

- <a id="activity-bear-mountain-loop"></a>**Bear Mountain Loop** (2.5-3 hours) - Popular 3.7-mile loop with 1,145 feet elevation gain featuring rock scrambling on the ascent and well-maintained stone steps on the AT descent, with views over the Hudson River at the AT's lowest elevation crossing; Bear Mountain State Park, NY [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-york/bear-mountain-loop-trail), [Hiking Project](https://www.hikingproject.com/trail/7023693/bear-mountain-loop)]

- <a id="activity-harpers-ferry-to-c-o-canal-towpath"></a>**Harpers Ferry to C&O Canal Towpath** (1.5-2 hours) - Easy 5-mile flat hike combining the AT with the historic Chesapeake and Ohio Canal towpath along the Potomac River, perfect for a relaxed walk with river views; Harpers Ferry, WV [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/west-virginia/harpers-ferry-to-chesapeake-and-ohio-canal-trail)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-fullhardt-knob"></a>**Fullhardt Knob** (4-5 hours) - Lesser-known 7.8-mile out-and-back trek near Troutville, VA offering multiple beautiful views of McAfee Knob and Tinker Mountain without the crowds; Fullhardt Knob Trailhead, VA [Source: [Visit Roanoke VA](https://www.visitroanokeva.com/blog/post/10-hidden-gems-appalachian-trail-day-hikes-virginia/)]

- <a id="gem-kelly-knob"></a>**Kelly Knob** (4-5 hours) - Approximately 6.5 miles out-and-back with a striking collection of rocks providing views of the New River Valley, less crowded alternative to popular Virginia viewpoints; Kelly Knob access, VA [Source: [Visit Roanoke VA](https://www.visitroanokeva.com/blog/post/10-hidden-gems-appalachian-trail-day-hikes-virginia/)]

- <a id="gem-jones-falls"></a>**Jones Falls** (variable) - Beautiful waterfall with a secret lunch spot and campsite at the top, accessed via a 0.1-mile blue blaze trail about ten miles north of Roan Highlands; unmarked spot discovered by following FarOut app comments; TN/NC border area [Source: [The Trek](https://thetrek.co/appalachian-trail/a-thru-hiker-reveals-their-favorite-secret-and-not-so-secret-spots-on-the-appalachian-trail/)]

- <a id="gem-brush-mountain-section"></a>**Brush Mountain Section** (variable) - Features the Audie Murphy Monument, a tribute to America's most decorated WWII soldier killed in a plane crash on the mountain in 1971, along a less-traveled section of trail; Brush Mountain, VA [Source: [Visit Roanoke VA](https://www.visitroanokeva.com/blog/post/10-hidden-gems-appalachian-trail-day-hikes-virginia/)]

- <a id="gem-carter-range"></a>**Carter Range** (full day) - For solitude in New Hampshire's White Mountains, this alternative to the crowded Franconia Ridge and Presidential Range offers equally stunning alpine scenery with far fewer hikers; White Mountains, NH [Source: [Greenbelly](https://www.greenbelly.co/pages/best-appalachian-trail-hikes)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-harpers-ferry-national-historical-park"></a>**Harpers Ferry National Historical Park** (3-5 hours) - Explore the historic town at the confluence of the Potomac and Shenandoah Rivers where John Brown's famous raid occurred, featuring museums, restored buildings, Jefferson Rock overlook, and over 20 miles of hiking trails; about 0.3 miles from AT Visitor Center; Visitor Center at 171 Shoreline Drive, Harpers Ferry, WV 25425; daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Harpers Ferry](https://www.nps.gov/hafe/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g60722-Activities-Harpers_Ferry_West_Virginia.html)]

- <a id="nearby-antietam-national-battlefield"></a>**Antietam National Battlefield** (2-3 hours) - Site of the bloodiest single-day battle in American history with self-guided driving tour, visitor center exhibits, and preserved battlefield landscapes; 16-17 miles from Harpers Ferry (20 minute drive); 5831 Dunker Church Road, Sharpsburg, MD 21782 (39.474, -77.745); Visitor Center open 9:00 AM-5:00 PM daily except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 301-432-5124 [Source: [Free Tours by Foot](https://freetoursbyfoot.com/dc-day-trip-antietam-national-battlefield-and-harpers-ferry/), [NPS Antietam](https://www.nps.gov/anti/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- <a id="nearby-monocacy-national-battlefield"></a>**Monocacy National Battlefield** (1-2 hours) - Site of the 1864 Civil War battle that saved Washington D.C. from Confederate forces, with visitor center, trails, and historic farm buildings; approximately 23 miles from Harpers Ferry; 4632 Araby Church Road, Frederick, MD 21704 (39.371, -77.392); Visitor Center open Sunday, Monday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; 301-662-3515 [Source: [NPS Harpers Ferry Nearby Attractions](https://www.nps.gov/hafe/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm), [NPS Monocacy](https://www.nps.gov/mono/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]
