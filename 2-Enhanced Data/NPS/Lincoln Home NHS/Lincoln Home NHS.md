# Lincoln Home NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-lincoln-home-visitor-center"></a>**Lincoln Home Visitor Center** - 426 S 7th St, Springfield, IL 62701 (39.797353, -89.644925); Open daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed January 1, Thanksgiving, and December 25; 217-492-4241 [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/liho/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/liho/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** Call 217-492-4241 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-lincoln-home-guided-tour"></a>**Lincoln Home Guided Tour** (30-45 minutes) - Free guided tour through the only home Abraham Lincoln ever owned, where he lived from 1844-1861 before becoming president; tours depart regularly throughout the day with groups of 15 people maximum; free timed tickets must be picked up at the visitor center, which often distribute quickly on weekends (arrive early or visit Monday-Thursday for better availability); 426 S 7th St, Springfield, IL 62701 (39.797353, -89.644925); Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Lincoln Home Tour](https://www.nps.gov/liho/planyourvisit/lincoln-home-tour.htm), [TripAdvisor Reviews 2025](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60887-d143197-Reviews-Lincoln_Home_National_Historic_Site-Springfield_Illinois.html), [NPPlan Tour Information](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/illinois-national-parks/lincoln-home-national-historic-site-park-at-a-glance/lincoln-home-national-historic-site-house-tour/)]

- <a id="activity-historic-four-block-neighborhood-walk"></a>**Historic Four-Block Neighborhood Walk** (30-60 minutes) - Self-guided exploration of the four-block historic district with 12 structures restored to their 1860 appearance, providing context for the community Lincoln lived in; free to explore at your own pace; 426 S 7th St area, Springfield, IL 62701; Daily during daylight hours [Source: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60887-d143197-Reviews-Lincoln_Home_National_Historic_Site-Springfield_Illinois.html), [Visit Springfield](https://www.visitspringfieldillinois.com/LocationDetails/?id=Lincoln-Home-National-Historic-Site)]

- <a id="activity-visitor-center-film-and-exhibits"></a>**Visitor Center Film and Exhibits** (30 minutes) - Watch the park film about Lincoln's Springfield years and explore exhibits including a scaled model of 1860 Springfield; free admission; 426 S 7th St, Springfield, IL 62701; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [TripAdvisor Visitor Center Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60887-d12852815-Reviews-The_Lincoln_Home_Visitor_Center-Springfield_Illinois.html), [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/usa/great-lakes/springfield/attractions/lincoln-home-national-historic-site/a/poi-sig/1247398/361931)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-dean-and-arnold-houses"></a>**Dean and Arnold Houses** (20-30 minutes) - Two neighboring homes open to the public with exhibits about Lincoln's neighbors and others who played a role in his life; often overlooked by visitors who only tour the main Lincoln Home; Dean House explores Lincoln family life during Springfield years, Arnold House focuses on historic preservation; provides valuable historical context about the community; free admission; located within the four-block historic district, 426 S 7th St area; Daily 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM [Source: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60887-d143197-Reviews-Lincoln_Home_National_Historic_Site-Springfield_Illinois.html), [Enjoy Illinois](https://www.enjoyillinois.com/explore/listing/lincoln-home-national-historic-site/), [NPPlan Dean House](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/illinois-national-parks/lincoln-home-national-historic-site-park-at-a-glance/lincoln-home-national-historic-site-neighborhood-tour/lincoln-home-national-historic-site-dean-house/)]

- <a id="gem-specialized-walking-tours"></a>**Specialized Walking Tours** (1-1.5 hours) - Illinois Department of Natural Resources offers unique guided walking tours including the Underground Railroad Walk (explores sites significant to the Underground Railroad), Abolitionists to Suffragists: Amazing Illinois Women tour (begins at Lincoln Home Visitor Center, focuses on women's empowerment), and Architecture Walking Tour (explores notable downtown buildings); free admission, donations appreciated; tours run June through November; check schedule at visitor center or dnrhistoric.illinois.gov; 426 S 7th St, Springfield, IL 62701 [Source: [Illinois DNR Walk Hike Bike History](https://dnrhistoric.illinois.gov/experience/promotions/walk-hike-bike-and-history.html)]

- <a id="gem-living-history-presentations"></a>**Living History Presentations** (varies) - Presentations by Mary Todd Lincoln and Abraham Lincoln impersonators at the Visitor Center; schedule varies, check at visitor center upon arrival; free with site admission; 426 S 7th St, Springfield, IL 62701 [Source: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60887-d143197-Reviews-Lincoln_Home_National_Historic_Site-Springfield_Illinois.html)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-abraham-lincoln-presidential-library-and-museum"></a>**Abraham Lincoln Presidential Library and Museum** (2-4 hours, 0.5 miles) - Comprehensive museum with over 40,000 square feet of galleries featuring holographic shows, the "Eyes of Lincoln" theatrical presentation, interactive displays, and more than 1,500 original signed Lincoln documents; widely considered one of the best presidential museums in the country; 212 N 6th St, Springfield, IL 62701 (39.8033, -89.6472); Open daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; 217-558-8844; Admission charged [Source: [Presidential Library Hours](https://presidentlincoln.illinois.gov/visit/plan-your-visit/hours-of-operation/), [TripAdvisor Museum Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60887-d270646-Reviews-Abraham_Lincoln_Presidential_Library_and_Museum-Springfield_Illinois.html), [Visit Springfield Museum](https://www.visitspringfieldillinois.com/LocationDetails/?id=Abraham-Lincoln-Presidential-Museum)]

- <a id="nearby-lincoln-tomb-at-oak-ridge-cemetery"></a>**Lincoln Tomb at Oak Ridge Cemetery** (30 minutes, 2 miles) - Final resting place of Abraham Lincoln, his wife Mary, and three of their sons; 117-foot granite monument with observation areas; no admission charge; free parking adjacent to tomb; wheelchair accessible; 1500 Monument Ave, Springfield, IL 62702 (39.8235, -89.6557); Open daily 9:00 AM - 4:45 PM (interior), 7:00 AM - 5:00 PM (exterior); Closed New Year's Day, MLK Jr. Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas; 217-782-2717 [Source: [Lincoln Tomb Illinois Historic Sites](https://dnrhistoric.illinois.gov/experience/sites/central/lincoln-tomb.html), [Illinois History Lincoln Tomb](https://illinoishistory.org/iltomb/), [Visit Springfield Tomb](https://visitspringfieldillinois.com/LocationDetails/?id=Lincoln-Tomb)]

- <a id="nearby-old-state-capitol-state-historic-site"></a>**Old State Capitol State Historic Site** (30-60 minutes, 0.3 miles) - Greek Revival building where Lincoln served as a lawyer and legislator and delivered his famous "House Divided" speech in 1858; guided tours (30 minutes) or self-guided exploration available; free admission, donations appreciated; 1 Old State Capitol Plaza, Springfield, IL 62701 (39.8007, -89.6425); Open Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; Reservations accepted but not required; 217-785-9363 [Source: [Visit Springfield Old Capitol](https://www.visitspringfieldillinois.com/LocationDetails/?id=Old-State-Capitol), [Illinois Historic Sites Old Capitol](https://dnrhistoric.illinois.gov/experience/sites/site.old-state-capitol.html), [Enjoy Illinois Old Capitol](https://www.enjoyillinois.com/explore/listing/old-state-capitol-state-historic-site/)]

- <a id="nearby-lincoln-depot"></a>**Lincoln Depot** (15-20 minutes, 0.2 miles) - Historic 1852 train depot where Abraham Lincoln delivered his farewell address on February 11, 1861, before departing for Washington D.C. to become president; first floor houses self-guided museum with exhibits about the farewell address and Lincoln's journey to the presidency; 930 E Monroe St, Springfield, IL 62701 (39.7977, -89.6365); Open weekdays 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM, weekends/holidays by appointment; Free admission; 217-544-8441 [Source: [Lincoln Depot Contact](https://www.lincolndepot.org/contact), [Enjoy Illinois Lincoln Depot](https://www.enjoyillinois.com/explore/listing/lincoln-depot/), [Wikipedia Lincoln Depot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Depot)]

- <a id="nearby-lincoln-herndon-law-offices-state-historic-site"></a>**Lincoln-Herndon Law Offices State Historic Site** (20-30 minutes when open, 0.3 miles) - The only surviving structure where Lincoln maintained a law office; Lincoln and William Herndon practiced law here from 1843-1852; **Note: Currently closed for restoration, call ahead to confirm reopening status**; 1 S Old State Capitol Plaza (6th & Adams Streets), Springfield, IL 62701 (39.8007, -89.6425); 217-785-7289; Free admission when open [Source: [Illinois Historic Sites Lincoln-Herndon](https://dnrhistoric.illinois.gov/experience/sites/central/lincoln-herndon.html), [TripAdvisor Lincoln-Herndon Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60887-d268966-Reviews-Lincoln_Herndon_Law_Offices_State_Historic_Site-Springfield_Illinois.html), [Abraham Lincoln Online](https://www.abrahamlincolnonline.org/lincoln/sites/law.htm)]

- <a id="nearby-dana-thomas-house-state-historic-site"></a>**Dana-Thomas House State Historic Site** (1-1.5 hours, 1 mile) - One of the best-preserved Frank Lloyd Wright homes featuring "organic architecture" with over 100 pieces of custom furniture and 250 works of original art glass; designed for progressive heiress Susan Lawrence Dana in 1902-1904; guided tours only (no self-guided tours); 301 E Lawrence Ave, Springfield, IL 62704 (39.7885, -89.6398); Tours daily at 9:30 AM, 11:00 AM, 1:30 PM, and 3:00 PM; Free admission, donations appreciated; Reservations encouraged; 217-782-6776; Closed major holidays [Source: [Dana-Thomas Hours](https://dana-thomas.org/tours/), [Illinois Historic Sites Dana-Thomas](https://dnrhistoric.illinois.gov/experience/sites/central/dana-thomas-house.html), [TripAdvisor Dana-Thomas Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60887-d285325-Reviews-Dana_Thomas_House-Springfield_Illinois.html)]

- <a id="nearby-cozy-dog-drive-in"></a>**Cozy Dog Drive In** (30-45 minutes, 2.5 miles) - Historic Route 66 restaurant and home of the original corn dog on a stick since 1949; features Route 66 memorabilia and gift shop; full menu including breakfast, burgers, and classic diner fare; 2935 S 6th St, Springfield, IL 62703 (39.7624, -89.6487); Open Monday-Saturday 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM; Closed Sundays and major holidays; 217-525-1992 [Source: [Cozy Dog Official Site](https://www.cozydogdrivein.com/), [Visit Springfield Cozy Dog](https://visitspringfieldillinois.com/LocationDetails/?id=Cozy-Dog-Drive-In), [Enjoy Illinois Cozy Dog](https://www.enjoyillinois.com/explore/listing/cozy-dog-drive-in/)]
