# Shiloh NMP (National Military Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Shiloh Battlefield Visitor Center** (1055 Pittsburg Landing Road, Shiloh, TN 38376 (35.1497, -88.3394); Daily 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 731-689-5696) - National Park Service passport cancellation stamps available at visitor center. [Source: [Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/shil/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60965-d109465-Reviews-Shiloh_National_Military_Park-Shiloh_Tennessee.html), [Tennessee Vacation](https://www.tnvacation.com/local/shiloh-shiloh-national-military-park)]

**Note:** Call 731-689-5696 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. Park located 9 miles south of Savannah, Tennessee, preserving April 6-7, 1862 battle site where nearly 24,000 soldiers were killed, wounded, or missing.

## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center Film and Exhibits** (45-60 minutes) - Watch 45-minute film about the Battle of Shiloh providing essential context for battlefield tour, explore permanent exhibitions and displays about the two-day battle that was the bloodiest in American history up to that time; 1055 Pittsburg Landing Road, Shiloh, TN 38376; Daily 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM; FREE [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/shiloh-national-military-park/), [Shiloh Tours](https://shilohtours.com/guided-tours/), [Battlefield Trust One Day Tour](https://www.battlefields.org/visit/itineraries/tour-shiloh-battlefield-one-day)]

- **Battlefield Driving Tour** (2-3 hours) - Drive 12-13 mile self-guided tour visiting 20-22 numbered stops including Shiloh Log Church, Hornet's Nest, Sunken Road, Peach Orchard, Bloody Pond, General Johnston's death site, Pittsburg Landing, and Grant's Last Line; information panels at each stop detail battle events; download NPS app for audio tour; open to vehicles, bicycles, and foot traffic; tour road accessible during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [NPPlan Battlefield Tour](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/tennessee/shiloh-national-military-park-park-at-a-glance/shiloh-national-military-park-shiloh-battlefield-tour-park-version/), [Battlefield Trust Visit](https://www.battlefields.org/visit/battlefields/shiloh-battlefield), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60965-d109465-Reviews-Shiloh_National_Military_Park-Shiloh_Tennessee.html)]

- **Shiloh National Cemetery Visit** (30-45 minutes) - Pay respects at cemetery containing approximately 3,600 Union soldiers buried (2,359 unknowns), established 1866, one of first national cemeteries; features beautiful landscaping and monuments; located in northeast corner of park adjacent to visitor center; cemetery accessible daily; FREE [Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiloh_National_Military_Park), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/shiloh-national-military-park/)]

- **Shiloh Military Trail (SMT1)** (4-5 hours) - Hike best-rated trail (4.4 stars from 128 reviews) covering 10.1 miles with 426 feet elevation gain through battlefield following troop movements; provides alternate perspective on battle terrain; accessible from battlefield tour road; open during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [AllTrails Shiloh](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/tennessee/shiloh-national-military-park)]

- **Riverside and Indian Mounds Trail** (30-45 minutes) - Walk 1.1-mile longest trail in park featuring awesome views of Tennessee River and abundance of informative plaques; connects to Shiloh Indian Mounds; accessible from park; open during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [AllTrails Shiloh](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/tennessee/shiloh-national-military-park)]

- **Ranger-Guided Battlefield Tours** (varies) - Join National Park Service rangers for in-depth guided tours providing expert interpretation of battle tactics, soldier experiences, and historical significance; offered seasonally; check visitor center for schedule; 1055 Pittsburg Landing Road; times vary; FREE [Source: [Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/shil/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Shiloh Indian Mounds National Historic Landmark** (30-45 minutes) - Explore remnants of Mississippian Era Indian village predating Civil War battle by centuries; features preserved mounds offering insight into pre-Columbian Native American culture; accessed via Riverside and Indian Mounds Trail; open during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/poi/us/tennessee/shiloh/shiloh-national-military-park)]

- **Sunken Road Walk** (20-30 minutes) - Walk along historic Sunken Road lined with heritage trees where Union troops made desperate stand at Hornet's Nest against repeated Confederate assaults, buying time for Grant to establish final defensive line; accessed from battlefield tour Stop 10; open during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/poi/us/tennessee/shiloh/shiloh-national-military-park)]

- **Hornet's Nest** (30-45 minutes) - Visit site of most intense fighting where Union General Prentiss's division held position for nearly six hours against 12 Confederate assaults, earning nickname "Hornet's Nest" from Confederate general; Tour Stop 10 includes walking trail and monuments; open during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [NPPlan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/tennessee/shiloh-national-military-park-park-at-a-glance/shiloh-national-military-park-shiloh-battlefield-tour-park-version/)]

- **Bloody Pond** (15-20 minutes) - Reflect at small pond (Tour Stop 19) where wounded soldiers from both sides crawled to drink and bathe wounds, turning water red; one of battlefield's most poignant sites; includes interpretive signage; open during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [Shiloh Tours](https://shilohtours.com/guided-tours/), [NPPlan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/tennessee/shiloh-national-military-park-park-at-a-glance/shiloh-national-military-park-shiloh-battlefield-tour-park-version/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Corinth Civil War Interpretive Center** (1-1.5 hours, 30 minutes/23 miles south in Mississippi) - Explore 15,000 square-foot facility featuring interactive exhibits, multimedia presentation on Battle of Shiloh, video on Battle of Corinth, and historic Corinth hiking/biking trail connecting Civil War Corinth National Historic Landmark sites; 501 W Linden Street, Corinth, MS 38834 (34.9373846, -88.5299611); Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; FREE [Source: [Battlefield Trust Corinth](https://www.battlefields.org/visit/heritage-sites/corinth-civil-war-interpretive-center), [NPPlan Corinth Center](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/tennessee/shiloh-national-military-park-park-at-a-glance/shiloh-national-military-park-visitor-centers/shiloh-national-military-park-corinth-civil-war-interpretive-center/)]

- **Corinth Contraband Camp Site** (30-45 minutes, in Corinth, MS) - Visit significant Civil War site documenting journey to freedom for thousands of formerly enslaved people who sought refuge behind Union lines; part of Corinth Civil War sites; Corinth, MS; accessible during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/poi/us/tennessee/shiloh/shiloh-national-military-park)]

- **Savannah, Tennessee** (30 minutes - 1 hour, 9 miles north) - Small town offering dining, lodging, and services; convenient base for exploring Shiloh battlefield; Savannah, TN 38372; hours vary by establishment [Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiloh_National_Military_Park)]

Plan extra time for Corinth Civil War Interpretive Center (adds 2-3 hours with travel).
