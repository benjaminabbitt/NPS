# Friendship Hill NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-visitor-center"></a>**Visitor Center** (inside State Dining Room of Gallatin House)
  **Address:** 223 New Geneva Rd., Point Marion, PA 15474
  **Geocoordinates:** (39.7807755, -79.9200728)
  **Hours:** Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM May-September, weekends only October-April; closed Veterans Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day
  **Phone:** 724-329-2501

**Note:** Call ahead to confirm stamp availability, especially during October-April when the Visitor Center is only open weekends. [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/frhi/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Friendship Hill](https://www.nps.gov/frhi/)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-gallatin-house-self-guided-tour"></a>**Gallatin House Self-Guided Tour** (1-3 hours) - Explore Albert Gallatin's historic home with exhibits, cell phone audio tour (60-90 seconds per stop), and interpretive film; the house features multiple stories with elevator access, quarter-mile walk from parking on steep hill; 223 New Geneva Rd., Point Marion, PA 15474; Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM May-September, weekends only October-April [Source: [NPPlan House Tour](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/pennsylvania-national-parks/friendship-hill-national-historic-site-park-at-a-glance/friendship-hill-national-historic-site-house-tour/), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/frhi/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- <a id="activity-hiking-trails"></a>**Hiking Trails** (1.5-3 hours) - Explore over 10 miles of trails through old-growth forest, along the Monongahela River bluffs, and past ponds and meadows; main Friendship Hill Loop Trail is 3.8 miles and takes approximately 1 hour 28 minutes, rated easy; sunrise to sunset daily [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/pennsylvania/friendship-hill-tour-and-hike), [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hiking-friendship-hill.htm), [NPPlan Hiking](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/pennsylvania-national-parks/friendship-hill-national-historic-site-park-at-a-glance/friendship-hill-national-historic-site-hiking-trails/)]

- <a id="activity-wildlife-viewing-and-birding"></a>**Wildlife Viewing and Birding** (1-2 hours) - Observe fox, squirrels, wild turkey, white-tailed deer, pileated woodpeckers, snapping turtles, red-tailed hawks, ovenbirds, and other streamside birds; eBird birding hotspot designation; 675-acre estate with diverse habitats; grounds open sunrise to sunset daily [Source: [NPS National Park Getaway](https://www.nps.gov/articles/getaway-frhi.htm), [NPS Outdoor Activities](https://www.nps.gov/frhi/planyourvisit/outdooractivities.htm), [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/gateways/2713)]

- <a id="activity-picnicking"></a>**Picnicking** (1-2 hours) - Picnic shelter/pavilion and tables available with accessible restrooms and trash receptacles near parking area; no food sold on site; grounds open sunrise to sunset daily [Source: [NPS National Park Getaway](https://www.nps.gov/articles/getaway-frhi.htm), [PA Bucket List](https://pabucketlist.com/exploring-friendship-hill-national-historic-site-in-fayette-county-pa/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-monongahela-river-overlook-trail"></a>**Monongahela River Overlook Trail** (1-2 hours) - Hike along the river bluffs with scenic photography spots overlooking the Monongahela River; trails offer breathtaking vistas with views of ducks and geese along the shore; best during spring wildflowers or fall foliage; designated scenic view/photo spots; sunrise to sunset daily [Source: [PA Bucket List](https://pabucketlist.com/exploring-friendship-hill-national-historic-site-in-fayette-county-pa/), [Florida Man on the Run](https://floridamanontherun.com/2024/friendship-hill-national-historic-site/), [Hiking Ohio](https://hiking-ohio.com/friendship-hill-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="gem-seasonal-nature-photography"></a>**Seasonal Nature Photography** (1-2 hours) - Capture wildflowers and mountain laurel in spring, lush greenery in summer, blazing autumn colors, and serene winter landscapes across 10 miles of trails; mix of evergreens and hardwoods provide year-round interest; grounds open sunrise to sunset daily [Source: [NPS National Park Getaway](https://www.nps.gov/articles/getaway-frhi.htm), [PA Bucket List](https://pabucketlist.com/exploring-friendship-hill-national-historic-site-in-fayette-county-pa/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-morgantown-west-virginia"></a>**Morgantown, West Virginia** (15 minutes south, 10 miles via US-119) - College town with Coopers Rock State Forest (50+ miles of trails, sandstone cliffs for climbing, Cheat River Gorge overlook), West Virginia Botanic Gardens, Art Museum of WVU, Spark! Imagination and Science Center, Cheat Lake (boating, kayaking, 4.5-mile trail), and downtown dining/breweries; plan 2-4 hours for Coopers Rock or 3-6 hours for full Morgantown experience [Source: [Distance Cities](https://www.distance-cities.com/distance-point-marion-pa-to-morgantown-wv), [Visit Mountaineer Country Things to Do](https://www.visitmountaineercountry.com/things-to-do-in-morgantown/), [Visit Mountaineer Country Coopers Rock](https://www.visitmountaineercountry.com/coopers-rock-visitors-guide/), [WV State Parks](https://wvstateparks.com/parks/coopers-rock-state-forest/)]

- <a id="nearby-fort-necessity-national-battlefield"></a>**Fort Necessity National Battlefield** (30 minutes east, approximately 25 miles) - Site of George Washington's first military defeat in 1754 that began the French and Indian War; features visitor center with 20-minute film "Road of Necessity" and interactive exhibits, reconstructed Fort Necessity, Mount Washington Tavern, ranger programs, and hiking trails; 1 Hundred Fort Necessity Rd., Farmington, PA 15437; grounds open sunrise to sunset daily, visitor center hours vary by season (closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day); plan 2-3 hours [Source: [Saving Time in a Bottle](https://savingtimeinabottle.com/2021/08/04/friendship-hill-fort-necessity-and-ohiopyle/), [NPS Fort Necessity](https://www.nps.gov/fone/index.htm), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/fone/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Distance Cities](https://distances.io/from/farmington-pa)]

- <a id="nearby-ohiopyle-state-park"></a>**Ohiopyle State Park** (45-50 minutes northeast) - Gateway to Laurel Highlands with 20,500 acres featuring the busiest whitewater rafting east of the Mississippi (Class I-IV rapids on Youghiogheny River), 20-foot Ohiopyle Falls, 30-foot Cucumber Falls, Great Allegheny Passage rail-trail (part of 318-mile Pittsburgh-DC trail system), 79+ miles of hiking trails, natural waterslide at Meadow Run, mountain biking, rock climbing, and fishing; open year-round, best spring and fall for foliage; plan 3-6 hours minimum [Source: [Saving Time in a Bottle](https://savingtimeinabottle.com/2021/08/04/friendship-hill-fort-necessity-and-ohiopyle/), [PA DCNR](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dcnr/recreation/where-to-go/state-parks/find-a-park/ohiopyle-state-park), [Ohiopyle.org](https://ohiopyle.org/), [Uncovering PA](https://uncoveringpa.com/things-to-do-in-ohiopyle-state-park)]
