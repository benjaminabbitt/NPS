# Petroglyph NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Petroglyph National Monument Visitor Center** (6510 Western Trail NW, Albuquerque, NM 87120; Daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM, closed New Year's Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas; 505-899-0205) [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/petr/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Source](https://www.visitalbuquerque.org/listing/petroglyph-national-monument-information-center/900/)]

**Note:** There are no petroglyph viewing trails at the visitor center itself - drive to the separate canyon locations to view petroglyphs. Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Boca Negra Canyon Trails** (1 hour total) - Three short, unpaved trails (Mesa Point, Macaw, and Cliff Base) ranging from 5 to 30 minutes each, passing approximately 100 petroglyphs; monument's only fully developed area with restrooms, shade, and water fountain; Boca Negra Canyon Day Use Area, 6900 Unser Blvd NW, Albuquerque, NM 87120; Daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM (last entry 4:00 PM strictly enforced); $1-$2 per vehicle depending on day [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/petr/planyourvisit/bn.htm), [Source](https://bottger.com/blog/petroglyph-national-monument-trails/), [Source](https://www.cabq.gov/parksandrecreation/open-space/lands/boca-negra-canyon)]

- **Rinconada Canyon Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - Easy 2.2-mile loop trail crossing sand dunes to a volcanic escarpment with approximately 300 petroglyphs scattered along the way; Rinconada Canyon Trailhead, Unser Blvd NW (north of Boca Negra), Albuquerque, NM; Sunrise to sunset; Free admission; No facilities [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/petr/planyourvisit/rinconada.htm), [Source](https://www.discovernewmexico.com/rinconada-canyon-and-petroglyph-national-monument), [Source](https://bottger.com/blog/petroglyph-national-monument-trails/)]

- **Piedras Marcadas Canyon Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - Easy 1.5-1.9 mile round trip undeveloped trail along the basalt escarpment to the densest concentration of petroglyphs in the monument - approximately 300-500 images from an estimated 5,000 documented in this canyon; Piedras Marcadas Trailhead, Golf Course Rd NW & Taylor Ranch Dr NW, Albuquerque, NM; Sunrise to sunset; Free admission; No facilities, bring water and sun protection [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/petr/planyourvisit/pmc.htm), [Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/piedras-marcadas-canyon), [Source](https://wnpa.org/plan-your-trip/trip-ideas/park-stories/how-to-plan-your-perfect-trip-to-petroglyph-national-monument-1)]

- **Volcanoes Day Use Area - Three Volcanoes Loop** (1-1.5 hours) - Moderate 3-mile trail with 318 feet elevation gain circling three dormant volcanoes (JA, Vulcan, and Black); JA Volcano section is paved and accessible; V olcanoes Day Use Area, Paseo del Norte & Atrisco Vista Blvd NW, Albuquerque, NM; Sunrise to sunset; Free admission [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/petr/planyourvisit/vol.htm), [Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-mexico/the-volcanoes-trail), [Source](https://hobbytrap.com/volcanoes-day-use-area-in-the-petroglyph-national-monument/)]

- **JA Volcano and Albuquerque Overlook** (30-45 minutes) - Easy 1-mile round trip paved trail to scenic overlook with views of the Rio Grande Valley, Albuquerque, and the Sandia Mountains; Volcanoes Day Use Area, Paseo del Norte & Atrisco Vista Blvd NW, Albuquerque, NM; Sunrise to sunset; Free admission [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/petr/planyourvisit/vol.htm), [Source](https://wnpa.org/plan-your-trip/trip-ideas/park-stories/how-to-plan-your-perfect-trip-to-petroglyph-national-monument-1)]

- **Visitor Center Exhibits** (15-30 minutes) - Explore exhibits and orientation displays about the monument's 20,000+ petroglyphs carved 400-700 years ago by Native Americans and Spanish settlers; 6510 Western Trail NW, Albuquerque, NM 87120; Daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM; 505-899-0205 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/petr/index.htm), [Source](https://www.archaeologysouthwest.org/explore/petroglyph-national-monument/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Piedras Marcadas Canyon (Less-Visited Trail)** (1-1.5 hours) - The least-visited section of the monument's three main petroglyph areas, offering a peaceful walk among ancient art with over 400 petroglyphs in a more immersive, less-crowded experience than Boca Negra; Piedras Marcadas Trailhead, Golf Course Rd NW & Taylor Ranch Dr NW, Albuquerque, NM; Sunrise to sunset [[Source](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/new-mexico/albuquerques-secret-petroglyph-paths/), [Source](https://thetouristchecklist.com/petroglyph-national-monument/)]

- **Petroglyph Escarpment Exploration** (2-3 hours) - The volcanic escarpment stretches for over 10 miles within the monument, offering off-trail hiking opportunities (not encouraged but permitted) to discover new petroglyphs away from popular areas with rugged terrain and stunning views; Various access points along the monument; Sunrise to sunset; Bring plenty of water, sun protection, and sturdy footwear [[Source](https://discoveringhiddengems.com/petroglyph-national-monument-albuquerque-new-mexico-things-to-do/), [Source](https://www.americansouthwest.net/new_mexico/petroglyph/national-monument.html)]

- **Hidden Petroglyph Hunting** (1-2 hours) - Beyond the obvious rock art along the main trails, look for less-visible carvings tucked into rock crevices and around corners; requires careful observation and patience to spot petroglyphs that most visitors miss; Throughout canyon trails; Best during canyon hours [[Source](https://ourdownsizedlife.com/hidden-pictures-petroglyphs-national-monument/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Sandia Peak Tramway** (2-3 hours, 15 miles east) - World's longest aerial tramway carrying passengers 2.7 miles along suspended cables to the 10,378-foot summit of Sandia Peak with panoramic views, hiking trails, and seasonal restaurant; 30 Tramway Rd NE, Albuquerque, NM 87122; Summer: Daily 9:00 AM-9:00 PM, Winter: Daily 9:00 AM-8:00 PM; $32 adults; 505-856-7325 [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60933-d102667-Reviews-Petroglyph_National_Monument-Albuquerque_New_Mexico.html), [Source](https://www.visitalbuquerque.org/listing/sandia-peak-tramway/1302/)]

- **Old Town Albuquerque** (2-3 hours, 10 miles southeast) - Historic plaza dating to 1706 with adobe buildings, shops, galleries, museums, and restaurants surrounding the San Felipe de Neri Church; Old Town Plaza, 2107 Central Ave NW, Albuquerque, NM 87104; Shops and restaurants vary, generally 10:00 AM-5:00 PM; Free to explore; 505-243-3215 [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60933-d102667-Reviews-Petroglyph_National_Monument-Albuquerque_New_Mexico.html), [Source](https://www.visitalbuquerque.org/albuquerque/old-town/)]

- **Albuquerque BioPark (Aquarium & Botanic Garden)** (2-3 hours, 12 miles southeast) - Combined aquarium with 285,000-gallon shark tank and botanical garden featuring desert, Mediterranean, and Japanese gardens; 2601 Central Ave NW, Albuquerque, NM 87104; Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM (closes at 6:00 PM during peak summer season); $15.50 adults for combo ticket; 505-764-6200 [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60933-d102667-Reviews-Petroglyph_National_Monument-Albuquerque_New_Mexico.html), [Source](https://www.cabq.gov/artsculture/biopark)]

- **Indian Pueblo Cultural Center** (1.5-2 hours, 8 miles southeast) - Museum and cultural center owned and operated by New Mexico's 19 Pueblos, featuring exhibits on Pueblo culture, history, art gallery, and traditional bread-baking demonstrations; 2401 12th St NW, Albuquerque, NM 87104; Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; $8.40 adults; 505-843-7270 [[Source](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60933-d102667-Reviews-Petroglyph_National_Monument-Albuquerque_New_Mexico.html), [Source](https://indianpueblo.org)]

**Total Recommended Time:** 3-4 hours for a comprehensive visit including the visitor center and one or two canyon trails. Plan 1-2 hours minimum for a single trail experience. Half-day (4-5 hours) recommended to visit multiple canyon areas and the Volcanoes Day Use Area.
