# Palo Alto Battlefield NHP (National Historical Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Palo Alto Battlefield Visitor Center** (7200 Paredes Line Road, Brownsville, TX 78526 (26.0049965, -97.4817606); Visitor Center: Tuesday-Sunday 9:00 AM-4:00 PM, Park Grounds: Daily 8:00 AM-5:00 PM; (956) 541-2785 ext. 333) [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/paal/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Contact](https://www.nps.gov/paal/contacts.htm)]

**Note:** Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Palo Alto Battlefield Trail** (0.5-1 hour) - Walk the half-mile paved trail (1.8 miles total with loop) with interpretive waysides along the Mexican and American battle lines from the first battle of the Mexican-American War; elevation gain of only 6 feet, suitable for all ages, pets on leash allowed; 7200 Paredes Line Road, Brownsville, TX 78526; trails open daily 8:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Hiking Trail](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hiking-the-battlefield-trail.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/texas/palo-alto-battlefield-trail)]

- **Visitor Center Experience** (30-45 minutes) - Explore interactive exhibits featuring artifacts, Mexican War era military uniforms, and watch the 15-minute film "War on the Rio Grande" available in English and Spanish; 7200 Paredes Line Road, Brownsville, TX 78526; Tuesday-Sunday 9:00 AM-4:00 PM [Source: [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/palo-alto-visitor-center.htm), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/palo-alto-battlefield-national-historical-park/)]

- **Resaca de la Palma Battlefield** (45 minutes-1 hour) - Visit the site of the second battle of the Mexican-American War, featuring a half-mile circular paved trail with interpretive waysides; 1024 Paredes Line Road, Brownsville, TX; Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM-3:00 PM [Source: [NPS Resaca de la Palma](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/visit-resaca-de-la-palma-battlefield.htm), [NPPlan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/texas-national-parks/palo-alto-battlefield-national-historical-park-park-at-a-glance/palo-alto-battlefield-national-historical-park-resaca-de-la-palma-battlefield/)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (30 minutes-1 hour) - Complete activities within the visitor center and on park trails to earn a Junior Ranger badge; time varies depending on age of participant; 7200 Paredes Line Road, Brownsville, TX 78526; during visitor center hours [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/palo-alto-battlefield-national-historical-park/)]

- **Living History Demonstrations** (1-2 hours, seasonal) - Chat with costumed historians, listen to stories about the battles, and watch live weapons demonstrations (weather permitting); held year-round but schedule varies; check NPS calendar for dates; 7200 Paredes Line Road, Brownsville, TX 78526; times vary by event [Source: [NPS Living History](https://www.nps.gov/paal/planyourvisit/living-history.htm), [NPPlan Ranger Programs](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/texas-national-parks/palo-alto-battlefield-national-historical-park-park-at-a-glance/palo-alto-battlefield-national-historical-park-ranger-programs-and-special-events/)]

- **Ranger-Led Talks and Birding Walks** (1-2 hours, seasonal) - Join park rangers for lectures about the battle or guided birding walks on the battlefield; available December 1-April 1 depending on staffing and weather; contact (956) 541-2785 ext. 333 for schedule; 7200 Paredes Line Road, Brownsville, TX 78526 [Source: [NPPlan Ranger Programs](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/texas-national-parks/palo-alto-battlefield-national-historical-park-park-at-a-glance/palo-alto-battlefield-national-historical-park-ranger-programs-and-special-events/), [NPS Birding](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/birding-at-palo-alto-battlefield.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Butterfly Watching** (1-2 hours) - Spot rare butterfly species including the manfreda giant-skipper, one of the rarest butterflies in Texas and endemic to the state; best viewing March-October when butterfly diversity peaks; trails open daily 8:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [WNPA Natural Side](https://wnpa.org/see-the-natural-side-of-the-unique-palo-alto-battlefied/)]

- **Bird Watching for Rare Species** (1-3 hours) - Observe rare birds of prey like Harris Hawk and Aplomado Falcon, plus colorful species like Roseate Spoonbill; during fall and spring migrations, spot Painted Bunting and Bullock's Oriole; park is a recognized birding hotspot along a major migration route; trails open daily 8:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [WNPA Natural Side](https://wnpa.org/see-the-natural-side-of-the-unique-palo-alto-battlefied/), [NPS Birding](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/birding-at-palo-alto-battlefield.htm)]

- **Texas Tortoise Monitoring** (if available, 30 minutes-1 hour) - Learn about the park's active Texas tortoise monitoring program; contact visitor center for availability; (956) 541-2785 ext. 333 [Source: [WNPA Natural Side](https://wnpa.org/see-the-natural-side-of-the-unique-palo-alto-battlefied/)]

- **Brownsville Historic Battlefield Trail Connection** (varies) - Access the 9-mile paved trail connecting Palo Alto Battlefield to Southern Pacific Linear Park downtown; the park serves as the northern trailhead; parking at visitor center; trail open during daylight hours [Source: [NPS Battlefield Trail](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/explore-the-brownsville-historic-battlefield-trail.htm), [TrailLink](https://www.traillink.com/trail/historic-battlefield-trail/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Sabal Palm Sanctuary** (1-3 hours, 20 miles) - Walk 5 miles of signed nature trails through one of the last stands of old-growth Sabal Palm forest in a 557-acre nature reserve on the Rio Grande; excellent birdwatching and butterfly viewing; 8435 Sabal Palm Road, Brownsville, TX 78521; daily 7:00 AM-5:00 PM except Wednesday [Source: [Sabal Palm Sanctuary](https://www.sabalpalmsanctuary.org/), [Audubon Texas](https://tx.audubon.org/sabal-palm-audubon-sanctuary)]

- **Laguna Atascosa National Wildlife Refuge** (2-4 hours, 35 miles) - Explore 97,000 acres of subtropical, temperate, coastal and arid landscapes featuring 417 bird species and endangered ocelots; wildlife watching trails, kayak tours, and tram tours available; best wildlife viewing in early morning (daylight-9:30 AM) or late afternoon (4:30 PM-sunset); Buena Vista Blvd., Los Fresnos, TX 78566; Visitor Center Wednesday-Saturday 7:30 AM-3:00 PM, trails open sunrise-sunset daily [Source: [USFWS Laguna Atascosa](https://www.fws.gov/refuge/laguna-atascosa/visit-us), [Friends of South Texas Refuges](https://www.friendsofsouthtexasrefuges.org/default.asp?id=290)]

Yturria Education Center; 641 E Madison St, Brownsville, TX 78520; Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM-4:00 PM; Adults $6, Seniors $4, Students $2, Children under 6 free; (956) 548-1313 [Source: [Brownsville Historical Association](https://www.brownsvillehistory.org/historic-brownsville-museum.html)]

- **SpaceX Starbase at Boca Chica** (1-2 hours viewing, 25 miles) - View SpaceX facilities and rocket prototypes from public areas along Boca Chica Beach; note that road and beach access may be closed during testing or launch activities; check Cameron County website for closures before visiting; Highway 4, Boca Chica; public road access 24 hours but subject to closures [Source: [Everyday Astronaut](https://everydayastronaut.com/how-to-visit-starbase/), [Visit Brownsville SpaceX](https://visitbtx.com/members/spacex-launch-facility-starbase/)]
