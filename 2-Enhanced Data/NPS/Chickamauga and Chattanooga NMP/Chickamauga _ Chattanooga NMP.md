# Chickamauga & Chattanooga NMP (National Military Park)

- **Chickamauga Battlefield Visitor Center** (3370 LaFayette Road, Fort Oglethorpe, GA 30742 [34.94025, -85.2601]; 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM daily, closed Christmas Day and New Year's Day; 706-866-9241) [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/chch/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/chch/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- **Lookout Mountain Battlefield Visitor Center** (110 Point Park Road, Lookout Mountain, TN 37350 [35.0111, -85.3440]; 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM daily, closed Christmas Day and New Year's Day; 423-821-7786) [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/chch/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/258588)]

## Key Activities

- **Chickamauga Battlefield Auto Tour** (1.5 - 2 hours driving, 3-4 hours with stops) - Self-guided driving route through the historic battlefield with interpretive stops at key battle locations including Wilder Brigade, Horseshoe Ridge, and Snodgrass Hill; begins at Chickamauga Battlefield Visitor Center, 3370 LaFayette Road, Fort Oglethorpe, GA 30742; 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [American Battlefield Trust](https://www.battlefields.org/visit/itineraries/tour-chickamauga-battlefield-one-day), [NPS Tours](https://www.nps.gov/chch/planyourvisit/tours.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g60770-d105137-t1537235-How_much_time_should_be_figured_to_experience.html)]

- **Visitor Center Exhibits and Orientation Film** (45 minutes - 1 hour) - Watch the 26-minute orientation film (shown every 30 minutes) and view the 7-minute automated lighted map presentation explaining battle strategies, plus explore museum exhibits; Chickamauga Battlefield Visitor Center, 3370 LaFayette Road, Fort Oglethorpe, GA 30742; 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [WhichMuseum](https://whichmuseum.com/museum/chickamauga-battlefield-visitor-center-chickamauga-and-chattanooga-national-military-park-fort-oglethorpe-29400), [NPPlan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/georgia/at-a-glance-chickamauga-and-chattanooga-national-military-park/visitor-center-chickamauga-battlefield/)]

- **Ranger-Guided Car Caravan Tour** (1.5 - 2 hours) - Park ranger-led car caravan providing basic overview of the Battle of Chickamauga; departs from Chickamauga Battlefield Visitor Center; check with visitor center for schedule [Source: [NPS Tours](https://www.nps.gov/chch/planyourvisit/tours.htm)]

- **Point Park on Lookout Mountain** (1 - 2 hours, longer with Sunset Rock hike) - Explore Civil War fortifications and monuments with panoramic views of Chattanooga and the Tennessee River valley from the "Battle Above the Clouds" site; 110 Point Park Road, Lookout Mountain, TN 37350; 6:00 AM - sunset; $10 per adult (16+), free for under 16 [Source: [Point Park Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/point-park-lookout-mountain), [Two Worlds Treasures](https://twoworldstreasures.com/point-park-chattanooga-tennessee/), [NPPlan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/georgia/at-a-glance-chickamauga-and-chattanooga-national-military-park/park-units-chattanoogalookout-mountain-battlefield/point-park-chattanoogalookout-mountain-battlefield/)]

- **Confederate Line Trail** (3.5 hours) - 6-mile hiking trail following the Confederate battle line through Chickamauga Battlefield; trailhead at Chickamauga Battlefield; dawn to dusk [Source: [NPPlan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/georgia/at-a-glance-chickamauga-and-chattanooga-national-military-park/hiking-trails-chickamauga-battlefield/confederate-line-trail-chickamauga-battlefield/), [Outdoor Activities](https://www.nps.gov/chch/planyourvisit/outdoor-activities.htm)]

- **Sunset Rock via Craven's House Trail** (1.5 - 2 hours round trip) - 3.2-mile out-and-back hike with 469-ft elevation gain to scenic overlook with stunning views of the Tennessee River and Chattanooga valley; trailhead at Cravens House or Point Park, Lookout Mountain [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/tennessee/sunset-rock-via-cravens-house-trail), [Atlanta Trails](https://www.atlantatrails.com/chattanooga/sunset-rock-point-park-lookout-mountain/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Signal Point** (15 - 30 minutes viewing, 2.5 - 3 hours if hiking to Edwards Point) - Lesser-visited overlook with scenic views of Moccasin Bend and the Tennessee River Gorge, access point for Cumberland Trail hiking to Edwards Point (5.1 miles round trip); Signal Point Park, Signal Mountain; dawn to dusk; **Note:** As of November 2025, trailhead closed for construction [Source: [NPS Signal Point](https://www.nps.gov/chch/learn/historyculture/signal-point.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g55337-d561017-Reviews-Signal_Point-Signal_Mountain_Tennessee.html), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/tennessee/signal-point-park-to-edwards-point)]

- **Moccasin Bend Trails** (1 - 2 hours) - Two quiet trails in a national archaeological district: Blue Blazes Trail (2-mile loop with Tennessee River overlook) and Brown's Ferry Federal Road Trail (1.2 miles out and back); Moccasin Bend; dawn to dusk [Source: [NPS Moccasin Bend](https://www.nps.gov/chch/learn/historyculture/moccasin-bend-national-archeological-district.htm), [NOOGAtoday](https://noogatoday.6amcity.com/short-hikes-on-moccasin-bend/), [Outdoor Chattanooga](https://outdoorchattanooga.com/moccasin-bend/)]

- **Chickamauga Bluff** (30 minutes - 1 hour) - Quieter alternative viewpoint with equally stunning vistas, offering escape from crowds and chance to immerse in nature; location within park boundaries [Source: [Hidden Gems Chattanooga](https://livelinden.com/p/hidden-gems-in-chattanooga/)]

- **Orchard Knob Reservation** (30 minutes) - Small preserved hilltop where General Grant established his headquarters to observe the Battle of Chattanooga, featuring monuments and interpretive markers; Orchard Knob Avenue, Chattanooga; open year-round [Source: [Tennessee River Valley](https://tennesseerivervalleygeotourism.org/entries/chickamauga-and-chattanooga-national-military-park/5166bfd9-fc43-404a-9898-3894124f07f6)]

## Also Nearby

- **Tennessee Aquarium** (3 hours) - One of the world's largest freshwater aquariums with 10,000 animals; downtown Chattanooga, approximately 20-30 minutes from Chickamauga Battlefield; 1 Broad Street, Chattanooga, TN 37402; Monday-Friday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Sunday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed Thanksgiving and Christmas [Source: [Tennessee Aquarium](https://tnaqua.org/visit/), [TripHobo](https://www.triphobo.com/places/chattanooga-united-states/tennessee-aquarium)]

- **Ruby Falls** (2 - 3 hours) - 145-foot underground waterfall inside Lookout Mountain with guided cave tours; approximately 15-20 minutes from Lookout Mountain Battlefield Visitor Center; 1720 S. Scenic Highway, Chattanooga, TN 37409; 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM daily; advance reservations required; 423-821-2544 [Source: [Ruby Falls FAQ](https://www.rubyfalls.com/plan-your-visit/faq/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g54946-d104473-Reviews-Ruby_Falls-Chattanooga_Tennessee.html)]

- **Lookout Mountain Incline Railway** (1 hour for round trip experience) - Historic railway climbing at steep 72.7% grade between St. Elmo and Lookout Mountain summit; approximately 15 minutes from Chickamauga Battlefield; St. Elmo Station: 3917 St. Elmo Avenue, Chattanooga, TN 37409; Lookout Mountain Station: 827 East Brow Road, Lookout Mountain, TN 37350; Tuesday-Friday 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Saturday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 7:00 PM, closed Mondays, Thanksgiving, and Christmas; 423-821-4224 [Source: [Ride the Incline](https://ridetheincline.com/), [Incline FAQ](https://ridetheincline.com/faq/), [U.S. News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/Chattanooga_TN/Things_To_Do/Lookout_Mountain_Incline_Railway_63178/)]

- **Rock City Gardens** (2 - 4 hours) - Unique rock formations and gardens atop Lookout Mountain with views of seven states; approximately 10 minutes from Lookout Mountain Battlefield Visitor Center; 1400 Patten Road, Lookout Mountain, GA 30750; seasonal hours vary, closed Christmas Day; 706-820-2531 [Source: [Rock City](https://www.seerockcity.com/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g35073-d266189-Reviews-Rock_City_Gardens-Lookout_Mountain_Georgia.html), [Lookout Mountain](https://www.lookoutmountain.com/rock-city/)]
