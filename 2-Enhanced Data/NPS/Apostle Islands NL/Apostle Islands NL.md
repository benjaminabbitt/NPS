## Key Activities

- **Sea Cave Kayaking** (3-7 hours depending on tour) - Paddle through the famous red sandstone sea caves along the mainland shoreline near Meyers Beach or explore island sea caves with guided tours suitable for beginners, with short tours of 2-2.5 hours on the water (3.5-hour total commitment) or full-day tours available; tours depart from various outfitters in Bayfield, WI; guided tours operate mid-June to early October; guided tour pricing varies by outfitter, rental kayaks start at $40 for six hours (requires 1-2 hour safety course); 715-779-3397 [Source: [NPS Kayaking](https://www.nps.gov/apis/planyourvisit/kayaking.htm), [Lost Creek Adventures](https://www.lostcreekadventures.org/seacaveexploration), [Apostle Islands Kayaking](https://bayfieldkayak.com/), [Paddling Magazine](https://paddlingmag.com/trips/destinations/apostle-islands-kayaking/), [Travel Wisconsin](https://www.travelwisconsin.com/article/water-activities/tour-the-apostle-islands-with-help-from-these-pros)]

- **Grand Tour Lighthouse Cruise** (2.5-3.25 hours) - Take a narrated 55-mile scenic cruise passing the Raspberry and Devils Island Lighthouses, dramatic sea caves, standing rocks, sandstone cliffs, and marine wildlife on Lake Superior; departs from Bayfield City Dock, Bayfield, WI 54814; operates daily from late May through early October with departures typically at 10:00 AM (early season, returning at 1:00 PM); adult tickets approximately $45-55; 715-779-3925 [Source: [Apostle Islands Cruises](https://www.apostleisland.com/grand-tour-cruise/), [Superior Trails](https://www.superiortrails.com/apostle-island-cruise.html), [Atlas Obsession](https://atlasobsession.com/apostle-islands-cruise/), [Bayfield Tourism](https://www.bayfield.org/apostle-islands/getting-to-the-islands/apostle-islands-boat-tours/)]

- **Hike the Lakeshore Trail at Meyers Beach** (2-3 hours round-trip) - Walk the 4.1-mile out-and-back trail (2.3 miles one-way) with 196 feet of elevation gain featuring boardwalk sections and views of the mainland sea caves from above, including natural formations like "The Bowl" and a natural bridge; trailhead at Meyers Beach parking lot on Meyers Road, 4 miles east of Cornucopia or 17 miles west of Bayfield off State Highway 13; open dawn to dusk daily; parking fee $3-5 per vehicle; 715-779-3397 [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/wisconsin/meyers-beach-sea-cave-trail), [NPS Meyers Beach](https://www.nps.gov/places/meyers-beach.htm), [Outdoor Project](https://www.outdoorproject.com/united-states/wisconsin/meyers-beach-sea-cave-trail), [Bayfield Tourism](https://www.bayfield.org/things-to-do/attractions/mainland-sea-caves/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Raspberry Island Lighthouse Tour** (3.5 hours total, 2 hours on island) - Take a ranger-guided tour of the restored 1863 lighthouse including all rooms in the Lightkeeper's residence with narration about daily life a century ago, then explore the island grounds on your own; boat shuttle from Bayfield City Dock, Bayfield, WI; tours operate mid-June to mid-September, four days a week from late June to early September, tours given 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM; boat shuttle approximately one hour each way; adult tickets approximately $45-55; 715-779-3925 [Source: [Apostle Islands Cruises Lighthouse Tours](https://www.apostleisland.com/lighthouse-tours/), [Scenic Pathways](https://www.scenicpathways.com/raspberry-island-lighthouse/), [Superior Trails](https://www.superiortrails.com/apostle-island-raspberry-lighthouse.html), [Lake Superior Circle Tour](https://lakesuperiorcircletour.info/location/raspberry-island-lighthouse/)]

- **Stockton Island Tombolo Trail and Julian Bay** (4.5 hours total including boat shuttle, 2 hours on island) - Hike the 3-mile Tombolo Trail through pine forest and wetlands to reach Julian Bay's pristine "singing sands" beach where sand squeaks beneath your feet, with opportunities to see one of the largest populations of carnivorous pitcher plants; boat shuttle from Bayfield City Dock to Stockton Island (45 minutes each way), trail starts near ranger station past campsite #19; shuttle operates late May through early October; camping permits required for overnight stays (available at Bayfield headquarters); shuttle pricing varies; 715-779-3397 [Source: [NPS Stockton Island](https://www.nps.gov/places/stockton-island-apis.htm), [Van Life By Design](https://vanlifebydesign.com/2025/08/06/exploring-stockton-island-trekking-the-tombolo-trail/), [Apostle Islands Cruises Shuttles](https://www.apostleisland.com/hiking-and-camping-shuttles/), [Friends of Apostle Islands](https://friendsoftheapostleislands.org/2022/07/22/stockton-island/), [Apostle Islands.com](https://www.apostleisland.com/2023/07/19/5-incredible-stockton-island-hiking-trails/)]

- **Winter Ice Caves at Meyers Beach** (2-3 hours, seasonal and rare) - When Lake Superior freezes sufficiently (typically requiring over 90% ice coverage), walk on the frozen lake to explore the spectacular ice formations in the mainland sea caves, with frozen waterfalls and ice curtains; access from Meyers Beach parking lot on Meyers Road off State Highway 13; accessible only when conditions permit (last full access was 9 days in 2015); $5 per person age 16+ when accessible; check "Ice Line" at 715-779-3397 ext. 3 for current conditions [Source: [NPS Ice Caves](https://www.nps.gov/apis/mainland-caves-winter.htm), [NPS Ice Caves Rarity Article](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/apostle-islands-ice-caves-exploring-their-rarity-and-access-challenges.htm), [Bayfield County](https://www.bayfieldcounty.wi.gov/731/Ice-Caves), [Travel Wisconsin](https://www.travelwisconsin.com/article/natural-attractions-and-parks/explore-the-ice-caves-at-wisconsins-apostle-islands), [Rosct Guide](https://www.rosct.com/usa/wisconsin/apostle-islands-ice-caves/2117/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Lost Creek Falls** (1-1.5 hours round-trip) - Hike a peaceful 2-3 mile round-trip trail through the forest to an 8-foot waterfall with cascades above and below where you can walk behind the falls, a rare feature for Lake Superior waterfalls; trailhead on Trail Drive, 0.5 miles from County Highway C, south of Cornucopia, WI (17.4 miles north of Washburn, about 20 miles from Bayfield); open dawn to dusk daily; free admission [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/wisconsin/lost-creek-falls), [Travel Wisconsin](https://www.travelwisconsin.com/natural-attractions-and-parks/lost-creek-falls-254404), [The Outbound](https://www.theoutbound.com/wisconsin/hiking/hike-to-lost-creek-falls), [Lake Superior Circle Tour](https://lakesuperiorcircletour.info/location/lost-creek-falls-trail/)]

- **Madeline Island Ferry and La Pointe Village** (2-3 hours for round-trip ferry and village exploration) - Take the 25-minute ferry ride (2.5 miles) across Lake Superior to Madeline Island's historic village of La Pointe with shops, restaurants, heritage center, free walking tours, beaches, playgrounds, and bike/moped rentals within walking distance of the ferry dock; ferry departs from Washington Avenue off State Highway 13, Bayfield, WI; ferries run every 30 minutes in summer, operates March/April through freeze-up (weather permitting), reduced schedule Christmas and New Year's Day; round-trip fares vary by vehicle/passenger; ferry information: visit madferry.com [Source: [Madeline Island Ferry](https://madferry.com/), [Madeline Island Chamber](https://www.madelineisland.com/madeline-island/getting-here/madeline-island-ferry-schedule/), [Travel Wisconsin](https://www.travelwisconsin.com/boating-sailing/madeline-island-ferry-line-203643), [Bayfield Tourism](https://www.bayfield.org/apostle-islands/madeline-island/getting-to-madeline-island/)]

- **Copper Crow Distillery** (1 hour for tour and tasting) - Visit the first Native American-owned distillery in the United States on the Red Cliff Band of Lake Superior Chippewa Reservation, featuring unique spirits made from whey (a cheese byproduct) including vodka and gin, with tours, tastings, and outdoor seating available; 37395 State Highway 13, Bayfield, WI 54814 (on the Red Cliff Reservation); Thursday-Friday 3:00 PM - 7:00 PM, Saturday 1:00 PM - 7:00 PM, closed Sunday-Wednesday; tours and tastings encouraged (pricing varies) [Source: [Copper Crow Website](https://coppercrowdistillery.com/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/copper-crow-distillery-bayfield), [Bayfield Tourism](https://www.bayfield.org/listing/copper-crow-distillery-l-l-c/428/), [Travel Wisconsin](https://www.travelwisconsin.com/breweries/copper-crow-distillery-301780)]

**Total Recommended Time at Apostle Islands NL (National Lakeshore):** Full day minimum (6-8 hours) for mainland activities including visitor center, sea cave kayaking or lighthouse cruise, and Meyers Beach trail; 2-3 days recommended to explore multiple islands, lighthouses, and hiking trails; seasonal activities like ice caves (when accessible) add 2-3 hours.
