# Knife River Indian Villages NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="visitor-center-stamp"></a>**Visitor Center** (564 County Road 37, Stanton, ND 58571 (47.3315297, -101.3858376); Memorial Day-Labor Day: 9:00 AM-5:00 PM CT, Labor Day-Memorial Day: 8:00 AM-4:30 PM CT, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day; 701-745-3300) - The National Park Passport cancellation stamp is available at the visitor center, which should be your first stop when visiting the site. Free admission to the site, museum, and visitor center. [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/knife-river-indian-villages-national-historic-site/), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/knri/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/knri/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** Call 701-745-3300 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- <a id="visitor-center-museum"></a>**Visitor Center and Museum** (30-45 minutes) - Explore the visitor center featuring interpretive exhibits about Hidatsa culture and history, a bookstore operated by Theodore Roosevelt Nature & History Association, and a 15-minute orientation film about Buffalo Bird Woman describing the life of the Mandan and Hidatsa tribes; 564 County Road 37, Stanton, ND 58571 (47.3315297, -101.3858376); Memorial Day-Labor Day: 9:00 AM-5:00 PM CT, Labor Day-Memorial Day: 8:00 AM-4:30 PM CT, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day; 701-745-3300. [Source: [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g28955-d217947-Reviews-Knife_River_Indian_Villages_Historic_Site-North_Dakota.html), [NPS Videos](https://www.nps.gov/knri/learn/photosmultimedia/multimedia.htm), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/knri/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- <a id="earthlodge-tour"></a>**Reconstructed Earthlodge Tour** (20-30 minutes) - Tour a full-scale replica of a Hidatsa earthlodge built in 1997, fully furnished with replica artifacts during summer season, located just outside the visitor center; ranger-led tours available hourly in summertime, or self-guided exploration during park hours; same address and hours as visitor center. [Source: [North Dakota Living](https://www.ndliving.com/explore-more-knife-river-indian-villages), [NPS Kniferiver Article](https://www.nps.gov/articles/kniferiver.htm), [Guide to Knife River](https://www.beyondmydoor.com/knife-river-indian-villages-national-historic-site-north-dakota/)]

- <a id="village-trail"></a>**Village Trail** (30-60 minutes) - Walk this easy 1.3-mile round-trip flat gravel trail from the visitor center through two Hidatsa village sites (Awatixa Xi'e Village/Lower Hidatsa Site and Awatixa/Sakakawea Village) and along the Knife River, with visible remains of earthlodge dwellings, cache pits, and travois trails; the trail includes a loop at the second village site with a peaceful riverside walk; trailhead at visitor center. [Source: [AllTrails Village Trail](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/north-dakota/village-trail), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/knri/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Guide to Knife River](https://www.beyondmydoor.com/knife-river-indian-villages-national-historic-site-north-dakota/)]

- <a id="north-forest-trail"></a>**North Forest Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - Hike this 2.2-mile trail through bottomland forest to the top butte area for the best view from the park, overlooking the Knife and Missouri rivers; the trail goes along the northern part of the site through prairie lands and has a small shoot-off to visit the Big Hidatsa Site (also known as Hidatsa Village, established around 1600 CE); trailhead at visitor center parking area. [Source: [North Dakota Living](https://www.ndliving.com/explore-more-knife-river-indian-villages), [AllTrails Parks](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/north-dakota/knife-river-indian-villages-national-historic-site), [Guide to Knife River](https://www.beyondmydoor.com/knife-river-indian-villages-national-historic-site-north-dakota/)]

- <a id="traditional-gardens"></a>**Traditional Hidatsa Gardens** (15-20 minutes) - View two traditional gardens maintained during the growing season, planted as the Mandan, Hidatsa, and Arikara people did for hundreds of years, featuring the "Three Sisters" (corn, beans, squash) and sunflowers; women were the primary farmers who specialized in this intercropping technique; gardens located near visitor center, best viewed summer through early fall. [Source: [North Dakota Living](https://www.ndliving.com/explore-more-knife-river-indian-villages), [NPS Gardens](https://www.nps.gov/knri/learn/historyculture/the-gardens.htm), [Islands Article](https://www.islands.com/1995505/knife-river-indian-villages-national-historic-site-riverside-north-dakota-park-historic-gem-gardens-scenic-trails/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="two-rivers-trail"></a>**Two Rivers Trail** (3-4 hours) - Hike the longest trail at 6.2 miles to reach the peninsula at the confluence of the Knife and Missouri rivers, where you can see old-growth cottonwoods that were traditionally used to build earthlodges; this trail offers a more remote experience of the historic site's natural landscape; trailhead at visitor center parking area. [Source: [Guide to Knife River](https://www.beyondmydoor.com/knife-river-indian-villages-national-historic-site-north-dakota/), [Islands Article](https://www.islands.com/1995505/knife-river-indian-villages-national-historic-site-riverside-north-dakota-park-historic-gem-gardens-scenic-trails/)]

- <a id="inaturalist-program"></a>**iNaturalist Citizen Science Program** (flexible timing) - Participate in ongoing research by using the iNaturalist app to document and study plants and animals found throughout the park, contributing to scientific understanding of the site's biodiversity; available year-round during park hours. [Source: [Tourist Secrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/north-dakota/hidden-gems-of-north-dakotas-knife-river-indian-villages-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="lifeways-program"></a>**Lifeways of the Northern Plains Program** (1-2 hours) - Attend this cultural program featuring traditional Mandan, Hidatsa, and Arikara activities, particularly recommended during fall season; includes specialty programs with MHA Nation musicians and local artists; contact park for current schedule at 701-745-3300. [Source: [Tourist Secrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/north-dakota/hidden-gems-of-north-dakotas-knife-river-indian-villages-national-historic-site/), [NPS Education](https://home.nps.gov/knri/learn/education/index.htm)]

- <a id="kids-camp"></a>**Kids Camp** (full day, July-August) - Free one-day camp for children ages 6-12 featuring canoeing, fishing, mini-earthlodge building, ethnobotany exploration, Native games, scavenger hunts, and crafts; offered for three full weeks in July; reservations recommended via email or by calling 701-745-3300. [Source: [NPS Kids Camp](https://www.nps.gov/knri/learn/kidsyouth/kids-camp.htm), [Frommers](https://www.frommers.com/trip-ideas/family/exploring-the-knife-river-indian-villages-of-north-dakota/)]

- <a id="junior-ranger"></a>**Junior Ranger Program** (30-60 minutes) - Kids can complete activities to earn a Junior Ranger badge, with both on-site and online program options available year-round; pick up activity booklet at visitor center or access online at nps.gov/knri. [Source: [NPS Be a Junior Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/knri/learn/kidsyouth/beajuniorranger.htm), [NPS Online Junior Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/knri/learn/kidsyouth/online-junior-ranger.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="lewis-clark-center"></a>**Lewis & Clark Interpretive Center** (2-3 hours, 25 miles) - Explore world-class exhibits, hundreds of period artifacts, and engaging displays about the Lewis and Clark Expedition's winter of 1804-1805 at Fort Mandan; combine with tour of reconstructed Fort Mandan (2 miles away, 1-hour tour included in admission); 2576 8th St SW, Washburn, ND 58577 (47.301202, -101.041687); Summer (May 1-Sept 30): Daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Winter (Oct 1-Apr 30): Mon-Sat 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; 701-462-8535. Approximately 25 miles from Knife River (about 30-35 minutes drive). [Source: [Lewis & Clark Interpretive Center](https://www.history.nd.gov/historicsites/lcic/), [ND Tourism](https://www.ndtourism.com/washburn/attractions-entertainment/educational-attractions/lewis-and-clark-interpretive-center), [Lewis and Clark Trail](https://www.lewisandclark.travel/listing/lewis-and-clark-interpretive-center-north-dakota/)]

- <a id="fort-clark"></a>**Fort Clark State Historic Site** (45-60 minutes, 7.5 miles) - Self-guided tour of archaeological remains preserving a Mandan village, cemetery, and two fur trading posts at one of North Dakota's most important archaeological sites; includes modern restrooms, picnic area, and observation deck with interpretive signage; ND-200 Alt., 7.5 miles southeast of Stanton, ND (47.253248, -101.2753493); Open year-round (no snow removal), optimal May-September; free admission, donations welcome. Approximately 7.5 miles from Knife River (about 15 minutes drive). [Source: [Fort Clark Historic Site](https://www.history.nd.gov/historicsites/clark/), [ND Tourism Fort Clark](https://www.ndtourism.com/stanton/attractions-entertainment/landmark-attractions/fort-clark-state-historic-site), [Tripadvisor Fort Clark](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60879-d1469346-Reviews-Fort_Clark_State_Historic_Site-Stanton_North_Dakota.html)]
