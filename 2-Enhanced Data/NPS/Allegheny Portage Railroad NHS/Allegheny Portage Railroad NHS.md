# Allegheny Portage Railroad NHS (National Historic Site)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Summit Level Visitor Center** (110 Federal Park Road, Gallitzin, PA 16641; daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 814-886-6150)

**Note:** Call 814-886-6150 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/alpo/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Summit Level Visitor Center** (30-45 minutes) - Explore the renovated museum featuring detailed exhibits, dioramas, and an information video about the historic portage railroad system; walk through a full-size replica canal boat and see the Lafayette locomotive replica; 110 Federal Park Road, Gallitzin, PA 16641; daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/alpo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Uncovering PA](https://uncoveringpa.com/allegheny-portage-railroad)]

- **Lemon House Historic Tavern** (30 minutes) - Tour the restored 1840s tavern with period-furnished rooms including the barroom, fancy parlor, and dining room; self-guided tour with cell phone audio option available; 0.4 mile walk from Visitor Center (0.8 mile round trip); 110 Federal Park Road, Gallitzin, PA 16641; daily 9:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Lemon House](https://www.nps.gov/alpo/planyourvisit/lemon-tavern.htm), [NPS Plan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/pennsylvania-national-parks/allegheny-portage-railroad-national-historic-site-park-at-a-glance/allegheny-portage-railroad-national-historic-site-sites-to-see/allegheny-portage-railroad-national-historic-site-lemon-house-tavern/)]

- **Engine House 6 Exhibit Shelter** (20-30 minutes) - View full-scale models of steam engine mechanisms and interactive exhibits showing how the inclined plane system worked; watch four short videos explaining steam engine operations; 10-minute walk from Visitor Center via boardwalk or old Gallitzin Road; 110 Federal Park Road, Gallitzin, PA 16641; Friday-Tuesday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM (closed Wednesday-Thursday) [Source: [NPS Engine House 6](https://www.nps.gov/alpo/planyourvisit/engine-house-6.htm), [NPS Plan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/pennsylvania-national-parks/allegheny-portage-railroad-national-historic-site-park-at-a-glance/allegheny-portage-railroad-national-historic-site-sites-to-see/allegheny-portage-railroad-national-historic-site-engine-house-6/)]

- **Summit Level Hiking Trails** (varies) - Explore over 15 miles of trails throughout the park including boardwalk trails connecting historic sites; trails open daily sunrise to sunset weather permitting; 110 Federal Park Road, Gallitzin, PA 16641 [Source: [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/alpo/planyourvisit/hiking-and-biking.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/pennsylvania/allegheny-portage-railroad-national-historic-site)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Staple Bend Tunnel Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - Hike or bike 2 miles each way on a flat, level limestone trail to America's first railroad tunnel (901 feet long); bring a flashlight to explore inside the historic 1833 tunnel; wheelchair accessible; trailhead parking at Beech Hill Road, Mineral Point, PA; open daily sunrise to sunset [Source: [NPS Staple Bend](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hike-or-bike-to-the-staple-bend-tunnel.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/pennsylvania/staple-bend-tunnel), [Visit Johnstown](https://www.visitjohnstownpa.com/partner/staple-bend-tunnel-trail)]

- **Skew Arch Bridge** (10-15 minutes) - View this masterwork of cut stone construction near the Lemon House; the bridge is 60.4 feet long and 22.2 feet high, demonstrating advanced 1830s engineering; accessible from Summit Level trails; 110 Federal Park Road, Gallitzin, PA 16641; open daily sunrise to sunset [Source: [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/alpo)]

- **Heritage Hikes with Rangers** (varies) - Join ranger-led guided hikes through different sections of the park during summer months; reservations required; call 814-886-6150 for schedule and availability [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/alpo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Horseshoe Curve National Historic Landmark** (1-1.5 hours, 12 miles west) - Witness active freight trains navigating this engineering marvel where the tracks curve 220 degrees around a mountainside; ride the funicular to the observation area with visitor center featuring historical artifacts; 2400 Veterans Memorial Highway, Altoona, PA; call 814-946-0834 ext. 2102 to confirm hours and funicular availability [Source: [Railroad City](https://www.railroadcity.org/horseshoecurve), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/horseshoe-curve-national-historic-landmark-altoona)]

- **Gallitzin Tunnels Park & Museum** (30-45 minutes, 3 miles) - Watch modern trains emerge from the Allegheny Tunnel at this free park; explore the small museum with railroad exhibits and restored 1942 PRR caboose; 411 Convent Street, Suite 20, Gallitzin, PA 16641; Tuesday-Sunday 12:00 PM-5:00 PM (closed Monday and November-April); 814-886-8871 [Source: [Gallitzin Tunnels](https://www.gallitzin.info/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60715-d2452950-Reviews-Gallitzin_Tunnels_Park_Museum-Gallitzin_Pennsylvania.html)]

- **Railroaders Memorial Museum** (2 hours, 12 miles west) - Explore Pennsylvania Railroad history in the 1882 Master Mechanics Building with comprehensive exhibits about railroad workers and operations; 1300 9th Avenue, Altoona, PA 16602; Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday 11:00 AM-5:00 PM; 814-946-0834 ext. 2102 [Source: [Railroad City](https://www.railroadcity.org/), [PA Bucket List](https://pabucketlist.com/exploring-the-altoona-railroaders-memorial-museum/)]
