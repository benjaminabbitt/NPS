# Stones River NB (National Battlefield)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Stones River National Battlefield Visitor Center** (3501 Old Nashville Highway, Murfreesboro, TN 37129 (35.876085, -86.4307839); Mondays & Thursdays-Saturdays 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sundays 11:00 AM - 5:00 PM, November 1 - February 29: 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM; Closed Tuesdays, Wednesdays, Thanksgiving, and December 25; (615) 893-9501) - Passport stamp is located in the lobby of the visitor center on a wooden table; bonus stamp also available at the stamping station [Source: [Tennessee's National Parks Passport Cancellation Stamps](http://nostalgia.esmartkid.com/tnpassport.html), [Visitor Centers - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/stri/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Operating Hours - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/stri/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

**Note:** Call (615) 893-9501 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center Museum & Film** (30-60 minutes) - Explore comprehensive exhibits featuring Civil War uniforms, artillery, and period memorabilia; watch orientation film about the Battle of Stones River; browse bookstore; 3501 Old Nashville Highway, Murfreesboro, TN 37129; Mondays & Thursdays-Saturdays 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sundays 11:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [Visitor Centers - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/stri/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [TN Vacation](https://www.tnvacation.com/local/murfreesboro-stones-river-national-battlefield)]

- **Self-Guided Driving Tour** (1.5-2 hours) - Follow the one-way auto tour route visiting six key battlefield locations that tell the story of how the battle transpired; call the cell phone audio tour at each stop for 2-minute narratives at no charge; five stops concentrated west of Old Nashville Highway plus McFadden Farm; tour road open 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [Battlefield Tour - NPPlan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/tennessee/stones-river-national-battlefield-park-at-a-glance/stones-river-national-battlefield-battlefield-tour/), [The Roaming Monk](https://roamingmonk.com/stones-river-national-battlefield/), [Guided Tours - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/stri/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)]

- **Stones River Battlefield Loop Trail** (1-1.5 hours, 3.1 miles, 85 ft elevation gain) - Hike the main loop trail past key battle sites including the Hazen Brigade Monument and the Slaughter Pen; relatively flat mowed grass and natural trail; 3501 Old Nashville Highway, Murfreesboro, TN 37129; sunrise to sunset [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/tennessee/stones-river-battlefield-loop-trail), [Hiking - NPS](https://home.nps.gov/stri/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)]

- **Stones River National Cemetery** (15-30 minutes) - Visit the final resting place of more than 6,100 Union soldiers (2,562 unknown); short vehicle driveway reaches center, all graves visited on foot; located across the street from the Visitor Center; open daily 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [Stones River National Cemetery - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/stri/learn/historyculture/nationalcemetery.htm), [NPPlan Cemetery](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/tennessee/stones-river-national-battlefield-park-at-a-glance/stones-river-national-battlefield-stones-river-national-cemetery/), [Visit Stones River - American Battlefield Trust](https://www.battlefields.org/visit/battlefields/stones-river-battlefield)]

- **Hazen Brigade Monument** (15-20 minutes) - View the oldest intact Civil War monument still standing in its original battlefield location, erected by Col. William Babcock Hazen in 1863-1864 to honor the 45 men who died defending the Round Forest; quadrangular pyramidal shaft with officers' names carved on four faces; accessible via driving tour or hiking trails; sunrise to sunset [Source: [Hazen Brigade Monument - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/hazen-brigade-monument.htm), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazen_Brigade_Monument), [Iron Brigader](https://ironbrigader.com/2015/09/25/hazen-brigade-monument-stones-river-nations-oldest-civil-war-memorial/)]

- **McFadden Farm (Tour Stop 6)** (30-45 minutes) - Explore the site where Union artillery pushed back Gen. Breckinridge's advance on January 2, 1863; view Artillery Monument, McFadden Family cemetery, and replica cannons; hike the Artillery Trail (0.5 miles) and other loop trails through farm fields down to Stones River; McFadden Farm parking area; 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [Tour Stop 6 - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/stri/learn/photosmultimedia/battle_stop6.htm), [The Roaming Monk](https://roamingmonk.com/stones-river-national-battlefield/), [NPPlan McFadden Farm](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/tennessee/stones-river-national-battlefield-park-at-a-glance/stones-river-national-battlefield-battlefield-tour/stones-river-national-battlefield-tour-stop-6-mcfadden-farm/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Fortress Rosecrans** (30-45 minutes) - Explore the preserved remains of this major Union logistics base built after the battle; much of the fortress has been preserved in town; dawn to dusk [Source: [Fortress Rosecrans - American Battlefield Trust](https://www.battlefields.org/visit/heritage-sites/fortress-rosecrans-stones-river-national-battlefield), [Visit Stones River - American Battlefield Trust](https://www.battlefields.org/visit/battlefields/stones-river-battlefield)]

- **Ranger-Led Programs** (1-2 hours) - Join special programs offered April through December including lectures, battlefield hikes, bike tours, evening cemetery walks, living history demonstrations, and weapons demonstrations (cannon and musket firing); daily programs once school is out; weapons demonstrations typically weekend events once or twice monthly; check with visitor center for current schedule; (615) 893-9501 [Source: [Visitor Centers - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/stri/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Things To Do - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/stri/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Stones River Cedar Glade and Barrens** (30-45 minutes) - Walk the 185-acre natural area within the battlefield featuring trails, interpretive exhibits, and boardwalks that traverse earthworks; view unique cedar glade ecosystem; accessed from battlefield grounds; sunrise to sunset [Source: [TN Vacation](https://www.tnvacation.com/local/murfreesboro-stones-river-national-battlefield), [Stones River Cedar Glade - TN.gov](https://www.tn.gov/environment/program-areas/na-natural-areas/natural-areas-middle-region/middle-region/na-na-stones-river-cedar-glade.html)]

- **Volunteer Programs** - Participate in volunteer opportunities to experience the park from behind the scenes; learn about unique natural resources and ecosystems; contact visitor center for current opportunities; (615) 893-9501 [Source: [Things To Do - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/stri/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Oaklands Mansion** (45-60 minutes, 5 miles) - Tour this antebellum mansion where Confederate cavalrymen under Nathan Bedford Forrest defeated Union forces on the front lawn during the Battle of Stones River; children watched the battle from a second-story window; self-guided tours with staff assistance; explore Oaklands Park native tree arboretum, wetlands, heritage gardens, and Maney Spring; 901 North Maney Avenue, Murfreesboro, TN 37130 (35.85421, -86.385458); Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM, Closed Mondays (except large groups by reservation); Adults $15, AAA/AARP/Military $12, Students/Children $6, Under 5 free; (615) 893-0022 [Source: [Oaklands Mansion](https://www.oaklandsmansion.org/), [Hours & Admission](https://www.oaklandsmansion.org/hours-admission/), [TN Vacation](https://www.tnvacation.com/local/murfreesboro-oaklands-mansion)]

- **Cannonsburgh Village** (1-2 hours, 4 miles) - Explore this living history village representing 100 years of early Tennessee life (1830s-1930s); see gristmill, one-room schoolhouse, town hall, log home, church, doctor's office, museum, general store, caboose, working blacksmith shop, and the World's Largest Cedar Bucket; self-guided tours free; 312 S. Front St., Murfreesboro, TN 37130 (35.8441092, -86.3955915); Summer (April 1 - October 31): Tuesday-Friday 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM; Winter (November 1 - March 31): Tuesday-Friday 9:00 AM - 3:00 PM; call ahead for private rentals: (615) 890-0355 [Source: [Cannonsburgh Village - City of Murfreesboro](https://www.murfreesborotn.gov/164/Cannonsburgh-Village), [TN Vacation](https://www.tnvacation.com/local/murfreesboro-cannonsburgh-village), [Rutherford County History](https://rutherfordtnhistory.org/a-visit-to-murfreesboros-cannonsburgh-village/)]
