# Ste. Geneviève NHP (National Historical Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Ste. Genevieve Welcome Center** (66 S. Main Street, Ste. Genevieve, MO 63670 (37.9783, -90.0442); Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 573-880-7189) - National Park Service passport stamps available at visitor information desk where rangers provide city maps and answer questions about the park. [Source: [Ste. Genevieve NHP Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/stge/ste-genevieve-nhp-visitor-center.htm), [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/10077125), [Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/stge/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** Call 573-880-7189 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. Park established October 2020 as newest National Historical Park preserving Missouri's oldest town founded 1735.

## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center Exhibits and Film** (30-45 minutes) - View 10-minute introductory video about Ste. Genevieve's history, explore exhibits featuring detailed diorama showing how French colonists lived and established this first permanent European settlement west of the Mississippi River; rangers available to answer questions and provide orientation; 66 S. Main Street, Ste. Genevieve, MO 63670 (37.9783, -90.0442); Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; FREE [Source: [Visit Missouri Things to Do](https://www.visitmo.com/things-to-do/ste-genevieve-national-historical-park-2), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/ste-genevieve-national-historical-park/)]

- **Green Tree Tavern Tour** (30-45 minutes) - Tour this historic French Colonial tavern with National Park Service rangers; tours offered daily depending on staffing and weather, limited to 15 individuals; reservations available for guaranteed tour time; located within walking distance of visitor center; tours during park hours; FREE [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/ste-genevieve-national-historical-park/), [National Parks Foundation](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/ste-genevieve-national-historical-park), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ste._Genevieve_National_Historical_Park)]

- **Jean Baptiste Vallé House Tour** (30-45 minutes) - Explore this significant French Colonial house featuring what is believed to be the first rose garden west of the Mississippi River; National Park Service ranger-led tours offered daily depending on staffing and weather, limited to 15 individuals; reservations available; located within walking distance of visitor center; tours during park hours; FREE [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/ste-genevieve-national-historical-park/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ste._Genevieve_National_Historical_Park)]

- **Historic District Self-Guided Walk** (1.5-2 hours) - Stroll narrow streets of America's oldest permanent European settlement west of the Mississippi (founded 1735) viewing numerous 18th and 19th century French Colonial and early American buildings; pick up walking map at visitor center; historic district accessible during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [Little Time and Keyboard Blog](https://www.alittletimeandakeyboard.com/2021/04/ste-genevieve-missouri.html), [Visit USA](https://www.visittheusa.com/experience/ste-genevieve-missouri-vacation-steeped-history), [Great River Road](https://www.greatriverroad.com/ste-genevieve-attractions)]

- **Centre for French Colonial Life Tours** (1.5-2 hours) - Tour multiple historic French Colonial structures including Louis Bolduc House, Beauvais-Amoureux House (1792 vertical log poteaux-en-terre construction - only 5 such houses remain in entire United States, 3 in Ste. Genevieve), and Guilbourd-Valle House; tours every hour on half hour (10:30 AM, 11:30 AM, 1:30 PM, 2:30 PM, 3:30 PM, 4:30 PM) except noon; operates separately from NPS visitor center; admission charged [Source: [Centre for French Colonial Life](https://www.lewisandclark.travel/listing/centre-for-french-colonial-life/), [Little Time and Keyboard Blog](https://www.alittletimeandakeyboard.com/2021/04/ste-genevieve-missouri.html), [Travel South USA](https://industry.travelsouthusa.com/ste-genevieve-national-historical-park/)]

- **Felix Vallé House State Historic Site** (1-1.5 hours) - Tour 1818 American-Federal style stone house furnished in 1830s style showing American influence on French community after Louisiana Purchase; includes authentically restocked Menard & Valle mercantile store, plus access to Beauvais-Amoureux House and Dr. Benjamin Shaw House with rotating exhibits and gift shop; Second and Merchant streets, Ste. Genevieve, MO 63670; tours at 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 2:00 PM, 3:00 PM; April-October 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM daily, November-March 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM Thursday-Sunday; admission charged, gardens FREE [Source: [Felix Vallé House State Historic Site](https://mostateparks.com/park/felix-valle-house-state-historic-site), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g44888-d2265051-Reviews-Felix_Valle_House_State_Historic_Site-Sainte_Genevieve_Missouri.html), [Great River Road](https://www.greatriverroad.com/fcc-attraction-pages/felix-valle)]

- **French Heritage Festival** (half to full day, annually) - Attend Missouri's largest French heritage celebration featuring colonial reenactments, traditional music and dance, French colonial crafts demonstrations, historic home tours, and French food; held annually in August; Ste. Genevieve historic district; check website for dates; various admission options [Source: [Visit Missouri](https://www.visitmo.com/things-to-do/ste-genevieve-national-historical-park-2)]

## Hidden Gems

- **La Veille French Colonial Summer Social** (2-3 hours, seasonal) - Experience authentic French colonial social gathering with period activities, music, dancing, and demonstrations of 18th century French colonial life; held during summer months; Ste. Genevieve historic district; check park website for schedule; FREE [Source: [Visit Missouri](https://www.visitmo.com/things-to-do/ste-genevieve-national-historical-park-2)]

- **Mississippi River Ste. Gen-Modoc Ferry** (30-45 minutes crossing) - Ride historic ferry transporting passengers from Main Street ferry landing in Ste. Genevieve across Mississippi River to Modoc and Fort du Chartres in Illinois; connects to Illinois Highway 3; one of few remaining Mississippi River ferries; Main Street ferry landing; hours vary seasonally; nominal fee [Source: [Visit USA](https://www.visittheusa.com/experience/ste-genevieve-missouri-vacation-steeped-history), [St. Louis Magazine](https://www.stlmag.com/culture/travel/where-to-eat-play-and-stay-in-ste-genevieve-missouri/)]

- **Ste. Genevieve Garden Walk** (2-3 hours, annually) - Tour private gardens and historic properties not normally open to public; showcases beautiful period gardens behind historic French Colonial and American Federal homes; held annually in spring; Ste. Genevieve historic district; check Missouri State Parks calendar for dates; admission charged [Source: [Missouri State Parks Events](https://mostateparks.com/event/58572/ste-genevieve-garden-walk)]

- **Shaw House Rotating Exhibits** (20-30 minutes) - View changing exhibitions about Ste. Genevieve history and French colonial life in Dr. Benjamin Shaw House at Felix Vallé State Historic Site; includes gift shop; Second and Merchant streets; site hours; FREE admission [Source: [Felix Vallé House](https://mostateparks.com/park/felix-valle-house-state-historic-site)]

## Also Nearby

- **Ste. Genevieve Wine Country** (2-4 hours) - Visit more than dozen wineries and vineyards within town limits including Cave Vineyard (unique tram ride through cave with wine tasting), Chaumette Vineyards & Winery (rolling hills views, excellent food and wine), Crown Valley (winery, brewery, and distillery), Weingarten Vineyard (spectacular tasting room overlooking vines, try Norton Missouri grape), Lewsi Winery and Sainte Genevieve Winery (both in historic district); various locations in Ste. Genevieve; hours vary by winery; tasting fees apply [Source: [Spotlight Missouri Wine Country](https://missouriwine.org/news/spotlight-missouri-wine-country-ste-genevieve), [St. Louis Magazine](https://www.stlmag.com/culture/travel/where-to-eat-play-and-stay-in-ste-genevieve-missouri/), [Visit Ste Gen](https://visitstegen.com/)]

- **Hawn State Park** (2-4 hours, 30 minutes from Ste. Genevieve) - Hike through majestic pine trees, camp, or enjoy family picnic at this beautiful Missouri state park featuring unique geological formations; 12096 Park Drive, Ste. Genevieve, MO 63670; dawn to dusk; FREE [Source: [Visit USA](https://www.visittheusa.com/experience/ste-genevieve-missouri-vacation-steeped-history)]

- **Fort de Chartres State Historic Site, Illinois** (1-2 hours, across Mississippi River via ferry) - Explore reconstructed French fort (1720s-1760s) that was administrative center of French colonial Illinois Country; accessible via Ste. Gen-Modoc Ferry; 1350 State Highway 155, Prairie du Rocher, IL 62277; hours vary seasonally; minimal admission [Source: [Visit USA](https://www.visittheusa.com/experience/ste-genevieve-missouri-vacation-steeped-history)]

- **Ste. Genevieve Historic District Restaurants** (1-2 hours) - Dine at local restaurants including Audubon's Hotel restaurant (lunch through late-night in 1900-era building), plus various winery restaurants; throughout historic district; hours vary by establishment [Source: [St. Louis Magazine](https://www.stlmag.com/culture/travel/where-to-eat-play-and-stay-in-ste-genevieve-missouri/), [Visit Ste Gen](https://visitstegen.com/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time at Ste. Geneviève NHP (National Historical Park):** Minimum 2-3 hours for visitor center and one or two NPS house tours; 4-6 hours for comprehensive visit including visitor center, NPS tours, and Centre for French Colonial Life; full day recommended for complete experience with Felix Vallé State Historic Site, historic district walk, and wineries; weekend visit ideal for French Heritage Festival or Garden Walk events. Location: 1 hour south of St. Louis. [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/ste-genevieve-national-historical-park/), [Visit USA](https://www.visittheusa.com/experience/ste-genevieve-missouri-vacation-steeped-history)]
