## Key Activities

- **Belle Grove Plantation Manor House Tour (45 minutes-1 hour)** - Guided tours of the 1797 limestone manor house built by Isaac Hite Jr., a large Federal-style National Historic Landmark that became part of Cedar Creek & Belle Grove NHP in 2002; tours explore the lives of plantation owners and enslaved workers; 336 Belle Grove Road, Middletown, VA 22645; April 1-October 31: Monday-Saturday 10:00am-4:00pm, Sunday 1:00pm-5:00pm (tours begin 15 minutes after each hour) [Source: [NPS Tour Manor House](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/tour-the-1797-manor-house.htm), [Belle Grove](https://bellegrove.org/visit/tours), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/cebe/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Morning Attack Walking Trails (30 minutes-1 hour)** - Three easy interpretive trails totaling approximately 1.5-1.7 miles following places where soldiers fought during the early hours of the Battle of Cedar Creek on October 19, 1864; trails follow a line of trenches built by the US 19th Corps in the fall of 1864; includes the 8th Vermont Monument Trail (300 yards/600 yards round-trip), Thomas Brigade Loop Trail (0.6 miles), and Hayes-Ramseur Loop Trail (0.7 miles); trails connect with Belle Grove Plantation where remnants of trenches and earthworks are still visible; trailheads accessible from various points along the battlefield; daily sunrise to sunset [Source: [NPS Walk Morning Attack Trails](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/walk-morning-attack-trails.htm), [NPS Explore Cedar Creek](https://www.nps.gov/tripideas/explore-cedar-creek-battlefield.htm), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/cebe/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **19th Corps Trenches Trail (30-45 minutes)** - One-mile interpretive trail along the XIX Corps trenches built in 1864; accessible with a stamped pass from the Cedar Creek Battlefield Foundation visitor center; woodland trail features remnants of Civil War fortifications; 8437 Valley Pike, Middletown, VA 22645; daily sunrise to sunset [Source: [NPS Explore Cedar Creek](https://www.nps.gov/tripideas/explore-cedar-creek-battlefield.htm), [CCBF Visit](https://ccbf.us/visit/)]

- **Cedar Creek Battlefield Foundation Museum (30-45 minutes)** - Free museum featuring original Civil War artifacts from the Battle of Cedar Creek and mid-19th century Shenandoah Valley, handmade dioramas, artwork, farming equipment, Digital Media Center playing a documentary film about the Battle of Cedar Creek, research library, and archaeological artifacts; exhibits change periodically with something new to see; 8437 Valley Pike, Middletown, VA 22645; daily sunrise to sunset (museum and visitor center outdoor space); free admission [Source: [CCBF Visit](https://ccbf.us/visit/), [NPS Visit Battlefield Museum](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/visit-ccbf-hq.htm), [NPS CCBF Museum](https://www.nps.gov/places/ccbf-museum-visitor-center.htm)]

- **Self-Guided Battlefield Driving Tour (1+ hour)** - A 17.5-mile auto tour with 9 stops chronicling the Battle of Cedar Creek, going chronologically through the morning Confederate attack and afternoon Union counterattack; tour passes key battlefield locations and monuments; self-guided tour materials available at visitor centers; various locations throughout the park; daily sunrise to sunset [Source: [NPS Tour Cedar Creek Battlefield](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/cebe-battlefield-auto-tour.htm), [Winchester Visitor Bureau](https://visitwinchesterva.com/event/cedar-creek-battlefield-driving-tour/), [NationalParksData](https://nationalparksdata.com/a-visitors-guide-to-cedar-creek-and-belle-grove-national-historical-park/)]

- **Belle Grove Plantation Grounds & Gardens Self-Guided Tour (30-45 minutes)** - Explore the historic landscape, outbuildings including a smokehouse, icehouse, and slave quarters that provide insight into daily activities of residents; view exhibits beyond the manor house tour; 336 Belle Grove Road, Middletown, VA 22645; April 1-October 31: Monday-Saturday 10:00am-4:00pm, Sunday 1:00pm-5:00pm [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/cebe/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Belle Grove Visit](https://bellegrove.org/visit), [NationalParksData](https://nationalparksdata.com/a-visitors-guide-to-cedar-creek-and-belle-grove-national-historical-park/)]

- **Ranger-Led Programs (1-2 hours, varies)** - NPS offers ranger-led programs throughout the year on topics including the peopling and rich cultural history of the Shenandoah Valley, the Civil War, and the Battle of Cedar Creek; special weekend programs with costumed interpreters available; check with visitor centers for current program schedule; various locations; seasonal, call for schedule [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/cebe/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [CCBF Visit](https://ccbf.us/visit/)]

- **18-Minute Battle Map Presentation (18 minutes)** - Lighted battle map at the NPS Visitor Contact Station that illustrates the October 19, 1864 Battle of Cedar Creek; provides excellent orientation to the battlefield before driving tour or trail walks; 7712 Main Street, Middletown, VA 22645; hours vary, call for schedule [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/cebe/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Virginia.org CEBE](https://www.virginia.org/listing/cedar-creek-and-belle-grove-national-historical-park/4985/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **8th Vermont Monument & Battlefield Markers (15-20 minutes)** - Unlike large Civil War battlefields with hundreds of markers, memorials and cannons, Cedar Creek features only a few signs and markers creating a peaceful, "mercifully uncrowded" experience; the 8th Vermont Monument marks a key position during the battle; accessible via the 8th Vermont Monument Trail; 8th Vermont Monument Trail trailhead; daily sunrise to sunset [Source: [NationalParksData](https://nationalparksdata.com/a-visitors-guide-to-cedar-creek-and-belle-grove-national-historical-park/), [Virginia.org](https://www.virginia.org/listing/cedar-creek-and-belle-grove-national-historical-park/4985/)]

- **Historic Outbuildings at Belle Grove (20-30 minutes)** - Several preserved outbuildings on the Belle Grove property including a smokehouse, icehouse, and slave quarters that provide intimate insight into the daily operations of the plantation and lives of enslaved people; often overlooked in favor of the manor house tour; 336 Belle Grove Road, Middletown, VA 22645; April 1-October 31: Monday-Saturday 10:00am-4:00pm, Sunday 1:00pm-5:00pm [Source: [NationalParksData](https://nationalparksdata.com/a-visitors-guide-to-cedar-creek-and-belle-grove-national-historical-park/), [National Parks Org](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/cedar-creek-and-belle-grove-national-historical-park)]

- **Visible Earthworks & Trenches Along Woodland Trails (included in trail time)** - Well-preserved remnants of trenches and earthworks built by the US 19th Corps in 1864 are still visible along the woodland trails connecting the 128th New York Monument with Belle Grove Plantation; these authentic Civil War fortifications provide tangible connection to the battle; accessible via Morning Attack Trails; daily sunrise to sunset [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/cebe/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [National Parks Org](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/cedar-creek-and-belle-grove-national-historical-park)]
