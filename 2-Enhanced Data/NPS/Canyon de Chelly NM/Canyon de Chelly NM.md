# Canyon de Chelly NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-canyon-de-chelly-welcome-center"></a>**Canyon de Chelly Welcome Center** (Indian Route 7, 3 miles east of Highway 191, Chinle, AZ 86503 (36.1535, -109.5348); 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 928-674-5500) [Source: [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/cach/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/cach/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** During inclement weather, the Welcome Center may open later or be closed for the day. Call 928-674-5507 for general information. No entrance fee. [Source: [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/cach/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-drive-south-rim-drive"></a>**Drive South Rim Drive** (2 hours) - Travel 16 miles along Canyon de Chelly to seven spectacular overlooks including Spider Rock, an 800-foot spire rising from the canyon floor; best for afternoon photos; free with no guide required [Source: [Visit USA](https://www.visittheusa.com/experience/insiders-guide-canyon-de-chelly-national-monument), [We're in the Rockies](https://wereintherockies.com/canyon-de-chelly/)]

- <a id="activity-drive-north-rim-drive"></a>**Drive North Rim Drive** (2 hours) - Travel 15 miles along Canyon del Muerto to viewpoints of Antelope House cliff dwellings and Massacre Cave; best for morning photos; free with no guide required [Source: [Visit USA](https://www.visittheusa.com/experience/insiders-guide-canyon-de-chelly-national-monument), [We're in the Rockies](https://wereintherockies.com/canyon-de-chelly/)]

- <a id="activity-take-guided-canyon-floor-tour"></a>**Take Guided Canyon Floor Tour** (3-4 hours) - Experience required Navajo-guided tours into the canyon via Jeep, horseback, or hiking to see ruins like Antelope House and lush valley floors surrounded by sheer sandstone cliffs; popular "shake and bake" tours by Thunderbird Lodge available; authorized guide required for all canyon entry except White House Trail [Source: [Visit Arizona](https://www.visitarizona.com/like-a-local/a-guide-to-visiting-canyon-de-chelly-the-right-way), [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/cach/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- <a id="activity-view-spider-rock-overlook"></a>**View Spider Rock Overlook** (30 minutes) - Walk short trail from parking lot to spectacular views of dual sandstone spires towering 800 feet above canyon floor; very popular at sunset; located at end of South Rim Drive [Source: [Visit USA](https://www.visittheusa.com/experience/insiders-guide-canyon-de-chelly-national-monument)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-hike-white-house-ruins-trail"></a>**Hike White House Ruins Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - Walk the only self-guided trail into the canyon, a 2.5-mile round-trip with 600 feet elevation gain leading to ancient Navajo cliff dwellings; no guide required; starts at White House Overlook on South Rim Drive, 6 miles from visitor center [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/cach/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [We're in the Rockies](https://wereintherockies.com/canyon-de-chelly/)]

**Note:** White House Ruin Overlook and trail currently open Friday-Sunday only due to safety concerns. Call ahead to confirm. [Source: [Visit USA](https://www.visittheusa.com/experience/insiders-guide-canyon-de-chelly-national-monument)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-monument-valley-navajo-tribal-park"></a>**Monument Valley Navajo Tribal Park** (1-2 hours, 95 miles north) - View magnificent red sandstone buttes made famous in classic westerns; located on Navajo Nation; admission required [Source: [Discover Navajo](https://discovernavajo.com/canyon-de-chelly/)]

- <a id="nearby-hubbell-trading-post-national-historic-site"></a>**Hubbell Trading Post National Historic Site** (1-2 hours, 30 miles south in Ganado) - Shop for authentic Navajo arts and crafts at the oldest operating trading post on Navajo land; open daily; free admission [Source: [Visit USA](https://www.visittheusa.com/experience/insiders-guide-canyon-de-chelly-national-monument)]

- <a id="nearby-petrified-forest-national-park"></a>**Petrified Forest National Park** (2-3 hours, 90 miles southwest via I-40) - See vibrantly colored tree fossils and explore the Painted Desert's eerie badlands; 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily [Source: [Discover Navajo](https://discovernavajo.com/canyon-de-chelly/)]
