# Abraham Lincoln Birthplace NHP (National Historical Park)

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-visit-the-memorial-building-and-symbolic-birth-cabin"></a>**Visit the Memorial Building and Symbolic Birth Cabin** (30-60 minutes) - Climb 56 steps (one for each year of Lincoln's life) to enter the neoclassical Memorial Building housing a symbolic log cabin representing Abraham Lincoln's birthplace, or take the accessible 0.2-mile Pathway of a President Trail boardwalk to the rear entrance; 2995 Lincoln Farm Road, Hodgenville, KY 42748; Memorial Building open 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM Eastern Time daily (Visitor Center open until 5:00 PM), closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 270-358-3137 [Source: [NPS Memorial Building](https://www.nps.gov/places/abraham-lincoln-birthplace-memorial-building.htm), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g60708-d108188-t3440486-How_long_should_I_plan_on_being_here.html), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/abli/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

- <a id="activity-explore-the-visitor-center-and-watch-the-film"></a>**Explore the Visitor Center and Watch the Film** (30-45 minutes) - Browse exhibits including the Lincoln family Bible, watch a 15-minute film about Lincoln's Kentucky years, and visit the park store; located at 2995 Lincoln Farm Road, Hodgenville, KY 42748; 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM Eastern Time daily, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 270-358-3137 [Source: [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/10001967), [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/abli/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60708-d108188-Reviews-Abraham_Lincoln_Birthplace_National_Historical_Park-Hodgenville_Kentucky.html)]

- <a id="activity-hike-the-big-sink-trail"></a>**Hike the Big Sink Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - Walk a series of three loops (A, B, and C) that combine for 1.36 miles through hardwood forest exploring the natural karst landscape of Sinking Spring Farm where the Lincoln family lived, with opportunities to spot deer and see old wagon ruts from the historic wagon road; trailhead at Birthplace Unit picnic area across the highway, 2995 Lincoln Farm Road, Hodgenville, KY 42748; open dawn to dusk daily; 270-358-3137 [Source: [NPS Hiking Trails](https://www.nps.gov/abli/planyourvisit/hiking-trails.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/kentucky/abraham-lincoln-birthplace-trails), [Nomadic Niko](https://nomadicniko.com/kentucky/abraham-lincoln-birthplace-nhp/)]

- <a id="activity-walk-the-boundary-oak-trail"></a>**Walk the Boundary Oak Trail** (10-25 minutes) - Hike a short 0.3-mile easy trail through the woods on the original Sinking Spring Farm to see where the historic Boundary Oak once stood marking the edge of the Lincoln family property, with opportunities to spot wildlife including birds, squirrels, deer, and wild turkey; trail begins to the left of the Memorial Building, 2995 Lincoln Farm Road, Hodgenville, KY 42748; open dawn to dusk daily; 270-358-3137 [Source: [NPS Boundary Oak Trail](https://www.nps.gov/abli/planyourvisit/boundary-oak-trail.htm), [NP Plan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/kentucky-national-parks/abraham-lincoln-birthplace-national-historical-park-park-at-a-glance/abraham-lincoln-birthplace-national-historical-park-hiking-trails/abraham-lincoln-birthplace-national-historical-park-boundary-oak-trail/), [Nomadic Niko](https://nomadicniko.com/kentucky/abraham-lincoln-birthplace-nhp/)]

- <a id="activity-picnic-at-the-park-grounds"></a>**Picnic at the Park Grounds** (30-60 minutes) - Enjoy free picnic areas with tables in a scenic natural setting near the hiking trails at the Birthplace Unit, with ample free parking including space for RVs, campers, and trailers; picnic area located across the highway from the main park entrance at 2995 Lincoln Farm Road, Hodgenville, KY 42748; open dawn to dusk daily; 270-358-3137 [Source: [National Parks Org](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/abraham-lincoln-birthplace-national-historical-park), [Family Travel Unplugged](https://familytravelunplugged.com/abraham-lincoln-birthplace-national-historical-park/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-visit-the-sinking-spring"></a>**Visit the Sinking Spring** (15-20 minutes) - Descend a stairway to see the historic karst spring feature where groundwater briefly emerges before sinking underground, which served as the Lincoln family's primary water source and gave the farm its name; located adjacent to the parking lot, a short walk from the Birthplace Visitor Center at 2995 Lincoln Farm Road, Hodgenville, KY 42748; open dawn to dusk daily; free admission; 270-358-3137 [Source: [Kids in Parks](https://www.kidsinparks.com/abraham-lincoln-birthplace-national-historical-park), [Abraham Lincoln Online](https://www.abrahamlincolnonline.org/lincoln/sites/birth.htm), [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/abli/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

- <a id="gem-complete-the-junior-ranger-program"></a>**Complete the Junior Ranger Program** (1-1.5 hours) - Pick up a free age-appropriate activity booklet (ages 5-6, 7-9, or 10-12) at the Visitor Center and complete activities including tool matching, cabin building puzzles, and Lincoln Heritage Trail map exercises while exploring the park, then share answers with a ranger to receive an official Junior Ranger Badge; available at Birthplace Visitor Center, 2995 Lincoln Farm Road, Hodgenville, KY 42748; 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM Eastern Time daily; free; 270-358-3137 [Source: [NPS Junior Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/abli/learn/kidsyouth/beajuniorranger.htm), [Junior Ranger Programs Blog](https://jrpfmount.wordpress.com/2014/08/13/abraham-lincoln-birthplace-national-historical-park/)]

- <a id="gem-join-a-ranger-led-program-or-tour"></a>**Join a Ranger-Led Program or Tour** (varies, typically 30-90 minutes) - Participate in guided tours and educational programs led by knowledgeable park rangers who provide in-depth information about Abraham Lincoln's early life and the significance of the park, including family programs at 2:00 PM and morning nature hikes from Memorial Day through Labor Day at the Knob Creek Unit; schedule and availability varies seasonally, check at Visitor Center for current offerings at 2995 Lincoln Farm Road, Hodgenville, KY 42748; 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM Eastern Time daily; free; 270-358-3137 [Source: [Family Travel Unplugged](https://familytravelunplugged.com/abraham-lincoln-birthplace-national-historical-park/), [NPS Things To Do](https://home.nps.gov/abli/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Visit Hodgenville](https://visithodgenville.com/)]

- <a id="gem-explore-the-boyhood-home-at-knob-creek-unit"></a>**Explore the Boyhood Home at Knob Creek Unit** (30-45 minutes, or 2.5-3 hours with Overlook Trail) - Visit the site where Abraham Lincoln lived from ages 2-7 and formed his earliest childhood memories, featuring a historic log cabin similar to his boyhood home and optional Overlook Trail (1.5 miles with 364 ft elevation gain, most challenging trail in the park); 7120 Bardstown Road, Hodgenville, KY 42748 (approximately 10 miles from Birthplace Unit); grounds open dawn to dusk daily, Knob Creek Tavern Visitor Center open seasonally; free admission; 270-358-3137 [Source: [NPS Boyhood Home](https://www.nps.gov/abli/planyourvisit/boyhood-home.htm), [NPS Hiking Trails](https://www.nps.gov/abli/planyourvisit/hiking-trails.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/kentucky/abraham-lincoln-birthplace-national-historical-park)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-lincoln-museum"></a>**Lincoln Museum** (45-60 minutes) - Explore Kentucky's official tribute to Abraham Lincoln through self-guided tours featuring life-size dioramas, wax figures, and exhibits covering Lincoln's life from his cabin years through Ford's Theatre; 66 Lincoln Square, Hodgenville, KY 42748 (3 miles north of the Birthplace); Monday-Saturday 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM, Sunday 12:30 PM - 4:30 PM, closed Easter Sunday, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day; 270-358-3163 [Source: [Lincoln Museum](https://www.lincolnmuseum-ky.org/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/lincoln-museum-hodgenville)]

- <a id="nearby-lincoln-jamboree-restaurant"></a>**Lincoln Jamboree & Restaurant** (2-3 hours for show and dinner) - Experience Kentucky's longest-running family entertainment and country music show operating since 1954, featuring live performances every Saturday night at 6:45 PM, plus a restaurant serving lunch daily; 2579 Lincoln Farm Road, Hodgenville, KY 42748 (adjacent to park entrance); restaurant open Monday-Sunday 11:00 AM - 3:00 PM, shows Saturday 3:00 PM - 10:30 PM; 270-358-3545 [Source: [Lincoln Jamboree](https://www.thelincolnjamboree.com/), [Visit Hodgenville](https://visithodgenville.com/the-lincoln-jamboree/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60708-d3533438-Reviews-Lincoln_Jamboree-Hodgenville_Kentucky.html)]

- <a id="nearby-downtown-hodgenville-lincoln-square"></a>**Downtown Hodgenville & Lincoln Square** (30-60 minutes) - Stroll through the Downtown Hodgenville National Historic District featuring the Abraham Lincoln Statue (erected 1909), art galleries, boutiques, farm-to-table shops, and dining at Laha's Red Castle (famous for burgers for 90+ years) and the 16TH Restaurant, plus The Sweet Shoppe offering award-winning fudge; Lincoln Square, Hodgenville, KY 42748 (3 miles from park); most shops open Monday-Saturday during business hours; free [Source: [Visit Hodgenville](https://visithodgenville.com/), [City of Hodgenville Downtown](https://cityofhodgenvilleky.com/downtown), [Hodgenville Tourism](https://vacationidea.com/kentucky/hodgenville-ky.html)]

- <a id="nearby-my-old-kentucky-home-state-park"></a>**My Old Kentucky Home State Park** (1 hour for mansion tour) - Take a guided tour by costumed interpreters through the historic Federal Hill mansion that inspired Stephen Foster's famous song, with tours departing hourly on the hour; 501 East Stephen Foster Ave., Bardstown, KY 40004 (approximately 20 miles from Hodgenville); tours daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (last tour at 4:00 PM Eastern Time), closed Tuesdays in January & February and on Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve, and New Year's Day; 502-348-3502 [Source: [My Old Kentucky Home Hours](https://www.visitmyoldkyhome.com/hours), [Visit Bardstown](https://www.visitbardstown.com/listing/my-old-kentucky-home-state-park/53/)]

- <a id="nearby-kentucky-railway-museum"></a>**Kentucky Railway Museum** (1.5 hours for train ride, plus museum time) - Experience scenic train excursions on historic steam and diesel locomotives traveling between New Haven and Boston, Kentucky, and explore railroad exhibits; 136 S Main St, New Haven, KY 40051 (11 miles/17 minutes from Hodgenville); Tuesday-Friday 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM, Saturday 11:00 AM - 4:30 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM - 4:30 PM, closed Mondays; train rides typically 1.5 hours long [Source: [Kentucky Railway Museum](https://kyrail.org/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/kentucky-railway-museum-new-haven), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g39704-d107233-Reviews-Kentucky_Railway_Museum-New_Haven_Kentucky.html)]
