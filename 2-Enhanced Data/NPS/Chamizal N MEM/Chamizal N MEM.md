# Chamizal N MEM (National Memorial)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Chamizal National Memorial Visitor Center** - Located at 800 South San Marcial Street, El Paso, TX 79905 (31.767°N, -106.454°W); Visitor Center: Tuesday - Saturday 10 AM - 5 PM; Park grounds: 5 AM - 10 PM daily; Closed Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day; Phone: 915-532-7273 [Source: [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/cham/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Contact](https://www.nps.gov/cham/contacts.htm), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/chamizal-national-memorial-el-paso)]

**Note:** Call 915-532-7273 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Museum & Visitor Center Exhibits** (30 minutes - 1 hour) - Discover the story of the Chamizal border dispute and the voices of the local neighborhood through exhibits at the newly remodeled visitor center and museum, which details the history of the U.S.–Mexico border; 800 South San Marcial Street, El Paso, TX 79905 (31.767°N, -106.454°W); Tuesday - Saturday 10 AM - 5 PM [Source: [NPS National Park Getaway](https://home.nps.gov/articles/getaway-cham.htm), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60768-d208938-Reviews-Chamizal_National_Memorial-El_Paso_Texas.html), [WNPA Plan Your Trip](https://wnpa.org/plan-your-trip/trip-ideas/park-stories/plan-your-trip-to-chamizal-national-memorial-1)]

- **Art Galleries** (15-30 minutes) - View temporary exhibits featuring artwork of national and international borderland artists, with shows changing every 2-3 months; 800 South San Marcial Street, El Paso, TX 79905 (31.767°N, -106.454°W); Tuesday - Saturday 10 AM - 5 PM [Source: [NPS Museum Exhibit](https://www.nps.gov/cham/planyourvisit/museum-exhibit.htm/index.htm), [WhichMuseum](https://whichmuseum.com/museum/chamizal-national-memorial-cultural-center-el-paso-29481), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamizal_National_Memorial)]

- **Chamizal Memorial Trail Walking/Jogging** (30-60 minutes) - Enjoy an easy 1.5-mile loop trail with red gravel surface and gentle grade (3% or less), perfect for walking, jogging, or biking around the memorial grounds; 800 South San Marcial Street, El Paso, TX 79905 (31.767°N, -106.454°W); Park grounds: 5 AM - 10 PM daily [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/texas/chamizal-national-memorial), [Hiiker](https://hiiker.app/trails/texas/el-paso-county/chamizal-national-memorial-loop), [Visit El Paso](https://visitelpaso.com/places/chamizal-national-memorial-trail)]

- **Ranger-Led Programs & Tours** (varies) - Participate in ranger-led programs, guided hikes, bike tours and art activities; advance reservations required for large groups by calling 915-532-7273; 800 South San Marcial Street, El Paso, TX 79905 (31.767°N, -106.454°W); Park hours: 5 AM - 10 PM daily [Source: [NPS National Park Getaway](https://home.nps.gov/articles/getaway-cham.htm), [WNPA Plan Your Trip](https://wnpa.org/plan-your-trip/trip-ideas/park-stories/plan-your-trip-to-chamizal-national-memorial-1)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (1 hour) - Monthly Junior Ranger club for kids ages 5 to 11, meeting on the third Saturday of each month from 11 AM to noon at the Cultural Center; 800 South San Marcial Street, El Paso, TX 79905 (31.767°N, -106.454°W); Third Saturday of each month 11 AM - 12 PM [Source: [WNPA Plan Your Trip](https://wnpa.org/plan-your-trip/trip-ideas/park-stories/plan-your-trip-to-chamizal-national-memorial-1)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Music Under the Stars Concert Series** (2 hours) - Attend free outdoor concerts featuring local, national and international artists in genres including salsa, bluegrass, rock, tribute, and jazz; every Sunday evening from June through August, 7:30-9:30 PM; bring chairs and blankets (coolers allowed, no glass containers); special 4th of July patriotic concert with fireworks; 800 South San Marcial Street, El Paso, TX 79905 (31.767°N, -106.454°W); Sundays 7:30-9:30 PM (June-August), call 915-532-7273 for current schedule [Source: [Kiss El Paso](https://kisselpaso.com/music-under-the-stars-gets-underway-sunday/), [Visit El Paso](https://visitelpaso.com/events/music-under-the-stars), [NPS News 2017](https://www.nps.gov/cham/learn/news/20170630.htm)]

- **Cultural Events & Performances** (varies) - Experience the Chamizal Festival and other musical and theatrical performances throughout the year on indoor and outdoor stages, showcasing cultural heritage of borderlands communities; 800 South San Marcial Street, El Paso, TX 79905 (31.767°N, -106.454°W); Check calendar at 915-532-7273 or [NPS Calendar](https://www.nps.gov/cham/planyourvisit/calendar.htm) [Source: [WNPA Plan Your Trip](https://wnpa.org/plan-your-trip/trip-ideas/park-stories/plan-your-trip-to-chamizal-national-memorial-1), [US News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/El_Paso_TX/Things_To_Do/Chamizal_National_Memorial_65375/)]

## Also Nearby

- **El Paso Mission Trail** (3-4.5 hours, 2 miles southwest) - Explore a 9-mile route featuring three historic Spanish missions: Ysleta Mission, Socorro Mission, and San Elizario Chapel, with artisanal shops and restaurants along the route; visitors can see all three missions in 3-4.5 hours or spend a full day exploring the entire trail; missions located along Mission Trail in El Paso, TX; hours vary by mission, call ahead [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60768-d536209-Reviews-El_Paso_Mission_Trail-El_Paso_Texas.html), [Frommer's](https://www.frommers.com/destinations/el-paso/attractions/el-paso-mission-trail/), [Get Lost in the USA](https://getlostintheusa.com/el-paso-mission-trail/)]

- **Franklin Mountains State Park** (varies, 10 miles north) - Hike over 100 miles of trails in the largest urban wilderness park, including the strenuous 8-mile North Franklin Peak Trail, moderate 3-mile Cardiac Hill/Low Hill Trail, and easy ¾-mile Nature Trail loop; 1331 McKelligon Canyon Rd, El Paso, TX 79930; Park hours: 8 AM - 5 PM, with extended hours (6:30 AM - 5 PM) on Fri-Sun from first Saturday in April to first Saturday in September [Source: [Texas Parks & Wildlife](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/franklin-mountains), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/texas/franklin-mountains-state-park), [Visit El Paso](https://visitelpaso.com/places/franklin-mountains-state-park)]

- **Scenic Drive Overlook** (15-30 minutes, 5 miles northeast) - Enjoy panoramic views of El Paso, Ciudad Juárez in Mexico, and southern New Mexico from the best vista accessible by car; free admission; especially stunning at sunset; Scenic Dr, El Paso, TX 79902; 6 AM - 10 PM daily [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60768-d6988231-Reviews-Scenic_Drive_Overlook-El_Paso_Texas.html), [Visit El Paso](https://visitelpaso.com/places/scenic-drive), [Texas Gov Film Trails](https://gov.texas.gov/film/trail/el-paso-scenic-overlook)]

- **Hueco Tanks State Park & Historic Site** (2-3 hours for guided tour, 32 miles northeast/50 minutes) - View the largest assemblage of pictographic masks in North America with an estimated 5,000 painted symbols spanning thousands of years; advance reservations required; accessible via U.S. Route 62/180, turn at RM 2775; Tours: $5 per person ages 13+, $2 ages 2-12, plus park entry; Winter tours start at 9:30, 10:00, 11:00, or 2:00; Summer tours at 9:00 or 11:00 [Source: [Texas Parks & Wildlife](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/hueco-tanks), [Swept Away Today](https://sweptawaytoday.com/2020/04/27/rock-art-tells-ancient-stories-in-hueco-tanks-near-el-paso-texas/), [Visit El Paso Events](https://visitelpaso.com/events/hueco-tanks-rock-arts-tours)]
