# Belmont-Paul Women's Equality NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Belmont-Paul Women's Equality National Monument** (144 Constitution Ave NE, Washington, DC 20002 (38.89406, -77.00374); Friday-Sunday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM; 202-543-2240 or bepa_info@nps.gov)

**Note:** Contact the monument at 202-543-2240 or bepa_info@nps.gov to confirm current stamp availability before visiting. [Source: [NPS Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/bepa/planyourvisit/index.htm), [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g28970-d564561-Reviews-Belmont_Paul_Women_s_Equality_National_Monument_House_and_Museum-Washington_DC_Dist.html)]

## Key Activities

- **Guided Museum Tour** (40-60 minutes) - Ranger-led tour covering the National Woman's Party headquarters, women's suffrage history, and the fight for the Equal Rights Amendment including viewing historic artifacts, banners from White House picketing, and exhibits throughout the historic 1800 Federal-style house; 144 Constitution Ave NE, Washington, DC 20002 (38.89406, -77.00374); Tours offered at 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM Friday-Sunday [Source: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/event-details.htm?id=D41F31E9-B0F8-FF96-9AF62244B26599CC), [DC Travel Magazine](https://dctravelmag.com/belmont-paul-womens-equality-national-monument/), [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g28970-d564561-Reviews-Belmont_Paul_Women_s_Equality_National_Monument_House_and_Museum-Washington_DC_Dist.html)]

- **Self-Guided Museum Visit** (30-90 minutes) - Explore the permanent exhibits featuring more than 250 artifacts detailing the National Woman's Party story through photographs, objects, and archival materials including banners, political cartoons, and memorabilia from the early 1900s to present; 144 Constitution Ave NE, Washington, DC 20002 (38.89406, -77.00374); Friday-Sunday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [Outdoorsy](https://www.outdoorsy.com/guide/belmont-paul-womens-equality-national-monument), [Mainly Museums](https://mainlymuseums.com/post/595/belmont-paul-women-s-equality-national-monument/), [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/bepa/)]

- **Origins Gallery Exhibit** (15-30 minutes) - View the gallery exploring predecessors of the National Woman's Party and those who inspired Alice Paul and the NWP through the suffrage movement, featuring artwork, photographs, banners, and cartoons displaying their determination and creativity; 144 Constitution Ave NE, Washington, DC 20002 (38.89406, -77.00374); Friday-Sunday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Virtual Tour](https://www.nps.gov/articles/belmont-paul-virtual-tour.htm), [NPS Getaway](https://home.nps.gov/articles/getaway-bepa.htm)]

- **Equal Rights Amendment Exhibits** (15-20 minutes) - Examine displays about Alice Paul writing the 1943 language for the ERA (the "Alice Paul Amendment") and the NWP's nearly 50-year fight for its passage, including the gold-and-purple banner reading "We demand an Amendment to the United States Constitution Enfranchising Women"; 144 Constitution Ave NE, Washington, DC 20002 (38.89406, -77.00374); Friday-Sunday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [Wander Women Project](https://wanderwomenproject.com/places/belmont-paul-womens-equality-national-monument/), [NPS Getaway](https://home.nps.gov/articles/getaway-bepa.htm)]

- **Suffrage Movement Film** (15-20 minutes) - Watch an informative video about the history of the house and the Women's Rights movement, including footage of NWP women's meetings and planning protests from this historic location; 144 Constitution Ave NE, Washington, DC 20002 (38.89406, -77.00374); Friday-Sunday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [Wander Women Project](https://wanderwomenproject.com/places/belmont-paul-womens-equality-national-monument/)]

- **Historical Artifacts Collection** (20-30 minutes) - View significant artifacts including furniture from early women's rights leaders, a life-sized statue of Joan of Arc, a skeleton key smuggled out of the district jail, and a jail door-shaped pin testifying to the courage of imprisoned suffragists; 144 Constitution Ave NE, Washington, DC 20002 (38.89406, -77.00374); Friday-Sunday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [NPS Getaway](https://home.nps.gov/articles/getaway-bepa.htm), [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/usa/washington-dc/capitol-hill-south-dc/attractions/belmont-paul-women-s-equality-national-monument/a/poi-sig/386119/1329650)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Florence Bayard Hilles Feminist Library** (30-60 minutes, by appointment) - Access the oldest feminist library in the United States, featuring monographs and serials dating from 1720 to 2014, including materials from the National Woman's Party's extensive collection on women's suffrage and women's issues; 144 Constitution Ave NE, Washington, DC 20002 (38.89406, -77.00374); Contact bepa_info@nps.gov for research appointments [Source: [NWP Researchers](https://www.nationalwomansparty.org/researchers), [Fine Books & Collections](https://www.finebooksmagazine.com/fine-books-news/library-congress-and-national-park-service-receive-historic-collection-womens), [EBSCO Research](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/history/belmont-paul-womens-equality-national-monument)]

- **War of 1812 History** (10-15 minutes) - Discover the house's unique distinction as the only private residence burned by British troops during the 1814 invasion of Washington, D.C., after American flotilla men stationed there fired shots during the attack; 144 Constitution Ave NE, Washington, DC 20002 (38.89406, -77.00374); Friday-Sunday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/item/dc0963/), [SAH Archipedia](https://sah-archipedia.org/buildings/DC-01-CN02), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belmont–Paul_Women's_Equality_National_Monument)]

- **Federal Architecture & Louisiana Purchase Connection** (10-15 minutes) - Explore the 1800 Adam Federal-style architecture with center-hall Georgian plan built by Robert Sewall, who later rented it to Albert Gallatin, Secretary of the Treasury, who likely wrote the Louisiana Purchase agreement here; 144 Constitution Ave NE, Washington, DC 20002 (38.89406, -77.00374); Friday-Sunday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/usa/washington-dc/capitol-hill-south-dc/attractions/belmont-paul-women-s-equality-national-monument/a/poi-sig/386119/1329650), [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/item/dc0963/)]

- **Political Cartoons Room** (15-20 minutes) - View a dedicated room featuring political cartoons, early twentieth-century newspapers, and editorial cartoons documenting the struggle for women's rights and public perception of the suffrage movement; 144 Constitution Ave NE, Washington, DC 20002 (38.89406, -77.00374); Friday-Sunday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM [Source: [Wander Women Project](https://wanderwomenproject.com/places/belmont-paul-womens-equality-national-monument/)]
