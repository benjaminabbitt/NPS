## Key Activities

- **Scenic Parkway Drive (45 minutes)** - Experience a 29-mile scenic drive through thickly wooded landscapes featuring oak, tulip, ash, maple, sweet gum, sycamore, and pine trees; this ceremonial entrance route into the nation's capital from the north has carried visitors since 1954 with gentle curves, no commercial trucks, and a park-like setting with 400-800 foot buffers maintaining scenic woodland; Baltimore, MD to Washington, D.C.; Open 24/7 [[Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore–Washington_Parkway), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/bawa/), [Source](https://www.myscenicdrives.com/places/maryland/baltimore-washington-parkway)]

- **Greenbelt Park Perimeter Trail (2-3 hours)** - Hike 5.3 miles through a sylvan escape featuring 9 miles of trails including the Perimeter, Azalea, Dogwood, and Blueberry Trails with 396 feet of elevation gain; a hidden jewel just outside Washington, D.C. providing a peaceful woodland experience; 6565 Greenbelt Road, Greenbelt, MD 20770 (38.98303, -76.90114); Dawn to dusk daily; 771-208-1588 [[Source](https://www.nps.gov/gree/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/maryland/greenbelt-park-perimeter-trail), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/gree/index.htm)]

- **Greenbelt Park Camping (Overnight)** - Camp in a peaceful National Park Service campground just 10 miles from Washington, D.C. for $16 per campsite with flush toilets, drinking water, showers, and dump station; each site includes picnic table and combination grill/campfire ring plus free Saturday campfires with stories and Junior Ranger programs; 6565 Greenbelt Road, Greenbelt, MD 20770 (38.98303, -76.90114); Open year-round; 771-208-1588 [[Source](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/232474), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/gree/index.htm)]

- **Savage Historic Mill Trail (25 minutes)** - Walk an easy 0.8-mile trail alongside the Little Patuxent River through woods where the river gurgles smoothly along, providing inspirational views; this flat, gravel-covered trail is wheelchair, bike, and stroller accessible; Savage Mill, Savage, MD; 5 AM - 11 PM daily [[Source](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/maryland/savage-historic-mill-trail), [Source](https://www.savagemill.com/hit-the-trails/)]

- **Montpelier Mansion Tours (1 hour)** - Tour a Georgian mansion built by Major Thomas Snowden in 1783, a National Historic Landmark offering glimpses into Maryland's colonial history including stories of the Snowden family and enslaved individuals who lived and worked on the estate, with special behind-the-scenes tours available; located near the MD 197 exit from the parkway; 9650 Muirkirk Rd, Laurel, MD 20708; Thursday - Monday 10 AM - 4 PM; 301-377-7817 [[Source](https://www.visitmaryland.org/listing/history-heritage/montpelier-mansionhistoric-site), [Source](https://www.yelp.com/biz/montpelier-mansion-laurel-3), [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore–Washington_Parkway)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Laurel Historic District and Museum (30-60 minutes)** - Explore a mill town that originated in the 1830s, featuring the Laurel Railroad Station (still used by MARC Train), an 1844 Queen Anne house, and the Laurel Museum in an 1840s millworker's duplex with free admission; accessible from the parkway in the town of Laurel; 817 Main Street, Laurel, MD 20707; Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays, 12-4 PM (closed December 21 - January 31); 301-725-7975 [[Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore–Washington_Parkway), [Source](https://www.laurelhistoricalsociety.org/directions-and-hours.html), [Source](https://www.yelp.com/biz/laurel-museum-laurel)]

- **College Park Airport Historic Site (1-2 hours)** - Visit the world's oldest continuously operating airport where Wilbur Wright established the first runway to give flying lessons, on the National Register of Historic Places including a 1919 airmail hangar; the College Park Aviation Museum features full-size reproductions of early planes and demonstrates the incredible development of powered flight from shaky machines to WWI combat capability; 1985 Corporal Frank Scott Dr, College Park, MD 20740 (38.9788355, -76.922275); Tuesday - Sunday: 10 AM - 4 PM; 301-864-6029 [[Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore–Washington_Parkway), [Source](https://www.pgparks.com/facilities/college-park-aviation-museum), [Source](https://www.yelp.com/biz/college-park-aviation-museum-college-park)]

- **Beltsville Agricultural Research Center Historic Views (30 minutes drive-through)** - Drive through a USDA research facility featuring historic buildings, barns, silos, fields, and animals; note that the former Visitor Center (Log Lodge) has been permanently closed and the facility generally does not grant public access, though historic buildings are visible from the parkway; 10300 Baltimore Avenue, Beltsville, MD 20705; Facility hours 7:30 AM - 6 PM; 301-504-6591 [[Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore–Washington_Parkway), [Source](https://www.ars.usda.gov/northeast-area/beltsville-md-barc/beltsville-agricultural-research-center/)]

## Also Nearby

- **NASA Goddard Space Flight Center Visitor Center (1-2 hours)** - Explore a key NASA space research laboratory and visitor center approximately 15 minutes from the parkway in Greenbelt; **IMPORTANT NOTE:** NASA announced plans to close the visitor center on October 1, 2025, so it may already be closed as of November 2025; 8800 Greenbelt Rd, Greenbelt, MD 20770; Previously Thursday - Friday: 10 AM - 3 PM, Saturday: 12 PM - 4 PM; 301-286-8981 [[Source](https://www.nasa.gov/goddard/visitor-center/), [Source](https://www.yelp.com/biz/nasa-goddard-space-flight-center-greenbelt), [Source](https://www.thecrazytourist.com/15-best-things-to-do-in-greenbelt-md/)]

- **Patuxent Research Refuge National Wildlife Visitor Center (2-3 hours)** - Discover wildlife viewing, interpretive displays, educational films, and public programs at one of the nation's premier wildlife refuges approximately 20 minutes from the parkway; 10901 Scarlet Tanager Loop, Laurel, MD 20708; Visitor Center: Wednesday - Saturday 10 AM - 4 PM, Grounds: sunrise to sunset except Tuesdays (grounds close at 4 PM); 301-497-5772 [[Source](https://www.fws.gov/refuge/patuxent-research/visit-us), [Source](https://www.fws.gov/refuge/patuxent-research/patuxent-visitor-center), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/bawa/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm)]

- **Buddy Attick Lake Park (1-2 hours)** - Enjoy a 23-acre picturesque lake in Greenbelt (approximately 10 minutes from parkway) with a 1.25-mile dirt and stone path perfect for walking, fishing (license required for ages 16+), canoeing/kayaking (permit required), picnic pavilions with grills, playground, and tot lot; no swimming or motorized boats allowed; 555 Crescent Rd, Greenbelt, MD 20770; 5 AM - 11 PM daily [[Source](https://www.greenbeltmd.gov/Facilities/Facility/Details/Buddy-Attick-Lake-Park-17), [Source](https://www.yelp.com/biz/buddy-attick-park-greenbelt), [Source](https://www.thecrazytourist.com/15-best-things-to-do-in-greenbelt-md/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 45 minutes to 1 hour for the scenic parkway drive itself. The Baltimore-Washington Parkway is primarily a scenic roadway rather than a traditional park destination. Most visitors experience it as a 29-mile scenic drive taking approximately 45 minutes without traffic. For those wishing to explore the parkway's historical and natural features more thoroughly, allow 3-4 hours to include stops at Greenbelt Park trails, Montpelier Mansion, or the Savage Mill Trail.
