# Stones River NB (National Battlefield)

## Key Activities

- **Visitor Center Museum and Orientation Film** (30-60 minutes) - Explore comprehensive exhibits featuring Civil War uniforms, artillery, and period memorabilia, followed by a 9-minute orientation film describing the Battle of Stones River; excellent museum winds clockwise through exhibits starting with Civil War origins and progressing to the battle itself; 3501 Old Nashville Highway, Murfreesboro, TN 37129 (35.881861, -86.434846); Mondays & Thursdays-Saturdays 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (4:00 PM November 1 - February 29), Sundays 11:00 AM - 5:00 PM; 615-893-9501 [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/stri/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [NPS Orientation Film](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/orientationfilm.htm), [NPPlan Visitor Center](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/tennessee/stones-river-national-battlefield-park-at-a-glance/stones-river-national-battlefield-visitor-center/)]

- **Battlefield Driving Tour with Audio Narrative** (1-3 hours) - Drive the 5-mile tour road visiting six key battlefield locations with cell phone audio tour (call 1-585-797-0076) or NPS app; includes stops at The Slaughter Pen, Hell's Half Acre (Round Forest), and McFadden's Farm; thorough tour with hiking at each stop takes 3 hours, quick tour takes 1 hour; tour road open 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM; 3501 Old Nashville Highway, Murfreesboro, TN 37129 (35.881861, -86.434846); 615-893-9501 [Source: [NPPlan Battlefield Tour](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/tennessee/stones-river-national-battlefield-park-at-a-glance/stones-river-national-battlefield-battlefield-tour/), [American Battlefield Trust](https://www.battlefields.org/visit/battlefields/stones-river-battlefield), [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/stri/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)]

- **Stones River National Cemetery** (15-30 minutes) - Visit the oldest intact Civil War national cemetery with over 6,100 Union soldiers buried (2,562 unknown); view the U.S. Regulars Monument (1882) and monument honoring the 43rd Wisconsin and 108th Ohio Infantry; easy walk from Visitor Center across the street; cemetery open daily 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM; 3501 Old Nashville Highway, Murfreesboro, TN 37129 (35.881861, -86.434846); 615-893-9501 [Source: [NPS National Cemetery](https://www.nps.gov/stri/learn/historyculture/nationalcemetery.htm), [NPPlan Cemetery](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/tennessee/stones-river-national-battlefield-park-at-a-glance/stones-river-national-battlefield-stones-river-national-cemetery/), [American Battlefield Trust](https://www.battlefields.org/visit/battlefields/stones-river-battlefield)]

- **Hazen Brigade Monument** (15 minutes) - Visit the oldest American Civil War monument still standing in its original battlefield location, built just six months after the battle by brigade members; 45 brigade members who died in battle are buried within the stone wall enclosure; accessible along tour route; grounds open sunrise to sunset; 3501 Old Nashville Highway, Murfreesboro, TN 37129 (35.881861, -86.434846) [Source: [Hazen Brigade Monument Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazen_Brigade_Monument), [National Park Foundation](https://www.nationalparks.org/explore/parks/stones-river-national-battlefield)]

- **Fortress Rosecrans** (30-45 minutes) - Explore one of the largest earthen fortifications built during the Civil War; remaining features include Redoubt Brannan, Lunettes Palmer and Thomas, and Curtain Wall 2; built by Federal Army after the battle to fortify Murfreesboro; open dawn to dusk; separate location in Murfreesboro, TN [Source: [American Battlefield Trust Fortress Rosecrans](https://www.battlefields.org/visit/heritage-sites/fortress-rosecrans-stones-river-national-battlefield), [NPPlan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/tennessee/stones-river-national-battlefield-park-at-a-glance/)]

- **Stones River Battlefield Loop Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - Hike the 3.1-mile easy loop trail with 85 feet elevation gain through scenic battlefield terrain; highest-rated trail in park (4.6 stars from 1,409 AllTrails reviews); experience the battlefield landscape as soldiers did; trailhead at battlefield; sunrise to sunset [Source: [AllTrails Stones River Loop](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/tennessee/stones-river-battlefield-loop-trail), [NPPlan Hiking Trails](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/tennessee/stones-river-national-battlefield-park-at-a-glance/stones-river-national-battlefield-hiking-trails/)]

- **McFadden Farm Artillery Trail** (30 minutes) - Walk the 0.5-mile northern loop trail through mowed grass paths around farm fields with historic replica cannons; site where Union artillery pushed back Gen. Breckinridge's advance; relatively flat with 29 feet elevation gain; 2600 Van Cleve Lane, Murfreesboro, TN 37129 (35.8630895, -86.4324672); sunrise to sunset [Source: [NPPlan Hiking Trails](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/tennessee/stones-river-national-battlefield-park-at-a-glance/stones-river-national-battlefield-hiking-trails/), [NPS Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/stri/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)]

- **Ranger-Led Programs and Living History Events** (varies) - Attend regularly scheduled ranger programs (May-October), artillery demonstrations, and living history encampments featuring Civil War-era weaponry and reenactments; monthly special interpretive events; check park calendar for specific dates and times; McFadden Farm (2600 Van Cleve Lane) and Visitor Center locations; 615-893-9501 [Source: [NPS Interpretive Programs](https://www.nps.gov/stri/planyourvisit/programs.htm), [NPS Living History](https://www.nps.gov/stri/planyourvisit/lh.htm), [Tennessee Vacation](https://www.tnvacation.com/local/murfreesboro-stones-river-national-battlefield)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Hallowed Ground Lantern Tour** (1 hour) - Experience the cemetery by lantern light with soldiers and civilians sharing their experiences through letters; deeply moving hour-long walk connecting personal stories to the battle; reservations required, no walk-ons permitted; special summer evening event; 3501 Old Nashville Highway, Murfreesboro, TN 37129 (35.881861, -86.434846); 615-893-9501 [Source: [American Battlefield Trust Lantern Tour](https://www.battlefields.org/events/hallowed-ground-lantern-tour-stones-river-national-cemetery-0), [NPS Interpretive Programs](https://www.nps.gov/stri/planyourvisit/programs.htm)]

- **Stones River Cedar Glade and Barrens** (30-45 minutes) - Explore the 185-acre natural area within the battlefield featuring federally endangered plant species including Pyne's ground plum and Tennessee purple coneflower; unique limestone glade ecosystem; accessible via trails within park; sunrise to sunset [Source: [Tennessee Natural Areas](https://www.tn.gov/environment/program-areas/na-natural-areas/natural-areas-middle-region/middle-region/na-na-stones-river-cedar-glade.html), [Tennessee Vacation](https://www.tnvacation.com/local/murfreesboro-stones-river-national-battlefield)]

- **Murfreesboro Greenway Connection** (varies) - Extend your visit with the 4.5-mile Murfreesboro Greenway that connects to battlefield trails; peaceful paved walking/biking path through natural areas; access from Tour Stop #5 or Visitor Center parking lot after tour road closes at 5:00 PM; sunrise to sunset [Source: [NPPlan Hiking Trails](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/tennessee/stones-river-national-battlefield-park-at-a-glance/stones-river-national-battlefield-hiking-trails/), [American Battlefield Trust](https://www.battlefields.org/visit/battlefields/stones-river-battlefield)]

- **163rd Battle Anniversary Commemoration** (multi-day, December 31-January 2 annually) - Attend the annual commemoration featuring real-time walks retracing battle events, Union artillery demonstrations at 11 AM, 1 PM, 2 PM, and 3 PM, ranger talks with soldier accounts, and living history events; immersive experience at McFadden Farm unit (2600 Van Cleve Lane, Murfreesboro, TN 37129 (35.8630895, -86.4324672)); 615-893-9501 [Source: [WGNS Radio 163rd Commemoration](https://www.wgnsradio.com/article/96195/163rd-commemoration-of-battle-of-stones-river-dec-31-2025-jan-2-2026), [NPS Ranger Programs](https://www.nps.gov/stri/planyourvisit/ranger-programs.htm)]

- **Arts in the Park Month** (October) - Experience special October programming featuring art exhibits, musical performances, and historical demonstrations blending culture with history; check park calendar for specific events; 3501 Old Nashville Highway, Murfreesboro, TN 37129 (35.881861, -86.434846); 615-893-9501 [Source: [NPS Interpretive Programs](https://www.nps.gov/stri/planyourvisit/programs.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Oaklands Mansion** (45 minutes-1 hour, 7 miles) - Tour this historic Italianate mansion on the National Register with self-guided tours of the home and grounds; nestled in Oaklands Park featuring native tree arboretum, natural wetlands, Maney Spring, and heritage gardens; 901 North Maney Avenue, Murfreesboro, TN 37130 (35.85421, -86.385458); Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM (last tour 3:00 PM), Sunday 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM, closed Mondays; Adults $15, AAA/AARP/Military $12, Students/Children $6; 615-893-0022 [Source: [Oaklands Mansion](https://www.oaklandsmansion.org/), [Oaklands Hours](https://www.oaklandsmansion.org/hours-admission/), [Tennessee Vacation](https://www.tnvacation.com/local/murfreesboro-oaklands-mansion)]

- **Cannonsburgh Village** (1-2 hours, 5 miles) - Explore this living history village representing 100 years of Tennessee life (1830s-1930s) with gristmill, schoolhouse, general store, blacksmith shop, Wedding Chapel, and the World's Largest Cedar Bucket; free self-guided tours with activity-loaded backpacks available; 312 S Front Street, Murfreesboro, TN; Summer (April 1-October 31): Tuesday-Friday 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM; Winter (November 1-March 31): Tuesday-Friday 9:00 AM - 3:00 PM; 615-890-0355 [Source: [Murfreesboro Cannonsburgh](https://www.murfreesborotn.gov/164/Cannonsburgh-Village), [Tennessee Vacation Cannonsburgh](https://www.tnvacation.com/local/murfreesboro-cannonsburgh-village)]

- **Discovery Center at Murfree Spring** (2-4 hours, 5 miles) - Experience hands-on STEAM education at this 32,000 sq ft interactive museum with exhibits on science, technology, engineering, arts, and math; features two-story slide, live animals, 20 acres of outdoor wetlands with boardwalk trails, and daily programs; 502 SE Broad Street, Murfreesboro, TN 37130 (35.8387063, -86.3871485); Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM - 5:00 PM, closed Mondays; General admission $8 (ages 2+); 615-890-2300 [Source: [Discovery Center](https://explorethedc.org/), [Tennessee Vacation Discovery](https://www.tnvacation.com/local/murfreesboro-discovery-center-murfree-spring)]

- **Nashville, TN** (30-45 minutes, 33 miles northwest) - Visit Tennessee's capital and country music hub featuring Country Music Hall of Fame and Museum, Ryman Auditorium, Grand Ole Opry, Parthenon replica, Fort Negley, Tennessee State Museum, and the Hermitage (Andrew Jackson's home); vibrant downtown with live music venues and dining; approximately 40 minutes via I-24 West [Source: [Rome2Rio Nashville-Murfreesboro](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Nashville/Murfreesboro), [Wanderlog Nashville-Murfreesboro](https://wanderlog.com/drive/between/58171/58521/nashville-to-murfreesboro-drive)]

- **Jack Daniel's Distillery** (45-60 minutes, 43 miles southeast) - Tour America's oldest registered distillery in historic Lynchburg, TN; guided tours of the distillery where Jack Daniel's Tennessee Whiskey has been made since 1866; 182 Lynchburg Highway, Lynchburg, TN 37352 (35.2851105, -86.3701765); Daily 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day [Source: [Jack Daniel's Visit](https://www.jackdaniels.com/en-us/visit-us), [Tennessee Vacation Jack Daniel](https://www.tnvacation.com/local/lynchburg-jack-daniel-distillery)]

- **Franklin, TN Historic District** (30 minutes, 20 miles northwest) - Explore this charming historic town between Nashville and Murfreesboro with Civil War sites including Lotz House and Carnton Plantation; downtown features boutique shopping, antique stores, and diverse dining; scenic views and cultural attractions; approximately 30 minutes north via I-65 [Source: [Wanderlog Nashville-Murfreesboro](https://wanderlog.com/drive/between/58171/58521/nashville-to-murfreesboro-drive), [Rome2Rio Nashville-Murfreesboro](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Nashville/Murfreesboro)]
