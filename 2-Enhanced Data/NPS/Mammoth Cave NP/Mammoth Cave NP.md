# Mammoth Cave NP (National Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Mammoth Cave Visitor Center** (1 Visitor Center Parkway, Mammoth Cave, KY 42259 (37.1862, -86.1004); Daily 8:00 AM - 6:30 PM summer, hours vary by season, closed Christmas Day; 270-758-2180) - National Park Service passport cancellation stamps available at visitor center. [Source: [Mammoth Cave Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/maca/planyourvisit/mammoth-cave-visitor-center.htm), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/mammoth-cave-national-park-mammoth-cave-2), [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/259008)]

**Note:** Call 270-758-2180 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. Mailing address: P.O. Box 7, Mammoth Cave, KY 42259-0007. Visitor center is departure point for all cave tours.

## Key Activities

- **Historic Cave Tour** (2 hours) - Explore classic 2-mile route through historic areas that originally made Mammoth Cave famous, visiting tunnels humans have used for thousands of years including Gothic Avenue, Bottomless Pit, and huge rooms; moderate difficulty with 540 total stairs including 155 at Mammoth Dome; advance reservations required; departs from visitor center; year-round; tour fees apply [Source: [Cave Tours](https://www.nps.gov/maca/planyourvisit/cave-tours.htm), [Historic Tour Details](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/event-details.htm?id=45AECE69-A523-CD36-FB8A9767F5A73EA6), [Earth Trekkers Guide](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/how-to-plan-your-visit-mammoth-cave-national-park/)]

- **Domes & Dripstones Tour** (2 hours) - Descend 280 stairs into sinkhole entrance, pass through massive domes and amazing breakdown, ending in spectacular Frozen Niagara dripstone section; difficult difficulty covering 0.75 miles with 640 total stairs; advance reservations required; departs from visitor center; year-round; tour fees apply [Source: [Cave Tours](https://www.nps.gov/maca/planyourvisit/cave-tours.htm), [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/how-to-plan-your-visit-mammoth-cave-national-park/), [Atlas Obsession](https://atlasobsession.com/mammoth-cave-national-park-which-tour-should-you-do/)]

- **Extended Historic Tour** (3-4 hours) - More comprehensive version of Historic Tour exploring additional passages and historic areas of the world's longest cave system; moderate to strenuous difficulty; advance reservations required; departs from visitor center; seasonal availability; tour fees apply [Source: [Recreation.gov Extended Historic](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/234640/ticket/1012), [Cave Tours](https://www.nps.gov/maca/planyourvisit/cave-tours.htm)]

- **Wild Cave Tour** (6 hours) - Most extreme and challenging tour at Mammoth Cave; all-day crawling adventure off the beaten path into labyrinthine depths focusing on cave exploration history; participants must be physically fit and comfortable in tight spaces; advance reservations required; departs from visitor center; seasonal; tour fees apply [Source: [Recreation.gov Wild Cave](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/234640/ticket/96), [National Park Magazine](https://nationalparkmagazine.com/2025/04/29/cave-tours-mammoth-cave-national-park-guide/), [Atlas Obsession](https://atlasobsession.com/mammoth-cave-national-park-which-tour-should-you-do/)]

- **Green River Bluffs Trail** (1-2 hours) - Hike 1.3-mile out-and-back trail along scenic Green River featuring river overlook and wildflowers; accessible from visitor center area; open during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [AllTrails Mammoth Cave](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/kentucky/mammoth-cave-national-park), [Cave Country Trails](https://cavecountrytrails.com/trails/green-river-bluffs-trail/), [Green River Bluff AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/kentucky/green-river-bluff-echo-river-springs-sinkhole-and-heritage-trail)]

- **River Styx Spring Trail** (45 minutes - 1 hour) - Easy 1.2-mile hike to cave where underground River Styx Spring emerges; beautiful destination showcasing connection between cave system and surface; accessible from visitor center area; open during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [Best Hiking at Mammoth Cave](https://www.itschrisandalex.com/the-best-hiking-at-mammoth-cave-national-park/), [Latino Heritage Guide](https://latinoheritageintern.org/visitor-guide-hiking-trails-at-mammoth-cave-national-park/)]

- **Green and Nolin Rivers Paddling** (2-6 hours) - Canoe or kayak more than 30 miles of rivers winding through the park; fish for bass, bluegill, perch, crappie and catfish; backcountry camping available along riverbanks; multiple access points; seasonal; bring own equipment or rent locally; FREE park access [Source: [Great American Hikes](https://www.greatamericanhikes.com/post/a-guide-to-cave-tours-in-mammoth-cave-national-park)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Cedar Sink Trail** (45-60 minutes) - Hike 1-mile lollipop loop offering sweeping views into massive Cedar Sinkhole sprouting wildflowers in spring, summer, and fall; less crowded than main trails; accessible from trails south of visitor center; open during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/kentucky/mammoth-cave-national-park), [Passport to Paradise](https://thepassporttoparadise.com/2024/03/things-to-do-in-mammoth-cave-national-park/), [Best Hikes North Carolina Go](https://northcarolinago.com/best-hikes-in-mammoth-cave-national-park/)]

- **Ranger-Led Stargazing Programs** (1.5-2 hours) - Join rangers for nighttime stargazing with park-provided telescopes viewing planets and constellations in dark sky environment; offered seasonally; check visitor center for schedule; 1 Visitor Center Parkway; evening hours; FREE [Source: [Passport to Paradise](https://thepassporttoparadise.com/2024/03/things-to-do-in-mammoth-cave-national-park/)]

- **Ranger-Led Hiking Programs** (1-3 hours) - Participate in variety of ranger-led hikes learning about area's natural and cultural history; topics and trails vary; offered throughout year; check visitor center for schedule; various trailheads; times vary; FREE [Source: [Passport to Paradise](https://thepassporttoparadise.com/2024/03/things-to-do-in-mammoth-cave-national-park/)]

- **Backcountry Camping Along Rivers** (overnight) - Camp at primitive sites along Green and Nolin Rivers accessible by paddling; experience true wilderness solitude; free backcountry permit required from visitor center; year-round; FREE permit [Source: [Great American Hikes](https://www.greatamericanhikes.com/post/a-guide-to-cave-tours-in-mammoth-cave-national-park)]

## Also Nearby

- **Bowling Green, Kentucky** (45 minutes - full day, 30 minutes/30 miles north) - Visit nearby city featuring National Corvette Museum showcasing America's sports car with over 70 Corvettes on display, plus downtown dining, shopping, and entertainment; Bowling Green, KY 42101; hours vary by attraction; museum admission charged [Source: [Visit BG KY Mammoth Cave](https://www.visitbgky.com/things-to-do/caves-nature/mammoth-cave-national-park/), [Passport to Paradise](https://thepassporttoparadise.com/2024/03/things-to-do-in-mammoth-cave-national-park/)]

- **Dinosaur World** (1-2 hours, 15 minutes from park) - Family-friendly outdoor dinosaur park featuring life-size dinosaur replicas, fossil dig, and museum; 711 Mammoth Cave Road, Cave City, KY 42127 (37.1343164, -85.9816705); hours vary; admission charged [Source: [Attractions Mammoth Cave Online](https://mammothcave.com/attractions/)]

- **Crystal Onyx Cave** (1 hour, nearby) - Tour privately-owned show cave offering different perspective on region's karst geology; Cave City, KY; hours vary; admission charged [Source: [Attractions Mammoth Cave Online](https://mammothcave.com/attractions/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time at Mammoth Cave NP (National Park):** Minimum 3-4 hours for single cave tour and visitor center; full day (6-8 hours) recommended for comprehensive visit with cave tour, hiking, and surface exploration; multi-day visit ideal for multiple cave tours, extensive hiking on 85+ miles of trails, and river paddling. Tours book quickly in summer - reserve in advance at Recreation.gov.
