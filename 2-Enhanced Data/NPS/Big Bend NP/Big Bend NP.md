## Key Activities

- **Lost Mine Trail** (3 hours) - One of the most popular moderate hikes in the park featuring stunning views of Casa Grande, Juniper Canyon, and the Chisos Basin; 4.8 miles round-trip with moderate difficulty. Accessed from Chisos Basin area. [Source: [NPS Day Hikes](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/day_hikes.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/texas/big-bend-national-park), [Hiking the World](https://hikingtheworld.blog/trip_reports/the-best-of-big-bend-texas/)]

- **Santa Elena Canyon Trail** (1.5 hours) - Explore one of the most scenic areas in the park with lush riparian vegetation and towering 1,500-foot vertical canyon walls along the Rio Grande; 1.7 miles round-trip, moderate difficulty. Located at the end of Ross Maxwell Scenic Drive. [Source: [NPS Day Hikes](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/day_hikes.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/texas/big-bend-national-park), [Sol Salute](https://solsalute.com/blog/the-best-hikes-in-big-bend-national-park/)]

- **Ross Maxwell Scenic Drive** (5-6 hours with stops) - A 30-mile scenic drive from Panther Junction Road to Santa Elena Canyon featuring numerous overlooks and short trails including Sotol Vista, Burro Mesa Pour-Off, and Tuff Canyon; allow half to full day depending on stops. [Source: [NPS Scenic Drives](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/ross-maxwell-scenic-drive.htm), [Balabanova All Over](https://balabanovaallover.com/ross-maxwell-scenic-drive-santa-elena-canyon/), [Road Trip Locals](https://www.roadtriplocals.com/blog/ross-maxwell-scenic-drive)]

- **Chisos Basin** (2-4 hours for area exploration) - Mountain area at 5,400 feet elevation offering cooler temperatures, hiking trails, visitor center, lodge, and campground; scenic 7-mile drive from main park road. [Source: [NPS Suggested Itineraries](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/suggested-itineraries.htm), [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/3-days-in-big-bend-itinerary/)]

- **Window Trail** (3-4 hours) - Popular moderate hike descending through desert landscape to a dramatic pour-off with views framed by rock formations; 5.6 miles round-trip. Accessed from Chisos Basin area. [Source: [NPS Day Hikes](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/day_hikes.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/texas/big-bend-national-park)]

- **South Rim Trail** (5-7 hours) - Strenuous full-day hike offering some of the best views in Big Bend; 10.4 miles round-trip with 2,440 feet elevation gain. One of the most rewarding ways to experience the park. Accessed from Chisos Basin. [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/texas/big-bend-national-park), [Hiking the World](https://hikingtheworld.blog/trip_reports/the-best-of-big-bend-texas/)]

- **Hot Springs Historic Trail** (1 hour) - Easy 1-mile round-trip walk to natural riverside rock pools with 105°F thermal spring water along the Rio Grande, plus historic ruins; located in eastern part of park near Rio Grande Village. [Source: [NPS Hot Springs](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/soakinthesprings.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/texas/big-bend-hot-springs-trail)]

- **Boquillas Canyon Trail** (1 hour) - Easy 1.5-mile round-trip hike along the Rio Grande to a beautiful canyon overlook; 150 feet elevation change. Located near Rio Grande Village in eastern part of park. [Source: [NPS River Hikes](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/river_hikes.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/texas/boquillas-canyon-trail)]

- **Stargazing at Dark Sky Park** (2-3 hours) - Big Bend is an International Dark Sky Park offering exceptional night sky viewing; best spots include Sotol Vista, Fossil Discovery Exhibit, and Rio Grande overlook. Give eyes 15-20 minutes to adjust; best viewing 2 hours after sunset or before sunrise during new moon. Peak Milky Way visibility March-October. [Source: [NPS Stargazing](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/stargazing.htm), [Summit Big Bend](https://summitbigbend.com/pages/stargazing-in-big-bend), [Dark Sky Reserve](https://www.bigbenddarkskyreserve.org/visiting)]

- **Fossil Discovery Exhibit** (30 minutes-1 hour) - Free outdoor self-guided exhibit showcasing Big Bend's 130-million-year fossil record; located 8 miles north of Panther Junction on Highway 385; open dawn to dusk daily. [Source: [NPS Fossil Exhibit](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/fossil-discovery-exhibit.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/texas/fossil-bone-exhibit-trail)]

- **Grapevine Hills Trail to Balanced Rock** (1-2 hours) - Moderate 2.2-mile round-trip hike through desert landscape to iconic balanced rock formations; requires high-clearance vehicle for rough access road. Located in northern part of park. [Source: [NPS Grapevine](https://www.nps.gov/places/balanced-rock-trail.htm), [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/balanced-rock-hike-big-bend-national-park/)]

- **Boquillas Crossing to Mexico** (3-4 hours) - Cross the Rio Grande by rowboat ($5 round-trip) to visit the village of Boquillas del Carmen, Mexico; enjoy authentic Mexican meals, handicraft shopping, and village exploration. Valid passport required. Open 9:00 AM-4:00 PM (Friday-Monday May-November; Wednesday-Sunday November-April). Horse/burro transportation available from landing to village ($5-8). [Source: [NPS Boquillas](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/visiting-boquillas.htm), [Visit Big Bend](https://visitbigbend.com/visit-boquillas/), [Sol Salute](https://solsalute.com/blog/boquillas-del-carmen-crossing-to-mexico-from-big-bend/)]

- **Dog Canyon Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - Easy 4.3-mile round-trip desert hike with 331 feet elevation gain; good half-day hike option through varied desert terrain. [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/texas/dog-canyon-trail), [Big Bend Guide](https://bigbendguide.com/inside-the-park/hikes/day-hikes/dog-canyon-trail/)]

- **Rio Grande Village Nature Trail** (30 minutes) - Short easy loop through riverside wetland habitat excellent for birdwatching; located at Rio Grande Village in eastern park. [Source: [NPS Day Hikes](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/day_hikes.htm)]

- **Tuff Canyon Trail** (20 minutes-1 hour) - Very short 0.5-mile round-trip with 3 viewing platforms overlooking volcanic canyon formations; located along Ross Maxwell Scenic Drive. [Source: [NPS Tuff Canyon](https://www.nps.gov/places/tuff-canyon-trail.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/texas/tuff-canyon-trail)]

- **Chihuahuan Desert Nature Trail** (30 minutes-1 hour) - Easy 0.5-mile interpretive trail introducing visitors to Chihuahuan Desert ecology and plant life; located near Dugout Wells. [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/texas/fossil-bone-exhibit-trail)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Ernst Tinaja Trail** (1-1.5 hours) - One of the least-visited trails in Big Bend featuring ancient limestone canyons and mysterious water-carved tinajas (natural water holes); 1.6 miles round-trip with 195 feet elevation gain, easy to moderate difficulty. Located along Old Ore Road in eastern park. Rock scrambling possible beyond main destination. [Source: [Texas Monthly](https://www.texasmonthly.com/travel/eight-great-hikes-off-beaten-path-big-bend/), [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/hike-ernst-tinaja-trail-big-bend-national-park/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/texas/ernst-tinaja-trail)]

- **Upper Burro Mesa Pouroff** (1.5-2 hours) - Hidden water-carved canyon ending in a giant sculpted pothole with vertical crack where water drops hundreds of feet; trail drops into Javelina Wash and narrows dramatically. Moderate difficulty. Located off Ross Maxwell Scenic Drive. [Source: [Texas Monthly](https://www.texasmonthly.com/travel/eight-great-hikes-off-beaten-path-big-bend/), [Texas Highways](https://texashighways.com/things-to-do/hiking-biking/where-to-find-4-hidden-gems-off-big-bends-beaten-path/)]

- **Hot Springs Canyon Trail** (3 hours) - Less-traveled alternative approach to the hot springs through staggering red mountains with views of Chisos and Del Carmen mountains and Rio Grande River below; 5.5 miles round-trip, moderate difficulty with 921 feet elevation gain. Alternative to shorter hot springs route. [Source: [Texas Monthly](https://www.texasmonthly.com/travel/eight-great-hikes-off-beaten-path-big-bend/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/texas/big-bend-hot-springs-trail)]

- **Marufo Vega Trail** (5-8 hours or overnight backpack) - Perhaps the most strenuous and remote day hike in Big Bend; 14-mile loop crossing exposed rocky terrain of canyons, cliffs and plateaus with 2,150 feet cumulative elevation gain. No shade or water sources. Can be done as overnight trip camping beside Rio Grande. For experienced hikers only. Located in eastern park. [Source: [NPS Marufo Vega](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/bc_marufovega.htm), [American Southwest](https://www.americansouthwest.net/texas/big_bend/marufo-vega-trail.html), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/texas/marufo-vega-trail)]

- **Old Maverick Road** (1 hour drive) - Less-traveled 14-mile improved dirt road through Terlingua Creek badlands connecting west entrance to Santa Elena Canyon; offers historic sites and scenic badlands views. Often impassable after rain. Most vehicles can navigate when dry but may be rough and washboarded. [Source: [NPS Old Maverick](https://www.nps.gov/places/old-maverick-road.htm), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g60733-i2791-k5289449-Time_to_drive_Old_Maverick_Road-Big_Bend_National_Park_Texas.html)]

- **River Road** (Full day, 4+ hours) - Remote 51-mile backcountry dirt road following the Rio Grande through stunning desert landscape; requires high-clearance vehicle, 4WD recommended for sandy/rocky sections. Often impassable after rain. Allow full day due to length and rough conditions. Primitive camping available along route. For adventurous visitors with proper vehicle and preparation. [Source: [NPS River Road](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/river-road.htm), [Driving Line](https://www.drivingline.com/articles/exploring-texas-off-road-on-big-bend-national-park-s-river-road/), [Wander-Filled Life](https://wanderfilledlife.com/driving-dirt-roads-big-bend/)]

- **Mule Ears Viewpoint** (Accessible by car) - Distinctive twin rock formations visible from various park locations; excellent for stargazing and photography. Located along road to Rio Grande Village. [Source: [NPS Mule Ears](https://www.nps.gov/places/mule-ears-overlook.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Terlingua Ghost Town** (30 minutes-2 hours; 10-15 minutes from park west entrance) - Historic mining ghost town featuring adobe and stone ruins, photogenic cemetery, Starlight Theatre restaurant with live music and Southwestern cuisine, Terlingua Trading Company gift shop, and art galleries. Mix of historic ruins and thriving artist community. Located in Terlingua, TX. [Source: [Visit Big Bend](https://visitbigbend.com/terlingua/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g56758-d105953-Reviews-Terlingua_Ghost_Town-Terlingua_Texas.html), [Discover Texas Outdoors](https://discovertexasoutdoors.com/places/terlingua-texas-ghost-town-adventure-near-big-bend/)]

- **Big Bend Ranch State Park** (Half to full day; adjacent to Big Bend NP, west side) - Texas' largest state park featuring Closed Canyon Trail (1.4 miles, 30 minutes), scenic FM 170 drive (designated by National Geographic as one of North America's most scenic drives), 70 miles of unmaintained roads, and 238 miles of multi-use trails for hiking, mountain biking, and horseback riding. Excellent stargazing. Located between Lajitas and Presidio, TX. [Source: [Texas Parks & Wildlife](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/big-bend-ranch), [TRAVEL WITH MIKE & DEE](https://deandrandmiketravel.com/a-guide-to-texas-most-remote-state-park-big-bend-ranch/)]

- **McDonald Observatory** (2-3 hours for tour; 112 miles north via Highway 118) - Major astronomical research facility offering guided tours, solar viewing, and star parties with large telescopes. Located atop Mount Locke near Fort Davis, TX. Approximately 2-hour drive from park. [Source: [McDonald Observatory](https://mcdonaldobservatory.org/visitors/directions), [Solo Trips and Tips](https://solotripsandtips.com/solo-road-trip-austin-west-texas/)]

- **Marfa, TX** (2-4 hours; 2 hours from Panther Junction) - Quirky art town featuring contemporary art installations, galleries, boutique shopping, and dining. Home to the famous Chinati Foundation art museum. Known for minimalist art and creative community. Located 2 hours northwest of park. [Source: [Big Bend Guide](https://bigbendguide.com/the-big-bend-area/marfa/), [Lost with Lydia](https://www.lostwithlydia.com/destinations/usa/tx/Marfa-Big-Bend-Road-trip/)]

- **Prada Marfa** (15-30 minutes; 35 miles west of Marfa) - Permanent art installation resembling a Prada boutique in the middle of the desert; popular photo opportunity along Highway 90. Located near Valentine, TX, accessible during road trip between Marfa and Big Bend. [Source: [Solo Trips and Tips](https://solotripsandtips.com/solo-road-trip-austin-west-texas/), [Campfires & Concierges](https://www.campfiresandconcierges.com/west-texas-road-trip/)]

- **Marathon, TX** (1-2 hours; 45 minutes to north park entrance) - Historic gateway town featuring the Gage Hotel and Gage Gardens, Brick Vault Brewery and Barbecue, V6 coffee shop, and essential services (gas, lodging). Closest town to north park entrance. [Source: [Visit Big Bend](https://visitbigbend.com/marathon/), [She Makes Her Own Adventure](https://shemakesherownadventure.com/2019/12/20/day-1-marathon-big-bend-terlingua/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 3 full days minimum (4 nights/3 days ideal) to experience the park's three distinct regions: Ross Maxwell Scenic Drive area (desert), Chisos Basin (mountains), and Rio Grande Village (river). More time allows for extensive hiking, backcountry exploration, and relaxation. [Source: [NPS Suggested Itineraries](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/suggested-itineraries.htm), [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/3-days-in-big-bend-itinerary/), [Brooke in Boots](https://brookeinboots.com/big-bend-itinerary/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g60733-i2791-k8825138-BBNP_how_many_days-Big_Bend_National_Park_Texas.html)]
