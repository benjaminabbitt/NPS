# Grand Portage NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Grand Portage National Monument Heritage Center** (170 Mile Creek Rd, Grand Portage, MN 55605 (47.9609724, -89.6857677) (47.961062775879, -89.686439037323); Heritage Center open year-round 9:00 AM-4:30 PM daily (starting 10/14); Historic Depot open 9:00 AM-5:00 PM daily from Memorial Day weekend through Columbus Day; (218) 475-0123) [Source: [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/grpo/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/grpo/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/grand-portage-national-monument-grand-portage-2), [GPS Coordinates](https://flatlong.com/grand-portage-national-monument-minnesota-grand-portage.html)]

**Note:** Stamp is located at the Heritage Center front desk. Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Heritage Center Exhibits** (30-60 minutes) - Opened in 2007, the Heritage Center features exhibit galleries about Ojibwe culture and the fur trade, a bookstore, multi-media programs, park offices, archives and classroom; watch a wonderful 20-minute video that explains the development of Grand Portage; interactive displays include collections of artifacts, art, and photographs from the trading era; 170 Mile Creek Rd, Grand Portage, MN 55605 (47.9609724, -89.6857677); open year-round 9:00 AM-4:30 PM daily (winter hours starting 10/14) [Source: [Heritage Center](https://www.nps.gov/grpo/planyourvisit/grand-portage-national-monument-heritage-center.htm), [From Two Hours to Two Days](https://www.nps.gov/tripideas/from-two-hours-to-two-days-at-grand-portage-national-monument.htm)]

- **Reconstructed North West Company Depot and Great Hall** (1-2 hours) - Explore three reconstructed post and sill log buildings (Great Hall, Kitchen, and Canoe Warehouse) and four outside areas including Ojibwe village, Voyageurs Encampment, dock and historic gardens; the Great Hall's four corner rooms are each decorated to represent different aspects of the fur trade, including a shareholder's bedroom, a trading post with examples of furs and goods that would have been exchanged, a London streetscape with finished goods, and a hatter's shop showing the process from beaver to fine hat; costumed interpreters in period costumes provide demonstrations and answer questions; 170 Mile Creek Rd, Grand Portage, MN 55605 (47.9609724, -89.6857677); open 9:00 AM-5:00 PM daily from Memorial Day through late October [Source: [Visit the Reconstructed Depot](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/visit-the-reconstructed-north-west-company-depot.htm), [Great Hall at Grand Portage](https://www.nps.gov/places/great-hall-at-grand-portage.htm), [Minnesota: Grand Portage](https://www.nps.gov/articles/grandport.htm)]

- **Mount Rose Trail** (1 hour round-trip) - One mile round trip trail (1-1.5 miles for the full loop) climbing 300 feet over stone steps with railings to the top of Mount Rose at 906 feet elevation; offers spectacular views of Grand Portage Bay, Lake Superior, and on clear days Isle Royale is visible; the trail is fairly steep but paved in many places with several sets of wooden stairs to help the ascent; trailhead located across Mile Creek Road from the historic North West Company Depot; open year-round (snowshoes recommended in winter) [Source: [Hike Grand Portage Trails](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hike-grand-portage-trails.htm), [Mount Rose – Superior Hiking](https://superiorhiking.com/mountrose/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/minnesota/mount-rose)]

- **Gichi Onigaming (Grand Portage) Trail to Fort Charlotte** (7 hours round-trip / 1-2 days with camping) - Historic 8.5-mile one-way trail (16.6 miles round-trip) extending from Lake Superior at the reconstructed Depot along a natural rift in the landscape to the Pigeon River at Fort Charlotte; gains about 1,000 feet elevation over the first 4 miles; this strenuous hike retraces the steps of voyageurs along the historic fur trade portage route; trails are steep and narrow in places with uneven, rocky footing and areas of standing water; shorter options available: from stockade to Old Highway 61 (8 miles round trip, 3-4 hours) or from Old Highway 61 to Fort Charlotte (9 miles round trip, 6-8 hours); backcountry camping available at Fort Charlotte with free permit (two tent platforms and canoe landing); trailhead located across Mile Creek Road from the historic depot; open year-round [Source: [Hike Gichi Onigaming](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hike-the-grand-portage.htm), [AllTrails Grand Portage](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/minnesota/the-grand-portage), [North Shore Visitor](https://northshorevisitor.com/things-todo/the-grand-portage/)]

- **Historic Heirloom Gardens** (30 minutes) - Visit the Three Sisters Garden (corn, squash, and beans) in the Anishinaabe Oodena and the Historic Kitchen Garden inside the palisade behind the kitchen; the kitchen garden is planted with a mix of Native American, European, and Asian vegetables and herbs including historic vegetables grown from heirloom seeds dating back to the fur trade era; main crop grown was potatoes, including the rare Cups Potato variety from the 1770s; gardens also feature sunflowers and asemaa (tobacco); located at the Historic Depot area; open seasonally May through October [Source: [Historic Gardens at Grand Portage](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/historic-gardens-at-grand-portage.htm), [Three Sisters Garden](https://www.nps.gov/places/three-sisters-garden-at-grand-portage-national-monument.htm)]

- **Ranger Programs and Demonstrations** (30-60 minutes) - Learn how to build a canoe, take a hike with a ranger, find out what is growing in the heirloom garden, and see what kind of food the men of the North West Company ate; staff available to answer questions and provide educational programs; schedule varies seasonally [Source: [Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/grpo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (1-2 hours) - Junior Rangers have fun discovering history while learning about why we need to protect the special treasures found in our National Parks; Junior Ranger books available for kids of all ages at the Heritage Center [Source: [Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/grpo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Mount Josephine Lookout Trail** (1.5-2 hours round-trip) - Moderate 2.5-mile out-and-back trail with 649 feet elevation gain; the trail starts relatively flat for the first half, then climbs nearly 700 feet in just 0.70 miles to the summit at 1,300 feet above sea level; offers the steepest climb and most dramatic views on the North Shore with breathtaking panoramic views of Lake Superior, Grand Portage Bay, Isle Royale, the Susie Islands, Pigeon Point, Canada to the north and Minnesota to the south; remains of an old tower foundation visible at the summit; trail is on land belonging to the Grand Portage Band of Lake Superior Chippewa who graciously allow public access; no water available along the trail [Source: [AllTrails Mount Josephine](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/minnesota/mount-josephine-lookout-tower-trail), [Best North Shore](https://bestnorthshore.com/amazing-mount-josephine/), [Superior Hiking](https://superiorhiking.com/mount-josephine/)]

- **Backcountry Camping at Fort Charlotte** (overnight experience) - Two remote backcountry campsites allow groups of nine; free permit available online at least two weeks in advance; experience camping where voyageurs from hundreds of years ago camped at the endpoint of the historic Grand Portage trail on the Pigeon River; tent platforms and canoe landing available; permits must be obtained at least two weeks in advance [Source: [Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/grpo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Plan Like a Park Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/top-10-tips-for-visiting-grand-portage-national-monument.htm)]

- **Pork Eater's Camp Picnic Area** (flexible) - Popular picnic area beside Lake Superior at the historic pork eater's camp; enjoy views of the bay while experiencing the shoreline where voyageurs once gathered; located near the Historic Depot area; accessible during Historic Site hours (Memorial Day weekend through Columbus Day) [Source: [Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/grpo/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Anishinaabemowin Language Learning** (self-guided) - The monument offers tips for pronunciation and recorded words in Anishinaabemowin (Ojibwe language) with English translation; unique opportunity to learn indigenous language connected to the site's cultural heritage; available at Heritage Center [Source: [Plan Like a Park Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/top-10-tips-for-visiting-grand-portage-national-monument.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Grand Portage State Park and High Falls** (15 minutes north, 1 hour visit) - Minnesota's tallest waterfall at 120 feet; gentle 0.5-mile (1 mile round-trip) paved and wheelchair-accessible trail from visitor center to the falls; spectacular year-round; additional 3.5-mile trail to Middle Falls (30-foot waterfall, 4.5 miles round-trip, moderately strenuous) with distant views of Lake Superior and Isle Royale from hilltops; located approximately 6 miles north of Grand Portage National Monument on Highway 61 near the Canadian border [Source: [Grand Portage State Park](https://www.exploreminnesota.com/profile/grand-portage-state-park/1909), [High Falls Loop](https://lakesuperiorcircletour.info/location/high-falls-and-middle-falls-loop/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Portage_State_Park)]

- **Isle Royale National Park Ferry** (2 hours each way, 4+ hours on island for day trips) - Take a ferry from Grand Portage to Isle Royale National Park, located only 22 miles offshore; Voyageur II operates May-September with 2-hour crossing to Windigo (departs Grand Portage 7:30 AM, arrives 9:30 AM); Sea Hunter III offers day trips June-August (departs 8:30 AM, returns 3:30 PM, allowing 4 hours on island for hiking, picnicking, and exploring); fall day trips available September weekends on Voyageur II (departs 8:00 AM, returns 1:00 PM); known as "Minong" by the Grand Portage Band of Ojibwe, this archipelago offers excellent hiking, fishing, kayaking, and wildlife viewing [Source: [Isle Royale Ferry Schedules](https://www.isleroyaleboats.com/schedules.html), [NPS Ferry Transportation](https://www.nps.gov/isro/planyourvisit/ferry-transportation-services.htm), [Visit Cook County](https://www.visitcookcounty.com/places-to-visit/grand-portage/)]

- **Judge C.R. Magney State Park and Devil's Kettle** (20 miles south, 2-3 hours visit) - Located along scenic Highway 61; features the mysterious Devil's Kettle waterfall on the Brule River; moderately challenging hike; second-to-last Minnesota State Park before the Canadian border; offers summer and winter outdoor activities including hiking and waterfall viewing [Source: [Devil's Kettle at Judge Magney](https://northshorevisitor.com/attractions/north-shore-state-parks/judge-magney/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judge_C._R._Magney_State_Park), [Superior Inn Grand Marais](https://superiorinngrandmarais.com/judge-magney-state-park/)]

- **Fort William Historical Park, Thunder Bay, Ontario** (36 miles / under 1 hour, 3-4 hours visit) - Canadian historical site containing reconstruction of the Fort William fur trade post as it existed in 1815; established in 1803 when the North West Company constructed a new fort replacing Grand Portage after it came under U.S. jurisdiction; guided tours last approximately 1.5 hours with additional time to explore after tour; English tours at 10:15 AM, 11:00 AM, 11:45 AM, 12:30 PM, 1:15 PM, 2:00 PM, 2:45 PM, 3:30 PM, and 4:00 PM; French tours at 11:30 AM; last admission at 4:00 PM; paid admission required; passport required for border crossing; King Road entrance [Source: [Fort William Hours & Admission](https://fwhp.ca/plan-your-visit/hours-admission/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_William_Historical_Park), [North Shore Visitor](https://northshorevisitor.com/communities/northeast-to-grand-portage/)]

- **Kakabeka Falls Provincial Park, Ontario** (under 1 hour from Grand Portage, 1-2 hours visit) - Ontario's second-highest waterfall at over 131 feet (40 meters) tall; known as the "Niagara of the North"; located 30 kilometers west of Thunder Bay off Highway 11/17; approximately 30 minutes from Thunder Bay and under one hour from the U.S. border crossing at Grand Portage; viewing areas and trails available; passport required for border crossing [Source: [Kakabeka Falls](https://www.destinationontario.com/en-ca/attractions/kakabeka-falls), [Tiny Footsteps Travel](https://www.tinyfootstepstravel.com/visiting-kakabeka-falls-everything-you-need-to-know-2023/)]

- **Grand Portage Rendezvous Days and Pow Wow** (second weekend of August, 2-3 days) - Annual event held in conjunction with the Grand Portage Pow Wow sponsored by the Grand Portage Band of Lake Superior Chippewa; American Indian cultural gathering focused on dance, song and family celebration; over 350 dancers in traditional regalia; mid-day Grand Entry Saturday and Sunday features U.S. flags raised to military veterans, staff bearers leading dancers into the Pow Wow ring; includes traditional foods, music, and craft stands; coincides with historic fur trade rendezvous re-enactment at the monument with costumed interpreters; free admission; no drugs, alcohol, or pets permitted on Pow Wow grounds; 2025 dates: August 8-12, 2025 at Grand Portage Monument Heritage Center [Source: [Rendezvous Days & Pow Wow](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/event-details.htm?id=A95269F7-9EB6-CF48-33892732FB9CD95D), [Visit Cook County](https://www.visitcookcounty.com/event/grand-portage-rendezvous-days-celebration-pow-wow/3552/), [Explore Minnesota](https://www.exploreminnesota.com/event/grand-portage-rendezvous-days-celebration-pow-wow/27639)]
