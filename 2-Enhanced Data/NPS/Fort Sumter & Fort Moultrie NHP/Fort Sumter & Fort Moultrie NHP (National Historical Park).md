## Key Activities

- **Fort Sumter Ferry Tour and Island Visit** (2.25 hours total) - Take a 30-minute ferry ride from Liberty Square in downtown Charleston or Patriots Point in Mount Pleasant to Fort Sumter island, where the Civil War began; enjoy 1 hour exploring the fort with ranger-led tours (10-15 minutes) and self-guided exploration of museum displays and historic fortifications; ferry returns after 1 hour on the island; 340 Concord Street, Charleston, SC 29401 or 40 Patriots Point Road, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464; daily departures, check [fortsumtertours.com](https://www.fortsumtertours.com/) for schedule [Source: [Fort Sumter Tours](https://www.fortsumtertours.com/tours/fort-sumter-tour/), [Visit Historic Charleston](https://www.visit-historic-charleston.com/fort-sumter.html), [TrolleyTours](https://www.trolleytours.com/charleston/fort-sumter)]

- **Fort Moultrie Self-Guided Tour** (1-2 hours) - Walk through 171 years of American seacoast defense history from 1776 to 1947, with five different historical eras preserved including the World War II Harbor Entrance Control Post and the site of the original 1776 palmetto-log fort; includes 22-minute orientation film at visitor center and museum exhibits; 1214 Middle Street, Sullivan's Island, SC 29482; daily 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fosu/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g54454-d143134-t6992672-How_long_should_you_plan_to_spend_at_Fort.html), [NPPlan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/south-carolina-national-parks/at-a-glance-fort-sumter-national-monument/touring-for-moultrie-fort-sumter-national-monument/)]

- **Fort Moultrie Ranger-Led Tour** (45 minutes) - Join a ranger for an in-depth guided tour of Fort Moultrie's defenses and history, learning how the fort evolved over nearly two centuries; no additional fee beyond entrance; 1214 Middle Street, Sullivan's Island, SC 29482; daily, check at visitor center for times [Source: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/fosu/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [Visit Historic Charleston](https://www.visit-historic-charleston.com/fort-moultrie.html)]

- **African Passages Exhibit** (30 minutes) - Explore the role Sullivan's Island played in the international slave trade through this powerful exhibit at Fort Moultrie; understand the historical significance of this entry point for enslaved Africans; 1214 Middle Street, Sullivan's Island, SC 29482; daily 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/fosu/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Battery Jasper Exploration** (30 minutes) - Visit this Endicott Program battery from 1898-1943 featuring 10-inch disappearing guns; one gun emplacement is open to the public showing the magazine, shell room, and gun deck; named for Sergeant William Jasper, hero of the 1776 attack; located immediately adjacent to Fort Moultrie; 1214 Middle Street, Sullivan's Island, SC 29482; daily 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM [Source: [NPS Battery Jasper](https://www.nps.gov/places/battery-jasper.htm), [Fort Moultrie Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Moultrie), [Friends CNP](https://www.friendscnp.org/fort-moultrie)]

- **Charleston Harbor Views** (15-30 minutes) - Enjoy panoramic views of Charleston Harbor from both Fort Sumter and Fort Moultrie, including views between the two forts; Fort Moultrie offers excellent distant views of Fort Sumter, and Fort Sumter provides scenic harbor and city views during the ferry ride; multiple vantage points at both locations [Source: [NPS Outdoor Activities](https://www.nps.gov/fosu/planyourvisit/outdooractivities.htm), [Tripadvisor Fort Moultrie](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g54454-d143134-Reviews-Fort_Moultrie-Sullivan_s_Island_South_Carolina.html)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Osceola's Burial Site** (15 minutes) - Visit the burial site of Osceola, one of the most noteworthy Seminole warriors who played a central role in the Second Seminole War (1835-1842); this lesser-known memorial at Fort Moultrie honors a significant figure in Native American resistance history; 1214 Middle Street, Sullivan's Island, SC 29482; daily 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM [Source: [Road Trips and Coffee](https://www.roadtripsandcoffee.com/fort-sumter-fort-moultrie-national-historical-park/), [Fort Moultrie NPS](https://www.nps.gov/fosu/learn/historyculture/fort_moultrie.htm)]

- **Sullivan's Island Beach Access** (1-2 hours) - Combine history with beach recreation on the beautiful 3-mile sandy beach at Sullivan's Island, just steps from Fort Moultrie; less commercial and crowded than other area beaches, offering spectacular Atlantic Ocean views; free beach access; Middle Street, Sullivan's Island, SC 29482; open year-round [Source: [Sullivan's Island.com](https://www.sullivansisland.com/fort-moultrie.html), [Charleston CVB](https://www.charlestoncvb.com/beaches/sullivans-island/), [Tripadvisor Sullivan's Island](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g54454-d143448-Reviews-Sullivan_s_Island_Beach-Sullivan_s_Island_South_Carolina.html)]

- **Edgar Allan Poe Connection** (included in fort visit) - Discover the literary connection at Fort Moultrie where Edgar Allan Poe was stationed from November 1827 to December 1828; he used Sullivan's Island as the setting for his famous short story "The Gold Bug"; interpretive displays share this unique historical detail; 1214 Middle Street, Sullivan's Island, SC 29482 [Source: [Sullivan's Island Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sullivan's_Island,_South_Carolina), [Visit Sullivan's Island](https://visitsullivansisland.com/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Patriots Point Naval & Maritime Museum** (2.5-3 hours, 10 minutes from downtown Charleston) - Explore the WWII aircraft carrier USS Yorktown, destroyer USS Laffey, and 27 historic aircraft; includes the Vietnam Experience, Medal of Honor Museum, and interactive exhibits; comprehensive maritime history museum across Charleston Harbor from downtown; 40 Patriots Point Road, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464 [32.7905, -79.9056]; daily 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, ticket office closes 4:00 PM; (843) 884-2727 [Source: [Patriots Point](https://www.patriotspoint.org/visit/first-time-visitors), [Patriots Point Hours](https://www.patriotspoint.org/visit/hours-operation)]

- **Charles Pinckney National Historic Site** (1-2 hours, 20 minutes from Fort Moultrie) - Explore Snee Farm, the plantation property of Founding Father Charles Pinckney who contributed to drafting the U.S. Constitution; features museum exhibits, park film, half-mile nature trail, and historic grounds with oak trees; free admission; note that the Visitor Center is closed until Spring 2025 but grounds remain open; 1254 Long Point Road, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464 [32.8470, -79.8234]; Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM, closed Monday-Tuesday and major holidays; (843) 577-0242 [Source: [NPS Charles Pinckney](https://www.nps.gov/chpi/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Tripadvisor Charles Pinckney](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g54354-d527414-Reviews-Charles_Pinckney_National_Historic_Site-Mount_Pleasant_South_Carolina.html)]

- **South Carolina Aquarium** (2-3 hours, adjacent to Fort Sumter ferry departure) - Discover nearly 5,000 amazing animals overlooking Charleston Harbor; features sick and injured sea turtles in rehabilitation at the Sea Turtle Care Center and two touch tanks with stingrays, sharks, horseshoe crabs, sea stars and urchins; waterfront views of Ravenel Bridge and Patriots Point; 100 Aquarium Wharf, Charleston, SC 29401; daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, last entry 3:30 PM, closed Thanksgiving and Christmas; (843) 577-3474 [Source: [SC Aquarium](https://scaquarium.org/), [Tripadvisor SC Aquarium](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g54171-d126553-Reviews-South_Carolina_Aquarium-Charleston_South_Carolina.html)]

- **Historic Downtown Charleston Walking Tour** (2 hours, 15 minutes from Fort Sumter ferry) - Explore Charleston's Historic District on foot through the French Quarter to White Point Gardens and the Battery; self-guided tours available or join guided tours departing from City Hall; discover antebellum architecture, historic churches, and centuries-old homes; downtown Charleston, SC 29401; various tour companies offer daily departures [Source: [Free Tours by Foot](https://freetoursbyfoot.com/historic-charleston-self-guided-tour/), [Walks of Charleston](https://walksofcharleston.com/tours/charleston-history-walking-tour/)]
