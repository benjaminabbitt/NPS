# American Veterans Disabled for Life Memorial

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Dwight D. Eisenhower Memorial Bookstore** (540 Independence Ave SW, Washington, DC 20024 (38.887486, -77.0192369); 10:00 AM-6:00 PM daily; call to confirm stamp availability)
- **Washington Monument Lodge** (15th Street NW, Washington, DC 20024 (38.8898328, -77.0329157); Sunday-Friday 8:45 AM-6:00 PM, Saturday 10:00 AM-6:00 PM; all National Mall area stamps available)

**Note:** Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- **Memorial Self-Guided Tour** (20-30 minutes) - Explore the nearly 2.5-acre memorial featuring a star-shaped fountain with ceremonial flame, 48 etched glass panels (each weighing 1,800 pounds) with veteran photographs and quotations, four bronze sculptures representing duty, injury, healing and renewal, white Vermont granite walls with Presidential inscriptions, and a triangular reflecting pool symbolizing how disabled veterans overcome obstacles; 150 Washington Avenue SW (at 2nd Street SW), Washington, DC 20001; open 24/7, not regularly staffed by rangers (check NPS website for ranger program schedule); Metro: Federal Center SW or Capitol South (Blue, Orange, Silver lines). [Source: [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/nama/planyourvisit/american-veterans-disabled-for-life.htm), [Washington.org](https://washington.org/dc-guide-to/american-veterans-disabled-life-memorial), [TripAdvisor reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g28970-d7191027-Reviews-The_American_Veterans_Disabled_For_Life_Memorial-Washington_DC_District_of_Columbi.html)]

- **Memorial Grove Walk** (10-15 minutes) - Stroll through the grove of 69 ginkgo trees and 23 cypress trees that form a backdrop to the memorial; the ginkgo trees turn brilliant gold around Veterans Day in November, creating a stunning seasonal display that symbolizes healing; same location and hours as main memorial. [Source: [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/nama/planyourvisit/american-veterans-disabled-for-life.htm), [ASLA](https://www.asla.org/2020awards/524.html)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Fountain Safety System Observation** (5 minutes) - Observe the ingeniously hidden pressure-plate safety detection system; the pentagonal granite platform surrounding the ceremonial flame is mounted over a sunken well containing a pressure-sensitive plate that triggers an automatic shutdown if anyone attempts to enter the star fountain; this safety feature is completely integrated into the design and invisible to most visitors; same location as main memorial. [Source: [SEGD](https://segd.org/projects/american-veterans-disabled-life-memorial/)]

- **Bartholdi Fountain Connection** (10-15 minutes) - Walk the accessible pathway connecting the memorial to adjacent Bartholdi Park to view the historic Bartholdi Fountain, a 30-foot-tall, 15-ton cast-iron "Fountain of Light and Water" created by Frederic Auguste Bartholdi (sculptor of the Statue of Liberty) for the 1876 Centennial Exposition; the fountain is illuminated at night and surrounded by a two-acre sustainable landscape garden; Bartholdi Park, adjacent to memorial at Independence Avenue and 1st Street SW; open dawn to dusk daily. [Source: [USBG](https://www.usbg.gov/gardens-plants/bartholdi-fountain-and-gardens), [Architect of the Capitol](https://www.aoc.gov/explore-capitol-campus/buildings-grounds/us-botanic-garden/bartholdi-park)]

## Also Nearby

- **United States Botanic Garden** (2 hours) - Explore one of the oldest botanic gardens in North America with a stunning conservatory featuring tropical, desert, and medicinal plant collections, plus the National Garden with regional Mid-Atlantic plants and a rose garden; located directly across the street from the memorial, making it an ideal companion visit; 100 Maryland Ave SW, Washington, DC 20001; Conservatory 10:00 AM-5:00 PM daily, National Garden 7:30 AM-5:00 PM daily (7:00 PM April 1-September 15); free admission; Metro: Federal Center SW. [Source: [USBG Hours](https://www.usbg.gov/visit/hours-and-location), [TripHobo](https://www.triphobo.com/places/washington-d-c-united-states/united-states-botanic-garden)]

- **U.S. Capitol Visitor Center Tours** (1 hour tour) - Take a free guided or self-guided tour of the U.S. Capitol including the Rotunda, National Statuary Hall, and the Crypt; the Capitol is visible from the memorial and within easy walking distance; First Street SE, Washington, DC 20515; open 8:30 AM-4:30 PM Monday-Saturday (last tour 3:20 PM), closed Sundays, Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day, and Inauguration Day; free admission, reservations recommended but same-day passes often available; fully accessible; Metro: Capitol South or Union Station. [Source: [Visit the Capitol](https://www.visitthecapitol.gov/visit/know-before-you-go/hours-info), [Washington.org](https://washington.org/DC-faqs-for-visitors/how-can-i-tour-capitol-how-can-i-see-congress-session)]

- **Library of Congress** (1.5-2 hours) - Visit the world's largest library and tour the stunning Thomas Jefferson Building featuring the breathtaking Great Hall with its ornate architecture, murals, and mosaics; view the Main Reading Room from the visitor gallery; located on Capitol Hill within 5 minutes walk of the memorial; 10 First Street SE, Washington, DC 20540; open Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM (last entry 4:30 PM), closed Sunday-Monday; free timed-entry tickets required (available online up to 30 days in advance, same-day tickets released at 9:00 AM); Metro: Capitol South. [Source: [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/visit/), [Washington.org](https://washington.org/visit-dc/library-of-congress-washington-dc)]

- **National Museum of the American Indian** (2-3 hours) - Explore exhibits celebrating indigenous cultures, art, and history from throughout the Americas at this architecturally distinctive Smithsonian museum; features the award-winning Mitsitam Café serving indigenous foods from various regions; located on the south side of the National Mall between the Air and Space Museum and the Botanic Garden; Third Street and Maryland Avenue SW, Washington, DC 20560; open daily 10:00 AM-5:30 PM (closed December 25); free admission, no tickets required; Metro: L'Enfant Plaza or Federal Center SW. [Source: [NMAI](https://americanindian.si.edu/visit/dc), [Washington.org](https://washington.org/find-dc-listings/national-museum-american-indian), [Kims History Travel](https://kimshistorytravel.com/national-museum-of-the-american-indian/)]
