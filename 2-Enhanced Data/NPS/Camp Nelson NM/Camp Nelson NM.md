# Camp Nelson NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-camp-nelson-national-monument-visitor-center"></a>**Camp Nelson National Monument Visitor Center** (6614 Old Danville Loop 2 Road, Nicholasville, KY 40356 (37.8936, -84.5958); Wednesday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed Monday-Tuesday starting September 2025; 859-881-5716) [Source: [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/cann/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Contact](https://www.nps.gov/cann/contacts.htm)]

**Note:** Call ahead to confirm current hours and stamp availability.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-watch-orientation-film"></a>**Watch Orientation Film** (17 minutes) - View the film about Camp Nelson's role as a Union supply depot and recruitment center for Black soldiers during the Civil War; shown in visitor center; 859-881-5716 [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/cann/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- <a id="activity-tour-reconstructed-barracks"></a>**Tour Reconstructed Barracks** (30-45 minutes) - Explore the reconstructed soldiers' barracks to understand living conditions for Union soldiers and refugees; located on monument grounds [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/cann/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Kentucky Travel](https://www.kentuckytourism.com/camp-nelson-national-monument)]

- <a id="activity-hike-the-trail-system"></a>**Hike the Trail System** (1-2 hours) - Walk through 5 miles of trails including interpretive paths through historic earthworks and fortifications [Source: [NPS Trails](https://www.nps.gov/cann/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/kentucky/camp-nelson-national-monument)]

- <a id="activity-join-junior-ranger-program"></a>**Join Junior Ranger Program** (30-60 minutes) - Complete activities to learn about Camp Nelson's Civil War history; pick up booklet at visitor center; free [Source: [NPS For Kids](https://www.nps.gov/cann/learn/kidsyouth/index.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-visit-camp-nelson-national-cemetery"></a>**Visit Camp Nelson National Cemetery** (30-45 minutes) - Pay respects at this historic cemetery located 1 mile south of visitor center containing graves of Civil War soldiers and refugees; 6980 Danville Road, Nicholasville, KY; open dawn to dusk [Source: [NPS Cemetery](https://www.nps.gov/cann/learn/historyculture/camp-nelson-national-cemetery.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-shaker-village-of-pleasant-hill"></a>**Shaker Village of Pleasant Hill** (2-3 hours, 15 miles northeast) - Tour restored Shaker community with 34 original buildings, demonstrations, and riverboat rides; 3501 Lexington Road, Harrodsburg, KY 40330; open daily 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM [Source: [Shaker Village](https://shakervillageky.org/)]

- <a id="nearby-kentucky-horse-park"></a>**Kentucky Horse Park** (2-3 hours, 25 miles north) - Experience equestrian heritage with horse shows, museum, and trail rides; 4089 Iron Works Parkway, Lexington, KY 40511; hours vary seasonally [Source: [Kentucky Horse Park](https://kyhorsepark.com/)]
