# Ulysses S Grant MEM (Memorial)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Washington Monument Lodge** (15th Street NW between Madison and Jefferson Drives, Washington, DC 20004 [38.8894796, -77.0333648]; Daily 8:45 AM - 5:00 PM; 202-426-6841)

**Note:** The Ulysses S Grant Memorial itself does not have a visitor center or stamp location on-site. The memorial is part of National Mall and Memorial Parks, and passport cancellation stamps for National Mall memorials are available at the Washington Monument Lodge, located on 15th Street between Madison and Jefferson drives at the eastern base of the Washington Monument. Multiple stamps for various National Mall sites can be obtained at this centralized location. Call ahead to confirm current stamp availability. [Source: [NPS National Mall Passport Stamps](https://www.nps.gov/nama/learn/https-www-nps-gov-nama-learn-where-can-you-get-passport-stamps-htm.htm), [TripAdvisor NPS Stamps Discussion](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g28970-i40-k11696057-NPS_Cancellation_Stamps-Washington_DC_District_of_Columbia.html), [Calgary Playground Review](https://calgaryplaygroundreview.com/us-national-parks-and-memorials-at-the-national-mall-washington-dc/)]

## Key Activities

- **View the Central Grant Equestrian Statue** (15-20 minutes) - The memorial's centerpiece features General Ulysses S. Grant mounted on his horse Cincinnati, standing at two and a half times life size atop a 22-foot-high marble pedestal. Grant faces west, overlooking the Capitol Reflecting Pool toward the Lincoln Memorial, symbolically linking the President and General who together steered the nation through the Civil War; Union Square, 1st Street NW/SW between Pennsylvania Avenue and Maryland Avenue, Washington, DC 20004 [38.889776, -77.012924]; Open 24/7; Free admission. [Source: [Architect of the Capitol](https://www.aoc.gov/explore-capitol-campus/features/ulysses-s-grant-memorial), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulysses_S._Grant_Memorial), [Smarthistory](https://smarthistory.org/seeing-america-2/ulysses-s-grant-memorial-sa/)]

- **Examine the Cavalry Group** (10-15 minutes) - Located on the north side of the memorial, this dramatic bronze sculpture depicts seven cavalrymen charging into battle with the commanding officer, sword drawn, leading the charge. The sculpture powerfully captures the chaos of battle, with one horse already fallen and pinning its rider beneath, modeled after sculptor Henry Merwin Shrady himself, moments from being trampled by onrushing horses; Union Square, 1st Street NW/SW between Pennsylvania Avenue and Maryland Avenue, Washington, DC 20004 [38.889776, -77.012924]; Open 24/7; Free admission. [Source: [DC Historic Sites](https://historicsites.dcpreservation.org/items/show/260), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulysses_S._Grant_Memorial), [Gettysburg Daily](https://www.gettysburgdaily.com/washington-dc-ulysses-s-grant-memorial/)]

- **Study the Artillery Group** (10-15 minutes) - The south-side sculpture shows three horses pulling a caisson carrying three artillerymen as they struggle to maneuver their cannon into position. The lead horse rears uncontrollably due to a broken bridle, while the guidon carrier astride the left horse signals a sharp right wheel, creating a vivid scene of the difficulties faced by artillery units in combat; Union Square, 1st Street NW/SW between Pennsylvania Avenue and Maryland Avenue, Washington, DC 20004 [38.889776, -77.012924]; Open 24/7; Free admission. [Source: [Architect of the Capitol](https://www.aoc.gov/explore-capitol-campus/features/ulysses-s-grant-memorial), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulysses_S._Grant_Memorial), [Military Wiki](https://military-history.fandom.com/wiki/Ulysses_S._Grant_Memorial)]

- **Walk Around the Capitol Reflecting Pool** (15-20 minutes) - The six-acre Capitol Reflecting Pool stretches between the Grant Memorial and the Washington Monument, offering stunning reflections of the U.S. Capitol Building. The broad, gently sloped limestone coping and steps provide excellent seating for visitors to enjoy the views and photo opportunities, particularly beautiful during sunset when reflections are at their brightest; Union Square, 1st Street NW/SW between Pennsylvania Avenue and Maryland Avenue, Washington, DC 20004 [38.889776, -77.012924]; Open 24/7; Free admission. [Source: [Architect of the Capitol - Capitol Reflecting Pool](https://www.aoc.gov/explore-capitol-campus/buildings-grounds/capitol-grounds/reflecting-pool), [TripAdvisor - Capitol Reflecting Pool](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g28970-d15264555-Reviews-Capitol_Reflecting_Pool-Washington_DC_District_of_Columbia.html)]

- **Photography of the Memorial** (10-15 minutes) - At 252 feet long by 71 feet wide by 44 feet high, this is the largest equestrian monument in the United States, offering exceptional photo opportunities from multiple angles. The memorial provides excellent views looking both directions along the National Mall. Sunset photography is particularly stunning when the memorial is silhouetted against the Capitol Building. Check the intricate details on the horse-drawn artillery statues on the south end, which are among the most detailed and overlooked statues in DC; Union Square, 1st Street NW/SW between Pennsylvania Avenue and Maryland Avenue, Washington, DC 20004 [38.889776, -77.012924]; Open 24/7; Free admission. [Source: [Foursquare](https://foursquare.com/v/ulysses-s-grant-memorial/4bb64dd9f562ef3b7ef22f97), [iStock Photo Description](https://www.istockphoto.com/photo/the-ulysses-s-grant-memorial-located-in-front-of-the-united-states-capitol-on-the-gm871679676-145641703), [O'Reilly - Photographing Washington DC](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/photographing-washington-dc/9780470586877/ch19.html)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Bronze Relief Panels on Grant's Pedestal** (5-10 minutes) - While most visitors focus on the dramatic cavalry and artillery groups, Grant's central pedestal is decorated with bronze reliefs depicting Union infantry soldiers. These detailed panels showcase the common foot soldiers of the Grand Army of the Republic and are often overlooked by visitors rushing between the larger sculptural groups; Union Square, 1st Street NW/SW between Pennsylvania Avenue and Maryland Avenue, Washington, DC 20004 [38.889776, -77.012924]; Open 24/7; Free admission. [Source: [DC Historic Sites](https://historicsites.dcpreservation.org/items/show/260), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulysses_S._Grant_Memorial)]

- **Protective Lion Statues** (5 minutes) - Flanking pedestals on either side of Grant's statue hold bronze statues of protective lions, symbolizing strength and guardianship. These sculptural elements are frequently missed by visitors focused on the more dramatic battle scenes but add important symbolic depth to the memorial's design; Union Square, 1st Street NW/SW between Pennsylvania Avenue and Maryland Avenue, Washington, DC 20004 [38.889776, -77.012924]; Open 24/7; Free admission. [Source: [DC Historic Sites](https://historicsites.dcpreservation.org/items/show/260), [Architect of the Capitol](https://www.aoc.gov/explore-capitol-campus/features/ulysses-s-grant-memorial)]

- **Early Morning Solitude** (30 minutes) - Visit early in the morning before tourist crowds arrive for a peaceful experience of the memorial. The quieter atmosphere allows for contemplative viewing of the sculptures and better photography without crowds. Early morning light also provides different photographic opportunities than the more popular sunset visits; Union Square, 1st Street NW/SW between Pennsylvania Avenue and Maryland Avenue, Washington, DC 20004 [38.889776, -77.012924]; Open 24/7; Free admission. [Source: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g28970-d3478443-Reviews-Ulysses_S_Grant_Memorial-Washington_DC_District_of_Columbia.html), [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/usa/washington-dc/national-mall/attractions/ulysses-s-grant-memorial/a/poi-sig/1357794/1329647)]

## Also Nearby

- **United States Capitol Building Tours** (2-3 hours, 0.2 miles) - The U.S. Capitol is located directly east of the Grant Memorial at the top of Capitol Hill. Free guided tours showcase the Rotunda, National Statuary Hall, and the Crypt. Tours must be reserved in advance through the Capitol Visitor Center website; U.S. Capitol Visitor Center, First Street NE, Washington, DC 20515; Monday-Saturday 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM, closed Sundays and federal holidays; Free admission with advance reservation. [Source: [U.S. Capitol Visitor Center](https://www.visitthecapitol.gov/visit/book-a-tour), [U.S. News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/Washington_DC/Things_To_Do/U_S_Capitol_19641/)]

- **Library of Congress Jefferson Building** (1.5-2 hours, 0.3 miles) - The world's largest library houses over 164 million items including books, manuscripts, maps, and photographs. The Thomas Jefferson Building features stunning architecture with the Great Hall and Main Reading Room. Self-guided visits allow exploration of public spaces; volunteers throughout the building provide information and stories; 10 First Street SE, Washington, DC 20540 [38.8889, -77.0047]; Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM (last entry 4:30 PM), late hours Thursday 5:00 PM - 8:00 PM; Free timed-entry tickets required, available online 30 days in advance or same-day at 9:00 AM. [Source: [Library of Congress Visit](https://www.loc.gov/visit/), [Washington.org LOC Guide](https://washington.org/visit-dc/library-of-congress-washington-dc), [Free Tours by Foot](https://freetoursbyfoot.com/library-of-congress-tours/)]

- **United States Botanic Garden** (1-1.5 hours, 0.3 miles) - Located at the southwest corner of the Capitol grounds, this living plant museum houses 4,000 species in its conservatory and outdoor gardens. The facility includes tropical, desert, and orchid collections plus seasonal displays. The Bartholdi Fountain and outdoor gardens provide additional viewing opportunities; 100 Maryland Ave SW, Washington, DC 20001 [38.8880, -77.0129]; Conservatory: Daily 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Bartholdi Fountain and Gardens: Dawn to dusk; Free admission, no tickets required. [Source: [USBG Visit Page](https://www.usbg.gov/visit/hours-and-location), [Washington.org USBG](https://washington.org/find-dc-listings/united-states-botanic-garden), [Free Tours by Foot](https://freetoursbyfoot.com/guide-to-visiting-the-united-states-botanic-garden/)]

- **Supreme Court of the United States** (1 hour, 0.4 miles) - The Supreme Court building is located directly across First Street from the Library of Congress. While guided tours are not offered, visitors can explore public spaces including the Great Hall and courtroom at their own pace. When court is in session, visitors can attend oral arguments or docent lectures; First Street NE between East Capitol Street and Maryland Avenue, Washington, DC 20543 [38.8906, -77.0047]; Monday-Friday 9:00 AM - 3:00 PM, closed weekends and federal holidays; Free admission. [Source: [Supreme Court Visiting](https://www.supremecourt.gov/visiting/visiting.aspx), [Supreme Court Hours](https://www.supremecourt.gov/visiting/hoursanddirections.aspx), [Free Tours by Foot](https://freetoursbyfoot.com/supreme-court/)]

- **National Gallery of Art** (2-3 hours, 0.8 miles) - One of the world's premier art museums features works from the Middle Ages to modern times across two buildings connected by an underground passage. The collection includes masterpieces by da Vinci, Rembrandt, Monet, and American artists. The Sculpture Garden between the two buildings features outdoor sculptures and a fountain that becomes an ice rink in winter; Constitution Avenue NW between Third and Ninth Streets, Washington, DC 20565 [38.8913, -77.0197]; Daily 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed December 25 and January 1; Free admission, no tickets required. [Source: [National Gallery Visit](https://www.nga.gov/visit), [Washington.org NGA](https://washington.org/visit-dc/national-gallery-of-art-washington-dc)]

- **National Mall Walking Tour** (2-4 hours, 2 miles) - A self-guided or guided walking tour westward from the Grant Memorial along the National Mall covers major memorials including the Washington Monument, World War II Memorial, Lincoln Memorial, Vietnam Veterans Memorial, and Korean War Veterans Memorial. The full circuit covers approximately 3 miles at a casual pace on flat, paved paths; National Mall from U.S. Capitol to Lincoln Memorial, Washington, DC 20024; Open 24/7; Free access. [Source: [Moon Travel Guides](https://www.moon.com/travel/outdoors/walking-dc-national-mall-memorials/), [TripAdvisor Discussion](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g28970-i40-k3784818-How_long_will_it_take_to_do_all_the_Monuments_memorials-Washington_DC_District_of_Columbia.html), [DC Design Tours](https://dcdesigntours.com/walking-tours/national-mall-monuments-memorials-tour/)]

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 1-1.5 hours for a complete visit to the Ulysses S Grant Memorial including viewing all three major sculptural groups, walking around the Capitol Reflecting Pool, and photography. Most visitors spend 15-30 minutes for a basic visit, while those interested in closely examining the detailed sculptural work and enjoying the setting will want to allow a full hour to 90 minutes.
