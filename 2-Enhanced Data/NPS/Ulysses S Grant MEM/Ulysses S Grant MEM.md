# Ulysses S Grant MEM (Ulysses S. Grant Memorial)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations {#stamps}

- <span id="stamp-eisenhower">**Dwight D. Eisenhower Memorial Bookstore**</span> (540 Independence Ave SW, Washington, DC 20024 (38.887486, -77.019237); Daily 10:00 AM-6:00 PM; National Capital Regional Stamp available) [[NPS DDEM Bookstore](https://www.nps.gov/ddem/learn/bookstore.htm), [NPS DDEM Hours](https://www.nps.gov/ddem/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

**Note:** The Ulysses S. Grant Memorial is an outdoor monument on the Capitol grounds and does not have its own visitor center. The nearest NPS passport stamp location is at the Dwight D. Eisenhower Memorial Bookstore, located across from the National Air and Space Museum. Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities {#key-activities}

- <span id="activity-memorial">**View the Memorial Sculptures**</span> (20-30 minutes) - Observe the largest equestrian monument in the United States, featuring the central bronze sculpture of General Grant on horseback (5.2 meters tall) flanked by dramatic cavalry and artillery groups, with four guardian lions marking the corners; Union Square, 1st Street NW/SW at the base of Capitol Hill, Washington, DC 20004 (38.889776, -77.012924); Accessible 24/7 (outdoor monument) [[TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g28970-d3478443-Reviews-Ulysses_S_Grant_Memorial-Washington_DC_District_of_Columbia.html), [Architect of the Capitol](https://www.aoc.gov/explore-capitol-campus/features/ulysses-s-grant-memorial)]

- <span id="activity-sculptures">**Examine Detailed Bronze Sculptures**</span> (15-20 minutes) - Study the intricate bronze work including the Artillery Group with its broken bridle strap detail showing battle chaos, and the Cavalry Group featuring sculptor Henry Merwin Shrady's self-portrait as a fallen rider about to be trampled; Union Square, 1st Street NW/SW, Washington, DC 20004 (38.889776, -77.012924); Accessible 24/7 [[Smarthistory](https://smarthistory.org/shrady-and-casey-ulysses-s-grant-memorial/), [Architect of the Capitol](https://www.aoc.gov/explore-capitol-campus/features/ulysses-s-grant-memorial)]

- <span id="activity-photography">**Photography at Capitol Reflecting Pool**</span> (15-30 minutes) - Capture photos of the memorial with the U.S. Capitol as backdrop, especially beautiful during late afternoon and early evening when sunlight illuminates the Capitol's West Façade and creates reflection opportunities in the pool; Union Square at Capitol Reflecting Pool, Washington, DC 20004 (38.889776, -77.012924); Accessible 24/7; best lighting late afternoon to sunset [[Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/usa/washington-dc/national-mall/attractions/ulysses-s-grant-memorial/a/poi-sig/1357794/1329647), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g28970-d3478443-Reviews-Ulysses_S_Grant_Memorial-Washington_DC_District_of_Columbia.html)]

- <span id="activity-capitol-grounds">**Explore U.S. Capitol Grounds**</span> (30-60 minutes) - Walk the surrounding Capitol grounds to view other monuments and the historic landscape architecture while enjoying views of the Capitol building; U.S. Capitol, First Street and East Capitol Street, Washington, DC 20515 (38.889931, -77.009003); Grounds accessible 24/7 (building tours require advance reservations) [[Visit the Capitol](https://www.visitthecapitol.gov/), [washington.org](https://washington.org/find-dc-listings/us-capitol-capitol-visitor-center)]

## Hidden Gems {#hidden-gems}

- <span id="gem-shrady-portrait">**Shrady's Self-Portrait in the Cavalry Group**</span> (5-10 minutes) - Discover sculptor Henry Merwin Shrady's hidden self-portrait as the fallen cavalry rider on the right side of the Cavalry Group, depicted moments before being trampled by onrushing horses—a remarkable personal touch in this monumental work; Union Square, north side of memorial, Washington, DC 20004 (38.889776, -77.012924); Accessible 24/7 [[Smarthistory](https://smarthistory.org/shrady-and-casey-ulysses-s-grant-memorial/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulysses_S._Grant_Memorial)]

- <span id="gem-broken-strap">**Artillery Group's Broken Bridle Detail**</span> (5-10 minutes) - Look for the subtle broken strap on the right bridle bit in the Artillery Group, showing how the horse continues lunging forward despite loss of control—a powerful artistic representation of battle chaos; Union Square, south side of memorial, Washington, DC 20004 (38.889776, -77.012924); Accessible 24/7 [[Architect of the Capitol](https://www.aoc.gov/explore-capitol-campus/features/ulysses-s-grant-memorial)]

- <span id="gem-grant-demeanor">**Grant's Calm Amidst Chaos**</span> (5-10 minutes) - Notice the striking contrast between Grant's serene, almost disaffected expression on the central statue and the violent cavalry and artillery battles raging on either side—reflecting his historical reputation for calmness under fire; Union Square, central statue, Washington, DC 20004 (38.889776, -77.012924); Accessible 24/7 [[Architect of the Capitol](https://www.aoc.gov/explore-capitol-campus/features/ulysses-s-grant-memorial), [DC Historic Sites](https://historicsites.dcpreservation.org/items/show/260)]

- <span id="gem-conservation">**Recent Conservation Work**</span> (5 minutes) - Observe the results of the 2015-2016 conservation project that restored over 150 castings of missing pieces and removed green corrosion to return the bronze to its original brown color, completed again in 2023; Union Square, Washington, DC 20004 (38.889776, -77.012924); Accessible 24/7 [[EverGreene](https://evergreene.com/projects/ulysses-grant-memorial/), [Architect of the Capitol Conservation](https://www.aoc.gov/what-we-do/projects/grant-memorial-conservation)]

## Also Nearby {#also-nearby}

- <span id="nearby-botanic">**United States Botanic Garden**</span> (1-2 hours, 2 blocks/5-minute walk) - Visit one of the oldest botanical gardens in America (founded 1820), featuring a living plant museum with more than 65,000 plants from around the world in indoor conservatory and outdoor gardens; 100 Maryland Ave SW, Washington, DC 20001 (38.888267, -77.013032); Daily 10:00 AM-5:00 PM; 202-225-1281; Free admission [[USBG Hours](https://www.usbg.gov/visit/hours-and-location), [USBG Visit](https://www.usbg.gov/visit)]

- <span id="nearby-air-space">**Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum**</span> (2-4 hours, 3 blocks/7-minute walk) - Explore the world's largest collection of aviation and space artifacts including the Wright brothers' plane and Apollo 11 command module; 600 Independence Ave SW, Washington, DC 20560 (38.888160, -77.019868); Daily 10:00 AM-5:30 PM; Free admission (timed entry passes required) [[Smithsonian](https://airandspace.si.edu/)]

- <span id="nearby-national-gallery">**National Gallery of Art**</span> (2-4 hours, 4 blocks/10-minute walk) - View masterpieces spanning the history of Western art from the 13th century to the present in the West Building (classic art) and East Building (modern art); Constitution Ave NW & 6th Street NW, Washington, DC 20565 (38.891298, -77.019965); Monday-Saturday 10:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday 11:00 AM-6:00 PM; 202-737-4215; Free admission [[National Gallery](https://www.nga.gov/)]

- <span id="nearby-eisenhower">**Dwight D. Eisenhower Memorial**</span> (30-45 minutes, 2 blocks/5-minute walk) - Visit the memorial honoring the 34th President and Supreme Commander of Allied Forces in Europe during WWII, opened in 2020 with striking bronze sculptures and columned design; Independence Ave SW across from National Air and Space Museum, Washington, DC 20024 (38.887486, -77.019237); Open 24 hours (rangers on-site 9:30 AM-5:00 PM); Free admission [[NPS DDEM](https://www.nps.gov/ddem/), [NPS DDEM Hours](https://www.nps.gov/ddem/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]
