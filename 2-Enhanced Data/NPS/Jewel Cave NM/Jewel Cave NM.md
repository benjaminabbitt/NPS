# Jewel Cave NM (National Monument)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-visitor-center"></a>**Jewel Cave Visitor Center** (11149 U.S. Highway 16 Building B12, Custer, SD 57730 (43.730359, -103.829051); Summer Season (May 25 - September): 8:00 AM - 5:30 PM MT Monday-Sunday; Winter Season (September - May): 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM MT Wednesday-Saturday; Closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 605-673-8300) - Passport cancellation stamps are available at the visitor center during operating hours. The visitor center features an information desk, park store operated by the Black Hills Parks and Forests Association, drinking fountain, restrooms, a theater for the park film, and elevators. [Source: [Operating Hours & Seasons - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/jeca/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Visitor Center - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/jewel-cave-visitor-center.htm), [Basic Information - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/jeca/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Passport Cancellation Locations](https://americasnationalparks.org/passport-to-your-national-parks/passport-cancellation-locations/)]

**Note:** Call 605-673-8300 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-scenic-tour"></a>**Scenic Tour** (1 hour 20 minutes) - The most popular way to explore Jewel Cave, this moderately strenuous ranger-guided tour features a 1/2-mile (0.8 km) loop through the cave with 734 stair steps (equivalent to 40 flights of stairs). The cave maintains a constant temperature of 49°F (9°C) year-round. Visitors should wear long pants and a sweatshirt, sweater, or light jacket. Advance reservations highly recommended through Recreation.gov (available 30 days in advance at 10 a.m. ET on a rolling daily window). Some same-day tickets may be available on a first-come, first-served basis if staffing allows. Visitors should arrive at least 30 minutes before tour time. Jewel Cave Visitor Center, 11149 U.S. Highway 16 Building B12, Custer, SD 57730 (43.730359, -103.829051); Tour times vary by season; 605-673-8300. [Source: [Scenic Tour - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/jeca/planyourvisit/scenictour.htm), [Guided Tours - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/jeca/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [Scenic Tour - Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/10088103/ticket/10088104)]

- <a id="activity-discovery-tour"></a>**Discovery Tour** (20-25 minutes) - An introductory wheelchair-accessible guided tour featuring a ranger presentation in the first room of the cave. Visitors view beautiful calcite spars from different platforms within the large "Target Room," learning about the cave's natural and cultural histories. This is the shortest and most accessible cave tour option. Children 15 years and younger must be accompanied by an adult. Advance reservations recommended through Recreation.gov. Some same-day tickets may be available on a first-come, first-served basis if staffing allows. Jewel Cave Visitor Center, 11149 U.S. Highway 16 Building B12, Custer, SD 57730 (43.730359, -103.829051); Tour times vary by season; 605-673-8300. [Source: [Discovery Tour - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/jeca/planyourvisit/discoverytour.htm), [Guided Tours - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/jeca/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [Discovery Tour - Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/10088103/ticket/10088105)]

- <a id="activity-historic-lantern-tour"></a>**Historic Lantern Tour** (1 hour 45 minutes) - A strenuous guided tour covering approximately 1/2 mile through low, narrow passages with roughly 500 steep wooden ladder-like stairs. The only light is provided by lanterns that most tour participants carry, recreating the experience of early cave explorers. This tour offers a more challenging and immersive cave experience. Reservations highly recommended through Recreation.gov. During summer months, Historic Lantern Tours may also be available on a same-day basis if staffing allows. Jewel Cave Visitor Center, 11149 U.S. Highway 16 Building B12, Custer, SD 57730 (43.730359, -103.829051); Tour times vary by season; 605-673-8300. [Source: [Historic Lantern Tour - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/jeca/planyourvisit/lanterntour.htm), [Guided Tours - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/jeca/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)]

- <a id="activity-visitor-center-exhibits"></a>**Visitor Center Exhibits and Film** (45-60 minutes) - Explore two rooms of interactive exhibits and engaging displays highlighting surface and sub-surface resources of Jewel Cave National Monument. Watch the 23-minute park film in the theater to learn about the cave's formation and history. The visitor center features beautiful formations that give Jewel Cave its name, along with exhibits about the cave's geology and discovery. Free with park admission. 11149 U.S. Highway 16 Building B12, Custer, SD 57730 (43.730359, -103.829051); Summer Season (May 25 - September): 8:00 AM - 5:30 PM MT Monday-Sunday; Winter Season: 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM MT Wednesday-Saturday; 605-673-8300. [Source: [Visitor Center - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/jewel-cave-visitor-center.htm), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60865-d143180-Reviews-Jewel_Cave_National_Monument-Custer_South_Dakota.html)]

- <a id="activity-roof-trail"></a>**Roof Trail** (30 minutes) - A short, easy 1/4-mile (0.4 km) loop trail meandering through a ponderosa pine forest above the cave. An overlook along the trail provides views of the surrounding canyons and Black Hills landscape. This self-guided trail is accessible year-round and does not require a reservation. Free with park admission. Trailhead at Jewel Cave Visitor Center, 11149 U.S. Highway 16 Building B12, Custer, SD 57730 (43.730359, -103.829051); Year-round access during daylight hours. [Source: [Hiking Trails - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/jeca/planyourvisit/trails.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="activity-wild-caving-tour"></a>**Wild Caving Tour** (3-4 hours) - An extremely strenuous adventure tour covering 2/3 of a mile through undeveloped passages of Jewel Cave. Participants must pass through an 8-1/2 inch by 24-inch crawl space before beginning the tour, which involves crawling, climbing, and squeezing through tight spaces. This tour provides an authentic caving experience for those seeking adventure beyond the developed tour routes. Tours conducted on select Fridays, Saturdays, Sundays, and Mondays. Reservations required at least 7 days in advance through Recreation.gov. Currently accepting reservations for July & August 2025; tours end for the season on August 30, 2025. Jewel Cave Visitor Center, 11149 U.S. Highway 16 Building B12, Custer, SD 57730 (43.730359, -103.829051); 605-673-8300. [Source: [Wild Caving Tour - Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/10088103/ticket/10089110), [Guided Tours - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/jeca/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)]

- <a id="activity-canyons-trail"></a>**Canyons Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - A moderate 3.5-mile (5.6 km) trail with 436 feet of elevation gain offering very little tree cover. The trail passes through ponderosa pine forest interspersed with open meadows and fire-scarred landscapes from the Jasper Fire of August 2000. Hikers will encounter Lithograph and Hell Canyons with rocky terrain, cliffs, shelter caves, and spectacular views of the southern Black Hills. Wildlife sightings may include bighorn sheep and white-tailed deer. Visitors pass by the historic cabin and historic cave entrance along the route. This trail is well-maintained, easy to follow, and popular with trail runners. Free with park admission. Trailhead at Jewel Cave Visitor Center, 11149 U.S. Highway 16 Building B12, Custer, SD 57730 (43.730359, -103.829051); Year-round access during daylight hours. [Source: [Hiking Trails - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/jeca/planyourvisit/trails.htm), [Canyons Trail - AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/south-dakota/canyons-trail), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60865-d143180-Reviews-Jewel_Cave_National_Monument-Custer_South_Dakota.html)]

- <a id="activity-historic-cabin-area"></a>**Jewel Cave Historic Cabin Area** (30-45 minutes) - Located approximately 1 mile (1.6 km) west of the visitor center, this seasonal area features a historic log cabin Ranger Station built by the Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC) that served as the first park headquarters at Jewel Cave. The site includes a picnic area, vault/composting toilet, scenic views and photo opportunities, parking, and the historic area cave entrance. Historical and interpretive exhibits provide information about the CCC era and early cave exploration. The area serves as a trailhead for surface trails. Free with park admission. Historic Area, approximately 1 mile west of visitor center on U.S. Highway 16, Custer, SD 57730; Open Memorial Day to Labor Day; Restrooms available seasonally. [Source: [Historic Cabin - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/historic-cabin.htm), [Jewel Cave Historic Ranger Cabin - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/jewel-cave-s-historic-ranger-cabin.htm), [Basic Information - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/jeca/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-wind-cave"></a>**Wind Cave National Park** (30-40 minutes, less than 20 miles southeast) - Home to one of the world's longest and most complex caves, Wind Cave is known for its unique boxwork formations. The park offers several ranger-guided cave tours of varying difficulty levels, plus 30,000 acres of mixed-grass prairie above ground where visitors can view bison, elk, pronghorn, and prairie dogs. Offers a distinctly different cave experience from Jewel Cave, and many visitors tour both caves in one day. Wind Cave Visitor Center, 26611 US-385, Hot Springs, SD 57747 (43.563611, -103.482222); Visitor Center open year-round 8:00 AM - 4:30 PM (hours vary seasonally); 605-745-4600. [Source: [Wind Cave Visitor Center - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/wind-cave-visitor-center.htm), [Basic Information - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/wica/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [Wind Cave Road Trips](https://blackhillsparks.org/wind-cave-road-trips-connecting-the-black-hills-and-beyond/), [A Tale Of Two Caves](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/2018/09/tale-two-caves-going-underground-wind-cave-and-jewel-cave)]

- <a id="nearby-custer-state-park"></a>**Custer State Park** (25-35 minutes, approximately 20 miles) - One of South Dakota's premier state parks spanning 71,000 acres of rolling prairie hills, open grassland, pine forests, lakes and streams, and tall granite peaks. Famous for large herds of wildlife including bison, elk, antelope, and bighorn sheep. The 18-mile Wildlife Loop Road takes 1.5-2 hours to drive with frequent wildlife viewing opportunities. Additional highlights include scenic drives on the Needles Highway and Iron Mountain Road, Sylvan Lake, and numerous hiking trails through spectacular Black Hills landscapes. Custer State Park, 13329 US Highway 16A, Custer, SD 57730 (43.732222, -103.426944); Open year-round, visitor center hours vary by season; 605-255-4515. [Source: [Nearby Activities - Custer State Park Resort](https://www.custerresorts.com/activities/nearby-activities), [Wind Cave Road Trips](https://blackhillsparks.org/wind-cave-road-trips-connecting-the-black-hills-and-beyond/)]

- <a id="nearby-mount-rushmore"></a>**Mount Rushmore National Memorial** (45-50 minutes, approximately 33 miles northeast) - America's iconic presidential sculpture carved into the granite face of Mount Rushmore, featuring 60-foot-tall faces of George Washington, Thomas Jefferson, Theodore Roosevelt, and Abraham Lincoln. Visitors can walk the Presidential Trail for closer views of the sculpture, explore the visitor center and museum, and attend the evening lighting ceremony during summer months. The memorial offers an impressive and patriotic experience complementing the natural wonders of Jewel Cave. Mount Rushmore National Memorial, 13000 SD-244, Keystone, SD 57751 (43.878889, -103.459722); Open year-round, visitor facilities 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (extended summer hours); 605-574-2523. [Source: [Nearby Attractions - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/jeca/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm), [Driving Directions to Jewel Cave](https://www.distancesto.com/driving-directions/us/mt-rushmore-rd-to-jewel-cave-national-monument/history/320187.html), [Things to Do Near Mount Rushmore](https://planreadygo.com/things-to-do-near-mount-rushmore/)]

- <a id="nearby-crazy-horse"></a>**Crazy Horse Memorial** (25-30 minutes, approximately 19 miles north) - An ongoing mountain carving project honoring Lakota warrior Crazy Horse, designed to be the world's largest sculpture. The memorial complex includes the mountain carving in progress, the Indian Museum of North America, the Native American Educational & Cultural Center, and the Avenue of the Chiefs. Visitors can view the sculpture from the visitor complex year-round and tour the base of the mountain during the summer months. This attraction provides important Native American perspective and history complementing the Black Hills experience. Crazy Horse Memorial, 12151 Avenue of the Chiefs, Crazy Horse, SD 57730 (43.820279, -103.640092); Open daily year-round 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM (hours vary by season and facility); 605-673-4681. [Source: [Crazy Horse Memorial Hours](https://crazyhorsememorial.org/plan-your-visit/hours-and-schedule), [Crazy Horse Memorial - Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/crazy-horse-memorial-crazy-horse-3), [Nearby Attractions - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/jeca/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm)]
