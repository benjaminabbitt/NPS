# Niobrara NSR (National Scenic River)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-visitor-center"></a>**Niobrara National Scenic River Visitor Center** (214 W US Highway 20, Valentine, NE 69201 (42.872783, -100.550967); Memorial Day-Labor Day: Daily 8:30 AM-4:30 PM CT, Rest of year: Monday-Friday 8:30 AM-4:30 PM CT, closed federal holidays; 402-376-1901) - The main visitor center houses the park's official stamp. New in fall 2024, the center installed a Free Little Library outside the main entrance that houses the park's official stamp, allowing visitors to mark their national park passports even when the center is closed. [[Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/niob/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [Source: NPS News](https://www.nps.gov/niob/learn/news/niobrara-nsr-visitor-center-announces-off-season-hours.htm)]

**Note:** Call 402-376-1901 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-float-trips"></a>**River Float Trips - Kayaking/Canoeing** (2-5 hours) - The most popular activity on the Niobrara is floating the scenic river by kayak or canoe. The most common route is from Fort Niobrara National Wildlife Refuge's launch site to Brewer's Bridge, covering approximately 3-4 hours of paddling with opportunities to stop at Fort Falls, Berry Falls, and Smith Falls along the way. Multiple outfitters offer rentals and shuttle services; Fort Niobrara NWR (39111 US Highway 12, Valentine, NE 69201 (42.880100, -100.450996); Refuge grounds: daylight hours only; Visitor Center: Memorial Day-Labor Day daily 8 AM-4:30 PM, rest of year Monday-Friday 8 AM-4:30 PM; 402-376-3789). [[Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/niob/planyourvisit/canoeing-kayaking-tubing.htm), [Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/float-the-niobrara-river.htm), [Source: Brewers Canoers](https://www.brewerscanoers.com/trips.html)]

- <a id="activity-tubing"></a>**River Float Trips - Tubing** (4-6 hours) - Tubing is a popular, relaxing way to experience the Niobrara River, though it takes approximately double the time of kayaking/canoeing. Popular tubing routes include Berry Bridge to Brewer Bridge (4 hours), Berry Bridge to Penbrook Landing (5.5 hours), and Fort Niobrara to Nichols Landing/Smith Falls State Park (6 hours). Multiple outfitters provide tube rentals and shuttle services; contact outfitters at Valentine area for reservations. [[Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/niob/planyourvisit/canoeing-kayaking-tubing.htm), [Source: Dryland Aquatics](https://drylandaquatics.com/), [Source: Brewers Canoers](https://www.brewerscanoers.com/trips.html)]

- <a id="activity-smith-falls"></a>**Smith Falls State Park** (1-2 hours) - Home to Nebraska's highest waterfall at 70 feet, Smith Falls is accessible via a short 0.7-mile round-trip hike from the parking area or by river access. The park is a popular stop for river floaters and hikers alike; 90159 Smith Falls Rd, Valentine, NE 69201 (42.89229, -100.31171); Daily 9 AM-5 PM; 402-376-1306. [[Source: Nebraska Game & Parks](https://outdoornebraska.gov/location/smith-falls/), [Source: Visit Nebraska](https://nebraskapassport.com/stops/smith-falls-state-park-bonus-stop), [Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/smith-falls-state-park.htm)]

- <a id="activity-fort-niobrara"></a>**Fort Niobrara National Wildlife Refuge Wildlife Viewing** (2-3 hours) - View herds of 350 bison, elk, deer, coyotes, eagles, and grassland birds on the 3.5-mile wildlife drive. The refuge also offers excellent wildlife viewing opportunities along the 5.6-mile river section open for public floating; 39111 US Highway 12, Valentine, NE 69201 (42.880100, -100.450996); Refuge grounds: daylight hours only; Visitor Center: Memorial Day-Labor Day daily 8 AM-4:30 PM, rest of year Monday-Friday 8 AM-4:30 PM; 402-376-3789. [[Source: USFWS](https://www.fws.gov/refuge/fort-niobrara/visit-us), [Source: Visit Nebraska](https://visitnebraska.com/valentine/fort-niobrara-national-wildlife-refuge)]

- <a id="activity-fort-falls"></a>**Fort Falls Trail Hiking** (0.5-1 hour) - A 1.0-mile round loop trail featuring the 45-foot Fort Falls waterfall with scenic views of the Niobrara River. The trail uses metal walkways and stairs to descend to the canyon floor. Great for all ages and abilities; Fort Niobrara National Wildlife Refuge, trailhead located 0.25 miles from visitor center (42.880100, -100.450996); Dawn to dusk; free admission. [[Source: USFWS](https://www.fws.gov/refuge/fort-niobrara/visit-us/trails), [Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hike-to-fort-falls.htm), [Source: AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/nebraska/fort-falls-trail--2)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-berry-falls"></a>**Berry Falls** (0.5-1 hour) - Less than three miles east of Smith Falls, Berry Falls is a popular stop for tubers and kayakers but less visited by land. This scenic waterfall is located right on the shoreline of the Niobrara River and easily accessible from the water; accessible via river float from Berry Bridge area (approximately 42.88, -100.35); no set hours (river access). [[Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/niob/learn/nature/waterfalls-and-springs.htm), [Source: Visit Nebraska](https://visitnebraska.com/trip-idea/beaten-path-discover-nebraskas-lesser-known-waterfalls)]

- <a id="gem-refrigerator-falls"></a>**Refrigerator Falls (Icebox Falls)** (0.5-1 hour) - Visitors can walk behind the frigid water of this unique waterfall, named for its extremely cold water temperatures. Located in a narrow side canyon accessible from the river; accessible via river float, Fort Niobrara NWR area (approximately 42.88, -100.45); no set hours (river access). [[Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/niob/learn/nature/waterfalls-and-springs.htm)]

- <a id="gem-stairstep-falls"></a>**Stairstep Falls** (0.5-1 hour) - Located on the Niobrara Valley Preserve downstream of Big Cedar, this scenic waterfall is easily accessible from the river. Floaters will spot it shortly after passing Sharp's Landing with a sign indicating its location; accessible via river float from Niobrara Valley Preserve area (approximately 42.78, -99.95); no set hours (river access). [[Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/niob/learn/nature/waterfalls-and-springs.htm), [Source: Visit Nebraska](https://visitnebraska.com/trip-idea/beaten-path-discover-nebraskas-lesser-known-waterfalls)]

- <a id="gem-rocky-ford"></a>**Rocky Ford Access Point** (flexible) - A lesser-known access point along the river offering opportunities for hiking, wildlife observation, and river activities. Rocky Ford rapids is a popular area for experienced paddlers; Rocky Ford Camp & Outfitters, 41619 River Rd, Valentine, NE 69201 (approximately 42.85, -100.40); dawn to dusk. [[Source: NRS Community](https://community.nrs.com/duct-tape/2020/09/09/escape-to-nebraskas-niobrara-river/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-snake-river-falls"></a>**Snake River Falls** (1 hour, 25 miles southwest) - Nebraska's largest waterfall by volume when the Snake River is full, tumbling over a 54-foot wide ledge. Because access is on private property, there is a $1 per person fee with drop boxes provided. The trail to the left of the Snake Falls Sportsman's Club building affords the best view. Note: no potable water or public restrooms available; Highway 97, 23 miles southwest of Valentine (42.675, -100.857); Daily 8 AM-5 PM; $1 per person. [[Source: Visit Nebraska](https://visitnebraska.com/valentine/snake-river-falls), [Source: Visit Valentine](https://visitvalentine.org/places/snake-river-falls/), [Source: Farm Progress](https://www.farmprogress.com/farm-life/nebraskas-largest-waterfall-by-volume-snake-river-falls-offers-unique-viewing-experience)]

- <a id="nearby-merritt-reservoir"></a>**Merritt Reservoir State Recreation Area - Dark Sky Park** (2-3 hours, 25 miles southwest) - Designated as an International Dark Sky Park in 2022, Merritt Reservoir offers world-class stargazing, fishing, boating, and camping. It was the 200th certified International Dark Sky Place and hosts the annual Nebraska Star Party each summer. Located in the scenic Sandhills; 25-26 miles southwest of Valentine on Highway 12 (42.595, -100.933); park hours vary by season, state park permit required. [[Source: DarkSky](https://darksky.org/places/merritt-reservoir-dark-sky-park/), [Source: Nebraska Game & Parks](https://outdoornebraska.gov/location/merritt-reservoir/), [Source: Visit Nebraska](https://visitnebraska.com/press-releases/merritt-reservoir-designated-international-dark-sky-place)]

- <a id="nearby-cowboy-trail"></a>**Cowboy Recreation and Nature Trail** (flexible, starts in Valentine) - A 192-mile rail-trail from Valentine to Norfolk on crushed limestone surface, perfect for biking or walking. The trail starts just blocks from the Niobrara NSR Visitor Center. Bike rentals available in Valentine. Towns and trailheads are spaced 10-15 miles apart; trailhead in Valentine near visitor center (42.873, -100.551); dawn to dusk; free. [[Source: Nebraska Game & Parks](https://outdoornebraska.gov/parks/explore-trails/hiking-biking/cowboy-trail/), [Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/bike-the-cowboy-trail.htm), [Source: TrailLink](https://www.traillink.com/trail/cowboy-recreation-and-nature-trail/)]

## Review / Personal Notes

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 8-14 hours for a comprehensive visit to Niobrara NSR. This includes a 2-5 hour kayak/canoe float trip (or 4-6 hour tubing trip) stopping at major waterfalls, 1-2 hours at Smith Falls State Park, 2-3 hours for Fort Niobrara Wildlife Refuge wildlife viewing and driving tour, and 0.5-1 hour for the Fort Falls Trail hike. Additional time can be spent exploring hidden gem waterfalls like Berry Falls, Refrigerator Falls, and Stairstep Falls during float trips. Most visitors plan a full day (8-10 hours) for a river float and major attractions, or spread activities over 2 days for a more relaxed pace. [[Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/niob/planyourvisit/canoeing-kayaking-tubing.htm), [Source: NPS](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/float-the-niobrara-river.htm), [Source: USFWS](https://www.fws.gov/refuge/fort-niobrara/visit-us)]
