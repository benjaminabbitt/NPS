# Gateway Arch NP (National Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a name="stamp-visitor-center"></a>**Gateway Arch Visitor Center** (11 North 4th Street, St. Louis, MO 63102 (38.624545, -90.185009); Daily 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM, extended to 8:00 PM on summer Fridays and Saturdays, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 314-655-1600) - The National Park Passport Stamps are available in the bookstore inside the visitor center. There are four official stamps for Gateway Arch National Park, including stamps for the Lewis and Clark National Historic Trail and the Underground Railroad Freedom Network. [Source: [Visiting Gateway Arch National Park](https://nationalparkobsessed.com/visiting-gateway-arch-national-park/), [Gateway Arch Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/jeff/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Gateway Arch Contact](https://www.nps.gov/jeff/contacts.htm)]

- <a name="stamp-old-courthouse"></a>**Old Courthouse** (11 North Fourth Street, St. Louis, MO 63102 (38.624545, -90.185009); Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 314-655-1600) - Part of Gateway Arch National Park, the Old Courthouse reopened on May 3, 2025, after a two-year renovation. The facility now features brand-new exhibits, enhanced accessibility with elevators and ramps, and updated structural systems. Passport stamps should be available here as part of the park. [Source: [Old Courthouse St Louis](https://explorestlouis.com/partner/old-courthouse/), [Old Courthouse Renovation](https://www.nps.gov/jeff/planyourvisit/old-courthouse-renovation.htm), [Old Courthouse Reopened](https://www.stlpr.org/economy-business/2025-03-27/st-louis-old-courthouse-renovation-more-light-new-exhibits)]

**Note:** Call 314-655-1600 or 877-982-1410 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- <a name="activity-tram-ride"></a>**Tram Ride to the Top** (45-60 minutes total, 10 minutes at top) - The signature experience at Gateway Arch National Park includes a tram ride to the 630-foot summit of the Arch. The 45-60 minute experience includes interactive boarding exhibits featuring 1960s era animations, Gateway Arch trivia, and the tram ride itself. Visitors spend up to 10 minutes at the top enjoying panoramic views of St. Louis and the Mississippi River. Arrive at least 30 minutes ahead of scheduled tram time. During busier times, expect a 15-minute wait to pass through security; 11 North 4th Street, St. Louis, MO 63102 (38.624545, -90.185009); tram operates daily 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM (extended summer hours); advance ticket purchase strongly recommended. [Source: [Tram Ride to the Top](https://www.gatewayarch.com/gateway-arch-national-park/tram-ride-to-the-top/), [Gateway Arch Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/jeff/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Gateway Arch FAQs](https://www.gatewayarch.com/plan-your-visit/faqs/)]

- <a name="activity-museum"></a>**Museum at the Gateway Arch** (1-2 hours recommended) - Free museum reenvisioned in 2018, featuring six galleries highlighting different periods in the nation's and St. Louis' history with interactive elements for all ages. Galleries cover colonial history from 1764 (St. Louis origins, Indigenous and Creole cultures), Lewis and Clark's westward journey (Louisiana Purchase, Indigenous assistance, expedition tools), and westward expansion history. Features state-of-the-art graphics, sound, and immersive experiences. No time limit to explore during facility hours; 11 North 4th Street, St. Louis, MO 63102 (38.624545, -90.185009); Daily 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM (extended summer hours); free admission. [Source: [Museum at Gateway Arch](https://www.gatewayarch.com/gateway-arch-national-park/museum-under-the-gateway-arch/), [NPS Museum Information](https://www.nps.gov/jeff/planyourvisit/museum-at-the-gateway-arch.htm), [How to Visit Gateway Arch](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/how-to-visit-gateway-arch-national-park-st-louis/)]

- <a name="activity-documentary"></a>**Monument to the Dream Documentary** (35 minutes) - Academy Award-nominated 1967 documentary film directed by Charles Guggenheim traces the adventures of the Arch's evolution, from early concepts on the drawing board to fabrication of stainless steel sections and the triumphant placement of its final section in fall 1965. Shown in Tucker Theater located under the Gateway Arch; 11 North 4th Street, St. Louis, MO 63102 (38.624545, -90.185009); showtimes during visitor center hours; timed tickets required (Adults $7, Children 3-15 $3, America the Beautiful Pass $4). [Source: [See Monument to the Dream](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/see-monument-to-the-dream.htm), [Gateway Arch Documentary](https://www.gatewayarch.com/gateway-arch-national-park/documentary/), [Monument to the Dream Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monument_to_the_Dream)]

- <a name="activity-old-courthouse-tour"></a>**Old Courthouse Tour** (1 hour) - Explore the historic Old Courthouse featuring new exhibits on Dred and Harriet Scott's fight for freedom, remodeled courtrooms, and stunning mid-19th century Greek Revival architecture. The courthouse played a pivotal role in civil rights history. Self-guided tours available; 11 North Fourth Street, St. Louis, MO 63102 (38.624545, -90.185009); Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; free admission. [Source: [Old Courthouse St Louis](https://explorestlouis.com/partner/old-courthouse/), [Old Courthouse NPS](https://www.nps.gov/jeff/planyourvisit/och.htm), [Start Planning 2025 Trip](https://www.gatewayarch.com/about/newsroom/start-planning-your-2025-trip-to-gateway-arch-national-park/)]

- <a name="activity-riverboat-cruise"></a>**St. Louis Riverfront Cruise** (1 hour) - Daily sightseeing riverboat cruises on replica paddlewheel boats along the Mississippi River. The 1:30 PM and 3:00 PM cruises are narrated by both the captain and a National Park Service ranger. Operates March through November; departs from Gateway Arch riverfront; Adults (16-59) $26, Seniors (60+) $24, Children (3-15) $16, Infants (0-2) free but require ticket. Two-hour Skyline Dinner Cruise also available evenings (Adults $64, Children 3-12 $28). [Source: [Riverboat Cruises at Gateway Arch](https://www.gatewayarch.com/riverfront/riverboat-cruises/), [Riverboats Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g44881-d2665270-Reviews-Riverboats_at_the_Gateway_Arch-Saint_Louis_Missouri.html), [Explore St Louis Riverboats](https://explorestlouis.com/partner/riverboats-at-the-gateway-arch/)]

- <a name="activity-park-grounds"></a>**National Park Grounds Exploration** (30 minutes - 2 hours) - The 91-acre national park grounds feature walking paths, green spaces, ponds, and incredible views of downtown St. Louis, the Eads Bridge, and Mississippi River. Great for picnicking, photography, and relaxation; 11 North 4th Street, St. Louis, MO 63102 (38.624545, -90.185009); grounds open daily 5:00 AM - 11:00 PM year-round; free admission. [Source: [National Park Grounds](https://www.gatewayarch.com/gateway-arch-national-park/park-grounds/), [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/jeff/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Earth Trekkers Guide](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/how-to-visit-gateway-arch-national-park-st-louis/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a name="gem-secret-arch-trail"></a>**Secret Arch Trail** (30-45 minutes) - Lesser-known path offering unique perspectives of the Gateway Arch, often overlooked by tourists. Provides stunning views of the iconic Gateway Arch and Mississippi River with peaceful walks surrounded by lush greenery, offering a serene escape from city life away from the crowds that gather for the tram ride; within Gateway Arch National Park grounds, 11 North 4th Street area, St. Louis, MO 63102 (38.624545, -90.185009); accessible during park grounds hours 5:00 AM - 11:00 PM; free. [Source: [St. Louis Secret Arch Trail](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/missouri/st-louis-secret-arch-trail/)]

- <a name="gem-north-gateway"></a>**North Gateway** (30 minutes - 1 hour) - A 7.5-acre section of Gateway Arch National Park featuring a natural amphitheater (site of concerts and events throughout the year) with amazing views of the Arch and city skyline. Provides accessible routes and a peaceful escape from the main visitor areas; North section of Gateway Arch National Park, St. Louis, MO 63102 (38.624545, -90.185009); open during park grounds hours 5:00 AM - 11:00 PM; free. [Source: [Points of Interest Gateway Arch](https://www.archpark.org/visit/points-of-interest)]

- <a name="gem-riverfront-promenade"></a>**Riverfront Promenade** (30 minutes - 1 hour) - 1.5-mile riverfront promenade along the Mississippi River, site of community events throughout the year. Offers sightseeing, outdoor activities, and access to Laclede's Landing historic district; Gateway Arch riverfront area, St. Louis, MO 63102 (38.624545, -90.185009); accessible during daylight hours; free. [Source: [Gateway Arch Riverfront](https://www.gatewayarch.com/riverfront/), [Points of Interest](https://www.archpark.org/visit/points-of-interest)]

- <a name="gem-virtual-reality"></a>**Virtual Reality Theater** (15-20 minutes) - Virtual reality experience featuring St. Louis Riverfront history. Provides immersive historical perspective not available elsewhere in the park; Tucker Theater area, 11 North 4th Street, St. Louis, MO 63102 (38.624545, -90.185009); available during visitor center hours; ticketed experience (pricing available with combo packages). [Source: [Top Things to Do Gateway Arch](https://www.gatewayarch.com/plan-your-visit/), [Earth Trekkers Guide](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/how-to-visit-gateway-arch-national-park-st-louis/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a name="nearby-city-museum"></a>**City Museum** (3-5 hours recommended) - Architectural playground and museum in a converted shoe factory featuring tunnels, slides, caves, and multi-level structures for hands-on exploration. Highly interactive and unique attraction suitable for all ages. Most visitors recommend at least 3-4 hours to fully experience the museum; 750 N 16th St, St. Louis, MO 63103 (38.633640, -90.200425); approximately 1 mile from Gateway Arch (5-minute drive, 20-minute walk); Monday-Wednesday 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Thursday closed, Friday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 9:00 PM, Sunday 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM (check citymuseum.org for current schedule); admission fees apply. [Source: [City Museum Hours](https://citymuseum.org/plan-your-visit/things-to-know/hours/), [City Museum Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g44881-d107810-Reviews-City_Museum-Saint_Louis_Missouri.html), [Exploring City Museum](https://thewanderingwhittens.com/2025/05/15/city-museum/)]

- <a name="nearby-forest-park"></a>**Forest Park** (3-8 hours depending on activities) - 1,300-acre urban park larger than New York's Central Park, home to five major cultural institutions. Includes St. Louis Zoo (free admission), Saint Louis Art Museum (free admission), St. Louis Science Center (free admission), Missouri History Museum (free admission), and the Muny outdoor amphitheater. Offers trails, golf course, and green spaces; Forest Park, St. Louis, MO 63110; approximately 6.8 miles from Gateway Arch (10-minute drive); St. Louis Zoo: 1 Government Drive (38.635500, -90.290500); Saint Louis Art Museum: One Fine Arts Drive (38.639440, -90.294440); attractions within park located 2,993 feet from each other; hours vary by institution; free admission to major attractions. [Source: [Forest Park Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g44881-d532158-Reviews-Forest_Park-Saint_Louis_Missouri.html), [Experience Forest Park](https://explorestlouis.com/neighborhood/forest-park/), [Gateway Arch to St Louis Zoo](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Gateway-Arch/Saint-Louis-Zoo), [Saint Louis Zoo GPS](https://latitude.to/articles-by-country/us/united-states/24497/saint-louis-zoo), [SLAM Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Louis_Art_Museum)]

- <a name="nearby-anheuser-busch"></a>**Anheuser-Busch Brewery Tour** (45 minutes - 2+ hours depending on tour) - Historic brewery offering multiple tour options at one of the world's largest beer plants. Tours include the Clydesdale stables, Beechwood Aging Cellar, and Historic Brew House. General Brewery Tour (45 minutes, complimentary), Day Fresh Tour (75 minutes, $10), Beermaster Tour (2+ hours, $35), and Horses & Heritage Tour ($25); 1200 Lynch St, Saint Louis, MO 63118 (38.598562, -90.209909); approximately 3 miles from Gateway Arch (8-minute drive); Monday-Sunday 11:00 AM - 6:00 PM (hours vary seasonally); 314-577-2626; tour fees vary by experience. [Source: [Budweiser Tours STL](https://www.budweisertours.com/stl), [Anheuser-Busch Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g44881-d10404011-Reviews-Anheuser_Busch_Brewery-Saint_Louis_Missouri.html), [Anheuser-Busch Coordinates](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Anheuser-Busch+St.+Louis+Brewery/@38.5992699,-90.2144318)]
