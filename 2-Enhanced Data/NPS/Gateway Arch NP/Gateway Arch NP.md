# Gateway Arch NP (National Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Gateway Arch Visitor Center** (11 North 4th Street, St. Louis, MO 63102 (38.62497, -90.1857); 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM daily (winter), 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM daily (summer), extended hours Fridays and Saturdays in summer until 8:00 PM; 314-655-1600) - Four official cancellation stamps available at the visitor center desk under the Gateway Arch, entrance from Smith Square west of the Arch across from the Old Courthouse. [Source: [Gateway Arch Hours](https://www.nps.gov/jeff/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Recreation.gov Gateway Arch VC](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/258936), [TripExpress GPS](https://www.tripexpress.org/attraction-the-gateway-arch-208618/)]

**Note:** Call 314-655-1600 to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. Gateway Arch has four official passport stamps available in St. Louis.

## Key Activities

- **Tram Ride to the Top** (45-60 minutes total, under 10 minutes at top) - Experience the unique egg-shaped tram capsules ascending 630 feet to the top of the iconic Gateway Arch for incredible views stretching 30 miles in every direction; journey includes interactive boarding exhibits featuring 1960s era animations and Gateway Arch trivia; 11 North 4th Street, St. Louis, MO 63102 (38.62497, -90.1857); operates during visitor center hours; $19-$23 adults, $14-$18 children (3-15), discounts for National Park Pass holders [Source: [Tram Ride to the Top](https://www.gatewayarch.com/gateway-arch-national-park/tram-ride-to-the-top/), [Buy Tickets](https://www.gatewayarch.com/buy-tickets/), [More Than Just Parks Gateway Arch Guide](https://morethanjustparks.com/gateway-arch-guide/)]

- **Museum at the Gateway Arch** (1-2 hours) - Explore over 200 years of American history FREE in six interactive galleries chronicling westward expansion, Lewis & Clark's expedition, Native American cultures, colonial St. Louis, and the construction of the Gateway Arch; National Park Service Rangers offer programs throughout the day; 11 North 4th Street, St. Louis, MO 63102 (38.62497, -90.1857); open during visitor center hours; FREE admission [Source: [Museum Under the Gateway Arch](https://www.gatewayarch.com/gateway-arch-national-park/museum-under-the-gateway-arch/), [Gateway Arch NP](https://www.nps.gov/jeff/index.htm), [NPS Programs](https://www.gatewayarch.com/gateway-arch-national-park/national-park-service-programs/)]

- **Documentary Film: Monument to the Dream** (35 minutes) - Watch the dramatic story of the Gateway Arch's design and construction, featuring rare footage and interviews about this engineering marvel and its significance as a symbol of westward expansion; shown in theater at Gateway Arch; 11 North 4th Street, St. Louis, MO 63102; during visitor center hours; included with tram ticket or separate ticket available [Source: [Plan Your Visit Sample Itineraries](https://www.gatewayarch.com/plan-your-visit/sample-itineraries/), [US News Travel Gateway Arch](https://travel.usnews.com/St_Louis_MO/Things_To_Do/Gateway_Arch_62010/)]

- **Old Courthouse Tour** (45 minutes - 1 hour) - Visit the historic courthouse where over 300 freedom suits were tried, including the pivotal Dred & Harriet Scott case and Virginia Minor's case for women's right to vote; explore restored courtrooms, grab a gavel or take a seat at the bench, and view free exhibit galleries about Lewis & Clark, Native Americans, and colonial St. Louis; designated site on the National Park Service's National Underground Railroad Network to Freedom; 11 North 4th Street, St. Louis, MO 63102 (38.62497, -90.1857); reopened to public May 3, 2025; FREE admission [Source: [Old Courthouse](https://www.gatewayarch.com/gateway-arch-national-park/old-courthouse/), [NPS Programs](https://www.gatewayarch.com/gateway-arch-national-park/national-park-service-programs/), [Park Ranger John Things to Do](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/things-to-do-gateway-arch-national-park/)]

- **Gateway Arch Park Grounds Walk** (30 minutes - 2 hours) - Stroll the beautifully landscaped 91-acre National Park featuring five miles of paved paths, reflection ponds perfect for lunch, grand staircase and ramps providing riverfront views of the Mississippi River, native plantings, and multiple vantage points for photography of the iconic Gateway Arch; Gateway Arch National Park, St. Louis, MO 63102; grounds open dawn to dusk; FREE [Source: [Gateway Arch National Park](https://www.gatewayarch.com/gateway-arch-national-park/), [Upgraded Points Guide](https://upgradedpoints.com/travel/gateway-arch-national-park-travel-guide/), [Points of Interest](https://www.archpark.org/visit/points-of-interest)]

- **Riverboat Cruise** (60 minutes) - Enjoy narrated sightseeing cruise along the St. Louis Riverfront on the Mississippi River with views of the Gateway Arch, downtown skyline, and historic landmarks; departs from Gateway Arch riverfront; operates March-November (seasonal); tickets available at Gateway Arch ticket office; separate ticket required [Source: [Plan Your Visit](https://www.gatewayarch.com/plan-your-visit/), [Things to Do](https://www.lehmannhouse.com/blog/what-to-do-at-the-st-louis-gateway-arch/)]

- **Ranger-Led Programs** (15-60 minutes) - Join National Park Service rangers for free daily talks and special events beneath the Gateway Arch including Indoor Museum Tours, Outdoor Walking Tours, and educational programs about westward expansion and the Arch's construction; rangers staff the Information Desk and Top of Gateway Arch to answer questions; 11 North 4th Street, St. Louis, MO 63102; program times vary, check schedule at visitor center; FREE [Source: [NPS Park Rangers Programs](https://www.gatewayarch.com/gateway-arch-national-park/national-park-service-programs/), [Gateway Arch Visit Guide](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/how-to-visit-gateway-arch-national-park-st-louis/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Virtual Reality Theater Experience** (10 minutes) - Experience immersive virtual reality presentations bringing Gateway Arch history to life; located in Gateway Arch visitor center, 11 North 4th Street, St. Louis, MO 63102; during visitor center hours; separate ticket may be required [Source: [US News Travel](https://travel.usnews.com/St_Louis_MO/Things_To_Do/Gateway_Arch_62010/)]

- **B.A.R.K. Ranger Program** (30-60 minutes) - Join this unique National Park program inviting visitors and their dogs to explore the 91-acre park grounds while learning about responsible pet ownership principles including bagging waste, keeping dogs on leashes, respecting visitors, and understanding where dogs can go in the park; Gateway Arch National Park grounds; during daylight hours; FREE [Source: [NPS Park Rangers Programs](https://www.gatewayarch.com/gateway-arch-national-park/national-park-service-programs/)]

- **Junior Ranger Program** (1-2 hours) - Complete activities to earn an official Junior Ranger badge at the Gateway Arch, Old Courthouse, or on the Riverboats when a ranger is on board; pick up activity booklet at visitor center and return when completed for badge ceremony; 11 North 4th Street, St. Louis, MO 63102; during visitor center hours; FREE [Source: [NPS Programs](https://www.gatewayarch.com/gateway-arch-national-park/national-park-service-programs/), [2021 National Park Week](https://www.gatewayarch.com/2021nationalparkweek/)]

- **Running with a Ranger Program** (45-60 minutes) - Join National Park rangers for guided running tours through the 91-acre Gateway Arch park grounds, combining exercise with education about the park's history and features; check with visitor center for schedule; Gateway Arch National Park grounds; schedule varies; FREE [Source: [NPS Programs](https://www.gatewayarch.com/gateway-arch-national-park/national-park-service-programs/)]

- **Reflection Ponds and Grand Staircase** (15-30 minutes) - Discover peaceful reflection ponds perfect for relaxing lunch breaks and photography, connected by the impressive grand staircase and ramps leading to Mississippi riverfront views; often overlooked by visitors focused on the Arch itself; Gateway Arch National Park grounds; dawn to dusk; FREE [Source: [Points of Interest](https://www.archpark.org/visit/points-of-interest), [Gateway Arch Park](https://www.gatewayarch.com/gateway-arch-national-park/)]

- **Puppet Shows** (30 minutes) - Attend family-friendly educational puppet shows presented by National Park rangers covering Gateway Arch history and westward expansion themes; offered periodically in visitor center area; 11 North 4th Street, St. Louis, MO 63102; check schedule at visitor center; FREE [Source: [NPS Programs](https://www.gatewayarch.com/gateway-arch-national-park/national-park-service-programs/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Laclede's Landing** (30 minutes - 2 hours, 8 minutes/0.5 miles from Gateway Arch) - Explore this historic nine-block cobblestone district on the Mississippi riverfront featuring restaurants, bars, nightlife, outdoor activities, and numerous attractions in restored 19th-century buildings; 720 North 1st Street, St. Louis, MO 63102; hours vary by establishment [Source: [TripAdvisor Near Gateway Arch](https://www.tripadvisor.com/AttractionsNear-g44881-d104318-The_Gateway_Arch-Saint_Louis_Missouri.html), [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/how-to-visit-gateway-arch-national-park-st-louis/)]

- **Forest Park** (2-4 hours, 15 minutes/6 miles west of Gateway Arch) - Visit one of America's largest urban parks spanning nearly 1,300 acres with three FREE world-class attractions: Saint Louis Zoo, Saint Louis Art Museum, and Saint Louis Science Center, plus golf course, jogging trails, boating waterways, and tennis courts; Forest Park, St. Louis, MO 63110; park open dawn-dusk, attraction hours vary; FREE admission to major attractions [Source: [Forest Park Info](https://www.tripadvisor.com/AttractionsNear-g44881-d104318-The_Gateway_Arch-Saint_Louis_Missouri.html)]

- **City Museum** (2-4 hours, 10 minutes/1.5 miles from Gateway Arch) - Experience this completely unique, eclectic museum featuring a 10-story jungle gym made from repurposed architectural and industrial objects, caves, slides, a rooftop Ferris wheel, and MonstroCity playground; includes BBQ restaurant inside; 750 North 16th Street, St. Louis, MO 63103; hours vary, check website; admission charged [Source: [TripAdvisor Near Gateway Arch](https://www.tripadvisor.com/AttractionsNear-g44881-d104318-The_Gateway_Arch-Saint_Louis_Missouri.html)]

- **Anheuser-Busch Brewery Tour** (1-1.5 hours, 10 minutes/3 miles south of Gateway Arch) - Tour one of America's most iconic breweries featuring the historic Brew House, Clydesdale stables, and beer tasting for guests 21+; 1200 Lynch Street, St. Louis, MO 63118; tours offered daily, reservations recommended; complimentary tour, tasting requires ID [Source: [Things Near Gateway Arch](https://www.tripadvisor.com/AttractionsNear-g44881-d104318-The_Gateway_Arch-Saint_Louis_Missouri.html)]

**Total Recommended Time at Gateway Arch NP (National Park):** 2-3 hours minimum for tram ride, museum, and grounds; 4-6 hours for comprehensive visit including Old Courthouse and ranger programs; full day possible with riverboat cruise and extended exploration. Allow at least 1.5 hours if only riding tram (30 minutes security, 1 hour round-trip tram).
