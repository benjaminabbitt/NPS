# Wilson's Creek NB (National Battlefield)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="visitor-center-stamp"></a>**Wilson's Creek National Battlefield Visitor Center** (5242 S. State Hwy ZZ, Republic, MO 65738 (37.10069, -93.41036); Visitor Center: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, Battlefield: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily; 417-732-2662)

**Note:** Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [Source: [NPS Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/wicr/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/wicr/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Visitor Center Hours](https://wilsonscreek.com/visitor-center-hours)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="auto-tour"></a>**5-Mile Auto Tour** (1.5-2 hours with stops) - Self-guided paved one-way tour road winding through the heart of the battlefield with eight interpretive stops including Ray House (historic field hospital with rock cellar), Bloody Hill (main battlefield), Edwards Cabin/Price's Headquarters, Gibson Mill site, and Ray Cornfield. Tour takes approximately 15 minutes to drive without stops or 2 hours to walk at average pace. Features wooded areas, meadows, fields, and seasonal wildflowers with opportunities to observe wildlife including deer, wild turkeys, and bird species. No bathroom facilities or water available along route; 5242 S. State Hwy ZZ, Republic, MO 65738 (37.10069, -93.41036); 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily [Source: [NPS Tour Road](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/travel-the-tour-road.htm), [Wilson's Creek Foundation](https://wilsonscreek.com/battlefield), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/missouri/wilson-s-creek-national-battlefield-road)]

- <a id="visitor-center-exhibits"></a>**Visitor Center Exhibits and Film** (45 minutes - 1 hour) - Renovated visitor center featuring 28-minute film in HD with surround sound, 8-minute fiber optics troop movement map showing battle progression, museum exhibits with approximately 1,800 square feet of display space housing extensive Civil War artifact collection including General Patrick Cleburne's sword belt, John Brown's telescope, Civil War Medals of Honor, rare weapons, General Nathaniel Lyon's sword and personal effects, and historic Civil War flags; 5242 S. State Hwy ZZ, Republic, MO 65738 (37.10069, -93.41036); 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily [Source: [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/246877), [Visit Missouri](https://www.visitmo.com/things-to-do/wilsons-creek-national-battlefield), [National Parks Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/2021/05/wilsons-creek-national-battlefield-opens-renovated-museum-and-visitor-center)]

- <a id="bloody-hill-trail"></a>**Bloody Hill Trail** (20-30 minutes) - Walking trail spanning 0.5-0.75 miles crossing Bloody Hill at the heart of the Battle of Wilson's Creek battlefield. Features 60 feet of elevation change with one mildly steep climb/descent. Points of interest include the 1928 "Lyon Marker" and a natural sinkhole that served as temporary mass grave for Union soldiers; 5242 S. State Hwy ZZ, Republic, MO 65738 (37.10069, -93.41036); 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily [Source: [NPS Bloody Hill](https://www.nps.gov/places/wilson-s-creek-bloody-hill.htm), [Hiking Project](https://www.hikingproject.com/trail/7050638/bloody-hill-trail), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/missouri/wilsons-creek-national-battlefield)]

- <a id="ray-house"></a>**Ray House Historic Site** (30 minutes) - Historic structure dating to before the Civil War that served as field hospital following the battle. Visitors can explore the farmhouse, springhouse, cornfield battle site, and walk down to the rock cellar where children hid at the battle's start. Accessible via the auto tour road; 5242 S. State Hwy ZZ, Republic, MO 65738 (37.10069, -93.41036); 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily [Source: [NPS Tour Road](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/travel-the-tour-road.htm), [Wilson's Creek Foundation](https://wilsonscreek.com/battlefield)]

- <a id="living-history"></a>**Living History Programs** (variable timing, seasonal) - Annual 164th Anniversary Battle Commemoration scheduled for August 9-10, 2025, with programs 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM both days. Sunday ceremony at 1:00 PM on visitor center lawn with memorial wreath presentation and infantry salute. Programs explore battlefield preservation importance and battle significance to Missouri and the nation. Free entrance; 5242 S. State Hwy ZZ, Republic, MO 65738 (37.10069, -93.41036); Check calendar for specific event times [Source: [NPS Event Details](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/event-details.htm?id=9F7FAA44-A844-830E-D4F1DBB6D9533001), [Wilson's Creek Calendar](https://wilsonscreek.com/project/detail/2025-calendar)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="hulston-library"></a>**John K. and Ruth Hulston Civil War Research Library** (1-2 hours for research) - Specialized research library containing approximately 12,000 volumes concentrating on Civil War period and Trans-Mississippi Theater. Non-circulating materials open to public by appointment. Outstanding resource for Civil War researchers; 5242 S. State Hwy ZZ, Republic, MO 65738 (37.10069, -93.41036); 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM and 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM Tuesday-Saturday (closed Sunday-Monday), by appointment; 417-732-2662 [Source: [NPS Hulston Library](https://www.nps.gov/wicr/learn/historyculture/hulston-civil-war-research-library.htm), [NPS Library Info](https://www.nps.gov/wicr/planyourvisit/library.htm)]

- <a id="gibson-mill-trail"></a>**Gibson Mill Trail** (30-45 minutes) - Lesser-known walking trail that meanders through forest following a stream to the site of former mill, offering quieter, more nature-focused experience. Can be combined with Gibson Oatfield Trail or Gibson Mill Extension Trail for added mileage. Provides peaceful alternative to main tour stops; 5242 S. State Hwy ZZ, Republic, MO 65738 (37.10069, -93.41036); 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily [Source: [Hiking Project](https://www.hikingproject.com/trail/7043021/gibson-mill-trail), [NPS Tour Road](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/travel-the-tour-road.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/missouri/wilsons-creek-national-battlefield)]

- <a id="edwards-cabin"></a>**Edwards Cabin** (15-20 minutes) - Historic cabin accessible via walking trails from auto tour, less commonly visited than Ray House or Bloody Hill. Served as Price's Headquarters during battle; 5242 S. State Hwy ZZ, Republic, MO 65738 (37.10069, -93.41036); 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily [Source: [NPS Tour Road](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/travel-the-tour-road.htm), [Wilson's Creek Foundation](https://wilsonscreek.com/battlefield)]

- <a id="horseback-trail"></a>**7-Mile Horseback Riding Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - Equestrian trail system offering unique way to explore battlefield. Trail connects to other walking paths throughout park. Open to both horseback riders and hikers seeking extended exploration beyond main tour route; 5242 S. State Hwy ZZ, Republic, MO 65738 (37.10069, -93.41036); 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily [Source: [NPS Outdoor Activities](https://www.nps.gov/wicr/planyourvisit/outdooractivities.htm), [Explore Christian County](https://www.exploreccmo.com/parks-trails/wilsons-creek-national-battlefield/)]

- <a id="wire-road"></a>**Historic Wire Road** (variable timing) - Historical path where buffalo and wildlife created natural path across the Ozarks, later used by Native Americans for migratory routes. Offers connection to pre-Civil War history of the region; 5242 S. State Hwy ZZ, Republic, MO 65738 (37.10069, -93.41036); 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/missouri/wilsons-creek-national-battlefield)]

Number one tourist destination in Missouri attracting visitors from across country. Plan 3-4 hours minimum to experience both wildlife galleries and aquarium adventure; 500 W Sunshine St, Springfield, MO 65807 (37.181539, -93.29673); Monday-Thursday 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Friday 10:00 AM - 7:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM - 7:00 PM, Sunday 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g44926-d12883088-Reviews-Johnny_Morris_Wonders_of_Wildlife_National_Museum_and_Aquarium-Springfield_Missou.html), [Wonders of Wildlife](https://wondersofwildlife.org/), [Springfield MO](https://www.springfieldmo.org/things-to-do/attractions/wonders-of-wildlife-national-museum-and-aquarium/)]

Features aquariums, waterfalls, swamp with alligators and turtles, archery and shooting ranges, restaurants, and museums. Top tourist destination attracting 4 million visitors annually; 1 Bass Pro Dr, Springfield, MO 65807 (37.1794, -93.2972); Monday-Saturday 8:00 AM - 9:00 PM, Sunday 9:00 AM - 7:00 PM; 417-887-7334 [Source: [Bass Pro Shops](https://stores.basspro.com/us/mo/springfield/1-bass-pro-dr.html), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g44926-d107328-Reviews-Bass_Pro_Shops_Outdoor_World-Springfield_Missouri.html), [Springfield MO](https://www.springfieldmo.org/things-to-do/attractions/bass-pro-shops-outdoor-world/)]

- <a id="botanical-gardens"></a>**Springfield Botanical Gardens at Nathanael Greene/Close Memorial Park** (1-2 hours, 12 miles/18 minutes from battlefield) - Beautiful botanical gardens featuring Mizumoto Japanese Stroll Garden (small admission fee) and free park grounds. Botanical Center open for exploration with gardens showcasing seasonal plantings and native landscapes; 2400 S. Scenic Ave., Springfield, MO 65807 (37.171683, -93.331949); Botanical Center: Monday-Saturday 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday 11:00 AM - 6:00 PM; Mizumoto Garden: May-September, Monday-Friday 10:00 AM - 7:30 PM, Saturday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM (call to confirm: 417-891-1515); Park grounds: sunrise to sunset daily [Source: [Springfield Park Board](https://parkboard.org/274/Springfield-Botanical-Gardens), [Springfield MO](https://www.springfieldmo.org/listing/springfield-botanical-gardens-at-nathanael-greene-close-memorial-park/1065/), [Friends of the Garden](https://www.friendsofthegarden.org/plan-your-visit/)]

- <a id="route-66-museum"></a>**Route 66 Car Museum** (1-2 hours, 14 miles/20 minutes from battlefield) - Classic automotive museum featuring more than 75 classic, sports, and vintage cars celebrating America's Mother Road. Tour takes 1-2 hours depending on interest level; 1634 W College St, Springfield, MO 65806; 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily (closed Thanksgiving and Christmas); 417-459-2452 [Source: [Route 66 Car Museum](https://66carmuseum.com/), [Route 66 Planning](https://66carmuseum.com/route-66/planning-your-visit-to-the-route-66-car-museum/), [Springfield MO](https://www.springfieldmo.org/listing/route-66-car-museum/1804/)]
