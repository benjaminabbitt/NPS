- **Chancellorsville Battlefield Visitor Center** (9001 Plank Road, Spotsylvania, VA 22553 [38.3115,-77.6487]; 9am-5pm daily, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, January 1; 540-693-3200) [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/frsp/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [NPS Operating Hours](https://www.nps.gov/frsp/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Latitude.to Battle of Chancellorsville](https://latitude.to/articles-by-country/us/united-states/1964/battle-of-chancellorsville)]

## Key Activities

- **Fredericksburg Battlefield Visitor Center Film and Exhibits** (1-2 hours) - Start your visit with the 22-minute orientation film (shown every 30 minutes) and explore three exhibit rooms covering the four major battles; 1013 Lafayette Boulevard, Fredericksburg, VA 22401; 9am-5pm daily [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/frsp/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/frsp/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Sunken Road Walking Tour** (1.5 hours) - Walk the famous 0.5-mile route along Sunken Road and up Marye's Heights where Confederate forces held a nearly impregnable defensive position; Fredericksburg Battlefield; sunrise to sunset [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/frsp/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [American Battlefield Trust One Day Tour](https://www.battlefields.org/visit/itineraries/tour-fredericksburg-area-battlefields-one-day)]

- **Chancellorsville Battlefield Visitor Center and Exhibits** (1-2 hours) - View exhibits covering three battles (Chancellorsville, Wilderness, and Spotsylvania) and watch the 22-minute film (shown every 30 minutes); 9001 Plank Road, Spotsylvania, VA 22553; 9am-5pm daily [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/frsp/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm)]

- **Chancellorsville History Trail** (2-3 hours) - Hike this full-day trail exploring the site where Stonewall Jackson was wounded and Lee achieved his "perfect battle"; Chancellorsville Battlefield; sunrise to sunset [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/frsp/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Spotsylvania Court House Battlefield Trails Loop** (1.5-2 hours) - Hike the 5-mile loop trail through the battlefield with 285 feet elevation gain, rated 4.5 stars by 1,004 AllTrails reviewers; Spotsylvania Battlefield; sunrise to sunset [Source: [AllTrails Spotsylvania Loop](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/virginia/spotsylvania-court-house-battlefield-trails-loop), [NPS Spotsylvania Battlefield](https://www.nps.gov/frsp/planyourvisit/spotsylvaniabattlefield.htm)]

- **Bloody Angle Trail** (1 hour) - Walk the 1.1-mile loop or 0.5-mile rubber mulch section on flat terrain with interpretive waysides discussing the intense fighting at this location; Spotsylvania Battlefield; sunrise to sunset [Source: [National Parks Traveler](https://www.nationalparkstraveler.org/2010/11/trails-ive-hiked-bloody-angle-fredericksburg-and-spotsylvania-national-military-park7193), [NPS Places Bloody Angle](https://www.nps.gov/places/bloody-angle.htm)]

- **Chatham Manor Tour** (1-2 hours) - Tour five exhibit rooms in this 1771 Georgian mansion that served as a Union headquarters and hospital, with a 13-minute film about the manor's history; 120 Chatham Lane, Fredericksburg, VA 22405 [38.3071,-77.4547]; 9am-4:30pm daily (closed for maintenance check website) [Source: [NPS Chatham](https://www.nps.gov/frsp/learn/historyculture/chatham.htm), [Virginia.org Chatham Manor](https://www.virginia.org/listing/chatham-manor/139/), [Virginia Hometown Locator](https://virginia.hometownlocator.com/maps/feature-map,ftc,2,fid,1749087,n,chatham%20manor.cfm)]

- **Self-Guided Battlefield Driving Tours** (3-4 hours) - Follow driving tours through Fredericksburg, Chancellorsville, Wilderness, and Spotsylvania battlefields with brochures available at visitor centers; various battlefield locations; sunrise to sunset [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/frsp/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [NPS Self-Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/frsp/learn/photosmultimedia/self-guided-tours.htm)]

- **Ranger-Led Battlefield Tours** (45 minutes) - Join park rangers for guided walking tours covering no more than 0.5 mile over mostly flat terrain, including "Investigating Chancellorsville" program on weekends at 10:30am and 2pm; various locations; check calendar for seasonal availability [Source: [NPS Programs](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/fredspot-programs.htm), [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/frsp/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)]

- **Lee Drive Trail at Fredericksburg** (2-3 hours) - Hike this longer trail offering extended battlefield exploration; Fredericksburg Battlefield; sunrise to sunset [Source: [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/frsp/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Ellwood Manor** (1 hour) - Tour the first floor of this Civil War headquarters and hospital where General Stonewall Jackson's arm was buried, with exhibits on the Wilderness battles and care of the wounded; Wilderness Battlefield; grounds open sunrise to sunset, manor hours vary by season, restrooms 9am-5pm daily [Source: [NPS 2022 Ellwood Operations](https://www.nps.gov/frsp/learn/news/ellwood-manor-open-on-weekends.htm/index.htm), [Virginia.org Ellwood Manor](https://www.virginia.org/listing/ellwood-manor/4469/)]

- **Stonewall Jackson Death Site** (30-45 minutes) - Visit the preserved plantation office building where Confederate General Thomas "Stonewall" Jackson died on May 10, 1863; 12019 Stonewall Jackson Road, Woodford, VA [38.15,-77.41]; building open weekends 9am-5pm in summer, grounds dawn to dusk, closed New Year's, Thanksgiving, Christmas [Source: [NPS Jackson Death Site](https://www.nps.gov/frsp/learn/historyculture/jds.htm), [History Hit Jackson Shrine](https://www.historyhit.com/locations/jackson-shrine/), [Clio Jackson Shrine](https://theclio.com/entry/36501)]

- **Old Salem Church** (30 minutes) - View the historic 1844 church with Civil War graffiti and bullet holes still visible inside from its use during battle; near Old Salem Church Road and Plank Road (Route 3), Spotsylvania County; grounds open sunrise to sunset, interior open on limited occasions [Source: [NPS Salem Church](https://www.nps.gov/frsp/learn/historyculture/sc.htm), [NPS Places Salem Church](https://www.nps.gov/places/salem-church.htm)]

- **Bernard's Cabin Trail** (45 minutes) - Hike to the remains of Bernard's Cabin where you can still see the old chimney and foundation; Wilderness area; sunrise to sunset [Source: [AllTrails Historic Trails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/virginia/fredericksburg-and-spotsylvania-national-military-park/historic-site)]

- **Jackson Wounding Trail** (15-30 minutes) - Walk the 0.1-mile loop with rubber mulch on flat terrain where Stonewall Jackson was accidentally shot by his own troops; Chancellorsville Battlefield; sunrise to sunset [Source: [NPS Visitor Centers](https://www.nps.gov/frsp/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm), [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/frsp/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Laurel Hill Trail** (1 hour) - Hike this moderate 1-mile trail at Spotsylvania with interpretive features; Spotsylvania Battlefield; sunrise to sunset [Source: [Stone Sentinels Laurel Hill Trail](https://stonesentinels.com/spotsylvania/battle-spotsylvania-auto-tour/laurel-hill-trail/)]
