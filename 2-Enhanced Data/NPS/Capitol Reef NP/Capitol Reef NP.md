# Capitol Reef NP (National Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Capitol Reef Visitor Center** (52 Scenic Drive, Torrey, UT 84775 (38.2872, -111.2618); 8:00 AM - 4:30 PM daily fall/winter, 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM Easter to mid-October, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 435-425-3791) [Source: [All Moab Visitor Center](https://www.allmoab.com/capitol_reef_national_park/visitor_center.php), [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/hours.htm)]

**Note:** Visitor center located 6 miles from west park entrance off Utah Highway 24, 8 miles from east entrance. Passport stamps available at visitor center. [Source: [NPS Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/visitorcenter.htm)]

## Key Activities

- **Drive Capitol Reef Scenic Drive** (1-2 hours) - Travel 8-mile paved road suitable for passenger vehicles with two dirt spur roads (Grand Wash and Capitol Gorge) entering canyons and leading to trailheads; usually suitable for passenger cars and RVs up to 27 feet [Source: [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-things-to-do-in-capitol-reef/), [NPS Trail Guide](https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/trailguide.htm)]

- **Hike Hickman Bridge Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - Trek 1.7-mile round-trip trail with 416-foot elevation gain to large natural bridge; moderate difficulty [Source: [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-things-to-do-in-capitol-reef/), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/us/utah/capitol-reef-national-park)]

- **Explore Fruita Historic District** (1-2 hours) - Visit pioneer orchards where you can pick your own fruit June-October during harvest; walk around historic buildings and structures in most accessible section of park [Source: [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-things-to-do-in-capitol-reef/), [Visit Utah](https://www.visitutah.com/places-to-go/parks-outdoors/capitol-reef)]

- **Hike Cassidy Arch Trail** (2-3 hours) - Experience astounding views with option to walk out to top of arch itself; strenuous trail [Source: [Earth Trekkers Best Hikes](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/best-hikes-in-capitol-reef-national-park/)]

- **View Fremont Petroglyphs** (15-30 minutes) - See ancient rock carvings by Fremont people from over 1,000 years ago depicting animals, human figures, and geometric shapes; located along Highway 24 near visitor center [Source: [As They Are](https://astheyare.org/2024/11/28/capitol-reef-national-park-petroglyphs/), [Tourist Secrets](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/utah/secrets-of-utahs-capitol-reef-rock-art/)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Drive Cathedral Valley Loop** (6-8 hours) - Navigate 58-mile journey through remote area featuring Temple of the Sun and Temple of the Moon monoliths, volcanic dikes, and vast desert vistas; high-clearance vehicle recommended [Source: [Bryce Canyon Country](https://www.brycecanyoncountry.com/secret-places-in-capitol-reef-national-park/), [WillowWind RV](https://www.willowwindrvpark.com/blog/capitol-reef-national-park-hidden-gems-and-scenic-drives)]

- **Hike Burro Wash** (2-3 hours) - Experience solitude while scrambling over boulders, wading through small pools, and squeezing through Navajo sandstone narrows opening to expansive Waterpocket Fold views [Source: [Bryce Canyon Country](https://www.brycecanyoncountry.com/secret-places-in-capitol-reef-national-park/)]

- **Explore Waterpocket Fold** (varies) - Discover park's most distinguishing geologic feature, a 100-mile protuberance in earth's crust eroded into maze of winding canyons, towering monoliths, and massive domes with small water-collecting depressions throughout [Source: [Tourist Secrets Hidden Treasures](https://www.touristsecrets.com/destinations/united-states/utah/hidden-treasures-of-utahs-capitol-reef/)]

## Also Nearby

- **Goblin Valley State Park** (1-2 hours, 45 miles north) - Explore thousands of mushroom-shaped rock formations (hoodoos) in otherworldly landscape; day-use $20 per vehicle [Source: [Capitol Reef Country](https://capitolreefcountry.com/capitol-reef-national-park/)]

- **Kodachrome Basin State Park** (2-3 hours, 2.5 hours southwest near Cannonville) - Discover unique rock spires and colorful sedimentary rock formations; excellent for Milky Way viewing; open 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM year-round; located 7 miles south of Cannonville off Utah Route 12; day-use fee required [Source: [Utah State Parks](https://stateparks.utah.gov/parks/kodachrome-basin/), [Sailing Stone Travel](https://sailingstonetravel.com/kodachrome-basin-state-park-what-to-do-see-in-a-day/)]

Could easily spend 5 days and never run out of things to do.
