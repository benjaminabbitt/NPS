# Bluestone NSR (National Scenic River)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-sandstone-visitor-center"></a>**Sandstone Visitor Center** (330 Meadow Creek Road, Meadow Bridge, WV 25976; 37.7855, -80.8628; Daily 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM seasonal; Phone: 304-465-0508) - New River Gorge National Park and Preserve visitor center that serves Bluestone NSR. Exhibits, maps, and passport stamps for both parks. [Source: [NPS Bluestone](https://www.nps.gov/blue/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="stamp-canyon-rim-visitor-center"></a>**Canyon Rim Visitor Center** (162 Visitor Center Road, Lansing, WV 25862; Year-round; Phone: 304-574-2115) - Alternative stamp location at New River Gorge NP&P. More accessible hours year-round. [Source: [NPS New River Gorge](https://www.nps.gov/neri/index.htm)]

**Note:** Bluestone NSR has no dedicated visitor center. Contact New River Gorge visitor centers for stamps and information. Call 304-465-0508 for current conditions.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-fishing"></a>**Fishing** (2-4 hours) - Bluestone River offers excellent fishing for smallmouth bass, rock bass, and various sunfish species. Walk-in access via state parks or by watercraft. West Virginia fishing license required. Year-round. [Source: [NPS Bluestone](https://www.nps.gov/blue/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-ranger-led-programs"></a>**Ranger-Led Programs** (1-3 hours) - Join rangers for guided hikes and stream search programs exploring the river's ecology. Programs explore aquatic life, geology, and natural history. Check park calendar for schedule; often held in conjunction with New River Gorge. [Source: [NPS Bluestone](https://www.nps.gov/blue/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-hiking-at-pipestem-resort-state-park"></a>**Hiking at Pipestem Resort State Park** (1-4 hours) - Over 20 miles of trails in the adjacent state park with access to Bluestone River views. Trails range from easy walks to challenging descents into the gorge. 3405 Pipestem Drive, Pipestem, WV. [Source: [WV State Parks](https://wvstateparks.com/park/pipestem-resort-state-park/)]

- <a id="activity-bluestone-river-paddling"></a>**Bluestone River Paddling** (4-8 hours) - The 10.5-mile designated Wild and Scenic river section offers Class I-II paddling through remote gorge. Put-in at Pipestem Resort State Park or Bluestone State Park. Intermediate paddling skills recommended. [Source: [NPS Bluestone](https://www.nps.gov/blue/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-bluestone-state-park"></a>**Bluestone State Park** (2-6 hours) - West Virginia state park on Bluestone Lake with boat ramp, hiking trails, swimming beach, and camping. Access to the scenic river's lower section. Hinton, WV. [Source: [WV State Parks](https://wvstateparks.com/park/bluestone-state-park/)]

- <a id="activity-hunting"></a>**Hunting** (seasonal) - Permitted in designated areas following West Virginia game regulations. White-tailed deer, wild turkey, and small game. West Virginia hunting license required. Check season dates. [Source: [NPS Bluestone](https://www.nps.gov/blue/planyourvisit/index.htm)]

- <a id="activity-wildlife-viewing"></a>**Wildlife Viewing** (variable) - Remote river corridor provides habitat for diverse wildlife including deer, wild turkey, beaver, and numerous bird species. Best viewing at dawn and dusk from trails or watercraft. [Source: [NPS Bluestone](https://www.nps.gov/blue/index.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-pipestem-aerial-tramway"></a>**Pipestem Aerial Tramway** (30-45 minutes) - Descend 3,600 feet into the Bluestone River Gorge via aerial tramway at Pipestem Resort State Park. Access to Mountain Creek Lodge and hiking trails at gorge bottom. Seasonal operation, fee required. [Source: [WV State Parks](https://wvstateparks.com/park/pipestem-resort-state-park/)]

- <a id="gem-canyon-rim-trail-at-pipestem"></a>**Canyon Rim Trail at Pipestem** (2-3 hours) - Scenic trail along the gorge rim with views into Bluestone River canyon. Less crowded than main state park areas. Pipestem Resort State Park. [Source: [WV State Parks](https://wvstateparks.com/park/pipestem-resort-state-park/)]

- <a id="gem-bluestone-turnpike-trail"></a>**Bluestone Turnpike Trail** (3-5 hours) - Historic trail following old turnpike route with river views. Remote hiking experience through forested gorge. Access from state parks. [Source: [NPS Bluestone](https://www.nps.gov/blue/index.htm)]

- <a id="gem-mountain-creek-lodge"></a>**Mountain Creek Lodge** (variable) - Unique lodge accessible only by tramway or hiking, located at the bottom of Bluestone Gorge. Restaurant with views of Bluestone River. Pipestem Resort State Park, seasonal. [Source: [WV State Parks](https://wvstateparks.com/park/pipestem-resort-state-park/)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-new-river-gorge-national-park-and-preserve"></a>**New River Gorge National Park and Preserve** (2-8 hours, adjacent) - America's newest national park (2020) with whitewater rafting, rock climbing, hiking, and the iconic New River Gorge Bridge. Multiple visitor centers. Fayette County, WV. [Source: [NPS New River Gorge](https://www.nps.gov/neri/index.htm)]

- <a id="nearby-gauley-river-national-recreation-area"></a>**Gauley River National Recreation Area** (half-day+, 15 miles north) - World-class whitewater rafting on fall "Gauley Season" releases (September-October). Class IV-V rapids. Summersville, WV. [Source: [NPS Gauley River](https://www.nps.gov/gari/index.htm)]

- <a id="nearby-hinton"></a>**Hinton** (1-2 hours, 5 miles) - Historic railroad town with restaurants, lodging, and the Hinton Railroad Museum. Gateway to Bluestone Lake. Hinton, WV. [Source: Visit Southern WV]

- <a id="nearby-sandstone-falls"></a>**Sandstone Falls** (1-1.5 hours, 10 miles) - Largest waterfall on the New River (1,500 feet wide, 10-25 feet high). Accessible boardwalk and viewing platforms. Part of New River Gorge NP&P. [Source: [NPS New River Gorge](https://www.nps.gov/neri/index.htm)]

## Total Recommended Time

**Total recommended visit time for Bluestone NSR: Half-day to full day**

A half-day allows exploring one state park with river views and hiking. A full day enables paddling the river or combining with Pipestem aerial tramway and gorge exploration. Note that camping is not permitted within the scenic river itself; use adjacent state parks for overnight accommodations.

[Sources: [NPS Bluestone](https://www.nps.gov/blue/index.htm), [NPS Bluestone Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/blue/planyourvisit/index.htm), [WV State Parks](https://wvstateparks.com/)]
