## Key Activities

- **Bluestone Turnpike Trail** (3-5 hours) - A moderate 9.1-9.5 mile hiking trail running the length of the National Scenic River at the bottom of Bluestone Gorge, following the riverbank and home to a diverse collection of plants and wildlife; hikers might discover artifacts from earliest settlers as they pass near the site where the town of Lilly once stood; trailhead accessible from Pipestem Resort State Park, 3405 Pipestem Drive, Pipestem, WV 25979 [37.534, -80.995] or Bluestone State Park, 2104 Bluestone Park Road, Hinton, WV 25951 [37.618, -80.939]; park hours vary by season [Source: [Bluestone Turnpike Trail - AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/west-virginia/bluestone-turnpike-trail-north-to-south), [The Bluestone Turnpike Trail](https://appvoices.org/2018/10/17/the-bluestone-turnpike-trail/), [Hiking - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/blue/planyourvisit/hiking.htm), [Pipestem Resort State Park](https://wvstateparks.com/parks/pipestem-state-park/), [Bluestone State Park](https://wvstateparks.com/parks/bluestone-state-park/)]

- **Ranger-Led Bluestone River Walk** (1 hour) - Easy two mile round trip guided hike along the Bluestone River from late spring through fall, starting at the bottom of the aerial tramway at Pipestem Resort State Park; participants should bring good walking shoes, drinking water, and $2.00 for the return tram ride; Pipestem Resort State Park, 3405 Pipestem Drive, Pipestem, WV 25979 [37.534, -80.995] [Source: [Ranger Programs - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/blue/planyourvisit/ranger-programs.htm), [Bluestone Walk - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/event-details.htm?id=E7CB7BC1-155D-451F-67EF69E5785A91A1), [Outdoorsy Guide](https://www.outdoorsy.com/guide/bluestone-national-scenic-river), [Hiking Time Calculator](https://trailsnh.com/tools/hiking-time-calculator.php)]

- **Fishing for Smallmouth Bass** (2-4 hours) - Excellent warm water fishing along the Bluestone River with smallmouth bass, rock bass, and bluegill as favorite catches; best fishing times are early morning from dawn until 2 hours after sunrise and late afternoon from 3 hours before sunset until dusk; accessible from multiple points along the river including Bluestone State Park and Pipestem Resort State Park [Source: [Fishing - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/blue/planyourvisit/fishing.htm), [Things To Do - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/blue/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Best Time Smallmouth Bass](https://freshwaterfishingadvice.com/smallmouth-bass-best-time-day/)]

- **Aerial Tramway Ride at Pipestem** (30 minutes) - Unique access to the remote Bluestone River Gorge via aerial tram that descends 3,410 feet into the gorge over six minutes; the tram will transport canoes, kayaks, and bicycles for an additional charge; Pipestem Resort State Park, 3405 Pipestem Drive, Pipestem, WV 25979 [37.534, -80.995] [Source: [Pipestem Resort State Park](https://wvstateparks.com/parks/pipestem-state-park/), [Access to Bluestone](https://appvoices.org/2018/10/17/the-bluestone-turnpike-trail/)]

- **Canoeing and Kayaking** (2-4 hours) - Limited opportunities with the spring paddling season most likely to provide adequate water levels; this remarkable 10.5-mile section of river is remote yet worth the journey; access available at Bluestone State Park and via Pipestem Resort State Park aerial tram [Source: [Things To Do - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/blue/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Outdoorsy Guide](https://www.outdoorsy.com/guide/bluestone-national-scenic-river)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Ghost Town of Lilly** (30 minutes) - At the confluence of the Bluestone and Little Bluestone rivers near Bluestone State Park, the community of Lilly once flourished before being condemned and removed prior to construction of Bluestone Dam; artifacts and remnants can still be discovered along the Bluestone Turnpike Trail; accessible via trails from Bluestone State Park, 2104 Bluestone Park Road, Hinton, WV 25951 [37.618, -80.939] [Source: [Bluestone/Little Bluestone - WVU](https://diyoutdoors.wvu.edu/swimming-holes/bluestone-little-bluestone), [The Bluestone Turnpike Trail](https://appvoices.org/2018/10/17/the-bluestone-turnpike-trail/)]

- **Wildlife Watching and Biodiversity** (1-2 hours) - More than a thousand species of plants grow in several diverse Appalachian forest habitat types; two hundred species of birds have been spotted in the park; the remote gorge with limited road access provides excellent opportunities for solitude and wildlife observation; best accessed through Bluestone State Park or Pipestem Resort State Park [Source: [Outdoorsy Guide](https://www.outdoorsy.com/guide/bluestone-national-scenic-river), [Things To Do - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/blue/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- **Boulder-Strewn Lower Gorge Exploration** (1-2 hours) - American Indians called the river "Momongosenka" (Big Stone River), believed derived from their travels along ancient pathways through the boulder-strewn lower gorge; explore this historical landscape along the Bluestone Turnpike Trail where massive boulders create a dramatic riverside setting [Source: [Outdoorsy Guide](https://www.outdoorsy.com/guide/bluestone-national-scenic-river), [Hiking - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/blue/planyourvisit/hiking.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- **Sandstone Falls** (30 minutes, 9 miles from visitor center) - The largest waterfall in New River Gorge National Park & Preserve, spanning over 1,500 feet wide with the river dropping 10-25 feet; accessible year-round via easy 0.2 mile ADA-accessible boardwalk; popular for swimming, fishing, and scenic viewing; located off River Road (WV Route 26), approximately 9 miles from Sandstone Visitor Center; Sandstone Visitor Center hours: March-November 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, December-February 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM [Source: [Sandstone Falls - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/neri/planyourvisit/the-sandstone-falls.htm), [Sandstone Falls - WV Tourism](https://wvtourism.com/company/sandstone-falls/), [Sandstone Falls Boardwalk - AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/west-virginia/sandstone-falls-boardwalk)]

- **Pipestem Resort State Park Adventure Activities** (2-6 hours, adjacent to Bluestone NSR) - Pipestem Peaks Zipline Tour with nine exhilarating zips crossing the Bluestone Gorge three times at heights exceeding 300 feet; Adventure Zone featuring axe throwing, 3D archery, disc golf, laser tag, and skeet shooting; championship 18-hole golf course and nine-hole par 3 course; Olympic-sized swimming pool; 3405 Pipestem Drive, Pipestem, WV 25979 [37.534, -80.995]; park open year-round, activity hours vary [Source: [Pipestem Resort State Park](https://wvstateparks.com/parks/pipestem-state-park/), [Ultimate Guide to Pipestem](https://visitmercercounty.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-pipestem-resort-state-park/)]

- **New River Gorge National Park & Preserve** (15 miles, 20-30 minutes) - America's newest national park features some of the state's best whitewater rafting and canoeing, the iconic New River Gorge Bridge, and extensive hiking trails; center of outdoor recreation in West Virginia; headquarters located at 104 Main Street, Glen Jean, WV 25846 [37.926, -81.155]; park open year-round [Source: [Nearby Attractions - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/blue/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm), [New River Gorge NP](https://www.nps.gov/neri/index.htm)]

- **Hinton Railroad Museum** (1 hour, 5 miles from Bluestone State Park) - Museum showcasing artifacts and photos from the C&O steam engine era, model train exhibits, tools from the time of John Henry, uniforms, and historical photos; 206 Temple Street, Hinton, WV 25951; Fridays and Saturdays 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM (seasonal operations, call ahead to confirm); 304-446-5420 [Source: [Hinton Railroad Museum - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/places/hinton-railroad-museum.htm), [Hinton Railroad Museum - Visit WV](https://visitwv.com/company/hinton-railroad-museum/)]

- **Bluestone Wildlife Management Area** (variable time, adjacent to park) - Set on 17,632 acres adjacent to Bluestone Lake, popular for fishing with access to boating and fishing on the state's third-largest lake as well as stocked trout fishing in Indian Creek; also offers hunting opportunities; accessible from Bluestone State Park area [Source: [Nearby Attractions - NPS](https://www.nps.gov/blue/planyourvisit/nearbyattractions.htm), [Bluestone Lake WMA - WVDNR](https://wvdnr.gov/bluestone-lake-wma-offers-hunting-fishing-lake-adventures-in-southern-wv/)]
