## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-explore-the-national-prisoner-of-war-museum"></a>**Explore the National Prisoner of War Museum** (1-2 hours) - Tour exhibits featuring artifacts, graphics, text, and video programs telling the stories of American prisoners of war throughout history, and watch one or both of the 30-minute introductory films "Voices from Andersonville" and "Echoes of Captivity" that explore the Civil War story and POW experiences; 496 Cemetery Road, Andersonville, GA 31711; 9:30 AM - 4:30 PM daily, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day; 229-924-0343 [Source: [NPS POW Museum](https://www.nps.gov/ande/planyourvisit/natl_pow_museum.htm), [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60718-d103697-Reviews-Andersonville_National_Historic_Site_and_National_Prisoner_of_War_Museum-Andersonvi.html), [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/258605)]

- <a id="activity-drive-or-walk-the-prison-site-tour"></a>**Drive or Walk the Prison Site Tour** (1-1.5 hours) - Take a self-guided audio tour around the 26.5-acre Camp Sumter prison site with roadside pull-offs and exhibits at the Wisconsin Monument, the North Gate, Providence Spring, the Star Fort, and the reconstructed northeast corner of the stockade showing the hastily-built stockade wall; 496 Cemetery Road, Andersonville, GA 31711; grounds open 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, audio tours available until 3:15 PM; free admission; 229-924-0343 [Source: [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/andersonville-national-historic-site/), [NPS Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/ande/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Travel the Parks](https://traveltheparks.com/a-complete-guide-to-andersonville-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="activity-visit-andersonville-national-cemetery"></a>**Visit Andersonville National Cemetery** (45-60 minutes) - Walk through the active national cemetery containing nearly 13,000 graves of Civil War Union prisoners and over 20,000 total interments including U.S. Armed Forces veterans from subsequent conflicts, with wayside exhibits and monuments throughout; 496 Cemetery Road, Andersonville, GA 31711; open 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily (accessible through Cemetery Entrance Gate even on major holidays when museum is closed); free admission; 229-924-0343 [Source: [NPS Andersonville National Cemetery](https://www.nps.gov/ande/planyourvisit/andersonville_national_cemetery.htm), [Travel the Parks](https://traveltheparks.com/a-complete-guide-to-andersonville-national-historic-site/), [NPPlan](https://npplan.com/parks-by-state/georgia/at-a-glance-andersonville-national-historic-site/andersonville-national-cemetery-andersonville-national-historic-site/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-join-a-ranger-led-prison-site-walking-tour"></a>**Join a Ranger-Led Prison Site Walking Tour** (1-1.5 hours) - Walk the historic prison grounds with a park ranger who provides detailed interpretation and answers questions about the 45,000 Union prisoners held at Camp Sumter, offering a more intimate experience than the self-guided driving tour; meet at National Prisoner of War Museum, 496 Cemetery Road, Andersonville, GA 31711; tours offered at 2:00 PM on weekends, weather and staff permitting; free; 229-924-0343 [Source: [NPS Guided Tours](https://www.nps.gov/ande/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm), [I Love National Parks](https://ilovenationalparks.org/andersonville/), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/andersonville-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="gem-complete-the-junior-ranger-program"></a>**Complete the Junior Ranger Program** (1-1.5 hours) - Pick up a free Junior Ranger booklet at the main desk in the National Prisoner of War Museum and complete the "Captured! A Prisoner of War Story" program that puts children in the shoes of U.S. prisoners of war, making tough choices about what to bring into prison and how to react to prison realities, then return to be sworn in as a Junior Ranger and receive a badge and certificate; available at 496 Cemetery Road, Andersonville, GA 31711; 9:30 AM - 4:30 PM daily; free; no registration required but parent/guardian must accompany child; 229-924-0343 [Source: [NPS Junior Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/ande/learn/kidsyouth/beajuniorranger.htm), [NPS Kids & Youth](https://www.nps.gov/ande/learn/kidsyouth/index.htm), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/andersonville-national-historic-site/)]

- <a id="gem-discover-hidden-artifacts-and-original-earthworks"></a>**Discover Hidden Artifacts and Original Earthworks** (30-45 minutes) - Search for the well-hidden log from the original stockade wall displayed in a dark glass case surrounded by log wallpaper on the left side of the museum, and walk the original earthworks built by prisoners that surround the prison site, intended to defend against a Union attack that never came; National Prisoner of War Museum and prison grounds, 496 Cemetery Road, Andersonville, GA 31711; Museum: 9:30 AM - 4:30 PM, grounds: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily; free; 229-924-0343 [Source: [TripAdvisor Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60718-d103697-Reviews-Andersonville_National_Historic_Site_and_National_Prisoner_of_War_Museum-Andersonvi.html), [NPS Places](https://www.nps.gov/ande/learn/historyculture/places.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-jimmy-carter-national-historical-park"></a>**Jimmy Carter National Historical Park** (2-4 hours minimum for NPS sites) - Visit the Plains High School Visitor Center and Museum, the Jimmy Carter Boyhood Farm restored to its 1930s appearance, the Plains Train Depot where Carter headquartered his presidential campaign, and the newly-opened Carter Gardens and Gravesite memorial; 300 North Bond Street, Plains, GA 31780 (22 miles from Andersonville); 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM Eastern Time daily, closed New Year's Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas; free admission; 229-824-4104 [Source: [NPS Jimmy Carter Hours](https://www.nps.gov/jica/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [NPS Jimmy Carter](https://www.nps.gov/jica/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60866-d102633-Reviews-Jimmy_Carter_National_Historical_Park-Plains_Georgia.html)]

- <a id="nearby-the-drummer-boy-civil-war-museum"></a>**The Drummer Boy Civil War Museum** (30-45 minutes) - Explore Civil War artifacts including authentic Union and Confederate uniforms, Fire Zouave uniforms, drummer boy uniforms with original drums, revolvers, carbines, muskets, swords, documents, and photographs in downtown Andersonville; 109 East Church Street, Andersonville, GA 31711 (adjacent to NHS); Monday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; adults $5, ages 13-17 $1, children under 13 free with adult; 229-924-2558 [Source: [Explore Georgia](https://exploregeorgia.org/andersonville/arts-culture/museums/the-drummer-boy-civil-war-museum), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g60718-d3267835-Reviews-The_Drummer_Boy_Civil_War_Museum-Andersonville_Georgia.html), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/drummer-boy-civil-war-museum-andersonville)]

- <a id="nearby-georgia-rural-telephone-museum"></a>**Georgia Rural Telephone Museum** (1-2 hours) - Discover the history of rural telephone service through extensive exhibits of vintage telephone equipment, switchboards, and communications artifacts; 135 North Bailey Avenue, Leslie, GA 31764 (approximately 15 miles from Andersonville); Monday-Friday 9:00 AM - 3:30 PM; admission $8 (cash only, $3 with SAM Shortline train ticket); 229-874-4786 [Source: [GRTM Website](https://grtm.org/), [Explore Georgia](https://www.exploregeorgia.org/leslie/general/historic-sites-trails-tours/georgia-rural-telephone-museum), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g35062-d4611581-Reviews-Georgia_Rural_Telephone_Museum-Leslie_Georgia.html)]
