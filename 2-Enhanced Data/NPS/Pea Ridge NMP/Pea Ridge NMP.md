# Pea Ridge NMP (National Military Park)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-pea-ridge-national-military-park-visitor-center"></a>**Pea Ridge National Military Park Visitor Center** (15930 National Park Dr, Garfield, Arkansas 72732 (36.4547, -94.0344); Visitor Center: 9:00 AM–5:00 PM daily (closed Tuesdays/Wednesdays mid-November to early March), Park Grounds: 8:00 AM–5:00 PM daily; Closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day; (479) 451-8122) - Passport cancellation stamps are available at the visitor center desk. Call to confirm current stamp availability. [Source: [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/peri/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting.

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-visitor-center-and-museum-experience"></a>**Visitor Center and Museum Experience** (1-1.5 hours) - Explore the comprehensive museum exhibits featuring artifacts and displays about the March 1862 Battle of Pea Ridge, the most pivotal Civil War battle west of the Mississippi River. The visitor center houses a 28-minute orientation film "Thunder in the Ozarks," exhibits updated as of 2010, and one of the finest Civil War book collections in the National Park System. Located at 15930 National Park Dr, Garfield, Arkansas 72732 (36.4547, -94.0344); Visitor Center: 9:00 AM–5:00 PM. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/peri/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

- <a id="activity-self-guided-driving-tour"></a>**Self-Guided Driving Tour** (1.5-2 hours) - A 7-mile scenic auto tour with 11 numbered stops featuring interpretive waysides and monuments commemorating key battlefield locations. A tour app provides short informational videos at each stop. The tour accesses the main areas of the 4,300-acre battlefield. Located at 15930 National Park Dr, Garfield, Arkansas 72732 (36.4547, -94.0344); Park Grounds: 8:00 AM–5:00 PM. [Source: [NPS Outdoor Activities](https://www.nps.gov/peri/planyourvisit/outdooractivities.htm)]

- <a id="activity-pea-ridge-national-military-battlefield-loop-trail"></a>**Pea Ridge National Military Battlefield Loop Trail** (1.5-2 hours) - A 6.5-mile loop trail rated 4.5 stars by 344 AllTrails reviewers, featuring 347 feet of elevation gain. This paved asphalt pathway (12+ feet wide) traverses the battlefield with mostly gentle grades but steeper sections at 1.2, 3.1-3.2, and 4.8-5.0 miles. The trail is accessible for hiking, biking, or jogging and connects to all key park areas including overlooks and cannon positions. Located throughout the 4,300-acre park (36.4547, -94.0344); Park Grounds: 8:00 AM–5:00 PM. [Source: [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/arkansas/pea-ridge-national-military-battlefield-loop)]

- <a id="activity-trail-of-tears-national-historic-trail-segment"></a>**Trail of Tears National Historic Trail Segment** (1-1.5 hours) - Approximately 2.5 miles of the historic Trail of Tears travel through the park, including a large maintained field at Reddick's farm where 10,370 Cherokee camped during the forced relocation. The trail follows the historic road used during the Trail of Tears, offering a meditative walk through preserved landscape. Located at multiple access points throughout the park (36.4547, -94.0344); Park Grounds: 8:00 AM–5:00 PM. [Source: [NPS Trail of Tears](https://www.nps.gov/places/pea-ridge-national-military-park-the-trail-of-tears.htm)]

- <a id="activity-elkhorn-tavern-historic-site-and-exhibit"></a>**Elkhorn Tavern Historic Site and Exhibit** (30-45 minutes) - Visit the reconstructed Elkhorn Tavern, a site of fierce fighting during both days of the Battle of Pea Ridge (March 7-8, 1862). The tavern, located on the old Springfield to Fayetteville Road, features exhibits about the Trail of Tears and serves as a trail access point. A short walking path through new growth forest extends north from the tavern. Located at park interior (36.4547, -94.0344); Park Grounds: 8:00 AM–5:00 PM. [Source: [NPS Trail of Tears](https://www.nps.gov/places/pea-ridge-national-military-park-the-trail-of-tears.htm)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-east-overlook-vista"></a>**East Overlook Vista** (20-30 minutes) - A short 150-yard hike from the Elkhorn Tavern area leads to the park's best panoramic viewpoint, where nearly 50% of the battlefield grounds are visible. On clear days, surrounding communities and Bottom mountains are visible, providing perspective on the scale of the 1862 conflict and the distances soldiers traveled. Described as "one of the most beautiful places on the battlefield." Located within park boundaries (36.4547, -94.0344); Park Grounds: 8:00 AM–5:00 PM. [Source: [NPS East Overlook](https://www.nps.gov/places/east-overlook-at-pea-ridge.htm)]

- <a id="gem-little-sugar-creek-trenches"></a>**Little Sugar Creek Trenches** (45-60 minutes) - An off-the-beaten-path site featuring well-preserved Federal trenches on a bluff overlooking Little Sugar Creek. Visitors can see eroded earthworks that remain from the Civil War era and hike a portion of the historic road used during the Trail of Tears. This location is separate from the main battlefield tour and offers solitude and authentic archaeological interest. Located at park's northern section (36.4547, -94.0344); Park Grounds: 8:00 AM–5:00 PM. [Source: [Park Chasers](https://www.parkchasers.com/2020/04/a-guide-to-hiking-at-pea-ridge-national-military-park/)]

- <a id="gem-williams-hollow-loop-and-hospital-site"></a>**Williams Hollow Loop and Hospital Site** (1-1.5 hours) - A moderate to strenuous 2-mile loop trail starting at Elkhorn Tavern, traversing historic Telegraph Road northward to the Tanyard site and Williams Hollow Field Hospital location—two critical sites during the Battle of Pea Ridge. The trail passes through areas where soldiers experienced intense combat and medical care during the battle. Located at park interior (36.4547, -94.0344); Park Grounds: 8:00 AM–5:00 PM. [Source: [Park Chasers](https://www.parkchasers.com/2020/04/a-guide-to-hiking-at-pea-ridge-national-military-park/)]

- <a id="gem-junior-ranger-program"></a>**Junior Ranger Program** (2-3 hours) - An interactive educational program available for both Pea Ridge National Military Park and the affiliated Trail of Tears National Historic Trail. Children and families complete activities and earn badges while learning about the Civil War and Native American history. Available at the visitor center (15930 National Park Dr, Garfield, Arkansas 72732 (36.4547, -94.0344)); Visitor Center: 9:00 AM–5:00 PM. [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/peri/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-war-eagle-cavern-on-beaver-lake"></a>**War Eagle Cavern on Beaver Lake** (1-1.5 hours, 18-22 miles south) - Arkansas's only lakeside cave entrance features guided 1-hour tours revealing stalactites, stalagmites, fossils, Arkansas quartz crystal, an underground stream with a rimstone dam waterfall room, and viewing areas for 100,000 Arkansas brown and grey bats. Wide, level walkways are pet- and stroller-friendly; the cave maintains a constant 58-degree temperature year-round. Located at 21494 Cavern Dr, Rogers, AR 72756 (36.2954, -93.9047); Open Monday–Sunday 9:30 AM–5:00 PM (March–December; closed winter months). Phone: (479) 789-2909. [Source: [War Eagle Cavern](https://wareaglecavern.com/)]

- <a id="nearby-beaver-lake-recreation-area"></a>**Beaver Lake Recreation Area** (variable duration, 10-15 miles south) - A 28,000-acre man-made lake spanning 73 miles with 450 miles of shoreline offering water sports, world-class bass fishing, hiking (487 miles of trails), swimming, boating, kayaking, and picnicking. Trails include Bench Rock Nature Trail at Indian Creek Park (45 minutes), Lost Bridge Hiking Trail (1-1.5 hours), and Rimrock Trail at Prairie Creek Park (1 hour). Year-round access with no entrance fee. Located throughout Rogers/Eureka Springs area (36.3330, -94.1176); various access points. [Source: [Arkansas.com](https://www.arkansas.com/rogers/outdoor-recreation/beaver-lake)]

- <a id="nearby-daisy-airgun-museum"></a>**Daisy Airgun Museum** (1-1.5 hours, 10 miles south) - A unique museum preserving the history of Daisy airguns (company relocated to Rogers in 1958) featuring 26 display stations in a restored 1896 brick building in downtown Rogers. Self-guided audio tour with electronic wand or QR code options via smartphone explains each exhibit, including antique airguns dating to the 1600s. Located at 202 W. Walnut Street, Rogers, Arkansas 72756 (36.3330, -94.1176); Open Monday–Saturday 9:00 AM–5:00 PM (closed Sundays). Admission: $2 for ages 16+, free for children under 16. Phone: (479) 986-6873. [Source: [Daisy Museum](https://www.daisymuseum.com/)]

- <a id="nearby-tanyard-creek-nature-trail"></a>**Tanyard Creek Nature Trail** (1.5-2 hours, 11-13 miles south) - A scenic 2.2-mile nature trail in Bella Vista built by community volunteers, featuring a cave, pond, overlook of Tanyard Creek Falls, and historic ruins. This relatively easy trail offers beautiful creek scenery and waterfall views. Trailhead located off Highway 340/Lanshire Boulevard less than 1 mile west of Town Center in Bella Vista (36.4678, -94.2670). [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g31437-d8008901-Reviews-Tanyard_Creek_Nature_Trail-Bella_Vista_Arkansas.html)]

## Review / Personal Notes

---

**Total Recommended Time:** 3.5-5 hours for a comprehensive visit to Pea Ridge NMP (including visitor center, driving tour, one major trail or Trail of Tears segment, and viewing East Overlook). This time allows adequate opportunity to experience the museum, learn the battle history, and experience the landscape where the March 7-8, 1862 battle occurred. Visitors seeking more in-depth trail exploration can extend this to 5-6 hours. [Sources: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/peri/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/arkansas/pea-ridge-national-military-battlefield-loop)]
