# Oklahoma City N MEM (National Memorial)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- **Oklahoma City National Memorial Museum** (620 N Harvey Ave, Oklahoma City, OK 73102 (35.4730, -97.5171); Museum: Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday Noon - 5:00 PM; Outdoor Memorial open 24/7; 405-235-3313) - National Park Service passport cancellation stamps available at Memorial Museum Front Desk (ask if not visible). [Source: [NPS Basic Info](https://www.nps.gov/okci/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm), [NPS Hours](https://www.nps.gov/okci/planyourvisit/hours.htm), [Park Ranger John](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/oklahoma-city-national-memorial/)]

**Note:** Call 405-235-3313 to confirm stamp locations before visiting. Outdoor memorial is FREE and open 24/7; Museum requires paid admission.

## Key Activities

- **Outdoor Symbolic Memorial Walk** (30-60 minutes) - Walk through 3.3-acre outdoor memorial open 24/7 featuring Gates of Time (monumental bronze gates framing 9:02), Field of Empty Chairs (168 chairs honoring victims), 318-foot Reflecting Pool, Survivor Tree, Survivor Wall, Rescuers' Orchard, Children's Area with 5,000 hand-painted tiles, and Memorial Fence; powerful self-guided experience; 620 N Harvey Ave; open 24/7/365; FREE [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/okci/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [TravelOK Walk Through](https://www.travelok.com/articles/a-walk-through-the-oklahoma-city-national-memorial-museum), [US News](https://travel.usnews.com/Oklahoma_City_OK/Things_To_Do/Oklahoma_City_National_Memorial_and_Museum_62277/)]

- **Memorial Museum Tour** (1.5-2 hours) - Experience chronological self-guided interactive tour through 10 chapters covering bombing history, archived news footage, survivor stories, recovered artifacts from April 19, 1995 tragedy; housed in Journal Record Building; 620 N Harvey Ave; Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Sunday Noon - 5:00 PM; admission charged [Source: [Memorial Museum](https://memorialmuseum.com/museum/), [Visit OKC](https://www.visitokc.com/listing/oklahoma-city-national-memorial-&-museum/804/), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g51560-d271178-Reviews-Oklahoma_City_National_Memorial_Museum-Oklahoma_City_Oklahoma.html)]

- **Gates of Time Photography** (15-30 minutes) - Photograph monumental bronze gates marking 9:01 (last moments of peace) and 9:03 (first moments of recovery); particularly stunning after dusk when illuminated; excellent photo opportunity; outdoor memorial; open 24/7; FREE [Source: [TravelOK](https://www.travelok.com/articles/a-walk-through-the-oklahoma-city-national-memorial-museum), [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g51560-d271178-Reviews-Oklahoma_City_National_Memorial_Museum-Oklahoma_City_Oklahoma.html)]

- **Field of Empty Chairs Reflection** (20-30 minutes) - Observe 168 bronze, glass, and stone chairs, each etched with a victim's name; arranged in rows representing floors of the building; smaller chairs honor 19 children killed; moving tribute; outdoor memorial; open 24/7; FREE [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/okci/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma_City_National_Memorial)]

- **Survivor Tree Visit** (10-15 minutes) - Visit the American Elm that survived the 1995 bombing, standing as testament to resilience and community; seeds from tree distributed nationwide; inspirational symbol; outdoor memorial; open 24/7; FREE [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/okci/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Visit OKC](https://www.visitokc.com/listing/oklahoma-city-national-memorial-&-museum/804/)]

- **Memorial Fence Tribute** (10-20 minutes) - Leave small items or view tributes at Memorial Fence where visitors continue tradition started after bombing; personal mementos, flowers, notes; touching community tribute; outdoor memorial; open 24/7; FREE [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/okci/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [TravelOK](https://www.travelok.com/articles/a-walk-through-the-oklahoma-city-national-memorial-museum)]

## Hidden Gems

- **Evening/Night Visit** (30-60 minutes) - Visit outdoor memorial after dusk when lights illuminate Gates of Time and Field of Empty Chairs; creates deeply moving atmosphere with fewer crowds; particularly powerful experience; outdoor memorial; accessible 24/7; FREE [Source: [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g51560-d271178-Reviews-Oklahoma_City_National_Memorial_Museum-Oklahoma_City_Oklahoma.html), [US News](https://travel.usnews.com/Oklahoma_City_OK/Things_To_Do/Oklahoma_City_National_Memorial_and_Museum_62277/)]

- **Survivor Wall Exploration** (10-15 minutes) - Study salvaged granite comprising Survivor Wall bearing names of those who survived but were changed forever; often overlooked but meaningful element; outdoor memorial; open 24/7; FREE [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/okci/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [TravelOK](https://www.travelok.com/articles/a-walk-through-the-oklahoma-city-national-memorial-museum)]

- **Children's Area Tiles** (15-20 minutes) - View sampling of 5,000+ hand-painted tiles created by children across US and Canada after bombing; touching display of nationwide compassion; outdoor memorial; open 24/7; FREE [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/okci/planyourvisit/things2do.htm), [Visit OKC](https://www.visitokc.com/listing/oklahoma-city-national-memorial-&-museum/804/)]

- **Rescuers' Orchard** (10-15 minutes) - Thank rescuers and volunteers in dedicated orchard area; honors those who helped relief efforts; peaceful tribute space; outdoor memorial; open 24/7; FREE [Source: [NPS Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/okci/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)]
