# Oklahoma City N MEM (National Memorial)

## Cancellation Stamp Locations

- <a id="stamp-oklahoma-city-national-memorial-museum"></a>**Oklahoma City National Memorial Museum** (620 N. Harvey Ave., Oklahoma City, OK 73102; Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM-5:00 PM, closed Thanksgiving and Christmas; 405-235-3313; Museum admission $15 adults, outdoor memorial free) [[Source](https://memorialmuseum.com/museum/), [Source](https://www.nps.gov/okci/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)]

**Note:** National Park Passport stamps available in memorial museum. The outdoor memorial is open 24 hours daily, 365 days a year. Call to confirm current stamp locations before visiting. [[Source](https://memorialmuseum.com/experience/the-memorial/), [Source](https://www.parkrangerjohn.com/oklahoma-city-national-memorial/)]

## Key Activities

- <a id="activity-outdoor-symbolic-memorial"></a>**Outdoor Symbolic Memorial** (45-60 minutes) - Walk through the memorial standing on the sacred ground where the April 19, 1995 bombing occurred; experience the Gates of Time (9:01 and 9:03) framing the moment of destruction, the Field of Empty Chairs (168 bronze, glass, and stone chairs representing each victim), and the Reflecting Pool filling the corridor between the gates; Outdoor memorial grounds, NW 5th Street between Robinson and Harvey; Open 24/7; Free [[Source](https://memorialmuseum.com/experience/the-memorial/), [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma_City_National_Memorial)]

- <a id="activity-memorial-museum"></a>**Memorial Museum** (1.5-2 hours) - Explore comprehensive exhibits in the former Journal Record Building documenting the bombing, the investigation, trials, and the memorial's creation through artifacts, photographs, recorded testimonies, and interactive displays; experience the moment-by-moment account of April 19, 1995; Museum building, 620 N. Harvey Ave.; Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM-5:00 PM; $15 adults [[Source](https://memorialmuseum.com/museum/), [Source](https://www.visitokc.com/listing/oklahoma-city-national-memorial-&-museum/804/)]

- <a id="activity-survivor-tree"></a>**Survivor Tree** (15-20 minutes) - Visit the American elm tree that survived the 1995 bombing and became a symbol of resilience; read interpretive panels about the tree's significance and view the fence where visitors leave remembrance items; Near outdoor memorial; Open 24/7; Free [[Source](https://www.travelok.com/a-walk-through-the-oklahoma-city-national-memorial-museum), [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma_City_National_Memorial)]

- <a id="activity-children-s-area"></a>**Children's Area** (15-30 minutes) - Reflect at the memorial area dedicated to the 19 children killed in the bombing, featuring chalkboards where young visitors can draw and share their feelings; tiles sent by children from around the world line the walls; Children's Area section of outdoor memorial; Open 24/7; Free [[Source](https://memorialmuseum.com/experience/the-memorial/), [Source](https://www.visitokc.com/listing/oklahoma-city-national-memorial-&-museum/804/)]

- <a id="activity-memorial-museum-audio-app-tour"></a>**Memorial Museum Audio/App Tour** (flexible timing) - Enhance your visit with the official memorial app providing walking tours, interactive content, and suggested routes through both the outdoor memorial and museum; Download app before or during visit; During memorial hours; Free app [[Source](https://memorialmuseum.com/)]

## Hidden Gems

- <a id="gem-rescuers-orchard"></a>**Rescuers' Orchard** (15-20 minutes) - Walk among fruit and flowering trees planted to honor the thousands of rescue workers who responded after the bombing; peaceful area for contemplation; Western edge of outdoor memorial; Open 24/7; Free [[Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma_City_National_Memorial)]

- <a id="gem-9-03-reflection-moment"></a>**9:03 Reflection Moment** (5-10 minutes) - Time your visit to be at the memorial at 9:03 AM or 9:03 PM to stand between the Gates of Time at the exact symbolic moment healing began after the attack; Gates of Time; Open 24/7; Free [[Source](https://memorialmuseum.com/experience/the-memorial/)]

- <a id="gem-memorial-fence"></a>**Memorial Fence** (flexible timing) - View and contribute to the ongoing tradition of leaving remembrance items on the fence surrounding the memorial site; the fence has become an ever-changing display of love, remembrance, and hope; Perimeter of memorial; Open 24/7; Free [[Source](https://www.travelok.com/a-walk-through-the-oklahoma-city-national-memorial-museum)]

## Also Nearby

- <a id="nearby-bricktown-entertainment-district"></a>**Bricktown Entertainment District** (2-4 hours, 1 mile east) - Explore Oklahoma City's revitalized warehouse district featuring restaurants, shops, river walk, canal boat tours, and entertainment venues; Bricktown area, Main Street and Mickey Mantle Drive; Various hours by establishment; Free to explore [[Source](https://www.visitokc.com/listing/oklahoma-city-national-memorial-&-museum/804/)]

- <a id="nearby-myriad-botanical-gardens"></a>**Myriad Botanical Gardens** (1-2 hours, 0.5 miles south) - Walk through 15-acre urban park featuring gardens, walking paths, children's garden, dog park, and the Crystal Bridge Tropical Conservatory; 301 W Reno Ave, Oklahoma City, OK 73102; Park daily 7:00 AM-11:00 PM, Conservatory Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM-5:00 PM; Park free, Conservatory $8 adults [[Source](https://www.myriadgardens.org)]

- <a id="nearby-oklahoma-state-capitol"></a>**Oklahoma State Capitol** (1-2 hours, 2 miles northeast) - Tour the only capitol in the world with active oil wells on its grounds, featuring art, history exhibits, and the working oil derrick on the lawn; 2300 N Lincoln Blvd, Oklahoma City, OK 73105; Monday-Friday 7:00 AM-6:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM-4:00 PM; Free [[Source](https://www.visitokc.com)]

- <a id="nearby-national-cowboy-western-heritage-museum"></a>**National Cowboy & Western Heritage Museum** (2-3 hours, 5 miles north) - Explore world-class collections of Western art, cowboy artifacts, and Native American exhibits celebrating Western heritage; 1700 NE 63rd Street, Oklahoma City, OK 73111; Daily 10:00 AM-5:00 PM; $15 adults [[Source](https://www.visitokc.com)]
